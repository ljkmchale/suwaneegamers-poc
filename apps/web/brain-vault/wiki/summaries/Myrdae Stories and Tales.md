# Myrdae Stories and Tales

Source: [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1cB30vxRCQXjrUt-JV4z8alDVZWvHtqFkYIwhoGlXYJ0/edit)

## Reference

This note represents the Google Doc titled `Myrdae Stories and Tales`, pulled into `raw/myrdae-stories-and-tales.md` on 2026-05-13.

The source is a collection of campaign-agnostic tales passed down across [[Myrdae]]. It belongs to the **World** layer. Use it to support all campaigns, but keep campaign-specific party events separate unless a campaign explicitly intersects with one of these myths.

## What The Source Contains

- The betrayal of Amriel by Di'Belgi and the theft of the [[Crown of Lyess]].
- The arrival of the Blood Wars on Myrdae and the creation of Runeweaving with Yibrix.
- The [[Affirmation of Strife]] and its prophecy of the Year of Rising.
- The forging of dwarves by Muerg through Goldraen.
- The story of Layeth and Brault, including their hidden parentage through Tiash and Smott.
- The founding, collapse, and refounding of [[Dunduar]].
- The rise of Diverra as the Ardent One.
- Osanna's appearance at [[Oldport]] and the origin of the Festival of Fair Winds.
- The Laztyr, Suthrin's release, and the Bistron mine incident.
- Year of Fending hero tales around Nethyndria, Tol'behgekt, Tratta, Guardian's Day, and the final demonic push before the Awakening.
