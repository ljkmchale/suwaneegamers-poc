# Ahndashere Gazetteer

## Scope

World-source summary for `raw/ahndashere-gazetteer.md`. This is campaign-agnostic canon for the marsh-and-hill town [[Ahndashere]] in Baltwood.

## Summary

The Ahndashere Gazetteer describes Ahndashere as a modest town where the [[Northern Stoneflow]] reaches the northern coast of the [[Glimmering Sea]]. Before the Awakening it was a marsh village centered on a stone shrine to Torec. After the Glimmering Sea expanded and swallowed much of the old shoreline, the settlement adapted by rebuilding lowland homes on stilts and boardwalks while many families moved into the dry central hills.

Ahndashere is socially split but interdependent: boardwalk marshfolk, pilots, sailors, reed cutters, crabbers, salt workers, and smugglers share the town with hillstead halflings whose burrows and gardens provide stability. The town's traditions are practical and seasonal, including Harvest Rest, pebble offerings to the Stoneflow before travel, Osanna ribbon charms, and townwide lantern-lighting during storms.

Government rests on the appointment of a Lord or Lady by the elder every six years. Elder Myranda Fenberry, a century-old halfling shaman, has held spiritual authority since the Awakening. She appointed Lord Hommof Tsuldor, a young fisherman, four years before the source's present frame. Captain Hester Rinby leads the small guard force.

The source includes Ahndashere's laws, economy, religious life, major NPCs, and a partially blank city-map scaffold. Its later shop and service reference sections appear to be reusable templates rather than populated Ahndashere locations.
