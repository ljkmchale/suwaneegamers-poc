# Basctdelm Gazetteer

## Scope

World-source summary for `raw/basctdelm-gazetteer.md`. This is campaign-agnostic canon for [[Basctdelm]], capital of Halesworth and imperial seat of the Bathaen Empire.

## Summary

The Basctdelm Gazetteer describes Basctdelm as a fortified capital on a massive island along the eastern edge of [[Lake Tribathe]], where the [[Dibaryn River]] meets the lake before flowing toward the Broken Deep. The city is deliberately isolated, defensible, and controlled through two major crossings: the Star Reach Bridge toward Wheldrake and the Historic Crossway Bridge into Halesworth.

Basctdelm rises from the Trade District at the water's edge through lower and interior districts to the High District on the northwestern heights. Its geography reinforces imperial power: commerce enters through regulated docks and customs halls, while noble estates, law centers, towers, walls, and ruling institutions occupy the elevated city.

The city's history begins as a neutral seat of order administered by appointed regents and custodial governors. Power later shifted to the Compact Lineages, then to House Talward, whose consolidation unified Basctdelm while leaving old noble grievances under the surface. King Irion Talward helped form the Bathaen Empire through a pact with Palason and Qal'dynn. After the Awakening and the assassination of King Moldan Talward, Queen Breya Talward rebuilt imperial unity through negotiation and restraint.

The gazetteer includes Basctdelm's districts, government, law, Watch, Weave Wardens, service availability, map locations, and several notable establishments, including Braethyn's Archives, Double Mice Inn, Last Prince Inn, Monbereth's Armory, Strongbow, Talward Garrison, The Promissory, and the Vault of Basctdelm.
