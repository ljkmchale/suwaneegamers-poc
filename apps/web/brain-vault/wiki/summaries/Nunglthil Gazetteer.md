# Nunglthil Gazetteer

## Scope

World-source summary for `raw/nunglthil-gazetteer.md`. This is campaign-agnostic Underdark settlement canon.

## Summary

The Nunglthil Gazetteer describes Nunglthil as a neutral Underdark trading enclave on the black shores of Rothenloch. It is a reliable crossing point between surface merchants and Underdark cultures because five enclaves uphold the Pact of Neutrality: Gral'thune, Selraeth, Kar'Solar, Durrak, and Glimmerdel.

Nunglthil began during the Demon Wars as a refuge on Rothenloch. Ulbin, duergar, svirfneblin, drow, and later surface traders survived by suspending rivalries and defending the cavern together. After the demonic threat broke, the stronghold became a neutral trade hub. The old Refuge Pact became the social and legal foundation of the settlement.

The settlement is carved into the cliffside bordering Rothenloch and organized in four layers: a busy dock-and-market main layer, a residential second layer, a controlled third layer holding higher-status residences and the Pact Council Chamber, and a fourth-layer lookout above the lake.

Law is pragmatic and rooted in neutrality. Violence, sabotage, destabilizing theft, and actions that endanger trade are punished by restitution, loss of Favor, trade sanctions, forced labor, exile, or, rarely, execution. Each enclave governs its own interior matters, while the Pact Council handles shared spaces and pact violations.

## Source Notes

- The source uses both Glimmerdel and occasional Glimerdel/Nungylthil variants. The wiki normalizes to Glimmerdel and Nunglthil except when naming map labels exactly.
- The source spells the drow administrative location as `Seat of Salraeth`; the enclave is otherwise Selraeth. The map label is preserved, with a note on the Nunglthil page.
