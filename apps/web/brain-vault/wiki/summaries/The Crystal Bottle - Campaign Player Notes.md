# The Crystal Bottle — Campaign Player Notes

**Setting:** Arbescar (part of Myrdae)
**Campaign period:** May 2023 – November 2025 (Sessions 0 through 7.3)
**Home base:** Arbescar Light and Magic (ALM), Tal'basar

---

## What Is This Campaign?

The Crystal Bottle centers on a group of adventurers who stumble upon a mysterious crystal bottle inscribed in Terran: "Death to whomever frees him. Wishes to whomever puts him back." The bottle once contained a D'jinn King, and the party is now entangled in the fallout of its opening — a resurgent cult, a corrupted immortality-seeker, a chained angel, and divine powers awakening across the land.

The party is affiliated with Arbescar Light and Magic (ALM), a magical institution in Tal'basar, and has been tasked by its Council of Six with investigating and stopping the A'urea'Pura Cult.

## Major Plot Threads

1. **The Crystal Bottle & the D'jinn** — The D'jinn King was freed; the bottle must be used to put him back. The bottle's inscription warns of death to the freer and wishes to whoever re-imprisons him.
2. **The A'urea'Pura Cult** — An air genasi cult led (now demonically) by Malik. They seek to reach the celestial city (the Lost City, Athralis) and harness the ancient portal there.
3. **Malik and Kal'dar** — Kal'dar (Fizban's former apprentice) made a deal with the D'jinn for immortality but was hollowed out and became Malik's vessel. Malik now claims heavenly power.
4. **Angel Aurelion** — Chained in an unstable golden-white portal in the Lost City, powered by two cracked blue godstones. Yvaine spoke with him in Celestial: get the key from Malik's neck to free him.
5. **Harlowe the Witch** — Yvaine's mother; an enemy of the party. Located in the Bathaen Empire.
6. **Alteya's Lost Family** — Her husband and child have been missing for 25 years. The party has her husband's journal.
7. **Tass's Legacy** — Tass died saving Angela in Kravwynhold. His body needs to be returned to his family in Mahruud.
8. **The Awakening** — The old god Muerg appeared at Everlight and declared his protection over the city, announcing "The Awakening has begun."

## Current Status (as of Session 7.3)

The party is inside the Lost City (Athralis) in a chamber with walls pulsing blue light. Malik has stepped out of an unstable portal with an angel (Aurelion) chained inside. Malik has revealed Kal'dar's deal with the D'jinn and claims heavenly power. The party is attempting to negotiate while Yvaine seeks the key around Malik's neck.
