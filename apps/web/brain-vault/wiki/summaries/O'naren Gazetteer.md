# O'naren Gazetteer

## Scope

World-source summary for `raw/onaren-gazetteer.md`. This is campaign-agnostic canon for the elven settlement [[O'naren]] in the Borealian Sway.

## Summary

The O'naren Gazetteer describes O'naren as a small elven settlement in the wooded foothills west of Qal'dynn. It serves as a waypoint for travelers and a quiet center of contemplation, craftsmanship, mining, art, and Fralee-centered religious practice.

O'naren is built around Driftglow Pond, a circular body of water fed by cascading falls. A small island at the pond's center holds an ancient tree with pink foliage, believed to be a relic of the Qal'daefar. The town's finely carved stone and wood buildings blend with the gray-green forest and terraced foothills.

The town began as an elven defensive outpost west of Qal'dynn. Mining, hunting, and local resources helped it grow into a respected settlement in the Borealian Sway. It is ruled by Lord Itham Elle'neros, loyal to High King Artes Highvale of Qal'dynn, and protected by Realmwardens led by Captain Salaal Windseek.

The gazetteer includes the town's traditions, government, economy, Fralee worship, map locations, and major services, including Evonstride Stables, the Hall of O'naren, the Temple of Tranquility, Nestling Soar, the Truffle Trap, the Oracle, Ore Masters, the mines, the Overlook, Welthas' Market, and Vocehna's Gallery.
