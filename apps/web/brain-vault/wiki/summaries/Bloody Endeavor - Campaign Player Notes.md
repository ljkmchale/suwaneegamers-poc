## The Big Picture

Three interlocking threats drive the campaign:

1. **The Cold Breath (Monks of Iohir)** — led by Master Huldi, seeking to assemble the three pieces of the **Rimestone** to cause a new ice age. Their original intent was to re-seal an ancient eldritch undead aberration accidentally awakened by dwarven dark magic in the Yag'vur Sierra. Dwarven mages created the Rimestone to freeze the aberration, then broke it into three pieces. The Cold Breath's plan would freeze much of the world and kill countless people.

2. **The Eldritch Threat (The Awakened Aberration)** — an undead horror sealed under the Yag'vur Sierra mountains by the original Rimestone. The Awakening disturbed its prison. It has begun affecting the minds of those nearby, particularly the Duergar of Khagdaruhl through nightmares. Albross received a vision from his patron of tendrils binding the Dwarven King.

3. **The Rostiena / Dalasor War** — the Malithea Empire (Dalasor Coast) is building armies along the border of the Rostiena Foothills. Witguard, a defended frontier town, is the flashpoint. Magistrate Oddnel sent the party to recruit the Runegrip dwarves and the dwarven kingdom of Khagdaruhl to the Rostiena side. The Dalasor Coast has sent their own ambassadors to Khagdaruhl.

## Where We Are (End of Session 35)

The party (now Albross, Caelion, Lucerion, Pagern, Rhody) is in **Khagdaruhl** (KAG-DA-RRULE) for a private audience with **Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV**. The Lord has heard: that Huldi took the Runegrip's piece of the Rimestone; that an eldritch threat lurks under the mountains; and that the party represents Witguard's Magistrate. The conversation is ongoing. Session 35 ends mid-audience.
