# Myrdae World Guide

Source: [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PGWzoocfjPNQ69Q-JsVmNXCFo76a3Z_IkcBuBeDj4yQ/edit)

## Reference

This note represents the Google Doc titled `Myrdae World Guide`, pulled into `raw/myrdae-world-guide.md` on 2026-05-13.

The source is a campaign-agnostic world guide for [[Oberra]] and [[Myrdae]]. It is narrated in-world by Avant Sinestia Thornage and should be treated as world canon unless a newer world source supersedes it.

## What The Source Contains

- Introductory overview of [[Myrdae]] and the role of Avants as scholars, travelers, and historians.
- World-setting differences from baseline Dungeons & Dragons assumptions.
- Player-facing species lore for dwarves, elves, gnomes, halflings, humans, [[Species of Myrdae|Gabuk]], and [[Species of Myrdae|Ulbin]].
- The planet [[Oberra]], its known continents, the moons Endelo'ar and Tudara, the Broken One, and Lunar Facets.
- The five major regions of Myrdae: Bathaes, Inverland, Jehsle, Orbansia, and Var'Shala.
- The Harmon Order calendar, seasons, All-bright, Nocturn, and calendar terminology.
- The pantheon of Myrdae, including the Assembly of Essence / Old Gods and the New Order.
- World-level organizations and factions, including Advents of Harmony, Anders, Cloak of Defiance, Everdawn, Meridian, and Tegenwald Phet.
- Appendix tables for recommended species, territories, settlements, and featured areas.

## Current Read

This source clarifies that Myrdae should be organized as five major regions rather than the older unresolved "three landmasses" note. It also confirms that [[Oberra]] has multiple continents and that Myrdae's calendar is tied to the paired moons Endelo'ar and Tudara.

For campaign separation, this source belongs to the **World** layer. It can support all campaigns, but it should not cause campaign-specific people, events, or party knowledge to bleed between campaigns.
