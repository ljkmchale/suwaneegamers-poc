## Reference

This note represents the Google Doc titled `Dungeons III - Campaign Player Notes`.

The source is the player-facing campaign tracker for Dungeons III. It contains the party roster, passive scores, session attendance, ten session notes, current goals, NPC scaffolding, and campaign-prep material.

## What The Source Contains

- Party roster for [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Nova]], and [[Aeon]].
- Character reference basics: player, class, species, AC, passive Perception/Investigation/Insight, senses, defenses, languages, and deity.
- Session notes from Session 01 `It's Eye'on!` through Session 10 `It's a Long Ass Way`.
- The opening Hesfal arc: Salvus crowds, the Covered Hooch, Hall of Many Hands, Boar's Best, the murder rumor, tavern robbery, escaped prisoner, K9 Crew, and the Scorpion message.
- Scarwatch Hold arc: prisoner delivery, Outer Gate mimic, Warden Haliun, Nambra, Umlin's mapping job, Radiant Eye defacement, Isgo lead, and the Osmanae celestial instrument lead.
- Active goals: bring in the fugitive/bandits, find Isgo, investigate the Radiant Eye cult, map the area around Scarwatch Hold, and recover the celestial instrument from an ancient Osmanae outpost.

## Current Read

Dungeons III is early in its campaign. The party has formed around Hesfal, earned its first reputation by stopping the Boar's Best robbery and capturing the K9 Crew, then moved into Scarwatch Hold as a recognized problem-solving group called [[The kNight Watch]]. The current arc has the party tracking Isgo and possible Radiant Eye cultists into the crags east of Scarwatch Hold, where they have just encountered robed attackers.

- The [[Scorpion Network]] hired the [[K9 Crew]] to terrorize Hesfal during Salvus, then contacted the party through the blue sending stone after the K9 Crew failed.
- [[Nova's Dunduar Brooch]] is magical and carries a warning not to separate stone from lattice.
- The [[Radiant Eye Cult]] defaced the Scarwatch Hold Sanctuary and may have an outpost in the eastern crags.
