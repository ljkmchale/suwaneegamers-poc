# Emberstran Gazetteer

## Scope

World-source summary for `raw/emberstran-gazetteer.md`. This is campaign-agnostic city canon for [[Emberstran]], not a record of Heroes of Emberstran party events.

## Summary

The Emberstran Gazetteer describes Emberstran as a coastal city in eastern Orbansia on the Glimmering Sea, south of the Severed Crease and near Mount Emberstran. The city began as Winbalt before the Awakening. When Mount Emberstran erupted and the Glimmering Sea expanded, much of the Baltwood Forest was consumed or transformed; refugees gathered in Winbalt, and the city took the name Emberstran in memory of the volcano and as a beacon on the changed coast.

Emberstran is divided between a busy southern port district and a wealthier elevated northern district. The port holds warehouses, docks, taverns, working-class housing, and fog-heavy maritime commerce. The northern city holds manors, gardens, broad roads, the Lord's Keep, temples, and wealthy civic life. The city is protected by walls, while vineyards, farmland, rural homes, and trade routes spread beyond its gates.

The city is governed by an elected Lord or Lady Protector and a Council of Five. In 1246 AF, the Lord Protector is Lord Garod Dusklight. The council offices are Magistrate, Collector, Advisor, Minister, and Hand. Emberstran has no standing army or formal city militia; enforcement is handled by hired mercenary bands coordinated by the Lord Protector and appointed captains.

Emberstran's economy rests on the port, coastal trade, wine, fertile volcanic soil, shipbuilding, fishing, and guild politics. The Vintner's Guild, Shipbuilder's Guild, and Fisher's Consortium are the most important professional guilds. Religious life includes many shrines, with special local emphasis on Crael, Osanna, and Diverra.
