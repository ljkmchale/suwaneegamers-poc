## Reference

This note represents the Google Doc titled `Abbey of Light`, pulled into `raw/abbey-of-light.md` on 2026-05-13.

The source is a campaign-agnostic world location gazetteer for [[Abbey of Light]], also historically known as the Abbey of Mont Rest.

## What The Source Contains

- Overview and history of the Abbey of Mont Rest / Abbey of Light.
- Its location south of O'naren, atop a foothill descending from the Distancion Mountains into the Borealian Sway.
- Founding as a sanctuary to Neera and later expansion into a multi-faith center for all gods of Myrdae.
- The Four Orders: Nurturing Light, Protective Light, Reflective Light, and Rising Light.
- The Monks of Lighted Breath, including breath discipline, herbcraft, attire, Silent Breath observance, and shop goods.
- The Stone Giant pact and the giants' ongoing guardianship.
- Abbey laws, economy, religion, attire, holidays, map locations, NPCs, meals, specialty items, and storage details.

## Current Read

This is a World-layer source. It should be available to all campaigns as a canonical location, but campaign-specific events at the abbey should be recorded on campaign pages if any party later visits it.
