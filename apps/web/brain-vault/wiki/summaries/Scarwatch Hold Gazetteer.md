# Scarwatch Hold Gazetteer

## Scope

World-source summary for `raw/scarwatch-hold-gazetteer.md`. This is campaign-agnostic Scarwatch Hold canon. Dungeons III party actions, judgments, discoveries, and open quests remain on the Dungeons III location page and related campaign pages.

## Summary

The Scarwatch Hold Gazetteer describes Scarwatch Hold as a repaired border keep rising from the Jagged Waste Crags. It is built from pale stone, repaired in darker stone where the Awakening damaged walls, towers, and parapets. The hold is functional rather than grand: drill yards, lookout platforms, gatehouses, a citadel, stables, shops, a bank, a sanctuary, an inn, and defensive palisades.

Scarwatch Hold has always been a border keep, not a seat of power. Its purpose is vigilance: watching movement through the crags, guarding the eastern approaches of the Yearning Vale, and providing a warning before danger spills west. It was damaged during the Awakening but never abandoned.

The Warden governs from the Citadel. The source names Warden Naevaris Haliun, advisor Nambra Maerth, advisor and scribe Umlin Stonewatch, guard captain Nikalia, outer gate guards Lynion and Atreise, stable owner Ireosh Barridge, barn hand Lyerith Lilbuck, banker Thiddle Ferndew, and palisade guards Thak and Dempsey.

The gazetteer includes a location map for the Hold: Outer Gatehouse, Barn & Stables, Private Apartments, Smithy, Ilho's Gems, General Goods, Mercantile, Bank, Trader's Market, The Hoot & Howl Tavern, Sanctuary, Watchtower Inn, Scarwatch House, Inner Gatehouse, Palisades, and Citadel.
