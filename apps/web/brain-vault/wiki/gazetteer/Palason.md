# Overview

Palason is the northern capital of the **Frystarian Strand**, set inland amid cold forests and broad fields rather than upon the coast it governs. Though the territory borders both the **Broken Deep** to the northeast and the **High Sea** to the northwest, the city itself stands removed from salt air, positioned where land routes converge and where fertile ground can still be coaxed from a short growing season. Its location reflects intent over convenience—chosen for stability, control, and endurance rather than trade winds and harbors.

The city is dense and inward-looking, with older districts clustered tightly around its core and newer growth spreading unevenly where terrain allows. Streets wind and overlap rather than follow strict planning, shaped by centuries of rebuilding and adaptation to climate. Stone and dark timber dominate the skyline, giving Palason a weighty, grounded presence that feels prepared for long winters and harder years.

Palason carries the quiet authority of a city that has remained while the world around it shifted. It is neither ornate nor ostentatious, favoring permanence over display. To visitors, Palason may seem reserved and austere; to its people, it is dependable and resolute—a steady heartland capital that reflects the disciplined character of the Frystarian Strand itself.
