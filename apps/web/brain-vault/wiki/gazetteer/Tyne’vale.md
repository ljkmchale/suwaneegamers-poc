# Overview

 

## 

## Tyne’valé sits just to the southeast of the massive Hazing Trudge swamplands.  This was one of the greener areas of old Mahruud, but is now well overgrown.  Structures are newly built in this town to resemble old elven architecture.  Ruins of previous buildings still lie about the town, representing its heritage.

## History

Mere miles away from the massive swamplands known as the Hazing Trough, Tyne’valé was a small town that lived as one with nature.  The fine stone architecture of its elven heritage sat on a high hill and blended peacefully with the land.  One could pass by the structures with barely a notice.  Although predominantly inhabited by elves, other species called the Tyne’valé its home.

Legend has it that an old green wyrm, Theldratha the Bitter lived for decades in the Hazing Trough. This great wyrm was long revered for never bothering the people of Tyne’valé.  After years, the people of Tyne’valé adopted the heraldry of a green dragon head in respect for the peace that stood between the people of Tyne’valé and Theldratha. 

Stories and tales were written on the numerous aerial battles between Theldratha and the bronze wyrm Annagos.  Annagos seemed to prevail in many fights and Theldratha went to the people of Tyne'valé to gain an upper hand.  When the people refused, the green dragon destroyed the town and its people.  This act went unknown by the people of Myrdae, as it occurred the same time as the awakening ravaged the southwestern portion of the Mahruud.

Theldratha was successful in securing aid from other unknown forces and ultimately was responsible for the death of Annagos.  Theldratha’s unannounced aid dishonored traditions of the Myrdaen wyrms.  For this act, Karalafene Whiteflare (an Ancient Silver) descended from the Distancion Mountains, subdued and imprisoned Theldratha the Bitter beneath the ruins of Tyne’valé.  The people of Myrdae have been unaware of the prison that Theldratha was confined to.

Baron Manius Reiter found the ruins of Tyne’valé and chose to resurrect the town anew.  He found the long honored symbol of the town, the green dragon’s head, and chose to continue the use of the heraldry.
