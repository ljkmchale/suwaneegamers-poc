# Adsuren

# Sections

# **Overview**

Adsuren is a small town in a wooded valley underneath **Mount Ethea** and borders on the **Wehgyn River**. It functions as a humble spot for those who call it home and a traveling spot for those who wish to get to the Eustera Ridge and don’t wish to go by the coast or up through Gibuldon. The village is littered with small buildings that branch off the river and form around a large open space where the town houses a market. 

Open and welcoming to all sorts of people, Adsuren houses a diverse crowd of different races, but still predominantly shows off humans and elf-kind. The village has seen great prosperity recently under the rule of **Intendant Eyovar Dunajor**, a noble who is part of the Camburne Vec. Under his eye, the village has seen a surge of growth with the local fruit, the Precura Fruit, that is only found in this valley. The **Precura** fruit is a small, cool orange-colored fruit that hangs from large deciduous trees, and is like a mix between an apple and a pomegranate. Deemed a delicacy around Inverland, it is the town's primary source of trade. 

Additionally, a contributing factor to the recent spike of interest in Adsuren is from **Mollywap the Magnificent**. Mollywap was a high ranking mage within the [**Tegenwald Phet**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PGWzoocfjPNQ69Q-JsVmNXCFo76a3Z_IkcBuBeDj4yQ/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.3s2gej7lorq), a hero across Inverland, and a close ally and advisor to the Chief of the Camburne Vec. In more recent times, she has retired from the Phet and frequents her hometown of Adsuren to participate in its celebrated Market Day. 

Adsuren offers a safe and appealing environment for both residents and travelers, known for its warm community, tasty cuisine, and charming local market.

## **History**

Adsuren was originally settled as a few small houses of people who were attracted by a unique fruit growing in the valley. The natural defenses and resources, including a mountain to the west and a river to the east, facilitated rapid growth into a permanent settlement. The ever present trees, water, and fruit, meant that in only a few short years, people migrating from Caldwyn set up shop and did not leave. 

The village's true legacy, however, began to unfold in the 1210s with the rise of a promising young mage. As she grew into a powerful sorceress, her adventures brought fame to Adsuren when she, along with a band of adventurers, helped defeat a powerful fey spirit in Loch Cerule that was harming the cities on its water, including the capital of Gibuldon. During her heroic deeds, she shared the fruit from the valley and it became a widespread delicacy, which eventually drew in chefs and farmers who wished to cultivate and keep this fruit alive. Since much time has passed, the village remains a humble size, but still sees frequent travelers who come for the fruit, and stay for the people who make this place home.

### **Style and Aesthetic**

The people of Adsuren favor comfortable, humble clothing over a specific style, though a few citizens can afford to wear fine silks or furs. Men and women alike typically wear simple tunics and pants or large skirts. In colder weather, they add cloaks, coats, or drapes made from local furs. Wool from [**gertot**](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5643255-gertot) and lambs, often dyed in the town's signature violet and pastel yellow—colors inspired by the valley's famous fruit—is a luxury reserved for the affluent.

The town's buildings are constructed from the warm brown wood of the nearby Celedon forest, accented with dark stone from the mountain and intertwined with vines from the fruit trees that define the region, the Precura Fruit.

## **Holidays and Traditions**

The only tradition that is celebrated by the town is the Market Day which is celebrated once a stretch.

## **Government**

Adsuren is governed by **Intendant Eyovar Dunajor**, a mature man in his 40s who maintains a "man of the people" approach to leadership, regularly engaging with citizens to understand their needs. Despite his humble demeanor, he does not hesitate to exercise authority when necessary. A local man raised by the church, he now raises his own family in the community he leads. His intimate, personal style defines Adsuren's governance, which prioritizes the warmth and well-being of every resident. 

To maintain order among the constant flow of travelers, the Adsuren Watch is led by **Captain Alinia Sarralei**.

### **Adsuren Watch**

The Adsuren Watch is a simple village guard tasked with overseeing the community and providing defense for the village when needed.

## **Economy and Trade**

Adsuren’s economy was developed from the gathering of the abundant natural resources in the valley and the trade of the Precura fruit that grows here, allowing it to sustain itself through a continuation of mining, chopping, and gathering. The valley provides plenty of wood, stone, and metal for the artisans of the town to use. This allows Adsuren to be quite self-sufficient, and the fruit adds to economic growth as a primary source of gold trade that comes up the river. 

## **Religion**

Adsuren welcomes all religious viewpoints equally as long as everyone in the community contributes to the health and good fortune of others. With the village’s abundance of natural life blossoming all around, and the ever present Precura fruit, the village does have a soft spot for Fralee, who promotes tranquility, and Villari, who promotes nature. 

The oratory near the center of town serves as a meeting spot for all religious worshipers. The church’s main hall has statues of both goddesses but it also has many rooms that can be reserved to pray to all gods. Visitors who are new to town or seeking religious enlightenment can turn to Tanluar Rotvori (half-elf) or his few acolytes who tend to gardens and other natural spots around the town.

## **Adsuren Center**

The Adsuren Center is at the physical center of the village, though the market feels like the real heart of power for the village. Intendant Eyovar Dunajor rules over the town from here with a couple of assistants. The main building has a lobby, a small library, a centralized meeting area, a few smaller meeting areas for the public, and some back rooms that are reserved for officials who meet for important matters.  The daily affairs of the Center are managed by assistant Jerborin Omakian, who is a semi pompous high elf who reports only to the Intendant and the Captain and manages how laws by the Intendant are put into place.

## **Docks**

Adsuren’s location on the edge of a large river necessitates massive docks. Remarkably, these structures have remained in high perfect condition since their construction at the settlement's inception. There is enough space at the docks for 4 large ships to dock, but there is usually a ship that travels the width of the river on a daily basis to supply the town with its much needed lumber from the **Celedon Forest**. There is also an occasional ship dedicated to travelers that goes up and down the river. The dock is run by Dock Master MacCaanain.

## **Falling Fish, The**

The Falling Fish is the local tavern for the village folk of Adsuren. It is a humble establishment that serves customers on the first floor, while the second floor is where the workers and owners sleep. Stepping into the Falling Fish, one immediately notices that a completely round bar dominates the center of the room. The dark wood interior is dimly lit by a plethora of orange lanterns or wide candle sticks. In the left foremost corner of the room, there is a large statue of a fish descending down a moving waterfall. Children and other superstitious folk often throw coins of various rarities in the small pond that the waterfall makes, but the coins are “magically” gone by the next stretch. The Fish, as it is commonly known, is run by the Carlee family, where Loisa Carlee, the matriarch, runs the bar, and her husband and children do other work around the tavern.

## **Guard Post**

Positioned near the heart of town, the Adsuren Guard Post is a sizable structure with a defensive tower built off to one side. The building itself serves as a meeting place for the Watch, as well as a small armory, a barracks, and housing for the Captain of the Watch, Captain Alinia Sarralei. To the side of the building, is a stone and wood tower that serves as a good watchpoint with a prison for criminals underneath.

## **Mage Tower**

This small tower was built around the 1210’s by an aspiring young mage. It sits just outside the town proper as a 3-story structure. Its first floor is what is seen by guests of the tower and it has a small kitchen and resting area. The other floors, while not frequently seen, are presumed to be a bedroom/study for the mage. In times when the mage is not around, the tower's door seems to be shut and locked by a powerful enchantment.

## **Market**

The Adsuren Market is the centerpiece of the village and what draws in most of the people who visit this humble settlement. The open-air venue is illuminated by lanterns suspended from the sparsely distributed trees, providing ample light to keep the space lively. The path is a mix of durable cobblestone and tired grass, worn down from heavy foot traffic. The market changes each stretch, but it usually houses many different tents or stalls from both the village folk and strangers who come to sell their wears. The market offers a wide variety of goods, including foods, services, and general merchandise. At the center of the market lies a large communal well, and at the edge of the market, there is a small pond where a few village fish are taken care of by the Adsuren folk. There are usually 6–8 fish in the pond at any given time.

## **Nature’s Oratory**

Nature’s Oratory is an old temple that was dedicated to the goddess of life before the Awakening when Adsuren was first settled. It was built before the market was as prominent, but still stands today. The Oratory is constructed from the dark stone sourced from the Western Mountain and roofed with deep purple, dyed clay tiles. Its exterior is covered in carefully maintained vines, and a beautiful garden borders each side of the building. A simple graveyard is situated behind the Oratory.

The Oratory’s layout is a large hall, flanked with arches that lead into various smaller rooms. A large, arched opening dominates the center, featuring a faded mural that seems to be a dancing druidic figure amidst a sea of flowers. Large braziers flank the arch's base; they remain barely lit, yet provide enough light to bathe the entire interior in a warm orange glow. 

Tanluar Rotvori, the “head” priest of sorts, helps advise all religious happenings. He and his few acolytes tend to the Oratory and the gardens outside. Each of the acolytes knows a bit about each god within the pantheon, but their specific devotion is reserved for either Vallari or Fralee.

## **Rugged Rooster, The**

The Rugged Rooster is a general store that sits as close to the river as any building could. The building's older design beautifully showcases the remarkable wonders a determined person can create through sheer hard work. The once proud, vibrant wooden walls are now dulled and grown over with moss and vines. Various sections of the roof have been replaced over time, resulting in an uneven, patchwork appearance where the materials don't entirely match. Outfront, a rusted metal rooster—seemingly older than the building and held up only by willpower—is mounted above the sign. Inside, the store is cozy and stocked with every basic necessity, but it appears seldom used since the village’s market offers all the same items.

## **Spider’s Silk**

The Spider’s Silk is an elegant dark stone and dark roofed building built beneath the shade of two large willow-like trees. Multiple threads string out between the trees and the building, holding various lanterns that cast a faint purple glow, shining beautifully once night falls. Inside the shop, a large room serves as the meeting place between Anabu and his customers. He has an eye for detail, can spot a person's dimensions from a glance, and he seemingly has the uncanny ability to be almost always right, even when the individual is clothed under multiple layers. Despite his elegant demeanor, he primarily offers common clothing; however, he is also capable of crafting custom garments from the selection of fine wools and silks he keeps in stock.
