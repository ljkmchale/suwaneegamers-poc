# Overview

Sitting on the coast of the Sea of Sondera on the western portions of the Rostiena Foothills, Stoneshore is the premier city of trade in Orbansia.

## History

## Citizenry

There are around 125,000 people living in StoneShore, most of the population is within the city border, some are outside the walls in nearby farms. Different segments of the population live in wards, there are 6 Wards:

1. the Noble Ward  
2. the North Ward  
3. the East Ward  
4. the South Ward  
5. the Trade Ward  
6. the Common Ward

### **Races**

Stoneshore is a very diverse city, comprising of: humans (30%), half-orcs (10%), elves (20%), dwarfs (20%), weefolk (10%), other (10%)

## Government

Stoneshore functions as a monarchy, ruled by King Erdmann Giantsbane.  The king is accompanied by his queen (wife) and prince (son).

## Laws

Stoneshore is a city with strict laws and quick to bring justice.  Characters engaging in criminal activity are arrested by the Stoneshore watch and charged per the [City Ordinances](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BGx_-fz7LsgElP6Lk2RFbh2-RRFTnOhx7RJ15ymoShs/edit#heading=h.9ltjapyzb7pv).  Once charged, one is brought before a magister for judgment.

## Economy and Trade

Being a large trading point for western Orbansia, Stoneshore has access to most goods and services that a large city has to offer.

## Professional Guilds

## Religion

All gods are worshiped in Stoneshore. There are 5 big churches here dedicated to: **Coralei, Muerg, Addan, Tyvarian,** and **Layeth**.  However, with enough searching, smaller religious sites and places of worship to any god can be found.

## None for Noone (alchemist)

## 

## The large round building has a purple shingle roof and 3 chimneys, which all are seemingly just for decoration.  There is a large black wood plank over the entrance that has seen some years, but in fresh white paint reads “None for Noone”.  Upon looking closer, the paint covers the apparent original name that is etched deep into the wood but is very difficult to make out.

Once the second most famous magic shop in all of Myrdae, The Magic Emporium, it was sold off when its previous owners wanted to go out adventuring.  They struck a bet with a gabük, and when he lost, he took over the large round building and has owned it since. The previous name of the establishment is etched in arcane magic and can’t be removed … although the sign could be.

The owner is Noone Giax (pronounced noo͞n), an old alchemist.  He has turned the place into one that suits his specialty, offering potions for sale.
