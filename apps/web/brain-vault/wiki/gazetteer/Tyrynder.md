# Overview

## History

2 and a half centuries ago, a powerful wizard, Drest Ver’rader, had full dominion over a small hunting village south of Stoneshore, when inhabitants started going missing and trade to the village ceased, the king of Stoneshore at the time (name tbd) sent one of his finest Knights, Captain Elidar Tyryn, and a squadron of soldiers to figure out the situation and apprehend Ver’rader. At the behest of his king Captain Tyryn marched down the coast and came across the village, where it was abandoned, destroyed, and all seemed desolate except for a lone tower in the center of the rubble. Taking heed of the possible threats, Captain Tyryn launched an assault on the village when the moon fell from the sky, a strange occurrence that happens in this village every so often where it looks like the two moons are gone, but they are both only showing their glow off of the horizons, masking the sky in a swirl of blue and red. With this battle, Captain Tyryn and his men stormed towards the tower where they were ambushed by a horde of undead. While his men held off the undead, Captain Tyryn made his way through the tower, finding many torture and prison cells where many villagers were being held captive. He freed them all and made his way to the top where he engaged in a fierce battle with Ver’rader. The deadly fight ensued and at its climax, Captain Tyryn laid down his life in order to slay the wizard. With the wizard’s defeat, the tower collapsed and the undead turned to corpses, and from the markings of that battle, a town was built up. As one last guard against threats of undeath and to support priests who were at the battle, four churches were made in each of the quadrants of the town.

Over time, the town had grown in size and support was shown for Stoneshore, who had come to its aid to free the civilians. Nowadays, it has blossomed into a large settlement where it has ease of access to lumber from Ruvaenwood and the animals that live in the trees. It is run by Tyryn’s great great great grandchild, Vuduin Tyryn, who strives for good as Elidar once did.

## Citizenry

There are around 3,750 people in the Town of Tyrynder.

### **Races**

Humans (35%), Half-races (20%), Dwarf (15%), Weefolk (15%), Other (5%)

### **Holidays and Traditions**

They follow normal traditions that Stoneshore’s territory holds (i.e. holidays) but they also hold a parade for the battle of Moonfall, where Captain Tyryn led an assault on a Wizard Tower and defeated him rescuing hundreds at the cost of his life. They built the town over the site in which the tower was destroyed.

## Tyryn Hall

## 

## \<Description for players\>

This is the town hall of sorts, run by the ruler of the town Burgomaster Vuduin Tyryn.  He works here while his wife and children live at home. He operates his burgomaster requirements here, meets with important people here, and otherwise he sits in his office contemplating the warfront to the south.

A thing of note, in the center of the building, in a glass box, the cracked sword of Captain Tyryn that occasionally pulses with purple energy stays on a stand. It is always guarded on a rotating watch both, so no one messes with it and to make sure nothing comes of it since people do not know why it is pulsing.

## Witch’s Hut, The

## 

## \<Description for players\>

This the most uppity tavern in Tyrynder and a legend was made about how it was set up: as a part of a pact a witch and Captain Tyryn had where she would give up information about the Wizard and in return Tyryn would give land once a town would be set up. 

### **Non-Player Characters**

The Hut is run by a single woman, Cassia Autum and her three Gabuk companions, who are known by spell names: Brew, Sleep, and Bolt. The hut usually cooks via an unseen servant ensemble, but she mans the taverns bar. The gabük servants run food and take care of guests.
