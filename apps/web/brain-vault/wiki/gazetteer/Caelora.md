## About Caelora

Caelora \[SAY-LORE-ah\] rises amid rolling woodlands alive with the seasonal chorus that gives the city its name—an ancient elven phrase meaning “**Song of the Dawn**.” Blooming scents ride the wind alongside shifting birdsong, rustling leaves, and the hum of bees, turning the very landscape into living music that changes with every season. Predating even the Fracturing, this oldest surviving elven city stands as the heart of High Elven art, beauty, and living tradition. Its people see their home as a song written by the gods themselves—ever changing, yet always harmonious.

Visitors and residents alike delight in seasonal trade fairs brimming with new paintings, sculptures, and music; open performances and artisan exhibitions; lush gardens that honor Diverra; the shimmering **Daefar** pool where ancient elves first stepped into Myrdae; and the daily fusion of craft and worship that honors Tyvarion’s divine melody.

### **The People**

Nearly 80% of the city are high elves, with elves or half-elves making up 90% of the population; the remaining 10% are other species and visitors drawn by trade or artistry. Locals lean traditional and somewhat insular—outsiders are welcomed for their coin or talent, yet watched with polite distance until they prove they understand the city’s rhythm. 

### **Beliefs**

All gods are worshipped, but three gods align to the lifestyle of Caeloreans:

* **Diverra**, *Goddess of Love and Beauty*—celebrated in lush gardens and the shimmering Daefar pool.  
* **Tyvarion**, *God of Art and Expression*—patron of the city—honored in every song, carving, and performance.  
* **Utheri**, *the Truth Bringe*r—revered for echoing the city’s ancient elven ideals.

Worship flows through art, festival, and devoted temples. Tyvarion’s priests are typically musicians or poets; Diverra’s serve as matchmakers, healers, and garden tenders; Utheri’s act as judges, mediators, and truth-wardens. 

### **Economy and Trade**

Caelora thrives on the land’s bounty—hunting stags and boars, foraging herbs and flowers, and harvesting timber—while sitting on a major Bathaen Empire trade route. Caravans flow in with ores and goods; they leave laden with Caeloran paintings, sculptures, glasswork, and music sought across Myrdae.  Artistry is the true industry: painters, sculptors, glassblowers, and musicians sustain families through work that doubles as worship. The city is also a major provider of elegant elven arms and armor, forged from fine metals mined in the dwarven kingdom of Thel Kholdur and finished with Caeloran grace and enchantment. 

### **Holidays and Traditions**

The people of Cealora practice the tradition of Threadbinding.  Each season, pairs or small groups weave a single living vine or flower chain while silently exchanging truths under Utheri’s gaze. The chain is hung in public until it withers. The Cealoran’s view broken chains as omens of hidden lies.

## History

Caelora, the oldest surviving elven city in the Borealian Sway, predates even the Fracturing. Its name comes from an ancient elven phrase meaning “Song of the Dawn”, for beginning a life in a world surrounded by rolling hills and forests alive with seasonal choruses. As flowers bloom across the hillsides, the winds carry their scents in tandem with natural melodies — subtle shifts in birdsong, rustling leaves, and the hum of bees blending into a song that changes with each season of the year.

In its earliest days, when the settlement numbered barely five hundred elves, the city’s destiny became bound to its most celebrated figure: Teren Elle’berian, the Hand of the King.  At the king’s command, Teren left Caelora to help define the new territory that would become the Borealian Sway. His first task was to select the site of the ruling center — the grounds that would grow into Qal’dynn, the heart of elven power. Once established, Teren led a company of skilled military elves across the forests, hills, and rivers, securing the region’s borders. These elves became the first Realmwardens, guardians of the Sway whose traditions endure to this day.

After many years in service to the king, Teren returned to Caelora to spend his final years among his kin. His legacy would ripple far beyond his lifetime: the very term Elle’berian came to describe not only his family but the entire heritage of the elves of the Borealian Sway.

During the Time of Growth, Caelora became both a prize and a battleground. Its hills witnessed invasions and sieges, and the city was razed and rebuilt twice over. From these struggles emerged resilience — and the rise of three noble families who helped stabilize Caelora during its cycles of ruin and renewal:

* **House Airithel** — remembered for their wisdom and their role as keepers of Caelora’s archives and histories.  
* **House Rulendor** — famed as patrons of artisans and protectors of Caelora’s culture of beauty and song.  
* **House Venathrial** — renowned for their martial tradition and long service among the Realmwardens.

Together, these families rallied behind their sovereign, Lady Faevyr Venathrial, ensuring the city not only endured but flourished again. Their descendants remain powerful to this day, serving as counselors to the current ruler, Lady Alandri Venathrial.

## Citizenry

Caelora is predominantly made up of high elves (80%), with a total population of elves or half-elves accounting for 90% of the population.  The other 10% of the population is humans, dwarves, halflings, gnomes and other species.  Non-elven people in Caelora usually serve as merchants, artisans, performers or visitors.

## Economy and Trade

Caelora’s economy thrives on a blend of natural bounty, artisan craft, and mercantile exchange.

**Living off the Land**: The surrounding forests provide abundant game, with stags, boars, and other woodland creatures forming the backbone of local hunting and food trade. Foraging for herbs, fruits, and wildflowers also supplements daily life, while carefully tended groves supply timber in modest amounts.

**A Road of Commerce**: Caelora sits on one of the busiest trade routes of the Bathaen Empire, ensuring a steady flow of merchants and travelers. Caravans bring textiles, ores, and finished goods from distant cities, and in turn carry Caeloran artistry throughout the empire. This strategic location has made Caelora both wealthy and cosmopolitan, contributing to its unusually diverse population.

**Artistry as Industry**: More than any other product, Caelora is renowned for its artistry. The city’s painters, sculptors, glassblowers, and musicians enjoy near-mythic status, producing works that are sought across Myrdae. These are not mere luxuries but expressions of the city’s devotion to Tyvarion and Diverra, with artistry considered a form of worship. Many families sustain themselves through some form of craft, and apprenticeships in the arts are as common as martial or mercantile training elsewhere.

Trade fairs and festivals further stimulate Caelora’s economy. At the turn of each season, artisans unveil new works, musicians debut songs inspired by the changing hills, and merchants flock to purchase goods for export. 

## Religion

Caelora’s worship is communal and celebratory rather than austere. Priests are often artists, musicians, or poets who guide devotion through performance and festival rather than sermons, creating a spiritual culture that values:

* Expression as the highest form of worship.  
* Love and connection as proof of the gods’ blessings.  
* Honor and good as the guiding hand of Caelora’s heritage, embodied by Teren Elle’berian.

Since the city is not defined by solemn temples or rigid dogma, but by the living expression of divine presence in art, music, and beauty, three deities in particular shape the city’s soul:

* **Tyvarion, God of Art and Expression** – Patron of Caelora, Tyvarion is honored in every song sung, every arch carved with verse, and every seasonal festival.   
* **Diverra, Goddess of Love and Beauty** – Diverra’s presence is shown throughout the city’s gardens, terraces, and gatherings.  Diverra stirs passion, ensuring that every performance, every bloom, and every bond among citizens carries warmth and devotion.   
* **Ol’farium, Crowned Glory, God of Good and Honor** – Caelora holds a unique reverence for Ol’farium, tracing back to the city’s greatest folk hero, Teren Elle’berian. Long before the Awakening elevated Ol’farium to godhood, Teren invoked the memory of a mighty platinum dragon as the guiding star of his deeds. This dragon, later revealed as Ol’farium, became a living emblem of courage and honor in the Borealian Sway.  Even today, Caelora remembers him as a presence that once stirred their hero to greatness.

## **Academe Lyrael’vin**

The premier academy of Caelora, Academe Lyrael'vin trains High Elves (and rare gifted outsiders) in the sensory arts as an act of worship to Tyvarion. It occupies a sprawling complex of living groves, crystal halls, and song-walls built around the original Daefar pool overlook. Masters enforce cross-sensory mastery; students produce works that engage multiple senses simultaneously. The academy supplies finished elven arms and armor to the city’s export trade and serves as a quiet center for subtle Weave research.

## 

## As you pass beneath arching boughs carved with centuries of harmonious lines, the Academe Lyrael'vin unfolds like a living symphony. Scents of rare blossoms drift on the breeze, faint music echoes from every direction, and the very stones seem to pulse underfoot. Here the air itself feels tuned to the Song of the Dawn—students move in graceful patterns, brushes whisper across canvases, and the faint chime of dawn-lyres lingers long after the notes fade.

### **Important NPCs**

The Headmaster is a female high elf. Tall and silver-haired, she wears flowing robes embroidered with shifting sensory patterns. Calm, exacting, and deeply attuned to every discord in her domain.

A senior instructor of the Knowing Hand is a male high elf. Lithe and scarred from forging practice, he moves with precise economy. Stern but fair, obsessed with perfect tactile resonance.

A master of the Awakened Scent is a non-binary high elf. Delicate features framed by flower crowns, always surrounded by custom fragrances. Enigmatic and emotionally perceptive.

### **Unique Items & Services**

Sensory Commissions: Custom multi-sensory artworks, instruments, or enchanted armor that blend sight, sound, touch, and scent (expensive; 6–12 weeks).

Dawn-Lyre Rentals: Master-crafted string instruments that subtly amplify natural environmental chorus.

Essence Blends: Rare olfactory infusions tied to specific memories or emotions, used in rituals and performances.

Cross-Sensory Training: Short apprenticeships or masterclasses open to talented visitors (requires successful Performance or appropriate skill challenge).
