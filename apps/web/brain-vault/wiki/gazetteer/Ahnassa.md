## About Ahnassa

Ahnassa is a resilient coastal town perched on the edge of the Broken Deep, protected by towering bleached-rock sea walls and massive natural cave-arch gateways. Once destroyed during the Shattering of the Third Moon, the town was raised from the depths by Coralei, who ascended from mortal oracle to demigod to save her people. Today, Ahnassa is considered the holiest site of Coralei’s faith, though Nauldeaus has grown larger. The town is known for its dramatic white cliffs, and its reputation for exceptional non-fish seafood delicacies that draw travelers from across the region.

### **The People**

Ahnassa is one of the most diverse settlements along the Broken Deep. Its population is roughly equal parts elves, humans, and half-orcs, with the remaining residents consisting of dwarves, halflings, gnomes, genasi, and other ancestries drawn from across Myrdae. The people call themselves “the Queen’s Own” and view their survival as living proof of Coralei’s favor. Three noble houses hold significant influence: 

* House Nemonis, high-elves who claim direct descent from Coralei and serve as the town’s spiritual leaders,   
* House Karaek, half-orcs descended from the ancient ruler of the Mahruud era, who command the defenses, and  
* House Tragol, humans who dominate fishing, salvage, and trade. 

Despite their differences, the three houses work together to advise the Tide Queen and maintain the town’s stability.

### **Beliefs**

The people of Ahnassa hold a deep and personal reverence for Coralei, the Tide Queen who rose from their own ranks to save the town. They believe she watches over them still, and many carry small pieces of bleached cliff rock as talismans. The town’s identity is shaped by two powerful ideas: that Ahnassa was literally raised from destruction, and that the sea both gives and takes.  The people are welcoming to outsiders that  show proper respect for the Queen and the Deep.

### **Economy and Trade**

Ahnassa’s economy is built on the sea. Fishing and wreck salvage form the backbone of daily work, supported by active shipyards that repair and build vessels. The town has developed a strong reputation for high-quality non-fish marine delicacies — particularly scallops, urchin, octopus, and other specialty catches — which draw visitors specifically to dine here. These delicacies are also preserved and exported to other ports. 

### **Holidays and Traditions**

The most important celebration in Ahnassa is Embrace of the Deep Queen, observed on the 44th of Luros. This holiday carries two intertwined meanings. First, it commemorates the day Coralei ascended and saved the town. The citizens honor this by performing a ritual cold plunge into the Broken Deep, reaffirming their trust in their Queen and the sea. Second, the same name is given to the town’s funeral tradition: rather than burying their dead, the people send the deceased out to sea to be taken by a circular tidal whirlpool that appears twice daily off the coast. Both the living and the dead are said to be embraced by the Deep Queen.

## History

Ahnassa, a modest port town on the shores of the Broken Deep, began as a quiet fishing settlement long before the Fracturing. Its name—“She Who Queens the Deep”—originally honored the natural cave system beneath the harbor where oracles delivered prophecies in the name of Torec, Lord of the Elements.

The town’s fate changed forever during the Shattering of the Third Moon. A colossal aberration plunged from the Broken One into the sea beside Ahnassa, unleashing a cataclysmic surge that flooded and destroyed the settlement, killing hundreds. As doom closed in, the elf oracle Coralei prayed to Torec from her submerged cave, offering her mortality in exchange for the power to save her people. Torec answered. Coralei ascended as Tide Keeper, slew the aberration in a furious battle upon the waves, and then—using her newfound divine might—lifted the sunken ruins of Ahnassa from the depths.

The town rose behind towering bleached-rock sea walls formed from the ancient seabed, their sheer cliffs resembling the white walls of legend. The natural cave-arch entrances that once led to Coralei’s oracle chamber became the town’s protected gates. Her original throne room rose intact as the first Temple of the Tide Queen. Though Nauldeaus later grew into the largest center of Coralei’s worship, Ahnassa remains her holiest site—the place where a mortal queen became a goddess.

The town has endured as a resilient coastal stronghold ever since, its people viewing themselves as living proof of their queen’s eternal watch over the seas.

## Citizenry

Ahnassa is one of the most diverse settlements along the Broken Deep. Its people are roughly equal parts elves, humans, and half-orcs, with the remaining being a mix of other species. This mix arose after Coralei raised the town from the depths—survivors, refugees, and those drawn by the Tide Queen’s miracle flocked here and stayed.

The people of Ahnassa are pragmatic, and fiercely proud. They view their survival as living proof of Coralei’s favor. Daily life revolves around the sea: fishing, ship repair, and the harvesting of deep-sea resources. Outsiders are welcome.

### **Noble Families**

Three prominent noble houses hold the greatest influence in Ahnassa:

* **House Nemonis** — High-elf family that claims direct descent from Coralei herself. They are the traditional Tide Wardens of the town, overseeing religious ceremonies and the oracle caves. Arrogant yet genuinely devout, they view themselves as the rightful spiritual heirs to the Tide Queen.  
* **House Karaek** — Half-orc lineage descended from Thulan, a powerful ruler during the time Ahnassa belonged to the Mahruud. They control much of the town’s military and defensive forces, particularly the Deepwardens who guard the cliff gates. Honorable and martial, they take pride in their ancient ties to the land.  
* **House Tragol** — Influential human merchant family that dominates fishing fleets, salvage operations, and coastal trade. Shrewd and ambitious, they maintain strong ties with the Tratta Merchant Consortium and other ports. Their wealth funds much of the town’s infrastructure and shipyards.

These three houses form a loose council that advises the Tide Queen and helps govern day-to-day affairs. While rivalries exist, all three publicly honor Coralei and work together when the Deep grows restless.

# Notable Locations

## Raised Reef Harbor

The central dock district built into the massive natural cave-arch gates. Ships pass beneath towering bleached-rock arches (remnants of Coralei’s oracle cave) into a sheltered harbor. At high tide the lower docks become canals. House Tragol controls most warehouses and shipyards here.

## The Oracle’s Throne (Primary Temple of Coralei)

The original throne room where Coralei preached and ascended now serves as the town’s central temple. It sits elevated on the cliffs, half inside the ancient sea cave. A massive bleached coral throne dominates the sanctuary. House Nemonis maintains it and performs daily rituals. A giant conch shell is a horn above the throne that sounds at high tide.

## The Drowned Quarter

The lowest district where buildings are raised on bleached pillars of coral reef and is partially flooded at high tide. Rebuilt from the original sunken ruins, it contains many of the oldest homes.

## Blow Hole (Notable Tavern)

A large, lively tavern built into the base of the white cliffs near the main gate. Its interior incorporates massive whale bones and salvaged ship timbers.  Famous for deep-sea stew, strong kelp ale, and nightly storytelling.

## Trade Hall

The largest warehouse and trading post on the docks, owned by House Tragol. Sells exotic salvage, ship supplies, deep-sea oils, pearls, and rare artifacts pulled from the Broken Deep. Also serves as the de facto merchant guild hall.

## Watchtower

A fortified bastion built into the highest point of the sea cliffs. Serves as headquarters for the guard. Offers commanding views of the Broken Deep and early warning of threats from the sea.

## Bleached Market

Open-air market square in the middle tier of the town. Stalls carved directly into the white cliff face. Best place to buy fresh seafood, coral jewelry, enchanted tide charms, and salvaged magic items.
