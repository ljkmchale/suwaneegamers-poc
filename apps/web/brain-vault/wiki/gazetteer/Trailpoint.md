# Overview

Trailpoint is a modest but well-respected town situated on the crossroads of a wooded region south of the Severed Crease in Orbansia. Though small in size, its reputation far outweighs its footprint, as the town is famed across Myrdae for breeding powerful and swift horses. Caravans, merchants, and adventurers often pass through, making the settlement a reliable waypoint of trade and travel.

## Citizenry

Trailpoint’s population is primarily human, halfling, and half-elf, with a scattering of elves and dwarves who contribute to leatherworking, smithing, and woodcraft that support the horse trade. The town has a frontier-minded character—practical, hospitable to travelers, and proud of their breeding stock.

## Ebony House

The duke’s residence is home to the ruler of Trailpoint, Duke Sonder Holloway (LN male high-elf noble).  He came to power 8 years before modern time from the Bathean Empire. He came down south in order to escape his bad reputation up north and has made the town profitable by promoting festivals and tourism. His Ebony House is a large house seemingly covered in ash and soot, but at a touch doesn’t leave off any residue. He says the house has been magically gifted to the town due to this strange nature.

## Dock, The (pub)

This pub/dock is situated in the middle of the town and is where watery trade happens.  The pub is run a nice couple, Doc Groovrous Ashhand (TN male dwarf commoner) and Fera Ashhand (TN female dwarf commoner).  Fera takes care of the trade and seating, while Groovrous takes care of the drinks

Food:  There is little food here apart for some snack like stuff… mainly all different types of drinks 

Trade:  Inside the pub, there are a few rooms in which you can rent for trade, the rooms are small in nature, 2 silver to rent

## Air of Flair

## Beat the Heat

This is Trailpoint’s local blacksmith. It is run by Sot Sar (N male Weefolk commoner). It has all the basic weapons an adventuring party and the guards could need.

## Plain Gabük

A general store, run by Hobby the Gabuk (NG male weefolk painted green (DC 15 perception check) commoner), that has everything adventures could probably need, plus a few kits and stuff for mounts.

## House of Cembus

Near the part bottom center of the town lies the church of Cembus, a humble yet definitive white colored church that has a graveyard out back. A firbolg priest, Olivar Skylark, runs the church

## Horses Run

This is Trailpoint’s stables for the towns many different mounts. It is run by Venlira Yelcyne (N female half-elf commoner). It has room for around 30 different mounts, mainly different horses, a few kalbets, and a few exotic ones (DM discretion)

## Spinning Stag Inn

This inn is situated in the front of the village, facing the east.  The inn is run by Carmine Zepplee (NG male half-elf commoner) and Andrea Zepplee (TN female half-elf commoner) twins.  They work in unison to get most of the chores done, however Carmine is the one who is usually out front as Andrea is in the back

Rooms:  There are all types of rooms (1-4 people) available for 4 sp per room, or 6sp for a bigger one.

Food:  Meals are mainly gourd based, random pies or cut up gourds dot every meal along side various meats. 

## Yam’s Post

This is Trailpoint’s singular guard post. It is run by Yam Hofbar (LN male copper dragonborn Veteran). He is the towns main defense as he has an uncanny ability to turn into mist to get around quickly, there are a few townsfolk in reserve to help out during festivals and such though, 2d12 \+3
