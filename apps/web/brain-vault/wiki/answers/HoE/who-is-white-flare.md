# who is  White Flare

White Flare is Orah Nelcrath, a dragonborne from the halls of Dunduar, who rode Karalafene, a silver dragon.

Orah was a dragonrider and paladin, carrying the same aura as her old circle. She was part of a group that hid three relic pieces: Moganna's heart-shaped gem, Soulroot, and Starvein. The locations of these pieces are unknown.

White Flare is connected to Ainslie through their shared history with Irragosa, who guarded the heart gem and was ridden by Tsulanan. Orah's disappearance before the Awakening has left a void in her relationships, particularly with Irragosa, who seems emotionally invested in Ainslie's presence.

Currently, White Flare is missing, and no one has seen her since before the Awakening.
