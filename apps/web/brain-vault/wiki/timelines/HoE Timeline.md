## Current Open Arc

The party entered the vault beneath Mount Emberstran in Session 25. Ainslie passed the bloodline trial, took the heart-shaped gem from Moganna's vault, and was left exhausted enough that Irragosa directed the party back to Emberstran's Temple of Diverra. The party has returned to Emberstran on the 44th of Talil, 1246, with Lady Rook openly admitting she had them followed and Tessa warning Ky'tha of misfortune in Ulgrey.

Active threads after the vault:
- **Soulroot and Starvein** — Irragosa names two other relic pieces; locations unknown.
- **Heart gem cost** — Ainslie can take the gem, but contact drains him and requires Diverra's light.
- **Ahn'Tsuel cycle** — the three relic pieces may extend life and break the 50-year soul cycle of a timeless aberrant being.
- **Ulgrey trouble** — Phira is approaching from Ulgrey, Arnol was ambushed, and Tessa says the Etterset has had misfortunes there.
- **Jerik's threat** — red-rider warned the party on the trail that Jerik "now has your scent."
- **Og and Dandelia of the Veil** — the Life Tree named her as Og's required next step after the vault.
- **Lady Rook's surveillance** — Rook admits she had the party followed and claims she wants to help with ancient bloodline knowledge.
