# Bloody Endeavor Timeline

## Scope

Timeline for the Wyrm Bane campaign only. Do not merge events from HoE, SoD, or The Silent Vanguard.

## Sessions

- [[WB Session 01 - Prisoners]] — Party meets as slaves at the garrison/prison Alasfolen, ruled by the pale elf "Mistress" Mylaerla. Fellow prisoners: Sitmus Runegrip, Lagakh Morn, Oskard, Azer Launce, Halynor Caifir, Lyeneru Olokas.
- [[WB Session 02 - Escape]] — The party escapes Alasfolen. A dragon arrives mid-escape. They flee through the woods, fighting giant eagles.
- [[WB Session 03 - The Cabin]] — Party reaches a cabin (Goddard Family Cabin) south of Climbor. Find two gold rings, a smoking pipe, an ornate dagger, and a key with a note.
- [[WB Session 04 - Splooting]] — Party arrives at Climbor. Visit the Caifir Estate (noble family, butler Inriel). Encounter the Gabüks in the underground Hole of Climbor. Brief fight.
- [[WB Session 05 - The Half Orc]] — Party meets Scipio Grudshnook, half-orc monster hunter. A beast killed a bear and destroyed a campsite. Party investigates a cave, fights a chimera, Bakah is downed.
- [[WB Session 06 - The Keep]] — Party reaches Colbath's Keep. Kill Abdul and Andreas. Learn that a Bone Marigold flower can cure Lord Colbath's dying daughter Eva. The Tamed Moons inn introduced.
- [[WB Session 07 - To The Cave]] — Party heads southwest to find the Bone Marigold. Encounter kobolds; interrogate Sirn Zuss. Enter the cave.
- [[WB Session 08 - Rise & Fall]] — Inside the kobold cave. Letters from Sirn Zuss to "Nabatrix" reference "Maitruk." Mushroom poison room, 80-foot rope climb. Meet Aishka the Braizen (blue half-dragon). Bakah falls; nearly wiped. In-game date: not yet specified.
- [[WB Session 09 - Am-Effin-bush!]] — Back at Colbath's Keep. Learn Aishka was seen in the Matahal Valley fighting pit. Slavers ambush the party — pale elves who turn to smoke and blow horns. Necklace of Prayer Beads (5 magic beads) found; Caelion takes it.
- [[WB Session 10 - Fangs or Tusks]] — In Climbor. Quest for Mr. Crawbert (whose uncle's glass eye was stolen). Sewers investigation. Torture of Paragain. Cult of Verander referenced.
- [[WB Session 11 - Off You Go!]] — Catwalk; Bakah throws Crawbert off the bridge. Eyeless skinned goliaths attacked. Meet Lilian, a woman hunting the Cult of Verander.
- [[WB Session 12 - I'm Getting it Described]] — Explore the Cult of Verander's underground complex: password "Verander," alchemy lab (4 healing potions, 2 heroism, 2 angel ichor), water hall. Fighting shadows (strength drained). Party levels up; Bag of Holding acquired from Lurch.
- [[WB Session 13 - Witguard Bound! Just Kidding]] — Travel toward Witguard. Encounter orc fort; combat paused mid-battle at round 8.
- [[WB Session 14 - The Dragon Appears]] — Orc battle concludes. Lozire frees prisoner who transforms into **Saildreith the Copper Dragon** (from Rostiena Foothills). Challenge Orc Chief Sharpmire to 1v1 — Bakah loses, chaos ensues. Saildreith blasts. Treasure found including Scroll of Raise Dead, Thornfeeder, Ring of Protection. In-game date: around 22nd of Talil.
- [[WB Session 15 - The Bat Letgoers]] — 22nd of Talil. Orc fort aftermath. Freed: Saildreith (Shield of Witguard, goes by "Blair"), Ana Belen Sandoval (thief, Pelveron), Knight Ligart. Major treasure haul including 260 gp, Arrow of Monstrosity Slaying, Saildreith's Bull Horn (given to Bakah). Party name "Wyrm Bane" chosen. New companion: Rhody the Goatherder.
- [[WB Session 16 - Safe What?]] — 23rd of Talil. Gregory the little kid leads party to the safehouse. Meet Elzz Ebme (tiny goblin wizard). Shadows drain party strength. Descend 100 feet into the safehouse; fight inside. Lucerion falls.
- [[WB Session 17 - Disunity]] — 24th of Talil. Looking for the Spikethroats inside the safehouse. Lozire's debt collectors (Jasper "Five-fingers," Weebert, Otis, Horus, Borus) ambush sleeping party. Jasper spared and promises to call off the boss. Giant octopus fight. Insect experiment room.
- [[WB Session 18 - Where's the Floor?]] — 24th of Talil. Bone Dragon room with reversed gravity. Fight giant fleshy needle-man. Zebadee dies. **Lozire dies.** Bones dragon had Scroll of Greater Restoration. Women monster — super dangerous.
- [[WB Session 19 - Longtoe's Stuff]] — 24th–26th of Talil. Loot distributed from Longtoes and Lozire. Meet Magistrate Oddnel and Raul Nebe. Travel. Fight guard/messenger and worgs. Meet Ralajyre (devotee of Natafae) and Ms. Ade (Paragon Tear). Clarence joins.
- [[WB Session 20 - Strategic Shenanigans]] — Evening of 26th of Talil. Ola Gretel (Witguard guard) sent party to find Seventh Company of Paendley (Captain Dorralei). Bakah scouts invisible, finds transformed soldiers. Big battle begins.
- [[WB Session 21 - Hockie]] — Evening of 26th of Talil. Fight "Jason" (Masked Man / cabin murderer), who kills Captain Dorralei and steals an amulet; flees. Party follows cave. Amulet is the Paragon Tear. Learn: Sable Shadows criminal org based in Gevakaln; Dalasor Coast (Malithea Empire) building armies on Rostiena border; Thornfold Bastion is the key fort. Magistrate rewards 400 gp. Aishka the Braizen spotted in Witguard fighting pit. Party chases Aishka; ends mid-chase.
- [[WB Session 22 - They Have a Pact!]] — 29th of Talil. Aishka escapes; Lucerion takes his tabard. Travel toward Khagdaruhl via Climbor. Party learns Rhody and Albross have pacts. Rhody's hand turns black (swollen, something moving under skin) from grabbing the amulet. Slavers attack camp on 32nd of Talil — pale elves with small horns, not from Myrdae. Drop body at Cathedral of Utheri; learn these elves are not from Myrdae, potentially not from this plane — use divine and fey power, gods interrupt speak-with-dead.
- [[WB Session 23 - Petal Hole Quest]] — 33rd of Talil. Lady Coarsepetal leads party into the Hole of Climbor. Black pudding fight. Mural triggers poison darts (triggered multiple times). Skeletons and skeleton wizard. Caelion and Rhody encounter **Lilian** outside: she says she destroyed the safehouse; Rhody's infection will kill him; the Coldbreath Cult (Monks of Iohir) seeks a Snowglobe. **Rhody has 29 days to live.**
- [[WB Session 24 - Albross Speaks]] — 34th of Talil. Party leaves Climbor for Yag'vur Sierra to find Khagdaruhl and the nomads. Backtrack to Colbath Keep; learn a monk of Iohir left. Begin tracking Neha into the mountains. 8 days of travel. Rock People (Stone Giants) on day 8 give info: dragon north, cave five peaks, Clan of Iohir. Four frozen dead adventurers in ice pillars. **White dragon Presagiath attacks** — nearly wipes party; Rhody alone survives, saves Caelion who saves rest. Lucerion almost severs its claw; Presagiath flees.
- [[WB Session 25 - You Can Leave. Ok.]] — 46th of Talil. Recovering near the dragon grounds. All mounts dead except one donkey. Meet young Runegrip dwarves (Crabold and Ondus). Reach **Hol'gardur** — Runegrip clan village. Elder Fomeck Runegrip. Crops affected by Coldbreath frost. Spirit of the woods: Sennogam (Sinn'ogin). Druid Groretta Runeeyes holds a trial. **Rhody goes through the trial** — three stages (undead dwarves, crossing to dwarf table, drinking contest). Presagiath (the "Cold Breath") confirmed as a threat. Rhody is marked by the spirit.
- [[WB Session 26 - Goin' Up the Mountain]] — 1st of Paramor. Heading up Fyrstholith (first peak of Yaggermon). Rhody leads; spirits aid him. Giant with ice nunchukas hunts elk — elk frozen. Reach Hol'kulrum (destroyed by giants). Trophy ice of dwarves in town; party flees. Zombie giant with bling. Reach **Hol'durog** — find **Monks of Iohir** (humelvish). Fight two monk guards. Enter the camp, fight through several rounds. Find tiny dwarf prisoners in a cage. **Neha killed by Lucerion.** Harhet Steelfoot (Advent of Harmony) captured: reveals monastery in Vanapur mountains, Caves of Kroher have a Rimestone, the "Cold Breath" is the real boss, another large fighter guards the caves. Party frees ten dwarf miners.
- [[WB Session 27 - Bald-Rick & The Rimestone Cowboy]] — 1st of Paramor (continued). Speak with Baldric Stonehide (gold-haired dwarf). Enter the Cave of the Cold Breath. Large dwarven hall covered in ice; dwarven skeleton soldiers reforming. Left fork; dwarven constructs; fight in the Royal Lounge (3 monks). 4× Greater Healing potions found. Kitchen — people get eaten; teleported to slime dimension. Armory — Lucerion opens void. **Fight The Cold Breath (boss Huldi)**. **Bakah falls.** Session ends mid-boss-fight.
- [[WB Session 28 - Better off Dead]] — 3rd of Paramor. Party made it down the mountain (Bakah died to The Cold Breath). Harhet tells them the 2nd Rimestone piece may be in Khagdaruhl; 3rd unknown. **Bakah's memorial cairn** built at Hol'gardur. Runegrip dwarves divide his possessions. Meet **Pagern Stonebuckle** (gnome wizard, apprentice of the Celadon Wizard) on 7th of Paramor — wolves attacking him; Pagern joins the party. 10th of Paramor: Rhody lost (illusion spell), eventually finds **Madam Mabel**'s apple orchard. **Madam Mabel** removes Rhody's infection/curse in exchange for: bags (unopened), a body part from each of three locals, and a ruby.
- [[WB Session 29 - Hags with Bags]] — 10th of Paramor. Through Madam Mabel's tree portal to the Matahal Valley (south tip, east of Vanapur). Meet an elf fleeing the **Thunderspear orcs** (Sharpmire's clan). Orc combat in six rounds; orc shaman killed. Enter a mine, pay gold tribute to a pool spirit. Encounter three Redcaps fighting over a toad. Find three hags around a mine cart: noble lady, female dwarf, human — all hags. Lucerion attacks when threatened; two hags killed. Large blue hag **Viola** arrives; has the ruby; proposes deal to betray Madam Mabel.
- [[WB Session 30 - Let's Break A Deal!]] — 10th of Paramor. Albross missed the portal; Madam Mabel's patron (The Holy Light) appears as a horse-turned-seraph and warns Albross: Breemos will be kidnapped within a harmon if Albross doesn't act. Vision of the Cold Breath victorious; the Dwarven King throne bound by tendrils. Party returns through the tree. **Fight Viola** (six rounds). Lucerion loses his right arm. Viola slain by Pagern. Viola's loot: 4 moving bags, a ruby, topaz (500 gp), 3 jade (100 gp each), diamond (500 gp), a second blade shard, scroll of imprisonment, bag of magic beans. **Lucerion's arm grows back red** — the color of Natafae. Party heads to Fort Mornock (dwarf tomb) for treasure to fund Pagern's wizard tower. Party levels up (level 8).
- [[WB Session 31 - Don't Let This Drag-on]] — 13th of Paramor. Fort Mornock (Fortress of the Flayer) — tomb of dwarven warlord Mornock the Flayer. Explorer notes scattered through the dungeon. Large golem fight in grand hall. Barrack rooms with thieves' tools and 10 gems (5 gp each). Secret door to small chest: 2 red vials, partial axe head. War-room with map naming Krorhear, Kag'daruhl, and Iohere; animated armor fight. Sleeping quarters with dead Rubyguard dwarf; letter reveals Rimestone location was protected. Mess hall; Presagiath bursts through the ice wall — "I remember you!" Dragon fight. Spirit fight in the chasm room. Tomb opened. Session ends at the sarcophagus.
- [[WB Session 32 - Over the Edge]] — 13th of Paramor. Sarcophagus triggers fire explosion; copper dwarven statue attacks; slain. Spirit reveals the real tomb above. Ascend seven stories. Presagiath was hiding on the ceiling. **Dragon fight**: Presagiath grapples and tosses characters off the cliff. **Pagern kills Presagiath** with a tiny firebolt. Treasure hoard collected on 14th of Paramor: 5,400 cp, 12,000 sp, 4,200 gp, 200 pp, 22 gems, art pieces, multiple potions, spell scrolls, Medallion of Thoughts (Pagern), Boots of Levitation (Pagern), Bowl of controlling water elementals (Albross), OrcReaver (Rhody). Depart toward Khagdaruhl. 21st of Paramor: Find an orc fort attacked by the **Sacred Crows**.
- [[WB Session 33 - Caw!]] — 21st of Paramor. Meet the **Sacred Crows** (five adventurers sent by Magistrate Oddnel). The Sacred Crows need to find **Bandit the Blind Brown Braided Goat** to gain entry to Khagdaruhl. Trail leads to a cave; Elder Ettin inside. Beyond: two dwarven towers occupied by **drow**. Party clears right tower through multiple combats (guards, drow scholars, spider boss). Session ends mid-tower.
- [[WB Session 34 - Greatest Of All Time]] — 21st of Paramor. Drow tower top floor: **Madam Mizna Er'Arachna** (regal drow). Her male guard killed; Mizna escorted out. Amethyst illusion gem destroyed (removed fog/darkness). Sacred Crows found the goat — **Bandit the Blind Brown Braided Goat** recovered. Long rest in the tower. Depart with Sacred Crows for Khagdaruhl. Meet Ambassador Udohi Greythane (duergar) on the road; he leads party (heads covered) into Khagdaruhl. The dwarven kingdom: lava-lit cavern, gemstone walls, mixed dwarves (55% duergar, 35% mountain, 10% hill). **Ring of Respect** combat trials. Sacred Crows vs. fire elementals; Wyrm Bane vs. hellhounds. Fight **Zhis'ta the Purple** (half-drow/half-spider champion). Wall drops — Wyrm Bane must fight Sacred Crows. Session ends mid-fight.
- [[WB Session 35 - Bloody Endeavor Civil War]] — 21st–22nd of Paramor. **Wyrm Bane defeats the Sacred Crows** (4 rounds; no deaths; Pagern finishes Rina with a chromatic orb). Victory room. Met the robed six: Sir Duvall (noble from Narzon) and five short guards. **Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV** (black-skinned duergar, white bearded, black crown). Private audience. Albross speaks for the party: Witguard's request for alliance, the Runegrip's stolen Rimestone piece, Albross's vision of tendrils on the throne. Lord reveals the history: dwarves used dark magic, awakened an undead aberration, Rimestone was made to freeze it, broken apart. The aberration is affecting the Duergar (nightmares). Khagdaruhl is currently selling arms to the Dalasor Coast. Party asks to see the Rimestone piece. Session ends mid-audience.

## Current Open Arc

The party is in private audience with Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV of Khagdaruhl as of the end of Session 35 (22nd of Paramor, 1246). The Rimestone situation is escalating — Huldi (The Cold Breath) holds at least one piece; Khagdaruhl holds one; the third is unknown.

Active threads entering the next session:
- **Khagdaruhl alliance** — Lord is weighing alliance with Witguard (vs. Dalasor Coast, who also sent ambassadors). Audience ongoing.
- **The Rimestone** — Huldi holds piece 1 (taken from Runegrip clan). Khagdaruhl holds piece 2. Third piece location unknown.
- **The Eldritch Aberration** — Sealed under Yag'vur Sierra by the Rimestone. Duergar experiencing nightmares; tendrils binding the king in Albross's vision.
- **Albross's brother Breemos** — in Gevakaln; risk of kidnapping by Sable Shadows within a harmon of Paramor 10th. Sending spell delivered the warning.
- **Lucerion's red arm** — gift from Natafae after losing the real arm; significance unclear. Burns when touching a holy symbol of Osanna.
- **The Orcs heading toward Nurador** — Lucerion's hometown; party was told this during their time near Witguard.
- **Sir Duvall** — mysterious noble from Narzon, met in Khagdaruhl's Great Hall with five hooded guards. Talking privately with the Lord Emperor before the party's audience.
- **The broken sword blade** — mithral/dunduar/silver, human/dwarven/orcish make; party holds the main blade and both shards; missing the tip and hilt.
- **Pale elves from another plane** — slavers with divine and fey power, not from Myrdae. Mylaerla (The Mistress) at Alasfolen is connected. The Sable Shadows of Gevakaln may also be linked.
