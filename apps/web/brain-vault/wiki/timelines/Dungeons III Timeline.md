# Dungeons III Timeline

## Scope

Timeline for the Dungeons III campaign only. Do not merge events from HoE, SoD, The Silent Vanguard, or Bloody Endeavor.

## Sessions

- [[Dungeons III Session 01 - It's Eye'on]] - Party introductions in Hesfal; Moonfern Sanctuary, Covered Hooch, Hall of Many Hands, first murder rumors, and party convergence.
- [[Dungeons III Session 02 - Pocket Prism]] - Salvus crowds arrive; Nova joins; Pequin's Pocket Prism is bought; Boar's Best robbery and prisoner break begin.
- [[Dungeons III Session 03 - Chase the Runners]] - Bandit escape aftermath; Koren Lonsit identified; mayor asks party to pursue the bandits; murder weapon found near Bru's Beastie Barn.
- [[Dungeons III Session 04 - I Can't Even Take A Shit Man]] - Party tracks bandits southeast from Hesfal, fights owlbear and stirges, finds bandit horses at Sherdin's lean-to.
- [[Dungeons III Session 05 - The Hots for Nova]] - Sherdin and Ralli context; party reaches cave hideout, defeats and captures K9 Crew members, learns the Scorpion paid them.
- [[Dungeons III Session 06 - Rope-A-Dope]] - Blue sending stone and magic-amplifying cavern found; grick and roper fights; party returns bandits to Hesfal and agrees to escort them to Scarwatch Hold.
- [[Dungeons III Session 07 - Brooch the Subject]] - Lavoruud tries to recover Nova's brooch; Blackshield confirms Dunduar qualities; party travels with prisoners to Scarwatch Hold.
- [[Dungeons III Session 08 - The Stone (finally) Speaks]] - Outer Gate mimic fight; Warden Haliun receives the Hesfal charges; Scarwatch Hold NPCs and jobs introduced; Scorpion contacts Draelith through the stone.
- [[Dungeons III Session 09 - A Cart-time Job]] - Party names itself The kNight Watch; Umlin hires mapping work; Radiant Eye defaces the Sanctuary; Embryl raises the Osmanae celestial instrument lead.
- [[Dungeons III Session 10 - It's a Long Ass Way]] - Party tracks Isgo's black horse, follows cultist signs toward the crags, finds a hidden lockbox and corpse, kills a xorn, and is attacked by tan-robed figures with kahlbits.

## Current Open Arc

The party is in the crags east of Scarwatch Hold on the 21st of Evalee, 1246. Meles scouted ahead and saw two tan-robed figures with kahlbits; they attacked as the session ended.

Active fronts:

- **Radiant Eye cult** - defaced Scarwatch Hold Sanctuary; believed to have an outpost in eastern crags; tan-robed attackers may connect.
- **Isgo** - wanted bandit/thief; his black horse Bullseye was found riderless; signs suggest he was taken by two riders, possibly cult-linked.
- **Osmanae celestial instrument** - Embryl believes an ancient outpost/observatory in the crags may hold an instrument that should go to the Advents of Harmony.
- **Scorpion Network** - hired the K9 Crew to terrorize Hesfal; contacted Draelith after K9 Crew judgment and suggested the party may be useful.
- **Nova's Dunduar brooch** - Blackshield warned never to remove the stone from its lattice; full function unknown.
- **Mapping work for Umlin** - party has been paid 10 gp up front to chart areas around Scarwatch Hold.
