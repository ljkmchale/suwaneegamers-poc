# Nature's Oratory

## Scope

SoD-only faction/location page. World-canonical Nature's Oratory description is in [[Adsuren]] (`wiki/world/locations/`).

## Summary

Nature's Oratory is Adsuren's primary religious institution — an older half-elf priest named Tanluar Rotvori presides over well-maintained grounds with an attached cemetery. It is Escanor's spiritual home and institutional base. The party used it as a triage and meeting point across the investigation. Rotvori has been a consistent, cautious ally: he referred the party to investigate the poisoned well, helped with crypt access, and took charge of the cult aftermath alongside the Guard.

## The Oratory

- Well-maintained, lovely grounds. The building is functional and clean.
- Attached cemetery of ~50 graves, fenced. Used as a monitoring point during the undead threat.
- Has a crypt below — the party entered it during the initial undead investigation.

## Key Figures

- **[[Tanluar Rotvori]]** — older half-elf priest; Adsuren's main religious authority; knows Escanor and Zephyra. Cautious but cooperative; gives the party useful information and access while staying away from direct action. Took charge of aftermath when the cult was exposed, alongside the Guard and Captain Alinia.
- Acolytes — unnamed; present at the Oratory when the party brings poisoned victims.

## Events Connected To The Oratory

- **Session 01**: The poisoned man was brought to the Oratory; Rotvori directed the party to investigate the well. They gained crypt access here and investigated the undead below.
- **Sessions 03-04**: Party returned here after the swamp investigation. Escanor briefed Rotvori on the cemetery disturbance and learned the priest had looked into prior records about swamp problems.
- **Session 08**: Escanor woke Rotvori after the cult reveal; Rotvori joined the Guard and Captain Alinia in taking public charge of the aftermath. This was the Oratory's clearest civic moment.

## Party Relationship

- Escanor: strongest bond; the Oratory is his home base; Rotvori trusts him.
- Zephyra: local connection; Rotvori knows her by name.
- Broader party: uses Oratory credibility when dealing with public moral and undead/cult issues.
- Kenton: no direct relationship; Rotvori does not know him well.

## Capabilities And Resources

- Rotvori can search records for historical information (checked for prior accounts of swamp problems).
- Party discussed whether the Oratory could provide anti-mind-control protections — not confirmed.
- Cemetery watch can be arranged (Hugo was posted here).

## Open Questions
