# Adsuren Guard

## Scope

SoD-only faction. World-canonical description of the Watch and Guard Post is in [[Adsuren]] (`wiki/world/locations/`).

## Summary

The Adsuren Guard is the town's law force under Captain Alinia Sarralei. It is a small organization — at least 12 guards on duty at any given time — strained by Market Day security loads, the tax theft, the cult reveal, and the death of guard Heckudor. The party's standing with the Guard is mixed: Zephyra has the strongest local trust, Escanor has institutional credibility, Kenton is a hidden liability.

## Key Members

- **Captain Alinia Sarralei** — young elf, light skin, bushy hair, scar on both sides of her face circling her neck (never explains it). Sober and direct. Resents Mollywop. Commands the Guard's response to Market Day, tax theft, and cult aftermath.
- **Heckudor** — guard killed by an imp in Ganon's room during the investigation.
- **Hugo** — assigned to cemetery watch during the cult investigation.
- Unnamed reception guard at the Guard House — recognized Zephyra, relayed information.
- Unnamed guard posted outside the graveyard at night — found sleeping (woken by Escanor).

## The Guard House

- Oldest building in Adsuren. The Captain's office is inside.
- Standard access point for Zephyra and Escanor; Kenton and Therric were turned away at the Adsuren Center during the theft investigation.

## Pressures And Events

- **Market Day security**: Zephyra was hired as a Market Day guard before the campaign begins.
- **Tax theft**: Gold pouches stolen from the Adsuren Center during the chaos. Kenton actually took them and covertly returned them later via Ganon's house as a drop-off (framing cover). The Guard investigated without knowing the real culprit.
- **Cult reveal**: The entire Cult of the Immured operation was running beneath the Intendant's own office. When exposed, the Guard, Captain, and Rotvori took charge of the aftermath. Captain Alinia was one of the three named figures who "stepped up" publicly.
- **Heckudor's death**: An imp at Ganon's apartment killed him; the party witnessed this.

## Party Relationship

- Zephyra has deepest Guard credibility — known to guards, gate staff, and the Captain.
- Escanor has institutional credibility via the Nature's Oratory connection.
- Kenton: the Guard does not know he stole the tax pouches. If this ever surfaces, his standing in Adsuren collapses.
- During cult operations, Escanor presented fake guard-request pretexts (false written statements) to gain access — the Guard was partially deceived.

## Open Questions

- How does the Guard's authority and morale stand after Eyovar's betrayal?
- Does Captain Alinia know more about the cult network than she has disclosed?
- What happens to the Guard's relationship with Mollywop now that the cult is gone?
