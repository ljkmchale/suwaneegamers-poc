# Cult of the Immured

## Scope

SoD-only faction.

## Summary

The Cult of the Immured was a multi-city ritual network dedicated to freeing Aeolenne, a succubus-type fiend who had been imprisoned in a ritual mechanism. They operated beneath Adsuren Center for 20 years under Eyovar Dunajor (as Summoner Karranok). Their method: defiling black salt and using it to petrify human hearts, which were then used as fiendish fuel. They did not know, or did not fully understand, that they were building toward Aeolenne's freedom. The party dismantled the ritual and freed Aeolenne in the process.

## Structure

- **Summoner Karranok** — title used by Eyovar Dunajor; the local/highest known leader.
- **Ganon** — local cultist who received black salt via the Caldwynn shipping network; rented a room from the Windbelly family in Adsuren's south.
- Field cultists: came from Caldwynn, Gibuldon, Morrey, and elsewhere — the network spanned multiple cities.
- Familiars sent throughout town to monitor for suspicion.

## The Ritual

- Required defiled black salt (prepared by the cult) to strip and petrify human hearts.
- Sentient hearts were preferred because they hold more soul-essence.
- Ritual also used animal sacrifices as secondary fuel.
- Petrified hearts served as fiendish anchors — Mollywop's heart analysis identified Aeolenne's imprint on the sample recovered.
- After 20 years of escalating sacrifices, the accumulated power was enough to release Aeolenne from her prison. When the party destroyed the ritual mechanism, the binding broke and she was freed.

## Eyovar's Confession

From Summoner Karranok directly, under questioning after capture:
- He performed his first ritual bargain approximately 20 years ago.
- He did not know he was building toward Aeolenne's release; he believed he was accumulating power for himself.
- He did not know who or what Aeolenne was until the party asked.
- His power source was the accumulated fiendish essence in the hearts; his stated goal was civic control and stability in Adsuren.

## Key Locations

- **Adsuren Center Cellar / Ritual Chamber** — primary operation site; beneath the Intendant's own office.
- **Ganon's Room** — apartment at the Windbelly estate; held the black salt, rat-circle ritual marker, and hidden compartment with the Ganon letter.
- **Tanner/Tannery** — the barrel letter ordered delivery here; a transit or cover point for black salt.

## Key Items

- Black petrified heart — analyzed by Mollywop; confirmed Aeolenne's imprint.
- Defiled black salt — the ritual material; came via Caldwynn shipping network.
- Ganon letter — detailed instructions; found in his room.
- Fiendish focus — ritual mechanism; destroyed by the party.
- Caldwynn barrel letter — shipping instructions; confirmed the supply chain.

## Status After Session 08

- Local cult: dismantled. Eyovar captured. Ganon killed (or incapacitated) during investigation.
- Guard and Rotvori took charge of the public aftermath.
- Aeolenne: freed; fled south toward Caldwynn.
- External network: unknown status; cult members in Caldwynn, Gibuldon, and Morrey may still be active.

## Open Questions

- Who is Aeolenne's Caldwynn ally — a surviving cultist, or a separate contact?
- Did Eyovar have communications with the Caldwynn cell, and were those records recovered?
- Are there other imprisoned fiends in other towns with similar ritual structures?
