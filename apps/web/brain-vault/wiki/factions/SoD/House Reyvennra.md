# House Reyvennra

## Scope

SoD-only faction. Connected to Kenton's Gibuldon past and Sir Rodolf Norran.

## Summary

House Reyvennra is a powerful Gibuldon noble house (Vesci tier, according to Laudneyr). Sir Rodolf Norran is tied to Reyvennra by the healing potion/name/mark and by his comments about the family, but the notes do not yet prove whether the house directly sent him. The connection appears to trace to a fire at a Gibuldon warehouse where Kenton's crate of silks was involved — but Kenton maintains he did not start it and barely remembers being there. Kenton sent the house a provocative, flamboyant letter through Laudneyr, inviting them to meet and blaming Rodolf for the fire. No response has arrived.

## What Is Known

- Kenton partied with tradesmen in Gibuldon; sold fine clothes and silks. At some point a warehouse fire occurred. Kenton says he didn't start it, doesn't remember being at the warehouse, and thinks his silks crate came from a cart.
- House Reyvennra lost a shipment of goods in the fire (Esylla, who has a noble background, confirms a family matching Kenton's description lost goods).
- A healing potion with the Reyvennra name/mark was left after the ambush, confirming a connection to the house.
- Laudneyr identified Reyvennra as "Vesci" — a powerful tier of noble family. He agreed to carry the letter but was clearly nervous.
- Kenton's memory includes a conversation about silks, a ring, a dwarf, overthrow, rightful heirship, and needing someone to pass along silks.

## Kenton's Letter

Sent via Laudneyr (a young clerk at Adsuren Center) for 5gp total delivery fee. The letter is written in Kenton's characteristic voice:

> *"To Lord and Lady Reyvennra — Ahhh, so Sir Rodolf has finally graced me with his presence... the 'incident' in the warehouse? A performance. A diversion! Every flicker of chaos — crafted by Rodolf's own hand to pull your eyes away from his verdadera intención. He dances while you watch... and all the while, the real game unfolds elsewhere... Come, let us meet. At your leisure, of course."*

The letter implies Rodolf orchestrated the fire himself and asks the house to come find Kenton, while also confirming Kenton is in Adsuren.

## Relationships

- **Kenton** — the target; possibly a scapegoat, witness, or inconvenient thread from the fire.
- **Sir Rodolf Norran** — connected actor/enforcer; tracked Kenton to Adsuren and ambushed him, but his exact authority from the house is unconfirmed.
- **Esylla** — recognizes the noble context; interpreted the shipping/watermark background.
- **Laudneyr** — delivered the letter; estimated a stretch or two for a reply.

## Open Questions

- Has Laudneyr delivered the letter, and what response (if any) is coming?
- Why did Rodolf pursue Kenton specifically? Was Kenton a witness, a scapegoat, or owed something?
- Who tortured the dwarf connected to this thread, and what did they know?
- Was Kenton actually framed, or is there something he genuinely did that night?
- Could House Reyvennra become an ally if they believe Kenton's claim about Rodolf?
