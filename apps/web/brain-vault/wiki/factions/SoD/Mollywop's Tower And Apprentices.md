# Mollywop's Tower And Apprentices

## Scope

SoD-only faction/group page. World-canonical Mage Tower description (and canonical name "Mollywap") is in [[Adsuren]] (`wiki/world/locations/`).

## Summary

Mollywop's tower is both a magical resource for the party and a small faction with its own politics. Mollywop herself is a powerful wizard who used to adventure with Tegenwald Phet and Dionara; now she teaches three apprentices (Doderi, Thunderfall, Lix), keeps a demon specimen under containment (Zuz), and maintains a ward over Adsuren that monitors unusual entry. She hid information from the party during the investigation and is in open tension with Captain Alinia. Her heart analysis identified Aeolenne's connection and named her specifically.

## The Tower

- Three stories, misshapen exterior. White marble door with golden veins.
- Ground floor: cluttered workspace, multiple worktables, books, jars.
- Upper floors: unknown but active (apprentice activity).
- Ward/alarm covers Adsuren — triggers on unusual entries, but Market Day traffic makes it noisy.

## Key Members

- **Mollywop** — wizard; adventuring past with Tegenwald Phet (deceased) and Dionara; defensive and evasive about her past; became suspicious in Session 07 when she told the party that someone had paid her to keep secrets. Full profile: [[Mollywop]].
- **Doderi Galton** — apprentice; sent the party a sending message relaying Mollywop's words.
- **Thunderfall** — apprentice; present at the tower.
- **Lix** — apprentice; present at the tower.
- **Zuz** — a demon Mollywop captured years ago and keeps under containment as a study specimen. Used in her heart analysis work.

## What The Tower Provides

- **Ward/alarm**: Covers all of Adsuren; Mollywop can query who has entered. Noisy on Market Day.
- **Sending**: Doderi relayed messages for Mollywop during the investigation.
- **Heart analysis**: Mollywop examined the black petrified heart and identified Aeolenne's imprint on it. She knew Aeolenne's name. After the cult fight, confirmed Aeolenne headed south.
- **Identify/analyze magic**: Available for potions, staffs, and other items.
- **Speak with Dead**: Capability present; not yet confirmed used.
- **Item trading/contacts**: High-cost items potentially accessible via Mollywop's network.
- **Anti-mind-control item**: Potentially accessible, but expensive.

## The Hiding Something Note

In Session 07, Mollywop told the party that someone had paid her to keep certain information secret. She did not identify who, and the content of what she was hiding was not disclosed. This is an active open thread.

## Risks

- Mollywop may continue withholding information depending on whatever arrangement she has.
- Captain Alinia resents Mollywop — this tension could become a problem if the Guard and the tower are both called on.
- Zuz's containment: if disrupted, unknown consequences.

## Open Questions

- Does she know about Aeolenne's Caldwynn ally?
- Can the party commission the anti-mind-control item?
- What is the relationship between Mollywop and whatever remains of the cult network?
