# Caldwynn Shipping Network

## Scope

SoD-only clue/faction network page.

## Summary

The Caldwynn shipping network is the supply chain behind the black salt barrel that reached Ganon and the Cult of the Immured. The paperwork trail points to Caldwynn as the origin or transit point; an overpayment was built in to avoid inspection; a letter ordered delivery to the Tanner without opening it. The spiral watermark on the letter has not been matched definitively. After the cult is dismantled, Mollywop confirms Aeolenne fled south — and the party knows from cult letters that she has an ally in Caldwynn.

## Known Facts

- Black salt barrel paperwork came from Caldwynn (5 days south of Adsuren).
- The accompanying letter ordered delivery to the Tanner without opening the barrel.
- Taxes were overpaid on the shipment to discourage inspection at the docks.
- The paper bore an angular spiral watermark. Esylla and the party checked a noble-family book and identified possible matches: **Cunningter**, **Idsoul**, **Walgett**. Jerborin Omakian (Adsuren Center clerk) did not recognize the watermark as a noble house — it may not be one.
- **MacCannain** (dockmaster) handled local dock paperwork for the shipment.
- **Panwin** moved the barrel from the docks to Ganon.
- Ganon received and used the black salt for cult rituals.

## Aeolenne's Caldwynn Connection

- Mollywop confirmed after the cult fight that Aeolenne headed south.
- Cult letters referenced an ally in Caldwynn — this is the only confirmed external arm of the cult network.
- Whether the ally is a noble, a merchant, or something else is unknown.

## Relationships

- **MacCannain** — dockmaster; handled incoming paperwork; knows the Caldwynn shipment.
- **Panwin** — moved the barrel to Ganon; direct transport link.
- **Ganon** — recipient and ritual user of the black salt.
- **Esylla** — best positioned to interpret shipping/noble context; her family is in shipping.

## Open Questions

- Did the barrel originate in Caldwynn or only pass through it?
- What does the spiral watermark identify — a house, a guild, a cartel?
- Are Cunningter, Idsoul, or Walgett involved?
- Who is Aeolenne's Caldwynn ally, and are they still active?
- If the party follows up, is the cult network in Caldwynn still operational?
