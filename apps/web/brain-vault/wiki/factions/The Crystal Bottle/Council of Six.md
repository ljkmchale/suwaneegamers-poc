# Council of Six

**Status:** Active (allied)
**Location:** [[Tal'basar]]

## Overview

The Council of Six is the governing body of [[Tal'basar]]. They have recruited the party on at least one occasion (sending Yvaine to join the group in [[Scarbrook]], Session 3.1). The party sided with the Council against [[Kal'dar]] when he confronted the town demanding surrender (Session 5.2).

## Known Members / Contacts

- **[[Belzander Umbridge]]** — Head Master of ALM; wants blue crystals returned
- **[[Serrenia Netherfall]]** — Head Mistress of ALM; associated with the Council; has a small annoyance with Tass; is a friend to Lothim
- **Carl** — ALM Professor; was sent home after arguing with Serina; was looking for Fizban

## Relationship with Party

Friendly. The Council recruited Yvaine; the party defended the Council when Kal'dar threatened Tal'basar. Erick's shop was handed over to Council authorities after the cellar investigation.
