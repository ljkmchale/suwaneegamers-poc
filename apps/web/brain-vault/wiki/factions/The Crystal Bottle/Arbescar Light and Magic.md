# Arbescar Light and Magic (ALM)

**Status:** Active (allied)
**Location:** [[Tal'basar]]

## Overview

Arbescar Light and Magic (ALM) is the institution where the party formed at the start of the campaign (Session 0, in Marrowdale). It functions as a governing academy or magical organization in Arbescar.

## Leadership

- **[[Belzander Umbridge]]** — Head Master; wants any blue crystals found returned to him
- **[[Serrenia Netherfall]]** — Head Mistress; friendly with Lothim (silver dragon); can be used to contact him; has a small annoyance with Tass; sent Carl home after an argument

## Resources

- **[[ALM Crystal]]** — The party holds an ALM Crystal that allows them to use speed travel, provided they notify [[Belzander Umbridge]] or [[Serrenia Netherfall]] at ALM before use
