# A'urea'Pura Cult

**Status:** Active (hostile)
**Base of Operations:** Blustery Waste excavation site; formerly [[Oasis of the Night|Oasis of the Night]]

## Overview

The A'urea'Pura Cult is an air genasi cult originally formed to worship a Djinn they call the D'jinn King. Their stated goal is to take over Arbescar. Cultists worship at night and have ties to the "broken part of the desert" � the Blustery Waste.

## The Crystal Bottle Connection

The D'jinn King is imprisoned in [[The Crystal Bottle|a crystal bottle]] inscribed in Terran: *"Death to whomever frees him. Wishes to whomever puts him back."* The bottle was found in an abandoned mine. The cult's power derives from the D'jinn's influence.
