# Salt And Cinder Press

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Summary

The Salt and Cinder Press is a broadsheet operation in Tratta characterized as "raw anti-corruption" — the opposition voice against the authority-aligned Gilded Ledger. It is named as part of the Tratta corruption picture discovered in Session 02. An anti-corruption press suggests it may be an ally, an asset, or a target depending on who controls it and how vulnerable it is.

## What Is Known

- Named alongside the Gilded Ledger in the Session 02 clues after the party reported to Thorin.
- Described as a "raw anti-corruption" broadsheet — it publishes against the civic power structure.
- No named individuals have been connected to it.
- Its physical location in Tratta is unknown.

## Role (Inferred)

The Salt and Cinder Press exists in direct tension with the Gilded Ledger. If the Gilded Ledger is the voice of a corrupt authority, the Salt and Cinder Press is the voice trying to expose it. This makes it a possible information source for the party — or a target for suppression by the colosseum network.

## Open Questions

- Who operates the Salt and Cinder Press — a journalist, a faction, a secret network?
- Has it published anything directly about the colosseum or south warehouse?
- Is it under threat from the authority aligned with the Gilded Ledger?
- Could the party use it to expose the trafficking operation?
