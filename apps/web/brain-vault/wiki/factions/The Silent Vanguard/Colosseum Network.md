# Colosseum Network

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Summary

The Colosseum Network is the suspected endpoint of the trafficking operation discovered in Brinecross. A warehouse letter found there describes crates, chains, cages, fish, spice, people, guards, and a shipment scheduled in 20 nights — consistent with arena fight trafficking feeding into a colosseum operation in or near Tratta. This is the probable financial and operational center of the corruption the Silent Vanguard is tasked to disrupt.

## What Is Known

- The south warehouse (near the old stone ramp in Tratta) is the staging point. The warehouse letter was addressed to **Gultren the Canvas Master** at the Sticky Pear Inn.
- The letter lists cargo: crates, chains, cages, fish, spice, people, guards. The combination suggests live human cargo mixed with mundane shipping as cover.
- The shipment is 20 nights from the letter's date — roughly the 40th of Ezale.
- The Gilded Ledger (authority-friendly broadsheet) and the Salt and Cinder Press (anti-corruption broadsheet) both appear connected to the broader colosseum corruption picture, suggesting the information war around the operation is active.
- **Spiky** — a famous triceratops referenced as a gladiator figure. Significance and alignment unknown; may be a draw for the colosseum.

## Key Named Figures

- **Gultren the Canvas Master** — recipient of the warehouse letter; based at the Sticky Pear Inn; unknown faction.
- **Spiky** — triceratops; gladiator; unknown alignment.

## The Party's Standing

- Recovered the warehouse letter from Brinecross and briefed Thorin (Silent Vanguard handler) on the zombie outbreak.
- The party leveled up after Session 02 and has confirmed knowledge of the 20-night shipment deadline.

## Open Questions

- Who controls the colosseum or organizes its fights?
- Where is the colosseum physically — Tratta, or another city?
- Who is Gultren the Canvas Master, and is he a middleman or a principal?
- What is Spiky's role — a victim, a gladiatorial attraction, or a symbol for the network?
- How does the Gilded Ledger financially support or launder for the colosseum?
- What happens at the south warehouse on night 20?
