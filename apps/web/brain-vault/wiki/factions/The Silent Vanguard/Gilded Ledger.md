# Gilded Ledger

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Summary

The Gilded Ledger is a broadsheet operation in Tratta characterized as "authority-friendly" — the establishment voice in the city's information space. It is named as part of the Tratta corruption picture discovered in Session 02. An authority-aligned press suggests it either supports, normalizes, or covers for the colosseum network and the trafficking operation.

## What Is Known

- The Gilded Ledger was identified (alongside Salt and Cinder Press) from the Session 02 clues after the party reported to Thorin.
- It is an authority-friendly broadsheet — it aligns with or amplifies the civic power structure, which is suspected to be corrupt.
- No named individuals have been connected to it.
- Its relationship to the colosseum network, south warehouse, and trafficking operation is inferred but not confirmed.

## Role (Inferred)

The Gilded Ledger likely serves to shape public perception in ways that protect the corruption network: suppressing stories, discrediting witnesses, or giving civic legitimacy to the colosseum. The contrast with the Salt and Cinder Press (anti-corruption) suggests active information competition in Tratta.

## Open Questions

- Who owns or controls the Gilded Ledger?
- Is it actively complicit, or simply aligned with the wrong authorities?
- Does it have direct financial ties to the colosseum or the south warehouse operation?
- Are there named individuals the party could approach or pressure?
