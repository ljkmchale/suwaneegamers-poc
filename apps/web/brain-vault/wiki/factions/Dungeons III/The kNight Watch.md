# The kNight Watch

## Summary

The kNight Watch is the name the Dungeons III party gives itself at [[Scarwatch Hold]] after delivering the K9 Crew prisoners.

## Members

- [[Meles]]
- [[Draelith]]
- [[Nixie]]
- [[Seraphine Veyne]]
- [[Nova]]
- [[Aeon]]

## Known Deeds

- Helped stop the Boar's Best robbery in [[Hesfal]].
- Pursued, captured, and returned [[K9 Crew]] bandits.
- Delivered K9 Crew prisoners to [[Scarwatch Hold]].
- Defeated the Outer Gate mimic.
- Accepted Umlin's mapping work and Nambra's Radiant Eye investigation.

## Current State

The group is operating east of Scarwatch Hold, tracking Isgo and Radiant Eye leads into the crags.
