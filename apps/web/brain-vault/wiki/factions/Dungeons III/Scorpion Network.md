# Scorpion Network

## Summary

The Scorpion Network is the current name for the hidden force behind the K9 Crew's contract in Dungeons III. The source identifies the employer as "the Scorpion," but does not yet give a personal name, organization name, or full motive.

## Known Facts

- The Scorpion paid the [[K9 Crew]] to terrorize [[Hesfal]] during Salvus.
- The Scorpion used a message/pouch routed through the K9 Crew chain.
- The Scorpion could speak through or into the K9 Crew hideout via a [[Blue Sending Stone]] and a magic-amplifying cavern.
- After the K9 Crew judgment, Draelith heard: "your interference has complicated matters, but perhaps you are more useful than they were."

## Open Questions

- Is the Scorpion a person, title, faction, or criminal network?
- Why did the Scorpion want Hesfal terrorized during Salvus?
- How does the Scorpion connect to Scarwatch Hold, Ralli, Ireosh, or the later Radiant Eye activity?
