# K9 Crew

## Summary

The K9 Crew is a bandit group in the Dungeons III campaign. They were hired by the [[Scorpion Network]] to terrorize [[Hesfal]] during Salvus.

## Known Facts

- The group included the Cap'n / Kinrik, Koren Lonsit / Kolo, Corin, and other bandits.
- One member was accused in Hesfal's murder case after being found with the victim's hairpin.
- The group staged or supported the Boar's Best robbery and Town Ward prisoner break.
- They fled to a cave hideout beyond Sherdin's lean-to.
- The party captured the group and returned them to Hesfal, then escorted them to [[Scarwatch Hold]].
- At Scarwatch Hold, the Cap'n confessed to the Scorpion interaction.

## Outcome

Koren was sentenced to marking and hard labor. The Cap'n was sentenced to conscription at Scarwatch Hold.
