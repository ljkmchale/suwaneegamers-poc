# Radiant Eye Cult

## Summary

The Radiant Eye Cult is an active threat in the Dungeons III campaign around [[Scarwatch Hold]] and the eastern crags.

## Known Facts

- The cult defaced the Scarwatch Hold Sanctuary with its symbol.
- Nambra says there is a lot of activity in the cult and that it is an affront to good.
- Rumors place an outpost in caves in the crags east of Scarwatch Hold.
- The previous curate, Selris, was thought to be associated with the cult, though the source does not prove the connection.
- Guards think two medium humanoids on horseback scaled the wall and committed the defacement between 2 and 3 in the morning.
- Embryl believes the cult may also be looking for the [[Osmanae Celestial Instrument]].

## Current State

The party rode east from Scarwatch Hold to investigate the cult, track Isgo, and pursue the Osmanae lead. At the end of Session 10, two tan-robed figures with kahlbits attacked in the crags.

## Open Questions

- Are the tan-robed attackers Radiant Eye cultists?
- Did Radiant Eye abduct Isgo?
- What does the cult want with the Osmanae observatory or celestial instrument?
