# Our Exquisites / Etterset

## Scope

HoE-only faction / contact network.

## Summary

The Etterset is Emberstran's independent contact network — not a thieves' guild in the traditional sense, but an organization that values freedom from coercion. Its core ethic: don't become anyone's piece. Members earn rank via presented coins. Tessa is now the street face and Our Exquisites (an arts studio at the Dyed Cane's street level) is the new base. Ky'tha holds a rank-2 coin introduced by Belavie Lightstrider. The Etterset is aligned against the Cloak and receptive to allying with Lady Rook — but cautious about overreach.

## Membership Known

- **Belavie Lightstrider** — pale elf, silvery eyes; senior member; lives in Emberstran; introduced Ky'tha to the organization. Handles higher-level relationships.
- **Tessa** — street face; introduced Ky'tha to Belavie; previously based at Charum's Oils; now new face of Our Exquisites.
- **an'Davo** — associate; paid for Ky'tha at the Dyed Cane; has good stories (lost caravan, etc.); recommended Tessa to Ky'tha. Thinks Rook was the intended hit at the Old Oak dinner.
- **Bordi** — figure present at the Dyed Cane introduction. Connected to the network.

## Rank System

- Rank conveyed via coin — must be earned and presented.
- Members are expected to safeguard and hide their coin.
- Ky'tha holds rank 2, given by Belavie.
- Higher ranks exist above rank 2 (Belavie implied the organization has a structured hierarchy).

## Base Of Operations

- **Charum's Oils** — Tessa's original base of operations; likely still an access point.
- **Our Exquisites** — new arts studio base; wide space, pedestals, sculptures, abstract carvings; smells new; located near or replacing the Dyed Cane storefront. Tessa is the public face.

## Values And Posture

- Core ethic: independence. Do not become anyone's piece — explicitly stated to Ky'tha at introduction.
- The Etterset has watched the Cloak "twist people" and values its own neutrality from both the Cloak and Lady Rook.
- Lady Rook needs the Etterset's help to gain ground in Emberstran; that relationship is cautious and strategic.
- The Etterset wants to take down the Cloak; willing to coordinate with the party and with Lady Rook's resources.

## Relationship To The Party

- Ky'tha is a rank-2 member, the party's primary link.
- Tessa asked for help taking down the Cloak (Session 24). Ky'tha said they have plans with Lady Rook toward that end.
- Tessa agreed to put out feelers about Zymve, discretely.
- Etterset is not surprised by Cloak surveillance of the party — they knew the Cloak was watching their return.

## Open Questions

- What does the Etterset know about the vault, the Cloak's leadership, or Jerik's location?
- Can Ky'tha earn a higher rank?
