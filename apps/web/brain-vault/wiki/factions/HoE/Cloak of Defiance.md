# Cloak of Defiance

## Scope

HoE-only faction / antagonist network.

## Summary

The Cloak of Defiance is Emberstran's dominant criminal-political power. It controls the town through assassination, civic record manipulation, sewer-network operations, and leverage over the Lord himself. Their stated goal: no changes in Emberstran. Their leader, Jerik, is feared by name alone. The party has killed three Cloak agents (Dust, Lockjaw, Smiley) and impersonated them in the sewers, making themselves known enemies.

## Leadership Hierarchy

- **Jerik** — top of the hierarchy; feared citywide; called "Ruthless Jerik." Has never been directly encountered.
- **Selvara Nendris ("The Black Quill")** — civic head, signed EGD documents under the Lord's seal. Operates from the Crimson Swan. Reports to Jerik; waits for his word before moving.
- Field operatives: **Silverheart**, **Whisper** (killed Jae Vynaar; likely also killed Bricco at the docks), **Lockjaw**, **Dust**, **Smiley/Talon**.
- Rank structure references an "Edge" tier; field agents call Smiley a "childhood friend with Edge."

## Methods

- Operate through a sewer network beneath Emberstran — the party found their back entrance and fought agents there.
- Use fake or forged EGD documents to establish civic legitimacy. The EGD itself was signed by the Black Quill, not the Lord.
- Employ assassination by poison (Whisper's method) for political targets.
- Maintain a red-cloak visual identity; one agent surveilled the party on a rooftop before their departure.
- A red-cloaked rider intercepted the party on the mountain trail and threatened that Jerik "now has your scent and will not forget."

## Goals And Interests

- "Don't want changes in town" — the Cloak benefits from the current power structure and acts to preserve it.
- Opposed to Lady Rook's rising influence; she was apparently the intended hit at the Old Oak & Grapes dinner (via Whisper/poison).
- Tried to shake down the Lord; the Lord "knows where the power is" per Lady Rook.
- Influenced or pressured the old Lord to falsify land records removing Hap's family claim.

## Relationship To The Party

- Selvara offered Ainslie and Og membership at the Crimson Swan (Session 13). They accepted provisionally. She said Jerik must give the word before she moves.
- Party impersonated Cloak agents in the sewers (Smalls/Talon/Biggie/Ivory cover aliases).
- Ky'tha told Etterset (Belavie) the location of the Cloak's sewer back entrance.
- Party is on Jerik's radar after the red-rider threat on the mountain trail.

## Connection To Hap's Family

- Benoit Garemon was murdered after the Cloak of Defiance took over the Malfease. He fled to the swamps and was eventually found and killed.
- The old Lord was "influenced by the Cloak or the Vintner's Guild" when he ordered Jae Vynaar to falsify records erasing the Garemon land claim.

- Is Silverheart still active in Emberstran?
