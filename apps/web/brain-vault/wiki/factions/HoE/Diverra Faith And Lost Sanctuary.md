# Diverra Faith And Lost Sanctuary

## Scope

HoE-only faction/faith thread.

## Summary

Diverra is the faith Aurelius serves as a cleric. The Lost Sanctuary (Bloomrest) is the original source of Diverra's doctrine and the place where Aury received Bloom Aeterna — the original words of Diverra herself. What the temple in Emberstran teaches is an incomplete version. The faith has an active quest embedded in it: find and restore the Shattered Hearts. Aury now holds knowledge Priestess Meladra does not, creating an asymmetry in the relationship.

## Core Doctrine (From Bloom Aeterna)

- Diverra is the Ardent One. The faith centers on passion, spark, and beauty in its natural form.
- "All beings hold divine fire." The faith affirms the inherent worth of all living creatures.
- **Shattered Hearts**: Fragments of lost passion scattered across the world. They must be found and returned to the proper place of worship dedicated to Diverra — shrines, temples, groves where her name is still honored. There, in sacred act, they are restored.
- **The Chalice**: At the Lost Sanctuary, Ky'tha found a carving of hands offering a chalice. Aury understood this to mean he must find a physical chalice as a material component for placing a Shattered Heart. The Lost Sanctuary itself may be the specific chalice location for one heart.

## Key Sites

- **Temple of Diverra, Emberstran** — Meladra's temple; active; the party's spiritual base in the city. May be teaching incomplete doctrine.
- **Diverra's Grove** — surface grove connected via waypoint; the party teleported here from the Lost Sanctuary using a rose petal ritual (reading from Bloom Aeterna).
- **The Lost Sanctuary (Bloomrest)** — original sacred site; underground grove accessed through a gate in the Crimson Lake (Rothenloch underground harbor area). Roses everywhere, hidden grove, ancient altar with a chalice carving. The Life Tree here was blighted; Og cleared it and bonded as E'erspan.

## Bloom Aeterna

- A tome given to Aury at the Lost Sanctuary. His original book was replaced — it now bears the words "Bloom Aeterna" and contains original Diverra scripture in Celestial.
- Aury has not disclosed its full contents to Meladra.
- Contains the Shattered Hearts mission and possibly other doctrine not currently known to the wider Diverra clergy.

## The Shattered Hearts Quest

- Material components of love (which is immaterial, per Aury's reading).
- Each heart must be placed at its proper shrine or grove while praying to Diverra.
- The Chalice at the Lost Sanctuary altar appears to be the vessel for at least one heart.
- No Shattered Hearts have been located yet.

## Relationship Dynamics

- **Aury and Hap** share a Diverra vision that linked them from early in the campaign.
- **Meladra** is a caring authority who senses that Aury's calling has grown beyond her scope. She senses his portal calling and releases him from obligations. She does not yet know what he found.
- **Blackrend** is the faith's active enemy — the blight that attacks Life Trees and sacred groves.
