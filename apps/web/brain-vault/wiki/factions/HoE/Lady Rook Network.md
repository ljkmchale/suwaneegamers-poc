# Lady Rook Network

## Scope

HoE-only faction/network.

## Summary

Lady Rook's network is a small, politically sophisticated operation centered on gaining access to the Mount Emberstran vault. She uses property leverage, hostage pressure, information brokers, and a reclusive wizard ally to maneuver the party toward the vault outcome she wants. She has now been elected to a civic leadership role in Emberstran and is officially positioned to act on whatever the vault yields.

## Known Members

- **Bivra Rook ("Lady Rook")** — leader; see [[Lady Rook]].
- **Imonorra ("Lady in the Tower")** — reclusive wizard; never leaves her tower; holds the rod that can bring the Marrowstone to its full potential; Lady Rook trusts her with her life; they "go way back."
- **Datheril** — greeter and doorman at Imonorra's tower; manages access on Rook's behalf.
- **Petal** — tracker/spy; blond wood elf; tracked the party toward Mount Emberstran; killed by Zymve at a trail fork. Rook does not yet know she is dead.
- Unnamed red-cloak Cloak of Defiance member observed on rooftops — watching party but possibly acting at Rook's direction or independently.

## Operations And Leverage

- **Property leverage**: Holds the deed to Hap's family land (Cataen property); offered the Digglepots' 2200gp purchase offer as a tool.
- **Hostage leverage**: Hap's brother-in-law (Phira's husband, Jerik's brother) is "surrounded by protection" — effectively held as security to keep the party from acting against her.
- **Marrowstone**: Asked the party to deliver the Marrowstone so Imonorra can attach her rod and restore it to full power; she believes it can be used against the Cloak.
- **Political position**: Was elected to civic leadership in Emberstran; Lady Tarathial allowed to remain at the Vintner's Mansion; Rook now operates from the Suncrest Cellarium.
- **Intelligence**: Tessa told Ky'tha to check in with her; Rook seems to maintain loose Etterset ties while pursuing vault goals.

## Relationship To The Cloak

- Rook's stated position: not aligned with the Cloak; her allegiance is to herself (per what she told Ky'tha).
- She was apparently the intended assassination target at the Old Oak & Grapes dinner (Whisper, Cloak agent, was responsible).
- She told Ky'tha that the Marrowstone could take down the Cloak — "not sure if it could take down Jerik, but most from the Crimson Swan."
- She told Ky'tha to have the Lord make the EGD legitimate.

## What She Wants

- Access to the vault beneath Mount Emberstran. Everything — the Marrowstone, the Petal tracking, the hostage leverage, the party's EGD cover — feeds toward this goal.
- Whether she wants the vault's contents for herself, for Emberstran, or on someone else's behalf is not stated.

- What is Imonorra's actual role — partner, asset, or something else?
- Is her protection of Hap's brother-in-law genuine, or leverage that ends when she has what she wants?
