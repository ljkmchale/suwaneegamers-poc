# Emberstran / E.G.D.

## Scope

HoE-only civic/group frame.

## Summary

The Emberstran Guard Detail (EGD) is the party's official civic cover. Ainslie and Og initially used self-created observer/analyst badges as improvised authority; after the party cleared the Vintner's Guild cellar blight and the Baltwood blight, Emlee and Haggis delivered a scroll at the Warm Bear Inn making the EGD official. The catch: the scroll was signed by the Black Quill (Selvara Nendris, Cloak of Defiance), not the Lord — meaning the party's legitimacy is entangled with the faction they may be opposing.

## What The EGD Is

- Official investigator status for Emberstran, signed and presented via scroll.
- Grants the party investigative authority to enter civic sites, question guards and citizens, and operate under guard protection.
- Used as a social/authority tool: Ainslie flashes the EGD at guards, crime scenes, and checkpoints throughout the campaign.

## How It Became Official

- Ainslie initially made fake badges and used "Observer and Analyst" titles on his own authority.
- After the Vintner's Guild blight and Baltwood work, Emlee and Haggis delivered a scroll at the Warm Bear Inn making them the "official investigators of Emberstran."
- The EGD scroll was signed by the Black Quill — discovered later. Selvara Nendris controls who has civic authority in Emberstran; the Lord signs what he is told.
- Ky'tha later told Lady Rook to have the Lord make the EGD "a real thing," suggesting she is aware of the legitimacy gap.

## Known Civic Structure Connected To The EGD

- **Lord Garrod Dusklight** — Emberstran's lord; "knows where the power is"; was pressured by the Cloak; politically cautious. Earlier notes also spell this as Garris Dusklite.
- **Vammad** — captain of the guard; long-tenured; Lady Tarathial directed the party to him for record access.
- **Captain Amilla** — human female guard captain; encountered at the docks zombie scene; interacted with Ainslie and Og (EGD).
- **Igabell Orebane** — civic authority figure referenced in connection with EGD operations.

## Open Questions

- Does the Lord know the EGD was signed by the Black Quill rather than himself?
- Now that Lady Rook has been elected to civic power, does the EGD fall under her jurisdiction?
- What obligations come with official EGD status — does it bind the party to Selvara?
- How does civic legitimacy shift if the Cloak falls?
