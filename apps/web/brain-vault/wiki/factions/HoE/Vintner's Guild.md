# Vintner's Guild

## Scope

HoE-only faction.

## Summary

The Vintner's Guild is Emberstran's winemaking authority, headquartered in a large establishment north of the city through farmlands and vineyards (~½ mile from the gates). The party's first real investigation led here: a murdered woman (Kelona), a tainted vintage, a blight-infested cellar, and a guild authority (Lady Tarathial) who is embroiled in civic rivalry with Lady Rook. The Guild's fate shifts when Lady Rook is elected — Lady Tarathial is allowed to remain at the Vintner's Mansion but is no longer the leading civic power.

## The Guildhouse

- Large central establishment on a hill; farmlands and vineyards on approach from Emberstran's north gate.
- Has a foyer, private rooms, cellars beneath with lean-to doors; stairs descend 10-15'.
- Cellars held the party's first combat: a Grapevine Blight infestation had destroyed most stock; only 5 untouched barrels remained ("Tears of Baltwood" — aged, special vintage).
- Old blood stains mixed with wine stains; stale, putrid air. The blight is cleared.

## Key Members

- **Lady Tarathial (Tarathial Rulendor)** — gray robes, unbrushed hair; older woman; the Guild's civic authority. Was embroiled in civic rivalry with Lady Rook throughout the campaign. After Rook's election she remains at the Vintner's Mansion, ceded from top civic position but still present.
- **Emlee Tryndel** — Kelona's closest friend; spoke at the service; later delivered the official EGD scroll to the party at the Warm Bear Inn (with Haggis).
- **Haggis** — guard dog handler at the Guild; keys to the cellar; delivered EGD scroll to party with Emlee.
- **Kelona** — murdered; throat slit at the Old Oak & Grapes; her death opened the investigation in Session 02.
- **Meglan Whistlebell** — Emberstran resident connected to the Guild investigation; party interviewed her at her house.

## The Murder And Blight

- Kelona was killed at the Old Oak & Grapes; this led the party to the Guild.
- The cellars contained a Grapevine Blight infestation — source unclear, possibly tied to the broader Blackrend blight or something more mundane.
- Ainslie identified the creature as a Grapevine Blight; combat cleared the cellar.
- A Balwood vintage ("Tears of Baltwood") survived and five barrels were recovered.
- Lady Tarathial was moved to tears when the cellar was secured.

## Civic Rivalry With Lady Rook

- Lady Tarathial did not know Imonorra; asked the party if Lady Rook was tied to vineyard purchases.
- Lady Rook wanted the Guild's civic position and the broader stewardship of Emberstran.
- After Rook's election, Lady Tarathial was allowed to remain at the Vintner's Mansion (per Lady Rook's terms), not expelled.
