# Blackrend And Blight

## Scope

HoE-only threat / spiritual corruption thread.

## Summary

The Blackrend is a blight-entity or corruption force that attacks Life Trees and sacred groves. The party encountered it directly at the Lost Sanctuary of Diverra (Bloomrest), fought dark druids who seemed to be conducting or channeling it, and cleared the blight from the Life Tree there after Og bonded with it as E'erspan. Separately, the Marrowstone carried necrotic energy described in similar terms. The blight's full nature — divine conflict, extraplanar origin, or something older — has not been explained.

## What Is Known

- **Appearance at the Lost Sanctuary**: Dark shapes burst from the earth at the grove; tree-like creatures with "black wispy-ness" around them; needle blights and vine-root grapples. Dark druids (two humanoid creatures described as "from some place not of this plane") were present.
- **The Alferumn**: The Life Tree at the Lost Sanctuary is described as part of the Alferumn — "the great cycle of green and shadow, the living pulse that binds the world's roots and branches." When the balance frays, the Alferumn suffers. Og heard this directly from the thornleaf.
- **Og's E'erspan calling**: The thornleaf directed Og to touch the tree and bond with it. He felt the tree's pain — roots starved, poisoned. This is the "second time he has felt the blight." He became E'erspan, guardian/thorn-bearer.
- **After the clearing**: The tree had new life the morning after. The Blackrend was "driven back" at Bloomrest.
- **Broader spread**: The Blackrend threat extended beyond Bloomrest. Meladra noted "the blight is now fixed" as one of the city-shifts she observed, suggesting it had been visibly present in or near Emberstran before.
- **Marrowstone connection**: The Marrowstone carried necrotic energy; Lady Rook said it could be used to take down the Cloak, and Imonorra had the rod to bring it to full potential. The relationship between Marrowstone necrotic energy and the Blackrend is unclear but thematically adjacent.

## Thornleaf And The Black Thorn

- Og's thornleaf carries spiritual consequence. After Zymve killed Petal, the thornleaf gained a black thorn.
- The thornleaf previously went silent when Og asked if Petal was important.
- These spiritual consequences suggest the thornleaf / Alferumn bond tracks moral weight, not just combat actions.

## Open Questions

- What is the Blackrend's actual origin — is it a divine opponent to Diverra, an extraplanar force, or something older?
- Is the blight still spreading in other groves, or was the Bloomrest clearing decisive?
- What does the black thorn on Thornleaf mean for Og's E'erspan responsibilities?
- Is the Marrowstone's necrotic energy a form of captured or weaponized Blackrend, or something separate?
- Are the dark druids at Bloomrest part of a larger organization?
