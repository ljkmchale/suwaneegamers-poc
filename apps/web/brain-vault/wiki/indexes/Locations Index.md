# Locations Index

## HoE

- [[Emberstran]] — volcanic coastal city; rebuilt after Mount Emberstran erupted over old Winbalt; wine is primary trade; civic power contested between Cloak, Rook, and the Lord.
- [[Docks And Anchor Point]] — busy foggy port district; vanished crew/tadpole case; first tablet clue; Berg Stugmyer (dockmaster); Darn (Ainslie's fisherman contact).
- [[Old Oak And Grapes]] — upscale pub in the hills; Kelona's murder site (Session 02); Cloak assassination attempt against Rook; Odith is barkeep.
- [[Vintner's Guild Cellar]] — cellar beneath the Guildhouse; Grapevine Blight cleared; Tears of Baltwood vintage survived; five barrels recovered.
- [[Diverra's Grove]] — surface sacred grove; teleportation waypoint from Bloomrest via rose petal ritual; Arnol behavior thread.
- [[Nunglthil]] — underground harbor settlement carved into cliff walls; accessed via Shenog's boat from Rothenloch; Darkbottom Draught (inn), Coppereyes (Scalamank), Miss Placed Tomes, Silken Secrets (Szoradryn).
- [[Thallgrove And Bimblefol]] — underground fungi biome; Makra (genling); Bimblefol village; Zymve/genling music bond; Ky'tha's Dunduar/Muerg vision; route to the Lost Sanctuary.
- [[Lost Sanctuary And Bloomrest]] — original Diverra sacred site underground; accessed through Crimson Lake gate; roses, Life Tree, altar with chalice carving; Aury received Bloom Aeterna here; Og bonded as E'erspan.
- [[Mount Emberstran And Vault]] — the volcano; Sparkwhisper hamlet (Emberbogs and Fibbergoans gnomes) inside; vault door opened by Ainslie; Session 25 starts at threshold.
- The Dyed Cane — main party gathering spot in Emberstran; Bordi runs it; private rooms; later houses Our Exquisites (Etterset base).
- Headless Hydra — inn; Devron (dwarf owner); Ky'tha's base in Emberstran.
- Warm Bear Inn — party inn; EGD meetings held here; Emlee delivered EGD scroll here.
- Crimson Swan — Emberstran's finest venue; Cloak of Defiance meeting place; Selvara Nendris operates here.
- Suncrest Cellarium — Lady Rook's current base after election; previously Vintner's territory.
- Imonorra's Tower — reclusive wizard Imonorra; Datheril is doorman; Lady Rook meets here.
- Baltwood — forest region on the approach to Mount Emberstran; Petal tracked the party here.
- Diverra's Grove / Meladra's grove lead — sacred grove connected to the Blackrend/blight thread; source notes sometimes label this "Maladra's Grove."

## SoD

- [[Adsuren]] — main settlement; poisoned well, Market Day, cult exposure, and civic scandal; Nature's Oratory, Guard House, Graying Grotto, Falling Fish, Mollywop's Tower, docks.
- [[Adsuren Center And Cellar]] — civic building and hidden multi-room cult complex beneath; storage → secret room → bunkroom → disposal room → prep room → ritual chamber; Eyovar exposed and Aeolenne freed here.
- [[Hath'lao Swamp]] — marshy terrain between Adsuren river crossing and Sapcutt Plantation; black fish origin; roar/silhouette of large unknown creature.
- [[Sapcutt Plantation]] — corrupted farmstead; Lady Sapcutt (FUKWAD); Lobella and Margaret rescued; black fish/fungal infection origin.
- [[Mines Near Adsuren]] — current party destination (departed 45th Hemist); Durgruck Bristleforge's mine sickness death; Bulas creature reward; current next arc.
- [[Caldwynn]] — major shipping hub 5 days south; Aeolenne fled here; cult has an ally here; spiral watermark shipping origin; possible next arc city.
- [[Gibuldon]] — large city; Inverland capital; seat of House Reyvennra (Vesci-tier nobles); Kenton's warehouse fire accusation and murky past here; bound/bloodied dwarf memory; Cult of Immured members from here; Kenton's letter to House Reyvennra in transit; home of Lords of Magic (Grand Tower), MEAD Society, Dean Bozous Dunsmoore.
- Graying Grotto — inn in Adsuren; Esylla's base and party accommodation.
- Falling Fish — tavern; used for Ganon investigation and Kenton/Rodolf context discussions.
- Windbelly Estate — southern Adsuren; Ganon's rented room (door 5); rat-head ritual circle discovered here.
- Nature's Oratory — Adsuren religious institution; Rotvori; cemetery attached; see Factions index.
- Ol'Morrey — Esylla's aunt's location; contacted via Mollywop's Sending.
- Gibuldon Warehouse — fire site tied to Kenton's silks and the Reyvennra accusation.

## Bloody Endeavor

- **Alasfolen** — garrison/prison where the campaign begins; ruled by pale elf "The Mistress" Mylaerla; 19 elf guards; all party members started as slaves here.
- **Climbor** — primary city in the Rostiena Foothills region; ruled by 4 Eldermen (Vies Eldorman); Caifir family estate; the Hole of Climbor (underground sunken district where the Gabüks live); inns: Tamed Moons, Drunk Doves, Paranormic Lady, Polite Well. Cathedral of Utheri is here. Major recurring hub throughout the campaign.
- **The Hole of Climbor** — underground area beneath Climbor; home to the oppressed Gabüks; contains catacombs, a mural of Climbor's founding, the sunken area; explored in Sessions 04, 10, 12, 23.
- **Goddard Family Cabin** — 2-story log cabin south of Climbor in a clearing; scene of an old murder; shadow figure (Masked Man) seen outside; key-with-note found inside.
- **Colbath's Keep** — noble keep southeast of Climbor near the mountains; Victor Colbath; Eva (dying daughter); site of Sessions 06-09.
- **Kobold Cave** — cave southwest of Climbor where the Bone Marigold grows; Sirn Zuss's kobolds guarded it; Aishka the Braizen was imprisoned here; explored Sessions 07-08.
- **Witguard** — frontier town defended against the Dalasor Coast (Malithea Empire); Magistrate Oddnel rules it; Thornfold Bastion is south of it; thousands came to defend it; major hub Sessions 15-23.
- **Thornfold Bastion** — fort south of Witguard between the Rostiena Foothills and the Dalasor Coast; key strategic location in the regional war.
- **The Safehouse** — ruined tower 100 feet above an underground complex; reached via 100-foot descent; was a Spikethroats base (Safe House 4); Cult of Verander connection; explored Sessions 16-18. Lilian later destroyed it.
- **Yag'vur Sierra** — mountain range where the second half of the campaign takes place; home to the Runegrip dwarves, giants, the Monks of Iohir, and the ancient sealed aberration.
- **Yaggermon Mountains** — the specific mountain sub-range at Fyrstholith (first peak); locations: Hol'gardur (foot), Hol'kulrum (destroyed by giants), Hol'durog (highest).
- **Hol'gardur** — Runegrip clan village at the foot of Fyrstholith; elder Fomeck Runegrip; druid Groretta Runeeyes (spirit trial); Runestout Inne; cold plague affecting crops; site of Bakah's memorial cairn.
- **Hol'kulrum** — large Runegrip city destroyed by giants; trophy ice of dwarves in the center; giant fort occupying the ruins.
- **Hol'durog** — highest Runegrip community; captured by Monks of Iohir when party arrived; three towers overlooking a frozen lake; dwarf prisoners freed here.
- **Caves of Kroher** — cave complex in the mountains where The Cold Breath (Huldi) held the first Rimestone piece; large dwarven hall, Royal Lounge, Kitchen/void portal, The Cold Breath boss fight (Session 27). Bakah died here.
- **Vanapur Mountains** — mountain range east of Matahal Valley where Fort Mornock is located; Madam Mabel's orchard is accessed via a tree portal to this area.
- **Madam Mabel's Apple Orchard** — in an illusioned clearing in the mountains; Madam Mabel's home; Sessions 28-30; the tree portal leads to the Matahal Valley.
- **Matahal Valley** — valley on the eastern side of the Vanapur Mountains; hag mine location; near hamlet Lensloh; southern tip where the tree portal arrives.
- **Fort Mornock** — "Fortress of the Flayer"; tomb of dwarven warlord Mornock the Flayer in the Vanapur Mountains; dragon Presagiath nested in the top level; explored Sessions 31-32. Major treasure hoard recovered.
- **Khagdaruhl** (KAG-DA-RRULE) — major dwarven underground kingdom; lava-lit cavern; gemstone walls; 55% Duergar, 35% Mountain Dwarf, 10% Hill Dwarf; Ring of Respect arena; smithing/mage/entertainment districts; party arrived Sessions 34-35.
- **[[Gevakaln]]** — coastal city in Rostiena Foothills; ~23,000 population; seat of the Sable Shadows criminal org; Albross's brother Breemos is here (kidnapping threat); Azer Launce originally from here; Masked Man/"Jason" connected to Sable Shadows/Gevakaln. Three districts: Fogwalk Underbelly (slums), Mittler Ward, Brydove Heights (wealthy). Ruler: Alred Brydove (High-elf Noble, rarely leaves his keep). Prison island Sciogost (~360 prisoners) offshore. Appears respectable; deeply corrupt underneath.
- **Nurador** — Lucerion's hometown; orcs reportedly marching toward it.
- **Narzon** — town on the other side of the Vanapur Mountains; wine-trading; home of the Duvall noble family (Sir Duvall met at Khagdaruhl).
- **Rostiena Foothills** — the broader territory the party has been defending; contains Climbor and Witguard; in conflict with the Dalasor Coast (Malithea Empire).
- **Dalasor Coast** — territory of the Malithea Empire; building armies on the Rostiena border; also sending ambassadors to Khagdaruhl.

## World — Dhá Chaomhnóir

*(No campaign has visited Dhá Chaomhnóir. Sourced from the Dhá Chaomhnóir Gazetteer.)*

- **[[Dhá Chaomhnóir]]** — wealthiest city in Myrdae; on the southern coast of Inverland (Aelbon territory); home of the largest RDE Vault in Myrdae; governed by Great Houses (Cur family dominant); Lionheart criminal syndicate; Populi Verde nature cult operates outside the gates; 28 named locations including The Vaults, Old Dicon House, Brym's Blades, The Pointy End (Tegenwald Phet), and multiple inns/taverns/brothels.

## World — Everlight

*(The Crystal Bottle party visited Everlight extensively. Sourced from the Everlight Gazetteer — see TCB section for campaign-specific events.)*

- **[[Everlight]]** — isolated village in Arbescar (Var'Shala); snow-kissed valley near the Blustery Waste and Frostbite Mountain; protected by Urlich's mercy 5,000 years ago when Sunlit Haven fell; perpetual winter + artificial Everlight sun; governed by a 5-member Village Council; 7 named locations; nearby Silver Dragon Lithium controls the north mountain pass.
