# Factions And Groups Index

## HoE

- [[Cloak of Defiance]] — Emberstran's dominant criminal-political power; Jerik (top) → Selvara Nendris (Black Quill) → field operatives; sewer network, forged EGD, assassination; has explicitly threatened the party.
- [[Our Exquisites - Etterset]] — independent contact network; coin-based rank system; values non-coercion; Belavie Lightstrider (senior), Tessa (street face), an'Davo; based at Our Exquisites arts studio; Ky'tha holds rank 2.
- [[Lady Rook Network]] — Lady Rook's vault-focused operation; Imonorra (reclusive wizard), Datheril (doorman), Petal (killed); uses property/hostage/Marrowstone leverage; Rook now elected to civic leadership.
- [[Emberstran EGD]] — party's civic cover; EGD scroll signed by the Black Quill, not the Lord; civic structure includes Lord Garrod Dusklight, Vammad, Amilla; legitimacy is entangled with Cloak.
- [[Vintner's Guild]] — Emberstran wine authority; Lady Tarathial (authority), Emlee Tryndel, Haggis; connected to Kelona's murder and cellar blight; Lady Tarathial remains at Vintner's Mansion after Rook's election.
- [[Diverra Faith And Lost Sanctuary]] — Aury's faith; Bloom Aeterna holds original doctrine (Shattered Hearts mission, Chalice); Meladra's temple teaches incomplete version; Lost Sanctuary is the original site.
- [[Blackrend And Blight]] — blight/corruption force attacking Life Trees; cleared at Bloomrest by Og's E'erspan bonding; dark druids were conducting it; black thorn on Thornleaf is a moral consequence thread.

## SoD

- [[Cult of the Immured]] — blood/heart cult beneath Adsuren Center; Eyovar Dunajor as Summoner Karranok; 20-year operation freeing Aeolenne; dismantled in Session 08; external cells in Caldwynn/Gibuldon/Morrey may remain.
- [[Adsuren Guard]] — Captain Alinia Sarralei's force; ~12 guards; strained by Market Day, tax theft, Heckudor's death, and cult exposure; Zephyra has deepest credibility.
- [[Nature's Oratory]] — Adsuren's religious institution; Tanluar Rotvori (older half-elf priest); Escanor's spiritual base; involved in well, crypt, graveyard, and cult aftermath.
- [[Mollywop's Tower And Apprentices]] — Mollywop's wizard tower with apprentices (Doderi, Thunderfall, Lix) and a contained demon (Zuz); town ward/alarm, Sending, heart analysis, identify; Mollywop was paid to keep secrets (undisclosed).
- [[House Reyvennra]] — powerful Vesci-tier Gibuldon noble house; connected to Rodolf Norran by name/mark and comments, but direct orders are unconfirmed; connected to warehouse fire, lost silks; Kenton sent a provocative letter inviting a meeting; no response yet.
- [[Caldwynn Shipping Network]] — supply chain for the black salt barrel; overpaid taxes, delivery-to-Tanner letter, spiral watermark; Aeolenne has an ally in Caldwynn; MacCannain and Panwin are chain members.
- Sapcutt Family — swamp farming family corrupted by a black fish/fungus plant bargain; Lady Sapcutt fought as FUKWAD; Lobella and Margaret rescued.
- Windbelly Household — halfling landlords for Ganon's rented room in southern Adsuren.

## The Silent Vanguard

- [[Colosseum Network]] — suspected trafficking endpoint in/near Tratta; warehouse letter documents crates, chains, people, 20-night shipment; Gultren the Canvas Master is the named contact; Spiky is a gladiatorial figure.
- [[Gilded Ledger]] — authority-friendly broadsheet in Tratta; civic/power aligned; probable information cover for the corruption network.
- [[Salt And Cinder Press]] — raw anti-corruption broadsheet in Tratta; opposition voice against the Gilded Ledger; possible party information ally.
- Silent Vanguard — the party's employer organization; Master Thorne runs assignments from the Lower Dock area of Tratta; the Wavehammer serves as party cover.

## Dungeons III

- [[The kNight Watch]] - party name adopted at Scarwatch Hold; members are Meles, Draelith, Nixie, Seraphine, Nova, and Aeon.
- [[K9 Crew]] - bandits hired to terrorize Hesfal during Salvus; captured and delivered to Scarwatch Hold.
- [[Scorpion Network]] - hidden employer behind the K9 Crew; contacts Draelith through the blue sending stone.
- [[Radiant Eye Cult]] - cult tied to Scarwatch Hold Sanctuary defacement and rumored crag outpost.

## Bloody Endeavor

- **Wyrm Bane** — the party itself; named in Session 15 after freeing the copper dragon Saildreith. Active members: Albross, Caelion, Lucerion, Pagern, Rhody Falco.
- **Monks of Iohir (The Cold Breath Cult)** — fanatical monks led by Master Huldi (The Cold Breath); seek to assemble the Rimestone and cause a new ice age, ostensibly to re-seal an eldritch aberration. Active in the Yag'vur Sierra. Neha was a disciple. Harhet Steelfoot represented a neutral faction within the order (Advents of Harmony).
- **The Sacred Crows** — allied adventuring party sent by Magistrate Oddnel to the Yag'vur Sierra; members: Armaros Eligora (wizard), Rina Sarthona (rogue), Hefeic Icehelm (cleric), Mhaenal "Maw" Cargwyn (ranger), Kotio Etric (barbarian). Started in Stoneshore. Fought Wyrm Bane in the Ring of Respect; lost.
- **Runegrip Clan** — dwarven clan living in Hol'gardur, Hol'kulrum, and Hol'durog at the Fyrstholith peak (Yaggermon Mountains); crops affected by Cold Breath frost; sent monks up the mountain unknowingly; held Rimestone Piece 1 (stolen by Huldi).
- **Khagdaruhl** — the dwarven kingdom; Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV rules; mixed dwarven population; historically allied with Monks of Iohir to seal the aberration; currently being courted by both Witguard and the Dalasor Coast.
- **Malithea Empire / Dalasor Coast** — the opposing faction in the regional war; building armies on the Rostiena border; sent Ambassador Nym Perxidor to Khagdaruhl to offer trade and alliance.
- **Witguard / Rostiena Foothills** — the party's patron territory; Magistrate Oddnel governs; seeking Khagdaruhl's alliance; Saildreith (copper dragon) serves as Shield of Witguard.
- **Sable Shadows** — criminal organization based in Gevakaln; Lozire owed them debts; Jasper "Five-fingers" was a debt collector; the Masked Man/Jason may be connected; Albross's brother Breemos is at risk from them.
- **Cult of Verander** — cult worshipping an elf named Verander; operates underground beneath cities (Safe Houses); C/4 was the Climbor safehouse; the Spikethroats were a group associated with this network. Lilian hunts this cult.
- **The Paragon Tear** — organization hunting the Cult of Verander and related threats; members include Lilian, Ms. Ade, Ralajyre. The Paragon Tear amulet was stolen by the Masked Man.
- **Advents of Harmony** — a neutral/reform faction within the Monks of Iohir; represented by Harhet Steelfoot; cooperated with the party after capture.
- **Seventh Company of Paendley** — military company from Paendley; Captain Dorralei (killed), Tralar (defected), Ms. Ade and Ralajyre (Paragon Tear members); sent to reinforce Witguard's region.
- **The Planar Slavers (Unknown Faction)** — pale elves not from Myrdae; divine and fey power; god blocks speak-with-dead; ran Alasfolen; targeted Lucerion specifically. Mylaerla (The Mistress) was their local face. Faction name and goals unknown.

## World (Gazetteers — No Active Campaign Tie)

- **Cur Family** — dominant power in [[Dhá Chaomhnóir]]; crest is a lion with a heart; control brothels and gambling houses throughout Dunmoore; leader = Don Cur; run several Dhá establishments (Puss & Boots, A Bud in Bloom) for money laundering. Connected to the Lionheart syndicate.
- **Lionheart** — mob-like criminal syndicate in [[Dhá Chaomhnóir]]; lion insignia on the heart; presents itself as a city protection organization.
- **Populi Verde** — nature enthusiasts who worship Madre Verde (conflated with "The Mother"); reviled by most towns; expelled from Dhá by the Great Houses; formerly "Wanted" (wrongfully); involved in defeating The Woeborn (Suthrin cult); former leader Ainran missing after All Bright ceremony. Base: Mother's Shrine outside Dhá's gates.
- **The Woeborn** *(Defunct)* — Suthrin-worshipping cult based near Dhá; taken down by the Populi Verde.
- **Realm Depository & Exchange (RDE)** — banking network spanning all of Myrdae; operates a "Reserve" of Vaults; the Dhá Vault is the largest; all personnel are male elves (guards, associates, Vault Keepers) except hooded Monks (various races). Vault Keeper in Dhá: Walik.
