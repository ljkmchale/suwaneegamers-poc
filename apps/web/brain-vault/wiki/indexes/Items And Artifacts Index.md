## Bloody Endeavor

**The Rimestone** — a three-piece magical artifact created by ancient dwarven mages to freeze an eldritch aberration. Piece 1 stolen by Master Huldi (Monks of Iohir). Piece 2 held at Khagdaruhl. Piece 3 location unknown. See [[The Rimestone]].

**The Broken Sword** — a blade of mithral, dunduar, and silver; combined human, dwarven, and orcish make. Party holds the main blade body plus two shards (stored in Caelion's Bag of Holding in two small black boxes). Missing the tip and the hilt. Found: Piece 1 in the orc fort chest (Session 15), Piece 2 from hag Viola (Session 30). Enchantment, evocation, and abjuration energies detected.

**Saildreith Bull Horn** — one-time use horn given to Bakah T'luk by the copper dragon Saildreith; Saildreith will hear it and know the location. Bakah is dead; horn's status unknown.

**The Paragon Tear Amulet** — amulet connected to the Paragon Tear organization; caused Rhody's infection when grabbed bare-handed; stolen by the Masked Man ("Jason") who used it to summon a beetle beast.

**Mabel's Monocle** — given by Madam Mabel to Rhody; Darkvision 150', See Invisibility. Carried by Rhody.

**OrcReaver** — magic handaxe from Fort Mornock hoard; carried by Rhody.

**Rune of Sennogin** — spiritual mark given to Rhody by the spirit Sennogam after completing the Hol'gardur trial; allows the spirit to aid him on the mountain; attuned.

**Boots of Levitation** — from Fort Mornock hoard; carried by Pagern.

**Medallion of Thoughts** — from Fort Mornock hoard; carried by Pagern.

**Bowl of Controlling Water Elementals** — from Fort Mornock hoard; carried by Albross.

**Scroll of Imprisonment** — recovered from hag Viola; carried by Pagern.

**Scroll of Globe of Invulnerability (6th level)** — from Fort Mornock hoard.

**Unidentified Abjuration Scroll (violet case)** — from Fort Mornock hoard; contents unknown.

**The Holy Symbol of Osanna** — found on a dead priest skeleton in Fort Mornock; burns Lucerion's red Natafae arm when touched; Pagern holds it.

**Lucerion's Red Arm** — not an item, but a divine gift from Natafae replacing Lucerion's real arm lost to hag Viola. Blood red, serrated edge at the join, functional but burns on contact with Osanna's holy symbol.
