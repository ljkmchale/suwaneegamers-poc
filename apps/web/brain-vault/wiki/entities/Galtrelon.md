# Galtrelon (Gal)

**Class:** Archdruid  
**Race:** Elf  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]  
**Player:** Sean Poole  
**Title:** Archdruid of the Howling Grotto  
**Home:** Howling Grotto

## Character Overview

Galtrelon — called Gal — is an Elf Archdruid who joined the party in Session 6.3 as a replacement for the fallen [[Tassarion Ranges]]. He met the party at [[Everlight]] after Tass's funeral. He was introduced as the Archdruid of the Howling Grotto and was tasked by [[Lothim]] to protect [[Everlight]] and investigate its time distortions. His first major contribution to the party was acquiring the [[Staff of the Slumbering Grove]] and leading the grove unsealing ritual that freed individuals trapped in the Everlight cave — including [[Alteya]]'s family.

## Weapons and Equipment

- **[[Staff of the Slumbering Grove]]** — acquired in Session 6.5 from the Everlight cave; used to lead the grove unsealing ritual
- **Wild Shape** — transforms into animals; used snake form in Session 6.4; used purple worm in later combat
- **Speak with Plants** — used to negotiate passage through vine tunnels (Session 6.5)

## Notable Session Events

- **Session 6.3** — Joined the party in [[Everlight]] as [[Tassarion Ranges]]'s replacement; introduced as the Archdruid of the Howling Grotto; gave [[Alteya]] her husband's journal and badge.
- **Session 6.4** — Fought an icy creature; transformed into a snake (Wild Shape) during combat; identified druidic runes indicating the cave functioned as a prison.
- **Session 6.5** — Used Speak with Plants to negotiate passage through vine tunnels; identified a preservation altar; fought howlers and a mimic; acquired the [[Staff of the Slumbering Grove]]; led the grove unsealing ritual, freeing trapped individuals including [[Alteya]]'s family.

## Motivations

1. **Protect Everlight** — Committed to guarding the land as tasked by [[Lothim]]; actively working to understand the time distortions affecting [[Everlight]].
2. **Preserve Druidic Legacy** — Uses his staff and knowledge to maintain druidic traditions; identified the cave's druidic function and led its unsealing.
3. **Pursue Kal'dar** — Aligned with the group's goal to stop [[Kal'dar]] from raising an army.
4. **Aid Alteya's Family** — Gave [[Alteya]] her husband's journal and badge (Session 6.3); helped uncover the cave prison holding her family; committed to resolving their fate.

## Relationships

- **[[Lothim]]** — Tasked Galtrelon with protecting [[Everlight]] and investigating its time distortions; the silver dragon is his patron and mission-giver.
- **[[Alteya]]** — Gave her her husband's journal and badge (Session 6.3); helped free her family from the cave prison (Session 6.5); committed to their wellbeing.
- **[[Tassarion Ranges]]** — Fills his role in the party; joined as Tass's replacement and carries the weight of that loss.
- **[[Narris]]** — Fellow scholar and explorer; both are investigating the Everlight time distortions.

## Open Questions

- What happened to [[Alteya]]'s husband and child — where are they now, and why were they in the cave prison?
- What does [[Lothim]] know about the time distortions in [[Everlight]] that he has not yet shared?
- What are the full capabilities of the [[Staff of the Slumbering Grove]]?
- How does Galtrelon's connection to the Howling Grotto relate to the larger druidic network in the region?
