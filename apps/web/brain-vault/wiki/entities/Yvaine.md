# Yvaine

**Class:** Mage → Warlock (Celestial)  
**Race:** Human  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]  
**Patron:** [[Layeth the Selfless One]] (Celestial)  
**Player:** Jenny McHale  
**Familiar:** Razor (griffon)

## Character Overview

Yvaine joined the party in Session 3.1 at the Council of Six's request. A human mage turned warlock, her transformation was facilitated by [[Fizban]], [[Gwyneth]], and [[Lothim]] in Session 6.3, when she chose [[Layeth the Selfless One]] as her patron. The biggest revelation of her story: [[Harlowe]], the witch the party fought in [[Kravwynhold]], is her biological mother. Harlowe has since fled to the Bathaen Empire.

Her sending amulet was used to spy on the party — likely by Gwyneth, who gave it to her.

## Weapons and Equipment

- **[[Yvaine's +2 Studded Leather]]** — divine gift delivered through [[Ashe]] from [[Muerg]]; received in [[Everlight]]
- **[[Fizban Tower Vault Items|Unidentified Bottle]]** — found in [[Fizban's Tower]] vault (Session 4.2); properties unknown
- **Sending Amulet** — was used to spy on the party (Session 5.2); likely [[Gwyneth]]'s doing
- **Razor** — griffon familiar; used for scouting

## Notable Session Events

- **Session 3.1** — Joined party in [[Scarbrook]] at the [[Council of Six]]'s request. Investigated Erick the Alchemist's shop.
- **Session 4.1** — Used magical patches to create horses; summoned Razor to scout; nearly died in air genasi battle but was saved by [[Tassarion Ranges]].
- **Session 4.2 ([[Fizban's Tower]])** — Cast Detect Magic and Knock to access vault; acquired the unidentified bottle; identified potions; investigated the telescope.
- **Session 5.1** — Fought ghosts and zombies in the haunted mansion; used Razor to scout the cave.
- **Session 5.2** — Discovered her amulet was used to spy on the party; confronted [[Kal'dar]] about his motives.
- **Session 6.3** — Transformed into a Celestial Warlock by [[Fizban]], [[Gwyneth]], and [[Lothim]]. Chose Layeth the Selfless One as patron. Involved 26 divine entities. Also learned [[Harlowe]] is her biological mother.
- **Session 6.4–6.5** — Fought icy creatures and howlers; used Detect Magic to identify druidic runes; sacrificed a spell slot for the grove unsealing ritual; suffered memory loss at the name rune.

## Motivations

1. **Resolve Family Ties** — Seeks answers about her mother [[Harlowe]], who is in the Bathaen Empire. Complex feelings about a woman she fought before knowing the truth.
2. **Protect the Council of Six** — Remains committed to defending the Council against [[Kal'dar]]'s forces.
3. **Understand New Powers** — Exploring her warlock abilities and connection to [[Layeth the Selfless One]]; grappling with the religious/spiritual shift from mage to celestial warlock.
4. **Avenge Tassarion** — Honors [[Tassarion Ranges]]'s sacrifice; committed to pursuing Kal'dar.

## Relationships

- **[[Harlowe]]** — Her biological mother; fought her in Kravwynhold before learning the truth; Harlowe has fled to the Bathaen Empire.
- **[[Gwyneth]]** — Participated in Yvaine's warlock transformation; may have used the sending amulet to spy on the party.
- **[[Fizban]]** — Facilitated her warlock transformation.
- **[[Lothim]]** — Participated in Yvaine's transformation.
- **[[Muerg]]** — Gave her the +2 studded leather armor through [[Ashe]].
- **[[Aurelion]]** — The angel she and the party are trying to free from the Lost City portal.
- **[[Ren (TCB)]]** — Close companion; the Yvaine/Harlowe family connection threads are intertwined with Ren's parents mystery.
