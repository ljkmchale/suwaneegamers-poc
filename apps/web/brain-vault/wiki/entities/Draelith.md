# Draelith

## Summary

Draelith is a player character in the Dungeons III campaign, played by Chip.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Draelith as a Dungeons III-only character.

## Character Reference

- Player: Chip
- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Class: Wizard
- Species: Human
- Armor Class: 12
- Passive Perception: 13
- Passive Investigation: 15
- Passive Insight: 13
- Languages: Common, Elvish, Gnome
- Deity: Celestine

## Current Read

Draelith is a socially theatrical wizard with a Celestine-centered identity and a strong interest in stars, performance, research, maps, and magical anomalies. He uses Minor Illusion and Prestidigitation constantly for misdirection, cover, intimidation, and stagecraft.

## Character Beats

- Introduced at the Covered Hooch as a tall, skinny human in muted blue pilgrim-like clothing with a steel headband and dark wood staff.
- Explains Celestine through a harp-shaped birthmark and draws a crude harp at the Hall of Many Hands.
- Uses illusion magic to support Nixie's star story for Gimri.
- Studies the blue sending stone and the magic-amplifying cave.
- Responds to the Scorpion through the stone after the K9 Crew judgment.
- Pursues Embryl's Osmanae information and Umlin's mapping job.

## Current Leads

- The Scorpion's contact through the [[Blue Sending Stone]].
- The [[Osmanae Celestial Instrument]] and ancient observatory.
- Mapping the area around [[Scarwatch Hold]] for Umlin.
