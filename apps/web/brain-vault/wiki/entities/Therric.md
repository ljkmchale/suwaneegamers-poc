# Therric

## Summary

Therric is a player character in the SoD campaign, played by Chip Poole.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Therric as an SoD-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the SoD campaign context and should not be applied to HoE or The Silent Vanguard.

## Character Reference

- Player: Chip Poole
- Campaign: SoD
- Class: Ranger / Monk
- Species: Half-orc
- Background: Hermit
- Armor Class: 14, or 16 with noted adjustment
- Passive Perception: 15
- Passive Investigation: 10
- Passive Insight: 13
- Senses: Darkvision 120'
- Languages: Common, Elvish, Orc
- Deities: Villari, Muerg, Cembus, Sylunarra

## Current Read

Therric is presented as a rugged ranger/monk figure with a hermit background and strong scouting instincts. In early SoD notes, he often investigates tracks, terrain, water, dirt, and other physical clues before the group commits to a course.

## Patterns

- Investigates the natural world through touch, smell, taste, tracks, and terrain.
- Moves ahead cautiously when scouting is needed.
- Carries fear and seriousness around cursed or hellish evidence, but still keeps working the problem.

## Goals

- Track the source of threats harming Adsuren, especially poison, corruption, and unnatural signs.
- Understand the black heart and its possible hellish implications.
- Help protect the party while making sense of wilderness and underground clues.

## Fighting Habits

- Hybrid ranger/monk skirmisher.
- Uses Hunter's Mark and mobile positioning.
- Disengages or repositions when grabbed or threatened.
- Can shift into staff and flurry-style attacks when forced into close quarters.

## Interests

- Wilderness signs, soil, grass, water, tracks, and old paths.
- Mystical or troubling dreams connected to cursed objects.
- Practical scouting and survival knowledge.

## Relationships And Suspicions

### Friends And Allies

- Kenton: Recurring party friend. Therric talks with him at market, observes his tavern behavior, apologizes for him socially, and later Kenton tries to pull Therric away from danger.
- Escanor: Trusted confidant. Therric privately tells Escanor about the nightmare and his fear that the heart may be hellish.
- Zephyra: Front-line ally and friend, though Therric is forced to fight her when Aeolenne influences her.
- Esylla and Lila: Party allies; Esylla temporarily carries the heart after Therric deceives her, and Lila later provides combat support in the Aeolenne fight.
- Arena / Ariana Duskwhisper: Missing mystic contact from Therric's pre-party routine and a potential personal lead.

### Enemies And Active Threats

- Aeolenne: Threat after manipulating Zephyra and forcing Therric into an awful fight against a friend.
- The black heart / possible hellish influence: Personal threat because Therric has nightmares and becomes frightened of what the heart represents.
- Undead in the Oratory crypt: Direct combat threats.
- The vine / plant creature: Nearly overwhelms Therric by wrapping vines around his head.
- The Intendant / cultists: Enemies tied to the ritual activity and Aeolenne's release.

### Suspicions And Leads

- The tainted water source: Therric follows natural signs and concludes the group needs to find a source of poison below ground.
- The heart's origin: Therric suspects hellish implications after his nightmare and the sulfur/burn signs.
- Ariana Duskwhisper's absence: A personal loose thread from his Adsuren routine.
- Aeolenne and Caldwynn: Follow-up threat after learning Aeolenne has an ally south of Adsuren.
- Bodies and aftermath: Therric helps bring the Intendant to the guard post and asks what should be done with many bodies, showing concern about fallout and cleanup.

### Goals And Active Leads

- Find Areana/Ariana or learn whether she is connected to current events.

- Keep Kenton alive without letting Kenton's choices destroy the party.

### Fighting Habits

- Staff-first martial combat with kicks, punches, flurry-style pressure, grapples/topples, and hunter's mark.
- Uses healer's kit under pressure, notably stabilizing/healing Escanor during the Karranok fight.
- Will pursue, restrain, or knock down, but can turn brutal against cultists or prisoners.

### Interests And Useful Question Hooks

- Ask Therric about tracks, smells, food, bodies, animal behavior, dreams, staff carvings, and whether a plan feels grounded.
- Ask him how Kenton is likely to move or lie, since he has watched Kenton closely in danger and downtime.
