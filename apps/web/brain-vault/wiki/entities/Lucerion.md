## The Red Arm

Lucerion lost his right arm in Session 30 when the hag Viola landed a Critical Hit with acid. During rest afterward, Lucerion had a dream of fighting wolf-like creatures alongside the woman from the Paragon's Tear. In the dream, as the wolf slew the woman and blood covered Lucerion, his arm transformed into a claw. When he woke, the arm had been replaced by a scarlet red limb — the color of Natafae's domain — with a serrated edge at the join. The arm functions normally. It burns when touching a holy symbol of Osanna. Lucerion used his remaining Lay on Hands to seal the wound.

## Notable Facts

Lucerion often leads the party in confrontations and social encounters. He is a devoted follower of Natafae (The Mistress of Pain) and believes suffering is transformative — he "cannot die and he likes to be hurt." He tends toward bold, sometimes reckless action.

He took Aishka the Braizen's tabard but let Aishka escape in Witguard (Session 22). He purchased the magical leather +1 from Lady Coarsepetal for Rhody. He cares for those he fights alongside.

Lucerion's hometown is **Nurador**. Orcs were reported to be heading toward Nurador near the end of the Witguard arc — a currently unresolved threat.

## Personal Thread

**The Natafae Red Arm** — given to him after losing his original arm to Viola the hag. The arm's spiritual significance (connection to Natafae's divine domain) has not yet been explained. The dream that preceded it featured the woman from the Paragon's Tear, and the arm emerging as chains in the dream.

**Nurador** — Lucerion's hometown; orcs may be marching toward it.
