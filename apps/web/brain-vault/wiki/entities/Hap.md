# Hap

## Summary

Hap is a player character in the HoE campaign, played by Ty Cooper.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Hap as an HoE-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the HoE campaign context and should not be merged with similarly named characters in other campaigns unless explicitly confirmed.

## Character Reference

- Player: Ty Cooper
- Campaign: HoE
- Full Name: Hap Garemon
- Class: Fighter (Battle Master)
- Species: Tiefling
- Armor Class: 17, or 19 with noted adjustment
- Passive Perception: 13
- Passive Investigation: 15
- Passive Insight: 10
- Senses: Darkvision 60'
- Defenses: Fire
- Languages: Common, Draconic, Sign Language
- Deity: Diverra

## Current Read

Hap is presented as a tiefling fighter with Battle Master tactics, melee and ranged capability, and an aerial scouting angle through Fleur. His major HoE thread involves finding his father's murderers and retrieving his father's ring.

## Patterns

- Brings a good-hearted, rural, self-styled purity to a character who could otherwise seem frightening.
- Uses Fleur and practical observation to scout before committing.
- Carries grief and suspicion from his father's murder, but still works openly with the party.

## Goals

- Find his father's murderers.
- Recover his father's ring.
- Track the Cloak of Defiance connection tied to his family tragedy.
- Follow Diverra's vision that brought him to Emberstran and the party.

## Fighting Habits

- Functions as a defender with melee and ranged options.
- Uses weapon attacks up close, including sword and scimitar work in early notes.
- Protects allies directly, including deflecting damage meant for others.
- Uses Fleur and ranged attacks when positioning matters.

## Interests

- His father Benoit, the stolen family ring, and the Cloak of Defiance.
- Diverra and the meaning of the vision that sent him to the party.
- Being seen as good, pure, and helpful despite tiefling assumptions.

## Timeline-Informed Profile Through HoE Session 25

### Updated Role

Hap remains a defender and tactical striker, but his role has expanded into prophecy anchor, sword-bearer, family-pressure point, and Fleur-based scout. His vision of joining Aury and the party gains new weight after the Lost Sanctuary of Diverra proves real.

### Major Bonds

- Aurelius / Aury: The Diverra vision bond is central and validated at the Lost Sanctuary.
- Fleur: Familiar, scout, and combat delivery partner. Fleur is even sacrificed temporarily in the volcano giant encounter.
- Shadowtrail: Talking weapon that gives cryptic guidance and pushes toward "neverending."
- Phira: Hap's sister. Ainslie's Sendings reveal she may be dangerous and fiercely protective; she is not Hap's mother.
- Ky'tha: Works with her around Lady Rook, Hap's land, and the Cloak of Defiance.
- Lady Rook: Political pressure point because of Hap's land, Phira, and the Marrowstone.

### Enemies And Suspicious Figures

- Cloak of Defiance / Jerik: Still tied to Hap's father's murder, stolen ring, and threats to Hap and Phira.
- Lady Rook: Useful but suspicious; she wants the Marrowstone/vault outcome and has sent Petal to watch the party.
- Petal: Her tracking of the party complicates trust and ends in her death.
- Blackrend / blight: Direct threat after the Lost Sanctuary fight.
- Irragosa / vault threats: Connected to the path Shadowtrail seems to anticipate.

### Updated Patterns

- Uses plainspoken rural wisdom, humor, and tactical common sense.
- Relies on Fleur for scouting and spell delivery.
- Protects allies directly through deflection and Battle Master-style pressure.
- Talks to Shadowtrail reluctantly and dislikes riddles without clear antecedents.

### Updated Goals And Leads

- Understand Shadowtrail and what "neverending" means.
- Resolve family danger around Phira, Jerik, the Cloak of Defiance, and inherited land.
- Continue following Diverra's vision alongside Aury.
- Help reach and understand the Emberheart Vault.
- Decide whether to keep or sell inherited land.

### Updated Fighting Habits

- Front-line multi-attack defender.
- Uses Shadowtrail, pruning dagger, short sword/dagger combinations, thrown weapons, and Fleur-delivered magic.
- Frightens, trips, and pressures enemies while protecting allies.
- Will engage dangerous targets directly, including giants, blight creatures, quarath, and fire newts.

### Current Inventory / Power Notes

- Shadowtrail.
- Pruning Dagger of Diverra.
- Amulet against Detection.
- Dagger +1.
- Fleur.
- Whistle linked to Sparkwhisper / Ryland Fibbergoan.

### Session 25 Update

Hap hears his weapon identify itself as Shadowtrail and learns it is willing to posture even against Irragosa. He helps inspect the crashed flying ship, connects the recovered transmutation orb to possible levitation/lodestone research by Iannan Rambibur of Tarnsport, and prepares emotionally/logistically for Phira's approach from Ulgrey.
