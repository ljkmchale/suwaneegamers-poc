# Ren

**Class:** Ranger  
**Race:** Unspecified  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]  
**Player:** Tiffany

## Character Overview

Ren was rescued by the party from wolf spiders in the Old Mine during Session 1.1 — that rescue was the event through which she joined. She is a reliable scout, fighter, and de facto leader in moments of retreat or crisis. Two threads define her story: her biological sister [[Angela]] (held captive by [[Harlowe]] for 10 years, now recovering in [[Everlight]]) and the mystery of her parents, which only [[Harlowe]] can fully explain.

## Notable Session Events

- **Session 1.1** — Was rescued by the party from wolf spiders in the Old Mine; this is how she joined.
- **Session 2.3** — Helped [[Tassarion Ranges]] defeat the chef cultist in the [[Oasis of the Night]].
- **Session 4.1** — Scouted a caravan attacked by green dragons and looted it; investigated Erick the Alchemist's house and staged his departure.
- **Session 4.3** — Led the retreat to [[Fizban's Tower]] during the air genasi battle; secured the horses and cart.
- **Session 5.1** — Scouted the haunted mansion; fought ghosts and zombies; unlocked a trapped chest; received a vision urging travel to the Blustery Wastes to save Angela (her sister).
- **Session 5.2** — Confronted [[Kal'dar]] in [[Tal'basar]] about the Witch Harlowe, learning of Harlowe's connection to [[Yvaine]]; sided with the [[Council of Six]].
- **Session 6.3** — Received a picture from Angela showing her trauma; helped arrange [[Tassarion Ranges]]'s funeral; planned to deliver his body to his parents in [[Mahruud]].
- **Session 6.4–6.5** — Fired critical arrows against the icy creature and howlers; scouted the [[Everlight]] cave; suffered memory loss at the name rune.

## Background and Family

### Angela — Ren's Sister

[[Angela]] is Ren's **biological sister**. [[Harlowe]] held Angela captive and unconscious for 10 years inside [[Kravwynhold]]'s tower. When the party rescued Angela (Session 6.1), Harlowe confronted Ren directly: *"So...your sister. I'll make a trade — your life for hers."* Angela, groggy and malnourished, embraced Ren and explained she had been unconscious for 10 years while Harlowe hunted children who had escaped. To free Angela from magical chains, Ren made a blood sacrifice. Ren then destroyed Harlowe's crystal orb, freeing trapped souls. Angela has PTSD from the experience and stayed in [[Everlight]] to recover while the party departed for the Lost City.

### Ren's Parents

In the Blustery Waste mountains (Session 5.3), Ren had a vision of her parents — they said they "didn't abandon her" and urged her to "find the truth." [[Fizban]] confirmed that only Harlowe will know anything about them. The Fizban to-do list in Session 6.3 mentions "Harlowe - Bathaen Empire (Ren's parents, vengeance for Tass)."

[[Yvaine]] told [[Narris]] about "the relationship between her father, step-mother, and Ren" — suggesting Yvaine has a familial connection to Ren through Yvaine's step-mother. This remains an open question.

## Motivations

1. **Find the Witch Harlowe** — Seeks Harlowe in the Bathaen Empire to learn about her parents and possibly avenge [[Tassarion Ranges]].
2. **Save Angela** — Driven by a vision to rescue Angela; Angela is her biological sister. Angela was rescued (Session 6.1) and is now recovering in Everlight.
3. **Honor Tassarion** — Committed to delivering his body to his parents in [[Mahruud]]; avenging his death.
4. **Protect the Group** — Takes on scouting and leadership roles; secured the retreat to Fizban's Tower (Session 4.3); first into the Everlight cave (Session 6.4–6.5).

## Relationships

- **[[Angela]]** — Biological sister; rescued from Harlowe's tower after 10 years captivity; now recovering in [[Everlight]].
- **[[Harlowe]]** — Ren's primary antagonist; the person who held Angela captive and who alone knows the truth about Ren's parents. Also revealed to be [[Yvaine]]'s biological mother.
- **[[Tassarion Ranges]]** — Bond and debt; Ren helped defeat the chef cultist with him (Session 2.3) and committed to delivering his body to his family in [[Mahruud]].
- **[[Yvaine]]** — Close companion; their family histories are intertwined through Harlowe and a possible connection through Yvaine's step-mother and Ren's parents.
- **[[Kal'dar]]** — Confronted him in Tal'basar (Session 5.2) about Harlowe; opposed his forces with the Council of Six.
- **[[Fizban]]** — Provided the critical confirmation that only Harlowe can reveal the truth about Ren's parents.

## Open Questions

- What is the full story of Ren's parents? What did Harlowe do to them, and are they still alive?
- What is the nature of the Yvaine/Ren family connection — is there a link between Yvaine's father, Yvaine's step-mother, and Ren's parents?
- Will Ren pursue Harlowe into the Bathaen Empire?
- Has the plan to deliver Tassarion's body to his parents in [[Mahruud]] been carried out?
