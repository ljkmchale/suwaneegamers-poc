# Sigil

**Class:** Fighter  
**Race:** Half-Orc  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]  
**Player:** Brian Winniford

## Character Overview

Sigil is a Half-Orc Fighter with a troubled past — his parents died in orc-related violence, and that trauma shapes much of his behavior. He is emotionally volatile but deeply loyal. [[Fizban]] has tasked him personally with finding his mother's sword (the [[Sunblade Hilt]]), tied to a rare positive memory of his mother. He has also been designated as [[Narris]]'s protective anchor when Narris enters extraplanar shadow realms.

A hint of the social: Sigil buys poet's hats, trades for status upgrades, and is trying to impress someone named Tala in [[Everlight]].

## Weapons and Equipment

- **[[Sunblade Hilt]]** — his mother's incomplete sword; being sought; needs a special smith beyond the Blustery Waste
- **Custom Club** — traded dragon scales and wings in [[Tal'basar]] for it (Session 5.2)
- **Armor Upgrades** — also obtained through the Tal'basar trade (Session 5.2)
- **Poet's Hat and Cape** — bought in Tal'basar to impress others (Session 5.2)
- **Goblinbane Flameblade** (former) — traded to [[Lothim]] the silver dragon for 100gp (Session 2.1)
- **Love Potion** — acquired from Erick the Alchemist (Session 3.1); status unknown

## Notable Session Events

- **Session 1.1** — Fought wolf spiders; helped rescue [[Ren (TCB)]].
- **Session 2.1** — Traded the Goblinbane Flameblade to silver dragon [[Lothim]] for 100gp.
- **Session 3.1** — Acquired a love potion from Erick the Alchemist in [[Scarbrook]].
- **Session 4.1** — Fought green dragon wyrmlings; looted a caravan. [[Gwyneth]] had given Sigil a ring inscribed with "mother" in Draconic. When the party met the gold dragons, [[C'mey]] identified the word, shifted into humanoid form (Ymec), and touched the ring — it glowed. Sigil and C'mey (as Ymec) embraced. The full significance is not yet known but implies a connection between C'mey and Sigil's mother.
- **Session 4.3** — Nearly broke during the air genasi and hill giant battle; retreated to [[Fizban's Tower]] vault in distress; pretended to kill prisoner Brandon to deceive Cornelius Woodrift, but actually freed him.
- **Session 5.2** — Traded dragon scales and wings in [[Tal'basar]] for a custom club and armor; bought a poet's hat and cape to impress others.
- **Session 6.3** — Stayed behind during the icy creature fight after receiving devastating/hopeful news about his mother's sword from [[Fizban]]. Tasked personally by Fizban to find it. Connected to a new, positive memory of his mother.
- **Session 6.5** — Guided by [[Morath]] and [[Fizban]] to protect [[Narris]] in the "mirror void"; activated the shadow rune; helped Tala in [[Everlight]].

## Motivations

1. **Find His Mother's Sword** — [[Fizban]] tasked Sigil with finding the [[Sunblade Hilt]], his mother's unfinished sword, linked to a new positive memory of her. This is deeply personal.
2. **Protect Narris** — Designated by Fizban as [[Narris]]'s anchor for extraplanar travel into shadow realms ("the mirror void"). A sacred responsibility.
3. **Overcome Trauma** — Seeks healing from his past: parents' death, orc-related violence. Visible in his emotional retreat during Session 4.3 and his discussions with [[Scarlytt Amyran]].
4. **Build Relationships** — Wants social standing and connection. Tries to impress Tala in [[Everlight]]; buys stylish gear for social effect.

## Relationships

- **[[Fizban]]** — Personal quest-giver; shared a positive memory about Sigil's mother; tasked him with the Sunblade Hilt.
- **[[Narris]]** — Designated protector/anchor for Narris's shadow-realm travels.
- **[[Morath]]** — Works with Morath in guiding Sigil and Narris toward the "mirror void."
- **[[Lothim]]** — Traded the Goblinbane Flameblade to him.
- **[[Scarlytt Amyran]]** — Emotional support during his crisis in Session 4.3.
- **[[Darveth]]** — The tiefling blacksmith in [[Kravwynhold]] who knows of a smith beyond the mountains who could complete the Sunblade Hilt.
- **Tala** — A person in [[Everlight]] Sigil is trying to impress; identity not fully established.
