# Nixie

## Summary

Nixie is a player character in the Dungeons III campaign, played by Chuck.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Nixie as a Dungeons III-only character.

## Character Reference

- Player: Chuck
- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Class: Druid
- Species: Deep Gnome
- Armor Class: 15
- Passive Perception: 15
- Passive Investigation: 14
- Passive Insight: 15
- Senses: Darkvision 60 ft.
- Languages: Common, Gnomish, Dwarvish
- Deity: Cembus

## Current Read

Nixie is the party's starstruck surface-newcomer, animal communicator, and map-minded scout. He is naive and awkward in presentation but frequently useful: he speaks with horses and cats, wild shapes for scouting, identifies magical traces, and becomes central to Umlin's mapping job.

## Character Beats

- Introduced as a tall-for-a-gnome svirneblin with big eyes, leather, and a staff.
- Describes the stars so vividly that Gimri paints his words.
- Buys into Pequin's tinkering conversation and later uses Speak with Animals on Bullseye and Slushball.
- Finds stashes and clues while scouting.
- Turns into a spider or panther during dangerous cave exploration and combat.

## Current Leads

- Mapping the Scarwatch Hold region.
- Reading animal clues around Isgo's abduction.
- Identifying magical traces near the crag routes.
