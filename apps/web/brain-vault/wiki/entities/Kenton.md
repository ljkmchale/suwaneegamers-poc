# Kenton

## Summary

Kenton is a player character in the SoD campaign, played by Larry/Lawrence McHale.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Kenton as an SoD-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the SoD campaign context and should not be applied to HoE or The Silent Vanguard.

## Character Reference

- Player: Larry/Lawrence McHale
- Campaign: SoD
- Class: Rogue
- Species: Human
- Background: Entertainer
- Armor Class: 14
- Languages: Common, Celestial, Giant, Orc, Thief's Cant
- Deity: Tyvarion

## Current Read

Kenton is presented as flamboyantly dressed, socially bold, and roguish. His player-developed pre-campaign concept frames him as Kenton Clawstar, a high-end clothing designer and artiste who treats fashion, language, and drama as performance. In fights, he tends to act as a mobile skirmisher rather than a defender: he uses his whip, looks for openings, disengages or relocates when needed, and helps extract allies when the fight turns dangerous.

## Patterns

- Performs confidence first: clothes, charm, masks, exaggeration, and social boldness are part of his default posture.
- Looks for opportunity, whether in conversation, sales, romance, or loot.
- Can be self-interested, but still responds when an ally needs extraction or the party needs action.

## Goals

- Sell fine clothes and silks, build reputation, and enjoy the social advantages of travel.
- Become a legendary fashion designer, escape obscurity, return to high society, and turn adventures into dramatic stories and outfits.
- Gain useful gear and money when opportunities appear.
- Help the party survive the SoD threats around Adsuren, the cult, and Aeolenne.

## Fighting Habits

- Mobile rogue skirmisher rather than defender.
- Uses whip attacks and positioning.
- Often tries to strike, disengage, or relocate rather than stand still.
- When the fight turns bad, he may prioritize escape and pulling allies out.

## Interests

- Fashion, laundry, fine clothes, tailoring, performance, flirtation, coin, and stylish entrances.
- Masks, whips, and magic items that enhance his swashbuckling image.
- Social scenes where charm can become leverage.
- Tyvarion, the Master of Masks, as patron of art, beauty, drama, music, poetry, and expressive performance.

## Relationships And Suspicions

### Friends And Allies

- Therric: A recurring close party connection. Therric talks with Kenton at market, later apologizes to women for Kenton's tavern behavior and calls him his friend, and Kenton tries to pull Therric away during the Aeolenne fight.
- Esylla: Party ally and useful social/magical connection. She talks with Kenton about his wares early on, treats the group to dinner, and later brings him a +1 whip from Mollywop.
- Lila: Party ally and support caster. During the Aeolenne fight, Lila gives Kenton temporary hit points.
- Escanor and Zephyra: Party allies, though Escanor is also a moral counterweight when Kenton gets grabby with treasure. Zephyra fights on the front line while Kenton operates as a skirmisher.
- Mollywop: Not exactly a friend yet, but an important magical ally/contact. Mollywop helps interpret the aftermath of Aeolenne's escape and identifies items Kenton brought forward.
- Captain Amara Hilei: Earlier travel contact; Kenton hitched a ride with her while traveling west to sell his wares.
- Rebecca, initially called Heather: A brief local romantic contact from the Graying Grotto.

### Enemies And Active Threats

- Aeolenne: Clear enemy after she manipulates Zephyra, vanishes with an invisibility potion, and leaves town as a fiendish presence.
- The Intendant / Sunmoner Karranok and the cultists: Enemies by association with the cult activity under Adsuren and the ritual activity that freed Aeolenne.
- Undead in the Oratory crypt: Skeletons, crawling hands, and the sarcophagus figure directly fight Kenton and the party.
- The vine / plant creature from the tainted stream: Battlefield enemy that restrains Kenton and the party.

### Suspicions And Leads

- The tainted stream: Kenton personally descends into the well, tastes the stream water, and identifies the stream as the likely source of the poisoned well.
- The black heart / sulfur smell: Kenton is one of the characters who notices the burned or sulfur smell when Therric knocks on the heart, making him part of the early evidence that the heart is unnatural or fiendish.
- Aeolenne's escape route and destination: After the fight, the party learns she headed south and had an ally in Caldwynn; this remains an active threat thread for Kenton and the group.
- The robbery rumor: Kenton later notes that talk of the robbery seems to have been squashed by the cultist takedown. This reads like a loose social/criminal thread he is watching, but the notes do not yet say whether he is involved, worried, or merely amused.
- Treasure and item opportunity: Kenton repeatedly notices valuables. He tries to palm coins and a crown in the crypt, grabs potions and the Intendant's staff after the Aeolenne fight, and later pursues gems and a silvered weapon.

- Will kill helpless enemies if he thinks it solves a problem, as with the tied cultist prisoner.

### Interests And Useful Question Hooks

- Ask Kenton about locks, theft, disguises, noble gossip, Reyvennra/Rodolf, silks, fashion, money, and what he is not saying.
