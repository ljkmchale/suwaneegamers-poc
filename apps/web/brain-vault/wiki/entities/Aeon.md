# Aeon

## Summary

Aeon is a player character in the Dungeons III campaign, played by Tom. The source repeatedly notes the pronunciation as "AY-on" or "Ay-ON."

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Aeon as a Dungeons III-only character.

## Character Reference

- Player: Tom
- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Class: Fighter
- Species: Half Elf
- Armor Class: 15
- Passive Perception: 12
- Passive Investigation: 12
- Passive Insight: 10
- Senses: Darkvision 60 ft.
- Defenses: Magical sleep
- Languages: Common, Elvish, Orc
- Deity: Various / seeking

## Current Read

Aeon is a charming, rough-edged sellsword and ex-soldier with a broken moral compass and a recurring interest in gods, brothels, and chapels. His early arc is strongly tied to finding a god or understanding the "angel" who healed his back scar.

## Character Beats

- Opens the campaign at Moonfern Sanctuary.
- Visits the Hall of Many Hands to ask about gods and speaks with Kadella about Osanna.
- Speaks with Laestrik, follower of Goldraen, and hears Draelith's Celestine explanation.
- Shows the party his horrific back scar and describes an angel who healed him.
- Recognizes Seraphine from treatment ten years earlier.
- Delivers major melee blows in fights against K9 Crew, grick, roper support, and xorn.
- In Session 09, hears a traveler suggest Amriel the Redeemer as a god to consider.

## Current Leads

- Identify the angel who healed him.
- Decide whether Osanna, Amriel, or another god fits his path.
- Survive the crag investigation and Radiant Eye threat.
