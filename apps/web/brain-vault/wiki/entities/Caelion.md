## Notable Facts

Caelion serves as the party's primary healer. He frequently uses Toll the Dead, Spiritual Weapon, Spirit Guardians, and Healing Word in combat. His Channel Divinity heals the party regularly.

He cast Sending to Albross's brother Breemos on the morning of 22nd of Paramor (Session 35), establishing contact and warning him about a kidnapping threat.

Caelion studied Lucerion's red right arm (the Natafae gift) after Lucerion lost his original arm in Session 30. The arm appears normal except for being blood red, with a serrated edge where it meets normal flesh. It burns when touching a holy symbol of Osanna.
