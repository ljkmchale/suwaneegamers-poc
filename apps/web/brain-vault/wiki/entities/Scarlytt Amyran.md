# Scarlytt Amyran

**Class:** Paladin  
**Race:** Elf  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]  
**Deity:** Sister of Solace  
**Player:** Lesley Poole

## Character Overview

Scarlytt is an Elf paladin serving the Sister of Solace. A founding party member from Session 0, she embodies justice and protection. Her death and resurrection at the [[Oasis of the Night]] early in the campaign has deeply shaped her sense of purpose. She carries a particular weight in the party — she is the one Fizban privately told about [[Gwyneth]]'s upcoming special role, and she alone knows Fizban believes [[Kal'dar]] must be eliminated.

## Weapons and Equipment

- **Flaming Scimitar** — primary weapon; used to smite ghosts, undead, and enemies
- **[[Scarlytt's Shield]]** — crafted from blue dragon scales and a phoenix feather; found in [[Fizban's Tower]] vault (Session 4.2); can activate a flame rune
- **[[Ancient Guards Gauntlet]]** — recovered from guardian constructs of [[Thal'astyr]] (Sessions 5.3–5.5)
- **Twine Ring** — given by [[Gwyneth]] to deliver to her father [[Fizban]] (Session 4.1); presumably delivered

## Notable Session Events

- **Session 1.1** — Fought wolf spiders and helped rescue [[Ren (TCB)]] from the Old Mine.
- **Session 2.3** — Died in the cave battle at the [[Oasis of the Night]]. Revived via A'urea'Pura cult resurrection rituals. Afterward helped defeat the chef cultist alongside Ren.
- **Session 4.1** — Convinced [[Gwyneth]] to let the Council of Six take over Erick's shop. Received a twine ring from Gwyneth to deliver to [[Fizban]].
- **Session 4.2 ([[Fizban's Tower]])** — Led the fight against crystalline creatures. Obtained her magical shield from the vault.
- **Session 4.3** — Stabilized [[Sigil (TCB)]] when he nearly broke during the air genasi and hill giant battle. Negotiated with Cornelius Woodrift for passage to ghost-guarded treasure; later led the ambush that killed his group after freeing his prisoners.
- **Session 5.1** — Used flaming scimitar to smite ghosts and zombies in the haunted mansion; helped secure it as a party base.
- **Session 5.2** — Confronted [[Kal'dar]] in [[Tal'basar]], advocating for justice, siding with the [[Council of Six]].
- **Session 6.3** — Privately learned from [[Fizban]] that [[Gwyneth]] has an upcoming special role; also learned Fizban believes Kal'dar should be eliminated.
- **Session 6.4–6.5** — Fought an icy creature and crystal-back howlers in the [[Everlight]] cave; used her shield's power to activate a flame rune; helped free trapped individuals including [[Alteya]]'s family.

## Motivations

1. **Pursue Justice** — Consistently acts against threats like [[Kal'dar]] and the [[A'urea'Pura Cult]]; sides with the [[Council of Six]].
2. **Honor Tassarion's Sacrifice** — Motivated to avenge [[Tassarion Ranges]]'s death and protect the children he died saving.
3. **Protect the Shield** — Committed to using [[Scarlytt's Shield]] wisely against Kal'dar; Fizban privately suggested Kal'dar should be eliminated.
4. **Support Gwyneth's Purpose** — Privately aware of [[Gwyneth]]'s upcoming special role as discussed with Fizban in secret (Session 6.3). Details unknown to the rest of the party.

## Relationships

- **[[Gwyneth]]** — Scarlytt is the one who received the twine ring from Gwyneth and has been privately briefed on Gwyneth's special role by Fizban. A unique trust exists.
- **[[Fizban]]** — Source of her shield; privately confided in Scarlytt about Gwyneth's role and Kal'dar's fate.
- **[[Ren (TCB)]]** — Together defeated the chef cultist in Session 2.3; close companions.
- **[[Sigil (TCB)]]** — Stabilized him in battle; aware of his emotional fragility.
- **[[Tassarion Ranges]]** — His death deeply motivates Scarlytt.
- **[[Kal'dar]]** — Primary antagonist target; Fizban believes he must be eliminated.
- **[[A'urea'Pura Cult]]** — Her death and resurrection by their rituals created a complicated relationship with mortality and her mission.
