# Seraphine Veyne

## Summary

Seraphine Veyne, also called Sera, is a player character in the Dungeons III campaign, played by Suzanne.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Seraphine as a Dungeons III-only character.

## Character Reference

- Player: Suzanne
- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Class: Cleric
- Species: Aasimar
- Armor Class: 16
- Passive Perception: 13
- Passive Investigation: 11
- Passive Insight: 15
- Senses: Darkvision 60 ft.
- Defenses: Resistance to necrotic and radiant damage
- Languages: Common, Elvish, Dwarvish
- Deity: Natafae

## Current Read

Seraphine is a healer with a pronounced fascination with injuries, wounds, pain, and medical practice. She is useful in triage and interrogation-adjacent social scenes because she notices wounds and asks direct questions about them.

## Character Beats

- Introduced at the Covered Hooch tending burned and scratched people.
- Recognizes Aeon from a medical case ten years earlier and has a sketch of his old wound.
- Hears early murder rumors from Wargus, Saquel, and Gimdy.
- Helps Wargus and later tends wounded enemies and horses.
- Visits Scarwatch Hold's Sanctuary and later appears with bandaged wrists/forearms, explaining that she likes to study pain.

## Current Leads

- Liquin at Scarwatch Hold may have a healer's journal she would find interesting.
- Her private worship/pain practice at the Scarwatch Sanctuary is not fully explained.
- Her connection to Aeon's old healing remains important to Aeon's angel story.
