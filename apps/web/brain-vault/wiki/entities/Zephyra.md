# Zephyra

## Summary

Zephyra is a player character in the SoD campaign, played by Jenny McHale.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Zephyra as an SoD-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the SoD campaign context and should not be applied to HoE or The Silent Vanguard.

## Character Reference

- Player: Jenny McHale
- Campaign: SoD
- Class: Barbarian
- Species: Tiefling
- Background: Soldier
- Armor Class: 13
- Passive Perception: 13
- Passive Investigation: 9
- Passive Insight: 11
- Senses: Darkvision 60'
- Defenses: Necrotic resistant
- Languages: Common, Giant, Goblin
- Deities: Brault; interested in Amriel

## Current Read

Zephyra is presented as a very tall tiefling barbarian with a soldier background and a connection to Adsuren's village watch. Her major SoD thread involves her fiendish heritage and Aeolenne.

## Patterns

- Moves toward trouble and physically intervenes when someone is being bullied or threatened.
- Acts like a protector even when she is not a perfect fit for formal watch duty.
- Carries a deep emotional thread around rage, identity, and being treated as monstrous.

## Goals

- Protect Adsuren and the party.
- Understand and survive her fiendish heritage.
- Resist or manage Aeolenne's influence after the possession / manipulation thread.
- Learn whether her succubus heritage can be harnessed without losing herself.

## Fighting Habits

- Front-line barbarian.
- Rages, closes distance, and attacks with heavy melee weapons.
- Often absorbs hits that would threaten more fragile allies.
- Can be turned into the center of a crisis when mind control or heritage-based influence targets her.

## Interests

- Strength, protection, armor, and direct action.
- Family history, especially Aeolenne and what her heritage means.
- Ways to prevent outside control, such as magic items or protective magic.

## Relationships And Suspicions

### Friends And Allies

- Escanor: Established Adsuren ally through the local priest and watch-adjacent circles.
- Therric: Regular field ally who scouts with her and later has to fight her when Aeolenne takes hold.
- Kenton, Esylla, and Lila: Party allies; they help respond to Aeolenne and the cult aftermath.
- Mollywop: Important magical advisor on Zephyra's heritage and possible protection from future control.
- Tanluar Rotvori / local priestly circle: Community connection through Adsuren and the early well investigation.

### Enemies And Active Threats

- Aeolenne: Deeply personal enemy/threat as Zephyra's mother, a fiendish influence, and the source of direct manipulation.
- Jimmy Meanpants and childhood bullies: Emotional enemies from Zephyra's memory, tied to shame, rage, and being called "devil spawn."
- The Intendant / cultists: Enemies whose ritual activity helps free Aeolenne.
- Undead and plant creatures from the early investigation: Direct combat threats to the party.

### Suspicions And Leads

- Her own heritage: Zephyra is trying to understand whether her succubus/fiendish side can be controlled or harnessed.
- Mind control risk: A central suspicion after Aeolenne commands her and makes her attack friends.
- Aeolenne's destination: Mollywop says Aeolenne headed south, and the party knows of a Caldwynn ally.
- Protective magic: Zephyra asks whether an amulet, potion, or higher magic could prevent future possession/control.
- Town safety: Zephyra repeatedly returns to guard/watch concerns and tries to talk with guards after the cult takedown.

### Current Role In The Party

- Front-line protector, watch-adjacent local, and emotional center of the Aeolenne arc.
- She is the party member most directly tied to Adsuren's guard structure and to Aeolenne's personal agenda.

### Friends And Trusted Bonds

- [[Escanor]] and Tanluar Rotvori: local Oratory/watch trust network.
- [[Therric]]: stays at her hut and helps her with meditation/rage control.
- Mollywop: source for tiefling/succubus heritage answers and future anti-mind-control help.
- [[Esylla]], [[Lila]], and [[Kenton]]: party bonds tested by theft, cover stories, and Aeolenne's control.
- Captain Alinia Sarralei: knows and praises Zephyra, but her Mollywop resentment is a complication.

### Enemies, Threats, And Suspicions

- Aeolenne, who claims to be Zephyra's mother and wants Zephyra to embrace her rage/evil.
- Cult of the Immured, Eyovar/Karranok, Ganon, imps, and Sapcutt fungus.
- Jimmy Meanpants memory remains emotionally important because it helped her break control.
- Captain Alinia is not an enemy, but her jealousy of Mollywop and dismissive guard judgment require caution.

### Goals And Active Leads

- Learn the truth of her tiefling/succubus heritage.
- Get protection from mind control or learn how to resist Aeolenne.
- Track Aeolenne south toward Caldwynn and understand the ally there.
- Protect Adsuren through Market Day without letting the Ganon/tax cover story collapse.
- Follow mine/swamp leads tied to Durgruck, bulas, and the possible dragon.

### Fighting Habits

- Direct, physical, and protective: rage, axe, spear/javelin, holding doors/lines, and standing between threats and others.
- Will announce authority and ask for surrender, but kills decisively when the threat is clear, as with the imp.
- Vulnerable to charm/domination effects, though she broke Aeolenne's control through memory and identity.

### Interests And Useful Question Hooks

- Ask Zephyra about guards, Adsuren routines, Mollywop vs. Alinia politics, rage, heritage, and who needs protection first.
- Ask her how Aeolenne sounded or felt when trying to control her; those details may matter later.
