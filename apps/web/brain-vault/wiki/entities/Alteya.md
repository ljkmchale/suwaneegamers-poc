# Alteya

**Class:** Ranger  
**Race:** Elf  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]  
**Player:** Emma Cooper

## Character Overview

Alteya is an Elf Ranger who joined the party around Session 5.6. Her defining personal thread is the search for her husband and child, who have been missing for 25 years — the mystery of whom connects directly to the Everlight cave prison. [[Galtrelon]] gave her her husband's journal and badge when he met the party (Session 6.3), and the party later freed her family from the frozen grove inside the Everlight cave (Session 6.5). She was present in [[Everlight]] during [[Tassarion Ranges]]'s funeral and is connected to [[Fizban]]'s circle of allies.

## Highlights

- Joined the party in Session 5.6 (Frostbite Mountain / ice castle), having been encountered near [[Thal'astyr]]
- **Session 6.3** — [[Galtrelon]] gave Alteya her husband's journal and badge when he met the party in [[Everlight]]
- **Session 6.5** — Her family was found trapped in the frozen grove inside the [[Everlight]] cave; freed by the party through the grove unsealing ritual led by [[Galtrelon]]
- Was present in [[Everlight]] during [[Tassarion Ranges]]'s funeral
- Stayed in [[Everlight]] to care for [[Angela]] (Ren's sister) while the party departed for the Lost City (Session 7)
- Active quest: find her husband and child, missing for 25 years

## Motivations

1. **Reunite with Her Family** — Husband and child have been missing for 25 years; the Everlight cave prison was connected to their fate.
2. **Recover Information from Her Husband's Journal** — The journal and badge given to her by [[Galtrelon]] may hold clues about what happened.
3. **Stand with the Party Against [[Kal'dar]] and the [[A'urea'Pura Cult]]** — Committed to the group's larger mission.

## Relationships

- **[[Galtrelon]]** — Gave Alteya her husband's journal and badge (Session 6.3); played the key role in freeing her family from the cave prison (Session 6.5). A significant bond of gratitude.
- **[[Angela]]** — Stayed with Angela in [[Everlight]] to care for her after the party departed.
- **[[Fizban]]** — Connected to Fizban's circle of allies in Everlight.
- **[[Tassarion Ranges]]** — Was present at his funeral in Everlight.

## Open Questions

- Where is Alteya's husband now? Was he among those freed from the cave prison?
- What happened to her child?
- What does her husband's journal reveal about the 25 years of absence?
- What was the nature of the cave prison — who put her family there, and why?
