# Escanor

## Summary

Escanor is a player character in the SoD campaign, played by Brian Winniford.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Escanor as an SoD-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the SoD campaign context and should not be applied to HoE or The Silent Vanguard.

## Character Reference

- Player: Brian Winniford
- Campaign: SoD
- Class: Cleric
- Species: Human
- Background: Guard
- Armor Class: 13, or 15 with noted adjustment
- Passive Perception: 15
- Passive Investigation: 10
- Passive Insight: 15
- Languages: Common, Draconic, Giant

## Current Read

Escanor is presented as a human cleric with a guard background and strong perceptive and insightful instincts. In early SoD notes, he is connected to the local priest and often takes a protective, religious, or stabilizing role in crises.

## Patterns

- Moves quickly toward responsibility when people are hurt, frightened, or in moral danger.
- Uses religion and local authority as stabilizing tools.
- Often serves as the party's conscience or calm adult in the room.

## Goals

- Protect Adsuren and its people from threats such as poisoned water, undead, cult activity, and fiendish influence.
- Honor Amriel and use divine power to cleanse or bless corrupted things.
- Keep the party's actions grounded when looting, desecration, or panic could make things worse.

## Fighting Habits

- Fights as a cleric who supports, blesses, heals, and attacks when needed.
- Will step into melee against undead or immediate threats.
- Uses holy symbols, prayer, and divine magic as part of both combat and aftermath.

## Interests

- Faith, protection, proper burial, blessing corrupted places, and helping ordinary townsfolk.
- Local religious leadership, especially the Nature's Oratory and priestly contacts.
- Making sure the party survives without losing its moral footing.

## Relationships And Suspicions

### Friends And Allies

- Zephyra: Local ally before the party fully forms. She has a connection with Escanor through Adsuren, the village watch, and the priest.
- Tanluar Rotvori and the Nature's Oratory: Important religious allies and local anchors for Escanor's faith-driven response to the poisoned well and later cult evidence.
- Therric: Trusted enough for serious private conversation after Therric's nightmare about the heart. Escanor agrees to keep an eye on people who hold it.
- Esylla, Kenton, and Lila: Party allies. Escanor often steadies or corrects the group when their choices risk moral or practical fallout.
- Mollywop: Magical ally after Aeolenne's escape; Escanor shares evidence with her and works with her to destroy the ritual space.
- Captain / guards of Adsuren: Civic allies after the cult discovery. Escanor helps bring letters and evidence into official hands.

### Enemies And Active Threats

- Aeolenne: Active fiendish threat after manipulating Zephyra and leaving Adsuren.
- The Intendant / Sunmoner Karranok and the cultists: Central enemies behind the sacrificial/cult activity in town.
- Undead in the Oratory crypt: Direct threat to Escanor and the party, including skeletal hands and skeletons.
- The black heart and tainted stream: Corrupting threat to Adsuren's water and spiritual safety.

### Suspicions And Leads

- The poisoned well: Escanor is an early responder and helps involve the priest/herbalist/local authority.
- The black heart: Escanor treats it as evil or corrupt and wants it blessed or destroyed.
- Therric's nightmare: Escanor takes Therric's fear seriously and watches who carries the heart.
- Cult evidence: Letters and ritual-room evidence point to the Intendant's alter ego and long-running sacrifice activity.
- Aeolenne's route: The party learns she headed south toward Caldwynn, making that a live follow-up threat.
