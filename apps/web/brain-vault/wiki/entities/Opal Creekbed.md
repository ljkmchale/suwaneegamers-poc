# Opal Creekbed

**Class:** Paladin  
**Race:** Human  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]  
**Status:** Departed — left the party at the start of Session 2.1

## Character Overview

Opal Creekbed was one of the five founding party members who formed at [[Arbescar Light and Magic]] in Marrowdale during Session 0. A Human Paladin, she was present in the earliest sessions but departed the party when Session 2.1 began. No reason for her departure is documented in the session notes.

## Session History

- **Session 0** — Founding member; one of the original five at Marrowdale.
- **Session 2.1** — Left the party. The session notes record only "Opal left" at the start of the session.

## Original Party (Session 0)
