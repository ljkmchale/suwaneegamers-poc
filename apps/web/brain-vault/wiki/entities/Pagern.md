## Notable Facts

Pagern joined the party in Session 28 (7th of Paramor, 1246) — found on the road in the lower hills, being attacked by wolves, with his scroll case at risk. He was an apprentice of the Celadon Wizard and appears to have come out on his own for reasons not yet fully explained.

He is the party's primary arcane blaster. His most common combat action is Fireball, to the point where it has become a running joke ("classic Pagern"). He also uses Misty Step, Burning Hands, Chromatic Orb, and Firebolt.

**Pagern killed Presagiath the White Dragon** — in Session 32, with a tiny firebolt that landed while the dragon was holding several party members, saving the party from certain death.

Pagern is pursuing the construction of a **wizard tower**. He is searching for treasure and resources sufficient to build it. The Fort Mornock treasure hoard was partly motivated by this goal.

## Personal Thread

**The Celadon Wizard** — Pagern's mentor. Not nice. The nature of their relationship and whether Pagern left on good or bad terms is unresolved.

**The Wizard Tower** — Pagern's personal goal; he bought expensive map information and has been accumulating resources. Pagern asked the dwarves of Khagdaruhl if their gemstone walls could fund it; they laughed.
