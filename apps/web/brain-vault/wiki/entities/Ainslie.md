# Ainslie

## Summary

Ainslie is a player character in the HoE campaign, played by Sean Poole.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Ainslie as an HoE-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the HoE campaign context and should not be merged with similarly named characters in other campaigns unless explicitly confirmed.

## Current Read

Ainslie is presented as a human sorcerer in the HoE party, with face, backup scout, and possible scholar roles noted during party formation. Early notes show Ainslie using social connections, messaging, spellcasting, and investigation during the Emberstran dock and vineyard threads.

## Patterns

- Uses social access and old contacts to gather information.
- Often frames the party's presence as official, useful, or connected to authority when it helps move a scene forward.
- Mixes magical communication with practical investigation.

## Goals

- Help unravel the Emberstran mysteries around the docks, the tainted vintage, the tablet, and the larger vault thread.
- Support the party as a face and secondary investigator.
- No distinct personal quest is documented yet beyond the party's current HoE mysteries.

## Fighting Habits

- Fights primarily as a ranged spellcaster.
- Uses fire and other sorcerous magic when the party needs damage from outside melee.
- Tends to contribute from a position where he can still observe, talk, or adapt.

## Interests

- Social leverage, town politics, and useful relationships.
- Strange magical evidence, old clues, and the tablet mystery.
- Using wit and presentation to make the party's actions seem legitimate.

## Timeline-Informed Profile Through HoE Session 25

### Updated Role

Ainslie has become the party's face, magical investigator, social manipulator, and inherited-vault focal point. He still fights from range, but the later HoE sessions show him using social pressure, Sending, Detect Thoughts, illusions, intimidation, and inherited bloodline clues as often as raw damage.

### Major Bonds

- Zymve: Ainslie and Zymve are increasingly tied through the Journal of Tsulanan. Zymve hears music and meaning from the journal that Ainslie cannot fully explain, and Ainslie agrees to tell her if the book reveals anything useful to her.
- Og: Og is one of Ainslie's closer trust bonds. Ainslie explains friendship to Zymve through Og's loyalty and shares private jokes and magical coordination with him.
- Ky'tha: Often works with Ky'tha on social/investigation pressure, but they can split morally, especially around Lady Rook, Petal, and hidden agendas.
- Aurelius: Aury provides Guidance and moral/religious counterweight, especially when Ainslie studies dangerous magic.
- Hap: Ainslie uses Sending around Hap's family and Phira, sometimes stirring more trouble than clarity.
- Irragosa / Tsulanan thread: Ainslie appears tied by bloodline to Tsulanan and possibly to dragonrider history, making Irragosa and the vault personal to him.

### Enemies And Suspicious Figures

- Lady Rook: Ainslie knows she wants what is inside the vault and has sent Petal to watch the party.
- Petal: Ainslie reads her thoughts, intimidates her, spares her once, then helps disguise her body after Zymve kills her.
- Cloak of Defiance / Jerik: Ainslie and Og are specifically under pressure from the Cloak, and Ainslie uses deception and official badges to manage fallout.
- Fire newts, giants, blight creatures, and Mount Emberstran threats: Active physical threats around the vault.
- The Journal of Tsulanan itself: Not an enemy, but definitely suspicious; it hurts, changes, or reveals itself through bloodline/meaning rather than normal language.

### Updated Patterns

- Uses identity as a tool: official observer, E.G.D. authority, noble/arcane bloodline, giant name Mogana, or frightening magical persona.
- Creates stories under pressure and commits to them hard, even when morally messy.
- Uses magic for conversation control: Sending, Detect Thoughts, Mage Hand, invisibility, telepathy, illusions, and social intimidation.
- Is drawn to inherited power and old secrets, especially the Journal of Tsulanan, the Marrowstone, Irragosa, and the vault.

### Updated Goals And Leads

- Understand the Journal of Tsulanan and what it means for his bloodline.
- Learn why Irragosa recognizes or reacts to him as connected to her master.
- Claim or understand the vault contents, which Ainslie increasingly frames as his by inheritance.
- Determine what Lady Rook wants from the vault and whether she can be trusted.
- Understand what the Marrowstone did to him after he drained its necrotic energy.

### Updated Fighting Habits

- Opens with control or enhancement when useful, especially Haste on Og.
- Uses lightning, sorcerous burst, shatter, sleep, telekinetic shove, and necrotic release.
- Avoids front-line danger when possible but will take risky magical actions if the payoff is large.
- Coordinates through telepathy and battlefield repositioning rather than only damage.

### Current Inventory / Power Notes

- Wand of Magic Detection.
- Journal of Tsulanan.
- Empty or altered Marrowstone thread after Ainslie drains its energy.
- Mount Emberstran heart gem, taken from Moganna's vault at the cost of exhaustion.
- Badges of Emberstran / E.G.D. by party association.
- Vault-opening connection through the Emberheart Tablet and bloodline clues.

### Session 25 Update

Ainslie passes the vault's bloodline/emotional trials, cuts himself on the bronze lance to open the "It is Within" barrier, and claims the heart-shaped gem from Moganna's vault. Irragosa confirms he was intended to take it, but touching it drains him into exhaustion and forces a return to Diverra's temple in Emberstran.
