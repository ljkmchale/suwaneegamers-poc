## Current Read

Lila is presented as a glamorous halfling sorcerer with jewels and crystals. In early SoD notes, she joins the party around the Nature's Oratory aftermath and later provides magical support, healing, and temporary hit points during dangerous confrontations.

## Patterns

- Arrives with a glamorous, crystal-adorned presence.
- Provides support from a safer distance rather than acting as a front-line combatant.
- Reacts quickly when a fight becomes dangerous for the group.

## Goals

- Help the SoD party survive and understand the threats around Adsuren.
- No distinct personal quest is documented yet.
- Keep supporting the group through magic, healing, and protective effects.

## Fighting Habits

- Sorcerer support and ranged caster.
- Uses healing and temporary hit points when allies are under threat.
- Moves away from danger when the battlefield becomes unsafe.

## Interests

- Crystals, jewels, glamour, and sorcerous magic.
- Magical support that keeps companions alive.
- Party safety during unpredictable supernatural threats.

## Relationships And Suspicions

### Friends And Allies

- Esylla: Friendly magical connection. Lila discusses her crystals with Esylla and gives her a crystal necklace.
- Kenton: Party ally supported directly in combat when Lila gives him temporary hit points during the Aeolenne fight.
- Therric, Escanor, and Zephyra: Party allies in the Aeolenne confrontation and the cult aftermath.
- Mollywop: Important allied magical figure who helps explain Aeolenne's escape and identify items.

### Enemies And Active Threats

- Aeolenne: Active fiendish threat after influencing Zephyra and escaping invisibly.
- The Intendant / cultists: Enemies tied to the cellar, ritual activity, and sacrifice evidence.
- Zephyra under Aeolenne's influence: A temporary battlefield danger, though still a friend underneath the control.
- Lingering cult/ritual remains: The ritual room and sewer connection remain suspicious.

### Suspicions And Leads

- Ritual-room sewer hole: Lila finds an indentation and a hole into the sewer system, possibly explaining how the gertot head reached the sewers.
- Aeolenne's invisible escape: Lila senses a wisp of wind in the fight sequence, consistent with Aeolenne moving unseen.
- Magic item aftermath: Lila has the Circlet of Blasting / tiara thread and is later seen polishing it.
- Party protection: Lila's suspicions are less investigative so far and more survival-oriented: stay back, heal, shield, and keep the party upright.

### Interests And Useful Question Hooks
