# Albross

## Campaign

Wyrm Bane (WB)

## Player

Cooper

## Character Basics

- **Species:** Pink Tiefling
- **Class:** Warlock (Celestial Pact)
- **Patron:** The Holy Light (seraph; appeared as a horse-turned-celestial being)
- **Deity:** Utheri
- **Armor Class:** 14
- **Passive Perception:** 11
- **Senses:** Devil's Sight 120' — sees normally in magical and nonmagical darkness
- **Defenses:** Fire resistance, Radiant resistance
- **Languages:** Common, Dwarvish, Halfling

## Magic Items

- Leather Armor +1
- Ring of Warmth (attuned)
- Luckstone (attuned)
- Bowl of Controlling Water Elementals (acquired Session 32, from Fort Mornock hoard)
- Potion of Flying (acquired Session 32)

## Notable Facts

Albross and Rhody have pacts, revealed during travel to Khagdaruhl (Session 22). His patron, The Holy Light, appeared to him at Madam Mabel's orchard while the rest of the party was fighting the hags (Session 30), warning him that his brother **Breemos** would be kidnapped in Gevakaln within a harmon if Albross did not intervene. The patron also showed visions of the Cold Breath winning, and of tendrils binding the dwarven king of Khagdaruhl.

Albross was originally a slave at the Alasfolen garrison. He escaped with the party in Session 02.

## Personal Thread

**Brother Breemos** — in Gevakaln. Caelion cast Sending to warn him on the morning of 22nd of Paramor (Session 35); Breemos confirmed he was alive. The vision from The Holy Light said Breemos would be kidnapped by a harmon from 10th of Paramor — putting the deadline around Luros.
