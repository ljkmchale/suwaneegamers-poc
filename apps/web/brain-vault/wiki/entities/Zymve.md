# Zymve

Zymve is a player character in the HoE campaign, played by Emma Cooper.

## Current Read

Zymve is a drow glamour bard who enters active HoE play around the Nunglthil / Spitespirit Cache arc and becomes a major party voice from Sessions 17-24. She is socially sharp, musically gifted, magically supportive, and much harder-edged than her glamour-bard presentation first suggests. Her backstory now clearly includes fleeing an Underdark cult and reaching the surface through the party.

## Patterns

- Uses music and performance to connect, inspire, comfort, intimidate, or dominate a room.
- Often says the brutal or socially sharp thing others avoid saying.
- Reacts strongly to betrayal, tracking, coercion, or being treated as a tool.
- Reads Ainslie's Journal of Tsulanan through melody and emotional meaning rather than normal text.
- Can be nurturing and ruthless in the same arc: she helps Brander regain confidence, but later kills Petal when she decides the spy will not stop.

## Goals

- Stay free from the Underdark cult she fled.
- Understand why the Journal of Tsulanan affects her and why it feels connected to her village / drow past.
- Find out whether the "evil goddess lady," spider-cult pressure, or drow pursuers can still reach her.
- Help the party reach the vault while making sure threats like Petal or Lady Rook cannot exploit them.
- Learn what friendship means without being controlled by a cult structure.

## Fighting Habits

- Uses bardic support, inspiration, temporary hit points, control, and psychic/magical pressure.
- Casts Darkness, Dissonant Whispers, Cloud of Daggers, Mirror Image, Vicious Mockery, Dancing Lights, and other support/control magic.
- Inspires allies at key moments, including Ainslie's Marrowstone study and major volcano fights.
- Can be aggressive in combat and aftermath; in Session 24 she helps swing the fire newt battle and later stomps a defeated enemy's head.

## Interests

- Music, performance, rhythm, bardic reputation, and original songs.
- Freedom from Underdark cult control.
- Drow identity, surface identity, disguise, and how others perceive her.
- The Journal of Tsulanan, Szoradryn, Ainslie's bloodline, and why the book sings to her.
- Fashion and presentation, including cloaks, disguises, and protecting herself from sunlight.

## Question Support Attributes

### Role In Party

Glamour bard, support caster, social pressure valve, Deep Speech communicator, performer, and ruthless problem-ender in HoE.

### Known Bonds

HoE party, Ainslie, Og, Ky'tha, Makra, the Bimblefol genlings, Brander / Bander, and a tense connection to Szoradryn through the Journal of Tsulanan.

### Tensions

Zymve wants freedom and trust, but she is quick to treat persistent threats as things to remove permanently. This creates tension with Ky'tha and Aury after Petal's death.

### Secrets / Unknowns

Zymve fled an Underdark cult about two years ago. She says she had no friends there, fears the cult or "evil goddess lady" may not let her go, and is unsettled by the Journal of Tsulanan because it came through a drow connection from her old world.

### Personal Quests

Understand the journal's music and its connection to her past, stay free from cult control, and decide what friendship means with this party.

### Current Leads

Journal of Tsulanan, Szoradryn, Ys'Nonterien, her former drow cult, Lady Rook's interest in the vault, Petal's death, Sparkwhisper, and the Mount Emberstran vault.

### Inventory Of Note

Studded Leather +1, black cloak, bardic instrument, favor beads, Valecap Extract, and possible forge-made item for Og from Sparkwhisper.

### Places Connected To

Nunglthil, Soothing Spores, Thallgrove, Bimblefol, Bloomrest / Lost Sanctuary of Diverra, Emberstran, Warm Bear Inn, Raddic's Rarities, Mount Emberstran, and Sparkwhisper.

### NPC Connections

Makra, Aniba, Bimblefol genlings, Nelka Nittabomb, Brander / Bander, Petal, Szoradryn, Tessa by indirect investigation, Lyra of Sparkwhisper, and Priestess Meladra by surface introduction.

### Combat Defaults

Start with control, inspiration, darkness, psychic pressure, or protective support; stay mobile and use glamour magic to reposition or fortify allies.

### Decision Patterns

Acts from survival instinct and hard-earned distrust. If someone repeatedly violates a boundary or threatens the party, Zymve may end the threat instead of negotiating further.

### Character Growth

Moving from hidden/late-arriving drow outsider into a full party member who is learning friendship, surfacing old trauma, and becoming one of the party's strongest interpreters of Ainslie's ancestral music.

## Timeline-Informed Profile Through HoE Session 25

### Major Bonds

- Ainslie: Central current bond. His journal sings to her, frightens her, comforts her, and links to her drow past. She threatens him into transparency, then begins to trust him.
- Og: A practical and protective party bond. Ainslie uses Og as an example of friendship, and Zymve later forges or makes something for Og at Sparkwhisper.
- Ky'tha: Trust-adjacent but tense. Ky'tha asks Tessa to investigate Zymve discreetly, and Ky'tha objects more strongly to Petal's killing.
- Bimblefol genlings: Zymve becomes the party's key communicator through Deep Speech and earns admiration through music.
- Brander: Zymve first insults his song, then later, disguised as Lynda, repairs his lute and performs a stunning duet with him.
- Petal: Enemy/spy who becomes Zymve's moral line in the sand.

### Enemies And Suspicious Figures

- Former Underdark cult / "evil goddess lady": Core backstory threat.
- Szoradryn: Not confirmed enemy, but deeply suspicious because he provides Ainslie's journal and is from a drow world connected to Zymve's fear.
- Lady Rook: Suspicious because she uses Petal and wants the vault outcome.
- Petal: Killed by Zymve after repeated tracking and failed attempts to make her leave.
- Fire newts / volcanic enemies: Direct combat threats that badly injure the party and drop Zymve in Session 24.

### Key Session Beats

- Session 17: Rejoins the party, researches Thallgrove fungi, fights stone giants, has Shadow of Rothenloch vision, and communicates with Makra.
- Session 18: Guides communication in Bimblefol and performs for the genlings.
- Session 19: Takes Studded Leather +1 and hears music from Ainslie's journal in the Lost Sanctuary.
- Session 20: Sees bright daylight for the first time, buys a cloak, performs as Lynda, confronts Ainslie about the journal, and admits she fled a cult.
- Session 21: Helps with provisions, keeps watch with Ainslie, and hears comforting music from his studies.
- Session 22: Intimidates Petal, then kills her when Zymve decides Petal will not stop.
- Session 23: Interprets journal music around guarding, sealing, darkened stone, and a heart.
- Session 24: Contributes heavily in the fire newt fight, is dropped, recovers, works at Sparkwhisper, and plays a farewell song.
- Session 25: Irragosa recognizes Zymve as carrying the glint of Vhurkir Inni, a drow dragonrider tied to Kaelix the Copper. Zymve connects Vhurkir to an ancestral memory of jewelcrafting, secrecy, and possibly vault design. Back in Emberstran, a Warm Bear bard recognizes her and abruptly stops performing.
