# Dalbyre Bramblesage

**Class:** Bard  
**Race:** Elf  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]  
**Player:** Chip Poole (later plays [[Narris]])  
**Status:** Departed — left Arbescar by letter at the Battle of Tal'basar (Session 5.2)

## Character Overview

Dalbyre Bramblesage was a founding party member and one of the five who formed at [[Arbescar Light and Magic]] in Marrowdale during Session 0. An Elf Bard, he was an active member of the party from Sessions 0 through 5.1. He departed without confrontation — leaving a farewell letter with the [[Council of Six]] in [[Tal'basar]] just before the Battle of Tal'basar, saying only that he was leaving Arbescar. Chip Poole later returned to the campaign as [[Narris]].

## Session History

- **Session 0** — Founding member; formed the party at [[Arbescar Light and Magic]] in Marrowdale.
- **Sessions 1–3** — Active party member through the [[Oasis of the Night]], Scarlytt's death and resurrection, Yvaine's recruitment, and Erick the Alchemist investigation.
- **Session 4.1** — Sent to gather rumors at the Inn while others split off for tasks. Severed ties with a blacksmith after objecting to the way [[Tassarion Ranges]] was described as [[Sigil (TCB)|Sigil]]'s "servant" during an armor sale.
- **Session 4.2 (travel to [[Fizban's Tower]])** — Shared Shadow, a black stallion, with [[Ren (TCB)]].
- **Session 5.1 (Haunted Mansion)** — Took a key marked with a "W" from a safe discovered in the mansion. Dalbyre left the party in Session 5.2, so the current location of this key is unknown — he may have taken it or left it behind.
- **Session 5.2 ([[Tal'basar]])** — Did not appear with the party. When they asked the [[Council of Six]] about him, the Council produced a letter Dalbyre had left for them. The note said he was leaving Arbescar. He departed before the battle against [[Kal'dar]].

## Character Notes

- Dalbyre had strong moral convictions about servitude — he visibly objected when Tass was framed as Sigil's servant and cut off a business relationship over it.
- His departure was quiet and premeditated — he wrote a letter rather than saying goodbye in person, and timed it ahead of the climactic battle.
