# Ky'tha

## Summary

Ky'tha is a player character in the HoE campaign, played by Lesley Poole.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Ky'tha as an HoE-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the HoE campaign context and should not be merged with similarly named characters in other campaigns unless explicitly confirmed.

## Character Reference

- Player: Lesley Poole
- Campaign: HoE
- Full Name: Ky'tha Fawnborn
- Also Known As: Ky
- Class: Rogue (Thief)
- Species: Dwarf
- Armor Class: 16
- Passive Perception: 16
- Passive Investigation: 18
- Passive Insight: 10
- Senses: Darkvision 120'
- Defenses: Poison
- Languages: Common, Dwarvish, Elvish, Orc, Thieves' Cant
- Deity: Goldraen

## Current Read

Ky'tha is presented as the HoE party's scout, with strong perception and investigation. Early notes connect her to contacts, stealth, searching, and the tablet mystery.

## Patterns

- Leads with scouting, searching, and quiet information-gathering.
- Maintains outside contacts and follows underworld or hidden-thread leads.
- Often notices physical details that help the party understand a danger before it fully reveals itself.

## Goals

- Pursue leads around cultists, the sewers, and the tablet fragments.
- Help the party solve the larger vault mystery.
- Use contacts and stealth to expose what polite society is hiding.

## Fighting Habits

- Rogue scout who can fight at range or in melee.
- Uses crossbow attacks and mobility.
- Breaks out of restraints or bad positions quickly when possible.
- Picks off threats that are already occupying stronger melee allies.

## Interests

- Secrets, contacts, hidden messages, tablet fragments, and physical clues.
- Places where stealth and investigation matter more than force.
- The intersection of crime, cult activity, and civic corruption.

## Timeline-Informed Profile Through HoE Session 25

### Updated Role

Ky'tha is the party's primary scout, tracker, underworld contact, lock/key handler, and practical investigator. By Session 24, she is also carrying a major personal mystery tied to Dunduar, Muerg, Cragfury, sealed flow, and a ring/gate vision.

### Major Bonds

- Og: Older friendship and combat trust. They often move near each other in danger.
- Tessa / Etterset: Ky'tha's most important local underworld bond. Tessa becomes the face of Our Exquisites and offers help against the Cloak.
- Hap: Works with Hap on Lady Rook, inherited land, Phira, and the Marrowstone.
- Petal: Ky'tha tries to negotiate and prevent a killing, then has to process Zymve killing her.
- Irragosa: Irragosa speaks privately to Ky'tha and asks her to watch Ainslie.
- Party: Ky'tha frequently pushes for group agreement when moral decisions get messy.

### Enemies And Suspicious Figures

- Lady Rook: Powerful and useful, but clearly working her own agenda through Petal and the vault.
- Petal: A tracker/spy who says she works for Lady Rook and knows Dandelia.
- Cloak of Defiance / Jerik: Threat to Hap, Phira, and the party's Emberstran safety.
- Blackrend / blight: Threat tied to the Lost Sanctuary and Life Tree.
- Dunduar / Cragfury mystery: Not an enemy yet, but Ky'tha's vision suggests sealed family or ancestral danger.

### Updated Patterns

- Scouts ahead, follows tracks, reads physical details, and notices hidden observers.
- Uses intimidation and underworld logic when negotiations stall.
- Looks for the social structure behind threats: Lady Rook, Etterset, Cloak, guilds, and hidden watchers.
- Is willing to compromise to keep the party moving, but can withdraw or go stealth when others cross lines she dislikes.

### Updated Goals And Leads

- Understand her Dunduar / Muerg / Cragfury vision and the sealed ring or flow.
- Work with Tessa and the Etterset against the Cloak of Defiance.
- Decide how much to trust Lady Rook.
- Help the party reach and understand the vault without handing it blindly to a power broker.
- Keep an eye on Ainslie because Irragosa asked her to.

### Updated Fighting Habits

- Uses stealth, crossbow, dagger, Vex/advantage setups, and sneak attacks.
- Often attacks enemies engaged with Og, Hap, or other front-line allies.
- Uses tools and items tactically, including keys, ropes, immovable rod, and scouting magic like tremorsense.

### Current Inventory / Power Notes

- Figurine of a Raven.
- Quiver of Ehlonna.
- Mantle of Spell Resistance.
- Immovable Rod.
- Jeweled knife from Mount Emberstran approach.
- T-marked saddle-horn-like object connected to Tsulanan / dragonrider clues.
- Flying ship transmutation orb recovered from the wreck near Mount Emberstran.

### Session 25 Update

Ky'tha is recognized by Irragosa as carrying a similarity to Orah Nelcrath, a dragonborn dragonrider from Dunduar. She touches Irragosa in dragon form, recovers the flying ship orb, challenges Lady Rook's timing after Rook admits surveillance, prepares for Phira's arrival, and finds Tessa at the Etterset headquarters just as Ulgrey trouble surfaces.
