# Meles

## Summary

Meles is a player character in the Dungeons III campaign, played by Brian.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Meles as a Dungeons III-only character.

## Character Reference

- Player: Brian
- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Class: Rogue
- Species: Wood Elf
- Armor Class: 13
- Passive Perception: 16
- Passive Investigation: 10
- Passive Insight: 12
- Senses: Darkvision 60 ft.
- Defenses: Immunity to magical sleep
- Languages: Common, Common Sign Language, Draconic, Dwarvish, Elvish, Thieves' Cant, Undercommon
- Deity: Phoe

## Current Read

Meles is the party's scout and quiet observer. He tracks bandits from Hesfal, scouts paths and caves, investigates crime scenes, and repeatedly moves ahead of the group to gather information. He is also opportunistic: he loots, picks locks, takes a flower into Thiddle's house after the bank job, and keeps his own counsel about morally gray choices.

## Character Beats

- Introduced at the Covered Hooch as a thin, relaxed wood elf with well-maintained dark leather armor, short bow, and scimitar.
- Nearly killed during the bandit/prisoner break outside the Town Ward.
- Tracks the K9 Crew from Hesfal toward Sherdin's lean-to and the cave hideout.
- Takes point repeatedly in rough country and in the cave.
- Has a past connection to Dempsey, Aviv, Gurimond, and Varenwood.

## Current Leads

- The Radiant Eye/cultist trail toward the crags.
- Isgo's abduction.
- Meles's unresolved Varenwood past, including Gurimond.
