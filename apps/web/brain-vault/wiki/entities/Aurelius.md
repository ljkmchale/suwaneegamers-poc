# Aurelius

## Summary

Aurelius Valeheart is a player character in the HoE campaign, played by Larry/Lawrence McHale.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Aurelius Valeheart as an HoE-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the HoE campaign context and should not be merged with similarly named characters in other campaigns unless explicitly confirmed.

## Character Reference

- Player: Larry/Lawrence McHale
- Campaign: HoE
- Also Known As: Aury
- Class: Cleric (Light)
- Species: Aasimar
- Armor Class: 16, or 18 with noted adjustment
- Passive Perception: 14
- Passive Investigation: 11
- Passive Insight: 17
- Senses: Darkvision 60'
- Defenses: Necrotic and radiant
- Languages: Common, Elvish, Goblin
- Deity: Diverra

## Current Read

Aurelius is presented as an elegant, reverent Aasimar cleric of Diverra, associated with sacred fires, prophecy, divine magic, and a gentle but intense sense of purpose. In party role terms, he is framed as the healer with ranged and some area-of-effect capability.

## Patterns

- Responds to danger through faith, ritual care, and measured support.
- Often steps into temple, burial, or divine-magic situations as a natural authority.
- Combines gentle warmth with a serious sense of spiritual purpose.

## Goals

- Follow Diverra's guidance and understand the divine forces moving through the campaign.
- Support Hap and the party after Hap's vision brings them together.
- Help with the Meladra / Diverra's Grove / Blackrend thread shared with Hap.

## Fighting Habits

- Acts as a healer and divine support caster.
- Uses ranged cleric magic such as Toll the Dead when direct damage is needed.
- Keeps allies alive while still contributing to damage and battlefield pressure.

## Interests

- Diverra, sacred fires, prophecy, divine magic, and temple service.
- Protecting companions through healing and spiritual guidance.
- Mysteries where faith, old magic, and party destiny overlap.

## Timeline-Informed Profile Through HoE Session 25

### Updated Role

Aurelius / Aury is now the party's primary divine authority, healer, reviver, and interpreter of Diverra's restored teachings. He is no longer just a cleric following signs; he has found the Lost Sanctuary of Diverra and carries Bloom Aeterna, the complete original teachings of his goddess.

### Major Bonds

- Hap: Hap's original vision is increasingly validated by the Lost Sanctuary. Hap and Aury remain tied through Diverra's guidance.
- Diverra: The central bond. Aury finds the Lost Sanctuary, receives Bloom Aeterna, and learns the Shattered Hearts doctrine.
- Priestess Meladra: A home-temple relationship, now complicated because Aury may have surpassed her in direct divine experience.
- Zymve: Aury protects and accepts Zymve despite surface prejudice against drow, and she witnesses his near-death and restoration.
- Og: Aury revives Og after the Session 24 fight and is concerned about Og's Thornleaf / Life Tree calling.
- Ainslie: Aury supports Ainslie's risky arcana with Guidance, but remains a moral counterweight around dangerous power.

### Enemies And Suspicious Figures

- Blackrend / blight: A direct spiritual enemy after the Lost Sanctuary corruption.
- Lady Rook: Suspicious because she wants the Marrowstone/vault power and may use it ruthlessly.
- Petal: Aury dislikes the idea of being watched and later opposes bringing her farther, but the moral aftermath of her death bothers him.
- Arnol: Not an enemy, but Aury notices he is acting strangely and may be drifting from the temple path.

### Updated Patterns

- Reads events through theology, duty, beauty, and the proper use of sacred power.
- Tries to keep people alive even when the party's choices are morally chaotic.
- Withholds some divine discoveries from Meladra when he fears overwhelming her or changing her relationship to faith.
- Struggles when the party chooses expedience over mercy, as with Petal.

### Updated Goals And Leads

- Understand and act on Bloom Aeterna.
- Recover or restore Shattered Hearts by returning them to proper sacred places.
- Protect the Lost Sanctuary and understand its link to Diverra, Bloomrest, and the underwater gate.
- Track Blackrend's spread and the blight affecting sacred groves and life trees.
- Watch Arnol's strange behavior at the temple.

### Updated Fighting Habits

- Heals, wards, uses Warding Flare, Radiance of the Dawn, Guiding Bolt, Toll the Dead, Mass Healing Word, and Revivify.
- Takes big risks to preserve allies; revives Og in Session 24 after one of the party's most dangerous fights.
- Uses divine light and area effects when enemies cluster.

### Current Inventory / Power Notes

- Scepter of Adoration.
- Breastplate +1.
- Emberheart Tablet by party association.
- Bloom Aeterna.
- Petrified rose petals that enable ritual return to the Lost Sanctuary.
- Badges of Emberstran / E.G.D. blessed through the temple.

### Session 25 Update

Aury studies Bloom Aeterna's rose-petal travel ritual, learning the ritual takes 10 minutes and can carry up to three other people at a higher petal cost. He blesses the flying ship dead after Irragosa admits guilt, then prays over exhausted Ainslie at the Temple of Diverra while reading from Bloom Aeterna in Meladra's presence.
