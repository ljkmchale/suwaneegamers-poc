# Og

## Summary

Og is a player character in the HoE campaign, played by Joshua John.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Og as an HoE-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the HoE campaign context and should not be merged with similarly named characters in other campaigns unless explicitly confirmed.

## Character Reference

- Player: Joshua John
- Campaign: HoE
- Full Name: Ogmund "Og" Crag
- Class: Barbarian (World Tree)
- Species: Goliath (Hill)
- Armor Class: 17
- Passive Perception: 14
- Passive Investigation: 9
- Passive Insight: 11
- Senses: Darkvision 60'
- Defenses: Acid and poison
- Languages: Common, Dwarvish, Giant
- Deity: Layeth

## Current Read

Og is presented as the HoE party's tank and melee defender, with backup scout potential. Early notes show him taking direct physical action in fights and investigations, including attacking threats, identifying poison on a murder weapon, and helping push through dangerous spaces.

## Patterns

- Takes direct action quickly, especially when there is danger in front of the party.
- Naturally fills the "hold the line" role.
- Notices practical physical details such as poison, wounds, and obvious threats.

## Goals

- Protect the party as its main melee tank.
- Help solve the HoE mysteries by surviving dangerous spaces others cannot safely enter.
- No separate personal quest is documented yet.

## Fighting Habits

- Front-line barbarian defender.
- Uses heavy melee pressure and durability.
- Moves toward threats rather than away from them.
- Often creates space for less durable allies to act.

## Interests

- Straightforward action, physical courage, and practical evidence.
- Guarding companions.
- Problems that can be solved by strength, endurance, or direct confrontation.

## Timeline-Informed Profile Through HoE Session 25

### Updated Role

Og is still the party's main tank, but the later HoE timeline makes him much more than muscle: he is now tied to the Life Tree, Thornleaf, the Alferumn, and the E'erspan guardian calling. He is also one of the party's best physical problem-solvers in terrain, hauling, climbing, mining, and survival-heavy scenes.

### Major Bonds

- Ky'tha: Longstanding friend and field partner.
- Ainslie: Strong trust bond; Ainslie uses Og as an example of friendship and loyalty when explaining trust to Zymve.
- Thornleaf / Life Tree: Og is called by the Life Tree at the Lost Sanctuary and named E'erspan, thorn-bearer.
- Zymve: Zymve later uses the Sparkwhisper forge to make something for Og.
- Aurelius: Aury revives Og after his death in Session 24.
- Sparkwhisper gnomes: Og's death/revival and Zymve's forge work tie him to this volcanic settlement.

### Enemies And Suspicious Figures

- Blackrend / blight: Direct threat to the Life Tree and Og's E'erspan calling.
- Dandelia of the Veil: Not an enemy, but a major lead the Life Tree sends Og toward.
- Fire newts / volcanic enemies: Nearly kill him in Session 24.
- Petal's death: Not an enemy after the fact, but it may have affected Thornleaf; one thorn turns black.

### Updated Patterns

- Moves first into physical danger and often becomes the anchor everyone else fights around.
- Uses strength for practical logistics: hauling ore, pulling allies, climbing, carrying bodies, and handling terrain.
- Trusts direct action, but later sessions show he also worries about whether the party's actions harm the balance.
- Communicates with Thornleaf or tries to, even when it does not answer.

### Updated Goals And Leads

- Understand E'erspan and the Alferumn.
- Find Dandelia of the Veil.
- Restore or protect Life Trees and fight Blackrend's blight.
- Understand why Thornleaf gained a black thorn after Petal's death.
- Continue protecting the party through the vault and volcano threats.

### Updated Fighting Habits

- Rages, closes distance, and attacks repeatedly.
- Uses World Tree-style temporary hit points and restraint/positioning effects.
- Takes the most dangerous physical positions, including underwater giant combat, lava exposure, and fire newt focus fire.
- Died in Session 24 and was revived by Aury, confirming his front-line risk level.

### Current Inventory / Power Notes

- Eroding Glaive.
- Mask Withering Breath.
- Thornleaf, now with at least one black thorn after Petal's death.
- Scale mail taken from fire newt-like enemies.
- Deep bond to Life Tree / Alferumn / E'erspan thread.

### Session 25 Update

Og carries exhausted Ainslie out of Mount Emberstran after the heart gem drains him. On the road back, Og mentors Zabeek in basic camp rules and gives the aspiring gnome adventurer a practical frame for what to do when trouble appears.
