# Esylla

## Summary

Esylla is a player character in the SoD campaign, played by Lesley Poole.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Esylla as an SoD-only character. Patterns, goals, fighting habits, and interests should be read only in the SoD campaign context and should not be applied to HoE or The Silent Vanguard.

## Character Reference

- Player: Lesley Poole
- Campaign: SoD
- Class: Warlock
- Species: High Elf
- Background: Charlatan
- Armor Class: 14
- Passive Perception: 12
- Passive Investigation: 11
- Passive Insight: 12
- Senses: Darkvision 60'
- Defenses: Magical sleep
- Languages: Common, Elvish, Draconic
- Deity: Tornia

## Current Read

Esylla is presented as a high elf warlock with a charlatan background and a polished, "fancy" appearance. In early SoD notes, she often approaches situations through social probing, magic, and ranged spell attacks.

## Patterns

- Probes scenes socially before committing, often asking practical questions that expose hidden details.
- Uses charm, polish, and controlled curiosity as tools.
- Keeps an eye on magical or occult implications, especially when a threat smells of fiends, curses, or strange power.

## Goals

- Help investigate the poisoned well, cult activity, and Aeolenne's escape.
- Follow leads tied to Mollywop, fiendish influence, and dangerous magical objects.
- No separate personal quest is firmly documented yet.

## Fighting Habits

- Fights mostly at range with warlock magic.
- Frequently uses Hex and Eldritch Blast-style attacks.
- Can miss early but keeps applying pressure until a target drops.

## Interests

- Magic, bargains, deception, beautiful or refined things, and social positioning.
- Occult clues and fiendish threads.
- Useful relationships with magical figures such as Mollywop.

## Relationships And Suspicions

### Friends And Allies

- Kenton: Early social contact through his wares and later a direct beneficiary of her errands, since she brings him the +1 whip from Mollywop.
- Lila: Friendly magical/social connection; Lila gives Esylla a crystal necklace.
- Mollywop: Key magical ally/contact. Esylla visits her, receives help with items, and uses the planned Sending contact during the Aeolenne crisis.
- Zephyra: Party ally whose heritage becomes a major concern; Esylla helps parse what Aeolenne's release means.
- Escanor and Therric: Party allies in investigation and combat, though Therric deceives Esylla into carrying the heart.

### Enemies And Active Threats

- Aeolenne: Fiendish threat whose escape and influence over Zephyra become central concerns.
- The Intendant / Sunmoner Karranok and cultists: Enemies connected to the ritual space and sacrifices.
- The black heart: Dangerous object Esylla carries after Therric shifts it to her.
- Undead and vine/plant threats: Direct combat enemies in the early investigation.

### Suspicions And Leads

- The well access and tainted water: Esylla asks early practical questions about alternate well access and participates in the investigation.
- The black heart's nature: She notices the sulfur/burn smell and becomes one of the characters tracking its occult implications.
- Aeolenne's release: Esylla realizes killing the cultists activated the ritual freeing Aeolenne.
- Mollywop / aunt / mind-control item thread: Esylla follows up with Mollywop about whether help against mind control exists.
- The sewer indentation: Lila finds a hole in the ritual room; this is relevant to Esylla's broader occult/cult investigation.
