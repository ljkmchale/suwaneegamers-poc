# Narris

**Class:** Bladesinger (Wizard)  
**Race:** Unspecified  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]  
**Player:** Chip Poole  
**Advisor:** [[Morath]] — guides Narris toward the "thread of shadow" and planar mysteries

## Character Overview

Narris is a Bladesinger — a Wizard subclass that blends arcane power with martial finesse. He is a precision combatant and scholar, drawn toward planar mysteries by both [[Fizban]] and [[Morath]]. Fizban communicates with him telepathically and has tasked him with following a "thread of shadow." Critically, Fizban has designated [[Sigil (TCB)]] as Narris's anchor for extraplanar travel into shadow realms — Sigil is meant to hold the tether while Narris enters places like the "mirror void."

## Weapons and Equipment

- **Booming Blade** — signature combat cantrip
- **Dust of Disappearance** — used for stealth and tactical escape
- **Silence** — used to suppress enemy spellcasting
- **Prismatic Potion** — received in Session 6.5; properties unspecified
- *Whispers of the Sands* — studying this book written by [[Fizban]] (Tsuul'physs Bhan) for clues about the Blustery Wastes and [[Everlight]]'s time distortions

## Notable Session Events

- **Session 4.3** — Fought air genasi and a hill giant; used Dust of Disappearance and Silence; helped rescue [[Yvaine]] and [[Sigil (TCB)]].
- **Session 5.1** — Assisted [[Ren (TCB)]] in disarming traps and unlocking chests; sent a Sending spell to his parents.
- **Session 6.3** — Studied the [[Everlight]] arch, discovering time distortions; spoke telepathically with [[Fizban]] about his path; learned that [[Morath]] the shadow seer has watched him since his first birth; learned that [[Sigil (TCB)]] has been designated as his anchor for shadow-realm travel.
- **Session 6.4–6.5** — Led exploration in the [[Everlight]] cave; fought howlers using Booming Blade; uncovered a frozen ranger; activated the flame rune; gave up a spell slot for the grove unsealing ritual; received a prismatic potion; suffered memory loss at the name rune.

## Motivations

1. **Uncover Secrets** — Guided by [[Fizban]] and [[Morath]] to follow the "thread of shadow" and explore planar mysteries, tied to surviving the "mirror void."
2. **Pursue Knowledge** — Driven to study artifacts, texts, and the history of the Blustery Wastes and [[Everlight]]; currently reading *Whispers of the Sands*.
3. **Avenge Tassarion** — Shares the group's commitment to honor [[Tassarion Ranges]]'s sacrifice by pursuing [[Kal'dar]].
4. **Protect the Party** — Takes risks to scout and protect through strategic spell use; shields companions with Silence and Dust of Disappearance.

## Relationships

- **[[Fizban]]** — Mentor and remote quest-giver; communicates telepathically; has revealed Morath's watch over Narris and Sigil's role as his anchor.
- **[[Morath]]** — Guides and watches Narris; the shadow seer has observed him since his first birth; actively directing him toward the thread of shadow.
- **[[Sigil (TCB)]]** — Designated by Fizban as Narris's protective anchor for extraplanar travel into shadow realms; their safety is intertwined.
- **[[Ren (TCB)]]** — Partnered with Ren in trap-disarming and scouting work (Session 5.1).
- **[[Tassarion Ranges]]** — Committed to honoring his sacrifice.
