## Notable Facts

Rhody joined the party in Session 15 as "Rhody the Goatherder." He arrived in Witguard with thousands of others who had come to protect the town. He quickly became a core party member.

**Goat:** Rhody had a beloved goat, Nutmeg (also referred to as Gertie in Session 25). The goat died when all mounts were lost during the dragon encounter (Session 24/25). Notably, Nutmeg arrived with a dead deer on its back at Hol'gardur.

**The Fey Pact and Madam Mabel:** Rhody has a pact with Madam Mabel, a witch who lives in an illusioned apple orchard in the mountains. When he was infected by grabbing an amulet (Paragon Tear) with his bare hand (Session 22), his hand turned black and something moved under the skin. Madam Mabel cured this infection in exchange for bags, body parts, and a ruby from three hags. In Session 30, the exchange was completed but Rhody's relationship with Mabel became complicated when he chose to fight hag Viola rather than betray the party's deal with her. In the aftermath, Rhody negotiated with Mabel and paid her in gemstones.

**The Spirit of the Woods — Sinn'ogin/Sennogam:** In Session 25, Rhody underwent a three-stage trial at Hol'gardur under druid Groretta Runeeyes. He was marked by the spirit of the woods, which gives him the Rune of Sennogin and allows spirits to aid him on the mountain. After the trial he can speak with trees and has some control over elemental forces.

**Albross's Pact:** Rhody and Albross both have pacts — nature unclear.

## Personal Thread

**The Farm and Family** — Rhody has a wife and a child (a toy was on the floor in his vision). His farm's crops were frozen, likely by Coldbreath influence. His wife appeared in a vision when Rhody accidentally struck Lucerion during the Viola fight (a flashback from his contract with Mabel). His farm's cold is the same plague affecting the Runegrip dwarves.
