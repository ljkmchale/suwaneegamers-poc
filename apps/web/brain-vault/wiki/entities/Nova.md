# Nova

## Summary

Nova is a player character in the Dungeons III campaign, played by Tiff.

## Campaign Scope

This page documents Nova as a Dungeons III-only character.

## Character Reference

- Player: Tiff
- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Class: Bard
- Species: Aasimar
- Armor Class: 12
- Passive Perception: 12
- Passive Investigation: 14
- Passive Insight: 12
- Senses: 60 ft.
- Defenses: Resistance to necrotic and radiant damage
- Languages: Common, Goblin, Halfling
- Deity: None listed

## Current Read

Nova enters the campaign during the Salvus caravan rush at Hesfal. She is bold, slippery, and socially forceful, with a habit of pushing into investigations and haggling hard. She owns or carries several key mystery items: [[Pequin's Pocket Prism]] and [[Nova's Dunduar Brooch]].

## Character Beats

- Introduced as an aasimar with dark hair, goggles, glowing eyes, and light studded armor.
- Buys Pequin's Pocket Prism after haggling him down.
- Goes to the Town Ward to learn about the accused murderer and later takes keys from dead guards.
- Uses the Pocket Prism to blind enemies during the prisoner-break fight.
- Arrives at Sherdin's lean-to riding Ralli's horse.
- Receives warnings from Blackshield not to separate her brooch's stone from its lattice.

## Current Leads

- [[Nova's Dunduar Brooch]] and its containment warning.
- Ralli's attachment to Nova and his horse/package connection.
- The Scorpion/K9 Crew chain that intersects with Scarwatch Hold.
