Overview
Welcome to Everlight, a hidden gem nestled in the heart of a snow-kissed valley surrounded by towering, frost-covered peaks. This charming village offers a warm haven for travelers and adventurers seeking respite from the harsh wilderness. At its heart stands the grand Twinkling Fir, a magnificent tree adorned with glowing lights and glittering ornaments that symbolize the unity and spirit of the town. Cozy wooden cottages line the streets, their snow-dusted rooftops framing a bustling central square alive with the scent of spiced cider and the sound of cheerful laughter. Visitors can explore the Winter’s Market for enchanted winter gear and handcrafted treasures, warm themselves at the inviting Twinkling Fir Tavern, or seek blessings at the serene Shrine of Everlight. The village is particularly magical during Winterfest Eve, when garlands of holly and festive decorations drape every building, and the community gathers to celebrate amid music and light. More than a peaceful retreat, Everlight serves as a gateway to legendary adventures, with paths leading to the perilous Frostbite Mountain, the desolate Blustery Waste, and the domain of Lithium, the Silver Dragon. Whether you stay for the warmth of its people or the lure of the surrounding mysteries, Everlight promises an unforgettable experience where hearth and magic meet in perfect harmony.  
History
Nestled within a secluded, snow-kissed valley, Everlight is a testament to the divine mercy of Urlich, the God of Winter, Frost and Ice. This village emerged from a cataclysm that reshaped the land 5,000 years ago—the fall of Sunlit Haven. As divine punishment for the hubris of Sunlit Haven's rulers, the surrounding lands became the Blustery Waste, a barren expanse of perpetual storm and sand. Yet, amidst the gods' wrath, the kind-hearted villagers who would become Everlight's founders were spared by Urlich. Shielding them within the Inner mountains of the Waste, the god granted them two gifts: perpetual winter to insulate them from corruption and a radiant artificial sun, the Everlight, which hangs eternally in their sky.
Over centuries, the villagers transformed their refuge into a thriving community. The Everlight itself became a symbol of hope, unity, and divine balance, infusing the village's culture with reverence for warmth amid cold and light amidst darkness. Though remote and isolated, Everlight draws wanderers, adventurers, and scholars with its reputation as both a place of solace and a gateway to great mysteries.


Citizenry
Tal’basar’s residents take pride in their town and are deeply connected to its development. The community spirit is evident through various social gatherings, festivals, and communal projects aimed at further enhancing the quality of life in the town. This shared sense of purpose and camaraderie helps foster a tight-knit and supportive community.
Races
Everlight's population reflects a mix of hardy and resourceful individuals who have adapted to the perpetual winter and isolation created by the gods. The demographics are shaped by the town's history, divine intervention, and the practical need for survival in the harsh Blustery Waste. The community is diverse but united by a shared reverence for the blessings of Urlich and a deep-rooted culture of cooperation and kindness.

Holidays and Traditions
Winterfest
The Winterfest Celebration serves as both a cultural event and an opportunity for villagers to reflect on the year’s achievements and challenges.
Government
Everlight operates under a council-based governance system, rooted in community cooperation and guided by tradition. While the village is small and close-knit, its leadership is structured to ensure fairness and stability in the face of the unique challenges posed by its location near Frostbite Mountain, the Blustery Waste, and the surrounding wilderness.
The Village Council
   * The governing body of Everlight consists of five council members, each representing key aspects of the village’s daily life and needs. Decisions are made collectively, with the Mayor serving as the chairperson and tie-breaker in critical matters.
   * Council Roles
      * Mayor Elsa Hearthwell (Chair): Oversees village welfare, diplomacy, and overall leadership.
      * Lyanna Frostveil (Priestess): Represents spiritual matters and maintains the balance between Everlight’s traditions and its connection to the surrounding magical forces.
      * Kaelen Froststrike (Ranger Captain): Advises on defense, hunting, and safety from external threats.
      * Gretchen Lightfoot (Innkeeper): Provides insight into trade, hospitality, and the needs of travelers.
      * Stellan Ironcoat (Blacksmith): Represents the working class and oversees infrastructure, crafting, and resource allocation.
Town Meetings
   * Villagers gather in the Town Hall once per month or during emergencies to discuss issues, share concerns, and propose changes or initiatives.
   * Major decisions, such as sending expeditions to Frostbite Mountain or negotiating with Lithium the Silver Dragon, are presented here for public input.
________________
Customs
   * Everlight places significant value on community participation and fairness. While the council holds authority, they often defer to a public vote for major decisions.
Interaction with External Forces
1. Frostbite’s Influence:
   * The council has been debating how to handle the growing threat of Frostbite, with some advocating for alliances while others call for proactive defenses.
   * Villagers fear that Frostbite’s power may one day overwhelm Everlight.
2. Lithium the Silver Dragon:
   * Lithium holds significant sway over the north route through the mountains. The council maintains a cautious but respectful relationship with the dragon, often offering tributes or gifts in exchange for safe passage and trade opportunities.


Laws
Characters engaging in criminal activity are arrested by the city guard and charged per the City Ordinances.  Once charged, one is brought before the Council for judgment.
Economy and Trade
Everlight’s economy thrives on its strategic location as a crossroads for adventurers and traders navigating the treacherous regions of the Blustery Waste, Frostbite Mountain, and the surrounding wilderness. While small, the village sustains itself through resourcefulness, barter, and its unique goods crafted to endure the challenges of its harsh environment. 
Note: Of course this has been a challenge due to the isolation.
1. Trade and Barter
Bartering Culture: The economy is largely barter-based, with coins supplementing transactions. Adventurers often trade rare artifacts, ingredients, or services for supplies.
Seasonal Goods: During Winterfest Eve, the market is particularly vibrant, drawing visitors to purchase festive treats, enchanted winter gear, and decorative wares.
2. Specialized Craftsmanship
   * Frost-Resistant Goods: Everlight is renowned for its high-quality winter gear, such as frost-resistant cloaks, boots, and gloves, crafted by local tailors and blacksmiths. These are essential for travelers heading into Frostbite Mountain or the Blustery Waste.
3. Local Resources
   * Snowberries: A unique fruit found in the region, snowberries are used to produce Snowberry Jam and Snowberry Wine, popular for their sweet yet refreshing taste.
   * Frostwood: A durable wood harvested from frost-resistant trees near the village. It’s used in construction and crafting tools, weapons, and furniture.
   * Crystalline Veins: Crystals mined from nearby caves are used for magical enhancements or sold to passing mages and alchemists.
Professional Guilds
In the towns and cities of Myrdae, craftspeople and merchants often form professional guilds, bound by official charters. These guilds not only protect the economic interests of their members but also ensure their personal safety, both at work and at home. Guilds take a strong stance against those who harm or exploit their members, with offenders often facing social exclusion from the profession or, in more severe cases, the intimidating intervention of the guild’s enforcers—wielding the very tools of their trade. These organizations maintain strong communal ties, blending professional solidarity with a protective brotherhood.
Religion
Central Beliefs
The Everlight Deity (Urlich)
The villagers revere a minor deity known as Urlich the Hearthkeeper, a guardian of winter, frost, and ice. The Hearthkeeper is symbolized by a glowing flame or a sun cradled by frosted branches, representing the balance between warmth and cold.
Domains: Winter, Frost, and Ice.
Beliefs
The Hearthkeeper blesses those who foster community, offer warmth to the lost, and protect others from harm. Acts of kindness, such as sharing food or offering shelter, are seen as sacred duties.
The Balance of Elements
Beyond the Hearthkeeper, Everlight acknowledges the spirits of nature and the elements, especially those tied to fire and light. These spirits are neither fully benevolent nor malevolent but require respect and offerings to maintain balance. Villagers believe that angering these spirits could lead to harsh winters or misfortune.
The Festival of Winterfest


This annual celebration honors the Hearthkeeper and the spirits of light and frost. During Winterfest, the villagers light candles, leave offerings at the shrine, and share stories of hope and perseverance.
Religious Practices
Daily Offerings
Villagers often leave small offerings—candles, food, or carved wooden tokens—at the Shrine of Everlight or their hearths to honor the Hearthkeeper.
Snowberry branches, representing both the harshness and bounty of the winter, are a common offering.
Blessings and Rituals
Travelers' Blessing: Before setting out into the wilds, villagers or adventurers visit the shrine for a blessing of protection.
Hearth Ritual: Each household maintains a small hearth flame, which is never allowed to go out during the winter months as a symbol of hope and endurance.
Sacred Sites
The Shrine of Everlight is the central place of worship. Located in the southern part of the village, it is a serene structure adorned with frost-kissed garlands and glowing crystals. The shrine’s altar features a golden flame that flickers perpetually, said to be the Hearthkeeper’s blessing.

FrostForge


The Frostforge is a compact, well-insulated forge located near the heart of Everlight. Built from heavy stone and reinforced with gleaming steel, the building stands firm against the harshest winters. Thick plumes of smoke rise from its tall chimney, mingling with the cold mountain air. The interior is warm and bustling, with the glow of molten metal illuminating rows of finely crafted tools, weapons, and armor.
The Frostforge is renowned for its enchanted weapons and frost-resistant gear, making it an essential stop for adventurers heading into the frozen wilds. Shelves and racks display finely honed blades, reinforced shields, and thick chainmail, while a special section showcases magical items imbued with protective runes against cold. The rhythmic clang of hammer on steel echoes through the air, a testament to the forge’s constant activity.
Adventurers often visit the Frostforge not only for its exceptional craftsmanship but also for advice on preparing for Frostbite Mountain's dangers. The blacksmith's attention to detail ensures every item is both functional and resilient against the icy wrath of the wilderness.

Shrine of Everlight


The Shrine of Everlight is a small, tranquil temple tucked near the South of Everlight, providing a haven of warmth and serenity amidst the harsh winter. Built from smooth stone and adorned with carved motifs of the sun, hearths, and protective runes, the shrine radiates a comforting glow. Its entrance is flanked by evergreen wreaths and softly burning lanterns, a beacon for those seeking solace.
Inside, the shrine’s air is perfumed with the scent of pine and beeswax candles. The walls shimmer faintly with magical light, said to be a blessing from the deity it honors. A modest altar stands at the center, draped in white and gold cloth. Offerings of food, candles, and pine branches are carefully arranged upon it, placed by villagers in hopes of blessings for protection and warmth. Surrounding the altar, benches and cushions provide a place for meditation and quiet reflection.
The shrine serves as both a spiritual sanctuary and a source of guidance for those preparing to venture toward Frostbite Mountain. Many believe that the deity worshipped here, a minor but beloved protector of hearth and home, watches over Everlight’s people, ensuring their survival against the cold.
Special Features:  
- Divine Glow: The magical light within the shrine provides dim illumination in a 30-foot radius. It also prevents frost from forming inside, creating a constant atmosphere of warmth.  
- Urlich’s Blessing: Visitors who leave an offering at the altar may receive a boon of temporary protection. 
  - Effect: Advantage on saving throws against cold-based effects for the next 24 hours.

General Goods Stall


Nestled west of Everlight’s bustling central square, Bernard’s Stall is a modest yet welcoming shop built from sturdy timber, its snow-covered roof adding to its rustic charm. Outside, barrels and crates are stacked neatly, showcasing wares like salted meats, dried herbs, and coiled ropes. A brightly painted sign above the door depicts a cheerful face surrounded by tools and provisions, inviting travelers and locals alike to step inside.
Inside, the shop is cozy and well-stocked. Shelves are lined with adventuring supplies such as lanterns, rope, rations, and cold-weather gear. Interspersed among these practical items are locally made goods, like jars of frostberry jam, hand-knit scarves, and carved wooden trinkets. A special section near the counter features festive treats for Winterfest, including candied nuts, snowflake-shaped biscuits, and bottles of Bernard’s famous spiced cider. The air is filled with the faint, comforting scent of cinnamon and nutmeg, while warm lanterns cast a golden glow over the wooden interior.
The shop is more than a place to buy supplies—it’s a gathering spot where adventurers can trade stories, locals can catch up on news, and everyone leaves with a smile.

	Frosted Firs (Tailor and Outfitter) 


Frosted Furs is a cozy and inviting shop located near the center of Everlight, known for its exceptional winterwear and enchanting designs. The exterior is modest, with frost-covered windows displaying beautifully crafted cloaks, hats, and gloves. A wooden sign shaped like a stitched snowflake hangs above the door, marking this as the place to find the warmest and most stylish apparel in town.
Inside, the shop is lined with racks of thick winter cloaks, fur-lined boots, and hand-knit gloves and hats. The soft hum of an enchanted sewing station fills the air, its magical needle stitching intricate snowflake patterns into garments without missing a beat. The walls are adorned with bolts of colorful fabric, while a small fireplace crackles in the corner, adding warmth to the shop. The scent of spiced tea lingers, a signature of the shopkeeper's hospitality.
Frosted Furs is more than a tailor shop—it’s a sanctuary for adventurers and villagers alike who seek warmth, durability, and a touch of charm in their winter attire. Many items carry minor enchantments, making them especially popular among travelers braving the icy trails of Frostbite Mountain.
