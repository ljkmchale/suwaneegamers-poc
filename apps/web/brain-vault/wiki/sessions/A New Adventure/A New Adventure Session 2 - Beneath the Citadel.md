# Session 02 - Beneath the Citadel

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  January 24th, 2025
In-game Date:  19th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  The Broken Citadel
The group takes a short rest. 
Ro and Kaizo decide to take the wolf pup back to Mossholde before darkness comes. Ro takes the powder with her.  Fungus takes the other items in his sack, but leaves the sack there. 
Beneath the Broken Citadel
Fungus performs a ritual over the trap door…looks for traps.  Affy opens the door.  There is a spiral staircase.  We descend the stairs - Affy, Cerul, Ham, then Fungus.  Arrive at 20’ sq. chamber.  Room lit by phosphorous moss.  There is an archway to the south.  Ham notices where dust has been moved around - not footprints - but where piles of dust have collected on the sides of the walls.  Affy notices patterns on the walls of the moss similar to the patterns on the floor.  Cerul pokes at the moss that Affy points out.  He notices that the glow happens at the roots/core of the moss…once you flick it the glow fades as it falls to the ground.  We go through the arch and the ceiling opens up.  
Very well crafted area.  Earthy smell.  We notice symbols on the walls. Not sure what they mean. Seems to be more symbols vs language.  The hall opens up into a 30’ square chamber.  Looks like a lab of some type (alchemy).  Initially looks long ago used.  Ham thinks it has been recently disturbed, but that most of the use might have been older.  Fungus looks beyond the doorway…doesn’t leave…but sees a hallway that extends as far as he can see.  Ham notices a groove in the south wall near the hallway.  He finds a secret door. Ham and Affy have a discussion about “not judging size,” while Fungus pulls the door open.  It is a corridor that is a dead end.  Affy steps in, but doesn’t find anything.  Cerul finds a secret door at the end.  Affy opens it.  Piles of bones are stacked in the corner of the room. There are skeletons against the wall.  Affy steps in - INITIATIVE.
Affy gets hit, Cerul is missed.  Affy is missed, Cerul is hit.  Fungus jumps in and casts a spell that hits. Ham throws a quill and misses.  Cerul casts a spell on 2 of them; one takes damage. Affy hits one and kills it. Two attack Affy and one hits her. One attacks Cerul and misses.  Fungus casts a spell at one. Ham sends another quill and hits. Cerul casts a spell and hits. Affy takes a swing and misses.  The skeletons attack and miss Cerul and Affy.  Then one attacks Cerul and (silvery barbs) misses and drops his weapon. Fungus misses.  Ham hits. Cerul casts a spell, one is hit and is taken out.  Affy finishes off one with her sword.  One left and it misses Affy.  Fungus is mad and attacks with hellfire and hits it. Ham throws a quill and hits.  Cerul uses rapier and hits and takes it out. We look around and see more lab supplies, tools - similar to what we saw in the other room.  There is another archway leading out.  We search the room and skeletons and don’t find anything of value other than the hammer.  Cerul takes it.  It is a magical light hammer. We look through the arch…Affy sees a long hallway with a door several feet down to the left and another even further down to the left. At the first hallway, Affy looks north and sees it is a hall that leads to another hallway and a set of double doors in the hallway.  Once in the hallway, we notice a familiar archway to the left.  To the right, the hall ends in rubble, but something is on the ground about 80’ down.  As Ham moves to investigate the item on the ground, Fungus tries to open the double doors (they are locked).  
INITIATIVE.
Ham casts mage hand and pokes the “goo” when he’s 30’ out.  Affy follows.  Fungus is at the door and tries to pick the locks. Ham throws a dart at the goo and hits and damages it.  Affy considers the situation.  Cerul throws a firebolt and hits.  Affy stows her sword and moves toward the goo 15’.  The goo moves up, but is still out of Affy’s range.  Fungus tries to pick the lock and succeeds:  Cerul gives him an exasperated look for opening another door while they are fighting something.  Ham throws and quill and misses and backs up.  Cerul uses a firebolt and kills it…oozing into the cracks.  Fungus opens the door and sees a dimly lit circular room.  He sees a summoning circle in the middle of the room.  Sees a number of symbols around the room.  There is a cube in the center of the circle covered with runes.  He hears something shuffle in the shadows…and sees two grotesque creatures emerge in the room, a smell of sulfur in the air and making terrible noises.  
INITIATIVE.
One of the creatures moves toward Fungus.  He closes and locks the door and runs down the hall like a huge baby.  Ham looks at where the ooze was to see if there is anything there.  Fungus watches the doors and hears something thumping them.  He goes to the door and fumes come out through the cracks and burn his lungs with acid damage.  Fungus unlocks the doors and opens as he runs away, using the door as a shield. 
INITIATIVE.
Ham moves closer to the door and holds his action to throw a quill.  Sees fumes coming out the door.  Cerul takes half cover and readies an action to fire off a spell.  One comes out the door and is hit by Cerul’s firebolt, but it doesn’t hurt him as much as he would have thought.  It was absorbed by the fatty flesh.  It is also hit by Ham’s quill…and radiant damage.  It moves forward and tries to bite Ham and misses.  Another comes out the door.  Fungus moves back and then casts a spell at the one in the back, but fumbles.  Affy shoots an arrow and kills it…its blubber splashing on the other one behind it as it turns into dust.  Fumes waft toward Ham…con save and fails and coughs, taking acidic damage.  He attacks and misses and moves back as far as he can.  Cerul steps out to firebolt him and hits.  It starts to head toward Fungus in a dash.  Fumes head toward Fungus and he is not harmed.  He then casts a spell and runs away.  Affy shoots an arrow and misses.  Ham moves next to Affy and attempts to message the creature in Common (“have you ever had anyone in your head before?”).  There is no reaction or response from the creature.  Cerul steps up to firebolt it and hits.  It turns around and moves toward Affy and Ham.  Affy chokes on the fumes, but takes no damage, thanks to Fungus bestowing luck on her.  Moves back 30’ and shoots an arrow and kills it, turning it into dust.  We close the door to the room, knowing that these creatures seem devilish and head back to the far hall and the alcove/room we didn’t explore (Affy, Cerul, Ham, Fungus).  We see a large intricate circle in the center of this room (this is a teleportation circle - out of character).  This was long ago used…ancient sconces.  Cerul senses that this is probably a teleportation circle that has probably not been tracked by the Tegenwald Phet…he makes notes to pass to “his people” (the Phet).  He copies down the symbol.  Fungus sits and tries to cast a ritual using his various vials and pouches.  Affy and Ham question who “these people” are that Cerul is talking about.  He mentions this group - who espouse freedom and magic and the light - and he is a member of it.  Ham ponders the history of this group…and knows they were almost banned a long time ago, but rose to power again 20 years ago. Ham vows to help Cerul get this information to his people if it will help him rise to prominence with them, something Cerul says he isn’t interested in, but does want to help them. Affy knows they are a magical group.  After about 10 minutes, Fungus realizes this is a conjuration circle/symbol. He wants to see if there is any other magic in the area.  He looks back at the ring (isn’t sure of the magical school - might be divination or enchantment) and hammer (it has a simple enchantment). Fungus leaves this area and starts to move south and east, looking to see if his spell identifies anything else.  This tunnel ends in a heap of rubble as well.  We see a large dark hole through the rubble.
INITIATIVE.
Fungus sees something undead heading our way. Ham throws a quill with radiant damage and it hits.  Then he backs up.  Fungus casts a spell on it and hits hard and then backs up, cursing it with disadvantage and it steps up to hit Affy.  He misses.  Affy hits it with her sword and kills it. She yells - “anyone else?”  
We hear a drip, drip.  We see a deep hole…very dark.  We get the impression that we aren’t ready to proceed here.  
We make our way back to the circular chamber where the 2 blubber men were.  Pentagram etched into the floor - we think it could be associated with Asmodeus (prince of darkness).  There is an arched gateway with vines in the far wall - there is no longer power here - but this looks to have been a long ago used portal.  The cube on the floor in the middle of the circle glows with magic.  The pentagram is a traditional summoning circle.  Fungus moves to the golden, metallic cube - drawn by its magical glow.  There are runes on each face of the cube - and there is a mechanism to move the faces about (out of character - Rubik’s cube meets hellraiser).  Fungus starts to manipulate the cube…runes shift positions.  Cerul and Ham are working on capturing the runes on parchment, as Affy looks at the portal.  After nine clicks of the cube [Fungus rolls 9 - quickness], magic extends from the cube and engulfs Fungus (he feels quicker - gets 10’ more movement per round).  He picks up magic from all schools as he holds the cube.  Ham walks into the circle to take the cube from him, but it is frozen and won’t move again.  Affy investigates the portal and realizes this was a gateway to/from the feywild (picks up hints from the vines).  Affy notices the keystone at the top is missing.  She notices the dent in the floor and figures that someone came through and took the keystone to effectively “close the gate.”
Ham tells Affy that he is impressed with her intelligence.  He muses that he never would have thought that of her.  Cerul continues to draw and note everything in the room…he studies the cube and realizes the cube has Sylvan properties and may have also come from the feywild.  We think the gremlins and bone collector may be tied to this as well, as they had fey properties.  Looks like they came from the feywild and were left here.  
This Old Citadel clearly had a lot of uses - feywild portal, summoning circle from the hells, an old teleportation circle long forgotten, and a hole/tunnel down deep to who knows where.  
Ham launches into a soliloquy to talk to Cerul about would the Phet use this information wisely…and would it help Cerul gain more favor with them.
We decide to go back upstairs and make our way back to Mossholde before we further explore this hole.  
Cerul knows he needs to get information to the Tegenwald Phet - might be best to go to Qal’dynn to find a contact to send this info.
We know Ro needs to get the powder to a library or someone who can identify its uses and see if there is an antidote that can be applied on her father.  Again - Qal’dynn might be the closest, biggest place to seek knowledge for this.
Fungus thinks the portal to the Feywild might help him figure out his power.  He pockets the cube in his bag.
Mossholde
In Mossholde, the group gets a long rest.  Fungus no longer feels energized by the power of the cube.  He realizes that the cube is no longer frozen - maybe this is something that could be used every day.
Ham studies the ring - it is a Ring of Truth.  Cerul finds that the hammer is a +1 light hammer.  Fungus gives the cube to Ham to study (he will “turn” it next session).
– The End of the Session –
