# Session 09 - Rel’Udomina Hideout

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  June 5th, 2025
In-game Date:  30th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Secret Passage beneath Qal’dynn
30th of Talil, 1246 |  Secret Passage
We decide we are going to go up the stairs.  Ro and Jasper decide to stay back in the chamber to stand watch.  Kaizo goes up to check at the top of the stairs and finds nothing.  Affy goes up and finds some give in the door and starts to push - feels like solid stone.  Kaizo hears a click and tells her to stop - she does.  Ham comes up and investigates and thinks it is just a latch and pushes it open.  We see a hallway, 10’ wide - going right and left.  Across the hall is a lit sconce and there is another sconce that looks like it is hanging off the wall - probably the mechanism to open the door from the hallway.  Cerul and Affy think this is dwarven construction.  Hall goes 55’ left and ends in door, 70’ to right and ends in a door.  Room to hide in the hallway between sconce shadows.  
Entrance Hallway
Kaizo stealths to the left to check it out…gets to the iron door (rusted) to listen.  He hears scratching/scraping noise (steel on rock).  Comes to the group to tell us what’s up.  Then goes to check out the other door to the right - it is also iron (similar wear and tear; touched recently).  Kaizo hears nothing.  Tells the group to come on down.  Ham looks around and doesn’t see anything else and tries to open it.  It pulls toward the group and opens (not locked).  
Strange Chamber
This opens to stairs that go down - dark staircase.  Fungus throws a lit marble to the bottom of the stairs, as Affy pulls on a sconce to the left of the door.  The marble bounced and rolled down rough stairs to a large open naturally circular chamber and then disappeared.  
Secret Chamber
We close the iron door and look at the new door Affy revealed.  This new opening leads to a 5’ hallway that goes 15’ that opens into a small room on the right and another hallway on the left and a hall continues beyond the small room.  We see a small shelf in the room area - there are books on it and a small chest next to it.  Ham checks and thinks the chest is trapped. He tries to disarm the trap (everyone else stands back).  A needle shoots out and poisons him. Cerul heals him as Fungus unlocks the chest.  We find a red flask, lots of sp and some gp.  Fungus starts putting the coins in his sack (65gp and 250sp) and hands the Potion of Healing to Kaizo.  Ham starts to examine the scrolls and tomes.  Scrolls appear to be maps (Vrathin’s handiwork).  Maps of the Abbey…but not areas Cerul is familiar with (secret areas revealed on the map). One of O'naren…with some spots that are familiar (all common knowledge). Other random maps we can’t identify yet - we would need to study them.  Cerul keeps all the maps.  We see in the tomes/paperwork Vrathin’s name again…as well as other names - with Esinni Qadul being a standout.  The title “The Prominent” is associated with Esinni’s name.  None of us have heard that title before, but we think that might be the name of the Rel’Udomina leader.  These documents are incriminating of that cult…and plans to overthrow towns, leaders, etc.  Ham carries the papers in his pack.
Ladder to Trap Door
To the left of this small room, the hallway continues to a grooved wall that looks like a ladder heading up.  Kaizo stealthily climbs up the stairs, but Affy grabs him to stop him.  Ham goes up to have a listen - trying to be stealthy.  There are wooden planks at the top.  He listens and hears snoring/breathing of a beast…and comes back.  Fungus explored the other passage and found a dead end.  
Pedestal Room
Affy goes to check and finds a door to push…she pushes on it until she hears the click.  She listens and doesn’t hear anything except a slight humming.  Affy pushes it open into a well lit 25’ diameter circular chamber.   Around the room are violet couches, a curved path ahead with stairs leading up, and a small pedestal in the center of the room that is giving off a small glow.  Affy sees that there are sconces in the room that open doors…and closes the door they came through.  The group heads to see the pedestal.  It has a thick stone base and a mirrored, almost glass top.  Fungus looks into the glass and sees an image of a grand elven hall.  He sees what looks to be King Highvale and three advisors.  Fungus hears conversation, but doesn’t make out the words.  He can tell the king seems unsettled.  One advisors has sigils floating around his head (arcane energy).  The king is tapping his blade.  Words in elvish (now that Ham and Kaizo have stepped forward)...the king is concerned about his position and the city.  Ham realizes this is a scrying tool…someone is spying on the king.  We pick up the glass and take it with us…wrapping a bedroll and ropes around it.  
Commons Area
We decide to go up the stairs that lead from this room.  Kaizo leads the way and stealths.  It ends in an iron door.  Kaizo hears people speaking elvish on the other side.  He isn’t sure how many people he hears, but knows it is more than two people.  Ham and Kaizo decide to open the door and pose as part of the group.  Fungus, Affy, and Cerul stay back…if they say “Affy” we come in guns blazing; if they say “Fungus” we come in as hired hands.  
They enter a large square room with 4 guys in it who ask “what are you doing here?”  Each wall has an iron door.  There are several bookshelves on the walls in between all of the doors.  Lots and lots of books.  There are 4 tables and the guys are standing in the middle, saying “I don’t believe we’ve met.”  Kaizo says “I thought we’d see you earlier” and Ham says “We are looking for books on the history of Qal’dynn.”  They say - those books are over here…did The Prominent send you down?  Ham: No - Esinni didn’t.  They: We don’t call him that.  Why did you come through that door?  Ham: We got lost in the tunnels.  IT IS INITIATIVE.
Fight happens.  Two with short swords and two with crossbows…all using poison.  Kaizo hits one.  Then both Ham and Kaizo are hit.  Cerul runs in and casts a spell that hits all 4.  Fungus runs in and kills one with a spell.  Ham asks if anyone wants to parlay…since Fungus took one down quickly…and casts command on two of them.  One fails (he is halted).  Affy hits one.  Kaizo hits one with his weapon and then again unarmed and kills him and starts to search him.  One attacks Ham, but Cerul distracts him and he misses.  The other attacks Cerul, even though Fungus tried to distract him, and hits with damage and poison.  Cerul attacks and misses and heals Ham.  Fungus uses poison spray to hit one and kills him.  With one left, Ham tries to persuade him to talk.  Then Affy hits him “you should have taken his deal, you @&*(& elf” and kills him.  We take a few moments to search the people and the books.  No armor - just clothes - they have hand crossbows and short swords (Fungus takes them all, except one of the short swords that Affy takes) and thieves’ tools (Fungus x 2, Ham, Kaizo) and one flask of poison (Fungus).  They also have 48sp and 8gp.  We also find small tattoo on inside of the forearm (flame with mask of stern grimace) of each person.  We think it is probably the symbol of the Rel U’domina.  Affy takes a short sword and cuts the tattoos off all 4 arms.  We take bodies to the pedestal room and put them on the couches, as Fungus cleans up the mess from the fight. 
Hired Guard
We decide to listen at the door the guys pointed to before they died.  Kaizo hears nothing.  Ham hears something but it is too far away to make out.  Ham and Kaizo open the door…same plan as before.  The halls turns…(Ham messages Affy to bring the others and follow).  Kaizo stealths to look down the hallway.  He hears the sharpening of a blade (rock on steel) to the passage to the north and sees a small alcove and a passage that turns to the right.  Group catches up and then stops, as Ham and Kaizo make the turn, heading toward the alcove. They see a guy sitting in the alcove in armor with javelins on his back, sharpening a long sword, a shield to the side.  Him:  I don’t remember seeing you come through.  Kaizo:  I think the others need you Him:  What are you talking about, what do you mean they need me?  Kaizo:  They want you at the library (persuade).  Him:  I don’t think you are from these parts.  Ham steps in, but the guy looks at Kaizo’s arm and doesn’t see the tattoo.  INITIATIVE.
Kaizo crits. Fungus runs in (hearing the fight) and tries to spray him and misses.  Affy runs in (saying Affy) and hits.  Cerul runs in and casts and spell and misses.  Ham tries to talk to him and asks him to throw his weapons down (persuasion).  The guy then says “gold speaks” and Ham shakes his head and he attacks Kaizo, but Kaizo deflects some of the damage with a reaction.  He then tries to attack Affy and misses.  Ham continues to babble…(thy runneth they mouth). Kaizo attacks and misses, then tries with his fist and misses.  Fungus tries to spray him again and hits.  Affy hits again with her sword.  Cerul tolls the dead on him.  Ham tries to persuade again.  The guy backs down and says he was hired to watch this place (if you don’t see the symbol on the arm, kill them).  He was paid 5gp to watch the hall.  Affy pulls out one of the skin tattoos and shows him, and the guy is grossed out.  Affy asks his name (Vognar)...he lives in the city.  Cerul questions why the city is closed - he doesn’t know much.  We ask if we think we could go through the University (through the spiral staircase…through the secret door…and through the study hall).  He thinks we could get out if we went unnoticed.  We talk to him about needing to get his wife and kids and flee the city, but decide to let him stay put and “do his job” and tell no one he has seen us.  He pulls out a kit to heal himself and Fungus cleans up the area.  Affy threatens to take care of his wife and kids should he betray him.  He says he will do what we’ve asked and Ham feels he is telling the truth.
We decide to stay in the tunnels to try to find others in the group, including The Prominent who passed by the guard hours ago.  Cerul heals the group, including the guard.  Guard tells us that left seems to be where the crowd goes, and right where The Prominent goes more often.  Kaizo tells the guard “we are here to save your city and your family - don’t disappoint us.”  
We double back the way we came. We go to the library room - Affy listens at the west door and it seems quiet.  Ham listens at the south door and it is quiet.  Ham opens that door.  It is a long hallway, and we pull sconces as we go - looking for hidden doors.  We go 70’ and it branches off with a hallway to the right and double doors to the east.  Affy listens at the doors and doesn’t hear anything, but she sees double doors to the other side.  
The Prominent’s Chamber (?)
She pushes the doors open in front of her - it is a well-lit study room.  She sees a desk and a lady standing up with 2 large dogs that leap out from behind her, turning to see a small dragon by my face, as it opens its mouth.  She says “Affy” and we stop here for the night.
– The End of the Session –
