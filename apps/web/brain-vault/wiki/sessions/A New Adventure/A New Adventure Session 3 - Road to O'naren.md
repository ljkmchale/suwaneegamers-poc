# Session 03 - Road to O’naren

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  February 2nd, 2025
In-game Date:  20th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Mossholde
Kaizo and Ro made it back to Mossholde (while the rest of the group were in the Citadel).  An animal merchant (Clarton Waitor) with an albino rongri pet and a wagon appeared in the town shortly after they arrived.  Said he was heading east.  Wasn’t really stopping there, but Ro decided to travel with him and the pup…said she would meet us eventually in Qal’dynn.  Kaizo noticed that the merchant’s horses seemed oversized.
The group meets up with Kaizo the following morning after returning to Mossholde.  Ham has been studying the cube to see what he can make of it. Cube has mysterious, puzzle-like power…not sure where/how it is drawing power.  Runes have ancient Sylvan linkage, but nothing he’d seen before.  Fungus needs a pearl to help. Ham decides to try to cube - Cerul stands far back, but Kaizo, Affy, and Fungus stand close to Ham.  [Ham rolls a 32-riches].  On the third turn, we hear a crack.  Cerul smells sulfur in the air. A puff of smoke flashes near Cerul and something stands next to him with a pointy hat, purple robe.  After 6 turns, the cube stops. We all feel our coin pouches get heavy from the cube.  This red-skinned guy shows up…we all think he is associated with the cube and question him. His says this seems like magic that his “parents would be interested in.” He says he wants to be “the great.”  He and Ham have that in common.  His name is Ireron.  Kaizo takes a long drag of his pipe and blows out smoke that resembles a mimic.  He questions Ireron about where he “poofed” from/to last time.  He went to a sheep farm.  This “poofing” happens quite a bit.  Fungus asks if he “poofs with a purpose.” He says he doesn’t really know.  Since maturity, he’s been poofing farther from home.  Ireron asks what Kaizo is a master of.  
Nylean’s Camp
We decide to head out.  Nice fall day.  About halfway through the day we find a used campsite - no one there.  At the end of the day we find a shelter/building that has been used by travelers.  Board set up to dress animals.  Not big enough to house folks.  A dark-haired elf comes out and introduces himself.  He is uncomfortable with the diversity of our group - Kaizo and Ham talk to him in elvish.  Affy catches on and steps forward and extends her hand to introduce herself.  Nillian shakes it (and wipes it off).  Kaizo and Ham convince/persuade him to be kind.  He offers to sell us rations at half price.  He is willing to buy/sell other stuff. We ask him about other travelers - he confirms Ro/Clarton passed through.  No other travelers in past couple of days.  Says travel slows down as it gets colder.  Right now most are hunting and gathering to stock up for colder weather. Nillian offers us a couple of tents to use for the night. Ireron tells us that his mom spoke of “camp rules”.  We ask him who his mom is - it is Mage Kasminos (Kaizo has heard of her).  Kaizo meditates outside a bit; Ireron casts an alarm around the tents.  In the morning, Nillian brings us hot beverages and we pack up the tent.  
Affy decides to give the cube a try.  Ireron stops her and asks if she is invested in it.  The group discusses how to turn.  Ham says he could recreate what he did yesterday (more coin).  Affy prays to her goddess and asks for a positive outcome.  Kaizo and Ham step back 30’ - everyone else close up.  [Affy rolls a 71-tired].  It moves fast and quickly…and then she manipulates it herself - more than a dozen times - then there is a flash.  Those of us close by have levels of exhaustion (1-3 depending on our rolls).  Cerul, Ireron, and Affy tried to pay attention (Affy, Cerul, Ireron, and Fungus think they could avoid doing this again - but will have to roll to try to avoid).    
We decided to travel despite being tired…moving at half speed.  We made it to the next campsite about 10 miles away.  Find another clearing off the path and set up camp with bedrolls.  Kaizo builds a fire and Ireron casts an alarm around the area, several 20” cubes.  We ask him how effective the spell is (tells us a story, “one time there was a squirrel”).  Kaizo meditates.  Winds pick up during the night.  Night is uneventful.  We get a long rest.
22nd of Talil
Next morning, Affy gives Ireron the cube.  Cerul gets 60’ out; Kaizo gets 30’ out. Fungus, Affy, and Ham get close.  [Ireron rolls a 79-relax]. Flash of light for 20’...those inside get benefits of a long rest. Fungus takes the cube from Ireron to hold. We decide to head out.  Still not at full speed (Cerul still has a level of exhaustion).  Fungus sings; Ham jumps in.  Cerul, Fungus, and Ham speak “giant” and sing in that language.  We only travel 10 miles and stop again. Ireron sets up the alarm again.  We all decide to rest.  Kaizo meditates. Ireron’s alarm goes off and Kaizo sees Ireron pop up to look around.  Kaizo relights the fire.  They both see a wild dog sniffing around who then scampers off. The two have a conversation about his perimeter.  The rest of the night passes uneventfully. 
23rd of Talil
We wake up and discuss the alarm and the dog.  Ireron mentions that Kaizo said to turn alarm off for wild animals and Affy disagrees.  Group decides that Ireron can try the cube again - trying to recreate the relax condition to help Cerul.  [Ireron rolls to get relax and avoid tired - he rolls a 12 - age].  Cerul, Affy, and Fungus observe.  Kaizo stays back with Ham.  Ireron learns that you cannot replicate a pattern in back to back days. Ireron, alone, is afflicted with aging - he is now in his 50s (permanent). He gives it to Fungus.  Ham breaks out in song.  We decide to travel on.  We pass strangers, make small talk.  We travel a little faster, but still a little slowed.  We travel 20 miles.  Find a clearing with lean-tos.  Ireron sets up the alarm.  Uneventful.
24th of Talil
Cerul feels great today.  Fungus wants to try the cube.  Cerul, Affy, Ireron get close, while Fungus rolls.  [Fungus tries to replicate riches…he rolls Int of 15, rolls 90 - comprehend].  A flash of light envelops Fungus and his head glows.  He feels more knowledgeable in the way of linguistics.  He knows the Sylvan language (but doesn’t know it yet). As he looks at the cube, he realizes that he now understands it better (it has Sylvan runes).  He feels like the name of the cube is “Feywoven Glyphic.”  He says it in Sylvan (Lelphfelthalis - group laughs). We head out on a day of travel.   Travel 20 miles and get to another campsite (probably a day outside of O'naren).  There are a couple of small hunting parties camping here as well.  We notice that these people seem to do night hunting - they don’t camp at night.  Fungus trades short swords for rations. Affy asks if the hunters looked at him weird and he says no…and tells her that one was looking at her like they “like” her.  You never know.  Usual provisions at night.
25th of Talil
Early in the morning, Kaizo catches movement near us as the day is breaking (he was meditating). He feels it is dangerous.  They are like large moving twigs.  He wakes the group.  Affy shoots an arrow at one and misses.  Kaizo stands up and moves behind a bush.  Cerul stands up and dons his shield and stands still.  Ireron moves and casts a firebolt and misses.  Fungus stands and moves and makes big noises and casts hex and hellfire and kills him. One attacks Cerul and misses.  Ham inspires Affy.  And then attacks one with a quill and hits. Then the rest attack:  Ham and hits, Fungus and misses, and runs up.  Affy shoots with a bow and hits; Kaizo hits one with his staff and shatters it; Ireron casts magic missile and hits; Cerul hits one with firebolt; Fungus kills one with hellfire; one tries to hit Affy and misses; Ham tries to hit with a quill and does so, then gives Affy inspiration again.  One attacks Ireron and crits. Affy hits one with an arrow.  Kaizo takes another out.  Ireron casts magic missiles on one and kills it; and on another and injures it.  Cerul attacks one but it saves.  He heals Ireron.  Fungus hexes and hellfires the remaining one and kills it.  We check over the dead bodies for loot - just see a bunch of sticks.  Ham decides to try the cube.  Kaizo doesn’t like it.  Ham tries to NOT age, then not tired. [Int roll - 16; He rolls a 76-unknown figurine].  Cerul and Kaizo step 60’ out; Affy and Ireron study, but Fungus is close.  Cube glows and rattles after 7 turns in a new pattern.  A shiny stone forms under the cube and drops down (it pooped a stone).  Ham picks up the stone and thinks it is magical. Ireron can identify the stone (got pearl from his mom).  After 10 minutes, he thinks it is a command word associated with it (once one attunes).  It will suck in the soul of a beast nearby into the ethereal plane and then they can call upon that beast to help. (stone becomes a figurine once you pick the beast). Ham keeps the stone for now (Fungus tells him it was his will that drew forward the stone).  Then we continue forward, travel for just under an hour.  A commotion breaks out in front of us - woman and child screaming.  Initiative.
Hired Killers
We hear horse hooves in the distance; the woman and child are scared.  Fungus welcomes them and casts a spell on himself and his club. Kaizo runs up and asks them to come up with him - trying to take them into a bush to shelter.  Ireron steps forward and readies a spell (firebolt).  Horses round the corner and one raises a crossbow to fire; Ireron’s spell goes off and misses.  Guy jumps off the horse and shoots an arrow at the woman and misses.  Another hits the child with an arrow and the kid falls prone and crawls.  Another moves up and sends firebolt at Fungus and stays on a horse.  Another shoots a crossbow at Fungus.  Cerul attacks and hits, heals child.  Ham runs up and casts spell on one of the horses to make it prone. Affy dashes forward, weapon drawn…ready to hit if attacked.  Woman is confused; she and her son hide behind a tree. Fungus pulls up next to Affy and casts bane on 3 of them (2 save and 1 fails).  Kaizo tries to convince them to move, he hides; Ireron observes them (2 elves and 2 humans) and asks what is going on; bad guy answers “you know not what you deal with, leave the woman and child and go”; Ireron fires a bolt into the air and asks everyone to stop; one attacks Fungus, one is crushed by his horse and it falls prone, but he is able to wedge free and hide behind his horse; another hides behind a tree with his horse; another hides behind a tree and uses crossbow to hit Fungus and then hides.  Ham moves forward, sings, and casts dissonant whispers (guy fails).  Affy steps forward to hit one, and misses (but still damages - yells at him).  Mom and kid hide in bushes.  Fungus attacks and hits.  Kaizo flips out of the bushes and lands, pauses, and then hits the prone guy. Ireron tries to interact again, is brushed off, he looks at the woman on the horse and holds his reaction of his firebolt to release if she does not validate her goodness; prone guy stands and attacks Fungus and misses; then attacks him and hits; another shoots at Cerul and hits; lady runs away and Ireron’s firebolt hits; Cerul hits one with firebolt; Affy takes the head off of one and runs after another; Mom and Child are hiding; Fungus casts hellfire on the horse running away and it saves; Kaizo dashes and catches up with the woman fleeing on horseback and swings his staff to knock her off and hits and kills her, knocking her off and falling and hitting her head on a rock.  Ireron tells last guy to surrender or die; he runs away and tries to hit Fungus and misses.  He tries to hide and does.  Cerul readies his toll the dead spell.  Ham talks to a horse - I’m going to call you “Washington,” and handles the horse successfully. Affy yells “come out, f…er…I have your horse,” horse steps away; Affy moves up and sees the guy hiding and points him out to Cerul.  Mom and kid come out of hiding and say, “are they gone?” Fungus runs over to try to see the guy who is hiding and beckons the guys to “come out,” and casts a spell on himself.  Kaizo handles the horse successfully.  Ireron had walked back to the woman and child and converses with her.  She says “I have to get out of O'naren.”  Remaining guy runs out into a clearing and tries to go after the woman and child, shooting an arrow at Ireron…it misses him and it kills the woman with his poison bolt.  Cerul tolls the dead and the guy fails and takes damage.  Ham mounts the horse and runs over and hoofs the man, but misses. Affy tries to handle the horse and fails, asks the guy what his horse is named.  Fungus tries to hellfire the guy and misses.  Kaizo loots the dead woman (holy symbol dedicated to Negrum, has mace, robe, chainmail shirt).  Ireron magic missiles the remaining bad guy and steps forward to block the guy.  Guy runs over to the kid and kills him.  Cerul firebolts him and he is injured.  Ham rides the horse to the guy and whispers to him and the guy saves and takes half-damage.  Affy tries to handle the horse and fails and walks toward the crowd.  Fungus casts a spell on the guy and hits and kills him (his head explodes).  
We collect all four horses.  We loot all the bodies.  Other items we find:  rusty scimitar, light crossbow, 6 daggers, leather armor, hand crossbow, very nice short sword, thieves tools.  We know that Negrum is the god of the undead.  4 gp, 32 sp, 38 copper.
We then search the Mom and Child:  tucked into the mom’s pouch is a scrap piece of parchment; sack of basic supplies (they are on the run).  Couple of bedrolls, a few days rations.  The parchment is a burned…appears to be part of a larger map.  Looks to be a town.  Along the edge of the parchment we see “routes compromised (and maybe a dash) Vrathin is….”  We don’t know of a Vrathin.  Ireron casts detect magic….the cube is very powerful, but also sees magic on the sword.  He identifies it…it is a +1 short sword.  Kaizo takes the sword.  We see no marks of any kind to distinguish any of the six.  Affy makes sure to give the mom and child a burial.
O’naren
We take/ride the four horses with us.  We continue to head out.  We reach O’naren.  We approach the stables on our way in.  There is a barn and home; large, fenced in area with a mix of horses.  There are two people at the stables.  We see a 2-story building with guards.  Affy goes up the stairs to tell the guards what happened in the woods.  The guards accuse Affy of killing the two and Affy gets upset.  She turns around and calls Kaizo up to help explain.  He goes up and they talk in elvish…he explains what happened.  He then asks who is in charge of the town and they tell him it is Lord Etham.  Kaizo says we seek his audience.  They suggest the Nestling Sword is the inn to stay in overnight and say they will tell the Lord where we are.  Meanwhile, Fungus tries to get in the water and the guards tell him to stop - this is the Driftglow and you cannot bathe in the waters (can’t even put your feet in).  Kaizo tells them we need supplies, food, a place to stay…and the guards start pointing everything out.  
– The End of the Session –
