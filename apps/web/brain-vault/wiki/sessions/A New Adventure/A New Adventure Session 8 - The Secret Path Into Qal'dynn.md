# Session 08 - Secret Path into Qal’dynn

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  May 18th, 2025
In-game Date:  29th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  The Elle’berian Shrine
The Elle’berian Shrine - 29th of Talil, 1246
We have the sealed scroll and other items.  We think we still want to go to the city of Qal’dynn, but will need to find the hidden entrance.  As we tidy up, Fungus detects magic - doesn’t sense anything other than with the onyx (petrification - already expended).  Ham thinks the people we killed were disguised as people of the land…and were the ones defacing the shrine.  They may have been part of the Rel Udomina…we aren’t sure.
Ham is trying to study the map to see what he can see.  He’s frustrated with it, but hands it to Affy to check.  There is scratching that she notices, but can’t see what it means.  Thinks the entrance is on the south side of the city - maybe near a large hill.  We will need to get closer to the town to figure it out. We decide to proceed forward and go as far as we can and then make camp.  Fungus holds the cube, Ham holds the unopened scroll, Affy holds the map, Cerul has the signet ring and the broken onyx amulet. 
Disciples of the Wind
We travel for a few hours and as the sun is disappearing, we see a fire and tents ahead.  There are lanterns - green/orange/gold - circus feel.  We hear music, smell herbs.  We see wagons that are familiar.  This is a group of nomads.  Fungus approaches, dancing (not very well).  They motion us in, offer us food.  Tell us we can join their camp for the night.  “Oh you like Yavin,” is the comment to Fungus (she’s the one playing, singing). 
They introduce themselves as “the disciples of the wind.”  Ham and Ro think these people are as they seem.  Nothing nefarious.  Affy thinks there are around 30+ people - mostly humans, elves, half-elves.  Cerul is looking around for musicians - sees the one lady with the dulcimer, and a few people with chimes.  Kaizo notices a female human sitting around who looks like the mom of the singer/player.  He talks to her about where they are travelling to/from.  They said they were trying to go to Qal’dynn…but the gates were shut and no one can enter.  They said they are going to O'naren tomorrow.  Affy asks who their leader is - and it is the woman.  Affy then asks if they would be willing to take a message to someone in the town for us and she says she will. Kaizo comments on the skill of Yavin…and how her mother must be proud.  And she says that she is proud.  He then asks about her husband (and the girl’s father - who the girl gets her talent from) and she reponds that he is deceased…based on something that happened near Palason back in the day.  Cerul asks Yavin if she will play his little flute and she does.  She asks where he got it and he tells her he’s always had it - isn’t sure where it came from.  He is happy to hear what it sounds like.  She tries to teach Cerul how to play it.  Affy asks the mom about the Palason incident and Affy sees that the lady is uncomfortable.  Affy tells her about the “roads are safer tonight” because of the fight they had earlier in the day.  Her name is Eraline - she is the “way god” for the disciples.  She asks Affy about where she’s from, but Affy dodges.  Conversation turns to Cembus and how he provides safe travels.  Affy says she will say a prayer to their god tonight.
As the night passes, Ham talks to members of the party about going to find the gate tomorrow.  Kaizo overhears the gnome talk about “I don’t know where Pearson went - he does this all the time.”  He asks about this Pearson…and finds out that this person is the merchant we encountered:  Pinprick.  We reveal that we encountered him.  The gnome asks if we saw her treasures.  Kaizo ends up pulling out the unbreakable vial he purchased and dumps the seeds in a pouch and then presents her with the vial.  He asks her to smell it and she says it isn’t hers - smells too skunky - and gives it back.  She asks us to join them if we want and asks us what to expect in O'naren.  Kaizo tells her we took care of dangers on the road and describes the town.  
Kaizo then goes back to talk to Eraline - he asks what guides her.  She says they are one with the earth, led by Cembus.  Affy asks if she has been to Bactdelm.  Kaizo tells her to seek out the herbalist in O'naren.  She asks if we were aware of the rare pools in that town and we tell her that they kept Fungus out of the pools.  She laughs and replies that they are elven pools.
The group gathers before going to sleep.  Ro is going to write a note to the Captain - telling him what happened on the road (we found the dead courier, have the sealed scroll that we are planning to try to take to the king).  Kaizo talks to Fungus about the vials that he has.  Fungus feels a little bad, but isn’t sure he will say anything or do anything.  Affy thinks we should keep a watch as well, but most of the group wants to sleep.  Affy will keep watch part of the night.  Kaizo will meditate by the fire.  Ro and Jasper will sleep by the fire.  Night is fairly uneventful.  
Visit from Tanana
Not long after Fungus goes to sleep, he wakes up a little disoriented - having had a riddle in his head - see below.  And wakes up.  Hears fluttering.  More rhymes.  He looks around and everyone is asleep.  He feels a touch on his left and hears more rhymes in his head.  Then he looks to the right and hears more in his left.  Then hears more rhymes.  Then he sees a small fairy.  “I’m Tenonna, she sent me” and disappears.  Fungus knows this is related to his patron.  
These are the rhymes he hears from the fairy:
“Dreamy Fungus, so overgrown,
Power's path is not your own.
Come from grime and shadow-fed,
Shoddy’s secrets bloom ahead.”


“Witchlight fingers, borrowed breath—
You cast with gifts not truly left.
A bargain blooms where roots entwine,
In feybound soil and warped design.


“She weaves with twine and tattered thread,
A crooked crone with thoughts unread.
She feels you so, dear mushroom child,
But love from hags? Now that’s wild.”


“She’ll ask you for a root once bound,
A glyph that grows in buried ground.
But watch, dear Fungus, what you seek,
For glyphs may whisper when they speak…”
When we all wake up, Kaizo just looks at Fungus (not knowing what happened) - just trying to make him feel guilty about the goods he has.  We got a long rest.  This area is the Cradlecrest…hills, low mountains.  We head towards Qal’dynn.  We travel for a few hours in the morning until we finally see the city start to come into view.  It is very Elven…mixed in with the natural surroundings (the city walls are beige and meld into the hills). We see tall spires behind the walls - adjusted for the hills inside the city.  “Beige” feel, very pretty and nice.  We approach on the river side - Raelura River.  Affy tries to study the map is is overwhelmed by the city.  She makes out the river and walls, but not much else.  Knows that we need to go to the southside of the city.  We approach in a valley between the hills and river.  Pass people who have been turned away.  We know it is going to be on the outside walls on the right, through the woods and forest.  The river cuts through the city.  We decide to go to the gates first - to try to get inside the city.  Fungus wants to go to the front of the line, but we end up waiting our turn.
Qal’dynn
We finally make it to the front of the line and see the wardens with a swan symbol on their chests.  Fungus says, “You have a swan.”  Ham then steps forward and tries to talk to the gate keepers - saying we have a message for the king.  They claim that they have heard this all day.  They pull out messages…some look real, some not.  Ham then speaks in elven and introduces himself.  Ham tries - asks if they know who Ravus is.  They said they would pass on a message to him, but won’t let us in.  Affy then questions him since Ravus isn’t inside the walls.  Just tells them they shouldn’t name drop unless they know who is or is not in the town.  They call her an orc…she turns and walks away, spitting as she goes.
Group leaves the gate area and heads into the woods, following the walls to the right.  Ham tries to find the entrance.  We think we find a path - that was trying to be hidden.  We go through the dense area, trees, hills, rocks, etc.  Path is not a trail…but we finally get to an area that dead ends into the hillside. Tall hill, walls of city, dense shrub.  We would not have found this except for the map.  
Secret Passage
Affy starts to inspect the rock for signs of an entrance to the hidden area.  Her hand passes through the rock and feels hurt.  She pulls back and feels pain in her hand.  She is poisoned.  She says “whisperbend” and tries again and goes completely through the rock.  Ham follows.  It is very dark inside as a tunnel opens up.  Ham casts a spell to see if he can light anything up and finds that it is dark because the tunnel turns to the left immediately.  He messages Cerul and he says the password and comes through.  Then Fungus goes through.  Then Ro.  Then Kaizo.  We decide to follow the tunnel.  Affy and Cerul lead (Cerul lights up his shield), followed by Kaizo and Ham, then Ro and Fungus.  We find that the light is magically suppressed.  We realize we need magical light to see.  We circle around, then it bends the other way…follow it for a while.  So far the tunnel is all earthen.  Eventually, the path opens into a broad cavern and at the center is a hulking statue 20’ in front of a door.  Nothing is lit in the room, but we make out this large figure. We advance forward and it is Initiative.
The Guardian
This figure looks strong and bulky - a construct of clay or earth.   Ham tries to cast a spell and it saves. Kaizo takes a glowing marble, advance forward, speaks the password, and hold his action.  It attacks Kaizo and Affy (hits both).  Fungus attacks (hexes him).  Cerul attacks with Radiance of the Dawn (it saves).  Ro attacks - casts Magic Missile. Affy attacks with her trident, hits and knocks him prone.  Ham commands him to halt and he does (no movement, loses a turn, effectively).  Kaizo attacks with advantage and hits. He misses his turn.  Fungus attacks and misses and steps back and the statue hits him.  Cerul hits with a rapier using truestrike.  Ro hits him with magic missile.  Affy hits him.  Ham tries to command again and it saves.  Kaizo attacks and hits.  Then it his Kaizo and Affy.  Cerul attacks with his rapier and hits and heals Kaizo.  Fungus misty steps and heads for the door.  Then hexes him and taunts him.  Ro casts magic missile again.  Affy hits him and knocks him prone, also dealing magic force damage.  Ham commands again and he hits (it is held).  Kaizo hits and also flurry of blows. Cerul hits with truestrike and his rapier. Ro tries to hit with mold earth.  Affy hits and then uses action surge and hits again and takes him down.  Long fight!
As the fog clears, our darkvision comes back.  We check him out…he is made of stones and earth.  Fungus takes a stone as a souvenir.  We look around the room.  We see a door in the far wall. It is an ancient stone door with carvings on the door frame. Ham reads the “Society of the Aetherion Academy” on the door frame.  Cerul knows this is one of the primary universities in the Borelian Sway.  Knows they are located in Qal’dynn. Cerul comes to investigate the door…he finds a spot under moss and lichen that looks almost like a heart with jagged edges.  Fungus goes to beat on the statue and he finds a gem shaped like a heart inside…and uses it to open the door.  Cerul puts the gem in the hole.  The door opens and slides to the slide, revealing a staircase going up.  We take the 100gp gem (amethyst) once the door is open. 
– The End of the Session –
