# Session 17 - Abbey Arrival

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  October 2, 2025
In-game Date:  43rd of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Path to the Abbey of Light
Combat on the Path
Session 16 ended with an ambush.
Session 17 starts with combat … Initiative - as 2 tendrils from a very large being rise up from a stone, 2 giant birds with colorful wings, and a large stone figure emerge (looks like an earth elemental). 
* ROUND ONE:  Ridley takes aim at the tendril creature and fumbles and then misses, as he realizes that this thing is hard as a rock. That creature lashes out at Ridley and hits, grappling him, then misses with a bite.  Then attacks Kaizo and pulls him in and bites him.  One bird flies in to attack Fungus, picks him up, and misses; then tries to claw him and misses. The other bird tries to grab Relys and misses and tries to claw her and fumbles. Kaizo tries to attack the tendril creature and misses twice, then tries to kick him and misses.  The stony creature steps back and then rolls in boulder form to Cerul and misses. Fungus blinks and turns large and interacts with the bird saying “fly away, fly away, fly away” - to see if it understands him. Cerul sprouts wings and casts spirit guardian in 15’ ring (on bird and stony creature).  Affy turns and attacks the bird on Fungus, hitting once and missing once.  Relys shifts into a bear and attacks the bird on her and hits twice.  She realizes that these creatures are in league with whatever is protecting the area…maybe summoned by the stone giants.
* ROUND TWO:  Ridley tries to attack the creature holding him and hits a tendril. The tendril shatters…and Ridley is released, as the creature is pushed 10’ away.  He then hits again, pushing it another 10’ away (dragging Kaizo with it).  Ridley then backs up.  That creature then attacks Kaizo.  A tendril then regrows. It then reaches for Relys and pulls her close and tries to bite her and misses - but she is grappled and has the poisoned condition. It then tries to grab Fungus and does - grappling him and giving him the poisoned condition and pulls him 30’ closer. One bird tries to grab Cerul and hits and then flies up 30’ and drops Cerul (but he flies and is ok).  The other bird attacks Affy and fumbles and then misses.  Kaizo attacks and misses twice, but hits with his unarmed strike on the tendril holding him. The stony creature animates a stone and an earth elemental emerges and it rolls towards Affy and hits her, knocking her prone.  Fungus misty steps away and tries to poison spray the bird and misses, and blinks away. Affy attacks the bird and hits, missing the stony creature.  She hits twice. Cerul attacks with his spell and tries to hit 3 creatures and does - some taking full, some taking half damage.  He then uses a weapon to attack the stony creature on Affy and hits.  Relys swipes at the creature grappling her and misses, then hits. She lets the group know these are creatures of nature (tells the group what she learned). 
* ROUND THREE:  Ridley shoots the bird that is flying and hits twice, killing it - then moves, trying to get out of range of the tendril creature. That creature then tries to grab Cerul from the air and misses. He then tries to bite Kaizo and misses; and tries to bite Relys and misses.  The bird flies to get Cerul (Affy misses the opportunity attacks) and hits him, grappling him, and tries to bite him and misses.  Kaizo attacks and misses twice, fumbling, then misses again.  The initial stony guy rolls toward Affy and misses.  The other stony guy rolls into the bear, hitting it, knocking it prone.  Fungus pops back in and casts acid spray on the bird fighting Cerul, hitting critically, killing it.  He then misty steps and heals Affy and blinks away.  Affy attacks the stony creature on her and hits twice, then yells “Affy” loudly to the group.  Cerul flies over toward the tendril creature and other stony creature, imposing his spiritual aura, damaging them. Then attacks the tendril guy and hits. Relys casts moonbeam on the tendril creature and hits.
* ROUND FOUR:  Ridley attacks the tendril creature and kills it, putting a bolt into its eye. Then tries to hit the stony creature and misses.  Kaizo attacks the stony creature on Affy and hits, then misses, then misses with flurry of blows and then hits with second flurry of blows. One of the stony creatures moves toward Kaizo (Relys misses opportunity attack).  It hits Kaizo, but he deflects the damage. The other stony guy then attacks Affy and hits. Fungus pops back in attacks one with a spell, and misses. Then he misty steps and pops away.  Affy attacks the one on her and hits once.  Cerul flies over and moves his spirit guardian aura to the final two stony creatures who do not save, both taking damage.  Cerul then attacks the second stony creature and misses.  Relys stands up and moves the moonbeam to attack both of the other stony creatures who save, but still take damage. 
* ROUND FIVE:  Ridley shoots one of the creatures and hits twice, good damage.  Kaizo attacks the creature in front of Affy, killing it, shattering the rock.  Then attacks the last creature, hitting it, then hitting again with flurry of blows.  That creature attacks Cerul and hits him, and the spirit guardian drops. He then moves toward Affy (and Kaizo misses his opportunity attack).  Affy then moves up and hits the last guy twice, including some additional force damage.  Cerul tries to attack and misses.  Relys moves the moonbeam over the creature and saves, but takes damage. 
* ROUND SIX:  Ridley attacks the last creature and hits, killing it. They are all dead.  
Affy apologizes to Relys that we had to fight the creatures of nature.  Cerul heals himself. Kaizo heals Affy. Ridley shifts his hunter’s mark to the stone statue to learn of his “conditions.”  We discuss making sure that other creatures of nature might come…so let’s try to free the stone giant. 
Helping Brundhalk
Cerul tries to use the scroll of Greater Restoration on the petrified giant, Brundhalk.  Cerul is going to make sure that he gives himself the best chance to free him.  He casts enhance ability on himself. Affy prays to Phoe.  Fungus gives him a back rub.  Fungus stands next to Cerul - he can speak giant and help calm emotions.  Cerul tries to use the scroll, but the magic doesn’t work - and the scroll dissolves into ash.  Brundhalk remains petrified!
Looking for Another Way
The group decides to proceed toward the Abbey and try and find a way in.  Cerul studies the map to see if there is an alternate way to enter.  He finds something that he didn’t know from when he lived there - an undercroft - though it isn’t clear where that is. Cerul thinks about all of the things he knows about the Abbey - clerics, priests, and monks - but thinks that stone giants are guarding the place.  With a history check, he knows there are three clans that exist in the Cradle Crest Rise.  Two have strong relationships with humankind:  Watchers of the Abbey and Watchers of the Bastion.  Kharos clan (Abbey)....they have a stone speaker who works for a high carver.  For this clan it is Ada, who is a matronly figure (and Cerul’s adopted mother).  The third clan - Highridge Dreamers - live high up in the mountains and he doesn’t know much about them.  Cerul thinks the giant that we saw from the campsite was probably one from the Kharos clan.  He thinks they might not be accommodating if we cross them, but thinks it isn’t hopeless.  Helping save the one who was petrified would have helped, but maybe he can convince them he at least tried.
Stone Giant Guardians
The group proceeds forward slowly on the forest line, trying to be quiet. After a couple of hours, we stop and allow Kaizo to take the lead and scout ahead. At a ridge, we can see the Abbey of Light in the distance. There is a large building with a large bell tower, lots of other stone buildings.  Kaizo sees two stone giants in the area on the road to the Abbey - talking to each other, keeping watch (they look bored).  He points this out to all of us. Relys thinks the Rel’Udomina are in the Abbey…and they have been able to disable the arcane waypoint (or something has done this).  Lorian told the group that people can’t get in…so we know something is wrong inside the Abbey.  Relys thinks the giants are reacting to their friend being petrified - probably on defense until they can figure out what happened. 
We discuss having Cerul talk to the giants to persuade them to let us in to fix things - telling him that he was a friend of Brundaulk and tried to save him. 
Cerul knows there is a free tavern/inn for travelers who go back and forth to the Bastion…in the Abbey grounds.  There aren’t many other resources in the Abbey…it isn’t a town.  But there are probably 400-500 people who live inside the walls.  Fungus notices that there is farmland in the valley area, but not inside the walls. 
Giant Protectors - Gidfar and Advar
The forest line is 500-600’ from where the giants are.  Ridley, Kaizo, Relys, and Affy stay in the woodline.  Fungus and Cerul walk down the path, singing giant songs and speaking in giant.  The giants take notice and flex and wait for them to get close.  Within 75’ away, they hear “none will pass.”  Cerul says “we come in peace.”  They say that “none with pass while Brundhaulk sleeps.”  Cerul explains that he tried to save him with the scroll, and that he knows Brundhaulk.  These two giants are Gidfar (who Cerul recognizes) and Advar and he mentions the others he knows and is able to convince Gidfar that he is the little boy that he knew.  He says “you are Ada’s boy” and Cerul says yes.  And then introduces Fungus, who speaks in giant.  Cerul tells them they have friends who are hiding in the hills who are there to help as well.  Cerul says that he might be able to help Brundhaulk if they let him and his friends into the Abbey.  Cerul and Fungus tell them about the Rel’Udomina, and tell them that they are the ones who did this to him with magic.  They ask how many friends. And they agree to let the group enter.  “Advar - what do you think?”  He says “why don’t you ask Ada?”  And he says that is a good idea.    They ask if he has anything to show…and he pulls out a carved wooden box with a polished stone…and says to show the box and stone to Ada.  She should know the box for sure.  He says he will take this to Ada and find us if she does not recognize it.
He says, let’s go get your friend - and puts out a hand to take them - but Cerul waves us to come forward…so the group advances to the giants.  None of the rest of the group understand “giant” so it sounds like gibberish.  They tell Fungus and Cerul - no one else can come or go until their friend is fixed.  They lead the group to the Abbey entrance.
Everything feels normal at first…as the group gets to the front gate. Cerul mentions three areas:  the orphanage - which is a large wing of the Abbey where children play. He remembers the transition of the Abbey of Mont Rest to the Abbey of Light.  He knows of the Rising Hearthlight (the tavern/inn).  He also tells the group of the Order of the Lighted Breath. This was a group who was into meditation, stepping into other areas of enlightenment.  Cerul thinks Kaizo would like to be here.  He remembers Brother Freya was a part of this group. 
Four Orders:  Monks of Lighted Breath, Nurturing Light (children, Matron Elbrade Mallow), Reflective Light (scholars, diplomats; led by ??); Protective Light (guardian order; led by Warden); Rising Light (religious group - transition of all pantheons to one Order of Light - led by Luminary).  Cerul studies the maps and thinks the Rel’Udomina is probably hanging out below ground, but we don’t know what all they have done here.  
At the Rising Hearthlight
The group heads to the Hearthlight. It feels like a dorm. The bottom is the dining hall area and there are stairs that lead to large communal rooms for sleeping.  No one is there to greet the group formally.  Cerul knows that they used to serve 3 meals a day. Today they are preparing vegetarian broth w/egg and onion.  When we walk in the hearth catches the eye, and we see a board there with a parchment.  We notice some people putting food at places on tables…setting it out for people to come and sit and eat,  Ridley and Relys walk up to inspect the parchment on the board.  Most of the requests are old and deal with trades, etc.  But one notice catches the eye (which is probably 3-4 stretches old) 
– NOTICE TO TRAVELERS AND PILGRIMS –
A cave entrance has been discovered approximately one mile west of the Abbey, beyond the old shepherd’s path that skirts the ridge.
Those who’ve entered have not returned.
The Protective Light advises against casual exploration. Entry is not sanctioned and currently considered a risk to public safety.
If you possess experience in navigating magically warded ruins or suppressed anomalies, report to Warden Valegar for clearance.
Let wisdom guide your steps, and silence guard your tongue.
— By Order of the Rising Light
	Cerul walks up to talk to someone who is putting food out.  He does not recognize them. He asks about Brother Freya - do you know where he is?  He says that Cerul needs to ask someone else.  She offers to bring someone older out to help him.  Cerul thinks the person is familiar, but doesn’t know their name.  They converse.  The man tells Cerul that he might want to ask the Matron.  He recognizes Cerul.  He thinks that she will know where Brother Freya went…he says he left.  Cerul asks about accommodations and he points to some rooms.  Then asks how we got in and Cerul explains.  He tells him about how one of them was turned to stone (how ironic…not funny at all).  Cerul says he is friends with the stone giants.  Affy asks if he knows anyone with the Rel’Udomina tattoo (shows him her arm).  He is tight lipped.
The man offers the group some broth and we all decide to eat. As we are sitting there, a lady nudges in and sits with the group, making herself at home.  “You don’t know me, right?”  I have a sense about you.  I’m Sister Elleran of the Reflective Light (speaking quietly).  She overheard us talking about how we got inside.  She says she can’t remain still…even though everyone else is playing along.  She says there are things going on underneath the Abbey.  We think she would know about this stuff.  She says something is going on.  Strangers have been down under the Abbey and we have been warned that if we speak, the children will suffer.  The others are all scared - and have been bound to silence.  That’s why I came over - it begins with you.  And she picks up the bowl and walks away.
– The End of the Session –
