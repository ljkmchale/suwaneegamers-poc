# Session 04 - In O’naren

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  March 3rd, 2025
In-game Date:  25th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  The Pale Lantern, Qal’dynn
In Qal’dynn
Continue at guard tower.  Guards are called Realmwardens. We ask them about the Inn…he points to a terrace. Ham goes to the Stables to board the horses and see if they also have riding horses for sale, too. Affy, Fungus, and Kaizo walk toward the terrace where the Inn is…in search of Ro and drinks.
Ham speaks to staff at the stable (female (Maynari) and male (Adris - he’s the ferrier)):  talks about origins of horses and needs (new shoes).  He pays 1gp per horse per night for stabling; 2sp for new shoes.  Talks about a trade of 2 draft horses for 1 riding horse, maybe purchasing another.  After talking, Ham talks to a new riding horse (“Jefferson”) and pulls out his magical stone and the essence of the horse is into the stone (figurine of wondrous power - treat like a Nightmare).  He tests the figurine and dismisses the horse and it transforms back into the stone, which now looks like a horse.  Stable lady says, “You got that from the Oracle” and starts to walk away.  
As we walk around we see that the buildings are community housing with shops on the lower level.  Kaizo notices a butcher nearby (Fungus goes to buy sausages-a gp worth - inquires about an alchemy shop and was told to go to Qual’dynn), a weaver (baskets), book shop, bath house...we head up to the Temple.  Kaizo looks around the temple, sees floral carvings (Fralee is primary one worshipped here).  Two elves in this area look eerily similar to the elves at the Stables.  We pass through and go out to the terrace, walk over to the tavern and walk in.  
Truffle Trap Tavern stone/wood building, has a 4-story tower with great views.  Ro is there and spots a half-orc on the tower’s second level…notices he’s been there a while and is loose-lipped (“made some good coin, no loose ends, brat wasn’t going to make it”).  Ro spots us coming and meets us on the lower level with Jasper, the wolf pup.  The group clues her in on what has happened to us - finding the cube on the lower level of the Broken Citadel, trying to save the mother and son and killing their pursuers.  
Kool’ok is a half-orc that Ro overheard…Affy approaches to ask about what he said.  He denies knowing anything about a child being killed, etc.  Ham approaches. Mentions Vrathin (and we know he is scared)...he gets up to leave.  Kaizo cuts him off…asks about herbs (is told to find the Nightbud).  Ham gets up to follow.  Kaizo and Ham are both trying to be stealthy.  Kool’ok notices them both and heads for the woods.  Ham continues to follow.  Kool’ok is disturbed and asks Ham to leave him alone. He walks to a realmwarden, but the guy shrugs him off.  Kool’ok heads to the stables and Ham follows (he messages Kaizo to gather all of us).  Ham messages Maynari to please delay that guy and she does (he messages again that it isn’t because he is a half orc, but because he needs his friends to come).  He says Vrathin is the “mapmaker” in town.  Talk commences.  He says he is a middleman. Vrathin paid him to do a job.  Ham messages a realmwarden.  One comes over and Ham fills him in.  Kool’ok tells the guard to take him in. They go and head into town.  Ham asks Maynari about Vrathin - she confirms he is a mapmaker.  She says she hasn’t seen his wife and son. Maynari says Vrathin has been in town a long time.  That he adopted the child from the Abbey (south of here).  Affy knows the Abbey of Montrest is home to one of the largest orphanages in the area.  Rumor is the Abbey is protected by stone giants.  It is now the Abbey of Light.  
We talk among each other as we walk to the mapmaker.  We see the guards take Kool’ok to the Town Hall.  We discuss that the child might have known something and that his dad (or parents) wanted him killed.  Kaizo says we need to go to the Abbey.  We also want to talk to Vrathin.
As we walk around we see shops of all types.  Fungus goes shopping for food.  Kaizo goes to herbalist.  Affy wants to go to the mapmaker.  Ro hangs with Fungus.  Ham hangs with Affy.
Kaizo shops for herbs, Ro and Fungus there also.  Meledra introduces herself.  They talk.  Fungus and Kaizo observe jars of powder similar to what Ro found. Ro shows her the jar she has and asks if she can identify it.  Meledra holds it up and shakes it (it lights up a little, seems to react to movement); she is uneasy.  She tells Ro it is alchemical, not an herb.  It reminds me of “an essence powder,” that is believed to anchor souls (suspending people between worlds).  Ro asks if she knows of anyone who might know more.  She says her cousin Iofyr (her male cousin who is “different” and has been into alchemy for a long time)...he is in Qual’dyn.  There is usually an ask if you want knowledge from him (quid pro quo).  She tells them about how he asked his mother to visit the silent circle for “nightbud.”  Kaizo asks her about nightbud and she tells him that the gnolls have made gathering that problematic.  She tells them that there is a path behind the tavern and inn that leads to the silent circle that is overseen by druids…but that gnolls have overtaken it. Kaizo offers a trade of nightbud for an herb from Ferndale.  She says to talk to the satyr (who must be the druid)...she has no nightbud.  
Meanwhile…Ham and Affy head to the mapmaker.  Ham pulls out the parchment.  Tells him that Kool’ok approached us about taking care of the job.  He tells us that he hired people to “bring his wife and kid in,” that she is “part of the Cloak” and is doing bad things.  He said that the Lord (Lord Etham) would like to talk to them.  Ham asks for payment and he gives us a bag of coins (10gp).  He wants them brought back alive. We think from what he says that the leader probably wants him dispatched.  Kaizo continues to watch Vrathin close up and leave his shop - looks like he headed to his apartment above the shop. We think there must be another ally in town who would have warned them about the route being compromised.  
Affy and Ro know the Cloak of Defiance is a thieves guild order that was sitting in pockets before the Awakening - in conflict with the Malfease.  It eventually overthrew the Malfease and is now the primary black market/underground group in Myrdae. 
We talk about going to the Inn to get rooms.  Plan to get up early and head to the silent circle to help the town with the gnoll problem.  We go to the Nestling Sword Inn.  Main house is nice; building to the left is better.  Proprietor comes up to help.  She is a little “racist” and Affy plays like she and Ham are a couple.  We all get rooms in the modest section (Affy and Ro; Kaizo and Ham; Fungus on his own; Ireron and Cerul).  But Fungus gets a nicer room in the modest section.  Ro smuggles Jasper into the room.  
O’naren - 26th of Talil
After a long rest and good night’s sleep, the group heads up to the silent circle before sunrise.  We see a satyr walking around “cleaning up” the area, tending to soil near various stones.  We see paw prints in the soil.  She asks us to help with the cleanup and tells us the gnolls took the nightbud last night.  We offer to help take out the gnolls…she tells us that 5-6 come each night.  She knows there are at least 7.  The leader has a bone whip.   She says this is a circle to worship Fralee.  This druid is named Kasani.   We ask her about the nighbud growing patterns and its use (to relax and meditate).  We decide to go back to town and then come back later that night to arrive around 10PM - stake out the place and prepare for the gnolls to come back.  Before we go, Ham asks Kasani about the Cloak of Defiance…she doesn’t know much about it.  Kaizo talks to her about “spreading the influence of the nightbud” around the world.  She says she will consider it, but let’s solve one problem at a time.
Group goes back to town, arrives at lunch.  Fungus and Ro go to jeweler.  They hear that the Director runs the mines. That the workers here are oremasters.  Everything appears normal.  Ro investigates a little - looking to see what is being mined.  They see a triad of buildings, connected to each other, evidence of a forge.  See an armorsmith, weaponsmith, and jeweler in these buildings.  All are elves and appear to be kin - have a unique look, not seen on others in town (very striking, jet black hair with silver eyes).  Fungus goes to the jeweler…he is trying to avoid making eye contact.  They notice a source of mithral might be there.  Ro asks if that is mined here and told yes.  The chain vest they see if worth 500gp.  They find out that this is the “Tooasaurs” family (very notable family in smithing and jewelcraft).  Fungus brings out the cube and shows it to them.  They don’t have any idea about it.  He wants to use it and tries to get the riches.  Ro stands next to him.  He is not trying to avoid anything. The armorsmith is next to him.  Fungus warns him that not everything is good…and the armorsmith decides to stand away from him.  They take the cube outside.  The weaponsmith decides to stand 15’ away with one of her shields.  The jeweler hands Fungus 3 stones to hold and stands 20’ back.  The armorer stands 15’ away next to his sister.  Fungus manipulates the cube [rolls an 80-enhanced; his two lowest stats (which were both an 8) increased by 1 point each] Fungus glows and starts to twitch…he is enhanced.  We don’t observe anything other than twitching. Ro watched the manipulation of the cube (might have a chance to repeat it since she was up close). The group around doesn’t observe anything. He then asks the jeweler if he can buy a pearl and is looking for a dog collar (advised to go to the leather worker).  He buys a 100gp pearl.
Affy, Kaizo, and Ham (meanwhile) go to the Town Hall.  We ask about the Lord and he is “away on business.”  We then ask if they know about the gnoll problem and they are aware.  We offer to help with it and they offer us 50gp to rid the town of the problem.  We ask who is the leader in the office right now…and are told Captain Salal is here, but currently entertaining a “diplomat” right now.  We see two elves down the hall having a discussion. Ham messages him…and is told to make an appointment with Caddius. He continues and presses him. The Captain invites us to join he and his companion for lunch (this is someone from Caelora…they are talking about problems in the Borelian Sway).  We start to tell him everything.  When Ham reveals he is related to the Anders, he stops us and says “This isn’t a Cloak problem, this is Rel’Udomina.”
– The End of the Session –
