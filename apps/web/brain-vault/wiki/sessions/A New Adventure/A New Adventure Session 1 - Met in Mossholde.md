# Session 01 - Met in Mossholde

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  January 1st, 2025
In-game Date:  18th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Mossholde
Mossholde - Talil 18, 1246
The campaign begins in Mossholde - a hunting and trading outpost (one building - multi-story) south of Mount Everbloom in the Borealian Sway territory.  The individuals there that night were:
* Az’efal “Affy” Fairhand,  a female half-orc fighter,
* Cerul Slate, a male aasimar cleric, 
* Kaizo, an old male elf monk, 
* Fungus Roundbelly, a hill giant goliath warlock, 
* Hamalex “Ham” Milton of The Andërs, a male high elf bard, and 
* Aerowen “Ro” Umberwood, a female sorceress
Everyone met at the outpost and Introductions were made, each sharing a bit about themself.  Ro’s dad was rendered ill from a powder that was thrown on him by a robed figure.  She is not sure who is behind the attack.
Marlowe, man in leathers, with a dagger, ran the outpost.  He had a half-orc female (messy, not attractive - does hard labor - ferrier) named Harva that assisted him.  Affy talked to Harva about her origins - she doesn’t really know.  Marlowe is her uncle and took her in.  She tells Affy that she would be more welcomed to the east, south, maybe in Inverland.
Marlowe makes stew - we figure out where to sleep.  Ro asks Affy to bunk together - and she agrees.  Fungus sleeps by the fire; Kaizo meditates by the fire.  Ham and Cerul take the single bed room (Ham said he would stand).  Fungus keeps trying to talk to Kaizo as he meditates - Kaizo does not break.  Fungus tries to go through K’s pack and he gets whacked out of nowhere by K.  Fungus sings all night…just because he is jolly.
Interruption at Night
Early in the morning (middle of the night) - we are jolted awake/out of trance.  We step into the courtyard and see Harva open the doors…figure stumbles in.  A bloody elf with an arrow embedded who calls out for help.  Ro goes to his aid (healing); Affy runs to the door to see if she sees anything.  Fungus gives him a hand up.  He says his group was ambushed by small blue skinned people in the valley.  Marlo thinks he must be hallucinating.  Cerul and Kaizo aren’t sure who they might have been.  Questions the man.  He says they killed a wolf…and they were looking at helping the cubs when they were ambushed.  These beings disappeared and then showed up and shot them.  He commented that they were careful not to go on the “old Citadel” lands…ruined during the Awakening…but maybe they people have no connection.  His friends (2 others) were killed.  Taberry Elich (elf) says he was ½ day away in the valley to the south.  
Kaizo checks out the arrow.  Seems more like a short bow arrow.  Fits the story of being attacked by small beings. Group decides to leave now - hoping to arrive by dawn.
Fungus and Ro get a lantern.  The group leaves.  Kaizo takes off in the front.  Affy has to correct his direction.  As we go, we hear the forest/wildlife waking up.  After 4 hours we find evidence of the fight, but do not see the bodies (just belongings).  Fungus grabs the weapons (2 long bows and 2 short swords), Ham and Cerul can’t track the cubs.  We are in a clearing and the ground is damp. We see an area off to the side that seems trampled…and see a pile of flesh sitting there (looks like it was cut off the bones and discarded, no bones).  Affy notices small canine footprints and humanoid footprints…and well as blood drops and a clear trail.  Fungus and Affy talk about the barbary of the actions (nothing like she has seen, even among orcs).  Kaizo starts to lead on the new trail.  Fungus sniffs his bottles and feels like we are close.  We see a ruined building across the clearing and the mountains in the distance.  The group decides to be sneaky…if they can. 
The Broken Citadel
Fungus and Kaizo lead the group, sneaking.  Kaizo is humming the song Fungus sang the night before.  Cerul is stealthy behind them.  Ro, Affy and Ham are 20-30’ back…also trying to be quiet, but doing a bad job.
Fungus and Kaizo start to approach on the right side, where the walls have crumbled and there is an opening in the corner.  They slow up to wait for the party.  We all catch up.  No one is able to be quiet from this vantage point.  Fungus catches a whiff of something cooking.  Fungus & Kaizo start to hear some conversation that they can’t understand behind a wall.  Affy understands what is being spoken…she knows it is goblin-ish, but not the dialect she knows.  She picks up 3 different voices.  Ham says he is going to go over alone to the point where he can see them to try to draw one out to the group.  He sees these warty, blue creatures.  He uses his whistle to try to lure one out (“approach”) with success 
IT’S INITIATIVE: Combat vs. Gremlins.  Ro decides to see what happens. As Fungus goes to hit him, he misses and the guy disappears and reappears next to where Kaizo was holding his action, waiting to see if his companions emerged from the ruins.  He attacks Kaizo and Kaizo reacts and kills him.  Affy runs to the entrance and yells “Surrender now and you’ll live.  You are surrounded.” in goblin.  Cerul runs up and casts firebolt - that misses (and the guy disappears).  Ro runs up and has no line of sight so holds her action.  Ham runs up - no line of sight.  Kaizo runs into the ruins behind full cover…saw them as he entered.  A figure emerges from the inner walls and speaks in an unrecognizable language.  He is in fancy clothes and holds a skull and he ducks behind a wall.  A blue guy shoots an arrow at Ham - misses.  Affy is hit by an arrow.  Runs in and sees a few and takes cover since they are out of range.  Cerul runs in and firebolts one and kills it. Ro runs in with a firebolt and hits one under partial cover and damages it. Ham runs in and tries to hit one with a quill.  Fight continues. One takes Ro down. Affy takes that one out.  Cerul heals Ro.  Ro takes one out.  Kaizo, Ham, and Cerul go after the “fancy” guy.  Fungus goes after the blue guy who ducks behind the wall. Kaizo misses - face to face with fancy man.  Man misses K with skull.  Affy gets the first crit of the campaign and kills the fancy man with the skull.  Cerul gives chase to the remaining blue guy and misses. Ro tries to hit with a fire bolt and misses.  Ham runs in with a quill and true strike and hits and takes him down. 
Kaizo thanks Affy and she steps forward and looks like she might embrace her…but she nods her head and a little smile cracks.  We notice they were dining on wolf.  We see one wolf pup in a cage.  We see cots, a bloated bladder with bumps on it.  Ham picks up with bladder and it feels like it has coins.  He pierces it and 12gp fall out.  Kaizo loots the fancy man.  He has a coin purse (24gp and 60sp) and has a magical ring.  Fungus goes upstairs to check and finds a room with stacks of bones of various sizes that have been organized.  Barrels and crates with supplies.  Alchemy kit, Poisoner’s Kit, other supplies.  Bones in the center of the room surround a trap door.  Other items in the room look like they were stolen goods from travelers.  Other room is a chamber with recently repaired ceiling.  Canopy bed.  Recently framed paintings and a wardrobe.  Next to the bed is a pedestal with a small jar with an unknown substance in it (powdery substance that looks familiar to Ro).  and a flask that has a liquid in it (not a healing potion - looks like poison).  Clothes here are very nice, some costumes.  Blue guys seem to be gremlins upon inspection - there is a fey connection here.  Ro investigates the chambers thoroughly - trying to figure out who this guy was.  She doesn’t find anything that reveals who he is. 
Each get 6gp and 10sp.  Kaizo has Ham inspect the magical ring - Fungus agrees it is magical (we don’t know what it does). Fungus says he will hold and he and Cerul will identify it later. Fungus puts the poison flask in his pouch.  Ro takes the jar of powdery substance.  We free the wolf pup and Ro plans to keep it, if possible.
The session ends with the party at the trap door.
– The End of the Session –
