# Session 19 - Abbey Undercroft

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  November 6, 2025
In-game Date:  44th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Abbey of Light
Day 4/8 of rest for Feywoven Glyphic
It is noon.  Fungus and the halfling are scouting just outside of the Abbey. They see someone coming down the path….it is Hamalex. He has a message from Loriem and has sending stones that he was given and told to take them to the arcane waypoint when it is restored.   Fungus decides to escort Ham back into the Abbey.  
They enter and the Warden tells them that the rest of the group is underneath his quarters and just went there.  Fungus vouches for Ham.  They are told the group is in the dungeons.  They are led to the door and let in…and locked back in.  They find the group, having just killed the Rel'Udomina.  Ham tells us about the stones and message from Loriem.  Kaizo tells the group that he and Nali will go above ground and let people know what happened down here.  Ham makes a copy of the ledger for him to take up and we tell him to avoid the Parson for now. 
Group takes a short rest and discuss what we know. Affy reveals that her dad is the one mentioned in the letter.  Ridley mentions that Rueh’unar Heildove is the one he is hunting (contract on his head) - is this Archon a relation of his contracted kill?  
We discuss options.  Open the secret passage, find/out the Rel'Udomina on the ledger and others, out the Parson (Ridley’s dinner), find the arcane waypoint and reopen it. Affy goes to check out the secret door - it is not locked.  Affy opens the door. The passage leads straight and then turns…and forms a Y.  She thinks they are going near the RH (#10).  Relys scouts and sees this is a well-used passage.  She sees more traffic going between the two ways.  Fungus closes the door and looks back and it looks like a dead end (so this would have been a secret door).  
Relys sends Hopper ahead to the right to scout.  After 10’ a large square room opens up and there are 6 people there at a table? There is an old guy in a robe who is yapping… they are waiting for the minister. There is a door, opposite the hall coming, it's clearly closed. 
The group discusses sending Ridley to go talk with them… Fungus calls up his girlie who is a mean bugger but she likes us, he hasn’t called her before.. It must've been a butt dial as he claims he wasn't expecting her. Affy has memory loss as clearly Relys had mentioned the plan before and Affy asks the group what to do again and if Ridley (who didn't see the group cause it was Relys’s little pet) if he had recognized them. Big-Toe-Joe wants to charm them. Affy wants Ridley to take a letter and read it to them. They discuss the elderly for some time. The man who is old is not Ridley's date. Ridley is joined by Relys - who is a frog.
Ridley walks in as the Jackal.  They don’t recognize him.  He tells them he is there to read a letter on behalf of the Minister.  He reads it. They ask him what they are supposed to do. He tells them they are supposed to get rid of the meddlesome party.  They aren’t buying this and want to go and talk to the minister.  They walk around the corner and see the party.  “There is the fat one.”  It is initiative.
* ROUND ONE:  Relys casts a spell on 3 of the people (erupting earth - dex ST - they fail).  They take 20pts damage, difficult terrain. Ham casts a spell on 3 of the people (shatter - they fail) and they take 10 damage. Fungus jumps in the middle of one of the packs and they have to make saving throws.  All three fail and take 11 damage. Fungus then does a burst like thingy (cantrip), and snipes with the spell point blank, hits, taking 11 more damage and dies dead. His girlfriend now goes, and like a stalker, teleports following Fungus, and attacks another guy, hits, and kills the guy. Affy, not caring that people died all around her, attempts to intimidate, and makes one dude drop his weapon in fear… is probably yielding. She calls Ridley out (actually calls him the Jackal and nods…maybe she’s Rel'Udomina…. even though the party around her is trying to kill people and made one drop their weapons in fear).  Ridley attacks the guys behind him and fumbles, but then hits, hunter’s mark and shoots, hitting again (nick). Cerul runs in the room and attacks, crits, killing the monk. One of the remaining Rel'Udomina calls forth a spiritual weapon and attacks the Jackal and misses. Then steps forward to hit him with a weapon and does, but Affy deflects some of the damage. The guy Affy intimidated yielded.  And the last guys steps forward and casts a spell and Ridley, Relys, and Affy fail their saves, taking damage.
* ROUND TWO:  Relys attacks one of the last guy, hitting with poison damage. Ham moves into the room and truestrikes one of his quills and misses. Fungus runs in the room and poison sprays the old man and hits, killing him. Affy runs forward to attack the last one and kills him with two hits. 
We are out of combat and want to question the guy who yielded.  This is Hagley.  We ask who the other people are - he gives names, but none are the people on the list.  He says the people on the list are all upstairs because it is daylight - and they are normal members of the Abbey.  He says he was recruited and came in from the outside.  He says they guy who turned the giant to stone left the Abbey and headed to Qal’dynn (the group killed him). 
We ask about the Looms - the first look is in Halesworth.  The second loom is Borelian Sway.  The third loom is another territory.  He doesn’t know much more.  He says the Archon is the head of each loom.  Affy and Ridley decide to take him to the jail cell and lock him up and tell the Warden.  The others loot the room, bodies.  
Loot - leather armor, healing potion.  Chain mail shirt, healing potion, mace. Monk clothes from the LB order.  Three hand crossbows, three sets of thieves' tools, and three short swords. A holy symbol of Tornia.  17gp, 29sp.  3 quivers of bolts. 
We put the bodies by the door to the hall and first go to check out the loot room. 
EVERYONE TAKES 8gp and 16sp. {Kaizo has added}
Group goes down the hall and it ends in a door, very tight fitting.  Affy checks to see if it is locked. It has a little grip. Affy (with RIdley’s help) doesn’t think it is trapped. She reaches to open it. The room is bigger than any room we have seen down here. There are doors on far walls, room it lit with magical light (dim, violet). We see a floor etched with intricate runes, and her attention is grabbed by…INITIATIVE  [Monks of the Lighted Breath and wraiths]
* ROUND ONE:  Ham casts a spell on a monk and misses.  Some of the monks take cover, one runs up and attacks Affy and another runs up to attack Ridley.  He hits Affy and stuns her; the other one hits Ridley.  Ridley crits the one who hit him, misses, then shoots with his crossbow and misses. Cerul casts a spell and they have to make wisdom saves (one fails, one succeeds). He then produces his wings. Relys casts a spell on a wraith (moonbeam).  The wraith flew into the hall and takes damage from Cerul, his spirit guardian, and Ham (opportunity attack). She grabs Relys and hits hard. The other wraith moves into the hall and takes damage from the spiritual guardian. She attacks Cerul, but misses.  The last wraith moves closer.  Fungus casts blink and casts a spell and tells the pixie to cast misty step and attack a monk and hits. 
* ROUND TWO:  Ham attacks a wraith with a dagger and hits and uses bardic inspiration on Affy and then fumbles and loses a reaction. The monks attack Ridley and hit once and miss once. One attacks the pixie and misses.  Another one attacks Affy twice and misses.  Another one steps up and takes damage from the spiritual guardian and attacks the pixie and hits once. Affy steps up and misses, then hits one, the cleaves another. Then RIdley attacks 3 times, hits once. The moonbeam gets moved to hit 1-4 things. She hits one with it, he fails his save, taking 18 damage, killing one. Cerul pops off hitting a bunch of people, lots of fails, hard to keep track of. Wraith moves past Relys (misses opp attack) and attacks Cerul and misses. The other wraith in the hall attacks Cerul and hits. The last wraith attacks Ridley and misses. The wraiths take damage from Cerul at the end of their turn and one dies (and the arcane runes on the floor flicker). Fungus poison sprays a wraith and hits and realizes it didn’t hurt it then misty steps ahead and heals his pixie and he does not blink.  Pixie misty step and moves to attack a monk and crits. 
* ROUND THREE:  Ham attacks a wraith and hits with a magical dagger and gives a bardic inspiration to the Jackal, then unarm strikes the wraith and hits.  A monk runs up and throws daggers at Affy and misses.  One attacks the pixie and hits once, then misses with flurry of blows.  Another one attacks Affy and misses again.  Two attack Fungus - one critically and one fumbling (hitting his ally).  The crit was hard, but Fungus saved it from being dire. Affy attacked the one on her and killed it, then attacked another and hit, but the cleave did not hit.  Ridley attacks the wraith and hits. Then shoots with his crossbow. Relys moves the moonbeam to attack monks and a wraith. Cerul casts his big spell and 2 wraiths and a monk take damage. The wraiths attack Cerul and Ridley and miss and then one moves away, taking opp attack from Ham and Relys. Fungus moves to a wraith and attacks and hits and kills it.  The runes in the center of the room start to flicker again…and the blackness in the monk’s eyes start to return. The pixie attacks the monk Fungus is on and hits.
* ROUND FOUR:  Ham attacks by throwing a magical dart at the wraith and misses. He inspires Fungus and then punches the wraith in the foot and hits.  A monk attacks Fungus and hits once, then crits with a furry of blows. Affy attacks a wraith and fumbles, but then hits.  Ridley attacks the wraith and hits three times.  Cerul moves up and imposes damage  on the wraith, which kills it.  The runes light up on the floor…and an arcane waypoint lights up.  When this happens, the monks come out of their possession. Cerul yells drop your weapons and they do. They converse…Cerul tells them they were fighting with the wraiths and they have no idea.  One of the monks yells - where is the Parson?  As this happens, Ham pulls out the sending stones. A guy steps through the door and says “What have you done - this was for a reason - now you will die?”  And we end here.  Fungus blinks. 
Ridley shot 4 bolts in this fight. 
The DM will start with a big speech next time.
