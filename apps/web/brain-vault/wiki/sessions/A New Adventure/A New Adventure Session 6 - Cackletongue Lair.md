# Session 06 - Cackletongue Lair

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  April 17th, 2025
In-game Date:  27th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Crackletongue Lair
Cackletongue Lair - 27th of Talil
We had a long rest in the purified Silent Circle.  We go to the gnoll cave - jagged maw is partially concealed behind tangled moss and twisted limbs of gnarled trees. The earth has a damp glow. When we arrived we heard a high pitched voice inside the cave saying “What’s that stench? Nasty angel blood!” We know that gnolls have some fiendish properties, possibly put on the material plane by a Demon Lord. We are unaware as a party that anyone has celestial blood.
The opening is 15-20 ft wide. At the top it’s about 10 ft wide. Kaizo goes into scout and sees the corridor turns 90 degrees to the right. It’s damp and there is a stench of decay. The walls are rough stone slick with moisture.  There are fragments of bones around. As he steps in, Kaizo notices light hues of violet and yellow bioluminescence on the walls. Kaizo comes out and explains what he saw.
We go back in as a group, with Kaizo scouting 30 ft ahead of the party. Kaizo sees the tunnel opens up into a foul-smelling chamber with a low ceiling. The room is wide, about 40 ft across and oblong shaped. The room is cluttered with bones, cracked bowls, gnawed wooden plans, and makeshift bedding. There is the earthy smell of clusters of mushrooms mixed with the stench of old blood and rot. Kaizo hears a high-pitched snicker from the shadows. He thinks he sees a sparkle in the debris.
Kaiso casts a spectral dancing fairy (Druidcraft) 30 ft away. He notices that where he saw the flicker, some of the larger of the mushrooms start to pulse and spores come up. He comes back to the party. Ham feels like the fungus is not natural - the fungus brought the gnolls back to life before, and these are bigger. Ro and Cerul walk back with Kaizo to try to burn the mushrooms. As Ro looks to the right, she sees the glitter in the debris and it appears to be fairly large. Cerul thinks it looks like a silver pole or cylinder. Ro fires a firebolt and takes out a mushroom. One of the mushrooms on each side starts to move closer to us. It looks like the whole room starts to shift. Next round they will be on top of us. Kaizo throws a dagger at a mushroom and it falls. Kaizo recommends to the party that we should take them out at a distance. Ham decides to try to dash across the cavern and makes it to the other side where it opens up and bends to the left. One mushroom takes an opportunity attack on him and Ham takes poison damage. A few spores sink into him and he hears “Ezulgarutt will get you” telepathically in his head. Cerul firebolts a mushroom and it explodes. Affy takes out her longbow and obliterates a mushroom. Ro takes out another. There are about a dozen left.
Fluttering is starting to come through the air. We hear “Ezulgarutt” echoing over and over and have to make a bonus action to make a wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on the attack. Kaizo fails the wisdom save but makes his attack, slicing the mushroom with his sword. He keeps running to the end of the cavern. Ham sees a rock land near his feet that came from the end of the tunnel.  He picks up the rock but doesn’t see anything distinctive about it.  He turns and fires a dart at the mushroom behind him and takes it out. There are 10 mushrooms left. Cerul fails his wisdom save, and runs out between them and burst Radiance of the Dawn in a 30 ft radius and took out all the remaining mushrooms. The telepathic voice is gone.
The silver object shines brightly in the radiant light from Cerul. It appears to be something covered up but Cerul sees a small flickering of green. Cerul lights up his shield. Affy nudges the silver thing with her foot - it might have been one of their weapons. It’s a huge silver club with a jade gemstone on the end. 
Ham and Fungus continue forward and the ground shifts beneath their feet.  They fall into a 15x15 ft pit with a large formless mass that begins to rise. They take bludgeoning, slashing, and piercing damage from their fall. Affy heard them yell and hastens forward to help her friends. The ooze creeps up, moving very slowly until it’s about 5 ft away.  Its greyish form extends upward and the pseudopod form turns into a brain shape and looks towards Ham. He takes 16 pts of psychic damage and goes down. Fungus misty steps out and tries to take Ham out, but can’t misty step with him. He gives 10 temp hp to Ham.
Ham stands up, his head throbbing. He sees a grey ooze reaching toward him. Ham casts mirror image. The side of the pit is 20 ft on an angle. The rest of the party dashes to try to catch up. Affy throws a trident down into the ooze and hits. The trident falls out. The ooze, unaffected by mirror image, swings its pseudopod towards Ham and misses.  Fungus casts Sorceror’s Burst at the ooze and causes psychic damage. Ham casts Command and tells the ooze to flee. Cerul throws a rope down over the edge. Kaizo throws his last dagger at the ooze and misses. Ro creates a ledge halfway up the wall. Affy fires her longbow at the ooze and hits, causing Slow. On his turn, the ooze flees 10 ft away towards the back of the pit. Fungus casts Sorceror’s Burst and causes some fire damage. Ham tries to climb the wall using the ledge, and then uses the rope to get to the top. Cerul casts Toll the Dead on the ooze. Kaizo holds his action in case the ooze comes up. Ro tries to use firebolt and it appears to be resistant. Affy fires her bow again but rolls a nat 1. The brain forms as it moves 10 ft towards the party. It attacks Ro with its brain but fails. Fungus casts thunder damage on the ooze. Cerul Tolls the Dead again and the ooze is whittled down to a much smaller ooze. Ham fires and misses and retreats from the edge. Ro’s Toll the Dead takes it out. Affy jumps down to retrieve her trident. She looks around and finds 3 gemstones: azurite, blue quartz, and turquoise (10 gp each).
Fungus picks up the silver club (the jade gemstone is worth 150 gp). We hear laughter from further down the corridor. We skirt the lip of the pit and continue down the corridor, with Kaizo scouting ahead. He gets a whiff of smoke down the corridor to the left.  To the right, he sees a room with a stale feeling - the scent has more of rusty, coppery smell. The ceiling is low, and the room is filled with torn furs and scraps.  In the far corner there is a hole and the edges seem fairly fresh. He catches the glint of a wiry antenna sticking up - a twitching form the size of a dog, but much fatter and rounder.  It appears to be on top of a shield gnawing on it. Kaizo points to the hole and casts Druidcraft in the hole, creating the faint odor of a skunk. He sees the antenna pop up and look around and then turns back to gnaw on the shield. It looks like a gold bug eating a shield. Ro has read of a creature called a “rust monster” that feeds on metal. 
Kaizo stealths and continues down the corridor.  Stairs descend into a huge chamber but the hallway continues to circle around.  In the chamber the ground is uneven, littered with stone, rotting plant matter, mold, and bioluminescent fungus.  In the center is a makeshift throne of giant rocks, with a gnoll sitting.  His fur is matted, his eyes are glassy, and he is puffing on a hookah.  Blue smoke swirls through the air.  Kaizo notices he is hovering slightly above his throne.  There are other gnolls in the room and they are sitting in a stupor. Kaizo returns to the group to describe what he saw. He thinks there were 3-5 gnolls, but it was hard to tell in the thick smoke.
Kaizo hears a voice saying “Come on, Nazex wants you. Go take it! Go take it!” The voice is coming from the room. Affy moves forward and fires her bow but misses. Kaizo uses Druidcraft to snuff out the hookah flame. Ham casts Command on chief (gnoll pack leader) and another gnoll, so they halt. The other two gnolls shoot with their bow at Kaizo, who uses a reaction to deflect the attack, minimizing the damage. The other gnoll shoots at Ham, causing piercing damage. Ro shoots a firebolt at a gnoll causing minor damage. Cerul uses Toll the Dead on the same gnoll, but he saves. Cerul casts Spiritual Weapon and drops it on the gnoll’s head, but it misses. Affy enters the cavern and strikes with her greatsword but rolls a nat 1. She uses Action Surge and takes an additional attack and hits with the sword, adding a psionic blast. She notices that there is a gnoll laying on its side. Kaizo jumps in to attack the same gnoll as Affy. He swings his short sword and hits. With his offhand he strikes but misses. He uses Flurry of Blows to cause more damage and the gnoll falls. Fungus runs into the cavern and casts Hex on the chief and he has disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws. Ham casts Command on the chief (AKA “hookah boy”) and he will stay there. A gnoll attacks Fungus and pierces him with his claw, but he makes his concentration save. A gnoll fires at Kaizo, and another swings at Affy. Ro hits the chief with Toll the Dead. Cerul hits the chief with his Spiritual Weapon and Tolls the Dead on another gnoll. A small creature, a quasit, joins the fight. A pulse of the fungi releases necrotic energy. Affy, Ham, and Cerul fail the con saving throw, but Cerul has resistance so doesn’t take damage. The quasit strikes at Fungus. Affy attacks and Action Surges. Kaizo attacks with his short sword and unarmed strike. He uses Flurry of Blows. Ham Commands the Chief again to Halt. Fungus attacked the quasit. The quasit returns the attack but misses. A gnoll fumbles his attack and the other shoots at Fungus and misses. Ro hits the chief with Magic Missile. Cerul attacks the chief and another gnoll with Toll the Dead. 
The room fungus pulses again and the party takes necrotic damage. Quasit poofs across the room. Affy targets another gnoll but misses. Kaizo takes down a gnoll with his bare fists. Fungus smacks the chief. Ham takes down the chief and his hookah explodes. Gnoll moves and Affy gets opportunity attack but misses. He shoots Ham. Ro heals Ham. Cerul starts to glow and sprouts wings from his back (huh, so that’s what kind of feather it is). Cerul bursts Radiance and two gnolls fail and both go down, and quasit takes damage. The room fungus starts to pulse but dies down with the pack leader gone. Quasit disappears with a snicker. Fungus casts Faerie Fire but nothing is illuminated. Cerul casts See Invisibility and sends his Spiritual Weapon after the quasit and takes him down. 
The pack leader was Gnazex - he has a ring with a garnet gemstone that looks valuable. He has a small pouch with 2 buds of nightbud in it - Kaizo takes both.. He also has a potion of healing on him - Ham puts it in inventory. Fungus identifies the ring as a ring of fire resistance.
– The End of the Session –
