# Session 13 - Qal’dynn Mysteries

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  August 7th, 2025
In-game Date:  34th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  The Pale Lantern, Qal’dynn 
Ham is no longer with the group - he stayed in his new quarters. Group discusses that they will miss him.  Fungus stares into his cup - and a voice enters into his head (no one else hears).  Tananan (fairy)…who speaks in riddles.  Wants him to find a flask.  Petal now floats on his drink. He spills his drink, telling us there was a fairy in it.  
“One was lost, the grove gone still,
The charm you held has paid its will.
Nell is watching, Nell is cross,
For time has stirred, and trees count loss.
A bud of night, in hand you’ll take,
But charms need roots that do not wake.
Seek the flask that stone does brew,
From hands that mix what gods once knew.
In alchem’s house the essence sleeps,
Where fungus crawls and shadow weeps.
You owe her craft, and blood, and breath,
Or bear the silence she calls death.”
Meanwhile…Affy notices a cloaked half orc figure - he looks her way and summons her over.  She gestures “me” and he nods.  She tells the group not to look, but she will be right back.  She gets up to leave as the group discusses the fairy.
Affy walks over.  He has heard of us.  Name is Borge.  He pulls out a parchment…and we see a charcoal drawing of an Orcish symbol.  It was burned into the chest of someone who was drowned in the Vaelura River.  She knows this is a symbol of someone who has been excommunicated from Affy’s clan.  Area was thick with brush and grasses. Might be clues there.  Realmwardens took the body.  He describes the area where we can look - and says we can’t miss it because there are Realmwarden all around.  She thanks him and asks if she can leave a message for him at the Lantern.  He says yes.
Lorian sends a message to Cerul (sending spell) - (our) names were given at the Bladed Mouth.  He wants to meet because he has something else for our group (he’s seen something further).  Affy asks Borge what the Bladed Mouth is…and he says it is the Shaded Mantle.  She takes offense at the fact that there is a nickname that makes fun of the injured barkeep.
Affy goes back to the group and Cerul sends back a message to Lorian to let him know we will meet him before sundown at the Shaded Mantle.  Fungus tries to tell the group about the fairy message.  He thinks he has powers from a hag.  Needs to go to the woods where there are roots and find a glyph.  Needs to ask Iofer about a flask that might help.  He tells Cerul what he heard and Cerul scribbles it down. 
We decide to investigate the dead body first…so we head to the river.  On the way, Fungus palms the cube as Affy looks on.  Everyone else is 60+’ away. Fungus rolls a 31 [COMPANION]...he’s spinning and it stops and we hear a pop.  There is a fairy dragon looking over his shoulder…InithiaI.  I will hang out with you today.  “How can I help?”  He tells her that we are looking for a tree/glade and glyphs.  She mentions that maybe it is in the Feywild.  She came from the Feywild.  Fungus shows her the poem and she ooohs and aaahs.  She says that he should do what the person in the poem says (the mention of killing her).  She tells Fungus that she needs to make another charm to replace the one that was lost - using a Nightbud.  She tells him he needs a petrified nightbud…that he needs to age one himself.
Kaizo asks her how we can get the love of nature (of the Fey) to spread in this world. Relys questions why we’d want to do that.  She says she doesn’t know. As we walk, she explains the craziness of the Feywild to Fungus.  Things are distorted, some act like fools, things are psychedelic. Kaizo says he’d like to go…and feels like he has been there (in his head, in his dreams).  
We continue to walk to the riverbank with her on Fungus’ shoulders.  We see that the river is moving slowly….we pass the dock area which is busy.  Affy looks to see if there are other half orcs…there might be one or two.  90% of the city are elven.  Then half-elf, humans.  Fungus and Affy stand out the most, but no one pays us any mind.  After a while of checking out people, we notice there are more smiles and people pointing us out…maybe our reputation is starting to spread (we hung out with the king in the noble district).  We see no negativity. 
As we approach the banks, we see a dozen Realmwarden standing around in the area.  We encourage Fungus to go over and talk to them…Kaizo gives him a hit on a pipe first (adv on insight and perception).  Fungus steps up and a female Realmwarden steps up.  They speak and he talks and then points to Affy.  They ask if he was ken to her…and she says she didn’t know.  The Realmwarden tells us that the body was burned and sent down the river last night.  She tells us that they don’t need any help with investigation.  Affy points out the Relys might be able to help.  Realmwarden says that she thinks that the guy was killed with a knife in the back…but no knife was recovered and she thinks it might be in the water (kelp).  Relys sends her spider into the brush to look around…and tells her that he sees where the body was found.  Affy tells the Realmwarden what the symbol was (Ghecbaurg - white bear - Affy’s Orc clan).  Fungus talks to the Realmwarden about the nobles and Ham…and persuades them to allow us to help.  Relys shifts into a rat.  Affy asks for a full description of the dead body.  It was a half orc…looks a lot like Affy.  
Relys jumps into the water as a rat.  Sees a fish and talks to it.  The fish is scared, but she asks it some questions.  Did you see a dead body?  Yes.  Did you see a dagger or a weapon?  No - just that thing over there - and points over there.  Relys swims over and has to hold breath as she sees a satchel and tries to swim with it in her mouth up to the bank…as the fish questions her.  Other fish start to come over.  She makes it to the bank.  Kaizo is looking and sees the satchel and opens it on the ground so Relys can see.  There is a cloth tied up around a ring and a soaking wet piece of paper.  Kaizo opens the paper and tries to read it.  He catches the word Basctdelm…father.  Find him before that started with a G….blurred name…Calden. The ring is a band of Dunduar.  There are rations. 
Fungus gives the Realmwarden some gold and tells them to grab lunch and let them take a break while we hold this down and search.  Kaizo and Relys share the note and items with the group.  Discussion about what the note means.  Affy puts on the band (after time she starts to have a sixth sense).  Decision is that Affy needs to get to Basctdelm to learn more about her father.  Big question is “who killed this messenger” and “why.”
Group decides to head out to the alchemist…and Fungus puts his head in where the Realmwarden are to say we didn’t find anything.  As we start to leave, Relys shifts back into herself. We cross the bridge and river and make our way to the Academy and quad.  We hear footsteps…and Pivric is there.  And he says he found out something about what he and Relys previously talked about.  Druids of Iparos, Varenwoods…ritual.  The ritual opens the mind to small visions.  Need a moonpetal, which is a fungus.  He wanted Relys to have the information.  He says he will continue to study.  Relys knows that when nature is twisted in the wrong way you get bad results so she knows she needs to nail down the specifics of the ritual before trying it.  She knows she will need the nightbud and a moonpetal.  He says he hopes to see her at the Pale Lantern.  Relys asks if any of us have heard of moonpetal. Fungus and Kaizo have heard of it.  It is not common, but might be able to see it. 
Relys then tells the group why she is here.  Her village was burned and raided and she’s trying to find clues as to who did it and what happened (she was on a nature walk when it happened).  They stole her people’s religious relics and she’s trying to find them.  She lost her family…Fungus picks her up and hugs her.  
We go see Iofyr - NE corner, top floor.  He’s there and working away.  He says he did not look into Fungus’ family yet because he was sidetracked.  Affy asks if he knows how to petrify things (out of the blue).  You need Lusterbee wax (and he throws a waxy candle at Affy and it explodes on her).  Relys questions where we find this wax.  He says Tully Goldbinder has all the lusterbees…she lives up in the hills. We determine that we need a 100gp quantity of the wax to make petrification materials.  Fungus then tells Iofyr about the person that raised him (Landon) and about the magical cube.  He calls in Drainian (his helper) to help research Landon (who was an alchemist).  Iofyr asks about the fairy dragon…and asks if he can keep her (she is going to stay with Fungus).  We decide to leave and head to the Shaded Mantle early.
As we walk that way, a woman stands out to Cerul.  She is wearing very noticeable robes - she is a monk from the Abbey.  Cerul says he wants to talk to her alone.  We walk past and then watch Cerul approach her and talk to her.
Are you from the Abbey?  Yes.  Have you been there recently?  Maybe a stretch - just before the King closed the gates.  Cerul looks to see if she is wearing a signet ring from the Abbey.  He tells her it has been a while since he’s been there, but he’s heard of strange goings on.  She confirms that it is the road to the Abbey…and not the Abbey itself that brings trouble.  She tells him that something is unnatural.  There was a stone Stone Giant…with a frightened look on his face.  She says that she can carry a message to the Abbey for him.  He asks about Freya…and she says she doesn’t know him.  She then asks him do you remember which order he was with?  And he says one of the original ones of the Light.  And he doesn’t know which one of the 4 light orders he was in.  She guesses that he is probably running the Protective order.  The Orders are:  Nurturing - runs orphanage  Reflective - scholarly side  Protective - those who are with stone giants Rising - help with transformation of sacred oaths.  Since the awakening - they’ve had to increase the population beyond those who worshipped Neera.  He says he plans to visit soon and if she sees Freya…to say hello.  Melain (this lady from the Nurturing order) says if she sees him she will deliver the message. [Cerul is trying to see if there is anyone at the Abbey that he trusts - he’s going to have to find someone who is not Rel’Udomina]
As we walk, we find a Realmwarden to send a message to the king…letting him know that if he has a message for us to send to O'naren….leave a message at the Pale Lantern.  
As we enter Moonroot Hollow, Relys is uncomfortable and rides on Fungus’ shoulder to make her feel better.  We make our way through this area…we’ve not been to this part of MH.  We make our way to the Shaded Mantle…and it is rough wood and black iron.  It is lively inside.  Inside it lit with greenish glow lanterns…very eclectic decor.  Lots of smoke and musky smell.  We hear a bard singing out of tune.  Barkeep studies us…he’s wearing a dark fiend-ish mask.  We see a heavily armored man standing next to a stairwell that descends.  We do not see Lorian yet.  Kaizo approaches the barkeep and orders a round of drinks and gives him gold (5gp).  I’m assuming you want the best drinks we have?  K-you look like a proud man with many stories.  B-this is not the time for that conversation.  K-I’d just like to hear something I’ve never heard before.  B-then maybe you should go downstairs…if you can get there.  Cerul walks up.  B-what are you doing in a place like this (referencing his eyes).  Cerul asks about Lorian and B says - then you should be on the list and points to the guy by the stairs.  He says you may pass.  Then asks us all our names…you may pass to all of us.  And we head downstairs, spiral staircase.
Space opens into a dark stone circular chamber.  Tables are in a ring around a staged area (ring of water, lots of reflective light).  There are booths with curtained velvet on the sides of the room.  Scent of the special liquor in the air.  We see Lorian in an alcove booth…curtain opened.  We see a female human in a silk dress with bangles…dancing in a seductive way.  We talk to her…and eventually go to the booth with Lorian (after Affy asks her about a lap dance).  She offers a “wet whisper” drink and Fungus buys one for 5gp.  Lorian is smoking a pipe and motions to us. We head over.
Kaizo pulls out his pipe and a smoke dragon emerges out of the pipe…watching him.  And Lorian frowns.  Cerul says - we took care of the youth for you.  L-yes, I believe you have.  I am assuming you can return it unbroken?  And Cerul pulls it out.  L-he takes it and tucks it away and says probably best you didn’t open it…since you showed discipline, I offer you this…and hands Cerul a piece of dark cloth.  Cerul isn’t sure what it is and tells him.  L-you never know when you might need a hole.  We know this guy has a lot going on.  He has history in Qal’dynn…and has his arms around magic.   Cerul thinks this guy has been at the Academy longer than most people; has some great power, but probably crossed someone somewhere…which is why he hangs out in MH.  He’s not good or bad…not dedicated to a cause.  Likely selfish, but could be lawful.  Not beholden to the King. 
Cerul also asks about the other ways we have helped (referring to the Rel’Udomina), but he answers back about an arcane rift that has opened from the rituals those kids were doing…this is from where the Ley Liness were messed with.  I can help you get down there to fix it.  Cerul is worried that we won’t know what to do when we get there.  Affy asks him if he has a way that we could communicate with him once we get down there.  He says he can help us out.  He hands the sending stone to Fungus, then Kaizo, then Affy, then Cerul.  It will allow the group to message him once we get down there.  He tells us that we can go to the gardens and he can open the door to get where we need to go.  Fungus gets his drink and steps out to drink it and we close the curtain.  Kaizo asks him why he frowned about the smoke and dragon.  And gifts Lorian something special (not a nightbud) to smoke once we leave (strong herbs that will eventually help him sleep….it will help him become one with nature).  Kaizo thinks he is more academic than natural.  Fungus drinks rapidly…and hears whispers:
1.  I saw a silver light coming through the gates in the sewers (confirms there is something wrong with the leythreads)
2. This is the last time, you have to be successful this time.  Soften roots right this time.  Need to make sure those nobles pay for this.
3. And he sees a halfling and hears “Yeah that dwarf, she’s a bitch.  Thought I was getting some good honey and it tasted like shit.”
We decide to go to the overgrown conservatory again and Lorian taps his staff and little glyphs pop open and part - ground shifts - and we see stones with light lavender color that descends down.  L-you’ll find the leypost I’m talking about down there.
We head down and it is dark. We see glowing stone, temp gets warm, cold, then warm again. We hear crackling energy.  We see forms floating around (almost like eels) an ornate pillar. This is the leypost.  Sparks feed and strike out at the creatures … we need to kill them.  It is INITIATIVE.
Round One: Relys casts a spell on a creature (starry wisp). The post shoots out and hits Kaizo with lightning damage.  Cerul attacks one of the creatures. The post then hits Kaizo with psychic damage.  Kaizo then runs forward to a creature and attacks it and misses, then tries again and hits, then hits with his next attack and stuns it. The one on Kaizo is stunned. One runs forward lights up Relys and attacks her and hits twice. One runs forward and attacks Cerul and lights him up.  Then hits him, but he shields.  The last one attacks Fungus and does not light him up.  Then attacks and misses Fungus twice.  Fungus then attacks him with poison spray and hits, then enlarges himself.   Then a pulse reaches out and hits no one. Affy hits twice (the one on Relys) and misses, but still does damage.
Round two:  Relys attacks and hits it, wounding it badly, then casts barkskin on herself. The post hits Affy with lightning damage.  Cerul attacks with radiance of the dawn on all 4 of them and they all fail, all taking damage (he sees them absorb some of the damage…but still take some damage).  The one on Relys dies.  The one on Kaizo dies.  The other two are still up.  The post attacks Kaizo and hits with psychic damage.  Kaizo then attacks the one on Fungus and misses twice, then hits.  The creature then attacks Fungus and he takes no damage. Then it attacks Kaizo and misses. Then one attacks Cerul and he puts up a flare and hits him, but Affy cuts the damage in half.  It then hits Cerul again.  Then Fungus hits one hard and misty steps away…taunting the one he approaches. The post flares and does nothing. Affy then attacks with her sword and hits once.
Round three:  Relys attacks the one on Kaizo and hits it, killing it.  The post then attacks Kaizo again, but Affy deflects the damage.  Cerul attacks the last one and hits it. The post attacks Cerul and he takes psychic damage and he loses a spell slot. Kaizo runs over to attack the last one and hits it, killing it. 
The leypost flickers and Fungus sees a pebble fall from it and tries to place it.  Cerul looks over and casts mending on the post. Fungus grabs the pebble with his mage hand and pulls it over to him….since it doesn’t seem to be a part of the post.  It takes Cerul a few minutes, but the spell is working.  Fungus realizes that he pulled over a wisdom ion stone (that needs attunement - gives +2 to wisdom).  He gives it to Cerul.   Relys and Kaizo look around the room…they see an almost invisible thread going through the walls.  Affy investigates the walls - there is no hidden door.  This room is just designed for the leypost.  Cerul is able to repair it.  We then walk back up the stairs and Lorian is not there.  Once we step through, the door closes by itself.  
Cerul uses the stone to contact Lorian - we think we have it managed.  What do you want us to do with the stone?  He says - meet me back at the Mantle and we can discuss.  When we walk in, the barkeep sees us and nods.  The goliath nods as we approach. And we head down the stairs.  Cerul, Affy, Fungus, Kaizo and Relys.  Cerul and Affy see that Lorian is talking to someone…and that person tries to shuffle by us.  Cerul notices that the person adjusts himself and he catches a Rel’Udomina symbol on that person.
– The End of the Session –
