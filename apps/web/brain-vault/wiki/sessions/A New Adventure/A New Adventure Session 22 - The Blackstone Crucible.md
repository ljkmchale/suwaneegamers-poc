# Session 22 - The Blackstone Crucible

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: February 5, 2026
In-game Date:  5th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Strange Black Structure
Day 4/8 of rest for the Feywoven Glypic
The Steps of the Strange Structure
We think maybe this was exposed with the Awakening, but are not sure.  
The Hall of Convergence
Fungus looks inside.  There are 4 pillars.  Can’t tell how tall the ceiling is - it looks to be 40’ tall, but can’t actually see a ceiling, it is just dark.  The sides are black stone walls and there are a pair of doors on each side and a set of double doors at the end of the room.  There is something etched into the floor about halfway across.  The place seems long abandoned.  Fungus passes gas.
Cerul looks around and it has an ancient feel, but doesn’t seem to be elemental - like it had an overhaul or remodel at some point.  Cerul thinks it probably dates back to the old gods, but is not someone he is familiar with.  He knows old gods existed and new gods have risen and there is a divine energy that exists - so it could be an even older god.  It doesn’t seem to be a god of light, but maybe of distorted or bent light - not evil, but nothing enlightening.  
We step in and the floor has repeating geometric shapes and somewhat up the walls.  At the center of the room there is one tile that turns and we hear the sound of chains.  It looks like a broken shard in the middle of the tile.  Fungus moves over to a pillar - it is octagonal and rises up.  As Affy and Relys approach the symbol it starts to glow.  The shard almost looks like it is moving.  The two of the them hear “all who pass are judged.”  Fungus walks over to one of the side doors and listens and doesn’t hear anything at first - then he thinks he hears a low hum/sustained energy.  Ridley goes to another door and hears nothing.  Relys goes to the far doors and hears “none who pass are unmarked.”  Affy checks another door and hears nothing and goes back to the tile.  She gets within 5’ of it and the entire group hears: “We are the keepers that bind and this is the Blackstone Crucible.”  Fungus decides to open the door where he is. Affy and Cerul move over to the door next to him, as Ridley casts darkvision on himself and goes into stealth (Rolls a 39 for Stealth).  
Binding Workshop
Fungus throws the door open and we see racks and shelves, manacles, rings, black dust clinging to things. No one is present. There is a hum in the air for those who step in.  Fungus asks Cerul to hide his light.  This room is large and rectangular with doors on the right and stairs at the far end.  Fungus and Affy step in.  This seems like a ceremonial dungeon.  Affy hears nothing at the first door.  Ridley comes out of stealth to join us and then goes into hiding again and goes to the stairs to check them out.  Relys casts Detect Magic and the manacles and collars have a magical aura (abjuration).  As Fungus touches manacles, he feels a pulse run through him - slight energy.  Affy opens the first door on the right and she sees a square chamber with black stone walls, a low stone platform is the in the room with iron rings set into the floor.  The raised platform has sigils and recesses where they are.  Relys sees that the iron restraints are magical, too.  
Ridley goes upstairs through a black stone shaft (the tower staircase), but he also sees they go down, too.  There is no rail going up or down the stairs - you could fall off the edge.  Stairs are well worn.  As he moves, he looks for secret passages.  At the top it dead ends into a door.  He hears wind, but not at the door, but more on the sides of him.  He pushes the door open and he sees a hallway - and thinks it goes to the center of the structure.  The hallway goes 25’ and then goes right and left (but not at a T - more diagonally).  He moves forward down the hall. 
Fungus goes to the next door on the right and opens it.  He sees a long hallway that disappears into his darkvision and goes straight.  He sees many doors on the left and one door on the right.  Fungus hears “one, two, three, ah forgive me…the echo distorted the count” and he looks and sees a small creature that looks impish, but isn’t.  He looks at Fungus, shakes his head, and disappears.  
Ridley gets to the fork and looks to the right and sees a hallway that goes as far as he can see.  On the left he just beyond 20’ there is a door on the left.  He moves up to that door and listens.  He hears snow hitting the wall outside.  
Communal Dormitories
Fungus walks to where he saw the imp.  Relys and Affy move into the room with the platform.  Looks like the recesses were used for melted metal (silver, steel)...can’t make out the sigils/runes.  Relys thinks the symbols are created in patterns for strength, to lift things up (lattice-like).  They open the door and see Fungus sniffing the air.  He opens the door on the left. There is a chamber with black stone walls and stone stabs and bed frames, but no mattresses.  On the stone bed there is a book.  Its surface is naked with creases as if it has been handled for many years.  It has leather and is made of 8 interlocking mantacles that form a perfect circle…and a dark obsidian shard with the word “N’dyr.”  This is journal-like.  Thin, but resilient pages, written in common.  He sees a wooden footchest with iron bands.  Fungus reaches to open the chest and a dart flies out of it and he takes poison damage.  Inside he sees several small folded black robes and a small lockbox.  He finds a key and opens the lockbox. Inside is a single amethyst.  With a note “if this fails, we endure.”  Fungus isn’t sure how much the gem is worth, but Cerul thinks it is worth 20gp.  
Affy walks to the next door and opens it.  It looks to be the same as the last room, including the book.  She calls Fungus over to check out the chest.  He sees a hole and Affy covers it, and Fungus is finally able to open it.  There are more robes, a healer’s kit (Relys), a key with geometric markings, and a potion of healing (Fungus).  
Cerul and Relys study the two books we have found so far.  They realize this is not a story or spell book, this writing is very purposeful.  Passages have numbers, parables.  There are themes that stand out.  Hymns and repetitive sayings.  This seems like a godly book.  “Some evils can not be destroyed.  Destruction is not mercy.  Binding is not cruelty, but responsibility.”  Text speaks of containment and of those who choose to bind rather than kill.  Indoctrination and persuasion.  You should understand that these bindings are all purposeful.  Trying to figure out what the binding is about.  They study a bit longer and realize this will take a long time to totally figure out.  The books are all the same (like a bible).  Books are not evil or cruel according to the text.  References to someone known or spoken of Malgri’n.  This seems to be the leader or champion or prophet of this message. 
As they study, Affy and Fungus go to the next couple of rooms, which seem to be copies of the other rooms.  In these four other rooms, these are all still in the dormitory: the first chest has a couple of robes, flask of body oil vial (QUOTING THE DM, “diddy oil”), and the inside chest says “We bind, We bind, We bind”.  Fungus opens chest two: a little book (names that are hard to read and scratched out, binding failures, a belief structure (instead of an eye for an eye, you do something wrong I kill you, it is just locking him up), a scroll (detect magic), a set of fine writing tools (Cerul). The half-big boy took a poison dart straight to the face, disfiguring him as the venom seeps inside fully, his big boy nature tanking the hit and keeping him standing, and inside a potion of poison resistance is inside (Affy) and an obsidian charm. Last chest: big boy failed, he took the dart to the face again… inside there is a small pouch with 12 silver pieces, a silver signet ring with the symbol of this place, and a folded scrap of parchment with a routine written down (wake up, brush teeth….discipline).
The half-big boy continues down the hall and it looks like the floor drops off and it angles to the left and there is a sheer wall of water. The water isn't moving (not frozen), more like a mirror.  
Chamber of the Architect
Meanwhile, Ridley, upstairs, opens the door where he is at. He sees the walls curve up from the floor and ceiling. He thinks this looks like a chamber of someone important (maybe the architect)..  Sees geometric patterns everywhere. He sees a stone slab desk and best and fixed seat. The desk has shelves and a stone armoire built into the walls, closet-like. He sees a scroll sitting in one of the pockets on a shelf and hears a loud “pop” behind him, but sees nothing.  He grabs the scroll and opens it up, but can’t read it/understand it, so he pockets it.  On the desk, he finds a book, like what we found in the rooms below.  
Chamber of the Custodean
Ridley continues down the hallway on the left side and he sees another door and listens at it and hears nothing.  He opens the door. This is a nice room as well, but maybe belongs to the custodian or person who tends to the place.  Similar layout to the other room he was in, except the desk is thicker and there is a geometric rune that is not attached to any others that sits in the middle of the room.  He checks the desk for a hidden compartment and thinks it might have something to do with that rune. He touches the rune and fails a wisdom saving throw and he feels necrotic energy shoot through him and hears a thud beneath the desk, as a box falls out of the desk.  He opens up the box and sees strange writing utensils: skull, frog, fish, that are holding bottles of pigment ink.  He pockets the box.  He continues on. 
Meanwhile, the rest of the group goes to the “water/mirror” wall to check it out. Affy steps forward first and sees off to the left stairs rising up, but to the right, sees a large floating platform that seems to be attached to the doors in the main pillar room.  The outside circle falls into emptiness and above is a dome.  Our view shows the walls, but think if we were inside it would look like we were looking at the night sky.  There is a 15’ gap between us and the dome.  There is a platform on top of the dome - likely from the stairs.  In the center of the domed room is a symbol that is embedded into the floor, but it is an actual piece of black obsidian - not a carved symbol.  We decide to climb the stairs.  
Council Chamber
Ridley is on the top floor and meets the group on the top floor.  Just as we meet, we see the area open up into a vast rectangular area with a door that leads to the platform.  There is a stone dais - like a counselor's chambers - with 4 large chairs.  There are 4 large insectoid/demon-like creatures….and 
IT IS INITIATIVE:  Combat vs. Mezzoloths
* Round One:  Ridley moves up and attacks one with a short sword and hits.  He hits again and then hits again with a hand crossbow, and a final hit with sneak attack.  Affy dashes forward as a bonus action and attacks another one with her greataxe and misses twice.  The one on RIdley casts a spell and darkness envelops Ridley.  And then he moves back from the darkness and throws his trident at Affy and misses.  Fungus runs into the darkness and moves toward another one he sees.  He hexes the creature as a bonus action and then sprays it with poison, using lucky and hits - (poison and necrotic) but he realizes that the poison didn’t do much damage and knocks it prone.  It then stands up and casts a spell (cloudkill).  Ridley and Fungus have to make saving throws or take poison damage.  This area is heavily obscured.  Cerul tries to dispel the cloud, but can’t so he stays put.  Relys casts a spell which makes her body light up and then moves through the cloud, taking poison damage then turns into a bear.  The last insect creature attacks Affy and grapples her with his claw then tries to hit her with a trident and does, but she mitigates some of the force damage with her psionic powers, but is restrained.  
* Round Two:  Ridley moves up and hunters mark the insect and fumbles and the foe got to attack him.  He then missed the creature with his second attack.  He then tries to shoot it and misses.  Affy tries to break the restraint and does not.  She spits on him.  The creature on Ridley misses with the first attack and then crits on Ridley and hurts his left foot (cutting his movement in half).  Fungus moves out of the cloud and bursts the one in the corner and uses lucky and hits, doing a little bit of damage.  He then casts a spell on himself and moves to the creature.  That creature then tries to grab Fungus with a claw and misses, then tries to hit with another claw and hits and grapples him, but he takes cold damage (half) when he does.  That damage makes the cloud drop.  Fungus takes damage and is restrained.  Cerul moves into the darkness and then dashes out so he can take stock of the area. Relys moves up between Affy and Ridley and attacks the one on Ridley as a bear (rends).  The first attack misses and then the second attack hits as a bear, but the glow does quite a bit of radiant damage.  The last one attacks Relys with a claw and fumbles, then tries again and hits and she is restrained, and she takes slashing damage. She then forces the creature to make a CON save and it does.
* Round Three:  Ridley attacks and misses with the first one then hits with the second attack and takes it down, turning it to ichor.  Affy then misses twice (hitting with disadvantage).  Fungus tries to misty step and can’t (the power of the blackstone crucible - he is bound).  He then tries to burst it and misses. The one on Fungus laughs and attacks with a trident, taking cold damage as he does (but in half) and then attacks again and hits (and Fungus’ armor drops).  Cerul runs over to attack the one on Fungus and hits, doing a lot of radiant damage.  He takes additional radiant damage failing his CON save. Relys attacks twice and misses twice.  The last one casts cloud kill on Affy, Relys, and Ridley.   Ridley fails the save and the other two make it, but all take damage. 
* Round Four:  Ridley moves out of the cloud. Affy heals herself and then attacks and hits once. Then action surges to attack again and hits once.  She takes poison damage at the end of her turn. Fungus attacks the one on him and hits, dealing psychic damage and knocking him prone.  He then uses a bonus action to turn large.  The creature stands up and tries to grapple Cerul, but misses due to Cerul’s warding flare.  He then tries to stab Fungus and hits.  Cerul attacks the creature on him and misses.  Relys misses with both her attacks.  She makes a CON save, but takes poison damage (halved).  The last one attacks Relys and misses and then attacks again and hits with a trident. 
* Round Five:  Ridley moves over and moves the hunters mark to the one on Fungus and Cerul.  He attacks and misses then throws the glowing dagger and hits, killing it. Affy attacks twice and hits once.  Then takes poison damage and sheaths her axe.  She yells at it.  Fungus runs to the edge of the cloud and casts a spell on himself.  Cerul can’t see inside the cloud and can’t see anything so he heals Fungus a little bit.  Relys fumbles and falls prone. Then attacks again and misses.  Then casts some healing on herself as a bonus action. Then takes poison damage.  The bad guy then attacks Relys and hits and then attacks Affy and hits.
* Round Six:  Ridley tries to attack and misses, fumbling hitting Relys with his bolt, taking her down. Affy breaks the restraint and moves out of the cloud. Fungus tries to attack it and misses.  Cerul tries to dispel the cloud kill spell and does and the cloud disappears.  He moves up next to Relys. Relys makes a death save.  The bad guy casts darkness and runs away. Affy hits with her opportunity attack.  Fungus yells that he is going through the doors.
* Round Seven:  Ridley moves over where he can see him and shoots with his crossbow and misses, then crits with the second hit.  He does full crit damage and kills him.  
Fungus pours a healing potion into Relys’s mouth and she comes back up.  We walk around to one of the rooms that Ridley checked out - it is the custodian room.  We take a short rest. After this, we hear a popping sound and the little creature appears.  He is counting and then stops and says, “I will let Varmuun know.” And pops away. 
Affy saw the guy and doesn’t think he is abyssal or infernal.  She thinks he is a yugoloth (neutral evil).  He seems to be keeping a toll/count of what is going on.  It is odd that people from another plane are here.  
The Mysterious Gate
The group decides to check the platform.  Affy and Fungus step up to open the doors.  It opens into a vast hallway…with smooth stone overhead.  Eyes twitch in/out focus on depth of stars/non-existence.  Strange we can’t see beyond.  We see black stone arches that create the dome of the ceiling.  As we get to the center of the room, it floats above the stone area below.  We can actually look down and see what is below (this area is rail-less).  We see an old gate above us with a stone circle around it.  The interior of it is shimmering (like a portal).  On either side of it, we see massive abacus-like contraptions with heavy stone beads.  The railing of the gate is obsidian. Before the gate, we see an obsidian pedestal.  We notice the abacus are positioned in specific points and the gate with the shimmering field…there is an image of a forest stretched out.  Relys knows that this forest is the Varenwood.  She thinks my people created a ritual and thinks that there is something going on with what her ancestors did.  She thinks if we step through it, we could go into the Varenwood.  Is this real or an illusion?  Relys thinks that if the Weaving Night goes wrong, corruption occurs.  She thinks the abacus has settings that allow us to dial in coordinates to send us to a certain place.  She memorizes this setting to know how to get to the Varenwood.  She thinks that the ritual in Varenwood made the Shadowfell travelers come here instead of to the Varenwood (and thinks the travelers from the Varenwood would come here with this setting).  
 The End of the Session –
