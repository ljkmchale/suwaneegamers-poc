# Session 33 - Two Heads Are Not Better Than One

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: August 21, 2026
In-game Date:  18th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Ruins of Adore
Standing in the smoky ruins of Adore
Seeing the wizard approach, Affy asks Rueh’unar, “what are you doing here.”  He replied that he heard what was going down here and had concerns and got here as quickly as I could. He asked about the people were escorting and we reply that we let them go ahead - and have plans to meet back up.  He replies that we need to take stock of what is left.  Ridley tries to determine if he is telling the truth and is confused.  He thinks that Heildove might be justified being here and is likely honest.  Kaizo speaks up and offers some thoughts about being a storm or a mountain when problems come.  Heildove thinks we need to rummage around to see who or what is left.
Fungus speaks up and is confused - we saw you in a cage last time and Ridley was supposed to kill you.  Ridley has never seen Heildove dressed like this - he appears to be well equipped as an adventurer. He notices a large folded satchel with one fastener on it that looks like a 3-pointed/4-green gem brooch.  
Heildove found the coffers.  Fungus walks over to shake his hand.  Affy speaks up that the family in Caelora is no longer going to be a threat.  Ridley jumps in to say that he is no longer hunting him.  Ridley tells him that the family has other concerns.  Affy mentions that Urovil is dead and Fungus adds that they are concerned with hell.
Heildove says he doesn’t trust just anyone with this gold. Kaizo pipes up with another analogy about crossing a river - swimming or being wise and learning where the stones are.  He says there are valuable items inside, but nothing he wants.  Affy says “you mean, you’ve taken what you wanted already.” And he replies that he has taken nothing.  He says there are things inside that need to find the way back to the monks. 
This area is very ruined, burned and smoke damaged.  There are dead bodies.  Fungus thinks there are no additional threats inside.  Affy asks exactly how it is that he arrived here and at just the right time.  She intimidates him.  He thanks her for revealing that the Caelora threat is no longer.  He mentions that his estranged brother - another Heildove - had business in Adore, which is why he is there.  As Relys looks around, she notices some Rel’Udonima tattoos on some of the dead.  She finds a tree limb with a bronze bell (and thinks it has some value-10gp).  Fungus finds two clay jars with a familiar red liquid (2 potions of greater healing). Affy presses further about his brother.  He says he found out that the King’s Outlanders had stopped the RU at the Abbey of LIght and Qal’dynn…so he pushed out to Adore with his RU influence.  She asks if he saw his brother and he said he came out when he heard the last of our fight.  Ridley asks how he was able to hold his own inside and he said that not all the monks were turned yet.  
Cerul walks in and notices that some areas were preserved - or at least someone took some care to try to save an area…places where there are water.  Cerul comments that we might need to look for an underground area and Affy agrees to go help him.  Ridley asks Heildove to show him the coffers.  There is an intact chest.  He tells Kaizo that he would be the best equipped to make sure it gets into the right hands.  Heildove uses his staff to levitate the chest and sets it in front of Kaizo.  He then uses a mage hand to help open it.  There is a huge pile of gold. Ridley thinks this is likely 5000gp. 
As Affy and Cerul walk around, Affy sees a set of seven smooth river stones etched with symbols of Fralee that are valuable (used for meditation) - 15gp.  Cerul notices a small bronze sculpture that has intertwined vines.  It looks like a dryad holding a bowl.  This looks valuable - he asks Kaizo what this could be.  It looks like it could be a statue of Fralee. Kaizo thinks it is her.  Kaizo then comments about religion - how it is not just following a god, but living like one.  As Cerul picks up the bowl, it fills with water (50gp).  Kaizo tastes it - it is good, clean and fresh.  This is a magical bowl.
Relys casts detect magic as a spell and sees a few things:  Heildove’s staff and something on his hand, the bowl with water (conjuration), the healing potions, and she sees a fallen tree to the right and ruins of a large chamber and senses divination magic coming from that direction.  She heads in that direction.  Ridley catches up to her and comments “no more scorpion” and she comments that she needed to cast spells.
Affy and Cerul are looking for underground passages and just find water springs, but don’t seem to find anything.  Affy yells to the group to look around as well.  Ridley finds a small metallic box; Fungus finds another small square locked case.  Fungus tries to open the lock and finds that it is a container of salt (4 containers).  Ridley finds calligraphy tools in the box. Fungus thinks this salt is likely used for alchemy.  He thinks they might be valuable and rare (10gp). Relys finds lots of symbols of Fralee in the chamber she enters. She sees a body of a woman crushed by a limb, but thinks she was killed from something else.  She sees on a table in the center of a room something that looks like a nice piece of ashwood, with dark etchings with elven script.  She reads and thinks this is a Ouiji board (at least a Myrdaen version). 
Affy goes off behind a bush to do what she needs to do. Fungus goes and conjures bananas to eat while walking around. The old man wizard is going back to rest. Kaizo is going to put the bodies to rest and clean the place up and do the best to address the situation, Relys and Ridley help him. Fungus with his bananas goes and calls out for Pa and then tries to search for Pa. While looking for tracks, trips on a banana (natural 1) and ruins any chances of finding tracks. Fungus is a bit distraught and begs the party to see if they can use magic to locate Pa. Relys makes the astute observation that Pa was at least a day ahead. The wizard feels pretty used and is going to sleep. Affy comes out from behind the bushes and goes to speak with the wizard. 
Relys looks around for any signs of life - of vegetation or animals.  She finds a cat's tail plant in the water in the central area. She speaks with plants. Hello - how are you? I’m glad you made out unscathed.  The plant said it was scary.  Relys asks what it saw.  The plant asks how far back she wants it to recall.  She says that she will tell her everything.  She saw monks walking around and wee folk arrived.  The monks welcomed the wee folk and told them to stay there.  It was late and they went to sleep.  The plant watched the moon - especially the blue one.  Then early in the morning the horse arrived.  It was still dark.  The horse had a rider, but the horse was burning (the rider was not).  The horse came through the sky burning and then stopped burning and landed.  A guy stepped off of the horse and didn’t tie him off.  The horse sniffed the water and didn’t drink.  The man went into that building (and points to a tree).  The plant heard yelling and then the rider came out and got his horse and when we rode away and the horse lit up and he flew off.  And then the wee folk came out and the little ones were crying - they weren’t happy.  And the father one said they needed to leave so they did.  The plant then said that later that day, before the sun was straight up, people started arguing with each other.  Another guy came out of that tree and started splashing oil on everything and set everything on fire. And then there was fighting and smoke and fire and then you guys showed up.  But that other guy showed up first and made fountains of water everywhere.  Relys asks if anyone else fled the area.  It responds that no one did - they all fought.  Relys thanks it for telling the story. She then tells the group the story and Affy asks what the person looked like who set the fire.  Relys asks this to the plant and he says that the firestarter was a monk leader.  And that the guy on the horse had on a mask. He was darkly robed, masked, and hooded.  She decides to cast plant growth and decides to spend the night here, enriching the plants.   She is hoping the vines will help “bury the dead.”
The rest of the group follows Heildove back to a clearing he had found for a campsite. Cerul wants to use the pool onsite to scry on Pa to make sure they are okay. He knows that the pools are sacred to the elves. They are rumored to have something to do with the elves crossover.  They are called daefar pools.  He feels confident they can be used for scrying.  Kaizo speaks some wisdom about this to Cerul and he proceeds.  Cerul sees Pa and he sees him.  Pa says something is off.  He is handing off his horse to someone - likely a stable person.  He hears flowing water close by.  He sees firelight shining off of a building and he sees a nice rock structure and empty docks next to a big body of water.  Things are dimly lit from one of the moons.  He sees Pa paying someone to take care of the horse.  And Pa puts him arm around his wife and asks about their children - the wife replies they are inside and sitting at a table, preparing to eat.  Cerul isn’t sure where they are - he’s never been there.  Ridley asks how Cerul can see them and if there was a way someone could avoid scrying.  Cerul tells him that this is the “power of the light” and there are ways to avoid it, but that you will get a funny feeling if someone ever scrys on him.  Cerul thinks they are at the Southern Arch. Cerul decides to send Pa a message to ask them to stay at the Southern Arch until we arrive. “Pa we are in Adore.  Glad to see you are safe.  We will catch up with you tomorrow.”  Pa says ‘ What - how do you know I’m safe.  That Adore place was creepy.  Did you get that same vibe?  See you tomorrow.”
We make camp with watches. Fungus comments that he misses the magical cube.  Heildove has a fire going and a tent set up.  It also looks like he has rabbits roasting.  Affy sits down and tells him what Relys heard.  He tells her that must have been his brother.  He has some nefarious ways to be sure.  He thinks that one step back just pushes his brother harder. He comments that we are just at the “edge of Halesworth” and maybe he is eyeing new territory. He says he was hoping that the RU were pressed back.  But is appears that they are trying something anew. Affy confides that we are the King’s Outlanders.  He pulls out a bottle of wine and says “I am in great company.” And then pulls out a box from his pack and opens it and it has a collection of brass goblets and passes them out with wine.  I had no idea based upon…and looks at Ridley…well, I just had no idea.  Fungus says he will gladly tell stories of our travels.  And proceeds to tell him stuff.  Fungus comments how Heildove’s bag looks “handy” - it is quite a haversack.  Fungus tries to tell everything.  Ridley watches with interest - knowing that Heildove is known for spellcasting.  The vibe is this guy is younger version of Lorien.  Is all about himself. A typical wizard. Affy then asks him about the brooch on his bag.  He comments that it is a “hey if you know me I’m here to do business” symbol.  This is a symbol of the Promissory.  Ridley has heard that they are a group who hires people to acquire goods and artifacts.  Fungus uses baby oil on his feet and Heildove asks what it is for.  Affy asks if there are others he works for.  He says he worked for the family in Caelora, but that is no more.  He then asks if Ridley wants to work for him, but he says he is talking more in jest.  But says the Promissory is always looking for people to help find items.  Ridley asks him to let his group know he is open for business - as he is always looking for coin. Fungus asks if they can speak in private and they get up to walk away, as Heildove hands the others another bottle of wine. 
As they walk away, Fungus asks him about a scroll that is powerful that he wants to sell, but isn’t sure where or how to sell it.  It is a scroll of imprisonment (level 9).  Fungus says this will get him into trouble.  Heildove offers him 5000gp for the scroll and says he will pay him when they get back to camp.  They walk back. 
While they are gone, Cerul, Ridley and Affy discuss future plans.  Ridley then comments about having our souls sold to the entity under the Blackstone Crucible.  As they return, they hear us talking about soul selling.  Heildove starts to take gold out of his pack to pay Fungus and we question what is going on.  Cerul pipes up to remind Fungus about why the group needs to scroll.   Fungus tells him that we need the scroll and Heildove puts the gold back in his pack.
When they come back, Affy tells him she knows a little of his group and asks if he knows of a Thornage in their service. There are a lot of people in the world I live and work with.  He is dodging her question.  Would it be enough to say yes or will there be follow up questions. She tells him it is important.  He says he doesn’t really know him.  He mentions both of his first names and says that he has quite a reputation.  He is known for getting what he wants.  There is a tale that he found a woman of strange blood, but he desired her so deeply and stole her heart.  But it is just a tale, right?  And she comments that she has heard the same tale.  And with that, he is ready for sleep.
We all decide to sleep. Ridley says he will cast the alarm spell.  Kaizo and Relys are together and Kaizo keeps watch over her.  Ridley then tells Heildove about his apprentice - he is curious how she is doing.  Ridley tells her about capturing her and taking her to Caelora, then about Kaizo talking to her, etc.  Ridley thinks that Heildove looks disturbed by the conversation.  He questions why she took Ridley along for the ride.  Time to rest. Affy asks if he is going to cast a spell as well.  And he does - and steps into his dome of safety.
It’s the next day, the 19th of Paramore, 1246
Near the end of the 8 hours, the spell goes off and Ridley wakes up with his weapon drawn - and it is just Relys and Kaizo.  Relys has one level of exhaustion. We can’t see from here the work that Relys did all night (we stayed a little far away and upwind).  Heildove comes out of his dome and offers everyone tea.   He says he will be heading in a different direction and offers us safe travels.  Affy asks if he has words of advice should we encounter his brother.  He says that surely we could let him know.  But then she counters with words to find out if he would like to see him alive or dead. He said he would understand no matter the condition.  Heildove says he likes Fungus’ bulas and pets him and heads out.
The group packs up, Relys sleeps on the carriage, and Cerul rides Relys’ horse. Affy, Relys, and Kaizo ride in the carriage.  After a little while, we see some snow flurries start to fall.   Cerul, Ridley, and Fungus are riding outside.  Ridley hears a loud tree snap and break ahead.  Cerul and Fungus didn’t notice it - they were talking.  Ridley offers to ride ahead to scout.  Ridley rides ahead (15 stealth).  He comes around a bend and he sees a tree crack off the edge of the road and fall to the side and hears something he doesn’t understand (but thinks it is giant).  He comes back to the group and tells everyone.    We agree to proceed.  
Cerul, Ridley and Affy advance.  We hear the giants talking about “smoke and eating”  and know they are talking about going to Adore.  Cerul uses a booming voice to tell them “friends ahead.” The giant laughs and says “we will eat raw” as he sees us.  
IT IS INITIATIVE – Combat versus Ravenous Ettins
* ROUND ONE:  Ridley casts hunters mark, then attacks with his bow. He hits and does piercing and force damage, pushing him back 10’.  Then attacks again and misses.  Fungus stirs up the folks in the wagon (Kaizo and Relys) and then flies up and ahead and grows larger.  Cerul knows these are Ettins. Cerul sprouts wings and flies up 40’. Then casts scorching ray on one of the creatures - two hits, one misses.   The first ettin looks over and says “let’s get the fat one” and he runs forward and grabs a branch off a tree and launches it at Fungus, as well as a rock on the ground.  Fungus uses lucky.  He fumbles the branch, hitting himself with it and doing some great damage.  The rock is aimed at Cerul, who casts warding flare and the rocks misses.  Kaizo gets out of the carriage and bonus action dashes ahead, casting longstrider.  He uses a point to dash ahead and makes it to an ettin.   The other ettin then attacks Kaizo - throwing a rock at him.  He blocks most of the damage and ends up taking 6 points of damage.  He then throws a rock at Cerul and misses.  Relys stands up in the carriage and opens the front door of the carriage and casts moonbeam on it.  He saves and takes half damage.  The last ettin moves away from Kaizo and runs up to attack Affy.  He misses with the first attack and then hits with his second attack, imposing disadvantage on her next attack.  Affy attacks and fumbles, falling prone.  She stands back up and attacks again and hits, imposing disadvantage on him. 
* ROUND TWO:  RIdley shoots an arrow at the one he marked and hits, pushing him 10’ out.  He then dashes on the horse and shoots again and hits again and pushes him back into the moonbeam.  He saves, but still takes more radiant damage. Fungus flies even higher (now 100’ up) and then blasts an ettin with poison spray and hits, imposing significant acid damage. He then knocks him prone.  Cerul is 30’ up and flies down and attacks the one on the ground with his sword and hits. He uses a spell in the sword and the guy saves, so takes no damage.  That one then stands up and moves away from Cerul (no opportunity attack) and runs over to grapple Ridley and he sticks him in his mouth and bites him.  He then walks over to a rock with Ridley in his hand/mouth, leaving the horse behind.  Kaizo moves to the closest ettin and attacks- it is critical hit.  The ettin drops the battle axe and breaks.  Kaizo uses a stunning strike and stuns him, in addition to inflicting 29 points of damage (using 2 points).  Then he punches him again - another critical hit.  He uses another point to inflict another 29 points of damage.  He then uses another point and flurry of blows and hits twice, inflicting another 18 points of damage and poisoning him.  That ettin is stunned. Relys gets out of the cart and moves forward. She moves the moonbeam to the stunned creature and it takes half damage. The last ettin leaves Affy and runs to Kaizo and fumbles with his disadvantage and falls prone and stands up and attacks again, but Kaizo imposes lucky and the ettin misses.  Affy dashes forward and hits, then hits again and uses a charge on her weapon to impose additional fire damage and disadvantage on his next roll.
* ROUND THREE:  Ridley uses steady aim and attacks the ettin and hits and pushes him back.  Then shoots him again and misses, using his heroic inspiration to avoid a fumble.  Fungus flies over and cannonballs on an ettin, taking damage with his fall as well, yelling “cannnnoooooooonballlllll” as he drops down.  Fungus takes 40 points of damage as he inflicts 42 points of damage to the ettin and they both fall prone. He then lays on the ground and casts shillelegh and smacks him and hits imposing more damage.  Cerul runs forward and casts radiance of the dawn on both ettins in his path.  Both save, but take radiant damage.  Then uses a bonus action to heal Fungus. The ettin on Ridley bites him again and hits and gnaws on him, imposing additional damage.  Then bites him again and gnaws for additional damage.  The guy yells “this guy is good I want to eat another one” and runs forward to the larger group.  Kaizo attacks and misses, then attacks again and hits.  Then uses a bonus action to punch another ettin and hits, imposing the next roll against him to be with advantage and his movement is halved.  That ettin grabs and grapples Kaizo and bites him, and he takes damage and the guy gnaws on him.  He then steps out of the moonbeam, pulling Kaizo through the moonbeam, as Kaizo takes 25 points of radiant damage from that moonbeam.  Relys whistles the horse over and jumps on.  She then moves the moonbeam to the prone ettin, who takes radiant damage, and dies from the moonbeam.  He raises into the air and dissolves.  Affy bonus action dashes over to the one holding Ridley and attacks, missing, but then hitting and slicing off his testacles and killing him.
* ROUND FOUR:  Ridley attacks the last ettin on Kaizo and crits, stunning him.  He then attacks with the second arrow and hits, killing him.  He flies up in the air as arrows are in both nipples.
The fight is over.  Fungus says “that was awesome,” as the rest of the party comments how it was crazy that he fell so high from above.  Cerul heals Ridley; Fungus heals himself; Affy uses second wind to heal herself.   The ettins have huge axes and Cerul throws two into the portable hole to sell in the future.  Kaizo speaks about hunger and the need to eat - words of wisdom.  Relys crawls back into the carriage to sleep. Cerul gives Fungus heroic inspiration for this day.
We continue forward and spend the rest of the day traveling.  We stay on the road.  We travel through the forest.  There is light snow. At some point, we reach a crest in the road and see the river ahead.  We see a bridge crossing the span of the river.  The bridge is stone and there are giant runes carved on it.  There is a building on the other side.  The building looks like the one that Cerul saw when he scryed on Pa. Cerul reads the runes…and we stop here - at the Southern Arch.  It is twilight.
– The End of the Session –
