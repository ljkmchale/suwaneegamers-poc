# Session 37 - Halloween Treats

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: October 29, 2026
In-game Date:  29th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Basctdelm (Greenscape and Central District crossroads)
Affy asks “can I help you?”  The goblin looks familiar to Affy - from her homeland.  Relys goes still - speaking in an older gnome's voice “You hunt the wrong blood.  The balance has already failed.  Leave the half orc and lightbearer.  The debt is older than your Chief.   So says Grokan.”   One of the half-orcs says “We will find the half blood another way” and walks off.  Relys starts to walk off another direction - and her body starts to go invisible - looks like she’s in between planes. Relys doesn’t know what is happening, but the old lady in her speaks again “only you can see my now…a summoning must be ended.” Relys knows that she is possessed by an elder of her old home (Iparos). She has knowledge that Relys does not have.  The group continues to follow the possessed Relys. She walks down the road through the Central District to the south then turns and starts heading into the South District (the working man’s district) and then heads northwest up the road.  She’s heading to an area under the High District.   The group, including Ridley, continues to follow.
We enter a large cave-like area and see suspect people sitting on the edge and we pass through an archway.  This is The Bellows.  The atmosphere changes completely.  From a peaceful area - no visible weapons - to a darker area with shady people.  We see a big lake stretching out to the right.  We see a starry looking “sky” over the lake.  On the left there are buildings carved into the rock.  Relys seems to walk through people.  We see other gnomish ghosts in this area and they appear to be walking across the lake toward Relys.  No one else seems to notice this. Relys goes a quarter-mile and then turns into an alley… Going for a bit until she walks into a dungeon-like area. It is very dark here and Ridley cannot see.  Cerul pulls out his mace and lights it up.   We can still see Relys, but only her outline.  We feel like we are bumping into the walls.  One moment she is there and then she is gone.  We don’t think she went through a wall…she just disappeared.  Affy yells out and she can’t hear us.  
We continue to walk forward.  Fungus steps through and sees Relys (not her outline).  This looks like they are outdoors now. Cerul goes next, then Affy, Kaizo, and Ridley.  This doesn’t look like The Bellows.  This looks like a forest of trees.  There is a clearing with a full moon sky.  This is not Myrdae’s moon sky.  This is a white moon sky.  We don’t see the portal we came through - we just see the forest.  We don’t think we are in Myrdae anymore.  
Relys feels a hint of the Shadowfel.   Fungus feels a hint of the Feywild.  Like this is a mash up of the two.  The colors aren’t right. We see a path forward and scarring on the trees ahead from fire - just a patch here or there.  Was the portal always there or did the possessed Relys create the portal?
We continue to walk on the path.  We follow the trail through the deep woods. We see a dark rider appear in the mist on a flying horse with fire in his eyes.  He throws a jack-o-lantern down from the horse in front of Relys.   All but Ridley and Fungus take some fire and necrotic damage.  It is INITIATIVE.
ROUND ONE:  Kaizo fumbles as he hits the pumpkin, losing his reaction until the end of his next turn.  He then tries again and hits. He then uses flurry of blows and misses and fumbles again, losing his movement until the end of his next turn.   A swirl of leaves encompasses all but Fungus, impairing the vision of everyone inside the swirl. The rider on the horse flies and picks out Fungus and gives him a terrifying glare and fails - he is frightened and paralyzed until the end of this creature (DM 1) next turn.  Relys steps forward and shifts into a scorpion.   She then steps outside of the swirl - as she does the flying rider howls and flies further back and higher.  Ridley, Affy, and Relys have to make CON saving throws - they take some force damage.  Relys casts moonbeam on the horse and rider.  They both take damage, but make their saves.  Affy steps out of the swirl and to the side.  The uses her javelin to hurl a lightning bolt at the horse and rider - it hits and they take damage.  Ridley flies up to a tree, draws his crossbow, and shoots the rider and hits, inflicting piercing damage and pushing him 10’ off his horse, where he falls 30’ to the ground, taking bludgeoning damage.   He shoots a second crossbow bolt then flies over to the horse and tries to mount the horse, flying into the moonbeam.  The horse looks back at his rider and pointed and the horse rode over the party - creating a trail of fire - then flew back off to the side. The little pumpkin man attacks Kaizo with a claw and hits, inflicting slashing damage. Cerul casts dispel magic on the swirl and it disappears  He then spouts wings and flies up.  Fungus is frightened and does nothing.
ROUND TWO:  Kaizo attacks the little pumpkin man and misses. Then attacks again and hits. Then bonus action flurry of blows and hits, killing him with force damage.  The rider motions to the horse and he flies and lands next to him (with Ridley).  As he flies he creates a new fire trail and flies through the moonbeam, injuring the horse and Ridley.  A new swirl appears around Relys.  Then the rider tries to shove Ridley off the horse and fails. He then glares at Rdley who must make a wisdom saving throw and fails. Ridley is frightened and paralyzed until the end of the rider’s (DM 1) next turn.  Relys moves the moonbeam to the rear of the horse, and hits it and the rider on the ground, both taking radiant damage.  Horse is bloodied.   Affy uses her bonus action to use her psi-leap to get next to the rider.  She attacks missing twice, but the rider takes damage from graze.  She then uses an action surge to hit twice, inflicting damage on the rider.  Ridley can’t move on his turn.  The horse flies and moves the fire wall.  Cerul casts sacred flame on the rider and he takes radiant damage.   He then casts healing word on Ridley.  Fungus moves up next to Cerul. He summons a fey creature.  The creature hits the horse. 
ROUND THREE:  Kaizo uses step of the wind as a bonus action to move forward to the rider.  He attacks and hits the rider, but also takes damage from the moonbeam.  He tries to use stunning strike and it didn’t work.  But he inflicts force damage.  Then he hits again with a critical hit and inflicts more force damage.  The rider steps next to Affy and out of the moonbeam, but Kaizo does not use his opportunity attack.  He yells “see you in hell my friends” and howls to the entire group, except Kaizo.   Huge damage inflicted to all - the horse dies and disappears, Fungus’ fey disappears, and Affy uses her relentless endurance to stand up with 1HP.  As a reaction to the damage, Fungus moves to the rider and gives Affy a couple of hit points.  The rider looks at Kaizo with a glare, but Kaizo saves.  Relys moves the moonbeam into the rider and he saves, but takes damage.  She then drops the moonbeam.  She moves forward to try to get close to the rider.  Affy heals herself with second wind then steps up and beheads the rider, killing him.
We look around and notice three things.  Where the jack-o-lantern fell down there are little sticks that look like bolts.  The head has an empty mask.  And there is a small burning horseshoe where the horse fell.   Ridley takes the bolts.  Fungus identifies them,  but we can’t use them yet. The mask is a Terrifying Hood.  Fungus puts this into the Party Inventory  The horseshoe is a Figure of Wonderous Power for an Obsidian Steed. Ridley takes this. The sticks are jack-o-bolts and Ridley takes them.   Ridley gives Affy six goodberries.
The party continues down the road.
We see pieces of flesh, cloth, and leather on the trail.  The path opens up and we see a grove with trees and roots that are up out of the ground…like vines pulling creatures to a cauldron in the middle of a grove.  There is a huge, obese witch stirring the pot - “Ah - my secret ingredients have arrived.”  It is INITIATIVE.
ROUND ONE:  Fungus casts doomtide (magical darkness) on the center of the grove, as the mist closes in, impairing the view to the center of the grove for the party - except Relys is outside of the mist.  We hear cackling “I was told about you Fungus.”  He also casts shillelagh on his weapon.  She gives him a witch’s eye and he saves.  “You are as powerful as Selbranan said.”   Ridley casts a spell on his crossbow and shoots her and misses. He then fumbles on his second attempt and inflicts 14 points of damage on himself.  We hear the witch cackle and Ridley and Fungus make CON saving throws - Ridley takes a point of exhaustion for the fail (this was based on her throwing water out of her cauldron but we didn’t see this).  She then says “Bring them to me -they are going in the pot”  Roots rise up from the ground and grab Ridley, inflicting piercing damage from the thorns on the vines and it moves him 20’ closer to the cauldron.   The vines also hit Fungus and inflict piercing damage and he misty steps vs. getting pulled closer to the cauldron.  He moves 30’ to the side of his sphere and turns invisible.  The witch’s splash hits Ridley and curses him and he cannot take reactions until the curse ends.   The witch takes some psychic damage from the doomtide. Affy dashes forward 60’ to the side of the dark sphere.  Relys steps into the fog and sees where everyone else is.  She moves to the edge of the sphere and tries to thornwhip her at disadvantage and misses.  She then shifts into scorpion form.  Kaizo moves to the other side of the sphere and holds his action.  Cerul moves to his left and then throws a fireball into the dark sphere.  She saves her throw, but still takes damage from the fireball and it sets everything in the circle on fire (if it is flammable).  She looks over at Affy and says “I see you” and did the witch’s eye on Affy.  She is charmed and cannot attack until the end of Affy’s next turn. 
ROUND TWO: The sphere moves 10’ from Fungus (toward Kaizo).  Fungus hits the witch with poison spray (he can see her inside the sphere) and knocks her prone. She laughs and Ridley and Relys have to make WIS saving throws.  Relys saves (and is now immune from future laughs); RIdley fails. Both take psychic damage, however. Ridley tries to shoot the witch twice with disadvantage and misses twice, then moves away from the mist (bonus action dash).  The tree behind Cerul hits him and inflicts bludgeoning damage. The witch stands up tries to pull Kaizo into the circle, along with Relys using vines and roots.  Relys is hit and pulled 20’ forward.  Kaizo is not pulled forward (he uses lucky).   The witch’s splash is going to try to hit Affy - it hits and she takes fire damage and is cursed and also takes poison damage. The witch resists damage from the doomtide, but still takes some damage.  The sphere drops (Fungus drops it). Affy is charmed and moves 30’ around the circle and then heals herself with second wind.  Relys tries to dump the cauldron and misses.  Then attacks the witch with a claw and a stinger.  The claw hits and inflicts radiant and cold damage and she is grappled.  The stinger misses. Kaizo moves behind the witch and attacks with unarmed strike and misses.  Then tries again and misses. The uses a bonus action to use flurry of blows and misses twice more.  She reaches down and blows at the fire under the cauldron and it bubbles over and it hits Kaizo and Relys.  They have to make DEX saves.  Kaizo saves; Relys does not.  They take fire and necrotic damage. Cerul moves toward the witch and uses his radiance of the dawn.  She saves, but still takes radiant damage.  He then heals Relys.
ROUND THREE:  Fungus casts hold monster on the witch and she saves.  He then moves 21’ away from the cauldron.  He then “goes large.” Ridley enters the mist and hunter’s marks her. She is immune to charm, exhaustion, petrified, and something else.  Resistant to cold, fire, and radiant damage (only Ridley knows this).  He tries to shoot her and misses once and hits once.  Suddenly, bats come flying out of the air heavily obscuring the area near the cauldron. The witch tries to bring RIdley in with roots and vines and misses, fumbling - she is prone, grappled by Relys and can’t stand back up.  She uses the stir to splash Cerul and Relys (DEX saves).  Cerul fails and Relys saves. Cerul is also cursed. Affy and Cerul both take poison damage (from being cursed).  The vines then try to pull Fungus closer and hits, inflicting piercing damage.  He then misty steps so he is not pulled closer.  His misty step tries to inflict damage on her, but she’s ok.  But another vine tries to lash at Fungus and he has the poisoned condition.  Affy steps up and hits her four times, using her action surge and a charge to inflict additional acid damage.  After that, she looks at the cauldron and blows again and fires it up.  Everyone within 20’ needs to make a DEX saving throw. All but Ridley take some damage - fire and necrotic.  Relys tries to push the cauldron over and does and the water drains out.  She then imposes damage on the grapple.  Then misses with her stinger. Kaizo steps forward to attack and hits with his unarmed strike, imposing force damage and tries to use stunning strike, but saves.  He then tries to hit her again and does, killing her, putting his hand deep inside of her and pulling out her heart.  
There are three things we find here:  Witch’s Sash, Cauldron Stir (Dissuader), and Pet’s Skull.
We pop back into The Bellows - bloodied and torn.  
– The End of the Session –
