# Session 15 - Empower the Charm

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  September 4, 2025
In-game Date:  36th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  The Pale Lantern, Qal’dynn
Sitting at the Pale Lantern, we divide our loot.  A courier walks in - delivery for the Crown’s Outlanders.  Hands over scroll with ink stained seal.  Kaizo grabs it and opens.  “To my uncommon companions - you are not gone to the hills I hope.  Meet me for midday tea at the Leaf Light (Cirandale district), table in the rear.  Developments at the Abbey and other places we need to speak about.”  -Lorian
We head to the Academy…and Fungus grabs the Feywoven Glyphic.  Everyone stands 61’ away, except Affy.  Fungus rolls 46 [Vunerable].  Almost feels like a sting…as a chill runs down Fungus’ spine.  He has a fear of cold (he is vulnerable to cold damage - not sure for how long…spoiler - it is permanent).
We continue on and go visit Iofyr.  As we walk, Kaizo smokes, Relys is nervous and carries a stick.  Kaizo feels a little dizzy as we walk.  We get there and Iofyr says fate must be on our side.  Fungus gives it to him. Need a pure gold bowl - must remain here.  He goes and gets it.  Says we need a Nightbud…and he hands a seed to Iofyr and immediately grows it.  He then starts to add everything and toss everything (like a salad)...wax eventually becomes solid.  Looks like a golden egg with a little purple.  “Let that cool - you’ll need to touch it and find a fey place for it to do its thing.”  Fungus remembers that in O’Nearen he was chased off of a pond -which was sacred (and said it was special…tree in the middle…tree is fey).  He mentions this and group discusses.  He asks how we came by the wax - Fungus tells him.  Affy tells him that land is now ours; Kaizo says the grand opening for the Spa will be in a harmon.  
We talk about going to visit the Magistrate (Crescent Courts)...and decide to go now.  We ask for an audience…and one will be granted later this morning. Lord Anceril comes to see us.  He converses with Kaizo about being busy.  Kaizo starts “selling” the spa/retreat idea.  Affy jumps in to talk about all of the things the group has done for the city.  Kaizo says the owner passed away and his dying wish was for us to have the land.  He doesn’t believe it all, but says he will do his diligence to research this and hopefully sign off on giving the group that land.
Fungus asks him about “fey” areas near here.  He says the Ring of Sylunara would be good place to go (Relys realizes that he was hiding something as he spoke).  He says he will send word to us tomorrow about the land (and if he doesn’t…we need to come back by).  
We decide to head to Cirandale…and to the ring since it is not time for tea yet.  We go across the northern path, through the Inner Sanctum.  Cirandale is like a park area - lots of green space, trees, pathways.  As we cross the bridge, a clearing opens.  We find a large mosaic on the ground, surrounded by dense shrubs that lean in…this must be the Ring.  Nothing is familiar to Relys…but this looks really old - likely here for a long time.  As we look around, an elf walks by and says we should see this at night - it almost comes alive.  Relys decides to pray to Sylunara and Affy decides to pray to Phoe (she doesn’t feel warmth, but Relys does).  
We head to the Leaf Light (dining area).  Lots of trees, lots of smells of natural herbs.  We see lanterns suspended by trees.  This opens up into a living courtyard with many tables, many people.  We see Lorian at one of the tables - ink covered robe, ebony staff, long dark hair.  He’s talking to someone with a long dark coat, crimson scarf w/decorative bone-colored fox head mask - Affy recognizes this person as the Informant we saw with Lorian before.  He comments how we are punctual and how rare and nice that is.  We say that we are sorry to disturb his other guest, but he motions for us to sit.  He introduces us to his friend, Ridley.  Ridley has been helping me and I’ve tried to help him.  Thought Cerul would want to meet Ridley…Relys tells him where Cerul went.  Kaizo looks over at Ridley and puffs out a dragon in smoke.  Lorian takes out a cloth satchel…and gives Kaizo some herbs to smoke (they are from Kaltera - mountains).  Kaizo asks what he owes him…and he gives Kaizo some advice (you are humming with something new - be careful, but enlightenment often draws attention).  We all make introductions.  Kaizo asks what we need to know about the Abbey.  He mentions that the Arcane Waypoint at the Abbey has been stilled…no one can use it (to get in, at least).  Affy asks about how he is an informant.  And he shows us his Rel Udomina tattoo.  Affy pulls out hers and slaps them on.  
Lorian then asks Affy about the half orc pulled from the river.  She mentions that she knows he might be tied to her clan.  She tells him that she didn’t know him.  Kaizo brings up the letter.  Affy shows it to Lorian.  He smirks as he reads it.  G stood for Grokan…we think that is the name of the dead half orc.  He says he knew Caldan…but that isn’t his real name.  There was grief in his eyes…he spoke of someone he lost (a soul that completed his).  He was with the Advents of Harmony…and questioned if he could stay in it.  His true name is Oedabrin Thornage.  He never said who he was running from, but spoke of his legacy. Affy knows of the Thornage name. Astirin Thornage was the one who developed the calendar and Sinistia Thornage was the Advent - not sure how is linked to that family, but I will repay the favor to you and reach out to him.  Affy doesn’t want to bring him sadness or harm…but would appreciate him reaching out.
Lorian spends time talking to us.  Tells us that Ridley is looking for companions…to help him gather additional information.  Affy mentions that we garner quite a bit of attention…so hanging with us will get him noticed.  Ridley says that he’s used to watchful eyes.  
He asks what we are doing later and Fungus and Kaizo tell him about going to the Ring…because of the fey.  He says that there are two fey areas - the Ring and the area under the Citadel which is currently a prison housing an unseelie.  Affy asks what that is - and he mentions there are two types of fey.  Seelie and Unseelie - Seelie are good and Unseelie are bad.  
Ridley is looking for a mage named Rueh’unar (he is a bounty under and the House Ruendor has hired Ridley to find him).  Ridley is from Caelora.  Lorian doesn’t know where the mage is.  We eat lunch here.
Ridley thinks the guy is in this town.  He is old and has golden faded hair, piercing blue eyes, and purple and green robes.  His bounty is to kill him onsite.  We decide to chill out in the park/woods area.  Relys decides to make a potion of healing - she buys materials from an herbalist and then makes one.  Fungus trades a philter of love potion for 2 potions of healing at the alchemist. Kaizo smokes.  Ridley and Affy walk around looking for the mage.  RIdley thinks this guy is making it very tough to find him. We all group back up just outside the ring.  Fungus notices that Kaizo is smoking…the smoke has a nice fragrance, but the smoke weaves patterns that drift opposite the breeze.  He doesn’t notice Fungus approaching…he is in deep meditation.  The shadows stretch away and he sees moonlight that isn’t ready to be there.  He is as high as a kite.  He can see the plant life preparing for winter.  The trees seem anxious to bloom in the spring.  And then a woman, radiant and gentle appears…gown flows, and she smiles and sits across from him in the air.  He hears an aged voice from long ago…familiar.  You passed through, but you didn’t push.  You pulled.  
I am Arasia.  I am the Mistborn.  I linger where the world sighs.  
You’ve brought hope and peace…and Your kindness grows against the dark confined corruption.  
You will go where your mentor once sat…where the stones hum with what lies beneath.  
Kaizo feels like he is on the right path.  Then he comes back…and sees Fungus staring at him.  Affy and Ridley show back up and see Fungus staring at Kaizo, who is smiling.  Then Relys shows up.  The last of the sunlight fades away….
Ridley asks why we are here at night.  Fungus tells him we are here about the “budplug” - his new charm.  And need to be here to make it work.  Relys is very excited to commune with her goddess.  The area starts to fall quiet…and the sound of nocturnal creatures can be heard.  The two moons start to appear in the sky…as moonlight is cast on the ground.  Runes on the ground start to glow…and almost look like ripples on a pond.  Center of the ring is more intense.  Fungus and Relys step into the center; Affy about 15’ from center; Kaizo and Ridley about 50’ from center. 
Arcane threads start to emerge and embrace Fungus - almost like a mother holds a son.  Fungus then hears - “you do not know me, my little root.  But I know you and watch you.  Embrace the energy and grow.  And I will call upon you when I need you.”  He sees light consumed into the charm.  He now has “The Charm of the Slumbering Grove.”  
A sharp sound emerges.  5 portals open.  Eladrin step through the portals.  A 6th figure steps through - a tall Eladrin, and addresses Fungus.  “You walk a path that leads to darkness and pain.  Give me that charm.”  Another speaks “Just kill him Glazia - just take it back to Lord Selbranan (Lord of the Fey).”  Silence you fool…now hand it over.   IT IS INITIATIVE.
* ROUND ONE:  Fungus casts spells and disappears.  One casts a spell at Affy and a spiritual weapon appears next to her and hits.  Another attacks Relys and hits.  Relys shifts into a Saber and pounces on the leader.  Misses with a claw.  Kaizo attacks one and misses, then attacks again and hits, then attacks again and misses.  Ridley then steps forward and casts hunters mark on one and shoots her and hits twice. The leader attacks the Saber and hits once.  Affy runs toward one and attacks and hits twice.  The one hit by Ridley’s bolts points at Ridley and hits twice.  The one on Kaizo attacks Kaizo with a thunderwave.  The last one holds staff out and casts a spell on the saber and misses.  
* ROUND TWO:  Fungus appears and moves to the Leader and casts a spell, hexing her, and poison sprays her and she’s knocked prone and he does not blink.  Fungus has to make a saving throw and does. Spiritual weapon attacks the saber and hits.  Weapon attacks Ridley and misses.  Affy has to make a wisdom save and does.  Relys pounces on the leader and hits with a claw and misses with a bite.  Kaizo flips back and attacks the one on him and fumbles, losing his reaction.  He then hits and hits twice more. Ridley hunters mark the one he had before and shoots arrows, hitting and killing her.  Pixie dust floats into the air when she dies and he sees a ring fall from chest height and land on the ground.  He then shoots the one on Kaizo and hits him, knocking him back 10’…and then he moves away.  The leader stands up, pulls out a seed and pops it on the ground and a gate opens up - she looks like she might want to make a move to the portal (uses dodge).  Affy attacks the one on her and hits it twice, killing it.  It also turns into pixie dust and she sees a dagger fall to the ground as everything else disappears…and she dashes toward the middle of the circle.  One bad guy looks at Fungus and points his staff and casts a spell and hits.  Another moves to the middle, and points at Fungus and misses with their spell.  
* ROUND THREE:  Fungus attacks the leader and misses with poison spray.  And he misty steps toward the portal…blocking the way.  The leader fails a saving throw and takes damage, while the other mage is okay.  Bad guy draws out a dagger with a glowing blade and rushes Fungus and misses.  Spiritual weapon attacks Affy and misses.  Relys attacks the leader and misses.  Kaizo runs up to the leader and attacks and hits her, killing her.  When she dies, pixie dust flies into the air and plate mail drops on the ground.  He then attacks another one and hits him three times. Ridley moves his hunters mark and shoots and hits twice, killing him.  Pixie dust floats up and a dagger falls to the ground.  Affy dashes over and hits one twice.  One throws down a seed and opens a portal and tries to step through - Affy misses her opportunity attack, as does Fungus.  The being passes through the portal.  The last one points at Fungus and casts a spell and hits and runs toward the portal.
* ROUND FOUR:  Fungus tries to poison spray her…and misses. Relys pounces on her and misses. Kaizo attacks her and misses three times, but hits once, killing her.  Pixie dust flies up and a staff falls…the staff has a valuable gem in it. The portal disappears. 
We collect the items that fell:  2 daggers, plate mail, a ring, and a staff.  Affy questions the power of the budplug…and Fungus tells the group that it is very powerful - it hums with power.  Daggers have gems (amber colored stone and citrine - 50-150gp each), staff has an emerald (25-75gp).  The ring is magical, but no one can identify it (pearl was lost by Fungus and Feywoven Glyphic cube).  Plate has sparkles of reinforcement on it - not magical (it is an adamantine plate).  Affy will need to take it to a smith to have it fitted.  
Kaizo questions is this what “coming alive means” - and Fungus says this is because they imbued the budplug. Affy recalls Lord Selbrenan was the name mentioned…the one who wants the budplug.  Fungus realizes that the Feywoven Glyphic cube and budplug are associated with the fey…and the sprite came out, also fey.  He just doesn’t understand seelie vs. unseelie.  He knows he’s been given power, but will have to pay off his debt at some point with his patron. 
We start to clear out and the Realmwarden run in (Ridley hides), asking what is going on.  Affy says all is well here.  One looks at her (she’s not sure why)...and they investigate the area, but the group cleaned it up.  Affy talks to him as they are leaving…he says that people would like to meet me.  He says he needs to talk to others before he follows up with me…says that some witnesses said it looked like we were fending off creatures from the stars.  Affy gets his name Aziar.  He says he will find her at the Pale Lantern.
We go back to the Lantern and sleep.  Get a rest.  Ridley sleeps with the ring (+1 ring of protection).
37th of Talil
Next day we wake up to try to sell the daggers.  We get 100gp and a 100gp pearl in exchange for the 2 daggers.  We sell the staff for 50gp.  We go to the smith - to get the armor fitted to Affy for 100gp.  Kaizo, Relys and Ridley each get 16gp.
We then go visit the Magistrate and he says he will grant the land to us, but needs to keep it on the down low.  Kaizo needs someone to get the place set up to make it a resort.  And he says he has just the person - Kesephion.  He is the best project manager.  He will meet us for lunch today.  He will hire the right people.  He will meet us at the Leaf Light for lunch.
On the way to meet him, Ridley spins the cube…Affy and Fungus are close (Affy puts her stuff to the side, as does Ridley).  Ridley rolls 32 [RICHES]...and he and Affy add 2x money, Fungus gets 3x. 
We meet Kesephion for lunch.  The Three Elders Spa & Resort at Qual’dynn.  Kaizo wants to bring in the herbalist (Tanzian)...who can help with nightbuds.  We want to teach balance, harmony, and transcendence - the “Kaizo” way.  He says that for 100gp, he can get started and hire people.  And he will need to meet with Kaizo again in a few stretches to go over everything.  Kaizo gives him 100gp and Fungus gives him 50gp more.  He asks how he can contact us.  Affy mentions going through Ham…and Kaizo says or through the herbalist.  
We retire to the Pale Lantern in the evening (after chilling all during the afternoon).  The door opens and Cerul appears… 
– The End of the Session –
