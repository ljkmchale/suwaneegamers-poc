# Session 32 - Adore Is Up In Smoke

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: August 6, 2026
In-game Date:  17th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Caelora
We head out on the road.  As we ride, we cross a river and are talking about our experiences.  Kaizo wonders what we are going to run into as we get close to the Southern Arch.  He’s concerned what he is going to find among the monks. He knows there is corruption and is worried about what is causing it.  He knows there is no balance in lands right now.  Fungus wonders how there was a gate to hell in an elven village and no one knew about it - he was surprised.  He also hopes that Affy’s dad is not going to be injured or killed since he decided to stay with Shotty Nel.  He also wonders if we should beat up Kulv (the moon-eyed one).  Is also wondering about Lindon and Tenanna.  Also thinks about his book and the nice lady that gave it to him.  He also loves his new fancy elven carriage. And he enjoyed breakfast.  And what does his new ring do? Cerul is worried about the monstrosity that killed Relys’ community.  He is worried it is doing more harm in the lands.  He will worry less when we know where it is and deal with it.  Relys is also thinking about this “monster” and is looking forward to putting this to rest.  This is the reason she is adventuring right now. We aren’t sure where the “bad one” is - we heard it was up north, but then got word there was something going on in Murfield.  Ridley is thinking about buying a house and a lodge to retire to.  Affy is concerned about selling her soul to the devil - and the deal that Kulv offered to maybe “fix” this.  She wants to find her dad, but is more concerned about her soul right now.  Kaizo also comments that he is missing his pipe.
Fungus finds out his ring is a Ring of Spell Storing with the fly spell inside.
We know that Pa got a headstart and said he was not going to the Southern Arch without us. Kaizo remembers a quaint campsite in the area named Rustlight - named so for a rusty lantern.  As we travel, we know it is getting cooler - especially at night.  The sun starts to set after a long day’s travels.  We are looking for the campsite.  We eventually see a clearing with a large rusted lantern.  Affy looks and sees wagon marks, but doesn’t see signs of Pa.  Campsite smells of pine needles and earth.   We find a spot to settle. Affy looks around for Kulv - doesn’t see him either. She asks Relys if she sees him.  She doesn’t either.  In fact, there is no one here yet - it is early in the night. 
We set up camp.  Fungus sings.  Kaizo asks Fungus to retrieve a high limb for the campfire (he uses a cantrip).  Relys forages for herself, but everyone else eats a ration. Kaizo climbs up on top of the wagon to meditate.  Cerul and Fungus discuss spells, his shield, etc.  Affy sits by the campfire.  We see a half dozen people coming down the path - all dressed alike.  They are dressed in loose, bulky clothing to keep warm.  They each have packs, except one guy who has a really big backpack.  They are all elves. They look our direction and give us a nod, but then go set up their own camp.  Fungus goes over to introduce himself - Cerul tags along.  Fungus tells him his name and asks who they are and their names and where they are going.  They reveal they are going to Cealora and he offers to tell them about it.  He asks what they are cooking tonight and offers to help them. Fungus and Cerul notice they are monks and Cerul thinks they are from Adore. Cerul tells them that we are traveling with a monk, too - he is meditating on the top of the carriage. He thinks Kaizo is from Adore.  Fungus comments that we have heard there is trouble that way. Cerul notices that the big pack was placed in a protective place.  Cerul tells them about the rumors of trouble at the Southern Arch - they claim they are not familiar with the troubles.    Cerul then guesses they are from Adore and they say “yes, yes we are from Adore.”  He then tells them about disturbances in the forest and they said they don’t know much about it, but they get a sense that these monks are not forthcoming (one guy nudges another to shut up as he starts to speak).  
Meanwhile, Ridley is tending to his horse: Relys goes to the edge of the forest to pray, holding her stone. Affy sits by the fire.  Kaizo is still meditating. Fungus takes a bowl of food to Kaizo and wakes him up (Fungus eats the food).  They tell him that they are from Adore and are not full of information.
Kaizo walks over wearing a coat he got from Fungus and says hello.  Kaizo asks where they are from - they answer from the monastery at Adore.  Lots of intros and bowing. Kaizo mentions Master Lang.  Then asks why they are leaving Adore.  They reply “to stretch their legs.”  And Kaizo challenges that - but they are not intimidated.  Affy picks up what is going on and walks over. Kaizo tries to survey the monks to see who might be the weakest link - he has a hard time picking one, but decides to pick out the cook (he thinks he is the most junior).   Kaizo tries to talk to him more about where he is from.  That guy  wants to walk around the campsite with him.  Ridley goes into stealth to try to sneak away, but knows that they probably took inventory of who was in the camp when they arrived.  Kaizo doesn’t feel threatened - just feels like they are being cautious.
Kaizo walks with the cook - asks him lots of questions.  The guy dances around answers, but he finally asks how Kaizo heard there was trouble at Adore.  Kaizo says he picked it up on the road. He asks if there is any truth to it?  And the monk asks if Kaizo has good intentions.  Kaizo affirms this and the guy reveals that there is trouble there.  They were sent to Caelora by Elandri Avunahorn, the spiritual leader there.  Kaizo knows this person - he knew her back when.  Kaizo asks how she is doing and he is told she is not doing well. Well, she is doing well, but the other leaders not so much. He says that half of the monks are acting out of character, but he cannot disclose more of his mission. He doesn’t know much more than that, but people are distancing themselves from each other, dividing into two clear groups. Kaizo asks for advice, and the monks tell him to pass Adore by. Kaizo presses for more information, and the monk says he fears for the monastery’s downfall, making Kaizo promise to keep it to himself. 
The chef monk walks over to Relys and asks what she found foraging, and if it could add flavor to his stew. She explains the herbs she found, and offers them to him.
Ridley positions himself in a tree to watch everything - as a lookout guard if needed.
Affy asks them about if they are fearful and comments how they have no weapons.  They comment that they “are protected.”  Affy comments how they don’t have any big guys like me and Fungus - and how that pack looks really big for them.  Fungus asks them what they have in it and they don’t answer, but instead ask him what is in his pack. He spends a long time revealing his contents.
After a while, Kaizo spends some time bidding the monks goodnight.   We decide to take watch.  Fungus comments that Ridley is always hiding.  Affy says he is watching - no worries.  She offers to take the first watch with Ridley.  Then Relys and Cerul on second watch.  Then Fungus and Kaizo last watch.
First watch - the stew the monks made smells very good - very enticing.   Affy communicates with Ridley telepathically here and there, but otherwise they both just stand watch.  Nothing happens and they eventually wake up the other two and then go to sleep.
Relys shares with Cerul that his grandmother’s spirit is in her stone and asks Cerul to take a look at it.  He thinks initially that there is dark, necromantic energy associated with the stone.  His insight tells him that there is a connection in the stone with a supernatural power.  But the stone is not dark.  But he is concerned that it could manifest itself in a negative way - that something could lash out. He feels the pain coming from it, but can’t sense much more than that.  He is disturbed and doesn’t know if someone who is exposed to this stone long term might corrupt the holder.  They discuss the motivations Relys has - that putting her family to rest will be good, but she shouldn’t hold those emotions too tightly.  That justice is better than revenge. Cerul just comments that there is a fine line that she shouldn’t cross.  They notice the monks have a watch, too.  Fungus is asleep with the monks and Kaizo is meditating with them. 
Cerul and Relys finish watch and wake up Fungus and Kaizo. As they pass the carriage they hear some strange noises coming from the carriage, but don’t investigate.  They then go back into the carriage to go back to sleep.
Fungus scrapes the remains of the stew from the pot.  Kaizo shares his mead. Fungus summons Tannana - he realizes that he can look through her eyes.  He gets Tanana to look through their big pack.  She finds that there are lots of scrolls inside. Fungus tells Kaizo and he wonders why they are taking them.  He thinks they are likely the most valuable scrolls from Adore.  
In the carriage, the others sleep, but Affy feels very cold and feels black water rising.  As she opens her eyes, she sees the devil.  She sees her friends being pulled away from her in chains.  She shakes her head, knowing she couldn’t do anything.  The devil leans in and says “I have yours, until you can provide a soul more powerful.” She feels the grip of chains of pulling her in…as she wakes up with the cold and black water clinging to her thoughts.  Then she feels something warm on her breasts. She pulls out the parchment that was put there.  There is writing in a dark language, but it resolves as she looks at it.    Affy tells them she has this paper, but only Ridley and Affy are the only two who can read it.  This is what it says:
This is a contract from the devil.   
By the cold waters of Stygia and the authority vested in me,
I already hold claim upon the soul of Az’efal Fairhand.
In recognition of this claim, and in the interest of mutual advancement, the following terms are offered:
Should the undersigned locate and freely present to me a soul of greater utility, value, or potential than her own, I shall release her from the existing claim and, in addition, grant her such knowledge and power as befits the exchange.
This offer remains open until accepted or until I choose to collect what is already mine.
To bind these terms, the undersigned shall sign below in her own blood.
— Ducrurith, of the Styx


She tells Cerul and Relys.  Fungus and Kaizo walk in and hear some of this. Discussion about this.  The thought is that Affy will have to try to trick someone else to offer their soul, and that maybe the devil might have someone in mind who he has a mission for. Cerul thinks this is bad business. Affy decides to hold off for a little while.  Ridley thinks that maybe if we were to kill the devil the contract would be voided. Fungus tells her to sign it and let’s move on - tells her to think of him as a “patron.”  The group decides to go.  Fungus yells “take care of your scrolls” to the monks as we pack up to leave (they look around nervously).
We pack up. Fungus drives the carriage and Kaizo and Affy sit inside.  Cerul walks next to the carriage.  Relys and Ridley ride horses. 
The group travels and smells wood burning and ash.  We see Adore on the horizon covered in flames and smoke.  We continue forward and try to rush.  We want to see if there are survivors.  Marching order:  Kaizo dashes with Ridley, Relys shifts into a bird to scout.  Fungus next.  Then Affy and Cerul in the back. 
Kaizo wonders if he messed up - if those monks were legit.
The temple is blackened.  Smoke rolls across the grounds (dim light inside of the smoke ring).  There are shapes forming ahead who see us and IT IS INITIATIVE - Combat vs Corrupted Monks
* ROUND ONE:  We see emerging from the smoke some creatures.  Ridley runs over to the nearest monk casts hunters mark, and stabs it with his dagger, hitting.  He then attacks again, missing.  Then fires his crossbow and hits. Kaizo moves up into the bush and pops out to attack a monk.  He misses twice.  Then tries his flurry of blows and misses twice.  He then jumps inside the bush (half coverage). Affy dashes forward with a bonus action and then attacks, fumbling, losing her reaction.  She then attacks again and hits. The monk attacks Ridley and hits twice. Another monk runs up and attacks Affy and misses twice.  Another monk runs up to Fungus and attacks and hits.  Then attacks and crits, and Fungus loses half his hit points after the crit damage. Another monk runs forward to try to punch Kaizo and hits, stunning him. The second attack misses.  Cerul casts spirit guardian. Fungus moves away (giving the monk an opportunity attack that hits, imposing damage - as Fungus imposes damage from that hit) and then misty steps near the bush where Kaizo is (and gives another one an opportunity attack which hits, but also imposes damage back).  Suddenly another wave of monks crashes out of the smoke and runs forward.  One attacks Ridley and hits (and he drops hunters mark) then attacks again and misses. One runs forward to attack Affy and misses twice.  Another runs forward to hit Fungus and does (but also takes damage). The second attack hits as well. Another one tries to attack Kaizo (who imposes disadvantage) and misses twice.  Relys casts a spell and is now glowing and flies into the smoke. Suddenly another two monks join the fight.  One attacks Ridley and hits.  Another one attacks Fungus and hits. 
* ROUND TWO: Ridley hits one of the monks and hits once, misses twice.  He disengages and moves away toward Cerul.  Kaizo is stunned, but comes out of it at the end of his turn. Affy attacks one and crits, killing him.  She then attacks another and hits twice, inflicting some decent damage.  She stays put telling them “you guys missed the first time - you should stay here and try again.”  One of the monks tries to move out of Cerul’s spirit guardians and runs to attack Affy and misses twice. Another tries to attack her and misses twice.  Another monk attacks Kaizo and misses twice.  That one disengages and moves away from the bush.  The others disengage and move away from Affy. Cerul runs up, moving his spirit guardians, and four of the monks have to make WIS saving throws. He then casts radiance of the dawn and they must make CON saving throws.  Then he uses his bonus action and heals Ridley.  Fungus goes invisible and then drinks a healing potion.  One disengages and runs away, but Affy swings, misses, and it still takes damage.  He then attacks Ridley and misses twice.  Another one runs to Ridley and attacks and hits Ridley, despite Cerul’s warding flare.   Then moves away from Ridley.  One attacks Kaizo and misses, but then hits. He makes a save, but still takes damage. Another one attacks Kaizo and misses twice. Relys flies back from the smoke and to the bush and transitions into a scorpion from her owl position.  Then attacks one and hits it, grapples it and inflicts damage from her moonlight, then more damage from her elemental fury.  Then attacks the other one and hits it and he takes radiant damage and is grappled. Then uses her tail to attack the one on the left and inflicts piercing damage and poison damage, plus radiant damage. A monk close to Relys attacks and misses twice and disengages.  Then another moves in to attack Cerul and misses twice and misses twice.
* ROUND THREE: Ridley attacks and hits, then moves and hits again.  Kaizo moves out of the bush and one gets an opportunity attack with disadvantage, and misses.  Kaizo then attacks one and hits.  Kaizo then attacks again and fumbles and falls prone, but stands up again.  Then tries flurry of blows and hits, but then misses.  Affy moves up and attacks one and misses, but then hits 3 times, using an action surge, and kills him. One monk enters Cerul’s spirit guardians circle and takes radiant damage.  It then attacks Cerul and hits, inflicting damage and it then disengages and moves away. One moves up to attack Kaizo and misses twice then disengages and moves away.  Cerul casts radiance of the dawn again and can go after four monks.  He then moves forward and his spirit guardians hit both. Fungus attacks a monk with poison spray and crits, killing it. He then uses a bonus action to heal himself. One disengages and moves and attacks Ridley and hits twice.  One then attacks Cerul and misses. Another moves up to attack Relys and misses.  Relys tries to squeeze the one in her claw and misses.  She then attacks with her tail and misses.  Then uses the other claw and hits, imposing radiant and lightning damage. The other monks attack.  One attacks Kaizo with disadvantage and misses twice and disengages. One moves up and attacks Ridley and hits twice.  Then it moves into the smoke as it disengages.
* ROUND FOUR:  Ridley attacks the one on him and misses, then hits, then crits on his last hit. Kaizo attacks the monk in the claw and misses once, then hits. Then uses flurry of blows and misses, then hits and kills the monk. Affy attacks one and kills him, then attacks another and kills it.  Two monks run away.  Cerul moves forward and hits one with spirit of guardians and it takes damage, killing it. Fungus casts poison spray on the remaining monk and crits, killing her, melting her skin. 
The smoke starts to drift away and a figure steps out into view.  He has a staff in one hand and he raises up another hand.  He is covered in soot and blood and he steps forward.  His face comes into view.  This is the prisoner from the wagon, Rueh’unar Heildove.  He says “Peace - I am not your enemy here.” 
– The End of the Session –
