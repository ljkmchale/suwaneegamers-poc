# Session 34 - Signs of Vhaulmast

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: September 8, 2026
In-game Date:  19th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Southern Arch
Approaching the Southern Arch
Cerul knows this is what he saw in his vision.  We look at the runes. Cerul and Fungus look at it, but can’t make them out, but thinks it means “breaker of shells.”  Affy remembers an old legend from her mother, that she heard from her father, about giants descending from the mountains and going into Lake Tribathe and having to fight a giant dragon turtle.  Once this was taken care of, the giants built this arch with stones they brought down from the mountains.   This was folklore, but might be true.
There is a place called the Dockhouse Inn on the riverbank that looks welcoming for travelers.  There is also a ferry present at the dock. We see a familiar wagon and know that Pa and his family are here.  We see people moving around on the docks.  Looks like some may have just disembarked from the longboat.  The inn is stone.  We smell good food coming from inside.  Kaizo approaches some travelers who are passing by but ignore him.  Affy suggests they find Pa and Fungus wants to eat.
We approach the inn and Fungus shouts “Pa.”  People look over, but we don’t see he or his family.  The ferryman looks frustrated, but can’t say why.  We stable the 2 horses and bulas and find a place to put the carriage near Pa’s wagon.  We go inside. 
The stew smells really good - herbs and fish. We see a bar along the back wall and four long tables in the front.  There are stairs going up and a few smaller tables nearby.  There is a big hearth and a stewpot over the fire.  We see a really large man in the back - as big as Fungus - with long white hair pulled back, soft peachy blue-ish skin.  He yells out “anywhere you like, be with you in a minute.”  
Fungus walks to look out the door.  He sees a short guy filling up a bowl of stew and realizes it is Pa.  Fungus picks him up and hugs him.  They talk and Fungus clues him in on what happened at the Abbey and in the forest.  Pa leads him over to the table with his wife and kids.  The group notices them all.  Pa tells Fungus that everyone at the Abbey was tense and tight…and they didn’t like it so they left.   He says they have been here for a day and have heard from travelers that it is unsafe to continue to Shademoor.  Pa says that he heard Gar - the big guy at the bar - talking about someone.  Ustina - came a couple of days ago - and she told Gar that she was expecting trouble ahead.  She left to go see and has not come back. 
Fungus says that he has an idea and will share it later.  Kaizo asks if he still has his shipment.  Affy says that we will escort them tomorrow - as the party has been paid to see them safely through.  Cerul and Kaizo comment on the ettins - but say not to worry.  The large guy comes over with bowls and throws them down and says that it is included with a room.  Asks how many rooms we need.  Relys notices that everything here seems a little bigger.  Fungus introduces himself to Gar.  Ridley pays for a room with 30 coppers, Fungus throws in a gold, and Affy gives him 3sp.  Gar gives us a spice jar to flavor the food.  
He comments how no one is coming back from Bloodbeak.  Ridley asks about Bloodbeak specifically.  He answers that no one has come in from that direction for 3 days.  Affy asks about Ustina - he briefly says she was here yesterday - she came in on the ferry and came to check out rumors of what she heard.  Kaizo asks about the boat - it goes to Lake Tribathe. Gar says we could talk to Thrand - the ferryman - about rumors. Affy again asks about specifics on Ustina.  Fungus asks if other travelers ask questions like we do.  He says not really.  He comments that for the past couple of days travelers have been up and down the river…not on the road.
Fungus then asks about the inn itself - how long the inn has been in the family.  He said it was passed down for a few generations for sure.  Ridley comments on his giant heritage.  Affy takes bowls and fills them up for the party - all except Relys since the stew is not vegetarian.  Gar gets drinks for the group.  
Kaizo asks Pa what the delivery to Shademoor is worth.  He says it is a lot.  Kaizo is trying to see what it would take to buy them out - he is anxious to get to Basctdelm.  The load is probably worth 1250gp.  Affy finishes her bowl and plans to walk outside with Ridley to talk to Thrand.  But before that happens, the door swings open and we hear the air blow in.  The ferryman walks in - thick moustache, auburn hair, sloshy boots.  He goes to the bar and leans over to Gar to refill his wineskin “fill this up - no one wants the south road - everyone wants the ferry.  They are wearing me out.”  Gar reassures him and tells him it will be okay.
Ridley walks over:  “I’ll keep this short and sweet” as he smells the liquor in the wineskin.  Thrand says he likes it that way.  Ridley asks what is going on at Bloodbeak.  He says that no one is going that way.  He says “it” has pushed up as far as Windbreak.  He isn’t sure what the danger is, but people are dying out there.  Thrand asks who Ridley is - and he responds that “we are the King’s Outlanders.”  Affy jumps in and tells him that the party will take care of his problem tomorrow - we are heading down south.   He says the Bloodbeak family are rugged people.  They have a compound - 3 houses - they raise blackberries.  They are a tough stock.  If people aint making it past them, good luck taking care of that problem. Affy asks about their numbers and he isn’t sure - but there are 3 families.  Ridley asks if you can get to Bloodbeak in a day and he says you can on a fast horse, but otherwise it is a two-day trip. 
Fungus snuggles up to Pa and his family at the table. He is trying to ease their minds about the dangers ahead (Pa, wife Lila, 2 kids).  He scares them at first (rolls a 1), but then pulls out his book and shows them he can put their names in his “book of protection.” Affy tells them we can travel ahead - Relys chimes in that she will send a message back.  That way they won’t be in harms way until we clear the way.  Pa likes that idea. 
Kaizo and Ridley hear some guys at the bar chuckle “I didn’t think the Ruelendors let their dogs off the leash this far.”  Kaizo comments to Ridley if he will let that go.  Ridley doesn’t immediately rattle. Affy notices people in a corner table talking and they say something about Muirfield Woods.  They say the trees are black and slick - leaves drop before they are ready. Affy takes her ale that way.  She sees someone in wool and interrupts them.  They comment is she going to hurt them?  She dodges it a couple of times, but they continue.  So she blows up at them - calling them names and making slurs and leaves them.  They send over an apology beer with Gar.
Meanwhile, Ridley goes over to talk to the guys who made the Ruelendor comment.  He says he just knows things. Ridley thinks this guy does get around and know things.  Ridley looks for markings or anything identifiable on them.   Ridley asks them if they have business with the Umbral Order and he says he doesn’t (he knows them, but probably isn’t one of them).  The guy offers to buy Ridley a drink, asking how he got out of the Borilien Sway and Caelora.  Ridley tells them he no longer works for the Rueldendors - he’s having some “off time.”  The guy says he didn’t mean to insult Ridley (but Ridley knows he is full of crap - he wanted to insult him and get his attention).  Ridley walks over to Gar and gives him 1pp.  He asks for some of Thrand’s stash.  He wants it poured into a glass and topped off with ale.  He then gives this glass to the guy.  And they both take a sip of their cups at Ridley’s insistence.  The guy comments how strong it is, but Ridley continues to persuade him to drink.  And he does.  Ridley is trying to get him drunk.  The guy then says off to bed and gets up to walk to his room.  Ridley gets up and finds that the guy had dumped some of his drink on the ground.
Kaizo wants Cerul to send the monks that we encountered at the campsite a message “From Master Kaizo - we met at encampment.  Adore is destroyed but freed. Take relics to Talis in Qal’dynn.  New Monastery Sanctuary of the Three Elders.”  Cerul hears “An interesting interruption to my mediation.  We already turned them over to our spiritual elder in Caelora.  I’m not sure what authority you have to suggest this.”  Cerul then tells Kaizo what they said.  Kaizo ponders this and asks Cerul to send another message.  “Master Kaizo on the authority of Alandre.”  Cerul questions this, but sends this message for him.  Cerul hears back “Wow.  Two meditation interruptions. I, uh, sure - what is another 6 or 7 days to get somewhere.  And where do we go.”  Kaizo has him send “to The Sanctuary of the three elders to Talis - your new master.”  He then gets back “understood.”
Affy walks by the table with those guys and thanks them for the ale.  Those guys comment that they have trouble with their lumber in Muirfield…and could get it from Shademoor, but it is more expensive…but either way aren’t going that way anytime soon.  Affy tells them that she is heading that way tomorrow to take care of their problem.  He comments that he is too much of a pussy (repeating her words to her) and she says “maybe so” and picks up Relys and carries her to their room.  Cerul and Kaizo head to their room. Fungus falls asleep in the bar - typical - from too much drink.  Ridley goes to his room.  Gar then helps Fungus to his room at some point. 
When all are in their rooms, Ridley and Kaizo notice something above the door - as if a pin or something had been stuck at the top with a dead moth.  Kaizo asks what this is about.  Ridley says he thinks this is the wizard chick (Eliyena).  Ridley and Kaizo remove the moths from the top of the doors and put them on Ridley’s room.  Affy comments out loud if Ridley is sure that he wants to sleep alone (and he tells her in his head that he will sleep in Fungus’ room).  He goes in and puts up the alarm spell.  After an hour he sneaks out, locks it behind him, and tries to find the guy’s room who he tried to get drunk at the bar.
He listens at every door in the hallway. He can’t hear much, so attempts to start picking locks. He goes to door one and picks the lock and sees Pa and his family.  He then relocks the door.  He tries the next door.  He opens it with ease.  He hears snoring but can’t see anything.  He hears two people breathing in the room and shuts the door.  He then tries another door and gets in.  He sees a lot of stuff that makes him thinks this is Thrand’s room.  He looks out the window and sees Thrand on the edge of the dock with his feet in the water.  He then thinks the room across the hall is Gar’s.  He tries the room next to that one and has no issue.  It is empty.  He then goes to the last room.  The lumberjacks appear to be in there (the ones who talked to Affy).  He doesn’t see who he is looking for.  He goes back to the “empty” room and stealths in and tries to smell to see if he hears or sees anything.  He hears people coming down the hall outside.  He goes to the window and jumps out, turning into a bat, shutting the window as he does.  He then flies as a bat and flies outside the window where he heard snoring.  He looks in that window.  It seems to be a family.  It dawns on him that the guy is not in the inn.  He flies and searches the stables and finds nothing.  He shifts back and walks down to Thrand at the edge of the water.  He finds him sipping tea in the moonlight.
He asks Thrand about that man.  He points and says he headed up river in his own boat.  Ridley seems satisfied.  Ridley tells Thrand that that guy is wanted for questioning by the King’s Outlanders.  He walks back to the Inn.  LONG REST.
It’s the next day, the 20th of Paramor, 1246
The group comes down to breakfast.  Ridley tells everyone that he broke into all the rooms last night.  Relys and Fungus give him grief.  Ridley tells them that he had a hunch that he knew more than he let on.  He thinks the moth pins might be tied to this guy.  Relys takes the side of the apprentice - that coming after Ridley would be justified.  Fungus agrees.  Fungus them tells Gar about the moth pins, but he knows nothing of it.  Gar admits that it is creepy.  
The group discusses some of the gold they have and splits it up.  As that happens, Thrand walks in upset that his patrons were late coming to the dock.  He heads upstairs. Cerul finalizes the plan with the group about asking Pa to stay another day or two.  The group hears Thrand yelling at people upstairs.  We see folks come down stairs and see other travelers outside to get on the boat - who showed up at the Southern Arch this morning.
We walk outside. We see boats tied off that are new.  See new horses.  Kaizo asks around to see where folks  came from.  No one came from the southeast.  Pa finally emerges.  Cerul tells him that the group will ride ahead and face the danger alone and asks them to stay at the Inn, telling him that he will message them - that “he will hear him in his head.” Pa says “you are a wise man, but asks if he can get a warning since he might be in the loo.”  Cerul gives him a look and then Pa thanks him and the group for his trouble. 
Fungus asks folks to write their name in his book of protection - all but Ridley and Fungus. The group mounts up, takes the carriage and heads out.  We start down the road.  Relys decides to shift into an owl and fly ahead.  First four hours passes uneventfully.  We continue with Relys flying ahead for another four hours.  We continue to pass no one.  She sees woods in the distance.  Nothing of interest.  We go another four hours that way.  Relys starts to see and smell something ahead.  It is decay that she sees and smells.  There is a ridgeline that she sees and off in the distance, twilight starts to fade into dusk, it looks like we are going to make it to a campsite.  She sees debris at a campsite.  Sees old ruins - maybe a non-functioning windmill - up a hill on the ridgeline.  She spots a wagon turned on its side. She returns to the group and tells us that we are a mile out from the debris and overturned wagon.  Kaizo and Ridley and decide to stealth ahead once we get a half-mile out.  Relys wants to commune with nature to see what might be ahead (the spell gives her some knowledge - of a creature, portal, and settlement - in the area ahead).  Kaizo and Ridley start to stealth ahead (24 and 27 on their rolls).   Ridley and Affy can talk in their heads. 
The guys are met with the smell as they approach the campsite.  It is nasty.  They notice a corpse on the side of the road, armor straps hanging loose, pack torn open, arm gone, the female body pulled apart.  They are not to the campsite yet.  This body is a few yards ahead.  They look around to see if this a trap - looking for something hiding.  Kaizo hears a growling noise behind them- heading to the rest of the party.  He sees a huge shadow.  They start to whisper to each other, discussing that they should both climb trees near the dead body.   But they are discovered. IT IS INITIATIVE
* ROUND ONE:  They see a large creature that looks like a bear, but it has red eyes.  The fur seems like mange, with stone-like skin falling off.  This is an all-black bear.  It emerges from behind bushes.  It is dusk (almost dim light). The guys agree to run.  Ridley uses a bonus action to hunter’s mark the bear (and he knows that the bear has no resistances or immunities) and he moves away from the party down the road and shoots the bear.  He hits and pushes it back 10’, inflicting damage. Then shoots it again and hits, inflicting more damage and pushing it back another 10’. The bear moves forward and dashes to the road and then charges Kaizo.  He is not knocked prone, but the bear is next to him.   Another bear runs out of the room and dashes forward and knocks Ridley prone.  Kaizo attacks the bear on him and hits twice and uses his bonus action and a point to disengage and dash away. 
* ROUND TWO: Ridley stands up and moves to get away, provoking an opportunity attack. The bear hits and does 20 slashing damage before Ridley dashes into the woods, casts ‘pass without trace’,  and successfully hides. Kaizo also takes off, outrunning the bear.  They think this body might be Ustina, the lady the heard about from the Inn.
Ridley messages Affy and tells them they were ambushed. Affy tells the group.  We decide to go forward.  Kaizo feels like this was a creature from the Shadowfel - thinks the creature was touched by the eerie Shadowfel.  Relys gains no information from her ritual…just thinks there might be a cabin in the woods about 3 miles away, but no settlements, no creature of the origins she could sense, no portals.  Ridley tells Affy that the dead body was likely Ustina.  
As the group moves forward, Kaizo eventually reaches the group.  He briefs the group about the encounter, mentioning they were Shadow-touched. Cerul suggests we keep going - ask if anyone is hurt.  As the group moves forward, Ridley checks out the dead body (using pass without trace to help).  He sees torn leather, in a patch of grass next to her, sees a flash of bronze - this is a locket on a broken leather necklace, shaped like a book.  He sees that the locket opens and manipulates it.  He sees the initials “UT” and hears the words 


“If anyone finds this then the rumors in Basctdelm are true.  The wildlife here is wrong and cursed.  
Vhaulmast is sweeping the land. Do not travel the road alone.”  
Ridley thinks she looks like an adventuring type.  She seems like a tinkerer since she made her locket speak. 
Ridley hears the group coming.  He thinks the bears have moved off further down the road - he thinks they are patrolling.  He comes out of hiding and stands in the road. Ridley shares the message he received.  We all think about this message - Vhaulmast must be the name of the thing that came through the portal and what wiped out Relys’ village, Iparos.  Relys doesn’t recall learning about Vhaulmast.  But she and Cerul think that might be the name of a pet that was named by the goddess Tornia. They reveal this to the party. 
Arrival at Windbreak Camp
The group discusses needing to escort Pa at some point. But decides to investigate the campsite that Relys scouted earlier.  The group rounds the bend and sees that the ridge is blocking the winter breeze, but there is an eerie feeling.  We find evidence of a scorched camp - this is a burned campsite.  We find other dead bodies.  This is Windbreak Camp, but it is destroyed.  We see the windmill on the hill and shifting shadows.  As we step into the campsite, we see bears on the hills, two are injured.  IT IS INITIATIVE.
* ROUND ONE: Ridley moves forward, casts hunter’s mark on the one he sees and shoots two arrows and misses twice.  Then he moves further away from the hill. That bear then moves along the ridge and charges the group and attacks Kaizo, knocking him prone. Fungus casts some armor on himself and casts poison spray on the bear that attacked Kaizo, hitting and inflicting damage, and knocking him prone. Kaizo stands up and attacks the bear with advantage, hitting with force damage. Then attacks again and hits with more force damage.  Then uses a flurry of blows with his bonus action and hits again two more times.  One of the new bears runs and dashes forward and charges Ridley on horseback.  The horse goes prone and Ridley is knocked off the horse but is not under the horse, and is on his feet. Cerul casts bless on Affy, Kaizo and Ridley and then sprouts wings and flies up 10’  The prone bear tries an opportunity attack on Cerul as he flies up and fumbles. The brown bear, wounded from before, runs forward, dropping down into the campsite.  The final bear runs along the top of the ridge, closing ground towards the party’s carriage.  Relys rides her horse near Fungus and wild shapes into a scorpion and casts bright light on herself.  Affy turns around and attacks the prone bear, hitting once and missing, then using action surge to hit twice again.
* ROUND TWO:  Ridley is hit by the bear’s hollow gaze and has disadvantage and is frightened. He uses steady aim to attack the bear and misses and is cursed by the bear taking 22 points of necrotic damage. He then tries to shoot another arrow and hits, inflicting damage and pushing it back 10’.  He then tells his horse to stand up and run away, but he does not run away.  The white bear stands up and attacks Kaizo and Affy, who both have to make CON saving throws, but still take 14 points of psychic damage each. Fungus casts hold monster on the brown bear, but he saves. He dismounts from the bulas and moves forward, toward the brown bear and turns bigger.  Kaizo succeeds on a WIS save then attacks the white bear and hits with force damage.  Then hits again and does more damage then uses flurry of blows to hit again twice more.  A bear runs up to Ridley and attacks, grappling with a critical hit, also hitting the horse, killing it and damaging Ridley. He then walks up to Relys and hits her, with Ridley grappled in one hand taking a squeeze.  Relys takes a fair amount of damage.  The bear makes his CON throw against Relys.  Cerul has to make a WIS save and does.  He then tries to hit a bear with his sword and does, inflicting damage.  He uses a charge on his sword to heal Kaizo.  He uses a spell to heal Ridley. Brown bear runs up to Fungus and hits him, but takes 25 points of damage as he does. Fungus is grappled and takes bludgeoning damage.  He then bites Fungus, but takes another 25 points of damage as he does.  The last bear on the ridge jumps down and charges Affy, but she saves.  He then grapples her. He then bites Affy, but her damage is reduced by Cerul.  Relys makes her WIS save then moves to the other side of the bear near her.  She misses with the first claw and she has to make another WIS save and fails, as a chill runs down her spine and she takes 16 points of necrotic damage.  She then attacks with another claw and misses.  She then attacks with a stinger and hits. Affy makes both WIS saves then attacks the white bear, even though she has two on her.  She kills the white bear.  Then attacks the other bear, hitting it twice and sapping it, also inflicting acid damage.
– The End of the Session –
