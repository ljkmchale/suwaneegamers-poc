# Session 10 - Prominent Rel’Udomina

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  June 25th, 2025
In-game Date:  30th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Rel’Udomina Hideout beneath Qal’dynn
Rel’Udomina Hideout
Lady has a tabard with Rel’Udomina, cloak that is “coat-like”...there are 2 slender dog-like creatures in the room, too.
ROLL FOR INITIATIVE
ROUND ONE: Lady tries to hit Affy with a spell and misses and takes cover.  Kaizo flips around the desk and gets face-to-face with her and hits once and misses once.  Affy hits.  One of the dogs trades places with the lady and then attacks Kaizo and bites him and knocks him prone. The other dog transposes with her and then attacks Affy - hits and knocks her prone.  Ham runs up and tries to command her and fails, moves back and the small dragon bites him.  The dragon then casts a spell on Affy and is incapacitated until the end of her next turn…and it disappears.  Cerul runs in and “shrouds up” and the dogs (and invisible dragon) have to make a saving throw (one dog is frightened until the end of Cerul’s next turn)...then casts a spell and it hits the lady and dogs. [Relys tries to hide…seeing a large creature (Fungus) in the hall ahead of her..she moves up]. Fungus runs in the room and hits her, knocking her prone - then steps back.
ROUND TWO:  Lady stands up and casts a spell, but it misses Cerul and Affy.  She then twists something on her hand and pops up in the hall.  Kaizo runs to the doorway and misses his attack. Affy has to randomly run, but now is no longer incapacitated. Ham tries to cast a spell on her, but something interferes…and he takes damage, before he blinds her (until the end of his next turn).  The dragon shows up in the room - and casts spell on Kaizo and Fungus, incapacitating them until the end of their next turn.  Cerul casts a spell and then brings forth a spiritual weapon, which misses. Relys turns into a bear and bites the dragon, then tries to claw it and misses.  Fungus runs in the opposite direction.
ROUND THREE:  Lady is blind, hears the bear, but knows where she is in the hallway.  She starts to run down the hall.  Kaizo runs into the hall, disoriented.  Affy runs into the hall when Cerul points in that direction and hits the lady.  Ham sends a spell toward the dragon and misses (he’s at disadvantage since it is invisible).  The dragon pops into space, creating a mirror image, which Relys can get an opportunity attack on and misses - then disappears.  Cerul has the spiritual weapon attempt to hit the lady and misses; then hits her with his rapier, as he takes 5 points of psychic damage.  Relys claws the remaining dragon…and it disappears as she hits it (it was an illusion) and then tries to bite the lady and misses.  Fungus runs to the hall and targets poison spray the lady and misses.  
ROUND FOUR:  The lady is trapped between the bear and Cerul/Affy.  She attacks the bear and hits with lightning damage.  Kaizo runs up and attacks the lady and hits with his fury of blows, killing her.  Affy picks up the dead body, asking the bear “are you with her” and then carries her to the room with the intent to loot she and the room.  Ham starts looking around the room and finds a trap door under the bed in the room.  Cerul talks to the bear…and the bear shifts to her gnomish form and mentions that the dragon flew down the hall.  Kaizo walks down the hall with his pipe…drawing a breath and exhaling with bear-shaped smoke.  
Ham realizes that we are in the room where we discovered a ladder that led up and we heard snoring before.
In the hall, Cerul and Relys talk, Ham and Kaizo joining in.  Fungus and Affy stay in the room to loot the lady.  We take the tabard off.  She has a shiny, light weight chain mail set, and a ring.  Cerul checks out the dogs and thinks that they are fey “blinking” dogs.  Fungus casts a spell and realizes that the ring is Dunduar (you can’t sense other people who are wearing that ore and can message each other).  Fungus takes the ring and puts it on.  The chain looks to be made of mithril - Cerul takes the armor. Ham pockets the tabard. We find a pouch with 50gp (we each take 8gp).  We don’t find anything else.  
We have a discussion with Relys…why is she here (to seek information).  Ham reveals we are seeking an audience with the king (much to Affy’s dismay).  Affy tells her she can tag along, but won’t take responsibility for her.  
Group walks across the hall to a door…Ham listens first and hears nothing.  He opens the door and finds racks of provisions, grains, jars of pickles, crates, herbs, etc. These look like they were taken from the city.  We can see a door on the far left corner.  Fungus grabs 3 pickle jars, as Ham starts to destroy some of their supplies.  Three crates have dried meats, preserved fruits, powdered minerals (sulfur, charcoal).  We all take provisions/rations.  One crate has 4 flasks of oil (Fungus takes them).  A footlocker has boots and cloaks…they are elvish.  Fungus casts detect magic in the room.  Nothing comes across as magic.  Affy takes the crowbar and Fungus some caltrops.
Kaizo opens the door and stealths down and sees a dead end.  He realizes this is a place where there is a secret door.  He pulls and hears a click.  He sees a lit hallway that we have been in. He sees the door where we heard scraping, but never checked out.  We all follow where Kaizo is, but we don’t hear the scraping.  Affy opens the door…it is tight…and she pulls and we hear a jingle (keys hanging on a hook on the door).  We see a candelabra dripping wax hanging overhead.  We see 6 cells with iron bars and on the far end, a door.  The cells are 10x10. We hear a couple of “prisoners” talking.  
We don’t see a guard.  We see on the far cell on the far left a goblinoid creature.  On the second cell on the right we see a young half elf.  Affy and Ham walk up to the cells.  Half elf looks like he’s been in a while…has no belongings.  Keanus Evergaze…says “you aren’t with them, are you?”  Affy asks who he is…and he replies. Fungus offers to replace his candle.  Affy questions why they have him…he is the nephew of Entryvian (the ambassador, former diplomat). Ham wants to unlock him…asks if he can get us an audience with the king. He says he can.
Goblinoid creature asks to be released as well.  Kaizo questions him.  He says he was taken from the woods, likely captured to be fed to the dogs.  He asks for his socks behind the door (same as Keanus asked for his clothes behind the door, too).  His name is Fzoid.  Cerul opens the door and returns everything to them.  We release the goblin with a jar of pickles from Fungus.  We tell him how to leave…and he does.
Kaizo doesn’t think Keanus is who he says he is…rolls insight. 
We decide to walk to the pit.  As we walk, Kaizo interrogates Keanus.  Affy and Fungus lead the way. Ham and Kaizo are messaging in their heads about him…Kaizo doesn’t think Keanus is in good favor in the city. 
As we get to the pit…we see a natural circular room, 25’ diameter.  There is blood everywhere. There are lines of blood that lead to the pit.  It appears to be very deep…Cerul throws something down, but we don’t hear it hit the bottom for some time. Looks like a disposal pit.
We head back to the library (room 3). Affy listens at the door, hears crackling fire.  There is an elegant chamber with all kinds of clothing hung on hooks and mannequins.  There are huge candleholders and mirrors.   There is a circular round table in the center with skin dyes, perfumes, disguise kits, etc.  Fungus takes a lot of the items in this room.  These are clothing from various cities and towns all over Myrdae.  
We leave and advance to the guard again.  He tells us that “something is coming - we need to leave.  Can you show me how you came in so we can get out of here?”  He heard something that “doesn’t sound good” and “has never heard this before”- we tell him how to escape and he does.  We advance to the door ahead of us to take cover to see what might be coming down the spiral staircase.
The door opens into a large gathering hall with a raised dais and curtain….with lanterns (not sconces).  Laying there is a dead body (of a Rel’Udomina agent?).  Affy closes the door, allowing the group a chance to talk and strategize.   Affy loots the dead guy and he appears to be one of the Rel’Udomina (12sp, a dagger).  Keanus tries to leave and Ham can’t grab him.  The group takes off after him…he gets to the base of the stairs and Kaizo tries to stun him and he does, falling on the stairs. We had given chase and are now in the hallway.  We hear feet coming down the stairs and hear the echoes of boots and sharp clinks.  Sounds like a battle above.  
We see someone who sees Kaizo…who sees a Qal’dynn Realmwarden who says “Halt.  You shouldn’t be here.”  
– The End of the Session –
