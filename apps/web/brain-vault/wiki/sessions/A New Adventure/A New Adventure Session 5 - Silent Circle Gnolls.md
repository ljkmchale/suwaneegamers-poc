# Session 05 - Silent Circle Gnolls

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  March 20th, 2025
In-game Date:  26th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  O’naren
O’naren - 26th of Talil
Cerul meets up with Ro and Fungus as they are leaving the jewelers.  They went to head to one of the higher terraces to find the Oracle - Cerul is hoping to find a Phet connection and share information with them.  As they head in that direction, they pass the Gallery, but don’t stop.  They then walk by the Tannery & Fletcher - Fungus wants to find a collar for the wolf pup.  At the tannery, a female high elf greets them (Vyen Rainsong).  Ro and Fungus describe what they want for Jasper - leather with many holes; maybe something with green; tooling with his name.  It will be 5sp and Fungus pays.   
Meanwhile…Affy asks who are the Rel’Uldomina.  The captain says he doesn’t know, but is trying to find out.  He says this group is spreading around - show up as standard business men, but try to get information about towns.  We tell him about the mapmaker and his wife and child. We show him the map we got from the wife’s things.  We tell him that she and the boy are dead - killed by sellswords.  We tell him the mapmaker hired Kool’ok.  We tell him it all seems suspect.  We tell him we are going to help with the gnoll problem.  Kaizo’s insight tells him that we are all on the same page; that the captain opened up when Ham revealed his origins. He offers help with arms, blades, armor….tells us to come back and talk to Caddius should we need anything. 
Affy, Ham, and Kaizo leave and head toward the Jeweler - looking for the rest of the group.
The Oracle
Cerul, Ro, and Fungus head to the Oracle - a two-story round building, walls lined with scrolls and books, circular staircase.  They see a female (brown skin, silver hair, violet eyes, tattoos) - violet/crimson dress.  Cerul reveals that he is looking for a Phet member; Ro asks if their coming was foretold.  They get wine and tea.  The Oracle’s name is Zanasee Tsal’daree.  Ro recognizes the surname (famous/strong in the arcane arts).  Cerul starts to talk about the information that he has - important for those who defend justice and goodness. He pulls out the papers and info about the Broken Citadel, describing how the teleportation circles and gates have been used.  She says she will need to do some research and get back to them.  She looks at Fungus and tells him to seek Lofyr.  She gives Fungus the Charm of the Slumbering Grove (an amber gemstone).  Tells him to talk to Lofyr about it.  It is encased in the petals of the Nightbud.  Ro then asks her about her father’s plight and the jar of powder.  She exclaims that Iofyr would also be a source of information - that they are on the same path.  Ro tells her that she and Fungus are also “co-parenting” a dog, too.  Some discussion about the dog’s name.  Ro then shows her the amulet around her neck.  She says that she will need to study to see if she can come up with information about it, and if she does, will contact Ro.  Cerul then tells her about his past - coming up in the Abbey, but also a past with giants - and shows her trinkets that were supposedly gifts from his parents.  She doesn’t know anything about them and suggests that answers might be at the Abbey (Cerul says that he asked questions then).  Ro asks how she is able to see visions (and shares how it happens in her village with pig entrails).  She mentions that she uses the “Weave” to see things.  She expresses interest in meeting her village Wisdom to learn more of their ways. Cerul puts away his trinkets and asks everyone about the large white feather and what bird it may be from.  They head out.  
The group eventually meets up with each other - Fungus isn’t difficult to spot. As they walk by the Fletcher, Cerul asks about the feather and they say it doesn’t look like any bird/animal around this town.  Looks like it is from a big white, flying bird.  Groups brief each other about what they did.  Fungus tries to figure out what his stone is…can’t figure it out.  Thinks it is some type of receptor.  Kaizo thinks it isn’t from this world.
The Silent Circle
Group heads back to the silent circle area. Fungus sings us a song as we travel; Ham playing the Lute.  Twilight turns into night as we arrive there.  It feels different by night - blackened veins, stench of decay. Kasani sits unnaturally still…eyes glowing with green light.  Three gnolls sit there and shake, covered in spores.  We see a wailing mushroom next to everything.  It is initiative. 
Kaizo and Cerul run up. Cerul shoots a firebolt at a gnoll and misses. Affy runs around the side, weapon drawn.  Ham runs up and messages the mushroom. Ro tries to determine if Kasani is alive…looks like she has been invaded, thinks she is beyond help (influence of demonic gnolls, likely).  Kasani stands up and moves.  Fungus dashes around the other side.  Gnolls stand up and move. Kaizo attacks and misses.  Cerul moves in and casts a spell.  Ham casts a spell, blinds the druid. Ro casts spell on the druid.  Affy swings her sword and misses, but grazes the gnoll. Kasani tries to attack Cerul and hits, but he reacts with a shield and it misses.  Fungus tries to attack and misses.  He, Affy, and Kaizo are all hit, Kaizo taking damage to his foot. Kaizo attacks and hits. Cerul hits. Ro hits and kills the possessed druid.  Affy hits.  Ham whispers to the gnoll.  Fungus attacks a gnoll, misty steps on a rock above the mushroom and heals Affy.  Gnolls miss Affy.  Mushroom wails. Kaizo attacks a gnoll and misses.  Cerul casts spell on gnoll and it saves.  Affy kills a gnoll and hits another.  Ham whispers to one and it saves.  Ro moves and casts spell on a gnoll and hits and kills it with a firebolt.  Fungus tries to chill the incoming new gnolls - one is indifferent.  Remaining original gnoll swings at Ham and stumbles.  Kaizo runs up to hit and misses, but does an unarmed strike and hits.  One new gnoll walks off.  The other attacks Fungus with disadvantage and misses.  Cerul sends a firebolt to the mushroom and hits it and kills it.  Ham attacks an infected gnoll and kicks it.  Ro attacks the last original gnoll (nat 20) and almost kills it.  Affy then runs up and kills it. Fungus swings at it and misses.  Kaizo limps in the direction of the new gnoll.  Gnoll turns to leave and Fungus tries to hit it and misses.  Cerul tries to firebolt the fleeing gnoll…and misses.  Ro heals Kaizo.  Affy and Ham dash after the gnoll.  Fungus and Kaizo take a moment to chill.  Cerul hits him with a firebolt.  Affy moves Ham forward 30’.  Ham commands the gnolls to come back, they turn around.  Ro tries to hit one with a firebolt and misses.  Fungus and Kaizo are sitting and meditating.  One gnoll attacks Ham and crits.  The other runs up, but can’t attack.  Cerul attacks the familiar gnoll and hits. Ham attacks and gives inspiration to Cerul and kicks one. Ro tries to firebolt and misses.  Affy runs up and misses, but it takes grazing damage.  Fungus casts a spell and hits. Kaizo stands and moves forward and bonus dashes and hits. The one left hits Affy.  Cerul misses.  Ro hits. Affy hits.  Ham kills it. Fight is over.
Affy sees a distinct path from here leading to a potential gnoll camp.  We loot the bodies.  The druid has nice boots.  We look around the circle.  What was a nice, natural area is corrupted.  We find 7 Nightbud seeds - all that remain.  Kaizo protects the seeds and takes them in his pouch. We find a potion in the druid pouch…it is yellow liquid with a bobbing eyeball.  We discuss the wailing mushroom - was it part of the chaos? Affy tries to determine if the circle was an origination point for the blackening.  We determine that the druid was defending the circle and something else interfered and took over.  Ro and Affy and everyone try to burn the dead bodies and corruption in the circle (all except Fungus who is trying to identify items we found).   We start a fire and the fumes are unfamiliar - not something we have smelled before, likely a smell not of this world.  Fungus reveals that the boots are Boots of Elvenkind. And the potion is Clairvoyance.  Kaizo gets the boots; Fungus keeps the potion and grabs ears before we burn them.
We take a long rest here…there are no issues. Kaizo tracks the group down the trail. We see a cave up ahead with dark fungus vines.  We see blue smoke emerging from the cave mouth.  We hear “nasty angel blood” come from the cave.  This is where we stop for the night. 
– The End of the Session –
