# Session 12 - Ambassador Hamalex

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  July 17th, 2025
In-game Date:  31st of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  The Royal Palace
The Royal Palace
Thone looks like a swan with raised wings, cradling the king. Ham speaks up - the Rel’Udomina.  King appreciates brevity.  Ham says it is a long story. Fills him in with the story that begins in O'naren. Describe the scrying device…what we saw.  Cerul presented the stack of maps as tangible proof (but didn’t include the Abbey map in the stack)…but let’s them know we have leads we’d like to follow using them.  Kaizo jumps in to tell part of the story and says he will gladly stay at one of his finest establishments.  Discussion about how the group “lost” the letter, scrying device, other items.
Fungus overhears - “is that the problem with the ley thread.”  One of the male elves we saw in the scrying device is looking at the maps with Cerul.  We talk about what we heard, what we saw.  The King believes us.  Ham tells him at Ravus Elderan is the one who arrived at O'naren and had just arrived from Qal’dynn.  About the time the voice of the king (Lord Anceril) arrives and whispers to the king.  
King asks Ham - what would you tell enemies of Qal’dynn?  Ham replies that our deeds have shown how we would face enemies.  But that we came peacefully initially - presented at the gate, etc.  in hopes to bring you the missive (that is now gone).  Cerul says as an act of good faith, we will leave most of the maps with you.   King says - what do you think, Fendari - do they belong to the kingdom?  Fendari says it would be ok for the group to keep some of the maps…and would you be willing to return them if called upon?  Cerul replies yes.  
Fungus replies that the ley threads are magical reservoirs built under Qal’dynn that pull the weave forward to help the elves use magic/makes the magic stronger in the city.  He did not catch the response when the question was posed.
King stands and steps to the group, addressing Ham.  “You stand before me…redemption…little evidence…but merit in you.  I would see you on my Council, but you will be tested in resolve first.  See if you can stitch peace where is frays.  Lord Anceril will provide the tests for you and your group.  You get rest for now…use it well.” Man walks forward with a chest. Here is token of our esteem.  Chest is full of gold.  Take the chest and have a day of rest.”  Guards offer to carry it for us.  But Fungus says he will carry it (he lifts it and is smokes a little).
We head back to the Pale Lantern.  900 gp are in the chest.  We decide to go shopping.  We ask around and find 
Inner Sanctum - noble houses; Crescent Courts - king; Shaded Arches - middle class, shopkeepers; Vaelura Landing - trade and working class that deal with river; Realmwarden Grounds to the north - where the Realmwarden are trained; Cirandale - parks and overlooks; Moonroot Hollow - bad side of town (overrun by factions and people competing for power - good to know someone if you want to venture there).
Rest in Qal’dynn
During rest:  Relys goes to Cirandale; Kaizo, Fungus, Ham, and Affy - going to Shaded Arches; Cerul stays in Crescent Courts to talk to Fendari (Keeper of the Throne) about the maps (his ancestry founded elven settlements).
Cerul working to figure out all of the locations on the maps…to see if the Rel’Udomina might have a presence in other places…and if so, can the king and his people handle that or do they need help from the group.  Abbey is front and center.
Kaizo visits the herbalist, Tansian. Talks about sharing his seeds, but not to sell. Offers to give him 2 seeds.  One seed for those who seek balance, harmony, and transcendence - those who come and ask for those things in order, they will get them for free.  The second seed (poof) is for you to plant.  Every harmon will produce 7 seeds.  You can then sell those new seeds for profit.  But I want to make sure that the first seed that multiplies - will always be given their enjoyment for free.  The alchemist offers (in payment - those Kaizo didn’t want them) 3 potions of healing (that cannot be used as a bonus action).  Kaizo always tells him about the Birthplace of the Three Elders that is in O'naren. 
Relys visits the Cirandale.  She leaves a message there for a druid…to let whomever know that nature is disturbed in the Varenwood…if you are interested in combating it, leave a message here. And she meditates.  As she is sitting there relaxing, she enters a trance and as she zones out she can almost feel the ground move (this is odd).  She spends time feeling this and notices it gets closer to her.  She interrupts her trance and sees that insects are starting to gather near her (common insects, “nice” bugs).  She talks to the butterflies - a “speak with animals” type of spell. She asks “hello - why are you and the other bugs gathering near me now?” Because that’s what we are doing.  Because you are the concerned one.  We want to know why you are concerned.  Nature is not ok right now…do you guys sense the disturbances.  No, but we would like to help.  Can you guys go out and fly away and scout for me?  Don’t you want to see “Etew?”  They look and see ants marching carrying a stick.  Relys picks up a stick.  What is the nature of Etew?  He is an ant.  She thanks him for the stick.  As she studies the stick, she notices it is a petrified stick (solid, not fresh).  She pockets the stick and continues with the meditation.  Etew hopes it helps…and she meditates with the stick in her hand.
Ham, Fungus, and Affy buy a bunch of stuff in the markets.  We ask at the marketplace for Iofyr’s whereabouts. We are told where to find him - back at the Academy.  We head there to the Chasari section (science section).   There are many metal items/objects in this section - unlike where we initially were.  Using the directions we were given, we find a large laboratory.  We hear “don’t touch anything.”  Fungus spins around and just about knocks something over…upsetting the man.  Introductions are made.  He is tall, wiry, and has a gravely voice.  Fungus asks if he can unravel mysteries. Why would I take on studying you.  Fungus says let me tell you a story.  His real name is “Teldo.”  He is looking for the man who raised him.  He asks who sent us - we tell him Melandra from O'naren.  He also says he has a powder that he needs to get information about.  Affy points out a third reason - that he now can’t touch cloth and paper.  He says that he will help him in 2 days time (by making a donation to the academy)…that we need to return then.  But Fungus also asks if he has any potions to sell.  Yes - I do.  Points to a potion - that causes hair growth.  They test it.  Decides to buy it for Kaizo.  The guy asks if he talks a lot while adventuring…Fungus says yes.  He sells it to him (it is a breath freshener).  
As they walk back to the group, Affy gives Fungus grief about his name. Asks why he doesn’t want to be called Teldo.  He tells her it means Protector (in truth - roof) in goliath.  She tells him she likes it and will call him that and seeks permission to tell the others…which he grants.  She likes the name and likes the meaning.  They head back to find the group.
We all meet back at the Pale Lantern. Ham has been back at the inn, practicing his music.  Relys figures out that if she attunes to the stick, she gets resistance to poison.  She can also use the stick once per day to enlarge an insect to fight on her behalf (dog sized).  As the group arrives, Ham is in the tavern area about to perform.   Cerul talks to Fungus about removing the condition of flaming things up…and tries to “remove curse” on him.  And it works.  He is cured. Ham performs the bagpipe (his first night)- quite well.  Affy tells the group about Fungus’ name. Relys buys one of the expensive chocolate drinks.  Fungus sings with Ham.  They sound great!  
We go to our rooms for the night.
32st of Talil
After breakfast, the group talks about the cube.  Affy and Ham leave all their stuff in their room.  Affy handles the cube while Ham and Fungus look on (all else are far away) [she rolls an 88 - rolls a d12 and rolls a 3 - flash of light and 5 arcane runes start to float around the cube as she holds it - Affy isn’t sure what they do, as Ham grabs one of the runes.  He feels a slight burn on his hand, then cools, and then all of the runes disappear…he is now resistant to fire damage ELEMENTAL EMPOWERMENT] - doesn’t know if it is permanent].
We then go to see Lord Anceril.  He tells us that the king honors few…had things for us to do, but now has to change plans as something more pressing has come up.   We will need to go to Moonroot Hollow…and find a peaceful resolution if possible, to the rising tension that has come up since the Rel’Udomina has been taken down in the city.  Seek out Lorian Omseer, an elder of insight.  Learn of the recent trouble with the ley thread (happened during the TIME OF GROWTH in the 800s)…understand it…then act.  He is a night owl…need to meet him in the plaza at twilight.  Ham studies the map - to make sure it looks legit. Kaizo thinks something is off - is paranoid about Lord Anceril.  Thinks maybe this guy isn’t on the up and up.  He realizes that Lord Anceril knows more than he is letting on.  We decide to all go at twilight.
Forward to Twilight
We step into the district.  We pass worn statues, draped in ivy.  Streets clearing.  In the center of the vine-choked plaza we see a dry reflecting pool…where a man is seated in robes and hunched over a glowing crystal.  He has violet eyes, “I wondered who they would send. You are the one.  I hope you are clever. I’m sure you know this is about the ley thread.  What is going on isn’t kind.  How can I assist you?”  Ham responds - yes, we were sent and understand that there is trouble.  Lorian is disappointed that we don’t know more about the ley thread.  The pulse currently stutters (normally it pulses regularly).  I believe the “threadborne” is behind the trouble.  They are a group of want to be wizards.  You need to go and talk them down.  I can go, but it would not end well for them - I am too powerful.  They are at an abandoned botanical garden ruins. Affy comments that it is odd that wizards would want to disrupt the steady flow of magic.  And he says they know not what they do…which is why we are being tasked to handle this. Fungus jokes about killing them - which would be Lorian’s way.  Ham comments that maybe they need to be trained at the academy.  They are the only ones to hang out there so we will know who they are.  Aleya Temser is their de facto leader.  This group are all novices and young.   We decide to head out.  Fungus asks if he has any aid he could render…and he doesn’t. Fungus says he likes him and will come back to visit him after we finish.  He tells Fungus he will test him - you can use the scroll OR if you bring it back untouched you will get a reward.  
We head out to the northern area of the Hollow.  Can feel the energy of the place on our skin; smells of ink and lavender. We see several people in robes, unkempt. A dry fountain in the facility. People see us and step out of the shadows and gather around.  One steps up with a patchwork robe…clearly homemade…but she has curly red hair that is circled by crystals, light eyes. Ham talks to her…she claims to be bringing back magic, after it was restricted for some time by the king and the Queen of Basctdelm.  Affy confirms who she is.  Ham says that maybe there is just a misunderstanding…he is okay (as is the king) with them practicing magic. She says that how else can they learn?  Ham points out the academy…but she says that they cannot afford to attend.  Ham talks about maybe the King could pay their way to go (scholarships).  Affy brings up the ley thread…and that they are doing something to cause it to stutter.  Cerul offers to observe their magical gifts to see what might be amiss.  They agree - admitting that the past few nights have been a little off. They call forth the weave to come and serve.  They gather around the fountain (there are a total of 8), which is basically an oversized bird bath, since the tiers have long crumbled. Cerul and Relys look on and don’t recognize what they are trying to do.  Ham knows they are trying to create something. They get about halfway through the 10 minutes of the ritual and we see an energy spark crack and something slowly starts to misty form in the center of the fountain and then fades and cracks away and the ground shakes beneath us.  They all look disappointed, as she blames us for being there.  Ham jumps in to say they need better training, coaching them that they are on to something, but that there is more in play that they need to figure out.  Ham persuades them.  They ask for advice…and Ham tells them they need more training.  Fungus tells them they need to accept help from Lorian.  Affy asks Cerul to cast a spell to show them.  He casts daylight.  They agree to seek help.  Ham asks them to come with the group now.  We take the group to the plaza to find Lorian.  
We do not immediately find him at the plaza, so decide to take them to the Pale Lantern and put them up for the night.  We send a message through the realmwardens that we want to talk to Lord Anceril.  Fungus pays for their rooms and food (5gp).  
33rd of Talil
In the morning we are met by Parnian, who says our message was received, but the Voice is off today.  So he is here to help.  Ham presents him with an apprenticeship/scholarship program to train these 8 young people at the academy.  He makes the compelling argument.  Parnian’s question is if Lorian is on board.  Ham calls Lorian an ass.  Parnian agrees to take this solution to the king.
Later on…Parnian comes back and says the King will see us.  Before Parnian walked the kids away, Fungus talks to her about her crystals…and she admits she can see further than most elves. 
We go to see the King.  He is pleased with the outcome and has flowery language.  He focuses on Ham…with his grandiose talk.  He draws forth a silver pendant with a swan and hands it to Ham.  “Ham is now Ambassador of Qal’dynn - he may speak for the king, etc.” He hears a voice in his head of Lord Anceril…you have done well.  Now let us see if you can continue to do this when it truly matters.”  This means that Ham will stay here…or travel at the will of the King.
– The End of the Session –
