# Session 36 - Leave it Hanging (I’m Going to 20)

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: October 15, 2026
In-game Date:  22nd of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  The Beasts Trail
Chasing the Beast
We are following the track of the beast through the night.   It is slow going because we often have to dismount and go around fallen and broken trees.  This is a dense forest. We find a clearing and decide to make camp just off the trail of the beast.  We smell death and realize there are likely dead animals around. 
As we set up camp, we see pairs of red eyes - evidence that animals have been turned by the creature.  These are likely smaller creatures - squirrels and the like.  They don’t approach.  It is cold so we make a fire with broken timbers. The skies are hazy and gray.  Ridley casts his alarm spell around the camp.  We decide not to have a watch - to get a rest and try to ride at first light.  The spell goes off a couple of times, but Ridley realizes they are small creatures and goes back to sleep.
23 of Paramor, 1246
We rise at dawn.  Relys shifts into her owl form to scout ahead and Ridley is able to cast a spell to see through her eyes. Snow flurries start to fall, limiting her line of sight somewhat. Using her scouting and navigating to follow the tracks, we travel the entire day without incident.  We think we probably went 10-15 miles this day. At camp, we pull out the board and Affy, Relys, and Fungus ask a question:  what are the weaknesses of the creature we are hunting?  It answers: M-A-S-T-E-R  We then ask:  Is Tornia the master?  It answers NO.  Who is the Master?  No movement at first, then the board moves to M-O-O-N-S.  We sign off of the board.  Ridley sets the alarms, no watch again.  Plan to travel again at first light.  Kaizo, who is meditating, sees something near Relys - two little red eyes.  They get close to her and then disappear briefly.  He’s not sure where they disappeared.  He walks over to investigate and sees a small mouse bite her.  She immediately wakes up and the creature scurries away.
Kaizo lets it flee and asks Relys how she is doing “How do you feel - do you feel different?”  They inspect the wound and determine they need to “Keep an eye on it.”  Kaizo positions next to her and she goes back to sleep.  He notices other red eyes outside of camp here and there.    We get a long rest.
24th of Paramor, 1246
We pack up at first light and are all told of the mouse encounter.  Cerul checks Relys out and notices that the bite is ugly with some pus, perhaps this is a normal infection and not something more sinister?  Cerul decides to cure wounds on Relys.  The radiance from Cerul lights up and the puffiness and swelling disappears, as does the pus. She seems normal otherwise.  We remember that we were bitten by the bears and nothing happened, so we decide not to worry.
We travel as we did the day before:  Relys in owl form and scouting ahead to lead the way, following the creature’s tracks. After a half a day’s travel, Relys loses sight of the tracks in the distance.  She decides to cast a ritual to try to determine if the creature is within three miles of the party - looking for portals, looking for a large creature, and a close settlement.  She finds no creature or settlement, but finds the residual effects of a portal.  She decides to scout ahead to see what that is. 
She travels 2 miles and finds that the ground is churned up a little bit.  She sees a series of bodies laying in a row.  These are humanoid bodies - probably 10 of them.   She goes back and tells the group and we all decide to investigate the scene.    Relys, in owl form, leads the group, but stays with the group.
We arrive and see trees crashed, gouges in the earth.  These bodies are intact, seem fresh, and are ordered - as if someone counted them out.  Ridley checks the bodies out closely.  They appear to be dead.  One has a gash in his chest that looks like a cut, not from a claw.  They are all pale elves and we are reminded of the people we saw at the Blackstone Crucible.  They all have a cut on their necks - like a marking.  Ridley thinks this is a crude Orcish mark, but might be more of a fey marking.  Fungus decides to take a look.  He gets a sinking feeling and knows this marking represents the fey word for “moon.”  He says that this would represent the Moon King from the Feywild.  The cut was not made by a dagger, but a spear. It looks like the marks were made after death.  Ridley notices a piece of torn cloth near one of the dead bodies and we think it came from Kulv.  His patron is the Moon King.  
Is Kulv the creature - maybe he shifts?  Or is he working in league with the creature?  Relys tries to find tracks from either the beast and/or Kulv, but she’s unable to make out any tracks that lead from here.  She checks and senses conjuration magic in the air, but can’t make out exactly what happened.  She just knows that magic happened in two spots.  Maybe two portals?   We think the Shadar-Kai elves that are here were an elite fighting force.  We decide to thoroughly search the bodies to find any clues.
The bodies have their Shadowfel blades, a mix of shortswords and daggers. We find a couple with a symbol similar to that we found in the Blackstone Crucible - a chain with the black shard.  Relys pulls out her book and studies this.  She thinks they are followers of Malgri’n.  We decide to rest here, planning to allow Cerul to talk to the dead tomorrow morning. 
Affy asks Cerul to communicate with Kulv - letting him know that she would like to meet with him. “This is Cerul on behalf of Affy.  She wants to meet if possible to continue your discussion.  Where and when can you meet her?”  He rolls well and the message lands. He responds “That may be difficult unless you are near Qal’dynn which I plan to be at tomorrow.  Eliana says hi.”  Affy recalls that wizards and warlocks have the ability to move around freely.  Cerul leaves the thread hanging.
We decide to rest here.  Relys wants to try to communicate with her goddess.  Ridley and Affy cripple the dead bodies while others are busy. We continue to talk about the creature and the master.  Maybe Kulv is the creature’s master.  Relys wants to ask her goddess:  Is this creature going to be a continued threat to Myrdae?  But we decided to wait until the morning to cast that spell, too.
The evening goes well.  Ridley casts alarm.  Nothing happens.
25th of Paramor, 1246
In the morning, we hear the sounds of birds.  We think the creature is no longer around for sure - where it now is, we are not sure. Cerul casts a speak with the dead spell - finding the most important looking one. Ridley and Affy watch the surroundings, as Kaizo, Fungus, and Relys watch Cerul speak with the dead one.
He asks:  Who were you fighting when you died?  The creature looks over at Affy and says “a half orc and Vhaulmast”.  As Cerul looked at Affy, he could have sworn her eyes were briefly red and blue, but just for a second.
He asks:  Did you travel here to try to kill them?  Answer:  no
He asks:  Why were you here?  Answer:  He was to be bound. 
He asks:  How exactly did you travel here?  Answer:  Our colleague opened a portal to your material plane.
He asks:  Who is your colleague?  Answer:  a wise one in the Shadowfel
Cerul then finds another dead body and speaks with him.
He asks:  Name of the wise one who opened the portal? Answer :  Veszven 
He asks:  Can Veszven help us get back to the Shadowfel?  Answer:  I don’t know
He asks:  How were you planning on binding the creature?  Answer:  The magic is gone.
He asks:  What does Veszven look like?  Answer:  Pale skin, white hair, gray robe, black eyes, pointy ears…
He asks:  What was your captain’s name here?  Answer:  Solhrys
Kaizo points out that we need to figure out how to travel.  Ridley and Cerul talk about how and why we should track this further. 
Cerul wakes up another dead.
He asks:  How were you able to wound The Vhaulmast?  Answer:  He can be cut.
He asks:  Did you know the half orc would be here or were you ambushed?  Answer:  We were ambushed.
He asks:  How would you like us to treat your dead?  Answer: Bury us at home.
He asks:  How do we get your home? Answer: Idk - Veszven
He asks:  Did the halforc have any unusual abilities? Answer: Besides looking him her (Affy), he summoned Fey, Banished, he did some other things.
Relys decides to communicate with her goddess.  Using divination, she asks her goddess if the Vhaulmast is going to be an immediate threat to Qal’dynn or the surrounding areas?  She hears:  In the world of Myrdae, surrounding areas can extend far and wide.  And yet, he will roam, but not near Qal’dynn.  Other threats may occur. Relys thanks her for the information, tears rolling down her face. 
Meanwhile, Ridley finds a shivering chickadee and uses animal handling to capture the bird.
As we discuss reaching out to Veszven, Affy hears something.  A voice reaches out (slightly familiar).  “Odabrin will meet you in Basctdelm.  He gave no place.  Another asked the Halls about him.  My letter might not be the only one.”  Affy thinks this was Odaphi.   Affy replies:  “Thanks. Was this other like me but with red and blue eyes?  Been delayed. Tell Odabrin I will come as soon as I can.”  Affy hears:  “No, it was a woman.  Odabrin asked if you touched the glyphic and was curious of others (My goat) reaching out to him.  Find the unwalked path near Greenscape.”  Affy responds:  “Yes, I touched the glyphic but only a couple of times.  I wonder who the woman is - mother, someone else?  I will find the path.”
Meanwhile, Kaizo hears:  “There is unfortunate news.  Realmwardens found dead monks on the road to Qal’dynn.  Their resources were gathered.  This is Lorien Ormseer from Qal’dynn.”  He responds:  “What happened to them and did the relics make it back to the Sanctuary?  Please secure their belongings and take them there. We need transport now: Bastdelm? Qal’dynn?” He doesn't get a response. Kaizo thinks we are out here away from everything, almost on purpose… 
Cerul casts a spell to Veszven.  “Greetings Vezven, I'm Cerul Slate and am with your fallen ones at the hand of our enemy Vhalmaus south of Bloodbeak.  Can we meet? ”  But he never gets a reply…and does not feel like he was blocked. 
The group decides to head to Shademoor.  We take two sets of the armor from the elves and leave.  We discuss, but decide not to contact Ham in Qal’dynn to let him know about Kulv.
26th and 27th of Paramor, 1246
Fast track.  We head through the woods to Shademoor.
Kaizo gets a message from Lorien.  “We have heard that there are threats made at your resort.  Are you aware of this?”  Kaizo answers:  “No, but beware of a half orc with red and blue eyes.”  He then hears:  “I’m not sure why someone would have raised a post that said “threaten me and see what happens.”  Kaizo realizes this is likely Eliana making waves at the Spa.  He replies “I think this is a wizard named Eliana who I had a run in with in Caelora.”  Relys asks why Eliyena is making waves and Kaizo tells everyone that he had insight that she was up to no good, that she was evil.  He responds “We are a day from Shademoor.  We hope to get there shortly.”  
We continue to move to Shademoor.  Cerul casts a spell to the Meridian members “We have news to report.  We are a day out of Shademoor and need to meet.”  He hears back:  “That’s good to hear.  Safe travels.  We cannot go to Basctdelm at dark so if we need to wait a day we can.” 
Cerul then contacts the Spa:  “Beware of a wizard named Eliyena.  Raise the guard” He hears back: “The Wizard is already here, and he has our back he says, I hope this isn't a distraction”
We get a long rest.
28th of Paramor, 1248
We finish our journey to Shademoor.   We arrive at dark in the snow. We stable the horses and head to the Hollow Root Inn, where we find the Meridian guards.  They are eating and wave.  We don’t immediately see Pa or his family. We are greeted by a young female human, late teens.  She comes out from behind the bar.  Pick any table….we just got our shipment in from Caelora.  
We settle in at a table.  One of the Meridian comes over to talk to us and shares that Pa and his family are here safely.  They say they can go to Basctdelm tomorrow.  The groundskeeper won’t let us into the Garden at night.  Meet you here in the morning.  Cerul, however, tells the news of tracking the creature, the battle with the binders from the Shadowfel, and how he disappeared with no trace.  We think this is not the end of this creature, but aren’t sure where he will pop up again.  They say they would like a full report in Basctdelm.
We recall that the Garden area was dark and misty as we looked in that direction (it is a graveyard).  
The barkeep comes over to take orders - we ask for the wine and the stew - well all except Relys.  She asks for a vegetarian version if possible.  She is told she can and comments on how cute Relys’s owl is.  We also pay for the rooms for the night (a nice room for Kaizo/Fungus and Affy/Relys and a standard one for Cerul/Ridley). She also drops off a cup of wine and tells Cerul it is from a guy in the corner who would like to chat with Cerul if he has time.
Cerul sees a guy dressed in dark clothes, a hood, smoking a pipe. He has salt/pepper hair, scruff on his face.  He shakes Cerul’s hand and says “You bring back some old memories from my childhood”  They introduce themselves.  He is Sengo. Cerul tells him where he is from and the guy questions that and mentions Pekanon.  Cerul and he discuss Pekanon.  He tells Cerul there was a tale of a boy born there with bright, radiant hair - and he thought it could be Cerul.  The timeline makes sense.  Cerul tells him he was an orphan.  The man shares that the tale was that “the child would be the last one to carry the light.”  He said he fled from there and it was destroyed by people who wanted to extinguish the light and never did we hear more of the child.  I ended up after many moons on the road in a place called A’erly in a place called the Tegenwald Phet.  Cerul says he was young then, too.  Cerul laughs and says this child was likely me…and what do I do with that information?  They laugh, unsure about the meaning.  He wishes Cerul the best.  Cerul says it would be a happy ending if all is light and good.  He thanks the man for the information and says he will research the town further. He asks if the man has anything to add.  He tells Cerul that he will let him know if he finds out anything as well.  Cerul tells him that maybe he will end up going to Pekanon at some point.  They toast and Cerul returns to the table.
We eat our stew and biscuits, drink wine, and head to bed. We plan to meet in the morning to head to Basctdelm after Kaizo shops and we find Pa.
 29th of Paramor, 1246
The next morning the Meridian asks “shall we be off?”  We tell them we need a slight delay. We go to the Night’s Favor to find Pa.  The wood/construction here is dark, black wood.  This is where the name of the town comes from.
We enter and notice two symbols:  the goddess Phoe and the goddess Sylunara.  Two priests walk around - one in purple (female) and one in blue (male).  Pa and his family are standing in front of the shrine to Sylunara.  He talks about his journey and what he might owe us and offers to get us a cask of his special wine.  He leaves and gets it and gives it to Fungus. Cerul assures him that we will continue to track the beast.  He says he will share our good name - The King’s Outlanders - and will hire us again, if needed.  He then asks about the woman and Kaizo says she was no good. We ask Pa if he will take care of the horse (anjou) and bulas for a while.  He says he can watch them for a stretch, but if we are not back, we can stable them.  
We head over to the Meridian and tell them we are ready to go.  They have validation that we are good to go to Basctdelm.  We see a document signed by “Countess” (scroll) - they say that they have to show this to the groundskeeper in order to gain passage.  And to never try to enter the Shademoor waypoint at night.  He asks if Cerul is familiar with Othwode?  He is the keeper of the Garden and ….how about you just see for yourself.
We cross and go to the Garden.  The gates are closed.  Standing between us and the large old chapel in the distance we see an ethereal form that raises a hand and the gates part open.  The guard says - there is the groundskeeper.  Just be on good behavior.  You have permission from the Countess.  You should be okay.  The old chapel hasn’t been used since the war.  Stay away from the Old Chapel undercroft - there was some bad stuff that happened there in the past.”  He hands the scroll to the creature and the form opens it then it pops and vanishes.  He steps aside and points to the chapel door.  We walk in and see dust covered evidence of a battle here.  We go down and see the arcane waypoint that sits under sconces.  Cerul makes note of the sigil here (he now has four).  They tell him that we must give the Meridian a heads up before we travel to Basctdelm in the future.  They lead us to the circle and we go to the House of Myr in Basctdelm. 
We arrive in a small room atop a tower. This sigil used to be in the local magic shop, but they had it relocated. This is the HQ for the Meridian, there are16 Floors. 
We are taken to a room and they take down formal statements of what happened on the road. We stick with the Vhalmast story. They advise of the various districts - where to visit, where we would be comfortable, telling us to shy away from the Bellows.  Cerul asks where Sylvandor is - if they know of him.  He is told to talk to the Watch about his whereabouts.  
Affy tells the group she wants to find her father.  Relys agrees to travel with her, but not near her.
Cerul, Fungus, Kaizo and Ridley try to find Sylvandor.  They ask the Watch.  They know of him, but aren’t sure where he is - or at least don’t want to reveal him to strangers.  
We get to the crossroads of the Greenscape and Central District.  We see a gabuk put his hands up to big people - two half orcs.  He points at Affy.  AND WE STOP HERE. 


– The End of the Session –
