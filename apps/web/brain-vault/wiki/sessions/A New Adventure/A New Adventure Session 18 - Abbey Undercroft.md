## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  October 29, 2025
In-game Date:  44th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Abbey of Light
Day 4/8 of rest for the Feywoven Glypic
Morning at the Rising Light
Morning, waking up in the Rising Light.  Cerul reviewing the maps of the Abbey that we got from the Rel'Udomina vs. what he knows from growing up here.  He remembers there were crypts/grottos that we talked about when he was a kid and he knows there was an arcane waypoint, but not sure where these maps tie into reality.  
We discuss that we need to be alert - Rel'Udomina have probably infiltrated the ranks of the various orders (look for tattoos).  Man steps in named Dahrin Moll (he is a guard)…he comes in to tell us that Fungus is with the scout Therin Hillbuck.  Cerul asks him about Brother Freya - he is told that he left some time ago on a research mission (not sure when/if he will be back).  Asks where Matron Mallow is - she is in the Matron’s Quarters.  Also tells us that if we have issues we should ask the Warden - who is in the Warden’s Quarters.  Affy asks him about if there are any crypts…and he has no answer for her.  Cerul brings up the arrangements with the giants - and again, is no help, but says to talk to the Warden (name is Warden Tersic Valegar - Cerul knows the name).  Ridley asks about wizards - he is told to check the House of Reflective Light. 
We talk about plans - where to go.  We decide to split up and go seek some quick information.  Ridley will go to the House of Reflective Light; Affy and Relys to the Warden; Cerul to the Matron; Kaizo to the Shop of Lighted Breath.  Plan is to gather high level, surface level info…and meet back in an hour at the Cloister.  We stop for breakfast downstairs first before heading out to parts of the Abbey.  We eat different food - honeyroot stew, flatbread (vegetarian, yummy).  Cerul tries to pay and is told to donate to the Great Hall.  
Luminary Sister Ysolde Vern Rides
As we are leaving we hear horses up the hill…and in front of the Great Hall is a procession.  Woman on white mare, golden cloak, with four escorts (Protective Light, Rising Light).  They pass near us and welcome us.  Affy says hello - asks where they are going and if she needs help.  She says that she is going to take care of the problem on the road - the giant turned to stone.  Cerul speaks up to tell her he tried yesterday and she thinks he looks familiar.  He warns her that the giant may be upset…she speaks giant and is not worried.  Ridley looks for tattoos and sees none (arms are covered).  Kaizo speaks up to warn of the danger; Cerul echoes his warning.  He says he would like to speak with her later and they agree to touch base in the morning.
Ridley & The House of Reflective Light
Ridley goes to the House of Reflective Light and the door is shut.  He knocks and a human female answers. “How may I assist you?”  I was told this is where to go to seek wizardly aid.  She says that she will try to help and invites him in.  This building is very different from where we were.  Elegant, gothic chandeliers with diamond-like light that illuminates everything, books and tomes everywhere.   Sees a large iron door and he thinks it would lead outside (it has a bar and lock on it). A staircase rises up next to the door. “Please let us conspire.”  Ridley talks about the arcane practices he’s heard associated with the Abbey…mentions an arcane waypoint and how he is looking for one.  He notices a guy sitting behind a desk with a quill who makes a face.  She says that is Parson Quinel Drael (half elf, male).  He only talks when someone has an appointment, but I’m happy to schedule you an appointment.  Ridley walks over to the Parson.  He mentions the waypoint.  Parson says they don’t have one, says he must have heard incorrectly.  He asks for Ridley’s name and he answers “they just call me the Jackal.”  Parson asks if he has a real name and Ridley dodges.  Ridley notices that he slid what he was working on under a book.  Ridley doesn’t see a tattoo (arms covered).  Ridley drops an Rel'Udomina catch phrase into the conversation (as he asks about a book), looking to see if the parson reacts. The Parson says he has that book and offers to bring it to the Hearthlight…or offers to have someone fetch him for a glass of wine to discuss later.  Ridley then asks the girl what is behind the iron door and she doesn’t know.  She quietly says that the Parson goes through the door (making sure the Parson doesn’t overhear her asking).
Cerul & Matron Elbrade Mallow
Cerul goes to the Matron’s quarters and knocks. Matron Mallow answers and knows Cerul - she knows his face (but not his name).  They hug. She says “you were here at the painting of the hall.”  (reference to a monk who painted a hall in the orphanage) They discuss why Cerul is here and she asks if they need privacy and he says yes.  There are a couple of people there (female, assistant to the Matron, Sister Aida; Brother Halrick, male).  Those two leave.  Cerul briefs her about Sister Melain…telling her how he put her up in O’Naren with the new orphans. She says she doesn’t get into politics, but asks what is going on.  He tells her about the Rel'Udomina and how they are behind much of the goings on. Tells her they are sowing chaos and power…and about disguises…and how they infiltrated Qal’dynn and O’naren.  He specifically mentions the underground nature of what they did in those towns.  He asks about underground places at the Abbey.  She tells him about the grotto that is only accessible through the Great Hall.  She also tells him that the Reflective Light group (who is tied to the Tegenwald Phet) has an arcane waypoint.  He asks how to get to the grotto.  Says that the stairwell near the main altar that goes down (the other goes to the Tower).   You need permission to go to the grotto/vault, but also an escort from the Rising Light. She’s been down there before - a handful of crypts, but also a vault. Cerul then shows her the map he got from the Rel'Udomina. She doesn’t recognize the places on the map, but knows of the circular staircase that leads to the grotto, but has never seen extra passages that are on the map (stuff behind the vault).  She says she didn’t know there was such a web of tunnels here.  Cerul then shows her the Rel'Udomina tattoo and says this is their symbol.  She says she doesn’t know what to do if she finds anyone with the tattoo and he says to let him know if she does.
Kaizo and the Monks of Lighted Breath
Kaizo goes to the Shop of the Lighted Breath. The doors are open, herbs and honey smell. Sees lots of stuff to buy.  He sees monks (yellow, knee-length robes, foot wrappings) in the courtyard in front of the Cloister and in the shop. He walks up to one who is going through their morning motions and “paint the fence” connects with one - encouraging more movement.  Inside the shop he sees a female elf and male dwarf.  They nod, busy.  She then walks over asking how he can help.  She is Sister Thaelen Muir and this is Brother Garris. They comment on Kaizo’s pipe, as he takes a drag and blows it out dramatically with a dragon.  They then discuss “weed.” Kaizo tells him if he “thinks about it enough” he can make images come out of his pipe, too.  “See it…be the dragon.”  He reintroduces himself to the Sister.  He tells her he is looking for balance, harmony and transcendence. She says, “be the…(fill in the blank) to mimic Kaizo.”  She says they have honeybrew mead - which is special - and breathleaf tea.  He wants something to connect him to the powers beyond and she suggests incense (longflower).   He asks for something stronger.  And she says we have “the spirit of the breath,” but we don’t trade it.  It is potent.  It is distilled here.  It is honeybrew mixed with frostleaf.  He pulls out his plant and says he is looking for something that rivals the Nightbud. She tells the Brother what Kaizo has.  She knows of it and asks where he got it.  He mentions his shop and the soon to be spa. They have never had that.  The brother offers to trade his 50gp hookah for the Nightbud. Kaizo declines, looking for something special. He then tells them where to go in O’Naren (Maledra) to get one. And again, “sells” the new spa near Qal’dynn.  Tells him to ask for “Master” Kaizo.  They discuss training together someday.  Kaizo buys two prepared herb pouches and two jars of salve. [wants to drop one of each off at the spa…to see if they have a need; might be able to have a future arrangement to purchase more].  He also buys a honeybrew mead for Fungus.
The Warden
Meanwhile, Relys and Affy head to the Warden’s quarters. Stone building, thicker, well maintained.  Stairs leading up and down.  The door at the bottom is thick iron; the door at the top is oak.  They knock and a human male answers. Command post mixed with a home.  Portrait hanging above the mantel; three “trophies” there.  He is armored.  Second in command is there (female, half elf, Sara). He greets us. Talks about fungus, the pair introduces themselves, he is the warden. Fungus bumped into a scout, the scout was following a strange trail. Footprints took him to a secretive path that led to him finding a metallic prism, 10” tall, buried in the evergreen. Something was in the metal, dunduar ore. It is not nearby (in the Warden’s Quarters). It might not be enough Dunduar to sense via magic. Fungus went looking for it, as he has experience in the matter, he and the scout went out. The HoRL gets visitors from the Phet. The Warden took over from the old man. He doesn’t know Cerul. Can’t tell if the warden is dumb/unwise or if the DM is just pulling our leg, and the Warden is apologizing for nothing of importance…. This goes on for longer than needed. Acting like a Naked Gun plot…. When something is odd, something is afoot, and when something is afoot it warrants checking out. Affy gets sussed out cause she revealed too much… had to reveal they were heroes (DM hit x to skip cutscene about how they became heroes). The waypoint is beneath the House of Reflective Light. The warden is wayyyyyy too trusting and just tells us anything. The entire conversation is strange. Affy is sowing seeds of doubt between the second in command and the warden, Affy clearly wants bad blood between the two. The warden is Leslie Nielsen. Affy shows off flesh and gets offered a job… Leslie offers Affy to be a second second in command as he believes she skins people alive. Dumb and Dumber try to figure out how the Rel'Udomina do business, Affy keeps revealing our info (maps), our meeting place, powers (talked in his head earlier). Nielsen asks the first in command (second) to show her forearm… They are all showing arms, talking about slapping skin. The pair doesn't even ask to see his arm. Affy calls out racism even though there were so many other strange things in this convo. More stuff happens and giants get brought up, information that was previously known gets revealed again…. Flicko is the guardian under the mountain (under the summit), he is a giant. “Are there caves?” “yes” “is he friendly” “yes” Flicko likes children. “Is the giant a stone giant” “yes”. The pair get escorted to go see a giant, they get escorted to a jail, through a secret door, they go meet the giant. Flicko can't speak commonly, only to children who speak giant, so the pair decided to not go see him. I hope Leslie isn't part of the Rel'Udomina. 
Meeting at the Cloister
Relys and Affy walk to the Cloister - looking for anyone standing out. Rising and Protective Light folks have armor; where Nurturing and Reflective might have more cloaks (easier to spot tattoos).  They see monks and scholars.  Stone pathways, archways. Group meets at the Cloister.  They discuss what they each learned.  Cerul thinks that if the prisms are used in a group it might create some type of anti-magic field that would cut off sending spells, teleportation, etc.  Maybe they are creating a barrier around the Abbey.  Cerul tells what he found out about the map.  Relys shares about Flicko.  Kaizo shares his purchases.  Ridley tells about his dinner with the Rel'Udomina (parson). Discussion about the fortified door at the HofRL.  Relys will try to infiltrate that door later.  
The Warden comes up…introductions made.  Cerul pulls out the map and the Warden suggests that we go back to his quarters to meet in private.  Warden recognizes the Great Hall component as well; he knows that there is a passage to the Underdark on the map.  He also knows about the jail cells under his place that lead to Flicko. The group discusses that maybe the Rel'Udomina are using those cells.  Warden thinks that the other map could be the arcane waypoint, but he’s never been there so he can’t say for sure. Affy tells Relys in her head that she should infiltrate the HofRL while Ridley is eating with the Parson.
Into the Undercroft
The group goes down to the iron door and into the cells.  Cerul lights his weapon.  The Warden locks the group in. Kaizo tells us a story about how a long time ago he saw a theatrical production about an Abbey in a similar state.  He says this doesn’t make sense. Relys says she will send Hopper (her pet jumping spider) ahead.  Ridley investigates in the jail cell area.  The place is sparsely furnished, not used in a long time. He clearly sees the hidden door that Sara showed Relys and Affy.  
Hopper takes off down the hall, past the secret door. He is looking for tracks, disturbances in the dust, or any creatures.  Relys notices that the hallway (when it banks off to the right) has lots of evidence of foot traffic.  We head that way and ask Ridley to check for a hidden door and he finds one.  [We think we are between 5 and 6 on the map].  Relys sends Hopper under the door of the “interrogation” room…but hears someone on the other side talking (about the group). There is a man sitting there on a very ornate stool reading a letter, looking at 3 others in the room.  In the cell in the room there is a caged dwarf who looks bruised and battered.  Ridley knows there is an Rel'Udomina agent in the Abbey (half elf, female, short auburn hair).  Relys tells us one of the three was she.  Ridley decides to go in and pose as an Rel'Udomina (he will know the female).  He opens the door.
“Excuse me.”  The lady says “The Jackal - he exists.”  She says he worked with us at Qal’dynn.  He says he brought the infidels here so we can take care of them.  He says that the group is totally fooled and is not far from here.  They call us “trouble.”  They discuss how Fungus is out scouting for prisms. The dwarf speaks up and spits and says “all of you are scum” and they tell her to shut up. She is one of the ones that the Parson handed over. They asked how he knew where they were and he says that the Parson told him (did the wraiths not bother you…no, I have my powers).  Does Archon Denarth know what you are up to? He says no, but the Illustrious might.  Ridley shares that someone else has taken over.  Ridley then offers to “deliver” the party to the group right now.   “Affy!”
INITIATIVE - A Fight with the Rel’Udomina
ROUND ONE:  Leader fires off a spell and hits his ally with a fumble, then misses, then hits Ridley. Ridley plays off of him hitting his ally and tells the others to attack the leader (the 2 guys believe him, but the female does not).  He then attacks the leader and fumbles, falls prone and stands back up and attacks again and hits, then attacks again and hits. The female tries to persuade her companions to not listen to Ridley (one does, one does not)...and then attacks Ridley.  She hits with a dagger twice and he goes down. She then walks over to close the door.  Then the other ones drag Ridley into the cell.  Affy runs forward, opens the door and attacks the female and misses twice.  She moves out of the door on the other side of the drow.  Cerul runs in and casts Radiance of the Dawn, and three fail the save and one makes it. And then casts healing word onto Ridley. Kaizo runs in and attacks the drow, missing once and then hitting.  Then he uses fury of blows and misses twice. Relys runs up to the door and casts moonbeam on the guy dragging Ridley, killing him. Then casts healing word on Ridley. 
ROUND TWO: Leader walks around the moonbeam and lets out a yell to the group (wisdom saving throw).  Several (including allies) take damage.  Ridley stands up and moves to the leader, taking an opportunity attack (which hits).  He then attacks with a dagger and hits, hunter’s mark, and attacks again, hits, then hits again (39 damage). The drow chick pulls out a wand and light flashes - attacking Affy, Ridley, Kaizo and the leader.   Ridley goes down again; Affy is blinded. The dwarf runs out of the cell and is attacked, but is missed.  The other guy attacks Kaizo and hits.  Affy attacks the drow and fumbles, hitting Kaizo.  She then uses cleave and fumbles again.  Then attacks again and fumbles and falls prone.  Cerul casts Radiance of the Dawn again on the group. And then casts Mass Healing Word, healing everyone some, including saving Ridley again.  Kaizo then attacks the guy next to him and hits, killing him. He then attacks the drow and misses.  Then uses fury of blows on the drow and hits twice.  Relys moves the moonbeam on the leader and he takes damage. She then heals Affy.
ROUND THREE:  The leader attacks Cerul with magical bolts, Cerul blocks the first one so he shifts to Kaizo and misses twice. Ridley picks up his dagger and tosses it at the leader and hits. He then tosses another dagger at the leader and misses. Drow attacks Affy and takes her down with her 3 attacks, but she pops back up with 1HP.   She then shuts the door. The dwarf is mad that the door is shut. Affy stands up and attacks and hits, then misses.  She uses second wind to heal and move.  Cerul attacks and also heals Affy.  Kaizo moves to the leader, taking opportunity attack from the drow, which takes him down.  Relys kicks open the door and she moves the moonbeam on the leader and he fails his save, killing him.  She heals Kaizo, saving him. 
ROUND FOUR:  Ridley stands up and moves over to the drow and attacks with a short sword and misses, then attacks again and misses, and then misses with a scimitar. The drow looks around and says halt to everyone but Kaizo, using a wand she casts a spell. Affy, Relys and Ridley are all paralyzed.  She tries to escape and Cerul casts a spell as an opportunity attack, hitting with good damage along with extra radiant damage. Kaizo also takes an opportunity attack and hits (with disadvantage).  The paralysis drops.   Affy runs out to chase her and kills her with her second attack and psionic damage.
Ridley says that we killed (the Prominent is the Minister…she leads cells) a Minister (of this cell).  We know there is another level above called an Arcon.
This dwarf is from the Order of Reflective Light.  She knows we are good guys.  She tells us that in her mind the Parson has been corrupted (which Ridley had figured out).  We see on the table a ledger and see several names.  Sister Nali Brassfell…that’s me (the dwarf).  The other names don’t appear as though they have had issues with.
