# Session 24 - Into the Shadowfell

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: March 19, 2026
In-game Date:  6th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Varenwood
A Visitor – Zaedra 
We sit at the camp around a campfire.  It is cloudy and getting colder.  We are a mile from the portal that we came through and stopped just outside a think wall of trees/bushes/shrubs.  This seems like a deliberately thick area.  Kaizo sees a hedgehog sniffing his pouch - they lock eyes.  Affy asks about the hedgehog - good eating or what?  Relys says “good morning” and it replies in common.  It then shifts into a wood elf female, Everdawn markings on her cloak.  Zaedra Windfury of the Everdawn (from Ferndale) - she smells Kaizo’s Nightbud.  Relys asks what she has found.  
She brings up the rift/portal…and we comment that we have been there.  She says the portal links to the Shadowfell and a building (the Crucible).  She doesn’t think it can be easily closed.  Thinks we would need to defeat something powerful - the thing that opened it - then can close it with abjuration magic.  Her friend Iris (with the Tegenwald Phet) has investigated this as well.  She thinks the cult members aren’t there because they went to the Shadowfell and never came back.  You either need to go through the rift to kill the powerful person or try to draw them out and kill them here.  
As for what took out the village, she doesn’t think it went back through the portal.  She says she found gnome corpses 3 miles away.  
Relys asks her about the ritual of the Weaving Night, which requires Moonpetals.  She has some and gives two to Relys.  She then tells Relys about the Everdawn and how she’s heard of our group and that she is recruiting for them.  She asks if any of us want to join?  Relys does and she is given a pin and told to speak with animals when she enters a new area and they will likely be able to tell her where the local Everdawn group is.
Affy and Ridley ask what type of damage the dead have (the gnomes all over from this town and the area.  She says they were drained of life.  Iris said traces of dark necrotic evil magic likely drained them.
We know that we are just on the outskirts of Relys’ town.  Relys thanks Zaedra for her help and Kaizo acknowledges that he is already a member of the Everdawn and will help figure out what evil did all this damage.  She says may Sylunara light your steps, turns into a sparrow, and flies away.
1. We need to figure out where the thing roaming the world is - it is killing.  
2. FInd a way to close the rift.  Draw out whatever is keeping it open.  Handle it.  Use a powerful dispel magic to close it
Fungus pulls out the cube and waves it.  He hears an ancient voice in his head (I am your hidden root. Shoddy Nell and the Glyphic sings through the rift; my binder hears it, too.  Guard the cube for Shoddy alone.)  Leaves a feeling behind that something powerful on the other side knows what he did.  Fungus says that he thinks that the people through the rift now know where the cube is.  Fungus doesn’t want to give it up.  
Into Iparos
Relys knows how to step through the branches and leads the group into the once hidden glade of Iparos.  This was once a peaceful, charming village.  It is now destroyed. Standing stones lean and are broken, floating lantern stones are on the ground, sacred spring is a black stagnant pool.   RIdley looks around for clues - he sees small stones all around. Relys thinks that whatever came in to kill the village did it with a shadowy presence because the barrier is still intact.   Relys notices that one of the lantern stones is still hovering - she walks over and touches it and the soft blue light turns to gray (she’s sad).  Affy asks to hold it (she’s mad) and it turns red.  She hands it to Fungus and it turns yellow (he’s chill).  She takes it back.  She then walks into her grandmother’s cottage, as she looks over and sees the cottage where she was raised, its door off the hinges.  
Inside, she sees part of the staff her grandmother used. She knows she needs this staff to perform certain rituals from her village.  She casts detect magic.  Staff is a +1 staff (but would have other properties should she attune to it).  A piece is missing.  It used to have a mushroom-like crown at the top, the color of Moonpetals. Cerul casts the spell Locate Object looking for the missing piece - he doesn’t detect it nearby (not within 1000’).  She also finds a crystal bell with magic (magic is waning) - it has a crack in it.  On the wall is a wreath of silver leaves that were used on the dead (slight magic there).   Cerul starts to mend the crack in the bell and the magic becomes brighter.  He continues to work on it until it is back to normal.  Wreath of Eternal Silver Leaves and Crystal Bell are things Relys puts in her pack.
Ridley sees huge talon marks (maybe like a dragon, but probably not one) are the source for much of the damage to the town.  He determines that the creature headed east, probably carrying something.  He asks if her village had an issue with a dragon and she says no.  Fungus starts looking around the outside of the village. He sees a nice ceremonial robe used when a ritual takes place. Affy looks around and notices a small pouch and picks it up.  Inside is a small night bud flower and a moon petal.  She hands them to Relys.  We ask if they had a wealthy resident…and she says they didn’t really use money.  It was “peace and harmony in the commune.”  We ask if she’s ready to visit her old house and she says she needs to go.  She goes inside and sees her bed is still made.  She finds her journal from when she was younger and pockets it.  Kaizo walks up and tries to console her with random words of loss and love.  
The Shadowfell Rift
Relys takes one more pass through the village and we decide to leave and head back to the portal. As we approach, we line up in a marching order (Affy, Ridley, Kaizo, Relys, Fungus and Cerul).  We pass through the rift into the Shadowfell.  We see it is similar to where we came from, but lacking life and color.  Tall, gaunt trees with no leaves; still air, gray skies.  We see movement deep in the trees…shadows.  All colors are muted and dull.  Kaizo feels terrible, it is drawing everything he has and is taking all of his zen.  We see a copse of trees with rocks nearby and take the Shadar-kai bodies out of the portable hole to place here.  Cerul starts to remove the bodies.  Kaizo feels like he is windblown - feels like he is in the middle of a tornado.  Ridley flies up to take a look around.  Kaizo feels like he is being squeezed.  That something is zero’ing in.  He starts to meditate.  Fungus starts to cast a ritual.  Ridley starts to see movements in the trees and messages Relys that “creatures are starting to close in on the group.”  He also feels like stealth doesn’t matter - that something knows where we are at.
IT IS INITIATIVE
* ROUND ONE:  Shadowy figures emerge.  Kaizo hits one and puts extra monk-stuff behind it, but it absorbs that damage.  He then hits again.  Then as a bonus uses a flurry of blows and hits for more damage.  He thinks necrotic damage helped and not hurt it.  Kaizo and Relys feel that this is something sent to the group - that they were hunting us, but are not the hunter (the person calling all the shots).  One then attacks Kaizo and hits with necrotic damage, but it drains his strength.  One attacks Relys and misses.  Relys shifts into a tiger and attacks.  Affy runs over and attacks the one on Kaizo and kills it, then moves over and attacks the one on Relys and kills it, hitting with her sword but also inflicting force damage.  Ridley hit one twice and then flew over to another tree.  Fungus casts a spell on himself and then hits one, but it does no damage.  They all start to look at Fungus.  One runs over to Fungus but can’t attack.  Another one runs over and attacks him and misses. Cerul casts Radiance of the Dawn and all the bad guys have to make saving throws, as two vanish in radiant light. One runs up to Cerul and fumbles.
* ROUND TWO:  Kaizo takes a few steps back and decides to rest next to the tiger.  A tree nearby cracks and crumbles and a large creature steps through it and moves next to the tiger and attacks. It attacks with chains and misses Relys.  She then disengages and steps behind Affy on the rocks and casts starry wisp at the new creature, and makes a critical hit and it lights up. Affy runs over to the new creature and attacks., missing twice but still doing damage.  Ridley shoots at the creature on Fungus from the trees and hits with a sneak attack, killing it.  Then shoots at the one on Cerul and hits.  Then puts a hunter’s mark on the new guy. Fungus blasts the new guy and misses. Another tree splits open and a creature advances to Fungus and attacks and it misses and then it hides. Cerul attacks the one on him and hits it, killing it.  Another tree splits open and a creature emerges and walks over to Fungus and attacks and hits.  The character takes some cold damage from hitting him. Fungus is grappled and restrained and injured, and pulls Fungus closer with the chains it has.
* ROUND THREE:  Kaizo attacks the creature near him, missing, then hitting once.  He disengages and moves back.  The creature near Affy attacks her, as Cerul puts out a flare and it misses.   Relys casts a spell on herself.  Affy attacks the creature on her and hits, but then misses, but still does damage.  She shifts closer to Cerul.  Ridley feels like these are minions of something bigger.  He attacks and hits and pushes the guy toward Fungus.  Fungus then misty steps away, inflicting damage on the one who had it grappled, and then blasts one and hits with thunder, killing it. The hidden one moves up to attack Affy and hits her with significant psychic damage - then moves away, hiding.  Cerul runs up and hits one, inflicting radiant damage. Then uses Word of Radiance and he takes additional damage.   The one that was hidden lights up, too.
* ROUND FOUR:  Kaizo runs over to the one that was just lit up and crits it.  We see swirling all around the battlefield…we can feel our hair moving with the wind. Relys steps down off the rock and attacks the one who has been hit, and hits it, lighting it up.  She then attacks again and hits again.  Affy runs up and fumbles, but then recovers and attacks again and kills it.  The swirl becomes a massive figure with chains in the center of the clearing.  He steps forward and looks at Fungus.  And he says “the cube now” and Fungus has to make a wisdom saving throw. Fungus is paralyzed.  He then takes a swing at Kaizo and misses.  We realize that we are in his world.  Ridley says “Woah! You’re a big boy” moves HM over to the “big boy”, shoots at it and misses. He then draws a hand crossbow and climbs out of the tree, runs over, and shoots with his hand crossbow, missing. One of the creatures tries to hide, then attacks Relys but misses.  The big boy used a legendary action to hit Ridley… missed due to my goat (the healer) popping off with a flare gun in the dude's face… WOAH 14 temp hp to Riddles... Again my goat… Cerul runs over to the frozen lesser big boy and frees him from paralysis.  And then casts Radiance of the Dawn again.  Both save, but still take damage.
* ROUND FIVE:  The big boy turns and looks at Kaizo and says “flee” and luckily he saves so he doesn’t flee. Kaizo then attacks the big boy and hits twice, inflicting force damage.  He then disengages far away. The big boy then gazes at Affy and she takes a lot of psychic damage and is paralyzed.  Relys attacks the final smaller creature (she notices that Tananan is whispering something in Fungus’ ear).  She hits the creature, killing it.  She then runs over to the big boy and hits.  Then disengages and moves away a little bit.  Affy is paralyzed.  Big boy looks at Fungus and sees the fairy on his shoulder and tries to hit him and misses (he used Lucky).  He then looks at Ridley and holds his hand up and he feels necrotic energy injure him, losing HM. (then Affy is no longer paralyzed)  Ridley pulls out a dagger and attacks, hitting with sneak attack. He then shoots him with a hand crossbow and hits.  He then attacks a third time and hits again.  The big boy turns and looks at Ridley, as a warding flare comes from Cerul, and he fumbles. Fungus then casts blink and another spell, but blink doesn’t work.  At the end of Fungus' turn the big boy looks at Relys and she saves.  Cerul attacks the big boy and he fumbles, but is ok.
* ROUND SIX:  Kaizo runs forward to Affy and heals her and the bonus action to run around the corner.   The big guy turns and looks at Fungus and he is paralyzed and takes significant psychic damage (Fungus hears, I can take it to her in his ear).  Relys crits on the big guy with rend, damaging his left leg. She then does a lot of radiant damage and extra fire damage.  She then hits again.  And disengages.  Affy fumbles, then hits.  Then takes an action surge to miss, but he still takes damage, but then she hits and also inflicts force damage.  The big guy goes after Fungus and inflicts damage, taking him down then grabs Fungus’ body with his claw. He then turns and yells to everyone else “you should flee.” Ridley attacks with his dagger and hits.  A swirl of necrotic energy starts to flow around the big boy and he vanishes and appears 60’ away.   Ridley shoots again and misses and dashes to him.  Then attacks again and misses. He then turns and looks at Affy and she takes necrotic damage.  Fungus rolls a death save and then blinks away.  The big boy then hits Ridley and restrains him. Cerul misty steps up next to him and casts spirit guardians and kills him.  
* ROUND SEVEN:  Tananan is flying around, frantically looking for Fungus.  Kaizo makes his way to the clearing and starts punching the air.  Relys jumps off the rock and moves into the clearing.  Affy heals herself with a second wind and dashes over to where she last saw Fungus. Ridley flies up to try to catch Fungus - he has seen him blink before. Fungus makes a death save. Then blinks in and falls to the ground as Affy makes sure he doesn't take damage with her reaction.  He then stays and Cerul runs over to heal him.  
Tenanan is thrilled to see Fungus.  He feels a vibration in his backpack.  She says she will take it to “her” - and gives her the cube.  As soon as she gets it, she disappears.  Ridley thinks this was an avatar of Malgri’n - that he is still at large.  
Cerul and Affy finish with the dead bodies, portable hole.  The group starts to leave and walk through the portal. As we are starting to leave, Fungus feels a coldness and he thinks his patron is no longer on this plane (something massive changed with Shoddy Nell).  We step through the rift and we see the overlapping rift.  We go into the Crucible and then step through and go to the Varenwood again. The portal is still there.  Cerul is going to try to dispel the portal.  He sees the rift collapse and it looks like it worked.  The gate now goes back and forth to the Crucible.  
– The End of the Session –
