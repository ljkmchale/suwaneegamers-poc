## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  August 21st, 2025
In-game Date:  34th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  The Shaded Mantle, Qal’dynn
Lorian sees us and says he owes us…gesturing toward a booth.  Affy says - I see you don’t conduct all your business in a booth. How can I show my gratitude - coin?  A favor? Something in the future?  Fungus - says magic. Affy says - what was that service worth?  So you want coin?  Fungus says yes - maybe so.  Kaizo mentions horses.  Then coin would be good for you - where are you going?  Affy mentions wanting honey…so he mentions going to see the dwarf. 
Then brings it back to payment.  All want coin - except Affy - who wants a favor at some point. He pays everyone 100gp.  Says he owes Affy a favor.  Affy asks about the previous person he met with and he said he was an informant - nothing more.  Cerul asks what Lorian thinks about the town - is it going to be opened back up. Lorian said it was opened up when Ham was announced.  He then asks what Lorian thinks about the Rel’Udomina.  He thinks that people should be able to do what they need to do.  But we mention that the King wants them out of the city.  And we mention that we’ve killed many of them, including their leader.  He asks if Affy wants to turn in her favor for more info…and Fungus says he will do that. 
Lorian asks why we are talking about the Rel'Udomina.  We say that we are well connected…and have an interest there.  He reveals that his informant is actually doing work for him at the Abbey…that he infiltrated the Rel'Udomina.  Cerul mentions that he is from the Abbey and has a personal reason to take interest in the Abbey.  Lorian says that the Rel'Udomina has infiltrated the Abbey and is planning to lock it down (he says that the arcane waypoint has been shut down).  Fungus mentions that we could restore order at the Abbey…and Lorian says that would be a fine feat…but not of his concern.  He mentions the stone statue…stone giant statue…and how they have the ability to turn someone to stone.  He says to approach the Abbey with care.  We then eat dinner and depart.
Cerul realizes that Lorian is likely a member of the Tegenwald Phet (with the reference to he and his colleagues).  We go to the Pale Lantern to rest. We get long rest. 
35th of Talil
Next morning before heading out, Fungus uses the cube (Affy watches) - all else 60+ away.  He rolled a 70 [LOST STEP]. As he turns it, it stops and it is a new combination. Nothing appears to have happened yet. As we start to walk, Affy notices that Fungus doesn’t make footstep sounds…until 6 seconds later (has advantage on stealth this day).  Affy feels like this one would be easy to recreate in the future.
Mistress Toilee Goldbinder
We work our way into the foothills.  See flowering trees and bushes in the hills…but not naturally propagated, more intentionally planted.  As we continue, we get the whiff of honey.  After a while, we see a figure with an apron/leathers step out from an awning that is cut into the hillside. She comes out and we ask if she is Mistress Goldbinder.  She says yes and asks if we are there for wax.  Says it is hard to come by right now.  Fungus mentions a favor and she says she needs help.  She is missing one of her Queen Lusterbees.  Says if we can find her Queen, she will give us Lusterbee wax.  We ask how big the queen is (small) and what happened.  She doesn’t know.  Affy asks Relys what she knows about lusterbees…and she doesn’t.  The dwarf says there were large footprints…and Relys goes to look at them and finds the trail that they took (behind the awning and up the hill).  The prints are big and then smaller…and Relys thinks someone was intentionally trying to mess up the trail.  But Relys figures it out (nat 20 roll).  Fungus asks how long the queen has been gone (a stretch) and Relys wants to speak with the bees. 
She casts a spell and talks to worker bees in the neighboring hive. She asks if they have seen someone who might have left those footprints.  They said they saw a little fella with pointed ears who is a little taller than her.  We decided to track the goblin on the trail.  Fungus asks about food and she points out apples …which he helps himself to.
We follow the trail to a dense forest. It is a game trail and not well worn.  We come to a place where we have to make a choice - up the hill or down back into the hills on a more worn path.  We decide to keep going up.  Eventually we start to hear echoing waterfalls…not loud, but noisy.  The path opens into a ravine.  We hear where water is trickling into a larger body of water…lots of stone.  A small pond to the right.  A few little buildings in the area on the left…and a cave.  We see a hanging rope bridge…and another building that is built on a ledge.  And we notice a very artistic beehive next to that building.
We see a goblin and a big burly guy positioning a cauldron over a fire. Affy and Kaizo decide to go over, Affy speaking goblin.  The small goblin tells us to leave…that there are students and a teacher here.  The teacher - big guy - is in a cave.  The leader here is making stuff to sell in town to bad people (a black network)...and need to bees to do so.  The goblin asks us to leave and offers to walk us out…and shares that information with us.  We notice that by entering the cave, you can get up to the higher level.  Affy asks the goblin if he is being held against his will and he says no - he likes it there.  Kaizo notices the goblin is under a spell of some type.  We see a Rongri guarding the beehive.  
As we communicate and think and strategize…we think they probably think we left, but they are on guard.  But in our favor, there is a lot of noise in the ravine.  The ravine is probably 60’ across. We decide that Relys will change into something to scout while we wait until it gets dark.  Kaizo will get in a hiding spot to scout from the path.  Cerul, Affy, and Fungus will wait to see what they say.  
Kaizo notices at least five elven graduates from the university…plus another one who is bigger.  Also see a bugbear (with a large silvered maul), a couple of goblins.  Hear a very loud, hearty laugh (must be something big).  Notices that the rongri jumps on a robed lady’s shoulder with a mace tied to her side who is near the upper cave where the hive is. 
Relys notices at least six goblins as she gets close to the cave (shifted into a spider). She sees that people are going to the cauldron to get bowls of soup.  They seem to be more friendly as they eat soup.  She sees them take the cauldron into the cave. She sees cots (one goblin taking a nap).  And she sees a huge being - much bigger than Fungus.  He is playing dice with some of the students.  He’s drinking from the cauldron.  Sees stairs leading up in the cave.  She then sees 4 huge spiders clinging to the ceiling…and a stack of bones under them (clearly they have been fed).  She leaves the cave and starts across the ravine…and is noticed by the rongri.  She sees a big table and a lab set up in one of the buildings.  She sees tentacles, but isn’t sure what that is.  She comes back to the group and tells what she has seen.
At night, we pass without trace to get into the cave.  Once we enter the cave, we see a large guy playing dice with two goblins, and another goblin asleep with boots hanging over his head. Affy leads everyone into the cot cave and then wakes the goblin instead of grappling it.  IT IS INITATIVE.
Round One:  The goblin jumps up and runs, trying to get out of this area.  Relys attacks it and kills it. Kaizo picks up the boots and Relys determine they are magical.  Relys puts them on {Boots of False tracks…any humanoid size}.  We search the dead body and find a pouch with 6sp (Kaizo gets it).
We decide to attack. IT IS INITIATIVE.
ROUND ONE:  When one goblin tries to leave the cave to get more soup, Fungus clubs him and hits.  Relys steps up to hit the same goblin and kills it.  Kaizo runs up and kills the goblin.  Cerul steps up and casts a spell against the big guy, damaging him with necrotic damage.  Affy misses 4 times…but he takes damage.  He then attacks Affy and Kaizo - and misses both.  Fungus steps up and poison sprays him.  
ROUND TWO:  Relys runs up a little and casts a spell and hits.  Kaizo then attacks the big guy and misses, tries again and hits, giving advantage to the next attack roll against him.  Cerul then casts a spell on the big guy and hits. Affy then attacks and hits twice.  He then attacks Kaizo and Fungus and hits both. He then takes a knee and says “no fight” in giant.  Fungus says if you surrender you fight for me.  He says I can’t fight..I’m about to die.  He asks if there is any danger up the stairs.  And the guy doesn’t know since he’s too big. Dilog tells us his name.  We tell him to wait in the cot cave and leave Hopper to watch.  He gives us a pile of coins (132sp and set of bone dice).  Fungus says he will play dice with him if he stays put.  
We decide to go up into the cave further, and look for the giant solifugids.  Cerul wants to light them up. We approach the stairs, trying to be stealthy.  We see them up on the 20’ tall ceiling.  Cerul casts a spell…IT IS INITIATIVE
ROUND ONE: Fungus transfers his hex to a creature, but doesn’t attack.  Kaizo holds his action. Relys casts a spell on one and misses.  One creeps down the walls and Kaizo hits as a reaction and he attacks Kaizo and hits, grappling him.  Another creeps down and attacks Cerul and misses.  Another creature walks down and attacks Relys and hits, grappling her.  And the final creature stays on the wall. Cerul casts spell on them all (radiance of the dawn) on them.  One creature dies.  Another creature saves, but takes damage.  The other two creatures die.  Affy then attacks one and kills it.
ROUND TWO:  The robed figure casts a fireball at the group.  We all take damage.  Fungus steps up and hexes and poison sprays her, knocking her prone and stunning her. Kaizo moves up and holds his action. Rongri (with a dagger in his tail) disappears.  Relys heals herself, shifts into a bear.  Hopper tells the group something is coming up from behind.  Cerul turns around and blasts it with a fireball spell on the creature coming up behind us and damages it solidly.  Affy runs to the creature on the stairs and kills it.  
ROUND THREE:  The robed figure stays stunned (for now).  Fungus moves up, misty steps, and acid splashes her.  Kaizo moves up and strikes her and hits once. The rongri reappears and attacks Fungus and then floats away and climbs up.  Relys attacks the rongri with a spell.  Three goblins attack Affy.  One puts fairy fire on her.  The next two hit her.  Cerul casts a spell on the three goblins, killing one.   Affy kills another goblin (crits) and hits the other.
ROUND FOUR:  Robed lady stands up and disappears.  Fungus casts a spell to try and light up the area where she was standing, but she’s not there.  Fungus misty steps near Kaizo and heals them both.  Kaizo heals himself.  The rongri attacks Fungus and misses and climbs back up the wall.  Relys moves outside the cave and sees the rongri on the wall and attacks and hits it, killing it. As it drops the dagger, Fungus tries to catch it, but doesn’t and is inflicted with poison damage. The goblin tries to hit Affy, but fumbles.  Affy then hits the goblin and then fumbles.  Cerul moves down and hits her with a rapier and kills her. 
ROUND FIVE:  Suddenly the robed figure appears in the cave and casts a spell on Kaizo, Fungus, and Relys, and they all fail their saving throws.  Kaizo and Fungus both go down.  Fungus and Kaizo take death saves. Relys attacks the robed figure and kills her with radiant damage, then comes out of bear form. Cerul and Affy run forward into the cave and Cerul stablizes Fungus.
ROUND SIX:  Fungus heals himself and then runs over the Kaizo to try to help him with a healer’s kit and stabilizes him. 
We loot the room and then gather near Cerul, who heals us all.  Bugbears, remaining goblins and students have all abandoned the camp.  The professor (robed figure) is dead.  We find a poisoner and alchemist kit in her hut.  We see that she was making spells and selling them on the black market. 
We find out that there were sacks with 22gp, 44sp, and 82cp; 11gp, 32sp, and 75cp in the huts.  We find the beehive - the mage had some kind of woodcrafting on it.  We find the queen bee inside.  The mage has a magical mace (Lightbringer) which Cerul takes.  Kaizo picks up the Dagger of Venom (poisonous dagger).   Inside the upper shack, we also find a chest of profits from her sales:  15pp, 50gp, and 275sp.  We go to the cave next door and find a crate of goods - there is a journal there: 6 philters of love, 2 potions of climbing, 2 potions of comprehension, 1 potion of fire breath, 1 potion of truth serum, poison (serpent’s venom), poison (carrion crawler mucus), vials/Flasks (12 empty).  We find +1 leather armor on the little goblin.  
We take a short rest here.
We then decide to leave, carrying the items with us.  We see that the people who left went back to town…we know they are there.  We go back to the beekeeper and give her the nice hive and her queen bee.  We ask for the 100gp worth of wax.  She hangs it and is very happy.  She gives Fungus the wax he needs (150gp worth - 50 extra).  
We head back to Qul’dynn and we make our way back to the Pale Lantern and get a long rest.
