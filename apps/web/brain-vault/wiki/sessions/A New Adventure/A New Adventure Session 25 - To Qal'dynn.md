## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: April 2, 2026
In-game Date:  6th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Varenwood
A Visitor – Zaedra 
We decide to go through the portal to the Blackstone Crucible - it stays open, no longer attached to the Shadowfell.  We get there and note the abacus…Relys decides to study it to see how it works, with help from Cerul.  She makes a note of the current coordinates - to note the ones that will take her near Iporos.  She learns that she can shut it down by moving the “beads” to one side.  And thinks you can control the x axis and y axis to move around the world - glimpsing a blurry view through the portal (mountains, forest, water, etc.).  She decides to move the beads a little to see where it resets (we lose vision while it does).  As it resets, she is not sure where the coordinates took it.   We decide to send a couple of us through to just quickly see where she moved the coordinates to (Cerul and Fungus stay back and the others go through).  Relys goes through first, followed by Ridley, Affy, and Kaizo.  
Relys steps through and sees no leaves (typical for a cold season).  Hears a scattering of something.  She thinks she is in Varenwood, perhaps to the east, but no idea how far.  Ridley doesn’t see anything specific to identify the area and Kaizo doesn’t see any herbs.  Seems like a normal forest.  Relys shifts into a owl and flies up to get a sense of the land, looking for water specifically.  She doesn’t see any large bodies of water, merely streams and ponds.  Looking west, she doesn’t see where we were.  Her gut tells her we went about 20 miles.   
Relys decides to push the x axis about twice as far.  We step through and it is forest again.  We come back through and she pushes it again another twice over and feels like she is over water.  Relys flies through alone and sees that she is over a large body of water.  She thinks it is likely Lake Tribathe.  She comes back and shifts back into her form.  She makes other notations in her journal of these coordinates, knowing this puts her over the water.
The group decides to head to the Abbey, taking the abacus with us. Cerul wraps it up in a bedroll and carefully places it in the portable hole.  We make our way out of the Crucible, marching 2x2 (Ridley and Kaizo leading).  We leave the building and there is a layer of snow in the canyon.  We are halfway between the temple and the opening to the cave and Ridley hears something.
We see a spectral stone giant’s head coming from the cliffside.  There are runes on the spectral form.  Translucent and hazy, it almost seems like a portal.  He says, I am Amag.  I see visitors leaving the temple.  Fungus says “Hello” in giant.  Little vessel of unclaimed light - looking at Cerul.  He continues - talking of Cerul, Muerg, etc.  Cerul says, “I don’t believe we have met, hello.”  I’m not really here, but hello to you, too.  Where are you, Cerul asks.  He realizes he knows the answer.  There are 3 stone giant clans in this area - they are the Ridgeworn (they protect the Cradlecrest Rise).  One clan is the Barg clan (they watch the Bastian).  One is the Karos clan (Stonespeaker Atta, knows Cerul).  Final one is the Aosul clan - Watchers of all - shamanistic.  The High Carver, Uspar, is the one who leads all 3 clans.  He told Cerul that this is the guy that took Cerul to Ada.   Cerul realizes that his magic is starting to fade.  This one says that he watches the emerging temple.  But you need to go where you were born…there is something there that he needs to address, but can’t do so until his light is ready.
What he actually said:
Something unresolved waits beneath where you were born, little one. The scar still dreams. Return there when the light within you grows steady… or the mountain may claim what the angel could not finish.
Ridley posits that maybe if Cerul prays to the light he will get answers, because Cerul doesn’t know where he is from.   Ridley notices a stone fall off the cliff wall as the figure went back into the cliff.  He goes over to pick it up.   The stone doesn’t match the other stones from the cliff.  This looks like it probably came from a mountain, maybe higher up on a mountain.  Ridley gives the stone to Cerul.  Thinks maybe it came from a mountain in the CradleCrest (Bastion of Order to Qal’dynn).  
The group continues to the Abbey, heading through the caves.  We pause as we exit - seeing nothing dangerous.  We finally make it to the Abbey of Light, having been gone 2 days.
ABBEY OF LIGHT
We make our way to the Warden, to report back on the status of the cave - what we found and did.   The group gives a brief description of the threat being removed, when Fungus starts to tell everything about the Crucible, Shadowfell, portal, dead gnomes, hedgehog person, cube, an abacus, etc.  The Warden is confused.  We ask for food and shelter and he tells us to go to the Rising Hearthlight.
Ridley asks if anyone here would know of Cerul’s birthplace and we are told to talk to one of the Matrons.  We go back to the RIsing Hearthlight.  Kaizo wants to meditate, looking for balance.  Most of us want to eat and rest.  Ridley sets alarm around him.  Relys goes through her things gathered from her village (she finds a silver key shaped like a crescent moon in the pocket of the ceremonial robe).  We rest.  
Kaizo starts to meditate and starts to get his zen back.  Fungus snores and farts, things feel right for a few hours.  Then he feels a disruption…like something found him, stronger than it was before.  He feels dark tendrils wrapping around him and stops meditating and walks into an adjacent room and tries to meditate there. He starts to feel good again, but then a different feeling hits - blood and fire. This feels like something is ripping apart anything it can find - and maybe coming for him.  He thinks he is absorbing this wrath for the entire group.  Something is very angry at the group and he isn’t sure what or who it is.  All he feels is darkness…blood, fire, tendrils.  He thinks it might be the Shadowfell.  He can’t relax because he thinks this “thing” is trying to bind him.  He goes back in the other room and curls up next to Fungus.  He gets his rest, but it is not peaceful.
7th of Paramore, 1246
We wake up.  Kaizo shares that he had a bad dream and couldn’t sleep.  Cerul shares that he spent a lot of time thinking about his birthplace.  
Kaizo wants to hang with the monks at the House of Lighted Breath.  They bow a bunch. Kaizo shares his experience and asks for guidance and help.  He is told that he has “disrupted the shield - what have you done to awaken something that could break through?”  He tells him of his recent travels - specifically about Malgri'n  and the disciples of his who the group fought.  He tells him of the cube that we had - an artifact.  He asks if the cube could be a key.  Kaizo thinks it could be.  He tells Kaizo that Malgri'n  might have been looking for the key.  He thinks that Malgri'n  is the source of his unrest or maybe something else in the Shadowfell.  Kaizo tells about losing his balance and about the portals, everything.  He tells Kaizo then the shadows are looking for something.  Kaizo tells him that we gave the key/cube up.  If this is the master of binding and we gave away a key that he is looking for, we probably released some bindings giving away that key…which would anger him even more.
Cerul finds Matron Mallow.  He tells her what happened on the road about the visit from the giant.  He asks if she was ever told about how he arrived at the Abbey as a child, since he doesn’t know much about his birthplace.  {Benediction is the key word}  She tells him he was brought to the Abbey. He asks how it was destroyed and she has no info about that.  She advises him to research at Uktali Monastery or Argent Halls to find information about this old city, if any information exists.  She tells him where these places are - neither too close by.    
Fungus and Relys go play with children, as Ridley looks on.  Affy goes to sit in the Great Hall of Radiance. Ridley and Fungus visit the Shop of Lighted Breath, and meet Kaizo there.  Ridley buys a communal hooka at the shop.  Kaizo buys herb pouches. Ridley shops for herbs with specific qualities, but can’t find them.
Around lunchtime, we all regroup and go to the House of Reflective Light to use the circle in the cellar.  Cerul uses sending to let Qal’dynn know we are coming - he reaches out to the Academy and to the nearest person at the arcane waypoint to let them know the King’s Outlanders would like to use the waypoint immediately.  He uses the arcane waypoint to take us there.  
QAL’DYNN
We talk about Affy needing to talk to Lorian and Ridley needing to seek information about the wizard he is seeking.
We are greeted as the Heroes of Qal’dynn.  We are at the Aetherion Academy - it is a cool day, as we step out into the Quad.  Affy asks Cerul to message Lorian about having a meeting today if possible.  He does and Lorian replies that we will meet at the Shaded Mantle for dinner.  
Affy, Cerul, and Fungus walk about and try to find a mapmaker and jeweler.  
Kaizo is going to Tansian’s Herbs (10) and Ridley will follow, stealthily (20), blending into the crowd.  Kaizo tries to be tricky and pop up in front of Tansian, but it doesn’t work.  Ridley looks around to see if Kaizo is being followed, and he spots someone that he knows looking at them both.  They lock eyes and that person pales and takes off running.  This person bolts down an alley and Ridley follows.  They turn left and cut down an alley to the right and Ridley continues to follow, closing ground.  She then cuts left and two heavily armored men step out and say “stay away from the lady - she is under the protection of the Meridian.”  The other one eyes him and says "what business do you have with her?”  Ridley knows this is Eliyena.  He says “she is a client.”  A client, the guard says, so you know?  So you can help us?  We thought she was the key to understanding the noble house.  Are you the key?  Tell us what you know. Ridley says this is best told in her presence.  And they say they haven’t been able to get her to talk. Ridley says he needs to talk to her.  They ask if he knows of the House of Myr.  They hand him a silver sigil of the Meridian and say that if he can help, then he could become one of them.  RIdley asks if he can continue…and they let him go.  He tries to track where she went and can’t pick up her trail.  He starts to ask others if “they saw a woman run by” and he gets some “yes” and some “no” responses.  He didn’t expect to see her in this town.

He asks how it is going.  She says it is going well and hands him a letter from (the foreman of the sanctuary)…the build is going well, but he needs another 100gp.  If you leave the money with Lord Anceril, they can do more before Natali.  He asks about the sales of nightbud.  She says they are averaging one per day. He then asks if she has herbs for a communal hooka, and she points him to a shelf.  Kaizo buys some “good stuff” for 1gp and leaves to go find Lord Anceril.  He finds him and pays him 100gp.
Relys is staying at the Academy to find Pivric.   She finds that he has had some classes, but no one has seen him in a few days.  This is not uncommon - he likes to go over to Cirandale and hang out in the ring.  He goes there and tries to become one with nature - he doesn’t bathe, etc. - and stays there until he has to return for class. She goes to that area to try to find him. If the group can make acquaintance with the House of Myr, then you might be able to use the arcane waypoint that they have in Basctdelm that used to belong to Versum Sal.”  He doesn’t have any additional information.
