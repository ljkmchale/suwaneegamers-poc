# Session 16 - To the Abbey

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  September 18, 2025
In-game Date:  37th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  The Pale Lantern, Qal’dynn
We are all in the Pale Lantern, eating, listening to a bard.  We see Cerul.  He comes over.  “Did you catch her?”  Yes - and I have some news.  I sent her to O’naren.  A messenger from O'naren had news for me.  He said the Abbey has been overrun.  One of the giants has been turned to stone.  Cerul suspects that the Rel’Udomina might be behind it.  He told the messenger to find Ham and tell him that our group will go there.  Hopefully we can go as soon as possible. He asks Fungus if he got his charm created.  Fungus responds - and tells him about the intervention of the fey.  We introduce Ridley to Cerul.  He talks about how Ridley is Lorian’s undercover guy.
Cerul tells us that while he was gone, he made some preparations…and might be able to help heal or bring someone back if needed.  We discuss needing to do a few things tomorrow:  get a diamond, rations, Affy needs to pick up her armor, visit Iofyr (de-petrification potion?).  We decide to turn in for the night.
Key evidence (about the Abbey)- map maker in O'naren, signet ring, onyx amulet (used), stilled arcane waypoint.
38th of Talil at the Pale Lantern
The next morning, the group splits up.  Affy and Kaizo to smith; Fungus and Relys to Iofyr; Cerul to find a diamond; Ridley looks around for Ruenor.  At the smith, Affy picks up her plate - it fits great - but doesn’t sell her chain mail (couldn’t persuade him to buy it for a fair price).  Kaizo looks for magical items - doesn’t get anything yet.  
Fungus talks to Iofyr about a potion to help the stone giant…says he doesn’t have anything, but maybe a potion or scroll of greater restoration would help or some type of divine intervention.  He holds up a small flask and says I made this - it is a “sticky potion (1d4 rounds, 1 target, 5’ space, no save) - Iofyr’s Sticky Stuff.”  Fungus buys the potion for 100gp.  He shares the formula with Fungus (who would need wax to make it).  Fungus tells him about the retreat that the group is building…and he says he has never been away from work.
Cerul finds a jeweler and buys two diamonds for 600gp (no haggle).  Then he tries to find Lorian…he finds Ridley in the street and Ridley shares where to find him.  Cerul follows those directions and finds him near where we first found him.  Cerul asks him about the Tegewald Phet and if he has any news.  Cerul says he left information with the Oracle about the Broken Citadel (what the group saw, the passage to the underdark, the arcane waypoint, etc.).  Lorian asks if he is in a rush and Cerul explains what is going on with the Abbey…and how the group is heading out later today.  Ridley walks up…and Lorian asks he and Cerul to join him at the Academy.  Cerul pulls out the drawings of the arcane waypoint…and Lorian studies them as they walk.  They go to the Arcanem…another quad the group has not visited.  People seem awestruck to see Lorian.  They go up to a door with two Realmwarden’s guarding…and Lorian says “me and my friends are going to make a trip - for a few minutes”...door is opened and a chamber in ahead with an arcane waypoint for the city.  Lorian takes all 3 to the Broken Citadel, based on Cerul’s drawing.  Lorian asks what each passage leads to - the underdark maybe, other areas - he pulls out a map.  Cerul is skittish because of what happened last time he was there.  Lorian is happy to have the new info and is thankful to Cerul.  He says he needs to tell the Phet about this.  Cerul confesses that he’s not satisfied with the Phet because he hasn’t been given any missions or information lately.  Lorian says that the Oracle gets distracted so she may have forgotten or may have gone down another rabbit hole….but not to fret.  Cerul asks if there is a waypoint in O'naren - there is not.  Cerul asks Lorian to put a good word into the Phet for him.  They then light up the circle and the 3 go back to Qal’dynn.  Cerul asks Lorian how to use an arcane waypoint.  He tells him that the one in Qal’dynn is always protected by Realmwarden’s…so don’t show up unannounced.  He tells him how and when to use the spell.  Ridley has never traveled through the weave, but now knows what it feels like.  As they arrive, doors open and the guards rush in…surprised by how quick the trip was.  
The group meets up and discusses traveling on foot or via horse.  Decide to leave and go on foot.  It is noon. We decide to travel to the desecrated shrine.  Kaizo picks up rocks along the way.  We discuss what Fungus and Relys learned from Iofyr about having to help the giant.  We ask Kaizo about the rocks…and he tells us about wanting to find where his mentor sat. Ridley thinks maybe the Uktali Monastery in the Distancion Mountains is where he should go.  Ridley wants to hunt….and isn’t successful right now, likely because this road is very well traveled.  We make it to the shrine at twilight. 
As we round the bend, we smell lumber and curing mortar.  We see that there is activity at the shrine - a new shrine is being constructed.  The Elle’berian symbol (moon and crescent) is being reconstructed here.  An area is being cleared out on the opposite side of the road and a pavilion is there - with lots of wagons, tents, and people.  One tent seems to be employers of the King (likely) who are rebuilding the area.  Another tent is set up with younger people - maybe Academy students - around a campfire (three wagons).  We see a couple of Realmwardens.  We also see a couple of smaller tents - looks like adventurers or travelers.  Realmwardens greet us and welcome us to camp.  Fungus tells them we are the “King’s Outlanders” and they have heard of us.  They point out the others (gypsies, workers, students)...offer to share their food with us. Tell us to make ourselves at home.  We set up camp where they pointed…between the construction of the pavilion and the students.  Kaizo, Relys, and Ridley are looking at people planting items near the newly built stone wall next to the shrine.  There is a young elven woman.  She looks to be planting natural wildflowers.  Kaizo introduces himself.  She mentions the Abbey….says they were supposed to go and study there, but have heard they can’t go.  She mentions an herb that grows near the Abbey…and says that she is trying to figure out if they should turn around or keep going.  Relys asks about the herb…it is called Silverlace.  Supposed to calm tormented souls…bring them back to normal when under distress.  Ridley says that they should go to O'naren and stay there a day or three.  Kaizo says to wait for a sign to see when it is safe to go to the Abbey.  Kaizo says to visit Meladra in O'naren…and tell her that she is seeking balance, harmony and transcendence.  She says she will find her. She asks if her friends can come.  Her friends would like to enter the sisterhood.  But Kaizo says this girl wants more than that.  Relys says the group will let her know when it is safe to go to the Abbey.  Ridley asks Kaizo why he cares about these kids and he replies that he is looking for Mentees.  Relys talks to Velsa (the girl) and helps her plant flowers.  Relys and Kaizo help make the flowers grow and bloom…with magic. 
Fungus and Affy go get stew from the Realmwardens.  Affy notices and points out to Fungus a group talking.  We see a half orc leaning forward over a map.  We hear him say “it wasn’t a rumor…and about the map, land, soil” - as we notice his companions.  We hear “right there” and a tiefling says “there is the buried hive…we will find out when we get to Tarnsport.”  Affy and Fungus ask to sit down.  Ornoc (half orc), Eleran (elf), Gruna Cragfury (dwarf), Jaren (human), Slickwink (little one), Sarlus (tiefling).  These are the Sellswords of Stoneshore.  They are headed to Tarnsport.  We ask why.  He says they are going through the Varenwood (north of Lake Tribath).  Ornoc asks Sarlus to tell what is going on.  Plucking Phesant…bard there…told a tale:  seek the seer for Aster’s eye in Tarnsport.  They are going to ask the seer where to go next on an adventure.  The mention Forgotten Tome of the Brudhold…this is something to do with powerful relics and monstrous insects.  Fungus asks if they have anything to trade.  Fungus shows the Philter of Love.  Affy mentions her chain mail…but they don’t need it.  Fungus makes jokes about the love potions.  They show us Bottled Breath (elemental air - gust of wind)...and trade the love potion for it.  Fungus asks if anyone is interested in the fey (and he pulls out the cube to show).  All, but the human gather close.  Affy warns them that the outcome can be good and sometimes it can be bad.  The cleric stomps off.  Slickwink seems interested.  Affy asks if he wants to replicate something we have done before or not.  We tell them of some of the outcomes. The halfling steps up to turn it. Fungus is next to the halfling, Ridley is 31’ away, Kaizo, Affy, Cerul, and Relys are 61’+ away.  The halfling turns it…and Fungus notices that something is off.  A glow on the cube fades and sparks…and the halfling glows and his eyes roll back and he mumbles and he shoots a firebolt. [63 - WEAVER - you get the Magic Initiate Feat].  He asks how much it would be to buy the cube and Fungus says it is not for sale.  Everyone else says that nothing happened to them…and he shoots another firebolt.  Fungus shakes his hand.  He says his real name is Piven Slickwick Dapplemear.  
Cerul talks to the Realmwardens about the Abbey - trying to see what the latest word is.  He was told that a party was headed in that direction a few days ago, but didn’t turn back…not sure what happened to them.  
Group gets free food.  Sets up camp.  We notice that the gypsies are looking over.  The leader - silver-eyed - looks over (she is human).  The leader goes into one of the wagons and someone follows her in.  Kaizo decides to go over and sit at their fire…he gets out his pipe and doesn’t say anything but smokes with them.  The leader comes out at one point and sits there.  The person in the wagon comes out and sits down, too (this person seems puzzled).  Kaizo talks to the leader.  She is kind and welcomes us.  He asks where they are headed.  They are heading to O'naren tomorrow.  He tells her we are going there, too.  They are then headed to Racelle.  She says she helps people find their destiny.  He tells her he has found his destiny.  He calls Affy over. Affy asks her name - Hamida.  She takes people into the wagon.  Kaizo tosses her 3gp. 
Affy goes into the wagon with Hamida.  There are stones hanging, fabrics, small table, bowl.  Smell of sage, floral, candle wax.  She takes Affy’s hands.  Affy feels her hands shift and move over her hands.  After a minute, she says “oh dear…a mountain of blood watches you still…the fire that shapes you may yet consume what you love…you have a long journey ahead.”  I’m afraid I can pull no more.   Affy talks about how non-specific it was.  She sits next to a guy and asks about his fortune…and it was very different.  He thinks that he was told he was going to make love to two women tomorrow.  Affy wishes him luck.  He says that Mooneyes does a good job.
Affy walks over to tell the group and Fungus wants to walk over.  He pays Affy 3gp to tell his fortune and she tells him predictable things about their upcoming travels.  Then he goes over to meet Mooneyes. Fungus goes over and she sits with Fungus.  “The song in your veins has 2 singers.  Beware of the one in shadow.  Your path touches thorns, but not all draw blood.”  That’s it.  He asks what it means.  And she tries to interpret it.  She asks if he knows who he is tied to. 
Fungus comes out and says she is wonderful. And asks Affy about her foretelling.  She challenges that the other guy was told about two people, too - just like Fungus.  Ridley offers to have his fortune read.  Fungus gives him 3gp to have his fortune read.  He goes over.  “A whisper in the tall grass follows your steps.  Not hunter, not prey, but one who knows her name.  A mask cracks in your future…not the one you wear, but the one you chase.”  That was the most unclear of the evening.  He asks - is Mooneyes what people call you.  She said Mooneyed.  She says she was called that from when she started getting visions.  He leaves and comes over. 
We turn in.  Ridley sets up an Alarm.  We get a long rest. 
39th of Talil
We wake up, the halfling is waiting on the side of camp for Fungus.  They want to turn the cube with Fungus again.  Affy, Fungus, and Slickwink walk away from camp to turn the cube.  Fungus says he will trade the tenderbox to let the little one turn the cube again.  As he gets ready to turn it, the cube doesn’t seem to glow as much.  He turns it…and rolls [14 -Whispers].  It stops turning and sparks a few times.  And then his eyes get big and we hear something in our head and it doesn’t go away.  Whispers haunt us constantly - and we have disadvantage on perception checks.  We go back to camp and pack up and leave for O'naren.
Group travels. Cerul gets the hammer back from Affy - wants it for negotiation.  Ridley hunts as we travel.  He kills a wild boar…and we stop while he cleans it (1 hour).  He has 6 days of rations (1 per person - but Relys doesn’t want it, so Fungus takes another).  He also takes a boar hide and two small tusks.  
We make it to O’naren toward the evening.  It is different from the last time we were here. Two Realmwarden step forward and recognize us…recounting our efforts.  Welcome back.  We ask for an audience to see the Captain (Windseek).  Cerul tells them we need to see them both - Kaizo, Cerul, Relys, and Affy go to Town Hall.  Fungus and Ridley go to the tanner (and sell the hide for 1gp).  
At the Town Hall, Lord Eleneros and Captain Windseek greet the group. Cerul tells him news:  of going to Qal’dynn and pushing back the Rel’Udomina, finding the messenger and the message being destroyed.  Of Ham’s promotion.  And of the maps - as he pulls the maps out - 1) your mapmaker is behind this (Vathern) 2) the locations of Rel’Udomina strongholds, including some in O’naren.  He shares that the mapmaker is missing.  The captain says that he will check out the locations.  Cerul mentions that we need to receive payment for our services (25gp/each for 6 people).  The Lord asks to get 150gp to give our group.  Then Cerul says that we can help, but first need to discuss the Abbey.  He tells them about the maps of the Abbey and the issues in the Abbey.  Affy mentions how the Rel’Udomina are good at disguise…including the infiltration of Realmwarden (mentioning the disguise room in Qal’dynn).  Cerul tells them that we would appreciate any help with preparing for our journey to the Abbey - i.e. putting in a good word with the armory.  Affy mentions the restoration scroll…and Cerul jumps in to talk about the giant turned to stone.  They mention that we need to visit the Oracle. He calls the assistant in to take his words down and creates a missive for Cerul to take to the Oracle.   Affy asks if there has been any other trouble in town…and they say nothing right now.  
We then leave.  Kaizo goes to visit Meladra about the night bud, etc.  She mentions that a few have passed through town.  Kaizo tells her about the establishment he set up in Qal’dynn, as well as the new spa that is being built (Sanctuary of the Three Elders….).  He tells her about the young elf he met on the journey (Velsa) who may be coming soon to meet her.  She offers to transcend with Kaizo and he declines.
Cerul, Affy, and Relys head to the Oracle.  Fungus, Kaizo, and Ridley meet there, too. Female elf…welcome back. She says “something important” but can’t come up with what it is that she was supposed to remember.  Lorian sends his regards.  She asks about the Broken Citadel…and Cerul says he took care of that with Lorian.  Cerul then tells her about the Abbey:  how there are enemies at the Abbey, about the stone giant who was petrified (allies), and about how she might be able to help undo that magic.  She says this might be pushing her abilities…but she agrees that a scroll might be a risk worth taking.  She says she gave 1000gp for the scroll…and Cerul presents her the letter.  She asks if there is anything that we can do to assist her…and agrees to hand over the scroll.  Affy confirms that we will owe her a favor.  Kaizo offers an all-expenses paid trip to the spa.  [Cerul knows that he will need to roll a 15 or higher to use the scroll].  Cerul says he will pay the debt.
We then swing by the armory.  Cerul is looking for an armor upgrade and shows them the scroll from the captain and Lord.  He says it will take some time to get Mithril Splint armor…and Cerul says that is ok.  He says he will consider the letter from the Lord and will make it…and will figure out what it will cost when we return.
We head to the Tavern for food and rest. The gypsies have not arrived.  We go to the Truffle Trap and walk in and hear whispers.  We see the tavern keeper look around and welcome us coming in - offering drinks on the house, and personally commending Affy for her help, saying she’s glad she’s back.  Elderbisque (mushroom soup) is the specialty here, but Relys is looking for something sweet - and they fix something for her.  Group in the tavern is warm, people get to know the newcomers.  She gives us a free meal.  Eventually we see some gypsies enter and check the place out.  Cerul is looking for Melaine (who he told to bring the kids here) - he doesn’t spy her (she got to town, but has been laying low in a room).  Ridley wants to look for the guy who said he was going to have sex with two women…and does eventually find him near the wagons next to the stables.  He decides to stalk the guy all night (34 stealth) to see if his fate comes to pass.  He eventually walks them to have their fortunes read…and then later sees them both go into a wagon with him.  But Ridley isn’t sure what happens.  He then heads back to the inn and go to bed.  Fungus buys rations.  Kaizo lays off the smoke tonight…focuses on meditation.  We all get a good night’s rest at the Nestling Soar.
40th of Talil
We get up and leave the next day - headed to the Abbey. Whispers are gone from Fungus and Affy.  Ridley hunts and finds a boar…and gets 30 days worth of rations (5 rations for all 6 of us - we will need to smoke them at night).  We know this will be a 4 day trip over hills.  
On Day 1, Fungus wants to roll the cube…and Affy and Ridley (dressing his pig) are close (she leaves her packs elsewhere).  He rolls [77 -FEY STEP].  Threads of light spiral and stutter…light flickers like dying candles.  Fungus can move through the ethereal plane…this is an instant misty step with each move.  A shape forms through the ripple and Taniana steps in.  We all see her.  She tilts her head, and a mischievous smile appears on her sweet face …
"The master felt your borrowed spark,
And sent me dancing through the dark."
She flutters lightly around you:
"Twice in sun and once in shade —
Such hunger makes the fabric fray.
Let once per stretch be your creed,
Lest you starve the Feywoven feed."
She shifts to your other side, looks up with sudden seriousness, voice softening:
"The charm you wield, a root now grows.
A bond, a knot — the patron knows.
When Nell does call, don’t look behind —
For not all debts are of the mind."
Fungus tells the group what she said.  Nell is in the shadow because someone in the fey is against her and thus, against Fungus.  Nell knows that Fungus has the cube and is abusing it…and only use it once per stretch.  
We continue on…until dark.  Set up camp.  Set up the meat to smoke - Ridley does this.  Kaizo sets up watch.  Ridley sets up alarm spell.  Night is uneventful.
41st of Talil
Day 1/8 of rest for the cube
We see a figure limping on the road as we pack up and head out.  It is bloodied elf…who says his friends were killed.  They had been at the Abbey.  We run up.  Cerul tells him to sit…and he casts Cure Wounds.  I’m Ruvian…thank you.  Don’t go that way - you are going to die.  Really - we were going to the Abbey.  I had heard there was trouble, but the boulder just crushed the horse and my friends.  I got hit with a big log…I was crushed and left for dead.  I didn’t see much.  I wasn’t at the Abbey….we didn’t make it there.  Ridley thinks this is truthful.  He said they were going to the Abbey to visit.  He wasn’t told how dangerous it was.  Fungus fixes him rations and stuff to take.  He warns us to turn around.  He recounts the tale more specifically. Kaizo tells him to ask for Meladra when he gets to O’naren.  Fungus adds - to ask for peace, harmony, and transcendence.  Affy asks who they worship (Fralee and Layeth).  We proceed forward.  Travel until night.  Camp is the same.  Smoke more meat.  Fungus conjures a fey wild spirit.  Ridley sets alarm.  Night is quiet. No issues. 
42nd of Talil
Day 2/8 of rest for the Feywoven Glyphic
We head out.  Kaizo talks about how it was too quiet on watch and we agree to have more on watch tonight.  We start to notice that the trail narrows.  We see trees snapped, shattered remains of wagons, gear buried under boulders.  Relys thinks the boulders were tossed.  As we get close to twilight after a day of travel, we see boot prints, lots of gear…and we notice something carved into the rocks.  Cerul says it is says “Turn Back, Not Welcome” in giant.  We look for an area in the trees where we can make camp.  Cerul decides to fly up and scout for a minute…to see what he sees.  He sees a couple of clearings that look like they might be good.  He also sees where a body has been ripped apart - not indicative of a giant, but some other predator.  He tells us there is something out here besides giants.  We go to the clearing further up.  We decide to not have a campfire.  Affy/Ridley on watch 1; Fungus and Kaizo on watch 3; Cerul and Relys on watch 2.  
Watch 1 - we hear sounds in the distance that sound like thunder.  Ridley wants to scout about (29 stealth; casts darkvision).  He heads to the right. Affy actively watches from the campsite.  Ridley travels a little ways - sees a giant walking in the distance (sees in silhouette about 30 minutes from camp).  He then heads back to camp.  They finish the watch.  
Watch 2 - Wake up Relys and Cerul and tell them about the giant.  Affy and Ridley go to bed.  The two stand watch at camp, but don’t leave.  They wake up Fungus and Kaizo.  
Watch 3 - The two stand guard over the camp.  Hear rumbling, but stay put.  Dawn comes.
43rd of Talil
Day 3/8 of rest for the Feywoven Glyphic
The group packs up and heads out.  As we travel, we see dead bodies and evidence of the danger on the trail.  We continue further, growing more uneasy.  We round a bend and the sounds of nature are gone.  Towering before us as we round a bend, we see a stone giant - not moving - he has been stoned.  This is Cerul’s friend (Brundhalk).  Before Cerul can cast a spell and use the scroll, the ground rumbles and a stony boulder turns into a monster, a tendril emerges (another creature) and two birds dive.  We enter combat – IT IS INITIATIVE.
– The End of the Session –
