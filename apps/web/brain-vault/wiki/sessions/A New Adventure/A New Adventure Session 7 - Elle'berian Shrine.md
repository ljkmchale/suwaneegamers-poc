# Session 07 - Elle’berian Shrine

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  May 1st, 2025
In-game Date:  27th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Cackletongue Lair
Cackletongue Lair - 27th of Talil, 1246
We decide to head back to town…to rest up and seek out the Captain to tell him what happened at the Silent Grove and the gnoll caves. Cerul talks about how surprised he was about the wings that emerged…it starts to make sense why the quasit mentioned “the stink of angel blood.”  It was Cerul…he has celestial blood.  Ham and Affy talk about “daddy issues” and “son of a whore” issues…and the fact that we are a group of misfits.
By the time we get back to O’Nearen, it is around noon.  Ham is itching to use the cube.  Cerul and Kaizo and Ro stand 30’+ away.  Affy and Fungus stand with Ham, trying to pay attention.  Ham rolls a [04] - he feels more vertically mobile, as do Affy and Fungus.  They can climb at a speed equal to our movement speed - [Climber].  These three can try to replicate this in the future (arcana and history are the skills that will help).  We don’t notice this, however, since we aren’t climbing at the moment.  Kaizo jokes that the three grew horns.
Half-orc Visitors
Ham hands the cube back to Fungus (he puts it in a side pouch).  As we enter the town, we see a group of 4, then 5, who stand out…because these are half orcs.  Affy looks at them - they are not familiar, but she observes they are all wearing a silver pendant…that reminds me of a story that I heard when I was little from B’denfu (she told me that my father wore a pendant like that).  Affy tells the group she’s going to talk to them.  About that time, the barmaid walks out and accuses them of not paying their tab and glares at me.  Affy steps in and tries to act like there was a misunderstanding - is able to persuade the group to take part and also tries to tell the realmwardens to back down.  Affy goes inside and pays the 1gp tab.  Kaizo flips her 3gp and we all go inside to eat.  Tavrus (who Affy called Rolf) and his 4 buddies, Affy, and Ham go inside to eat.  Kaizo heads to the herbalist, while Ro goes to get rooms.  Fungus and Cerul go to the Town Hall/Captain to tell him what transpired at the Grove and in the caves - and to get a reward. 
Affy, Ham and the half orcs sit down.  Tavrus asks what is up…Affy lays into them. They are travelling - heading from town to town, not working.  They hate people turning their noses to them.  Affy finally gets around to asking about the pendant.  They say they won it in a game of cards against a (older male, with scar) human named Calden in Basctdelm.  Affy and Ham think this isn’t the entire truth.  Ham challenges them to a card game.  Ham wins the first round and 1gp.  He then tries again - with the pendant on the line now. He wins the pendant and Tavrus is mad.  Ham offers him some coin for the story.  Calden was a prestigious guy who they were following around the city 2 years ago.  They could tell he was loaded.  They stole the pendant from him.  Ham pays them 7gp for the pendant and the information. 
The Herbalist
Kaizo heads to the herbalist.  Talks to Meledra about the nightbud, tells her it has all been destroyed except what he has.  He proposes that she reserve a back room as a sanctuary for the three elders for a plant he has druid-crafted from a seed in front of her.  For every nightbud, one seed goes to the sanctuary for anyone coming in looking for balance, harmony, and transcendence: those get it for free if available.  She can sell whatever other seeds it makes.  She agrees.
Hall of O’naren
At the hall, greeted by male half elf.  Asks what they are here for…and they say the reward.  They mention Ham, and he says “oh - Ambassador Hamalex….” and he is more helpful then.  He goes down the hall and opens double doors, but Cerul and Fungus can’t see what’s going on.  The guy returns and asks if he can fetch Ham…says there is other business that involves “the Ambassador.”  Cerul says he will run and get him, while Fungus asks for a drink.  They discuss wine options.  Cerul gets Affy and Ham and Cerul cleans up Ham on the way back.  They collect Kaizo on the way back.  When they arrive, Fungus says, “Oh look - it is the Ambassador.”  
The hall overseer, Caddius, is there to greet the group upon the return.  Opens the doors and sees an elongated chamber, benches, raised dias, banners…and realmwardens.  Looks like official business. Ham is keen to hear the use of the Ambassador title.  Captain steps forward and is glad to hear the news that the gnolls were taken care of - send Caddius to fetch something.  There is an elf - regal looking - in the chamber, too.  Allow me to introduce Ravus Eldoran.  He says I’ve just arrived from Qual’dynn…there is interest (bowing to Ham) in certain diplomatic matters that we should discuss.  We all take seats.  He has come from Oldport, has been to Qual’dynn…he is not from Oldport. He says that there have been mishaps in Oldport and that he has heard good things about Ham.  Says Ham’s name has been damaged, but not beyond repair.  Captain then share news - just received info that Qual’dynn has shut its gates.  King Highvale has suspicions that there are evil doings there and has shut the doors.  We sent a courier there, but have not heard back yet.  About that time, Caddius walks in with 50gp reward for the gnoll problem.  Ravus asks if we have heard of the Rel Udomina…and the Captain says we have talked about them.  He says they are the reason why the gates have been closed in Qual’dynn.  And says that we have an opportunity to help them out…which can also restore Ham’s reputation.  They are concerned about the Courier and the message he was carrying (from the Lord of O'naren to King Highvale).  We ask about the mapmaker - he has disappeared.  Tinvere Milan is the Courier (an elf).  We discuss in what capacity we will go forward to find the courier - as official reps of O'naren?  Ravus says that they would like us to rid the lands of the Rel Udomina.  This will be official business to find the courier, but unofficial for the rest.  Captain agrees to write a letter.  Fungus asks about coin.  They offer 10g/each to accept the quest and when/if we return with information, will pay us an additional 25gp/each.  They agree to cover expenses (will reimburse us up to 40gp).  Fungus says if we get more news, maybe we can get more coin?  They said if we can do something to resolve the problem, we will get more coin - and would probably get an audience with the Lord of O'naren.  Qual’dynn is a 2 day trip.
We leave the Town Hall and head to the Inn and Tavern.  Fungus plans to drink himself silly, while the rest want to rest.  Rooms at the Nestling Sword Inn are 5sp, 8sp, or 2gp.  We spend 10sp for rooms for Ro and Affy, 8gp for rooms for Cerul, Kaizo, Ham, and Fungus.  We take long rest. We decide to head out at first light.       
The first day has uneventful travel.  We pass people heading to O'naren since Qual’dynn was closed.  We ask about conditions and specifically about the courier, but they reply that there are lots of people near the city trying to get in the gates. 
Before making camp, Affy and Ham stand close (10’) to Kaizo as Fungus pulls out the cube on top of Kaizo (Cerul and Ro are not close by).  He didn’t try to replicate anything - just rolled.  Fungus rolled a 78 - as he makes the final turn we hear a noise and a bright light emerges behind Kaizo’s head and he is glowing. Fungus gets a +1 charisma bonus that is permanent [Vibrant].  The climbing buff disappeared at dawn.  We now make camp.  We set up a watch, but the night passes uneventfully.
29th of Talil
The next day we wake up.  Affy wants to try to replicate the roll from the previous night.  Fungus is next to Affy, but everyone else stands clear.  Affy rolls a 13 - and cannot replicate the turns (fails history check).  Affy freezes up on the last movement.  The cube starts to make her arm translucent - like glass.  Then her entire body is glassy…and she shakes it off…but now she is semi-translucent.  She feels like her movement speed is reduced by 5’.  Armor class is now 13 as a base (crystallized skin).  This is a noticeable difference in appearance, but won’t change her AC since she wears armor.  She’s upset, but Fungus says it is sexy.  Affy dirties herself up with dirt and mud as they head out.
Pinprick’s Peculiarities
Road winds ahead.  Around noon, we spot a splash of color - deep purple, reds, golds - on a round wagon.  [We did NOT use horses for this trip]  Looks like a barrel…and it is pulled by a mini shaggy, red elk (not bigger than a pony).  See gnome flailing at us…he is a travelling merchant (what will it be - fortune or folly).  He opens the wagon and we see Pinprick’s Peculiarities sign…if you don’t need it, you are boring.  What’ll you have?  Fungus says what do you have for me.  He shows him a magic blanket (full rest in 4 hours) - he buys it for 25gp.  He glances at Ham and says I have what you need and pulls out a little vial, an inkpot and says dip your quill in that.  Ham does and the ink is green (mood ink - based on how he feels).  The inkpot also giggles.  It is 30gp.  Ham says he will consider buying it, but asks first if Pinprick has seen the courier.  He hasn’t…then points to Cerul and shows him a snail shell.  You speak into it and the message will be delivered to anyone he wants (5gp).  Fungus says he will buy it (Snail of Echoing Whispers).  Kaizo asks what he has for an old man.  Pinprick shows him a powder and an extract…and then decides he would rather offer it to Ro.  
He looks over at Ro…that shimmer, that is not common glass.  May I see that (the small orb pendant that Ro wears around her neck)?  Ro lets him see it.  Pinprick has a fit about this…he says this is a magical device that is humming (Ro has never heard the hum)...he says he saw this once before in a ruined amphitheatre that was swallowed by the Seameet (Seameet is swamplands…brackish water).  He is out of sorts after handling the item.  He doesn’t want to buy or take it - just says it is very powerful.  
He changes the subject - asks if Ro wants the powder (gives you darkvision for 10 minutes - Moon Leaf Powder) - he has two of these and Fungus buys them both.  Kaizo asks him about the Extract Pinprick has - it makes thorns grow on you (+1 AC for 10 minutes - for 20gp).   Kaizo makes him a counteroffer - 15gp and an offer to sell Nightbud (tells him who to contact in O'naren, the herbalist).  He agrees - and Kaizo gets an Ironthorn Extract.  Fungus talks to Pinprick about joining him…they agree that when Fungus gets his own cart and his own Mifflehoof, he can.  Affy asks if he has something for her.  He mentions a ball of endless twine.  Then a glowball to see in the dark for an hour (he has 12, will be 60gp) - Fungus buys them all.  He throws a vial at Fungus and it falls slowly.  He has three of them - these vials protect the contents.  It is weightless and never breaks when dropped (would have to take a hammer to it to break it).  The vials are 10gp/each.  Fungus buys 2 and Kaizo buys 1.  Affy asks about the inkpot and persuades him to buy it for 7gp.  He does and she gives it to Ham (since he bought her the pendant).  Ham plays with it over time (Giggling Inkpot).   
Defaced Elle’berian Shrine
We continue on.  We see a small stone shine off the path.  We notice a symbol (of the Borelian Sway) on it that has been defaced. We see kneeling, cloaked figures at the shrine.  They see us (green/cream cloaks) and smile.  One stands up and approaches - “it is a shame - even the sacred places aren’t safe these days.”  He indicates that he and his companions are going to leave.  Ham asks who they are.  They are lonesome pilgrims.  Ham asks Fungus to clean up the shrine and he does.  These people are male elf, female half elf (the speaker), two humans (who look alike), a dwarf (wearing elvish cloak), and a tiefling.  Ham asks her if she knows who did this…and tells her it was the Rel Udomina.  She asks how he knows and he says it is a hunch.  They act like they don’t know who that is, but Kaizo picks up that she is full of crap.  He asks her “are you sure you don’t know who that group is?”  They don’t like being questioned…it is Initiative.
Fungus lights up one bad guy and Affy with faerie fire.  Ro steps back and firebolts the one who is lit up and hits. Affy hits the same guy with her sword.  Ham gives Affy bardic inspiration and then casts dissonant whispers on the same guy and hits, killing him (exploding head).  He then unarmed strikes the female and hits. Kaizo attacks the female with a sword and fumbles, then misses twice.  Cerul casts a spell on all bad guys but one (radiance of the dawn) and they all failed their save.  Dwarf shoots at Cerul and misses.  Another moves and shoots and misses Cerul.  Another moves away and misses.  The female misses and disengages and flees.  Another shoots a crossbow at Fungus and hits, with poison damage.  Ro fires magic missiles at the dwarf. Fungus casts sorcerous burst on the tiefling and misses. Affy moves to the female “surrender and you can live”.  Ham dissonant whispers the one Affy warned, doing some damage.  Kaizo swiftly moved to the other female but missed with his short sword.  Cerul fumbles with a firebolt and also misses with spiritual weapon.  Ham was hit with piercing and poison.  One took cover behind a tree.  One with a crossbow misses Kaizo and takes cover behind a tree.  One hits Ro for a little damage.  One casts thunderwave on Kaizo but he saves and only takes some of the effect.  One ran, but Kaizo gets in an opportunity attack and does some damage.  Fungus misty steps by one behind a tree and shilelegh’s but misses.  Ro casts toll the dead on one and does serious damage.  Affy dashes and attacks for a critical hit bringing one of the women down.  Ham casts a 2nd level dissonant whispers knocking out another one.  Kaizo attacks one of the 2 remaining hitting for some piercing damage, and follows up with flurry of blows adding a little. Cerul tried to hit one behind a tree, but she saved.  He moved closer to the tree. Kaizo is double teamed by the last two bad guys and is hit twice.  Fungus misty steps and uses sorcerous burst on the one on Kaizo and hits.  Ro moves and misses with a firebolt, but uses sorcery to hit and do some damage. Affy dashes forward and asks “who wants to surrender and live?”  Ham dashes over.  Kaizo hits the guy on his rear with his little sword and then uses flurry of blows and hits once and misses once.  Cerul tolls the dead and they save.  One attacks Kaizo and misses. The other tries to hit him with a spell and misses. Fungus hits the one on Kaizo and knocks him prone.  Ro moves and tries to firebolt and misses.  Affy dashes and tries to knock one out, but crits and kills him by pushing her sword hilt through his eye socket. She makes one last attempt to get the last guy to surrender (he tells her to f—off).  Ham tries to whisper to one and she saves, but still takes damage and dies.  They are all dead.
Ro - 17 hit points, spell slots:  uses 2 sorcery points  
We then rifle through their belongings.  We find normal stuff - leather armor, long bows, etc.  Fungus collects some of these things with the intent to sell.  We notice that the dwarf had a folded vellum parchment in a pouch.  It has a silver wax emblem, resembling a severed chain link.  We have suspicions that this is an organization’s symbol vs. a person, but we aren’t sure what it is.  We open the parchment to see what it says.  It is in code and is elvish.  Ham takes a stab to decode.  “The target’s location will be exposed once the final sigil is retrieved.  Strike the diplomat before he reaches the sanctuary.  No names, no witnesses.  Retrieve the charter, destroy the seal.  Qual’dynn must not send another envoy.”  We notice a torn corner on the side of the piece…says Ambassador Entryvian Vondrell.  We think this was the original diplomat at Qual’dynn (they told us that the king is looking for a new diplomat).  The parchment is worn.  It seems to show a discreet pattern.  Crescent arch carved into a rock with the word “whisperbend” and then an X…looks like a secret passage (into Qual’dynn?).  
We look at the lady next - she was carrying an odd pouch.  It has an internal stitching made out of something special - a shadow silk.  We think it must be symbolism of some type (like if you carry this it means something).  We find a silver signet ring with a symbol that is a stylized rising sun.  Cerul recognizes this as the Abbey of Mont Rest.  The onyx medallion is cracked.  Ham thinks this was likely once a magic item that has been used/burned.  This was a mage.  We think she must have been at the Abbey or interacted with someone at the Abbey.  She used some type of magic.  The onyx represents petrification (Ham is able to ascertain - must have turned something into stone at some point).
The Missing Courier
Everyone else has gear and gold. We find 27gp, 43sp. (we all get 4 gold and 12 silver).  Kaizo notices a disturbance behind the shrine - as if the ground has been upturned.  Ro starts to uncover a dead body of the Courier.  We find a scroll with the signet of the Lord of O'naren. 
– The End of the Session –
