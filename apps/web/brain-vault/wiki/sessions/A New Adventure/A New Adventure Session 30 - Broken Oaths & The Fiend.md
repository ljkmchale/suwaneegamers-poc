# Session 30 - Broken Oaths & The Fiend

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: July 02, 2026
In-game Date:  13th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Stygia
The Chamber of Broken Oaths, Stygia, the Fifth Plan of Hell
The party is in the area of two large rooms of frozen walls.  One room holding a balcony overlooking the river Styx and providing a basin of blood that magically returns one to the material plane.  The other room, a prison or torture chamber made of walls of frozen bodies.  A large Ice Devil and his imps have climbed through the balcony.  Coming from into the other room, a Bone Devil.
IT IS INITIATIVE – Combat vs. the Fiends
* ROUND ONE: An insect-like devil starts coming over the balcony and throws a wall of ice on the other side of the chamber, splitting the party.  Cerul was caught in the middle of it and takes cold damage then pops into the room with Affy and Ridley. The devil moves to the middle of the room next to the basin - and looks around threateningly.  Ridley spots a devil coming in the door of the chamber.  It is skeletal with a large tail and big wings (bone devil).  Ridley uses his tools to release the prisoner from his manacles.   Ridley then grabs the prisoner and tries to move to the door using a cunning action to dash.  He then tells his bird to fly by the devil and attack it.  Fungus casts the doomstide spell in the room he’s in - targeting the 2 imps and devil, but making sure he doesn’t hit Relys or Kaizo:  both imps fail their saves, but the devil saves.  He then pulls out his club and casts shillelagh and moves to the side, taking damage from an opportunity attack from the devil.  This attack forces him to lose his concentration and doomtide drops. Kaizo runs up and hits the devil with force and then uses stunning strike, but the devil saves.  He attacks again, getting a critical hit, imposing disadvantage on the devil’s next attack. He then tries to stunning strike again and the devil saves. He then uses a bonus action to flurry of blows, missing twice. He then moves 25’ away. Rel casts a spell and is now glowing and then wild shapes into a saber, then backs up to the wall.  The bone devil moves to Affy with ease. He attacks and hits once, misses once.  He then tries to hit her with his tail and fumbles, losing his reaction.  He then looks at Cerul and says “you do not belong here, but if you want to stay, we will let your friends go” in his head.  Cerul laughs and says “you are right, we don’t belong here.” Affy attacks and hits critically, and tries to use her sword to inflict additional fire damage (which does nothing).  She then gets an extra hit from hew and does.  Then takes her second attack and hits.  She stays put.  Both imps offer Fungus and Kaizo a “deal” to live, but both decline, and the imps fly away/disappear.  Cerul sees shadows in the distance, but doesn’t see additional threats approaching.  He casts bless on Ridley, Cerul, and Affy and heals himself.  Then moves away from the wall.
* ROUND TWO:  The insect devil moves forward closer to Rel and Kaizo. He says “I hate cats” to Rel, then says, “but more than cats, I hate elves” and attacks Kaizo, hitting twice.  He takes damage and he cannot take a bonus action or a reaction and his speed is reduced.  He then attacks Rel with his tail and hits. He then says telepathically to Fungus “I guess there are no deals to be made.”  Ridley pulls out his crossbow and hits.  He then tells his bird to attack and it does, hitting again. Then Ridley and the prisoner move toward the door (that is iced over).  Fungus charges at the insect-devil and tries to hit with his club and, feeling lucky, does, knocking him prone. Then misty steps away, trying to inflict extra damage on the devil, but it misses.  Kaizo attacks with advantage on the prone devil and hits, inflicting force damage. The uses a point to try to stun him, but he saves. He then hits again, more force damage. He then tried to stun again and it works, dropping the wall of ice.  He then moves away. Rel attacks and misses twice and then moves to the corner.  The bone devil attacks Ridley with disadvantage, giving Affy an opportunity attack that hits, and Cerul fires a warding flare as he tries to attack Ridley, fumbling, falls down, but stands up.  He then attacks Affy with his third attack and hits, damaging and poisoning her (imposing disadvantage and she cannot be healed).  Affy attacks and hits with the first attack, then misses. She uses action surge to attack again and misses twice. Cerul attacks with radiant damage kills him.  He then heals himself. 
* ROUND THREE: The insect devil stands up, smelling blood in the air, and looks into the other room (he can’t do anything because he is stunned).  Ridley shoots him from the other room, hitting and inflicting damage, pushing him 10’ away.  He then moves with the prisoner into the other room.  He then shoots the devil again and hits. The bird then flies in and crits on the devil. Fungus runs over to the devil and attacks with his club and misses. Fungus then teleports and gives the queen of rats 7 temp hp (over the 4 she had). Kai goes up and punches and hits and stuns him. Rel hits the devil and then notices an imp next to her.  She moves away from the devil and imp to the other side of the room and slips and falls.  Affy uses her psi-leap to fly 60’ into the other room, then attacks.  An imp appears and tries to hit her as she flies by, but misses. She misses the devil, but then hits and does damage and extra acid damage. One imp attacks Cerul and he imposes a warding flare, causing the imp fumble.  One imp attacks Ridley and hits. One attacks Kaizo and hits.  One attacks Rel and misses.  Cerul attacks the imp on him and hits, dealing radiant damage. 
* ROUND FOUR:  The bone devil is stunned and does nothing.  Ridley shoots the imp on Cerul and hits, killing it.  He then shoots the imp on Kaizo and hits, imposing damage. He then flies the bird over to attack that same imp and it hits, killing it.  The bird then flies closer to the doors.  Fungus moves over and attacks the devil and misses, but stays put.  Kaizo moves forward carefully to attack the devil and hits, dealing force damage.  He then tries to stun him and the devil saves. He then hits again, more force damage.  Then he uses a flurry of blows and hits. Rel attacks the devil, hitting critically, inflicting a lot of radiant damage and lightning damage.  She then attacks again and hits, doing more radiant damage.  Affy then attacks with her sword and hits, killing him “stay in hell beast.”  She then moves to the imp on Rel and attacks and hits. The imps blink and run, but Relys gets an opportunity attack because she has blindsight.  She hits it and kills it. The other imp got away.  
The fight ends.
Ridley comments that we need to go after the noble that we followed in, Urovil. We talk for a minute and heal up.  Affy uses her Second Wind ability (x2) as Cerul casts Mass Healing Word on the group and then a Cure Wounds on Kaizo.   Cerul notices shadows moving outside. 
The party starts to move to the far door, ready to find Urovil. Ridley says he will leave since he knows which way he went.  We talk about how “someone” is making deals from hell with people on the material plane. We think these folks frozen in the walls are ones who broke their contracts on the material plane who were forced into hell to pay the consequence.  We realize that the water from the River Styx on the material plane will bring people to hell, whereas the blood on this plane in that basin will take people back.
As we start to move, we realize that Rel glowing is going to attract attackers, as well as Cerul’s light on his sword.  They both kill their lights. We move carefully so we don’t fall.  Affy asks Ridley about his bird - he says he “tapped into the weave” to summon that beast.  Affy thanks the bird for its help.  Ridley tells her the bird’s name is Ada - which is coincidentally the name of Cerul’s mother and he tells us that.  We follow a path that Ridley scouted earlier and come to a door. 
At the door, an imp pops up.  “Oh - are you here to make a deal?”  Affy says “is Urovil in there - if so, maybe…and get the fuck out of the way.”  And the imp pops away.  Ridley listens at the door and hears Urovil’s voice, speaking in infernal “I’m sure they are coming for me, I’m sure.”  And then a response “You…let’s see how you handle them.”  Ridley then knocks on the door and the double door opens slowly.  We see Urovil standing the middle of the room.  He says “Be gone - I will have no more of this.”  Ridley:  your family wants you back.  Urovil:  I’m not going back.    The doors start to close, but Affy jumps through, and the door closes her inside alone with creatures.  Affy sees that the room opens up to a balcony that is over the River Styx.  
IT IS INITIATIVE – Combat vs. Urovil and the Bearded Devils
* ROUND ONE:  Affy sees Urovil getting ready to cast something and next to her she sees two guys holding glaives with tentacles for a beard.  She yells loudly what she sees, hoping her friends here her.  Then she attacks one of the bearded devils twice and hits both times. Urovil steps back and is holding a skull in his hand, but hasn’t cast anything...yet.  Ridley sees that the door is locked and uses his action to pick the lock and does, telling the group the door is unlocked.  He tells the bird to fly above him and take the dodge action as he moves to the side of the door.  Fungus opens the doors and a purple dot comes flying out of the skull’s eyes and lands in the middle of the party; he casts counterspell and the purple dot fizzles out.  Fungus recognizes that the skull is his pact focus.  He then uses poison spray at the warlock and hits, inflicting poison damage.  Cerul moves into the room and casts radiance of the dawn on the two bearded devils (one saves, one doesn’t), inflicting radiant damage.  Kaizo uses a bonus action step of the wind into the room, giving the bearded devil an opportunity attack, and makes it to Urovil.  Kaizo sees a shadow of a bulbous form over the docks. He attacks with unarmed strike, dealing force damage.  Then hits again, more force damage.  Rel runs and jumps forward to get to one of the bearded devils.  She then attacks and hits dealing radiant and lightning damage. She attacks again and hits, dealing more radiant damage. The bearded devils attack.  One goes after Affy - beard hits Affy, glaive misses Cerul. Affy takes damage and is poisoned.  The other devil attacks Rel and misses with both glaive and beard.  The two guys yell “we didn’t sign up for this at the docks” in infernal (that Ridley understands). 
* ROUND TWO:  Affy attacks the devil on her, killing him. She then moves over to the other devil and attacks it, killing it with her bonus attack and second attack. She looks at Urovil and says “You’re next unless you want to go back to the surface.”  Ridley yells, “we can’t kill him.”  Urovil moves around the room, Kaizo hits with an opportunity attack.  He sends out a bead of light from the skull which drops in the room.  Everyone but Kaizo needs to make a saving throw.  Ada the bird dies.  Everyone else takes necrotic damage (some half, some full).  Ridley moves forward and casts Hunter’s Mark on Urovil, then throws his dagger and hits.  He then draws this crossbow and shoots and hits.  Ridley sees the shadowy form at the end of the dock disappear.  Ridley then hits again from the nick property of his weapon. The group hears something behind Fungus.  This, this is what caused the shadow…A hulking, nightmarish figure— a grotesque parody of nobility wrapped in tattered finery appears. His mottled blue-green skin pulls tight over a skeletal face, lips peeled back in a perpetual, jagged grin full of razor teeth that gleam like wet bone. Glowing red eyes sweep the room and settle on Urovil.  One clawed hand grips a tall, ornate staff topped with a sinister finial, while the other rests lightly on his chest like he's savoring the moment.  The other reaches out towards Urovil. The skull in Urovil’s head crushes to ash.  Urovil’s eyes go blank and he falls to the ground.  The devil looks to the group, “Take this weakling and leave my realm or suffer my wrath.”  Fungus thinks this devil is likely Urovil’s patron, holder of this lair and in command of everything else we fought so far.  He tells everyone “he is dangerous - are we fighting or running?” and then goes invisible. The devil looks around and glances at Cerul.  Cerul says “RIdley - you got what you came for - can we go?”  Ridley says yes - I just thought you would hate devils.  Cerul says, “but they are in their domain - they can stay here.” Cerul looks at Urovil…and then sees that the skull that this devil is not holding is now Urovil’s skull covered in blood.  The devil then says “I suggest you leave.”  Kaizo asks if the devil has some nightbud.  The devil smirks and says “if you want to make a deal, I will get you some…I’ll make you the richest nightbud dealer.”  Kaizo says no - I have someone to meet at the tavern.  Kaizo starts to move and walks past the devil and out of the room and back onto the path - “c’mon guys, let’s go.”  The devil’s wings flutter and it flies to the other side of the group and looks at us.  Rel stays put and growls at the devil, but does nothing else.  The devil looks at Affy.
* ROUND THREE:  Affy dashes over and picks up the limp form of Urovil and says “Your hell…I’ll take him.  I hope I never see you or any of yours ever again” and starts to carry him out of the room.  The devil takes flight and starts to fly out of the room.  Ridley is trying to figure out if the pact is broken or if this would keep the pact going with that noble family.  He doesn’t know.  Ridley moves to the balcony and looks up and sees him 20’ in the air.  He casts hunter’s mark on him and shoots the devil twice, fumbling with the first shot, losing his reaction, then hitting and inflicting some force and piercing damage.  He then stows his crossbow. The devil turns and looks at Ridley and takes HUGE damage, he can’t cast spells, he can’t talk, etc.  and he drops unconscious.  Fungus asks if he can grab Ridley’s body and the devil says no.  He then says “we are leaving and I feel you got your justice by harming him, can we leave?  I’d like to leave with him.”  The devil says “that is not happening.” Fungus then moves back to Ridley and picks him up and starts to move away with his body, shouting “we have to go, we have to go, we have to go.” Cerul says “we are leaving and Ridley is coming with us and that’s that” and heals Ridley for 19 points and then advances forward 15’.  The devil then looks at Affy and Fungus and both fail wisdom saves.  They are blinded and take damage.  Kaizo looks back and says “c’mon.” The devil then targets Rel with a wisdom saving throw…and she passes.  She hears a mumble, but it doesn’t do anything (Cerul and Fungus think this was a command spell).  She gets behind Fungus to nudge him forward.  
* ROUND FOUR:  Affy tries to persuade him to let us go and he asks if she is willing to give up her soul. She offers up Urovil’s body.  He says that he already has his soul.   She says no and moves forward.  She doesn’t persuade him.  Ridley is in Fungus’ arms and shoots his crossbow at the devil, but it hits Fungus, as the devil charms Ridley as he is about to shoot.  He then stands up and moves out of the room through the doors. The devil steps forward and pulls out a whip and lashes at Rel and hits twice.  She goes down.  Affy then has to make an intelligence saving throw and something creeps into her brain…and she is stunned and takes damage…and forgets the last 5 hours of activity.  He looks at Cerul and says which one are you leaving me?  And he says “Urovil”  And the devil says “I already have his soul.”  Fungus goes large form and picks up Rel and action dashes forward, slipping and falling, standing back up and moving forward again.  Kaizo sees icy spokes shooting out of the path ahead and breaking into chips on the path.  He thinks the path up will be trapped.  Cerul sprouts wings and turns into his divine form and casts beacon of hope (advantage of wisdom saving throws for the group), then moves forward 15’.  In front of Kaizo, an ice shard shoots up in front of him.  Kaizo moves back to Fungus and asks for the flask of blood and puts a drop on Fungus and Rel (and they both disappear).  He then moves to Affy and hands her the flask and there is a drop coming out that hits Kaizo and he leaves.  Rel has to make a death save.
* ROUND FIVE:  Affy walks over to Ridley and says Kaizo says to take this. Then asks the devil “why do you need someone.” And he says after all the trouble you all have caused, I’m owed.  And she says “take me.” and a spike shoots up from the ground and knocks her to 0 hit points, but she doesn’t fall (relentless endurance).  Ridley takes the vial and pours it on Affy and Urovil and they disappear.  He then tries to shatter the vial on Cerul, but it crashes to the ground.  He then walks next to Cerul, but doesn’t touch the blood yet. Both Cerul and Ridley have to make wisdom saving throws.  Cerul saves, Ridley does not, taking necrotic damage and is blinded.  Fungus is in the cellar of the noble house.  He sees the prisoner, he sees Relys, Kaizo, and Affy.  He takes the healing potion out of Rel’s pack and feeds it to her.  Cerul grabs Ridley’s arm and pulls him to the ground then pulls his arm on the ground.  Ridley hears the fiend telepathically say “I will remember you - this is not the end” and they both disappear. 
We are all in the cellar, sometime after midnight on the morning of the 14th of Paramor, 1246.
– The End of the Session –
