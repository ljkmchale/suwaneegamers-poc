Session Date: October 1, 2026
In-game Date:  20th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Windbreak Camp
ROUND THREE:  Ridley moves his hunter’s mark to the beast in front of him and strikes one with his crossbow and inflicts damage.  Then hits again and pushes it back 10’ and then runs to the side 40’. Fungus then casts spirit guardians and two of them take 19 points of damage each.  Fungus failed his WIS save and now has the frightened condition and is now disadvantaged on his next turn from the creature’s gaze. Kaizo makes his WIS save.  He then attacks the one he stunned and misses with his first attack, then hits with the second attack and stuns him again, dealing force damage in the process. Then uses flurry of blows with a bonus action and hits and does this twice.  The one near Relys attacks her scorpion form and misses, then tries again and fumbles, falls, and stands back up. Cerul flies up and casts radiance of the dawn to try to hit all of them - they all make their CON saves.  All take damage (15 pts for one and 11pts for the other two).  The one on Fungus howls and he fails his CON saving throw and he takes 28pts of psychic damage and will have no reactions until the start of the bear’s next turn.  His spirit guardians drop because he loses concentration. The stunned bear just stands there looking around. Relys makes her WIS save.  She then attacks a beast with a claw and misses, then attacks again with a claw and hits and deals 17 pts of radiant damage and 1 point of fire damage and he is grappled.  Then she hits with the stinger and inflicts 18 points of radiant and a lot of poison damage.  Affy fails her WIS save and is disadvantaged, but hits twice and inflicts a fair amount of damage.
ROUND FOUR:  Ridley attacks with a crossbow and hits dealing 31 points of damage, killing it by shooting it between the eyes “you’ll never howl again, beast!”  Then moves and hits the best of Fungus, dealing 9 points of damage. Fungus fails his WIS save and is frightened again, disadvantaged on his attacks. He uses his lucky to neutralize his disadvantage and fumbles, he falls prone and bonus action misty steps away, to the other side of the bulas. The beast takes 11 points of psychic damage as Fungus moves away (dread).  Kaizo makes his WIS save. He attacks and does 5 points of force damage, then hits again for 5 more points of damage.  Then uses flurry of blows and misses, then tries again and misses.  Cerul hits them with radiance of the dawn - one makes his save and one misses.  He also makes his WIS save.  The beast that was on Fungus moves forward and howls and Cerul, Kaizo and Affy all have to make CON saves.  Kaizo saves and takes 10 points of damage; Cerul takes full damage of 21 points; Affy saves and takes 10 points of damage.  The beast attacks Kaizo and he takes 15 points of damage and is grappled.  Kaizo deflects all of the damage with his reaction.  He then tries to bite Kaizo and hits and takes 22 points of piercing damage and is bloodied.  He takes another 6 points of necrotic damage. Relys scuttles over bodies in her scorpion form and attacks the beast that was on Fungus and misses with a claw, hits with a claw and grapples it, killing it, disintegrating it into dust.  Then attacks the other one with her stinger, missing.  Affy steps up and takes out the last creature with her first blow, taking off its jaw in the process.
The group finds themselves in the wreckage of the camp.  Everyone gathers close and Cerul heals everyone except Relys for 22 points of healing and the effect of a short rest. Relys checks out the corpses of the beasts.  They had patchy black fur, brown tufts of fur between plates of hardened hide, and eyes burning a dull red.  She is bothered by them because they give her a Shadowfel presence.  
The group searches the camp and sees bodies trampled into the ground.  We see a clay bottle with a stopper (red wine), a waterskin, rations (crushed, but edible), a copper ring with a river stone attached (Relys casts detect magic), stoppered vial of alchemist’s fire, pouch with 18gp and 22sp, tinkerer’s tools. There’s also a winter cloak, and another 27 gp, 64 sp, and 40 cp.  Fungus takes a swig of wine. 
We decide to check out the windmill.  Relys and Affy lead, followed by Ridley and Kaizo, then Fungus and Cerul.  The blades of the windmill are shattered, and aren’t moving, but the structure itself is very dangerous. We decide to stop and let Ridley fly up as a bat and Relys as an owl.  They fly up and see the windmill rocking back and forth.  They see a dead owl in front of the windmill on the ground. Relys sees a lot of claw and scratch marks around the windmill and notices that boards have been pushed out of it. They decide to fly inside and see an owl’s nest in the corner with dead babies and one that is alive. Relys flies over the baby owl - it is a barn owl.  She tries to befriend it - she thinks she could perch near it and work her way over (as Relys).  She thinks it is a fragile creature. She picks it up with her talons as gently as possible and flies with it outside.  Meanwhile, Fungus decides to walk into the windmill.  He thinks he will have to be very careful.  He goes inside and thinks the place has been picked over.  He sees a corpse in the corner and goes over to check it out. This is a dead orc. He thinks the guy was likely trying to hide from bears, but it didn’t work. He searches him and finds a small pouch with two vials of green liquid (poison) and a dagger. The dagger looks well made and looks like it was crafted to hold poison (not a dagger of venom).  Fungus and Ridley look around the place, but neither finds much. Ridley finds a trap door, however.  It was overgrown with debris. He clears brush and listens and hears nothing.   Affy comes in as Fungus and Ridley start to open the trap door. They see bags of grain down below and a ladder leading down.  Ridley tries to quietly go down the ladder (31 stealth).  He sees the bags moving (maggots, larvae, worms - not sure).  He lights up a torch and sees a box tucked behind one of the bags.  He is able to grab the box and it turns out to be long, ornate and cut - it is a wooden box. Ridley starts trying to open up the lock on the box and does so.  He opens it up and he sees a long rod with jagged blades on each end.  The blades are silver and the pole is blue-ish steel.  Affy leans down and pulls up the box.  Ridley empties a sack and scoops up a bag of larvae for Ridley’s owl.
Meanwhile, Relys casts speak with animals and then tries to handle the owl.  She thinks she could nurse it with the larvae from Ridley.  She thinks it may take a few more days to be able to fly.  She asks if it has a name.  She says she will take care of it from now on.  
Kaizo thinks this place is NOT safe to camp.  The area has been trampled and we decide to go off road. Kaizo finds a path we can travel slowly.  He feels less of a sense of dread after 20-30 minutes.  We set up camp and watch. Ridley sets an alarm spell.  Cerul sends a message to Pa - “Ran into some dangerous and corrupt bears.  We killed them.  Stay at the Inn for now and await further word.  Will update you by midday.”  Fungus and Relys have first watch, Ridley and Affy next, then Cerul and Kaizo last. 
Ridley gives us goodberries and we all eat one (including the horse and bulas and owl baby).  Relys pulls out her magical board on her watch.  She studies the symbols and feels like they are elvish spiritual symbols.  Fungus polishes it up with wood oil and casts comprehend languages.  He feels like “yes and no” symbols are in the corners, various letters - and he thinks that three people are needed for the ritual.  They don’t think it is magical, but think there might be divination influence at play.  They ask for Affy’s help.  She agrees and they all start to look at the board.  Relys determines that the center piece pops out so they all need to touch this piece. Fungus asks “is there anyone out there” and the token moves to “yes.”  Affy asks “are there more of these corrupt beasts nearby” and the token moves to “no.” Relys asks “what is your name?”  And the token starts to spell out “E-L-D-E-R B-A-L-A-N-C-E.”  They decide to wake up Kaizo. He thinks this is the Elder of the spirit of balance.  He feels like this is the one who came to him in his vision.   Affy and Kaizo think that balance is the scales of justice, things are in harmony.  The opposite of balance is chaos and things out of order.  The old goddess of Chaos is Tornia.  Her pet is the one from the trinket.  This is validation that everything is connected.  Kaizo thinks this elder would not speak through a board unless things were bad.
Affy then opens the box so that Fungus can study it.  It is a javelin of lightning. Affy takes it.  He then studies the dagger - this is called Eredra’s Sting.  Ridley gets the dagger. This  is Midnight’s Tear poison (1500gp) - no effect until midnight - but if not neutralized before midnight the creature will take 9d6 damage.  Fungus keeps this.  This is ingested poison.  Serpent’s Venom (200gp) is the poison in the other vial. This is an injury poison.  Ridley gets this and Fungus gives him Carrion Crawler mucus that he had.
Now watch starts. First watch, Relys names her owl Soren.  Nothing else happens.  Then Ridley and Affy have watch.  Ridley climbs a tree.  He asks Affy if she signed off of the board and notices that her fingers are translucent now. Ridley thinks this means we need to say goodbye to a board.  Affy wakes up Relys and Fungus and they all agree to sign off (after some convincing - since all of their hands are glowing).  The rest of that watch is uneventful. On third watch, Kaizo and Fungus talk about balance. Kaizo is concerned about why the Elder contacted the party through the board. Fungus tells him it will be okay.  Fungus suggests getting the hookah out and they light up and drink the red wine (Fungus only - he fails a CON save - this causes him to be “whatever” for a while). They use a bag of Kaizo’s herbs. Kaizo notices a different look in Fungus’ eyes. He is more relaxed than ever (for the next 3 days he has advantage on every saving throw except wisdom, where he has disadvantage).  Group gets long rest. 
21st of Paramor, 1246
We wake up and decide to head down the road to Bloodbeak.  It is eerily quiet.  Soren is trying to find his balance.  The bulas travels slowly.  We see destruction here and there - like where a tree has been knocked down or where we see big bear tracks.  After a half day’s travel, Cerul contacts Pa and says “Stay put.  We will be at Bloodbeak this evening.  Hang one more night and we will contact you tomorrow.”  Pa responds sarcastically, but agrees to stay.
We travel and it is now dusk.  The road dips toward a shadow bowl-like valley.  This is Bloodbeak.  We see destruction, no vegetation (this place was known for berries and lush vegetation), remains of cottages, crushed structures.  We see enormous footprints in the earth.  These seem larger than they were at Relys’ home.  Kaizo thinks we should keep moving.  Relys checks the tracks to see if they continue. She sees they veer off the road.  She thinks they are a few stretches old.  Fungus yells “anyone out there?”  It is dead silence.  Ridley scouts around the buildings, Cerul looks around the campsite.  Relys starts a ritual to see if anything is close.  Ridley finds a crushed body on the side of a building.  He then sees other corpses and the smell hits him.  He realizes nothing has come through to scavenge the dead.  The corpse looks like something was just crushed - this is the nature of the injury.  Cerul sees bodies slung in the distance - a child was thrown, for example.  Inside of one of the walls he sees a horse half buried.  We see around the site a number of dead birds everywhere. Everything that lived here was destroyed/desecrated.  
Relys stops about halfway through her ritual and her arms splay out and she seems possessed.  She says “avenge me” in a low voice.  The words are a little different than last time.  She looks at Kaizo and he gets a visual of birds falling out of the sky and dropping to their deaths.  He sees a vision of the horse plowing into a building and dying.  She says “the balance has failed here.  The other two cannot steady while it roams.”  Then Relys falls and faceplants into the mud and debris. Affy runs over to pick Relys up.  They discuss what this means.  We feel like we are several days behind this creature.
We discuss chasing it, but are concerned with Pa.  Cerul sends Pa a message “Found the danger and are tracking it south.  It is safe to Bloodbeak, but we can’t vouch past that.  We need to go after this beast.” Pa sends back “Um what about Pebblewash and Shademoor. You were supposed to get us home.  Do we stay here?” Cerul responds “Can’t vouch for those areas, but we need to go after this.  We will let you know what we find over the next few days.”  Pa says “Well shit, I guess the wine is delayed.  I’m not leaving here because I don’t know if it is safe beyond this.”
Meanwhile, Fungus tries to connect to his patron.  He is able to make contact (makes his saving throw).  He describes what we have encountered and asks her if she knows of 1) what this creature might be.  She says yes.  2) Would it help you if we kill this thing and stop it from running through this land?  She says unsure. 3) It is not going to harm you if I hunt this down and kill it?  She says unsure.  4) But you won’t be upset with me?  She says no.  5) Is there any good food in the next day or two that I might come across - my belly is rumbling?  He then hears a pop and Tennana is next to him.  She says “where are you going?”  He points in that direction.  She says we don’t know what is in that direction.  Are you going to run through the woods?  And he asks her if there is a way to get good food.  She then says “did you see that red and blue-eyed guy?”  He is a thorn in her side.  Do you know where he is at?  She asks if he knows of Ashbarrel and tells him that he ran into Eliana in Ashbarrel.  She has me popping in and out - she thinks I like this world.  He says he will go with her to the Feywild if he can.  She says she can take him.  She is so happy that he did what he did for her.  He says that he will confront tde two-colored eye guy.  She wishes Fungus good luck and pops away.  
Fungus then pops up and tells the group that the two-colored eye guy is with Eliana in Ashbarrel.  Fungus would like to capture him - he promised his patron that he would.  Fungus cleans up Relys with prestidigitation.  We decide to travel through the night. Before we go, we see torchlight coming from the southeast. Affy starts to walk in that direction and she sees three brownish-black horses and a white horse.  The riders are wearing blue tabards.  They approach.  “Who goes there?”  Affy answers “The King’s Outlanders”  There is no king in Halesworth.  We have traveled a great distance.  A lady comes to the front.  “Hello - are the rumors true, have you come across it?”  Have you laid eyes on it?  No - but we are planning to travel that way.  Fungus asks if they have seen it.  She says they have been chasing it for some time.  Some say is comes across with more eyes than two as it sweeps the land and crushes everything.  Others say it is nightmarish and nothing but darkness.  I tell you that we represent the Meridian.  We are searching for this.  We have been to Ferndale, Murfield, and on the road since then.  I am Sera Vann.  I am responsible for this escort to ensure that the road is safe and it appears to be okay because the beast has gone south.  Cerul speaks up to tell her about the bears.  She asks their origins.  He tells her of Windbreak Camp.  She declares the roads clear from the Arch to Murfield based on her conversation.  We tell her of Pa and she agrees to help him.  She will escort Pa and his family to Shademoor and mentions an arcane waypoint that they can use to Basctdelm. Affy pipes up and asks if they could assist with future passage to Basctdelm. Ridley then jumps in and eventually shows him his Meridian badge.  They make introductions.  She makes a slight slur against Affy, but quickly overcomes it and confirms that she will help us with future passage to Basctdelm should we get to Shademoor.  Ridley asks if we can use the horses - we agree to give them the carriage as collateral. They agree to help - to give us two horses.  We tell them we would like to serve the Queen as well. We ask he names of the horses - Bugnut (Cerul) and Holespy (Affy to ride). Relys and Ridley will ride Anjou.  Fungus and Kaizo will ride the bulas.  She warns us that everything that has come across this creature has died.  Cerul will keep you updated with our travels.  And Cerul will let them know you are coming.  He agrees to tell them to start their travels.  They make camp and we head out.  They will go help Pa and his family.
As we depart, Ridley comments how it was nice that he got the Meridian badge some time ago.
