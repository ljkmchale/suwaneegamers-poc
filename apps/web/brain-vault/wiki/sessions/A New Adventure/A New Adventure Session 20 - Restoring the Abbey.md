# Session 20 - Restoring the Abbey

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  November 11, 2025
In-game Date:  44th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  Abbey of Light
Day 4/8 of rest for Feywoven Glyphic
Fight with the Parson Quinel Drael
We see someone who is of the Abbey at the top of the stairs.  Ridley recognizes him as the Parson that he was supposed to dine with later this evening.  He raises his staff and the onyx eyes on it make Ridley and Ham both frozen statues, petrified.  Kaizo runs in.
Parson says - we sealed the waypoint for a reason.  I’ll have to finish what my wraiths could not.
* ROUND ONE (SIX):  Kaizo runs in and dashes to the top of the stairs next to the Parson and attacks him, making a critical unarmed strike, but he is not stunned.  Two guards appear at the top of the stairs (Kaizo gets an opportunity attack on one).  One shoots at a monk and kills him.  The other shoots at Cerul and misses.  Affy dashes (bonus action) and runs to the top of the stairs and kills one of the guards with two hits of her great axe.  Parson attacks Kaizo with his staff and hits, but Kaizo is able to deflect some of the damage.  He attacks again and hits and takes Kaizo down…then moves behind his guard and wails at Affy, who takes psychic damage.  Fungus blinks back into the room and casts poison spray on the Parson, hitting significantly.  He then moves to the bottom of the stairs and does not blink out. His fey creature moves to the landing and attacks the Parson and hits. The last monk (who is no longer bad) runs up the stairs, but cannot attack.  Cerul flies up to the guard on the landing and his spirit guardians force the guard and Parson to make saving throws. He then heals Kaizo for 10 points. Relys runs into the room and moves her moonbeam to the middle of the staircases. Two more guards appear through the doors at the top of the stairs.  One it hit by the spirit guardians and takes damage. They look at Cerul through the doors and yell - “kill that guy.” Both guys miss Cerul and they move outside of the range of the spirit guardians and cast a bonus action spell (and wounds close up). 
* ROUND TWO (SEVEN):  Kaizo stands up and moves to the Parson and hits twice, then uses a bonus for furry of blows and hits again twice.  Affy steps through the door and kills both of the acolytes outside then moves back in.  The Parson wails and does damage on everyone, but Fungus and Affy are also frightened.  He then swings at Kaizo, but Cerul uses a flare to make him attack at disadvantage and he misses.  He then misses Kaizo again.  But he finally hits with the third attack and Kaizo deflects the piercing damage, does damage to the Parson, and then Kaizo falls.  The Parson then says “fine, you win – call the Warden, I’ll turn myself in.” He then takes damage from the spirit guardians and dies. Cerul reaches down to heal Kaizo.
Ham and Ridley start to crack - the petrification begins to flake off and both finally come back.  The arcane waypoint starts to glow.  Ham says should I call Lorian now?  He steps forward with the sending stones and updates Lorian.  “The waypoint is restored and we think the threat has ended.”  A few moments pass - “I’ll look to arrive soon and meet you tonight at the Hearthlight.”
We loot the guards, monks, and the Parson. We collect their goods and keep them for the Abbey.  The Parson has a couple of interesting things:  serpent-head staff (which was moving) is now wooden again and the other item is an odd ring (gold and violet), shaped like a brain.  We notice that this room is secured so that if anyone shows up on the waypoint unannounced, the normal guards can hold them in the space until their identity can be confirmed. 
We ask Kaizo what he said to the Warden.  He tells us that the Warden said he was going to round up people - which may be why the Parson came down.  We decide to take a short rest.  As we do, Relys studies the staff and Fungus studies the ring.   
Fungus decides to give the ring away.  Group decides to give it to Ridley (Ring of Mind Shielding - in party inventory).  Kaizo takes the Staff of the Adder that Relys studied.
We decide to check out the other hidden door in this room.  Affy doesn’t hear anything so Relys sends Hopper under the door to check. He sees that the hall turns and dead ends into a wall.  So we decide to open the door and go through to look for a hidden door at the dead end.  Affy finds a contraption to open the secret door and she opens it.  It is a square chamber - soft, pulsing light.  Seems like sunlight…but is magical. We think we are in a vault under the Great Hall of Radiance.  We see stairs rising on the other side. There are many treasures here - probably gifts to the Abbey that are stored here.  Fungus tries to palm something but everyone sees him and convinces him to put the angelic statues back. 
We decide to go up the stairs by the vault. Kaizo hears a crackling fire through the door, but says he doesn’t hear anything.  Affy opens the door and sees wall sconces that are magical, but look like real flames.  Smell of recent cleaning.  We see 4 ornate coffins around the room.  We see a spiral staircase leading up and a door on the right.  On the left we see a barred down. Fungus walks over to a coffin and see runes carved into them.  The have sun, lantern, chalice, and book symbols on them.  Cerul calls out to see if anyone is here, but doesn’t receive an answer.  Kaizo listens at the door on the right and doesn’t hear anything. He knocks, no answer.  Affy opens the door.  It is a small circular chamber.  Smells of wax and oil. This seems like a supply room used to maintain the room we were in. We close this door and go to the barred door and Kaizo listens.  He doesn’t hear anything (but he hears wind blowing).  Affy raises the bar and opens the door.
A passage winds down, cooler area.  It stops at the portcullis type door. Looks old, but free to rust.  Beyond the gate the tunnel goes deeper into the darkness. There is a big lock plate and Kaizo tries to pick the lock and does.  It opens (no sound, well maintained door).  Affy and Fungus lead.  The hall winds down 60-70’ and then starts to straighten and then opens into a large cavern.  It is a natural cave about 100’ across, before it leads into another cave…all going downhill.  Relys thinks that the door was locked to keep something from coming INTO the Abbey. Looking around she notices tracks to the side of the cavern, but not on the trail proper.  They are not humanoid.  They look lizard-like, but too big. Affy hears this and starts to look up the wall and sees tracks on the walls.  Whatever has comes through doesn’t come on the path, but on the wall.  Affy knows underdark races…that ride on giant lizards.  We think this is a passage that leads down to the Underdark. 
We go back and relock and rebar the door.  And go back to where the waypoint is.  We collect Ridley and Ham and head through the doors to surface.  We see guards up there and there are now guards of the protective light up there.  They nod at us.  
It is nice to be outside.   We hear the sound of approaching footsteps and we see the Warden, Matron Mallow, and maybe monks and acolytes.  He says “You did it.” You’ve helped restore the Abbey.  A crowd emerges and claps and and cheers us. We tell them about what happened.  A few step forward with hugs and mossback mushrooms that they are preparing for us tonight.  We are all going to feast at the Hearthlight later.
Cerul pulls the Warden to the side and briefs him about the map, the secret entrances into the vault. Relys asks the Warden about the prisms…were any more found.  
[As she asks, Fungus hears a fluttering in his ear.  Tanana appears to next to him.  She stepped out of a portal next to his ear.  “The prisms you stirred…they are hums that reach deep.  Shoddy wants one…a prism.  Please retrieve one for her and don’t let anyone know.”  Fungus knows he could approach the halfling he was scouting with and try to get one from him OR try to figure out where they are kept OR ask the Warden for one OR go out and try to find additional ones outside the Abbey.]
They found three more - they are in my quarters.  He didn’t want to bring them to the Reflective Light since he knew that some of them were corrupt by the Rel'Udomina.  Warden says he will give one to Fungus and Relys to study to see if they are dangerous. 
Affy asks he knows about the passage to the Underdark.  The Warden says that we should not go down there…there was a fight a long time ago when this was the Abbey of Mont Rest.  
We go the Hearthlight and get ready for dinner. 
THE PARTY LEVELS UP (TO LEVEL 7)
We come down to dinner.  There seems to be a new vibe since the Rel'Udomina has been pushed out.  They have reconfigured some tables together.  The Warden is at the head. Aida drops off the biggest mushrooms we’ve ever seen (braised).  Warden says “we can’t reward you like the king would, but we appreciate all you have done.”  Kaizo is walking around, popping some of the new monks with his new staff.  We sit down to eat.  Some of the orphans are running around - a little more freely tonight - one plants herself next to Affy.  Are you going to explore the cave next to the Old Shepherd's Path - is this your next adventure?  I’m Imry - my father died in the Varenwood (he had been staying at the Pine Vault Lodge).  He was going to catch a big deer and he said if only the gnomes would have helped him.  Gnomes that look like you.  My father was Im.  He went out hunting and didn’t come back.  He said the gnomes would show him how to hunt the stag.  
Relys and Affy say that we can go to the Path, as Kaizo and Fungus say that you can go, too. Relys asks the Matron about this child.  This is an option moving forward.
A door opens at the time.  Lorian steps in and comes up to the table and sits down.  Affy mentions her father and they chat on their own in a dark corner.  He looks outside and then starts to talk to her about him.  Oedabrin Thornage (aka Calden) has been in the shadows for a while.  His residence in Basctdelm is not abandoned…it could be him or he could still visit.  I’ve asked the Knights of Myr to watch and see if there is any activity.  He’s keeping a low profile.  He seems to be involved with an organization called the Promissory, but I’m not sure what his involvement is.  They talk for a moment and Affy shares the letter she found and the reference to her father.  He says not to underestimate her father - that he will help her when she is ready to find him - but that there is no need to hurry.
They go back to the table. He calls out our acts of heroism here at the Abbey and beyond. And tells that he has written to the Phet about our words and deeds at Qal’dynn and now again about our accomplishments here at the Abbey.  The Phet would like to know if Cerul or Fungus would like to join the Phet.  He says he can arrange an audience with them if he would like to accept their offer.  Both say they will accept the offer.  
Relys is entertaining the children with druidcraft as the night goes on.  
We have to decide what we want to do next time:
* Old Shepard’s Path (which is here at the Abbey) or 
* Varenwood or 
* Basctdelm or the 
* Tegenwald Phet or 
* Qal’dynn.
We know sigils for the arcane waypoints for the Broken Citadel and Abby of Light.  We could travel to Qal’dynn tonight with Lorian and then travel back to the Abbey tomorrow.  
We decide to go with Lorian and Ham back to Qal’dynn.  We want to figure out the protocol for the waypoint in Qal’dynn and get the sigil.  We will stay in Qal’dynn for a stretch (for downtime). Then come back to the Abbey via the waypoint and check out the Cave and visit the giants (get Cerul’s box).  Once resolved, then go back to Qal’dynn to either a) go to Varenwood or b) Tegenwald Phet.  [Fungus needs to figure out how to get the prism to Shoddy]
Everyone will need to tell the DM what we did during downtime!!!
NOTE - during the downtime, while at the Abbey, Fungus uses the cube on the 48th of Talil.  He rolled a 37.  This is [AQUATIC].  He developed gills on his neck and can now breathe underwater and his swim speed is equal to his movement speed.  And this can never be rolled again.
– The End of the Session –
