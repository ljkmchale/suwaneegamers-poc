# Session 23 - Releasing the Bound

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  February 20, 2026
In-game Date:  5th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Blackstone Crucible
Day 4/8 of rest for Feywoven Glyphic
Kaizo Returns
We stand at the portal, looking at the forest of Varenwood through it.  Light seems abnormal.  Footsteps approach from behind and we see Kaizo.  We ask how he found us - our tracks - and what he’d been doing - thinking.  He described how he had been following Ridley and things were off and then meditated.  Cerul gives him grief for meditating while we have been in danger.  Kaizo asks - insists - that we all sit down for story time.
He does so.  Tells us that he’s been having visions about how things have not been in balance - and balance is very important to him.  He’s been pulled into two places - an old shrine in Varenwood (a shrine that was meant to keep things balanced, but this shrine was destroyed).  Relys had not heard anything about this shrine aka druid circle being destroyed.  While this is going on, Ridley notices the fairy Tanana pop into existence and land on Fungus’ shoulder (no one else notices).  Kaizo mentions Arisia, he mentions good stuff will give you visions… during this vision he noticed her crying… Cut to fairy, pointing towards the portal, where she says, “Shoddy will be delighted at what you (Fungus) have found” Back to Kaizo, balance, it all needs to be released? Binding is a dam and balance is a river.  Tanana tells Fungus “you are near the source - much closer than anyone has been in a long time” –and then she pops away.  
We discuss that we need to remove the binding.  But where - how?  Arisia’s message was that “he has bound me. I am not evil, so I must be…” (and then faded) out.”  Binding may be happening here, but also in Varenwood…maybe Shadowfell, too.  
Kaizo then tells Ridley that he observed that people watched him, but never approached.  That he is being watched - maybe controlled or evaluated? Maybe he is being influenced by the same people who are evaluating Arisia? Something in the world is binding both good and evil and Ridley is on his radar.  
As we think about what to do, Relys realizes that the creatures that we fought came through that portal. Ridley sets an alarm at the portal to trigger if something comes through so that we can continue to investigate the rest of the building. 
We start to investigate the rest of the top floor, Ridley leading and Fungus in the back. We find a small passage that leads to a door.  This side seems to be symmetrical to the other side.  We go up to the door and Ridley opens it.  Smooth black stone walls…not much in the room.  Looks like a leader’s chamber - bed, desk, there is an amulet with the symbol of the BC on the desk.   Ridley finds a secret compartment with a small sack and a potion.  Affy picks up the amulet - thinks it is more than a holy symbol.  She hands it to Cerul and he thinks it might be dedicated to Malgri’n - but it is very pristine, and doesn’t look to be much used.   Relys takes a look, too.  She thinks the amulet with the symbol of the Cult of Eternal Binding is a key and she holds onto it.  The potion Ridley finds is purple with  a swirling cloud of pink.  Fungus isn’t sure what it is.  He also finds 27gp (party inventory).  Relys thinks that the amulet might fit into the abacus–the portal key. 
We travel to the next room.  It is similar in design and construction to the other room. Ridley gives Relys a scroll that he found (dimension door). There is an elongated box on the desk.  Ridley thinks the box is okay and opens it, doing some damage to both he and Affy.  There is another scroll inside.  Fungus looks at it.  This is beyond his means of usage, but thinks he could in later years.  It is a scroll of imprisonment.  He will hold onto it.  Affy looks around, but doesn’t find anything else.  
As we turn to leave the room, Fungus hears steps next to him and he looks down and he sees the little guy sizing him up.  Fungus asks where his master is.  He says “at the bottom - you’ll meet him - just take your time.”  
We decide to go back to the main level, Ridley leads us to the central staircase - the way we came.  Ridley and Kaizo walk ahead in stealth.  We come to a dining room area.  Very plain.  Stone tables carved directly into the floor.  Ridley doesn’t find anything hidden.  He leads us to an open archway and into a room with a door. He doesn’t see any traps and opens the door. Looks like a place to prepare food.  Jars, boxes, etc. in the room.  RIdley opens up a jar and the food seems preserved and fresh.  We each get 2 rations. 
The Shadar-kai Visitor
We go down the hall, heading east.  Ridley feels his alarm go off at the portal.  He tells the group and Relys tries to look through Hopper’s eyes and doesn’t see what sets the alarm off.  We all split up to run upstairs - 4 different directions.   As we all run, Fungus hears slight clanging metal and feels a chain around his throat and hears “don’t move, don’t speak…let’s go back down.”  As Cerul gets to the top of the stairs, he notices that Fungus isn’t there.  
Fungus is getting pulled back.  He tells him his name.  He pulls him down the stairs and let’s the chain go and then slips around in front of him.  He sees a bald elf with light skin and muted gray armor.  He has spiked chains in his hands.  Fungus has no idea if he is good or bad.  He didn’t attack him, but he also wrapped a chain around his neck.  He tells him he came through the portal.  He asks where he is.  Fungus tells him the Blackbinding Citadel (misspeaks).  “He is summoning them” and Fungus asks “who is he?”  He says “Kael Vorduun (a yugoloth).”  He tells him he cannot shout to his friends.  He says he was following demons through the Shadowfell and Fungus tells him that we killed the demons.  He relaxes a little.  Fungus tells him the bad guy is in the dungeon and that we are planning to go to the dungeon below to take on the bad guy.  He says he is going to get more Shadar-Kai to take on the bad guy - that he wants to go through the portal.  Fungus tells him that we will help him.  Proceeds to tell him about Kaizo and friends. Meanwhile, Cerul looks for Fungus and sees the elf.  The rest of the group sees no one…then sees them coming up the stairs.  
He says “what is that unnatural feeling” and starts to look at all of us.  He says he focuses on Kaizo and Fungus.  There is a fey presence; it is strong.  Fungus says it is him.  He asks what our plans are.  We tell him that we have been searching around and that the guy is waiting for us and about his familiar/friend who keeps popping up.  This guy mentions Gehenna–known for having yugoloth–is sending the fiends through the Shadowfell.  This guy is named Acil. He says he is going back through the portal to get help and will be back.  We ask him NOT to close the portal here yet.  
We decide to go back down. We go to the first door we see. This is a room that feels like the rooms on the top floor - bigger, 2 beds, desk, and a folded cloak at the foot of the bed, small stack of stone tablets, and a horned cup. Relys casts detect magic and notices the cloak is magical. It is black and looks leathery (transmutation).  Ridley decides to tell everyone that he found pigments that allow the user to paint something and it becomes a reality.  Kaizo takes the cloak for now - until we figure out what it is.  He puts it on and it shapes to fit him.  Relys looks at the tablets - something looks recently carved.  “Containment without renewal is merely delayed failure.”  Seems like someone was trying to be deep, but seems a little like word salad.  Affy picks up the horned cup.
We make our way to the next room.  Stone shelves holding tablets and tomes.  Geometric shapes on the floor.  This seems to be a record room, references to trials.  Detailed procedures about binding.  We take a moment to read and look around, trying to figure out how/when the group decided to bind people.  Ridley investigates and sees the names Shoddy Nell Rootshifter and Nelindra the Infused in the book ...and it looks likes it is next to the word “Judged.”  He also sees the name Selbranan the Moon-touched in the books.  Relys still has detect magic up and looks around - doesn’t see anything.  Fungus casts a couple of rituals - comprehend languages and identify.  Fungus looks at Kaizo’s cloak and realizes it is “Cloak of the Bat.”  Ridley ends up getting the cloak.  And Kaizo takes the magical pigments.
We go to the last door at the end of the hall and Affy opens the door.  This seems to be the biggest room we have found.  Discipline hall.  Curved walls. Grooves draw eye to the center of the room.  Kaizo feels a sense of balance here.  RItual markings and grooves where people have tried discipline in different forms.
We go to the center room and see the big emblem on the floor of the symbol.  This is a vast chamber with curved walls.  There is a circular platform in this room on the floor, but doesn’t look like it has a floor - darkness looks like it goes down to nowhere/is a void.  There is a globe of energy surrounding the room (like a snowglobe).  We see the cult symbol in the center of the room that is etched out and the obsidian shard in the middle of it is much larger.  Fungus goes to touch the globe of energy and takes necrotic damage and knocks him prone in the center of the room.  He looks dazed.  He touches the shard and it shifts and moves a little.   Affy walks over to look at it and picks it up.  It is a black diamond - she puts it in her bag.
The little creature appears and mentions “our designation” and then pops away.  
We decide to go downstairs.  It feels like we are walking through a sheet that stops light as we descend.  Anything lit is void of light.  Once we get on the other side of this “barrier” things go back to normal.  Like upstairs, the hallways lead to the center of the crucible.  Everything is quiet. There are skeletons in recesses in shackles.  In the distance we see a circular chamber ahead that has chains extending from the walls to the center of the room.  Cerul wants to shatter one of the skeletons in a recess and does.  And hears laughter ahead.  
Don’t Break the Oath
We start to walk down the hall.  There is a towering obsidian structure floating in the large chamber.  There are 8 manacles holding a tall, gaunt figure. The manacles move as if the figure is breathing.  “You have come very far which tells me you are capable of understanding what others cannot.”
Kaizo and Ridley discuss this guy (it must be Kael Vorduun).  He says he is the one that has been bound.  He says that Malgri’n is the one who bound him.  He says that he has been trying to get others to come and unbind him.  We ask why was he bound.  He says he was an example.  Fungus speaks up to ask what it would take to unbind him.  And he focuses on Fungus.  These spectral chains are not magic anyone has seen.  He says “I can help you” to Fungus.  He says he can help clear the way for your purpose to Fungus.  Ridley is trying to figure out if this guy is good or evil.  The crucible will not release me without precise contractual obligations.  You would have to take an oath to unbind him to allow his containment to go unresolved.  Then you must agree to find something else to put in his place.  He says he was brought here by Malgri’n as an example…that he has done nothing wrong.  It is hard to resist Malgri’n.  His name is Kael Vorduun and is from the plane of Gehenna.  He has been here 25 years.  
Fungus starts to take the oath to release him.  Six of the eight manacles reach out to grab us….and then the other two fall apart and he steps out and then manacles drop.  He says I am in your debt.  Fungus tells him the name Teldo and he asks each of our name.  Fungus tells him about the elf and his friends coming.  The creature pops in “is that all we need boss - we get to go home.”  
The Crucible has now bound us all to the oath that we need to bind someone else here.  We don’t have to bring the person here…if we find someone who is worthy of being bound, it will happen.  Malgri’n will make it happen.  Malgri’n is VERY powerful.
He turns to leave and floats at a dash speed.  Ridley decides to check another corridor here before we go up.  His alarm goes off.  We head up.  We hear the sound of combat on the top floor as we make our way to the main floor.  We hear flames, more than two people. We take the tower stairs and as we get to the top and hear the combat end.  We head towards the portal and see the bald elf dead…along with 2 others.  Relys runs over to loot the dead bodies. Studded leather armor (black) and spiked chains that they use for weapons.  Fungus casts detect magic and one of the studded leathers is magical.  Relys puts it on and it sizes to her.  It would make her look like Shadar-Kai.   We also put their bodies in the portable hole.
Portal to Varenwood
We decide to go through the portal.  As we step through the gate, the pressure that made us tense is gone.  We are in a forest - damp earth and bark.  We stand in a small stone circle, druidic marks, stones.  Relys feels like she is home.  Stones are low and made for small folk.  She recognizes this place.  Behind us the portal remains open…and we now see smoky glass on the other side. Affy steps up and it doesn’t look like the Crucible, but like the Shadowfell.  
Relys thinks that Kael took advantage of the Weaving Night and has triangulated the portal…that he probably took advantage of the ritual and destroyed her village in his attempt to pull people from the Shadowfell to help him at the Crucible. We think that Kael would now be a target of someone we can bind to fulfill the oath to the Crucible.  
We decide to make camp some distance from the portal.  We head toward the direction of the village - stopping in between the circle and village, under cover of the forest. Before going there, Ridley sets an alarm.  
Many things are linked.  Kaizo’s meditation & balance is disturbed by Malgri’n.  Malgri’n has kept Fungus’ patron Shoddy Nell bound to the Shadowfell, quite possibly because of the Archfey Selbranan.  This portal in Varenwood leads to the Shadowfell and must be closed so that Rely’s homeland is safe from intruders. 
What to do next?  
1. Find the Moonpetal mushroom for Relys in Varenwood in the event the Weaving Night ritual is needed to close the portal to the Shadowfell.
2. Determine how to release Shoddy Nell from the binding in the Shadowfell.  What is the relationship between Shoddy Nell, Nelindra the Infused and Selbranan the Moon-touched? 
3. See if freeing Shoddy and closing the portal sets things straight for Kaizo.
– The End of the Session –
