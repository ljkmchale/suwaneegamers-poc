# Session 27 - Don’t Threaten Us

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: May 7, 2026
In-game Date:  10th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Borealian Loop
On the Road to Shademoor and beyond
As we start to head out, Affy asks Pa if she can ride in the wagon to study the greatsword.  He agrees.  We continue forward.  After a few hours, 2 riders on horseback approach quickly.   Ridley and Cerul both see them coming from behind us.   They are guards - one burly, one elvish.  “Listen up - we are taking our friends back - open up the cage and stand back and no one gets hurt.”  The guards on the wagon bow up.  Affy walks to the back:  “Who are you?”  And they say if you aren’t going to release them, then we will be back.  
They turn to ride away.  Affy yells “stay here” and pulls out her bow to hit the retreating elf and hits, minimal damage.  Cerul hits the same one with a scorching ray and then shoots the other, burly guy, missing one and then hitting once.  Relys uses speak with animals to yell at the horses:  “please your riders are getting you into trouble, come back, we don’t want to hurt you.”  One stops and it is INITIATIVE.
INITIATIVE – Combat vs. 2 guys
* ROUND ONE:  The elf sends a fiery bead and then kicks his horse to run away.  The fireball hits the area around the wagon.  Horse and executioner make their saves; Affy, Relys, and Cerul fail their saves; Ridley makes his saves.  The two guards fall, the executioner and horse live.  Affy uses her rush to take the dash action and runs forward 60’ and shoots another arrow the additional 60’, hitting.  The burly guy runs away.  Fungus runs up and picks up Relys and uses dimension door to pass them, then uses a bonus action to turn himself large and moves 10’ from Relys.  Ridley moves up 80’ and shoots the burly guy’s horse twice, killing it.  The burly guy is prone as he falls off his horse.  Relys shifts into a tiger and jumps at the elf, trying to knock him off his horse, but she misses.  She moves to him and attacks with her claws and hits, but he stays seated on the horse. Cerul runs 30’ up and at 90’ away, and misses with his spell.
* ROUND TWO:  The elf attacks Affy with an arcane burst three times and she almost goes down, but her orcish traits allow her to stay up, barely alive.  Relys takes an opportunity attack and hits him, but the elf continues to move.  Affy uses a bonus action to heal herself and then moves up 30’ and fires two more arrows at the elf’s horse, hitting twice.  Burly guy stands up and climbs up on the elf’s horse and pulls out a spear.  Fungus runs to the horse and throws “sticky juice” at the horse, freezing it for 4 rounds.  Then casts a spell on his staff.  Ridley moves up 40’ and is 60’ away and hunter’s mark the elf and hits him twice, dealing 33 points of damage.  Relys runs up 35’ and jumps at the wizard and tries to knock the elf off the horse, and would have hit, but the elf uses a shield spell and block him.  She then attacks burly man and hits him, once and misses once, then disengages and moves back 5’.  Cerul is 150’ away and moves up 30’...and tries to firebolt the elf, hitting him.  
* ROUND THREE:  The elf talks to the burly guy and says “we will avenge you brother” and then misty stepped behind a tree.  Affy moves up 60’ and then misses twice with her arrows, targeting the burly guy.  She then yells “surrender, bastard.”  The burly guy jumps off the horse and attacks Fungus with a spear and misses twice, then hits once.  Fungus moves behind the tree where the elf was and doesn’t see him and is invisible.  Ridley moves up 80’ and is next to the tree, and takes the search action to look for tracks.  He sees two sets of footprints - one that is big next to the tree (Fungus) and another that sees prints trailing off into the woods.  Relys runs up to burly guy and, thinking he won’t give up, attacks him and hits twice, inflicting great damage.  She then disengages and jumps 10’ back.  Cerul runs forward 30’, yelling- “where did the elf go?” and casts see invisibility.  He sees Ridley and an invisible Fungus. 
* ROUND FOUR:  The elf is about 25-30’ away from Fungus and Ridley and Cerul can see him running in the trees. He becomes visible and starts floating in the air and is now above the tree line.  Affy runs forward 15’ and shoots her bow and hits the burly guy once, it is a crit, then stows her bow.  The burly guy runs up to Affy and misses three times. Fungus tries to hit the burly guy and misses and then taunts him, but it misses, but he is in between him and Affy.  Ridley follows the tracks on the elf, moving forward 30’ and they stop. He looks up to see if he can see him. He climbs the tree and sees the elf about 15’ away, partially covered.  He grabs his whistle and casts summon beast on the elf - and a hunting falcon shows up on the elf and misses his attack.  And Ridley climbs down a little. Cerul flies up and sees the elf flying, swatting at a falcon.  He then attacks with a fireball and aims it up - hitting the elf and falcon, killing the falcon and elf.  Relys runs up and attacks the burly guy, missing twice. 
* ROUND FIVE: Affy runs up, pulls out her sword and attacks the burly guy.  Affy fumbles, allowing the guy to take an attack of opportunity and he hits Affy with her spear.  Affy then hits him, and inflicts additional fire damage.  The burly guy then misses Affy 3 times.  Fungus tries to poison and paralyze him with a vial of carrion crawler poison, but misses. Ridley hides, then shoots the burly guy and hits, then shoots again, critting him, but the burly guy is still up.  Relys runs up and shifts back into her gnome form and then tries to calm the horse and does. Cerul flies 30’ closer and is now 60’ from the burly guy and tries to wound him with a spell, but the burly guy saves.
* ROUND SIX: Affy steps up and kills the burly guy with a big hit, using acid on her blade.  She then steps forward and crushes his face with her boot and spits on him : “We asked him who they are and this happens - it didn’t have to be like this.”  Relys heals the horse and befriends it.  She names him Anjou, after his favorite food, pears. She begins to look through his saddlebags.
Ridley loots the elf - sees strange tattoo markings on the back of his neck.  He doesn’t recognize them.   He cuts off his ear.  Fungus drags him back, telling Ridley he is a disgusting person.  Affy doesn’t recognize the tattoos, either.  They loot the body and find a nice ring on each hand, one is a platinum ring with a nice design (and is valuable - Relys thinks it is worth 50-100gp).  Pouch has 45gp.  The other ring looks valuable and is plain, but has a shield look - looks like a ring of protection (Affy takes it).  The burly guy has a pouch with 45gp.  We give both pouches to the families of the dead guards.  He also has 3 spears, a circular shield with spikes, and studded leather.  He also has tattoo markings on his neck.  Seems like “gangster” markings.  Ridley and Affy flay the tattoos off of the elf and burly guy.  
Relys goes through the saddlebags.  She finds 2 silver daggers, 6 days worth of rations, and a bag of pears.  We keep the saddlebag off the dead horse. 
We go back to the party.  Ridley comforts the executioner and hands the pouches of gold to him to cover the loss of the two guards.  Affy offers to ride with him, but he tells the group that the wizard is missing from the wagon.  Neither Cerul nor Relys see the wizard - Relys notices the other prisoners are very quiet.  Ridley asks Affy which criminal did the worse crime - she’s not sure.  RIdley pulls out a prisoner and he has the same tattoo as the ones on the guys we just fought.  Affy pulls out her tattoo and shows him.  The guy almost gets sick from seeing it and admits that the tattoo is an initiation marking for a group that works in the Bathean Empire - they try to take from the rich.  They are called the “Inherited” - they take from the rich and keep it (inherit it).  
The Rongri
Ridley and Affy have a conversation in their head about killing them - Ridley in favor, Affy not.  The rest of the party hears the rest of the prisoner whispering. Kaizo comes walking around, explains that Pa and kids saw what happened and asks where the Rongri came from?  He says that the rongri went into the trees. There is a discussion from the executioner about letting him take the bags and go - leaving the prisoners with us.  Affy convinces her friends to let justice play out and have the executioner continue to stay with the caravan.  Relys and RIdley go to the tree line and she finds claw marks on the trees, that matches the rongri.  Ridley then sees that there are many trees and that the rongri likely disappears.  He then sees a fuzzy moth fly by and he is contemplating how the wizard pulled off his spell.  Relys talks to the frost moth - “I only bloom in the winter.” She asks him if he helped the wizard.  The moth says he doesn’t know.  She thinks he is telling the truth.  She thinks that the wizard was able to polymorph with a moth cocoon.  
Ridley walks back to the lockbox and sees a set of keys on the floor. He opens the lockbox and sees that the magic tome is missing.
The dead guard bodies are put in Kael’s cart. A squirrel pops up in the cart and Relys talks to it:  did you see him turn into a rongri and he nods.  She asks why he is in the cage.  He responds that he was asked to watch the wizard by Ridley.  He describes what happened to Relys.  And he decides to jump on her horse and join the group.  Fungus names him Nuts.
The group gathers around Cerul for some healing and a short rest.
Pa pulls out his map and we compare it to our map - his notes the camp sites.  We decide to move forward to the next camp site.  It is called Silent Watch.  Relys and Fungus entertain the children as we move to the campsite.
Arrival at the Silent Watch campsite
The night smells like burning pine smoke as we climb a hill…and see a campfire off to the side, and a guy splitting wood.  We make it to the site.  Pa tells us that Realmwarden rangers like to hang out here - a good place to take watch.  This is Kinwae Drael.  Cerul and Relys recognize the name - this has historical significance (like a bad thing happened to someone with that surname).  He offers us to stay with him, there is a warm fire, and he has stew.  He tells us that the stones help keep the area warm.  Affy introduces herself and the group.  He says he is a patrol member of the realmwardens.   Fungus tells him about the ambush on the road.  Ridley tells him about the wizard who disappeared.  Kinawe asks for more details and steps off with Ridley to talk to him.  He tries to reason with Ridley that if his captive was the most important thing then he didn’t have to join the fight….so to take the blame off his party member. He offers to help track the wizard once he leaves and goes north - and will send word to Ridley.  He tells Ridley that things will work out.  Ridley tells him about the wizard, about his abilities, and his apprentices.  He asks why Ridley is hunting this guy.  Ridley says he is a wanted criminal who has assisted with the murder of guards. He again asks why he was initially hunting him and Ridley doesn’t share everything.  Kinwae just tells him to trust his companions.  They go back to camp.
Relys asks if the stew is vegetarian and says that she will forage - but Ridley gives her goodberries.  Relys organizes the saddlebags and her pack and the horse. Affy asks Kinwae about his travels and then Fungus runs his mouth and tells him everything.  Cerul pipes us to tell him about the trolls from that morning.  Affy asks about anything coming up in their travels and he tells them that there might be trouble in Halesworth.  Relys tells him about rumors of trouble at the Southern Arch…and he says that Gar wouldn’t let anything happen there (Gar - is a goliath who has a dockhouse at the Southern Arch).  Pa joins in that he thinks the trouble is closer to Bloodbeak.
We decide to eat stew, take watch, and get rest.  Cerul and Fungus take watch together and Cerul asks him about some of his spells and agree that Fungus will communicate when casting spells so the rest of the group knows what to expect.
It’s the morning of 11th of Paramor, 1246
We wake up.  Kinwae is leaving - safe travels my friends.  We pack up and get the caravan going.  Cerul helps preserve the dead bodies. The group heads out, with Ridley and Relys in the back. They are looking over their shoulder.  They see a mounted figure following the group. Ridley pulls back a little to move off to the side to let the person catch up so that he can follow them.  He notices this person is a woman and doesn’t look threatening.  She seems to close in slowly.  Relys seems to notice that the woman is looking off to the side at Relys. Ridley knows this is a lady from Qal’dynn.
RIdley looks over at Relys and coughs…”this is something I have to take care of, I will catch up momentarily.”  She walks to Ridley and says “where is he?”  He tells her that he escapes.  She questions that Ridley may have killed him and he tells her he didn’t.  She believes him and sighs. She says “I know you feel like you failed, but this is for the best.”  He asks where he went and she says that she has her suspicions but can’t tell him knowing that Ridley will hunt him and kill him.  She tells him that she can’t say much since she has no confidence that Ridley will do the right thing.  
The party is now 60’ away.  Once the party is 200’+ feet away from them, Ridley pulls his crossbow and it is INITIATIVE.  
* ROUND ONE:  He summons a beast behind her.  This is a jackal fox.  Ridley commands the fox to knock her out. He then closes the distance to her. The fox will then go and try to non-lethally shove her and fumbles, bouncing off the horse.  She gets off her horse and looks at Ridley and says “for Heildove” and she slaps the horse to run off to the party and walks up to Ridley and doesn’t attack.
* ROUND TWO:  Ridley puts manacles on her and the fox tries to hit the horse and does, and the horse takes damage. The woman surrenders.  
* ROUND THREE:  Ridley tries to knock her out so she can’t talk to the group and he does.  The horse runs by the group.  Relys speaks with animals - “whoa, friends, what is going on?” The horse stops and runs around Relys, as she jumps off her horse.  The fox says “got to knock it out” to Relys.  And the fox attacks the horse and hits, knocking it unconscious.  Relys can tell that the fox was under Ridley’s control.
He then carries the woman in chains up to the group. Relys cures the horse.  The group asks Ridley about the woman, and Affy asks her name.  Eliyena, says Ridley, she is a clue to him finding the wizard.  Ridley speaks with the horse and tries to talk to him.  Ridley dismisses the fox.  Ridley tries to feed the horse goodberries, but he isn’t interested.  Relys gives pears to the horse and Anjou.  Ridley throws the woman over the horse, after gagging and binding her, and rides the horse with her. Affy asks if she is okay and she shakes her head.  But the group leaves Ridley with his captive for the rest of the ride.
Arrival at Darafee
The caravan continues. Twilight starts to close. The group arrives in Darafee.  There are frost moths in the air.  Pa says we have to go to the Toad to rest since it is getting late.  Maybe some of us will be more welcome than others.  We find the stables to house the animals (3sp/each to house).  Ridley hands the stable boy 1gp to groom the horse.  Affy asks Ridley what is going on - is this woman a flight risk.  The executioner asks Cerul for help to escort the prisoners to a room - he agrees to help.  Fungus offers to help, too.  
We walk into the Cozy Toad.  We find the main level.  This is a nice place with a wooden smell and lanterns lit with fungi (very natural).  We find help and are able to get as many rooms as needed.  They also can serve food and drinks to the group as well.  There seem to be regulars.  There is also a light shining down on a guy with shaggy hair, telling stories to people.  RIdley asks for his own room, too.  And gets some pushback on getting a room.   Ridley leaves the inn with the lady.  Relys, Affy, and Kaizo get a room.  Fungus and Cerul get a room next door to the room with the guard and prisoners.  Relys shifts into a spider and follows Ridley.
Ridley finds a place in the bushes and casts alarm (but Relys makes it inside his circle).  Ridley keeps her gagged and tells her that he will have questions for her in Caelora.  Relys thinks that she is being submissive and is 100% committed to her cause.  She thinks her life and sacrifice is worth whatever this is with Ridley.  She thinks that Ridley is confused.
Meanwhile in the Cozy Toad, the group goes to their rooms.  It smells like evergreen and fresh bread.  This is very warm and comforting.  We get mugs and food.  Our attention is drawn to the human in the corner who is lit up and telling stories.  Everyone else here seems to be an elf.  Fungus walks over to hear the story.  He is talking about being on the road just before the Southern Arches.  He tells of a loud roar.  He was driven off by something that scared him.  He mentions that everyone at the camp scattered away.  He stretches the story into a very lengthy tale.  People are captivated - even the regulars. Fungus jumps into the area to tell a story….does so with sound effects.  Tells the story of the trolls under the bridge.  The crowd seems to be captivated, too.  Affy offers to buy him a cold one and walks over to join our group.  He says, “it is so good to see, uh.”  He then says that Affy seems comfortable.  This is Thallan Darego.  Affy tells him that what Fungus says is true and shows him her sword and tests him to see if he speaks giant (he doesn’t).  She then asks him about his stories and the truth of it.  He admits to embellishing the story, but he admits that he heard the roars.   Affy asks him where is coming from and where he is going.  She asks if he can take a message to the King of Qal’dynn.  Cerul offers to write the missive - it will be about the trolls and about Inherited.  Kaizo tells him about the weed he should find when he’s there.  We tell him to go to the Pale Lantern.  He then goes over to relieve Fungus and tell other stories to the group.
Meanwhile, Ridley summons the fox and asks him to watch over the woman while he walks away and goes to a general store. Relys sees Ridley leaving and sees the fox standing watch over her. She notices that the woman is bound and gagged just enough to keep her from casting spells or audibly talking.  She shifts into her normal form and unties the gag.  She is taken aback.  The fox growls. Relys then talks to the woman.  Look, I know my acquaintance can be a little much, but I’m trying to find out what is going on, because he won’t tell us anything.  She says, your friend has a lot of loyalty, because it is blinding him stubbornly.  She tells Relys that the wizard was falsely accused of cursing someone in Caelora that Ridley knows.  She knows that the wizard (Heildove) would not have cursed anyone.  That the Rulendors asked Ridley to help them.  She says that she knows that Heildove is innocent and she owes him her life.  Relys says that she is on the side of good and would like to help.  The woman says that the noble house of Rulendor in Cealora is corrupt and that they are going after Heildove because he would not cross a line that they wanted him to cross.  She says that she is not saying that Ridley is wrong, but that his loyalty to the Rulendors may be harming others.  Relys says that she will need to gag and keep her bound - that she doesn’t want to betray Ridley.  She asks to go to the bathroom.  Relys helps her and rebinds her, feeds her.  Relys can tell that she has hope that Ridley will find his way to the right side of things and that her mentor is safe for now. 
Thallen tells one more story to the room.  He talks about this area (we think back to Pa’s map) and the forest beneath Murfield. The woods to the south of Murfield are going dark and dead than winter brings.  Something is wrong there.  What he describes reminds us about the Shadowfel.  He says that hunters won’t go there now.  That the land seems to be possessed.  But mark my words, the forests to the south of Murfield - they grow hungry. Affy tells Fungus he did a good job with his stories.
Relys shifts into an owl and flies out of the circle and sets off the alarm.  Ridley hears the alarm spell go off and shoots the owl - thinking it was a wizard (and not Relys).  He hits and damages the owl and now knows this is not a polymorphed creature.  So he lets it go.
She flies through a window of the tavern and shifts and rejoins the group.
Ridley is there with the fox and woman.  The fox stares at him.  Ridley asks the woman what the owl was doing.  He asks if this was a friend of hers.  She responds that she kind of likes the owl.  He then re-asks - what was the owl doing here.  Ridley casts speak with animals and asks the fox about the owl.  That was some little cute person…it was Relys. He tells Ridley that she had a conversation with Relys and Relys gagged her and then left as an owl.  Ridley drops clothes and soap at the woman’s feet and takes the manacles off.  He says she won’t get far.  He gives her a waterskin.  He then burns the woman’s clothes.  After she cleans up , he feeds her goodberries and binds her again and they go to sleep.
It’s the morning of the 12th of Paramor, 1246
We wake up and will be heading out the next day with the caravan.  
– The End of the Session –
