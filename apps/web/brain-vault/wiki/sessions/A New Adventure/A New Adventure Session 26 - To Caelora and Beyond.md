# Session 26 - To Caelora and Beyond

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: April 16, 2026
In-game Date:  8th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Qal’dynn
Escort for Three Wagons
There are 3 wagons:  
1. Wagon 1 - Mottlebrook Family (Pa, Leila, 2 teenage helpers) with Affy and Kaizo
2. Wagon 2 - Kael’s Components, a tiefling wizard with Cerul 
3. Wagon 3 - The Iron Cage, two city guards, a mysterious guard (with a full black executioner’s hood) and 5 prisoners with Ridley, Fungus and Relys (on Fungus’ shoulders)
Fungus talks to one of the guards about chopping off heads.  Ridley thinks one of the prisoners is familiar. 
Kaizo tries to climb on the second wagon…in stealth.  He sees one crate and several different small bags in the back.  There are runes on the posts that hold up the walls of the wagons.  The hair on his neck stands up.  He tries to discern what might be in the box and how the runes might be linked.
Affy asks him about his cargo - do they make their ale or just distribute it?  They make it.  Pa encourages her to stay in Shademoor, and to visit the church there.  It is very special.  He comments about the lumber there - how they have a special person who makes things out of hardwood.  The Garden there is really a cemetery - they have a Gardenkeeper who watches the place.
Fungus asks how long the trip will take. It will take 4 days to get to Darafee.  Will stay the night there.  We will stop at the feytouched meadow. This is a clearing.  In the spring it is nice.  Mentions he has other stops planned.  Darafee to Caelora is another 2 days.  Then to the Southern Arch.  Then Shademoor.  Fungus talks to them about the profit in their wine (a carafe is 10gp; he has 5 barrels of wine).  
Pa mentions that there are rumors of people disappearing around the Southern Arch.  He doesn’t know much.  There were scratch marks and torn clothes and words that folks have disappeared.  Pa mentions that it has been a while since he hung out with the likes of someone like Affy (quick discussion here that Affy changes the subject on).  Random conversation all day.
We travel the full day and Kaizo eventually sneaks out of the wagon before we make camp.  
Camp in a Meadow
We arrive at the meadow.  Leaves circle in a pattern.  The guards on the prison wagon start to make camp and Affy moves back to question whether the prisoners need to be exercised, have a restroom break.  Ridley steps in as well - asks why they are prisoners.  The guard won’t say.  Ridley offers to help empty their chamberpots.  The guards affix manacles to a rope then opens the door to the back and starts putting manacles on each prisoner.  
Pa, Leila and their family stop, make camp, and eat.
The tiefling in the middle wagon looks at Kaizo and nods, and then casts a spell around his wagon. 
Fungus goes for a walk around the meadow.  Kaizo shares what he saw in the second wagon with Ridley - asks him to check it out. Ridley looks at the prisoners when they are exiting the third wagon and thinks one of them is the guy he has been hired to kill.  Ridley cleans the chamber pots.  Relys forages the meadow and Fungus follows her 100 yards back. She finds supplies to make 3 meals.  Affy hangs out with the guards and prisoners - these people are guards of places she hasn’t been.  These guards do not have markings of realmwardens…they have a bronze griffon with raised talons on their tabards.   Affy asks Cerul about the markings - he’s not that familiar either, but as they talk they think this is heraldry of Basctdelm.  
Prisoners in the cart:  2 human men that look like they are bandit/thug types, 2 other human men who are moaning and complaining - perhaps “white collar” criminals, and then 1 in robes (who is an elf).  The prisoners sit and eat - manacles and ropes confining them.  No belongings on them, but there is a crate on the wagon (that might be holding belongings).
As Fungus circles the meadow, he is looking for signs of fey.  He thinks maybe what he sees could be weather-related and not magical.  In the distance, however, he sees a light to the SW - thinks it could be a campfire a long way off.  He points this out to Kaizo.  
Ridley talks to Relys about food.  He ends up making goodberries and gives one to each member of the party.  Relys gives one of her meals (salad) to Fungus. Ridley talks to the guards about Relys, who comments that she is a cute gnome.  He offers a berry to a guard.  Another takes one. 
The guard who had the hood walks away from the group and takes his hood down.  As Ridley approaches, he puts his hood back up - no one is supposed to know who she is. Ridley offers him a berry.  Guard comments that Ridley is more his type.  They have a discussion about what to feed the prisoners.  Ridley mentions that he “executes” people as well. They talk about them both being badasses - the guard shows off a curved battle axe to Ridley, as Ridley takes this opportunity to observe the lock on the wagon and on the lockbox. 
Affy attempts to talk to the white collar criminals to find out what they did.  They “sold land” that was fraudulent.  The hardened guys were criminals - worked out of Basctdelm (mentioned the undercroft of the castle there).  Affy talks to the robed guy. He says this was a misunderstanding.  She inquires further.  He says that he poisoned a shipment that was headed to the Promissory.  I have this thing that people think I do, but I don’t really do that.  He says that he doesn’t want to go through some of the places we are going. Specifically talks about Caelora and how he doesn’t want to go there.  Says he will place a curse on everyone in the caravan to keep us from going there.  Says people there are corrupt and want to see him dead.  Mentions that Ridley is also one who wants to see him dead. He asks Affy to tell Ridley that “he didn’t do it” but won’t say what he is accused of there. 
Meanwhile, Fungus uses special oil on his feet to make sure they are soothed from the day’s walk.  He offers some balm to Cerul, and eventually to Affy to lure her away from the prisoners.
* WATCH 1 – Relys and Cerul on first watch.  Relys shifts into an owl to fly and circle. She sees four large 4-legged creatures and 2 humanoids around the far campsite.  She flies closer.  The creatures she sees are bulas, that are tied up. Two of them are male with antlers.  She sees the humans in a bedroll.  She flies back to tell Cerul what she sees. 
* WATCH 2 – They wake up Ridley and Affy.  They talk about the robed prisoner.  Ridley mentions that he is from Cealora and that the people who hired him are from there.  He is worried about being there without bringing Heildove to justice before we arrive.  Ridley notices that Affy’s eyes are sparkling and she feels warmth in her eyes and in her hands after she rubs her eyes.  Ridley walks on the outskirts of camp, presumably scouting.  He sees a guard step up to watch him.  They talk about Relys - and Ridley mentions the campfire to the guard that Relys noticed people at. He says he is going that way - get out of line of sight of the camp, turns into a bat, and flies back to our camp, then turns back into himself.  He hears mumbling inside the iron wagon - whispers.  He goes into stealth (rolls a 37 on his Stealth check).  Tries to overhear the whispering…and can’t understand what he hears. He notices that the second wagon has an alarm set.  He tries to pick the lock on the box on the prisoner wagon and succeeds.  He sees a large, decorative tome (with arcane writing), a very nice pouch (2 rings, a greater healing potion), a small sack (components), and a forgery kit and a binder of leather holding up pieces of paper (evidence of things forged).  He skims through the contents (noted).  He puts everything back in the box and relocks it.  He looks into the window and sees a prisoner awake and the other 4 have eyes that sparkle and they are asleep.   The robed figure who is awake is looking out the back.   Ridley takes the long way around and makes his way back to the campfire.  Affy is warm and takes off some of her clothing.  
* WATCH 3 – They wake up Kaizo and Fungus.  Affy briefs them about Relys’ findings, then asks about her eyes and what they might know.  She thinks that maybe the wizard did something to her.  After she briefs them, she decides to prematurely wake up Cerul to get his opinion on her condition.  He tries to dispel magic on her.  It starts to fade on her. She is better, but feels empty.  Ridley comments that the wizard is known for cursing people.  And that this is why he’s been hired to kill him.  He then says that he is going to kill him before we go to Caelora.  Kaizo and Fungus are apprehensive about this. Ridley, Affy, and Cerul try go to sleep.  Affy has a hard time getting to sleep - she is agitated and realizes that she shouldn’t have awakened Cerul.  She finally gets to sleep.  Fungus and Kaizo are on watch now.  The tiefling hands Kaizo a cup - “I noticed you haven’t been sleeping well.”  Kaizo declines, but Fungus takes it.  This is a tea that helps them rest.  The tiefling (Kael Vas) argues with Kaizo that he is not fine.  And says that if Kaizo every needs to unload to come talk to him.  Fungus talks to him about where he is going and why he is going there.  He says he is delivering materials to the Tegenwald Phet in Caelora.  He says they get their components from Kael Vas (him).  He asks them both if they need magical components.  He says if there is anything they need, they know who to call.  He says he has been “riding with someone.”  Kaizo comments that he thought he was riding alone and Kael laughs.  Clearly, Kael knew that Kaizo was was in the cart.  Fungus acknowledges this.  Kael also asks if Kaizo is his friend and Fungus says yes.  Kael says that Kaizo is not good and needs help. Fungus casts calm emotions on everyone - which works on everyone (and Affy can now sleep).  Kaizo asks for additional tea from Kael.  Kael comments that “the little one” can walk - she’s got many years ahead of her.  Kaizo asks what he is hauling and he says they are spell components.  Kael tried to figure out what Kaizo was thinking while he was riding in his cart all day - which is how he knows that Kaizo is troubled.  Kael mentions the dark tendrils. And Kaizo admits this.  Kael tells Kaizo that he should inquire with the Phet about his visions to see if they can help.  Kaizo asks about everything Kael saw and they discuss this.  He says that he needs to try to find a cure.
The Next Day:  9th of Paramore, 1246
We all get a long rest. Pack up and move along.  Kaizo tries to stealth into the second cart; Kael notices and tells him to relax there.  Relys joins him.  
Bulas Wranglers
As we travel, we see 2 big humanoids (Cerul’s height), each walking 2 bulas, and they reach the trail.  They are half orcs.  They notice Affy.  They ask if it is safe to travel north.  They are heading to Palason to sell the bulas (100gp for each pair: 40gp for male, 60gp for female).    These are beasts that they wrangled in the wild in the mountains.  Fungus wants to buy a male bulas.  He tries to convince them to take 3pp, 5gp vs. 40gp.  He ends up paying 40gp.  He looks at both males to see which one is friendlier; asks Relys to see which one would want to travel with Fungus.  She speaks with animals - asking if either one like to be a “vehicle for this fine fellow” and points to Fungus.  They mention the “oil” that they smell on Fungus - one likes it and one doesn’t.  She tells Fungus which would like him.  She asks him what his favorite food is and he mentions berries.  She makes berries for them all.  She asks for his name and he says “me” and Fungus says he will give him a magnificent name.  At Kaizo’s prompting, she asks if he is attached to any of the others and he responds “which night.”  Conversation ends.  Fungus has a new pet.
We continue to move on, bypassing other travels.  Pa mentions we will be sleeping in a lodge (Ashbarrel Lodge) on the road prior to Darafee.  But when Relys responds that she may not want to, he says that others could sleep in the woods.  Kaizo asks about danger - and Pa responds that it would be around the Southern Arch.  Kaizo then goes into stealth. Ridley looks around for Kaizo and doesn’t see him. He then goes up to Cerul and asks him about Kaizo - who hears this and then pops into view.  They talk about whether or not he still needs to look into the second wagon.  Relys hears this and offers to detect magic.  Kael hears this and says there is no need to do that - my wagon has no magic other than the runes and magical string.  Kael is then offended that Ridley or anyone would want to look around further on the wagon.  Kaizo encourages Ridley to offer to buy some of his components. They have a discussion about the type of magic Ridley uses.  They talk of holy water, poisons, herbs (he has none).  Relys buys the holy water for 25gp.  Kael points out “the little acorn” in the bottom of the box and Ridley offers to buy it. 
Trygg is what Fungus names his Bulas.  It means trusty, secure and safe in giant.  He asks Relys to tell him his name and Affy encourages him to tell him himself.
Ridley tells the group he still plans to kill Heildove.  Just as a matter of fact.  We ask that he doesn’t do anything to compromise our position with the caravan.  Fungus says he might help the wizard.  Kaizo, Fungus and Ridley talk about curses and the wizard…Ridley doesn’t know if the curses the wizard is accused of delivering killed anyone.  Relys jumps into the conversation…that maybe we need to find out more of the story.  As the group talks, something smells sweet.  It seems to be coming from Trygg.   We can’t figure it out.
The Ashbarrel Lodge
We finally arrive at the Ashbarrel Lodge. The wood looks is colored like wood from an ash tree, but constructed to appear more like a dwarven stone structure.  Very unique.  Pa walks in - very excited to be here “we are going to need some rooms.”  This is a big place, big rooms (meant to house a lot of folks).  We see some dwarves inside with a row of barrels, all tapped (made out of gray-ish colored ash).  They pass out keys to Pa, the guards, the tiefling.  We get one room for the group - Ridley will sleep outside (1sp/each).  
Only entertainment are storytellers.  Fungus decides to buy ale, listen to stories and go to bed.  After 3 ales, he is tipsy and sings and dances; after 2 more, he is passed out.  Relys takes the ale that Fungus didn’t use and gives it to them.  Kaizo meditates in the room.  His thoughts are dark and eeries…not great.  Ridley looks around outside and tries to speak with bugs in the area - he finds moths and a couple of beetles. He looks for squirrels.  He talks to one and offers it berries (it wants nuts).  He produces nut-flavored good berries and gives them to the squirrel - he asks him to follow him.  He goes over to the empty prisoner wagon and tries to pick the lock on the box on the wagon and can’t.  He asks the squirrel to squeeze through the bars and stay in the wagon for a few days.  Rest of the group goes to bed and to sleep.
The Next Day:  10th of Paramore, 1246
We wake up.  Relys wakes up Fungus who is passed out in the common room.  The group heads out.  Trygg is excited to travel today.  The group packs up and heads out.   It is cool and comfortable.  Around noon, we round a bend and we see a stone bridge that spans a ravine.  
Toll (Troll) Bridge
Next to it, we see a crude wooden hut.  There is a sign that says “toll required.”  Pa calls out “that is new folks, please check this out.”
Relys shape shifts into a spider and jumps on Ridley.  Ridley and Kaizo try to stealth forward, but Kaizo is visible so he waits.  Ridley and Relys go forward to the toll house. They notice that some trees have been recently cleared to build this hut.  They see the door is closed and the only windows to the hut are high up to let light in.  Hut is likely around 8’ around. Relys crawls up the side and looks through a window.  She sees a big, blue-skinned guy sitting inside.  He has small horns, sharp teeth, and he is scraping a great sword.  She has never seen anything like this.  She crawls back to Ridley and describes what she sees.  He doesn’t know what he is either.  Ridley takes Relys back to the group and she relays the info.  Fungus and Affy walk to the door, with Relys on Fungus’ shoulder.  As they get close to the door, the door opens and the guys steps out and plants his great sword.  He says “toll for crossing my bridge - I’ll take one warm body.”  Give me one now and you may pass.  Affy has not seen anything like this, but she’s heard tales and thinks this is a mix of a hag and demon.  Ridley climbs a nearby tree.  Affy says “we are not giving you a body.”  He says “then you are not crossing.”  Fungus says, “how long have you been here?” and “how many people have you eaten since you’ve been here” and he replies that few willingly, but I have to eat.   Fungus goes back to “put up his bulas” and grabs the group.  Affy asks him why he can’t hunt game.  She tries to intimidate him and can’t.  He says: “Boys let’s eat.”  Three trolls appear. 
IT IS INITIATIVE - Combat vs. the Oni and his troll minions
* Round One:   Ridley shoots the closest troll and hits., pushes him back 10’;  casts hunters mark on the blue guy.  Hits another troll and pushes him back 10’. Troll climbs up out of the ravine and rushes forward.  Kaizo rushes forward to the nearest troll and stuns it, misses, then hits again.  Another troll moves up the ravine and runs forward towards the group.  The blue guy casts darkness in the trees.  Fungus moves back, becomes large and casts a spell on the closest troll to him and inflicts damage. Relys is on Fungus’ shoulder.  She jumps off and becomes a saber and runs through the darkness and jumps onto the troll that Kaizo is attacking. She attacks and hits twice, dealing radiant damage. Cerul runs around the spell and casts a fireball on two of the trolls.  Affy attacks the blue guy - hits 3 times (he drops the darkness).  The last troll attacks Cerul and he throws out a warding flare.  He hits Cerul.
* Round Two:  Ridley hides, then shoots the blue guy and hits twice. Troll is no longer stunned.  Kaizo attacks that troll and hits, stunning him again. Then runs over to the blue guy and misses twice. The troll steps out of the blackness and attacks Cerul and hits once. Blue guy uses sword to attack Affy and hits twice and then disappears. Fungus moves his blackness 10’ away from him.   He yells to Affy to run away from the blackness.  He casts poison spray on the closest troll and knocks him prone. Relys crits on the troll, dealing radiant damage. Then crits again, killing him. Then she hides in the forest.  Cerul casts radiance of the dawn on the two trolls next to him.  Then casts healing word on himself.  Affy runs to the troll and attacks twice, with a crit, knocking him prone.  He then stands up and attacks Fungus, hitting once, and fumbling.  The blackness disappears. 
* Round Three:   Ridley looks for the invisible blue guy and doesn’t see him.  And hides.  Kaizo moves toward the other group, but holds his reaction to strike if something hits him.  A troll stands up and attacks Fungus and hits twice then fumbles. The blue guy attacked Fungus and missed. Then attacks Affy and hits. Fungus misty steps away then moves away, imposing disadvantage on one of the trolls and Affy.  He then casts poison spray on the blue guy and misses. Relys moves to the trolls and attacks one of them, hitting it.  Then attacks again and hits, killing it. She then tries to hide and can’t.  Cerul attacks the last troll with burning hands and kills him.  Affy attacks the blue guy and hits once.
* Round Four:  Ridley takes out the blue guy with a bolt.
We loot the bodies - nothing on the trolls.  The blue guy has a sword (rune covered), leather armor and a ring.  Fungus finds a potion of healing and a scroll (Scroll of Spider Climbing) in a pile in the hut.  Affy asks Fungus to check out the stuff on the blue guy.  There is magic on the sword and ring. As Fungus does this, we grab the caravan to move them forward, burn the trolls, and burn the hut.  Relys and Ridley look for a stash of treasure. They see a cave under the bridge and it smells bad.  They see rotten flesh and bones, fur.  But also find 1200cp, 850sp, 35pp, and 220gp.  They see wagons and other debris.  They find a silver necklace with a bloodstone (75gp), carved ivory kalhbit statue (150gp), a seal making kit (25gp), and a carved onyx figurine of a wagon (100gp), traveler’s clothes, chain shirts, arrows, quivers, wagon wheel, regular lanterns, cask of wine.  
Fungus identifies that the ring is a Ring of warmth (and takes it) and the greatsword says “Trollsplitter” in Giant (runes) - maybe for Affy?
The guards, Pa, Kael step up and say “money well spent.”  We pack up and cross the bridge.
– The End of the Session –
