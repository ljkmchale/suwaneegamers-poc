# Session 21 - The Emerging Temple

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  January 1, 2026
In-game Date:  5th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Abbey of Light
Day 4/8 of rest for Feywoven Glyphic
The party gathers after spending a stretch of downtime.  It is cold and snowing outside.  Everyone but Kaizo is here at the Rising Hearthlight; Kaizo is attending to duties with the monks.  The party is eating warm soup/stew and hanging out–catching up from being apart for a stretch.
* Cerul Update – The party notices that Cerul is wearing new, shiny mithiri armor he purchased in O’naren when escorting the orphans back to the abbey from Qal’dynn. 
* Fungus Update – Affy asks if anyone else got anything new and Fungus reveals his gills.  He says he got them from using the Feywoven Glyphic 4 days ago.  He says Tenana came back and he gave her the shard. And that now he is questioning who his powers are from since he had a dream about  Nelindeia - who may be the source of his power.  He doesn’t know if she changed names or is someone new.  He also made new potions (Animal Friendship).  He stayed at the Abbey the entire time.  
* Relys Update – Relys studied with Pivric trying to figure out why her village Iparos was destroyed.  Her studies revealed that there are things that can go wrong with the ritual called the Weaving Night.  When these things go wrong, they can open up a rift into the Shadowfell.  Her suspicions believe that may be what occurred, but she needs to return to that area of Varenwood to see if there are any distant traces of Conjuration magic used.  Relys also believes that she may be able to perform the ritual properly if she has some Nightbud and another component, the fungus known as Moonpetal.
* Ridley Update – Ridley says he was trying to find out more about Rueh’unar Heildove, someone that he is on the hunt for that he feels is a bad person.  Ridley believed that Rueh’unar was involved in criminal activity, but found new information that he may not be a criminal like he was led to initially believe.  Ridley is not questioning his mission, because Ridley knows that Rueh’unar is a wizard who curses others.  Ridley also mentions that Kaizo was restless…and didn’t sleep well.  Ridley stated that Kaizo seemed out of sorts and never visited his spa.
* Affy Update – Affy shares that she did research on her father and his tie with the Advents of Harmony in the libraries in Qal’dynn, but didn’t have luck.  Affy decided to search around the city to get additional information on her dad.  While in the city, Affy  observed her companions, noticing Ridley talking to people and Relys with Pivric.  Affy ended up going through Arcane Waypoint to the Argent Halls, which is now on an island floating in the air.  Affy didn’t reveal to the people there, other than to help out to earn trust.  A lady at the Argent Halls asked her to research the Feywoven Glypic, which Affy found ironic.  Affy didn’t reveal to the people of the Argent Halls that Affy knows about the cube, but researches the item in the Argent Halls archives.  Affy learns that the Feywoven Glypic is very powerful, tied to the Feywild, and wanted by an archfey lord named Selbranan.  The lady Affy was helping ends up mentioning Affy’s father, in which Affy reveals that Affy is looking for him.  The lady offers to contact Affy’s father and let him know that Affy is looking for him.  Affy then tells Fungus how powerful and dangerous the Feywoven Glyphic is and the group discusses that he needs to keep it a secret since it could attract those who attacked the party in the Ring of Sylunara in Qal’dynn; as well as others who would want to use the power.
Protective Light Visitor
After catching up, the door of the establishment opens and a familiar halfling steps in–it is Therin Hillabuck.  Therin pulls down the note from the board and brings it over to the party.  The note is the posting about the danger of the nearby cave.  Therein says Warden Valegar has already granted the party clearance to go to the cave, so the party is free to go.  Therin explains the route to get there - about a mile out from the Abbey (small cave on the side of a mountain).  Therrin saw bodies, bones–old and new–and he didn’t go further. Affy asks about rumors and Therrin says there are none since no one who has been there has come back.
To the Cave  
The group leaves and starts to follow the trail for the cave, gathering or purchasing cold weather gear since it’s now winter and the abbey on Cradlecrest Rise is high up.  After about 30 minutes of travel, the group sees the opening of a cave.  There are some of the bodies and bones that were described at the mouth of the cave.  Ridley spots nearby mountain sabers (large catlike creatures) and asks for everyone to be quiet so the group isn’t noticed.  Some of the mountain sabers are eating and others that look to have just eaten.  
Ridley casts Pass Without Trace, so that the party is more stealthy and can attempt to sneak past while Relys creates a distraction with Druidcraft.  The party hugs the left crag wall on the way to the cave, the plan works and the party makes it into the cave. 
Inside the Cave
Inside the cave is a long winding tunnel, with light filtering in for about 40 feet. The party has to dodge bones on the tunnel floor as they continue to be stealthy.  Once inside, Cerul activates Lightbringer and casts Light on Relys’ staff.  Ridley questions why the party is there and they mention the post which says there may be magic relics.  As the tunnel is lit, Ridley notices a ring on a corpse nearby—iIt is a Band of Dunduar–which he decides to keep for now.  Ridley also notices that it looks like some of the bodies were killed by others, while some killed themselves - perhaps cursed.
Fungus steps around the light to look at the cave.  He sees it goes down about 30’ and bends.  He sees that they are other dead bodies along the path.  Some of the corpses are long dead. The party continues down the path.  Relys sends her familiar, the spider Hopper, ahead and Relys jumps on Fungus’ back to carry her so she can see through Hopper’s eyes.  As the group proceeds, they notice a corpse move–something that Hopper didn’t trigger.  Hopper notices daylight at the end of the tunnel while scouting ahead, but Relys opens her eyes to deal with the moving corpse.
It’s Initiative – Combat vs. the Dead
* Round One:  Relys casts a spell (Starry Wisp) and misses.  Cerul casts a spell (Sacred Flame) and hits. Ridley climbs the walls and comes down on the other side of the zombie and attacks it and hits. Affy hits once with her greataxe.  Fungus hits it with Poison Spray, but it bubbles off and doesn’t damage him. The zombie attacks Ridley and hits him twice, but Affy reduces some of the damage a little bit.
* Round Two:  Relys casts the spell again and hits.  Cerul casts the spell again and hits. Ridley tries to hit the zombie with his shortsword and hits, then hits again, then with a dagger and hits again. Affy takes his head off with her axe, but the creature is still moving so she hits it again and then stomps on its head.  Fungus also hits it. It misses both Affy and Ridley.
* Round Three:  Relys misses with her spell, but Cerul casts a spell and takes it down forever.
After the fight, Cerul heals Ridley.  There is nothing of value on the dead body.  
The Ravine and a Blocked Path
The party moves forward and notices that the tunnel slopes upward and opens to the outside, to a large ravine.  Hopper continues forward and sees that the cliff walls from the ravine scale up 30-50’ in height.  The path for the group is blocked by boulders and rocks, from which appendages are seen beneath.  This rubble pile is about 10’ high.  The party doesn’t see any evidence that this was a landslide - perhaps it is the work of giants?  
The party decides to climb up.  As Fungus jumps ahead and climbs, the pile shifts a little as everyone hears bones break.  Ridley notices some gloves on one of the dead - making him think this is a recent death.  Each glove has a hidden pocket, where Ridley finds thieves’ tools in one and 2 platinum pieces in the other. Ridley gives the money to Cerul for safe keeping and keeps the gloves and tools.  Affy asks Fungus what he sees from the top of the pile.  He sees a giant coming around the bend. 
It’s Initiative – Combat vs. the Zombie Giant
* Round One:  Fungus casts doomtide - which makes a sphere of inky, black fog appear that will move toward the giant and cause damage.  He yells to us to not enter the darkness. The giant moves up, but can’t see Fungus (and sounds like a zombie), then turns to walk away.  Fungus shouts “big fella over here” in giant and sees him turn around to face the group again.  Relys casts wild shape and becomes a giant spider and climbs up the 50’ cliff. Cerul readies sacred flame and climbs up the pile.  Affy climbs up the pile and pulls out her greatsword.  Ridley dashes and climbs up to the top of the cliff and kneels for cover after he spots the giant and vice versa.
* Round Two:  the sphere moves forward toward the giant and Fungus casts a spell at the giant and hits (sorcerer’s burst).  The giant blindly throws a boulder at Fungus and hits, as Fungus shouts “come and get me.”  The giant moves into the darkness and takes damage. Relys moves up to the top of the cliff and moves forward and casts a spell and hits him - since she can now see him from the top of the cliff.  Cerul moves down to Fungus and helps him up.  Affy “flies” up to the top of the cliff and throws her trident at him and knocks him prone and tells the others he is prone.  Ridley stealths.
* Round Three:  the sphere moves forward a little.  Fungus uses the same spell to hit him and damages him. The giant stands up and moves forward and tries to find a boulder to throw at me and can’t. He spews toxic waste at the group and misses.  He then takes more damage from the fog.  Relys casts moonbeam on the giant and he takes damage. Cerul blindly casts a firebolt in the giant’s direction and hits. Affy shoots her bow and misses, then crits, slowing him.  Ridley shoots him and hits twice. 
* Round Four:  the sphere moves forward.  Fungus moves to the edge of the blackness and uses the same spell and hits, killing him with lightning in the face.  He then drops the spell and the blackness goes away.  
After combat, Relys drops the moonbeam.  Affy drops down to pick up her trident, one arrow and one of Ridley’s bolts. Fungus sits down and meditates for a minute.  Cerul checks out the body and sees it was a stone giant.
The Black Structure
The party moves forward and snow falls a little heavier. The ravine opens up and reveals a black expanse of black stone walls and towers that look like they have burst up from the ground.  There seems to be a fortress/temple that is made of black onyx stone or something - stone that seems to absorb the light.  There is an archway carved into the wall, made with precise masonry work.  There are geometric patterns carved on the stones. The party notices double iron doors in the archway inset 5’.  There are words above the archway–in runic fashion–that the party can’t read from this distance.  Noticing no immediate threats, the party heads up to the archway.
Affy and Ridley think the runes are an ancient use of the common tongue and it says “Oath to the Great One, Torec.”  The party knows that Torec was the old, elemental god.  The temple could have been here a long time, but might have been revealed with the Awakening.  Ridley tries to check the doors and doesn’t see anything.  Relys asks Hopper to check the keyhole and Hopper can’t get through, so he crawls back out.  Fungus thinks there is more to these doors than meets the eye–there is magic here–and that magic means might help.   Affy doesn’t see any other entrance.  Ridley notices no traps on the door. Fungus tries to pick the lock and it won’t budge.  Affy thinks maybe trying something elemental might help.  Fungus tries a sorcerer’s burst with cold damage to the door and the door absorbs it…and the door starts to crack and there is sound of iron against stone as the doors open.  As the doors part, the party sees stone tiles ahead covered with geometric patterns.  Inside is a huge entry wall with towering pillars. It is dark inside, and the ceiling is high. 
– The End of the Session –
