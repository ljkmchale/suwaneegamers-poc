# Session 28 - Caelora has Issues

Source: raw/a-new-adventure-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1tZbBbjOzgCiUSmUuepCE_qpsuOJpawrAia0nxYHTtjA/edit?usp=sharing

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: May 21, 2026
In-game Date:  12th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Borealian Loop
That morning in Darafee
We discuss what we know and what has happened recently.  Things began to turn strange when we discovered an artifact, the Feywoven Glyphic, beneath the Broken Citadel.  We continued on through the land of elves, the Borealian Sway.  When we arrived in O’naren, we learned that there was an underground network trying to undermine the elven leaders, called the  Rel’Udomina.  We spent time rooting out their evil and gained the favor of elven King Highvale in Qal’dynn.  Upon exploring a dangerous cave near the Abbey of Light, we learned of a structure known as the Blackrock Crucible that belonged to a group of cultists called the Keepers that Bind.  These cultists learned a group of druids in the gnome village of Iparos performed a ritual called the Weaving Night that went terribly wrong and opened a rift to the Shadowfell.  Something dark came through that rift and destroyed Iparos and took off somewhere across Myrdae.  Being that the cultists worshipped a demigod, named Malgri’n, which was known to inhabit the Shadowfell, the cultists used their Anchoring Gate to create a portal atop the rift so they could enter the Shadowfell.  The group was able to leverage the rift to use the Feywoven Glyphic artifact to release an imprisoned fey, Shoddy Nell.  Shoddy Nell was able to return to the Feywild as Nelindra the Infused.  
We wake up and head outside. The Executioner asks Ridley why he has a prisoner, which is discussed only briefly.  Ridley decides to let her ride her horse next to him.  Pa says that when we get to Caelora he’d like to have a couple of days rest there, as the Executioner says he will hire Realmwardens to go ahead and escort him to Basctdelm.  
And so, the party is following a lead for Affy in Basctdelm to find her father.  Upon that road, they have been able to learn more about the person that Ridley is looking for, although he recently got away.  
Back on the Road
On the road, Kaizo knows that there is a stop after Caelora called Adore, which  is a temple of elven monks.  Kaizo also knows the Southern Arch was built a long time ago by giants - huge stones, long bridge.  Cerul may have knowledge about that, too.  We head down the road - same formation.  Pa, tiefling, Executioner wagons. Next stop is Whisperpine (Kaizo knows this is just a campsite).  We pass some travelers, then some Realmwardens approach and head to the prisoner wagon and talk to the Executioner.  One is hired to join the party.  Fungus goes back to talk to him. His name is Realmwarden Eversoul (first name Hagmer in elvish). Affy asks him about Whisperpine and he comments about Illuminot (when Tudara is visible and Endolar is full).
At campsite Whisperpine
We make it to Whisperpine. There are many evergreens, common to Halesworth. We set up camp as dark is falling. We hear rustling to the side, through the evergreens.  Fungus and Affy go check it out. They see a clearing over a hill and vegetation is shorter over the hill, cloudless night.  They see an individual who is laid back against a big tree.  The noise was him snapping logs to get comfortable.  He is big for a human, midnight blue leathers trimmed in silver, a very nice spear next to the tree. He glances our way and smiles and he has tusks, well groomed, dark hair.  Fungus and Affy talk to him - he wants to watch the sunset and moon show from here and invites the two to join.  Kulv Farbreak makes sure Fungus knows that there is plenty of room for Affy, but not so much for him.  Conversation about whereabouts.  He mentions that Caelora is the oldest elven city in Myrdae - the origin of his spear.  He is originally from a clan in the Crimson March, but his family is from the original Mahruud - they are trying to rebrand and rebuild themselves. He talks to us about our adventures and accomplishments to date.  We ask what he does - lots of travel, adventuring. Fungus asks him to join us.   He declines and then Fungus dips back to camp, leaving Affy and him alone. 
Back at camp, Ridley feeds his captive a goodberry.  Relys talks about gagging the prisoner.  Kaizo notices that RIdley is treating her well.  Ridley asks if she is part of the Rel’Udomina - and she looks like she has no idea what he is talking about.  She looks at Relys for help.  Ridley explains that the prisoner who escaped was her mentor and that she has information about him.  RIdley has a goal to achieve and she is a tool to make that happen.  Relys says that unless she has committed a crime she shouldn’t be gagged and bound.  He takes the gag out and she says that she has no spells prepared other than Misty Step.  She says that she isn’t here to hurt Ridley, but is here to tell him that Ridley is wrong - and will ultimately have to tell the ones who hired Ridley that they are wrong. Kaizo weighs in here and there.  Cerul is off studying. The others in the caravan are nearby but not paying attention to this conversation. Relys looks and sees a reflection of light near the woods, a little flicker, over Ridley’s shoulder. Relys excuses herself to go potty, she tries to find a good space to squat down and do what needs to be done. She's like 30ish feet away from the group, and she whispers to the darkness “Oi, if ya here to help da lady, im on ya side” (in a high pitched voice as she normally does) She thinks it’s the wizard without any further thoughts. Relys fails a charisma save against some force and loses control of herself.  She is incapacitated condition.  When she returns a few minutes later she walks around and glances at everyone very slowly and makes a complete circle around the fire.  After she walks the circle, there is a chill. Kaizo thinks something is up, but can’t put his finger on it.   Relys feels a chill and hears in her mind - “avenge me.” Kaizo and Ridley see Relys stumble and catch herself and she comes back to herself.  Ridley asks her if going to the bathroom hit her that hard.   She feels like (with her nat 20 insight roll) she was possessed - like maybe an ancestor. She asks Ridley to walk back into the woods with her to investigate.  Once they leave, Fungus takes the gag out of the captive’s mouth and unlocks the manacles.  He tells her she has to stay.
Meanwhile, Affy and Kulv are sitting and watching the moons.  Kulv shares stories about his travels. He seems a little envious of the fame of our group. He talks about Sylunara.  His eyes mirror the moons, one red and one blue.  She comments on this and he says that he has followed Sylunara for a long time and has grown more fond of that goddess over time.   He says he is “moontouched” - it is where his abilities come from  - his ability to protect himself and in things “arcane.” He then grabs the spear and mumbles words and vines wrap around the spear. Affy comments that his abilities seem like Fungus’ abilities.   She then shows her psi-leap by flying 60’ away - and says that she doesn’t fully understand her powers.  She hangs for a little longer and then wishes him well and returns to camp.
Meanwhile, Ridley and Relys are in the woods trying to figure out who or what may have possessed Relys.  They can’t find anything.  They notice Affy flying.  Relys turns into an owl and Ridley turns into a bat and the both fly up to check things out.  They speak to each other as Relys casts speak with animals.  Ridley sees Affy walking away from the half orc.  He then sees the orc take the spear, twist it and pull it apart and take half of it and stick it into the ground and then small orbs, the color of the moons, circling his staff.  He points this out and the two swoop down to land on the tree near that half orc.  They notice him reattach his spear and then set out his bedroll and go to bed. They decide to go back to camp. 
Meanwhile, Cerul is off studying and meditating.   Kaizo pulls out his pipe and lights it up and looks up at the captive, trying to determine if she is telling the truth on why she is there and also if she is a threat to Ridley or the party overall.  He doesn’t think she is a threat to the party, but she could be a threat one-on-one.  He also thinks she is being truthful. He offers her some pipe, but she declines.  He asks her what her story is.  She says she would like to see that Heildove is okay and get the target off his back.  Kaizo comments that there is love between Ridley and the captive.  He asks her where she is from - she is from Palason. She met Heildove in Qal’dynn a few harmons ago - he needed to share his knowledge and took her under his wing.  He won’t give me the details of everything, but I know that the people coming after him want him dead and he is wrongly accused.  Kaizo senses that she is scared that she is going to die and comments as such.  She asks if he knows the Rulendors - he does not.  She says that she fears for her life since they want Heildove and he is not here, Ridley does not have him.  Kaizo is trying to figure out if he can protect her and he thinks that she thinks that he cannot.  Fungus tells her that she can leave if she wants - Kaizo says that he can’t let her go on his watch (his promise to Ridley).  
Ridley and Relys fly over to where Relys was originally possessed.  Ridley is trying to find out if his bat-sense helps him figure out what is going on. He notices something at the edge of his 60’ range triangulated with the campsite and they fly toward that. When they get within 10’, they see something - and must make wisdom saving throws (and both do).  It turns and looks at them - it is a ghost of a forest gnome and releases a terrible visage (which they save on).  Relys lands and shifts back “I’m a friend - what happened to you?”  It looks at her knowingly - it is one of the elder druids from her home that she recognizes - she says “Elder, do you remember me?”  He is hesitant to talk.  He turns to face north and then says “I just hope that your path doesn’t lead you too far astray,” and starts to go north.  She senses that he came specifically to find her to ask her to avenge their village.  She thinks that the spirit is not going to leave her alone, but is also not going to get in the way.  It is clinging to her…wherever she goes, it will go. Until she is able to put it to rest, it cannot rest. Ridley says to make sure that vengeance doesn’t consume her soul. They discuss killing the creature that killed her village…as they make their way back to the campsite.
Meanwhile, Kaizo starts to tell the captive about his backstory.  He then asks her why she is important to Ridley.  Ridley and Relys come back.  Affy asks Fungus if she noticed Kulv’s eyes and explains why.  Then asks Relys about what is going on with her.  Group talks about these things. Affy comments that the captive is no longer bound and gagged.  Discussion about Ridley being the “jury and executioner” and that she is not the target of his bounty, but that he is holding her, hoping that the wizard will show up.  She is the “bait.”  Affy calls for Cerul…and asks if he can help send a message to the wizard.  Fungus starts to cast a spell around the fire and calms the emotions of everyone around the fire. RIdley tells Cerul what to say.  The group advises that Cerul make it a friendlier message.  He does.
Discussion about how much Ridley is making on this bounty. He is making no money.  Cerul gets a message back from the wizard.  He says he cannot get there that fast…and that he will avenge his apprentice.  We go to bed.  Regular watches - no issues.
The morning of the 13th of Paramor, 1246
The next morning we wake and decide to head to Caelora. Ridley tells us his plans - he was going to secure her in the town then reveal to the Rulendor family that he has a valuable asset that he is holding.  We discuss the plans as we walk. Ridley describes himself as a lapdog of that family…and Relys asks if he wants to do that forever.  He says that he wants to retire from that….
In Caelora
We get into mid-afternoon and we see Caelora on the horizon. Ridley stops outside the city to dye his hair white.  This is a magnificent terraced town.  We come to the stables first - the Silver Hoofyard.   Pa says “let’s take 3 days and meet here on the morning of the third day at the Silver Hoofyard.”  The group agrees.  Realmwardens talk about the dead guards and about having the Executioner and prisoners move on the next day.  We ask about boarding the animals here and do.  We see a procession of Realmwardens approaching from another direction.  We notice a couple of them have banners - Dark Blue and Crimson banners (Crimson group is larger).  The Realmwardens are guiding two horses that have bodies laid over them. The Dark Blue group heads towards the Rulendors.  One notices Ridley and motions Ridley over. Ridley knows him as Urovil Rulendor.  He is a cousin of Nanise Rulendor.  “Welcome back” he says to Ridley.  He is covered head to toe with fancy elven clothing.  Ridley notices a burn scar on his face that he’s never noticed before.  “It is a pleasure to see you.”  Ridley: My apologies sir, you are catching me before I have a chance to dress up.”  Urovil: Hopefully you have good news?  RIdley: Yes - I do.  Urovil: That’s great news because I have had a bad day.  It would appear as though the Airithels had a little bit of a hunting accident.  Ridley says, “I would like to freshen up and formally present myself to the house.”  Urovil: When can we expect you?  They agree on tomorrow - for an early lunch.  Then he walks back and hands off his horse and goes on his way. 
The group decides to go to the trade terrace. Discussion of where to stay the night.  Ridley suggests the Gilded Pigeon rooming house.  Fungus pushes back since we have money.  Ridley explains that particularly Fungus and Affy will have a hard time fitting in.  Affy takes her scarf and fashions it around her head to somewhat disguise herself.  In response, the stablehand says that we don’t need to worry.  She tells us of the noble houses:  the Rulendors, the Airithels, and the Venathrials.  When Affy asks a follow up question, she changes the subject and walks away.  Ridley explains about the politics of the city - elves rule, money rules, noble houses don’t mix.  Ridley and Relys talk rats and Affy mentions that she had a pet rat (Smarmy) and grew up with goblins. 
Relys and Ridley take the captive to the Gilded Pigeon.
The rest of the group goes to the Forge, then the Daefar Terrace.  Something doesn’t sit right with Cerul - his gut is turning.  He feels sick.  As he follows the trail from where the horses carrying the dead bodies came from, there is something evil (opposite the light) that he feels.   They get to the Cast of the Knowing Hand - a 4-quadrant area (artisan, jewelcrafting, armorsmith, bladesmith) - and there is a centered courtyard with wares displayed. Everything is of high quality.  Cerul talks to the armorer and is looking for a shield of the enchanted variety.  She says that she will have to see if she can find something for him - she has nothing at the ready - but says to come back tomorrow. 
MAGIC SHIELD AND PRICE FOR A SMALL +1 LIGHT HAMMER
Kaizo spent 110gp to send 100gp back to the Sanctuary.
Group goes to the Daefar Terrace, passing and observing the bathhouses on the way. Kaizo is particularly observing the glass-roofed buildings, herbs, etc. - inspiration for the Sanctuary.  He sees a woman with vine tattoos, mossy colored hair wood elf and she says to Kaizo “welcome to Dawnpetal.”  He then asks if she has Nightbud and then goes into his pitch about his herbs and what he is spreading.  She misunderstands Kaizo’s intentions and calls her brother to come over.  He immediately intercepts the conversation and doesn’t give Kaizo a chance to explain…but he finally does and goes through his pitch again. But he is unable to convince them of his intentions.  He asks for their “behind the counter” best and they say they don’t do that here.  Affy doesn’t take kindly to their rebuff and tries to help Kaizo and gets mad and leaves. Kaizo decides to help a lesser vendor in the poorer sections of town since they don’t believe that he has Nightbud. 
Cerul mentions that he sensed something bad and noted they headed to House Rulendor. He doesn’t act on it for now, but keeps it in mind. 
Kaizo looks for trade space and can find it and then decides to find Ridley at the Pigeon to inquire about finding another herbalist. 
As Ridley, Relys, and Eliyena walk, they go around the top of the Daefar Terrace to head down to the quarters and hear someone trying to catch them, huffing and puffing. Before they catch them, the rest of the group is coming out of the herb shops and Affy catches a familiar person walking around the other side of the pool - it is Kulv.  Ridley turns and sees Nanise:  she says:  “I’m glad I caught you before tomorrow.”  Ridley introduces Relys to her.  Ridley says he was expecting to see her tomorrow.  She grabs a pouch and hands it to Ridley, saying:  “I’d like to relieve you of your duties and I will tell him tomorrow that you were unable to make it to lunch.”  And she is backpedaling…and he is trying to get to the bottom of what is going on, but can’t.
– The End of the Session –
