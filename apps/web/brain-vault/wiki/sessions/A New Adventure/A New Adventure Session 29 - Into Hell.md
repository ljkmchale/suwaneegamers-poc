# Session 29 - Into Hell

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: June 18, 2026
In-game Date:  13th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Caelora
Ridley & Nanise
Ridley, Relys, and Eliyena are walking.  Eliyena says to Ridley “is she the good one?”  He says I don’t know and runs after Nanise.  Eliyena asks Relys what that is about and Relys tells her hopefully it means that he no longer works for them.  Meanwhile, Ridley catches up and Nanise tells him that she cannot speak to him openly here and that she won’t meet at his attic.  They agree to meet at the Wayfarer’s Rest in two hours for dinner. 
Affy & Kulv
Affy starts toward Kulv and yells his name.  He looks back at her, grinning.  He hoped our paths would cross and she replies “of course they would - you knew where I’d be.”  After a brief discussion, they agree to meet at an overlook on the far side of the Daefar Terrace to watch the moons rise. 
Fungus & Cerul visit Vossel’s Oddities & Essences
Fungus and Cerul make their way to the Reverie to get washed.  Fungus decides to drop some coin buying random things.  He likes the Oddities store - it draws him in.  Smelling dried parchment and herbs, he catches alchemical scents.  He sees a half elven female, older, who is moving stuff on shelves and he offers to help her.  As he does, he notices a bottle.  She declines the help, but he asks for help with the bottle - it has a faded handwritten label that looks familiar and nostalgic. Cerul is looking around and he sees the woman walk up to Fungus.  She reaches up and snaps Fungus out of his trance “that is one of the old stock, most people don’t look at it twice.”  This looks like scented oil of some type that might be spread around to make wood and other things smell good.  Given how old this is no one wanted to spend the 10gp that Lyn’don wanted for it.  Fungus - “oh, you know Lyn’don?”  They talk about him….Fungus says he knows him - is not his biological father, but is close.  Fungus says I haven’t seen him - I presume he was dead.  Then she calls Fungus by his real name and he nods and she walks off and says I’ll be back.  Cerul - hearing this - asks Fungus who Lyn’don is and he says he was someone who took care of him when he was young.  While they wait, they look for additional bottles.  She comes from a back room holding a big book.  Fungus says - how do you know me?  And she said that he spoke of you as he was training me.  He gave me this book, but based on what he told me, I think it would be better for you to have it.  I don’t think there are any other Teldos.  And Fungus says “no - it is rather unusual.”  Looking at the book, he realizes this is a book that was used to teach him when he was young.  He doesn’t remember much.  She says - there is something in the back and she pulls out pages and hands them to Fungus.  He asks when she saw him last - he’s trying to figure out if he is dead.  She says that she thinks he is alive.  The last time he was here and he left that book, this bottle, and a few more - then headed to Basctdelm, he said he was looking for Maester Sylvandor.  He asks if she has more time to talk about him.  He decides to hang out and ask questions while she works. Cerul looks on as the torn pages, as Fungus continues to talk.  Cerul notices that the torn pages have markings that give off a soft golden glow before he closed the book back up…and points it out to Fungus.  They open the book and see the light.  As Cerul gets closer to the pages, the light gets brighter, then dimmer as Fungus pulls it away. They don’t recognize the markings - think this is in a language they don’t understand.  Fungus casts Comprehend Languages spell to read the text.  He reads the words and they start to come into view. It is about Cerul based on the way the light responds to Cerul:


“This mark was on the blanket he was wrapped in when I found him. Sylvandor called it ‘the last light of Pekanon.’ I don’t know what it means, but it glows faintly when the boy is near.  If something happens to me, he needs to know where he came from.”
The book itself doesn’t have anything to do with Cerul.  This is more of a journal of Lyn’don’s.  But those torn pages refer to Cerul and the mark glows because of him.  The mark seems familiar to Cerul, but he can’t place it.  He asks Lira if she knows of the mark - she says she has never seen it glow.  Clearly, Cerul and Fungus need to follow up with Sylvandor and/or Lyn’don at Basctdelm.  Cerul mentions that Uspar said that there is something unresolved at Pekanon…which is where Cerul is from. 
Fungus continues to shop as he talks to the shopkeeper, Lira Vossel.  He wants to find a healing potion and trade something for it - maybe his potion of fire breath (which is worth 200gp).  She finds a Greater Healing potion and trades for it.  She says that she hopes Fungus finds him and to tell him hello - and he says he will try to bring him back.
Warmth of the Lantern Stone 
Relys and Eliyena speak, waiting for Ridley to return.  She feels warmth in her pouch and investigates.  She feels the Lantern Stone - it is glowing warm, giving off a dim light.  She has never noticed the heat.  It appears to be yellow.  She feels that this is trying to tell her something but isn’t sure what.  This thing is more than a mood stone - it seems to be something else.  Ridley returns and sees her eyeing the stone. They talk about it and Ridley thinks that Caelora is a magical place and is the oldest elven town…so maybe the magic here is triggering something, but he isn’t sure what.  Perhaps someone who is from here might be able to help figure this out. He thinks the Airithels (nobles) might be able to help or someone in the Terrace of the Eternal Song. He offers to go with her, but that it will have to wait until tomorrow.  
What to do with Eliyena
He says that he needs to put Eliyena in the attic, but they discuss.  He checks the pouch he got (250gp).  Discussion continues about why Eliyena needs to be in the attic.  Ridley says that something else is going on and he wants to get to the bottom of it. And that because he is concerned for her safety he wants her to stay there.  Relys offers to keep Eliyena with her and Ridley says that he doesn’t trust her. They continue to discuss the attic - pros and cons. Relys calls Ridley an asshole and says he is being cruel to Eliyena.  He concedes that Eliyena can stay with Relys.  And then says that Relys should take Eliyena out and keep her company while he is having dinner.  She tells Ridley that she and Eliyena are going to the bathhouse. Ridley then offers for them to come with him…they agree to go and sit nearby and get a drink…if Ridley pays with his new coin.  Relys also says she is going to get them a nice room with a bed. Eliyena asks for her arcane focus and Ridley declines.  They then discuss the attic and Ridley says he fixed it up after he broke in and took it over. But either way, the gals will stay at the Inn.
Wayfarer’s Rest – Kaizo & the Stranger
Kaizo goes to the Wayfarer’s Rest - he’s the first to arrive.  It is very nice and very old, but well kept…warm and welcoming light. One person gets Kaizo’s attention because he is dressed like Kaizo and leaning over the bar, perhaps drunk.  Kaizo walks over and orders a drink, trying to catch what the guy is mumbling.  He can’t catch much, but the guy smells of booze.  He thinks this guy was a warrior of mercy.  He is a dark-haired woof elf. The guys sighs and looks at Kaizo - you carry yourself like one of them.  Kaizo: one of whom?  Him: One of those from Adore.  Kaizo: What does that mean? Monks go to the Abbey of Light or to Adore.  Most of the Warriors of Mercy go to Adore.  Kaizo:  I have spent some time there and you look like you have too, long ago.  Kaizo:  so what has you in such despair? Him:  I’m out of drink….and they don’t let me back in there anymore.  Discussion about ranks between the two - the guy asks if Kaizo was involved with the politics there and Kaizo says no.  Guy - they forced me out.  I was only there a decade.  I don’t think it is right that they forced me out.  Kaizo:  why did they?  Him:  I questioned them - just like the guy who was here asking for herbs, named Haleth. (Kaizo doesn’t know the name).  Kaizo:  what type of herbs?  Him:  I think he was looking for answers more than herbs…he was asking too many questions, mostly about me (he was one of his students). Kaizo tells him we will be passing through Adore - you know where Haleth is?  Him:  He is missing now - has been missing for a stretch or so.  More discussion of why he was kicked out…once Kaizo orders him another drink.  He offers that he thought they were changing their ways, stances, routines, practices, etc. - everything was off.  But I don’t want to drag you into this.  Maybe we can catch up later.  Kaizo - where are you staying?  Him:  I’ll be here. He then plops his head down.  Kaizo asks the inn keeper if there is a room.  They are 8sp/night (2gp for wealthy; 4gp for aristocratic).  He offers to get the man a room for 2 nights on him…and pays the barkeep 3gp and tells him to “keep him watered” for the next couple of days.  Kaizo wants to keep the man there for a couple of days until the group leaves.
Wayfarer’s Rest – Everyone is there
Everyone heads to the Wayfarer’s Rest, except Ridley.  We all sit at a table.  Ridley and Nanise get their own table. Affy tells Fungus and the group about Kulv.  Fungus agrees to follow later and try to listen, stealthily. Relys implies maybe there is something romantic, but Affy talks about how she has never liked her “orc-ish” looks, wishes she were more human.  But won’t judge the guy based on his looks…yet.  Kaizo is impressed by what Affy shares.  Everyone orders a meal (5/sp each). Kaizo asks why Ridley is sitting over there with a lady.  Eliyena and Relys answer. Kaizo implies that Eliyena and Ridley have something going on…but Eliyena denies this. Cerul pulls out the paper with the mark and it glows.  The group asks what this is.  Relys pulls out her glowing stone.  They both try to explain where/why/and how their items glow.  Relys says she will find out tomorrow hopefully.  Cerul explains the tie of him to Fungus. They then talk of their need to go to Basctdelm. They ask Fungus/Teldo about Lyn’don. He tells of how he ran away when a house burned down when he was younger and never saw Lyn’don again. Fungus then shows his wares - a large chest he bought, the aromatic oil, etc.  Kaizo shares about his new monk friend from the bar…and how we need to go to Adore because there is strange stuff afoot there. 
Ridley Speaks with Nanise
Just before Nanise gets to the table, Ridley tries to get the best bottle for his table (25gp).  He then sits and Nanise approaches the dark corner table. She is hooded. The group watches from across the room.  Ridley asks what is up.  She says that she has heard rumors of all of Ridley’s doings, like the Outlanders of Highvale, etc.  She says that she doesn’t want to hold him back.  Ridley thinks she is holding something back.  He says for her not to beat around the bush…she doesn’t need to hide anything from him.  She says that she shouldn’t speak with him, because it could be dire.  She says he deserves the truth:  house Rulendor has changed.  Urovil is not the man he was.  He has changed.  She says she doesn’t know what he is involved with but he spends a lot of time in the basement.  Ridley comments about the burn mark.  She says it is not old.  She reaches into a pouch and presses something into his hand “if you really are a hero - this will open a hidden entrance into our cellar (this is a key).  Go there and don’t confront him alone.  I don’t want you to kill him, I want to know what is going on with the House.  I would like to restore it.  Just know that he is dangerous and he has great influence over my father.”  Ridley agrees to do this as a favor to her.  They spend some time with small talk.  She says that she is scared.  She seems hopeful as she looks over to the party.  He confirms that she no longer wants him to go after the wizard…she says that he was framed and she released him from that duty.  He then tells her about Eliyena and how he was going to use her as leverage.  She replies that Ridley has become dark…and he reminds her of the things that she has paid him to do.  She says not to remind her - that she is glad that he has become a hero.  He is humble about it, but she says that she has faith in him and for him to stop chasing river rats.  She questions why he is hanging out with a half orc and he replies that all tools are useful.  He offers for Nanise to take the bottle, but declines.  She thanks him and says “may the jackal be wild” and leaves. 
Kaizo watches Eliyena to see if she is jealous that he is paying such attention to Nanise.  He realizes that Eliyena is very deceptive. Ridley then walks over to the group.  He briefs us - tells us that he is relieved of duty and has been asked a favor before we leave. He tells us what the favor is.  Then Cerul comments how he sensed evil from that man when we first arrived in town.  The group agrees to visit the cellar tomorrow night - Ridley will scout the route tonight (as a bat).  Then he tells Eliyena that she is free to go and offers her wine.  Cerul mentions that he will put her up for the night. 
Affy leaves, with Fungus trailing, to meet Kulv.  As Ridley and Relys scout together.  Affy approaches the  meeting spot and he is sitting there.  Kulv is looking at the moons.  He pulls out a leather casing around a glass jar and a cork and 2 cups. This is a nice steaming tea.  He says, let’s walk. And they do. Fungus sees them in the distance. He tries to get closer without being spotted. He notices a couple of areas where they walk and he’s holding a cup up and he reaches Affy’s back to guide her. He describes the moons as “honest.” He guides her to a bench and they sit and talk about nothing. Then they talk about the moons. I haven’t been honest with you, Affy.  I like you and I serve a greater power - Selbranan the Moon touched.  In all honesty, I’m a bit torn now that we have spent time together, but he’s been watching my giant friend.  He knows of the powers that binds him.  His pact is not a gift, it is a chain.  I think Selbranan (fey lord of the moons - he had a conflict with Shoddy Nel…had her excommunicated) can offer Fungus something far greater.  So why talk to me, says Affy.  You know him and he can help you, too.  How can he help me, she asks.  He knows what you seek.  So you know?  He knows about your father. He can help you.  You can help get Fungus to break the bond with the hag and turn to Selbranan and he will help you.  Selbranan recognizes the potential that Fungus has.  Can he meet with him, she asks?  Perhaps.  Think on it…I’ll be around.  He leaves and Affy looks for Fungus but doesn’t see him so she returns to the inn.
Meanwhile Ridley and Relys fly to the house - Ridley knows the way. They find the entrance to the cellar.  They see some Realmwardens facing outside, see a couple of sconces that give off dim light.  See a couple of doors that lead to a study and a kitchen.  Then they fly around to check for hidden traps or anything else dangerous - they see nothing.  They see no lights inside the cellar.  Might see someone in the kitchen or study, but it is late.  Think that if the guards can be distracted, they could get into the cellar.  The two think that tonight might be a good night to go…and fly back to the inn to rally the troops.
Affy sees Fungus. She tells him what happened. Discussion about how Selbranan tried to stop Fungus from helping Shoddy Nell. Affy convinces Fungus to at least talk to Kulv. She asks Fungus about the last time he heard from his pact leader - it has been a while. Discussion then about how the moon lord might know something about Cerul. 
Ridley and Relys return and he asks where Eliyena is - she went to bed. They tell what they discovered. Ridley goes to check on Eliyena; Kaizo says he is going to relieve himself and follows Ridley; Fungus is hesitant to go as Affy, Cerul, and Relys talk about going. Ridley goes into the room where Eliyena is…and sees her sit up. She says “what do you want?”  He pulls out the wine bottle and offers some to her.  He looks around the room for someone else and sees no one.  Kaizo pees in a corner at the end of the hall when he thinks Ridley saw him. He then goes by the room to see Eliyena.  She says “what again - but then says oh, it is you.”  He says it is Master Kaizo and she asks if he has glasses for the wine.  She asks if he is jealous that Ridley might be courting him. He says that she is creatively allergic to the truth and that she is free to go…and says that she should be gone when he comes back.  She asks if this is a threat and he says yes.  She says she will consider that and gives him the bottle and pushes him into the hall.  He goes back to the group.
Fungus questions if we should all go. We agree to go. Party gets on the outskirts of the estate.  Fungus goes on record that this is a bad idea.  Ridley and Relys get rats at the sewers and speak with them. They bargain with rations and then to go to the guards at the gate and annoy them. The rats agree to anarchy. A swarm of rats go towards the guards. The guards kill the rats. We sneak in (good wise). We go to the cellar door. The little queen of the rats (Relys) casts detect magic and she senses strong conjuration magic on the other side of the cellar door. We discuss for a moment and then open the cellar door. 
There are narrow stairs.  We go up single file.  The air is unnaturally heavy and cold.  Smell stagnant water and sulfur.  Come into a room with a waist-high marble basin.  Water in the basin.  Cerul is nauseous - it is almost smothering.  He lights up his lightbringer sword as a reaction.  When he does, we see the man with a fiendish winged creature that hisses on his shoulder.  He says “Ridley - I had hoped we could speak as civilized men.  But now that you know the truth - now that you have seen what is under my house and what I have turned to - join me to help bring the house up to its full power.  You will never have to be a hired hand.  Heildove would have seen us to ruin.”  Ridley says “your cousin is hot and it was easy for me to help her.”  He says - she is weak and I am strong.  What do you hope to accomplish?  Ridley says - I was asked to find out what is going on here. He then reaches for the water and starts to touch it, as Ridley hits the imp with a crossbow bolt.  As the man touches the pool, he turns an icy blue and disappears.  The imp remains.
IT IS INITIATIVE – Combat vs. Imp
* ROUND ONE:  Relys starry whips the imp and hits.  He drops into the ground and turns into a rat and looks helpless. Affy rushes forward and swings at the rat and misses, then reaches down and picks it up, grappling it.  Cerul runs forward and casts fairy fire to light up the room, but doesn’t see him.  Kaizo runs into the room and goes up to the door and stands in front of it and holds his action to attack should anyone attack.  Ridley walks over and says to the rat in infernal “I want you to tell me everything that is going on here.”  He has speak with animals going.  “Oh - he’s not here.  Smell it - smell it.”  RIdley - that is a portal to the 9 hells?  “Join us, join us.  Allow me to go get him.  We will offer you something.”  Only Relys understood the rat-speak.  Fungus walks in and asks what’s going on?  Does nothing.  
* ROUND TWO:  Relys walks in and the rat says “please rescue me” to her.  She says “I know what you are.”  She walks to the edge of the basin and investigates the basin, where she senses the magic.  It is dark and murky and smells of sulfur.  The water is not of this world.  She sniffs and says “that is foul.” The rat shifts into a huge hairy spider and wraps around Affy.  And says “please let me go.”  He releases himself from Affy and he runs. Affy tries to attack and misses. He runs away and makes it to the basin and jumps in and we are out of combat. 
Cerul checks the basin.  This water is from the Nine Hells.  Fungus wants to capture some of this water in a vial. Affy warns him not to do it, but he does, touches the water and disappears.  RIdley and Affy immediately follow.  Kaizo, Relys, and Cerul pause….then Cerul follows, gritting his teeth.  Relys follows.  Kaizo lights his pipe, takes a deep puff, and follows.
The group is pulled through darkness, freezing cold, and slams down in a rectangular chamber.  The air is so cold that it burns.  There is a low moan.  Blue light glows from the ice.  The walls are frozen ice that include victims who were entombed while alive.  At the far end of a chamber is a doorway.  There is a basin in the room that is bubbling blood.  There is a balcony on one side off the room, there are two doors opposite. As Ridley pops in, he sees Fungus filling a vial with blood and pop away.  He pops back into the other room. Fungus appears as Kaizo is still there.  Then retouches the water and pops back, followed by Kaizo.  The group makes CON saves - and all make it - fighting off the cold.
Ridley starts to walk to a door, slips and falls.  This is difficult terrain.  Affy notices footprints that approach the balcony and more that go to the door.  Ridley tries to walk to the door and falls.  Cerul thinks this is the frozen layer of hell - Stygia.  Cerul announces this. Affy says this is the frozen level of hell.  Fungus walks to the balcony. Ridley walks to the door. The group divides between the door and balcony.  Ridley gets to the door and sees another chamber that is even colder. Chained to the walls are 8 humanoids that are stripped bare and tortured.  Ridley notices that the ice walls are not empty - frozen within are more bodies. To the left one of the bodies twitches…maybe this one is still alive.  There is a door on the other side of this room that is open - to a field of ice. Ridley makes it halfway across the room (full speed).  Affy makes her way (half speed) to the door. Relys moves to the balcony (half speed).  Cerul standing by the basin with Kaizo.  Fungus moves to the balcony (half speed) with Relys.  Relys and Fungus can see all of Stygia in front of them.  Smell of frozen metal and ancient stagnant death.  The balcony is 70-80’ above the River Styx.  They see glaciers, stalagmites that encapsulate creatures, stormy skies with green lightning; green insect-like creatures gliding with grace on the ice; other creatures as well moving around.  They are horror-struck by what they see.  Cerul walks half speed to the door. Kaizo stays at the basin.
Ridley is at the door of the other chamber, as Affy watches from a quarter of the way through the chamber.  When Ridley steps to the open door he must make a CON save and he stands before a wind-swept plateau of blue and black ice.  There are icy stalagmites and twisted and frozen trees everywhere.  There is biting wind with a low, grinding moan.  He sees to the left a passage where the steps go down a cliff that is 90’ from the door.  In the distance he sees figures and shadows moving and flying toward him. Affy notices Ridley disappear.  Relys and Fungus notice two imps pop down on the balcony and say “they’re here” and laugh.  These imps are 15’ from them.  Fungus blasts one of them with poison and hits - it acts like it was hurt, but then flies up and laughs, unharmed.  Relys says to the other - who is expecting us?  It says “well the master…the master is maybe not expecting, but accepts all who come….and points to the wall and says, and we will put you up there (as he points to the ceiling and those frozen in it).  Relys and Fungus back into the room and toward the basin.
Ridley sees that the footprints go to the stairs. Affy asks in his head “where are you going?”  He says I’m going to check something out and come right back. Fungus shouts out “he knows we are here.”  Affy yells who is he…and Fungus says “the master.”  Affy moves further across the room to see if she can see Ridley.  Ridley is moving toward the path to try to follow the footsteps.  Cerul yells “Ridley, I think we got the info we came for.”  Ridley pulls out an acorn and tells a bird to fly over and look around.  He thinks the bird will make it back.  He stays put. Fungus moves closer to the doors to the second room.  Group has quick discussion about going back through and destroying the basin on the other side. 
Ridley hears from the bird that there are people advancing and that someone went into a door at the bottom of 90’ slick stairs.  Ridley thinks that something is coming and is heading his way.  They look like dark shadows that have no problem moving.  These seem to be fiends.  Ridley tells Affy he is on his way back.  Once he makes it into the chamber he goes to the prisoner who seems to be alive.  Ridley falls as he tries to go that way.  Cerul and Affy notice shadows approaching.  Kaizo sees a big hand come over the side of the balcony and start to come over.  He yells “it is time to go - danger coming over the balcony.”  Relys stands next to Kaizo.  Everyone starts to move back to the basin.  Affy sees the person twitch.  She moves over to the twitching person at full speed and pulls Ridley up.  
Meanwhile the creature stands at the balcony with a large spear and the imps say “I told you, I told you.”  Fungus casts a spell (doomtide).  Kaizo holds his action to attack if something attacks.  Cerul stays put in the doorway. He hears Fungus casting a spell and sees that Ridley is going to try to unlock shackles and he notices a dark shadow at the far doorway.
– The End of the Session –
