# HoE Session 13 - Emberheart Tablet

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 13
- Session Title: Emberheart Tablet
- Date Label In Notes: 10.23
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- Arnol brings Aury and Hap a black obsidian medallion on a copper chain marked with a "G," saying it came from the cult's stash and may have been taken from a grave. Hap touches it and the brazier flickers.
- Arnol admits he has been with bad people but says he did not kill anyone or rob graves. Aury counsels him to break a promise if keeping it means breaking vows to Diverra.
- Ainslie and Og discuss the stolen tablet piece, the Cloak dinner, and whether they are Cloak agents or double agents.
- Og's thorn leaf/vine attaches to his wrist, draws blood, and he hears "Alferumn." Ainslie connects this to Axis of Perpetualism / Ascension Tree / circle-of-life ideas.
- Ky'tha is warned by Tessa that the party is too known to be publicly in two opposing groups.
- The party meets at the temple and Ky'tha confronts Ainslie/Og over the Cloak. Ainslie shows the fourth tablet piece.
- Hap shows Ainslie the "G" medallion and chain. Ainslie's similar chain is missing its pendant/medallion.
- At the Dyed Cane, the tablet pieces are assembled. The completed tablet gives off orange sigils, burning smell, and transmutation/divination/illusion magic. Ky'tha feels magic enter her and passes it to Aury.
- Ky'tha and Ainslie go to Rook/Imonorra for an Identify scroll. Ky'tha confronts Rook about Bafast and the gem/material pressure.
- Rook says Bafast's line likely ties to Cragfury and says she helps underdark people make lives outside the Underdark.
- Ainslie asks Rook about Ahn/Ahn'Tsuel. Rook mentions dragon Irragosa, from the same hatch as Annagos, mount to a descendant of Ahn'Tsuel, and says answers are at Mount Emberstran.
- Ainslie reveals the Cloak recruited him and Og. Rook worries about a bound pact.
- While Ky'tha and Ainslie are gone, Aury attunes to the tablet and reads the Emberheart poem pointing toward Mount Emberstran.
- Ainslie later receives Rook's angry message: the EGD's neutrality is unacceptable, she has already engaged, and Jerik's brother is held in their defense.
- The session ends with the plan for Ky'tha to go to Lord's Keep to endorse Lady Rook.

- Ahn/Ahn'Tsuel answers may be at Mount Emberstran.
- Irragosa and Annagos are related dragons from the same hatch.
- Rook is holding or protecting Jerik's brother/Phira's husband as leverage.
- The EGD cannot stay neutral without angering Rook.
