## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: July 30, 2026
In-game Date:  4th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Lost Shrine to Guigzien
We start by recognizing Zabeek.  Aury prays for him and asks Diverra to take his soul.   Zymve looks at the drums that she gave Zabeek and mends them, as Ains picks up the wand he gave him.   Ky’tha stealths (31) and goes to check out the chamber, heading down stairs. She gets to the edge of the water to see if she sees anything.   She just sees rubble on the other side of the hallway - this entire hallway is under water.    She thinks this isn’t the highest quality stone, but thinks this happened during the Awakening.  
The group decides to pick up a rock and go down as a group.  We get 10’ from the edge of the water and hear coughing from above and hear “guys, help” and Aury, Zymve and Ains look and see Zabeek.  He is only wearing his shirt…no pants or shoes.  Aury starts to touch him to heal him….ROLL FOR INITIATIVE
ROUND ONE:  As Aury gets ready to cast the spell, the “Zabeek” character shifts into a toad-like creature and gets ready to attack.  Aury casts burning hands on the creature and it saves, but still takes damage.  It then needs to make a strength saving throw, as Og teleports him a few feet in front of him, reducing his speed to 0.   It attacks Ky’tha (Aury imposes disadvantage and give Ky’tha temp hit points) and Zymve, and it damages and frightens both, but also poison damage to Zymve.  Ky’tha uses uncanny dodge to take only half the damage. Og attacks recklessly attacks and misses, then hits, dealing damage, and he gives Zymve temp hit points.  Ains attacks and hits and deals thunder damage, but it absorbs some of the damage.   He then backs up and pulls Zymve back 15’.  Ky’tha attacks with her dagger, but misses.  Zymve attacks with vicious mockery and hits - unleashing a string of swear words at him - and he takes 3 psychic points of damage. Hap, using Fleur to fly by, misses his first attack, then tries with his pruning dagger and misses.  Then tries the sword again and fumbles, losing his movement.  Then tries the dagger again and misses.
ROUND TWO:  Aury looks around and, seeing no one is hurt, decides to bless Ky’tha, Hap, and Og (Diverra blesses you and makes you celibate).   The creature attacks Ky’tha and fumbles (loses his reaction), then hits her and frightens her (she uses uncanny dodge to lessen the damage).   Og attacks and hits once and then misses.  He inflicts acid and slashing damage on the hit. Ains attacks and hits using the wand. Ky’tha fumbles and hurts herself as she catches the dagger from falling down.  Zymve casts cloud of daggers on the creature, inflicting slashing damage.  Hap doesn’t use the fly by of Fleur and attacks with his sword and hits and tries to trip him up.  He takes damage and stays upright.  He then uses the dagger and hits.  Then tries the sword again and hits and tries to trip him again, and he stays upright. Then tries the dagger again and hits again.
ROUND THREE:  Aury casts toll the dead on the creature and it fails its saving throw, taking necrotic damage.  The creature laughs and farts and a poisonous cloud covers the group, heavily obscuring the group giving the group the blinded condition. Og attacks and hits twice, dealing slashing and acid damage, killing it.  The fart cloud disappears.
Ains goes up to check Zebeek and he is still there, but is shirtless.  The creature was able to take on the disguise of Zabeek.  Aury casts “death ward” on Ky’tha (if she goes to 0 she will go to 1 hit point). Hap realizes that Shadowtrail is sucking this in - he’s enjoying this fight (in his head, Hap taunts the sword “nice of you to finally show up”).
Og proceeds to throw the rock into the wet hallway ahead about 60’ ahead.  Ky’tha asks Aury to light up something and he lights up Ains’ shirt.  Ky’tha asks him to light up a crossbow bolt and she shoots it and embeds it across the hallway.  She shoots a bolt (at disadvantage) and successfully embeds it at the far end of the hallway.   Zymve moves her cloud of daggers through the water ahead of the group. She thinks the water is 3’ deep.  The party moves forward into the water. Og steps in and feels like it is slick and murky and tells everyone to proceed with caution.  Ky’tha moves forward stealthily (24) to check out the rubble at the far end of the hall.  She is thigh deep moving forward.  She telepathically tells Ains when she is halfway down the hall.  She makes it to the corner and sees the rubble and sees it turns and there is a door hanging on hinges 20’ down.  Checking out the rubble, she thinks something was waiting here and is no longer there.  She sees there is a dim light in the room beyond the door hanging off hinges.  She communicates to the group and then proceeds to the room.  Aury wants Ains to look for diamonds as they proceed.  
Ky’tha moves forward to the door and touches the wall and uses tremorsense to see if there is movement in the room beyond.  She senses at least 4 creatures in the room (35’, 40’, 50’, right and left and further back).   Zymve casts mirror image and Hap casts blade ward.
Ky’tha moves into the room, still deep and still slick.  It opens out to the right and left and she can see on the far side of the room that light is shining down on an altar. She moves in to the left and sees a shadow dip into the water.  She looks at the altar and sees it  has vines growing on it. She tells the others to come now!
There is a raised dais on the far in with a body wrapped in vines on it.  Several shapes move in the water and IT IS INITITATIVE.
ROUND ONE:  The guy in the back takes a step and disappears.  Ains moves into the room and surveys the situation.  He lights up his body and glows purple and holds his action to cast a spell before Hap.  One of the creatures raises his staff and casts a spell - fireball - on the party in the door (all except Ky’tha).  The group makes saving throws…all saved, but still take damage.  He then moves forward toward the party. Aury casts a fireball at the right side, aiming for the red and blue. One saves and one doesn’t.  He then takes a step back 30’.  Zymve casts hypnotic patterns on the red and blue on the right side.  The blue guy is charmed and incapacitated. All else save.  She thinks the person on the altar is a victim. Og rages and recklessly attacks the green guy and hits and then fumbles and falls prone, then stands up.  He inflicts slashing and acid damage.  The red guy advances to Og and attacks him and hits three times, dealing 34 piercing damage, but he only takes half damage.  The other red guy advances forward to Hap and misses twice, but then crits on the final hit, also hitting Og.  Ains pops off his reaction and they must make wisdom shaking throws.  This spell slows the invisible and one of the red creatures and the blue creature, makes them have disadvantage, makes them lose -2 to AC, speed halved, -2 to dex saves, one attack per turn, 25% chance of spell failing.  Hap attacks the red creature in front of him and hits with his sword.  Then attacks with the dagger and misses.  Tries the sword again and hits and frightens him (imposing disadvantage on attack and ability rolls).  Then uses a second wind to heal himself.  Ky’tha uses her crossbow to attack the red creature.  She hits with sneak attack and then cunning action hides. 
ROUND TWO:  The invisible creature tried to attack with a spell and failed (because of the slow) and is now visible.  Ains casts dissonant whispers on the non-slowed red creature and it fails its save.  It must run away. Hap and Og get opportunity attacks.  Hap hits and damages him.  Og hits and damages him.  Ains pushes Hap forward and yells - “I can only hold them for so long - take them out.”  The green one disappears.  Aury walks up to Og and heals him (33 points).  Zymve gives temp HP to Ains, Hap, and Og (2/each).  She and Ains teleport to the back corner of the room near the wall.  Og gives Aury temp HP (11) and he attacks slowed red and hits twice (the guy is further slowed - cannot move).  The red one who Hap frightened dashes back to the corner where Zymve and Ains are.  The slowed red creature attacks Hap and misses. Red and blue creatures both stay slowed.  Hap attacks the red creature and fumbles, but it is okay.  Then hits with the dagger.  Then attacks with the sword and hits.  Then attacks with the dagger and hits.  He uses second wind and hits again with his sword then hits again twice.  Ky’tha attacks with her crossbow with sneak attack and kills him.   Then uses cunning action to try to hide (23).
ROUND THREE:  The gray one goes invisible. Ains casts lightning bolt trying to hit the red, blue, and altar.  The red one saves, but still takes some damage (7 points).  The altar fails and takes 30 points of damage.  The blue one saves and takes damage (7 points).  He uses a bonus action to shove the red guy away and he succeeds so he doesn’t move. The green one pops into view next to Aury and Hap and attacks them.  He misses Aury twice.  Then attacks Hap once and Aury fires off a warding flare (giving Hap 11 temp HP) and hits Hap, who takes force damage (parrying some) and is incapacitated for a turn. Aury disengages and moves back 15’ and casts healing word on Hap. Zymve casts shatter on the altar, the person on the altar, and the red creature…who need to make CON saves.  The altar cracks and Zymve sees a symbol on the altar, but can’t make it out.  The Red creature and person on the altar take thunder damage (7).  Og hits the creature once and misses once, inflicting slashing and acid damage.  The blue creature looks fully healed.  The red creature looks at Ains and attacks twice and misses, but hits with the third attack.  He saves the curse.  He then turns and faces the altar and gets in a defensive stance.  Hap is incapacitated.  The group notices the person on the altar standing up and looks down at Zymve and Ains “who hit me with that?  I will help you dispense of these minions and then I will feast upon you.  I sought the power of the sludge god.  [Guigzien has been released].  After I slay these minions, I will seek him out.”    This is Blackrend.  
Ky’tha steps forward and shoots the red one, killing it with a sneak attack.  She looks at Blackrend.  She knows he caused the blight in Myrdae.  He came through the Shadowfell.  He wants the power of the god - he wants his power to create more sludge. Ky’tha thinks he is undead. 
Who summoned Guigzien?  We have to deal with him, the person who summoned him, and Blackrend.  Guigzien is somewhere in Myrdae. 
– The End of the Session –
