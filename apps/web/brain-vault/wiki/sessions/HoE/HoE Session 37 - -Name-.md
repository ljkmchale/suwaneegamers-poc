## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: October 14, 2026
In-game Date:  7th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Grey Fiddle Inn
At the Grey Fiddle Inn, Basctdelm
Hap shares his note with Aury.  They know that the Meridian is one of the most well known organizations - and is popular - in all of Myrdae.  They are trying to keep all of the information on the lands and organizations in check.  They pass news of threats.  They have a “know your enemy” perspective.  They decide it is ok to go to lunch.  Decide to share it with the group at breakfast.
Ky’tha takes off her locket that was hidden and shows it to Zymve…and says that the locket she got from the Shadow Sovereign is the other half.  Ky’tha tells her that he half came from her grandmother who got it from her her grandmother….and that there are old runes on them.  That should they unite, she was told it would have power.  As she clutches them close to each other, Zymve hears a song in her head of dwarves.  Ky’tha notices that Zymve seems really interested and asks and she tells her that she “hears” it.  Ky’tha does not.  She then starts to hum what she hears. Ky’tha thinks of songs she heard from the miners of her homeland.  We decide to try to unite the pieces.  Zymve helps Ky’tha.  As they get close, Zymve hears more to the music - it feels alive. Once they lock, she hears the music hit a high.  The pieces meld together and the chains become one.  Then she hears a click and the music stops.  The old runes are clear now - Cragfury and Umberfist.  Zymve thinks the click sounded like a lock opening. Ky’tha points out the two sides and explains the clans. Zymve sees a dragon head and a dwarf head.  Ky’tha doesn’t think the Cragfury clan had anything to do with a dragon.  The two study the locket and realize that the image is of a silver dragon. Zymve shares that Ains has a pendant that is magical - maybe it is similar to her new necklace.  Time to eat.
Meanwhile, Ains and Og discuss what is going on and their worry about the Vultures that might on their tails.  They want to assume other identities to protect themselves.  They talk about wanting to go and take care of the blight.   Time to get with the rest of the group to eat and talk about the day’s plans.   They decide to talk like each other at breakfast.
The group reunites for breakfast and share a table together.  Zymve asks to see Ains’ necklace - she gets to the point.  Ains offers it up and she compares the two - Ky’tha’s and Ains’.  She notices they are similarly shaped, similar in weight (not the chain, but the amulet).  She makes out a dragon’s head on Ains’ pendant - it is a bronze dragon.  She then asks to see Hap’s necklace - it doesn’t have anything to do with theirs so she gives it back.  She points out the similarities.  She gives it back to Ains.  Discussion about how maybe there is a necklace out there for Zymve, too.
Hap says he is feeling better today, but will probably be better in a couple of days.  He then shares that he has an amulet that keeps people from finding him and offers it to Zymve - who snatches it from him and wears it. 
Hap then shares that he was asked to lunch.  Some discussion about should we wait or not, but since Ky’tha hasn’t heard back about the horses yet, we aren’t in any hurry.  The group decides to head to the House of Myr.  Ky’tha leaves word at the front desk of the Inn that she is expecting a message and pays them a silver to bring it to her at the House of Myr should it come in.   As we start to leave Ky’tha feels a hand on her shoulder and is given a pouch from a stranger who says “this should help you procure what you need.”  They nod to each other and he walks off.  She then secretly looks and counts 50gp, 40pp. She realizes that the Cloak is resource-rich - much more than the Etterset.
They go to the House of Myr -Ains, Aury and Hap entered first, while the other three linger just behind.  Some look at Hap and comment how he looks much better.  Some offer their assistance, should we need it.  He presents his note and asks if Meren is available right now to meet vs. at lunch.  Hap is told that he is running the show here, but the person says he will go ask.  We notice several of the Meridian walking around - some in plate mail - most well dressed.  Meren comes forward and says “so lunch is off the table,” but Hap says no - he can wait until lunch.  He then says that Hap’s father was in the group and well known and they would be honored to have him join the group and/or help as needed.  He asks Hap if “he wants to rush a meeting like this?”  He further says that he really would really like Hap to join them.  Hap then tells them about the blight and explains that is why he was leaving.  He asks if they have information about it.  Meren replies that this could immediately be a help to the Meridian.  That if Hap could keep them informed about the blight and what is happening, it would be beneficial to the Meridian.  They don’t know about the blight - have just heard that there are issues in some of the nearby towns.  Hap offers to have lunch if his friends can come, too - especially since others in the party might have more information, especially Og.   Ains asks if he can talk to some of the more magically inclined in the group and is told that he can go to the top of the tower.  Ains messages Og for he, Ky’tha and Zymve to enter.  He told Og to “show off his aura” as he enters.
Ains then heads up 16 flights of stairs to the Tower.  Meanwhile, Meren starts to talk to the group about other groups over the years who have been gobbled up by the Meridian - the Regulators, the Eight, etc. He asks for our group name and Ky’tha responds “The Heroes of Emberstran.”  He tells the group that he has extended an offer to Hap for him to join the Meridian.  He then says it was fortuitous that the group was going to find the blight when he needed someone to find out what was going on in Shademoor and other towns.  
Aury starts to tell the story of Blackrend and the Shadowfel and the blight. He asks some questions - and Aury tries to answer.  Zymve mentions Guigzien.  He has heard of him.  Conversation then turns to the Ascension Tree and Villari’s tie to Diverra.  Og steps up to tell how he is the defender of the Tree.  Some discussion about the tree that Og melded to - Og thinks that the tree he bonded with was the Ascension Tree, but the way Meren described it, it should have been HUGE - much bigger than the House of Myr.  So Og realizes that the Ascension Tree is very hurt - and must have been for some time to be as small as it is now. This is a problem older than Blackrend. This might be something that happened during the Awakening.  What happened then?  Who knows?  But maybe Blackrend just took advantage of the fact the Tree was injured already. 
We notice that Meren is sizing up Hap.  Aury steps up to be his cheerleader. Hap says he has some questions and the group gets up to leave and give them some privacy.  Meren looks over to a guard and walks out, too.  Hap then asks him to tell him about his father and what he did for the Iron Brotherhood.   He says that Hap’s father was the holder of the ledger for the Iron Brotherhood.  The ledger was a magical tome - it kept track of people alive or dead doing good and bad in the world.  He was the only one that could open the ledger.  He had a group of 8 as part of the Iron Brotherhood who had different roles and responsibilities.  One kept Moganna’s power divided, for example.  There is another named Neris, this person came to fame by tracking the Cloak of Defiance.  He made sure that no Cloak names were erased from the ledger.  This binder was very important.  He says it is a shame that the tome was lost. Hap tells him that he was contacted by the Cloak about the ledger.  He tells him how the Cloak has eyes on Hap’s sister and has asked him for the ledger to ensure her safety.
Meren tells Hap that Neris might be the best key to find out where the ledger is.  She wanted to have a family and created a place in Basctdelm with her son - she is no longer an active Iron Brotherhood or Meridian member - but might have some clues.  Hap says that he will accept the offer to join in honor of his Father’s memory.  Hap gets a sense of Fatherhood about him and he doesn’t look like Hap, but he feels and appreciates who Meren is (top ranking official in Basctdelm).  He tells Hap that Neris runs a place called The Iron Star. Hap says that he isn’t sure when he can get there because of the blight, but will try to do so soon.  The rest of the group sees a couple of guys come in with a crate and take it over to Meren.  They open it up.  Meren says “I haven’t been on the road for some time, but hopefully you will take my mantle.”  Hap is stunned because Meren is offering him his platemail suit. He replies “I’d be honored, sir.”  He tells him that the Iron Star is at the Southwest Gate…and would be a quick stop to make on our way out of the city.  Hap tries on the suit of armor.
Meanwhile, Ains goes to the Tower and jokes about putting an arcane waypoint at the bottom of the tower.  He tells them that Hap is joining the group (the heir of Benoit Garamond).  He asks if Ains knew of Benoit and he dodges.  He then gets to the point.  He asks if he has access to other waypoints in Myrdae that he could use - specifically asking about Inverland.  He is told that guardians exist, but there is a waypoint in Ingriss. However, Ains is not furry enough to be a suitable envoy. He then makes himself taller and hairier, and is told that might work.  He also tells him about Oldport, but Ains wants Ingris. He is told that they will be outside at this waypoint, that he should not talk, that he will be introduced and should get the symbol.  Morey Stillflower is the second contact in Ingriss.  Pamaris Ostonn is the primary contact.  
They go. They land in a field of erected standing stones.  There are 8 stones.  Four guards are sitting there.  There are hedges with openings in three of the four directions.  There are areas that lead beyond this into a very natural wood elf city.  As they land, the guards step forwards and they have white, pale stone blades with pale wood.  They see the guy Ains is with and start to speak, but Ains speaks first.  He says that he has traveled far from the mainland and introduces himself as the speaker for Og (Storen the Half - in his disguise).  He says it is an honor to be here.  They put their weapons down.  They offer a ceremony in his honor, but he declines and says that can perform the ceremony when Og (official title) comes.  He tells them they are clearing the blight on the mainland. They mention “what of the revival of the Ascension Tree” and Ains responds that it is underway.  Ains tells them to carry on and he will be going soon.  The Meridian guy is bug-eyed since this was not the plan, but stays quiet.  Ains asks if they have heard of the blight and says that he will reach back out and then claims to know her name as a calling from Villari.  They then leave…and the Meridian isn’t sure what to think of this.  Ains drops the disguise.  He then has to persuade the guy that this was a good idea. The guy thinks this was for a good cause so he will let the powers that be know that Ains has special abilities to persuade people.  He asks if Ains got what he needed and says he felt a little used.  Then Ains leaves.
Ains rejoins the group and notices Hap’s suit of armor. The group leaves and discusses going to get horses courtesy of Ky’tha, and then stopping to see Hap’s lead on the way out.  Ains tells them about his brief trip to Ingriss. He tells about disguising himself and assuming a new name and new voice. He tells what he learned about the “revival of the Tree.”  Og then tells him about how the Ascension Tree is not at full power.  
They get to the stable and Ky’tha uses thieves cant to communicate and realizes this is a Cloak contact.  He tells her that the 6 horses, including one that is bigger, are ready and saddled and will require the full payment “I don’t make the rules.”  Ky’tha tries to pay less for 6 horses, but he says you can take fewer but you need to leave the money.  She takes all 6 horses.  One is dark brown and Zymve asks if she could trade it for a black one.  There are a couple of black ones to choose from.  She chooses the solid black - but it leaves no trail and will cost her an additional 50gp (Veldrin).  Ains asks for the black one with white stars - this is an equal trade (Chanson). Og has the large horse that is white and brown (Alfie).  Aury collects his horse (Gyft).  Hap’s horse (Gertrude), Ky’tha’s horse (Vertigo) and the extra horse (Sovereign) are all various shades of brown. 
Aury buys some pipe tobacco as they pass through town to the South Gate. The group rides through the town.  Hap hears “I think we are being followed.”  His sword is talking to him.  He asks, “by whom?”  And the sword can’t answer. He then whispers to Aury and Ky’tha. Ains casts the spell where we are all in each other’s head - except Aury.  Ky’tha hears hooves in front and underneath and behind - but doesn’t hear the horse next to her. She asks Og and Ains who are following us.  Og tries to look and doesn’t see anything but he feels something.  He thinks there are eyes everywhere.  We make our way to the Iron Star near the Southwest Gate.  There is a symbol that looks similar to the Meridian symbol, but is a little different.  It is silver.  There is a large clearing where we can rest the horses so Hap can have a talk.  
We know from being here a few days - there is the Queen’s Watch (normal guard, but griffon riders), there is the Cloak of Defiance, and there is the Meridian.  We know these three are those who have a pulse on the city.  We feel the Cloak are watching so as we arrive at the Iron Star we announce that we are eating and grabbing a drink - and Zymve wants “fresh air” and will stay outside (to guard the horses and keep watch).
The rest of the group goes in.  There is an older women and a younger man working here.  There aren’t many folks here.  We smell biscuits.  Hap asks to speak with Neris. We order cider and ale and Ky’tha communicates that we are here for food and drinks as Ains tells him in his head “do not speak of our business as you get Neris and our food.”  He goes to get the silver-haired lady behind the bar who looks over and sees Hap and drops the drink.  He gets up to walks over to her at the bar. “Can it be?” she says.  You look just like Benoit.  He introduces himself as Hap, Benoit’s son.  He tells her about his conversation with Meren and about the ledger.  He is direct and says he hopes he can trust her.  She says she can - “she thought a lot of his dad.”  He tells her that everyone wants the ledger and she says “Not the Cloak” and he replies that everyone wants it.  He tells her that he would like to find it.  She says “to the best of my knowledge, it never left Hap’s hometown.”  He tells her that the place was turned over - and she says that Benoit did a good job hiding it “Good on your dad.”  He realizes that he will need to go back and search - and figure out where his dad hid the tome.
Hap thinks back to his hometown.  There was a swamp and a raised area in the middle.  He knows that the town was rooted in faith and community support.  He also realizes that it felt like home.  There were many halflings there.  There were fisherfolk and those who wrestled gators.  It was a place of traditions.  It was peaceful except the night Benoit died.  He asks if she would have any clues about how Benoit might have concealed it - something that would be Meridian-specific that the Cloak might not have known about.   Hap remembers an Elder in the town.  He thinks that Neris might not be more helpful than to know that it was at the town, but that talking to Myranda Fenberry (the elder) might have more info.  He remembers that he and his dad used to visit a shrine to the old god of Torec.  That is why Ulgray meant something to Hap.  Torec had a significant presence in his town prior to the Awakening.  So he knows he needs to go home and find Myranda.  He knows that the Awakening took over the Shrine…and that this is remarkably similar to the Shrine where he died recently.  He asks more about his Father.  She comments that she hopes his sister is okay.  And that her father was a good man.  She tells Hap if there is anything she can do to help him, let him know. 
Meanwhile, Zymve is hanging outside flipping a dagger and looking around.  She realizes that a horse is missing. This was the riderless horse.  She doesn’t see it riding off.  She gets off to search for prints.  They walk right up to the horse and then away and she sees a little horse figurine laying on the ground. It looks like the horse that we are missing.  She picks up the horse figurine. The group comes out and Ains makes it a horse again and then a figure again.  Ky’tha takes it, calling it the Sovereign, and puts it away. She gives Zymve a bag of biscuits and a mug of ale (that she pocketed).  
We decide to leave the town now, following the south road to Padstow. Aury pulls out his pipe and smokes it as he travels.   This is Biddlebon tobacco and Zymve loves the smell and follows behind Aury.  The sky is gray and overcast.  There are some snow flurries.  We see a river and work our way into Padstow.  We see a large building known as the Heavy Loaf.  
WE END HERE.
