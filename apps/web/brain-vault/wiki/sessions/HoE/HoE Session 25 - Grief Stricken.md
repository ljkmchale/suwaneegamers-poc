# HoE Session 25 - Grief Stricken

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 25
- Session Title: Grief Stricken
- Source Header: 25 - Grief Stricken (05.14)
- Date Label In Notes: 05.14
- Real Date: 2026-05-14
- In-Game Date: 39th of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Mount Emberstran, outside the door to the vault
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party enters the newly opened passage beneath [[Mount Emberstran And Vault]]. The tunnel is clean, dwarven-built, and leads through two trial arches.
- The first arch, marked "flames of fervor," frightens the party until Ainslie casts Calm Emotions; the flames fade and the way opens.
- The second arch shows a ghostly Ainslie-like figure holding a real bronze lance. The runes read "It is Within." Ainslie cuts himself on the lance; his blood opens the golden fog barrier.
- Inside the obsidian vault, the party finds a black metal dragon-talon pedestal holding a fist-sized heart-shaped gemstone, with two empty sockets nearby.
- [[Irragosa]] appears in elven form, recognizes Ky'tha and Zymve through old dragonrider-era companions, and confirms she has guarded Moganna's vault.
- Ainslie touches the heart gem, is drained into exhaustion, and is told he must rest under Diverra's light at the Emberstran temple.
- Irragosa names the other two relic pieces as **Soulroot** and **Starvein**. She says she does not know where they are.
- The party leaves Mount Emberstran under Irragosa's protection. She shifts from elven form toward dragon form and gives safe passage past the giants.
- [[Zabeek]] is waiting with the party's gertot and insists on traveling with them to become a proper adventurer.
- On the road back, the party camps, discusses Ainslie's condition, and learns more about Aury's petrified rose-petal travel ritual.
- The party investigates the crashed flying ship, recovers coin/jewelry, and finds a transmutation orb with floating stone fragments, likely connected to the ship's flight.
- Aury builds a pyre, blesses the dead, and tries to carry an understanding of forgiveness back to Irragosa.
- On 44th of Talil, the party returns to Emberstran, where guards welcome them as heroes.
- At the Temple of Diverra, [[Lady Rook]] reveals she had the party followed, says the tablet was tied to ancient bloodlines, and offers a silver dragon scale as help. Ky'tha questions her timing; Rook takes back the scale and leaves offended.
- Meladra prepares the altar for Ainslie. Aury prays over him while reading from [[Bloom Aeterna]] in Meladra's presence.
- Ky'tha tells Hap that Phira is on the way from Ulgrey and asks about preparations to keep the Cloak of Defiance weakened.
- Meladra reports that [[Arnol]] was ambushed two nights earlier and is barely alive.
- At the Warm Bear, Zymve unnerves a regular bard who recognizes her; Zabeek tries to learn adventuring, including dagger fighting and apparently pickpocketing.
- Ky'tha finds [[Tessa]] at the new Etterset headquarters. Tessa says Ky'tha's message arrived too late and that the Etterset has had misfortunes in Ulgrey.

## Combats

- No major combat is recorded.
- The party sees dead quarathes on the road with lightning slashes, implying Irragosa protected or cleared the route.

## NPCs Introduced

- [[Zabeek]] - the gnome rescued at Sparkwhisper; waits with the gertot and joins the party as an aspiring adventurer.

## Clues Found

- The vault was designed by Moganna and guarded by Irragosa.
- The heart gem is one of three relic pieces; the other two are Soulroot and Starvein.
- If one person combines all three pieces, the effect differs from "reopening the circle" and keeping the relics from the wrong hands.
- Ahn'Tsuel is described as a timeless aberrant being that passes to a new soul every 50 years. The three-part relic could extend life and break that cycle.
- Orah Nelcrath was a dragonborn dragonrider from Dunduar / the Nelcrath clan, tied to Karalafene the White Flare.
- Vhurkir Inni was a drow dragonrider, tied to Kaelix the Copper and possibly to Zymve's ancestral line.
- Tsulanan rode Irragosa.
- Ainslie is connected to Moganna; Irragosa says when Moganna died, Ainslie was born.
- The flying ship's transmutation orb may relate to levitation / lodestone magic and to Iannan Rambibur of Tarnsport.
- Rook's silver dragon scale came from the Peak of Tibul through Endavo, who is preoccupied after finding a spitespirit key.
- Phira is likely three days from Emberstran, coming from Ulgrey.
- Arnol was ambushed two nights before the party's return.
- Something bad has happened to the Etterset in Ulgrey.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Ainslie gains or claims the heart-shaped vault gemstone, but at the cost of exhaustion.
- The party recovers 112 gp, 275 sp, and rings/necklaces worth about 100 gp from the crashed flying ship.
- Ky'tha finds and keeps the flying ship's transmutation orb with floating stone fragments.
- Lady Rook offers, then withdraws, a silver dragon scale from the Peak of Tibul.

## Decisions Made

- The party takes Ainslie to Emberstran's Temple of Diverra rather than Bloomrest because Irragosa specifically says Diverra's light at the old Wimbalt/Emberstran temple is needed.
- The party investigates the crashed flying ship before returning to Emberstran.
- Aury blesses the dead from the flying ship and seeks a way for Irragosa to reckon with their deaths.
- Hap moves toward selling his inherited land rather than letting it go to waste.
- Ky'tha arranges to prepare for Hap's sister's arrival and looks for Tessa instead of staying at the temple.

## Consequences

- The vault threshold question is resolved: the vault held one of three relic pieces and was guarded by Irragosa.
- Ainslie is physically depleted by the heart gem and must recover under Diverra's light.
- Irragosa's guilt over the crashed flying ship is now explicit.
- Lady Rook's surveillance of the party is confirmed, and her attempted help worsens trust with Ky'tha.
- Arnol's status has worsened from "troubled acolyte" to ambushed and barely alive.
- Ulgrey becomes an immediate live thread through Tessa, Phira's approach, and Etterset misfortune.

## Character Beats

- **Ainslie** opens and claims the vault's central inheritance, suffers exhaustion, and uses reckless Sending while recovering at the temple.
- **Aurelius** chooses Diverra's temple care for Ainslie, studies Bloom Aeterna's rose-petal ritual, blesses the flying ship dead, and prays sincerely over Ainslie at the altar.
- **Hap** learns Shadowtrail's name directly from the weapon and prepares for Phira's arrival.
- **Ky'tha** touches Irragosa in dragon form, claims the airship orb, challenges Lady Rook's timing, and follows the Ulgrey/Etterset warning.
- **Og** carries Ainslie out of the mountain and mentors Zabeek in basic camp/adventuring rules.
- **Zymve** connects Vhurkir Inni to her ancestral/jewelcrafting memory and unsettles a Warm Bear bard who recognizes her.
