# HoE Session 15 - Spitespirit Cache

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 15
- Session Title: Spitespirit Cache
- Date Label In Notes: 11.28
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party arrives in Nunglthil, an old cliff/cave city and underground harbor.
- At the Darkbottom Draught, they meet Bahwa, Fafi, Thak, Drogon, and learn the local favor/broker system.
- Zymve appears for the first known time in HoE: a drow woman with silver hair, red eyes, piercings, and rings. She knows Scalamank, needs coin and a way out, and offers to guide the party.
- Zymve explains Nunglthil's enclaves: Kar'Solar, Grol'Thun, Selraeth, Durrak, and Glimmerdale. Coppereyes runs the gem group and is tied to Scalamank.
- Ehkel an'Davo is also at Darkbottom. He asks Ainslie for a tale, warns caution, mentions Lady Rook favor, and implies sanctuary information requires favor.
- Ky'tha and Og gamble with Cadifynn Ziltogga at Eyes Out; Ky'tha wins and Og splits.
- The next day, Zymve guides the party to Coppereyes Gemcraft. Scalamank/Coppereyes says he has the third key and wants uncut gems from Mandel Spitespirit's cache. Others entered the cache and never returned.
- Ainslie pays 100gp and renegotiates the return reward to a 300gp gem.
- The party passes Glayig's guarded portcullis and enters a twelve-wall lantern chamber. They choose the xorn/gems door.
- Trap sequence: a rune hallway and collapsing boulder, Ky'tha using Hap's shield and an immovable/silver horse rod to brace the way, Fleur being crushed, and an oil/flame slope ignited by Aury sliding on a shield.
- At a handprint wall marked with the name Ythibius, Ainslie and Zymve identify the name as draconic/dragon-related. The handprint opens an illusion wall to a rope bridge, dais, violet light, and ornate box.
- The party fights a shadow/necrotic dragon named Ythibius. Ainslie hastes Og, and a lightning strike helps finish the dragon before it vanishes in smoke.
- The cache yields Shadowtrail, a violet prism shard, Mandel's Adventuring medallion, a bone scrollcase, coins, scrolls, a nonmagical ring, and fourteen huge uncut gems.
- The party returns to Nunglthil. Zymve stays with Hap and Aury while Hap studies Shadowtrail.

## Clues Found

- Nunglthil runs through favor, brokers, enclaves, and guarded local access.
- Zymve knows local routes, art, Scalamank, and how to qualify/help with favor.
- Spitespirit's cache is keyed through the twelve-wall lantern chamber.
- Ythibius is a dragon-name clue tied to the cache and Shadowtrail.
- Ehkel an'Davo has Rook/favor connections and may be moving on his own route.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Shadowtrail, ebon short sword with draconic runes, taken by Hap.
- Immovable rod / silver horse rod, kept by Ky'tha.
- Violet prism shard that creates violet light, taken by Ainslie.
- Mandel's Adventuring medallion.
- Bone scrollcase.
- Ring engraved "luck is for the living," nonmagical, worth about 80gp.
- 14pp, 112gp, 287sp.
- Feather Fall scroll, taken by Ainslie.
- Pass without Trace scroll, taken by Zymve.

## Decisions Made

- The party accepts Zymve as guide/help in Nunglthil.
- Ainslie pays to access the cache and negotiates a stronger reward.
- The party chooses the xorn/gems door in the twelve-wall chamber.
- Hap takes Shadowtrail.
- Zymve remains with Hap and Aury after the cache.

## Consequences

- Zymve joins the active HoE story as a guide, artist, and Underdark/Nunglthil contact.
- Hap gains Shadowtrail, a major weapon/thread item.
- Ky'tha gains the rod that will matter tactically later.
- The party earns valuable uncut gems and leverage for Nunglthil favor.
- Dragon-name/vault clues widen beyond Emberstran.
