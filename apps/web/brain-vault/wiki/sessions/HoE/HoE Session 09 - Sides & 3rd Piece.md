# HoE Session 09 - Sides & 3rd Piece

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 09
- Session Title: Sides & 3rd Piece
- Date Label In Notes: 08.11
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- Dinner with Lady Rook continues at Old Oak & Grapes. Ehkel an'Davo notices Hap looks familiar and mentions a woman in Trailpoint who looked like him and bought the Shimmering Stag Inn.
- Dandelia of the Veil in Kahlbit's Veil is named as a possible lead for Og's thorn leaf.
- Rook says Hap and his sister are rightful owners and offers to help put property back in Hap's orbit if the party supports her Vintner's Guild move.
- Ky'tha asks Rook/Imonorra to send a message to the Trailpoint proprietor.
- Ainslie follows an'Davo to Charum's Oils and asks about the Marrowstone vision. an'Davo warns him not to tell anyone else, says this has played out before, and says a trustworthy archivist may know who Ainslie is.
- Hap writes Phira through Fleur at the Shimmering Stag Inn.
- Aury writes the Lord warning about Lady Rook's swift influence and possible blight connection.
- The party debates sides: Ainslie and Og lean toward Rook, Aury supports neither and distrusts Rook, Hap is unsure why his opinion matters, and Ky'tha keeps the original document back.
- Tessa tells Ky'tha that an'Davo thinks Rook was the intended target at the show; the Cloak sent "Whisper" with poison and likely killed Jae.
- At the temple, Meladra gives Aury a +1 breastplate and the FLAME tablet piece from the vault.
- Og and Ainslie investigate Jae's house again and find small footprints leading toward water.
- Ky'tha is teleported by Rook/Imonorra to Trailpoint and finds Phira alive, married, and afraid for Hap. Phira gives Ky'tha a ring and apron for Hap.
- Ainslie is approached by a Sijulous Ghone / Eradic cultist with a bone-carved token and later sends Arnol toward the cult as a "secret mission."
- The party regroups at the temple. Ky'tha tells Hap about Phira and gives him the ring/apron. Aury shows the FLAME tablet.
