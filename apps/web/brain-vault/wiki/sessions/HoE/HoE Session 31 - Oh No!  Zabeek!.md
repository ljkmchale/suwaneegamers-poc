## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: July 23, 2026
In-game Date:  3rd of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Ulgrey
In the town of Ulgrey
We talk to Erland about the threat he saw outside of town. We see a wiry man approaching in leathers, limping (Qaddie). Ky’tha notices Erland making a “hmmmm” face at Qaddie. He asks if anyone has seen the Bergermeister - he says he needs to talk to her, too.  Ky’tha says get in line.  About that time a door to the Seeker’s Church (oratory) opens up.  A lady comes out and says hello to Qaddie and Erland - I need to speak with you both.  Ainslie thinks something seems off. Ky’tha interrupts them leaving to ask who she is - this is the Bergermeister Elsie.  She welcomes the group.  We question if our help is going to be required or not - and she inquires what about.  Erland shares a little and Ky’tha and Ainslie fill in a few blanks.   She bids us to come inside the church (Ky’tha questions why the church and not her mansion - which leads to Elsie questioning why Ky’tha would tell her what to do), but the group goes.
The enter the church.  It was initially a building dedicated to Neera.  There are recent paintings of maps on the walls.  The church is welcoming and bright.  There is an elderly robed lady there - she appears to be over this church.  Aury introduces himself out of respect to the sanctuary, as the group is motioned over to a sitting area in an enclave.  They notice the paintings are giving off Cembus vibes.  The priestess seems to be a follower of Muerg or Cembus.  This is Mother Velna. She welcomes everyone to “the Oratory of Ulgrey.”  She looks everyone up and down. Elsie reaches out to comfort Ky’tha - proud that she elicited some emotion - and says she hopes Ky’tha took no offense.  Ky’tha responds no more than you being upset that I suggested to you that it seems odd that you’d work out of the church vs. your home or office.
Ainslie introduces themselves as the EGD and says that the party is there investigating the murder and kidnapping. She asks for details.  Ainslie explains that we know there are ties to criminal organizations that are associated with the murder.  Ky’tha overhears Elsie say to Velna, “see I told you.”  Ky’tha then shares more of the details given what she overheard.  Elsie then asks Qaddie to share what he learned.  He says that he is traveled and it took him a while before he found clues.  He said he nearly got to Harbuck before he found their trail.  He found off the path the carriage - empty and no trace of anyone.  And he returned to town just now. Ky’tha comments that Harbok is the opposite direction of Basctdelm and Qaddie/Elsie agree.  But they question why Basctdelm - and Ainslie tells them that we had evidence that they were going to Basctdelm. Aury comments that our sources about Basctdelm were confirmed.  We discuss the situation as well as Erland’s issue.  Ky’tha mentions that maybe the Cloak has an operation in Harbok.
We notice an elven female escorting a shorter figure to a door across on the other side of the church. Ainslie flips Velna a donation as he casts a spell to read the thoughts of Elsie and Velna (maybe they are worthy, maybe they are the ones; I’m curious about the dark-skinned one) and Erland (I need to find a place, I’ve been on the road a long time and it is time) and Qaddie (I hope this is helpful, I’m trying to impress the Bergermeister).  He also picks up on the thoughts of one of the other figures (oh, they are the ones - but has no idea who that is referring to in the group).  Aury is trying to figure out who the short figure is.  He sees shadows.  Ky’tha wonders if they may have overheard the conversation and thinks they were likely preoccupied with their own affairs.
Ainslie tells the Bergermeister that he would like to read her mind - something he can do with the EGD.  But he can’t read her thoughts.  Elsie asks Ainslie what she is thinking and he tells her, then tells Velna what she was thinking.  When she hears this, Zymve speaks up expressing frustration about why they would be concerned about her.  Velna mentions that they are with the Advents of Harmony and asks Zymve if she knows of them - she doesn’t - and says that this would be a conversation for another time since there are more pressing issues, but that they meant no harm to Zymve - just found it curious to see her on the surface since there aren’t many like her around here.
Elsie asks Erland to give her more info about the pond and body and worms.  He says it is probably a half a day down the road on the way to Harbok.  Qaddie starts to speak up that it is close to the carriage and Ky’tha comments that the dead body could be Phira’s husband. Erland excuses himself to go get rest. 
Ainslie tells Elsie that there town is a little gray and Ky’tha tells him that Elsie doesn’t like to be told what to do.  And then she tells the group that the party is going to leave now to investigate the dead body - that Qaddie is welcome to join us, but we are going because it is important to our investigation. 
Zabeek shows up and asks if he can take Ewing on a stroll and Zymve tells him to escort us.  Velna asks if she can have a moment to finish her conversation.  Aury encourages her. Velna asks if Zymve is interested in learning more about the Advents of Harmony.  She says she is a retired Savant.  She says her services are now to Cembus, but she acts as a safe place for those seeking information or help. Aury tells Zymve that is a great place for her to be.  Zymve asks what the benefits are for joining the group.  Velna tells her that it would be networking and contacts…and access to information from the Argent Halls.  Zymve is not terribly trusting of surface dwellers and declines the offer.   Ainslie starts to ask questions to Velna and Zymve tells him “they didn’t invite you.”  Ky’tha then asks Velna if anyone would be welcome and she says yes and extends the invitation to all. No one responds immediately, but Ainslie tells her he has a journal of history and lore…and starts to share a little about what he is reading (dragon riders, etc.).  Zymve asks him to quiet down.  She asks if Ainslie is always this sensitive and Ky’tha and Zymve both say yes - that she needs to extend to him a personal invitation.  But she would like to know more and talk to him more about the contents of the journal. 
Bottom line:  we deduce that this church is where the Advents of Harmony meet up.  The group heads outside, but Aury stays back to talk to Velna.  He takes her hand and kisses it formally and gets down on one knee and says he is showing his respect and offers her a donation.  He then opens up his book - and she asks what it is - and he tells her it is the original text from Diverra.  She gasps and is taken aback that he is walking around with something that valuable.  He says he is not worried - that his goddess will take care of it.  She tells him of the Uktali Monastery and how they copy vital records and preserve the history of items.  She encourages him to protect the tome - with more than his life.  Aury thanks her for the information and reaches into the book and gives her a rose petal and explains the significance of it.  She walks over to the map, closes her eyes and touches a point on the map that “moved” her.  She pointed to a place in the middle of Shademeadow - this would be the surface location of the shrine and the life tree.  He memorizes the location.  She says that she will keep the petal safe and puts it away.  He tells her the name of the book.   Then he goes outside.
Meanwhile, Zymve and Ainslie discuss the book and how she is surprised that he told of some of the contents.  Ky’tha asks Hap if he is ready to investigate the dead body that might be his brother-in-law.  He is ready.  Ainslie the offers a trade to Zymve - he offers her the wand in exchange for the key.  She declines. When Aury comes outside, the group heads out. Zabeek is a little frightened by the prospect of seeing a dead body.  
The group heads to the stables to get Ewing and question the stablemaster. Discussion on the walk about what Zabeek would like to be - a bard, a mage, etc.  He needs training.  Ainslie offers him a wand.  Zymve offers him a drum. They try to each persuade him to be their own apprentice…but decide to share the duty
The group arrives at the Stables. An elven woman is brushing a horse. She asks if we are there to pick up our gertot.  We say yes.  Ky’tha asks if she is the one who works here and the lady gives her a smart ass answer.  Ky’tha rephrases to say the SOLE proprietor of this establishment, swearing as she asks, and the lady says no - it is a family business.  Ky’tha then asks Hap to tell her why we are there.  Hap asks her about the murder.  She tells him there were four people.  She overheard the name “Ni-Ro” mentioned (Neirroh).  Hap has not heard of that.  She tells the group that they were headed in the direction of Harbok.  Hap tells her that the carriage was just found.  She tells him that it was a full black carriage and stood out that way.  Hap asks if she is aware of anything in that area where the carriage would be abandoned.  She says not exactly in that direction - but the Tower of Myr is roughly in that direction.  Ky’tha then asks her about Kahlbits Vale. She says that no one goes back there anymore.  We ask why not?  She says that it used to be a nice forest with a pleasant grove and animals aplenty.  But that is no longer that way.  This happened a while ago - maybe even a year ago.  There used to be a Druid of the Vale and she interacted with the hunters with kindness.  But then the forest got too thick.  Rumors are that the druid may have started the problems.  She was a secluded wood elf, but has been hard to find.  Aury mentions that he saw a wood elf at the Sanctuary - but the group decides to head out of town - can follow up about who that might have been when we return.
Ainslie wants to return the collar at the Tower so the group walks by there on the way out.  He looks to see if the window is open.  Someone bumps into Ainslie and apologizes and hands him a brown journal.  Og picks up that the guy may have slight of handed a book to Ainslie.   The guys walks away.  Ainslie looks at the empty book and it has an owl’s head on it.  Ky’tha tells him that it is a journal for the Advents of Harmony and that must be his personal invitation. Ainslie sees someone sweeping at the front door and sees the cat in the window.  He realizes that the gabuk was the one in the church earlier.  He tells her that the cat left something in his room.  She replies that this is gift from the lady and are you sure you want to return it?  He says yes and she takes it and calls the cat over.  She thanks him and says no hard feelings - and he says “neutral.”  And Ky’tha interrupts and says ‘let’s go.’  Aury pulls out his map and she asks to see it, as he tells her that we are “mapping the lands of Myrdae.”  He then tells her about the Wefterians.  She tells him that the Oratory has maps and that he should use the walls to help fill in his map.  She responds that “oh - you were the ones I saw there earlier.”  She offers muffins, but the group declines.
The group starts to head out of town, headed toward Harbok.  As they go, Zymve and Ainslie trade off telling/teaching him things.  We know it will be a half a day’s travel to try to find the carriage.  Qaddie said we will come to a stream and then come to a road…if we go there, we have gone too far.  We need to turn around and go back 50 paces…and then go on the right side.  We think we will get there mid-afternoon.
We have some rations around lunchtime and continue.  We notice Og starts to pull back a little.  Og feels his wrist growing warmer where the thornleaf is.  There is heat and a “pumping of the life force” through his body.  His vision starts to go - eyes are milky.  A weight settles in to his mind about the blight.  He feels the corrupted roots choking life, but it feels disturbingly unnatural.  He feels the connection to the world tree…and feels a headache coming on.  He looks at the thornleaf and is expecting to see black thorns…and sees three of them. He thinks this is tied to the blight.  The thornleaf starts to hum and get warm and feels like it may be a stronger bond to him.  The haze then lifts and his vision returns and he sees the party looking at him intently.  [His bond has gone from Acquainted to Aware - level 1 to level 2].   Og talks of how he feels.  Ky’tha comments that Ewing broke wind.  More random talk- which leads to Zymve say that “she doesn’t need anyone.”
We continue.  We notice the grass is flattened off where a campsite was. Ky’tha walks over to investigate.  She is thrown off.  She sees where there were 3 horses.  The rocks are still warm from a fire.  There was only one bedroll used.  She sees a piece of burned paper in the ashes “Found 2 strange horses walking the road alone…(smudge)...message delivered.  Returning.  Bacna.” She shares this with the group.  No one has heard of them, except Og.  [Bacna was a messenger in the Gilded Vultures].  Discussion about what this means.  Zymve thinks this means that this was a courier that must have been delivering a note and trying to decide what to write down.  Like someone just wrote something down, but it wasn’t going to anyone.  Which is why it was burned.  We decide that the person probably has a half day’s jump on us and is on horseback.  
We continue.  Make it to the bridge and then follow Qaddie’s instructions to march back 50 paces and go to the right.  Og takes a look around and notices an area that looks like someone tried to cover tracks of a carriage.  He is able to find evidence of broken limbs and finds a trail to follow.  We take the trail and it leaves the road.  We go for a while…further than we expect to go. Fleur goes up to scout, but it is thick.  After a mile, Fleur catches site of the black carriage off to the side.  It sits at a strange angle (like a wheel is stuck).  We find things amiss.  Hard to tell what happened exactly.  Hap and Ky’tha investigate the scene and tell where they headed off, which direction the horses headed off, but can’t quite tell which direction the body is.  They find evidence on the carriage where a body was tied to it - dried blood and rope.  We see tracks, but aren’t sure where to look.  Hap says he wants to follow the footsteps.  Ainslie creates the mind link for the group, Zabeek, and Ewing - except Aury.   Og uses a spell to speak with plants.  We spread out and start to follow the tracks. He tells a tree he is looking for a body.  The tree tells him that he saw a horse.  And that the horse was eating.  He speaks to other grasses and plants.  They exclaim that they say a rabbit.  Og tells the group they have seen nothing other than horses and rabbits.  Nothing spoken about a dead body.  He then asks about a red creature.  And gets no response. Things just happened too long ago for the plants to remember.  
We continue deeper into the woods.  The ground gets softer.  Og is bothered - he doesn’t see blight, but he sees lack of growth.  Like something has stunted the ground/vegetation. Og asks a plant what is wrong - it says “I can’t breathe anymore - something has changed.  This just started recently.”  Og notices a smell of marshland.   The group is having issues trying to find clues. The swamp/marsh-like lands have obscured clues.  Og notices some stone areas up ahead and he sees a low haze around the bottom of a hill we are approaching.  The group reflects on the words from Erland about a mist.  Og again questions plants about a red figure.  We continue forward. Ainslie and Og seem to go up hill; the others find more swampland.  Ainslie tops the hill and sees footprints.  Og tops the hill and sees a door on the stone wall that is opened up with vines that have grown up the wall on the hillside.  He sees blackened evergreen junipers that have covered the walls and consumed the area. Above the door is the top of the hill.  He can’t tell if the door is part of the hill or not.  Doesn’t think it was constructed this way and is old, but sees recent footprints.   He asks the juniper tree why it looks the way it does.  It says Goo-igs-ian - he has returned (Guigzien).  Hap hears this and knows that Guigzien was aspiring to be one of Torec’s hands (Torec - god of elements).  Guigzien embraced earth and water (swamp and mud and muck).  He always heard that he was not a good guy.   He tells this to the group.  Og calls for the group to come up the hill - and everyone does.  Og yells for Aury to come up the hill, too.  Ky’tha asks Og if the plant saw this person and asks the plant.  It says that it just assumes it was the return of that demi-god now. Everyone gathers.  Og asks if someone has been here recently and they reply that no one has been here within the past day and that they are fading.  
Ky’tha offers to stealth forward (23).  She sees that the door is about to fall off the hinges.  That it looks like the roof is breaking down.  A fire pit near a place where the roof is more stable.  Discarded fabric in the area.  She sees scratches on the floor, purposefully made. She takes a moment to fill the group in and study the scratches further. It appears to be in a pattern that she doesn’t recognize.  There is coloration in the scratches.  She sees other papers in the items/gear.  Handwriting is not familiar.  Ainslie looks over her shoulder “filthy hole but it keeps the rain off and no one is looking.”  Ky’tha shows Hap her note “Phira secured.  Scroll used.  Basctdelm.  Clean the circle if you return.”   She points out the scratches and asks Ainslie to take a look.  Ainslie knows this is a teleportation circle.  Ainslie looks at the other papers.  “Lower stairs stay sealed…something moves the muck and it doesn’t like the light.  Not our problem.”  And another “mage practiced…something went sideways.  Let the town deal with it.  Distraction works.”   Og tells Aury about Guigzien and he thinks this place was destroyed 20 years ago during the Awakening.  He thinks this was a temple to the “muck god.”    Aury says we need to make sure to take care of the threat.  Ainslie tells the group that he isn’t full strength, but we decide to move forward.
Zabeek is scared.  Og tells him to stay in back.  Og rages and removes the boards.  As he does he notices a wider hall on the other side of the barrier.  IT IS INITIATIVE.
* Round One:  The board shatters and what leaps through is a big blue creature.  Zabeek gets squished by the creature as everyone else dodges the creature.  Zabeek dies.  We see a red creature ahead.  Og attacks the Blue behind him with a critical hit (20 on second roll) and damages him with slashing (26) and acid (7) and knocks him prone (giving temp HP to Zymve). And then moves up to the red guy and takes a critical opportunity attack against Og in his stomach (imposing disadvantage on Og’s next attack).  Og takes 15 slashing damage (rounded down to 7) and 4 points of poison damage.  He then hits the red creature (14 slashing and 2 acid).  He then takes another attack and red and hits (20 slashing and 1 acid) and reduces his speed by 25’ for this turn.  Zymve casts a spell on the blue one (phantasmal killer) and he saves and takes half damage (10 psychic) and he is frightened.  She then uses inspiration to give Hap, Ky’tha, and Og temp hit points.   Ky’tha attacks Blue with a sneak attack (32 points of piercing damage) and then disengages and moves closer to Og (out of range of both red and blue).   Blue closes some wounds a bit then hits Ainslie for some damage and he is also cursed and cannot regain HP and his HP maximum is decreased by 4.  He then turns to Aury and (warding flare) misses Aury.  He then hits Hap who reduces the damage (parry), but still takes damage but avoids the curse.  Ainslie blackens his hand and hits the Blue (he takes necrotic damage and cannot heal).  He then moves over and tells Ewing to disengage and move to the door (and he does).  Hap has Fleur buzz the blue and he attacks and hits him (10 piercing).  Then uses the dagger and hits (6 piercing).  Then tries the sword again and misses.  And finally another try with the dagger and fumbles (loses reaction until end of next turn).   The Red heals a little bit.  He then reaches out to attack Og with advantage and hits (10 piercing and Og is cursed).  He then attacks him again and hits again (9 piercing). Then hits Og again (8 piercing).  Aury attacks with burning hands on Blue and he saves and takes fire damage (5 - but he doesn’t think it did that much).  
* Round Two:  Og attacks Red and gives Ky’tha temp HP (13).  He hits twice and does damage (43 slashing and 3 acid damage - speed reduced by 25’).  Zymve uses cloud of daggers on the Blue and it hits and inflicts damage (13 slashing).  Ky’tha attacks Red with a sneak attack (28 piercing) and it falls…and she moves back.  Blue is still frightened by Zymve.  He attacks Ainslie and warding flare (gives Ainslie 11 temp hit points) and hits (16 slashing and 3 poison damage).  Then attacks Hap and misses twice. He is still frightened. He takes slashing damage from the cloud of daggers (20 slashing).  Ainslie attacks and misses.  Hap attacks Blue with a chill touch and misses.   Red does not get up.  Aury casts toll of the dead on Blue and he saves (20).  
* Round Three:  Og moves to the Blue character and attacks and hits twice and inflicts damage (46 slashing and 5 acid) and drops him.  
Ky’tha notices that the chamber descends down into a chamber where it looks like there is a big opening and she sees a splash.  She yells out to the group.
WE END HERE
