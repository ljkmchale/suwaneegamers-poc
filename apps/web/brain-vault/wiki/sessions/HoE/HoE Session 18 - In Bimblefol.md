## Major Scenes

- Makra guides the party through Thallgrove while warning Zymve that "the ground listens."
- The party fights Thall Mounds that have killed Makra's friends.
- Makra brings the party to Bimblefol, where Betuu gives them a drink that lets them understand the genlings.
- The party meets Aniba, who knows of the forgotten surface grove and trades a fungal map for wine.
- The party learns they will need water-breathing help and trades with Tatti the alchemist for Stillbreath Filters.
- Zymve performs in Bimblefol Commons, impressing the genlings and deciding to stay there while others visit Tatti.
- Ky'tha has a powerful hallucination / memory tied to Dunduar, Muerg, Cragfury, a sealed ring, and water.
- The party leaves Bimblefol and heads toward the Lost Sanctuary route.
