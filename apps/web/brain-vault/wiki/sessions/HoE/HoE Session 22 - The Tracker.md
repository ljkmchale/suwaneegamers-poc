# HoE Session 22 - The Tracker

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 22
- Session Title: The Tracker
- Date Label In Notes: 04.13
- Real Date: 2026-04-13
- In-Game Date: 36th of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Old Trader's Trail en route to Mount Emberstran
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party watches the dragon attack and crash a flying ship, then chooses to continue toward Mount Emberstran.
- Ainslie sends a message to the dragon and gets a response implying the dragon mistakes him for her master.
- The party realizes someone is following them.
- The party crosses a boiling river full of orange/bronze-scaled fish.
- Ky'tha confronts the follower, Petal, a blonde wood elf tracker apparently working for Lady Rook.
- Petal says she is being paid to watch them and help if needed.
- Ainslie and Zymve scare Petal away after reading her thoughts and learning Lady Rook wants the Marrowstone and tablet if the party succeeds.
- Petal returns anyway the next day, and the party argues over whether to let her follow.
- Zymve kills Petal when the argument stalls, saying she did it because the others could not.
- The party decides to take the right path toward the vault.

## Combats

- Brief nonlethal / restraining conflict with Petal after she tries to flee.
- Petal is killed by Zymve after the group debate.

## NPCs Introduced

- Petal, blonde wood elf tracker.

## Clues Found

- Ainslie may resemble or sound like the dragon's former master.
- The dragon is likely guarding the vault.
- Lady Rook wants what is in the vault and has shown restraint so far.
- Lady Rook ordered Petal to bring back the Marrowstone and tablet if the party found what she needed.
- Hap's sword says they are closer to "neverending."
- Petal knows Dandelia but tries to hide it.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Ky'tha obtains an invisibility potion from Petal.
- Petal drops a purse with 650 gp and potions of invisibility.
- Ainslie gives one invisibility potion back to Petal before discovering she still has one.
- After Petal dies, Ainslie takes a remaining potion of invisibility.

## Decisions Made

- The party continues toward the vault rather than detouring to the crash.
- Ainslie and Zymve intimidate Petal to leave.
- After Petal returns, Zymve kills her.
- The party chooses the right fork, guided by the tablet.

## Consequences

- Lady Rook's relationship with the party becomes much more suspicious.
- Zymve crosses a major moral line in front of the party.
- Ky'tha and others may be unsettled by Petal's killing.
- Ainslie disguises the body as dragon-burned.

## Character Beats

- Zymve is decisive, ruthless, and protective when she decides Petal will not stop.
- Ainslie uses Detect Thoughts, intimidation, Sending, and deception around Petal.
- Aury and Zymve both strongly distrust Lady Rook.
- Ky'tha tries to prevent unnecessary killing but is overridden by events.
- Hap's sword keeps pressing toward the vault.

## Open Threads

- What does Lady Rook want from the vault?
- Why does Petal know Dandelia?
- How will Petal's death affect the party's trust and moral balance?
