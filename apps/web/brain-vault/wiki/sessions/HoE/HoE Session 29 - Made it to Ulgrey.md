## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  June 23, 2026
In-game Date:  47th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  near Featherfall campsite on the Glimmercoast Way
At the Featherfall campsite
Og and Ains successfully defeat the harpy in the sea and swim back to shore, regrouping with the party at the campsite as Ains casts a spell and puts them there.   The Weftirins are dealing with their dead and we offer to stay the night, after getting our gertot, Zabeek, and our gear.  Og and Hap collect them.
We talk to everyone about healing, keeping watch - discussion about what we could have done better. Finn is the one who passed - he is the son of the clothiers Bram and Sable.  Ky’tha goes over to talk to Mira and overhears her talking to Marek - she mentions the name “Veyra Deamonne” (this is the Seer).  Ky’tha interrupts and asks about her and why they are seeking her out.  They reply that it is to determine what to do with Finn.  Ky’tha mentions that maybe Aury could help, but they decline to help because of wanting Cembus’ blessing and not Diverra’s.  We then discuss if we want to escort them further.   After a difference of opinion, we decide to wait until the morning to make the decision. 
Aury wanders from camp, but no one notices.  Ains and Og take the first watch with the Weftirins, and Zymve, Hap, and Ky’tha will rest.   During watch, they discuss how rough the night was, as they bandage themselves. The feel guilt for not making camp with them to begin with. Og steps away from the camp - he sees a shadow of a small humanoid form laying down looking over the edge of the bay.  He says hello and the head turns and puts his finger up to hush Og…he steps forward and sees this is one of the guys who takes care of the instrument wagon.  He feels bad about Finn.  Is questioning if there are more out there…asks where they came from. They briefly discuss this - Og says “they came from the sky.”  They are debating about what the creatures were and where they came from - as Ains joins the conversation.  Og apologizes that he wasn’t able to help.  Ains encourages him to sleep.  Ains tells him that he and Og are Heroes of Emberstran and promises to help by escorting them to Ulgrey.  He tells them that they are not going to stay in Ulgrey, but is thankful for the offer to assist until then.
Og’s Oath
Og then steps forward to perform a rite as part of his following of Leyeth. He vows that the food and coin and weapons will be in service to others.  He then rises to go back to camp and notices Aury kneeling in the woods.  He leaves Aury be.  Og feels warmth in his heart, knowing he is doing the right thing by helping this group and he is reassured that he did help, even though a life was lost.
Aury’s Commune
Meanwhile, Aury sits in the woods.  He feels a cold feeling - he is not getting responses that he is hoping for.  Last time he cast this ritual, he got a response.  As he looks up, he starts to have doubts.  He then feels warmth in the book he is holding and he sees sprouting from the sides of the binder a prick of a thorn hit him and a petal falls off.  He then sees another petal slip up to a page and he opens the book to that page - this is a part of the book he has never read.  This is a tale:  sometimes the direct can’t be answered (this is the message he gets).  As he does this, blood drips from his hand and the first drop that falls goes into the book on the letter Y.  It then blends into the pages, creating a rose watermark.  The letter Y is legible in this watermark.  He is now unsure of what this means.  This is new to him. The second drop of blood misses the book and lands on the ground.  He feels like he isn’t going to bleed anymore.  He closes the book and sees on the title, the blood is smeared into the book (on the N in the word ‘Aeterna’).  He thinks his goddess is trying to communicate in a new way.  These were answers to three questions that he asked privately. Aury picks up the fallen petal and stays there until Aury and Og complete their watch, contemplating what he learned and didn’t.
Ains sits watching - sees nothing new. Og returns.  They convince the boy to return to the instrument carriage.  Og asks his name-it is Root. Og wishes him to sleep well and asks for an introduction to Finn’s parents tomorrow.  The watch ends and they rise to wake up the others for the next watch.  As they stand, Og sees something sparkle on the water, but he can’t focus on what it was.  It was a brief glimpse. Og mentions this to Ains.  They decide to go and check this out, with Og climbing down the side of the cliff.  He makes it down without issue.  He looks out to the rocks and notices there is some type of cave entrance on the side of the rocky outcrop.   He swims out to check this out.  The cave mouth almost seems like a “wound” to the rock, but opens into a cavern. There is foul stink of rot inside.  He looks in and sees above the waterline - some filthy nests and smooth stone.  He sees bones, broken weapons, etc. but sees a 1’ tall statue.  It almost looks like a carving of a goliath.  He sees markings on its heads and arms, but it's hard to tell from afar. He sees a spark of static electricity on it.  He and Ains communicate to each other and Ains offers to go and help see what the object is.  Ains loses his footing and falls as he heads that way, but doesn’t scream out. He swims out and finally makes it to the cave and sees what Og sees.  He uses his wand to detect magic.  He picks up conjuration magic on the idol and also sees enchantment in another nest. They walk into the cave to check out the nests.  They go carefully, looking for traps and find none.  Og finds a bloodstained, large canvas pack and open it.  There are coins in it in a small purse inside the pack.  Tied around the purse is a small silver chain and a locket (25gp). There are 63cp, 18sp, 42gp in the purse. Ains finds a tattered backpack.  He finds two daggers, a torn cloak, with a silver bird clasp (15gp).  They continue on and find a leather satchel (spyglass, set of thieves tools, 2 vials of liquid, a journal where all the ink is smeared, the last entry says (beautiful women singing on the rocks), bottle of ink, quill, 12gp, carved bone whistle) and worn belt pouch (set of bone dice (Og), single pearl earring (25gp), 15gp), as well as various crap.  They also find a reinforced sack (3 days of stale rations, grappling hook (Hap), silver box (5gp)) and drawstring pouch (magic sensed - several coiled strings with enchantment magic; look like musical instrument strings).  They continue forward to the last nest and find an oil skinned sack (it would protect items from getting wet,  27gp, 41sp, small silver mirror (15gp), 2 amberstones) and then Og moves forward to grab the statue.  They think it is a version of Vo’egurn.  Og wraps a rope around the statue and lifts it up as Ains casts shocking grasp on it (he feels the lightning on the head).  They make the way back to the camp. This is an Idol of Vo’egurn with all magic depleted. Ains sits on the cliffside, but refuses to sleep.  He sits with the Idol.  Og goes to sleep.  Ains tells the Weftirins that they don’t need to wake anyone else up.  The leader says he will keep Ains company - this is Marek. 
He thanks Ains for the group’s help.  He says he noticed that he and Og came back from the caves.  He asks what all they found.  Ains shows him the Idol.  He says he knows exactly what that Idol is.  They then have a discussion about who or what might worship Vo’egurn - Ains questions if a follower of a gold dragon might worship him.  Aury walks up to the two sitting on the cliffside.  Aury has been crying.  He kneels in front of Marek and Ains.  Ains gives Marek the bird pendant as he excuses himself.  Aury holds up the petal to Ains and asks for forgiveness, exclaiming that he failed the group and vowing that it will never happen again.  Ains has to make a wisdom saving throw. As Aury does this, before Ains can answer, he looks and sees a red-headed woman giving him a rose. He says out loud - is this a disguise?  And he blinks and now sees Aury.  Ains says that he can stand up.  Aury says to please take my offering.  When Ains does, his wounds heal and Ains feels like he has had a long rest.  He looks spooked.  Aury gets up and walks away.  
Aury then walks back to the group to sleep and feels like the acceptance was more than forgiveness. He thinks there was something more to this.  He then gets a rest.
Ainslie Reaches Out to Irragosa
Ains then casts Sending to Irragosa, holding the Idol.  


“Hey - its Ains - do you worship Vo’egurn.  Trying to figure out god things.  He is lightning and you are lightning and I shoot lightning.  Coincidence.  Good morning.”  
He hears back:  


“Do you make a habit of waking others in the middle of the night?  Yes - I draw energy from Vo’egurn, but I let Ol’Farium guide me.  Good night.”  
He then spends time studying the Idol. He doesn’t think he can attune to it, but thinks it is good he saved this Idol.
The next morning – It’s the 48th of Talil, 1246
All get a long rest. Mira comes over to Ky’tha and says we are going back to Emberstran.  We have responses back and we think going that way will be better.  She tells her that the group was planning to escort them and Mira thanks her for the group’s help.  They start to pack up.  Ky’tha thanks Mira for the dreamcatcher and says she looks forward to sleeping with it soon.  Aury finds Finn’s parents and hands them a sack with 25gp.  They are thankful and sad.  Og tries to give much of the loot from the cave to Finn’s parents.  They accept the items, but present him with the Shademoor Lute in return - it is solid black.
Zymve rolls her eyes as she sees them proffer items to Finn’s family. The group splits the gold and silver that Ains and Og found.  Aury studies the Idol - thinks this is something that belongs in a temple.  Doesn’t think the magic will return.  He thinks about Ulgrey and remembers there is a hill or area there devoted to Torec (god of elements) who Vo’egurn was a demigod for - thinks he heard something about Cembus.  Thinks maybe a group there went from worshipping Phoe to Torec to Cembus.  Hap takes the grappling hook, Ky’tha takes the earring (with some coaxing from the group) and asks Zymve to pierce her ear at the next camp, Og offers Zymve the Shademoor Lute and she takes it. Ains says he is planning to give the Idol to the gold dragon (Aury tells him it might be worth several hundred gold pieces).
As the group walks, Zymve talks to the group about why everyone feels guilty and why no one woke her up.  Ains says he thought everyone could use more rest and says he wanted to be alone.  We discuss having a group earring.  Ky’tha knows that we are heading to the Old Lodge - and there will be a ferry on the other side of the river.
At the River Ring Rest
We make it to the dock and stand on the edge of the river.  We see a bell that we are supposed to ring.  It is small and on a pole.  Og rings it, but hears no sound.  We see the bell tower in the distance ring and see the ferryman start to pole over from the other side.  This ferry is large and flat.  It can bring over the entire group, including Ewing. This is the Harbok River.  He comments on the gertot and Og says his name is Ewing.  The ferryman is Tomas (discussion over the pronunciation of the name).  We introduce ourselves as the Heroes of Emberstran.  He welcomes us to River Ring Rest - encourages us to get drinks, unloads us, etc.  Unfortunately, Ewing cannot go inside.  There is a stable.  Tomas says that “Ol’Fen” will take care of Ewing and we comment how odd it is that the stableman’s name is Finn.  
There is an older lady walking around the lodge - catch as catch can.  We can all stay and it won’t cost much.  Aury offers her a gp, as does Ains, as well as Ky’tha and Hap.  4gp for the group.  They take good care of Ewing and offer us a good place to rest.  
A Shattered Heart Clue
There is a large fireplace, a warm place to rest.  We walk through and Zymve looks above the fireplace at the massive stone hearth and sees a tapestry fragment that isn’t quite complete and it looks foreign to her, but here it would look like a stitching from a grandma.  It is two hands intertwined together - one side looks pulled away on purpose.  As she studies it, she finds a need to tap Aury and points this out to him - this looks like you, Aury - she says.  Aury likes it. He thinks this is a love - torn piece - this is where something went wrong in someone’s life.  This is a shattered heart piece.  He recognizes that this is part of the tenants that he was supposed to find in the lands.  The group questions Aury - who is very excited - and tells him to pull out his book and figure out what he is supposed to do. 
He finds the passage he needs.  Whatever shattered heart symbol he finds, the only way for Diverra to intervene is for him to take it to a place of worship for Diverra.  The group decides that Ains will talk to the woman. The group gets a table.  Ains gets up with his stew and stands in front of the hearth, catching the lady as she walks by.  “Excuse me madam (lovely lady) - she is flattered.  She apologizes for the dull stew.  He spices his food up with magic and she tastes it.  He then brings up the tapestry and asks if she stitched it.  She says she made it for her husband.  He was caught in a storm and tried to save them in the river and he passed away.  Every year, she pulls a thread off of it in remembrance of him.  She balls it up and puts it under her pillow once a year on their anniversary.  She says they never found his body and she never got a chance to say goodbye as it was a chaotic night during the storm.  She asks if Ains can keep her company that night and he says he knows of someone who could stay with her, but she laughs it off and says she doesn’t need that care.  She says it has been 2 decades since he was killed.  He tells her that he is from Emberstran and shows her his badge, telling her he is an investigator.  He offers her closure to try to find his body.  She is freaked out, but says that he can help her if he wants.  This seems to offer her hope, but she is trying not to be hopeful.  He tells her about Aury. He says it is our responsibility to help her since she clearly loved her husband.  He persuades her and she says to make ourselves at home. 
Ains walks to the group and shares the story.  Og cries.  Zymve says it has been “two fucking decades.” Aury isn’t sure how to help.  Hap and Aury know that the Awakening occurred 2 decades ago…and that the river changed as the landscape changed.  We discuss that we can help her, but it may take time.  Aury offers to pray to Diverra to see if he can get answers.  Zymve, Zabeek, Ky’tha and Hap will go to get a piercing.  Zymve pierces Ky’tha’s right ear and then makes and pierces Zabeek’s left ear.  Turns out that ol’Hap already has cuffs on both ears. 
Ains and Og walk up to the lady (named Maren Bellright).  Tell her that Aury is reaching out to Diverra.  She says she has prayed to Diverra for 20 years.  Og produces the Idol for her to touch. They tell her they are taking her case, but it may take some time and it may take some extra connection.  They then ask for the tapestry and tell her that they need to study it then take it to a Diverra shrine or temple. She asks when will it come back…and they say that it will be back before her anniversary in 2 harmons.  She asks Og to lift her up and she pulls off a thread and rolls it up and gives it to Ains.  They say they will avoid lava-based situations (and then have to improvise about the lava situation).  Then they go to the room for the night.
Meanwhile, Aury prays and enacts his ritual spell.  He lights up  the candle and incense, uses his book, etc.  He gets a quick answer to his first question - a coolness that is a “no.”  The second question (about being close by) and gets no response.  Then for the third question the candle flickers and he hears a clinking noise and the ring where he held the candlestick fell off and rolled to his feet.  It has no damage.  This is a sign.  He needs to figure out a sign of what. He then gets up and thanks Diverra and impresses upon her that he is trying not to be a bother.  He feels a warmth.  He then goes back down the stairs to find Ains and Og and notices the tapestry is off the wall.  
He says that he got answers.  He says that the body is not recoverable and that it is therefore not close by. But then tells them about the ring on the candlestick. Ains looks at the object and knows it is a ring from a candlestick. They discuss that maybe they need to find the husband’s ring. They discuss if they have arcane skills to find the ring.  Aury says that he could ask his goddess questions like that once a day. He says he could sleep on this topic and might have an answer tomorrow.
Hap has a chat with his sword at bedtime.  It says “more, stronger” to him.  He thinks that it is waiting for him to fight something strong and powerful before it will bond with him further.
We go to sleep.
It’s the next day – the 1st of Paramor, 1246
In the morning, Aury feels like he could use magic to locate the ring.  We smell muffins and bacon.  There is fresh gertot milk.  We sit and eat. Aury shares his new found power and says he will try it.  He studies Maren’s ring then casts the spell, walking to the docks.  He picks up a signal downstream.  The group walks along the shore on the bank and he casts the spell again - and thinks it is in the center of the river down below.  Ky’tha offers up the immovable rod to help locate the precise point and keep him from moving with the current.  Og wades in - it is cold.  He stays on the surface following Aury’s command then dives down.  He has some issues.  Ains asks if he is doing ok.  He surfaces.  Zymve offers to inspire him and sings him a song. Ains levitates Aury over the water and asks him to tell him when he is directly over the spot.  Ky’tha offers to try and, inspired by Zymve, is able to find it.  She levitates up with Ains help and he dries her off.  She hands Aury the ring and Ains cleans it - he sees a worn design pattern. Zymve tries to mend it with her jewelcrafting skills.  With Aury’s guidance (it was a bell design), she casts her spell and is able to mend it and bring it back to its origin.  She gives it back to Ains.  Ains then takes it to Maren. 
We find Maren.  “That bacon was the shit” says Zymve.  Ains tells her to come outside and he lowers the ring into the water and then Zymve and Hap with spells make the wind blow and the earth shake and Ains touches Aury’s head.  Zymve plays her oboe.  Ky’tha’s raven flies around.  Og is having cool mist rise up from the river.  Ains asks her to describe the ring and makes a theatrical production of raising it from the water.  She holds her hands out and he levitates it to her and she holds out her hands and grabs it.  She starts crying, exclaiming “my precious.”  Ains tells her that her love protected its form.  She is very grateful.  She will tell everyone what the EGD did to help.  She says this is more than she had for 20 years…and is glad to know where he died.  She knows that divine magic was in use.  
We pack up and head out.  We travel all day and arrive at a plain campsite that night.  When we arrive there seem to be hunters hanging out.  We are welcomed by the hunters.  We take turns with the watch.  Ains and Zymve, Og and Ky’tha, Aury and Hap last.  
First watch - Ains apologizes for not reading the book lately.  She says she was mad that he didn’t wake her up the other night.  He says he thought she was tired.  They joke about her dark circles under her eyes.  They talk about reading, but he asks her to play music with her new instrument.  He makes a string break.  Wow - this lute is “a piece of shit.”  He says “if only you had a new set of strings” and pulls out the new strings.  She wonders if he caused the string to pop, but thinks he didn’t.  He pulls out the magical string and gives it to her to replace the broken strings.  Once they are in tune….they shrink to the size of the instrument.  Once she plays they never get out of tune.  Ains sees the enchantment magic coming off of the strings as if they are empowering Zymve.  He knows that any enchantment spell that she casts while playing, her difficulty level for the saving throw will increase by +1.  She doesn’t know that yet.  He says that she should play more often now that she sounds better now.  She casts vicious mockery on him in response to the teasing, but it has no impact on him.  
Ky’tha and Og take their watch - briefly talk about the cold water.  They wake up Aury and Hap.  Aury confesses that he should have saved the child and how he still feels bad.  Hap tries to make him feel better and he eases Aury’s woes.  
Night passes.
It’s the next day – the 2nd of Paramor, 1246
We see that the hunters are gone.  We pack up.  Zabeek says his piercing is itchy and asks if it is okay.  We spend the day traveling to Ulgrey.  We arrive by crossing a bridge that leads to the town.  Most buildings are constructed of wood.  We work our way around the town to the stables to drop off Ewing and then make it to the Riverdog Inn. 
The Gilded Vulture Marking
As we approach the Inn and are passing the tower to the left of the Inn.  Something grabs Og’s attention.  He sees a little stylized emblem at the base of the tall tower.  It looks like a bird in flight.  It is similar to the bird on the cloak pin that he gave to Finn’s parents.  He thinks this is a vulture.  He thinks that this is a sigil of the Gilded Vulture.  A note is stuck up under the emblem.  A silver-haired gabük steps out, pulls off the letter and walks back in.  A black cat sitting in a window in the tower looks down at us and jumps down and disappears.  Og doesn’t say anything but he looks distressed (mentally says to Ains the Gilded Vulture is here…not sure if the gabük recognized us).  He comments that we need to get inside the Inn - that he is starving.  
At the Riverdog Inn
We go into the Inn, noting that there are boats on the docks outside.  It is busy, but not bustling.  We smell meats, ale, the fire in the hearth, hear laughter.  A female looks our way and says “I’ll be right there (motioning with her hands).”  Ky’tha sees her using thieves’ cant to identify herself as Etterset...no one else notices this.  She walks away and we take a seat and the barmaid sets down drinks “the first round is on us, but the next round will cost you.”  Ky’tha asks about the specialty.  She says her name is Shanna.  The specialty is kahlbit - but that they don’t tell most people.  We all introduce ourselves - Zymve says “hmmmm” and Og says “stone” and Ains says “Mossy.”  Ky’tha signs back and says “how about bread and cheese and ale”, Aury says “meat” and Zymve says “the special sounds good.”  Shanna whispers in her “are you here about Phira?” As Ains overhears this.  Ky’tha responds that “yes - any cheese sounds great to me” as she nods.  
As Zymve asks about kahlbit, Ky’tha tells the group that she is already working on why they are there - that she has made contact.  The group is surprised by this, but trust Ky’tha.  Og is looking around and on guard about his surroundings. He thinks to himself - why is that group over here - the Vultures?  Ky’tha - reacting to his unease - tells him not to worry that the waitress is to be trusted.  Og tells the group that is not why is he is upset.  He tells the group that he used to work for a mercenary group called the Gilded Vultures.  Ains says that they rescued him a long time ago.  And Og says that the two of them ran from this group.  They say that their leader - a blue dragonborn - Vilmuss - is a bad person and wanted Ains for his magical powers.  Ky’tha asks how Og knows they are here in Ulgrey.  He tells the group that he saw the sigil on the tower - that it must be a place that messages are exchanged by this group. He says he did not recognize the gabük, nor does he think they she recognized him.  Ky’tha offers to ask her new contact about this group’s presence in the city. 
As we sit here, the door from the dock opens up and two patrons walk in.  The place goes quiet.  A female soldier in half plate wearing a Meridian tabard steps in, carrying herself with pride.  As much as she looks important, the person she is with is much more important - draws more attention.  This is a wizard in a regal blue robe with gold trim and an ornate staff.  He has a wiry, white beard.  He commands attention.  He comments “That was nice to travel that way once in a while.”  Shanna says “Welcome to Ulgrey - to what do we owe this visit?”  The wizard says “I have heard of some strange events in these parts and thought I would check them out for myself.”  
– The End of the Session –
