# HoE Session 19 - Lost Sanctuary

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 19
- Session Title: Lost Sanctuary
- Date Label In Notes: 03.02
- Real Date: 2026-03-02
- In-Game Date: 30th of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Underdark
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party reaches the Crimson Lake and identifies the stone ring beneath the water as the target from Ky'tha's vision.
- Aury activates the underwater gate through Diverra's guidance and the party passes through.
- The party emerges at the Lost Sanctuary of Diverra, where a dying Life Tree and corrupted temple grounds show Blackrend-like blight.
- The party fights shadowy blighted tree and root creatures.
- Aury falls during the fight but is later restored on the altar inside the sanctuary.
- Zymve claims the black Studded Leather +1 from the defeated dark druids; Ky'tha takes the Mantle of Spell Resistance.
- Og answers the call of the Life Tree and learns of the Alferumn, E'erspan, and Dandelia of the Veil.
- Aury receives Bloom Aeterna, the complete original teachings of Diverra, and learns about the Shattered Hearts.
- Zymve hears a melody connected to Ainslie's journal, takes the book, and senses the name Szoradryn.

## Combats

- Combat against blighted tree creatures, root vines, needle blights, and dark druid-like bodies.

## NPCs Introduced

- Dandelia of the Veil is named as a future lead.
- Diverra's presence is strongly active through the sanctuary and Bloom Aeterna.

## Clues Found

- The underwater gate is divine or demi-divine in origin.
- The Lost Sanctuary of Diverra is real and reachable through the gate.
- Blackrend-like blight has reached the sanctuary.
- The Life Tree is connected to the Alferumn and calls Og as E'erspan.
- Bloom Aeterna identifies Shattered Hearts as fragments of lost passion that must be returned to proper sacred places.
- Zymve hears music from Ainslie's journal and connects it to Szoradryn.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Zymve gains Studded Leather +1.
- Ky'tha gains Mantle of Spell Resistance.
- Aury gains Bloom Aeterna.
- Aury gains a ritual route back to the sanctuary using the book and petals.

## Decisions Made

- The party rests at the sanctuary after Aury's restoration and Og's tree communion.
- Zymve pockets Ainslie's journal for the moment.

## Consequences

- Aury's Diverra arc becomes central and concrete.
- Og's Tree of Life / Alferumn arc becomes active.
- The party now has a sacred return point through Bloom Aeterna and petals.
- Zymve becomes entangled with Ainslie's journal and drow-linked mystery.

## Character Beats

- Aury is restored by the sanctuary altar and receives his most important religious text.
- Og is called by the Life Tree and given a guardian identity.
- Zymve hears a complete, dark melody others cannot hear.
- Hap recognizes the original purpose of his vision pairing him with Aury.

## Open Threads

- Who is Dandelia of the Veil?
- What are the Shattered Hearts and where do they belong?
- Why does Ainslie's journal affect Zymve?
- What does Blackrend want with sacred groves and life trees?
- **Aury has not told High Priestess Meladra about Bloom Aeterna.** He received Diverra's complete original teachings directly, and senses that because he was chosen to receive it, Meladra may not be worthy of it. The tension between Aury and Meladra around this secret is unresolved.
