# HoE Session 07 - Divine Waypoint

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 07
- Session Title: Divine Waypoint
- Date Label In Notes: 07.09
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The stone platform rises into a cave with a Diverra statue and an arcane circle. The circle appears to be an Arcane Waypoint/teleportation sigil Aury can use with the right spell.
- Hap notices a smudge/rune on the statue. Og removes it, revealing runes that match the floor and suggest a route to sanctuary.
- Ainslie shows Aury the blight champion's scepter/mace; after rest, it is identified as the Scepter of Adoration.
- From a ledge, Og and Ky'tha see the forest arranged like Diverra's arms, with waterfalls and marked paths.
- Ky'tha retrieves a magical silver bird figurine with quartz eyes while scouting near waterfalls and dralg.
- Rongri, small gliding fey-like creatures, interact with Ainslie and Hap and guide the party toward a whirlpool/skeleton find.
- Og recovers a skeleton and amulet from the water and returns a primate skeleton to the rongri.
- The amulet is later identified as an Amulet of Proof Against Detection and Location.
- The party follows the frog path into a marshy clearing and fights swamp/fey creatures.
- Ainslie invisibly explores a cave with a large beast and two kits, finds a magical bone/scrollcase, and withdraws.
- Back in Emberstran, Meladra arranges or provides the Arcane Waypoint/Activate Sigil scroll and discusses training Arnol through Captain Vammad.
- The next day, Lady Rook sends carts for the party. Og and Ainslie decline by deception, Aury and Hap delay, and Ky'tha rides to the tower.

## Combats

- Swamp/fey frog creatures with sleep, grapple, and whirlpool effects.

## NPCs Introduced

- Rongri.
- Large cave beast and kits.
- Arnol's training thread with Meladra/Vammad.

## Clues Found

- The Diverra waypoint can be activated through sigils and divine/arcane interaction.
- The grove path points toward a hidden sanctuary.
- Rongri appear connected to the wisp/grove ecology.
- Rook immediately follows up after the grove is cleared.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Scepter of Adoration.
- Silver bird figurine with quartz eyes.
- Amulet of Proof Against Detection and Location.
- Protection Against Beasts scroll from bone scrollcase.
- Nine daggers from fey-creature bodies.
- Arcane Waypoint / Activate Sigil scroll from Meladra.

## Decisions Made

- The party studies the waypoint but returns to Emberstran before using it.
- Og gives the anti-detection amulet to Ainslie.
- Ainslie gives Og the bone/scrollcase to open.
- The party does not all answer Rook's summons together.

## Consequences

- Aury gains the practical key for using the waypoint.
- The sanctuary path becomes reachable but not yet taken.
- Ainslie gains a strong anti-scrying/anti-location protection.
- Rook's pressure on the party escalates.

## Character Beats

- Ainslie: Curious and risk-taking with the beast/kits; gains anti-detection protection.
- Aurelius / Aury: Becomes the party's waypoint activator and continues mentoring Arnol.
- Hap: Connects with the rongri/grove side of the route.
- Ky'tha: Scouts aggressively, retrieves the bird figurine, and becomes the party member who answers Rook directly.
- Og: Recovers the amulet, rescues Ky'tha during river crossing, and anchors the party physically.
- Zymve: Not yet in the HoE party.
