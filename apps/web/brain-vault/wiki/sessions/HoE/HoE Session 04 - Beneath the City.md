# HoE Session 04 - Beneath the City

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 04
- Session Title: Beneath the City
- Date Label In Notes: 05.28
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party fights the giant tick that landed on Aury; Ky'tha kills it and Aury burns it.
- Ky'tha and Fleur scout the tunnels beneath Emberstran.
- In an alcove with a ladder, the party overhears voices mentioning "backing the Etterset," "gathering them," and "Lady Rook."
- A sewer route reveals cultists of Sijulous Ghone operating below the city.
- Ky'tha climbs out behind a Northern District building, likely near the Crimson Swan, and marks the access point with three ball bearings.
- In an old tavern, the party fights ghouls and finds the BONE tablet fragment, matching the prior tablet/barrel mystery.
- The party finds a body in lava/rubble with armor and weapons.
- In a flooded chamber, the party fights a giant octopus and crabs; Hap kills the octopus and Ainslie retrieves a wand sheath.
- The party opens the records room/archive vault and recovers the land-record tome and a special Knock scroll.
- The records show the Cetaen family did not properly own the disputed land. The land passed through Benoit/Garemon history before being reclaimed.
- Hap realizes Benoit is likely his father and that Phira may be his sister, not merely a cousin.
- The next morning, Aury and Hap learn Priestess Meladra is missing.
