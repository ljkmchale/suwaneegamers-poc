# Session 28 - Those Effers Ruined My Boots

Source: raw/hoe-campaign-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ENCKlQLCpkjefs8AgZYXn0_89OgUmJx5ssIFMuOKut4/edit

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  June 23, 2026
In-game Date:  47th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  near Featherfall campsite on the Glimmercoast Way
At the Featherfall campsite
“Old Hap ain't skeert of no musical beguilements.”
The session begins picking up at the campsite with the party attempting to rescue the Weftirins that have been lured off the cliff by harpies.
* ROUND ONE:  Og appears in the camp and sees the campers trying to go down the cliff with a rope and chaos at the top.  He asks “what’s happening?”  The song has called our friends and two have fallen - he gets in reply.  He moves to the edge of the cliff and helps one of the campers with the rope - tying it off to a wagon.  A winged creature in the water pops up and starts to sing - Og makes a CON saving throw to avoid hearing it. The camper at the top of the cliff hears the song and falls down.  Three more winged creatures fly in.  One screeches and targets a camper who avoids it.  Ains gets to the edge of the campsite and interacts with Og who says “bird ladies singing and hurting others.”  Ains dashes forward and offers help, but can’t do anything this turn.  Hap enters the campsite and dashes forward and uses his action surge to hit one of the winged creatures with a firebolt.  Aury enters the campsite and prays and sends a spark out to winged creatures in the distance, casting fireball at two of them (one saves and one doesn’t).  One of the campers looks over the edge and says “they don’t look good at all.”  Another tries to climb down with the rope and slips and falls onto a ledge - hurting his leg.  Another is scared and climbs into a wagon.  Another grabs his child and climbs into a wagon yelling “save our family.”  Zymve dashes forward in the direction of Hap and hides behind a tree.  Kytha gets to the edge of the cliff and shoots her crossbow at the one Hap attacked and hits.  Two creatures emerge from rocks in the water and move forward - they sound like lightning as they crack when they move forward. 
* ROUND TWO:  Og finishes tying off the rope, rages, and then moves to the cliffside to help one of the fallen campers, trying to stabilize him and does so then moves him from the cliff’s edge. One of the winged creatures start to sing against Ky’tha and charms her.  Another one focuses on Ains and charms him. Another flies closer to the group as another flies behind a tree, seeking cover.  One flies to Hap and attacks - first squealing at him (and he has to make a saving throw and fails)...inflicting psychic damage and imposing disadvantage.  Then tries to claw him and hits, but he parries some of the damage.  One looks up at a camper on the cliffside and he is dropped unconscious.  Ains starts to move toward the creature who charmed it.  He continues to be charmed. Hap has Fleur negative disadvantage and then attacks with his sword and hits. Then hits with the pruning dagger. Then crits with his sword, damaging the creature’s wing/hand.  Then crits again with the dagger.  Aury moves forward 5’ to see past the wagon and casts another fireball at two in the trees (both fail their saves). One dies and a tree goes up in flames.  The campers who are out make death saves. Zymve casts a spell on the winged creature nearest to her and takes her down.  She then casts inspiration on Ky’tha, Ains, and Hap - giving 8 temp hp.  Ky’tha fails a save and walks off the cliff, hitting the shallow ground taking damage and swimming 5’ forward.  The strange creatures in the water move forward - one moves up to Ky’tha and sparks fly around - attacking her with lightning bolts, hitting once, then missing, but she takes additional damage because she’s in range of the creature.  The charm falls off.  The other one swirls up and there is a vortex and it rises in the water and flies next to Aury and bolts shoot out at him, missing twice.  But he takes damage because he’s standing next to it.  And two more similar creatures appear in the water.
* ROUND THREE: Og makes his way to the bottom of the cliff using the rope and asks Ky’tha if she needs help (she says she can take care of herself).  The winged creatures go - one flies behind some rocks, another tries to charm Zymve and she fails and tries again and saves, and another flies over the water and tries to charm Hap and doesn’t. Ains is no longer charmed.  He looks over the edge and levitates one of the camper children up in the air. Hap runs over to the strange creature in front of Aury and is able to determine his resistances and immunities - and yells some of this to the group (poison, weapons, paralysis, grappled).. Then attacks with his sword and hits.  He then tries to menace him and he takes additional damage. Then attacks with the dagger and hits. Then attacks with a short sword, making a critical hit.  Aury sprouts his wings, and the creature misses his opportunity attack, then he casts fireball at some of the creatures in the water (2 save and 1 fails). The campers make death saves. Zymve casts shatter at two of the creatures in the water (1 saves and 1 fails).  And she inspires Ky’tha.  Og’s reaction pulls Ky’tha  to him. She then cunning action dash-climbs up the cliff, shoots a crossbow at a harpy and hits, then moves another 10’ to get behind a wagon.  One of the creatures flies up next to Zymve and misses, then crits her with lightning, limiting her to half movement by injuring her foot (and ruining one of her boots), then she takes additional damage at the end of the turn.  Another one attacks Hap and hits, then misses.  And he takes additional damage, but uses a hellish rebuke and the creature takes a point of damage.  The other two move forward in the water, and Og takes some lightning damage at the end of its turn.
* ROUND FOUR: Og grabs a camper and realizes he is dead and then moves back to stab one of the elemental creatures and hits twice, pushing one 15’ away and slowing it.  The harpies go-one flies next to Ains and attacks, missing twice. Another flies up and looks at and screeches at Ains and he fails and takes damage. It then flies behind a tree.  Ains looks at the kid he is levitating and the man at his feet and decides to climb down to the child and he falls to a ledge below, he also takes damage from an opportunity attack from the harpy next to him.  He then stands up and gets under the child and releases the telekinesis.  He catches the child and stabilizes him.  Hap attacks the elemental creature and hits with his sword, then hits with the dagger. Then hits again with the sword and dagger.   Aury casts healing word at Ky’tha then casts sacred flame on the harpy on the cliff and it fails.  The campers make death saves. Zymve moves the cloud of daggers to the elemental next to her and it takes damage. Ky’tha turns and shoots a bolt at the elemental on Zymve and crits with a sneak attack, killing it.  An elemental tries to attack Hap and misses, but he takes a little lightning damage.  Another moves up on the ledge next to Og and attacks and hits twice and he takes additional damage from the bolts at the end of his turn. And a harpy moves back to the rocks.
* ROUND FIVE:  Og interacts with Ains and holds his reaction to try to catch a camper who is about to fall off the cliff.  One of the harpies flies to Ains and screeches and he fails, taking damage and imposing disadvantage.  Then misses her next attack.  Another harpy flies down and attacks Ains with her claws and hits with both. Ains telekinetically shoves a camper into Og’s hands, who is levitating - he catches him and stabilizes him.  Hap gets some help from Fleur and hits the elemental twice, with sword and dagger.  Then misses with his sword, but hits with the dagger again.  Aury looks around and casts mass healing word on the party and one of the campers. Then tolls the dead on one of the harpies and she saves. One of the campers stands up and runs next to Ky’tha and asks what he should do?  She replies “get in the wagon.”  Zymve moves on the cliff edge 15’ closer to the harpies and casts ray of sickness on the flying harpy and hits. Ky’tha shoots at the elemental on Hap and gets sneak attack damage.  That guy then hits Hap with one of his attacks and he takes additional lightning damage.  One flies up and hits Zymve with lightning damage and then additional damage from being in range, and she loses her concentration on her spell. Another moves across and Ains takes damage from being in range and the child drops down again from that damage.
* ROUND SIX:  Og holds his action to dash with the camper to put him to safety. Harpies:  one fumbles and another attacks Ains and misses twice.  The last one flies up on top of the cliff.  Ains moves Og to the wall and then stabilizes the child and moves him from the lightning, giving the harpy a chance to opportunity attack him, but she misses (shield).  He then drops the kid off and then moves back to put himself between the harpy and child.  Telekinesis drops and Og’s reaction sets in.  He tries to climb up the wall and makes it.  He drops off the camper at the top of the cliff and finishes his movement. Hap attacks the elemental and hits with his sword, then hits with the dagger.  Then hits with the sword again, then hits with the dagger again, killing it.  Then he runs up next to the elemental next to Zymve.  Aury uses radiance of the dawn to attack the harpy and elemental.  Then heals Zymve. The camper jumps in the wagon. Zymve attacks the harpy with a spell (it fails).  It takes psychic damage and disadvantage is imposed. Ky’tha shoots a bolt at the elemental on Zymve and hits with a sneak attack.  The elementals attack:  one attacks Ains and misses twice, but he takes damage from being in range, as does the child.  The other attacks Hap and Zymve, missing Hap and then hitting Zymve.  She takes damage, and then both take more damage being in range, and both lose concentration on their spells.
* ROUND SEVEN:  Og runs up to the elemental on the cliffside and attacks twice and hits both times. Harpies attack:  the one in the air attacks Aury and hits with her claw.  The other attacks Ains and misses twice. Ains moves the child to safety.  Hap attacks the elemental and then pushes it off the cliff and it dies.  And then he heals himself.  Aury casts mass healing word on the party and the child.  Then he flashes his channel divinity at the harpy (she fails save) and kills it.  The child stands up and starts to run to the wagon.  Zymve attacks the harpy at the bottom of the cliff with a dissonant whisper spell and it saves, but takes damage and is frightened.  Ky’tha runs to the edge of the cliff and looks down, climbs down and misses with her crossbow.  The last harpy screeches at Ains and he takes psychic damage and flies off.  The other elemental attacks Ains and hits then misses and he takes damage, plus damage in range.  Once the harpy flew off, the elemental disappears.
* ROUND EIGHT:  Og drops down and takes damage and jumps on the harpy, attacking her and killing her. Ains taps Og and they dimension door to the location of the harpy and try to fall on her and grab her as they do so.  Hap gets to the bottom to help the campers.  Aury mass heals the party near him and the camper with the broken leg.  The camper stands up and asks what is going on - and Aury tells him to get in the wagon.  Zymve is upset about the hole in her foot, exclaiming, “Those f***ers ruined my boots, do you know how much they cost?!”.  She proceeds to cast mending on them. Ky’tha climbs down to help the guy with the broken leg.  The harpy has to make a strength save and fails and it is wrapped in the branches in Og’s hands. It then charms Og.  
* ROUND NINE:  Og is charmed.  Ains looks into the water and lightning bolts it and it is immune.  He then selects Og to be able to talk in their heads. Hap goes down the rope to help at the bottom.  Aury flies out over the water, is now 80’ away (as Og and the harpy are 10’ underwater and Ains is bobbing on the surface).   Zymve helps with rescue efforts, as does Ky’tha.  The harpy swims to the surface and flies 30’ then dashes away.  
* ROUND TEN:  Og swims up and after her.  Ains looks in the air and misty steps to her and tries to grab her and restrains her and pulls her into the water.  Aury flies back to the cliff.  Harpy is restrained and charms Ains, incapacitating him.  Og is no longer charmed. {The rest of the party tends to the campers the remainder of the fight}
* ROUND ELEVEN:  Og swims to the harpy and rages, and is 10’ away from her.  Ains swims to the harpy. 
The harpy is under Og and underwater and restrained.
– The End of the Session –
