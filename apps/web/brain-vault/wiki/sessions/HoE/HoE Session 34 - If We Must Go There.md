# Session 34 - If We Must Go There

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date: August 27, 2026
In-game Date:  4th of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Lost Shrine to Guigzien
Resting In the Upper Level of the Lost Shrine
We all sit in the circular chamber.  Zabeek is smushed and dead.  We feel like we could rest here.  Aury says a prayer over Zabeek and Ains lights a fire to serve as a pyre for his body.  Ains sends a message to the rock gnomes to tell them of his passing (he tells them that he died heroically and sacrificed himself to save the party - he was very nice).   He receives another message back where the gnome says that he was not surprised, etc.
Ky’tha takes her cloaks and places one under Hap’s head and the other to cover Hap. We agree to take watch:  Ains and Aury first (and they mend the door); Og and Zymve second; Ky’tha third watch.  Aury and Ains discuss the circle that the people used and realize that it is not permanent and therefore not useful.  Ains shares the sigils he knows (Baltwood Forest, Diverra’s Grove (but he can’t use it without a priest), Emberstran, etc.  They discuss needing a diamond - and going to Emberstran, Basctdelm, or back to the underdark. Ains also shares that him pulling Og out of the fight was not an indictment of Aury’s skills - it was just something he could do to save his friend.  Aury says it was probably for the best and that he understands.  They wake up Zymve and Og and both take a rest.  Neither are talkative.  Zymve is carving Zabeek in a piece of wood. Og thanks Zymve for grabbing his leaf.  They wake up Ky’tha and take a rest. She talks to Hap’s dead body and tells him it will be ok.  Toward the end of her watch she hears something outside the doors - a loud fluttering.   Zymve is awakened by Yazzdyrin - sent from Thaelex.  He messages her.  She gets up to open the door and Ky’tha questions what’s going on.  Zymve opens the door as Ky’tha stands by.
They see a giant dragon fly transition into a small trinket and see a half elf putting it in his pocket.  He is holding the sending stone that belongs to Thaelex.  He looks slightly drow (half drow).  She doesn’t notice a house symbol.  Thaelex said it was bad - I’m hoping it isn’t as bad.   He introduces himself as a member of the Meridian, says he is from the Tower of Myr.  Ky’tha wakes up the rest of the group and introduces him.  Aury starts to cast “remove curse” from Ains and he feels better.  He then heals him.  Zymve talks to him about having his help to get the group to Basctdem.  Ains asks where the circle is at the Tower and he is told that it is beneath it.  Aury then casts remove curse and gentle response on Hap.  Ky’tha asks him what flasks he has and he says he has some healing potions (2 regular healing potions).  He offers to help the group pull the carriage out of the mud with his horse. The group has a huddle to talk about what to do.   Ky’tha and Zymve give up trying to convince the group to go to Basctdelm.  Aury, Og, and Ains win the argument to go to Emberstran.  
We go outside and find Yazzdyrin.  Ky’tha asks him if he will be the holder of the sending stone. He says that it will be him. Thaelex got a message from the Queen of Basctdelm (Lady Talward) and had to go there to take care of matters (of which he is not privy).  Meanwhile, Ains asks Befast Umberfist (jeweler in Emberstran) if he has a 500gp diamond available for purchase.  He replies that he’s on holiday and to get back to him in a few days.  Ains then reaches out to the gnome who worked for Umberfirst with the same question.  She replies that it is early and to give her an hour or two.  We then decide to take Yazzdyrin up on his offer of help.  We decide to use his horse and the abandoned carriage to take the group to Ulgrey.  From there we will take a boat to the Tower of Myr.  Then at least a couple of people will go to Basctdelm to get a diamond.
Back in Ulgrey
Og puts Hap into the carriage, Aury heals Ains (now that the curse is lifted), and the group heads to Ulgrey. We arrive at the stablemaster.  This carriage has a bad reputation in the town.  Ains how much refurbishment of the carriage would be and pays him 1gp to do it (double the price). We make our way to the Riverdog.  While Zymve and Yazzdyrin arrange passage on a boat, Ky’tha seeks out Shanna to talk with her.  Ains, Aury, and Og seek out a temple and ask if they can keep Hap’s body for a couple of days.  Zymve negotiates the price of 4sp (down from 5sp).  Ky’tha finds Shanna and asks if she knows of Etterset contacts in Basctdelm - she doesn’t.  As the others arrive at the Riverdog, Yazzdyrin puts his hand on Ains and tells him that Thaelex has spotted Phira in Basctdelm.  Ky’tha makes her way to the church to meet the others and runs into Yazzdyrin.  He asks her if there is anything else she’d want to know about Phira.  He messages back to Thaelex and tells Ky’tha that Phira was seen at The Open Quill with a tiefling.  She looked happy and was not in captivity and there was no Cloak presence. Plans continue to be discussed.  Aury won’t give up the body so we agree to keep him with us. Yazzdyrin goes to the docks to talk to Zymve.  They talk about the underdark and how she gets along with the others.
The group boards a boat for the Tower of Myr.  The entire party goes, including Hap. Zymve, upon learning about Phira, thinks that the Cloak won’t kill Phira since she’s our only leverage.  She lobbies hard for the entire group to go to Basctdelm.    The group consolidates their gold- Aury gives 119 gold, Kytha gives 80 gold, 133 gold are removed from Hap, and the items in the party inventory are in total worth about 900 gold.  Ky’tha asks Yazzdyrin to ask Thaelex if the Queen needs any help in Basctdelm.  He responds that the tiefling that Phira is hanging out with is Vesper Sall and that Phira has a house in the North district of the city.  And that the Queen needs no help - it is political.  
Arriving at the Tower of Myr
We arrive at the Tower of Myr and see the blue banner of the Meridian. Yazzdyrin speaks to the door and it opens (magic mouth).  We see a human male in his 40s dressed in mail and a human female in her 30s.  “These are the individuals that Thaelex was talking about”  - this is Sargent Calvendorr.  We introduce ourselves.  He asks how Hap died.  Ains speaks for the group and explains the backstory. They ask if we are associated with the Gilded Vultures, Cloak of Defiance, Etterset, etc.  We tell him that Hap might be someone he could recruit once he returns.  He tells us to consult with the SIlver Warden and that he will take us to him.  He takes us to a towering figure who is silver and as big as Og (silver knight) - “They are going to be using the waypoint.”  And the Warden nods his head.  Ains asks about future uses of the Tower and he says that it can be used for this instance.  Calvendorr tells us that he can activate it and we all go through with him.  More discussion about about all going or not.  We decide to all go.  He has to ask the Warden to use to the ring again.  And we all go.  He activates the arcane waypoint.  The runes glow and light up and Ains studies the sigil to remember it. 
Arriving at Basctdelm – House of Myr
We arrive in Basctdelm.  We are in a small room in the House of Myr. We see a man in a dark cloak who writes all the names down on the slate. Igdon says welcome.  We are only allowed to return if Idgon brings us back. 
We are at the top of a tower.  He shows us a map and describes the city. 
We ask about a map (an official one or a sketch). We ask for directions to local jewelers there - he tells us about 3 places (2 in the central district and 1 in the north district).  He also tells us about the Cloak’s headquarters - which are in the Bellows (he says the Meridian stays out of there and the Cloak stays out of the High district).  Zymve, Ky’tha and Ains decide to go to a jeweler and Og and Aury stay in the house with Hap.  We borrow the Meridian’s horses.  Ains casts a spell to make the group able to communicate. He and Zymve disguise themselves, as Ky’tha puts on her fancy dress and cloak. 
We make our way to the Flawless Crafts in the high district. We are on the way and notice a carriage that cuts us off - we have to make animal handling checks and do.  One of the people intentionally bumps into Ky’tha and makes a scene and walks away.  They left her a note.  She opens it:  
Welcome.  Would you do me the favor some night having dinner at the Crimson Seat. You can find what you want at the Dripping Dagger.
Landra  
Ky’tha recognizes the name as the Shadow Sovereign. The Cloak knows we have arrived.
We go into the store and the proprietor is an elitist prick.  But the store is nice.  A female high elf comes out to help us.  Ains introduces himself as Sir Indipity.  He says he is there to sell and Zymve comments that she is there to buy diamonds.  He shows them all of the jewelry and asks for 1000gp…they go back and forth and with his roll of 28, he convinces them to give him 600gp (for the items only worth 425gp).  They then ask what type of diamonds we need.  Some back and forth about value. Zymve tries to negotiate for the value of a diamond - tries to get it lower priced.  The Jewel cutting dwarf is insulted and the salesclerk and Zymve work out a discount of 450gp.  Ky’tha then negotiates and gets 800gp in dust for 675gp.  Ains then negotiates for another 500gp diamond for 450gp. 
We head back to the House of Myr.  As we are riding, Ky’tha shares about the note.  We notice the Temple of Roses as we ride by.  We know this is for Diverra.  We also see the Academy on the left and the Open Quill store where Phira was spotted. We get back and get the diamond to Aury, who is hanging out in an open courtyard at the house with Og and Hap. 
Aury and Hap go into a room and Aury starts the ritual. Hap is in his heavenly state and he sees a glowing silhouette of Aury walking towards him. Aury says - you have been my best friend for a long time and we really miss you.  We have things to do still.  I’m not ready for you to leave, you are too young, and Diverra still has plans for you.  Hap realizes he is dead.  He takes Aury’s hands and Hap’s body glows in a radiant light and he gasps for breath as his eyes open.  Ains gives him his necklace, Ky’tha asks if he could hear us (no).  He asks what happened.   We fill him in.  He agrees that he would like to get to the bottom of the Phira situation.
Ky’tha and Zymve agree to go - individually - to the Dripping Dagger, while the guys try to find Phira’s house (but they are going to do a pub crawl).  Zymve is disguised as a human male and Ky’tha is herself - fancily dressed. Ky’tha asks Ains to activate the communication beacon again and he does (Aury still declines).  
The guys go to the first pub (the Sunken Swallow) in the North District and get 3 wine and 1 ale (16sp). Ains asks Ky’tha through the channel what Phira looks like.  She has red-ish skin, curling pale white hair, horns like Hap, etc. Looks a little older than Hap. Ains makes a quick drawing of her and shows it around and no one recognizes it. Ains asks Aury to check to see if they are telling the truth.  He thinks they are not lying.  Ains says we are trying to find “his baby mama.”  
Meanwhile, Ky’tha goes into the Dripping Dagger tavern.  Looking into the Bellows, it feels like a crypt.  It seems very dark - light night - inside.  The top is almost lit star-like from mica.  It has a large lake area with a town that has alleyways, etc.  People carrying weapons.  Back to the tavern - the walls are moist and mossy. There is a black stone bar in the center of the room with a massive iron dagger hanging with “blood” on it.   Everything here is moist and dark.  Ky’tha walks in and sits down at the bar.  Someone immediately sits next to her.  20 seconds later, Zymve walks in.  The person is a female human with close-cut dark hair, wearing a leather coat.  She says “that didn’t take long.”  And says that “she” will be pleased that I’ve come quickly and alone.  They have a quick discussion.  This is Cutter and she says that she will be back in a little while - letting “her” know that we are available for dinner and flexible.  Zymve (disguised) and Ky’tha each get an ale for 2sp and food for an additional 2sp/each.
The guys head to the next tavern (the Terrible Terrasque). They order food and drinks. Food is 1gp.  They eat and drink. Og notices a female tiefling with dark hair and shorter horns across the room, and another male tiefling eating as well.  Og says “itsn’t it true that you all know each other?” And Hap says “no.”  But then Hap looks over and she looks a lot like Hap.  Hap is surprised to see she looks like him.  They talk about what to do to get her attention.  Aury yells out “Hey, Phira!”  Both tieflings turn and look at the guys - AND WE STOP HERE.
– The End of the Session –
