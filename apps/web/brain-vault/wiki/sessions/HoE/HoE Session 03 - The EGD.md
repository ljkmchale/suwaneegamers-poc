# HoE Session 03 - The EGD

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 03
- Session Title: The EGD
- Date Label In Notes: 05.06
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- Lady Tarathial pays the party for clearing the Vintner's Guild cellar and asks them to recover old Winbalt land records before the Cetaen land dispute fully slips away.
- Ainslie negotiates wine for the temple: one barrel now, with more promised if the party returns with the papers within a harmon.
- Aury visits Emlee Trendrel, who gives him a coin marked with two curved daggers that likely fell from the hooded figure tied to Kelona's murder.
- The party identifies the coin as Etterset-related. Ky'tha later speaks with Tessa at Charum's Oils and learns the coin is a serious internal marker, not common street money.
- Hernley delivers a note claiming to be from Inspector Elise and asking the party to meet at Old Oak & Grapes.
- Captain Vammad tells the party the old records room was buried by lava and can be reached through a hidden route near Points & Edges.
- At Old Oak & Grapes, the party meets a fake Inspector Elise. Vammad confirms he did not know any inspector had been assigned.
- Og and Ainslie follow the fake inspector but lose her. Ainslie glimpses a partial neck marking: two horizontal lines with two vertical marks, like an upside-down T or arrows. The party chooses "Regardez" as a callout if they see it again.
- The party finds the buried old-city entrance and explores ruined Winbalt streets under Emberstran, including an old guard post, residence, store, and a secret trapdoor route near the Lord's Keep/wine barrels.
- The session ends when something lands on Aury's back.

## Clues Found

- Cetaen land ownership may be false or manipulated.
- Old Winbalt land records may still exist beneath the city.
- The two-curved-daggers coin points to the Etterset.
- "Inspector Elise" is not legitimate.
- The fake inspector has a distinctive neck mark.
- The old-city route includes a trapdoor near Lord's Keep/wine storage.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- 50gp payment from Lady Tarathial.
- Fine vintage/carafe from the Vintner's Guild.
- Etterset coin with two curved daggers.
- Old guard-post finds: manacles and key, flail, halberd, and an undamaged quiver.
- Silvered short sword, kept by Hap.
- Bullseye lantern, taken by Ainslie.
- Dungeoneer's pack, taken by Og.
- Poisoner's kit, taken by Ky'tha.

## Decisions Made

- The party agrees to pursue the old land records for Lady Tarathial.
- Ainslie makes the temple wine arrangement part of the reward.
- Ky'tha follows up on the Etterset coin through Tessa.
- The party chooses not to fully explore the Lord's Keep trapdoor route yet.

## Consequences

- The Cetaen/Vintner's Guild problem becomes a land-record mystery rather than only a bad-wine problem.
- The Etterset enters the campaign as a real faction lead.
- Fake Inspector Elise becomes an active suspect tied to the murder and political coverup.
- The party gains access to the buried city beneath Emberstran.
