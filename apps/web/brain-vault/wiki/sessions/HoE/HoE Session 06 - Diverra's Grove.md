# HoE Session 06 - Diverra's Grove

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 06
- Session Title: Diverra's Grove
- Date Label In Notes: 06.24
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party meets Lady Rook at Imonarra's Tower. She says she has purchased the Cetaen land and wants help replacing Lady Tarathial as Vintner's Guild steward.
- Rook offers leverage around Hap's father's farm and frames herself as a practical answer to Emberstran's rot and trade problems.
- Rook reveals an ancient Adamont ritual scroll and a milky egg-shaped Marrowstone artifact of convergence. The ritual requires a life-focused focus and Og's thorn-leaf key.
- Ky'tha privately presses Rook about the Etterset and the Cloak. Rook admits the Etterset helped her and says she wants the party but does not need them.
- The party debates Rook versus Lady Tarathial. Ainslie is cautious, Ky'tha and Og lean toward Rook's practical option, and Hap/Aury remain more neutral or uncertain.
- Aury asks Meladra about the grove. She gives holy water, a Potion of Healing, and route guidance through hunter trails.
- Ainslie and Og gather hunter information at the Warm Bear: Southern Baltwood is corrupted, dralg are dangerous, and trails use feather/frog/spiral markers.
- Ky'tha retrieves the ritual artifacts from Rook, who warns her to protect Og and return the Marrowstone.
- In Southern Baltwood, the party follows the feather trail to the grove. A wisp appears to Aury and says the blight must be cleansed so the path may be revealed.
- Og performs the ritual at a rose-carved site while black mist rises and blight creatures attack.
- The ritual clears the blight. The crystal turns dark ebony, the scroll becomes gibberish, Og's leaf/key relaxes, and Diverra's symbol appears.
- Aury steps onto the altar with his medallion, and the party is pulled onto a vibrating stone/platform.

## Combats

- Blight entities at the ritual site.
- Darker vomiting blight forms.
- Blight champion with scepter/mace.

## NPCs Introduced

- Lady Rook / Bivra Rook, fully active in negotiation.
- Imonorra, referenced as Lord's right hand/Magistrate.
- Meladra as grove guide.
- Hunters at the Warm Bear.
- Blackrend, named as the grove corruption force.

## Clues Found

- Rook knows about Og's thorn-leaf key before the party tells her.
- The Marrowstone can absorb or redirect blight through ritual use.
- The sacred grove blight is tied to the crop/vineyard rot.
- Diverra's path/waypoint is hidden behind the cleansed grove.
- The ritual scroll is Adamont and becomes unreadable after use.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Marrowstone/egg artifact, temporarily used and darkened.
- Adamont ritual scroll, consumed/changed into gibberish.
- Og's thorn-leaf key changes/relaxes.
- Holy water.
- Potion of Healing.
- Blight champion scepter/mace, later identified as the Scepter of Adoration.

## Decisions Made

- The party agrees to use Rook's ritual tools to clear the grove.
- Og takes the central ritual role.
- The party withholds full trust from Rook while still using her solution.

## Consequences

- The Southern Baltwood blight is cleared enough to reveal a Diverra waypoint.
- Rook gains partial credibility because her ritual works, but her knowledge of Og raises suspicion.
- Og's life/ritual role becomes more important.
- The party is pulled from local politics into an older sacred/waypoint mystery.
