# HoE Session 24 - Let The Bodies Hit the Floor

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 24
- Session Title: Let The Bodies Hit the Floor
- Date Label In Notes: 04.30
- Real Date: 2026-04-30
- In-Game Date: 38th of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Mount Emberstran
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party enters a major fight against fiery riders, mounts, spellcasters, and other fire newt-like enemies while gnomes flee.
- Og is dropped and later revived by Aury's Revivify.
- Ainslie releases necrotic energy from the Marrowstone-like power through his mouth, damaging multiple enemies.
- Zymve tries to heal Og, moves Cloud of Daggers, kills several enemies with a spell, and is later dropped before Aury heals her.
- The party saves Zybeak, one of the fleeing gnomes, though his friend dies.
- Zybeak leads the party to Sparkwhisper, a gnome hamlet inside the volcano.
- The party learns the enemies were fire newts from below and that Irragosa flies around the mountain.
- Zymve uses the Whisper Forge to enhance and resize scale mail armor that Og found (looted from fire newt enemies), then plays a farewell song for Sparkwhisper.
- The party follows the tablet deeper into the mountain, climbs ancient dwarven obsidian stairs, and reaches a rune-marked vault slab.
- Aury tries the tablet in the depression, but Ainslie is the one who successfully triggers the golden light and opens the massive dark corridor.

## Combats

- Large combat against fiery riders, mounts, casters, and fire newt-like enemies.

## NPCs Introduced

- Zybeak.
- Fizzy, deceased friend of Zybeak.
- Lyra, elder / forge figure at Sparkwhisper.
- Sparkwhisper gnomes.

## Clues Found

- Fire newts come from "down below," where Zymve is from.
- Sparkwhisper gnomes have been inside the mountain for roughly 15+ years.
- Hap's whistle matches Sparkwhisper whistles and belonged to Ryland Fibbergoan.
- The vault approach uses ancient dwarven, hardened obsidian architecture.
- The tablet reacts to the runes but only opens the door when Ainslie presses it successfully.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Party loots heavy iron spears, longswords, shortswords, staffs, and metallic-lined saddlebags.
- Og grabs scale mail from two fire newt-like enemies.
- Aury spends Revivify resources to bring Og back.
- The tablet is used to open the vault door.

## Decisions Made

- The party helps Zybeak and follows him to Sparkwhisper.
- The party rests in Sparkwhisper before continuing.
- The party follows the tablet's pull deeper into Mount Emberstran.
- The party tests the tablet against the vault depression until Ainslie opens the door.

## Consequences

- Og dies and is revived, making this one of the party's most dangerous fights so far.
- Sparkwhisper becomes a new allied location inside Mount Emberstran.
- The Emberheart Tablet / vault thread reaches the door-opening threshold.
- The party is now staring into the massive dark corridor beyond the vault entrance.

## Character Beats

- Og goes down hard, dies, and returns through Aury's Revivify.
- Zymve is aggressive and effective in combat, then contributes culturally at Sparkwhisper through forge work and music.
- Ainslie channels necrotic power and ultimately opens the vault.
- Aury keeps the party alive at the edge of disaster.
- Ky'tha helps read and test the old runes.
- Hap reconnects the found whistle to Sparkwhisper.
