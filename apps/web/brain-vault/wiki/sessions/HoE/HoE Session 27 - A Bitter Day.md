# Session 27 - A Bitter Day

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  June 9, 2026
In-game Date:  45th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  City of Emberstran, Ghone cultist hideout
In Emberstran
We leave the hideout and stand next to two guards in an alleyway.  They gush. We tell them that we took care of everything.  They ask us to tell the Captain on our way back into the city.  Aury says he needs to tell the Priestess about what just happened.  We talk to Zabeek in the group thread and tell him to meet us at the Market.  Ains mentions that he is not feeling well and Aury offers to heal him as we walk to the Market.  Zymve appears anxious and Ky’tha tells her that she can calm down - we are out of the fight.  
Zabeek Meets Phryzn
We see the Market in the distance as we start walking and we see Zabeek walking to us.  He walks up and says “you made it out.  I think I did something - I was heading back - and this fella stopped me and said his name was Bitter or something weird like that and we just started to talk.” Zymve interrupts:  “what did you tell him?”  And he said that he was hanging out with adventurers and named us all, including Lynda - but don’t worry they are going to be leaving town soon and heading down the road.  He offered to give me a ring for the “goat.” And he said the ring looked too valuable and tried to decline the trade, but the guy was insistent.  He asked about if Lynda played musical instruments and how you played and I told him that you had the instruments with you.  Zymve remembers that she knew someone called Bitter - he is a distant cousin (Phryzn Inni is his real name - “Bitter” was the bullying nickname they gave him).
Ky’tha asks which way he went - maybe we could catch up to him.  He points to the Market and Og takes off after him.  Ky’tha asks what he looks like - silver hair, tied back, pale violet eyes, dark skin, dark traveling clothes, hooded cloak.  The group takes off to follow him and Ky’tha kindly admonishes Zabeek for making the trade.  I shouldn’t have done it, he asks?  She says simply “No.”
The Halfling Rudi 
As Og enters the market he sees a family of halflings with Ewing.  They are trying to stuff his packs with their gear.  Og looms up to them “excuse me, I hate to be a burden, but that is my goat.  It was traded without my permission.”  He asks Ewing to paw into the dirt to prove he is our goat.  He does.  And the halfling says the same thing - and he does.  We all approach and the halfling recognizes us and asks Hap to have a meal with him.  He says his name is Rudi Digglepot.  He even offers to throw the goat in.  But if Hap doesn’t want to sell the land - he will pay to rent it and look the land over.  Hap tells him that his proposal sounds sweet. “You seem like a right fine fella - you can go tend to the land and we will come to an arrangement later.”  They share a spit handshake.  The guy says we can meet at the Crimson Swan…as Ains says I’d rather not eat there.   The guy says he could meet when or wherever…and suggests other places.  But Hap says he will follow up - he will find you on his land at some point.  And the halflings say they will just move in…and seem pleased.
Meanwhile, Ky’tha points out that the stuff in the packs they are trying to overload is ours.  And the man says that the guy - that looked like Zymve - just offered to give it to them.  Zymve asks which way he went and takes off in that direction.  Aury yells for her to stop and Ky’tha follows her.  Aury follows, too.  They go through the center of the Market and it is dinnertime and busy - and we can’t find him.  Ky’tha thanks her for her zeal to find the guy who took our goat, but it’s ok.  Aury expresses concern about the ring - thinks maybe it is going to be used for spying.  Zymve recognizes the gem - this is from her family.  They wait and eventually the halfling family walks by, followed by Ains, Og, and Hap.
Zymve asks the halfling what was traded for a goat - and nothing was traded.  He was just offered the goat because they looked like they could use it.  She gives the ring to Ains and asks him to detect magic.  He uses his wand.  No magic is revealed on the ring - he sees magic on other people (various weapons).  Ains tells the group that he senses divination magic on the ring and she throws it to the ground and starts to break it with a hammer (this is worth at least 400 gold pieces).  She doesn’t care about the value and shatters the gem and ring.  Ains and Aury gather up the ruby dust.  Zymve and Aury reach for the pewter ring - but she grabs it first and chucks it into the water.  Aury does not like that she destroyed the ring and says that Zymve has anger issues.  
Ky’tha asks Zymve why she’s so upset.  She mentions that she thinks she was the target here.  Aury pipes up to disperse the crowd as a priest - telling everyone to move along.  Og and Ains flash their EGD badges.  We head to the Dyed Cane for some food and privacy.  As we walk, Ains expresses his desire to lay down and be alone - says he will catch up with Og at the Warm Bear Inn later.   He makes his way to the Inn and casts a spell on himself as he gets close to make sure he isn’t seen.  He tries to go and find a room.
Ainslie Goes to the Warm Bear Inn
When he gets there it is lively and he asks Gustavo how much a keg would be to purchase - or at least keep it open for a few hours.  He says for 5gp you can keep it flowing for the room.  He just asks to not be disturbed as he puts his head on the bar.  He says “free drinks for everyone as long as I’m not touched.”  And he takes a short rest while the bar drinks. Once he wakes up he gets his usual room.  He feels a little better and pays another 5gp to have another keg tapped and he goes to his room.   He props a chair against the door and sits on the bed and starts reading.  
At the Dyed Cane
We get to the Cane and find Bordi and Aury pays him 2gp.  He says we can’t take Ewing in, but we convince him that we will go straight to the private room and Og runs him up the stairs.  Zabeek expresses his disappointment in our activities, but we tell him this isn’t normal.  We then go back to the conversation.  Zymve says that she has seen thousands of them - that she’s cut them herself.  This is a family occupation.  Her family cuts gems for the drow nobility.  So a man with these rings is a sign that they are trying to get her back.   She says that “the spider queen” doesn’t just let people go.   She says that she didn’t want to do that for the rest of her life and snuck away in the night.  Ky’tha wonders why the man didn’t approach her personally and she doesn’t know.   Zymve says this is why we need to get out of town. Ky’tha posits that maybe we make her bait and put her in the open to draw him out and find out what he wants - that the group has her back. Aury agrees - thinks we need to find out what the problem is.  She says that she has been running for at least three years so maybe it is time she faces it.  
About that time, Bordi drops off a note - says a guy dropped it off for Zymve.  A black hair, pale skinned elf asked to pass this along. She says it says “my grandmother will forgive me if I can get “the relic” for my house’s matron Inidra Maerth.” It is signed Bitter.    We aren’t sure what relic this is referring to - the Marrowstone, the Emberheart, or maybe one of the other two pieces of the puzzle - one that Zymve knows is connected to her family.
Ky’tha Goes to Our Exquisites
Ky’tha goes downstairs to ask Bordi if Zymve can perform - he says yes and she thanks him.  Meanwhile, Zymve talks to the group about what she should sing about (subtly talking about rubies and relics).  Ky’tha then leaves to find Tessa, spreading rumors about the performance that will happen later tonight.  She goes to Our Exquisites - as she is walking she notices an elf and she follows him into the Hydra. She walks up to him at the front desk and asks if he is a courier of letters.  His name is Nindrol.  They agree to meet at the Cane later - after he professes to know who Ky’tha (full name, makes fun of her being a dwarf with en elvish name).  She then finds Tessa and briefs her on all the happenings - Ghone hideout, drow looking for Zymve, the pale elf who knows Ky’tha, etc.  She asks her to be on the lookout for the drow.  She says that the elf matching the description of Nindrol has been asking about the Etterset.  
Back at the Dyed Cane
Ky’tha then heads back over to the Dyed Cane and to the room to tell Zymve that she’s on and everyone is buzzing.  The group (sans Ewing) heads to the bar and Aury pays him 10gp to give us a great table and asks one of his children to feed the goat. He does. The female tiefling is our server. Sets us up with food and spirits.  The guy playing the harp steps off the stage - he was just okay.  People give a polite clap.  Zymve (as Lynda) steps up to the stage (she is average…brown hair and eyes f).  She performs a special song made for the occasion (nat 20).  The crowd goes wild…as she is performing the door opens and a few candles snuff out.  The tiefling starts trying to relight them.  Aury and Hap notice a hooded figure walk in.  As she is wrapping up he reaches into a pouch and throws 1pp at her feet and sits down at the bar.  He pulls his hood off and this is a silver-haired drow. Aury points him out to Og.  Zymve performs again with her yarding.
Ky’tha eases over to the bar, followed by Og. She asks him what he is having.  They agree to have the same thing and he orders his finest wine. Bordi says that will be 10gp and she says to him - you have this right.  And he pays 11gp.  He admits to being Nindrol.  He asks about “his” goat.  He asks her if this is how dwarves flirt.  She says that they should listen…and they do.  Ky’tha then talks more about why he’s here. She insists that he talk to Lynda.  He says that she knows what she wants.  He asks Og to get the item and bring it to his home.  He’d like it there “with spiders, in the dark, etc.”  
He comes over to the table to meet the group.  He knows everyone by name.  Aury says he is going to ask a question and does.  He says that Zymve has become a friend of ours.  He asks if Zymve is in danger?  He asks if we know much about Zymve…and if we do then we should know if she is in danger or not.  Aury asks is she in danger from him?  He says not tonight, but maybe tomorrow it could be different.  He asks Aury “which one we are going for next.”  And Aury says that we aren’t going for any of them.  He tells us we should reconsider.  Aury tells him that if he makes a threat to her then he makes a threat to all of us.  He thanks him for being direct.  Zymve casts Suggestion on him, but it doesn’t work.  
Meanwhile, Ains casts Sending to Og:  he let’s them know that he has a room, asks if Zymve is sleeping there tonight, tells him about the free drinks, asks for a response.  Zymve says she will come later.   Og tells her they need to go soon.
Ky’tha catches him and asks what his angle is.  He says he was asked to complete a task…which isn’t much different than taking tasks off the bulletin board (and points there).  She asks if he is being paid by the queen and he says he is being paid by the Matron.  But tells Ky’tha to ask Zymve how the drow pay - he is not being paid with coin.  As he leaves Zymve yells “I will not go back” across the bar.
Ky’tha walks back to the table and asks Zymve what the drow pay with. She says power and station and Hap pipes up “and the threat of death.” She says she wants to kill him, but they talk about how if she does, the Matron will send another in his place and she agrees.  She just says that as we get closer to finding the relic, more will come for the group.  Group splits up:  Ky’tha to the Hydra, Hap and Aury to the Temple, and Og and Zymve take Zabeek and Ewing to the Warm Bear Inn to find Ains.
Hap and Aury make it to the Temple.  Aury meets with the Priestess - tells her that Arnol got put on a ship.  Then he tells her about the cult - how they were zombies and a vampire. And how we killed them all - tells this very matter of fact-like.  Tells her how the daylight spell is effective.  Recounts the name of the vampire.  He then talks about the tome he has from Diverra…and how it is an original copy.  He then tells her about the rose petal that he gave her…and how to use it.  He asks her to keep the petal safe so that someday he may take her.  He then tells her they are traveling in the morning.  He tells her about the shattered heart mission and how this will take him all over the continent. She mentions telling Arnol but he counsels her to leave Arnol alone for now.  He just asks her to notify the acolytes and others in the practice to keep their eyes open for this mission.  Then goes to bed.
Ky’tha goes to the Hydra and gets the room number for the elf.  Goes up to the room and keys in.  Sees nothing, but sees a shadow, and checks it out but nothing is there. Sees something at the window and opens it and there is a human.  She closes the window and makes some comments “we could have had fun” then shuts the door, goes to her room, and goes to bed.
Og, Zymve, and Zabeek get to the Inn and can’t get in - there is line out the door.  They try to flash the EGD badge and also try to intimidate the crowd to let them pass.  Zymve is able to intimidate them and Og clears a path.  The crowd comments that the EGD and HOEs are a bunch of assholes…but let’s them by.  Og stables Ewing, but carries Zabeek on his shoulders and they work their way in.  Og and Zabeek go to the room, as Zymve grabs 4 mugs and takes them upstairs.
Og gets to the room and they discuss how Ains caused that riot because he “wanted peace and quiet.”  Zymve kicks open the door with the mugs, hands two to Og.  They drink two each.  Zabeek feels left out.  Og tells Ains that she gave a great performance. She then tells Ains about how her family wants the dragon-related relics.   She says “we might have to kill some people.”  Ains expresses surprise that the Cloak of Defiance nor Lady Rook have not made an appearance.  He casts a spell to make sure no one else is there.  He expresses some paranoia.  He says he doesn’t trust Ky’tha or Aury with the marrowstone.  But says he trusts Zymve with it and throws it to her.  Zymve agrees to sing for Ains and then casts a sending spell to Arnol privately, twice (the second one full of extended laughs). He gets back a confused response.   He then reads his book, as Zymve watches. He goes into a zone.  A message starts to convey to Zymve.  Ains picks up that it makes him think of dry land, desert land.  She gets uncomfortable heat as the melody comes from him.  It is dry and suffocating and begins to feel like brittle bones.  It gets very sad near the end of what he is reading.  He sees the word “loss” and it is bones and desert and feels like there is something deeper in the text that someone has lost something very important to them.  Zymve feels very vulnerable, but doesn’t show this.  They all agree to go to bed. 
It’s the Next Morning, the 46th of Talil, 1246
The next morning Ky’tha wakes up, grabs bread and cheese and walks to the gate.  Hap and Aury grab breakfast, Hap donates 10gp to the Temple, and they walk to the gate.  Og, Ains, and Zymve wake up and talk to Zabeek about the danger that is coming.  He says this may be too much for him and expresses his fear.  Ains try to persuade him to join in, as Zymve tries to intimidate him.  Og picks up Ewing.  Ky’tha makes it there and asks if everyone is ready.  Zabeek is nervous - Ky’tha gives him a dagger.  He says he wants to be a powerful wizard…but will have to wait.  Hap and Aury show up.  Everyone is ready…The group heads out. 
Weftirin at the Barebase Camp
We travel for a day.  Zabeek cozies up to Hap and tries to learn tips from as the party walks.  The day passes without incident.  We pass the hunter’s camp where we went into the grove.  We continue and the sun starts to set.  We come to the area we are going to make camp.  Ky’tha has been this way, but it has been a while.  This is Barebase. It is a traveler’s camp.  We see 4 covered wagons sitting around a campfire.  The wagons are decorated and painted…bright colors and beads and gems and trinkets.  We are greeted by a man who says “come over - the murder welcomes you.”  These are the Weftirins.  They are gypsies/hippies of Myrdae.  They ask us to join them - they have stew.  Ains asks why they referred to themselves as murderers.  Aury knows that this group travels in groups.  Each group takes the name of a group of animals.  This group took Murders (like crows).  We all make introductions.  There is a supply wagon (the greeter is named Marek).  There is a trinket wagon/dreamcatchers.  There is an instrument wagon.  And finally a fabric wagon.  We ask where they are going or coming from.  They say they are aiming to be at Stonewood by next Spring.  The skirted Emberstran and are headed east.  We share we are heading to Ulgrey.
Ains asks them about spitespirit…has he heard anything about him.  A few tales are shared, but nothing of importance.  He specifically asks about the keys but they don’t know much about it.  Aury asks him if they have a map of the lands. He says he will have to paint one and Aury offers him some parchment to draw one for him.  He asks where we want to go and draws Ulgrey, Kahlbit’s Veil, Trailpoint, etc.  Aury helps them put it down.  Ky’tha starts to talk and ask if they have noticed signs of blight or disease in their travels.  As Ky’tha speaks up…the trinket lady looks at Ky’tha curiously.  The group the Skulk says they saw some dying land near Halesworth.   They got their info from a Seer.  They think she’s traveling with the School group.  Myra (the tinkerer) walks up to Ky’tha and says “you know I’ve been around these parts, and it looks to me like you are carrying something heavy in your sleep.”  You are fighting those dreams aren’t you and she gives her a dreamcatcher and asks her to sleep with it tonight.  She says she’s good at reading people. Ains asks what everyone does for fun around here.  And a couple of players come out to play for the group.  
Dreams Caught
We take watch per usual.  Zymve and Ains; Og and Aury; Hap and Ky’tha.  Watch is shared by the members of the Murder.  During his watch, Ains lifts a wagon to impress the others.  He also lifts Zymve up and spins her then sets her down gently.  She then casts a spell on him and he takes some psychic damage.  During Aury and Og’s watch, Aury reads from his book. When Ky’tha rests, she sleeps with her dreamcatcher. When she wakes from her watch, she sees Myra and she walks over and asks for the dreamcatcher since Ky’tha responds that she slept the same.  
Myra says the catcher caught something old that carries weight - she says that she sees two groups standing across from one another.  


“You gave it a lot to hold last night,” Mira says, “It caught something old. Something that still carries weight.”
“I saw two groups standing across from one another. One bore the markings of your clan. The other… the Cragfury. There was anger. Fear. Words were spoken that could not be taken back. And then…” Mira’s brow furrows slightly. “There was a tearing. Not of flesh, but of the world itself. The Cragfury were pulled away — not by choice, but by force. Thrown through the spaces between places. I felt the land itself resist it.”
“There was something else at the end of it all. A steady, deep rhythm. Like the beating of a great heart. It was calm, suppressing everything that was torn apart into peace. A guardian’s heart, I think.”
You may want to keep this with you and hands her back the dreamcatcher.  She shows her how to work with the dreamcatcher.  Says that she should work with one of great wisdom in her “family.”
It’s the Next Morning, the 47th of Talil, 1246
Pack up and head out, thanking them for the hospitality.  We move along the road and we smell the salt and water from the Sea as the road gets closer to the water.  We find another campsite known as Featherfall.  We see this is at a cliff area.  There are many types of birds flying around.  It is noisy since this is still during the day.  Seems like an obvious place to camp.  There is no one else here.  We stop and listen and can hear the wagons not far behind. Zymve expresses an interest in moving ahead to get away from the noise and as far as we can before the sun sets.  As we do, Og looks for another camp site.  He is able to find something, but it is not as good as the Featherfall.  We set up camp.  We set up watch again - same pairs, opposite order.  Birds quiet down, but then get very quiet.  It is very peaceful and we hear the surf below.  Zymve is in a trance and she hears something, a song on the wind - this is faint in the distance.  Probably not far off from the Featherfall campsite.  Ky’tha and Hap don’t hear it.  Hap sends Fleur over to check it out and takes off.   Ky’tha pulls out her Silver Raven messenger (wonderous magic item) and sends a message to Myra “we are down the road if needed.”  Fleur observes things and makes it back to Hap as the raven tries to find Myra.
Fleur tells Hap “it is chaotic and looks like someone is about to fall and there are people trying to hold people.”  Hap tells the group that there is danger.  We wake the group and dash that way. 
We see in the distance the campfire as high as it can be, we see people trying to tie others off with rope, and we see others constantly moving.  We see someone climbing down the 60’ cliffs and we hear a song of Sirens (evil creatures) off to the left.
– The End of the Session –
