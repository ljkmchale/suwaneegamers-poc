# HoE Session 20 - Coming Home

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 20
- Session Title: Coming Home
- Date Label In Notes: 03.12
- Real Date: 2026-03-02
- In-Game Date: 32nd of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Bloomrest
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party wakes at Bloomrest / the Lost Sanctuary of Diverra and sees the Life Tree recovering.
- Aury uses Bloom Aeterna and a petrified rose petal to return the party to Diverra's Grove in the Baltwood.
- The party rides with Emberstran Timber Company workers Jorm, Veed, and Sigur toward Emberstran.
- Zymve experiences her first bright surface daylight and Ainslie creates magical sunglasses for her.
- The party meets Brander singing "Victors of the Vine"; Zymve sharply critiques his song.
- The party returns quietly through Emberstran's southern gate.
- Aury reports to Priestess Meladra at the Temple of Diverra.
- Hap and Ky'tha meet Lady Rook at the Suncrest Cellarium and discuss Hap's land, the Cloak of Defiance, Lady T, Phira, and the Marrowstone.
- At the Dyed Cane, the party agrees to give Lady Rook the stone after discussion.
- Zymve, disguised as Lynda, helps Brander recover confidence and performs a successful duet with him at the Warm Bear Inn.
- Zymve confronts Ainslie over the Journal of Tsulanan and reveals she fled an Underdark cult with no friends.
- Ainslie studies and empties the Marrowstone with Bardic Inspiration from Zymve and Guidance from Aury.

## Clues Found

- Lady Rook wants the Marrowstone for Emberstran and possibly to use against the Cloak of Defiance.
- The Marrowstone and rod form a two-part magic item with Circle of Death-like necrotic potential.
- Ainslie may be able to bypass the rod through his own power.
- Zymve fled a drow cult and fears the book's connection to her past.
- Agnett warns of Mist Springs, ash-covered monsters, and Irragosa the Griever.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Aury takes petrified rose petals from the sanctuary.
- Zymve buys a black cloak.
- Ainslie buys a platinum vial / flask and related strange components.
- Ainslie empties the Marrowstone.

## Decisions Made

- The party returns to Emberstran rather than immediately staying in the Underdark.
- The party initially agrees to give Lady Rook the Marrowstone.
- Ainslie decides to study the stone before handing it over and drains it.
- Zymve and Ainslie agree to share information if the Journal of Tsulanan reveals anything relevant to her.

## Consequences

- The Marrowstone is emptied before Lady Rook receives it.
- Zymve's drow/cult backstory becomes explicit.
- The party's public reputation as heroes grows in Emberstran.
- Mount Emberstran and Irragosa become the next destination.

## Character Beats

- Zymve sees daylight, buys a cloak, performs under disguise, and opens up about having no friends.
- Ainslie takes a major risky magical step with the Marrowstone.
- Aury grows beyond Meladra in religious experience but withholds the full book.
- Hap faces land and family pressure through Lady Rook.
- Ky'tha reconnects with Tessa and the Etterset's new base.
