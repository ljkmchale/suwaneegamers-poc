# HoE Session 23 - Into the Volcano

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 23
- Session Title: Into the Volcano
- Date Label In Notes: 04.21
- Real Date: 2026-04-21
- In-Game Date: 37th of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Base of Mount Emberstran, fork in the path
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party moves forward after Petal's death and follows the right path toward the vault.
- Ky'tha separately meets Irragosa in humanoid form; Irragosa asks her to watch Ainslie and hints she likes Ky'tha.
- During camp watches, Aury reflects on Petal's death and Og notices one thorn on Thornleaf has turned black.
- Zymve and Ainslie continue interacting through the music of the Journal of Tsulanan.
- Ainslie learns more about Tsulanan, a protector relationship, and Irragosa.
- The party finds a skeleton near Mount Emberstran, with a jeweled knife, a whistle, and a saddle-horn-like object marked with a T.
- The party fights dire wolves and then fire beetles while entering the mountain.
- The party reaches the interior of Mount Emberstran, with lava, lightning, paths, and signs of giant activity.
- Ainslie uses Giant and the name Mogana to convince a fire giant that this is his vault.
- The party ends near a commotion: small gnomes fleeing fiery warriors on red ostrich-like mounts.

## Combats

- Combat against dire wolves.
- Combat against fire beetles.
- Combat encounter with salamander and fire giant, resolved by deception and sacrifice of Fleur's temporary form.

## NPCs Introduced

- Irragosa, in humanoid form.
- Fire giant who responds to Ainslie's Mogana claim.
- Unnamed fleeing gnomes.
- Fiery mounted warriors glimpsed at session end.

## Clues Found

- Irragosa knows the party is coming and has a special interest in Ainslie.
- Thornleaf gains a black thorn after Petal's death, possibly reflecting moral or life/death imbalance.
- Zymve's song from the journal includes ideas of guarding, sealing, darkened stone, heart, and leaving no trail.
- The T-marked saddle horn likely connects to Tsulanan / dragon riding.
- Hap's whistle connects to gnomes near or inside the mountain.
- The tablet pulls the party along specific paths through Mount Emberstran.
- Ainslie's giant name Mogana carries legendary weight.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Ky'tha takes a jeweled knife.
- Party finds a bronze chain with whistle.
- Ky'tha finds a shiny T-marked saddle-horn-like object.
- Fleur is crushed by the giant but can presumably be resummoned as Hap's familiar.

## Decisions Made

- The party chooses the vault path rather than pursuing Irragosa directly.
- Ainslie attempts diplomacy/deception with the giant instead of fighting it to the death.
- The party follows the tablet's pull through the volcano.

## Consequences

- The party enters the heart of Mount Emberstran and reaches the volcanic vault approach.
- Ainslie's Tsulanan / Irragosa inheritance thread becomes central to the vault.
- The party is about to enter a major combat involving volcanic humanoids and gnomes.

## Character Beats

- Ky'tha is singled out by Irragosa as someone worth talking to.
- Zymve's music helps interpret Ainslie's ancestral journal.
- Aury seeks repentance or meaning after Petal's death.
- Og's Thornleaf visibly changes.
- Ainslie leans hard into inherited authority.
