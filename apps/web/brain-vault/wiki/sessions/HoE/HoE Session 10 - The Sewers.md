# HoE Session 10 - The Sewers

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 10
- Session Title: The Sewers
- Date Label In Notes: 08.20
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- At the Dyed Cane, the party plans to enter the sewers to find Whisper/Cloak and the fourth tablet piece.
- They use the Temple of Diverra sewer entrance through Meladra's vault. Aury initially waits by the door with the password "The Flower Blooms," but the party later retrieves him because the sewers are too dangerous.
- Ky'tha and Hap scout ahead through poisonous, foul sewer passages. Ainslie makes nose clamps.
- The party fights rats and finds a chewed skeleton with small supplies, a lockpicking kit, and a tattered Cloak of Defiance cloak.
- Ky'tha meets Lockjaw and Dust, Cloak members looking for Smiley. Ainslie attacks; Dust dies, and Lockjaw is spared after interrogation.
- Lockjaw reveals Kelona was killed because she knew about the plot to kill Lady Rook. Jerik ordered the Rook hit, Black Quill is a half-elf who changes appearance and blends into nobility, and Imonorra/Magistrate/Hand have some relationship with the Cloak.
- The party fakes Lockjaw's death with bodies and rats and adopts Cloak cover names: Hap/Ivory, Og/Biggie, Ainslie/Talon, Ky'tha/Smalls, and later Aury/Flower.
- They find a guard house / Upper City Gate sewer ladder.
- A gelatinous cube traps Ainslie, Ky'tha, and Og's weapon/hand before Ky'tha kills it.
- The party tracks footprints toward Imonarra's Tower and Ky'tha spots a three-foot figure in a Cloak cloak and plague doctor mask trying to get into the tower.

## Clues Found

- Kelona was killed because she knew of the Lady Rook assassination plot.
- Whisper/Quill is a half-elf who changes appearance and moves among nobility.
- Jerik ordered the hit on Rook.
- Imonorra/Magistrate/Hand and the Cloak have a working relationship or tolerance.
- The sewers connect to Upper City Gate and Imonorra's Tower.
- A small plague-doctor-masked figure is active at the tower access.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Cloak disguises and cover identities.
- Lockpicking kit, taken by Hap.
- 3gp, 12sp, tinderbox, torches, oil.
- 1pp and 50gp topaz from cube debris, topaz kept by Ky'tha.

## Decisions Made

- The party uses temple access rather than a public sewer entrance.
- Lockjaw is spared and allowed to leave under a faked death cover.
- The party adopts Cloak identities for infiltration.
- Aury is brought into the sewers despite initially guarding the entrance.

## Consequences

- The party gains actionable intelligence on the Cloak's murder/assassination structure.
- Ky'tha and Ainslie become deeper infiltration operators.
- The party is now directly tracking Whisper.
- The Cloak/Imonorra/Tower connection becomes hard to ignore.
