# HoE Session 14 - Drowned Waypoint

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 14
- Session Title: Drowned Waypoint
- Date Label In Notes: 11.12
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- Ky'tha goes to Lord's Keep and endorses Lady Rook to Batheus. She signs the document and asks him to be mindful of a suspicious guard.
- At the gate, Ainslie jokingly names it "Eye of the Mountain." Ainslie tells the party Rook has Hap's brother-in-law / Jerik's brother hostage or under protection.
- Lord's Keep sends ten rations through Batheus; Ky'tha adds Ainslie, Hap, and Og to the Rook-support roll while Aury remains neutral.
- In Lower Baltwood, hunter Kale Noven says no one has entered the grove since the blight cleared.
- The party peacefully handles a dralg encounter using rations and animal handling.
- The party reaches the Diverra waypoint cave and long rests. Ainslie sends Phira a message for Hap, and she says Rook is good/protecting her husband because of his troubled past.
- Ainslie sends Arnol an ominous/encouraging message.
- The next morning, Aury casts Activate Sigil / Arcane Waypoint. The party teleports to a protected dome beneath the sea, about 100 feet underwater.
- The party explores an underwater cave system with huge tracks and signs the circle has not been used since the Awakening/roughly 20 years ago.
- The party negotiates past three trolls by acting like food and then reaches an enormous underground lake.
- Ainslie finds a note: "lights under the water at night, moving like roots."
- The party meets Shenog the Fisher, a large gray half-orc woman with red braid and yellow eyes. She names the lake Rothenloch, warns of moving roots/lights, quaggoths, and the Shade of Rothenloch taking minds, then takes the party to Nunglthil for 6gp.
- The party arrives at the shady underground harbor settlement of Nunglthil.

## Combats

- Potential dralg encounter resolved peacefully.
- Potential troll encounter negotiated rather than fought.

## NPCs Introduced

- Batheus.
- Kale Noven.

## Clues Found

- Rook has Phira's husband / Jerik's brother as protective leverage.
- The Diverra waypoint can reach an underwater/undersea hub.
- The waypoint may not have been used since the Awakening.
- Rothenloch has moving underwater lights/roots and a mind-taking Shade.
- Nunglthil is an underground harbor/settlement tied to the next route.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Ten rations from Lord's Keep.
- Note about lights under the water moving like roots.
- 6gp paid/owed for Shenog's boat passage.

## Decisions Made

- Ky'tha formally endorses Rook.
- Aury remains neutral rather than joining the Rook endorsement.
- The party uses the Diverra waypoint before going to Mount Emberstran.
- The party chooses negotiation/avoidance with dralg and trolls.

## Consequences

- The party leaves Emberstran politics temporarily but with Rook endorsement consequences active.
- The campaign moves from Emberstran/Baltwood into Rothenloch/Nunglthil.
- Diverra's waypoint network is confirmed as functional and ancient.
- The Shade of Rothenloch becomes a looming threat.
