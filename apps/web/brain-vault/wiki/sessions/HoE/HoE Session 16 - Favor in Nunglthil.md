## Major Scenes

- The party wakes at the Darkbottom Draught and receives a letter from [[Zymve]] saying she has unfinished business and asking them to tell Coppereyes she will see him later.
- The party meets Endavo, who discusses Nunglthil, Miss Placed Tomes, the lake beast, the Wurmrise, and Lady Rook.
- Coppereyes confirms the recovered gems, gives the party Glimmerdel favor beads, sells a diamond, and asks for help understanding Szoradryn's insult.
- Bolshack Stonesniff explains Underdark maps, Thallgrove, genlings, and possible directions toward the lost Sanctuary of Diverra.
- The party researches at Miss Placed Tomes and learns more about arcane waypoints, the old restricted route, and a rose-scented blocked path.
- Og visits Soothing Spores and buys Valecap Extract, Glowseed, and Mindbloom.
- Ky'tha and Ainslie visit Silken Secrets, meet Szoradryn, and recover the strange journal connected to Tsulanan.
- Hap and Aury meet She'nog at the Trader's Tankard and learn about stone giants disrupting a fishing hole.

## Clues Found

- A lake creature in Nunglthil hunts for teeth and affects dreams or memory.
- Thallgrove is dangerous because its spores can cause madness.
- Aury's target likely lies near the eastern edge of the Thallgrove area.
- Old waypoints were built by the Tegenwald / Tegenwalk Phet and some were restricted or shortcut-built.
- Hap finds travel-journal evidence of a waypoint in the now-flooded lowlands, blocked by roses.
- Ainslie obtains the Journal of Tsulanan from Szoradryn.

## Items Gained Or Lost
