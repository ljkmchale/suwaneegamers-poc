# HoE Session 11 - Confronting Cloak

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 11
- Session Title: Confronting Cloak
- Date Label In Notes: 09.23
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party confronts the three-foot plague-doctor-masked figure at Imonorra's Tower sewer access: the Whisper, also the fake Inspector Elise.
- Whisper uses marbles that become rats, poison crossbow tactics, and mist-form escape through the mask. Ky'tha drops unconscious, Hap kills Whisper on the ladder, and Aury heals Ky'tha.
- The party loots Whisper's gear and identifies several magical tools: a +1 dagger, Mask of Withered Breath, Listener's Coil, and a divination crystal.
- Aury animates Whisper's corpse for 14 hours.
- The party continues through the sewers using Cloak disguises and fights more Cloak agents. A lightning bolt kills several.
- A hidden entrance near the Crimson Swan is confirmed.
- The party retreats to the temple after hearing more movement. Arnol opens the door and acts strange; Ainslie privately reminds him to stay quiet about the cult.
- High Priestess Meladra hears of chaos and lightning from the Northern District near the Crimson Swan and sends the party to help.
- The group splits: Ky'tha, Aury, and Hap go toward the Tower; Ainslie and Og go to the crime scene.
- Ainslie and Og see a zombie with a crossbow bolt and likely Jerik. Captain Amilla suppresses the investigation, and Torrick questions EGD legitimacy.
- Ky'tha rides with Rook and tells her Whisper is dead. Rook says it is time to change the status quo and offers to make the EGD real.
- Aury publicly heals Ky'tha and others and tells Meladra Arnol has changed. His prayer confirms his worry is right.
- The next morning, Emlee and Haggis bring an official EGD scroll signed by Black Quill and a 500gp box, with a note expecting the party for dinner at the Crimson Swan.

## Clues Found

- Whisper and fake Inspector Elise are the same person.
- Whisper had a listening/divination setup using crystal and Listener's Coil.
- Cloak access near Imonorra's Tower and Crimson Swan is confirmed.
- Captain Amilla and Torrick appear to protect or serve the Cloak/Jerik structure.
- Arnol is compromised or at least entangled with the cult.
- Black Quill can create or authorize civic-looking documents.

## Decisions Made

- Hap kills Whisper rather than letting her escape.
- Aury animates Whisper's corpse.
- The party splits to investigate Tower/Rook and crime-scene/Cloak angles at once.
- Ky'tha gives Rook enough information to accelerate Rook's anti-status-quo push.
- The party accepts possession of the EGD scroll and money, though the source is suspicious.

## Consequences

- Whisper is removed as an active assassin.
- The EGD becomes official on paper but compromised by Black Quill's signature.
- Ainslie and Og are pulled toward a direct Cloak dinner.
- Arnol's cult involvement becomes a temple crisis.
- Rook sees an opportunity to move openly against the status quo.
