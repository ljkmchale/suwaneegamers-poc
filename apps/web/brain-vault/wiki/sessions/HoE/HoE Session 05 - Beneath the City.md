# HoE Session 05 - Beneath the City

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 05
- Session Title: Beneath the City
- Date Label In Notes: 05.28
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- At the Temple of Diverra, Acolyte Tarley reports Meladra missing. Coken and Arnol searched; Meladra left after midnight in full service garb through the North Gate.
- Arnol shows admiration for Aury and Hap and wants to be a hero. Aury steers that energy toward future training.
- Ky'tha checks docks and tavern leads, including the Headless Hydra, then follows Jae and Etterset angles.
- At the Dyed Cane, Bordi talks about the empty barrel/tablet thread. Hap reveals he is Benoit's son and Phira's brother.
- Jae Vynaar admits he changed the records after Benoit's death/no-heir claim, under pressure from the old Lord, the Cloak, or Vintner's Guild influence.
- Jae says Lady Rook is likely positioning herself around the Cetaen land.
- A cart messenger from Lady Rook invites Aury to the Tower that night.
- Ky'tha meets Tessa again. Tessa says Cloak was talking in the sewer, Lady Rook has recently arrived, and the Etterset needs Rook to gain ground against the Cloak.
- Ainslie, Og, Hap, and Aury meet Lady Tarathial. Ainslie uses Detect Thoughts and senses Lady T wants stability, fears displacement, and is not simply the villain of the vineyard problem.
- Vineyard workers mention spotting/rot and unpaid work.
- Ky'tha finds Priestess Meladra, who says Diverra led her to a wisp and that Blackrend has captured a wisp in the sacred grove. This likely connects to the crop rot.
- The party cleans up and goes to Imonarra's Tower, where Dathreil Runewish receives them and Lady Rook is introduced.

## Clues Found

- Meladra left through North Gate and found a wisp tied to Diverra.
- Blackrend is blocking the sacred grove and has captured a wisp.
- Jae confirms the land records were altered.
- Benoit had money and influence and loaned the Cetaens money.
- Lady Rook is connected to the Cetaen land opportunity.
- Lady Tarathial is politically guarded and magically protected, but her concern for town stability appears genuine.
- Vineyard blight/rot may be tied to the grove corruption.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Lady Rook's invitation/note.
- Land-record proof remains in party possession.

## Decisions Made

- Ky'tha pursues both the Etterset and Lady Rook channels.
- Hap tells Bordi the Benoit/Phira connection.
- The party accepts the meeting at Imonarra's Tower.
- The party starts treating the grove corruption as connected to Emberstran's crop crisis.

## Consequences

- Lady Rook moves from rumor to active political player.
- Hap's family identity is no longer only private party knowledge.
- Meladra's grove lead creates the next outside-town objective.
- The party has to choose how much to trust Lady Tarathial, Lady Rook, the Etterset, or none of them.
