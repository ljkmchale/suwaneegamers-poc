# HoE Session 12 - The Eradic

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 12
- Session Title: The Eradic
- Date Label In Notes: 10.07
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- Ky'tha meets Tessa, Belavie Lightstrider, and Bordi in a private Dyed Cane room. Belavie offers Ky'tha a rank-two Etterset coin, praising her for playing Rook's games without becoming Rook's piece.
- Ky'tha tells Tessa the party caused the sewer chaos and explains how to reach the Cloak back entrance.
- At the temple, Meladra tells Aury and Hap that Emberstran is shifting: blight cured, Arnol changing, and lightning rising from the ground. Aury frames it as a power struggle involving the Lord, Cloak, Etterset, Rook, and Diverra's protection of the city.
- Aury asks Meladra to watch Arnol; she agrees to have Koken follow him.
- Og and Ainslie convert the suspicious 500gp chest into five 100gp rubies at Gems of Emberstran.
- Ky'tha, Aury, and Hap shop at Gems of Emberstran. Emiffi recognizes Ky'tha's half-locket and reveals there was another half. Bafast says a red-cloaked figure stole it.
- Bafast explains Rook pressured him into supplying materials to drow at the Silver Spaulder, including iron, steel, and dunduaar.
- At the Silver Spaulder, the group trades armor and pays for Hap's splint mail. Ky'tha later learns the smith has a profitable relationship with Rook and is not sharing fairly with Bafast.
- At the rarities shop, Aury buys a holy-water self-levitating censer; Ky'tha buys horn-removal balm for Hap.
- Ainslie and Og infiltrate the Eradic of Ghone hideout as recruits. They see Arnol and learn the cult digs bodies to capture trapped souls for Sijulous Ghone.
- In the cult chambers below the cemetery, Ainslie detects funeral treasure and the fourth tablet piece. Og is sent to dig bodies and is disturbed by the grave work.
- Ainslie pockets the fourth tablet piece and an onyx box, then convinces the cultist woman he needs to study the box.
- Ainslie and Og escape the hideout successfully.
- Later, Ainslie and Og attend dinner at the Crimson Swan with Selvara Nendris, the Black Quill. She praises the EGD, offers partnership "not as pawns, but as peers," and toasts, "Shall the Rook topple." Ainslie and Og accept the offer to be "in."

## Clues Found

- Ky'tha's locket has another half, stolen by a red-cloaked figure.
- The missing locket half ties to Cragfury/Umberfist/Dunduaar history.
- Rook pressured Bafast into supplying materials to a drow smith.
- Rook and the Silver Spaulder smith go "way back."
- The Eradic of Ghone are grave robbers trying to capture trapped souls for Sijulous.
- Arnol is actively entangled with the Eradic.
- Black Quill wants the EGD as anti-Rook partners.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Rank-two Etterset coin for Ky'tha.
- Five 100gp rubies converted from the 500gp chest.
- Hap's splint mail.
- Aury's holy-water self-levitating censer.
- Horn-removal balm for Hap.
- Fourth tablet piece, stolen by Ainslie from the Eradic.
- Onyx box worth about 120gp, taken by Ainslie.

## Decisions Made

- Ky'tha accepts Belavie's rank-two Etterset coin.
- Aury has Koken follow Arnol.
- Ainslie and Og choose infiltration over direct assault at the Eradic hideout.
- Ainslie steals the tablet piece rather than negotiating for it.
- Ainslie and Og accept Black Quill's partnership offer, at least outwardly.

## Consequences

- Ky'tha gains deeper Etterset standing.
- The party now has all four tablet pieces available for assembly.
- Og carries emotional fallout from grave-digging and seeing a decomposing child.
- Ainslie and Og become compromised with both the cult incident and Black Quill/Cloak dinner.
- Ky'tha's personal locket/Cragfury/Dunduaar thread becomes active.
