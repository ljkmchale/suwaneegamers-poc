# HoE Session 17 - Giant's Silver

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 17
- Session Title: Giant's Silver
- Date Label In Notes: 01.28
- Real Date: 2026-01-28
- In-Game Date: 26th of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Nunglthil
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- Ainslie studies the Journal of Tsulanan and begins to understand that the script is not meant to be translated normally.
- [[Zymve]] rejoins the party, gets questioned by Ky'tha, and researches Thallgrove fungi at Soothing Spores.
- The party goes to She'nog's fishing hole and discovers stone giants mining a silver deposit in the lake.
- Ainslie and Og try to deceive the giants in Giant, but the situation turns into combat.
- The party defeats the stone giants, recovers a large silver ore chunk, and earns Kar'Solar favor from She'nog's family.
- The party sells the silver at Firmforge and negotiates passage on Grindle Ashwake's vessel.
- Ky'tha receives a mental message from Lady Rook's side about needing help back in Emberstran.
- During lake travel, Zymve has a terrifying vision of the cavern ceiling opening and tentacles grabbing her, which she connects to the Shadow of Rothenloch.
- The party reaches Thallgrove and meets Makra the genling.

## Combats

- Combat against two stone giants at the fishing hole.

## NPCs Introduced

- Nelka Nittabomb, in Zymve's shopping scene.
- She'nog's sisters at the Fishery & Smokehouse.
- Grindle Ashwake.
- Makra the genling.

## Clues Found

- Thallgrove spores may enter through the skin and cause madness.
- Stone giants were mining a large silver deposit from the lake.
- Lady Rook is concerned about the party's location with the marrowstone.
- Zymve believes her vision may involve the Shadow of Rothenloch.
- Genlings can communicate telepathically and through Deep Speech.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Party recovers and sells a large silver ore chunk for gold and favor.
- Zymve buys Valecap Extract.
- Party earns Kar'Solar favor.

## Decisions Made

- The party chooses to help She'nog before leaving Nunglthil.
- The party charters Grindle Ashwake for passage toward Thallgrove.
- Zymve agrees to communicate with Makra and follow him toward his village.

## Consequences

- The party strengthens favor in Nunglthil.
- The route toward the Sanctuary enters Thallgrove.
- Zymve becomes a key Deep Speech communicator with Makra.

## Character Beats

- Zymve provides tactical magic against the giants and later handles Thallgrove-related research.
- Og kills a giant underwater after being dragged down.
- Ainslie continues leaning into identity, bloodline, and social deception.
- Ky'tha continues to receive outside political pressure connected to Lady Rook.
