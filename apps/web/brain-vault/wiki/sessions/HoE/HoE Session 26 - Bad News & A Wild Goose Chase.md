# Session 26 - Bad News & A Wild Goose Chase

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  May 25, 2026
In-game Date:  44th of Talil, 1246
Starting Location:  City of Emberstran
In Emberstran
At “Our Exquisites”, the headquarters for the Etterset, Tessa pulls a paper from her pouch and slides it to Ky’tha.  This came from Ulgrey.  


“Four of them pulled up in a carriage. Some wore the crimson cloaks. Not all. One pointed a finger at her husband… black fog wrapped around him and he dropped. Another cast something on Phira. She started groveling on the ground. They chained her and took her with them.”
She finds out that they left Ulgrey.  There is a quick discussion between Tessa and Ky’tha about how word of Phira’s travels got to the Cloak, but no conclusions are drawn.
Ky’tha heads to the Temple to Diverra and looks for Hap.  She tells him the news and he replies “confound it; bad news and a wild goose chase.”  They discuss needing to go to Ulgrey to investigate, but also maybe trying to confront the carriage on the road if we assume that they are heading to Emberstran from Ulgrey.    They discuss Ky’tha heading to the Warm Bear Inn to tell Og and Zymve; while Hap will catch Aury and Ains up to speed.
Ky’tha gets to the Inn and there is still frivolity in the air.  Og is drinking with the hunters, Zymve, and Zabeek.  Ky’tha briefs them.  Discussion about how Rook might not be the best to tell, but Ky’tha explains that she and Hap will visit her, while the rest provision up.  Ky’tha advises rest and heads back to the Hydra to rest as well.
It’s the 45th of Talil, 1246
The group wakes up.  At the Warm Bear Inn, Zymve goes down and sees the owner (Gustav) cleaning up.  She notices a platinum band on his hand with a ruby - and a distinctive style that looks drow. She is on edge and tries to make small talk to look closer.  He notices and holds up his hand to show her.  He says that someone left it here, settling his tab with it vs. coin.  It was a patron who had been there a few days.   This is a shadow thread gem - from Zymve’s family.  She inquires about the man and is told he was a nicely dressed drow, black leather and midnight blue silks, very polite.  Points out where he sat….like he was expecting something. Zabeek then comes down and asks where they are going, then remembers.  Og then comes down, yawning.  Zymve thanks him for the information and is on edge.  Og notices that Zymve is off today as they start heading to the temple.
Ains feels better today than he has in a long time.  He overhears a conversation in the room next to him.  He hears the Priestess talking to Arnol.  Arnol said he got a message saying he needed to get information and was going to Argent Halls when he was jumped.  He tells her he is not going to have anything to do with the cult anymore and that he should have listened to Aury sooner.  She then comes into the room and places a hand on Aury’s shoulder and tells him that Arnol will be ok; that he is leaving the cult.  She asks Aury to look into that cult and make sure that they are not a threat to Emberstran.  About this time, Ains wakes up and asks how he got here.  Aury tells him that Og carried him and that he prayed over him all night.   Ains says he feels “open” (but not open to Diverra when Aury asks him).  Aury says it might be best to send Arnol to another temple in another city to give him a fresh start and away from here.  They discuss where to send him.  
A Stranger Visits the Temple
Hap then comes into the room and notices an acolyte letting in a tall elf with ice blue eyes who smiles at Hap as he approaches him.  He hands Hap a small wrapped box wrapped in crimson silk with a bow. 


“Some gifts carry things beyond their size and this gift has waited long enough.”  
Hap says we have not met and asks him who he is:  “probably a story for another day,” as he accepts the box and the elf walks away and out of the Temple.  
Aury and the Priestess discuss sending Arnol to Stoneshore.  The Priestess leaves as Aury gives her 20 gold pieces for Arnol’s travel.  Hap approaches and briefs Aury on his sister and also carries this box.  Ains hears as well.  They discuss strategies.  He tells them he wants to go to Ulgrey.  Ains asks about the package.  No one recognized the elf who delivered it. They discuss the plan - needing to reprovision, maybe getting mounts.  Aury questions if Zymve would know how to ride. Ains mentions that he knows that drow have associations with spiders so maybe so.  Hap starts to open the package.   It is nice crimson silk with a black bow.  There is a very fine box inside - elven make, with inlay of silver in the wood.  He sees a severed finger wearing a signet ring.  This is definitely his father’s signet ring and the finger is fresh.  There is a note attached to the lid. 


“Hap - unfortunately the brothers Varmond had to be dealt with.  I’ve asked your sister to be sent to me for a discussion.  Maybe in time we can earn the respect of you and your companions.” 
– signed the Shadow Sovereign.  
Ky’tha is able to piece together the following:
* The Shadow Sovereign is the leader of the Cloak of Defiance and is likely in the Bellows in Basctdelm (successful History check)
* This finger was likely Jerik’s finger - he must have taken the ring from Hap’s father
* The elf that delivered the box is likely the new leader of the Cloak of Defiance in Emberstran
* This new leader likely is the one that took out Jerik Varmond
So Phira is being taken there to talk to the Shadow Sovereign…and we no longer need to go to Ulgrey.  Discussion about finding a quicker way to get there - maybe through Imonorra?  
Ains asks the group to sit around him for 10 minutes.  We do and it feels like we have webs in our brain that allows the group to talk telepathically to each other (this is the Telepathic Bond spell).  Aury chose to not take part….but Ains can still talk to him, he just isn’t part of the group chat.  Hap and Ky’tha head to talk to Lady Rook, while Ains goes to Imonorra’s.  Zymve, Zabeek, and Og go shopping. Aury stays back to heal Arnol and take him to a ship. 
At the Suncrest Cellarium
Hap and Ky’tha head to the Suncrest Cellarium out of the town to meet with Lady Rook.  They knock on the door and are met with someone who says that Lady Rook probably doesn’t want to talk to us.  But we insist and the guy goes to get her and we go in.  Ky’tha and Hap apologize and brief her on the situation.  Ask for the dragon’s scale.  Offers to give her information.  Tells her about the brothers being taken out and discussion about why someone would do that.  Hap shows her the finger and the note.  Discussion about new leadership in the Cloak.  Discussion about mutual trust.  She hands Ky’tha the silver dragon scale.  Asks for the funds returned from the spy (600 gold pieces).  Ky’tha gives her 50 gold pieces as a show of good faith.   Discussion about Ains asking for help with passage to Basctdelm from Imonorra and finds out that the wizard is out of town, returning tomorrow.  And they leave.
At the Silver Spaulder
Zymve, Zabeek, and Og go to the Silver Spaulder.  They see the owner, Vyaanis Aeldorien, opening up the shop.  Zymve disguises herself as Lynda again and asks Og and Zabeek to wait outside.  She goes inside and is welcomed.  She mentions that she ran into a drow like him the other day and asks him if he knew another drow - she mentions the ring he was wearing and that she knows that he came from the Underdark.  She asks if he has Underdark contacts.  She asks him if he has seen a drow in the last few days - trying to find out who that guy might have been.  He says his memory is fading and she pays him 1gp.  He then mentions that the guy was asking about fine armor.  She then pays him another 1gp and asks for his name.  He tells her he was from a big lake in the Underdark (Ys’nonterien).  He didn’t give him his name….said he was from a certain house, but can’t remember. She says that she will give him a 20% cut of anything she buys from the drow.  He is from House Maerth.  


Vyaanis leans in … “This guy seems very dangerous, you should be careful.”
She says if you ever run across him again, I’d be obliged if you would tell the Priestess to tell Aury and will pay you for information.  She then pays him another 1gp for his information. 
She walks outside and is more anxious than before.  Og asks if she is ok.  He hands her a bug and she squashes it.  He knows that she is grumpy.  They decide to go buy rations for Og and ink for Ains. Zabeek pulls up to Zymve and asks her what is wrong -”you don’t look fine.”  She says that you have to hide your feelings when you become a hero: “never let them know your weaknesses.”  Og then brings up his dead friend and Zabeek gets sad and says “this is really hard, Zymve” and tries to push the emotions down.  She tells him that is the healthy thing to do, as Og comments that drinking will help, too.  They then stock up. 
Aurelius to Escort Arnol
Aury heals Arnol and talks with him about moving him to another city to get a fresh start.  He is excited about this and is a willing participant.  He gathers his belongings and the two head to the docks to secure passage for Arnol on the ship known as The Wandering Lantern.
At Imonorra’s Tower
Ains goes to Imonorra’s Tower and the assistant comes to the door.  He asks to see the wizard and finds out that she is not there.  The butler goes to get tea for Ains and a small fire elemental emberling appears.  Ains mentions that the butler and wizard are close.  They talk about her powers to help transport people and Ains asks directly if she has a book of sigils.  He mentions that she probably has her books on her.  Says that she will be back tomorrow.  He questions if she has an extra set of books and asks to borrow it.  He says that he can’t loan out her books.  Ains offers a trade to him and he says to share what he has to offer.  Ains says he has important political information that the wizard would like and then asks for the Basctdelm Arcane Waypoint Sigil.  He says it takes it to the House of Myr and if you show up unannounced that they will kill you.  Ains asks what the man looks like who runs sigil in the House of Myr.  The butler describes the contacts at the House of Myr to reach out to before arriving:
* Primary contact:  Igdan O’bald – male human, mid-forties, short salt-and-pepper hair, trimmed beard, weathered features
* Secondary contact:  Gelora Fornak – female half-elf, long auburn hair, keen emerald eyes
* Alternate contact:  Abaddonn – May not always be there; male tiefling, tall and muscular, tattoos  
Ains then asks to get the sigil in return for the info.  Butler says that he needs to give up the information first.  Ains says that he has info about the Cloak of Defiance and offers it up.  The butler gets a book, goes to the opposite end of the table, turns to a particular page, shows Ains the Basctdelm sigil.  He tells Ains not to touch the book as he studies the image.   He sees the name of the person that he should contact before using the circle.  Ains thanks him for his help, drinks his tea, and Ains tells him about Jerik being killed and about the tall, blue-eyed elf that appears to now be running the Cloak of Defiance in Emberstran. Ains then tries to send a message to the blue-eyed elf from “the heroes of Emberstran.”  He receives a message back and tells the butler that the guy is in town and seems to be powerful and that the wizard should be “on her guard.”  Butler agrees to relay the warning to her and hopes she will find the information an equitable trade.  Ains then asks to see the wizard’s sigil and says he would never show up unannounced.  He agrees to show him.  They walk upstairs to the third floor where there is a circle.  Ainslie makes note of the Tower of Imonarra’s Arcane Waypoint Sigil.  Keep in mind that if you show up the arcane wards will, well ... and points to a display on the wall where there are gemstones set out.  One of them looks like a film inside…they hold the people who showed up unannounced.  Ains asks for her titles.  He says he isn’t sure, but maybe Imonorra the Magnificent.   And Ains leaves.  He tells us that the wizard was not at the tower, but “I got what we needed.”  Ky’tha says they will meet at the temple.  Hap asks Ains to reach out to Phira and see how she is - is she safe, well, where is she, that we know about her husband and Jerik is dead, too; Hap is worried about her; and that Ains will save her. 
Ainslie reaches out to Phira with a Sending spell.  Phira responds that she is not well, but safe. Says she doesn’t know where she is at.  Knows she will be questioned.  Is unable to be saved. Ains responds back and finds out that the group listened to his message to Phira. She says do not go to Basctdelm or she might die.  Ainslie knows these must be powerful people, reading her thoughts and having a way of cutting off any ability to locate her.   
Meanwhile, Aury puts Arnol on the ship - makes sure he is safe.  They discuss how Arnol doesn’t know how Aury adventures.  He then shares that the people who beat him up weren’t cultists, but seemed to be thugs.  He also says I don’t know why the master (of the cult) would have sent me out alone.  He is evil.  He says he hopes the cultists don’t hurt anyone else in the town.  He then offers to take Aury to the cultist hideout, but Aury convinces him that they can’t go, but asks Arnol for directions on how to get there.  Arnol admits that he thought Ains was smarter than he was.  Arnol tells Aury that Ains asked Arnol to spy on the cultists for them.  And tells Aury that Ains and Og also visited the cult group, too - that Arnol saw him there.  Aury asks if there is anything else Ains said.  Arnol just says he went to be a spy and got pulled in and that “those people are weird.”  Aury gives him instructions on what to do when he gets to Stoneshore:  start small, don’t be too ambitious.  Arnol hugs Aury and then says “stay celibate” as he turns to board the ship.
At the Dyed Cane
The group meets at the Temple and walks to the Dyed Cane.  There we ask to go up to the private room upstairs - Aury pays the owner 1gp.  He asks about the tablet that we were working on. Aury shows it to him and they talk about it a little bit.  Aury shares that the group met a dragon.  He knows of Iragossa.  He is surprised that we are still alive.  He says that we need to tell this story to the town.  That Zymve would be the best one to tell it.  She is not excited to jump in.  Discussion of changing our name.  Ky’tha brings up wanting to talk to Zymve at some point about her drow connections.  She says she doesn’t really have any.  Ky’tha asks her if she feels like the drow armorer is an honest broker. Zymve thinks so.  Aury brings up needing to make sure that the cultist group is not a threat to the town.  Zymve asks about the cult group - the Eradic of Ghone - how they rob graves.  
We discuss approaching the cult.  Hap puts the family signet ring on after Ains cleans it up.  Hap needs to thank the elf for the ring.  He wants to find out what the guy wants.  He has his sister and yet also wants to make peace with the Heroes of Emberstran.  “This is not congruent.”  As we talk, Aury starts to pray to Diverra.   Seems like we have multiple paths - take care of the cultist group, approach OR wait to talk to the new Cloak of Defiance leader(s), take the dragon scale and try to find the next gem. Aury lifts his head and says that “I got an actual answer from Diverra:  we are not meant to go after the Cloak (that is not Aury’s calling), the cult (the cult is no more), and about Phira (and she says that could hurt Phira).  
Aury asks the group to check out the cultist group.  We agree to go. It is mid-afternoon. We walk to the Church of Crael to gather for 10 minutes - all except Aury - including Zabeek. Zabeek goes to Warm Bear Inn to be a lookout.  He gets lost and goes to the hideout.  The rest of the group catches up.  There are people outside who look like hired Emberstran militia.  The entrance is heavily boarded over.  The area has been sealed off by the Lord. Ains and Og present their EGD badges and ask for entrance.  The guard tells us that he has heard noises inside - he thinks the cultist have all become zombies.  The guard pulls boards off to open up the entrance.  As the final board is pulled off and the door is opened - there is a nasty stench of rotting flesh, decay and sewage.  Many of the walls are busted down and broken.  We see a hole in the floor that leads to the sewers.
We go inside. We hear splintering boards above and dust and debris fall down. Haps asks Aury to light up Og’s glaive. We see something huge drop and stop and turn and look at us.  This is an eye with a lot of tentacles pointing to us.  
IT IS INITIATIVE – Combat vs. Zombie Beholder and Vampire
* ROUND ONE:  Og rages and moves up to the side of the creature and attacks and hits.  He tries to knock it prone, but can’t.  He then attacks again and hits again and stays put. Ains shoots an orb of lightning at it and misses.  He then runs into another room and pushes Hap 5’ to the side.  Ky’tha moves over to Og and attacks and misses then disengages.  Zymve casts a spell and it hits, inflicting psychic damage. Then moves to the corner of the adjacent room.  The creature casts a pulse of energy at Aury (wisdom) and sends another pulse of energy to Hap (constitution).  Both save. Aury casts radiance of the dawn on it and it saves, taking half damage. Hap runs up to the other side of him, Fleur providing a distraction, and hits with his sword.  Then he hits with the pruning dagger. He then attacks with his short sword again and fumbles, falling prone. From the ground he hits with the pruning dagger again.  
* ROUND TWO:  Zymve sees a mist come down and wrap around Ains and then appears behind him.  She is scantily clad with pale skin, long dark hair, eyes glowing red.  She whispers to Ains:  Countess Nas Verdidra will not be pleased with this (Hap overhears).  Both Ains and Hap know that the Countess rules a territory on the east side of Myrdae:  a place called Khasvadia in Var Shala.  She attacks Ains and misses him, as he throws up a shield.  She then hits him with her second attack and knocks him prone and tries to bite his neck as he is grappled.  She bites him and he takes additional damage, which also reduces his maximum hit points.Og hears something and sees a woman biting Ains on the neck beyond the beholder. He attacks the creature and hits. He then hits again and kills him - as he falls into the sewer below, where Og sees zombies below.  He then moves to the vampire and attacks her, hitting and knocking her prone.  Ains’ eyes flare purple with gold and reaches out his hands to cast a spell on her, imposing disadvantage on her strength save, but she chooses to save.  Ky’tha dashes over and attacks, hitting her with a sneak attack.  Zymve attacks with a spell, hitting with psychic damage.  She would be frightened of Zymve, but chooses to save.  She then gives inspiration to Ky’tha, Ains, and Og, giving them 8 temp hit points.  Aury casts daylight around the vampire and group.  Hap runs over to the vampire and hits her with his shortsword.  Then hits with the pruning dagger. Then hits with the short sword again and tries the dagger again and hits. 
* ROUND THREE:  Og tries something to move the vampire, but can’t.  She maintains her bite on Ains, inflicting more damage and reducing his max hit points further. She looks up at Aury and tries something on him that fails. She then uses her claws on Ains and misses then fumbles, staying prone. She stands up and moves and Hap hits with his opportunity attack.  Og gives Ains 9 temp hit points then moves to attack the vampire and hits twice, dealing huge damage and reducing her speed by 25’. She looks in the pits at a zombie and “misty steps” into the pit next to the zombie.  She claws a zombie’s neck.  Ains stands up and moves to the edge of the pit and tries to do something, but she chooses to save.  He then misty steps away from the hole.  She looks at Og and he has to make a saving throw and says “halt” to him.  Ky’tha moves next to the hole and looks down, she is 15’ down.  Ky’tha jumps into the pit on her and attacks and hits, then disengages.  Zymve runs to the hole and casts a spell and she saves.  She then gives bardic inspiration to Ky’tha.  Aury casts daylight in the hole.  Hap jumps into the hole and lands on her.  He then attacks her with his shortsword and hits and then hits with the pruning dagger.  She yells “avenge me countess” and the sunlight starts to fry her body.  
Hap and Ky’tha then kill the zombies in the hole.  They set fire to the zombies/vampire.
Then the group hears in their heads from Zabeek (except Aury) …


“Hey - this guy is really cool, he just gave me a ruby ring.” 
– The End of the Session –
