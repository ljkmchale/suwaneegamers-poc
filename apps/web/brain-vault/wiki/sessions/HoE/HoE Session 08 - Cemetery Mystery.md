# HoE Session 08 - Cemetery Mystery

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 08
- Session Title: Cemetery Mystery
- Date Label In Notes: 07.16
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- Ky'tha meets Lady Rook at the tower. Rook gives Ky'tha a Potion of Invisibility, knows Ky'tha's real name, and invites the party to dinner with like-minded people at Old Oak & Grapes.
- At the temple, the party discusses their growing reputation and Arnol's heroic self-image.
- A hunter messenger from Lady Tarathial invites the party to Suncrest Cellarium.
- At the Dyed Cane, Bordi repeats the rumor that Kelona was killed because she knew who poisoned the wine.
- The party buys better clothes. Ky'tha perfectly copies/forges land records, and Ainslie ages them.
- At Suncrest Cellarium, Lady Tarathial thanks the party, pays them, and says Benoit may have been rightful heir. She recalls Phira died in 1241 and is buried in Crael Cemetery.
- Lady Tarathial admits the Cloak has long been part of town life and she has dealt with them because she had to.
- At Crael Cemetery, the groundskeeper "Guy" is revealed as Rono. He says the body in Benoit's grave has no horns, Eldermer Cetaen is buried in Benoit's grave, and Phira's grave is empty.
- Rono says Ruthless Jerik paid him to do burial swaps.
- At Jae's house, the party finds Jae dead, stabbed in the back. Bloody footprints suggest a small figure. A hidden box under the floor contains 15pp and a crimson liquid vial pulsing like a heartbeat.
- At dinner with Lady Rook, Ehkel an'Davo attends. When Rook touches the Marrowstone, it flashes indigo and gives Ainslie a vision of a golden-mantled familiar figure, a dark-robed adversary, and the words "bound, bound and not broken."
