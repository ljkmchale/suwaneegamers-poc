# HoE Session 21 - He Ate a Baby

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: HoE
- Session Number: 21
- Session Title: He Ate a Baby
- Date Label In Notes: 04.08
- Real Date: 2026-04-08
- In-Game Date: 33rd of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Emberstran
- Campaign Scope: HoE only

## Major Scenes

- The party wakes at the Temple of Diverra after Ainslie empties the Marrowstone.
- Ky'tha confronts Ainslie because she and Hap had promised the stone to Lady Rook.
- The party debates whether the Marrowstone's energy is specific to Ainslie and whether it can be refilled to fight Blackrend.
- Aury presents the party with blessed Badges of Emberstran / E.G.D.
- Zymve shops under the name Lynda and intimidates a leatherworker into a better trade.
- Hap and Ky'tha visit Lady Rook's location but speak with a confused captain, framing the emptied Marrowstone as an accident.
- The party buys provisions, gains Ewing the disguised goat/gertot, and leaves Emberstran.
- A red-cloaked rider threatens the party on behalf of Jerik; Ainslie kills him and claims he "ate a baby" to justify the killing to witnesses.
- The party travels into the Baltwood toward Mount Emberstran.
- The party fights quarath / surface quaggoth-like creatures and finds a dead half-elf with warning notes about an orange shadow near the old sealed way.
- During watch, Zymve hears music connected to Ainslie's journal and finds comfort in it.
- Og and Hap see a flying ship and a dragon crossing the moon at dawn.

## Combats

- Red Rider confrontation near Emberstran.
- Combat against quarath / surface quaggoth-like creatures in the Baltwood.

## NPCs Introduced

- Ewing the goat/gertot.
- Red Rider of the Cloak of Defiance.
- Ferdinand, a local who offers to dispose of the body.

## Clues Found

- Ainslie feels changed after absorbing the Marrowstone's energy.
- The Badges of Emberstran / E.G.D. become an official-looking authority tool.
- The Cloak of Defiance knows the party returned and Jerik has their scent.
- A dead half-elf carries a warning about an orange shadow near the old sealed way.
- Zymve hears a personal, comforting chant tied to Ainslie's journal.
- A dragon appears to pursue or attack a flying ship.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Party receives Badges of Emberstran / E.G.D.
- Party buys provisions, bedrolls, ropes, wine, and Ewing.
- Party takes sword/dagger from the Red Rider.
- Party finds silvered short sword, magical hand crossbow, magical oily bolts, coins, rations, and warning note from the dead half-elf.
- Ky'tha takes a quarath claw trophy.

## Decisions Made

- The party keeps the emptied Marrowstone and leaves to refill or use it against blight.
- The party leaves Emberstran for Mount Emberstran.
- Ainslie sends messages to Phira, creating fresh anxiety around Hap's family.

## Consequences

- The Cloak of Defiance threat escalates.
- Ainslie's handling of authority and bodies becomes more morally messy.
- Zymve's connection to Ainslie's journal deepens.
- The dragon / flying ship thread becomes immediate.

## Character Beats

- Ainslie feels renewed and possibly altered by the Marrowstone.
- Zymve uses intimidation, sees more surface social life, and later bonds with Ainslie by the fire.
- Hap's sword continues cryptic communication.
- Aury works through Diverra doctrine and priestly responsibilities.
