1. First Sarcophagus – A heavy torque of blackened silver set with three cloudy green stones (75 gp) and a pair of matching bracers (40 gp).
2. Second Sarcophagus – A thick finger ring carved from a single piece of dark jade in the shape of a spiral (90 gp) and a small pouch of 12 tarnished gold coins.
3. Third Sarcophagus – A ceremonial dagger with a blade of green-tinged bronze and a hilt wrapped in what was once living vine (now desiccated); the dagger is non-magical but finely made (60 gp).
4. Fourth Sarcophagus – A necklace of alternating black iron links and polished river stones (55 gp) and a single large, cloudy emerald (120 gp).
5. Fifth Sarcophagus – A set of three matching arm-rings of corroded silver, each etched with the Clotted Spiral (80 gp total).
6. Sixth Sarcophagus – A heavy pendant of black stone carved into a clenched fist dripping spiral lines (100 gp) and a small sealed clay vial containing a thick, still-liquid sludge that smells of the tomb (worthless but disturbing).
The tunnel leads to the outdoors, where there is a pool.  This must be where the hunter saw the red slaad, the threat that led us here.
Sitting above in the upper level where Zabeek died, Aury heals Og and Zymve returns to him the Thornleaf.  Putting the pieces together, the party surmises:
* Blackrend was no longer a druid, but a corrupt undead, perhaps from his loyalty to Nigrum.
* The aboleth must have a history here in the shrine, feeding off the memories of the cultist, and using those memories to call forth Guigzien.
* Blackrend was used as part of the ritual to return Guigzien … who may be roaming the land today.
On the upper level the party plans is to return to Ulgrey to find a way to bring Hap back … if possible.  Aury plans on reaching out to his goddess Diverra after some time to rest and gather himself.  Zymve will also reach back out to Thaelex to see if he can help.
