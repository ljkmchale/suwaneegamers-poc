# Dungeons III Session 04 - I Can't Even Take A Shit, Man

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 04
- Title: I Can't Even Take A Shit, Man
- Real Date: 2026-12-15
- In-Game Date: 9th of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: [[Hesfal]]
- Ending Location: Lean-to in the woods near the crag trail
- Attending Characters: [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Aeon]]

## Major Scenes

- The party leaves Hesfal to track the bandits southeast toward the Mahruud, while Nova stays behind to investigate.
- Meles tracks hoofprints, signs of multiple riders on one horse, and blood from the horse he shot.
- The party meets Ralli, an Inner Gate guard who claims he abandoned Scarwatch Hold.
- Draelith realizes Ralli's route story does not add up.
- The party follows a game trail through difficult brush and craggy terrain.
- The party fights an owlbear and stirges.
- Nixie finds a recent stash in a hollow tree.
- The party finds three bandit horses tied near a lean-to and sets an ambush.

## Combats

- Owlbear and stirges.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Stash from hollow tree: 85 sp, 30 gp, bone dice worth 5 gp, rations, strong liquor, bedroll/backpack, and healer's kit.
- A share is set aside for Nova.

## Clues Found

- Bandits likely took a rugged route unsuitable for horses.
- Ralli may be lying or omitting why he was south of where he claimed.
- The three horses at the lean-to appear to be the bandits' escape horses.

## Decisions Made

- Leave the horses at the lean-to instead of taking them through the crags.
- Set an ambush and wait for the bandits to return.
