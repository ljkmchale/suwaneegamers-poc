# Dungeons III Session 09 - A Cart-time Job

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 09
- Title: A Cart-time Job
- Real Date: 2026-04-06
- In-Game Date: 14th-15th of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: [[Scarwatch Hold]]
- Ending Location: Road outside Scarwatch Hold
- Attending Characters: [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Nova]], [[Aeon]]

## Major Scenes

- The Warden asks the party's name; they answer [[The kNight Watch]].
- The Warden asks them to find Isgo.
- Dempsey, an Inner Gate guard from Varenwood, recognizes Meles from his past.
- Umlin hires the party to map the surrounding lands for 20 gp, paying 10 gp up front.
- Odo at the general store rewards the party with extra parchment after they solve his "Fire Ghost" problem.
- Seraphine visits the Sanctuary and speaks with Curate Warrall.
- Aeon visits Ilho and buys a small pendant with a light pink gem.
- At Hoot & Howl, Dempsey tells Meles about Aviv and Gurimond from Varenwood.
- Fikrak Blackshield inspects Nova's brooch and reads its inner warning.
- Warrall reports the Sanctuary was defaced with the [[Radiant Eye Cult]] symbol.
- Nambra says the cult is active and may have an outpost in caves in the crags.
- Embryl tells Draelith about the Last Watchtower of the Osmanae and asks the party to retrieve a celestial instrument.
- The party rides out toward the crags and sees a riderless black horse with a white blaze.

## NPCs Introduced

- Dempsey / Demp, Inner Gate guard and Meles's old acquaintance.
- Odo, general store owner.
- Warrall, current curate at the Sanctuary.
- Selris, previous curate, possibly linked by rumor to Radiant Eye activity.
- Embryl Lonfren, researcher interested in Osmanae records.
- Arlette, mercantile merchant.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Umlin pays 10 gp up front for the mapping job.
- Aeon buys a small pendant with a light pink gem.
- Nova's brooch inscription is read: "Bound against inner fracture. Do not separate stone from lattice. Containment preserves more than form."

## Clues Found

- Radiant Eye cultists defaced the Sanctuary by scaling the wall.
- Two medium humanoids on two horses likely committed the defacement around 2-3 in the morning.
- Selris, previous curate, may have been associated with the cult.
- Embryl believes the Osmanae celestial instrument may be in ancient ruins east of Scarwatch Hold, possibly near cult activity.

## Decisions Made

- Prioritize investigating Radiant Eye activity over Isgo after Nambra's warning.
- Travel east toward the crags.

## Open Threads

- Find Isgo.
- Investigate Radiant Eye cultists and their possible crag outpost.
- Recover the Osmanae celestial instrument for Embryl and the Advents of Harmony.
- Chart landmarks around Scarwatch Hold for Umlin.
