# Dungeons III Session 05 - The Hots for Nova

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 05
- Title: The Hots for Nova
- Real Date: 2026-01-05
- In-Game Date: 9th-10th of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: Lean-to in the woods
- Ending Location: K9 Crew cave hideout
- Attending Characters: [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Nova]], [[Aeon]]

## Major Scenes

- Sherdin arrives at the lean-to and explains he keeps horses for hunters and travelers.
- Nova arrives riding Ralli's horse and explains Ralli gave it to her.
- Sherdin says the Cap'n's group left their horses and went down a rugged trail.
- Sherdin explains Ralli received a package from Ireosh, Sherdin's brother at Scarwatch Hold, for the Cap'n.
- Draelith and Nixie observe Salvus from a clearing.
- The party follows the rugged trail toward the Jagged Waste Crags.
- The party finds a cave behind a tree in the rocks.
- The party fights bandits in the cave and forces surrender.
- The party learns the group is the [[K9 Crew]], hired to terrorize Hesfal during Salvus.
- The Cap'n reveals a pouch/message from the [[Scorpion Network]].
- A hum and voice asks, "Why did I pay you?"

## Combats

- Cave bandit encounter against the K9 Crew.

## NPCs Introduced

- Sherdin, lean-to keeper.
- Gimpy, Sherdin's pet rat.
- Cap'n / Kinrik, K9 Crew leader.
- Corin and other K9 Crew members.

## Clues Found

- Ralli's package to the Cap'n came through Ireosh at Scarwatch Hold.
- K9 Crew intended to terrorize Hesfal during Salvus.
- One K9 Crew member found a jeweled hairpin while casing Hesfal, which caused the murder accusation problem.
- Scorpion paid the K9 Crew and can communicate with them magically.

## Decisions Made

- Camp with Sherdin, then pursue the rugged trail.
- Capture rather than simply kill the surrendered K9 Crew.
