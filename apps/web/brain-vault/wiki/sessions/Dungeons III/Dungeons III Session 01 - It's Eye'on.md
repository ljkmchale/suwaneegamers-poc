# Dungeons III Session 01 - It's Eye'on

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 01
- Title: It's Eye'on
- Real Date: 2026-10-10
- In-Game Date: 8th-9th of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: [[Hesfal]]
- Attending Characters: [[Aeon]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Meles]], [[Seraphine Veyne]]

## Major Scenes

- Aeon wakes at Moonfern Sanctuary and hears about the Hall of Many Hands.
- Draelith, Nixie, and Meles are introduced at the Covered Hooch.
- Seraphine tends injuries around the Covered Hooch and speaks with Mimony about travel and the Awakening.
- Aeon visits the Hall of Many Hands, meets Kadella Tenbrook, hears about Osanna, and speaks to Laestrik, follower of Goldraen.
- The party members begin converging when Draelith, Nixie, and Meles overhear Aeon's god-seeking conversation.
- Seraphine hears a murder rumor from Wargus, Saquel, and Gimdy.
- Draelith, Nixie, and Meles visit the Artisan Exchange, where Irick and Gimri help with Draelith's headband and Nixie's star story.
- Aeon and Seraphine recognize each other from a medical encounter about ten years earlier.
- Frath and Ravina Rool announce the final big caravan arrival before Salvus.

## NPCs Introduced

- Ilyana and Alarine at Moonfern Sanctuary.
- Kadella Tenbrook at the Hall of Many Hands.
- Laestrik, follower of Goldraen.
- Mimony, traveling healer headed toward Basctdelm.
- Wargus of Scarwatch Hold.
- Saquel and Gimdy.
- Irick and Gimri at the Artisan Exchange.
- Frath and Ravina Rool at the Covered Hooch.

## Clues Found

- A young woman was murdered a few days earlier; rumor says the accused is held at the Town Ward.
- Wargus says Liquin at Scarwatch Hold may have a healer's journal Seraphine would find interesting.
- Mimony mentions an Arcane Waypoint at the Anchored Spire in Tratta.

## Character Beats

- Aeon is actively seeking a god and tries Osanna prayers.
- Draelith frames Celestine through a harp-shaped birthmark and magical presentation.
- Nixie is starstruck on the surface and inspires Gimri's painting.
- Meles quietly observes and eavesdrops rather than driving social scenes.
- Seraphine is strongly drawn to wounds, medical stories, and old injuries.
