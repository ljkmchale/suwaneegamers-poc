# Dungeons III Session 07 - Brooch the Subject

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 07
- Title: Brooch the Subject
- Real Date: 2026-02-24
- In-Game Date: 10th-13th of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: [[Hesfal]]
- Ending Location: [[Scarwatch Hold]]
- Attending Characters: [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Nova]], [[Aeon]]

## Major Scenes

- Nova catches Lavoruud going through her belongings for [[Nova's Dunduar Brooch]].
- Lavoruud says he felt hollow without the brooch and claims he won it from an orc in Tratta.
- Fikrak Blackshield examines the brooch and identifies Dunduar gold properties.
- The party receives prisoners and formal charges from Hesfal for delivery to Warden Haliun.
- On the road, the party meets Isgo, a half-orc on horseback, and learns he is wanted.
- Meles checks on the Blackshields after seeing signs of a recent fight with Isgo.
- The party reaches Scarwatch Hold and meets guards Atreice and Lynion.
- At the stables, the party meets Lyerith and Ireosh; Ireosh is Sherdin's brother.
- The party rooms at Watchtower Inn and visits the Hoot & Howl Tavern.
- Nikalia escorts Nixie and Aeon toward the Warden.
- Draelith and Nova help Ilho test lockboxes.
- Meles visits the barn just before an explosion at the Outer Gate.

## Combats

- Lavoruud is stopped and restrained after trying to retrieve the brooch.

## NPCs Introduced

- Fikrak Blackshield and Brumelynn Blackshield.
- Isgo, wanted half-orc thief/bandit.
- Atreice and Lynion, Scarwatch Hold gate guards.
- Lyerith, stablehand with pet mouse Puff.
- Ireosh, stable boss and Sherdin's brother.
- Dhorsol Blaregut, Watchtower Innkeeper.
- Nikalia, Scarwatch Hold guard captain.
- Ilho, gem store owner.

## Clues Found

- Nova's brooch is Dunduar-made and magical; its property is dampened.
- Isgo is wanted at Scarwatch Hold.
- Ireosh has already appeared in the Ralli/K9 Crew package chain.
- Outer Gate guards have a reputation for taking coin to let people in.
