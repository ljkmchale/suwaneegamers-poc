# Dungeons III Session 06 - Rope-A-Dope

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 06
- Title: Rope-A-Dope
- Real Date: 2026-02-02
- In-Game Date: 10th of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: Bandit hideout
- Ending Location: [[Hesfal]]
- Attending Characters: [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Nova]], [[Aeon]]

## Major Scenes

- Nixie finds a hidden box behind the wall where the Scorpion's voice came from.
- The box contains coin and a [[Blue Sending Stone]].
- Draelith studies the sending stone and concludes the cavern itself amplifies communication magic.
- The party fights a grick and a roper deeper in the cave.
- The party explores a steep descending tunnel and a chasm; Nixie suspects the path leads toward the Underdark.
- The party returns to Hesfal, meeting Kaiga on the road.
- The bandits are turned in for 20 gp.
- The party agrees to transport the bandits to Scarwatch Hold.
- Nova speaks with Mexan and gives him murder clues to take to Tratta/Scarwatch contacts.
- The session ends with a large figure going through Nova's belongings.

## Combats

- Grick.
- Roper.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Hidden box: 162 gp, 10 pp, 68 sp, and [[Blue Sending Stone]].
- Meles finds 5 gp, 120 sp, 30 cp, a tarnished silver ring with empty socket, and an exquisite warpick.
- Meles takes the Cap'n's nice scimitar.

## Clues Found

- The cavern enhances communication magic and may connect to gates or routes elsewhere.
- The deeper passage may lead toward the Underdark.
- The Scorpion can communicate through the stone and monitor outcomes.

## Decisions Made

- Bring the K9 Crew to justice.
- Escort the prisoners onward to Scarwatch Hold.
