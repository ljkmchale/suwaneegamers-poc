# Dungeons III Session 08 - The Stone finally Speaks

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 08
- Title: The Stone (finally) Speaks
- Real Date: 2026-03-16
- In-Game Date: 13th-14th of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: [[Scarwatch Hold]]
- Attending Characters: [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Nova]], [[Aeon]]

## Major Scenes

- An explosion at the Outer Gate reveals a wagon problem: a barrel that tried to bite a guard.
- The barrel is a mimic and attacks near the gate.
- Nixie and Aeon meet Warden Naevaris Haliun, Umlin Stonewatch, Nambra Maerth, and Nikalia in the Citadel.
- The Warden accepts the Hesfal letter and plans judgment for the K9 Crew.
- Lyerith and Seraphine tend the injured horse after the mimic fight.
- The party gathers at the Hoot & Howl Tavern.
- Meles takes a discreet bank job from Thiddle and discovers a burglar, likely Bryth.
- Meles also steals from a lockbox, then reports the burglary to Thiddle.
- At the public judgment, the Cap'n confesses to the Scorpion connection.
- The Cap'n and Koren are sentenced: Koren receives marking and hard labor; the Cap'n is conscripted to Scarwatch Hold.
- Draelith hears the Scorpion through the blue sending stone: "your interference has complicated matters, but perhaps you are more useful than they were."

## Combats

- Mimic barrel at the Outer Gate.

## NPCs Introduced

- Warden Naevaris Haliun.
- Umlin Stonewatch, dwarven scribe.
- Nambra Maerth, drow advisor.
- Barnes, wagon driver.
- Sagret, mercantile merchant.
- Thiddle, banker.
- Bryth, bored elven guard and likely bank burglar.
- Embryl Lonfren, Scarwatch House/library contact.

## Clues Found

- Isgo allegedly stole a crate and pouch from Barnes.
- Ireosh is rumored to be secretive and moving packages and gold.
- The Scorpion is aware of the party and may try to use them.

## Decisions Made

- Nixie and Aeon commit to attending the K9 Crew judgment.
- Meles resolves Thiddle's burglary quietly, but with his own theft mixed in.
