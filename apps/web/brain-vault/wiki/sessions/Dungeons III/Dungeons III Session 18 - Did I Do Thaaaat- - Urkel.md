# Session 18 - Did I Do Thaaaat? - Urkel

Source: raw/dungeons-iii-campaign-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1115KjT1J7g-jy4kQXBXzp4vHhoOhPyqrNxcTZkEEAHY/edit

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  October 26, 2026
In-game Date:  22nd of Evalee, 1246
Starting Location:  Jagged Waste Crags, Cave Terrace
At the Excavation Site; Watch Eastern Observation Point II 
We pick up in the bronze chamber.  Aeon takes some time to cast Detect Magic as a ritual.  Nova looks around the room (Perception check).  Some pieces and parts appear to be missing.  There are many markings all about, particular to celestial.  Nixie notices a basin with channels leading to it.  He sees knobs leading to the basin.  The Detect Magic that Aeon casts doesn’t reveal much.  Draelith sees a wheel that looks like it would move a reflecting glass, similar to what we saw in the other room.  Nixie uses a mage hand to move the knob.  Water is released and flows to the basin.  Eventually, the basin fills and the water stops flowing.  We stand still to see if the water settles.  Nixie sees calibration markings within the basin that appear to give some type of instruction.  The instructions show that the reflector needs to be used.  Draelith begins to move the wheel, but is having trouble lining it up.  Draelith calls Meles to help dial it in.  Soon we see light shoot up, reflect back down and show on a platform.  There is a bronze ring that has a sun and a bronze ring that has a moon.  Seraphine moves the bronze ring with the sun.  When she moves it, we hear a click and lighted lines move across the floor.  One line shoots out 20 feet and hits a blank wall.  As Draelith approaches, lights form into a harp.  Draelith feels warmth on his birthmark.  Seraphine examines the birthmark.  Nixie feels warmth on the bottom of his left foot.  As Seraphine examines the birthmark and notices the warmth.  Seraphine looks at Nixie’s foot and notices the harp mark on his foot as well.
Nova and Meles are checking out the door area because it withdrew into the wall a little bit. It opens when Meles touched the door, revealing a small chamber with keys, cases, magnifying glasses, workbenches… This area looks like it has been protected for a long time. No footprints, rubble, etc.
Aeon looks in a case in the room and there's an instrument in it. He senses the instrument has some sort of "power" and Draelith thinks this may be the instrument we're here for. There are markings on it, but we can't interpret them, yet. Draelith starts to crank up the Comprehend Languages ritual.  Draelith picks up that this was created somewhere else, some Underdark city named “Snarbaac”.  Nixie picks up the word “Cogspinner” .
Meles has moved up to the ceiling to provide a little bit of oversight. When Nixie looks at the instrument some more, he doesn't think it's a standalone thing or calibration device. Maybe more along the lines of the missing "brain" that Seraphine had a feeling about.  Draelith picks up the case that has the instrument.  As Draelith picks it up, he’s surprised that it is light and not connected.  Seraphine believes it is a metal that we’re not familiar with.  Seraphine grabs the parchments and books.
As we walk into the observatory chamber, we hear a loud “clunk” as if something is unlocked. Meles is off in another chamber, scraping a small symbol to Phoe in the wall.  The floor sinks and we feel vibrations.  Nova believes the place is going to collapse.  Draelith runs into the hall and falls into a hole.  The instrument case slides down the hall, but we pick it up.
Back in the chamber, we find three dials, each with the words Horizon, Meridian, Zenith.  Aeon starts turning the dials.  When he gets to HMH, a small door opens and there is a little staircase that descends.  Meles leads the way.  The staircase heads to a service tunnel that ends in a stone door.  Meles opens the door and it reveals the outside.  We look back and watch the observatory implode.  It’s about noon and cloudy.  
We work our way around the crags to find the caves that had Isgo.  We see a hobgoblin band, it’s the group that has Isgo, who is bound.  They give us the prisoner Isgo.  We don’t see signs of our mounts.  Meles look for our mounts, noticing khalbit prints.  After about an hour of following them we find the khalbit and the horse.
At camp on the travel back.
Seraphine studies Isgo’s bad health.  It's not anything that she’s ever studied.  He says that this illness has been there since he entered the caves.  Seraphine journals the symptoms and other signs of Isgo’s illness.  Nixie reads the Osmanae tomes.  There are references to “magic rocks” associated with the Broken One moon.  There appears to be disagreements on what to do with their studies.  It becomes obvious that one group became focused on magic and power, while the other group appears to be more interested in the studies.  The rocks are the same ones that the “sending stone” that Draelith has and that Nova’s brooch has, leading us to believe they are “magic rocks too”.  The people in the Osmanae were not magic wielders, but more of studies of the heavens.  Meles studies Gurimond’s journal.  It reveals many jobs and payments, with the latest entries being about the Radiant Eye.  Gurimond was hired in Tratta for 100 gold (half up front).  There is a mark of a “handler”, that actually hired Gurimond to take out people in a village; “strange to go back home.”  Meles doesn’t know the handler, but the mark is some bug or fly.  Aeon studies Selris’ journal.  “Maybe she was not so bad”. There is mention of the Radiant Eye base of operations in Ahnassa, where funds continue to show up.  (Someone has deep pockets that is funding the Radiant Eye).   She makes reference to someone named “Gas”.  She was a priest and then gathered a group for the expedition. 
It’s the Evening of the 27th of Evalee
We ensure the map is  up to date.  The head guard from the Inner Gate, Nikali, shows up and says she’ll arrange for us to meet with the Warden in the morning.  We stable the kahlbit and horse.  We take Isgo to the temple to be looked at by the priest.
 We go to the inn and get a good night's rest.  We level to 5.
It’s the morning of the 28th of Evalee
We go see the warden.  We are delayed while the warden is meeting with someone.  Eventually he shows up and welcomes us in.  He asks about Isgo and the cultists.  The dwarf comes in that told Nixie he would like an updated map.  Seraphine tells the warden that Isgo is at the Temple.  The warden questions us about the Radiant Eye.  Meles goes on about Gurimond, the squirrel killer.  We tell him that Selris, was involved.  Embryl shows up.  We tell him the story.  He says that Draelith and Nixie are “skytouched”.  Embryl has been studying the Osmanae for some time.  “Skytouched” are said to be born when the stars (heavens) align.  Embryl mentions his mentor, Gastavaven Nalen, who gave him the info on the Osmanae.
They pay us for the map and for Isgo, 20 gold each.  The Warden throws in another 30 gold for helping ensure that the Hold is secure.
Ahnassa is our destination
