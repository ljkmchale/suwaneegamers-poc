# Session 17 - We’ll Get His Foreskin

Source: raw/dungeons-iii-campaign-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1115KjT1J7g-jy4kQXBXzp4vHhoOhPyqrNxcTZkEEAHY/edit

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  September 28, 2026
In-game Date:  22nd of Evalee, 1246
Starting Location:  Jagged Waste Crags, Cave Terrace
At the Excavation Site; Watch Eastern Observation Point II 
After the battle, the lady that was sent away by Garamond and an elven male show up. We are staring at each other … she’s in awe at the carnage.  She turns to the elf and yells “run!”  We roll initiative to see what occurs:
* ROUND ONE:  The elf steps forward, casting a spell that obscures the path.  Meles casts Long Strider, hides and moves into the obscure area.  Nova casts Blade Ward and hides around the corner.  Nixie heals himself.  Draelith steps forward and casts Darkness down the hall, yelling “Halt, there is darkness beyond!”  Aeon moves up and casts Firebolt wildly, missing. He healed himself with Second Wind.  Seraphine moves for cover and drank a healing potion.  We are unable to see what they do with the Fog Cloud
* ROUND TWO:  We can’t tell what the elf is doing.  Meles runs forward through the fog cloud and into the darkness and bumps into the lady. Nova lays her hands on herself, making herself feel a little better then steps forward and cast  Vicious Mockery.  Nixie casts Message to Draelith and says “tell me if you see something”.  Draelith moves through the fog cloud and casts Charm Person, succeeding to charm her.  Aeon runs forward and runs into Draelith, knocking Draelith down.  Seraphine moves up near Nixie and Nova and waits.
* ROUND THREE:  The elf continues to run (dashing young lad).  Meles runs with all his might to chase the elf.  Nova heals herself and hands Seraphine a healing potion.  Nixie moves forward.  Draelith moves forward and speaks with the lady about a misunderstanding.  Aeon casts Blade Ward.  Seraphine takes the potion Nova gave her.
* ROUND FOUR:  Meles hears the elf yell “Go! Run! to others then pursues.  The rest of the party interacts with the lady.  Everyone but Meles and the other Radiant Eye members are out of combat.  
* ROUND FIVE:  Meles looks out while in stealth.  He sees other with horses.  One rides off.  One other tells the others to leave, that he’s staying.
The outdoor Radiant Eye members ride off, except for one that says “I’m going see this through.”  
Seraphine inspects the bodies: 45 gp, mason’s hammer, 2 measuring chords, Selris’ weathered journal and ring, Gurimond’s journal, surveys and drawings of the property.  Nixie goes in to assist to remove the blood and gore with prestidigitation.
Meanwhile, Meles sneaks up behind the guy heading back to the party and attacks him, then runs away.  The guy, a human, proceeds inside towards the group.  He yells, “Mira, what’s going on?”  Nova persuades her to tell him we’re okay.  “Dorin, they are not here to hurt us.”  He replies, “well, somebody is.”
Aeon tries to fool the two, “The assassin is with the people trying to kill you.”  Draelith tell Mira that he used magic to calm the situation and drops Charm Person.  They move to the camp site where their companions died.  Mira moves forward to the camp site with her.  Aeon and Nova convince Dorin to stay and wait for the assassin.  Seraphine appears to be praying over the bodies when Draelith and Mira go into the room.
On the other side of the fog cloud, Meles sneaks around, climbing up the wall, sneaking up.  Meles sneaks into the fog cloud, pulls out his pipe to smoke up some weed, steps out of stealth and emerges to speak with Dorin, Nova and Aeon.  Meles offers the group his “Moonweed” to take the edge off and they all partake.  
The fog clears, the bodies are covered in respect and everyone gathers at the camp site.
The group sets up watch and tries to take a long rest.  The next morning, we help move the bodies to the wagons and go get our horse to pull the wagon.  We ask about the Radiant Eye before they leave what they know about their cause.  They say they are studying the Osmanaes’ research and we should find them in Ahnassa at the Raise Reef Harbor if we get there.  Meles asks about the half-orc in case they’ve seen him, stating we were tasked to “get his foreskin.”
We return inside to check the door after identifying the ring and sword:  Ring of Protection and +1 Longsword.  The hallway reveals many images.  One catches Draelith’s eyes.  The woman in the carving has a similar “harp”shaped constellation as the birthmark he has.  Nixie notices the first panel pictures 3 intact moons and remembers that the ancient lore says that there used to be 3 moons even though there are currently only 2 and a Broken Moon.  At the door, Draelith notices the stone he got from the K9 crew grows warm.  We all use various skills to investigate how to open the door.  After an Investigation, an Arcana, a Perception and an Athletics check, we see dust and grinding as the carving in the door recedes and the door.  We see the center symbol almost as a key.  Nixie manipulates things and Meles joins in, then Draelith.  The symbol is moved to alignment.  The gears move and there is a sound that startles everyone.  The door opens.  Meles secures the doorway with a piton. 
There is a room beyond, with a bronze pedestal in a large room with a crystal that glows over the pedestal.  Meles searches for traps but doesn’t notice any.  There are three words on the pedestal, which we cannot understand.  Draelith casts Comprehend Languages and Aeon casts Detect Magic.  The crystal has magic and the words say: PLACE, SKY, WITNESS. As Draelith approaches the pedestal, Nova is hit with Radiant light, feeling a balance of Endelo’ar and Tudar (the moons of Oberra).  Nixie notices that there are lines on the floor around the pedestal and that the pedestal turns.
