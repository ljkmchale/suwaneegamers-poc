# Dungeons III Session 02 - Pocket Prism

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 02
- Title: Pocket Prism
- Real Date: 2026-11-03
- In-Game Date: 8th of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: [[Hesfal]]
- Attending Characters: [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Nova]], [[Aeon]]

## Major Scenes

- Salvus caravan traffic floods the Covered Hooch.
- Nova is introduced as an aasimar bard with dark hair, goggles, glowing eyes, and light studded armor.
- Pequin sells Nova [[Pequin's Pocket Prism]] after haggling.
- Seraphine tells the party about the murder rumors.
- At Boar's Best, Kadella greets Aeon and the party; the Jewel Tones perform, and Aeon performs well.
- Meles investigates the murder scene near the docks but finds little due to the delay.
- Nova goes to the Town Ward and learns the accused was found with the victim's hair clip.
- Five robbers hold up Boar's Best; the party fights them while town guards die.
- Meles and Nova go to the Town Ward, find guards tied up, and chase escaping prisoners/bandits.

## Combats

- Boar's Best robbery: party fights five robbers; one flees toward the kitchen.
- End-session setup: Meles and Nova confront five fleeing men behind the Town Ward.

## NPCs Introduced

- Nova.
- Pequin, local gnome tinkerer.
- Lavoruud Wrescot, self-styled "The Traveller."
- Ark and Herek, couriers.
- Mexan Tam, exhausted traveling elf/bard.
- Mardy at the Covered Hooch.
- Jewel Tones performers Onyx and Opal.
- Hizza, Saquel, and Gimdy at Boar's Best.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Nova buys [[Pequin's Pocket Prism]] for 1 gp and 5 sp.
- Party recovers robber gear: daggers, scimitars, leather armor, crossbows, bolts, and silver.

## Clues Found

- The murder victim was stabbed.
- The accused had the victim's hair clip.
- The tavern robbery and prisoner break appear connected.
