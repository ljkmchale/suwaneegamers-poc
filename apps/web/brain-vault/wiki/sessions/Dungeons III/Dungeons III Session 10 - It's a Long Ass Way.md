# Dungeons III Session 10 - It's a Long Ass Way

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 10
- Title: It's a Long Ass Way
- Real Date: 2026-05-04
- In-Game Date: 15th-21st of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: Road outside [[Scarwatch Hold]]
- Ending Location: Crags east of Scarwatch Hold
- Attending Characters: [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Nova]], [[Aeon]]

## Major Scenes

- The party finds Isgo's black horse with a white blaze, later named Bullseye.
- Nixie speaks with Bullseye and learns two riders took Isgo.
- Meles finds a scrap of yellow-dyed cloth, suggesting possible cultist presence.
- The party travels for days, following tracks, paths, and rough terrain toward the crags.
- A large cat enters camp; Nixie names it Slushball.
- Nova finds a hidden lockbox with gold and potions.
- The party finds a dead body under a rockslide, a fine shortsword missing a gemstone, and a partial note studying injuries.
- A xorn erupts from the earth and attacks.
- The party harvests teeth, acid, and hide from the xorn.
- Meles scouts ahead on the 21st of Evalee and sees two tan-robed figures with kahlbits.
- The robed figures attack as the session ends.

## Combats

- Xorn in the crag trail.
- End-session attack by two tan-robed figures with kahlbits.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Hidden lockbox: 52 gp, one red healing potion, one blue-green vial, initials "BL" inside lid.
- Corpse loot: fine shortsword with gemstone missing from pommel, two empty vials, one greater healing potion, partial injury-study note.
- Xorn remains: teeth, two vials of acid, hide possibly worth about 100 gp.

## Clues Found

- Isgo was taken by two people on horseback.
- The scrap of yellow cloth may connect the path to cultists.
- A crag campsite carried abjuration remnants and arcane weave traces.
- Someone with initials "BL" hid a lockbox in the area.
- The corpse's injuries were being studied; connection to xorn or cult unknown.

## Decisions Made

- Follow the tracks and paths toward the crags rather than return to Scarwatch Hold.
- Leave terrified horses behind when the trail becomes too rough.

## Current Cliffhanger

The party is in the crags east of Scarwatch Hold. Two tan-robed figures with kahlbits attack after Meles scouts them.
