# Dungeons III Session 03 - Chase the Runners

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Dungeons III
- Session Number: 03
- Title: Chase the Runners
- Real Date: 2025-11-17
- In-Game Date: 8th-9th of Evalee, 1246
- Starting Location: [[Hesfal]]
- Attending Characters: [[Meles]], [[Draelith]], [[Nixie]], [[Seraphine Veyne]], [[Nova]], [[Aeon]]

## Major Scenes

- Combat continues behind the Town Ward as bandits free prisoners.
- Nova uses Pequin's Pocket Prism to blind several enemies.
- Meles is shot unconscious, then revived by Nova.
- The party reunites with Mayor Estufus Windseek and explains the robbery/prisoner break.
- The escaped prisoner is identified as Koren Lonsit, also called Kolo.
- The party agrees to look for the bandits in the morning.
- Mexan provides more context around the murder rumor and suggests the wrong person may have been imprisoned.
- Mayor Windseek gives Nixie 10 gp for party expenses.
- At Bru's Beastie Barn, the party finds what appears to be the murder weapon and brings it to guard Crisby.
- The party borrows horses to pursue the bandits.

## Combats

- Bandits freeing the prisoners behind the Town Ward.

## NPCs Introduced

- Estufus Windseek, mayor of Hesfal and former Realmwarden.
- Koren Lonsit / Kolo, escaped prisoner.
- Crisby, guard connected to the murder investigation.

## Clues Found

- Bandits called one of their people "Cap'n."
- Koren Lonsit was to be transferred to Scarwatch Hold after Salvus.
- Mexan believes the murder may involve someone from the caravan or a friend-of-a-friend.
- The suspected murder weapon is found near Bru's Beastie Barn.

## Decisions Made

- Pursue the bandits and fugitive.
- Use borrowed horses for the chase.

## Open Threads

- Find the fugitive and bandits and bring them to Hesfal.
- Determine whether Koren was wrongly accused.
- Learn who wanted Hesfal disrupted during Salvus.
