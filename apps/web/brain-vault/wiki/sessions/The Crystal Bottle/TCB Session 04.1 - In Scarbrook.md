# Session 04.1 - In Scarbrook

**Date played:** February 24, 2024
**In-game date:** 21st of Hemist

## Summary

On the 21st of Hemist, the party continues in Scarbrook. They investigate Erick's keys and his home, finding a safe. They speak with [[Gwyneth]], learning about [[Malik]], [[Fizban]], and cult splits. They gather rumors at an inn, then travel toward Fizban's Tower. En route they encounter green dragon wyrmlings and loot a caravan. They then witness two gold dragons — Wyndessa and C'mey — defeat a larger green dragon named Elendore. After conversing with the gold dragons, the party makes camp.
