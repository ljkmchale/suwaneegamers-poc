# Sessions 05.3–05.6 - Blustery Waste and Ice Castle

**Date played:** Unknown (dates not recorded)
**In-game date:** Unknown

## Summary

These four sessions cover the party's journey through the Blustery Waste and into the ice castle at Frostbite Mountain.

**Session 5.3:** The party is in the Blustery Waste at Sunlit Haven.

**Session 5.4:** They continue exploring the Blustery Waste.

**Session 5.5:** The party explores Thal'astyr — a ruined celestial structure featuring a mosaic room, courtyard, library, overgrown garden, living quarters, ritual chambers, and a Doorway. The site is connected to Everlight. Father Frostel is captured during exploration. The party then heads to Frostbite Mountain.

**Session 5.6:** [[Alteya]] joins the party. The party crosses a bridge, fights mephits, enters an ice castle, navigates a labyrinth filled with spike traps, activates an altar (described as "The Altar is Activated"), and finds a huge cave.
