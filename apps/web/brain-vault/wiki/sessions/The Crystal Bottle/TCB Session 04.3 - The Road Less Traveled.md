# Session 04.3 - The Road Less Traveled

**Date played:** April 27, 2024
**In-game date:** Unknown

## Summary

Leaving Fizban's Tower, the party spots a giant and wyvern accompanied by an air genasi carrying a wanted poster for Tass. A battle ensues — Sigil and Yvaine nearly die — but the party prevails and loots the bodies. At a campsite they confront Cornelius Woodrift (father of the late Sillian Woodrift), who offers the party help in exchange for recovering a ghost-guarded treasure. The party agrees, but then ambushes and kills Cornelius's group after freeing prisoners from them. They claim loot and level up.
