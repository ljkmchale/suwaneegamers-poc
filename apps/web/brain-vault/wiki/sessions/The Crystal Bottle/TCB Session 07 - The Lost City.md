# Session 07 - The Lost City

**Date played:** August 23, 2025
**In-game date:** 27th of Hemist, 1227

## Summary

In Everlight on the 27th of Hemist, 1227, the party wakes from a long rest. The ancient Druid tells them the Lost City is six days' travel away. Sigil goes missing — later found to have gone to the Temple on his own. Before the party departs, a godlike entity named Muerg and a figure named Ashe emerge from the mountain. Muerg declares "Everlight is now under my protection" and announces the beginning of the Awakening. Ashe channels divine flame through the gates, creating a new mountain pass, and gifts [[Yvaine]] a suit of +2 studded leather armor. Muerg and Ashe then depart, and with them the time warp over the region is removed. The party departs for the Lost City. En route (29th–32nd of Hemist) they travel through dangerous territory.
