# Session 04.2 - Fizban's Tower

**Date played:** April 6, 2024
**In-game date:** 23rd of Hemist

## Summary

On the 23rd of Hemist, the party reaches [[Fizban]]'s Tower in Longwood Forest after navigating traps and fighting crystalline creatures. Magic fails outside the tower but works inside. They explore the tower, discover a vault containing magical items, and learn that Fizban created a potion of long life for [[Gwyneth]]. Through a telescope they view a ruined city. The party rests and identifies potions.
