# Session 07.2

**Date played:** October 5, 2025
**In-game date:** 33rd of Hemist, 2 AM — Lost City (Athralis)

## Summary

At 2 AM on the 33rd of Hemist, a portal tears open in the Lost City and [[Sigil (TCB)|Sigil]] falls out — burned, disoriented, and injured. It becomes clear he was captured by A'urea'Pura cultists: led by a half-orc named Gruk and supported by blue-skinned humanoids. The party races 1,200 feet to rescue him. A fierce battle erupts at a stone pavilion: [[Narris]] fireballs the guards, [[Galtrelon]] wild shapes into a purple worm, [[Ren (TCB)|Ren]] fires hunter's mark shots, and [[Yvaine]] and [[Scarlytt Amyran|Scarlytt]] press into melee. The fight involves manacles and a moonbeam. The party fights their way to the temple.
