# Session 07.3

**Date played:** November 1, 2025
**In-game date:** Unknown — Lost City (Athralis), inside the temple

## Summary

Inside a chamber with walls pulsing blue light, the party faces an unstable golden-white portal. An angel named [[Aurelion]] is chained in the middle of the portal. Two cracked blue godstones power it. [[Malik]] steps out and delivers a monologue: [[Kal'dar]] made a deal with the D'jinn for immortality but was hollowed out, becoming Malik's vessel. Malik now claims heavenly power. The party attempts to negotiate. [[Yvaine]] speaks to Aurelion in Celestial — he tells her to get the key from around Malik's neck to free him. The session ends in open confrontation.
