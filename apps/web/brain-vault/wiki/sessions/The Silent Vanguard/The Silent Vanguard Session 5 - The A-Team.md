# Session 5 - The A-Team

Source: SQLite session_summaries / the-silent-vanguard / Session 5 - The A-Team

## Approved Session Summary

The "A-Team" adventurers infiltrated "The Long Night" tavern to investigate an illicit drug production facility located in the basement. The facility is allegedly smuggling performance-enhancing drugs to gladiators in the Colosseum. Combat Engagement: The Bakery & Production Office The session opened with the party split between the bakery and the alleyway. A high-intensity skirmish ensued against guards, a hobgoblin spellcaster, and a bugbear overseer. Intelligence Recovered – The party successfully cleared the facility and gathered the following high-signal intelligence: * *The Substance: Recovered vials of "Troll's Blood." Analysis suggests it provides temporary vigor and regeneration but likely causes a toxic "crash" or long-term physical degradation in humanoids.* * *The Betting Slip: Found a slip for the Colosseum wagering 20 GP at 10:1 odds against Caspian Vesper for the upcoming weekend's major bout.* * *The Shadow: Identified Caspian’s likely opponent as "Shay, the Shadow of the Sands," an undefeated, wiry ghost-like fighter known for theatrical dismemberment.* Strategic Coordination – Post-raid, the team met with Master Thorne on their ship to discuss the implications of the drug ring and the threat to Caspian.
