# SoD Session 07 - A Solid Plan

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: SoD
- Session Number: 07
- Title: A Solid Plan
- Real Date: 2026-04-19
- In-Game Date: 44th of Hemist, 1246
- Starting Location: Adsuren
- Ending Location: Adsuren Center cellar, with a mimic attacking after Kenton opens the chest
- Attending Characters: [[Escanor]], [[Therric]], [[Zephyra]], [[Kenton]], [[Esylla]], [[Lila]]

## Major Scenes

- Guards question Mollywop about whether her alarm noticed people leaving after the theft.
- Esylla asks Mollywop to contact her aunt in Ol'Morrey and senses Mollywop may be hiding something or exhausted from the town alarm.
- The party confronts Kenton over the stolen tax money and develops a cover plan to "find" money at Ganon's house.
- Zephyra escorts guard Heckudor to Ganon's room; an imp familiar appears and kills Heckudor.
- Zephyra kills the imp and sells the guard post a story that Ganon/the imp are responsible for the stolen money and death.
- Lila and Esylla find Escanor at the Oratory while Kenton and Therric fail to pickpocket a wealthy patron at the Falling Fish.
- Escanor mends Kenton's tools after warning him to be responsible.
- Esylla and Therric visit Mollywop, who explains imps/familiars, the heart as a failed ritual, and the risk of the cult needing another heart.
- Mollywop gives Esylla and Therric magical protection before the cellar mission.
- The party infiltrates the Adsuren Center cellar at night and discovers a hidden passage, black salt storage, powdered silver, and a dampening metal arch.
- The group fights cultists and interrogates one prisoner; Lila's Detect Thoughts confirms cult hierarchy, ritual imagery, and a red/black robed leader.
- Kenton kills the tied prisoner.
- The party finds a chest; Kenton opens it and a mimic attacks as the session ends.

## Combats

- Zephyra vs. imp familiar at Ganon's room.
- Party vs. cultists in the Adsuren Center cellar.
- Mimic attack starts at the end.

## NPCs Introduced Or Developed

- Heckudor.
- Mollywop.
- Lix.
- Ganon, as an absent scapegoat/manhunt target.
- Esylla's aunt in Ol'Morrey, via Mollywop's Sending.

## Clues Found

- Imps can be invisible and may change forms.
- Mollywop can detect crossings through her town ward but may be stretched thin by Market Day traffic.
- The heart was likely a failed cult ritual.
- Storage under Adsuren Center contains black salt and powdered silver.
- Cultists confirm a blood/pleasure cult with leaders above them.
- The cellar includes dead animals, blood, and ritual disposal areas.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Kenton's thieves' tools are mended by Escanor.
- Kenton pockets 10 bags of powdered silver (each worth 10 gp; 100 gp total).
- Heckudor dies.

## Decisions Made

- Plant/fake-find part of the stolen money at Ganon's house.
- Keep Ganon as the public focus while secretly pursuing the cellar lead.
- Enter Adsuren Center cellar at night in pairs.
- Interrogate a cultist prisoner, then Kenton kills him.

## Consequences

- Ganon becomes the public face of the tax theft and guard death.
- Zephyra now carries the moral weight of a false story around Heckudor's death.
- The party confirms a hidden cult base beneath Adsuren Center.
- Kenton's willingness to kill a restrained prisoner becomes an important party-behavior marker.

## Character Beats

- Escanor balances law/morality with party practicality by mending Kenton's tools and continuing the mission.
- Therric enables Kenton's side-plans but also helps with Mollywop and infiltration.
- Zephyra protects the party's plan under pressure and kills the imp alone.
- Kenton keeps prioritizing loot/opportunity, from planted gold to powdered silver to the chest.
- Esylla manages Mollywop diplomacy and brings occult strategy to the infiltration.
- Lila confronts Kenton, reads cultist thoughts, and keeps pressing the group's conscience.
