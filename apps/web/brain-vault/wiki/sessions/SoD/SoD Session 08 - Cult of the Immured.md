# SoD Session 08 - Cult of the Immured

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: SoD
- Session Number: 08
- Title: Cult of the Immured
- Source Header Note: Source notes list "08 - <Name> (05.02.26)"; this page title is derived from the session's central reveal.
- Real Date: 2026-05-02
- In-Game Dates: 44th-45th of Hemist, 1246
- Starting Location: Adsuren Center Cellar
- Ending Location: Leaving Adsuren toward the mines at 2 PM on the 45th of Hemist
- Attending Characters: [[Escanor]], [[Therric]], [[Zephyra]], [[Kenton]], [[Esylla]], [[Lila]]

## Major Scenes

- The party fights the mimic; Kenton drops, Escanor heals him, and Kenton kills it.
- Loot reveals a golden tiara/Circlet of Blasting, scrolls of Speak with Animals and Unseen Servant, and notes naming the Cult of the Immured.
- The party enters the ritual chamber and fights cultists plus Summoner Karranok.
- Summoner Karranok is revealed as Intendant Eyovar Dunajor.
- Eyovar confesses that he began making sacrifices after the Awakening to keep Adsuren prosperous, eventually becoming trapped in escalating demands for hearts.
- Eyovar names Aeolenne as the voice/power behind the cult and says other cultists came from Caldwynn, Gibuldon, Morrey, and elsewhere.
- Aeolenne manifests from blood, corpse, and flame, looks like a more fiendish Zephyra, and reveals herself as Zephyra's mother.
- Esylla realizes killing the cultists freed Aeolenne from imprisonment.
- Aeolenne tries to take Zephyra, mind-controls her, drinks an invisibility potion, and escapes town.
- Zephyra breaks free through a childhood memory of Jimmy Meanpants and her father teaching her to stand up for herself.
- Esylla exits the wards to receive Mollywop's Sending; Mollywop appears and confirms a fiendish presence left town.
- Escanor and Mollywop mar/destroy the ritual circle, while the party turns over Eyovar and evidence to the guards.
- The party decompresses at Zephyra's house; Mollywop discusses possible but expensive/high-level protection from mind control.
- Next day, the party levels up, identifies loot, upgrades gear, and heads toward the mines after lunch.

## Combats

- Party vs. mimic.
- Party vs. Cult of the Immured cultists and Summoner Karranok / Eyovar Dunajor.
- Party crisis vs. Aeolenne's mind control of Zephyra.

## NPCs Introduced Or Developed

- Intendant Eyovar Dunajor / Summoner Karranok.
- Aeolenne.
- Mollywop.
- Captain Alinia Sarralei.
- Jimmy Meanpants, from Zephyra's memory.
- Acting Intendant, formerly Eyovar's assistant.

## Clues Found

- "Immured" is an old term tied to freeing something.
- The cult's rituals were freeing Aeolenne rather than only summoning a new patron.
- Aeolenne has an ally in Caldwynn and headed south.
- Cultists came from several towns, suggesting a wider network.
- Adsuren's prosperity was built on escalating sacrifices after the Awakening.
- A sewer hole/indentation in the ritual room may explain how the gertot head moved through the sewers.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Circlet of Blasting / golden tiara goes to Lila.
- Scroll of Speak with Animals goes to Therric.
- Scroll of Unseen Servant is pocketed by Esylla.
- Potion of Healing and Potion of Invulnerability identified.
- +1 staff taken by Therric; he breaks his old staff into a carved rod/keepsake.
- +1 whip delivered to Kenton through Esylla and Mollywop after selling/trading the prior +1 short sword.
- Kenton arranges a silvered weapon by trading powdered silver, 5 gp, and his rapier.
- Escanor upgrades into chain mail.

## Decisions Made

- Spare and turn over Eyovar rather than execute him in the cellar.
- Destroy the ritual circle with Mollywop.
- Prioritize Zephyra's anti-mind-control needs as a party problem.
- Delay deeper heart study until after Market Day.
- Follow up on bulas/shadows/swamp/mines leads while planning to return for Market Day.

## Consequences

- Aeolenne is free and outside Adsuren's wards.
- Adsuren discovers a cult was operating beneath its civic center.
- Eyovar is removed and his assistant becomes acting Intendant.
- The tax theft rumor is largely buried beneath the cult revelation.
- The party becomes publicly central to Adsuren's survival and scandal.

## Character Beats

- Escanor is downed by Karranok but returns to evidence handling, priestly cleanup, and armor upgrading.
- Therric heals Escanor with a healer's kit, restrains/surrenders Eyovar, and takes the +1 staff as his new carved focus.
- Zephyra faces Aeolenne as literal family and proves she can break free of manipulation through chosen identity and memory.
- Kenton kills the mimic, uses whip pressure on Eyovar, loots potions, and turns powdered silver into a future silvered weapon.
- Esylla identifies the Immured/freeing implication, executes the Mollywop contact plan, and negotiates the +1 whip for Kenton.
- Lila uses the tiara/circlet and her prior visions become validated by the ritual room imagery.
