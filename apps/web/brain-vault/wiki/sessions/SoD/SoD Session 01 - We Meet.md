# SoD Session 01 - We Meet

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: SoD
- Session Number: 01
- Title: We Meet
- Real Date: 2025-11-19
- In-Game Date: 40th of Hemist
- Starting Location: Adsuren, Camburne Vec, Inverland
- Ending Location: Back near the surface after the poisoned-water investigation
- Attending Characters: [[Escanor]], [[Therric]], [[Zephyra]], [[Kenton]], [[Esylla]]

## Major Scenes

- Market Day introductions in Adsuren.
- Jodon disturbance at a stall; Zephyra intervenes and Escanor counsels him.
- Sick man collapses after drinking well water.
- Kenton descends into the well and identifies the stream as likely tainted.
- Party enters the Nature's Oratory crypt and fights undead.
- Party investigates the underground stream and finds the gertot head with a black petrified human heart.
- Plant/vine creature emerges from the heart and is destroyed.

## Combats

- Oratory crypt skeletons and crawling hands.
- Sarcophagus figure / undead founder.
- Vine / plant creature from the tainted stream.

## NPCs Introduced

- Jodon.
- Tanluar Rotvori / Nature's Oratory priest.
- Perrin Patchwicket.

## Clues Found

- The well is likely poisoned by the stream feeding it.
- The black petrified heart smells of corruption and later suggests something nefarious.
- The gertot head and heart appear intentionally placed.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- 18 gp found in the stream.
- Black petrified heart and gertot head recovered for investigation.

## Decisions Made

- Investigate the well rather than only treat the poisoned man.
- Enter the Oratory crypt to access the underground path.
- Take the heart/head back for religious or magical assessment.

## Consequences

- Adsuren's poisoned-water threat is traced underground.
- The party begins functioning as a shared investigative/adventuring group.

## Character Beats

- Kenton shows flair and risk tolerance by descending into the well.
- Therric leads natural tracking and physical clue interpretation.
- Escanor takes religious responsibility for undead and spiritual aftermath.
- Zephyra acts as front-line protector.
- Esylla probes with magic and ranged attacks.
