# SoD Session 03 - FUKWAD

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: SoD
- Session Number: 03
- Title: FUKWAD
- Full Title Note: Fungipheliacs Using Kinfolk With Abandoned Desires
- Real Date: 2026-01-03
- In-Game Date: 41st of Hemist
- Starting Location: Pits beneath Sapcutt's Plantation, Hath'lao Swamp
- Ending Location: On the Sapcutts' boat, leaving the swamp
- Attending Characters: [[Escanor]], [[Therric]], [[Zephyra]], [[Kenton]], [[Esylla]], [[Lila]]

## Major Scenes

- Party wakes in fungal pits beneath the Sapcutt house after the drugged stew.
- Escanor wakes Zephyra and checks on Lila, Esylla, and Therric while they recover from vomiting.
- The group fights fungal tendrils and grub-like creatures beneath the house.
- The party climbs back upstairs and finds the house rapidly infected by unnatural mold and fungus.
- Levian, one of the Sapcutt sons, attacks as a rotted/fungal creature and appears able to heal or reanimate.
- The party explores trapped rooms, recovers valuables, and navigates the scorpion-key puzzle.
- Lobella Sapcutt is found alive and explains that a black fish, fungus, and a plant bargain started the family's corruption.
- The party confronts Lady Sapcutt and chooses escape/rescue over burning the whole house down immediately.
- The group flees by roof/window with Margaret and Lobella, leaves on a Sapcutt boat, and hears a loud roar behind them in the swamp.

## Combats

- Fungal tendrils in the basement.
- Grubs beneath the house.
- Levian the Rotted Halfling.
- Jotho / infected Sapcutt brother.
- Lady Sapcutt and the living/fungal house threat.

## NPCs Introduced Or Developed

- Margaret Sapcutt.
- Lobella Sapcutt.
- Levian Sapcutt.
- Jotho Sapcutt.
- Lady Sapcutt.

## Clues Found

- Sapcutt fungus is unnatural and seems tied to a black fish pulled from deep in the swamp.
- The plant intelligence fed on visitors through a bargain with the family.
- The house itself behaves as a hostile fungal/plant organism.
- A roar and sky silhouette behind the party suggests a larger swamp threat, possibly draconic.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- 23 sp found in rotted rubble.
- Red vials from the pantry.
- Chrysoprase gem from the scorpion-door design.
- +1 short sword from the armor trap, inscribed "To Abella, Love Dad."
- Broken scorpion-key pieces.
- Light blue fan.
- Sapcutt pie recipe copied under agreement not to profit from or share it.

## Decisions Made

- Rescue Lobella and Margaret instead of treating every Sapcutt as only a monster.
- Avoid burning the house down with survivors inside.
- Leave the swamp by boat after the escape.

## Consequences

- The swamp/fungus plot becomes larger than a missing-family errand.
- The party now has evidence and stories to bring back to Mollywop.
- The swamp roar becomes a new external threat lead.

## Character Beats

- Escanor defaults to triage and gentle rescue after the fall.
- Therric interprets fungus, food, and physical clues by smell/taste/terrain.
- Zephyra takes point in dangerous rooms and physically engages infected family members.
- Kenton checks traps, swaps the sword in the armor trap, and uses whip/skirmish tactics.
- Esylla investigates and communicates with Lobella, including practical rescue/valuable-recovery advice.
- Lila reads halfling writing, searches kitchens/rooms, and contributes fire/lightning spell pressure.
