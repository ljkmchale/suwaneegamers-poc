A hand is pressed against the wall and a spiral lights up.   “That is important.”  Yes - I will look for that.  Anything you can tell me about my traveling party?  Anyone specific you had questions about.  I wonder how honest Kenton is - is he trustworthy?  She then sees a vision of Kenton talking to an older lady who she met at Market Day. Thanks for the information.  Your party might be helpful to you in the future.  I feel that someone put me into this gem.  You are not strong enough to help me yet. Lila sleeps the next day.
Now back to the present - we are on the road.  Escanor drives the carriage and Esylla sits with him.  Lila and Kenton are in the carriage. Zephrya and Therric walk next to the carriage. We travel the entire day and the storm finally subsides at twilight.  
Overlook Camp
We find a clearing to make camp. Therric and Zephyra look around for dry wood.  Kenton and Escanor look around in different directions to check things out.  They think things are fairly safe here. We are a little higher altitude than Adsuren.  See no signs of anyone.  Therric doesn’t recall the name of this campsite.  There are trees as far as we can see.  We sit for a bit, find some herbs, Kenton cooks for the group (it was a good meal), we eat, etc.  Then go on watch.  First watch - Kenton and Esylla; second watch - Lila and Therric; third watch - Zephyra and Escanor.
1st watch - Esylla asks Kenton about cooking.  He says he learned to cook on the run.  Also a means of making friends.  2nd watch - Lila and Therric talk.  She speaks of her exhaustion.  He asks if he smells and asks if she can help him - she cleans him up.  She tells him about her shard - that it talks to her.  He questions this.  She tells him what it says and that a person is trapped inside.  She tells him where she needs to go and that she needs to grow stronger. They then talk about Kenton. And how “we didn’t need anything else on our plate,” but she said she is not making a big deal about it.  They talk about when to reach back out to Mayless - maybe in the next city.  Therric cautions her to be alert and make sure that Mayless is trustworthy. Agree to keep this between the two of them.  3rd watch - Escanor and Zephyra talk about how busy the last few days have been.  She expresses concern that they are not ready to face her mother again - the party is not strong enough. Escanor says he might be able to help with his magic.  She just says she wants to be prepared, especially if she is charmed again. She says she wants to take some time with her sword to get to know it better.  He advises that there might be arcane and divine powers among the group that might be helpful, especially against the darkness. 
It’s the 5th of Baegram, 1246
The next morning we rise and break camp.  Escanor says his morning prayers, everyone takes care of basic needs.  The group heads out - same order as before.  We travel most of the day without issue.  We are traveling southwest on the road.  There is a terrible storm that rolls in with hail after about 7 hours.  Kenton yells to the gods “What the hell?”  The horses are getting agitated.  Esylla asks everyone to whom they pray?  Therric launches into a speech about why anyone would pick any one god, when all of the gods touch the lands.  Zephrya tries to calm the horses, as Therric and Kenton go off the trail to find a place to pull over - trying to find a dense canopy.  They give up and head back to the trail…but they get ahead of the group and notice about 100’ away they see light in the storm.  It is torch-like and on the road.  They stealth up to check it out. Kenton is able to be stealthy and moves up.  
The Everest Inn
They see a two-story structure - it is a road-side inn.  Therric says “I’ve got them Kenton” and runs back to the party to get us.  Kenton stays in stealth and goes to the Inn.  Therric gets to the party and tells them about the shelter.  They all get there.  There is the Everest Inn and a small stables.  Therric says to Lila - “I think you can get a good night’s rest here if you want to wake her up.”  
There is a horse in the stables with a saddle on the wall.  Therric tries to determine how long the horse has been there and isn’t sure. Kenton finds a locket in the saddlebag (golden locket) as he steals it from the saddle. Zephyra and Therric stable the horses and carriage, as the rest of the group goes into the Inn.  
Inside, it is large room and we see 3 in the lounge area and 1 who is a barkeep/innkeeper.  He introduces himself (scrappy older man - named Kane Elword).  He asks that we stow our weapons in the lounge - this is his only rule.  No weapons in the rooms upstairs.  
The three other patrons - bronze dragonborne, and a younger half elven man (well dressed), and a half orc child.  The dragonborne has had a lot of drink, the half elf is drinking wine and reading, and the child is drawing on parchment at a table alone.  Esylla asks about the rooms - he has four common left (1/sp) and a nice one (5/sp).  Esylla and Lila take a common room.  Escanor and Therric take a common room each.
The box contains other weapons (a staff and a rapier).  Therric walks in and puts his weapons in the box.  Esylla drops off her daggers.  Lila drops off her crossbow.  Zephyra is not going to surrender her weapons so she offers to take the weapons.  She takes Escanor’s weapons.  She and Kenton will stay with the weapons in the carriage/stables. 
Therric asks the Innkeeper - how old is your daughter (the half orc)?  He says that she parents are upstairs asleep.  He then orders an ale and a glass of wine and pays for them (with a nice tip).  He asks if anyone stays in the lounge overnight and he says that he will need to get Aldric up to bed at some point.  Therric gives the wine to Esylla and apologizes for being hasty.  
Lila walks up to the fancy-dressed elf.  He says his name is Ciliren Maquin.  She sits down at his table and compliments him on his dress.  He comments that Kenton’s clothing is patchwork.  He says he is reading a book.  It is about the finer things about the ocean.  She then asks him about his travels - to the Tideless Span, the Sea of Sondera, etc.  She asks if he has been to the west and he says not yet.  Why are you learning about the oceans?  I’m trying to pass the time and find inspiration.  He says he is a tailor and a merchant of fine silks.  He has nothing in stock right now.  He is on his way to buy silks to the south.  She asks where he is coming from and he replies Rosevale.  He says he has had a lot to drink and asks if they can continue the conversation in the morning.  She asks if he is going to read and offers to introduce him to Kenton.  He yells “Hey Patchwork” and Kenton ignores him.  He continues “Hey Patchwork Bard.”  Kenton continues to write.  Escanor noticed a symbol of Goldraen on him (embroidered on his doublet).  The young man heads up to his room - drunk.
Therric buys another glass of wine and gives it to Lila.  Escanor casts detect poison to see if any of the wine is tainted.  It is not. This wine is very nice.   Esylla offers to Kenton to taste her wine.  He likes it - but it is not as good “as the Lord’s wines” he has had before.
Therric checks out the doors in the Inn.  He latches the back door.  Esylla asks Kenton to play something for the room.  He pulls out his dulcimer and plays (this is about the “rude seamstress”). 6th of Baegram, 1246

Therric walks up to get Esylla and they walk to room 10 and open the door.  It looks like a bedroom. They walk outside, close the door, mess with the handle, and open it again and the hallway appears.  She tries to message Lila and gets no response.  She then yells VERY LOUDLY for each companion to come.  As she waits, the door closes.
Escanor looks around to take stock of the situation.  He sees Shogi get wide-eyed, but no one else seems to care.  Everyone else comes upstairs to Esylla. She explains to everyone what happened.  She says look and toggles the doorknob and tries again.  It is a room.  She tries again and it is a room.  She claims that maybe because there are weapons upstairs it isn’t working.  Kenton says watch this and tries again.  He tries to unlock the unlocked door.  And opens it and it is a room.  And Therric says let’s go downstairs.  Zephyra, Kenton, and Escanor go downstairs with the weapons, as Therric, Lila, and Esylla stay upstairs. 
Downstairs, Escanor casts Zone of Truth on the room and everyone in it. Everyone in the bar fails except for Shogi and Kenton.  Escanor asks the tailor - have you seen her parents?  He says no.  Have you ever seen them?  No.  How long have you been here?  About a harmon.  Have you been able to sleep?  Yes.  He says I recommend leaving.  He then asks the same questions of Aldric.  He says he saw her parents when they first arrived, but it has been a while.  He has had good nights and bad nights.  He has been there a very long time.  He then goes into the kitchen to try to find the Innkeeper.  He finds two doors.  He sees a narrow passage opening one of the doors.  It is damp, there are lanterns.  This is a long hallway with doors the entire way.  He puts the mace in the initial doorway so that it won’t shut.  He goes to find Kenton and Zephrya and tells them about the hallway.  He then goes to open the front exterior door.

He sees a copper piece on the floor and a tapestry with crops on it.  This seems to be art and not heraldry.  There is another one with a harvest.  It seems to be connected to the wall. They vary from hanging on the wall to being connected to the wall.  Therric is going to head downstairs to get the others.

We determine that this is a magical inn that moves around and captures people.  We need to find the source of the magic. 
Kenton tries to open the door to the banquet hall again and it is the same banquet hall.  He tries another door and it is a long hallway.  There are sacks that are merged into each other, no doors.  Goes on as far as he can see.  Lanterns at regular intervals.  We all go to the kitchen and find Kenton.  He is there and we go to the lounge.  Kenton leaves a mote in the room.

She rolled high (21) and also had to roll a percentile die (29%).  The room shifts axis and shifts 29 degrees.  Water starts to pour into the room - like we are underwater.  Therric grabs his stuff and Shogi.   The water is too strong.  Esylla casts the spell again.  And the room shifts again.  The water stops coming in.  There is now sky outside.  The entire building is falling…
