- Campaign: SoD

## Major Scenes

- Party returns toward Adsuren after the Sapcutt escape and hears/observes signs of a large swamp creature.
- The party splits for rest and errands: Zephyra and Therric at her hut, Escanor at the Oratory/graveyard, and Lila/Esylla/Kenton at the Graying Grotto.
- Therric dreams of hunting as a clawed creature and tracking a female elf-like being.
- Mollywop reviews the Sapcutt report, the short sword, and the black heart.
- Mollywop reads the black heart as a petrified human heart with necromancy, enchantment, fiendish quality, soul tethering, and despair/bane-like implications.
- The party investigates a disturbed grave: Durgruck Bristleforge's grave has been violated and the heart removed.
- The party fights undead Bristleforge; Kenton finishes him with a whip strike that takes the head off.
- Zephyra and Therric learn about Durgruck, the mines, a big bat/creature, and Captain Alinia Sarralei's tension with Mollywop.
- Escanor detects fiendish/evil influence near the Tanner.
- Kenton is pulled toward Sir Rodolf Norran's ambush setup.

- Durgruck was tied to a mine investigation involving a large bat-like creature and mine sickness.
- Captain Alinia praises Zephyra but resents or distrusts Mollywop.
- Escanor senses fiendish/evil influence around the Tanner thread.
