# Session 12 - Scyldgar Keep

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  July 26, 2026
In-game Date:  48th of Hemist, 1246
Starting Location:  Adsuren
Watch on the road north of Adsuren
The group makes camp.  Zephyra and Esylla take the first watch, while Lila and Therric take the second watch, Kenton last.
* Zephyra & Esylla – Zephyra spends some time checking out her sword.  Esylla tells her there is dark magic associated with it (as all magic users of the group would attest).  She also tells her that the man wielding it is a relation.  And warns her to use caution that it could be cursed or dark.  Zephrya asks her to advise if she starts to change.  They talk about the plot against Mollywop and the drama and dark energy in Adsuren.  
* As they wake up Therric and Lila, Therric realizes he had a dream during his rest time. The dream:  he was later in the castle of his last dream - still in a beast (cat-like) form following a white elf (a much younger Duskwhisper - someone he knows from his past).  She is wielding a stick with string [long bow] and shiny stick [sword]. There are killings, dead bodies.  They continue to venture the calls of the castle. They enter an octagonal room, all stone, and a staircase leading down. The elf follows the hall.  Therric sniffs behind him and smells metallic [blood] smell.  He dashes to the elf. They are in a room with stuff [fireplace and furniture] and the elf is fighting two others. He sees another figure enter from a door and pull a bow, about to fire at the elf.  He runs forward to take the arrow, saving the elf.  He is hit and goes down. And then feels Zephyra waking him up.  Zephyra and Esylla go to sleep.  Lila shows Therric the tigers eye gem that she got from Kenton.  He asks her if she dreams and knows the people in her dreams.  They discuss his dream and how he dies in them.  She tells him this is not a good omen.  He tells her that if growls, it is related to his dream - tells her that he is a big cat in his dreams.  They look at the gem and Therric tries to help her figure out how to help her wear it.  She says that she will try to help dream his dreams.  He tells her he is looking for small stones: blue, brown, red, and white to put on his staff. She pulls out various stones and shows him.  He wants to make an “L”out of the stones on his basic rod, to symbolize the elements.  He takes the stones and starts wielding the elements using his book.  He thinks the one who wrote the book is the one he sees in his dreams (Areana Duskwhisper). He tells Lila that he tried to get Zephyra to read the book to help control her inner fire.  Lila asks if he loved her and he says no.  He says they were killing people in the dreams - says he was her cat following her in the dreams.  Like he was her protector.  Lila insists that she must mean something to him.  
* Kenton’s watch.  As he sits, it starts to rain.  As this is mid-summer, it is warm rain. He pulls up his cloak to keep from getting wet.  Uneventful watch.  He waits an extra hour once watch ends.  Rain ends.  Wakes all.
It’s the next day, the 1st of Baegrum,1246
Therric picks up the trail with Esylla’s guidance, aided by her goddess Tornia.  He tracks and finds a place where a half dozen tracks went left, but the blood soaked trail goes right.  The blood seems to be consistent - he has not tended to his wounds at this point, nor have we found a camp site where anyone would have stopped.  We think this path is turning toward the river.  We travel another 40 minutes through deep woods and we find a clearing that seems like a place where he stopped, tended his wounds, and moved on.  We continue.  We go another hour and the sun is setting.  The men in the group notice a larger clearing ahead and find an old road, overgrown.  It appears the man turned onto the road, traveling east, heading to the river. After a while, we find a spot where the horse went another way from the rider.  It appears he may have sent the horse elsewhere to throw off any pursuit. We follow the blood trail and see a large structure in the distance.  
A Ruined Keep
We ride up to within 100’ and tie off the horses.  Lila, Kenton, Escanor, and Zephrya approach on foot from the left.  Therric and Esylla approach on foot on the right.  Lila feels that this is familiar, as her necklace warms on her neck.  She recalls this from a vision she had in her head. 
Therric and Esylla approach and see a stone castle with a wooden bridge and a moat full of water.  This is familiar to Therric.   The drawbridge is down.  It is 30’-40’ across, 30’ at the bridge.  They approach the water. The depth of the water at this edge is 10’-15’ deep, and they can hear a nearby river.  The keep is somewhat rundown.  Therric takes the guise of an old traveler, hobbling across the bridge.  Esylla stays on the side, watching.  
The group of four approaches on a side, not near the drawbridge.  They can see a figure hobbling across the bridge in the distance - not sure who this is. 
Therric hobbles across and notices there are two skeletons against the wall and he reflects back to his dream - recalling that these are the figures that were previously killed by the elf in his dreams.  He looks and see Zephyra in the distance to the right and gives a slight nod.  She tells her group that is Therric.  Therric sees that the door is ajar.  He thinks back on his dream and tries to remember how many were killed here, as well as the layout of the keep. He looks in and sees familiar areas.  Esylla messages Therric (first time using this) and asks if it is safe to cross and if he sees the blood droppings.  He tells her to tell everyone to come in.  She walks in the direction of the party and speaks until Zephyra replies.  She relays the message to the group and they start heading that way.  She then proceeds to cross the bridge herself.  The women and Kenton notice there are scorch marks on the stones - perhaps from a dragon?  Lila recalls a dragon in her visions.  Therric pulls out his book and looks around and really thinks back on his dreams.  He then opens his eyes and recalls other items in the keep from his dreams.
Lila recalls the present-day keep; Therric recalls the keep from some time ago. There are green banners with gold inlay and horns intertwined.  Eslyaa recalls that this is from the Scyldgar [SCY-LID-GAR] family from the Steoleahs.  There is some discussion about the Vecs and clans and how the Steoleahs have hailed from Glenleah, the most powerful family in Inverland.  She is not sure how powerful the Scyldgar family is now.  The group of four approaches and Esylla asks Kenton directly what he might know of them - since he has connections.  Kenton recalls that this family is very cutthroat - if you cross them, they will cut you down.  Kenton knows he encountered someone from that family at some point and he knew their power was diminished, considered a smaller family now. This was the same night that he overheard someone talking about overthrowing someone else.  He isn’t sure who was who - he didn’t see anyone - just heard voices. 
The doors are unlocked.  The group hears nothing but the river.  Therric opens the door.  He sees a chamber and skeletons on the ground, one headless.  He tells Lila “this was the cat dream.”  Zephyra asks about the dream.  He points to the headless skeleton and says “I did that.”  She asks when he was here and he isn’t sure.  Escanor checks to see how long these skeletons have been dead.  He thinks 20 years or so, maybe a little less.  As he touches the skeleton, the wrist grabs him. 
IT IS INITIATIVE - Combat vs. Undead Skeleton Guards
* ROUND ONE:  All of the skeletons stand up, some drawing short bows and swords.  One attacks Therric and misses.  They miss Zephyra.  They attack Kenton and miss.  And they crit on Escanor.  Two attack with bows on Therric and hit, but miss Escanor. Lila cast mage armor on herself.  Therric attacks and hits the headless skeleton with bludgeoning damage, killing him.  He then attacks another one and hits with his elbow.  He then moves a little, clearing the way for others to enter.  Escanor holds his holy symbol and “bids them rest” from Amriel.  This targets the skeletons all around him and all fail their saves.  All of the skeletons burst and die.  We are out of combat.
Zephrya asks Therric if he knows where to go.  He isn’t sure of the way - these openings were not in his visions.  Therric goes into the room on the left and sees a spiraling staircase up to the level above. Zephrya looks through the opening ahead and sees an open courtyard with trees with something stuck to them.  Escanor checks out this room and sees that it was an entrance way and to the right is a long hallway.  Therric looks at Esylla and taps his head - she connects her awakened mind to him and he tells her he is stealthing upstairs.  She tells the group.  Zephyra tells the group what she sees - and that she sees no footprints or blood. Esylla asks Escanor if he sees any footprints or blood down the hallway - he does not.  Esylla asks Therric in his head if he sees anything - signs of anyone.  He does not.  She reports to the group.  Therric explores upstairs.  The group walks into the courtyard.  Therric sees skeletons that look like they were burned with a fire from below.  Also sees stone statues all along the walls.  He tells Esylla what he sees.  She tells the group.  The group is going to check out the courtyard.
The rest of the group sees that skeletons are impaled on the tree.  The tree seems to be dead or at least is dark brown and has no leaves.  Esylla suggests that the group spread out in the courtyard to see if anyone notices any signs of life anywhere.  No one notices any sign of anyone having passed through this area.  Zephyra looks through the door at the back of the courtyard to see if there is a way out of the back of the keep.  She heads in that direction and sees a hall and double doors.  Lila, Kenton, and Escanor study the tree. Esylla goes up the stairs in the courtyard - small hall to the right that ends in a double door.  Kenton, Lila, and Escanor see there are 12 skeletons in the tree.  Lila ponders that maybe someone could have influenced the tree 20 years ago to have been offensive and killed those people itself.  She doesn’t know if it would still be evil.  Therric, on the roof, is trying to figure out about the skeletons and statues on the roof without touching them.  He isn’t sure if the statues are people that were turned to stone or just stone statues.  He thinks they are likely decorative. He sees a chest on the other side of the courtyard, untouched by fire. He works his way over to the chest and makes it there.  There are bows and arrows next to the chest.  This seems to be an area of stored equipment.  Therric pushes the chest to the edge of the roof, but not over the edge. He tells Esylla to message Kenton and she does.  He looks up and sees Therric waving down to him.  Kenton decides he wants to go up.  He tells the group. Lila and Kenton discuss asking Therric to push it over.  They won’t.  They decide to go upstairs. Esylla messages Zephyra and then Therric.  She then heads down the stairs and starts to cross the courtyard, behind the other three, heading for the stairs that Therric took initially.  Zephyra decides to check the back door first and finds a garden-like room, sees a cracked fountain, overgrown and withered flowers and weeds.  She can hear a river on the other side.  She decides to walk through the room, trying to get a view through a window she sees.  She is able to make it and sees the river through the window. She decides to follow the group.
As Esylla is heading to the stairs, she hears some rustling in the stables area.  She tries to message Zephyra, but hears nothing.  She waits a minute or two and Zephrya eventually comes into the courtyard.  
Meanwhile, Therric waits for the group to come upstairs and walks around a little.  Kenton, Escanor, and Lila head up the stairs to meet him.  Therric sees crates and boxes with scorch marks.  They have rotted provisions, arrows, linens, random items, but nothing that interests him.  He then uses his staff to rummage through things.  The three make it upstairs and see everything that Therric saw.  Kenton is hyperfocused on the chest.  Escanor looks over the edge and Esylla sees him.  She messages him that she heard something and is waiting for Zephyra to check out the noise and asks him to stand watch in case something happens.  Meanwhile, Kenton and Lila check out the chest.  They decide to use Lila’s mage hand to touch the chest from 30’ away.  She opens it and all 4 on the rooftops hear rumbling and gravel and the statues that are on the roof suddenly come alive and take flight.  
IT IS INITIATIVE - Combat vs. Gargoyles
* ROUND ONE: Escanor sees a statue take flight. He attacks one with bludgeoning damage and hits.  They fly around and decide to attack various people on the roof:  one hits Escanor once and misses.  One swings at Kenton and Lila misses both.  One hits Zephyra and misses Esylla.  And one attacks Therric twice and misses. Therric casts hunter’s mark on his foe and hits.  Kenton hits one with a whip and then disengages and steps back. Zephyra rages and attacks one and hits. Then attacks again and hits again.  Lila attacks with a spell and hits two and knocks them prone. Esylla attacks with a blast and hits once and misses. 
* ROUND TWO:  Escanor attacks with mace, hitting a prone gargoyle.  The gargoyles attack, successfully hitting Zephyra. Theric hits with an unarmed attack.  Kenton hits a gargoyle with his whip.  Zephyra attacks a gargoyle and hits once. Lila uses a sacred flame and the foe saves. Esylla uses Eldritch blasts, fumbling on the first one, but connecting with the second to hit the gargoyle and kill it. 
* ROUND THREE:  Escanor hits his foe and uses bludgeoning damage to inflict harm. The remaining gargoyles attack:  they hit Therric twice, but he deflects one of them; then hits Escanor and crits him with a second hit and an angelic figure blocks some of the damage as he gets knocked back. Then one attacks Lila twice and hits once and misses once.  Therric hits his foe and he stays up.  He then uses a focus point, disengages, and dashes to join his companions on the other side of the roof.  Kenton whips the gargoyle near him and hits. He then disengages and moves near Escanor.  Zephyra moves up and stores her great sword and pulls out her javelin and tries to hit the one on the edge of the roof.  She fumbles and it flies over the wall and loses it into the moat. Lila shoots a burst on the gargoyle on Escanor and misses.   Esylla blasts and fumbles and then misses then heads 30’ toward the stairs.
* ROUND FOUR: Escanor swings at the gargoyle and hits.  The one on the edge attacks Zephyra - one is critical and one is normal.  It rakes across her thigh and came close to tearing her leg off.  It does some damage.  The other one attacks and hits Kenton and then attacks Escanor, but has to change the attack to hit Kenton and he crits.  This one tries to trip Kenton and he rights himself, but takes damage.  Therric runs up to the foe on Kenton and Escanor and swings and misses.  He then tries to sweep the leg and misses.  Kenton attacks the one on him with the whip and hits. Zephrya attacks the one on her and hits.  Attacks again and hits, cleaving it half.  Lila bursts the remaining one and hits. 
* ROUND FIVE: Escanor disengages and steps back.  The creature attacks Therric and misses and then misses Kenton.  Therric moves his hunter's mark and fumbles, but it was just embarrassing.  Kenton whips the gargoyle and hits with sneak attack and takes off its wings, killing it.  
With the gargoyles dead, we are out of combat.  
Still raging, Zephyra heads to the stables.  Therric and Escanor start down the stairs and see Esylla coming up.  They turn and head to the stables to help her. Zephyra goes to the doors of the stables and kicks them open, where she sees in a stable stall, a red, smoky figure of a horse.  
Meanwhile, Kenton and Lila went to investigate the chest. Kenton and Lila see a golden stick inside the chest.
 – The End of the Session –
