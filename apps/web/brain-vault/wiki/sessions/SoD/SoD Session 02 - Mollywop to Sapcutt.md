# SoD Session 02 - Mollywop to Sapcutt

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: SoD
- Session Number: 02
- Title: Mollywop to Sapcutt
- Real Date: 2025-12-11
- In-Game Dates: 40th-41st of Hemist
- Starting Location: Adsuren / Nature's Oratory
- Ending Location: Sapcutt plantation, dropped into the pits beneath the house
- Attending Characters: [[Escanor]], [[Therric]], [[Zephyra]], [[Kenton]], [[Esylla]], [[Lila]]

## Major Scenes

- [[Lila]] is introduced at the Nature's Oratory.
- Party reports the water-taint cause; Kenton exaggerates and omits details.
- The black petrified heart is examined, smelled, and magically sensed as evil.
- Party eats and rests at the Graying Grotto; Kenton gets laundry and private attention from Rebecca / Heather.
- Therric has a nightmare connected to the heart and privately tells Escanor he is scared of it.
- Esylla asks Lila about her crystals and receives a crystal necklace from her.
- Party visits Mollywop's tower and leaves the heart for deeper study.
- Mollywop introduces the Sapcutt family errand after not seeing their famous pies at Market Day.
- The party ferries across the river with dockmaster MacCannain and travels through the Hath'lao Swamp.
- The party arrives at the Sapcutt plantation, eats gator/fungus stew, and most of them are drugged.
- The floor opens and the party falls into the pits beneath the house.

## Combats

- No major combat resolved before the session break; the danger is the Sapcutt ambush and drugging.

## NPCs Introduced Or Developed

- [[Lila]] Tealeaf.
- Mollywop.
- Mollywop's apprentices, including Thunderfall.
- Zuz, Mollywop's trapped demon specimen.
- Dockmaster MacCannain.
- Margaret Sapcutt and the Sapcutt family.
- Rebecca / Heather at the Graying Grotto.

## Clues Found

- The black heart is unsafe to carry and can torment the bearer.
- Mollywop reads the heart as enchantment and necromancy with torment around it.
- Mollywop's ward/alarm around town is part of her defensive routine.
- Sapcutts have been absent from Market Day, which worries Mollywop.
- Strange swamp creatures follow the party on the way to Sapcutt's.
- Sapcutt stew includes a recently found fungus and appears to drug the party.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Mollywop gives the party a pouch of coins for the Sapcutt errand.
- Esylla receives a crystal necklace from Lila.
- The black heart is left with Mollywop for study.

## Decisions Made

- Seek Mollywop's magical insight.
- Rest before visiting Mollywop rather than disturbing her late at night.
- Accept Mollywop's request to check on the Sapcutt family.
- Travel to the Sapcutt plantation despite swamp danger.
- Eat cautiously or not at all, but the stew still incapacitates several party members.

## Consequences

- The black heart remains an active mystery in Mollywop's hands.
- The party is trapped beneath the Sapcutt house at the session break.
- The Sapcutt family situation becomes a fungal/plant corruption lead.

## Character Beats

- Therric becomes afraid of the heart and confides in Escanor.
- Escanor takes responsibility for whether the heart is spiritually safe.
- Kenton performs charm, cleanliness, and self-presentation even during crisis setup.
- Esylla becomes an early magical/social bridge with Lila and Mollywop.
- Lila's crystals become party-facing through her gift to Esylla.
- Zephyra provides local knowledge about Mollywop and remains suspicious enough not to eat.
