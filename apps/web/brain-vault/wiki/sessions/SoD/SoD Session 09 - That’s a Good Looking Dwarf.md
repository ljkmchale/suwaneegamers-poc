# Session 09 - That’s a Good Looking Dwarf

Source: raw/sod-campaign-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pKpiVcOl-mjtJMUD4tuTS6A4UZP3w6ISnehpX8LORH8/edit

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  May 30, 2026
In-game Date:  45th of Hemist, 1246
Starting Location:  Adsuren
In Adsuren
Childhood memories are shared …
* Kenton learning how to sew from his mom
* Therric’s first hunting alone experience where he successfully returned with a rabbit
* Zephyra being strong with her weak dad
* Esconar’s father was killed trying to break up a bar fight, so Esconar would run errands for guards, but happy stepping into his dad’s shoes
* Esylla went out on uncle Tray’s ship with her Aunt Ree and the feeling of her hair in the wind.
The Road to the Mines
We continue down the path.  Escanor and Zephyra lead, followed by Kenton, then Therric and Esylla in the back.  Kenton every so often steps out of the marching order and tries to pickpocket Therric, but Therric notices (Esylla doesn’t).  He says to Kenton - this is going to take more practice.  Kenton responds:  that’s why I’m working on it.   Conversation about how Esylla is not surprised because of Kenton’s previous actions and taking the town’s coin.  She expressed displeasure because she paid the town most of her purse to insure that the Market Day would continue.  Therric replies that he didn’t know about this and she says of course - she was trying to be discreet.  Therric then privately asks Kenton to try to slip 10gp into Esylla’s purse as they walk. He is successful - Therric sees, but Esylla doesn’t notice.
As we continue, a donkey pulling a cart and a few people approach from the mines. Zephyra greets them.  They are trying to get back to Adsuren before nightfall.  Escanor joins in:  how goes it at the mines?  They reply that things are ok.  Normal work, but they say they have heard cries of a “devilish” beast near the mines.  A large “devil cow” which is a raging bulas.  They tell of hunters that went out to deal with it and were unable to bring it down with arrows.  They tell further of how the bulas evaded capture and more about what they know.  Zephyra asks to see the contents of their cart - to make sure that nothing unusual passes into the town.  This seems to be normal ore (iron and coal) and mining supplies, as well as chunks of stone.  Therric inspects the wheels of the cart - all seems ok.  Zephyra asks about the mine - will it continue to be fruitful and is told it is still thriving. They then depart.
We Camp  
Zephrya finds a small patch of land - there doesn’t seem to be a clearing.  Escanor, Esylla, and Zephyra set up camp while Therric forages - he finds Adsuren precura fruit and some berries.  While he is doing that, Kenton runs around with his new whip trying to kill a rabbit.  He sees some movement and attacks with his whip and realizes he attacked a grasshopper. Therric attempts to make a stew with his findings. We establish a watch order:  Kenton and Zephyra; then Escanor and Esylla, then Therric last.
1. First watch.  Campfire is set up and Kenton and Zephyra look around. Kenton mimics her as they sit there. They notice a little bit of a creeping fog around, but nothing else unusual.  Kenton plays with a coin in his fingers and Zephyra realizes he pocketed it from her and takes it back.  Zephyra plays with her tail and it feels forced…she has never messed with it.  They wake up the others.  
2. Second watch.  They notice the fog as well. The two have a discussion - Escanor asks Esylla how she is. Esylla talks to him about her unease with revealing her past, especially about her family.  He tells her not to worry or feel guilty and she feels better.  She tells him that she has reached out to her Aunt Ree and hopes she will keep her confidence. She also asks Escanor to reach out to her family if anything happens to her and he says he will, but that he will also help make sure she is ok.  The fog seems to roll over Therric as he enters a dream state… 


In Therric’s head, he wakes up and is on all fours in a prowling position, none of his friends around, no lit campfire.  He is still in the fog.  He notices a familiar female and he walks up to her, she is crouched down as well, dressed in white leaves with a tree branch on her back.  He sees her raise a “paw” and reaches out to her.  Therric is thinking like a beast, not a man.  She says something in a language he can’t communicate with, but understands “just stay behind me.” He is not sure who this is.  He follows her and they hear strange noises in the distance.  They continue until she tells him to stop.  They come to an area of packed dirt and then looks left and right and goes right.  He continues to follow. He notices that they seem to be on a “cleared strip” (a road) and he sees a print of a hooved creature.  He has seen creatures with similar prints, but isn’t sure what it is (Therric knows it is a horse, but in this state, he doesn’t know).  They press forward. There seem to be humanoid prints as well.  They are following the prints. They travel another 15 minutes until the woods thicken and they continue.  He smells dust and stone and he sees a large stone structure (this is a keep, but he doesn’t know this).  He sees figures in front of the structure (guards) in front of a wooden plank with ropes (drawbridge).  They stay hidden and observe a few hundred feet away.  He notices that the female notches arrows into her bow and kill both guards at one time with one shot.  She then stands up and dashes forward.  He follows her.  They advance over the drawbridge and past the dead and to another humanoid, who she kills. He notices another figure advancing that she doesn’t notice so he jumps to kill it and rips its head off. She nods at him and they continue into the stone structure.  There are (green tapestries with golden threads with three horns interwoven on it) fabrics hanging.  He stares at the fabric.
Meanwhile, Esylla and Escanor notice the fog, but it seems natural. Esconar enters a trance…


Escanor starts to notice nature sounds start to dim, as well as the sounds of Esylla.  He seems to focus in and only notice the flame of the campfire.  The air is colder.  He has a bad gut feeling about himself and isn’t sure why.  The flame is anchoring him and seems to be the only thing that keeps him from spiraling.  He starts to pray to Amriel. As he prays the fire starts to dim and he tosses a new log on the fire…but as he tosses it, it is actually an arm.  He realizes this and starts to pull the arm out. But a shadowy figure suddenly emerges from the flames and it is a shadowy silhouette of himself.  He says “beware” and pulls his mace.  This figure is all black with red eyes and seems to dance from the flames. Esylla doesn’t notice this at all.  He then hears his voice:  
“There is no redemption from you.  No god can cleanse my sins.  
A broken man like you can only mimic what it is like to be holy.  We all know what you did.” 
And the party all at once turns to look at him with red eyes and says “why?”
Esconar appears to wobble and fall into the fire.  Esylla reaches to grab him and can’t pull him out, but also screams out for help. She is unable to pull him out and notices a figure in the fire. 
IT IS INITIATIVE – Combat vs. Flaming Combatant (Image of Escanor)
* ROUND ONE:  Zephyra wakes up and sees a flaming figure of Escanor holding Escanor in the fire.  She tries to jump up and grab Escanor. She rages and pulls him out of the flames and says “what is going on?” Escanor says something is in the fire and it can affect our minds.  He casts a spell and it saves, but still takes damage.  The figure oozes the weird energy and oily smell, but the group saves.  It then tries to grab Escanor and does.  We see it reach into Escanor’s chest and grab and squeeze his heart and he goes unconscious, as he takes slashing damage.  It then tries to bite Esylla and does. She then touches Escanor and spares the dying and uses her awakened mind feature to yell at Therric in his head “wake up - Escanor needs you.”  This snaps Therric back to reality and he wakes up and sees the scene. He stands up and tries to put the fire out with elementalism, spraying water on it.  When this happens, the figure turns to ash and disappears.
Zephyra tries to wake Escanor as Therric moves forward and forces a Goodberry down his throat.  Escanor comes back and starts to look at the wet campfire.  He feels a sense of guilt. We all discuss what we saw and observed (or didn’t observe) and there are no answers.   Escanor clutches his holy symbol and lays down to think and pray.  Kenton and Therric investigate the flames and find nothing unusual.
Therric then starts to tell of his amazing dream, but not much.  Escanor is going to rest.  Esylla tells the others to sleep and she will finish her watch.  They fall asleep, as Escanor rests and prays and Esylla keeps watch.  She eventually wakes Therric up at the normal time and goes to sleep.  
3. Third watch:  Therric asks Escanor if he wants to be healed (he does not) and Escanor goes to sleep.  No issues during his watch; wrapping up his watch going through his monk drills.
It’s the next morning – the 46th of Hemist, 1246
As the group wakes up, Kenton plays his dulcimer and gives heroic inspiration to Escanor and Esylla.  Therric seems very well rested.  Escanor seems upbeat. The group eats rations.  We set out again with the same order.  The morning passes uneventfully.  We eventually reach a more hilly area.  After another couple of hours, we reach a staging area for the mines.  Zephyra and Escanor look for hunting trails that head north based on the advice of the miners we passed.  They notice a sign that says:  “Danger - white horns head.  Turn around.”  
Zephrya shows the group the sign and we know we are headed in the right direction. We start that direction and Therric brings up the events of the night before.  We discuss.  Escanor tells the group that it spoke to him.  Esylla and Therric think and study together about the situation and realize that sometimes beings with emotions can call forth a creature of that emotions to the present.  That Escanor called this being forward without realizing it.  Esylla mentions that he is a follower of Amriel, right?  And then they discuss redemption and who and how Escanor talks to and prays to. Esylla also mentions that maybe there is someone in the group who needs redemption - which leads to conversations about the chaos around the group (Zephrya’s mom, for example).  He isn’t sure how to interpret this, but clearly sees this as a message from his god. 
We continue to move forward and Zephyra notices that some of the trees are split ahead and she looks for prints and finds some large ones.  She also sees a dead deer, some bones on the ground, arrows in the trees, etc.   Esylla and Therric hear a noise from behind and Esylla sees a large beast with white matted fur and large antlers with white horns.  Esylla yells “it is behind us.”  
IT IS INITIATIVE – Combat vs. Big Bad Bulas
* ROUND ONE:  Escanor blinds the creature and moves to be in between the beast and everyone else.  Kenton hides behind a tree.  Zephyra runs up and tries to attack it with her javelin and it sticks.  She yells:  it has a thick skin.  Therric puts on hunter’s mark and then moves over and casts a spell to entangle it.  Esylla casts hex on it and then blasts it and hits. She then moves next to a tree.  The beast runs forward and into Zephyra and hits with disadvantage and knocks her prone and hits her again with a horn. 
* ROUND TWO:  Escanor runs forward and heals Zephyra. Kenton moves up and uses his whip and hits the beast, which slows it, then moves back.  Zephyra stands up and moves to the side a little (still in range) and attacks and hits. Therric moves on the other side of Escanor and slams his staff on the ground causing lightning to streak toward the beast and misses.  He then pulls the lightning up and tries to make an elemental strike and misses.  Esylla blasts it again and hits, as she moves off to the side.  The beast attacks Zephyra and hits twice, bloodying her.
* ROUND THREE:  Escanor turns to Zephyra and heals her. Kenton moves out and whips the beast and hits and then runs back behind the tree again.  Zephyra attacks with her axe and misses. Therric reaches toward the tree and pulls sap to throw acid on him and hits (as Kenton taunts him from the trees). Then uses Flurry of Blows and misses.  Esylla blasts him again and hits, using her heroic inspiration.  The beast disengages and tries to run away, crashing through trees.
* ROUND FOUR:  Escanor casts a spell, but it saves, so he moves 30’ after the beast, chasing it.  Kenton moves toward the retreating creature and whips it, hitting it, killing it.  
We step forward to check out the body. Therric guts the innards of the creature, with the help of others.  This takes most of the day.  Escanor takes six (6) +1 arrows that were stuck in the hide of the beast.  Therric is a leatherworker and makes Escanor a quiver from the doe.  We end up with a huge hide (like a large blanket), the large antlers and horns.  Therric thinks the meat would be good for one day’s worth of rations.  
We make camp.  
Escanor and Kenton take first watch; Zephrya and Esylla second watch; Therric alone at the end. 
1. First watch – passes uneventfully.  No fog, no strange flames.  Escanor is nervous and watches the fire, but he hears nature and doesn’t feel like he did last night.  
2. Second watch – uneventful.  Zephyra tries to stretch her tail and Esylla asks about it. They discuss whether or not she was tempted to travel with her real mother.  She mentions that she never knew where she came from - always curious.  Esylla suggests that she has a good reason now to talk to her real parents, who are still alive. She mentions that she wanted to make them proud.  But she now wants to stop her real mother from doing more harm to the world.  Esylla reminds her of the magic item that Mollywop can likely get - it is just costly.  They wake Therric.
3. Third watch – Therric practices his drills, circling the camp.  It is uneventful.
It’s the next morning, the 47th of Hemist, 1246
Kenton wakes up and plays his music, giving Therric and Zephyra heroic inspiration.  We carry the hide to the staging area for the mines, then take and use a wheelbarrow to help transport it back to town.  We make it to town and it is bustling at sunset.   
We make our way to the Tanner’s shop. 
When we arrive she is setting up merchandise in front of her shop.  She comments how quickly we returned and is quite pleased.  The tanner offers to trade us an enchanted pair of boots for the hide, they are bright green and help with being quiet.  Kenton gets the Boots of Elvenkind from the tanner and Therric buys leatherworking tools for 4gp. 
As we leave, Therric and Kenton head to the tavern. Esylla heads to the Grotto to sleep and catch up with Lila.  Escanor heads to the Oratory.  And Zephyra heads to her house. 
Escanor heads to the Oratory. 
He notices the crowds and hitched horses - there are many travelers in town.  He makes it there and notices there is a stand outside with a large pot and wooden bowls outside.  One of the acolytes, Beatrice, is cleaning the pot and greets Escanor.  He asks to speak to the priest - who he finds is inside.  He offers to help, but she declines.  He asks about any updates over the past couple of days - she responds that it has mainly been preparing for the Market Day and that there are a lack of guards for tomorrow, mostly from a lack of funds available to pay them.  He then goes inside and finds the priest.  Escanor briefs him on the encounter in the woods and asks if he has heard of anything like that.  He responds that Escanor may have been tested by his god - that this is something he has heard of - gods sending down avatars.  The priest mentions that he has heard rumors in Caldwyn that there is a religious void at a church there.  That there is a dark presence there in one of the churches. He mentions that if he finds himself in Caldwyn that he should visit the shrine to Amriel and then see what he can make of that rumor.  He then mentions that he knows that our group is helping Mollywop with something at the Market Day and Escanor confirms this.  He asks Escanor to stop by a couple of times tomorrow to help lead prayers as needed.
Therric and Kenton make their way to the Falling Fish.  
There is a line out the door - it is very crowded.  The buzz is about Market Day. Therric thinks there could be 1000 people in town for the event and worries there might not be enough amenities in town.  He is looking for people who might meet the profile of the ones who kidnapped Kenton.  Therric sees someone and points her out to Kenton.  She is very well dressed and is flanked by well dressed, bodyguard types.  She looks to be the most wealthy in this part of town.  Therric tries to distract one of the guards, but isn’t successful.  He tries to tell the tale of the bulas fight and specifically how it tried to take Zephyra down. He asks where the guard is from - Unstead.  He comments that his “lady is buying.”  Therric tells him about his figs. As they talk, Kenton plays his pan flute and walks through the crowd. The crowd inside is packed - it is more comfortable to be outside for sure.  He makes his way to the lady and greets her.  She asks if he is the local jester?  He says he is Sir Kenton and thinks that she will know him.  He mentions he if from Gibuldon and mentions that he has connections on high.  She whispers something to her guard and he moves away.  They continue to speak.  Kenton mentions that she needs a proper drink and flips 2gp to the remaining guard who goes to get in line to purchase the drink.  Kenton then continues to talk to the lady about his reputation.  She confirms that she has heard of some of the rumors and he asks her if she wants to “sample of those rumors” and she initially declines.  She then asks if he knows of his reputation to the higher class.  She tells him that he is known to have stolen from a wealthy family and fled the town. She says that many noble families are on the lookout for him.  Therric walks away and starts to spread rumors that Kenton had sex with a dwarf in Gibuldon.  Kenton mentions that he came across Rodolf.  She says that she doesn’t know him, but knows his family.  She just says that should would be weary when he approaches people of status and then nods her head and motions and a guard behind him, steps to her side and she starts to walk away. Kenton looks to see how she is dressed - she is dressed for the night, not for travel. He decides not to steal anything, as he realizes that he has a reputation that he needs to fix.  Therric finds the guard in line and points out that the lady and guard are leaving and he steps out of line to follow his lady.
Therric walks up to Kenton and asks what is up.  Kenton says he has reputation problems.  Kenton also says he needs to steal 2gp to make up for his loss just down with the lady.  Therric looks around to try to find a mark for Kenton to pickpocket and points them out.  Kenton tries to steal from them and is able to snag the pouch and keeps walking.
Zephyra makes it to her home and cleans up. She thinks about the past few days with her mother and the travels. She decides that she wants to let her family know that she has new information.  She writes a letter to her parents and “has found her relative” that explains who she is.  Also writes about her new friends.  Plans to take the letter to mail at Market Day tomorrow.
Esylla makes it to the Grotto.  
It is packed.  Kids are running around and managing tables.  One of the kids stops her.  Arthur McLunge stops and tells her they have set aside a room and she pays him 1gp for the room and 1cp for his tip.  She then goes up to find Lila and tells her about their travels.  Then goes back down to watch for Therric and Kenton to return and people watches. She gets a bread and cheese tray.
Therric and Kenton stop by the Guard Post on the way back to the Grotto.  They talk to the guards inside about Market Day, working, and their pay.  He talks about how crowded it is and how pickpockets are likely rampant.  Therric offers to help out and/or to try to find extra guards.  They then make their way to the Grotto.
At some point, Escanor makes his way to the Grotto and arrives and Therric and Kenton arrive.  They talk to Esylla. She fills them in about their room, the upcharges, etc.  One of the children stops and he and Therric discuss economics and figs.  Therric challenges the kid to do something…and the kid convinces him to get a dwarf to proposition Kenton.  “Next round is on me, big boy.”  Oh - I like that you play hard to get.  “I have a headache”  “We can fix that.”  Kenton is mildly upset with Therric.  Everyone goes up to the rooms.  Once there, Kenton opens the pouch he opened and sees an old soldier’s medallion (1-5gp). 
Escanor heads back to the Oratory for bed. We all go to bed and get a long rest.
It’s the next day, the 48th of Hemist, 1246 - MARKET DAY
Esylla wakes Therric up in his head early - he then goes to his house and gets 12 servings of figs to sell at Market Day (2 hours round trip).  Kenton wakes up and finds Therric gone.  Escanor makes his way outside and eats some soup and helps out where he can.  He and Zephrya notice the town setting up for the day. Zephrya goes to the Guard Post to try to volunteer to be a guard - no one is there.  She then goes to the Adsuren Center and finds someone to ask about helping out.  She asks about the head guard and where she is making rounds.  She then leaves to make her way to find the Guard Captain.  Therric and Esylla find their way to the breakfast area and eat then make their way outside to try to find Mollywop.
They find Escanor and Therric and group up.  Meanwhile, Zephyra finds the Captain of the Guards and discusses helping out for free. She says she will help out at the Market area.  She says to let her know if he needs additional help.  He agrees that he expects heavy traffic at the Market area and that she would be most helpful there.  The other four start toward Mollywop’s tower and run into Zephyra.  Therric grabs her tail, but she says she hasn’t been able to do anything with it yet. She continues to the Market area, after Esylla makes an awakened mind connection with her.  Therric then asks Kenton how the dwarf was and he says “disappointed.”  There is a brief conversation about dwarves and how Kenton does not like them.
The group of four makes it to Mollywop’s Tower
We see one of the apprentices (the rat one - male halfling).  The group tells why they are there and he says that she is inside eating breakfast.  He says that he just put the alarm spell in place.  Therric and Escanor mention the pickpocketing epidemic and ask if there is any cause for concern.  The group walks in and sees Mollywop.  She is in a casual, summer robe.  She looks tired, but better than the last time we saw her. She says that she was going to message the group, but since we are here, she would tell us know.  She asks for help with the finale - as Escanor mentions and demonstrates that he was planning to sing.  She says that she doesn’t specialize in evocation magic, but was hoping we could help with conduits to help lightning jump around during the finale.  She tells us that she will gather a storm and we will get a sign of when to throw the metal rods into the sky to chain the lightning.  She asks for ideas of what to convey.  We throw out bulas, dragons, beholders, angels, etc.  She tells us that she will message us later in the day to see if we have an idea.  As we are about to leave, she gets a twitch and tells the group that her town alarm was triggered to the north.  She asks us to check it out.  Therric seems a little scared and concerned.  She tells us that there is a fisherman’s hut up the coast where we might be able to intercept. She says that in case there is danger, this will help - and she wards the four of us (we get 30 temp hp each).  We then start out towards the coast. 
After a few minutes, Zephyra arrives at the tower.  They brief her and the apprentice casts a spell on Zephya. She now has haste on her for an hour. She should be able to catch up to the group easily.  
The group jogs and makes it to a fisherman’s hut on the coast with a small dock.  Esylla, Kenton, and Escanor notice there is a boat with supplies on the dock - thinking he was going to fish early for Market Day, but there is no sign of the fisherman.  Esylla goes to the hut and sees no one.  Escanor looks around the hut.  Therric goes to the dock to investigate.  Kenton looks around outside. Therric splashes a stone in the water at a distance to make a distraction. Esylla notices Zephrya running up the coast to catch up.  The boat shoots up from the water and drenches Kenton and Therric.  The sun is then immediately blotted out.
 The End of the Session –
