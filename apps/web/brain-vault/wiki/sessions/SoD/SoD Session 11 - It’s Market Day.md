# Session 11 - It’s Market Day

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  June 28, 2026
In-game Date:  48th of Hemist, 1246
Starting Location:  Adsuren
We make our way back into the town.  Mollywop cleans us up en route - tells us that she will find us when she needs us later (she is heading to her Tower).  Have a discussion about not bringing up the dragon; talking to the Intendant about the taint in the swamp.
The town is decorated for Market Day - ribbons, bunting, etc. Discussion about what to do.  Kenton wants to visit the blacksmith.  Lila shares that someone said “the plan is almost upon us.”  We discuss that we need to keep our eyes open and be aware of anything out of the ordinary. 
Esylla, Lila, and Escanor are going to walk and shop and scan the crowd (and Lila sell/trade her trinkets).  Zephyra is going to follow.  Kenton is going to the blacksmith. Therric is going to sell his figs.
Kenton goes to the blacksmith - following up to see if his rapier is finished.  He notices that things are cleaned up more than usual. The dwarf comes up and tells Kenton it is finished and goes to the back to get it.  Blue/purple scabbard, silver rapier.  Dwarf says she added flair and Kenton is pleased.  He takes it and gives her his old rapier and secondary whip. 
Therric eyes the area to see if there is a place he can set up to sell his figs. He looks for food items for sale - sees folks eating, but not the stall it came from.  He sees a kid eating pie and asks where it is acquired.
The rest of the group notices Therric talking to a boy and then heading off to the side.  Lila and Escanor notice a tall pole covered with ribbons and folks playing/walking around it.  They also notice people eating pies, drinking ale.  Esylla is looking more at the shops and doesn’t notice the activities.  
Pie Maker Mahgee
Therric sees a few tables set up under a canopy where dwarves and halflings are baking pies, dressed up like chefs. He talks to Pie Maker McGee…he says he has really good figs that he wants to give to him to make pies, that he isn’t really into selling them.  If anyone asks where they came from to say that this great “fig bearer gave them to me…he is retiring.”  He is offered an apple pie in exchange for the figs. McGee then asks if Therric wants to be part of the next pie eating contest. Winners get “cool shirts”; all white silk shirt.  The next contest is in 30 minutes - says he will come back and take part.
Lila’s Stall
The rest of the group walks around and notices an empty stall with an elf standing there.  Escanor and Zephyra keep watch - notice there don’t seem to be many guards in this area.  Esylla asks if she is already sold out.  This is a druid…she said that she was holding the stall for Therric.  Lila says she will sell her stuff at this stall until Therric comes up with his figs.  And the druid says ok and walks off.  She sets up her trinkets.  Escanor fawns over them.  Over the course of an hour, she sells everything, making an extra 20gp on top of the value of 6gp she thought she’d earn.  
Pin & Cloaks - Xiphior the Cloaksmith
Esylla walks around visiting other stalls - talking up Lila’s stuff, but also looking at what she can buy.  She takes special notice of a pin seller.  She talks to the seller about his cloaks and pins.   Esylla talks to the cloak vendor, who claims he is from Gibuldon (was disgraced from the Lords of Magic).. He is selling fancy and “magical” cloaks; claiming one billows and the other has displacement properties. Esylla buys a dove shaped silver cloak pin for 2sp.  As Lila sells out her trinkets, she runs over to talk to the cloak seller, just as Esylla is walking away.  She wants to buy the enchanted cloak - makes a bargain to buy it for 53gp (down from 60gp) and has a white cloak thrown in for free (saves 3gp) for Escanor. 
Horn Adorning – The Jewelry Seller
Zephyra is walking around looking for trouble. She is also looking to purchase something that will make her feel more feminine and sexy.  She sees a jewelry stall selling fine jewelry.  Talks to the shopkeeper about which jewels might be best for her.  She looking to adorn her horns (she’s a horn adorner).  He offers 2 silver rings with a chain for 15gp…then haggles down to 13gp, flirting with the man.  He offers to adorn her horns and she agrees. 
Impressionable Kids
As Kenton makes his way to the market area, he is approached by a young girl.  She asks if he can teach her to sword fight….and if he is an adventurer.  He offers to help.  A group of kids runs over and picks up sticks and starts to fight.  He then pulls out the rapier and strikes a pose and starts to tells stories.  He also pulls out his instrument and plays and sings and talks of defeating a dragon.  This goes on for several minutes.  They give him hugs and he is warmed from their actions. He is seen by Escanor who calls him over and tells him of the cloak merchant.
Kenton walks over the cloak merchant. The guy shows him the billowing cloak for 55gp.  Kenton tries to barter with him.  They have a discussion how this guys makes cloaks for all the noble families. He asks the merchant if he knows Sir Rudolph and he doesn’t.  He then asks if he knows of the Reyvennra family and he knows of them. Kenton says he will buy the cloak for 53gp 5sp…and asks the guy to put in a good word to the nobles on his behalf.
Therric is looking for something to protect his book from water.  Is trying to find a leather bag maker while he waits for the pie eating contest.  He notices two people near the Spider’s SIlk store who seem shifty - a human and half orc.  He walks up and asks “how are you fine gentlemen doing today?”  It is a great market day, they say.  Therric talks about the pie eating contest.  They say they are going into the silk shop - a place they go every market.  The human says his name is Rudy…and the half orc is a mute. Therric calls them out because he saw them talking, but Rudy says the guy was reading his lips.  Therric think they are probably from Tirncall and asks when they are heading back.  They say - they are going back after Market Day.  Therric asks them if they heard what happened in the town - in the past stretch.  They say they didn’t.  He then tells them about how Adsuren was under the influence of corrupt people and how there are now lots of undercover people walking around to make sure that Adsuren is now safe.  Basically giving them a warning - how they choose to take it, who knows?
Therric then goes to the pie eating contest, making a mental note of those two.  He  doesn’t see a guard or Zephyra.  He sees a few seats with 3 pies at each seat - and a few others sitting down ready for the contest.  Therric warns the chef of shady characters and pays his 1gp admission to the contest.  He decides to eat cherry pie instead of apple.  Contest is about to start. Therric tries to slip goodberries into competitor’s pies - he laces two of the pies trying to make them feel full.  He does successfully.  Contest begins.  Therric downs his first pie with ease. The other 3 also down their pies.  After two pies, one of the other competitors drops out,- feeling full.  Third pie coming - down to 3 people.  Another drops out from goodberry.  Down to 2 people.  They eat 3-6 pies with no problem, but on the 7th pie, Therric wins as the goliath throws up his pie.  Therric wins a white silk shirt - which he selects a size for Esylla.  He gets cherry pie on the shirt and tries to make it a design.
Escanor finds a healer’s kit - he heals Esylla (8hp).  Therric buys some oil to make a pouch water proof.  Escanor has the arrows identified that we got elsewhere and finds that they are +1 arrows.  Therric makes Kenton a leather belt. 
Kenton looks around for someone to steal a dagger from.  He sees someone - a bard/juggler - who has several daggers on his back.  He is preoccupied by children at the moment.  He steals 3 daggers from the juggler and walks away.  And as he walks off he hears the guy notice that he is missing 3 daggers.
Esylla goes to the blacksmith to try to sell the sickles the group acquired.  She puts the funds in the party inventory (35% of the price).
Lila and Zephyra meet up at the jeweler, as Zephya is finishing having her horns adorned.   Escanor walks up and sees them.  He notices a matted gold band that stands out for some reason.  The shopkeep tells him it is magical - used by adventurers to see in the dark.  He asks about the price and Zephrya tries to intervene on his behalf.  The haggled price is 51gp - and Escanor buys it (Adventurer’s Ring).  Nothing jumps out at Lila.  She then looks around to find a gem seller…and tells Zephyra and Escanor she is going over.
Lila notices the wealthy lady there looking at buying gems.  She overhears the shopkeep trying to sell a 100gp jade to her.  She greets Lady Ellspeth with a hello.  They talk.  Lila looks at the case of gems. Lila tells her that the jade is a very nice gem.  And Lady Ellspeth buys it with 10pp and thanks Lila for her “keen eye.”  Lila then looks at gems for herself - she is trying to see if any are magical - none seem to be. She decides to pass on a purchase.  Escanor buys a hair clip with a gem from the gem seller (3gp).  
Kenton also finds his way to the gem seller and buys a tiger’s eye gem for 10gp (saving for future bribe). 
Therric pauses at the decorative weapons cart (daggers with gems, for example).  He is looking for something that might stand out. Some are weapons, some are show pieces.  One catches his eye - it is a dagger with a gem-encrusted pommel with green gems.  This is the nicest one and is 15gp.  The shopkeeper tells of his inspiration for creating it and Therric buys it for 15gp.
Lila looks around for a sweets seller and finds a pastry seller selling scones - 1 dozen for 1gp.  She buys a dozen.  These are Adsuren berry scones (precura fruit).  
The group meets back up. Therric says he is stuffed and buys things for people.  He gives Esylla a silk shirt, gives Kenton a jeweled dagger.  Escanor gives Kenton the ring he bought.  Therric notices that Zephrya adorned her horns and asks her if she is doing her meditations.  Escanor gives Zephrya a silver hair comb.  They notice Lila’s new cloak.  Kenton shows off his new cloak. He then gives Lila the tiger’s eye gem that he purchased…and she is pleased.  Escanor gives Therric the magical arrows. Lila gives everyone the scones to eat.  She then cleans the shirt for Esylla and Therric’s beard.
Tirncall Thugs at the Silk Shop
Therric mentions the shady characters he saw from Tirncall to the group.  Esylla questions if he saw them doing anything illegal. He says he didn’t and didn’t mean to profile them, but said something didn’t feel good about his encounter.  The group walks over to the Spider’s Silk to check out the situation in pairs.  Most of the group doesn’t see anything out of the ordinary, but Zephyra sees people slipping inside the shop who match the description Therric gave.  As she and Esylla walk in, they see a robbery in progress.  Esylla mind slivers the half-orc as Zephyra tries to intimidate them both.  She does and they agree to run away.  Esylla asks them to apologize and they do, but she asks if they have already done this.  She tries to mind sliver the human, but he saves.  They run out of the store.  Lila hears them talk and thinks they sound familiar.  Kenton and Therric try to stop them.  Zephyra stays in the store to assist the merchant, as Esylla runs after them. 
IT IS INITIATIVE – Combat vs. Tirncall Thugs
* ROUND ONE:  Therric casts entanglement and restrains the half orc, Rudy.  Lila bursts  Rudy - who is also bleeding from his mouth. She crits, dealing 3 points of damage.  Esylla runs forward 30’ (90’ away) and tries to blast Jerry, but misses.  Kenton dashes forward to within 30’.  Jerry tries to break Rudy out and can’t.  Rudy can’t either.
* ROUND TWO:  Therric runs forward and asks them to stop “if you stop now, you might get out of this alive.”  Lila bursts Rudy and hits, inflicting more damage, then runs forward 30’.  Escanor dashes forward telling them to repent “repent - justice is coming.”  Kenton moves forward and whips Jerry - trying to hold him and fumbles.  Esylla holds her reaction to blast Jerry if they move.  Jerry and Rudy surrender, revealing their plans.  
Therric tells them - you know we are going to have to take you in.  Escanor disarms them.  They have crappy hand crossbows and 1 bolt each.  Zephrya ties their hands up and walks them to the guard house, as Lila yells “you do not steal on market day.”  Escanor escorts Zephyra to the guard house.
At the guard house, Zephyra hands them off to a guard and explains what happened.  
Therric and Esylla walk into the silk shop.  Therric looks to see why he is a target - realizes that he is one of the wealthy shopkeepers.  He asks Esylla for the shirt he gave her. He then asks the shopkeeper to make the shirt look a little nicer for Esylla and he agrees - in exchange for the help he was provided.
Evening Festivities on Market Day
Hours pass…night comes. Lanterns are being hung on trees.  A large crowd gathers.  The party makes it way to the center stage.  The acting Intendant gets ready to give a speech.  The group sees Mollywop in the crowd and an assistant casts a spell on the person giving the speech to enhance his voice…he then adjusts it to be his own voice.  Mollywop finds the group and asks how the day went. And verifies the help she needs.  She reminds the group about the conduit stones she gave us and asks what she should make.  Esylla suggests a dragon.  She assigns places for the group to stand with our stones in the square.  And tells us to toss the stones as hard and high we can when she gives the hand signal.
The group then overhears the Intendant praising the group publicly, then he introduces Mollywop (mentioning she will perform before the group Bows & Lilies).  The group spreads out.  She starts to send out color sprays, lightning, water waves that spray the crowd, other spells for several minutes.  The crowd is very impressed.  Then acts about the finale. And casts a blue dragon illusion into the sky then gives the signal and we all toss our stones up…as she casts lightning.  Then chain lightning…as the dragon explodes.
Escanor sees someone plunge a sword through Mollywop’s back, with the blade emerging from her chest with poison visibly dripping from the blade…proclaiming, “I finally got you.”  The dwarven apprentice is clutching her chest on the back of the stage.  The assassin has plunged a great sword into Mollywop’s chest.   Lila recalls that the shape of the assassin matches the shape of the man in the vision that talked to the dragon.
IT IS INITiATIVE – Combat vs. Assassin and his thugs
* ROUND ONE:  The assassin pulls out the sword and plunges it back into Mollywop and leaves the blade in.  Then wraps a chain around Mollywop, grappling her.  And yells out “now” and a bunch of people with hoods are in the crowd suddenly reveal themselves (12 of them).  Mollywop makes a CON save and does.  Then yells out to evacuate the civilians then casts a Time Stop spell.  She casts mirror image and a few of her appear. She also casts another spell to attack the assassin with motes of energy, a hand with fire and another with frost.  The assassin falls off the back of the stage, prone. A chain is still attached to Mollywop.  Therric stands next to a thug and tries to attack, but misses.  But then hits him with an unarmed attack.  Zephyra yells for the townspeople to “get to the Adsuren Center.”  She then attacks the thug next to her with her axe and hits. Lila attacks the thug on her and hits. Escanor moves forward to get closer to the stage and the thug next to him tries to net him, but he dodges it as another net hits him from another thug who emerges from close by.  He is restrained.  He tries to break free of the net and does, pulling the net off of him. He moves 25’ forward and the dwarf apprentice gets 11 points of healing.  Mollywop uses a special action to burst one of the thugs and hurts it and pulls him closer.  Kenton will attack the thug on him and fumbles, losing his reaction.  He then uses a bonus action to disengage.  The thugs start to beat on civilians.  They also try to net each hero.  Therric is restrained and knocked prone.  They are unable to restrain Kenton.  Two try to net Escanor and do then try to knock him prone and can’t.  Lila is not restrained or prone.  Zephyra is netted and restrained and the thug she hit heals himself with a potion.  She is also hit with a weapon.  Esylla is netted, restrained, and knocked prone.  Some civilians are able to run away and leave the town square.  Another bad guy enters the square and walks up to a civilian and attacks him, but Mollywop stops it.  But he attacks again and caves in his head and dies.  Kenton and Esylla think this is a gang from Tirncall (the red jackals)...they know that the guy who just killed someone is Brownwin Redmouth, their leader.  Mollywop moves the sword out of her chest and hides it under the stage.  The guards then try to escort civilians out of the square and free Esylla from her net (and does) and run up to the assassin.
* ROUND TWO:  The assassin drinks a potion (haste) and then hits one of the guards and kills him.  He then attacks another guard and crits him, breaking his skull against the stage.  He then runs toward Mollywop, drawing a blade.  He hits two of the mirror images and they disappear.  Mollywop blasts the gang boss with a blast for a legendary action, pushing him 10’ away.  Then she tries to figure out what poison is hurting her.  This is one extracted in Inverland that is unable to be healed with magical means. She then attacks the assassin and hits.  The dwarf apprentice jumps off the stage and bursts one of the thugs on Kenton, after Mollywop tells her to help others.  Therric tries to break the restraint and doesn’t.  Zephyra tries to attack and misses.  Lila moves away, taking 2 opportunity attacks, but then casts a spell using her circlet - aiming at the one she hit with 2 rays and the other with 1 ray.  She hits one twice and the other once, inflicting damage.  Escanor tries to break free of the net and does.  He moves to the stage, taking opportunity attacks, getting hit only once.  Mollywop tries to hold the assassin back with a legendary action. Kenton attacks one of his attackers with a whip and hits, then disengages and moves next to Therric. Thugs go:  two attack Therric and both miss. Two chase after Kenton and try to hit - both do.  The leader swings at a civilian and hills him, then attacks the cloakmaker, but Mollywop wards him and the guy misses.  Escanor is attacked by two and one misses. Lila is attacked by one and is hit. Zephyra is attacked by two thugs and both hit.  Esylla is attacked by two and is hit by both, breaking her foot (movement is halved).  Mollywop tries to take out a thug and does damage.  Esylla stands up and shoots her blast at a thug in range and hits.  The guards attack thugs and hit.  Mollywop takes a thug down.

The assassin then hits Escanor, taking him down. Then he circles around Mollywop.  Mollywop stands up and yells out about her poison. She says “your fight is with me” and tries to attack him and misses twice.  Then uses chain lightning to hit the leader, two thugs, and the assassin (who saves).  Three thugs are killed.  Assassin sidesteps the lightning and evades it completely.  The dwarf runs forward, picks up Escanor and disappears.  Therric breaks his netting and attacks a thug and hits. Zephyra attacks the thug on her and hits.  Lila shoots an orb at the one next to her and hits. Mollywop uses a legendary action.  Escanor wakes up with the dwarf next to him, they are in the Nature’s Oratory - he is healed.  Kenton attacks the one next to him and hits, then disengages and runs over to the well.  Mollywop uses an action to kill another, but fumbles and falls prone.  Thugs go:  they kill some civilians.  The two on Therric attack and both hits, but only one deals damage.  One runs to Kenton and misses.  Two attack Lila - both hit.  One on Zephyra hits.  One on Esylla misses.  One hits a guard, one misses a guard.  The cloak maker is hit by the gang leader.  Esylla tries to blast one and misses.

She tries another blast and misses, fumbling again.  She then shoots a large spell at the assassin (disintegrate)...and his left arm falls off and he starts to cough up blood, taking a chunk out of his lung. The group is out of combat.  The group knows this is one of the deadliest poisons in Myrdae.  Esylla knows that it cuts off connections to divinity and magical ways. One vial of this poison costs 8000-10000gp. 
Esylla asks who this might have been - who would have had the wherewithal to do this to Mollywop.  Who was the assassin?  She recognizes that the assassin was from a Vec to the south. Ifryn Ciaradain (he is Fordevae).   Esylla recognizes that this was her uncle.

We ask who she might know who would be knowledgeable about the poison?  Thieves guild, high ranking nobles, or maybe the Lords of Magic in Gibuldon.  
We decide to track the assassin.  Escanor picks up the great sword that the assassin used.  Mollywop reveals that it broke a magical spell.  Esylla starts to identify the sword. 
The priests come and take Mollywop’s body.  We follow the blood tracks to the stables and see that the assassin fled on horseback.  Plan is to follow him.  Group rides into the night until we can rest.  Arcane Executioner is the sword…Zephyra gets it.
Esylla asks the dwarf to send a couple of messages:  to her Aunt Ree and to her “uncle Trey.”  
 – The End of the Session –
