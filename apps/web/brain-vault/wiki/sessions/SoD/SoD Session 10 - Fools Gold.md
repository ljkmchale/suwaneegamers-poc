# Session 10 - Fools Gold

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  June 17, 2026
In-game Date:  48th of Hemist, 1246
Starting Location:  Adsuren
Rewind for Lila (2-1/2 days before) – It’s the 45th of Hemist for Lila
Deciding to stay at the Grotto vs. heading to fight the Bulas, Lila decides to stay there to handcraft jewels for Market Day. She makes four small trinkets worth 1gp each to sell.  
It’s the 46th of Hemist for Lila
The next day the party has not returned so she decides to walk around the town.  She notices that the town is getting crowded as ships arrive with people coming in for Market Day.  She makes her way around to see if anyone is selling anything she is interested in, mainly supplies for jewelcrafting.  Most seem to want to hold off until the 28th, hoping for an upcharge selling it that day.  She tries to detect thoughts and finds a lady flanked by two bodyguards.  This is Lady Ellspeth.  She seems to be wealthy.  Is chatting with children - seems to be a good person.  As she walks around she is trying to pick up to see what others are thinking.  She hears “the plan is almost upon us” but can’t figure out who is thinking that. 
That evening one of the children comes up to her at the Grotto and says that she will now need to start paying for the room…since the town is crowded and they can no longer rent it for free.   “My Pa has a plan for your friends when they return, but it will be 1gp for 2 days.”  She happily pays.  
It’s the 47th of Hemist for Lila
Her friends have still not returned.  She decides to make additional jewelry for Market Day.  She makes one trinket worth 5gp.  This takes up most of her day.  Others she tried to make weren’t the quality she needs to sell them.
During her sleep, she hears someone enter the room - it is Esylla.  She goes back to sleep.  At some point during the night, she wakes up with a warm sensation in her chest.  She opens her eyes and feels her necklace is warm.  It appears to be not its usual color (dull gray/purple), but is black obsidian that is glowing gold.  She brings the necklace to her face and feels like she is looking like a starry sky in space.  The colors of the nebula move around until it eventually forms a shape that looks like a cave made of stars - she can’t tell if she is inside the cave or outside.  But she sees the shape of an oval start to form and as she stares at this, the oval collapses in on itself and it starts to move like a serpent.  The stars are all gold. The serpent seems to be moving around in the cave.  She isn’t sure what it means.  Some time passes….it feels like it is years.  The serpent is now moving through a forest, going after small game in the forest (squirrels, mice, etc.).  Seems to be a natural predator of the creatures.  More time passes…and now the serpent has legs (it always had legs) and it is moving up the trees and jumps from tree to tree.  More time passes.  Now it looks like the serpent is watching wolf hunting a rabbit…it then jumps between them and scares off the wolf.  Then it let the rabbit goes away.  More time passes.  It is perched on top of a tree. And you see a red group of stars going toward a village.  You see the serpent bow up.  You think those stars are bad, but the village and serpent are good.  More time passes - decades.  The serpent is large and patrols over the top of the forest and comes to a clearing.  In this clearing is a silhouette of a humanoid.  It is waiting on the serpent.  The serpent then approaches the humanoid and she sees that they have a conversation.  An image of a swamp and large tree come to mind.  The man pulls up a hood and walks away. The serpent sits for a time and a few days pass.  Then the serpent seems to be thinking about the man.  Then it travels and finds a swamp and a large tree (the vision) - it then goes inside the large tree and rests there.  You get the impression that the serpent is at home.  A few days pass.  Vines and mushrooms start to cover the serpent.  The serpent stirs….
And she wakes up.  It is Market Day.  She doesn’t see Esylla.  She already left and briefly thinks that Esylla woke her up and she asked for 5 more minutes.  Who knows how much longer she slept.
As she reflects, she doesn’t think she knows the man.  There were no details to see - it was in shadow, nut thinks he was armored and probably 6’ tall.  She saw two scabbards at his hips - one larger than the other.    But the serpent - she feels a little fear from it.  Something felt off - she thinks it was a force of good, but still fills fear.  She thinks that despite the time that passed, this is a young serpent.
It’s the 48th of Hemist (for all)
Lila goes outside to try to find the group.  When she doesn’t find them in the Grotto, she heads out into the town to see if they are already at Market Day.  She sees everything being set up for the event. Lots of folks around.  She doesn’t see her friends.  Also doesn’t see Mollywop. A guard walks up to Lila - looking tired. He tells her that Zephyra went to Mollywop’s Tower.  She sees a flash of purple - doesn’t know what that was. She heads that way…the door is open.
She finds Mollywop and she seems tired.  She is with her elven apprentice.  She greets Lila.  Are you looking for your friends?  She tells her that her alarm was triggered a half mile up the river…and that Zephrya was just here.  She offers to speed up Lila, too.  Lila confirms that the purple blur was Zephyra.  She asks the helper to cast a spell on Lila.  He says he’s been trying to learn how to teleport people…and asks if she wants to try it. He isn’t 100% sure that it will work, but offers and she accepts.
At a Fisherman’s Hut In Adsuren
Kenton and Therric are on the dock and they are splashed by a geyser of water as the boat crashes back into the water. They are both pushed into the water as a large golden beast emerges from the water and blocks out the sun.  It is 30’ up. 
IT IS INITIATIVE – Combat vs. Dragon
* ROUND ONE:  Zephyra is 140’ away from this combat. She sees the figure appear in the distance. She dashes forward with haste and bonus action rages. The dragon swoops down to the ground and lands at the bottom of the dock and it blasts fire at Escanor (saves) and Esylla (failed). THe shack starts to burn as well.  It then flies up 15’ and the end of the dock, surveying the area.  As it hits Esylla, her hellish rebuke goes off, but it does no damage.  Therric climbs up on the dock and sees an upturned barrel with spears. He picks up one and moves forward and throws a spear at the dragon and misses.  Kenton climbs up to the dock and casts a shadow of himself that appears 5’ away.  He then jumps back into the water and swims under the dock.  Esylla hexes the dragon and hits it with a blast and then runs behind the shack, telling Escanor to “avenge (me).”  Lila suddenly appears next to Escanor and sees the dragon.  It clicks in her dream that this is the serpent that was in her dreams. She sees that the dragon is beautiful, but covered with spores and fungus.  She yells out “golden dragon - I am your friend.  I saw you grow up.  I saw you asleep in a tree.”  A word a Cabristrasz appears in her head…and she yells it out, thinking it is the dragon’s name.  She can see that the dragon’s right eye is messed up - with fungus.  The left eye seems to look at her - with the tendrils separating slightly.  She then runs behind the shack.  Escanor thinks back to the infected creatures we fought before and tries to think of what to do.  He blesses himself, Lila, and Esylla.  He feels like whatever infection has hit this dragon must be severe - that he is probably too far gone to save.  He says outloud “I think he is too far gone - even Amriel can’t help him.”  He then moves behind the shack.
* ROUND TWO: Zephrya moves forward 50’ and slings a hand axe at it and hits it. She then throws another axe at it and crits, noteworthy.  She then backs up 10’. The dragon pops out the axes and the vines close up over the wounds. The dragon’s left eye twitches and the dragon’s arms wrap around its body and it falls to the ground prone, taking minor damage as it does.  Therric moves forward and calls forth Voegurn’s strike and hit the dragon with thunder, then uses furry of blows at it and misses, but then hits. Kenton swims forward under the dock to get closer to the dragon, but stays in the water. Lila holds her necklace to her face and asks “show me a way to save this dragon” - the gray crystal with the purple hue shifts to a red color.  She isn’t sure what that means.  She moves forward and to the side and becomes invisible.  Esylla steps to the side and tries to blast it - with disadvantage - and misses.  Then runs behind a tree. Escanor tries to call to Amriel and specifically thinks about his spells - he thinks that he might be able to help, but that he couldn’t cure it.  He runs next to Esylla and heals her.
* ROUND THREE:  Zephyra draws her great axe and attacks and hits twice, doing some good damage. The dragon stands up and the vines start to patch up the wounds. And the left eye gets covered over with vines.  It swipes at Zephyra with a claw and hits. The dragon then opens its mouth and Therric feels a wave of energy come over him - he feels weakened (disadvantage on all strength-based items and must subtract a d6 from attacks).  Then it tail swipes Zephrya for more damage. Therric attacks with his staff and misses. Then tries to kick it and misses.  Kenton surfaces in the water at the edge of the dock and uses his whip to hit it and then goes back under water. Lila (invisible) shoots a chromatic orb at it and imposes acid damage.  Esylla moves forward and uses a shadow touched feature to inflict poison damage and the poisoned condition.  Then moves back toward another tree.  Escanor steps forward 30’ next to the shack.
* ROUND FOUR: Zephyra attacks with her greataxe and misses twice. Then she calls for healing from the warrior of the gods to heal herself.  Vines are not able to help the dragon on its turn.  It flies up in the air 15’ - giving Zephyra and Therric attacks of opportunity.  He hits. It then breathes fire at Zephyra and Therric.  Zephyra goes down and Therric is hurt by the flame but dodges the damage as the dock shatters as the dragon dives through it into the water.  Therric uses his elemental damage to throw acid at the dragon in the water and misses with one attack, but hits with one attack.  Kenton tries to attack under the water with his whip with disadvantage and hits. Then disengages and crawls to shore. Lila yelled out to the dragon to fly away, but doesn’t see it in the water.  Esylla dashes over to Zephyra.  Escanor moves forward and heals Zephyra.
* ROUND FIVE:  Zephyra rages and stands up and throws two spears into the water at the dragon and misses.  The dragon swims into the water and pops up off to the side and it opens its mouth and a wave of energy overcomes Zephrya and Kenton - neither are weakened.  Lila held her action and sees the dragon head and attacks with her orb and misses. It then tries to bite Kenton and hits twice. It then flies up in the air 20’.  Kenton gets an attack of opportunity with his whip, but misses with luck. Therric drops Hunter’s Mark on it and tries to hit with his crossbow and does. Kenton throws a dagger at the dragon and hits. Then swaps places with his shadow.  Lila yells his name and says “I don’t want to do this” and casts her orb and misses, but gets to redo it and hits, inflicting huge acid damage, blowing a chunk of his wing off.  Esylla hits it with her blast and then moves next to the shack. Escanor moves to Zephyra and heals her and then moves back away from the dock.
* ROUND SIX: Zephyra tries to recklessly lasso the dragon and is able to get the rope around one of its legs.  She then uses her movement to try to pull it to the shore - she pulls it 5’ closer.  So it is 15’ away.  It then shoots flames at Zephyra and Kenton’s shadow.  The shadow disappears, the barrel of spears blows up, and Zephyra takes half damage, going down again. It then hovers above the water and moves to the end of the dock. Therric makes sure Escanor has Zephrya covered and then hits the dragon with his crossbow. He then adrenaline rushes to give himself temp HP. He is no longer weakened. Kenton moves forward and jumps in the water to attack the dragon and misses.  As he hits the water he uses a bonus action to get under the dock. Esylla yells “be at peace” and blasts the dragon, killing it.  Lila moves forward toward the water.  Escanor heals Zephyra.
* ROUND SEVEN:  Zephyra stands up.  The vines on the dragon start to heal it and it attacks Kenton under the dock with two claws, hitting with both. Therric hears the water break, hears Kenton yell, and notices movement.  He runs to the edge of the dock and jumps on it coming down with his staff, then two unarmed strikes. He misses with all three attacks.  Kenton disengages and swims away toward the shore - yelling “the dragon is alive.”  Lila moves to the edge of the river and holds her attack.  Esylla moves to the side and can see it and blasts it again “I said be at peace, Tornia be damned” and kills it again, blowing a chunk of its face off.  Escanor moves near Esylla and casts toll the dead on it, and misses.  
* ROUND EIGHT:  Zephya swims in the water and gets to the dock that is still intact and climbs up.  She takes a spear and raises it, holding her action to attack if she sees it.  Its head starts to reform, as the fungus heals it.  It breathes a wave of energy on Therric and he fails - and is weakened.  It then tries to attack Therric twice and fumbles twice.  Therric climbs on the dock. He tries to hit twice with unarmed strikes and does with one, inflicting acid damage. Kenton swims closer to the shore, dashing. Lila casts chromatic orb and hits, inflicting acid damage, melting the head, dropping the dragon for the final time. 
Escanor tells Zephyra to go get Mollywop.  She heals herself and then takes off to town.  Therric tries to grab the rope that is still attached to the dragon to try to tie it off to the dock post.  Therric grabs a rope from Escanor and swims down to tie off another body part to try to get leverage against the current.  
Eight minutes pass and Mollywop and Zephrya appear.  She sees the destruction of the shack and dock and asks what she can do to help. We tell her what is going on.  She lifts the creature using telekensis and moves it to the shore. We ask her about the fungus and if she can heal the dragon. She says that she knows of magic - she had a former colleague - that could probably help.  As this discussion is going on, Kenton tries to grab a scale off of it, but the scale has fungus so he throws it down. 
The group asks Lila about the name that she was shouting out.  She tells everyone that she had a vision.  And Mollywop says that she knew of this dragon a couple of decades ago.  It was a young then.  Very friendly and explorative. She then tells about the vision and how it went to sleep in a tree and infected it. Escanor pushes her to message her cleric friend about helping with the dragon.  He says he can come soon and take care of the dragon. Mollywop moves the dragon back to the dock, hoping this will help with its protection.  She is going to put a ward around it until the friend can come. Escanor prays over the body.
We discuss that the swamp is where the Sapcutts lived. We decide to go back to Market Day.  We start walking back to the town. Kenton asks if there are other gold dragons around.  She responds that she thinks there is another bigger gold dragon on Inverland.  She agrees to message that dragon to see if they can help with the fallen dragon.  
WE STOP HERE with a plan to walk to Market Day (then go to Caldwynn in a day or two)
 The End of the Session –
