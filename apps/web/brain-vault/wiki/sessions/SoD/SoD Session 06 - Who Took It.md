# SoD Session 06 - Who Took It

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: SoD
- Session Number: 06
- Title: Who Took It?
- Real Date: 2026-03-27
- Header Date Note: Source header lists 03.28.26, while Session Date says March 27, 2026
- In-Game Date: 43rd-44th of Hemist, 1246
- Starting Location: Adsuren, outside Mollywop's Tower
- Ending Location: Mollywop's Tower, where Esylla finds Mollywop surrounded by guards
- Attending Characters: [[Escanor]], [[Therric]], [[Zephyra]], [[Kenton]], [[Esylla]], [[Lila]]

## Major Scenes

- The party leaves Mollywop's tower and continues investigating Ganon, the Tanner, and Adsuren Center.
- Kenton explains more of the Reyvennra/Rodolf/dwarf-torture backstory and wonders whether he is wanted for what he saw rather than for silks.
- At the Falling Fish, Escanor publicly invents an "inheritance" hook to draw out people who know Ganon.
- Lila reads Panwin's thoughts and confirms he moved the black salt barrel from the docks to the Tanner after being underpaid by Ganon.
- Dockmaster MacCannain produces the Caldwynn letter that ordered delivery of the barrel under penalty of regulation, with overpaid taxes.
- Esylla identifies a spiral watermark and notes Caldwynn's role as a shipping hub; her own family is in shipping.
- Kenton writes to House Reyvennra and sends the letter through Laudneyr from Adsuren Center.
- Adsuren Center records lead the party to Ganon's room at the Windbelly estate.
- Ganon's ritual room evidence points to storage under AC / Adsuren Center.
- Kenton breaks into Adsuren Center, steals a noble-family book and six tax-payment pouches, then breaks his tools on the cellar lock.
- Overnight, Lila dreams of Zalyas and wakes with temporary hit points.
- On the 44th of Hemist, the town discovers a major theft from Adsuren Center.
- Party members investigate separately; Lila correctly suspects Kenton.
- Kenton partially confesses to the party, Escanor frames the money as a donation problem, and Esylla donates personally to help guards.
- Esylla arrives at Mollywop's tower and sees five guards questioning Mollywop.

## Combats

- No major combat; this is an investigation, theft, fallout, and social-pressure session.

## NPCs Introduced Or Developed

- Panwin.
- Peadar MacCannain.
- Jerborin Omakian.
- Laudneyr.
- Windbelly family.
- Captain Alinia Sarralei.
- Zalyas, from Lila's dream.

## Clues Found

- Panwin confirms Ganon's link to the black salt barrel.
- The barrel delivery was routed through Caldwynn paperwork and overpaid town taxes.
- Ganon's room contains ritual evidence, rat-head remains, and instructions pointing under Adsuren Center.
- The noble-family book flags Cunningter, Idsoul, and Walgett as possible watermark matches.
- Idsoul feels familiar to Escanor and Zephyra but remains unidentified.
- Adsuren Center has a cellar hatch hidden behind overgrowth.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Kenton steals a noble-family book and tax-payment pouches.
- Kenton's thieves' tools break.
- Ganon-room incense and letter are evidence.

## Decisions Made

- Use social deception to find Ganon's contacts rather than openly accuse him.
- Send Kenton's letter to House Reyvennra.
- Search Ganon's rented room.
- Try to enter the Adsuren Center cellar at night.
- Delay or reroute blame for the stolen tax money.

## Consequences

- Adsuren's Market Day guard pay is threatened by the theft.
- Guards begin questioning people around town and Mollywop.
- Kenton's theft becomes an internal party trust problem.
- The cellar beneath Adsuren Center becomes the next clear target.

## Character Beats

- Escanor uses public trust and a fake inheritance story to draw out leads.
- Therric coaches Kenton to be kinder in the Reyvennra letter but still helps him operate at the edge of legality.
- Zephyra works through guard-facing channels and knows enough town structure to suspect an emergency underground facility.
- Kenton steals when left alone with an opportunity, even while investigating a real cult lead.
- Esylla spots the shipping/watermark importance and tries to soften the civic harm of Kenton's theft.
- Lila uses Detect Thoughts effectively and becomes the first strong internal voice that Kenton did it.
