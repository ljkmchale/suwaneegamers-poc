# SoD Session 05 - Thugs & Cultists

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: SoD
- Session Number: 05
- Title: Thugs & Cultists
- Real Date: 2026-02-21
- In-Game Date: 43rd of Hemist, 1246
- Starting Location: Adsuren
- Ending Location: Outside Mollywop's Tower after Ganon's corpse is questioned
- Attending Characters: [[Escanor]], [[Therric]], [[Zephyra]], [[Kenton]], [[Esylla]], [[Lila]]

## Major Scenes

- Lila experiences crystal visions: five figures at a grave, red-floor ritual room, ruined keep, marketplace/well, Kenton with a starry sword surrounded, and night forest with shadow/roar.
- At the Tanner, the group learns about Almadia, Redbeard, Ganon, Bulas hide, and White Horns bulas.
- Escanor senses evil around Ganon's leather; touching it releases a barb-like evil sensation.
- Kenton is ambushed by Sir Rodolf's hired thugs after Little Jimmy throws manure at him.
- Therric, Lila, and Zephyra chase the rooftop/alley fight and help save Kenton; Rodolf leaves a healing potion with the name Reyvennra.
- Escanor and Esylla confront Ganon, who uses fiendish magic and appears to need hearts for a cult ritual.
- Esylla's Detect Thoughts reveals a stone room, blood pool, dead animals, blood circle, burning heart, robed people, and a crimson leader.
- Ganon dies; the party brings body, black salt, focus, and clues to Mollywop.
- Mollywop uses Speak with Dead; Ganon names Summoner Karranok and says the rituals are in Adsuren but not in a building.
- Mollywop warns the escaped familiar/imp may know the party is on the cult's trail.
- Lila receives a Longstrider scroll from Mollywop.
- Esylla is made invisible and leaves ahead of the others.
- Therric asks Mollywop if she can contact Areana/Ariana Duskwhisper and is told she will try.

## Combats

- Kenton vs. Sir Rodolf's thugs.
- Escanor and Esylla vs. Ganon.

## NPCs Introduced Or Developed

- Ganon.
- Sir Rodolf Norran.
- Little Jimmy.
- Mollywop.

## Clues Found

- Cultists use defiled black salt and fiendish symbols.
- An imp or familiar may have been watching the Tanner situation.
- Ganon serves Summoner Karranok.
- The ritual site is in Adsuren, but Ganon says it is not in a building.
- The black salt may be used in the ritual that petrifies hearts.
- Kenton's Reyvennra/Rodolf trouble involves silks, a fire accusation, a bound/bloodied dwarf, a ring, rightful-heir language, and possible overthrow talk.

## Items Gained Or Lost

- Fiendish focus from Ganon, bent by Escanor.
- Black salt sample/barrel evidence.
- Ganon's corpse burns after Speak with Dead, apparently from a failsafe.
- Lila receives a one-use Longstrider scroll.

## Decisions Made

- Question Ganon's corpse with Mollywop rather than only reporting him.
- Bring Ganon and the fiendish evidence to Mollywop immediately.

## Consequences

- The cult investigation points deeper into Adsuren, but the exact site is still unknown.
- Ganon is confirmed as cult-connected and subordinate to Summoner Karranok.
- The party now knows a cult leader/title: Summoner Karranok.

## Character Beats

- Escanor acts as spiritual detective and destroys/bends the fiendish focus.
- Therric acts as Kenton's rescuer and tactical pursuer during the ambush.
- Zephyra chases public violence as a watch-adjacent protector.
- Kenton survives a targeted ambush, reveals parts of his Gibuldon trouble, and creates a new robbery problem.
- Esylla uses Detect Thoughts and noble/shipping context to connect occult and trade clues.
- Lila's crystal visions become an explicit campaign compass.
