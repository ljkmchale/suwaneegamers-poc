# Session 13 - Cockroach in the Keep

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  August 16, 2026
In-game Date:  1st of Baegrum, 1246
Starting Location:  Scyldgar Keep 
Inside Scyldgar Keep
Zephyra walks into the stables and sees the smoky, red figure of a horse staring at her. She is still raging.  She walks up to the horse (it is a war horse) and notices it is translucent, but seems to be acting normally and is saddled. She looks for signs of anyone else here and finds nothing. 
Therric, Escanor, and Esylla make their way to the stables.  Zephyra makes her way out and briefs them on what she saw.  Esylla tells Lila what Zephyra said and let her know that we are going into the stables.  We walk in.  Therric checks out the stalls and walks up to pet the horse and it doesn’t go well.  He pulls out rations to feed it and tries to grab the reins and the horse tries to bite him again.  Escanor thinks he seems normal, despite his appearance.  Therric gives him water in a bucket and the horse drinks from the pail, but reluctantly, then disappears.  He reaches to touch the horse and feels nothing.  But he sees a small mahogany whistle in the bucket.   Therric pockets the whistle. Esylla lets Lila know there doesn’t seem to be any danger now.  
On the roof, Kenton and Lila look in the chest. They see a golden stick.  If they hold it straight up, it looks tree-shaped.  There are gems on each of the branches.  Kenton and Lila both check it out - trying to determine if this is a scepter or a weapon or what.  This doesn’t feel sturdy enough to be a weapon.  They discuss this and may offer it to Therric in trade for the horse.
The group meets in the center courtyard. Esylla comments how disappointed she is to have lost the trail of her uncle.  Escanor heals Zephyra.  Kenton shows the group the “stick” they found upstairs and Esylla offers to identify it.  While she does that, Therric tries to check out the bodies and finds nothing so Kenton checks as well as finds 2sp. Zephyra decides to check out the roof -specifically looking behind the keep for river access.  She notices that there is a dock off the back of the keep.
Esylla realizes this is a magical wand.  She describes that the properties are somewhat left up to the fates.  The group decides to give the wand to Lila. 
Discussion about the dock and the need to check it out to see if there are recent signs of use.  We ask Therric if there is anything else we need to check out based on his dreams.  And ask Lila as well. Neither knows of a reason to stay, outside of the dock. Therric decides to scout ahead (not in stealth) as the group follows - we are going on the right side of the building. We come to double door and Therric hears something behind the doors making an “eating” sound.  Therric and Escanor are in the front, followed by Kenton and Zephyra, then Esylla and Lila in the back. 
Therric opens the double door and sees it goes into a hallway and a door is on the right, there is a door broken on the left, in addition to other doors.  The hallway ends with stairs.  He hears the noise coming from the right. He stealths ahead and sees a dining room/feasting hall.  He sees a shadowy figure sitting on the table eating.  He comes back to the group and tells Escanor there may be death in this room. Escanor comments that we need to lay them to rest.  Therric then stealths around to the other side to get a view from another place.  He notices that the creature is eating a bone.  He motions for Escanor to enter and bring the group.  When Escanor steps in, the creature immediately notices him.  IT IS INITIATIVE as the ghoulish figure turns to attack.
* ROUND ONE:   Kenton creates a mote then steps into the room and through the broken door and to the side to hide. Therric, still in stealth, steps closer to the table, but against a corner wall, holding his action to dash to combat if melee combat starts.  The creature dashes forward to Escanor.  Therric then runs to Escanor in reaction.  As two other creatures emerge from the other side of the room and sah to behind Therric.  Zephyra moves forward to attack the original guy on the table with her sword and hits. She then attacks again and fumbles, hitting Escanor instead. Esylla moves up 5’ and shoots her blast, hitting with one beam and missing with one beam.  Lila then uses her burst to hit with lightning damage. Escanor feels the rotting stench from the creature in front of him and has to make a CON save, and does.  The creature smells horrendous.  His amulet begins to glow and he takes aim at the undead in the room - and all 3 creatures must make saving throws.  Two that fail take radiant damage and are incapacitated and frightened.  The one who saves is still ok. 
* ROUND TWO:  Kenton steps out of the hallway and uses his whip and fumbles, having the whip catch on the ceiling tiles in a very embarrassing hit, and loses his reaction and steps into the hall again. Therric has to make a CON save and seems to be good from the smell.  He crits on the initial creature, killing him by sweeping the legs, breaking them off.  Therric tries to hit one of the remaining ones with his elbow, but misses.  The creatures move away from Escanor in fright on their turn.  Zephyra moves up to attack the one that Therric misses with her sword and misses twice.  Esylla moves up and shoots her blast at one of the creatures and hits, killing it.  Lila then moves forward to attack one her burst, killing it.
Into the Undercroft
Escanor is not able to determine what type of undead these are. We decide to descend the stairs, Zephyra first.  We proceed down a hall and see an alcove with a statuette in it that appears to be some type of religious representative.  We continue forward, bypassing the statue. Therric asks Escanor to check out the statue.  Zephyra looks out the window and sees water almost at the window height.  She continues to follow the hallway and Therric yells for her to hold up.  Escanor checks out the statue and realizes this was to Goldraen.  Therric shows the statue what he has crafted.  Lila pulls out something she has made and leaves it on the altar. Escanor tells him not to steal from a god’s altar. We continue forward and see double doors and a small alcove with double doors.  Zephyra and Therric both listen at the doors.  Zephyr hears nothing.  Therric hears there is a big room.  Escanor sees writing on the door, but can’t read it.  Esylla tells him (since she can) that this is the family crypt.  Escanor says we should look, but that we should not disturb the dead.
Therric opens the door.  This is a large square room.  There is a hall on the far left and right that both ends in doors. We see sixteen undisturbed sarcophagi in the room.  There are cracks in the stone work, but otherwise these seem to be ok.  Escanor says a prayer over the dead.  We decide to check out the door on the left hallway.  It opens into a chamber with a stone sarcophagus and water that is trickling down the back wall.  This is a crypt of the Lord and Lady, with wording that states as much.  Escanor touches the water.  It is cold, but he thinks it is holy water.  He empties a water skin and fills it up with holy water.  We then leave this room and go to the room on the right.  This opens into a short hallway with a spiral staircase that goes up.  We decide to go back to the hallway to the other set of double doors, as Escanor listens to see if he can hear anything up the stairs.  
We open the double doors out in the hallway and see this is a priest’s bedroom.  There is a desk with a tome. This is an Amriel holy book. Escanor sees that this is a sign and takes the book.  Esylla opens the wardrobe in the room. There are a few priest robes.  She asks Escanor to come over and take a look to see if there is anything he needs.  We leave the room and go down the hallway. We find double doors further down the hallway.  We walk in and this is a large, half-octagonal room with rotting crates.  This is a storage room, nothing of value or importance, but she grabs a set of mason’s tools and carpenter’s tools (party inventory).  We set another set of doors and Zephyra listens and hears water.  She opens the doors and sees a dock that is covered.  
Meanwhile, Kenton casts light in the room and dumps out his water skin and fills his with holy water.  Once this happens, the water is no longer flowing.  He then decides to check out to see if anything is valuable in the room.  He can’t see anything of value to start, but decides to desecrate the sarcophagus. He uses a crowbar and is able to pry the lid off.  He sees a body under a sheet, but only one.  He starts to raise the sheet and looks around - he notices a special ring on one of the skeletal fingers.  He casts a shadow mote and has the mote attempt to remove the ring.  The mote disappears as a hand hits it when it takes the ring.  IT IS INITIATIVE for Kenton versus the mummified corpse.
* ROUND ONE:   The skeletal figure sits up and looks at Kenton with glowing eyes and has to make a WIS saving throw.  He is frightened as he is filled with dread and moves toward Kenton and crits, imposing bludgeoning damage and necrotic damage (uncanny dodge and he takes 18 points of damage).  He is cursed and can’t regain hit points right now. It then hits again and tries to grapple him and does.  Kenton screams like a girl and then summons a mote in the hall behind him and switches places with the mote and runs 30’ away. 
* ROUND TWO:  The mote is destroyed by the Lady and then closes the door to the crypt.  Kenton is able to get away.
The group is out on the dock and Escanor and Zephyra look to see if there are any signs of recent use.  There are rotted boards, old ropes.  No signs of blood.  
We head back inside and to the room with the spiral stairs and collide with Kenton who is trying to run away.  He yells that he was getting water and the sarcophagus opened and a “she-dead” came out of it.  Escanor rubs his head in disappointment.  Kenton pulls out his healing potion and drinks it but nothing happens - he says “it didn’t work.”  Escanor checks out the vial, then performs a medicine check on Kenton. We notice that the door is closed and decide to leave the crypt be.
We proceed to look around. Therric whispers to Kenton “how nice was the loot” and Kenton says “it was nice.”  And the group continues.  Zephyra finds a hidden door that leads to the outside courtyard. 
Into the Chapel
Escanor goes to the chapel and hears a “drip, drip.”  He opens the doors.  There are three rows and a small balcony and a dried fountain.  There is a back wall with 3 panes of stained glass that are 10-sided:  there is an angel with chained demons, priests praying, Amriel falling.  He puts a flask of holy water in the fountain and says a prayer to Amriel.  Therric asks him if this place is abandoned or if there is something wrong with it.  Escanor tells him about the book he has from the priest. As he pours the water, he sees an inscription in the fountain.  He then puts another flask in the fountain.  The sconces start to light up in the chapel and in the courtyard.  He reads the inscription:  “As water purifies the flesh, so too may fate redeem the soul.”  He is praying hard and wants to make sure the chapel is consecrated.  Therric looks around to make sure everything is okay and asks Kenton to investigate.  He finds that there are areas of disrepair.  Lila, Esylla and Therric start to mend and clean as best they can.  Escanor feels that he will need to come back here in the future to bring this place fully to life.  He notices a ghostly image (a statue) of Amriel in the balcony.  He thanks him for his redemption and asks for forgiveness for Kenton. 
We leave to go up the stairs to the second floor.  As we walk, Kenton gives Escanor his water skin of holy water, which he dumps into the fountain in the chapel.  Therrric decides to scout ahead with Zephyra following behind.  At the top of the stairs, Therric pauses - to the left is the chapel, there are doors ahead, and a set of double doors to the right.  He asks Zephyra to check out the chapel.  She goes that way and opens the doors and finds the balcony - she sees the statue and a cloth mound. Escanor follows behind.  Zephyr doesn’t really see anything as she investigates.  Escanor looks at the statue and sees a mace sitting on the statue, but it isn’t a part of it.  He feels the mace, which is warm to the touch.  He picks it up and feels like he was supposed to have it.  When he picks it up, the torch lights go out.  He puts the mace in his inventory. He picks up the cloth and sees a skeletal body on the ground.  He tries to pick up the body and thinks this may be the priest.  He takes it to the altar of the chapel.  Esylla offers to examine the mace while he takes the body to the altar below. Esylla tells him it is a Mace of Disruption.  
The Lord’s Planning Quarters
Zephyra opens the door that is at the top of the stairs.  This room has a glass ceiling.  There is a table in the center with a parchment on top of the table.  This looks to be a map of Inverland with lines that shows segments of the land.  There is a person at the back of the room.  He says “are you for real?”  This is Ifryn.
Esylla calls out to her uncle.  She asks him to surrender.  While he seemed strong and vibrant in the city at Market Day, he is no longer wearing any of the same items, is pale and bleeding, missing part of his arm.  He is bandaged. She tells him we can heal him.  Zephyra says he better stand down.  Esylla asks if he will stand down and he replies that he is in no position to fight.  Escanor also speaks up saying that he hurt Mollywop so that she can no longer fight either. Escanor performs a medicine check.  He is in bad shape.
He says that a group called the Crimson Court paid him to kill Mollywop.  He was offered 35,000gp.  He was told to wait here for the drop - there was going to be a boat arriving.  He stayed up all night waiting and they never came.  He says he bought the poison in Dha from an underground market.  We ask his future plans and he says that he hadn’t come up with that yet.  
Escanor preaches to him - that being an assassin is not a good practice.  He says that Mollywop had a lot of enemies.  He says he took the job because it was a challenge.  
Esylla has a memory…of being a child.  There is a fairy tale of a princess who defeats a bad guy.  She hears from a cousin about Ifryn the Disgraced (or the cockroach).  That he forsake his gift and was a stain on the family bloodline.  He was worse than a rat, a weak man.  Cousins want to see what he looks like.  As a child, she goes to find him to see him for herself.  He goes into a garden and runs into a tall man with short hair with black tunic.  He apologizes and moves on.  She continues to look for this guy and doesn’t find him. 
Back to the present:  Esylla tells him that he lived up to the predictions about him.  Escanor talks to him about redemption.  He says that he could be something other than an assassin.  He says that the gift in the family wouldn’t work with him - his body rejects magic.  He says he has no remorse.  He holds up his right hand and shows he is missing a finger.  He got the sword (that Zephyra now has) from a crypt in Eustera Ridge - he had heard that there was a weapon there to kill a mage.  This was a crypt for Jenku the Black.  A few in the group have heard of this powerful wizard who lived 300-400 years ago - he was an evil necromancer (the first in Inverland). Esylla asks about the weapon that Zephyra now wields.  
Esylla asks what his ideal situation here is - he says that he would like for us to let him go.  He says he needs to seek healing and would like to follow up with those who hired him. Escanor comments that those who paid him are the actual killers of Mollywop.  Esylla asks how he would go about finding those who hired him and he said that he would contact those in the underground, but doesn’t want to give up all of his plans and contacts.  Escanor realizes that he doesn’t feel bad for killing Mollywop or the townspeople.  He shakes his head.   Esylla asks if there is anything he would like to tell anyone in the family and he counters with is there anything you’d like for me to take back to the estate.
* ROUND ONE (again): Esylla casts blade ward on herself and hexes him.  She moves over to the side.  He runs behinds her and crits on his attack, knocking her down unconscious.  He then kicks Escanor and knocks him to the side.  A wave of bright light emits from Escanor and heals Esylla.  He then moves next to Esylla.  Lila moves 30’ away from the group and casts mirror image on herself (3 other Lilas appear).  Kenton casts a shadow mote and runs out of the room. Therric casts elemental attunement and casts hunters mark. He then jumps on the table and makes an unarmed attack and misses. Zephyra rages and attacks with the sword and misses, but he takes 3 points of damage from the miss.  She then attacks and hits for 18 points of damage.  He then disappears from her line of sight and reappears behind Zephyra.
* ROUND TWO:  Esylla regains consciousness at the end of her turn.  He then tries to attack Zephyra with his backhand and hits.  Then tries to slam her into the ground and she takes additional damage.  He then disengages and gets on the table behind Therric.  Escanor heals Zephyra and takes the dodge action.  Lila uses innate sorcery to cast guiding bolt and crits for 37 points of damage - he looks stunned as blood sprays from his mouth as he takes the hit.  The next attacker gets advantage.  Kenton steps into the room and throws a dagger and hits for 8 points of damage plus sneak attack of 7 points.  He then steps back into the hall. He moves the mote onto the table. Therric turns around and attacks him with his staff and hits with force and bludgeoning damage.  He then tries to knee him in the groin and hits, inflicting 9 points of bludgeoning damage. Zephyra jumps on the table and attacks him and hits twice, inflicting good damage.  She then jumps off the table.
* ROUND THREE: Esylla stands up and shoots her blast at him, hitting once, saying “uncle - it didn’t have to be like this.”  At the end of his turn, he is no longer stunned.  Escanor runs over and touches Zephyra’s blade with a spell, giving her advantage on one roll.  Lila casts guiding bolt again and hits, doing damage and imposing advantage for the next attacker.  At the end of her turn, Ifryn misty steps behind Lila.  Therric moves closer to Infryn and crits with his attack, pulling him 10’ closer to him and imposing lightning damage. He then uses hus furry of blows to attack again, unarmed strike and hits with 11 points of bludgeoning damage.  Then hits again with 10 more points of damage. Kenton moves into the room and throws a dagger at him and misses.  Then runs back outside.  He then moves the mote near him.  Zephyra hits him and then misses.
* ROUND FOUR:  Esylla moves forward and jumps on the table and casts her blast, hitting then fumbling, losing her reaction.  He then kicks Therric, critting him and knocking him back against the table where he takes bludgeoning damage twice and he drops hunters mark.  He then attacks Zephyra and hits her and knocks her against the wall.  He then moves behind Therric next to the table. Escanor heals Esylla and moves up 5’ and casts a spell on the assassin and he saves.  He then pops behind Escanor.  Lila then casts guiding bolt again and hits with advantage, killing him blowing a hole in his chest.  He looks up at Esylla and says “ask my sister about Kasi Um’cata” and throws something at Esylla.
Esylla looks down at what she caught.  It is a scroll case. There is a scroll inside (“a study on Infryn”).  She reads it.  No one has heard of Kasi Um’cata other than Escanor - he thinks she might be a famous scribe. She loots his body.  He has a tunic, boots, and pants.  Nothing magical.  She asks Escanor to say one last prayer over him from Amriel.  Therric picks him up and takes him outside to remove the evil from the keep and puts his body outside.
