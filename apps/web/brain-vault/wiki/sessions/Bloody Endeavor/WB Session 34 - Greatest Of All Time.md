# WB Session 34 - Greatest Of All Time

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 34
- Title: Greatest Of All Time
- Real Date: March 23, 2026
- In-Game Date: 21st of Paramor, 1246
- Starting Location: Drow right tower, 3rd floor
- Ending Location: Khagdaruhl (Ring of Respect, mid-fight with Sacred Crows)

## Major Scenes

- Top floor of right tower: **Madam Mizna Er'Arachna** — regal female drow in violet pancho, male drow guard. Male guard killed; Madam Mizna escorted out. She reveals a hidden trap door with a large Amethyst — an illusion gem that creates the fog/darkness protecting the drow outpost from sunlight.
- Lucerion smashes the Amethyst, removing most of the fog. Sacred Crows found and freed the blind goat — **Bandit the Blind Brown Braided Goat**.
- Drow towers stand in a huge crater between the mountains. Long rest inside a tower.
- Discussion with Hefeic Icehelm: the Icehelm clan is from the mountains north of the Rostiena Foothills. He thinks a larger dwarven fortress was once in this area.
- Depart for Khagdaruhl. Albross asks about the war; Armaros believes it may be the rising of an apocalypse.
- Meet Ambassador Udohi Greythane and dwarves on the path — they appear out of thin air (Duergar using invisibility). Party heads covered; led into Khagdaruhl.
- **Khagdaruhl**: large cavern, lava light, gemstone walls. Mixed dwarves (55% Duergar, 35% Mountain, 10% Hill). Sacred Crows part ways at a fork.
- Ambassador reveals: the monks are in league with the dwarves; years ago, dwarves and monks used the Rimestone to freeze the Sierra and seal an eldritch evil. The Awakening disrupted the dwarven kingdom. Huldi (the Cold Breath) took Rimestone pieces and sought all three.
- To gain audience with Lord Emperor, party must fight in the **Ring of Respect**.
- **25th of Paramor**: wait a day. **Ring of Respect day** — over 100 dwarves watching. Notable factions observed: black-uniformed Dalasor Coast delegates (knight, priest, wizard), Monks of Iohir scattered in the crowd, six robed figures (5 short, 1 human-sized, faces in shadow) seated near the Emperor.
- Fight 1: Wyrm Bane vs. Hellhounds — won in 2 rounds.
- Fight 2: Wyrm Bane vs. **Zhis'ta the Purple** (half-drow/half-spider champion) — won.
- Fight 3: The invisible wall drops — **Wyrm Bane must fight the Sacred Crows.** Session ends before combat.

## NPCs Introduced

- Madam Mizna Er'Arachna — regal drow of the outpost, escorted out safely.
- High Justice Flintfall — jester/announcer of the Ring of Respect.
- Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV — black-skinned Duergar, white beard with rings braided in, black gem-studded crown; seen at distance.
- Zhis'ta the Purple — half-drow/half-spider Ring champion; defeated.
- Ambassador Nym Perxidor — chatty Dalasor Coast ambassador.
- Grenthren Moltenfist — master armorsmith in Khagdaruhl's smithing district; commissioned Lucerion's new Natafae platemail for 2,000 gp (ready ~30th of Paramor).
