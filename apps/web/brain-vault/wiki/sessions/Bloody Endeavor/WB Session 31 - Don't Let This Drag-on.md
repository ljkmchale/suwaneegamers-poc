# WB Session 31 - Don't Let This Drag-on

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 31
- Title: Don't Let This Drag-on
- Real Date: February 3, 2026
- In-Game Date: 13th of Paramor, 1246
- Starting Location: Vanapur Mountains
- Ending Location: Fort Mornock (sarcophagus room)

## Major Scenes

- Pagern has a map (elven inscriptions: "Dwarven treasure, lots of it... enough to build your own tower"). Three-peaked mountain range. Rhody finds a stair-like path under snow; dark stone dwarven statues at the base with cylindrical objects.
- **Fort Mornock** — "Fortress of the Flayer." Tomb of **Mornock the Flayer**, dwarven warlord said to have killed 1,000 orcs single-handedly.
- Large cave entrance (40 feet wide), two iron doors inside. Rope from a stalagmite descending the peak; two explorer packs and ice picks.
- Notes from dead adventurers scattered through the dungeon — from an expedition including Edwyrd the Everstance (apprentice of the Celadon Wizard), Binsley (scholar), Charmi, Guntolpho, Jamrere.
- Grand hall: dwarven pillar statues hold lanterns (lit when party enters). Doors close behind them. Large copper door with silver axe inlaid.
- **Golem fight** — a dwarf golem defends the hall (killed by Lucerion).
- Left hallway: ice wall 30 feet down.
- Right hallway: ceiling-trap kills two previous explorers. Trap disabled (Lucerion takes damage). Right path: stairs and rooms. Barracks room with corpses: thieves' tools, 10 gems at 5 gp each, secret door to a small chest (2 red vials + one axe-head piece).
- War-room: map showing Krorhear, Kag'daruhl, and Iohere. Animated armor fight (2 suits). Won; took the map.
- Sleeping quarters: dead Rubyguard dwarf. Note: "I did not tell them where the Rimestone is... my king." Ruby-encrusted circlet (200 gp); ceremonial axe (30 gp, Caelion takes).
- Break through ice wall; descend to mess hall. **Presagiath bursts through the ice wall: "I remember you!"**
- **Combat vs. Presagiath (Round 1-2)**: Dragon marks Rhody, claws and tails. Rhody's Hunter's Mark; Lucerion smites. Presagiath scrapes ice for cold AoE. Pagern casts Fireball. Albross casts Psychic Lance. Round 2: Presagiath uses Cone of Ice — drops Rhody and Albross. Lucerion heals both. Party heals and retreats to the tomb area.
- **Chasm room**: chains hold a platform with a Brault statue. Hum; fire in its eyes. **Combat vs. Spirit (Aberration)** — Pagern's Fireball, Caelion's Spirit Guardians, party attacks. Spirit defeated quickly.
- Doors swing open into an **octagonal burial chamber**: copper chandelier, dwarven decor, sarcophagus in the center. Pagern casts Knock on sarcophagus — doors close, separating Pagern and Albross outside. Session ends.

## Lore

- The Rubyguard protected the location of the Rimestone in this dungeon. The dying dwarf's note implies the Rimestone's hiding place was NOT revealed even under extreme duress.
- The Celadon Wizard was supposedly an "old friend" of Mornock and had a map leading here — and a large X on the map.
