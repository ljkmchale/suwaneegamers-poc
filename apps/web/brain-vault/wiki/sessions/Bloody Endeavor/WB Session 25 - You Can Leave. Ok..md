# WB Session 25 - You Can Leave. Ok.

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 25
- Title: You Can Leave. Ok.
- Real Date: September 22 (2025)
- In-Game Date: 46th of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Yag'vur Sierra (recovering from Presagiath)
- Ending Location: Hol'gardur

## Major Scenes

- Recovering from the near-wipe. All mounts dead but one donkey. Rhody's goat Nutmeg/Gertie is dead.
- Camping away from the dragon grounds. Donkey survives.
- Nutmeg arrives with a dead deer on its back (at Hol'gardur).
- Young Runegrip dwarves **Crabold and Ondus Runegrip** meet the party, lead them to **Hol'gardur** — a fortified village at the foot of the first Yaggermon peak (Fyrstholith).
- **Fomeck Runegrip** — elder of Hol'gardur, tattooed old dwarf with a walking-stick hammer.
- Runegrip Clans at Fyrstholith: Hol'gardur (foot), Hol'kulrum (large city, destroyed by giants), Hol'durog (highest community).
- Coldbreath is frosting the crops — same plague affecting Rhody's farm.
- Spirit of the woods: **Sennogam/Sinn'ogin** (Sinnogam). Local druid Runeeyes usually communes with it, requires a trial.
- **Groretta Runeeyes** — druid with tattooed eyes. Knows of the fighting trial (strength and wisdom).
- Advents of Harmony (monks) came through Hol'gardur 16 days ago.
- **Rollgood Runehearth** at Runestout Inne.
- Rhody undergoes the **Three-Stage Trial** of Sennogam:
  - Stage 1: Ghoulish undead dwarves in a dream state.
  - Stage 2: Cross to a dwarf at a table; drinking contest (multiple party members poisoned).
  - Final: "Winnie the Poo" honey bear creature — Claws, Honey radiant damage, Honey breath weapon.
- **Rhody is marked** by Sennogam's spirit. Rune of Sennogin received.
- Presagiath confirmed as a nearby predator ("Cold Breath" of the mountains).

## NPCs Introduced

- Crabold Runegrip and Ondus Runegrip — young dwarf cousins.
- Fomeck Runegrip — elder of Hol'gardur.
- Groretta Runeeyes — druid, tattooed eyes.
- Rollgood Runehearth — dwarf at Runestout Inne.
