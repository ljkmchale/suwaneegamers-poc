- Royal Lounge: 3 monks resting — fight them. 4× Greater Healing potions found. An overlook to a training ground opens to a Royal Chamber.
- Baldric touches the ground to sense what is behind the kitchen door.
- **Lucerion opens the armory and opens a void.** A terrible creature appears — "this ugly fuck." Fight in the kitchen; people get eaten; teleported to the slime dimension.
- Party follows stairs past a portcullis (cold). Very locked door; path full of traps.
- Enter a large room of ice with many normal dwarf statues on a wall.
- Beyond a large room: a man holding a part of a sphere. He's large and "aura farming."
- **All party members paralyzed.**
- **Fight The Coldbreath (Huldi, the boss).**
- **Bakah T'luk falls.**
- Session ends mid-fight.
