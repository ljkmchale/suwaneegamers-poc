# WB Session 28 - Better off Dead

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 28
- Title: Better off Dead
- Real Date: December 8 (2025)
- In-Game Date: 3rd of Paramor, 1246 (starts); 7th–10th of Paramor
- Starting Location: Back at Hol'gardur (made it down the mountain)
- Ending Location: Madam Mabel's apple orchard

## Major Scenes

- **Bakah T'luk died to The Cold Breath.** Party made it down the mountain.
- Harhet Steelfoot: the 2nd Rimestone piece may be in Khagdaruhl; marks it on the map. Third piece location unknown.
- Fomeck Runegrip (ruler of Hol'gardur) honors Bakah.
- **Bakah's memorial cairn built by the Runegrip dwarves.** Dwarves fight over scraps of Bakah's belongings.
- **7th of Paramor:** Party sees a gnome with wolves attacking him. Party saves the gnome — **Pagern Stonebuckle** (gnome wizard, apprentice of the Celadon Wizard). Pagern joins the party.
- Sending spell attempted — went to the wrong person. ("Don't give up on sending, just a shotty memory.")
- **10th of Paramor:** Rhody leads them astray (an illusion spell). Albross dispels magic to see clearly. Party travels through illusioned woods; some see a waterfall where someone is bathing (Rhody does not notice / it glosses over his mind).
- Party arrives at a large clearing with an **apple orchard** — **Madam Mabel's** home.
- Madam Mabel: elderly woman (~80 years old), puffy short hair, long puffy dress. Removes Rhody's curse/infection in exchange for: bags (unopened), a body part from each of three locals, and a ruby that they keep.
- She gives Rhody **Mabel's Monocle** (Darkvision 150', See Invisibility).
- Party walks through a tree portal to reach the hags' location.

## NPCs Introduced

- Pagern Stonebuckle — gnome wizard, new party member.
- Madam Mabel — elderly fey witch with an apple orchard; Rhody has a pact with her.
