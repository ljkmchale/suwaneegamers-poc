# WB Session 05 - The Half Orc

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 05
- Title: The Half Orc
- Real Date: December 9 (2024)
- In-Game Date: Unknown
- Starting Location: Climbor
- Ending Location: Cave in the woods

## Major Scenes

- Party rests at a bar after previous events; learns to blow horns.
- Sneaks out of the Caifir estate past two guards.
- Meet **Scipio Grudshnook** — lighter green half-orc with constellation tattoos, mace with an upside-down skull (real skull, not a child's). Member of Clan of the Bear Killer. He hunts monsters and gets paid in bones.
- Tablet found with the phrase "May the cold breath freeze all." References: Colbath (funding is drying up), Ravi (to gather more), Kana (sending Ravi). First mention of the Cold Breath.
- A monster killed a bear in the woods; the bear's boneless, headless body was found draped in a tree.
- Something flew overhead — large, moving fast, in the other direction.
- Party finds a cave. Rogue (Longtoes with the owl) sent in to scout.
- **Chimera attacks** the rogue/Longtoes — owl is gone, Bakah goes down.
- Loot: 57 cp, 71 sp, 21 gp (party split).

## NPCs Introduced

- Scipio Grudshnook — half-orc monster hunter, Clan of the Bear Killer. Eventually joins party temporarily.

## Clues

- Tablet inscription: *"May the cold breath freeze all"* — first Cold Breath reference.
- Colbath funding referenced — connects to Colbath's Keep.
