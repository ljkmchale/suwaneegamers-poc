# WB Session 30 - Let's Break A Deal!

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 30
- Title: Let's Break A Deal!
- Real Date: January 26, 2026
- In-Game Date: 10th of Paramor, 1246
- Starting Location: Madam Mabel's orchard
- Ending Location: Camp south of Mabel's orchard / heading to Fort Mornock

## Major Scenes

- **Albross was left behind at the tree portal.** He rests under a tree with Madam Mabel. She is clever and seems confused by the situation.
- Albross sees a chestnut horse with flowers in its mane — it stands bipedal and transforms: six wings, large white form, four arms, halo, calming feeling. It's **Albross's patron, The Holy Light**.
- The Holy Light's warning: Albross is shown a vision — his brother Breemos is hurt, wounded, missing fingers, with a green hand twisting a black knife in his shoulder. "I have looked forward a harmon into the future. This is the fate that beholds your brother if you do not aid him."
- Additional visions: the party fighting the hags (current events), the Cold Breath victorious, the Dwarven King of Khagdaruhl bound to his throne by tendrils.
- Albross wakes; the burn mark of where The Holy Light stood is in the grass.
- **Party emerges through Viola's tree portal**, about 100 feet from Albross.
- Brief debate about betraying Viola — Pagern secretly messages Mabel that they're attacking.
- **Six-round fight vs. Viola the hag:**
  - Viola uses acid blobs, a staff, cursing, and spawns evil myconids.
  - **Lucerion loses his right arm** in Round 6 when Viola lands a Critical Hit with acid.
  - Viola is killed by Lucerion in Round 5, regenerates, finally slain by Pagern's Firebolt.
- **Lucerion's arm grows back red** — the color of Natafae's domain. His Lay on Hands seals the wound.
- During the fight, Rhody accidentally strikes Lucerion; a flashback hits: Rhody's farm, his wife, his goat, a toy on the floor, his child's place setting — then back to battle.
- Madam Mabel left the scene mid-fight and goes back to her cottage to bake sour apple pie. She is disappointed.
- Rhody negotiates with Mabel and pays her in gemstones. Exchange complete. Mabel creates a green energy ball from a 5,000 gp ruby — she eats it and transforms from ~80 years old to her 30s.
- Albross shares his patron's visions with the party.
