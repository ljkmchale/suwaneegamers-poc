# WB Session 22 - They Have a Pact!

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 22
- Title: They Have a Pact!
- Real Date: August 11 (2025)
- In-Game Date: 29th of Talil (starts) through 32nd of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Witguard
- Ending Location: Climbor (Drunk Doves inn)

## Major Scenes

- Pick up chasing Aishka the Braizen. Lucerion takes Aishka's tabard and lets him go; Aishka is heading to a temple north of Witguard.
- **Travel to Climbor en route to Khagdaruhl.**
- Party discovers Rhody and Albross both have pacts (nature not elaborated).
- Camp set — Rhody falls asleep during his watch.
- Saildreith has set up a timber post on the road (marker).
- **32nd of Talil: slavers attack the camp at night.** Two archers, one spellcaster. Pale elves in black and white with little horns. Bakah knocked unconscious and stabbed in the neck. Spellcaster says "He may have the information we need" (referring to Lucerion). Barely survives.
- **Rhody's hand turns black** — swollen, something moving under the skin. The hand he used to grab the Paragon Tear amulet.
- Arrive in Climbor. Body dropped at the **Cathedral of Utheri**.
- At the Cathedral: the pale elves are not from Myrdae, potentially not from this plane. They use divine and fey power. The god of the elves interrupted any attempt to speak with their dead.
- Lady Coursepetal met at the Drunk Doves. She offers the party access to the Hole of Climbor again.
- Cafir tried to cover tracks re: missing noble.
- Battlebeard defended Cafir.
- Sold slaver armor and bows (14 gp held for Bakah; later received).

## Key Revelations

- The pale-elf slavers are **not from Myrdae** and may not be from this plane of existence. They have divine and fey power.
- Rhody's hand infection: from grabbing the Paragon Tear amulet bare-handed.
