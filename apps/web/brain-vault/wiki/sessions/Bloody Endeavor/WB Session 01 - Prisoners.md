## Major Scenes

- Party meets as slaves at the garrison/prison Alasfolen.
- "The Mistress" Mylaerla (pale elf female) and Shadow Guard Aurn (burned face pale elf) run the facility.
- 19 elf guards total. Stuff stored in a guard tower at the southwest near the gate.
- Party meets fellow slaves: Sitmus Runegrip (male dwarf, his tribe sent him to find help), Lagakh Morn (female orc, worships The One-Eye), Oskard (male bugbear, loner/wood hermit), Azer Launce (male human, guard from Gevakaln), Halynor Caifir (female half-elf, noble's daughter from Climbor), Lyeneru Olokas (female wood elf, speaks a strange language).
