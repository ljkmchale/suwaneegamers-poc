# WB Session 29 - Hags with Bags

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 29
- Title: Hags with Bags
- Real Date: January 12, 2026
- In-Game Date: 10th of Paramor, 1246
- Starting Location: Madam Mabel's orchard (tree portal)
- Ending Location: Mine chamber (with hag Viola)

## Major Scenes

- Through Madam Mabel's tree portal — arrives at the southern tip of the Matahal Valley, eastern side of the Vanapur Mountains near hamlet Lensloh.
- An elf bumps into Pagern: "You need to run from the Thunderspears. There are six of them. Their wolves have my scent." The Thunderspears are a clan from the orc fort they fought near Witguard (Sharpmire's clan).
- **Combat vs. Thunderspear Orcs** (6 rounds) — shaman says "Victory for Sharpmire" and tries to kill himself; only blinds himself. Pagern kills the shaman. Elf had been a commoner from Antissi (between Verallein and Limslou). Party takes full-plate from orc leader.
- Northwest: entrance to a mine. Green gas (pungent, acrid). A pool with a voice in elvish/dwarvish/common: "None shall pass." Pay gold tribute: "You shall pass." Follow tunnel to the mined-out interior.
- Three **Redcaps** fighting at a table with a toad. Rhody approaches; acts strange (DID behavior). Two Redcaps attack one; Rhody fights; **Combat vs. Redcaps** (3 rounds). Toad killed; Rhody digs out hair from the toad's goo.
- Path leads to a large chamber with mine rails and three women around a mine cart:
  - Noble-looking lady, female dwarf (hairy, sleeveless), human with hood.
  - Bags hanging from them. The mine cart is on a turntable.
- Lucerion attacks when the human lady threatens his homeland. **All three hags killed.**
- Liquids in the mine cart: chalky white, gray, pink, light brown & blue, dark brown & blue, two red.
- **Viola the large hag** arrives — much bigger, less attractive. She has the ruby Mabel wants. Refuses to give it. She proposes a deal: she will follow the party into Myrdae, confront Madam Mabel, care for Rhody's family, care for the orchard, and give Rhody his fey powers — in exchange for the party's help defeating Mabel.

## NPCs Introduced

- Viola — large blue hag. Sister to the three killed hags. Has the ruby and a proposal.

## Clues

- Two blade shards now in party inventory (Caelion carries both in the Bag of Holding): one from the orc fort (Session 15), one from the hags here.
