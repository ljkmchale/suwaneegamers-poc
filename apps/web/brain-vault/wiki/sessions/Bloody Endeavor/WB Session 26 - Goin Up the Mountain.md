# WB Session 26 - Goin' Up the Mountain

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 26
- Title: Goin' Up the Mountain
- Real Date: October 27 (2025)
- In-Game Date: 1st of Paramor, 1246
- Starting Location: Hol'gardur
- Ending Location: Hol'durog (short rest after battle)

## Major Scenes

- **Runegrip clan crops are affected by the same cold plague as Rhody's farm.** The monks came through and the dwarves unknowingly sent them up the mountain.
- Rhody leads the ascent up Fyrstholith; spirits aid him.
- A giant with ice nunchukas hunts an elk in a clearing — the elk is frozen dead by some kind of "creature effect."
- Rhody controls elements and can speak with trees after being marked.
- **Hol'kulrum**: a ruined clearing. Party rests in a general store. Giants stomp around outside. A fort of giants in the town; trophy ice of dwarves in the center. Party flees like cowards.
- A zombie giant with an ice-cicle through its neck — had bling (loot).
- **Hol'durog**: three towers, spikes, overlooking a frozen lake. Humelvish figures (taller than dwarves, smaller than giants) — Monks of Iohir.
- Albross tries "Greetings fellow mountaineers" — deception fails; fight with two monk guards; one explodes from an IceBall (Fireball-equivalent). Sneak in past the guards.
- Inside: tiny sleeping dwarf prisoners in a cage; one dwarf has an owl tabard.
- Knee-High (a prisoner) finds Neha.
- Combat with a big monk (a large fighter guarding the caves) and Neha. Big guy goes down; **Lucerion kills Neha** ("she lied").
- **Harhet Steelfoot** (Venture — Advent of Harmony) captured. Key intel:
  - Monastery in the Vanapur mountains.
  - Caves of Kroher have a Rimestone piece.
  - The Rimestone causes cold and does something with spirits; Harhet knows of 2 other pieces; monks believe there may be more.
  - Another big guy like the one they fought guards the Rimestone caves.
  - "The Cold Breath" — the real leader.
  - Dwarf spirits remain to defend the caves.
- Party frees ten dwarf miners; they will clean up the camp. Short rest.

## NPCs Introduced

- Harhet Steelfoot — Advent of Harmony (a semi-neutral monk faction). Captured; cooperative. Provided Rimestone intel.
- Neha — wizard who had been keeping Eva Colbath alive with ice magic (Cold Breath disciple). Killed by Lucerion.
