# WB Session 23 - Petal Hole Quest

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 23
- Title: Petal Hole Quest
- Real Date: August 18 (2025)
- In-Game Date: 33rd of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Climbor
- Ending Location: Climbor (Temple of Utheri)

## Major Scenes

- Lady Coarsepetal leads the party into the Hole of Climbor.
- The Gabüks are worse off than before.
- Natural cave opening with mutated moss/tendrils at the entrance. Caved-in areas.
- Right path leads to catacombs; the path is MOIST.
- Party fights a black pudding.
- Large room with a mural of the founding of Climbor — triggers poison darts (Indiana Jones style). Party triggers the pressure plate multiple times.
- Rhody stumbles into a secret passage leading to a tomb room.
- Bridge across a stream to a flooded collapsed catacomb room.
- Big skeletons and a skeleton wizard attack — blinded and immobilized, but party defeats them.
- Party leaves the Hole; Coarsepetal goes back in.
- **Caelion and Rhody run into Lilian** outside:
  - She says she took out the safehouse.
  - She says Rhody's infection will **kill him** unless treated.
  - She says the Coldbreath cult (Monks of Iohir) is looking for a **Snowglobe**.
- **Rhody has 29 days until he dies.**
- At the Temple of Utheri: the pale elves are confirmed **not from Myrdae**, possibly not from this plane. They have divine and fey power. God interrupted any speak-with-dead attempt.

## Key Revelations

- Monks of Iohir / Coldbreath = looking for a Snowglobe. (Later revealed: this is likely the Rimestone.)
- Rhody's infection = 29-day death clock, needs treatment from Madam Mabel.
- Lilian destroyed the Climbor safehouse.
