# WB Session 33 - Caw!

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 33
- Title: Caw!
- Real Date: March 9, 2026
- In-Game Date: 21st of Paramor, 1246
- Starting Location: Orc fort (Sacred Crows site)
- Ending Location: Drow towers, right tower (3rd floor, mid-session)

## Major Scenes

- Party meets the **Sacred Crows**: Armaros Eligora (Male High Elf Wizard), Rina Sarthona (Female Half-elf Rogue), Hefeic Icehelm (Male Mountain Dwarf Cleric of Addan & Utheri — scarred chainmail, bald with tattoos on head), Mhaenal "Maw" Cargwyn (Female Tiefling Ranger), Kotio Etric (Male Copper Dragonborn Barbarian).
- **Sacred Crows' mission**: They need to find a **blind brown braided goat** (Bandit) belonging to a dwarven knight in Khagdaruhl. Without it, the Sacred Crows cannot gain entry. Ambassador Udohi Greythane sent them.
- Sacred Crows had encountered and killed some Monks of Iohir.
- Armaros believes the dwarves may be aligned with the Cold Breath.
- Goat trail leads up the hills to a large cave. Clearing outside has over half a dozen 10-foot-wide craters (from thrown rocks).
- **Rina scouts**: a large sleeping mound (Elder Ettin) in the cave. Stench causes poison (Con save).
- **Combat vs. Elder Ettin** — Pagern's Fireball drops the Elder Ettin immediately (failed Dex save). Armaros's Arcane Burst finishes it.
- Beyond: tunnel to a large chamber with two dwarven towers (old, approximately 40 feet in diameter). Left tower: 3 stories, fangs banner. Right tower: 4 stories, web with eight eyes banner. Drow patrol both.
- Party takes right tower; Sacred Crows take left. **Left tower** vs. Drow Guards (Rounds 1-2): Lucerion kills 4 guards in Round 1.
- **Right tower** — three separate fights:
  - Ground floor: Drow with Fairie Fire; one drow drops Rhody; Pagern clears with Fireball.
  - 2nd floor: Drow scholars; one Misty Steps upstairs; spider boss drops from ceiling; spider shifts through floors.
  - Session ends mid-3rd floor.

## NPCs Introduced

- The Sacred Crows — rival/allied adventuring party.
- Armaros Eligora, Rina Sarthona, Hefeic Icehelm, Mhaenal "Maw" Cargwyn, Kotio Etric — Sacred Crows members.
- Ambassador Udohi Greythane (Duergar) — Khagdaruhl's contact; sent the Sacred Crows on the goat quest.
