# WB Session 32 - Over the Edge

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 32
- Title: Over the Edge
- Real Date: February 23, 2026
- In-Game Date: 13th–15th of Paramor, 1246
- Starting Location: Octagonal burial chamber, Fort Mornock
- Ending Location: Vanapur Mountains (descending, then camp; 21st of Paramor — orc fort encounter)

## Major Scenes

- Sarcophagus lid shifts (from Session 31's Knock). Copper wires explode — fire damage. Sarcophagus becomes a **copper dwarven statue** and attacks.
- **Combat vs. Copper Dwarven Statue**: Pagern shapes Fireball (30 hp damage); statue critically hits Lucerion (41 slashing). Rhody killing blow. A spirit forms from the dust: "Well done. You may proceed to my real tomb." Statue moves to reveal a spiral staircase.
- Short rest.
- Ascend seven stories. Cold air, roar of wind. Sunset light. **Large cavern with a collapsed far wall** opening over mountaintops. Ice columns; a throne with a dwarf statue; gold coins frozen under ice. Rhody checks for Presagiath sign — she was here recently.
- **Presagiath was hiding on the ceiling the whole time.**
- **Six-round combat vs. Presagiath:**
  - Round 1: Cold breath. Ice wall blocks Pagern at stairs. Albross's Wall of Fire. Presagiath grapples Lucerion, moves to Rhody.
  - Round 2: Presagiath grabs Lucerion and Rhody, hovering over cliff. Lucerion crits, stuns dragon. Rhody falls 100 feet — unconscious. Lucerion Misty Steps.
  - Round 3–5: Dragon rages. Drops Lucerion and Albross. Grabs Rhody and Lucerion in jaws. Healing keeps party alive.
  - Round 6: Dragon fumbles — drops all three held characters. Rhody and others down. **Pagern kills Presagiath** with a tiny Firebolt while she holds three party members.
- Party rests at the bottom of the stairs. Long rest. Pagern makes the area comfortable.
- **14th of Paramor**: entire day spent collecting the frozen hoard.
- **15th of Paramor**: Depart for Khagdaruhl. First three nights uneventful. 18th–21st of Paramor: lower hills, snow, travel.
- **21st of Paramor**: Rhody spots smoke. Caelion spots a camp. Caelion finds tracks: 5–6 humanoids. Party finds a group that attacked an orc fort — **the Sacred Crows**.
