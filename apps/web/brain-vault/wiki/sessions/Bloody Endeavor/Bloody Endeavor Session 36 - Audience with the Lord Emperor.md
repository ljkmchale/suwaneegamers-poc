# Session 36 - Audience with the Lord Emperor

Source: raw/bloody-endeavor-campaign-player-notes.md / https://docs.google.com/document/d/1p35JgGjlsAk6Ul8Y3cJC5P6Jdedr3pHSQQ29Y0ljBuc/edit

## Verbatim Session Notes

Session Date:  May 11th, 2026
In-game Date:  22nd of Paramor, 1246
Starting Location:  Khagdaruhl
Escorted to the Great Hall
The morning of the 22nd, a guard comes and offers to escort us to the Great Hall. We walk by the Great Forge and the mines, seeing elevators going up and down into the mines. We approach the Great Hall and see a set of guards. The escort leads us through the corridor into a 200 x 100 room filled with 100 pillars, each with their own designs. Some shaped like dwarves, some made of intricate material. The ceiling looks like waves of precious metals. We hear a hubbub of noise as 2 dozen groups, roughly 100 people, all well dressed and talking. Most are dwarvish, some are drow. They all seem to be talking within their own groups. On the far wall are 2 guarded golden doors to the throne room. We see a hooded individual with their hood down, we assume a delegate of the Dalasor Coast. There are also the 5 short robed figures that Caelion saw at the ring of respect, they aren’t talking with anyone. Caelion checks to see if there are any Monks of Iohir in the room but doesn’t see any. We run into Greythane and he lets us know this is where nobles meet while the Lord Emperor is talking with someone in private. He says he’s meeting with an advisor named Sir Duvall, who visits on occasion. He doesn’t know Sir Duvall well, nor does he know what they’re talking about, but they’ve been talking for about an hour. Pagern and Lucerion recognize the Duvall name, a noble family from a town on the other side of the mountainside called Narzon, known for being great wine traders.  
While we wait for our turn with the king, we choose to eavesdrop a bit on the nearby conversations. Rhody tries to listen in on the Lord’s conversation. He doesn’t hear anything, but notices that the 5 short black robed individuals are paying attention to him. They appear to be armed, and Rhody discerns they don’t like us. We piece together that the taller one they were with earlier was Sir Duvall. Caelion listens in on other conversations going on in the room; the hill dwarves are disgruntled with their lack of representation in Khagdaruhl, the mountain dwarves are talking about the gem trade, and specifically mention getting diamonds with the Dalasor Coast. The Duergar are talking about the nightmares they’ve been having, blaming the other dwarves somewhat for their bad dreams. 
Eventually the gold doors open, and a figure with a black robe and silver linings steps out. Lucerion quickly says a prayer and casts Enhance Ability (Charisma) on Albross, and Caelion casts Guidance. As Sir Duvall exits, the 5 guards surround him. Lucerion bows and greets him, but he is paid little heed. We’re invited inside, offered a private audience. Greythane tells us not to call him king, only to call him Lord Emperor. We enter the throne room, a string of fire warming up the room. We see 3 raised sections with 3-4 dwarves and on the far back wall a silver chair with faint electricity sparking off of it with a black-skinned dwarf. His white beard has rings braided into it, and he wears a black crown shining with gems embedded in it. 
The Lord Emperor greets us and asks what our purpose is for meeting with him. In common, Albross lets him know of the situation in Witguard and how we are ambassadors for the Magistrate. We offer the scroll we got from the magistrate and the Lord Emperor takes it and reads it. He summarizes that much like the Milithia Empire, Witguard wants to ally with Kahgdaruhl. Albross mentions as well that we met with the Runegrip dwarves, and how the piece of the Rimestone that they guarded was taken by the Monks of Iohir. Lucerion notices that the main dwarf of each section are pretty stoic, while the other dwarves chat amongst themselves. Rhody notices that despite his stoic face, his brows furrow as he tries to read Albross’s expression to gauge his honesty. He says that “Master Huldi” did not tell him that last night. He was briefed by Greythane about what we knew, and knows that the relationship with the monks is strained. He lets Master Huldi see the piece of the Rimestone, Rhody speaks suddenly in Dwarvish to say that The Cold Breath is a threat. Albross says that he’s had a vision of the eldritch threat as a result of the Rimestone being taken by Master Huldi. Lucerion whispers to the group asking how the Cold Breath completing the Rimestone to release the eldritch threat is a good thing, but the Lord Emperor hears him and asks what we know about the Rimestone. He explains that it was originally meant to destroy the undead aberration. He brings forward a nerdy duergar, who explains that hundreds of years ago, the Yagvur Sierra were more rolling fields. The dwarves and giants made great cities. The dwarves began using dark magic to dig deeper and deeper, eventually stumbling upon a corpse. Their dark magic eventually awakened it, and it began to destroy their civilization. The mages of the time made the Rimestone to freeze it, then broke it apart and spread their pieces around the mountains. The being has begun messing with the minds of people. 
Caelion asks what the relationship with Witguard and Khagdaruhl could be, he says he would speak with his advisors. Lucerion lets him know of the battles occurring between the two. Lucerion tells that Khagdaruhl is supplying arms and gems to the Dalasor Coast. The Lord Emperor tells us that the scroll offered Khagdaruhl money for the same. As the meeting is wrapping up, he asks how long we intend to say and we let him know we’ll be here for a stretch. We ask to go see the Rimestone as representatives of the Runegrip dwarves. 
To the Rimestone
A staircase is revealed underneath the throne, and a blast of cold wind blasts out, blowing out the fires. A cold dwarf is underneath shivering. We go down the stairs, and the dwarf introduces himself as Dalgurn, Watcher of the Stone. He says it’s about a 30 minute walk to the Rimestone.  There is no light, and the ice crunches underneath our feet. It’s about 20 below freezing. We see a frosted over image of mages spewing out energy as the staircase continues down. We enter a 100 x 50 room with 2 to 3 dozen mounds around the room covered in snow. Some of us notice that the mounds look like large dwarves, like a frozen over terracotta army. The temperature is slightly warmer here. Lucerion hears a crack of ice, as a dwarven statue begins to move. 
IT’S INITIATIVE – Combat vs. Dwarven Statues
         * ROUND 1: A dwarven statue calls us the servants of Ziv’ik’nok and casts Slow on Lucerion, Caelion and Albross. Rhody raises the Rune of Sennogin and declares us as representatives of the Runegrip dwarves. Rhody fails in figuring out if this did anything, but the others figure out that this is a room of last defense as the room is starting to warm up. Albross releases a Chill Touch, Caelion talks to the statue, telling him that the threat to the Rimestone is down the hall and then dashes down the hall. Pagern casts Dispel Magic on Lucerion. Lucerion determines that the statue didn’t pay heed to Caelion's call, and attacks the statue with his swords, breaking its concentration on Slow. We notice ice is breaking off of it.
         * ROUND 2: The Statue attempts to slam into Albross and Rhody but misses. Rhody hits him with the Orcreaver. Albross casts Chill touch again but misses. Caelion looks down the hallway and sees a staircase 120 ft away. He attempts to cast Toll the Dead, but the dwarven statue succeeds the save. Pagern casts Chromatic Orb (Acid) but misses, running further down the hallway. Lucerion attacks with his swords again, missing his attacks and dropping one of his scimitars.
         * Round 3: The statue attacks Rhody and Lucerion, hitting Rhody and making him lose concentration on Hunter’s Mark. Rhody recasts Hunter’s Mark and makes 2 more attacks with Orc Reaver, making it look very damaged. Albross disengages and backs up into the hallway. Caelion casts Mass Healing Word and casts Toll the Dead on the statue. Pagern casts Chromatic Orb (Acid), hitting this time. Lucerion attacks with his swords, hitting this time as well and landing the fatal blow. END OF COMBAT
We leave the room and go down the hallway. The staircase widens out and ice is all across the ceiling, reducing the height of the room by 15 ft. After 15 minutes, we come across a frozen over hamlet, buildings in ice. Caelion, Rhody and Pagern realize that we’ve gone about 2 miles down, approaching the Underdark. Rhody looks for dead bodies and notices frozen over mounds. 
Rimestone Pedestal Room
We travel a bit longer and eventually enter a semi-domed room. It’s similar to the last location we visited that held the Rimestone piece. We see 2 dead dwarves, the guards that the Lord Emperor gave to go with the Cold Breath. Caelion notices that there is a scuffle around the pillar in the center, looking for the Cold Breath. By the doors, there appears to be shadows in the ice. Caelion casts Light on a piece of ice, illuminating the door and revealing what appear to be tree branches trying to reach out through the door. There are also 3-4 shapes, one of which looks like a huge creature with multiple appendages and a mass of tentacles for a mouth. Caelion figures these are the servants of the eldritch entity. Caelion casts Sending to the Lord Emperor: 


Lord Emperor apologies. Huldi was here, creature now thawing. Please send help. Watch out for Golems. Anything you would like us to do?
The Lord Emperor responds:


A creature is thawing? I will send my guards right away. Stand at the ready. I will arrive myself soon.
Except Caelion realizes that wasn’t the Lord Emperor, it was the aberration. It breaks out of the ice and we begin combat.
ITS INITIATIVE – Combat vs. some Stranger Thing
         * ROUND 1: Rhody focuses his Hunter’s Mark on the creature and as he goes to strike him, the creature swaps places with Lucerion and Rhody accidentally strikes him. Lucerion goes to attack him with his swords. Rhody deflects the attack with the staff, but he’s also grabbed and shunted through space, coming back partially encased in ice. Caelion casts Healing Word on Rhody and uses Sacred Flame which misses. Albross casts Hex on the creature and casts Eldritch Blast, hitting once. Pagern casts Chromatic Orb but misses. 
         * ROUND 2:  Rhody recasts Hunter’s Mark and throws his pitchfork at it, but it swaps with Lucerion again. Lucerion fails the Charisma Saving Throw and witnesses the entirety of the history of the Universe in a moment, teleporting behind Rhody. He approaches the creature and goes for more attacks with his sword, smiting him with a Divine Smite. He crits on his next attack, dealing severe damage to its left arm. A branch starts growing from the wound. Lucerion starts freezing in place as he was in space for a moment. The creature releases a blast of star dust from its mouth, dealing radiant damage to Lucerion and searing his skin with star dust. Caelion also sees the history of the Universe and is teleported right next to the creature. He runs to Lucerion and casts Cure Wounds using the prayer necklace. He casts Toll the Dead on the creature, hitting it. Albross sees the history of the Universe and is teleported next to Rhody. He casts Eldritch Blast, hitting once. Pagern sees the history of the Universe, then casts Chromatic Orb (Acid), almost hitting it before the creature swapped places with Caelion who is hit instead. 
         * ROUND 3:  Rhody sees the history of the Universe, then hits it with his pitchfork and Orcreaver, missing it with the pitchfork. Lucerion hits with his swords then heals himself with Lay on Hands. The stars burn him for more damage. The creature sends Albross to space with his nub, but Albross doesn’t come back frozen. The tentacles open up again as Stardust covers Pagern, Albross and Rhody. Albross goes down. Caelion casts Mass Healing Word, bringing back Albross. He attempts to Toll the Dead, succeeding. Albross brushes the stars off and casts Armor of Agathys. Pagern sees the history of the Universe and is teleported right next to the creature
         * ROUND 4:  Rhody attacks the creature, which uses its reaction to swap place, causing Rhody to attack Albross.  Lucerion brushes off the stars and Misty Steps to a spot close to him. The creature freezes Pagern in place then tries to breathe the stardust breath on Albross, Rhody, and Caelion but misses. He starts to run, tricking Rhody into attacking Albross again. Caelion casts Spirit Guardians, shredding the creature to bits. Combat ends.
– The Session Ends Here –
