# WB Session 24 - Albross Speaks

## Session Metadata

- Campaign: Bloody Endeavor (WB)
- Session Number: 24
- Title: Albross Speaks
- Real Date: September 1 (2025)
- In-Game Date: 34th of Talil, 1246
- Starting Location: Climbor
- Ending Location: Yag'vur Sierra (after dragon encounter)

## Major Scenes

- Party leaves Climbor for the Yag'vur Sierra to find: Khagdaruhl, nomads, and the Coldbreath cult.
- Options considered: Road through Alasfolen, meeting noble Colbath for info, backtracking to Rhody's home, tracking slavers.
- Lucerion offers 150 gp to buy Alavara out of her contract with Lurch.
- Party learns a monk of Iohir left Colbath Keep; a map shows the leftmost mountains are called the Yaggermon.
- Party arrives at Colbath Keep. Lord sent scouts to find Neha.
- Begin tracking Neha into the wilds of the Yag'vur Sierra.
- 8 days of travel. Getting very cold. Albross bags a deer (shot down, then cleaned by Lucerion — "enough meat for 11 meals").
- Day 8: Meet **Rock People** (Stone Giants). They share: dragon north of here (female); Cave five peaks; small clan of dwarves; Clan of Iohir.
- Party finds **four people frozen dead in pillars of ice**: adventurers frozen by Presagiath. Speak with dead reveals they were looking for dwarven ruins near the peak. Wizard's scroll found (protection from energy).
- **Presagiath attacks.** Party nearly wiped. Rhody is the last one standing; saves Caelion; Caelion saves the rest. Lucerion almost cuts off the dragon's claw. Presagiath flees.

## NPCs

- Presagiath — adult white dragon, female. Lair in the Yag'vur Sierra, five peaks area. Highly dangerous.
- Stone Giants — neutral; gave information.

## Clues

- Neha's trail leads into the mountains.
- Clan of Iohir is up in the peaks near five caves.
