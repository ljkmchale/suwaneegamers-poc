## Major Scenes

### Part 1: Ring of Respect — Wyrm Bane vs. Sacred Crows

- High Justice Flintfall announces the groups must fight (in their heads: not to the death). Wall of Force drops.
- Party debated; Pagern and Armaros message-spell to plan; Rhody tries to stall. Ultimately, they fight.
- **Four rounds of combat**. Key moments:
  - Kotio's acid breath hits Rhody, Lucerion, Maw, and Pagern. Maw goes down early.
  - Pagern's Fireball is counterspelled by Armaros; Armaros's spell is counterspelled by Pagern.
  - Caelion grabs Albross's tail and casts Cure Wounds through Necklace of Prayer Beads.
  - Albross slain by Hefeic's cold flame + Rina's critical shot; Albross uses Hellish Rebuke to take Armaros down with him.
  - Rhody knocks out Maw non-lethally; Lucerion knocks out Kotio; Hefeic is burned by Pagern's Wall of Fire, healed by himself, then dropped.
  - Rina is last standing; Pagern's Chromatic Orb finishes her.
- **Wyrm Bane wins.** Crowd erupts. Clerics rush in to heal all combatants.

### Part 2: Audience with Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV

- Escorted to the Great Hall — 200×100 room, 100 pillars, 2 dozen groups of well-dressed dwarves.
- Notable: Sir Duvall (noble from Narzon, wine traders, black robe with silver lining) meets privately with the Lord Emperor for over an hour before the party is admitted. Sir Duvall has 5 short hooded guards who don't like the party.
- **Caelion casts Sending to Breemos** (Albross's brother): warns of kidnapping; advises him to leave Gevakaln and meet in Witguard. Breemos responds: surprised but alive; doesn't know why he'd leave Gevakaln.
- Party enters the private throne room. Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV — black-skinned Duergar, white beard with rings, black gem crown, silver chair with faint electricity sparking. Three raised sections of advisors.
- **Albross speaks for the party**: presents Witguard's scroll requesting alliance. Reveals the Runegrip's piece of the Rimestone was taken by Huldi. Shares vision of tendrils on the throne.
- The Lord reveals the history (explained by a nerdy Duergar advisor): Dwarves used dark magic to dig deep; awakened an undead aberration; Rimestone was created by their mages to freeze it; broken into three pieces and scattered. The aberration is now messing with the minds of people nearby (Duergar nightmares).
- The Lord says "Master Huldi did not tell him that last night" — Huldi has been in contact with Khagdaruhl.
- Lucerion says Khagdaruhl is supplying arms and gems to the Dalasor Coast; the Lord confirms the scroll from Witguard also offered Khagdaruhl money.
- Party asks to see the Rimestone and to have time in Khagdaruhl.
- Session ends mid-audience.

## Key Revelations

- **Huldi (The Cold Breath) was at Khagdaruhl last night.** The Lord met with him.
- **The eldritch aberration**: awakened by dwarven dark magic, sealed by the Rimestone. Duergar nightmares are a symptom of its growing influence.
- **Khagdaruhl holds the 2nd Rimestone piece.** Location of 3rd is still unknown.
- **Sir Duvall** of Narzon is more significant than he appears — privately meeting the Emperor, with five armed hooded guards.
