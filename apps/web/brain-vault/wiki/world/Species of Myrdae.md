# Species of Myrdae

## Scope

World lore - major peoples, unique species, and player-facing species guidance for [[Myrdae]].

## Overview

Myrdae is home to humans, elves, dwarves, halflings, gnomes, and rarer peoples who arrived from other planes or emerged from Myrdae's own history. Humans are the most widespread, but older peoples are deeply tied to specific regions, gods, and historical events.

## Major Species

### Dwarves

The first dwarves were shaped through Muerg the Earth Father and Goldraen, a master smith who later ascended as Goldraen the Evercast. Dwarven peoples were called for different purposes:

- **Mountain Dwarves** - guardians of high peaks, strongholds, and deep mines.
- **Barren Dwarves / Crag Hill Dwarves** - lively surface wanderers of crags, hills, trade, brewing, and adaptation.
- **Duergar / Gray Dwarves** - keepers of the Underdark's depths, guarding the world's underbelly.

Known dwarven clans include Blaregut, Brightmaul, Cragfury, Ironbrow, Kegbuster, Orebane, Ramhearth, and Umberfist.

The [[Myths and Tales of Myrdae]] source preserves a fuller dwarf-origin saying: "By Muerg's stone and Goldraen's fire, we hold." It also records [[Dunduar]] as a major dwarven historical anchor founded by Umberfist and Cragfury elders.

### Elves

The first elves stepped from the Feywild through the sacred Daefar, living pools between realms, into the ancient wilds of the Borealian Sway.

- **High Elves / Qal'daefar** - associated with beauty, artistry, refinement, Caelora, and Qal'dynn.
- **Wood Elves** - closest to the wild heart of Myrdae.
- **Sea Elves** - descended from elves who followed waters out toward the open seas.
- **Drow / Dark Elves** - exiled faction of High Elves who followed Eredra the Spider Queen into betrayal; transformed by the Underdark and regarded warily by surface elves.

### Gnomes

Gnomes are a common species in player guidance. The Pleriteas surname is associated with Skrettel and known for travel and trade.

### Halflings

Halflings are common and are part of the broader "weefolk" category used in Myrdae. Known surnames include Hallowpot, Illdor, Mosspot, Peddlefoot, Tossbrook, and Wilybuck.

### Humans

Humans are very common and widespread across the continent. Known human surnames include Blackren, Glorbane, Stormvigor, Taldwell, and Thornage.

## Unique Myrdaen Species

### Gabuk

Gabuk, also called half-goblins, are believed to have arisen around 500 PF during harsh times in the Mahruud, when goblins, orcs, humans, and weefolk banded together for survival. They now live across Myrdae but remain most common in the Mahruud.

Gabuk are generally accepted across Myrdae. They are usually about three feet tall, with dirty-green skin, dark or dyed hair, and brown, black, red, or yellow eyes. A player using gabuk should select the halfling species mechanically unless the DM rules otherwise.

### Ulbin

Ulbi, plural of ulbin, are human-sized goblinoids who lived for centuries in the Underdark before the Fracturing. Demonic chaos nearly destroyed them, but survivors fled to safer Underdark regions and have increased in number. Since the Awakening, ulbi are seen more often on the surface.

Ulbin usually have pale or grayish skin, black/white/gray hair, and blue or gray eyes, with rarer violet, red, amber, or yellow eyes. A player using ulbin should select the human species mechanically unless the DM rules otherwise.

## Restricted Species Guidance

Some species should be used only with DM approval because they are rare, not native to Myrdae, considered monstrous, or historically distrusted. The guide calls out aarakocra, bugbear, centaur, changeling, drow, duergar, fairy, gith, goblin, grung, hobgoblin, kenku, kobold, lizardfolk, locathah, minotaur, shadar-kai, shifter, svirfneblin, tabaxi, tortle, and similar species as requiring care or avoidance.
