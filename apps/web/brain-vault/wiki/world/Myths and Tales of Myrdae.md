# Myths and Tales of Myrdae

## Scope

World lore - campaign-agnostic stories, myths, and passed-down tales from [[Myrdae]].

## Overview

Myrdae preserves many myths that explain divine origins, old wars, heroic eras, artifacts, and the cultural memory behind modern factions and faiths. These tales are not campaign session notes; they describe the world's inherited stories.

## Major Tales

### Betrayal of Amriel and the Crown of Lyess

Di'Belgi, the Craving Queen, deceived Amriel while hiding her demonic nature. At her request, Amriel stole the [[Crown of Lyess]], a sacred artifact of Lyess, old goddess of magic. When Di'Belgi revealed herself, Amriel fought her, the crown's eight school-of-magic orbs scattered across Myrdae, and Di'Belgi's demonic invasion began.

### Blood Wars Arrive

Di'Belgi's incursion brought the Blood Wars to Myrdae. Devils of the Nine Hells aided mortals against her demons in a rare alignment of interests. Seeking redemption, Amriel descended beneath Myrdae and met Yibrix, a rune mage. Together they developed Runeweaving, and its secrets were preserved in the [[Affirmation of Strife]].

### The Forging of Dwarves

Muerg the Earth Father sensed the young world's instability and gave primordial earth essence to Goldraen, a master smith. Goldraen shaped the first dwarves from living stone and ascended as the Evercast when the final clan awakened.

### Prophecy of the Year of Rising

The [[Affirmation of Strife]] warned of the fall of the Assembly of Essence and the rise of four great threats: Di'Belgi, Itoro Gradel, Nethyndria, and Suthrin.

### Layeth and Brault

Layeth and Brault were born as twins to Tiash and Smott, though Smott had deceived Tiash by taking another form. To preserve divine order, Tiash raised Layeth and Smott raised Brault while the truth was hidden. They later met as adults without knowing their kinship, exposing the flaw in that old divine arrangement.

### Dwarves of Dunduar

The mining village of [[Dunduar]] was founded in year 49 by Dundrim Umberfist and Uarmil Cragfury. After Underdark conflict and mind flayer tunneling, the village collapsed on the 18th of Hemist in year 59, an event remembered as Morn of Dunduar. The survivors refounded Dunduar as a mountain stronghold and later discovered magical Dunduar Ore.

### Rise of the Ardent One

Diverra was a beloved sorceress from a Bathaen noble family who devoted herself to love and beauty. In the most common tale, she died confronting the demon Zarak'Nor at [[Ulgreer]] and ascended as the Ardent One.

### Oldport and the Smiling Duchess

Osanna appeared in [[Oldport]] during a period of shipwrecks, dangerous currents, sea monsters, and storms. She taught sailors and traders how to read the Tideless Span and became the embodiment of fortune. Oldport's Festival of Fair Winds commemorates her.

### The Laztyr and Suthrin

In 1224, missing villagers near [[Bistron]] led adventurers to mines repurposed by the Barrenscars for slave trafficking into the Mahruud. They found a Brault cult temple and the shattered [[Laztyr]], a crystal artifact used to recall Suthrin, son of Brault and Tornia, from exile in Stygia.

### Year of Fending Heroes

In 1226, heroes defeated Nethyndria in the Shadowfell and Tol'behgekt on the coast of Orbansia. Leaders later gathered in [[Tratta]], Guardian's Day was proclaimed, and the final demonic push by Di'Belgi helped lead into the divine rupture remembered as the Awakening.
