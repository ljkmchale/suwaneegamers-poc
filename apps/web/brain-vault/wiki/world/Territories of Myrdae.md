# Territories of Myrdae

## Scope

World lore summarizing campaign-agnostic political and geographic territories across [[Myrdae]]. This page combines the Territories clipper source with the later Myrdae World Guide clarification that Myrdae is organized into five major regions: Bathaes, Inverland, Jehsle, Orbansia, and Var'Shala.

## Regional Frame

| Region | Territory Notes |
|---|---|
| **Bathaes** | Contains the Bathaen Empire and Vanlaug-linked northern territories. The Bathaen Empire includes Borealian Sway, Wheldrake, Halesworth, and Crimson March. Vanlaug is formed from Esgaar and the Tundra of Nalscour. |
| **Inverland** | Domains are called Vecs; their representatives sit on the Dicon. |
| **Jehsle** | Island-chain region including Nandahere, Qae'thesvorin, and the Corse of Veinspear. |
| **Orbansia** | Region containing Baltwood, Dalasor Coast, Kholdur, Matahal Valley, Rostiena Foothills, Severed Bend, Yag'vur, and others. |
| **Var'Shala** | New regional name adopted after the Awakening changed old Mahruud. Mahruud remains one dry territory within Var'Shala. |

## Featured Areas

The Myrdae World Guide also lists featured natural and subterranean areas. Important active-campaign anchors include:

- **Celedon Forest** - Inverland forest tied to Adsuren resources.
- **Dragonspine Mountains** - mountain range in Var'Shala.
- **Gloamspire Sea** - vast lightless Underdark sea with bioluminescent currents.
- **Rootreach** - territory-scale Underdark root network from ancient surface mega-trees.
- **Rothenloch** - very large subterranean lake.
- **Thallgrove** - fungal biome in the western Underdark.
- **Yearning Slough** - Var'Shala swamp southwest of Tratta, home to the ancient gray dragon Zovahesdria.

## Borealian Sway Sites

- [[O'naren]] - elven foothill settlement west of Qal'dynn, centered on Driftglow Pond and tied to Fralee, mining, craft, and Realmwarden protection.
- [[Abbey of Light]] - former Abbey of Mont Rest, a multi-faith sanctuary south of [[O'naren]], protected by Stone Giants in the foothills descending from the Distancion Mountains.
