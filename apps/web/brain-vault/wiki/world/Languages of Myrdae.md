# Languages of Myrdae

## Scope

World lore - common dialects and language expectations for [[Myrdae]].

## Overview

The common human language of Myrdae first developed from communication with the wood elves of the Bathaen Empire before those elves left the land. The human version is cruder and has split into dialects aligned with major regions and social contexts.

Non-human peoples also have their own languages, often with regional accents. They have developed "common" variants that allow basic communication with humans of a region, including greetings and insults. A speaker can communicate proficiently in a language they know, but unfamiliar dialects may reveal that the speaker comes from somewhere else.

## Common Dialects

| Language / Dialect | Description |
|---|---|
| **Bathaen** | Spoken across the land by the upper class, wealthy, or educated; complex and context-dependent. |
| **Boreal** | Local common dialect of the Borealian Kingdom, influenced by elvish properties. |
| **Hales** | Local common dialect of Halesworth, typically used by lower-class or uneducated speakers. |
| **Inverness** | Single common dialect developed in Inverland and used by all Vecs of the region. |
| **Mahruudian** | Single language with many tribal slangs; people bound to tribes through slavery learn the tribe's version. |
| **Northern** | Local common dialect of the Kingdom of the North Coast, typically used by lower-class or uneducated speakers. |
| **Sentry** | Unspoken language of gestures and facial expressions once used by Sentry guards during the Blood Wars on Myrdae. |

## Use At Table

When a character hears or reads an unfamiliar dialect, an Insight check can determine whether they recognize where the dialect originates.
