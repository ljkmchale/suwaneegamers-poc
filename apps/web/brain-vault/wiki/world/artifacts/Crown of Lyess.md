# Crown of Lyess

## Scope

World lore - divine artifact of Lyess, old goddess of magic.

## Overview

The Crown of Lyess was a sacred artifact belonging to Lyess. It was adorned with eight orbs, each representing one of the schools of magic and containing vast ancient knowledge of that school. Someone attuned to the crown could access deep magical secrets, making it one of the most coveted relics in [[Myrdae]].

## Mythic History

Di'Belgi deceived Amriel into stealing the crown. When Amriel presented it to her, Di'Belgi revealed her true nature. Amriel fought her, and the eight orbs were scattered across Myrdae during the battle. Di'Belgi's hunt for the orbs helped unleash the demonic invasion remembered in the Blood Wars.
