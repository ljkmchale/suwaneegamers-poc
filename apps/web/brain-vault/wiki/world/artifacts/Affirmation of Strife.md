# Affirmation of Strife

## Scope

World lore - tome of Runeweaving secrets and prophecy.

## Overview

The Affirmation of Strife is a powerful tome tied to Amriel, Yibrix, Runeweaving, and the Blood Wars on [[Myrdae]].

Amriel met Yibrix, a mage skilled in runes, while fighting Di'Belgi's demonic legions in the depths of Myrdae. Together they developed **Runeweaving**, an art that infused magical power into runes tattooed onto individuals. The secrets of this practice were captured in the Affirmation of Strife.

## Prophecy

The tome also contained a prophecy warning of the fall of the Assembly of Essence and the rise of four great powers during the Year of Rising:

- **Di'Belgi, the Craving Queen** - demoness seeking to reignite the Blood Wars and reclaim the orbs of Lyess.
- **Itoro Gradel, the Time Bender** - sorcerer able to manipulate time and reality.
- **Nethyndria, the Immortal Wyrm** - ancient dragon with the secret to eternal life.
- **Suthrin, the Son of Vengeance** - figure of hatred and revenge whose coming would bring war.

The tome was lost for ages and is said to have been discovered by Yedera, an Avantee in the [[Factions of Myrdae|Advents of Harmony]].
