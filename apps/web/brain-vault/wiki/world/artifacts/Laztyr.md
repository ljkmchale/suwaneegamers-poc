# Laztyr

## Scope

World lore - crystal artifact connected to Suthrin.

## Overview

The Laztyr was a large crystal artifact kept as the centerpiece of an ancient cult temple to Brault near [[Bistron]]. Ancient writings said the Laztyr could recall Suthrin, son of Brault and Tornia, from exile in Stygia.

## Bistron Incident

In 1224, during the Year of Being, adventurers investigating missing villagers near Bistron discovered mines repurposed by the Barrenscars for slave traffic into the Mahruud. Near an attack on the slave traders, they found the Brault temple and the Laztyr shattered into dozens of shards.

The artifact appeared to have been extracted from a nearby tunneled mine rich in orange opals. The attack on the slave traders may have been caused by Suthrin, returned from the hells and loose on the surface of Myrdae.
