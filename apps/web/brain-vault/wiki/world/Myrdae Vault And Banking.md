### **Setting up a vault account**

When an account is set up with the vault, the account owner will receive a transaction scroll with the Reserve seal on it, stating details of the account.  If the account holds more than 500 gold pieces, a vault key is issued: a magical way of accessing the account and validating the balance.

### **Accessing the vault**

The person accessing the vault account must have the approved scroll to do anything with the account, deposit or withdraw.  

### **Using the vault key**

For large accounts with a vault key, both the scroll and the key should be used to validate who has access to the vault.  Additionally, these large accounts are kept in vaults accessing the original repository.  

### **Exchange Fees**

If funds are accessed from a vault that is not where the funds are held, there is a 2% charge on the fund exchange, with a minimum of 10gp.  This is because the key is associated with a magical lock for authentication that ties the Reserve locations together.
