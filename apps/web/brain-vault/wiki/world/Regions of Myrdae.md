# Regions of Myrdae

## Scope

World lore - major regions, old provinces, and governance structures across [[Myrdae]].

## Regional Model

The Myrdae World Guide describes Myrdae as a continent reshaped by the Awakening. Before that cataclysm, four major governing regions were known as provinces: the Bathaen Empire, Mahruud, Orbansia, and Inverland. After the Awakening, the main lands are usually referred to as regions.

The five major regions are:

- **Bathaes**
- **Inverland**
- **Jehsle**
- **Orbansia**
- **Var'Shala**

## Region Notes

| Region | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Bathaes** | Contains the Bathaen Empire and Vanlaug-linked northern island territories. The Bathaen Empire includes Borealian Sway, Wheldrake, Halesworth, and Crimson March. Vanlaug is formed from Esgaar and the Tundra of Nalscour. |
| **Inverland** | Cold northwestern region whose domains are known as Vecs. Vec representatives sit on the Dichotomous Convocation, shortened to Dicon. |
| **Jehsle** | Pronounced JES-LEE. Southwestern island-chain region associated with seafaring, trade, and distant territories such as Nandahere and Qae'thesvorin. |
| **Orbansia** | Southern/eastern region of hills, forests, rivers, old ruins, and coastal cities. It includes Baltwood, Severed Bend, Rostiena Foothills, Yag'vur, and other active campaign-linked territories. |
| **Var'Shala** | Region born from the fractured remains of old Mahruud. The Mahruud remains as one dry, barren territory within Var'Shala, while surrounding territories are independent lands ruled by sovereign Mahls. |

## Governance

### Bathaen Empire

The Bathaen Empire uses noble and appointed ranks:

| Title | Position |
|---|---|
| King / Queen | Ruler of a kingdom. |
| Archduke | Reports to the king or queen. |
| Duke | Reports to the king, queen, or Archduke. |
| Prince / Princess | Son or daughter of a king or queen. |
| Count | Noble ruler of a thorp, hamlet, or village. |
| Baron | Noble ruler of a town. |
| Consul | Appointed ruler of a thorp, hamlet, or village. |

### Inverland

Inverland domains are known as **Vecs**, originally clans. Representatives sit on the **Dichotomous Convocation**, or **Dicon**.

| Title | Position |
|---|---|
| Chief | Ruler of a Vec, usually determined by bloodline. |
| Dean | Presides over the Vesci; changes every few years. |
| Doyenne | Eldest members serving the Vesci. |
| Vecsi | Representative of a Vec or major belief on the Dicon. |
| Elder | Ruler of a city. |
| Intendant | Ruler of a town or village. |

### Var'Shala

Var'Shala consists of multiple independent territories. Most are ruled by a sovereign **Mahl** and allow varied local government. The remaining Mahruud territory is stricter and ruled by a **Tor**.

| Title | Position |
|---|---|
| Mahl | Sovereign ruler of a full territory, holding final authority on law, war, and major trade. |
| Thulan | Local ruler of a settlement, town, or stronghold, answering to the territory's Mahl. |
| Tor | Dictator-ruler of the Mahruud territory; all Mahruud Thulans report directly to the Tor. |
