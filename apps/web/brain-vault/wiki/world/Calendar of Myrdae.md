# Calendar of Myrdae

## Scope

World lore - calendar, seasons, moons, and timekeeping for [[Myrdae]] and [[Oberra]].

## Moons

Oberra has two primary moons, called the Eyes of Time:

- **Endelo'ar** - the Scarlet Moon, reddish in color.
- **Tudara** - the Frost Moon, bluish in color.

The ruined third moon is called **the Broken One**. It appears as magically bound fragments following the old orbit.

The paired phases of Endelo'ar and Tudara are called **Lunar Facets**. These recurring sky patterns are used for festivals, prayer, superstition, and timekeeping.

## Harmon Order

The calendar used across Myrdae is the **Harmon Order**. Asterin Thornage charted the movement of Endelo'ar and Tudara and aligned the calendar to their recurring Lunar Facets.

| Period | Description |
|---|---|
| Year | 384 days; the time it takes Oberra to orbit the sun. |
| Harmon | One of 8 periods in a year; each Harmon is 48 days. |
| Stretch | One of 6 periods in a Harmon; each Stretch is 8 days. |
| Day | 24 hours. |

## Seasons

Myrdae has four seasons that drift across the rigid Harmon framework:

| Season | Known As | Similar To |
|---|---|---|
| Natali | Season of Birth | Spring |
| Sultra | Season of Light | Summer |
| Garnest | Season of Gathering | Fall |
| Briscarn | Season of Fading | Winter |

Two major days mark seasonal extremes:

- **All-bright** - a full day of light at the peak of Sultra.
- **Nocturn** - a full day of darkness at the peak of Briscarn.
