# Factions of Myrdae

## Scope

World lore - campaign-agnostic organizations and factions across [[Myrdae]].

## Overview

The Myrdae World Guide distinguishes broad well-known organizations from adventurer-facing factions that heavily recruit or interact with player characters. Faction pages here are world canon; campaign-specific interactions belong on campaign faction pages.

## Recruitable / Adventurer-Facing Factions

### Advents of Harmony

The Advents of Harmony are the oldest organization in Myrdae. They began as adventurers and explorers and now maintain the largest historical archive on the continent.

- **Goals:** adventure into the unexplored; record and preserve history.
- **Values:** body and spirit, knowledge and lore, travel and exploration.
- **Ranks:** Avantee, Venture, Seeker, Savant.
- **Membership marker:** a journal.
- **Story note:** Yedera, an Avantee of the Advents, is said to have discovered the lost [[Affirmation of Strife]].

### Anders

The Anders appear to be poets, storytellers, and writers. Beneath that surface, they are a covert diplomatic network that gathers political insight and counsels leaders toward stability and diplomacy.

- **Goals:** inform leaders, guide rulers toward diplomatic resolutions, promote peace and cooperation.
- **Values:** words as tools for change, subtlety, knowledge as prevention.
- **Structure:** local Preceptors supported by Adjutants in larger cities.
- **Membership marker:** settlement-specific token such as a ring or amulet.

### Cloak of Defiance

The Cloak of Defiance grew from a loose network of thieves' guilds and supplanted Malfease leadership in 1232, becoming one of the most powerful shadow networks in Myrdae.

- **Goals:** attain power and influence; acquire wealth and powerful items.
- **Values:** Prime, Ambition, Bias.
- **Ranks:** Tenderfoot, Edge, Agent, Cloak.
- **Membership marker:** a coin bearing the faction symbol and the alias of the member who vouched for the recruit.

### Everdawn

The Everdawn are widespread nature advocates who observe from the edges of civilization and react quickly when balance between people and nature is threatened.

- **Goals:** help people coexist with nature; remove unnatural threats to nature.
- **Values:** preservation, balance, restraint.
- **Ranks:** Scout, Watcher, Sentry, Warden.
- **Membership marker:** crescent moon pin surrounded by vines.
- **Story note:** after the Year of Fending, Elora established Everdawn training grounds in Ferndale, helping the faction grow around cities and towns.

### Meridian

The Meridian began as the Halesworth Charge and is now the major organization recognized across Myrdae for law, alliance, and order.

- **Goals:** advocate honor and order; fight anarchy and chaos.
- **Values:** Undivided, Order, Never Fear.
- **Ranks:** Pledge, Stalwart, Gallant, Templar.
- **Major embassies:** Adamont, Basctdelm, Gibuldon, Thrargael.
- **Membership marker:** silver amulet insignia.
- **Story note:** after the Year of Fending, Queen Breya Talward struck a deal with Abaddon for a sky castle that became the Meridian headquarters over Basctdelm; Isaiah's Grey Cloaks later joined the Meridian as a premier force for good.

### Tegenwald Phet

The Tegenwald Phet formed in 777 PF when the Runic Brotherhood, Leybearers, and Nightweave merged in Tratta. The group worked in secrecy for centuries and regained public respect during the Year of Fending.

- **Goals:** learn and advance magic; preserve personal freedom.
- **Values:** Swift, Prong, Veil.
- **Ranks:** Herald, Covet, Scholar, Magister.
- **Leadership:** the Phet'extant council, led by a Master Arcanum and representatives of major magical disciplines.
- **Base:** Lyedin, an extradimensional fortification named in honor of Lyess.
- **Membership marker:** a faction tattoo that can identify and locate members.

## Campaign Intersections

- Heroes of Emberstran already contains campaign-specific Cloak of Defiance and Emberstran thieves' guild material. Keep those events in HoE pages and use this page only for the world-level organization.
- Souls of Destiny already includes Mollywap as a former high-ranking Tegenwald Phet mage in Adsuren context.
- The Silent Vanguard touches Tratta, where the Tegenwald Phet was originally formed.
- The [[Bistron]] / [[Laztyr]] tale places Barrenscar slave traffic in mines reaching through the Severed Crease into the Mahruud.
