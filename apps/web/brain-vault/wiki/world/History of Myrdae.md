# History of Myrdae

## Scope

World lore from the History clipper source and later world-story sources. This page records the broad era timeline for Myrdae and flags campaign-relevant intersections without turning the world timeline into campaign session history.

## Source

- Obsidian Clipper file: `Clippings/Suwanee Gamers 1.md`
- Web source: `https://sites.google.com/view/suwanee-gamers/history`
- Raw source: `raw/myrdae-stories-and-tales.md`

## Era Overview

| Era | Years | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-Fracturing | before 1 AF | Early empire history, War of Might, Mahruud transformation, and major divine/ancestral events before the Fracturing. |
| Time of Growth | 0-941 AF | Fracturing aftermath, calendar formation, Crungess threats, empire recognition, and capital establishment. |
| Cycle of Change | 942-1110 AF | Myrdae pushes back a demonic Underdark threat; Mahruud political exile follows a failed Thrargael guard coup. |
| Era of Ascendance | 1111-1226 AF | Prophecies, the Astereal Accord, Sinestia Thornage, Tether Core, Itoro Gradel, Mahruud coups, and rising villains. |
| The Awakening | 1227-present | The Old Gods stop answering, the New Order rises, torn lands are repaired or reshaped, and adventuring becomes common again. |

## Campaign Intersections

- **Awakening, 1227:** explains current divine upheaval and New Order context across the wiki. Souls of Destiny explicitly references the Awakening in Eyovar's bargain confession. Bloody Endeavor's Rimestone history also says the Awakening destabilized the Yag'vur Sierra region.
- **Year of Emergence, 1240:** strengthens world context for Underdark surface contacts, including Heroes of Emberstran's Nunglthil/drow material and Dungeons III's cave/Underdark-adjacent discoveries.
- **Year of Discovery, 1246:** matches the active adventure era and the History source's note about heroes being gathered to re-establish the lost domain in Mahruud.
- **Mahruud coups and capitals:** provide world backing for Mahruud, Thrargael, Gulkta, and related Dungeons III references.
- **373 AF capitals:** ties Basctdelm, Gibuldon, and Thrargael to the long political structure behind Dungeons III, Souls of Destiny, and Mahruud material.
- **Dunduar history:** provides world backing for Ky'tha's Dunduar / Cragfury / Muerg vision in Heroes of Emberstran and Nova's Dunduar brooch in Dungeons III without merging those campaign-specific mysteries.
