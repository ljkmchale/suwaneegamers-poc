# Myrdae

## Scope

World lore - the primary continent where all active campaigns take place.

## Overview

Myrdae is a continent on the planet [[Oberra]]. Before the Awakening, its main governing regions were provinces: the Bathaen Empire, Mahruud, Orbansia, and Inverland. After the Awakening reshaped the land, the continent is now described through five major regions: **Bathaes**, **Inverland**, **Jehsle**, **Orbansia**, and **Var'Shala**.

The active campaign wiki currently places Heroes of Emberstran, Souls of Destiny, The Silent Vanguard, Bloody Endeavor, and Dungeons III within Myrdae. World pages describe what exists; campaign pages describe what happened there.

## Major Regions

| Region | World Notes | Campaign Links |
|---|---|---|
| **Bathaes** | Eastern/central imperial region; includes the Bathaen Empire, Borealian Sway, Wheldrake, Halesworth, Crimson March, and Vanlaug-linked northern island territories. | Dungeons III references Halesworth, Basctdelm, Wheldrake, and Lavoruud Wrescot's family context. |
| **Inverland** | Cold, rugged northwest of clans, Vecs, coasts, forests, and Loch Cerule. | Souls of Destiny centers on Adsuren, Camburne, Gibuldon, and Inverland governance. |
| **Jehsle** | Misty southwestern island-chain realm of trade, seafaring, tropical forests, and draconian ancestry sites. | No primary campaign focus yet; available as shared world canon. |
| **Orbansia** | Southern/eastern mainland region of rolling hills, ancient ruins, forests, rivers, and coastal cities. | Heroes of Emberstran uses Baltwood/Emberstran; Bloody Endeavor uses Yag'vur, Rostiena, Dalasor, and Matahal context. |
| **Var'Shala** | Southeastern region born from the fractured remains of old Mahruud; fertile reclaimed lands border the remaining dry Mahruud territory. | Dungeons III and The Silent Vanguard connect to Yearning Vale, Tratta, Hesfal, Scarwatch Hold, and Mahruud. |

## Geography & Locations

Key locations documented in this wiki:

- [[Adsuren]] - village in Inverland on the Wehgyn River; primary Souls of Destiny setting.
- [[Hesfal]] - artisan village in Var'Shala, halfway between Deadfield and Tratta on the Tor March; opening Dungeons III settlement.
- [[Scarwatch Hold]] - renovated old keep on the Jagged Waste Crags overlooking Mahruud; Dungeons III hub.
- [[Tratta]] - great Yearning Vale port city on the Broken Deep; key Silent Vanguard destination.
- [[Trailpoint]] - crossroads town in Severed Bend, Orbansia.
- [[Nunglthil]] - neutral Underdark trading outpost on a black lake.
- [[Abbey of Light]] - former Abbey of Mont Rest, a multi-faith sanctuary and orphanage south of O'naren in the Borealian Sway.
- [[O'naren]] - elven foothill settlement west of Qal'dynn in the Borealian Sway, centered on Driftglow Pond.
- [[Emberstran]] - large city in Baltwood / Orbansia on the Glimmering Sea; major wine and port city, and the main Heroes of Emberstran city.
- [[Ahndashere]] - smaller Baltwood town at the Northern Stoneflow's mouth on the Glimmering Sea, with stilted marsh districts, halfling hill burrows, and elder-appointed civic rule.
- **Gibuldon** - capital city on Loch Cerule in Glenleah / Inverland.
- [[Basctdelm]] - fortified island capital of Halesworth and seat of Bathaen imperial authority on Lake Tribathe.
- **Thrargael** - capital of Mahruud and one of the historically recognized capitals.

## History Anchors

- **1 AF - The Fracturing:** Neera threw Brault from the heavens, creating the Crungess / Great Crevasse and ending the Great War.
- **329 AF:** Bathaen recognizes three empires; Inverland recognizes Vecs.
- **373 AF:** Basctdelm, Gibuldon, and Thrargael become capitals.
- **1227 AF - The Awakening:** the eight major deities stop answering and the New Order rises.
- **1240 AF - Year of Emergence:** Underdark denizens are found more often on the surface.
- **1246 AF - Year of Discovery:** current broad adventure era; heroes are gathered to re-establish the lost domain in Mahruud.
