# Ulgreer

## Scope

World lore - canonical mythic location tied to Diverra's ascension.

## Overview

Ulgreer was a small peaceful village on the southwestern edge of the Western Crimson Pines forest, between Ferndale and Brimhaven. It was known for beautiful sunrise views and local pride in the natural beauty around it.

## Diverra's Ascension

In the most common version of Diverra's ascension myth, the sorceress Diverra confronted the demon Zarak'Nor at Ulgreer after he terrorized the village. Diverra was mortally wounded, but her body began to glow and she ascended as the goddess of love and beauty.

The tragedy at Ulgreer became an important symbol of bravery, sacrifice, hope, and resilience against darkness.
