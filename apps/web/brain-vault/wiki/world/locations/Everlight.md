# Everlight

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences belong in campaign-specific location pages.

## Overview

Everlight is a small, isolated village nestled in a snow-kissed valley within the [[Territories of Myrdae|Arbescar]] territory of [[Territories of Myrdae|Var'Shala]], surrounded by towering frost-covered peaks. It is defined by three things: an artificial sun called **the Everlight** that hangs perpetually in its sky, a divine history rooted in Urlich's mercy, and its position as the only warm haven on the edge of the [[Blustery Waste]].

The village centers on the **Twinkling Fir** — a grand decorated tree in the central square that symbolizes community and hope. Cozy wooden cottages line the streets; spiced cider and cheerful laughter fill the air. Despite its remoteness, Everlight draws wanderers, adventurers, and scholars as both a place of solace and a gateway to the surrounding wilderness: Frostbite Mountain, the Blustery Waste, and the domain of **Lithium**, the Silver Dragon.

## History

Five thousand years ago, the city of **Sunlit Haven** fell — a divine punishment for its rulers' hubris. The gods transformed the surrounding lands into the [[Blustery Waste]], a barren expanse of perpetual storm and sand. However, Urlich took mercy on the kind-hearted villagers who would become Everlight's founders and shielded them within the inner mountains. Urlich granted them two gifts:
- **Perpetual winter** — to insulate them from the corruption that destroyed Sunlit Haven
- **The Everlight** — a radiant artificial sun that hangs eternally in their sky

Over centuries, the founders' descendants transformed their refuge into a thriving community. The Everlight sun became a symbol of hope, unity, and divine balance — and an enduring reminder that their survival was an act of grace.

## Holidays and Traditions

### Winterfest

Annual celebration honoring Urlich and the spirits of light and frost. Villagers light candles, leave offerings at the Shrine, and share stories of hope and perseverance. Garlands of holly and festive decorations drape every building; the community gathers for music and light. Winterfest Eve is when the market is most vibrant — enchanted winter gear, festive treats, and decorative wares.

### Interaction with External Forces

**Frostbite** — A growing, unspecified threat near Frostbite Mountain. The council debates how to respond: some advocate alliances, others call for proactive defenses. Villagers fear Frostbite's power may one day overwhelm the village.

**Lithium the Silver Dragon** — Controls the north route through the mountains. The council maintains a cautious, respectful relationship — offering tributes and gifts in exchange for safe passage and trade opportunities.

## Laws

Citizens who engage in criminal activity are arrested by the guard and charged per the City Ordinances. Cases are brought before the Village Council for judgment.

## Economy and Trade

Largely barter-based; coins supplement transactions. Adventurers often trade artifacts, rare ingredients, or services for supplies. Economy is tight due to isolation.

**Local resources:**
- **Snowberries (Frostberries)** — sweet, refreshing fruit used to make Snowberry Jam and Snowberry Wine
- **Frostwood** — durable wood from frost-resistant trees; used in construction, tools, weapons, and furniture
- **Crystalline Veins** — crystals mined from nearby caves; used for magical enhancements or sold to passing mages and alchemists

**Specialty exports:** High-quality frost-resistant cloaks, boots, and gloves; enchanted winter gear from the Frostforge.

## Religion

### Urlich the Hearthkeeper

Everlight worships **[[Urlich]]** under the epithet "the Hearthkeeper" — a guardian of winter, frost, and ice whose symbol is a glowing flame or sun cradled by frosted branches, representing the balance between warmth and cold.

> **Canon note:** The wider Myrdae Pantheon describes Urlich as "The Regent of Ice" — cold is purity, cold strips away weakness, very few followers. Everlight's tradition emphasizes Urlich's *protective* and *merciful* aspect: the god who shielded their ancestors from the Blustery Waste and gave them the Everlight. These are compatible but distinct faces of the same deity. Everlight may be one of the few places where Urlich is actively venerated with warmth rather than fear.

**Beliefs:** The Hearthkeeper blesses those who foster community, offer warmth to the lost, and protect others. Acts of kindness — sharing food, offering shelter — are sacred duties.

**The Balance of Elements:** Villagers also respect spirits of nature and the elements (especially fire and light), which require offerings to maintain balance.

**Religious Practices:**
- *Daily offerings* — candles, food, or carved wooden tokens at the Shrine or household hearths; snowberry branches are traditional
- *Traveler's Blessing* — adventurers visit the Shrine for protection before heading into the wilds
- *Hearth Ritual* — each household maintains a flame that must never go out during winter; a symbol of hope and endurance
