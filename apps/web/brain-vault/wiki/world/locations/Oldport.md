# Oldport

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography and mythic history of [[Myrdae]].

## Overview

Oldport is in Dunmoore, a southeastern [[Regions of Myrdae|Inverland]] territory. In world myth, it is strongly associated with Osanna, the Smiling Duchess, and the origin of the Festival of Fair Winds.

## Osanna Tale

During a dangerous period of shipwrecks, monstrous sea creatures, strange currents, and storms in the Tideless Span, a mysterious woman named Osanna appeared in Oldport. She taught sailors and traders to read the sea: favorable winds, monster seasons, and safer sailing times.

Oldport came to see Osanna not just as lucky but as fortune embodied. When she left, the people began the **Festival of Fair Winds** on the anniversary of her arrival. Sailors, traders, and townsfolk gather to exchange sea stories, share fortunes, and honor the goddess who once walked among them.
