# Basctdelm

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences belong in campaign-specific location pages.

## Overview

Basctdelm is the capital of [[Territories of Myrdae|Halesworth]] and the seat of imperial authority for the Bathaen Empire. It rises from a massive island on the eastern edge of [[Lake Tribathe]], separated from the mainland by water except for two controlled crossings. The city was shaped first for defense and governance and only secondarily for trade; its isolation is intentional. Basctdelm is not a crossroads, but a stronghold.

The city ascends from east to northwest. At the water's edge, the Trade District sits where the [[Dibaryn River]] meets Lake Tribathe before flowing east toward the Broken Deep. Docks, warehouses, customs halls, and merchant enclaves make this the busiest and most diverse quarter. Inland, the city rises through the Lower District toward the High District, whose walls, towers, noble estates, law centers, and ruling institutions crown the island's northwestern heights.

Basctdelm connects to the mainland through two major crossings: the **Star Reach Bridge** to the northeast toward Wheldrake and the **Historic Crossway Bridge** to the southeast through the Lower District into Halesworth. Both are monitored and defensible. From Lake Tribathe, Basctdelm appears as a stone citadel of bridges, towers, waterfalls, banners, patrols, and watched commerce.

## History

Basctdelm was founded as an instrument of order rather than a commercial city. Its island position let early rulers control movement between the Dibaryn River and Lake Tribathe, adjudicate disputes among neighboring territories, and rule from a neutral seat removed from local ambitions. Early administration relied on appointed regents and custodial governors from legal, military, and scholarly traditions. That structure was effective, but surrounding lands often saw Basctdelm as an imposed arbiter rather than a protector.

As the city grew, authority shifted toward the **Compact Lineages**, noble houses that controlled civic functions such as defense, taxation, river traffic, and law. Their shared rule kept civil war at bay but bred corruption, secret alliances, and deadlock.

House Talward rose during one of these periods of paralysis. The Talwards first became stabilizing stewards, then consolidated power through legal reform, strategic marriages, and the quiet removal of dissent. Their rule unified Basctdelm, but old grievances from the displaced lineages remain under the city's orderly surface.

King Irion Talward, grandfather of Moldan Talward, laid the foundation for the Bathaen Empire by forging a binding pact with the leaders of Palason and Qal'dynn. The pact recognized Basctdelm as the empire's keystone while balancing shared responsibilities and mutual defense. The Awakening shattered that old framework as borders changed and territories splintered or drew closer to imperial protection. After King Moldan Talward's assassination, Queen Breya Talward inherited the throne and rebuilt the imperial accord to fit the changed world.

## Districts and Citizenry

Basctdelm has a population of almost 2,000,000 people. Roughly three-fourths live in the island city proper, with the rest in surrounding neighborhoods and farmland.

The city proper is divided into five districts:

| District | Notes |
|---|---|
| High District | Northwestern heights; ruling institutions, noble estates, centers of law, and imperial power. |
| North District | City district inside the water-ringed capital; source lists several civic and commercial anchors here. |
| Central District | Interior city district between the high authority zones and lower/trade areas. |
| South District | City district inside the walls. |
| Trade District | Shoreline district at the Dibaryn / Lake Tribathe meeting point; docks, customs, warehouses, merchants, and most arrivals. |

The **Lower District** lies just outside the walls on the southwestern side of the city. Basctdelm is highly diverse; the source says almost any ancestry can be seen there.

## Government

Basctdelm is ruled by **Queen Breya Talward**, who ascended in 1218 AF after the sudden death of her husband, King Moldan Talward, at age 34. By the source's present frame she has ruled for nearly three decades. She has two children: Alskar, born in 1208 AF, and Feyna, born in 1211 AF.

Queen Breya is described as tall and slender, with sharp elegant features, brunette hair marked by a silver streak, a royal blue and gold gown, and a sapphire-set crown. Her presence is stern and commanding, but the source emphasizes that she listens carefully and weighs her words.

The queen's personal guards are known as **Talward's Tend**.

## Law and Security

Basctdelm has strict laws and swift justice. People charged with crimes are arrested by the Basctdelm Watch and brought before a justiciar under the City Ordinances.

The **Basctdelm Watch** patrols the streets in royal blue tabards trimmed with gold and marked by a golden griffon. Mounted patrols are common, and griffon riders answer threats beyond the reach of foot patrols.

The **Basctdelm Weave Wardens** police misuse of magic. They work alongside the Watch but usually avoid obvious uniforms, blending in as scholars, merchants, laborers, or other ordinary citizens while investigating arcane abuses.

## Economy and Trade

Basctdelm has access to most goods and services expected of a large city. Commerce is strongly regulated because most imports, exports, and visitors pass through the Trade District's docks, customs halls, and merchant enclaves.

The source's service table confirms public availability for many standard urban trades and services, including apothecaries, bakers, barbers, bathhouses, bookshops and scribes, butchers, carpenters, coopers, fletchers, general stores, herbalists, inns, jewelers, armories, weaponsmiths, stables, tailors, tanners, taverns, temples, thatchers, wainwrights, and weavers. The same table explicitly marks magic shops as not generally available.

## Religion

Basctdelm contains shrines, temples, and churches representing the gods worshipped by the people of Myrdae. The source specifically names the **Temple of Tiash** and **Shrine to Addan** among the numbered city locations, though broader religious detail is marked as needing expansion.

## Notable Locations

**Braethyn's Archives** is the largest library in Basctdelm. It includes an admittance area where visitors are cleared, a main library with books and scrolls arranged through a massive three-story chamber, a restricted fourth-floor Preserved Collection for rare archives, and an undercroft where scribes transcribe and preserve loose materials. Visitors must sign in and be cleared before access; rare items require authorization and an escort.

**Double Mice Inn** is the largest and most popular inn in the Lower District. The source describes it as a sprawling three-story establishment with poor-quality but welcoming charm, a busy tavern, cheap-to-comfortable lodging, and food and drink served with a half-salt rim.

**Last Prince Inn** lists Finn Brom as owner, Yvonne Brom as co-owner, and Angelika Brom as attendant. Its lodging and meal pricing matches the common lower-to-comfortable inn pattern in the source.

**Monbereth's Armory** offers many standard armor and weapons, with space in the source for unique smith goods.

**One for Everyone** is marked as a brothel and bath-house.

**Strongbow** is a fletcher. The source has a placeholder owner name in one section, but describes the shop's signature displayed bow as a silver-branch weapon with a bright blue string and dark blue leather grip.

**Talward Garrison** houses a large guard force. The source says there are over 1,000 guards in the watch, though it appears to contain an accidental "Stoneshore watch" wording in this Basctdelm section. Listed ranks include warden, sergeant, lieutenant, captain, major, and commander.

**The Promissory** is an opulent establishment for deals, treasures, relics, maps, and expeditions. A later section notes a basement accessible only to members of **Dissent Oblation**.

**Vault of Basctdelm** is part of the **Reserve** network of repositories across Myrdae and follows the standard vault rules in the Myrdae DM reference.
