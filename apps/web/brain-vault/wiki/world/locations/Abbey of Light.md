# Abbey of Light

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography and site reference for [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences should be recorded in campaign-specific pages if a party visits the abbey.

## Overview

The Abbey of Light, formerly the **Abbey of Mont Rest**, is a sanctuary south of [[O'naren]]. It sits at the summit of a tall foothill descending from the Distancion Mountains into the [[Territories of Myrdae|Borealian Sway]].

The abbey was originally dedicated to Neera of the Old Gods. It has since broadened into a place of study, reverence, and sanctuary for all gods of Myrdae. The site is built from natural mountain stone and has stood for roughly 150 years.

The abbey houses scholars of divine lore, monks, clergy, pilgrims, and an orphanage for abandoned or displaced children.

## Stone Giant Pact

Stone Giants from the surrounding Distancion foothills aided the abbey's original construction and have remained its silent guardians. They do not enter the abbey, but they patrol the surrounding hills and high paths. Their bond with the abbey is old, sacred, and unexplained.

Visible signs of the pact include:

- Stone Giant silhouettes at dawn and dusk.
- Carved runes on nearby stones.
- Giant patrols echoing through the valleys.
- A law called the Guardians' Accord, which permanently banishes anyone who harms, deceives, or exploits the giants.

## Citizenry

The abbey's residents are predominantly high elves, humans, and half-elves. Dwarves, halflings, gnomes, tieflings, occasional half-orcs, and dragonborn make up about a quarter of the population.

Most residents are tied to prayer, study, healing, protection, teaching, or service. A large part of the community is the orphanage, overseen by the Nurturing Light. Children come from across the Borealian Sway after famine, plague, conflict, abandonment, or displacement.

## Four Orders

| Order | Head | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Nurturing Light** | Matron Elbrade Mallow, female half-elf | Caretakers of the orphanage and healers of body and spirit. They oversee the orphanage, infirmary, gardens, and communal kitchens. |
| **Protective Light** | Warden Tersic Valegar, male human | Defenders of the abbey, guardians of sacred relics, and enforcers of spiritual boundaries. They oversee guards, sanctum vaults, and outer walls. |
| **Reflective Light** | Parson Quinel Drael, male high elf | Scholars, diplomats, mediators, and doctrine keepers. They oversee archives, lecture halls, civic correspondence, and public doctrine. They quietly support causes like the Tegenwald Phet. |
| **Rising Light** | Luminary Sister Ysolde Vern, female high elf | Leaders of ritual, memory, faith, transitions, and sacred oaths. They oversee ceremonial halls and services. |

## Monks of Lighted Breath

The Monks of Lighted Breath are a distinct tradition within the abbey. They combine martial discipline, healing, breath work, and herbcraft. Their philosophy teaches that each inhalation draws in life and each exhalation carries away suffering.

They cultivate herbs for braziers, teas, pipes, poultices, salves, incense, and ritual distillations. They also lead the abbey in the monthly Silent Breath observance.

## Attire

Most abbey residents wear simple beige linen or flax garments with practical dark brown or rusty-crimson leather. Each order has a distinct accent:

- **Nurturing Light:** dark brown accents or sashes.
- **Reflective Light:** white accents or sashes.
- **Protective Light:** rusty-crimson accents or sashes.
- **Rising Light:** golden-yellow accents or sashes.
- **Monks of Lighted Breath:** golden-toned knee-length robes over loose brown pants wrapped from calf to ankle.

## Holidays And Traditions

- **Children's Day of Dawning:** spring festival honoring the orphans. Overseen by the Nurturing Light, with plays, songs, games, storytelling, dancing, and a feast where children are served first.
- **The Silent Breath:** once per harmon, the Monks of Lighted Breath lead a day of silence, meditation, mindful breathing, and herbal smoke throughout the cloisters and halls.

## Government

The abbey recognizes no worldly lord, though it keeps respectful ties with nearby settlements and the Elle'berian crown. Its autonomy rests on spiritual standing, neutrality, scholarship, diplomacy, and the Stone Giant guardians.

It is governed by a council formed from the Four Orders rather than by a single abbot or abbess.

### Order Leaders

- **Matron Elbrade Mallow:** former field medic turned abbot; fiercely protective of the children and beloved by many.
- **Warden Tersic Valegar:** broad-shouldered, greying, loyal to abbey tradition, and increasingly troubled by quiet leadership shifts.
- **Parson Quinel Drael:** precise high elf teacher whose influence has shaped Qal'dynn diplomacy for decades.
- **Luminary Sister Ysolde Vern:** ritual leader whose voice has guided thousands through rites of passage.

## Laws

The abbey's code is rooted in sanctuary and mercy:

1. **Sanctity of Sanctuary:** violence, bloodshed, and vengeance are forbidden inside the abbey; even fugitives receive temporary protection until judged by the council.
2. **Oath of Truth:** deception in abbey business is sacrilege; false witness can mean exile and loss of sanctuary.
3. **Balance of Service:** every resident contributes through labor, study, or devotion.
4. **Reverence of Relics:** sacred relics and archives are inviolable; unauthorized access can mean confinement, fasting penance, or expulsion.
5. **Mercy in Judgment:** discipline aims at correction and restoration.
6. **Guardians' Accord:** the Stone Giant bond is sacred; harm, deception, or exploitation against the giants means permanent banishment.

## Economy And Trade

The abbey sustains itself through gardens, orchards, fields, foothill herds, preserved stores, alms, pilgrim offerings, noble patronage, and support from the Bathaen Empire for the orphanage.

The Monks of Lighted Breath sell and trade herbal teas, poultices, incense, candles, salves, smokes, mead, pipes, and hookahs. The Reflective Light copies and preserves records. The Nurturing Light offers healing in exchange for donations. The Protective Light sometimes escorts caravans through dangerous routes.

## Religion

The abbey began as a sanctuary to Neera, but in recent decades it has become a multi-faith sanctuary for all deities of Myrdae. The Great Hall of Radiance contains alcoves to many gods, and ceremonies emphasize reverence rather than dogma.

The Four Orders interpret faith through compassion, doctrine, vigilance, and ritual. The Monks of Lighted Breath practice religion through breath, herbcraft, and merciful discipline.
