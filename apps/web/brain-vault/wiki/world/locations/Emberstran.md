# Emberstran

## Scope

World lore - canonical city geography, civic structure, and public setting. Campaign-specific events in Emberstran belong on the Heroes of Emberstran location page, not here.

## Overview

Emberstran is a large coastal city in eastern [[Orbansia]], on the [[Glimmering Sea]], south of the [[Severed Crease]] and near Mount Emberstran. It is the capital of [[Baltwood]] and one of the major trade and wine cities of Myrdae.

Before the Awakening, the settlement was Winbalt, a growing town near the northeastern Baltwood Forest. When Mount Emberstran erupted during the Awakening, the coast changed, the Glimmering Sea expanded, and much of Baltwood was consumed or transformed. Refugees gathered in Winbalt, and the renamed city became both a memorial to disaster and a symbol of survival on the changed shore.

Emberstran is known for its volcanic soil, vineyards, wine trade, port commerce, shipbuilding, fishing, and layered civic power. It is also a city of sharp contrasts: wealthy elevated neighborhoods in the north and fog-bound docks, warehouses, taverns, and working-class streets in the south.

## Economy and Trade

Emberstran's economy rests on coastal trade, fertile volcanic vineyards, fishing, shipbuilding, and the taxes and tariffs gathered through its port. Wine is the city's signature export, and the volcanic soil around Mount Emberstran gives the surrounding vineyards unusual value.

Three professional guilds carry special influence:

| Guild | Leader | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vintner's Guild | Tarathial Rulendor, female high elf | Controls much of the wine economy; Tarathial is stern, pragmatic, and politically important. |
| Shipbuilder's Guild | Janri Torwell, male human | Supports harbor infrastructure, vessels, repair, and maritime labor. |
| Fisher's Consortium | Marisa Winbrook, female human | Coordinates fishing crews and fish-market interests; Marisa is known as warm and welcoming. |

The source also records a broad services table. Emberstran has common urban trades such as apothecaries, bakers, bathhouses, chandlers, coopers, fletchers, foundries, general stores, herbalists, inns, jewelers, mapmakers, mills, shrines, smithies, shipwrights, stables, tailors, taverns, temples, and textile trades. It does not present public access to services such as a university, magic shop, vault or bank, formal library, large forum, or public infirmary.

## Government

Emberstran is a free city governed by an elected Lord or Lady Protector, chosen every six years. In 1246 AF, the Lord Protector is Lord Garod Dusklight.

The Lord Protector is supported by the Council of Five:

| Office | Official | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Magistrate | Igabell Orebane, female dwarf | Legal disputes, criminal verdicts, and justice. |
| Collector | Doyt Torwell, male human | Taxes, tariffs, and city revenue. |
| Advisor | Imonorra Asuun, female human | History, magic, and arcane affairs. |
| Minister | Terelda Cinderbell, female halfling | Public relations, religion, morale, and civic confidence. |
| Hand | Baetheas Maldehr, male half-elf | Informants, especially around the port, taverns, and trade networks. |

Three appointed captains coordinate mercenary defense and patrol:

| Captain | Area |
|---|---|
| Amilla Stassyr, female human | North |
| Mordan Pass, male human | Western |
| Vammad Peakwarder, male stone goliath | Southern |

Emberstran has no formal city militia or standing army. Law enforcement and defense are handled through hired mercenary bands coordinated by the Lord Protector and the captains.

## Law and Enforcement

City law is organized through written ordinances and Magistrate hearings. Hired patrols may arrest suspects, but accused citizens are brought before a Magistrate appointee for a hearing. Straightforward cases are handled quickly; complex cases may escalate to the Magistrate directly. Punishments can include fines, exile, labor assignments, or imprisonment.

## Districts and Citizenry

The southern port district contains docks, warehouses, taverns, shops, working-class housing, and much of the city's daily maritime labor. It is busy, noisy, foggy, and commercially dense.

The northern district is elevated and wealthier. It contains manors, gardens, broad roads, temples, civic institutions, and the Lord's estate on a central hill. The city walls protect the urban core; farms, vineyards, rural homes, and roads spread outside the boundary.

The citizenry is mostly human, with halflings, elves, and dwarves common enough to be familiar. Tieflings, gnomes, goliaths, gabuks, and dragonborn are present in smaller numbers. The city has a large middle class, visible dockside poverty, and a wealthy upper city.

## Holidays and Traditions

**Emberfast** is celebrated on the first day of Vaegrum in memory of Winbalt becoming Emberstran. The city lights lanterns, fire basins, and bonfires; people tell stories of the day the world changed, wear ember masks, eat ash-cakes, and hear bells. Nobles host the Fire's Ascent Ball, and the Lord Protector gives a public speech.

**Murkroot Eve** is held on the eve of the first full moon of Endelo'ar in Talil, recorded as the 27th. It remembers the Baltwood forests swallowed during the Awakening. Customs include blackthorn or ash branches over doors, bark and moss masks, rootcake, and ghost stories.

**Knock Twice for Vine's Blessing** is a winemaking superstition. Vintners, merchants, and drinkers knock twice on a barrel, cask, gate, wagon, or glass before drawing wine or making a toast.

## Religion

Emberstran has shrines to many gods, but local devotion emphasizes Crael, Osanna, and Diverra.

- Crael is tied to fate, death, and the city's survival through disaster.
- Osanna, Lady Luck, is tied to trade winds, sea fortunes, commerce, and the port.
- Diverra is tied to vineyards, gardens, wine, festivals, romance, and the social life of the city.

Key religious sites include Lady Luck's Temple, the Temple to Diverra, and the Church to Crael.

## Notable Locations

**Anchor Point** is an inn and tavern on Grand Pier in the Docks. It overlooks the harbor and serves sailors, merchants, and travelers. Calro Tunn, a one-eyed half-elf former sailor, runs it with fair prices and a strict rule against dockside brawls.

**Imonorra's Tower** stands near the northeast edge of the city. It is a four-story grey stone and black iron tower with wooded grounds, wards, displacement sigils, fire runes, illusions, a study, library, observatory, and private quarters. Imonorra Asuun lives there with apprentice Dathriel Runewish and Brunna, a sentient emberling fire elemental familiar.

**The Lord's Keep** is Lord Garod Dusklight's residence and the city's symbolic political center. It stands at the highest elevation in the northern district, with four stories, four towers, black ironwork, council rooms, private quarters, and views over the docks.

**The Market** is the open-air trading hub of the dock district, busiest in late morning. Named vendors include Marah Nell, Tolgar Freen, Serra Ol'brook, Worn Kaldmir, Eebie Talbot, and Gabbie Gluster.

**Old Oak & Grapes** is a refined artistic tavern in the northern district. It is known for interactive performances, including murder mysteries. Otith Hunkel owns it; Meglyn Whistlebell performs there, while Pem and Ledra serve guests.

**Points & Edges** is a dockside blacksmith offering quality weapons and armor. Marcen Vaelrick owns it, Yorel Orebender works as a dwarven smith, and Tassa Winmere handles customers.

**Raddic's Rarities** is a large dockside specialty shop with rare finds, curiosities, distant goods, and minor magic. Raddic Haldir, a half-elf merchant, runs it with Irog, a half-orc guard.

**The Silver Spaulder** is an upscale armory near the docks owned by Vyaanis Aeldorien, a male drow. His assistants are Nebist and Lombit Wimplerag, deep gnome brothers. Specialty pieces include Silver-Edged Plate, Vyaanis Link Mail, and Crimson Ceremonial Breastplate.

**Temple to Diverra** is a pale stone, red wood, and gilded metal temple in the northern district. The source names High Priestess Maladra Sent, acolytes Cokin and Tarly, and altarboy Arnol.

**Warm Bear Inn** is a lodge-style inn near the southeastern gates for hunters, trappers, and adventurers. Gustav Beargrin runs it, Fynotta Cragfury cooks and brews, and Booth Havarr assists guests.
