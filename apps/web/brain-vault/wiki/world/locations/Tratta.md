# Tratta

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences are in campaign-specific location pages.

## Overview

Tratta is a large city in [[Var'Shala]] at map coordinate **I - 33**. It is the capital of the [[Territories of Myrdae|Yearning Vale]] and stands on the coast of the Broken Deep.

The city is known for bustling sea trade, sandstone architecture with domes and arches, a renowned arena, a grand theater, and an ancient mage tower.

## World Significance

- The Yearning Vale's rivers and swamplands run toward the Broken Sea and Tratta.
- In 777 PF, the Runic Brotherhood, Leybearers, and Nightweave merged in Tratta to form the [[Factions of Myrdae|Tegenwald Phet]].
- The [[Territories of Myrdae|Yearning Slough]] lies southwest of Tratta and is home to the ancient gray dragon Zovahesdria.
