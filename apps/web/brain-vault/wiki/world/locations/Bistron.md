# Bistron

## Scope

World lore - canonical location connected to the Laztyr and Suthrin story.

## Overview

Bistron is a village where, in Sultra of 1224, a group of traveling companions arrived as villagers were going missing. Their search led to nearby mines whose old rails had been repurposed by the Barrenscars for slave traffic through the Severed Crease into the Mahruud.

## Laztyr Incident

Near an attack on Barrenscar slave traders, the companions discovered a passage to an ancient cult temple to Brault. The temple held the shattered [[Laztyr]], a crystal artifact used to recall Suthrin from exile in Stygia.

The source implies Suthrin may have returned from the hells and attacked the slave traders before becoming loose on the surface of Myrdae.
