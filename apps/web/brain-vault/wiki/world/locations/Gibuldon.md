# Gibuldon

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences belong in campaign-specific location pages (see [[SoD - Gibuldon]] for active campaign context).

## Overview

Gibuldon is the capital of [[Territories of Myrdae|Inverland]] and the seat of the **Dicon**, Inverland's governing body. It sits in the Glenleah territory and is home to the **Grand Tower** (headquarters of the Lords of Magic) and the **MEAD Society**, which carries out the Dicon's investigative work. The city replaced [[Dhá Chaomhnóir]] as Inverland's capital roughly 100 years ago, when Inverland's leaders decided money and political power should not share the same city.

Gibuldon is described as a large city and is the seat of **House Reyvennra** (a powerful Vesci-tier noble house). It has connections to multiple active campaign threads in SoD.

## Notable Locations

### Crown & Arms

A large tavern that doubles as a house of ill repute. Interior: open standing/seating area, bar, stage, and stairs to an upstairs balcony. A minstrel performs on the stage. Food is notably good; all drinks served with a half-salt rim. Some patrons use upstairs rooms with companions.

NPCs: Owner, co-owner, and attendant roles exist — names not yet assigned.

### Grand Tower

Headquarters of the **Lords of Magic**. The Lords originally occupied the top of the Vault pyramid in [[Dhá Chaomhnóir]] before the capital transfer to Gibuldon ~100 years ago, which is why the Dhá Vault retains a teleportation circle.

### MEAD Society Headquarters

The **MEAD Society** (Meet, Expose, Ascertain and Discover) is an investigative organization that does the practical work of the Dicon. Previously based at "Ms. V's cottage." Caretakers are the **Pallas** — green-skinned goblins who drive carriages and tend the gardens.
