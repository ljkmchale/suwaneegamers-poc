# Dhá Chaomhnóir

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences belong in campaign-specific location pages.

## Overview

**Dhá Chaomhnóir** (commonly called simply **Dhá**) is a major city on the southern coast of [[Territories of Myrdae|Inverland]], in the [[Territories of Myrdae|Aelbon]] territory. It houses the largest Vault of the Realm Depository & Exchange (RDE) in all of [[Myrdae]] and boasts more wealthy permanent residents than any other city on the continent.

Dhá is one of the busiest ports in Inverland, benefiting from idyllic weather, calm water, and easy trade routes to mainland Myrdae. It is both a hub for Inverland imports/exports and a year-round home for the ultra-elite — unlike most cities, the wealthy here do not merely visit seasonally. The city also has a substantial working class and an active criminal element.

Streets and neighborhoods are well-marked and largely defined by class or purpose. The iconography of the **Guardians** — the twin stone golems housed in the Vault — appears throughout the city.

Dhá was historically the capital of Inverland. About 100 years ago the Dicon moved the capital to Gibuldon, believing that money and political power should not share the same city. The old capital grounds ([[Old Dicon House]]) remain as public property.

## Organizations

### Cur Family
The most powerful family in Dhá. Crest: a lion with a heart. They control many of the brothels and gambling houses throughout Dunmoore; gold-laden wagons transport payments from those operations back to the Vaults. The family leader is titled **Don Cur**. Multiple family-run establishments operate in the city:
- **Puss & Boots** (Map #15) — brothel; run by Steiner (Cur cousin); used for money laundering
- **A Bud in Bloom** (Map #21) — bathhouse/spa; run by Lornan Cur (Don Cur's adult daughter); used for money laundering

### Lionheart
A mob-like criminal syndicate. Identified by a lion insignia on the heart. Presents itself as a group guarding Dhá.

### Populi Verde
Environmental enthusiasts who worship **Madre Verde**, whom they mistakenly conflate with "The Mother." Reviled by most towns in the region. Involved in the takedown of **The Woeborn** (a cult of Suthrin) and were briefly "Wanted" in Dhá (wrongfully accused of the city's crime). The Great Houses have since kept them out of the city. They now patrol roads outside Dhá and hassle merchants who fail to show proper respect to Madre Verde.

Former leader: **Ainran**, a powerful and charismatic figure who was the subject of a Cur family bounty. Ainran disappeared after leaving for the most recent All Bright ceremony — the Greens believe he was taken by the Mother to become a god; most others assume he was killed for the bounty.

The Mother's Shrine (Map #24), located in the hills outside the city gates, is their unofficial base.

### The Woeborn *(Defunct)*
A cult that worshiped Suthrin. Taken down by the Populi Verde.

### Realm Depository & Exchange (RDE)
The banking network spanning Myrdae. The Dhá Vault is the largest in the Reserve network. See [[The Vaults]] section below.

## Government

Dhá is governed by several powerful **Great Houses** in an uneasy truce, each controlling different aspects of the city. The Cur family is the dominant power.

- **Don Cur** — Cur family leader; most powerful figure in the city

## Notable Locations

### The Vaults — Realm Depository & Exchange (1)

The largest Vault in all of Myrdae. A pyramid-shaped building surrounded by 30' circular walls at the city's center. Three entrances, each staffed with round-the-clock security. Open gravel grounds inside the outer wall with four smaller structures housing personnel.

**Bottom floor:** Circular chamber (50' tall walls). Two large stone Guardian golems seat on thrones in the center — they animate if security is breached. Twelve wooden doors (elvish numerals 1-12 arranged like a clock), sorted by client wealth:
- Door 12 = wealthiest clients
- Doors 10-11 = wealthy
- Doors 3-9 = well-off merchants
- Doors 4-8 = middle class
- Doors 1-3 = poorest clients
Additional guards posted at doors 9-12. Vaults are 300' halls with 5' niches every 10' for clients.

**Upper chamber:** 30' stairs rise to two 15'x15' outer rooms (for RDE transfer business) and a locked inner room housing a **teleportation circle** (with sigil) used for transferring assets between Vaults and — for the elite — personal travel. The Lords of Magic originally occupied the top of the Vault pyramid, which is why the teleportation circle exists here.

**Personnel:** All guards, associates, and Vault Keepers are male elves. Monks are hooded, various races/genders; speak only when spoken to; never reveal names. Associates are multilingual with excellent memories and math aptitude.

### House of Krem (4)

Once a powerful noble house. The original Warlock Krem staffed it with undead. He and his concubine were found murdered in their bed; the house was nearly burned down. Cur family led the cleanup and investigation. Now in disrepair, occupied by **Lionel** (stepson of Warlock Krem). Heir Stacia (nee Lillitu) married Kivuss (Lionel's brother) and left Dhá with the family money.
