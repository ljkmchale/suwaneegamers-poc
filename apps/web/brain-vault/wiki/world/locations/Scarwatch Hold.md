# Scarwatch Hold

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences are in `wiki/locations/Dungeons III/`.

## Overview

Scarwatch Hold is a medium settlement and repaired border keep in [[Var'Shala]]. The Myrdae World Guide places it at map coordinate **K - 34**. It sits atop the Jagged Waste Crags on the western border of the [[Territories of Myrdae|Yearning Vale]], overlooking the [[Territories of Myrdae|Mahruud]].

The hold rises from the crags in pale stone walls and towers. Sections of darker replacement masonry mark places where collapse and fire damaged the keep during the Awakening. Its design is practical rather than grand: conical-roofed towers, narrow arrow slits, worn drill yards, lookout platforms, gatehouses, stables, shops, a sanctuary, and a citadel built for command and defense.

Scarwatch Hold has always been a border keep, not a seat of power. Its purpose is vigilance: monitoring movement through the crags, guarding the eastern approaches of the Yearning Vale, and serving as the last warning before danger spills westward. Though damaged during the Awakening, it was never abandoned.

It is reached from [[Hesfal]] by road and serves as the nearest fortified hub for settlements and travelers near the Jagged Waste Crags.

## Government and Defense

The Warden governs the Hold from the Citadel. The source names **Naevaris Haliun** as Warden: a tall, muscular human guard captain with salt-and-pepper hair, strategic judgment, and a just battlefield temperament.

Naevaris is supported by two advisors:

| Figure | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nambra Maerth | Advisor, mage | Female drow; cautious, refined, arcane, and difficult to please. |
| Umlin Stonewatch | Advisor, scribe | Male dwarf; scholarly, fastidious, widely read, and curmudgeonly. |

The hold's guard structure includes a garrison at the Inner Gatehouse, watches in the Palisades, guards at the bank, gate guards at the Outer Gatehouse, and patrols around the hold. Captain **Nikalia** leads the Inner Gatehouse garrison. The source describes her as strong, just, experienced, and respected.

The Citadel can muster up to thirty-six trained soldiers in a crisis. It also shelters emergency resources, municipal chambers, armed forces, private quarters, guest rooms, a provisioned undercroft, a cistern, and a guarded dungeon with four stout cells.

Scarwatch Hold acts as a well-armed judicial guardian for parts of the Yearning Vale, Gaelscape, and Mahruud.

## Law

Criminal activity in Scarwatch Hold is handled by the guard and charged under city ordinances. The source does not provide a full legal code, but places arrest and enforcement under the Hold's guard and Warden-led civic structure.

## Notable Locations

**Outer Gatehouse** is the first stop for visitors. It has two stone towers, a raised wooden drawbridge over a dry moat, an iron portcullis, oak doors, arrow slits, and roof battlements. Visitors are challenged before entry. Gate guards **Lynion** and **Atreise** can lower the bridge and give directions around the Hold.

**Inner Gatehouse** separates the civilian area from the Palisades and Citadel. It houses on-duty soldiers, has a massive oak double door, murder holes, and battlements. **Nikalia** commands a garrison of twenty-four soldiers here.

**Barn & Stables** shelters livestock, mounts, and wagons. It has stalls, pens, a hayloft, a trough, and wagon storage. **Ireosh Barridge** owns the barn, and **Lyerith Lilbuck** works there with her mouse companion Puff. Services include animal boarding, dairy products, wagon storage, farrier work, feed, and washing.

**Bank** stores residents' and visitors' valuables. It is not part of the Realm Depository & Exchange System. **Thiddle Ferndew**, a frugal and talkative gnome banker, runs the counter. The bank has a service counter, iron grate, vault, strongboxes, deposits, withdrawals, and small loans up to 10 gp at 5 sp interest per day.

**Citadel** is the Warden's seat, command center, refuge, and defensive core. It includes an armory, barracks, great hall, offices, private chambers, towers, undercroft, cistern, provision stores, and dungeon cells.

**Palisades** are the defenders' training and living grounds. The area includes a performance or jousting field, cavalry stable, greenhouse and gardens, four guard towers, and constant watches. **Thak**, a half-orc sergeant of the guard, and **Dempsey**, an elven lead guard, are named here.

**Smithy**, **General Goods**, **Mercantile**, **Trader's Market**, **The Hoot & Howl Tavern**, **Sanctuary**, **Watchtower Inn**, **Ilho's Gems**, **Private Apartments**, and **Scarwatch House** are confirmed map locations.
