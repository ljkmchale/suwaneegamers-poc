# Gevakaln

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences belong in campaign-specific location pages (see [[Bloody Endeavor]] for active campaign context).

## Overview

Gevakaln is a coastal city in the [[Territories of Myrdae|Rostiena Foothills]] with approximately 23,000 residents, most within the city borders and some in surrounding farms. It projects a clean, respectable image to outsiders while being deeply corrupt underneath. The city is divided into three distinct districts — Fogwalk at the bottom, Brydove Heights at the top — and watches over the island prison **Sciogost** offshore.

Gevakaln is known in the Bloody Endeavor campaign as the seat of the **Sable Shadows** criminal organization.

### Sciogost

The island **Sciogost** is watched over by Gevakaln and serves as Rostiena's most dangerous prison. Currently holds approximately 360 humanoid prisoners.

## Government

Ruler: **Alred Brydove** (Male High-elf Noble). He seldom leaves his keep and governs through representatives who report city happenings to him. The city takes its heights district name — Brydove Heights — from the ruling family.

*(Note: the gazetteer lists "Arryn Daetoris" as a government subsection. This is a copy-paste error from the Climbor gazetteer, where Arryn Daetoris is one of the four Viers Ealdormen. He has no documented role in Gevakaln.)*

## Districts

Gevakaln presents a respectable face to visitors while hiding significant corruption beneath. The city is layered into three districts:

| District | Character |
|---|---|
| **Fogwalk Underbelly** | Slums; high crime; high guard presence to contain it |
| **Mittler Ward** | Middle-class district |
| **Brydove Heights** | Wealthy district; home of the ruling class |

## Laws

Follows standard **Stoneshore Territory** law — no additional city-specific ordinances. The city's orderly public appearance belies how thoroughly corrupt it is in practice. Violations are brought before city authority per territory standards.
