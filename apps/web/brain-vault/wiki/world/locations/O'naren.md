# O'naren

## Scope

World lore - canonical settlement geography, civic structure, and public setting.

## Overview

O'naren is a small elven settlement in the wooded foothills west of [[Qal'dynn]], in the [[Territories of Myrdae|Borealian Sway]]. It serves as a waypoint for travelers on the road between Qal'dynn and Raselle, and as a quiet place of contemplation, craftsmanship, lorekeeping, mining, and worship.

The town is centered on Driftglow Pond, a pristine circular pond fed by cascading falls from the hills. A small island stands at the pond's center, crowned by an ancient tree with striking pink foliage. The tree is believed to be a relic of the Qal'daefar, the original elves of Myrdae.

O'naren's architecture blends finely carved stone and wood with the surrounding gray-green forest. Buildings with high arches wrap the eastern and southern edges of the pond, while terraced foothills hold additional homes, shops, the mines, and public structures. O'naren heraldry includes the Elle'berian pearl-white orb and crescent, as well as the town emblem of the mountain boar.

## History

O'naren began as a small elven outpost west of Qal'dynn, positioned to protect the larger elven city from threats emerging out of the western territories. The surrounding foothills proved rich in ore, and mining quickly became important to the settlement's development.

The region also provided abundant wildlife, food, hardwood, natural dyes, herbs, and alchemical ingredients. Over time, O'naren evolved from a military outpost into a respected elven town known for natural harmony, strategic location, mining, hunting, artistry, and trade.

## Citizenry and Aesthetic

O'naren is about 95% elven, with about 5% non-elven residents who have earned the community's trust. Its pace is measured, and outsiders are expected to respect elven etiquette, tradition, and local reverence for nature.

Local fashion favors elegant, flowing clothes made from fine natural materials. Men commonly wear long tunics or robes with embroidered or beaded sashes. Women often wear flowing dresses or tunics with delicate nature-themed embroidery.

## Traditions

**The Verdant Oath** is an annual late-spring festival honoring Fralee, goddess of peace, harmony, and the land's vitality. Townsfolk gather at Driftglow Pond, offer flowers, garlands, and herbs at the ancient tree, recite an oath of protection and balance, dance, make music, feast, and set lanterns afloat on the pond.

**The Hunt of Renewal** is an early-autumn festival unique to O'naren. Hunters and foragers gather food under strict stewardship rules: only what is needed is taken, and offerings of thanks are left in the forest. The celebration ends around Driftglow Pond with stories of past hunts, survival, and wild wisdom.

## Government and Defense

O'naren is governed by Lord Itham Elle'neros, a calm, perceptive elven lord loyal to High King Artes Highvale of Qal'dynn. Lord Itham values harmony and balance, and often consults Lady Naleana and Priest Mathaed Skymane before making decisions.

The town is protected by the Realmwardens, led by Captain Salaal Windseek. The Realmwardens are elven guards trained in martial and magical defense. They patrol the outskirts, keep watch from the Overlook, and maintain order without disrupting the town's serenity.

The Hall of O'naren is the civic center and seat of Lord Itham. It contains a circular central chamber, two rectangular wings, public gathering space, judicial and diplomatic rooms, dining space for important visitors, and a secure dungeon for prisoners awaiting trial or exile. Attendant Cadlas Saj'esuru manages daily operations.

## Law

The source states that criminal activity is handled by the guard and charged under city ordinances. O'naren's leadership favors diplomacy and communal well-being, but the Hall of O'naren includes a dungeon for those awaiting trial or exile.

## Economy and Trade

O'naren's economy is built on mining, hunting, resource gathering, craftsmanship, and trade. The foothills provide iron and rare minerals. Local forests provide hardwood, game, natural dyes, herbs, and alchemical ingredients.

O'naren trades regularly with Qal'dynn, sending raw materials and finished goods in exchange for luxury goods, fine textiles, and magical components. Its artisans are known for nature-inspired elven weaponry, armor, ornamental metalwork, bows, leather goods, clothing, and fine art.

## Religion

O'naren is spiritually devoted to Fralee, goddess of tranquility, renewal, peace, harmony, and natural balance. Daily life includes small prayers, dawn thanks, meditation beneath sacred trees, and seasonal ceremonies.

The Temple of Tranquility is the primary place of worship. Priest Mathaed Skymane oversees religious life with four acolytes: Areysa Evonstride, Erendaar Evonstride, Padeera Faeweld, and Sh'leer Starbaugh. The temple includes a central altar hall and two shrine wings for prayer and meditation, and may offer healing and spell services at the DM's discretion.

Source note: the raw text includes spelling variants for Mathaed/Mathead, Areysa/Aresysa, Erendaar/Erenaar, and Padeera/Paderra.

## Notable Locations

**Commons Area** is a tranquil southeastern clearing on the first terrace, with a large stone gazebo and a view of the falls feeding Driftglow Pond. It is used for meditation, celebrations, public announcements, town discussions, and quiet community gatherings.

**Evonstride Stables** is the town's primary horse and mount facility, attached to the Evonstride family home. Maenari and Adryss Evonstride run the operation as skilled farriers and stablekeepers; Balalean Evonstride is mostly retired. Areysa and Erendaar Evonstride live in the home but serve as acolytes of Fralee.

**Fletcher / Tannery** is a paired workshop on the upper terrace. Vyen Rainsong crafts bows and arrows; Krennan Dalsteyer handles leatherwork; Datius Starbaugh handles taxidermy and is Sh'leer Starbaugh's brother.

**Hall of O'naren** is the town's civic and judicial center. Lord Itham rules from here, Cadlas Saj'esuru manages daily affairs, and Captain Salaal Windseek connects the Hall to Realmwarden security matters.

**Mines** lie on the southwest side of town, where a cave entrance opens into the rocky foothills. Beatror Rulendor directs mining operations from a circular stone office near the entrance.

**Nestling Soar** is a well-kept elven inn on the upper northeastern terrace. Lathae and Andella Fardawn manage the inn, which provides modest, comfortable, and wealthy rooms. It has no formal tavern but provides light meals and warm drinks from the nearby Truffle Trap.

**Oracle** is the home of Zanasee Tsal'daree, the Weavereader. Her circular two-story sanctuary contains counsel spaces, arcane motifs, a hidden lower chamber, and a secret Arcane Waypoint known only to those she trusts. She has ties to the Tegenwald Phet.

**Ore Masters** is a three-shop complex near the mines operated by the Tu'osaar siblings: Suonot Tu'osaar the armorsmith, Easethe Tu'osaar the jeweler, and Siasel Tu'osaar the weaponsmith. They buy, sell, and repair jewelry, gemstones, armor, and weapons.

**Overlook** is a tall round stone tower on the northwestern edge of Driftglow Pond. It serves both as a Realmwarden vantage point and as an open-air plaza where merchants and artisans set up temporary stalls.

**Pieces of Ra'lorn** is an elegant tailor shop run by Taana Dawnspar, focused on elven fashion, pre-made garments, and custom alterations for non-elven clients.

**Purposeful Poise** is a stylist's parlor run by Sha'ulna Aultea with apprentice Rabbani Onn'bane. It offers hair styling, nail styling, facials, perfume, piercings, and personal refinement.

**Tenement Shops and Services** occupy the ground floor of O'naren's largest residential structure. Services include baker, bathhouse, bookshop/scribe, butcher, chandler, cobbler, cooper, herbalist, mapmaker, tailor, and weaver.

**The Truffle Trap** is O'naren's main tavern, adjacent to Nestling Soar. Jaedent Du'cerie owns it, Nythasia Du'cerie handles wine, and Lenrie Embersong, Thyal Bolderie, Galodrie Tyndera, and Daseis Lalroth serve guests. Its specialty dish is Elderbisque, a truffle bisque from the Borealian Sway.

**Welthas' Market** is a two-story general store run by Welthas Truflant and his half-elf son Sinnas Truflant. It sells provisions, travel gear, tools, common goods, rare herbs, elven trinkets, and uncommon items.

**Vocehna's Gallery** is an upper-terrace art gallery run by Vocehna Maepholaer. It displays vases, sculptures, paintings, ornamental elven crafts, and notable enchanted or semi-magical works such as The Gilded Veil, The Silent Lyric, and Whispers of the Canopy.
