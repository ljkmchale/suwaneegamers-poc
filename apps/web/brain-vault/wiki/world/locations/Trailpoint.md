# Trailpoint

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]].

## Overview

Trailpoint is a medium crossroads town in [[Regions of Myrdae|Orbansia]] at map coordinate **Q - 31**.

It lies in the center of the [[Territories of Myrdae|Severed Bend]] territory on the eastern side of Orbansia. Trailpoint is known for horse racing and huge varieties of gourds.
