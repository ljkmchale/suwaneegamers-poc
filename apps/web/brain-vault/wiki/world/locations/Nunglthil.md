# Nunglthil

## Scope

World lore - canonical Underdark settlement geography, politics, and public setting.

## Overview

Nunglthil is a neutral Underdark trading enclave on the black shores of [[Rothenloch]]. It is one of the rare places where surface folk and subterranean peoples can trade without immediate threat of attack.

The Myrdae World Guide describes it as a crooked crescent of docks, shanties, and cavern-carved halls clinging to the edge of a vast black lake. The Nunglthil Gazetteer expands this into a cliffside settlement of lantern-lit caverns, wooden docks, carved trade halls, and enclave-controlled districts.

Nunglthil survives through strictly enforced neutrality. It is a sanctuary only in the practical sense: violence threatens everyone's livelihood, so violence is punished. Beneath that peace, the settlement is full of politics, secrets, trade leverage, old suspicion, and careful bargains.

## History

Nunglthil began as a refuge during the Demon Wars. As demon incursions shattered Underdark settlements and made tunnels deadly, ulbin, duergar, svirfneblin, and drow were forced into the same defensible cavern on Rothenloch.

They forged the Refuge Pact out of necessity, not trust. The pact suspended rivalries, shared defenses, and held the cavern against the demonic threat. The lake cliffs, controllable dock access, and river routes deeper into the Underdark made the site defensible enough to become a joint stronghold.

When the demonic advance was broken, the stronghold no longer needed to be purely martial. It transformed into a neutral trading hub and later admitted surface traders. The Refuge Pact survived as the settlement's vow of peace and became the foundation of Nunglthil's neutrality.

## Citizenry and Enclaves

Nunglthil is divided among five pact-bound enclaves. Its people include ulbin, dwarves, drow, duergar, svirfneblin, and surface folk. They share proximity and reliance, not a single culture. Reputation, called Favor, matters as much as coin and determines access, trust, trade opportunities, and information.

| Enclave | Epithet | People and Role |
|---|---|---|
| Gral'thune | The Strongback | Mostly ulbin and dwarves; maintains physical stability, defenses, peacekeeping, stonework, metalwork, raw materials, and escort services. |
| Selraeth | The Silken Dominion | Drow enclave of diplomacy, intelligence, mediation, rare fungi, fine silks, poisons, magical components, secrets, and social maneuvering. |
| Kar'Solar | The Sunbearers | Surface-dweller enclave tied to trade routes, logistics, smugglers, thieves' guild offshoots, merchants, fortune-seekers, and gray-market goods. |
| Durrak | The Ironbound | Duergar enclave built around contracts, profit, masterwork weapons and armor, strict labor, security, and enforceable bargains. |
| Glimmerdel | The Glow Borough | Svirfneblin enclave known for gemcraft, fungal alchemy, trinkets, charms, route awareness, warnings, and a more approachable communal culture. |

## Traditions

**Tokens of Safe Return** are personal tokens left behind before dangerous journeys. If the traveler returns, the token is reclaimed and worn or displayed. If they do not return, the token is offered at the Dais of Devotion or passed to kin.

**The Open Feast** occurs on the first day of every harmon. The docks and harbor platforms become a communal gathering where food, drink, warnings, stories, and successes are shared. Old rivalries are expected to rest for the evening.

## Government

Nunglthil is governed by the Pact Council, made up of one Pact Voice from each enclave. The council only governs matters affecting the settlement as a whole: neutrality, shared trade spaces, harbor access, and defense against outside threats. Internal laws and discipline remain with the individual enclaves.

Minor matters require majority agreement. Major actions, such as changing neutrality terms, expelling an enclave, or mobilizing settlement-wide defenses, require unanimous consent.

| Enclave | Pact Voice |
|---|---|
| Durrak | Odik Grumwall, male duergar |
| Glimmerdel | Antadda Coppereyes, female svirfneblin |
| Gral'thune | Kaduu Settibel, female ulbin |
| Kar'Solar | Vansia Kelmoor, male human |
| Selraeth | Zeyona Jandori, female drow |

Enforcement is delegated:

- Durrak enforces contracts and penalties.
- Glimmerdel coordinates warnings and route surveillance.
- Gral'thune oversees physical defenses and peacekeeping.
- Kar'Solar maintains surface relations and logistics.
- Selraeth manages intelligence and mediation.

## Law

Nunglthil's law is founded on the Pact of Neutrality: violence and actions that threaten trade or stability are forbidden in shared spaces. Neutral zones include docks, markets, the Dais of Devotion, and major thoroughfares. Crimes in enclave spaces are handled by the enclave; pact violations are judged by the Pact Council.

Punishments favor consequence over long imprisonment. Penalties include loss of Favor, restitution, trade sanctions, edicts of restriction, forced labor, short-term imprisonment, exile, public stripping of a favor badge, and, rarely, execution.

Grave offenses include murder in neutral zones, sabotage of docks or defenses, inciting enclave war, demon worship or summoning, and poisoning shared water or food. Major offenses include market theft, espionage, repeated violence or threats, and destabilizing smuggling. Minor offenses include disorderly conduct, weapon brandishing, fraud, and violation of edicts.

## Economy and Trade

Nunglthil exists for trade without bloodshed. Rothenloch and the river route deeper into the Underdark make it a rare waypoint between subterranean realms and the surface world. Goods, information, influence, and secrets all move through its docks.

The economy is interdependent rather than unified. No single enclave can dominate without endangering the whole:

- Gral'thune provides industrial labor, metalwork, stonecraft, raw materials, escorts, and defense oversight.
- Selraeth trades rare fungi, silks, poisons, magical components, secrets, and influence.
- Kar'Solar handles surface goods, smuggling, relics, salvaged artifacts, and illicit enchantments under internal codes.
- Durrak specializes in masterwork weapons, armor, and precision trade contracts.
- Glimmerdel supplies gemcraft, fungal alchemy, charms, trinkets, delicate goods, and route intelligence.

## Religion

Religion in Nunglthil is personal, quiet, and non-dominant. No deity, pantheon, or creed is elevated above the others. Worship is permitted as long as it does not threaten the Pact of Neutrality or settlement safety. Coercive rites, aggressive proselytizing, and divine mandates calling for violence are not tolerated.

The Dais of Devotion is the central religious space. It is open to all faiths and contains a circular white marble altar divided into four quarters, carved with symbols of Old Gods, New Order deities, and lesser-known powers. No permanent clergy rule the space, though itinerant priests or mystics may occasionally lead brief observances.

Named religious figures at the Dais include Mogduhr Darkstone, priest of Goldraen; Onnizi Dirgellu, priestess of Crael; and Silanria of House Ssorghess, priestess of Eredra.

## Layout

Nunglthil is carved into the cliffside bordering Rothenloch and arranged in four layers.

| Layer | Function |
|---|---|
| Main Layer | Docks, harbor platforms, shops, trade halls, cargo, enclave offices, and most daily commerce. |
| Second Layer | Residential tier overlooking the harbor, including long-term homes and upper guest rooms of the Darkbottom Draught. |
| Third Layer | Smaller and more controlled; high-status residences, secure chambers, and the Pact Council Chamber. |
| Fourth Layer | Single fortified lookout chamber overlooking Rothenloch, used for lake traffic, threat watching, and warnings. |

## Notable Locations

**Armstack** is a bright arms and armor shop run by Reddig Oddbrow, a talkative duergar from Dhaltarune. Most stock comes from the Doomhorn smiths of Firmforge, with additional pieces acquired through trade.

**Coppereyes Gemcraft** is Glimmerdel's premier gemcutting and jewel-trading shop, operated by Scalimank Coppereyes under the authority of his aunt Antadda Coppereyes. It offers gemcutting, appraisal, gemstones, enchanted stones, and high-value exchanges often shaped by Favor.

**Dais of Devotion** is Nunglthil's enclave-neutral worship chamber. Its massive white marble altar is divided into four quarters and covered with candles, glow-fungi, offerings, prayers, and trinkets.

**Darkbottom Draught** is the only inn and tavern in Nunglthil. Faffi Oobelyn, a female ulbin, runs it with cook Gark Rhymess, quaggoth bouncers Thak and Drogan, assistants Tikrin Eeldoop and Bahwa Broadheart, and Cadafynn Xil'tagga, a drow gambler who manages private gaming.

**Post of Kar'Solar** is Kar'Solar's administrative and coordination office. It manages surface routes, trade contacts, internal codes, correspondence, gray-market boundaries, and quiet dispute arbitration.

**Scales to Skin** is a leatherworking shop run by Ahlea Shadestalker, a wood elf from Shademeadow, assisted by Odjo Longwort, an elderly male gabuk from Meadowgarde. Ahlea specializes in Slumber Newt leather from Rothenloch.

**Seafarer's Supplies** is a dockside boating and fishing outfitter run by Ronsen "Rigger" Drace, a surface-born human from Mulshear, and Phoebe Drace, his half-elf wife and co-owner.

**Seat of Selraeth** is the administrative and diplomatic office of the Selraeth enclave. It handles contracts, favors, trade arrangements, and political monitoring through quiet ledgers and warded rooms. The source spells this map label Salraeth, while the enclave is otherwise Selraeth.

**Silken Secrets** is a Selraeth shop for rare textiles, luxury clothing, drow craftsmanship, perfumes, poisons, and dangerous refinements. It is run by Szoradryn of House Yerenduhl with attendant Yatash of House Yerenduhl.

**Soothing Spores** is a Glimmerdel fungal-remedy shop run by Nelka Niddlebom, an elderly female svirfneblin. It sells balms, salves, teas, calming snacks, mood remedies, and guarded hallucinogenic or memory-oriented fungi.

**Sweetspring** is a natural bathhouse with a warm spring-fed pool, renewing freshwater, bioluminescent cleaning fungi, and small eddies that ripple the surface. It is overseen by Cidus, a male storm giant goliath.

**Trader's Tankard** is a quieter dockside pub used for discreet conversation, subtle deals, and information exchange. Ormagg Sternbelcher, a male duergar, runs it with a strict no-performers policy.

**Trading Stalls** are the open harbor market where temporary vendors buy, sell, and barter. Droann Fungadyn, a male ulbin, supervises the area with his sons Padon and Kren. The attached **Storeroom** offers controlled storage leased at 1 sp per night.

**The Holding** functions as both a stable for Underdark mounts, especially Leatherback giant lizards, and a secure holding area for prisoners or dangerous individuals awaiting judgment. Tholumn Blaregut oversees it.
