# Hesfal

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences are in `wiki/locations/Dungeons III/`.

## Overview

Hesfal is a small artisan village in [[Var'Shala]], located halfway between Deadfield and [[Tratta]] on the Tor March on the western edge of the [[Territories of Myrdae|Yearning Vale]]. The Myrdae World Guide places it at map coordinate **J - 34**.

It is the opening settlement for the Dungeons III campaign and hosts Salvus crowds, traveler camps, a multi-faith hall, and local tavern/market activity.

## Known Locations

- Covered Hooch - large traveler camp/yurt area.
- Moonfern Sanctuary - spa/sanctuary.
- Hall of Many Hands - multi-faith temple.
- Boar's Best Inn & Tavern - tavern and gaming venue.
- Town Ward - local jail/guard location.
- Artisan Exchange - vendor/artisan area.
- Bru's Beastie Barn - animal/hire location tied to early investigation.

## Campaign Notes

Dungeons III party experiences in Hesfal belong in `wiki/locations/Dungeons III/`. The party first converges here during Salvus; the murder rumor, Boar's Best robbery, prisoner break, and K9 Crew plot all begin here.
