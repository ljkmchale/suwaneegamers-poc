# Climbor

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences belong in campaign-specific location pages.

## Overview

Climbor is a city in the [[Territories of Myrdae|Rostiena Foothills]], home to approximately 22,000 people. Most residents live within the city walls; only a couple thousand live outside them. Roughly 3,000 people live within "The Hole," a sunken district inside the city associated with the Gabuuk population.

Twenty years ago an event known simply as "the hole" occurred — the origin of the sunken Hole district.

### The Hole

The Hole is a sunken district within Climbor, home to roughly 3,000 residents — predominantly Gabuuks. The Polite Well inn and Gliort's Goods general store are located here. Gabuuks in the Hole look unfavorably on Muerg worship.

## Government

Climbor is ruled by **The Viers Ealdormen**, a council of four leaders chosen as successors to previous Ealdormen. They hold complete legal immunity within the city. The four current Ealdormen:

- **Arryn Daetoris**
- **Mollanis Vensalor**
- **Katar Raloro**
- **Heilcaena Caifir** (pronunciation: Hail-say-e-nah K-eye-f-ere) — female elf; the Caifir family is a notable noble house in Climbor

## Laws

Climbor has strict laws and dispenses swift justice. Citizens who engage in criminal activity are arrested by the Climbor town guard and charged per the City Ordinances. Cases are then brought before the Viers Ealdormen for judgment.

## Religion

Citizens mostly worship non-evil aligned gods openly.

- **[[Utheri]]** — the majority faith; strong favoritism throughout the city
- **[[Ossana]]** — modest favoritism
- **[[Muerg]]** — frowned upon by Gabuuks in the Hole; not actively suppressed city-wide

### The Paranormic Lady (Inn, Map #3)

An inn on the north side of the city catering to common folk travelers. 20 comfortable rooms at 4 sp per person per night. Owner: **Ninwalyn Fenfiel**.

### The Tamed Moons (Inn, Map #4)

An inn on the west side of the city; described as "the Texas Roadhouse of Inns." 20 comfortable rooms at 4 sp per person per night. Owners: **Colet Basuinee** (dressed like a cowboy) and **Emelnie Basuinee** (dressed like a wench).

An inn near the center of the city; a common stopping place for adventurers. 20 comfortable rooms at 4 sp per person per night. Owners: **Dyryke Warner** and **Godiva Warner**.
