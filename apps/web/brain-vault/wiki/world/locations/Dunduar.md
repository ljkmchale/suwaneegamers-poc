# Dunduar

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography and history of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences and item mysteries belong in campaign-specific pages.

## Overview

Dunduar began as a mining village in the eastern foothills of the Distancion Mountains on the southern half of the Severed Crease. It was founded on Kesero 11, year 49, by dwarven clan elders Dundrim Umberfist and Uarmil Cragfury.

The mines produced iron ore and occasional gemstones, then extended deep enough to break into the Underdark.

## Morn of Dunduar

Underdark denizens assaulted the dwarves after the mines broke through. While defenders fought drow and illithids, the mind flayers used dominated umber hulks to dig layered tunnels beneath the village.

On Hemist 18, year 59, remembered as **Morn of Dunduar**, enough earth had been moved that the mining village collapsed about 100 feet beneath the surface, leaving a huge pitted chasm. Only a small number of villagers survived alongside clan members.

## Refounding

Less than a harmon later, on Baegrum 7, year 59, the survivors founded a new stronghold within the Distancion Mountains itself. They built interlocking foundational columns and beams rooted deep into the mountain to prevent another collapse.

By year 69, the new fortress was underway and mining resumed. By the turn of the century, Dunduar was home to more than 5,000 dwarves.

On Talil 30, year 81, Dunduar miners discovered magical **Dunduar Ore**, drawing smiths, jewelers, and mages from across Myrdae.

## Clan Notes

- **Dundrim Umberfist** died in year 378. His son Boreth took over as elder of Umberfist; his daughter Gloreth became an ambassador and mineral trader.
- **Uarmil Cragfury** died in year 317 from an unknown disease. His son Saeviul inherited clan leadership.
- Saeviul Cragfury and his immediate family vanished during an annual regional trip in year 271.
