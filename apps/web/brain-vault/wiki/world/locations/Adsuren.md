# Adsuren

## Scope

World lore — canonical geography of [[Myrdae]], region of Inverland. Campaign party experiences are in `wiki/locations/SoD/`.

---

## Overview

Adsuren is a small village in a wooded valley beneath **Mount Ethea** (to the west) and along the **Wehgyn River** (to the east). It sits in **Inverland**, governed under the **Camburne Vec**, and serves as a travel waypoint for those heading to the **Eustera Ridge** who prefer to avoid the coast or Gibuldon.

The village is built around a central open-air market, with small buildings branching off the river. Diverse in population but predominantly human and half-elven, Adsuren is known for its warm community, good food, and the celebrated Market Day.

**Heraldry:** Three dark purple fruits (Precura) on a pale wood-colored shield.

---

## Geography & Layout

- **Mount Ethea** — the mountain directly to the west; source of dark stone used in local construction.
- **Wehgyn River** — borders the village to the east; supports the docks and daily lumber shipments.
- **Celedon Forest** — forest across the river; primary lumber source for the town.
- **Eustera Ridge** — major destination north; Adsuren serves as a waypoint.
- **Gibuldon** — the regional capital on Loch Cerule; north via Insloe.
- **Caldwynn** — nearby settlement to the south; original settlers migrated from here.
- **Tirncall** — road heading south along the river.

---

## History

Adsuren was originally settled by people attracted by a unique fruit growing in the valley. The mountain to the west and the river to the east provided natural defenses that enabled rapid, permanent settlement by migrants from Caldwynn.

The village's true legacy began in the **1210s** when a young mage from Adsuren rose to become a powerful sorceress. She joined a band of adventurers who defeated a powerful fey spirit in **Loch Cerule** that was threatening cities on the lake including the capital, Gibuldon. During those heroic deeds, she spread the valley's fruit across Inverland, making it a widespread delicacy and drawing chefs and farmers to Adsuren. The mage was **Mollywap the Magnificent**, who has since retired from the **Tegenwald Phet** and returned to her hometown.

*Spelling note: The campaign notes use "Mollywop" — the gazetteer canonical spelling is Mollywap. See [[Mollywop's Tower And Apprentices]] for SoD party context.*

## Culture & Aesthetic

- **Colors:** Violet and pastel yellow (inspired by the Precura Fruit).
- **Clothing:** Comfortable, humble tunics and pants or large skirts; cloaks and furs in cold weather. Dyed wool is a luxury for the affluent.
- **Architecture:** Warm brown Celedon wood, dark mountain stone, and Precura vines intertwined throughout.
- **Tradition:** Market Day, celebrated once per stretch (the only village-wide holiday).

---

## The Precura Fruit

A small, cool **orange-colored fruit** that grows only in this valley, hanging from large deciduous trees. Flavor is a cross between an apple and a pomegranate. Deemed a delicacy across Inverland; Adsuren's primary export and source of gold trade via the river. Food and drink made with Precura are a local specialty (available at The Falling Fish and The Graying Grotto).

---

## Government

Adsuren falls under the **Camburne Vec**. The village is governed by **Intendant Eyovar Dunajor** — a "man of the people" who regularly engages with citizens. Daily administration is managed by his assistant **Jerborin Omakian**. Order is maintained by the **Adsuren Watch**, led by **Captain Alinia Sarralei**.

### Laws
Criminal activity is prosecuted per the City Ordinances (arrests made by the Watch).

---

## Economy & Trade

Self-sufficient via local resources (wood, stone, metal from the valley). The Precura Fruit is the primary gold-generating export, traded up and down the river. The valley and the Celedon Forest supply artisans with ample raw materials.

---

## Religion

All faiths are welcome. Soft local preference for **[[Fralee]]** (tranquility) and **[[Villari]]** (nature and life), reflected in the Precura abundance and natural setting. The **Nature's Oratory** serves all faiths; main statues honor Fralee and Villari.

## Notable Locations — Detail

### Adsuren Center
Physical center of the village. Houses a lobby, small library, central and smaller meeting areas, and back rooms for officials. Run day-to-day by Jerborin Omakian.

**NPCs:** Intendant Eyovar Dunajor (mayor, noble); Jerborin Omakian (assistant, commoner).

### Adsuren Stables
Simple stable near the Graying Grotto. Services include boarding, farrier services, feed, and washing.

**NPC:** Roibeart Cuimein (stable hand, commoner — small, sensitive about his height).

### Docks
Large, well-maintained docks on the Wehgyn River; capacity for 4 large ships. Daily lumber ship from the Celedon Forest. Occasional passenger/traveler ship running the river.

**NPC:** Peadar MacCaanain (dock master, tough — old man, luscious blonde hair, deep blue eyes, large groomed sideburns, navy coat, blue scarf).

### Ethea's Stone (Smithy)
Only fully stone structure in the village; two chimneys. Located near the river's edge (uses water to cool the forge). Stocked with armor, blades, spears, bows, maces. Run by "siblings" Nosgrim and Alfonita Goldenmace.

**NPCs:** Nosgrim Goldenmace (armorsmith, tough — tall broad half-orc, gray skin, dark hair in fine braid); Alfonita Goldenmace (weaponsmith, tough — short slender dwarf, auburn-gold braided hair, unusually clean for a forge).

### The Falling Fish (Tavern)
Local tavern for village folk. Distinctive round bar at center; dim orange lantern lighting; large fish-descending-waterfall statue in the corner with a coin pond (coins "magically" disappear by next stretch). Run by the Carlee family.

**NPCs:** Loisa Carlee (barkeep, commoner — young half-elf, dark auburn curly hair, green eyes); Geflael Carlee (assistant/husband, commoner — large scarred human, rarely talks).

### The Graying Grotto (Inn & Tavern)
Premier inn for visitors. Large graying wooden frame with gray thatch. Warm interior with round tables and a curved bar along the right wall; large staircase to rooms. Run by Aonghas MacLuinge and his large family of 9.

**NPCs:** Aonghas MacLuinge (proprietor/barkeep, commoner — old man, glossy blue eyes, auburn-going-gray hair); Eleanor MacLuinge (assistant/wife, commoner — old woman, brown-going-gray hair, green eyes).

*Lodging:* 5 squalid (7cp/night), 10 poor (1sp/night), 10 modest (5sp/night). No comfortable/wealthy rooms.

### Guard Post
Sizable structure near the center of town with a defensive watchtower. Serves as Watch meeting hall, armory, barracks, and Captain's quarters. Prison beneath the tower.

**NPC:** Captain Alinia Sarralei (Watch Captain).

### Mage Tower
Built in the 1210s by Mollywap. Sits just outside town proper; 3-story structure. Ground floor: small kitchen and resting area (visible to guests). Upper floors presumed bedroom/study. When Mollywap is away, the door is sealed by a powerful enchantment.

**NPC:** Mollywap the Magnificent (Mage — middle-aged human woman, dark brown hair streaked gray, sea blue eyes, flowing purple robe fading to gold at sleeves and hem, golden sash holding a red leather spellbook with buckled lock). Former high-ranking mage of the Tegenwald Phet; hero of Inverland; retired to Adsuren.

### Market
Open-air central market — the true heart of the village. Lit by lanterns in trees; cobblestone-and-worn-grass path. Stalls from locals and traveling merchants; changes each stretch. Central communal well; small fish pond at the edge (6–8 fish, tended by villagers).

### Nature's Oratory
Old temple predating the market. Dark mountain stone exterior; deep purple clay tile roof; vine-covered walls; gardens on all sides; simple graveyard behind. Large hall with arched rooms off the sides. Central arch features a faded mural of a dancing druidic figure amid flowers; large braziers provide warm orange light. Multi-faith; rooms available for private prayer to any god. Main statues honor Fralee and Villari.

**NPCs:** Tanluar Rotvori (priest, half-elf — older, brown-gray hair, deep green eyes, faded green robe); Beatris Tod (acolyte — lean young woman, warm brown hair, blue eyes, light blue robes); Torcull Dubhach (acolyte — young man just past teens, wild red hair, green eyes, blue-green robes).

### The Rugged Rooster (General Store)
Oldest building in town — moss-covered, patchwork roof, rusted metal rooster sign. Stocked with basic necessities but rarely busy since the market offers the same. Run by Tearlach Sogot, believed to be one of Adsuren's original settlers (and infuriated if you ask).

**NPC:** Tearlach Sogot (proprietor, commoner — extremely old human, white hair, deeply weathered face, hobbles in old worn clothes and decades-old boots; the village "crotchety old man").

### Spider's Silk (Tailor)
Elegant dark stone building under two large willow-like trees. Lanterns on threads between trees and building cast a faint purple glow at night. Run by Anabu Mievriat, a drow tailor with an uncanny eye for measurements even through layered clothing. Primarily common clothing; also custom garments in fine wool and silk.

**NPC:** Anabu Mievriat (tailor, drow — slender male drow, white hair slicked back and long, purplish gray skin, deep violet robe with white spider-web-and-dewdrop sash in red gemstones).

### Tanner
Dark stone hut with fresh thatch on the north side. Leather goods including boots, sheaths, and leather armor. Run by Almadia Oriroris (pronounce: Or-ee-roar-us), who wears her own work daily and sends hunters out for exotic leathers.

**NPC:** Almadia Oriroris (tanner, commoner — female wood elf, short light greenish-orange hair, lean frame, always in her own leather designs).

## World Context

- Part of the region **Inverland**, governed under the **Camburne Vec**.
- Governed locally by an **Intendant** (currently Eyovar Dunajor); order maintained by the **Adsuren Watch**.
- Mollywap the Magnificent is a former high-ranking member of the **Tegenwald Phet** (mage organization) and close ally/advisor to the Chief of the Camburne Vec.
- Connected to **Gibuldon** (regional capital) via Insloe to the north.
