# Nauldeaus (Wave's Edge)

## Scope

World lore - canonical geography of [[Myrdae]]. Campaign party experiences belong in campaign-specific location pages.

## Overview

Nauldeaus (Wave's Edge) is a city in [[Territories of Myrdae|Halesworth]]. Nauldeaus started out simply as the port town “Wave’s Edge” before becoming the large city it is today. As the gateway to Lake Tribathe, the large body of water that is known as the hub of the Bathaen Empire, almost all goods that arrive by sea into the three kingdoms come through Nauldeaus.
