# Oberra

## Scope

World lore - the planet on which all campaigns are set.

## Overview

Oberra is the world that contains the continent of [[Myrdae]]. The active campaign wiki currently places Heroes of Emberstran, Souls of Destiny, The Silent Vanguard, Bloody Endeavor, and Dungeons III within Myrdae.

## Known Continents

- [[Myrdae]] - the primary continent documented in this wiki.
- Taragoh - named in the Myrdae World Guide; not yet populated in this wiki.
- Ekruthe - named in the Myrdae World Guide; not yet populated in this wiki.
- Subesrias - named in the Myrdae World Guide; not yet populated in this wiki.
- Ah'nurra - named in the Myrdae World Guide; not yet populated in this wiki.

## Moons

Oberra has two primary moons, known together as the Eyes of Time:

- **Endelo'ar** - the Scarlet Moon, reddish in color.
- **Tudara** - the Frost Moon, bluish in color.

A third moon once existed. Its name is forgotten; it is now called **the Broken One**. Its magically bound fragments still follow the old orbit and can appear over Myrdae as scattered bright stones in the night sky.

The recurring paired phases of Endelo'ar and Tudara are called **Lunar Facets**. They are used for festivals, prayer, superstition, and calendar awareness. See [[Calendar of Myrdae]].
