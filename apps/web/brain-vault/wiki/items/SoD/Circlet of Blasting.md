# Circlet of Blasting

## Scope

SoD-only item page.

## Summary

The Circlet of Blasting is a golden tiara with blood-orange gems recovered in the Adsuren Center cellar and claimed/worn by Lila.

## Known Facts

- Found after the mimic/chest sequence.
- Detected as evocation magic by Esylla.
- Identified by Mollywop as Circlet of Blasting.
- Lila polishes/wears it after the cult reveal.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 08: The circlet is recovered after the mimic/chest sequence in Adsuren Center.
- Session 08: Esylla detects evocation magic on it.
- Session 08: Mollywop identifies it as a Circlet of Blasting.
- Session 08: Lila polishes/wears it after the cult reveal.

## Strong Inferences

- The circlet becomes part of Lila's combat kit after Session 08.
- It is loot from the cult-site/cellar sequence, but not necessarily cult-made.
