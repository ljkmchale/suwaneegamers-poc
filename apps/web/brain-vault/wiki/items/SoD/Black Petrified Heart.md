# Black Petrified Heart

## Scope

SoD-only item/artifact.

## Summary

The black petrified heart is a human heart tied to necromancy, enchantment, fiendish quality, torment, soul tethering, and the Cult of the Immured's heart rituals.

## Known Facts

- Found with/near the gertot head in the tainted stream investigation.
- Unsafe to carry; may torment the bearer.
- Mollywop identifies necromancy, enchantment, fiendish quality, and soul tethering.
- Appears to be a failed heart ritual according to later Mollywop interpretation.
- Related to grave theft from Durgruck Bristleforge.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 01: The black petrified heart is found during the poisoned well / tainted stream investigation.
- Session 02: Mollywop analyzes it and identifies necromancy, enchantment, fiendish quality, soul tethering, and torment.
- Session 04: Durgruck Bristleforge's grave/heart theft connects the heart ritual thread to missing bodies/hearts.
- Session 07/08: The Cult of the Immured's heart rituals explain the broader use of hearts.

## Strong Inferences

- The heart is part of the Cult of the Immured's failed or incomplete heart ritual pattern.
- It is dangerous evidence rather than a safe carried item.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whose heart it is.
- Whether the tethered soul is recoverable.
- Whether it points to other cult cells.

## Open Questions

- Whose heart was it?
- What soul is tethered to it?
- How does it relate to Aeolenne's freeing?
- Can it point toward other cult cells?
