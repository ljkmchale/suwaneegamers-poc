## Scope

SoD-only item/thread page.

## Summary

Kenton's weapon track after Session 08 includes a +1 whip acquired through Esylla/Mollywop and a pending silvered weapon order made with powdered silver from the Adsuren Center cellar.

## Known Facts

- Esylla brings Kenton the resulting +1 whip.
