# Defiled Black Salt

## Scope

SoD-only ritual component.

## Summary

Defiled black salt is a cult ritual component delivered to the Tanner through Caldwynn paperwork and used in heart preparation/petrification.

## Known Facts

- Found in a barrel at/near the Tanner.
- Escanor rolls/dumps a barrel toward the water in Session 05.
- Mollywop says it is likely unholy/arcane and used in the ritual that petrifies hearts.
- Ganon's letter says hearts should be dried in defiled black salt.
- More black salt is found beneath Adsuren Center.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 05: Black salt is found around the Tanner/river thread and Escanor rolls or dumps a barrel toward water.
- Session 06: The Caldwynn barrel/shipping paperwork connects black salt to delivery instructions.
- Session 06: Ganon's letter says hearts should be dried in defiled black salt.
- Session 07/08: More black salt appears beneath Adsuren Center in the cult area.
- Mollywop identifies it as likely unholy/arcane and linked to heart petrification.

## Strong Inferences

- Defiled black salt is a ritual processing material for the Cult of the Immured's heart work.
- The Caldwynn paperwork suggests the salt is part of a larger supply chain.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Where it was made/defiled.
- Whether dumping it in water created contamination.
- Whether Caldwynn is source or waypoint.

## Open Questions

- Where was it manufactured or defiled?
- Did dumping it in water create a new contamination risk?
- Is Caldwynn the origin or only a routing point?
