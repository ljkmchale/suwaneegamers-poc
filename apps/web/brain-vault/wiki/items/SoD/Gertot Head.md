# Gertot Head

## Scope

SoD-only item/clue.

## Summary

The gertot head was recovered during the poisoned stream/well investigation and may have entered the water system through the sewer/ritual-room route later found beneath Adsuren Center.

## Known Facts

- Found with the black petrified heart in the tainted stream lead.
- Part of the initial poisoned-water crisis.
- Ritual chamber beneath Adsuren Center has a hole/indentation possibly connected to sewers.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 01: The gertot head is found during the poisoned water / tainted stream investigation alongside the black petrified heart clue.
- Session 08: The ritual chamber beneath Adsuren Center includes a hole/indentation possibly connected to sewers.
- The item remains part of the initial contamination evidence.

## Strong Inferences

- The gertot head may connect to the cult's earlier animal-sacrifice phase, but the source does not explicitly prove it.
- It may have entered the water system through cult disposal routes.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

## Open Questions
