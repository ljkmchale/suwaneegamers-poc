# Caldwynn Barrel Letter And Spiral Watermark

## Scope

SoD-only clue item.

## Summary

The Caldwynn barrel letter ordered MacCannain to deliver the black salt barrel to the Tanner without opening it. Its angular spiral watermark may identify a house, trade network, or cult-linked source.

## Known Facts

- Came from Caldwynn or passed through Caldwynn.
- Included overpaid taxes to discourage inspection.
- MacCannain handled it locally.
- Esylla noticed the spiral watermark.
- Noble-family book suggests possible matches: Cunningter, Idsoul, Walgett.
- Idsoul feels familiar to Escanor and Zephyra.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 06: The party investigates dock/shipping paperwork tied to the black salt barrel.
- Session 06: The letter instructs MacCannain to deliver the barrel to the Tanner and not open it.
- Session 06: Taxes are overpaid, discouraging inspection.
- Session 06: Esylla notices an angular spiral watermark.
- Session 06: Noble-family research narrows possible matches to Cunningter, Idsoul, and Walgett.

## Strong Inferences

- The letter is a supply-chain clue pointing toward Caldwynn and the wider Cult of the Immured network.
- The overpaid taxes look like deliberate anti-inspection behavior.
