# Ganon Letter

## Scope

SoD-only clue item.

## Summary

The Ganon letter gives the clearest early written instructions for Cult of the Immured operations: familiars, sentient hearts, black salt, avoiding the Mage, and summoning their lord.

## Known Facts

- Found in Ganon's room under a loose board/ritual area.
- Mentions sending familiars into the village to report on suspicious activity.
- Demands acquisition of a sentient heart.
- Orders hearts dried in defiled black salt.
- Mentions storage under AC.
- Warns not to be spotted by the Mage.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 06: The letter is found in Ganon's room under a loose board/ritual area.
- It mentions familiars reporting suspicious activity.
- It demands a sentient heart.
- It orders hearts dried in defiled black salt.
- It mentions storage under AC.
- It warns not to be spotted by "the Mage."

## Strong Inferences

- "AC" likely means Adsuren Center, based on later cellar/cult discovery.
- "The Mage" likely means Mollywop, but the letter does not name her.
- The letter is an early written bridge between Ganon and the larger Cult of the Immured operation.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Who wrote it.
- Whether "our lord" means Aeolenne or the cult's misunderstanding of her.
- How many familiar reports reached the writer.

## Open Questions

- Who wrote it?
- Does "the Mage" mean Mollywop specifically?
- Did "our lord" mean Aeolenne, or did the cult misunderstand her role?
