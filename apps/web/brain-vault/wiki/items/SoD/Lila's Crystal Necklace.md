# Lila's Crystal Necklace

## Scope

SoD-only character item.

## Summary

Lila's crystal necklace is a vision source and symbolic guide. It has shown visions that later map onto real events and unresolved leads.

## Known Visions

- Five figures at a grave.
- Dark room with red shifting floor.
- Ruined keep/castle with overgrowth.
- Marketplace/well.
- Kenton with a starry sword surrounded.
- Night forest floor, looming shadow, and monster roar.

## Known Effects

- Feels warm/euphoric/comforting during vision activation.
- Lila later has a Zalyas dream and wakes with temporary hit points.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 05: Lila's crystal visions include scenes later tied to campaign imagery and unresolved leads.
- Session 06: Lila experiences the Zalyas dream and wakes with 6 temporary hit points.
- The necklace is treated as Lila's personal vision source in SoD.

## Strong Inferences

- The crystal is magical or supernaturally connected because it produces visions and dream-adjacent effects.
- Some visions are likely prophetic or symbolic, but each must be matched to source events before being treated as fact.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether the crystal is sentient.
- Whether Zalyas is connected to the crystal or a separate force.
- Whether all visions are literal.

## Open Questions

- Is the crystal prophetic, sentient, enchanted, or connected to an outside entity?
- Are all visions literal?
- What is the starry sword around Kenton?
- What is the forest shadow/roar?
