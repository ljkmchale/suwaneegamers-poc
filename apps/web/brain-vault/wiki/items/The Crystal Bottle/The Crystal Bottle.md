# The Crystal Bottle

**Type:** Magic artifact / prison vessel
**Holder:** Unknown — still unrecovered (active quest)
**Status:** Still in a cave; believed linked to Kal'dar's former location

## Description

A crystal bottle with an inscription in Terran: *"Death to whomever frees him. Wishes to whomever puts him back."* The bottle imprisons the **D'jinn King**, an unnamed D'jinn of immense power.

The entire campaign is named for this object.

## What Is Known

- Found in an abandoned mine (session 1.1)
- Appears protected from being moved — a wizard's help may be required
- **Malik's skeleton was wrapped around the bottle when the party found it** — he died freeing the D'jinn
- The D'jinn is **unnamed**
- The D'jinn became Kal'dar's master after Kal'dar made a deal for immortality and was hollowed out
- The bottle is still in the cave; the party needs it to put the D'jinn back in (session 6.3 to-do list)
- Location: "in a cave, might be linked to [[Kal'dar]]" — possibly near Zeprock's Crown or connected to his former operations

## The Inscription Logic

- Freeing the D'jinn = death (as Malik discovered)
- Returning the D'jinn to the bottle = wishes (incentive to put it back)
- The bottle is the key to ending the D'jinn threat

## History

- Made or found by [[Fizban]] / Tsuul'physs Bhan (implied by his role in the D'jinn's history)
- [[Malik]] freed the D'jinn (session 1.1 discovery context; confirmed session 4.1)
- D'jinn hollowed [[Kal'dar]]; Malik emerged from the hollow
- The bottle itself sits unguarded in a cave; connected to the [[Athralis And The Oasis|Athralis]] excavation through the D'jinn's broader influence
