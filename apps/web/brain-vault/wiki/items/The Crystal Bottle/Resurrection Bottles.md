# Resurrection Bottles

**Type:** Consumable
**Holder:** Party
**Status:** 4 remaining (5 original; 1 used on Scarlytt)

## Description

Bottles of blood-like fluid that can resurrect a dead creature. Single-use per individual. One was used to revive [[Scarlytt Amyran - Personal History And Goals|Scarlytt]] after she died in the [[Oasis of the Night|Oasis of the Night]] (session 2.3).

## History

Acquired from cultists.
