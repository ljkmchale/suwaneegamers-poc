# A'urea'Pura Emblems

**Type:** Disguise item
**Holder:** Each party member (one each)
**Status:** In party possession

## Description

Emblems taken off dead [[A'urea'Pura Cult]] members. The party plans to use them to blend in with other cultists when needed.

## History

Collected from dead A'urea'Pura cultists during the campaign.
