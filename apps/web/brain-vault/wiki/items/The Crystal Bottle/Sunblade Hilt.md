# Sunblade Hilt

**Type:** Unfinished magic weapon
**Holder:** [[Yvaine]]
**Originally sought for:** [[Sigil (TCB)|Sigil]]'s mother's sword quest
**Status:** In party possession; needs materials to complete

## Description

The hilt of an ancient sunblade ("The Sun Blade"), found in the living quarters of Thal'astyr / Sunlit Haven (session 5.5). Sun engravings cover it. The blade is broken but retains magical potential. It is partially made of **Dunduar ore**.

With the right materials, a skilled smith could remake the blade into a functional sunblade. Darveth in Kravwynhold mentioned a smith on the other side of the mountains (outside the Blustery Waste) who might be able to help.
