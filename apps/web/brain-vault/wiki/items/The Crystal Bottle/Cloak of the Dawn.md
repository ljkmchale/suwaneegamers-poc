# Cloak of the Dawn

**Type:** Magic cloak
**Holder:** Ren
**Status:** In party possession

## Description

A silk cloak with sun-themed patterns, found in the living quarters of Thal'astyr / Sunlit Haven (session 5.5). It was found on a creature killed outside the city.

**Properties:** Resistance to radiant damage; emits a glow of light out to 10 feet.
