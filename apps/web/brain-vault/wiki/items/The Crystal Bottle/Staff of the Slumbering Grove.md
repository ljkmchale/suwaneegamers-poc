# Staff of the Slumbering Grove

**Type:** Magic staff
**Holder:** Galtrelon (Gal)
**Status:** In party possession

## Description

A druidic staff connected to grove magic. Gal used it in the unsealing ritual that freed trapped individuals including Alteya's family from the frozen grove beneath Everlight.

## History

Acquired during the Everlight cave expedition in Session 6.5.
