# Portal Stone

**Type:** Key / magic item
**Holder:** [[Ren (TCB)|Ren]]
**Status:** In party possession

## Description

A portal stone that serves as a key to the celestial plane excavation site in the Blustery Waste. The [[A'urea'Pura Cult]] needs this stone to reach the celestial plane where they believe T'suul resides. Its possession by the party is a critical tactical advantage.

## History

Obtained during the campaign. [[Ren (TCB)|Ren]] is the designated carrier. The excavation site connects to the [[Lost City (Athralis)]].
