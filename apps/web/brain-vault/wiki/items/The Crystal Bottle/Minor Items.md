## Erick's Keys (×2)

**Owner:** Party (likely [[Ren (TCB)]] or shared)  
**Acquired:** Session 4.1 — from Erick the Alchemist's house in [[Scarbrook]]  
**Status:** Unidentified — two keys with unknown purposes

Two keys were found in Erick's home. Their exact purpose is unknown. The party holds them but has not yet identified what locks they open. One is noted as having a "W" marking — this was the key [[Dalbyre Bramblesage]] took from the haunted mansion safe (Session 5.1). It is unclear if "Erick's keys" and "the W key" are the same or different items.

**Open Questions:** What do these keys open? Were they Erick's personal keys, or connected to something larger (ALM? The cult? His safe network)?

## Unidentified Bottle (Yvaine's)

**Owner:** [[Yvaine]]  
**Acquired:** Session 4.2 — from [[Fizban's Tower]] vault  
**Status:** Unidentified

An unidentified bottle found in Fizban's vault. Yvaine cast Detect Magic during the vault exploration and took this item. Its contents and properties remain unknown. Given that [[Fizban]] (Tsuul'physs Bhan) is an ancient artificer known to craft unusual items — including the [[Deck of Many Things (TCB)]] and [[The Crystal Bottle|the Crystal Bottle itself]] — the contents could be significant.
