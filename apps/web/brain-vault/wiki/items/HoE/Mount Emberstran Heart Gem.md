# Mount Emberstran Heart Gem

## Scope

HoE-only item/artifact.

## Summary

The Mount Emberstran Heart Gem is the fist-sized, heart-shaped crystal recovered from Moganna's vault beneath Mount Emberstran in Session 25. It is one of three relic pieces connected to Moganna, Ainslie's inheritance, Irragosa's long guardianship, and the cycle of Ahn'Tsuel.

## Known Facts

- Found in Moganna's vault beneath Mount Emberstran.
- Rested on a black metal pedestal shaped like a dragon talon.
- Two empty sockets stood beside it; Irragosa names the other two relic pieces as **Soulroot** and **Starvein**.
- When Ainslie touches the gem, it draws on his energy and leaves him exhausted.
- Irragosa says Ainslie was intended to find the vault and take the gem.
- Irragosa says if Ainslie combines all three parts himself, the effect differs from finding them and reopening the circle.
- The three-part relic was broken up and hidden by dragon/rider pairs so no one person would use it wrongly.
- The combined relic could extend life and break the 50-year soul cycle of Ahn'Tsuel.

## Strong Inferences

- The gem is keyed to Ainslie's Moganna / Tsulanan / Ahn'Tsuel inheritance.
- The gem is powerful enough that divine care is needed after contact.
- "Heart" may be the first of the three named relic functions, while Soulroot and Starvein remain separate objects.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether "Heart Gem" is the object's proper name or only a descriptive label.
- What the gem can do by itself.
- Whether Ainslie is now attuned to it, marked by it, or only physically drained.
- Who currently knows Ainslie has it besides the party, Irragosa, and possibly Lady Rook's surveillance network.
