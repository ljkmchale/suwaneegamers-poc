# Bloom Aeterna

## Scope

HoE-only divine text/artifact.

## Summary

Bloom Aeterna is the complete original teaching of Diverra received by Aury in the Lost Sanctuary. It changes Aury's role from follower of signs into keeper/interpreter of direct doctrine.

## Known Facts

- Received by Aury in the Lost Sanctuary.
- Contains or reveals Shattered Hearts doctrine.
- Used with petrified rose petals to return from Bloomrest / Lost Sanctuary.
- Aury withholds some details from Priestess Meladra.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 19: Aury receives Bloom Aeterna at the Lost Sanctuary / Bloomrest.
- Session 19: The text reveals deeper/original Diverra doctrine and Shattered Hearts language.
- Session 20: Aury returns to Meladra but does not fully disclose everything in Bloom Aeterna.
- Petrified rose petals are used for the return from Bloomrest / Lost Sanctuary.

## Strong Inferences

- Bloom Aeterna makes Aury a carrier of doctrine Meladra's temple does not fully have.
- It will likely shape Aury's next religious obligations, but the exact Shattered Hearts path is not fully documented.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- What all Shattered Hearts are and where they are.
- Whether Bloom Aeterna connects directly to the Mount Emberstran vault.
- When Aury should reveal the full text to Meladra.

## Open Questions

- What are the Shattered Hearts and where are they?
- What should Aury reveal to the temple?
- How does Bloom Aeterna connect to the vault?
