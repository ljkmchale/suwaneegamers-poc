# Marrowstone

## Scope

HoE-only item/artifact.

## Summary

The Marrowstone is a dangerous necrotic power object tied to Lady Rook's agenda and Ainslie's risky arcane growth. Ainslie drains or empties it with help from Zymve and Aury.

## Known Facts

- Lady Rook wants it or what it enables.
- Ainslie studies and drains it with Bardic Inspiration from Zymve and Guidance from Aury.
- It is now empty or altered.
- Its effect on Ainslie remains unresolved.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 06: Lady Rook provides an egg-shaped Marrowstone artifact of convergence for the grove ritual.
- Session 06: The Marrowstone/egg absorbs or changes during the Blackrend/blight-clearing ritual and becomes dark.
- Session 08: When Rook touches the Marrowstone, it flashes indigo and gives Ainslie a vision.
- Session 20: Ainslie studies and drains/empties the Marrowstone with Zymve's Bardic Inspiration and Aury's Guidance.
- Session 20/21: The stone is treated as emptied or altered afterward.

## Strong Inferences

- The Marrowstone is central to Lady Rook's anti-Cloak/vault agenda.
- Its necrotic/dark energy is dangerous to Ainslie, but the exact transformation is not documented.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- What the drained energy did to Ainslie.
- Whether the Marrowstone energy is Blackrend, necrotic power, vault energy, or something else.
- Whether Rook can still use the stone without the drained energy.

## Open Questions

- What did the Marrowstone do to Ainslie?
- Why did Lady Rook want it?
- Does its drained energy connect to the vault?
