# Pruning Dagger of Diverra

## Scope

HoE-only item.

## Summary

The Pruning Dagger of Diverra is Hap's Diverra-linked weapon, part of his battle kit and faith/vision thread with Aury.

## Known Facts

- Belongs to Hap.
- Listed among Hap's current notable inventory.
- Used alongside Shadowtrail and other weapons.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 13: Meladra gives Hap the Pruning Dagger of Diverra before the party leaves.
- It is described as a +1 dagger in session extraction.
- It is Diverra-linked by name and source.
