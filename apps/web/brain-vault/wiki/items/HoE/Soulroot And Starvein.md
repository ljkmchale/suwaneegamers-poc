# Soulroot And Starvein

## Scope

HoE-only relic thread.

## Summary

Soulroot and Starvein are the two missing companion relic pieces named by Irragosa in Session 25 after Ainslie takes the heart-shaped gem from Moganna's vault beneath Mount Emberstran. Their locations are unknown.

## Known Facts

- Irragosa says the heart gem in Moganna's vault is one of three items.
- The other two items are called **Soulroot** and **Starvein**.
- Irragosa does not know where Soulroot and Starvein are; that knowledge was not given to her.
- The three relic pieces were separated and given to different dragon/rider pairs to hide and guard without telling each other where the other pieces were hidden.
- If all three pieces are combined by the same person, the relic could extend life and break Ahn'Tsuel's 50-year soul cycle.
- Irragosa distinguishes combining all three pieces from finding them and "reopening the circle."

## Related Guardians And Riders

- Irragosa guarded the heart gem and was ridden by Tsulanan.
- Orah Nelcrath rode Karalafene / White Flare.
- Vhurkir Inni rode Kaelix the Copper.
