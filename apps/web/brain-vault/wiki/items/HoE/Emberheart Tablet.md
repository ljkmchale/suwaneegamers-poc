# Emberheart Tablet

## Scope

HoE-only item/artifact.

## Summary

The Emberheart Tablet is the central ancient tablet/vault artifact, first emerging from the dock/barrel thread and later pointing toward the Mount Emberstran vault.

## Known Facts

- First tablet clue appears in early dock trouble.
- Session 13 is named Emberheart Tablet; Aury attunes to it in that session and reads the poem.
- The tablet is roughly 6–7 inches by 8 inches. Assembled magic type: transmutation, divination, and illusion.
- Aury tries the tablet in the vault depression in Session 24.
- Ainslie, not Aury, successfully opens the vault door.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Sessions 01-13: The tablet is assembled from multiple pieces, including VINE, BONE, FLAME, and the Eradic-held fourth piece.
- Session 13: The assembled tablet shows transmutation, divination, and illusion magic.
- Session 13: Aury attunes to it and reads the poem pointing toward Mount Emberstran.
- Session 24: Aury tries to place the tablet in the vault depression, but it resists.
- Session 24: Ainslie presses the tablet in and the vault opens with golden light.
- Session 25: The opened passage leads through bloodline/emotional trials into Moganna's vault. The tablet's route/key role appears fulfilled, but the relic thread continues through the heart gem, Soulroot, and Starvein.

## Strong Inferences

- The tablet is a vault key or route-finder, but it requires more than possession to operate.
- Ainslie's bloodline/Moganna/Tsulanan connection matters to the vault-opening event.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Who made the tablet.
- Why Ainslie succeeded where Aury did not.
- Whether the tablet has another purpose inside the vault.

## The Emberheart Poem

When assembled and attuned, the tablet displays this poem in glowing orange runes:

> *"Where ember roots and thorns entwine,*
> *Beneath the mount, the vault shall shine.*
> *In Baltwood's breath, you'll find the trail,*
> *My warmth, a heartbeat without fail."*

Aury feels the heartbeat of the mountain after reading it and senses a pull toward a specific spot at Mount Emberstran.

## Open Questions

- Why does Ainslie have the stronger vault-opening connection?
- How does the tablet relate to Tsulanan, Irragosa, and dragonriders?
- Does the tablet help locate Soulroot and Starvein?
