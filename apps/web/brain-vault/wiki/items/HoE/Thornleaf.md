# Thornleaf

## Scope

HoE-only item/bond.

## Summary

Thornleaf is Og's Life Tree-linked object/bond. After Petal's death, one thorn turns black, suggesting moral, spiritual, or blight-related consequence.

## Known Facts

- Tied to Og's E'erspan / Life Tree calling.
- Og tries to communicate with it.
- One thorn turns black after Petal's death.
- Connected to Blackrend/blight and Dandelia of the Veil leads.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 06: Og's thorn-leaf/key is required for Rook's grove ritual.
- Session 13: The thorn leaf/vine attaches to Og's wrist, draws blood, and Og hears "Alferumn."
- Session 19: Og is called E'erspan by the Life Tree.
- Session 23/24: After Petal's death, Thornleaf gains a black thorn.

## Strong Inferences

- Thornleaf is tied to Og's Life Tree/Alferumn calling.
- The black thorn is likely a moral/spiritual consequence marker, but the notes do not define it.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- What "Alferumn" means in full.
- Whether Thornleaf is judging Og, the party, or the world state.
- What Dandelia can do for the bond.

## Open Questions

- Why did a thorn turn black?
- Is Thornleaf judging Og, the party, or the world state?
- How does Dandelia help?
