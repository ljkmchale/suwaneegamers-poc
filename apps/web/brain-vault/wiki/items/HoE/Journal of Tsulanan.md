# Journal of Tsulanan

## Scope

HoE-only item/artifact.

## Summary

The Journal of Tsulanan is Ainslie's dangerous/inherited book. Zymve hears music and emotional meaning from it, and Ainslie may be bloodline-connected to Tsulanan or dragonrider history.

## Known Facts

- Comes through Szoradryn / Miss Placed Tomes thread.
- Reacts through bloodline/meaning rather than normal language.
- Zymve hears music from it and feels it connect to her drow past.
- Irragosa reacts to Ainslie's connection and asks Ky'tha to watch him.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 16: The Journal of Tsulanan reaches Ainslie through the Nunglthil / Szoradryn / Miss Placed Tomes thread.
- Session 19: At the Lost Sanctuary, Zymve hears music from the journal that others do not.
- Later sessions: Ainslie identifies Tsulanan as his ancestor / dragonrider connection.
- Session 23: Irragosa asks Ky'tha to watch Ainslie, tying Ainslie's bloodline thread to the mountain/vault route.
- Session 25: Irragosa confirms Tsulanan rode her and that Ainslie is tied to the vault inheritance. Zymve recognizes Vhurkir Inni as an ancestral figure connected to jewelcrafting and secrecy.

## Strong Inferences

- The journal is keyed to Ainslie's bloodline and to Zymve's musical perception.
- The journal is likely connected to the vault's history, but it is not yet a fully decoded map.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether the journal has its own will.
- Why only Zymve hears its music.
- Whether Szoradryn intended all of its effects.

## Open Questions

- What does the journal want or reveal?
- Why does it sing to Zymve?
- How does it connect to the vault?
- Did Vhurkir Inni design or influence any part of Moganna's vault?
