# Badges of Emberstran / E.G.D.

## Scope

HoE-only item/group credential.

## Summary

The Badges of Emberstran / E.G.D. give the party a form of civic/official identity that Ainslie especially uses as social leverage.

## Known Facts

- Presented/blessed by Aury.
- Used to frame the party as official or legitimate.
- Connected to Emberstran civic standing after the party's public successes.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 21: Aury presents/blesses badges associated with Emberstran/EGD identity.
- The party uses EGD identity as a civic/social tool in Emberstran.
- EGD legitimacy is complicated by the official scroll being signed by Black Quill rather than clearly by the Lord.

## Strong Inferences

- The badges help the party lean into official status, but authority depends on who recognizes them.
- They may become politically dangerous if the Cloak/Rook power balance changes.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether the badges carry legal authority or only social weight.
- Whether Lady Rook, Selvara, or Lord Dusklight can revoke or contest them.

## Open Questions

- What authority do the badges truly grant?
- Who recognizes or contests them?
