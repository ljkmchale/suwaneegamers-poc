# Flying Ship Transmutation Orb

## Scope

HoE-only item/artifact.

## Summary

The Flying Ship Transmutation Orb is a metallic orb casing recovered from the crashed flying ship near Mount Emberstran in Session 25. It appears to have been mounted on a pedestal on the ship and may be connected to how the ship flew.

## Known Facts

- Found in the wreckage of the flying ship that Irragosa admits she brought down near Mount Emberstran.
- The casing is metallic and orb-shaped.
- Inside are floating pieces of stone.
- Ainslie detects transmutation magic on it.
- Hap recalls Iannan Rambibur, a gnome in Tarnsport who studied magic that could make things levitate and fly.
- The party suspects lodestone or flight magic and takes the orb.

## Strong Inferences

- The orb was likely a flight, lift, or stabilization component for the airship.
- Its stone fragments may be magically suspended or levitation-linked.
- It could become evidence for identifying the ship's origin, builder, or owner.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Who built the ship.
- Whether Iannan Rambibur made or influenced the orb.
- Whether the orb is repairable or dangerous.
- Whether any faction was expecting the ship to arrive near Mount Emberstran.
