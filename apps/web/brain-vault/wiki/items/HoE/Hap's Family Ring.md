# Hap's Family Ring

## Scope

HoE-only item/quest object.

## Summary

Hap's missing family ring is tied to his father's murder, the Cloak of Defiance, Jerik, Phira, and inherited land.

## Known Facts

- Stolen/missing after Hap's father's murder.
- Connected to the Cloak of Defiance thread.
- One of Hap's core personal goals is recovery.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Hap's personal quest includes finding his father's murderers and retrieving his ring.
- The Garemon/Cetaen records and grave swaps connect Hap's family history to Cloak/Jerik activity.
- Session 13: Arnol brings Hap a separate black obsidian "G" medallion on a copper chain from the Eradic stash; this is not confirmed to be the missing ring.

## Strong Inferences

- The missing ring likely matters to Hap's inheritance/family proof, but the source does not define its legal or magical role.
- Jerik/Cloak involvement is strongly suspected through the wider family-coverup chain, not directly proven for the ring itself.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Who has the ring.
- Whether the "G" medallion is related to the ring.
- Whether the ring has legal, sentimental, or magical importance.

## Open Questions

- Who has the ring now?
- Does it prove inheritance, land rights, or something magical?
- How does Jerik fit?
