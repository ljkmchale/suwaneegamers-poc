# Shadowtrail

## Scope

HoE-only item/weapon.

## Summary

Shadowtrail is Hap's talking weapon and cryptic guide, pushing toward "neverending" and likely connecting to the vault path.

## Known Facts

- Belongs to Hap.
- Talks cryptically.
- Uses or repeats the concept "neverending."
- Anticipates or connects to Irragosa/vault threats.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 15: Hap takes Shadowtrail from Mandel Spitespirit's cache after the Ythibius fight.
- Session 15: The sword is described as an ebon blade / short sword with draconic runes.
- Later sessions: Hap uses Shadowtrail and the item becomes part of his route/vault thread.
- The word or concept "neverending" is associated with it in later notes.
- Session 25: While camping after the vault, Hap asks the weapon for a name and hears "Shadowtrail" in his head. When Hap notices Irragosa nearby, the sword says "we could take her," referring to the dragon.

## Strong Inferences

- Shadowtrail is connected to dragon/vault-era history through its cache origin, runes, and Ythibius context.
- It may guide Hap, but its alignment or agenda is not documented.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Who made Shadowtrail.
- What "neverending" means.
- Whether Shadowtrail serves Hap, Diverra, the vault, or its own purpose.

## Open Questions

- What does "neverending" mean?
- Who made Shadowtrail?
- Is it aligned with Diverra, the vault, or Hap's family thread?
- Why does it believe it and Hap could take Irragosa?
