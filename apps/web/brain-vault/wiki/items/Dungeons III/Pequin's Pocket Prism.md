# Pequin's Pocket Prism

## Summary

Pequin's Pocket Prism is a light-storing device bought by [[Nova]] from Pequin in [[Hesfal]].

## Known Facts

- Pequin says it stores light.
- The longer it collects light, the more it can shine.
- Pequin initially wants 3 gp; Nova buys it for 1 gp and 5 sp.
- Nova uses it during the bandit/prisoner-break fight to blind three enemies.
