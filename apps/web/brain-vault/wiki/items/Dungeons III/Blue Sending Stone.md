# Blue Sending Stone

## Summary

The Blue Sending Stone is a palm-sized blue stone found in the K9 Crew cave hideout. It enables or carries communication from the Scorpion.

## Known Facts

- Found in a hidden box with coin in the K9 Crew hideout.
- Draelith determines it is not acting like a normal sending stone.
- The cave appears to amplify communication magic, as if the stone vibrates against the place itself.
- The Scorpion later contacts Draelith through the stone after the K9 Crew judgment.

## Open Questions

- Is the stone special by itself, or mainly because of the cave?
- Can the Scorpion locate or hear the party through it?
- Does the stone connect to larger tunnel/gate magic?
