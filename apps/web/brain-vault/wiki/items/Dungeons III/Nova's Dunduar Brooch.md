# Nova's Dunduar Brooch

## Summary

Nova's Dunduar Brooch is a magical brooch carried by [[Nova]]. Lavoruud tries to steal it back after claiming he feels hollow without it.

## Known Facts

- Lavoruud claims he won it from an orc in Tratta and sold it for 25 gp.
- Fikrak Blackshield identifies Dunduar gold properties and says the brooch sings to his ring.
- Blackshield later reads an inscription inside the brooch:

> Bound against inner fracture. Do not separate stone from lattice. Containment preserves more than form.

- Blackshield warns Nova never to remove the stone from the jewelry.
- He says a Dunduar smith may understand the stone treatment better.

## Open Questions

- What is contained or preserved by the brooch?
- Why did Lavoruud feel hollow without it?
- Who made it, and why did it leave Dunduar?
