# Osmanae Celestial Instrument

## Summary

The Osmanae Celestial Instrument is an unrecovered quest object from Dungeons III. Embryl believes it may be at an ancient Osmanae outpost or observatory in the crags east of [[Scarwatch Hold]].

## Known Facts

- Embryl found a damaged record referencing the Last Watchtower of the Osmanae.
- The Osmanae were an ancient group who observed stars and heavenly bodies.
- The instrument calibrated celestial records disrupted since the Shattering.
- Embryl wants it recovered, restored, and taken to the Advents of Harmony.
- The Radiant Eye Cult may also be looking for it.

## Current State

The party has accepted the lead and is already heading toward the crags where the outpost may be located.
