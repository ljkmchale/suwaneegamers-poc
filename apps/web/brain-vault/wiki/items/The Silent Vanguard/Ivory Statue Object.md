# Ivory Statue Object

## Summary

An ivory or statue-like object recovered from Harold Stonesheep's backpack in Brinecross. Its significance has not yet been determined.

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Known Facts

- Recovered from Harold's backpack during Session 02.
- Described as ivory or statue-like; specific form and size not documented.
- May be the "object of value" Harold was tasked with holding for the party per Thorne's Session 01 briefing.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Silent Vanguard Session 01: Thorne says Harold has an object of value.
- Silent Vanguard Session 02: An ivory/statue-like object is recovered from Harold's backpack.
- The party keeps/retrieves the object during the Brinecross escape sequence.

## Strong Inferences

- It may connect to The Long Night or the warehouse letter because it comes from the same Harold/Brinecross mission.
