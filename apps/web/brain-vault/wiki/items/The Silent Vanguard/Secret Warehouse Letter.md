# Secret Warehouse Letter

## Summary

A letter recovered in Brinecross during Session 02. Addressed to Gultren the Canvas Master at the Sticky Pear Inn in Tratta, it documents a trafficking shipment and is the party's most significant evidence of the operation.

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Known Contents

- References crates, chains, cages, fish, spice, people, and guards.
- Describes a shipment arriving in 20 nights.
- Points to the south warehouse near the old stone ramp as the staging location.

## Current Holder

The party. They chose to keep the letter rather than deliver it.

## Strategic Value

- Can be used to approach Gultren under a cover delivery scenario.
- May be usable as evidence against the operation if authorities are trustworthy.
- The 20-night window may have already passed depending on in-game time.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Silent Vanguard Session 02: The letter is recovered in Brinecross.

- It references crates, chains, cages, fish, spice, people, and guards.
- It says a shipment arrives in 20 nights.

## Strong Inferences

- The letter is direct evidence of trafficking or forced transport.
- It can be used as a cover approach to Gultren or as evidence, if the party finds trustworthy authorities.
