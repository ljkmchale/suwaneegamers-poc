# Arena Duskwhisper

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Therric's mystic contact in Adsuren. Also spelled "Areana Duskwhisper" in player notes. Current whereabouts unknown.

## Summary

Arena Duskwhisper is a mystic Therric connected with in the Celedon Forest on his way into Adsuren. She was a regular presence at Market Day, but Therric has not seen her in some time. When he asked Mollywop in Session 05 whether she had a way to reach Arena, Mollywop said she would try — but advised Therric to take time to find himself first.

## Known Facts

- A mystic; met Therric in or near the Celedon Forest as he traveled northwest into Adsuren.
- Had a routine of attending the Market in Adsuren.
- Therric has not seen her in some time as of Session 01 (before the party assembled).
- **Session 05**: Therric asked Mollywop if she could contact Arena Duskwhisper. Mollywop said she would try, and also told Therric it might be best for him to take time to find himself first.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- What kind of mystic she is — magic school, tradition, or affiliation unknown.
- Why she has not been seen at Market.
- Whether Mollywop was able to make contact after Session 05.
- Whether she has any connection to the events unfolding in Adsuren.

## Relationships

- Therric — made a connection with her before the campaign opened; her absence concerns him.
- Mollywop — Therric sought Mollywop's help to reach her; Mollywop indicated she might be able to try.

## Open Questions
