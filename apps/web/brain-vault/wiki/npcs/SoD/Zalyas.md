# Zalyas

## Scope

SoD-only dream figure tied to Lila.

## Summary

Zalyas is a halfling figure from Lila's dream. He is romantic, tied to a crystal-shop scene and a lilac gem/ring, and the dream left Lila with temporary hit points.

## Known Facts

- Appears in Lila's dream during [[SoD Session 06 - Who Took It]].
- Helps Lila after she is knocked down near a crystal vendor.
- Later appears in picnic/romantic imagery.
- Gives or is associated with a lilac-colored gem/ring, described as extremely valuable/beautiful.
- Lila wakes with 6 temporary hit points.
