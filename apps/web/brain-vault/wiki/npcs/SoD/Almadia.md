# Almadia

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Proprietor of the Tannery in Adsuren.

## Summary

Almadia is the female owner and master tanner of the Tannery in Adsuren. She greeted the party on their visit in Session 05, recognized them, and was unknowingly employing Ganon — a Cult of the Immured operative — as one of her apprentices. She expressed strong interest in obtaining the hide of the White Horns bulas for leatherworking.

## Known Facts

- Female proprietor of the Tannery in Adsuren.
- Greeted the party during Session 05 and recognized them.
- Employs at least two apprentices: Redbeard (a balding rugged dwarf with red hair, longtime local) and Ganon (now dead — Cult of the Immured cultist).
- Stocks hides from forest and swamp beasts; has a bulas hide on display.
- Wants the White Horns (a massive horned bulas near the mines) brought in — its hide would make exceptionally strong leather.
- Investigated the broom clatter when Escanor entered through the back door; Esylla covered for him.
- Her tannery was later identified as a black salt transit point tied to Ganon's cult operation.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 05: Almadia (Tanner) greets the party and recognizes them.
- Session 05: She expresses interest in the White Horns bulas hide for leathers.
- Session 05: She employs Ganon, unaware of his cultist activities.

## Strong Inferences

- Almadia is likely unaware that her tannery was used for black salt transit — Ganon operated without her knowledge.
- Redbeard (the dwarf apprentice) appears to be a legitimate long-term employee with no cult connection.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether she has any connection to the black salt operation beyond employing Ganon.
- Whether she is still operating the tannery after Ganon's death and the investigation.

## Relationships

- Ganon — former apprentice; cult operative who used the tannery as cover (she was unaware).
- Redbeard — dwarf apprentice; legitimate, longtime local employee.
- Party — recognized them on sight; cooperative.
