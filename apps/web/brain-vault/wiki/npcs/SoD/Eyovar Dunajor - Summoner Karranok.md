# Eyovar Dunajor / Summoner Karranok

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Former Adsuren Intendant and Cult of the Immured ritual leader.

## Summary

Eyovar Dunajor was the civic Intendant of Adsuren and secretly operated as Summoner Karranok beneath Adsuren Center. His bargain with Aeolenne began as a prosperity bargain after the Awakening and escalated from animal sacrifices to hearts.

## Known Facts

- Revealed as Summoner Karranok in [[SoD Session 08 - Cult of the Immured]].
- His confession (verbatim): *"About 20 years ago, the Awakening happened in the world. Adsuren was smaller then. Ever since Mollywop left the town it became a tourist spot and became overcrowded. I was newly elected. I reached out into the world and asked for help and aid, and eventually heard a voice that said she could bring prosperity to the town if he sacrificed an animal once per harmon. Then over time that became once a stretch. It became too much for me to do. So people arrived to help. They formed a cult to help with the ritual. Then a little over a stretch ago, the demands became a desire for hearts so we used a heart from a dead body. Then the well happened. And I was told that we couldn't stop the rituals or doom would befall the town."*
- When asked about his eyes and the source of his power: says the power does not come from him.
- When asked what Aeolenne's goal is: says he does not know.
- When asked where Aeolenne dwells: thinks it is in the lower planes.
- Was not sure where the cultists got the dead body used for the heart.
- Cultists arrived from Caldwynn, Gibuldon, Morrey, and other places.
- His previous civic title was Intendant of Adsuren. Also known as Jerborin Omakian's superior.
- Surrendered and knelt when subdued. Escanor and Therric pushed for lawful handoff rather than execution.
- Turned over to Captain Alinia and the guards by Therric and Kenton.
- His assistant became acting Intendant after the reveal.

## Strong Inferences

- Eyovar is both perpetrator and compromised public official; his confession suggests fear/coercion, but not innocence.
- The cult network is larger than Adsuren because he names multiple external origins.
- He may not have understood that the ritual would free Aeolenne, but the notes do not absolve him.
