# MacCannain

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Dockmaster at Adsuren.

## Summary

MacCannain is the dockmaster at Adsuren's docks, overseeing the ferry system and ship traffic that crosses the river. He is a minor civic figure who recognized the party after the poisoned well incident. He is documented as having interacted with the party during Session 02 and indirectly relevant to Ganon's black salt barrel operation, which moved through the docks.

## Known Facts

- Dockmaster at Adsuren; runs the ferry crossing and oversees ship traffic.
- Session 02: The party spoke to him at the docks. He recognized them from "tales of yesterday" (the poisoned well incident). He noted he hadn't seen the Sapcutt family in some time.
- He described the lumber ship ferry system — ships leave when enough people have gathered to cross.
- Session 06: Lila and/or others spoke with MacCannain to gather information about Ganon's barrel delivery and dock operations as part of the shipping investigation.

## Strong Inferences

- MacCannain is a passive witness to cargo and people moving through Adsuren's docks. His knowledge of unusual shipments (the black salt barrel tied to Ganon) is likely limited to what passed openly through the docks.
- He is cooperative with the party but not an active investigator.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- What exactly he knew or observed about Ganon's barrel operation.
- Whether he has any ongoing relationship with the party post-cult-reveal.

## Relationships

- Party — recognized them after the well incident; cooperative.
- Ganon — Ganon's black salt barrel was transported through dock infrastructure; MacCannain's awareness of this is unclear.
