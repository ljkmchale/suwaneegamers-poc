# Tanluar Rotvori

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Priest of the Nature's Oratory in Adsuren.

## Summary

Tanluar Rotvori is the older half-elf priest who presides over Adsuren's Nature's Oratory. He is Escanor's spiritual home-base contact and one of Zephyra's established connections in town. Across the SoD arc he has been a consistent, cautious ally — directing the party toward the poisoned well investigation, providing crypt access, searching historical records, and ultimately taking public charge of the cult aftermath alongside Captain Alinia Sarralei.

## Appearance And Background

- Older half-elf.
- Runs the Nature's Oratory on the edge of Adsuren with an attached cemetery of roughly 50 graves.
- Known to both Escanor (who stays at the Oratory) and Zephyra (pre-existing connection from her years in Adsuren).

## Known Facts

- **Session 01**: Arrived when the poisoned man was brought in; took charge and directed the party to investigate the well. Facilitated crypt access during the undead investigation below the cemetery.
- **Sessions 03–04**: Briefed by Escanor on the cemetery disturbance. Searched Oratory records for prior accounts of swamp problems; found some earlier references.
- **Session 06**: Escanor checked with him about family names from the Adsuren Center book; he knew nothing. He agreed to spend time watching over the graves for any further disturbance or missing persons.
- **Session 08**: Escanor woke Rotvori after the cult was exposed. He joined Captain Alinia and the Guard in taking public charge of the aftermath — the Oratory's clearest civic moment.

## Party Relationship

- **Escanor** — strongest bond; the Oratory is Escanor's base and Rotvori trusts him. Information flows freely between them.
- **Zephyra** — established local connection; he knows her by name from her years in town.
- **Broader party** — uses the Oratory's credibility for moral authority and undead/cult-adjacent issues.
- **Kenton** — no direct relationship.

## Strong Inferences

- Rotvori's pattern is to offer information and access while staying out of direct action — he is a civic stabilizer, not an adventurer.
- His control of the cemetery and Oratory records makes him a passive intelligence asset for the party.

## Open Questions

- What records does the Oratory hold about Adsuren's deeper history — specifically pre-cult activity or the black salt shipments?
- Can Rotvori provide any anti-mind-control support related to entities like Aeolenne?
- Will his civic role expand now that Eyovar has been removed and the Intendant's position is in transition?
