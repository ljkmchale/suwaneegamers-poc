# Captain Alinia Sarralei

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Adsuren Guard captain.

## Summary

Captain Alinia Sarralei is Adsuren's guard captain. She praises Zephyra and represents civic authority, but her resentment toward Mollywop may distort her judgment.

## Known Facts

- Has a scar around her neck and does not discuss it.
- Praises Zephyra for Market Day heroism and local service.
- Dismissive of Durgruck Bristleforge as a guard.
- Sent Hugo to cemetery watch, which Zephyra knows is a weak assignment.
- Resents Mollywop's influence and flamboyance.
- Takes control after the Cult of the Immured is exposed.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 04: Captain Alinia is encountered as Adsuren Guard authority during the graveyard/guard thread.
- Session 04: She has a scar around her neck and does not discuss it.
- Session 04: She praises Zephyra and is dismissive of Durgruck Bristleforge.
- Session 06/07: Guards question Mollywop about alarms/missing funds under Alinia's authority or pressure.
- Session 08: After Eyovar/Karranok is exposed, Alinia and the guards take custody/authority over the aftermath.

## Strong Inferences

- Alinia is a useful lawful contact for Zephyra but not a neutral observer around Mollywop.
- Her resentment of Mollywop may shape guard decisions, but the notes do not prove corruption.
- She may be embarrassed or politically exposed by the cult operating beneath Adsuren Center.
