# Aeolenne

## Scope

SoD-only NPC / antagonist. Do not apply this page to HoE or The Silent Vanguard.

## Summary

Aeolenne is a fiendish/succubus-like being tied directly to [[Zephyra]]. She was freed during the Cult of the Immured ritual beneath Adsuren Center and escaped south, likely toward Caldwynn.

## Appearance

Tall, slender, and beautiful. Green eyes. Lilac skin. Black, oily leather dress/gown. Black boots. A tail and two black horns. Looks like Zephyra but "a little more fiendish." Can also appear fully human — she demonstrates the switch during her confrontation with the party.

## Known Facts

- Manifested from blood, corpse, and central flame in the ritual chamber beneath Adsuren Center.
- Eyovar named Aeolenne as the voice behind the cult's escalating demands — she spoke to him approximately 20 years ago after the Awakening.
- Esylla's interpretation: not demonic or infernal (Mollywop's heart analysis) — likely a succubus-type fiend.
- Names herself: "Yes, that is one of my many names — some call me mother, others father." Notably uses both gendered terms.
- Claims to be Zephyra's mother. Zephyra insists her parents are both human. Aeolenne responds by demonstrating a human form, then switching back.
- Her stated want: she says she just needed a year and wanted to grow stronger. Implies her current power is not yet what she wants it to be.
- Mind-controlled Zephyra in the fight: Zephyra's eyes turned green and she sided with Aeolenne momentarily. Zephyra broke free through a childhood memory of Jimmy Meanpants bullying her and her father teaching her to stand up for herself.
- Combat: she said "kill the half-breed first" (directed at the party), then drank an invisibility potion and fled. Party could not pursue.
- Mollywop confirmed a fiendish presence left Adsuren's wards heading south.
- Has at least one ally/contact in Caldwynn, ~5 days south of Adsuren (from the Ganon letter).

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 08: Aeolenne appears in the ritual chamber beneath Adsuren Center.
- Session 08: She claims Zephyra as her child and demonstrates a human appearance after Zephyra says her parents are human.
- Session 08: Zephyra is briefly controlled or compelled during the fight, then breaks free through a memory of her father.
- Session 08: Aeolenne drinks an invisibility potion and escapes.
- Session 08 aftermath: Mollywop confirms a fiendish presence left Adsuren and headed south.

## Strong Inferences

- Aeolenne is probably a succubus-type fiend or adjacent entity; the page should keep "succubus-like" rather than assert a monster category as final.
- Her claim to be Zephyra's mother may be true, manipulative, or technically true through fiendish heritage. The notes do not fully resolve it.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

## Relationships

- Charm/domination or mind-control style influence.
- Fiendish/succubus-like manipulation.
- Long-term growth goal: she said she needed time and wanted to grow in strength beyond Adsuren.
