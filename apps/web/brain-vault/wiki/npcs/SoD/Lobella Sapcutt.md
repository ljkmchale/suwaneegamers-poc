# Lobella Sapcutt

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Sapcutt survivor and source for the fungus/black fish explanation.

## Summary

Lobella is the surviving Sapcutt daughter found alive in the fungal house. She explains that her brothers found a black fish in the swamp and that the family became entangled with fungus and a plant bargain.

## Known Facts

- Found alive in the Sapcutt house during [[SoD Session 03 - FUKWAD]].
- Had a bulbous fungus on her chest that Esylla destroyed.
- Explains that the family ate/cooked a black fish from deep in the swamp.
- Explains the mother made a deal with a plant intelligence to feed it visitors in exchange for keeping family alive.
- Shares pie recipe location/context.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 03: Lobella is found alive in the Sapcutt house.
- Session 03: Esylla destroys a bulbous fungus on Lobella's chest.
- Session 03: Lobella explains the black fish came from deep in the swamp and was eaten/cooked by the family.
- Session 03: Lobella explains Lady Sapcutt made a plant bargain to feed visitors in exchange for keeping family alive.
- Session 03: Lobella and Margaret are rescued from the house.
