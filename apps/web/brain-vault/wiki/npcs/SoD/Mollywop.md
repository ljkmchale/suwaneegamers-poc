# Mollywop

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Magical ally/contact near Adsuren.

## Summary

Mollywop the Magnificent is a powerful, title-laden sorcerer whose misshapen three-story tower sits on the edge of Adsuren. She is one of the party's most important resources across the entire SoD arc: she interprets the black heart, handles dangerous evidence, provides magical contacts, and coordinates the cult aftermath.

## Appearance And Background

- Title: "Mollywop the Magnificent" — she has many titles.
- Height: approximately 5'6" (per Zephyra).
- Tower: three stories, misshapen, white marble stone with a golden-vein-patterned door. A squirrel sits by the door and can utter something to open it.
- Familiar: cat. Also has a squirrel associate near the door.
- Background: from adventuring days 30+ years ago. Associated with the **Tegenwald Phet** and **Dionara** — helped bring the Phet into the light and improved their reputation across Myrdae.
- Has been away from Adsuren and the town grew as a tourist spot in her absence.
- Tower password (Esylla's access): spoken in Draconic.

## Known Facts

- Maintains an alarm/ward around Adsuren every day — habit from adventuring days. Detects things coming and going through the barrier.
- Apprentices: **Daudery** (halfling, light tan skin, bronze hair; tends the plants outside; opens the tower door), **Thunderfall** (dwarf, cooks breakfast), **Lix** (half-elf, studies upstairs).
- Keeps a demon, **Zuz**, under an arcane bubble to study — trying to learn its true name to teach apprentices.
- Heart analysis (Session 02): identifies necromancy, enchantment, fiendish quality, soul tethering, and calls it "an object of torment." Believes it is NOT demonic or infernal — Esylla later connects this to succubus rather than demon.
- Suspects the swamp creature from the party's description might be a dragon, but thinks there is probably a link between the swamp and the fungus.
- Concerned about the Sapcutt family — had been scrying them and asked the party to check.
- Session 07 (guards at tower): Guards question her about the alarm and missing town funds (related to Adsuren Center scandal). She believes Captain Alinia has it out for her. Is tired from being up most of the night.
- Esylla senses Mollywop is hiding something / not fully disclosing when they speak before the cellar mission.
- Session 08: Esylla exits the wards so Mollywop can reach her; Mollywop appears instantly in silk pajamas via Sending. Confirms a fiendish presence left town. Helps destroy/mar the ritual circle with Escanor.
- Post-cult: at Zephyra's house, tells Zephyra there is a way to protect against mind control, but it requires an old friend's help and is expensive/high-level; tells Zephyra that her succubus heritage will come in time as she grows in power.
- Confirms Aeolenne headed south. Notes the Caldwynn ally from the letters.
- Identifies potions and the +1 staff for the party.
- Helped Esylla arrange Kenton's +1 whip trade.
- Told Therric she would try to contact Areana Duskwhisper, but suggested he find himself first.
- Sent Sending reply about Esylla's aunt (surprised aunt heard she was dead; debate in the family). Later assumes the aunt is on the way.
- Has an old friend who can supply anti-mind-control help, but the cost is too high now.
- Lila received a scroll of Longstrider from her.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 02: The party visits Mollywop's tower after the black heart is discovered; she analyzes the heart and identifies necromancy, enchantment, fiendish quality, and soul tethering.
- Session 02: Mollywop asks the party to check the Sapcutt family after scrying concerns.
- Session 04/05: Mollywop and tower resources remain tied to Adsuren's magical investigation and party support.
- Session 07: Guards question Mollywop about alarms and missing town funds; she appears tired and under civic pressure.
- Session 08: Mollywop reaches Esylla by magic, confirms a fiendish presence left Adsuren, and helps disrupt the ritual circle.
- Session 08 aftermath: She discusses anti-mind-control protection for Zephyra and identifies items for the party.

## Strong Inferences

- Mollywop is one of the party's strongest SoD magical allies, but not a transparent one.
- Her daily ward makes her a town-scale sensor, though Market Day and other traffic may limit certainty.
- Her old contacts matter, especially for Zephyra's protection and Esylla's family thread.
