# Sapcutt Family

## Scope

SoD-only NPC group. Halfling family living on a plantation in the swamps outside Adsuren.

## Summary

The Sapcutt family are a halfling family renowned in Adsuren for their famous, sugary pies — the highlight of every Market Day. After Levian and Jotho fished up a black fish from deep in the swamp and the family ate it, a fungal rot took hold. Margaret made a deal with a sentient plant intelligence to feed it visitors in exchange for keeping the family alive. Their plantation became a trap, and by the time the party arrived in Session 03 the house itself had become a living organism. Jackin (the father) was dead; his sons were rotted beyond saving inside the house; Margaret and Lobella were rescued and escaped.

## Family Members

### Jackin Sapcutt
- Husband and father. Known for the family's famous pies.
- Found dead in the master bedroom — Escanor determined he had died of old age and disease, not the fungus.
- Margaret was tending his body when the party arrived, trying to revive him using the fungal "strength."
- Left behind an inscribed sword in the armor stand: **"To Abella, Love Dad."**
- His ring was taken off the nightstand by Margaret as she fled.

### Margaret Sapcutt
- Wife and mother. 2½' tall; ragged clothes; braided hair.
- Made the deal with the sentient plant to feed it visitors in exchange for protecting the family.
- Fought the party in the master bedroom (Combat vs. FUKWAD Lady Sapcutt); subdued when Lila knocked the fungus off her with a chromatic orb.
- Warned the party that the brothers could not be killed permanently while still inside the house.
- Escaped through a window with Lobella, grabbing Jackin's ring off the nightstand.
- Agreed to let the party copy the pie recipe in exchange for not sharing or profiting from it.

### Levian (Lebioun)
- One of the rotted halfling brothers. Called "Lebioun" by Mollywop (who had been scrying them) and "Levian" in the combat encounters.
- First encountered in the living room; attacked the party.
- Could not be permanently killed inside the house — Escanor determined the fungus granted him a form of immortality while in the building.
- Multiple combat encounters; party eventually stopped fighting him and left the house.

### Jotho
- The other rotted halfling brother. Found sleeping in his room.
- Attacked the party with spore attacks; Zep decapitated him but he continued to regenerate inside the house.

### Lobella Sapcutt
See [[Lobella Sapcutt]] — her own NPC page covers her role as the survivor and primary exposition source.

## The Plantation

- A large swamp ranch with a mansion-size house, barn, and side buildings.
- String lights hanging from trees mark the property border.
- The table and chairs in the dining room are extensions of the living organism (the house itself).
- The floor of the dining room opened up and swallowed the party.
- Boarded windows, black mold, necrotic vines, fungal growths throughout.
- The sentient plant deal meant the house actively trapped visitors as food.

## Current State

- Jackin: dead and left in the master bedroom.
- Levian and Jotho: still inside the house, unable to be permanently killed while there.
- Margaret and Lobella: rescued, escaped via window; took Jackin's ring; keeping the pie recipe secret.
- The house is still "alive" and dangerous.

## Open Questions

- Who or what is "Abella" — another child, or a nickname for Lobella?
- Can Levian and Jotho be saved or cured if removed from the house?
- What is the origin of the sentient plant — connected to the swamp corruption, the bulas, or something else?
- Where did Margaret and Lobella go after the rescue?
