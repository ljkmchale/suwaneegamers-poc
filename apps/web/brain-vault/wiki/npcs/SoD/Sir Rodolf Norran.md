# Sir Rodolf Norran

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Kenton's active adversary from the Gibuldon/Reyvennra thread.

## Summary

Sir Rodolf Norran ambushed Kenton in Adsuren using hired thugs and Little Jimmy as bait. A healing potion marked with Reyvennra was left after the fight, suggesting Kenton was meant to survive.

## Known Facts

- Connected to Kenton's hazy Gibuldon party memory: Kenton was drunk at a building/castle/manor-like place associated with the Reyvennra thread, partially undressed, at what seemed to be a party. He heard talk of silks, a ring, a dwarf, overthrow, rightful heirship, and passing silks along. He saw a bound, bloodied dwarf at a table. Kenton does not fully remember the night.
- Rodolf accuses Kenton of starting a fire at a warehouse and stealing silks. Kenton insists he did not start the fire and believes a crate of silks he received fell off a cart and was not stolen.
- Ambush method: paid Little Jimmy to throw manure-like tar at Kenton to lure him into an alley, then closed in with two hired thugs (armed with clubs and a crossbow).
- Kenton was downed (death save) during the fight. Rodolf left a healing potion on the ground when he fled — marked with Reyvennra — suggesting he wanted Kenton alive.
- Rodolf ran on Round 7-8 when the rest of the party arrived. A remaining thug said "it was business" and fled.
- Kenton's letter to House Reyvennra (written after the fight) accuses Rodolf of staging the warehouse incident as a diversion to cover his "real game," hints at secrets, and taunts Reyvennra with the implication that Rodolf has his own agenda. This is Kenton's accusation, not yet confirmed fact.
- A messenger named Laudneyr was sent toward Gibuldon with Kenton's letter.
- Uses hired hands and money to manipulate locals (Little Jimmy, thugs).

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 04/05: Rodolf's threat becomes active through Kenton's Gibuldon/Reyvennra past.
- Session 05: Little Jimmy is paid to lure Kenton into an alley.

- Session 05: Rodolf leaves a Reyvennra-marked healing potion when he flees.

## Strong Inferences

- Rodolf wants Kenton alive, not dead, based on the healing potion.
- He is tied to House Reyvennra by name/mark and by the Gibuldon incident, but whether as loyal agent, rival actor, or internal schemer is not fully documented.
- Kenton's missing memory is central to why Rodolf is pursuing him.
