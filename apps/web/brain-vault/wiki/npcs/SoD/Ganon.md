# Ganon

## Scope

SoD-only NPC. Tannery apprentice and Cult of the Immured cultist.

## Summary

Ganon is a tannery apprentice/cover identity for a cultist involved in black salt, fiendish magic, sentient-heart acquisition, and familiar surveillance.

## Known Facts

- Worked around the Tanner/Tannery.
- Had black salt barrel delivered via dock/Caldwynn paperwork.
- Used or carried a crude fiendish focus.
- Attacked Escanor and Esylla near the river/tanner lead.
- Killed by Escanor/Esylla.
- Mollywop questioned his corpse; he named Summoner Karranok.
- His room contained rat-head ritual evidence, incense, and orders.
- His letter mentioned familiars, sentient hearts, black salt storage under AC, avoiding the Mage, and summoning their lord.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 05: Ganon attacks Escanor and Esylla near the river/tannery lead and is killed.
- Session 05: Mollywop questions Ganon's corpse and Summoner Karranok is named.
- Session 06: The party investigates Ganon's room and finds ritual evidence involving rat heads, incense, and orders.
- Session 06: The Caldwynn barrel/shipping paperwork and black salt trail connect to Ganon's operation.
- Session 07: Ganon's evidence points toward ritual activity beneath Adsuren Center.

## Strong Inferences

- Ganon is a low-to-mid-level Cult of the Immured operative rather than the cult's leader.
- His "avoid the Mage" wording likely refers to Mollywop, but the notes do not make that fully explicit.
- His familiar/imp network suggests the cult had surveillance capacity.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Ganon knew Eyovar was Summoner Karranok in public life.
- Who wrote Ganon's orders.
- Whether any familiar escaped with useful intelligence.

## Relationships

- Cult of the Immured - subordinate cultist.
- Summoner Karranok / Eyovar - master/leader by title.
- Panwin - underpaid dock worker who moved the barrel.
- MacCannain - dockmaster involved in the delivery paperwork.

## Open Questions

- Did Ganon know Eyovar's public identity?
- Which familiar/imp escaped or reported on the party?
- How many similar low-level cultists exist outside Adsuren?
