# Ryland Fibbergoan / Sparkwhisper

## Scope

HoE-only NPC / location contact.

## Summary

Ryland Fibbergoan is a gnome name connected to the Sparkwhisper hamlet inside Mount Emberstran — a settlement of gnomes who have built round homes from odd metal pieces and vent pipes, deep inside the volcano. "Emberbogs and Fibbergoans" are the two family names in the hamlet. The hamlet is named Sparkwhisper, and it has a forge called the Whisper Forge. Zymve worked at the forge and made something for Og there. Hap's whistle turned out to match the gnomes' whistles exactly.

## Sparkwhisper — The Hamlet

- Located at an edge/interior of Mount Emberstran's volcanic cavern system.
- Small homes built into rocky caves — round, assembled from odd pieces of metal, vent pipes, etc.
- Gnomes signal with whistles; when the portcullis opened for the party, the hamlet went quiet, then whistles began as the gnomes observed them.
- Gnomes eat mushrooms and fungi; haven't been in the volcano all that long.
- **Whisper Forge** — the forge inside Sparkwhisper; Zymve used it that night.
- **Lyra** — gnome at the Whisper Forge; Zymve talked to her while working.
- **Zybeak** — a gnome who accompanied or vouched for the party to get them past the portcullis into the hamlet.

## Hap's Whistle

- Hap had a whistle from outside the mountain.
- On the morning after the long rest in Sparkwhisper, the party learned that the gnomes' town whistles are identical to Hap's whistle.
- The party told the gnomes they found a dead gnome outside and returned the whistle — they recovered the gnome.
- What the whistle means as a plot element is unclear.

## What Zymve Made For Og

- The notes do not specify what Zymve made for Og at the Whisper Forge.
- It was made in a few hours using forge work; she talked to Lyra while doing it.
