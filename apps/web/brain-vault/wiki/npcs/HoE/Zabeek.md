# Zabeek

## Scope

HoE-only NPC.

## Summary

Zabeek is a gnome connected to Sparkwhisper and the Mount Emberstran route. After the party rescues him, he waits with the party's gertot during their vault descent and then insists on traveling with them to become a proper adventurer.

## Known Facts

- The party rescues Zabeek during the Mount Emberstran / Sparkwhisper arc.
- In Session 25, he is waiting on or near the gertot when the party leaves the mountain.
- Says he kept watch the entire time and that no one touched the beast.
- Wants to go with the party because it is time for him to be a proper adventurer.
- Says he can tinker and starts to pull out a whistle, but the party stops him from blowing it.
- Helps gather wood at camp.
- Asks about camp rules and dagger fighting.
- At the Warm Bear, Zymve catches him trying to snag something from her pouch.

## Strong Inferences

- Zabeek is brave and eager but inexperienced.
- His tinkering and whistle may connect him to Sparkwhisper gnome devices, including Hap's whistle thread.
- He may become a comic liability, a useful tinker, or both.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Zabeek will remain with the party long-term.
- Whether he knows anything important about Sparkwhisper, the crashed flying ship, or Mount Emberstran.
- Whether the spelling Zabeek supersedes the earlier index spelling "Zybeak."
