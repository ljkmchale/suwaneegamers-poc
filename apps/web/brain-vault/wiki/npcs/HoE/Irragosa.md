# Irragosa

## Scope

HoE-only NPC.

## Summary

Irragosa is a dragon from the dragonrider era who is connected to Ainslie's Ahn'Tsuel bloodline and to Mount Emberstran. She appears to Ky'tha in humanoid form near the mountain, privately asks her to watch Ainslie, and later reveals herself as the guardian of Moganna's vault.

## Appearance In Humanoid Form

Described as an elf with orange and white robes and long blond hair.

## Known Facts

- Named by Lady Rook in Session 13: a dragon that used to fly over Emberstran. Came from the same hatch as **Annagos**. Was the mount for a descendant of **Ahn'Tsuel**. Rook says Irragosa "will have answers" for Ainslie and will be at Mount Emberstran.
- Appears to Ky'tha in humanoid form on the trail near Mount Emberstran (Session 23). Ky'tha suspects the identity and Irragosa does not deny it.
- First question to Ky'tha: asks if there is a dark-haired, darker-skinned young person in the party — asking about Ainslie.
- Asks Ky'tha to keep her eye on Ainslie. Says it would be worth her while. Does not explain why.
- Says: "If we are not supposed to be somewhere, we won't be." — implying she has some influence over what the party finds or is allowed to access.
- After the encounter, wing beats are heard — she departs in dragon form.
- Ky'tha later reveals the encounter to the party. They note she was a little choked up when asking about "the dark-haired member."
- Ainslie connects the "T" marking near the volcano to Tsulanan — a dragon rider (from riding records). Irragosa's request to watch Ainslie fits the Tsulanan bloodline interpretation.
- The party discusses how fast a dragon flies after the encounter, suggesting she is nearby.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 13: Rook names Irragosa as a dragon from the same hatch as Annagos and connected to Ahn'Tsuel's line.
- Session 13: Rook says answers for Ainslie are at Mount Emberstran.
- Session 23: Irragosa appears to Ky'tha in humanoid form on the route near Mount Emberstran.
- Session 23: She asks Ky'tha about a dark-haired, darker-skinned party member, meaning Ainslie.
- Session 23: She asks Ky'tha to watch Ainslie and says it will be worth her while.
- Session 23: Wing beats are heard after she leaves, supporting that she departs as a dragon.

## Strong Inferences

- Irragosa recognizes Ainslie through Tsulanan/Ahn'Tsuel bloodline significance.
- Irragosa is emotionally invested in Ainslie's presence, based on party discussion that she sounded choked up.
- She is connected to the vault, but whether as guardian, witness, or interested party is not documented.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Irragosa helped open the vault.
- Whether she wants Ainslie protected from himself, Rook, the vault, or something else.
- Her current relationship with Annagos.

## Relationships

- Ainslie: She sees him as tied to Tsulanan's line and is emotionally invested in his presence.
- Ky'tha: Chose Ky'tha specifically for the private request; says she likes her.
- Annagos: From the same hatch — a sibling dragon.
- Tsulanan: Her former rider or the ancestor of the bloodline she is watching.
- Mount Emberstran / vault: Presumably connected; Rook said answers would be there.

## Open Questions

- What exactly does she want Ky'tha to protect Ainslie from?
- Did she help Ainslie open the vault door, or was she watching?
- What is her relationship with Annagos now?
- Where are Soulroot and Starvein?
- How will she try to right the wrong of destroying the flying ship?
- What does she know about White Flare's disappearance?

## Session 25 Vault Reveal

- Appears inside the Mount Emberstran vault and says she has been guarding Moganna's vault until the burden can be lifted.
- Recognizes Ky'tha as carrying the same smell/aura as Orah Nelcrath, "stone sister," and Zymve as carrying the glint of Vhurkir Inni, "shadow child."
- Says Tsulanan rode her, Orah rode Karalafene the White Flare, and Vhurkir rode Kaelix the Copper.
- Identifies the heart-shaped vault gem as one of three pieces; the others are Soulroot and Starvein.
- Says the three-piece relic could extend life and break Ahn'Tsuel's 50-year soul cycle if combined by one person.
- Directs the party to take exhausted Ainslie to Diverra's temple in Emberstran.
- Provides safe passage out of the mountain and past the giants.
- Admits guilt over bringing down the flying ship near Mount Emberstran.
