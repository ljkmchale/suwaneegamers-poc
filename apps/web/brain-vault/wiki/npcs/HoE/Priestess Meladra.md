# Priestess Meladra

## Scope

HoE-only NPC.

## Name Note

The raw player notes alternate between "Priestess Maladra" (early sessions) and "Priestess Meladra" / "High Priestess Meladra" (later sessions). These are the same person. The NPC Index entry "High Priestess Maladra — connected to Maladra's Grove" refers to her, not a separate figure. Her grove is the sacred grove taken by Blackrend that drove the Session 05–06 storyline.

## Summary

Priestess Meladra is Aury's home-temple authority at Emberstran's Temple of Diverra. She is caring, spiritually attentive, and clearly fond of Aury — but also politically caught between the temple's role, civic pressure, and Arnol's troubled situation. After Aury returns from the Lost Sanctuary with Bloom Aeterna, their relationship carries a new asymmetry: he may now hold deeper original doctrine than she does.

## Known Facts

- Runs the Temple of Diverra in Emberstran.
- **Before Session 04 (disappearance)**: Meladra went missing — acolytes reported her gone and priests were meeting about it. She had followed a wisp connected to Diverra's sacred grove.
- **Session 05 (wisp/grove)**: Confronted Ky'tha outside the Old Oak & Grapes while distressed and rambling. Had followed the wisp, who told her Diverra confirmed this is the ultimate sanctuary for followers — but Blackrend had captured the grove and the wisp with it. She gave Ky'tha landmarks to find the corrupt grove. She later returns to the temple without full explanation of her absence.
- **Session 12 (the "bigger than Diverra" scene)**: Aury and Hap wake up in the temple and are told the High Priestess wants to meet them. She looks like she has been up all night. She tells them: *"Something is shifting in Emberstran — the blight is fixed, people are changing personalities, lightning has shot up from the ground. These are not coincidences, they are signals. I keep praying but have heard nothing. What is your assessment?"* The conversation turns theological. Aury discusses power, corruption, humility, the Lord, the Cloak, and Etterset. She listens and then says: **"His calling is now far beyond that of Diverra — he is bigger than that."** Aury pushes back, insisting Diverra called him to keep the city safe. She replies: *"He is not lost. Diverra is continuing to guide him."* She asks to join them for breakfast. Ky'tha arrives and thanks Meladra for her discretion letting the party use the temple cellar; Meladra asks Ky about her background. After breakfast, Aury asks Meladra to watch Arnol; she agrees to have Koken follow him.
- **Session 13**: Asks Aury to take Arnol under his wing. He cannot — the party is departing for the vault. She senses his calling to the portal and releases him from the Arnol responsibility. She gives Hap the **Pruning Dagger of Diverra** (+1 dagger) and a vial of holy water before they leave.
- **Session 20 (return from Lost Sanctuary)**: Extremely happy to see Aury when he returns from the Lost Sanctuary. Grasps his hand and asks if he found what he was looking for. Captivated and distracted by what he has found. Aury does not fully disclose the Bloom Aeterna contents to her.
- **Session 25 (return from Mount Emberstran)**: Prepares the Temple of Diverra altar for exhausted Ainslie after Irragosa says Diverra's light is needed. The altar room is arranged with rose tapestries and candles. Aury reads from Bloom Aeterna while praying over Ainslie in Meladra's presence. Meladra also reports that Arnol was ambushed two nights earlier and is barely alive.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 04/05: Meladra is reported missing; she had followed a wisp connected to Diverra's sacred grove.
- Session 05: She tells Ky'tha that Blackrend has captured the grove and the wisp. Provides grove landmarks.
- Session 06: She gives Aury holy water and a Potion of Healing and provides grove-route guidance.
- Session 07: She can provide an Arcane Waypoint / Activate Sigil scroll and discusses Arnol's training.
- Session 10: She lets the party use the Temple of Diverra sewer entrance.
- Session 12: The "bigger than Diverra" scene. She tells Aury he has grown beyond the calling of Diverra. Aury rejects the frame; she affirms he is not lost. Agrees to have Koken watch Arnol.
- Session 13: Provides Hap the Pruning Dagger of Diverra and holy water. Releases Aury from Arnol responsibility; senses his calling to the portal.
- Session 20: Welcomes Aury back from the Lost Sanctuary. Aury withholds the full Bloom Aeterna revelation.

## Strong Inferences

- Meladra is caring and spiritually sincere; this is inferred from how she treats Aury, Arnol, and the party.
- She is overwhelmed by the scale of Aury's discoveries and by city responsibilities, which may limit how effectively she handles Arnol.
- Aury now has access to older/original Diverra doctrine that Meladra likely does not know.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Meladra's early disappearance has any hidden cost or consequence.
- Whether she will accept, fear, or resist the Bloom Aeterna's full doctrine.
- What she knows about the Shattered Hearts before Aury tells her.

## Relationships

- Aurelius / Aury: primary bond; she trusts and is emotionally invested in him. The "bigger than Diverra" scene reveals she perceives him as having outgrown the faith's framework — Aury disagrees. Post-Lost Sanctuary, the relationship has a second asymmetry: he carries Bloom Aeterna, which she does not know. He is choosing how much to tell her.
- Arnol: troubled ward; she watches him but cannot fully intervene without more information. Assigned Koken to follow him at Aury's request.
- Hap: gave him the Pruning Dagger of Diverra.
- Koken: temple figure trusted to follow/observe Arnol.
- Ky'tha: thanked for discretion; asked about Ky'tha's background at breakfast.
- Diverra's faith: temple authority and sincere follower, but may not know the original doctrine Aury now carries.

## Open Questions

- What does "bigger than Diverra" mean to her theologically — is she saying Aury is outpacing the faith, or that Diverra herself has something larger in mind for him?
- How will she react when the Bloom Aeterna's full content is disclosed?
- What caused her early disappearance and grove-following before Session 04?
- Does she know more about the Shattered Hearts or Lost Sanctuary than she has shared?
- What happens to Arnol under her watch after the party leaves?
- Who ambushed Arnol while the party was away?
