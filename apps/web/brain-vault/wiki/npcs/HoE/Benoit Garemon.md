# Benoit Garemon

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Hap's father. Deceased.

## Summary

Benoit Garemon was a tiefling and Hap's father. He was a servant of the Cetaen family who loaned them money to keep their vineyard. He was murdered — his death was recorded in the official records, but Hap witnessed people killing his father at a time after the reported date of death, meaning the records were falsified. His grave in Emberstran's cemetery contains Eldermer Caetean's body, not his. The official record of his death was changed by Jae Vynaar at the old Lord's instruction, under pressure from the Cloak of Defiance or Vintner's Guild. His ring — significant to Hap — was stolen by Cloak thugs.

## Known Facts

- Tiefling; Hap's father.
- Phira Garemon is his daughter.
- Was a servant of the Cetaen family; loaned them money to keep their vineyard when they were struggling.
- Murdered. Hap saw people kill him in the streets at a time after the official records state he died — meaning the death was pre-recorded or the date falsified.
- His death record was changed by Jae Vynaar on instruction from the old Lord (now deceased), under influence from either the Cloak of Defiance or the Vintner's Guild.
- The falsified records also changed the Cetaen land inheritance so the property reverted to the Cetaen family upon Benoit's "recorded" death (no heir listed, even though Hap and Phira exist).
- **His grave in Emberstran's cemetery is occupied by Eldermer Caetean's body**, not his. (Rono the groundskeeper confirmed this; he was paid by Ruthless Jerik to swap the burial.)
- **Phira's grave is empty** — she was never actually buried there despite having a gravestone.
- His ring was stolen by Cloak thugs; recovery is one of Hap's driving motivations.
- Lady Tarathial (Vintner's Guild) did not recognize the name Benoit when Ainslie mentioned him — suggesting she was not directly involved in the murder.

## Strong Inferences

- Benoit's murder and the record falsification were orchestrated to erase the Garemon claim to the Cetaen land.
- The Cloak of Defiance is the most likely orchestrator, given Ruthless Jerik paid Rono to manipulate the graves.
- His ring being stolen by Cloak thugs reinforces Cloak involvement in his murder.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Where Benoit's actual body is.
- Exactly who ordered the murder and why beyond the land dispute.
- Whether Benoit knew he was being set up before it happened.
- The full significance of his ring.

## Relationships

- Hap — son; Hap's primary motivation for investigating Emberstran politics.
- Phira Garemon — daughter.
- Cetaen family — employers; he loaned them money.
- Ruthless Jerik — likely connected to orchestrating his murder and the grave manipulation.
- Jae Vynaar — recorded his death (falsified); changed land records at the old Lord's instruction.
- Old Lord (deceased) — gave the order to falsify the records.

## Open Questions

- Where is Benoit's actual body?
- Who exactly ordered his murder?
- What is the full value and meaning of his stolen ring?
- Does recovering the ring advance Hap's ability to claim the Cetaen land?
