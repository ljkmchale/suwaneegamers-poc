# Captain Vammad

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Captain of the Guard in Emberstran.

## Summary

Vammad is the Captain of the Guard in Emberstran — a stone giant goliath who works under the Lord Protector. He is a long-serving civic authority. Lady Tarathial pointed the party toward him as a reliable resource. He is cooperative but grudging — he hired a guard to protect Emlee for 2gp at Aury's request, though he did it "begrudgingly." He was also unaware that any inspector had been assigned to the Kelona murder investigation, which was the first signal that Inspector Alese was not legitimate.

## Known Facts

- Title: Captain of the Guard, Emberstran.
- Species: Stone giant goliath.
- Works under Lord Protector Garrod Dusklight.
- Long-serving; Lady Tarathial described him as someone "who has been here a long time."
- **Session 03**: Lady Tarathial advised the party to seek Vammad to find the old record keeper (Jae Vynaar).
- **Session 03**: At the Old Oak & Grapes meeting where the fake Inspector appeared. Vammad arrived as Aury dismissed the Inspector. Confirmed he had no knowledge of an inspector being assigned to the case — key evidence that Inspector Alese was a fraud.
- **Session 03**: Aury asked him to hire a guard to protect Emlee (Kelona's sister). He agreed "begrudgingly" for 2gp and left to arrange it.
- He mentioned "a new lady — an uppity up" named Lady Bidva (likely a shortened form of Lady Bodiva Rook) as someone possibly connected to the recent strangeness.
- **Session 08**: When Jae was found murdered, Og went to find Vammad but could not locate him. A different captain (of the SW district) responded instead.

## Strong Inferences

- Vammad is a legitimate civic authority who cooperates with the party out of professional duty, not personal investment.
- His unawareness of Inspector Alese confirmed her as a plant or operative, not a legitimate guard appointment.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

## Relationships

- Lord Protector Garrod Dusklight — Vammad's authority.
- Party — cooperative, grudging; hired a guard on their behalf.
- Inspector Alese — unaware of her; her presence exposed her as illegitimate.
- Lady Tarathial — referred the party to him.
