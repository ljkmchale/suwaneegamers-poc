# Makra

## Scope

HoE-only NPC.

## Summary

Makra is a genling — a small creature descended from myconids, affected by the bloodwars — encountered in Thallgrove. He communicates exclusively via telepathy. Only Zymve could understand him initially (through Deep Speech); a potion in Bimblefol later allowed the rest of the party to hear genlings. He guided the party from Thallgrove through the underground tunnel system to Bimblefol, fought alongside them against the Thall Mounds, and introduced them to Bimblefol's elder, Aniba.

## Physical Description

Small and wiry. Unblinking eyes. Has a trunk, which he holds a finger to as a "be quiet" signal. Carries a staff he uses to collect fungi as he travels, and a blowgun in combat.

## Known Facts

- **First contact (Session 17/18)**: Spotted by Ky'tha moving ahead in the tunnels. Communicates telepathically — Og hears *"Wow — you are big,"* Zymve hears *"you understand, don't you?"* Only Zymve can speak back to him through Deep Speech. He introduces himself as Makra and says he will take the party to his village.
- **Guide behavior**: Leads the party through tunnels with shifting fungi colors. Signals silence by pressing a finger to his trunk and says to Zymve "the ground listens." Simultaneously collects fungi specimens by hitting them with his staff (Og and Hap hear "ouch" from the fungi) and pocketing them — practical forager throughout.
- **Healing moment**: Finds a rare violet pod, has it pulse it, and tells Zymve that Og should eat it. Og does and is cured of his spore condition. Makra says it is rare but he will look for more.
- **Thall Mound combat**: When the Thall Mounds attack and eat two of his companions, Makra hides with his staff and fights. Uses blowgun (crits on one creature, giving disadvantage) and mind sliver (psychic damage). After the fight, runs to Zymve and says "wow," then sees his two dead friends and says "Bimblefol," pointing — asking the party to carry the bodies back. Og carries them.
- **Arrival at Bimblefol**: Takes the party to a hut at the edge of the lake where Betuu (a female genling) gives the party a liquid that lets them hear all genlings. Other genlings at the watchtower badmouth Makra for bringing outsiders. Makra insists Og come along despite the bridge concerns. Takes the party to see elder Aniba.

## Relationships

- Zymve: primary bond; the only party member he could communicate with initially; she is the interpreter and trust anchor.
- Betuu: female genling at the Bimblefol hut; likely a partner or village companion.
- Aniba: elder of Bimblefol; Makra defers to her.
- Party: guide and protector on the Thallgrove-to-Bimblefol route; some social cost within his own village for bringing outsiders.
