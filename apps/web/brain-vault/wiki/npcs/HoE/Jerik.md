# Jerik

## Scope

HoE-only NPC / antagonist lead.

## Summary

Jerik is the Cloak of Defiance authority who sits above Selvara Nendris (Black Quill) in Emberstran and is directly tied to Hap's family tragedy. He is a threat name before he is a face — the party hears about him repeatedly but does not directly encounter him through Session 24.

## Known Facts

- Referenced as "Ruthless Jerik" by the Etterset; described as a person of influence in the town.
- Senior to the Black Quill / Selvara Nendris; Selvara says "once Jerik gives the word, I'll be in touch."
- A red-cloaked rider warns the party after they return to Emberstran: "Jerik now has our scent and will not forget."
- Tied to Hap's father Benoit Garemon's murder — Hap's family ring connects to the Jerik/Cloak thread through the grave-robbery and falsified inheritance records.
- Lady Rook's counter-leverage: Rook tells Ainslie she is holding "Jerik's brother" (Phira's husband) as protection for the EGD. If the Cloak moves against them, Jerik's brother will be killed.
- Ainslie and Og are specifically on Jerik's radar after the Cloak dinner and their subsequent attempt to remain neutral.
- The party in Session 20 establishes their alibi as "we've been in the vault" before re-entering town.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 08: Rono says Ruthless Jerik paid him for burial swaps connected to Benoit/Phira graves.
- Session 10: Lockjaw says Jerik ordered the hit on Lady Rook.
- Session 11: Selvara/Black Quill's later language establishes Jerik as the person whose word she waits for.
- Session 13: Rook tells Ainslie that Jerik's brother is held in the party's defense.
- Session 20/21: A red-cloaked rider warns that Jerik has the party's scent and will not forget.

## Strong Inferences

- Jerik is a senior Cloak authority in Emberstran.
- Jerik is deeply tied to the Garemon/Cetaen coverup, but the notes do not yet prove he personally killed Benoit.
- Rook's leverage over Jerik's brother is a deterrent against the Cloak moving openly on the party.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Jerik directly murdered Benoit.
- Whether Jerik has Hap's family ring.
- Whether Jerik's brother is Phira's husband by blood, marriage, or another family tie in the notes.

## Relationships

- Selvara Nendris (Black Quill): subordinate; acts on his word.
- Silverheart: likely another operative in the hierarchy.
- Hap: family legacy and inherited land thread; Jerik's involvement in [[Benoit Garemon]]'s death remains unconfirmed but strongly implied.
- [[Phira Garemon]] (Hap's sister): Rook holds her husband as a hostage specifically keyed to Jerik.
- Lady Rook: adversaries; Rook's hostage threat is her direct counter against him.
- Ainslie and Og: directly targeted after the failed neutrality attempt.

## Open Questions

- Did Jerik personally arrange or commit Benoit Garemon's murder?
- Where is Hap's family ring and does Jerik have it?
- What does Jerik want from the Garemon land specifically?
- Will the party ever face him directly?
