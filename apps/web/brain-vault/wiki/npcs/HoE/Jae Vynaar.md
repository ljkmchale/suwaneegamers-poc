# Jae Vynaar

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Former town record keeper of Emberstran. Deceased.

## Summary

Jae Vynaar was the elderly former record keeper of Emberstran — the man who maintained the town's official ledgers and land records before being replaced by Doit Torwell. The party visited him to ask about Benoit Garemon's death records and the Cetaen land situation. He had been paid (by the old Lord, under pressure from the Cloak of Defiance or Vintner's Guild) to falsify records showing Benoit's land reverted to the Cetaen family. He also received paid "tea" visits from Inspector Alese. He was murdered — stabbed in the back — before the party could follow up. His house contained hidden items including 15 platinum pieces and a pulsing crimson vial.

## Known Facts

- Elderly; cramped home. Hard of hearing.
- Formerly the town's official record keeper; replaced by Doit Torwell.
- **Session 05 (first visit)**: The party arrived and he prepared tea. He revealed:
  - Benoit Garemon had loaned money to the Cetaen family to keep their vineyard.
  - He had been instructed to record Benoit's death on a specific day and to change the land records so the property reverted to the Cetaens upon his death (no heir on record).
  - He was asked to make these changes by the old Lord (now dead), who was himself influenced by either the Cloak of Defiance or the Vintner's Guild.
  - A woman in a burgundy coat and feathered hat (Inspector Alese) visited him regularly on the 4th day — he described it as paid "tea" visits, implying she was paying him for information.
  - He didn't know much about the Cloak directly (though Ky sensed he was lying).
- **Session 08 (Jae is Dead)**: The party returned to find him stabbed in the back. Bloody footprints at the scene matched a roughly 3' stride (halfling or gnome). Books had been disturbed. A hidden trap door revealed 15pp and a small vial of crimson pulsing liquid (Ainslie detected transformation magic).
- Lady Tarathial mentioned having spoken with Jae — she had some knowledge of Benoit through him.

## Strong Inferences

- Jae was killed to prevent the party (or others) from learning more about the falsified records and Benoit Garemon's death.
- The 3' stride of the murderer points toward a halfling or gnome operative — possibly Cloak-affiliated.
- The pulsing crimson vial is likely significant; its purpose is unresolved.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Who ordered Jae's murder.
- What the crimson vial is and what it was doing hidden under his floor.
- What else Jae knew that he hadn't yet shared.
- Whether Inspector Alese's "tea" visits were payments for information about Emberstran records.

## Relationships

- Benoit Garemon — recorded his death; knew of his financial dealings with the Cetaens.
- Old Lord (deceased) — instructed him to falsify the land records.
- Inspector Alese — regular paid visitor; she had leverage or was buying information.
- Lady Tarathial — had spoken with him; aware of Benoit through him.
- Party — visited twice; cooperative on first visit; found murdered on second.

## Open Questions

- Who killed Jae and on whose authority?
- Was Ruthless Jerik (who paid Rono to manipulate grave records) also involved in Jae's murder?
- What was in the crimson vial?
