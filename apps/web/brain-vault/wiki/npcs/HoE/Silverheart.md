# Silverheart

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Do not apply this page to SoD or The Silent Vanguard.

## Summary

Silverheart is a Cloak of Defiance contact who approaches Ainslie and Og at the Warm Bear Inn in Session 13. She is described as a dark-skinned, blue-eyed half-elf wearing a red cloak.

## Known Facts

- Appearance: Dark-skinned half-elf, blue eyes, red cloak.
- Name used in notes: Silverheart.
- Affiliated with the Cloak of Defiance; appears to operate at a higher level than a standard messenger.
- Bumps into Ainslie and Og intentionally at the Warm Bear Inn in Session 13.
- Was sent to check on the EGD's progress and ask for their support against Lady Rook at the upcoming Council meeting.
- Asked the EGD to help solve the mystery of who killed the Cloak members in the sewers.
- Declined to take back the coin offered by Ainslie.
- Says she is in Emberstran for "bigger things" beyond just reporting to Jerik or the Black Quill.
- Implied she will not be reporting to the Quill directly.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 13: Silverheart bumps into Ainslie and Og at the Warm Bear Inn.
- Session 13: She is described as a dark-skinned, blue-eyed half-elf in a red cloak.
- Session 13: She asks the EGD to support against Rook and to help solve the murdered Cloak members in the sewers.
- Session 13: She refuses to take back money/coin Ainslie tries to return.
- Session 13: She says she has bigger tasks in Emberstran.

## Strong Inferences

- Silverheart is a Cloak operative above street level, but her exact rank is not documented.
- She operates with some independence from Selvara/Black Quill.
- Her "bigger tasks" may relate to Rook, the EGD, or Emberstran power structure, but the source does not specify.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Her rank.
- Whether she reports to Jerik directly.
- What her bigger tasks are.

## Relationships

- Cloak of Defiance: operative, higher-rank function.
- Ainslie and Og: EGD contacts she was sent to check on.
- Selvara Nendris (Black Quill): organizational superior or peer; Silverheart appears independent.
- Jerik: referenced as authority above Silverheart.

## Open Questions

- How high is her rank within the Cloak?
- Will she interfere with the party's Mount Emberstran plans?
