# Phira Garemon

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Hap's sister.

## Summary

Phira Garemon is Hap's sister and daughter of the murdered Benoit Garemon. She is alive, based in Trailpoint, and under the protection of Lady Rook. She is married to Jerik's brother. Ky'tha made contact with her in person and retrieved a ring and apron for Hap. Phira has proven to be politically savvy and dangerous — she warned the party that Jerik is bad, vouched for Rook, and made clear that if anything happens to Hap, Aury will answer for it personally.

## Known Facts

- Relationship correction: Phira is Hap's sister, not Hap's mother.
- Daughter of Benoit Garemon (tiefling, murdered).
- Hap's sister; connected to him through Fleur (Hap's owl), initially via a written note asking if she was Phira and if she wished to meet her brother.
- Not Hap's mother.
- Lives in Trailpoint.
- Married to Jerik's brother; Lady Rook is protecting her husband because of his troubled past.
- Ky'tha met her in person and delivered a ring and an apron to Hap on her behalf.
- Communicated (via Fleur/Ainslie's Sending) that Rook is good: *"Rook is good and is protecting her husband because of his troubled past."*
- Warned via earlier communication that Jerik is BAD — the party interpreted this as a reason for Hap to leave Emberstran.
- Threatened Aury directly: *"Aury's name will be carved in blood on the very blade that takes him"* should anything happen to Hap (relayed by Ainslie; Session 23 or 24 camp scene).
- Described by Ainslie as dangerous — "she may be as much or more of a badass than her husband."
- Has knowledge of Emberstran politics and the Rook/Cloak situation.

## Party Relationship

- **Hap** — her brother; deeply protective; her threat toward Aury is framed as protective loyalty to Hap.
- **Ky'tha** — made direct in-person contact; trusted intermediary.
- **Ainslie** — relayed her messages via Sending; reported her threat; personally noted her dangerous quality.
- **Aury** — the subject of her threat; her opinion of him is unknown beyond the warning.

## Strong Inferences

- Phira is well-connected and likely has her own information network — she knew about the party's activities in Emberstran before they contacted her.
- Her protection under Rook and her husband's "troubled past" may mean the Garemon family has deeper entanglements with the factions of Emberstran than Hap knows.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- The identity of her husband (Jerik's brother) and the nature of his troubled past.
- Whether she has met or plans to meet Hap directly.
- What Phira knows about Benoit's murder and who is responsible.
- Whether her relationship with Rook is trust or necessity.

## Relationships

- Benoit Garemon — father (deceased; murdered).
- Hap — brother; the primary bond driving her protectiveness.
- Jerik's brother — husband (unnamed); troubled past; protected by Rook.
- Lady Rook — protector; Phira trusts her.
- Ky'tha — in-person contact; carried items and messages between Phira and Hap.
- Ainslie — relayed her communications.
