# Shenog the Fisher

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Underdark ferryman operating on Rothenloch, the underground lake beneath Emberstran. Also spelled "Shanog" in player notes.

## Summary

Shenog is a half-orc fisher and boat operator who has lived on and around the underground lake Rothenloch for a long time. The party encountered her on the ledge path near what they suspected was a passage into the underdark. She ferried them to Nunglthil for 6gp, shared smoked eel on the journey, and warned them about the Shade of Rothenloch — a predatory entity that takes people's minds.

## Appearance

- Half-orc. Big woman.
- Red braided hair.
- Gray skin; yellow glowing eyes.
- Leather jerkin.
- Operates a lantern-lit pole boat on the underground lake.

## Known Facts

- **Session 14**: Party encounters her on a ledge path near the underground lake. She warns them the bank drops off fast.
- Introduced herself: "Shenog — been fishing this lake for a long time."
- When asked about ruins or buildings, she knows of some in Nunglthil.
- Lives near or on the lake; trades in Nunglthil.
- Agreed to ferry the party to Nunglthil for 6gp (Ky'tha negotiated the price in orcish, including some intimidation).
- Shared smoked eel with the party during the ~1-2 hour paddle.
- Confirmed the **Cloak of Defiance** is present in Nunglthil.
- Told the party about **the Shade of Rothenloch** — it takes people's minds and they never return the same, if at all.
- Described Rothenloch: mostly quaggoths in the area; said to keep eyes on purses in Nunglthil.
- **Session 15**: Referred to as "Shanog" as she brought the party into Nunglthil's underground harbor. Directed them to start at the Darkbottom Draught.

## Strong Inferences

- She is a long-term underdark resident, not a surface traveler. She lives down here by choice and is well-adapted to the environment.
- Her knowledge of Cloak activity in Nunglthil suggests she is observant and well-connected locally, though her allegiances are unclear.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

## Relationships

- Nunglthil — trades there; familiar with its layout and factions.
