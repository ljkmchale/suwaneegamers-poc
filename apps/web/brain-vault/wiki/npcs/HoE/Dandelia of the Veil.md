# Dandelia of the Veil

## Scope

HoE-only NPC / lead.

## Summary

Dandelia of the Veil is a mysterious figure tied to the Life Tree and Alferumn network. The Life Tree at the Lost Sanctuary directly named her to Og after he bonded as E'erspan: "You feel the pain. Seek Dandelia of the Veil." An'Davo (Ehkel) previously encountered her in Kahlbit's Veil while chasing a stag and described her as a green-eyed woman who told him "the forest keeps its own keys." Petal (Lady Rook's tracker) also knew Dandelia — through unknown means.

## Known Facts

- **Named by the Life Tree** directly to Og during the E'erspan bonding moment in Session 19: "you feel the pain. Seek Dandelia of the Veil."
- **An'Davo's encounter**: Ehkel an'Davo encountered her in a forest called Kahlbit's Veil (described as having a name that "always changed"). She was a green-eyed woman. She told him: "the forest keeps its own keys." He thought of her when Og described his thornleaf origin and suggested she might help Og understand the leaf.
- **Petal knew Dandelia**: Petal, Lady Rook's tracker, had a connection to Dandelia — the nature and method of this connection was never extracted before Zymve killed Petal.
- Og's post-E'erspan goals explicitly include finding Dandelia of the Veil.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 09: Ehkel an'Davo names Dandelia of the Veil in Kahlbit's Veil and says she may help Og's thorn-leaf situation.
- Session 19: The Life Tree directs Og to seek Dandelia of the Veil after he becomes E'erspan.
- Session 22: Petal knows Dandelia, but the details are not extracted before Petal's death.

## Strong Inferences

- Dandelia is tied to Life Tree / Alferumn knowledge.
- Dandelia is likely important to Og's next personal quest step.
- The phrase "the forest keeps its own keys" suggests she guards access, knowledge, or permission, but the mechanism is not documented.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Dandelia is a person, title, spirit, or faction role.
- Whether Kahlbit's Veil is a fixed place.
- Whether Petal knew Dandelia through Rook's network or independently.

## What She May Be

- A druidic figure or hidden grove guardian connected to the Alferumn.
- A keeper of Life Tree knowledge — the phrase "the forest keeps its own keys" suggests she has access to information others cannot reach.
- Her title "of the Veil" suggests either a specific grove/veil location, or a spiritual status within the Alferumn network.

## Relationships

- **Og** — the Life Tree sent him toward her; she is his E'erspan next step.
- **An'Davo (Ehkel)** — encountered her previously; she spoke to him; he connected her to the thornleaf thread.
- **Petal** — knew Dandelia; what Petal knew about her died at the trail fork.
- **Blackrend / Alferumn** — likely tied to the blight opposition and Life Tree guardianship.

## Open Questions

- Where is Kahlbit's Veil — and is it the same location each time?
- Is "Dandelia of the Veil" a name, a title, or both?
- What did Petal know about her — and was it from Lady Rook's intelligence network or something personal?
- What does Og need to bring or say to find her?
- Does she know about the Blackrend's current status after Bloomrest?
