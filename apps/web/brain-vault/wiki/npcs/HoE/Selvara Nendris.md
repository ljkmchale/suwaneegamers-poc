# Selvara Nendris / The Black Quill

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Do not apply this page to SoD or The Silent Vanguard.

## Summary

Selvara Nendris, known as the Black Quill, is the leading face of the Cloak of Defiance's civic and political operation in Emberstran. She recruits Og and Ainslie at the Crimson Swan in Session 12 and frames the anti-Rook agenda as partnership rather than servitude.

## Appearance

Not fully described in source. Operates from a private dining room at the Crimson Swan, Emberstran's most upscale establishment.

## Known Facts

- Name: Selvara Nendris. Known as "the Black Quill."
- Operates out of or is associated with the Crimson Swan.
- Connected to Torrick, who can "speak on the party's behalf."
- Works under Jerik in the Cloak hierarchy; says "once Jerik gives the word, I'll be in touch."
- Officially recognized the party's EGD status — the EGD document was signed by the Black Quill, not by Lord Dusklight.
- Her toast at the Crimson Swan dinner: **"Shall the Rook topple."** — an explicit ask whether Og and Ainslie are in or out against Rook.
- Frames the anti-Rook partnership as peer-to-peer: "not as pawns, but as peers."
- Promises the next chest won't be "so light."
- Her stated goal: increase efficiencies and establish a real partnership to clear objectives set by her authorities.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 11: The official EGD document and 500gp box arrive through Emlee and Haggis; the document is signed by Black Quill.
- Session 12: At the Crimson Swan, she introduces herself as Selvara Nendris and says everyone calls her the Black Quill.
- Session 12: She is with Torrick's network and says Torrick can speak on the party's behalf.
- Session 12: She asks Ainslie and Og to join the Cloak side "not as pawns, but as peers."
- Session 12: She toasts "Shall the Rook topple" and says once Jerik gives the word, she will be in touch.

## Strong Inferences

- Selvara is the Cloak's civic-legitimacy face, not just a field operative. This is inferred from the EGD paperwork, Crimson Swan setting, and recruitment language.
- She likely outranks Torrick operationally, but her exact rank below Jerik is not directly stated.
- **Selvara is not the same person as the Whisper.** The Whisper (Inspector Alese) was a separate Cloak field operative killed by Hap in Session 11 sewers — a halfling plague-doctor who conducted physical surveillance. Selvara is a distinct individual operating at the political and recruitment level; the two share Cloak affiliation but are separate NPCs with separate roles.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Her specific instructions from Jerik.
- Whether she knows Rook's counter-leverage involving Jerik's brother.
- Whether she can legally bind the EGD or only fraudulently empower it.

## Relationships

- Og and Ainslie: Recruited as EGD/anti-Rook partners. Ainslie accepts, then later tries to reframe as neutrality.
- Jerik: Superior; gives the word before Selvara acts.
- Torrick: Cloak contact who vouches for the party.
- Lady Rook: Primary antagonist target.
- Silverheart: Silverheart is a separate Cloak operative who approaches Og and Ainslie independently; not clear whether she reports to Selvara.
- [[Inspector Alese]] (the Whisper): a separate Cloak field operative, not Selvara. Killed in Session 11 before Selvara recruits the party in Session 12.

## Open Questions

- What specific objectives does Selvara have authority to direct?
- How does she respond when Ainslie tries to reframe the EGD as "apolitical"?
- Does she know Rook is holding Phira's husband/Jerik's brother as leverage?
