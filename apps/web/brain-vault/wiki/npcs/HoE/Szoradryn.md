# Szoradryn

## Scope

HoE-only NPC / lead.

## Summary

Szoradryn runs a shop called Silken Secrets in Nunglthil — a fairly ornate establishment with a metal door, shiny steel features, and a purple/violet color scheme inside. He is a drow-associated or drow-adjacent figure (Zymve's reaction implies this). His most significant action: providing or routing the Journal of Tsulanan to Ainslie. Why he would do this is unexplained, making him potentially the most opaque figure the party encountered in the underdark.

## Appearance And Setting

- Silken Secrets: ornate exterior; metal door with shiny steel features; purple/violet color scheme inside.
- Located in Nunglthil's market district.
- Ky'tha asked Scalamank (Coppereyes) where to find Szoradryn — he confirmed Szoradryn runs Silken Secrets.

## Known Facts

- Provides or is connected to Ainslie's Journal of Tsulanan — the journal that is now reorganizing its chapters and speaking to Ainslie about a "catastrophe that came from hunger."
- Drow-associated world context alarms Zymve; her fear of him is specific, not generic.
- The full nature of the Session 16 encounter with Szoradryn in Silken Secrets is not fully captured in notes. What was exchanged is uncertain beyond the journal.
- The journal later began playing music at the Lost Sanctuary that only Zymve could hear — words forming about the catastrophe, Ains seeming to be in pain while reading.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 16: Szoradryn is associated with Silken Secrets in Nunglthil.
- Session 16: The Journal of Tsulanan reaches Ainslie through the Szoradryn/Silken Secrets thread.
- Session 19: The journal produces music at the Lost Sanctuary that only Zymve hears.
- Later sessions: Ainslie connects the journal to Tsulanan and his dragonrider-era bloodline.

## Strong Inferences

- Szoradryn is a major hidden-hand figure because the journal becomes central to Ainslie and Zymve.
- Zymve's fear suggests Szoradryn's drow context may connect to her past, but that is not confirmed.
- Szoradryn may know more about Tsulanan/Irragosa/the vault than he revealed.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Szoradryn serves Irragosa, another drow faction, himself, or the journal's own agenda.
- Whether he knows Zymve's former cult.
- What exact exchange occurred in Silken Secrets beyond the journal handoff/routing.

## The Journal Of Tsulanan

- The journal connects Ainslie's Ahn'Tsuel bloodline to Tsulanan (a dragon rider from an older era), and this to Irragosa the dragon.
- Irragosa asked Ky'tha to watch Ainslie; she was choked up when asking about "the dark-haired member of the party."
- Whether Szoradryn knows Irragosa, or routed the journal on her behalf or someone else's, is unknown.

## Relationships

- **Ainslie** — bloodline/journal connection; the journal was given to or directed toward Ainslie.
- **Zymve** — she reacts with specific fear to the drow-adjacent context; the journal's music at the Lost Sanctuary spoke to her specifically.
- **Tsulanan / Irragosa** — the ancient thread the journal traces.

## Open Questions

- Why did Szoradryn provide or route the Journal of Tsulanan to Ainslie?
- Is he working on Irragosa's behalf, someone else's, or his own agenda?
- Is he connected to Zymve's former drow cult?
- What does he know about the vault, Tsulanan, or the Ahn'Tsuel bloodline?
- Did he know what the journal would do to Ainslie?
