# Petal

## Scope

HoE-only NPC.

## Summary

Petal is Lady Rook's tracker/spy who follows the party toward Mount Emberstran. Ainslie reads her thoughts and intimidates her, Ky'tha tries to manage rather than escalate, and Zymve kills her at the trail fork — a decision that becomes a significant moral fault line for the party.

## Known Facts

- Tracked the party on Lady Rook's behalf, following them into the Baltwood.
- Aury spots her during first watch at a campsite — she is sitting next to a tree watching the group. He makes eye contact; she pulls off her hood and gives him a look.
- Ky'tha learns Petal worked for Lady Rook. The group discusses why Rook would want the party to succeed.
- Ainslie reads Petal's thoughts and intimidates her; she is spared the first time.
- Killed by Zymve at a trail fork.
- After the death: Og asks his thorn-leaf if Petal was important; no response. Og thinks it was the wrong thing to do but is glad it was not life-altering. Aury says he agrees but the notes suggest he may not fully.
- Party discusses whether Petal had family and what that means.
- Thornleaf gains a black thorn following Petal's death — a possible spiritual consequence of killing Rook's agent.
- Ainslie helped cover or disguise her body afterward.
- Connected to Dandelia of the Veil; how and why is unclear.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 22: Petal is discovered following the party and is connected to Lady Rook.
- Session 22: Ainslie reads Petal's thoughts and sees Rook instructing her to follow the party, help if possible, and return the Marrowstone/tablet if needed.
- Session 22: Zymve intimidates Petal and tells her to leave.
- Session 23: Petal is found following the party again.
- Session 23: Zymve kills Petal after the second confrontation.
- Session 23/24 aftermath: The party discusses whether the killing was wrong, and Og's Thornleaf gains a black thorn afterward.

## Strong Inferences

- Petal was more frightened operative than main villain, but she was still knowingly tracking the party for Rook.
- Her death is a moral fracture point because the party had already warned her and then caught her again.
- The black thorn likely marks a spiritual consequence, but the notes do not define it.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Petal had family or deeper obligations to Rook.
- What she knew about Dandelia.
- Whether Rook knows or will learn the truth of Petal's death.

## Relationships

- Lady Rook: employer; Rook does not know Petal is dead as of Session 24.
- Zymve: killed by her.
- Og: Thornleaf's black-thorn reaction may tie her death to the Life Tree bond.
- Ky'tha: tried to manage rather than kill; was not present or could not prevent the killing.
- Ainslie: read thoughts, intimidated, and helped cover the body.
- Aury: morally troubled; the post-death campfire conversation implies he is not at peace with it.
- Dandelia of the Veil: unexplained connection; Petal knew Dandelia.

## Open Questions

- Did Petal have information about Dandelia that the party did not extract?
- How does the black thorn on Thornleaf relate to her death?
- When will Lady Rook learn that Petal is dead?
- Was Petal willing or coerced in her service to Rook?
