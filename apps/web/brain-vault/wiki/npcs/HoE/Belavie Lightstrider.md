# Belavie Lightstrider

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Do not apply this page to SoD or The Silent Vanguard.

## Summary

Belavie Lightstrider is a pale elf Etterset senior member in Emberstran who formally elevates Ky'tha to rank-two status. She is quiet, precise, and watching — not a street contact like Tessa, but a decision-maker inside the organization.

## Appearance

- Pale elf with silvery eyes.
- First met in a private room at the Dyed Cane alongside Bordi and Tessa.

## Known Facts

- An established Etterset member who lives in Emberstran.
- Tessa brings Ky'tha to meet her in Session 12 as a reward for not becoming Rook's piece.
- Her words: *"Your friend Tessa has told us so much…and you have played Lady Rook's games without becoming her piece. This is what the Etterset values."*
- Gives Ky'tha a rank-two Etterset coin on first meeting.
- Says she has been too preoccupied to know much about the cult group Ky'tha asks about.
- Gives Ky'tha a hug when they part.
- In Session 13, Tessa suggests that if the whole party needs to go before Belavie, that can be arranged.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 12: Tessa brings Ky'tha to meet Belavie in a private Dyed Cane room with Bordi present.
- Session 12: Belavie identifies herself as Belavie Lightstrider.
- Session 12: Belavie praises Ky'tha for playing Lady Rook's games without becoming her piece.
- Session 12: Belavie gives Ky'tha a rank-two Etterset coin.
- Session 12: Belavie says she has been too preoccupied to know much about the cult group Ky'tha asks about.
- Session 13: Tessa says arranging for the party to go before Belavie is possible.

## Strong Inferences

- Belavie outranks Tessa or at least represents a more formal Etterset authority.
- Belavie values independence from both Rook and the Cloak.
- Belavie is a gatekeeper for deeper Etterset trust.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Her exact rank and title inside the Etterset.
- Whether she knows about the vault/tablet thread.
- How she would respond to Ainslie and Og's Cloak dinner.

## Relationships

- Tessa: Tessa acts as the introducer and street-level face; Belavie is the institutional authority above her.
- Ky'tha: Mentored/elevated; views Ky'tha as a trustworthy independent operator.
- Bordi (Dyed Cane): Bordi is present at the first meeting and says he has seen the Cloak of Defiance twist people; he trusts Belavie's judgment.

## Open Questions

- What is Belavie's rank within the Etterset relative to Tessa and other members?
- How does she respond to the party's Cloak dinner / Og and Ainslie situation?
- Is she aware of the vault thread and does the Etterset want a stake in it?
