# Lady Tarathial

## Scope

HoE-only NPC.

## Full Name

Tarathial Rulendor. Referred to in play as "Lady Tarathial" or "Lady T."

## Summary

Lady Tarathial is the Vintner's Guild authority in Emberstran. She hired the party in Sessions 02–03 to investigate the murder at Old Oak & Grapes and to clear the blighted cellar beneath the Guild. She is politically cautious, loyal to the Guild's interests, and openly opposed to Lady Rook. After Rook's election, Rook allowed her to remain at the Vintner's Mansion rather than displacing her — an uneasy accommodation. Ainslie's Detect Thoughts read her as not good or bad, but "true to her purpose."

## Physical Description

Older woman in gray robes, unbrushed hair. First appears at Kelona's funeral service.

## Known Facts

- **Sessions 02–03 (employer)**: Runs the Vintner's Guild. She arrives at Kelona's funeral, confirms Emlee was Kelona's closest friend, and engages the party to investigate the death and clear the cellar. She offers 50gp and a barrel of wine.
- **Session 03 (Detect Thoughts)**: Ainslie casts Detect Thoughts as she enters. When Ainslie mentions the Cataen family being forced off their land, she thinks: *"No — they are not against me — they are helping me."* She does not know who is buying the Cataen land. Ainslie concludes she is not good or bad but "true to her purpose." She detects Abjuration and Evocation magic on herself or nearby as they leave.
- **Session 03 (Phira)**: She vaguely recalls Benoit's heir Phira having passed away around 1241 (five years prior) and buried in Crael cemetery. Aury senses she is unsure of the story but being truthful.
- **Session 05–06 (Rook rivalry)**: Lady Tarathial is opposed to Rook. She describes Rook as "nefarious." She tells the party she is building a case to keep the Cataen lands out of the hands of whoever is trying to buy them. She thinks the Cataen patriarch may have had Benoit killed. She later knows the party visited Diverra's grove and asks Aury for details after the blight lifts.
- **Party split**: Aury saw nothing wrong with Lady Tarathial but thought Rook had ambitions above her station — he preferred to support neither. Ainslie decided to back Rook as "the stronger horse." Ky'tha and Og supported Rook for now. The party gave Lady Tarathial a forged copy of the land records she wanted.
- **Post-election**: Lady Rook, now Emberstran's civic leader, allowed Lady Tarathial to remain at the Vintner's Mansion. Rook expected she wouldn't be vengeful. Lady Tarathial still holds some position and standing in Emberstran.
- **Cloak context**: Rook told the party that Lady Tarathial "was ok" in Rook's assessment — she knows where the power lies and accommodates it.

## Strong Inferences

- She was a legitimate employer and probably a decent authority figure, not corrupt in the way the Cloak was.
- Her Detect Thoughts read suggests she had her own agenda around the Cataen lands, though that agenda may have been defensive rather than predatory.
- She may become a political resource or complication post-vault, depending on what the party's relationship with Rook looks like.

## Relationships

- Party: hired them, gave them legitimate cover, was politely manipulated by Ainslie and Ky'tha.
- Lady Rook: rival; Rook took civic leadership from her sphere; accommodates her now but the truce is uneasy.
- Emlee Tryndel: trusted Guild associate who mourned Kelona.
- Haggis: Guild guard dog handler; she whispered instructions to him during the party's visit.
- Kelona: murdered guildmember she wanted investigated.

## Open Questions

- Is she still building her case around the Cataen/Garemon land?
- What happens if Hap asserts his land claim — does Lady Tarathial see that as aligned or threatening to her own position?
- Does she know more about who ordered the land purchases than she admitted?
