# Lady Rook

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Do not apply this page to SoD or The Silent Vanguard.

## Summary

Lady Rook (real name: Bivra Rook, also called "The Hooded Lady") is a powerful, useful, and suspicious figure tied to Hap's inherited land, Phira, the Marrowstone, Petal, and the vault. The party repeatedly treats her as a possible ally with a dangerous agenda.

## Known Facts

- Real name: Bivra Rook.
- Also known as The Hooded Lady.
- Operates out of / associated with The Crimson Swan.
- Has a connection to "The Lady in the Tower" (Emmanora).
- Wants something connected to the vault or Marrowstone.
- Sends Petal to track/watch the party.
- Is connected to Hap's land and Phira pressure.
- Holds Jerik's brother/Phira's husband as a hostage.
- Helped underdark people (Bafast, gnomes) make lives outside the Underdark in Emberstran.
- Arrived in Emberstran around the same time as the Silver Spaulder smith; they "go way back."
- Is distrusted by Aury and Zymve and treated cautiously by Ky'tha and Ainslie.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 05: Jae says Lady Rook is probably taking over the Cetaen family land and is a visitor to Imonorra's tower.
- Session 05: A cart messenger delivers Lady Rook's invitation to the tower.
- Session 06: Rook asks the party to help replace Lady Tarathial as Vintner's Guild steward and offers to make things right around Hap's father's farm.
- Session 06: Rook provides the Marrowstone/egg artifact and Adamont ritual scroll for the grove ritual, and she knows Og has the thorn-leaf key.
- Session 08: Rook gives Ky'tha a Potion of Invisibility and knows Ky'tha's real name.
- Session 09: Rook uses Hap and Phira's rightful ownership as political leverage for support.
- Session 13: Rook tells Ainslie that Jerik's brother is held in the party's defense.
- Sessions 21-22: Petal is identified as working for Rook and tracking the party toward the vault.
- Session 25: Rook waits at the Temple of Diverra after the party returns from Mount Emberstran. She admits she had the party followed, says the tablet was tied to ancient bloodlines, and offers a silver dragon scale from the Peak of Tibul as help. Ky'tha questions her timing; Rook takes back the scale and leaves offended.

## Strong Inferences

- Rook is anti-Cloak in practice, but not necessarily morally clean; the notes show her working against Cloak interests while using leverage and surveillance herself.
- Rook's interest in the vault appears to be the center of her Emberstran operation, but the exact thing she wants inside is not documented.
- Her underdark supply relationships may be exploitative, protective, or both. The Bafast/Silver Spaulder material chain supports suspicion, but not a single clean conclusion.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether "holding" Phira's husband means hostage, protective custody, or both.
- What Rook intended Petal to do if the party failed or refused to hand over the tablet/Marrowstone.
- Whether Rook is serving someone else or only herself.

## Relationships

- Petal - agent/tracker/spy.
- Hap - leverage through land, Phira, and inheritance.
- Ky'tha - negotiation and suspicion.
- Ainslie - sees her as interested in vault contents.
- Aury - moral suspicion around her goals.

## Open Questions

- What exactly does Lady Rook want from the vault?
- Is she opposing the Cloak of Defiance, using the party, or both?
- What was Petal authorized to do?
- Did she know about Soulroot, Starvein, or only the ancient bloodline requirement?
