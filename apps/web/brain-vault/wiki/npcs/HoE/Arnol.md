# Arnol

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Do not apply this page to SoD or The Silent Vanguard.

## Summary

Arnol is a young acolyte at Emberstran's Temple of Diverra. He starts as Aury's familiar face in the temple but becomes a recurring problem when Ainslie recruits him as a double-agent inside the Eradic of Ghone cult. By the time the party leaves Emberstran, Arnol is troubled, guilt-ridden, and under Meladra's watch. When the party returns from Mount Emberstran in Session 25, Meladra reports that Arnol was ambushed two nights earlier and is barely alive.

## Appearance And Demeanor

- Young enough that Ky'tha can describe his absence as "puberty and learning" without being challenged.
- Described by Meladra as "not himself" after the Eradic period.
- Willing to help but bad at keeping secrets; cracks under gentle pressure from Aury.

## Key Events

- **Early sessions**: Arnol is a background acolyte at the temple. He and Coken whisper about Aury fighting monsters. He is not part of the main plot.
- **Session 05 (Beneath the City)**: Aury asks Arnol to clean Phira's room; Arnol confirms it looked like she left, not that she was taken.
- **The Eradic recruitment**: Ainslie spots Arnol in the dock/merchant area and recruits him as an inside informant for the Eradic of Ghone. Gives him money for a dark cloak. Tells him to join and learn everything he can.
- **Session 12**: Arnol acts as a food courier inside the Eradic tunnels, ferrying a gertot leg to a cultist. He encounters Og and Ainslie undercover and initially does not recognize them. When he does, he calls them out and asks if they outrank him.
- **Session 13**: Arnol brings Hap a black obsidian "G" medallion on a copper chain, saying it came from the cult's stash and may have been taken from a grave. When pressed by Aury, he admits he has been with bad people but insists he did not kill anyone or rob graves himself. Aury gets him to agree to speak to Meladra and consider leaving the cult. Aury magically cleans him up.
- **Later sessions**: Meladra asks Aury to take Arnol under his wing. Aury cannot (he is leaving for the vault), so he asks her to keep watching him. Ainslie sends Arnol a Sending pretending to be a lich: "he mentions looking into an artifact known as the marrowstone and keeping the information to himself." Aury later sees Arnol edging toward Ainslie and speaks to him to redirect him.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 05: Arnol is at the Temple of Diverra and admires Aury/Hap as heroic figures.
- Session 07: Aury discusses getting Arnol trained by Captain Vammad.
- Session 09: Ainslie sees Arnol near the docks and encourages him into the Sijulous Ghone / Eradic cult as a "secret mission," giving money for a dark cloak.
- Session 12: Ainslie and Og see Arnol inside the Eradic hideout while they are undercover.
- Session 13: Arnol brings Hap the black obsidian "G" medallion and admits he was around bad people, while denying killing or grave-robbing himself.
- Session 20: Meladra says Arnol has not been himself, and Aury notices him edging toward Ainslie.

## Strong Inferences

- Arnol is impressionable and approval-seeking, especially around Aury and Hap.
- Ainslie's recruitment is a major reason Arnol becomes entangled with the Eradic, but the notes do not make Arnol malicious.
- Arnol is a liability because he carries cult knowledge, guilt, and party secrets.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Arnol fully leaves the Eradic after Session 13.
- Whether Ainslie's lich-like Sending changes Arnol's future choices.
- Whether Arnol can become a stable ally under Meladra and Aury's guidance.

## Relationships

- Aury: Spiritual anchor. Aury is gentle but firm with Arnol; Arnol respects and fears disappointing him.
- Ainslie: Recruiter and manipulator. Ainslie uses Arnol as an inside source, gives him money and a dark cloak, and later sends ominous Sending spells pretending to be a supernatural figure.
- Meladra: Direct supervisor and concerned handler. She wants to help Arnol but is distracted by larger events.
- Eradic of Ghone: Former unwilling entanglement. Arnol worked as a food courier and low-level helper; did not participate in kills or grave-digging.
- Coken: Fellow acolyte; they whisper about Aury's fights together.

## Open Questions

- Did Arnol fully break from the Eradic after the party left?
- What does he do with the information Ainslie fed him about the Marrowstone?
- Is he a liability, a future ally, or just a troubled kid who gets left behind?
- Who ambushed him two nights before the party returned from Mount Emberstran?
