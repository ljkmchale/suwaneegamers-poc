# Bafast Umberfist

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Do not apply this page to SoD or The Silent Vanguard.

## Summary

Bafast Umberfist (also spelled Befast in some notes) is a dwarf gem proprietor at Gems of Emberstran. He is connected to Ky'tha's missing locket half, being exploited by both Lady Rook and the Silver Spaulder smith, and quietly wanting the city to be what it should be.

## Appearance

- Dwarf, first seen going through a big box of coins.
- Has a short, gray gnome assistant named Emiffi with violet eyes.

## Known Facts

- Proprietor of Gems of Emberstran.
- Surname: Umberfist — a Dunduaar clan. Emiffi's siblings (or related gnomes) also work at the Silver Spaulder.
- Had the other half of Ky'tha's locket. Says it was a good-luck charm given to him by someone he calls "my nephew" (not actually a nephew), a Cragfury dwarf. The vein drove the Cragfury away and he misses him.
- The locket half was stolen from him by "a cloak rat" wearing a red cloak. Emiffi saw who stole it.
- Pressured by Lady Rook to supply iron, steel, and Dunduaar materials to the drow smith at the Silver Spaulder.
- The Silver Spaulder smith is not paying him fairly; takes the supplies and does not share profits.
- Rook and the Silver Spaulder smith "go way back," both arriving in Emberstran around the same time.
- Emiffi and the Silver Spaulder's gnome workers are from the same family or kin group.
- Says he gets shipments (presumably tied to Rook's supply pressure).
- Tells Ky'tha: "I get the sense that there is too much that is strange around here… I'm kind of trapped. Caught between two stones."
- Wants to see the town where it should be. Not happy about his situation. Not comfortable sharing more when first met.
- The Silver Spaulder smith sends a message after the armor fitting: he will help "with the wooing of Bafast."
- In Session 13, Ky asks Rook about Bafast's situation; Rook gets defensive and suggests the Cragfury clan may be the real thieves, framing her involvement as helping underdark communities make lives outside Dunduaar.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 12: Ky'tha meets Bafast at Gems of Emberstran while selling an opal.
- Session 12: Emiffi notices Ky'tha's half-locket and says "you've got one, too."
- Session 12: Bafast says he had the other locket half until a red-cloaked "cloak rat" stole it.
- Session 12: Bafast says Lady Rook pressured him into supplying materials to the drow at the Silver Spaulder.
- Session 12: Ky'tha learns the Silver Spaulder smith is not paying Bafast fairly or sharing profits.
- Session 13: Ky'tha raises Bafast's situation with Rook.

## Strong Inferences

- Bafast is a pressured middleman in Rook's underdark material chain.
- His locket connection is important to Ky'tha's Cragfury/Dunduaar identity thread, but its exact meaning is not yet documented.
- Emiffi knows more than she can safely say in front of Bafast or customers.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Bafast recovered the stolen locket half.
- Whether the red-cloaked thief was Cloak of Defiance, a Rook-aligned agent, or another faction using the color.
- Whether Bafast is victim, collaborator, or both in the supply chain.

## Relationships

- Ky'tha: Tentative and suspicious; Ky'tha promises to watch for rubies and to get him more coin. He asks her to find the locket if she can.
- Emiffi: Gnome assistant; clearly knows more than she lets on and visibly wants to communicate around Bafast's caution.
- Silver Spaulder smith: Business exploiter. Gets Dunduaar materials at below-market rates, doesn't share profits.
- Lady Rook: Pressure source. Rook recruited him into the supply arrangement.
- Cragfury (unnamed nephew figure): The missing-locket connection and Dunduaar history.

## Open Questions

- Did Bafast acquire the other locket half back as Ky'tha asked?
- What does Emiffi know about who stole the locket?
- What exactly is in the supply shipments Bafast is handling for Rook?
