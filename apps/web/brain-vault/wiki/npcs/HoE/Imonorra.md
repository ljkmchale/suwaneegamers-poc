# Imonorra

## Scope

HoE-only NPC.

## Summary

Imonorra is a reclusive wizard who never leaves her tower. She is Lady Rook's most trusted ally — Rook trusts her with her life. She holds the conduit rod to the Marrowstone, meaning the stone cannot be brought to its full necrotic potential without her cooperation. She has teleported at least once on Rook's behalf (Ky'tha to Trailpoint), showing her magical capability is significant. Her political alignment is murky: a captured Cloak agent spit her name with contempt when listing Emberstran figures who tolerate the existing power structure.

## Physical Description

Silver-haired. Entered a scene to speak privately with Lady Rook. Beyond that, appearance is not detailed in notes. Her tower has a rectangular base; the approach path is winding and dark with arcane items and shifting leaves. Datheril Runewish serves as her doorman.

## Known Facts

- **Never leaves her tower**: Jae Vynaar confirmed this. Multiple interactions happen when others visit her, not when she comes to them.
- **Holds the Marrowstone rod**: Lady Rook told the party that "Imonorra has the conduit to bring it to its full potential." Affixing Imonorra's rod to the Marrowstone stone releases the necrotic energy and allows it to be used again. Ainslie drained the stone in Session 20; Imonorra still holds the rod.
- **Rook's trust**: Rook trusts Imonorra with her life. She uses Imonorra's tower as a meeting and operations base.
- **Teleportation on Rook's behalf**: She teleported Ky'tha directly to the Spinning Stag Inn in Trailpoint so Ky'tha could confirm Phira was alive. Returned Ky'tha safely.
- **Cloak agent reaction (Session 10)**: During interrogation, Lockjaw named Imonorra alongside the Magistrate and Hand as figures in Emberstran who are "ok with the Cloak." He said her name with contempt ("he spits"). This may mean she tolerates or accommodates the Cloak's presence rather than opposing it — or the agent simply dislikes her power.
- **Session 06 dinner**: Imonorra entered a Rook meeting and needed to speak to Lady Rook privately, interrupting. Rook excused herself, apologized to Ky and company, and invited them to a twilight dinner instead.
- **Lady Tarathial's read**: Lady Tarathial does not know Imonorra and expressed ignorance about whether Imonorra works for Rook.
- **Session 20**: Rook tells the party that between herself, Imonorra, and the Captains, she thinks they can keep the town safe.

## Strong Inferences

- She is a significant magical power in Emberstran, likely more capable than any typical NPC wizard.
- Her cooperation is required to make the Marrowstone usable again — which means the party would need to go through her if they ever wanted that option.
- Her relationship with the Cloak is ambiguous: the interrogated agent's contempt could mean she's independent and powerful enough that even the Cloak resents her, or that she has accommodated them.

## Relationships

- Lady Rook: closest ally; Rook operates from her tower and trusts her completely.
- Datheril Runewish: doorman and household staff.
- Marrowstone: holds the rod/conduit component.
- Cloak of Defiance: relationship unclear; Lockjaw's comment implies accommodation or uncomfortable coexistence.
- Party: interacted through Ky'tha (teleport to Trailpoint); otherwise not directly engaged.

## Open Questions

- What does Imonorra actually want from Emberstran's power situation?
- Why does she never leave her tower — discipline, magical anchoring, or risk?
- What is her full read on the Cloak? Does she work with them, tolerate them, or simply outpace them?
- If the Marrowstone is ever recharged, what does Imonorra ask in return for the rod?
- Does she know what is inside the vault beneath Mount Emberstran?
