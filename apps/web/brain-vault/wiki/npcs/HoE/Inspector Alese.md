# Inspector Alese

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Fake inspector; Cloak of Defiance operative. Also known as **the Whisper**. Deceased (killed by Hap, Session 11).

## Summary

Inspector Alese — also known as the Whisper — was the Cloak of Defiance operative who posed as a Lord-appointed inspector investigating the Kelona murder. She was quickly identified as a fraud, disappeared before she could be tailed, and was later killed by Hap in the sewers beneath Emberstran when she was caught trying to drill a surveillance peephole into Imonorra's Tower. When unmasked after death, she was confirmed as the same person who had posed as the Inspector. She had also been paying regular visits to Jae Vynaar, likely cultivating him as an information source before his murder.

## Appearance

- Halfling.
- Raven black braided hair.
- Burgundy coat and vest; feathered hat.
- Neck marking: two small horizontal lines with two vertical lines pointing upward from them (like two upside-down T's or upward arrows). Only partially visible on her neck.

## Known Facts

- **Session 03 (first encounter as Inspector)**: Appeared at the Old Oak & Grapes claiming to be an inspector appointed by the Lord to investigate Kelona's murder. Said she knew of the party through Meglan. Left when Aury dismissed her as Vammad arrived. Ainslie and Og tailed her but lost her. Ainslie glimpsed the neck marking (two upside-down T's).
- **Captain Vammad confirmed no inspector had been assigned** — she was not legitimate.
- Og deduced she was fake because she didn't know about the EGD.
- **Jae Vynaar**: Per Jae, a woman matching her description visited him for "tea" on the 4th day of each stretch — likely extracting information from him.
- **Session 11 (killed as the Whisper)**: Found in the sewers near Imonorra's Tower, 3' tall in a plague doctor mask, trying to drill a peephole through the wall. Killed by Hap after a brief sewer combat. When Hap removed her mask, she was confirmed as the same person who had posed as the Inspector. Aury animated her corpse for 14 hours.
- **Items recovered from her body**: studded leather armor (taken by Ky), 9gp, a small crystal — Divination magic (Listener's Coil, taken by Ky), a fancy +1 dagger (taken by Ky), blue cloak with no markings, plague doctor mask — Transmutation magic (Mask of Withered Breath, taken by Ainslie), magical ear cuff — Divination magic (Listener's Coil).
- Her plan: set the crystal as a peephole into Imonorra's Tower and listen via the ear cuff — she was conducting surveillance on Imonorra.
- The neck symbol she bore is unknown; Hap suggested the phrase "ree ga day" (Regardez) as a signal if anyone else bearing the mark is spotted.

## Strong Inferences

- She was a Cloak of Defiance operative tasked with both intelligence gathering (Jae, surveillance on Imonorra) and disruption (fake inspector role to intercept the party's investigation).
- Her surveillance of Imonorra's Tower suggests the Cloak had interest in monitoring Imonorra — possibly because Imonorra hosts Lady Rook.
- Her paid "tea" visits to Jae suggest she was cultivating him before his murder — she or her employers had strong motive to silence him after he talked to the party.

- What the neck symbol means and who else bears it.
