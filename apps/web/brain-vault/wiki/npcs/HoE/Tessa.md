# Tessa

## Scope

HoE-only NPC. Do not apply this page to SoD or The Silent Vanguard.

## Summary

Tessa is Ky'tha's primary Etterset contact in Emberstran, operating out of Charum's Oils. She starts as a cautious street-level informant and, by the later sessions, becomes the new face of the Etterset after Belavie elevates the organization's local presence.

## Known Facts

- Operates out of Charum's Oils in Emberstran.
- First contact: Ky'tha secretly finds her after the early dock/cellar jobs. Tessa has no help on the cellar or dock threats but may have a clue to help with the tablet mystery.
- In Session 03: Ky and Tessa discuss the Etterset coin (two curved daggers). They agree neither will reveal they know about it, and both will share anything they learn. Tessa warns that deeper involvement with the organization means real danger from the Cloak.
- In Session 05: Tessa says the Cloak was talking in the sewers and that Lady Rook has recently arrived in Emberstran. Notes that the Etterset needs Rook to gain ground against the Cloak.
- She sends word to "Bru" to help fix up Og after an injury.
- Is acquainted with an'Davo; he pays for a session for Ky'tha at Charum's Oils and Tessa is described as knowing him.
- Tessa tells Ky'tha that an'Davo believes Rook was supposed to be the hit at the Cloak show that night.
- In Session 12: Tessa introduces Ky'tha to Belavie Lightstrider at a private room in the Dyed Cane. Belavie gives Ky'tha a rank-two coin.
- After the Cloak dinner revelation (Session 13): Tessa comes to Ky'tha's room at the Headless Hydra, uninvited, damp and tired. "We need to talk. Tell me your friends did not have dinner with the Cloak." She warns that the party is too well known to visibly be in two opposing groups. Later softens and says she can arrange for the whole group to go before Belavie if needed.
- Later sessions: Ky'tha stays with Tessa when the party leaves for the vault. Tessa becomes the new face of the Etterset at Our Exquisites, which has become the organization's base. She puts out feelers for Zymve when Ky'tha asks but with discretion. Knows Hap came into something soon and is being discreet about it.
- Passes Ky'tha and company on the way to the Cellarium and mentions Hap's news.
- In Session 25, Ky'tha finds Tessa at the new Etterset headquarters after the party returns from Mount Emberstran. Tessa says Ky'tha's message arrived too late and that the Etterset has had misfortunes in Ulgrey.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Session 03: Ky'tha meets Tessa at Charum's Oils and discusses the two-curved-daggers Etterset coin.
- Session 05: Tessa tells Ky'tha that the sewer voices sounded like Cloak activity and that Lady Rook's rise matters to the Etterset.
- Session 09: Tessa tells Ky'tha the Cloak planned a hit on Lady Rook and that Whisper/Quill likely killed Kelona and Jae.
- Session 12: Tessa brings Ky'tha to meet Belavie Lightstrider and Bordi in a private room; Ky'tha receives a rank-two coin.
- Session 13: Tessa warns Ky'tha that the party is too visible to be tied to both Rook/Etterset and the Cloak.

## Strong Inferences

- Tessa is Ky'tha's safest Emberstran underworld contact, but she is still protecting Etterset interests.
- Tessa is not the top Etterset authority; Belavie appears to hold a higher or more formal rank.
- Her warnings are practical, not moralistic: she wants Ky'tha alive and useful.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Her exact rank in the Etterset.
- What she learns about Zymve from later feelers.
- Whether she would choose Ky'tha over Etterset strategy if those came into conflict.

## Relationships

- Ky'tha: Primary bond. Tessa was the entry point to the Etterset and trusts Ky'tha enough to confront her directly.
- Belavie Lightstrider: Organizational superior or peer; Tessa is the street face, Belavie is the institutional one.
- an'Davo: Known associate; he pays for Ky'tha's session at Charum's Oils, suggesting some mutual arrangement.
- Bru: A contact Tessa can call on for physical patching up.
- Etterset / Our Exquisites: By the later sessions she is running or co-running the Emberstran operation.

## Open Questions

- How does Tessa handle the party's Cloak entanglement with Ainslie and Og now that they're gone?
- What does she learn from putting feelers out about Zymve?
- What does she know about Hap's inheritance and why is she watching it closely?
- What misfortune hit the Etterset in Ulgrey, and does it connect to Phira's approach from Ulgrey?
