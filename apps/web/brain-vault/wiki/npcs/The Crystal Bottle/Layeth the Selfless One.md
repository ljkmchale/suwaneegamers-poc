# Layeth the Selfless One

**Role:** Deity / Warlock Patron  
**Type:** Celestial  
**Status:** Active (divine)  
**Worshippers/Bonded:** [[Yvaine]]  
**Known Agents:** [[Ashe]] (herald)

## Overview

Layeth the Selfless One is [[Yvaine]]'s warlock patron, chosen during her transformation in Session 6.3 at [[Everlight]]. She is a Celestial deity — the source of Yvaine's warlock powers under the Celestial subclass. [[Ashe]], the daughter of [[Muerg]], delivered Yvaine's +2 studded leather armor as a gift from Layeth during the Awakening.

## The Choosing (Session 6.3)

During [[Yvaine]]'s warlock transformation — facilitated by [[Fizban]], [[Gwyneth]], and [[Lothim]] — Yvaine felt multiple divine beings vying for her soul. [[Fizban]] told her she must choose one. Among them, Yvaine felt Layeth's presence and chose her. Fizban confirmed: *"The Celestial is one who has power in her now… helping to guide her."*

Afterward, [[Ashe]] appeared alongside [[Muerg]] during the Awakening and delivered the armor to Yvaine, saying: *"Your goddess wishes you to have a gift."*
