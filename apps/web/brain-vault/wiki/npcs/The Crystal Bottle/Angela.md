# Angela

**Status:** Rescued; in Everlight (as of session 7)
**Relationship:** Ren's younger sister

## Description

Ren's biological sister. She was held captive and unconscious by Harlowe for 10 years in Kravwynhold's tower. When rescued (session 6.1), she was groggy and malnourished. She recognized Ren immediately and embraced her. She said: *"It has been 10 years. When you got away, she captured me and said she wanted to get all of the children who ran away. I've been unconscious for 10 years."*

She is psychologically traumatized (PTSD). In the days following rescue, she drew pictures of "the shadow lady" (Harlowe) — a sign of her mental harm.

## How She Was Freed

Yvaine determined that the chains holding Angela required a blood sacrifice to break. Ren sliced her own hand and made the sacrifice, freeing Angela. After breaking the chains, Ren also destroyed Harlowe's soul-trapping crystal orb (taking 13 damage) — freeing the trapped souls.

## Current Status

She stayed in [[Everlight]] to recover while the party departed for the Lost City (session 7). [[Alteya]] also stayed to be with her own family — both are in Everlight together.

## Connection to Harlowe

Angela said Harlowe wanted "all of the children who ran away" — implying Ren herself once escaped from Harlowe, which is why Harlowe captured Angela as leverage/revenge.
