# Muerg

**Status:** Divine — Active
**Reputation:** Divine / Unknown alignment

## Description

Muerg is an Old God who appeared at [[Everlight]] in Session 7 alongside a companion named [[Ashe]]. He emerged from the mountain without warning and declared "Everlight is now under my protection." He also announced that "The Awakening has begun." After Ashe channeled divine flame through the gates of Everlight (creating a new mountain pass), Muerg and Ashe departed — and with their departure, the time warp that had plagued the region was lifted.

Ashe gifted [[Yvaine]] [[Yvaine's +2 Studded Leather|+2 studded leather armor]] before they left.

## Role in Campaign

Muerg represents a cosmic-scale force entering the campaign. His declaration of the Awakening suggests divine powers are stirring in response to the events surrounding [[Malik]], [[Aurelion]], and the ancient portal. His relationship to [[Ashe]] (described as a companion or daughter figure) and his protection of Everlight signal he may be both an ally and a force with his own agenda.
