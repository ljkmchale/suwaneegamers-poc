# Morath

**Status:** Unknown (spirit/entity)
**Also known as:** The Shadow Seer

## Description

[[Fizban]]/Tsuul delivered this message to [[Narris]] telepathically in session 6.3:

*"Morath, the shadow seer, weaver of secrets, servant of Iuz'Obal, has watched Sigil since his first breath. Follow the thread of shadow, child of light; when the two are one, the veil will part."*

Morath is a shadow-aligned entity, a servant of a being called **[[Iuz'Obal]]**. He has been watching [[Sigil (TCB)|Sigil]] since birth. The connection between Morath, Sigil, and Narris ("the seeker" / "the protected") forms a major planar mystery thread.

## Open Questions

- Who or what is Iuz'Obal?
- What is "the two are one" referring to (Sigil and Narris? Light and shadow?)
- What is the "veil" that will part?
- What does Morath want?
- Why has he watched Sigil since birth?
