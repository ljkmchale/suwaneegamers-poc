# Ashe

**Role:** Divine Herald / Celestial  
**Affiliation:** [[Muerg]] (father); [[Layeth the Selfless One]] (serves)  
**Status:** Active  
**Location:** Beyond the mortal plane; appeared in [[Everlight]]

## Overview

Ashe is a divine figure — daughter of [[Muerg]], the Old God who declared the Awakening at [[Everlight]]. She acts as a herald and agent for [[Layeth the Selfless One]], [[Yvaine]]'s warlock patron. Ashe appeared alongside Muerg during the Awakening event, channeling divine flame to reshape the mountains around Everlight.

## The Awakening at Everlight (Session 6.3)

During the Awakening, two figures appeared before the townspeople of Everlight and the party:

- **Muerg** — a large divine figure, Old God, who declared: *"Everlight is now under my protection and will be part of this time!"* and *"The old gods silent and spent have begun to retreat… a new order arises and I will not allow this world to fall into ruin without guidance."*
- **Ashe** — the smaller figure beside him. Muerg addressed her as "Daughter"; she replied "My lord, please."

Ashe channeled divine flame into the main gates of Everlight, guided by Muerg, cleaving a new road through the mountainside and creating a new mountain pass. The act took only minutes.

## Gift to Yvaine

After reshaping the pass, Ashe turned to [[Yvaine]] and said: *"Your goddess wishes you to have a gift."* A beam of light focused on Yvaine, and her armor glowed — this was the conferral of [[Yvaine's +2 Studded Leather]]. Ashe and Muerg then ascended into the sky.

The "goddess" she referenced is [[Layeth the Selfless One]], Yvaine's chosen warlock patron — confirming that Ashe acts as Layeth's messenger in the mortal world.

## Key Connections

- **[[Muerg]]** — her father; the Old God she serves alongside
- **[[Layeth the Selfless One]]** — the goddess on whose behalf Ashe delivered Yvaine's armor
- **[[Yvaine]]** — recipient of Ashe's gift; Yvaine's patron's herald
- **[[Everlight]]** — site of the Awakening where Ashe appeared
