# Aurelion

**Status:** Captive — chained in the Lost City portal
**Reputation:** Ally (captive)

## Description

Aurelion is an angel currently chained inside an unstable golden-white portal in the [[Lost City (Athralis)]]. Two cracked blue godstones power the portal that holds him. In Session 7.3, [[Yvaine]] spoke to him in Celestial, and he communicated that the key around [[Malik]]'s neck is what is needed to free him.

## Role in Campaign

Freeing Aurelion is an active party quest. He may be the key to stopping Malik's claim to heavenly power. His celestial nature and the divine mechanics of his imprisonment connect to the broader themes of divine power, the Awakening declared by [[Muerg]], and the ancient portal discovered in [[Athralis And The Oasis|Athralis]]. The D'jinn imprisoned in [[The Crystal Bottle|The Crystal Bottle]] is part of the same chain of power that brought Malik to this point.
