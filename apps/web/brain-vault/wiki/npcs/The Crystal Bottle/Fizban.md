# Fizban (Tsuul'physs Bhan)

**Status:** Alive (location: Everlight as of session 6.3; likely the Lost City / Athralis as of 7.3)
**True Name:** Tsuul'physs Bhan
**Reputation:** Allied (patron/advisor)

## Description

An old man in blue robes carrying a staff. He matches portraits the party had seen in Fizban's Tower. He confirmed to Scarlytt that "Tsuul'physs Bhan" is a name he has gone by. He is an Artificer — a mad genius type who worships all the gods and teaches that "threads connected the gods and they need to be in balance." He is of indeterminate age; old enough to have lived for centuries.

## Family and Relationships

- **[[Gwyneth]]** — his daughter ("daughter, they are all here, that's good")
- **[[Kal'dar]]** — his exiled "son" (former apprentice who betrayed him; Fizban calls him "exiled son")
- **[[Lothim]]** — the silver dragon carries out tasks for Fizban and counts as a trusted ally

## Role in Campaign

Appeared to the party in [[Everlight]] after Tass's death (session 6.3). Confirmed identity as Tsuul'physs Bhan. Tasked each party member individually:
- Told Sigil about his mother's sword (cave in Nesbitt's Grove)
- Spoke telepathically with [[Narris]], sharing the message about [[Morath]] the shadow seer
- Told [[Scarlytt Amyran|Scarlytt]] the history of her shield (from Krynn, dragon Indryss, Galtrelon's great-grandfather's grove)
- Told Ren that only [[Harlowe]] knows about her parents — and that Harlowe is now in the Bathaen Empire
- Facilitated Yvaine's transformation into a warlock (with Gwyneth and Lothim)
- Directed the party to pursue Kal'dar at Zeprock's Crown to prevent raising a new army

He also revealed that Kal'dar had been at the tower for 70 years. He parted ways with Kal'dar 70 years ago when Kal'dar demanded the secret to eternal life and Fizban refused.

## Backstory

Fizban/Tsuul was trapped in his tower by Kal'dar 70 years ago. He was freed (possibly by the party's actions — unclear how). He created the Elixir of Timelessness for Gwyneth, traveling to the world of Krynn to obtain a phoenix feather. At Aelinithalos's Hollow — an enchanted grove consecrated by Galtrelon's great-grandfather — the blue dragon Indryss gave Fizban the dragon's scales to craft Scarlytt's shield, after finding him to be peaceful.
