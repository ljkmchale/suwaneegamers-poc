# Harlowe

**Status:** Escaped (fled through portal during battle, session 6.1)
**Race:** Human
**Reputation:** Enemy
**Location:** Bathaen Empire (confirmed by [[Fizban]]/Tsuul in session 6.3)

## Description

An older woman who looks like an older version of Yvaine — suggesting Harlowe is Yvaine's **biological mother**. She ran Kravwynhold as "the Protector," using enslaved children as labor in shops and mines.

## Key Role in Campaign

- **Yvaine's mother** (their physical resemblance confirmed in session 6.1)
- Held **[[Angela]]** (Ren's sister) captive and unconscious for 10 years — after Ren escaped Harlowe previously
- Kept 32 children enslaved in [[Kravwynhold]]; children worked in mines, shops, taverns
- Controls guards through rune tattoos (she "sees through them")
- Has a crystal orb on a pedestal filled with trapped souls (broken by Ren after rescue)
- The password to her tower was "daughter lost" (Yvaine intuited it)
- The Kravwynhold shield / magical protections fell when Harlowe fled

## The Kravwynhold Tower

Multi-floor fortress: chain-golem halls, puzzle rooms (clock/keys), sigil-locked doors, chain-room, and a ritual chamber at the top with purple glyphs where Angela was held. Harlowe had a study filled with books on planar travel and time. [[Tassarion Ranges]] died in the tower saving Angela. [[Grisha Thornhand]] (the town blacksmith) was recruited to help clear the town before the party entered the tower.

## Connection to Ren's Parents

[[Fizban]] confirmed: "only Harlowe will know anything about them [Ren's parents]." The Blustery Waste gave Ren a vision of her parents saying they didn't abandon her. Harlowe is the key to the truth about Ren's family.

## Connection to Yvaine

"The relationship between her father, step-mother, and Ren" — Yvaine's family has a deeper connection to Ren. Harlowe's identity as Yvaine's mother means Ren's capture of Angela was also personal to Yvaine. **Open question:** What is the full familial web between Harlowe, Yvaine's father, Yvaine's step-mother, and Ren's family?
