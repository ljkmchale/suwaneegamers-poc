# Serrenia Netherfall

**Status:** Alive
**Reputation:** Neutral

## Description

Serrenia Netherfall is the Head Mistress of [[Arbescar Light and Magic]] (ALM) in [[Tal'basar]]. She is the senior female administrator of the institution. She had a minor conflict with [[Tassarion Ranges]] (Tass) at some point, and later sent ALM professor Carl home after an argument.

Serrenia serves as the party's contact for reaching [[Lothim]] the silver dragon. The party's [[ALM Crystal]] speed-travel use is coordinated through ALM contacts.

## Role in Campaign

Serrenia is the party's primary administrative contact at ALM. She is a reliable but neutral figure — more institutional authority than personal ally. Her role as the conduit for contacting Lothim gives her practical ongoing significance.
