# Grisha Thornhand

**Status:** Alive; declared new leader of Kravwynhold
**Race:** Human
**Reputation:** Neutral/Feared

## Description

A Sigil-sized human woman who runs a blacksmith shop in Kravwynhold called the **Ironclad Crucible**. She has a mechanical gauntlet hand. She keeps brutal tools — torture devices, spikes, shackles, maces — on her walls. She makes weapons and armor for Harlowe's guards. She cannot see through the rune tattoos the guards have (Harlowe can).

She was afraid of [[Viktor]] and [[Harlowe]]'s tower. Agreed to help the party clear the town guards (but not Viktor or the tower). Armed with a huge war hammer.

After the party defeated [[Viktor]] and the tower, Grisha declared herself the new town leader and led the remaining townspeople toward [[Tal'basar]]. [[Narris]] warned her: "If you make it to Tal'basar and harm a hair on anyone's head, I will hunt you down and kill you myself."
