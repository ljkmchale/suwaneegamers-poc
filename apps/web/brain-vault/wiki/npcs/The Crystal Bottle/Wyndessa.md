# Wyndessa

**Type:** Gold Dragon  
**Location:** Eye of Arbescar (home base)  
**Role:** Guardian of [[Arbescar]] (the region)  
**Status:** Active  
**Companion:** [[C'mey]] (fellow gold dragon)

## Overview

Wyndessa is a gold dragon who, alongside [[C'mey]], serves as one of the guardians of [[Arbescar]] — the region itself. They make their home in the Eye of Arbescar. [[Yvaine]] knew of Wyndessa from her personal history before the party encountered them.

## Session 4.1 — Encounter

Wyndessa and [[C'mey]] were witnessed by the party defeating the green dragon Elendore in an aerial battle. The golds were reluctant to fight but are bound to guard [[Arbescar]].

After the battle, the party approached. They confirmed their identities as Wyndessa and C'mey. Wyndessa was present during [[C'mey]]'s emotional moment with [[Sigil (TCB)]] over the ring that says "mother" in Draconic.

Yvaine warned both dragons about the D'jinn.
