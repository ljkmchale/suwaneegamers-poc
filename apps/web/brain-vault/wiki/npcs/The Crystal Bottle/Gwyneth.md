# Gwyneth

**Status:** Alive
**Race:** Dryad
**Reputation:** Helpful (ally)
**Location:** Scarbrook forest (primary); travels via portal

## Description

A beautiful dryad who lives in the forest near [[Scarbrook]]. She is Fizban's (Tsuul'physs Bhan's) **daughter**, and has lived for hundreds of years due to the **[[Fizban Tower Vault Items|Elixir of Timelessness]]** Fizban created for her. She has children's toys in her yard ("for Y'mec"). She holds a special upcoming purpose known only to Fizban and [[Scarlytt Amyran|Scarlytt]].

## Key Details

- **Daughter of Fizban / Tsuul'physs Bhan**
- **Has a son named Ymec**
- Has a **sending stone / magic focus necklace** that she used to monitor (spy on) the party without their consent; party discovered this in session 5.3; Yvaine ultimately left the necklace with Tass's body at the Everlight temple (session 6.3), severing Gwyneth's link
- Participated in Yvaine's warlock transformation at [[Everlight]] alongside Fizban and Lothim
- Managed communication during the [[Tal'basar]] battle
- Gave [[Scarlytt Amyran|Scarlytt]] a **twine ring** to give to Fizban ("say hello to my father when you see him")
- Gave [[Sigil (TCB)|Sigil]] a **ring inscribed with "mother" in Draconic** � confirmed by [[C'mey]] (in Ymec form) during Session 4.1; when C'mey touched the ring it glowed and Sigil and C'mey embraced, this was because the ring was given to Sigil by Gwyneth who is Ymec's mother.

## What She Told the Party (Session 4.1)

- Malik is an air genasi whose cult split: Malik went to the "dark side"
- **[[Malik]] is the skeleton wrapped around the bottle � he died when he released the D'jinn**
- Fizban is her father (alchemist)
- Kal'dar set traps on approach to the tower
- She is in contact with Fizban; Carl is looking in the "right place"

## Role in Campaign

Introduced as an ally in the [[Scarbrook]] area. Has guided and aided the party multiple times. After Tass's death, she met the party at [[Everlight]] to help Yvaine's transformation. She winked at Yvaine after the warlock ritual, stepped through a portal, and has not been seen since session 6.3.
