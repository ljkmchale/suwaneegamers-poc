# Gretchen Lightfoot

**Role:** Innkeeper  
**Location:** [[Everlight]] — The Twinkling Fir inn  
**Status:** Active  
**Affiliation:** Independent

## Overview

Halfling innkeeper of the Twinkling Fir in [[Everlight]]. The party stayed at her inn during their time in Everlight following the rescue of [[Angela]].

## Known Interactions

- **Session 6.3–6.4:** Hosted the party at the Twinkling Fir during their stay in Everlight.

## Notes

The Twinkling Fir is the primary inn in Everlight.
