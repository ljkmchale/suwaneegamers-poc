# Cornelius Woodrift

**Role:** Bandit / Mercenary Leader  
**Status:** Deceased (killed by party, Session 4.3)  
**Affiliation:** Independent; father of Sillian

## Overview

Cornelius Woodrift was a mercenary or bandit leader encountered during the party's travels. He is the father of Sillian (the person who killed Whisk, a recurring thread from early sessions).

## Events

- **Session 4.3:** The party encountered Cornelius at a campsite. He offered to help them in exchange for recovering a ghost-guarded treasure. The party initially agreed, but after securing the treasure and freeing his prisoners (including Brandon), they ambushed and killed Cornelius's group. [[Scarlytt Amyran]] led the attack. [[Sigil (TCB)]] pretended to execute prisoner Brandon in order to deceive Cornelius, but actually freed him.

## Notable Details

- His map led the party to a haunted mansion (Session 5.1) that was afterward used as a hidden party base.
- Son/child: Sillian, who killed Whisk.
