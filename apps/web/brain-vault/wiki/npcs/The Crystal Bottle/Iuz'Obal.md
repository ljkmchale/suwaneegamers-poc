# Iuz'Obal

**Role:** Unknown — powerful entity or deity  
**Status:** Unknown  
**Affiliation:** [[Morath]] serves Iuz'Obal  
**Known Agents:** [[Morath]] (the shadow seer)

## Overview

Iuz'Obal is the master of [[Morath]], the shadow seer who has watched [[Sigil (TCB)]] since birth. The only known reference comes from [[Fizban]]'s telepathic message to [[Narris]] in Session 6.3:

> *"Morath, the shadow seer, weaver of secrets, servant of Iuz'Obal, has watched Sigil since his 1st breath."*

Beyond this, Iuz'Obal's nature, goals, and power level are unknown. The name suggests a significant entity — possibly a shadow deity, Old God, or planar power.

## What Is Known

- Iuz'Obal commands [[Morath]], who is described as "shadow seer, weaver of secrets."
- Morath has been observing [[Sigil (TCB)]] since the moment of his birth — suggesting Iuz'Obal has a long-term interest in Sigil specifically.
- [[Fizban]] knows of Iuz'Obal and chose to warn Narris, indicating Fizban views this as a threat worth tracking.

## Key Connections

- **[[Morath]]** — Iuz'Obal's agent; has watched Sigil since birth
- **[[Sigil (TCB)]]** — the subject of Iuz'Obal's surveillance through Morath
- **[[Narris]]** — warned by Fizban; told to "follow the thread of shadow"
- **[[Fizban]]** — aware of Iuz'Obal; revealed this to Narris
