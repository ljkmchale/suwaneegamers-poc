# Belzander Umbridge

**Status:** Alive
**Reputation:** Neutral

## Description

Belzander Umbridge is the Head Master of [[Arbescar Light and Magic]] (ALM) in [[Tal'basar]]. He is one of the senior administrators of the institution that sponsors the party.

He has tasked the party with returning any blue crystals they find to him. The party holds an [[ALM Crystal]] that enables speed travel, which must be coordinated with ALM before use.

## Role in Campaign

Belzander represents the party's institutional connection to ALM and the [[Council of Six]]. His interest in blue crystals ties into the campaign's broader mystery around blue godstones and the [[A'urea'Pura Cult]]'s operations.
