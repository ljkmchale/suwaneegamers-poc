# C'mey

**Type:** Gold Dragon  
**Humanoid Form:** Ymec  
**Location:** Eye of Arbescar (home base)  
**Role:** Guardian of [[Arbescar]] (the region)  
**Status:** Active  
**Companion:** [[Wyndessa]] (fellow gold dragon)

## Key Details

- **Son of Gwyneth who is daughter of Fizban / Tsuul'physs Bhan**
- **Companion to Wyndessa**

## Overview

C'mey is a gold dragon who, alongside [[Wyndessa]], serves as one of the guardians of [[Arbescar]] — the region itself, not merely a single tower or forest. They make their home in the Eye of Arbescar. C'mey has a humanoid form named **Ymec**. [[Yvaine]] knew of C'mey and Wyndessa from her own history before the party met them.

## Session 4.1 — Encounter

The party witnessed C'mey and [[Wyndessa]] defeat the green dragon Elendore in a 10–15 minute aerial battle. The golds did not want to fight but were bound to guard [[Arbescar]].

After the battle, the party approached. [[Scarlytt Amyran]] told them they had been sent to the tower by [[Gwyneth]]. Yvaine offered C'mey a gold dragon scale she carried — C'mey allowed her to keep it.

**The Ring:** Scarlytt asked C'mey about a ring [[Gwyneth]] had given [[Sigil (TCB)]]. C'mey identified the word on it as meaning "**mother**" in Draconic. C'mey then shifted into Ymec form and touched the ring — it began to glow. Sigil and C'mey (as Ymec) embraced. The significance of this moment is not yet fully explained, but the ring connects to Sigil to Gwyneth and C'mey clearly has a deep connection to it.

Yvaine warned C'mey and Wyndessa about the D'jinn. C'mey, still in Ymec form, seemed calm.

## Key Connections

- **[[Wyndessa]]** — companion gold dragon; they guard the Eye of Arbescar together
- **[[Sigil (TCB)]]** — the ring Gwyneth gave Sigil says "mother" in Draconic; C'mey's emotional response in Ymec form suggests a deep connection
- **[[Gwyneth]]** — gave Sigil the ring; sent the party to the tower knowing C'mey and Wyndessa were there
- **[[Fizban]]** — C'mey and Wyndessa guard all of Arbescar, which includes the lands near Fizban's Tower; connection to Fizban's history likely
- **[[Yvaine]]** — knew of C'mey from her personal history; offered and was allowed to keep a gold dragon scale
