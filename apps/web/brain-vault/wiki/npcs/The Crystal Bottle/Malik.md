# Malik

**Status:** Uncertain — original body dead; demonic entity active
**Race:** Air Genasi (original body)
**Reputation:** Villain

## True Nature

Malik was an air genasi and the **original leader of the A'urea'Pura Cult**. He sought the D'jinn King and found the Crystal Bottle. **He died when he freed the D'jinn** — he is described as "the skeleton wrapped around the bottle." He died releasing it.

However, Malik's essence or the D'jinn's influence did not die with him. In session 1.2, Malik somehow merged with / possessed Tassarion, sending Tass into a catatonic state. He was later separated from Tass.

By session 6.3, Fizban explains: the unnamed D'jinn made Kal'dar hollow, and "from that hollow, I came forth" — Malik says this in session 7.3. So the entity now calling itself Malik is something that **emerged from Kal'dar's hollowed body** after the D'jinn consumed him.

In session 7.3, Malik monologues: "Kal'dar — the great apprentice of Fizban — do you know what his last words were: 'I will prove him worthy.' Kal'dar thought he could cheat death and impress his master. The D'jinn didn't bless him, but hollowed him. And from that hollow, I came forth. But mortals don't command storms, they are swept away by them."

## Current Situation (Session 7.3)

Malik is in the **[[Lost City (Athralis)]]** (see also [[Athralis And The Oasis]]) with the chained angel [[Aurelion]] in a broken golden-white portal. He claims to be ascending to the heavens. He holds a key around his neck that would free Aurelion. He controls the two cracked blue godstones powering the portal.

## The D'jinn Connection

- Malik freed the D'jinn from the Crystal Bottle (dying in the process)
- The D'jinn (unnamed) became Kal'dar's master
- Kal'dar sought immortality; was hollowed
- From the hollow, Malik emerged as a demon-like entity
- The D'jinn is now master of the whole situation; Kal'dar was called to Zeprock's Crown "to answer for his failure"
