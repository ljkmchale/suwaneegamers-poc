# Tassarion Ranges (Tass)

**Status:** Deceased (died session ~6.1, saving Angela)
**Race:** Elf
**Class:** Cleric
**Campaign role:** Original party member (Sessions 0–6.1)
**Player:** Sean Poole (also plays [[Galtrelon]], who replaced Tass in Session 6.3)

## Description

A passive elf cleric who "won't conduct violence." Had a complicated personality — described as annoying certain NPCs (Serrenia had a "small annoyance with Tass"). He kept a journal of people "he hated and would like to have killed — had he had the will to do so" (Sigil burned it after his death).

## Key Moments

- **Session 1.2**: Became catatonic after merging with Malik from the A'urea'Pura Cult
- **Session 5.4–5.5**: Drew "The Mist" card from the Deck of Many Things and disappeared for several sessions; returned semi-transparent and ghost-like in 5.5; could speak but moved slowly
- **Session 5.5**: In ghost form, told the party Sigil "will return when most needed"; sent a sending spell to Sigil; guided the party
- **Session 6.1**: Died in [[Harlowe]]'s tower saving [[Angela]]; the party carried his body out of [[Kravwynhold]]; Scar carried him
- **Session 6.3**: Funeral held in [[Everlight]]; [[Fizban]] comforted Scarlytt, saying Tass "was welcomed to the arms of his gods and will travel to the next plane"; Yvaine left her amulet with Tass at the temple; [[Leanna]] (temple priestess) received his body

## Current Status

His body is being preserved for delivery to his family in **[[Mahruud]]**. This is an active quest: **Return Tass's body to his family in [[Mahruud]].**

## Belongings Left Behind

- His effects were split among the party in session 6.3
- He wore the overalls from Fizban's Tower (no longer needs the restroom)
- Had a scroll from Fizban's vault
- Had a journal about the Blustery Waste (the Fizban history book Narris was meant to study)
- Had a ring described in Fizban's book of magic items
