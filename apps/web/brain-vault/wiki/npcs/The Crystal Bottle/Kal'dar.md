# Kal'dar

**Status:** Hollow — vessel for Malik
**Reputation:** Villain

## Description

Kal'dar was once [[Fizban]]'s apprentice. Seeking immortality, he made a deal with the D'jinn — the same entity trapped in [[The Crystal Bottle|the Crystal Bottle]]. The deal granted him a form of immortality but at a devastating cost: Kal'dar was hollowed out from within and became a vessel for [[Malik]]. The man who was Fizban's apprentice no longer effectively exists; what remains is Malik wearing Kal'dar's body.

He was last known to be heading to [[Zeprock's Crown]] — a small island off the southeast coast. The party has an active goal to kill him (and thus stop Malik's physical form).

In Session 5.2, Kal'dar confronted [[Tal'basar]], demanding the city's surrender. The party sided with the [[Council of Six]] against him.

## Role in Campaign

Kal'dar represents the consequences of desperate bargaining with the D'jinn. His hollowing is the mechanism by which Malik gained a physical presence in the mortal world. Killing Kal'dar is one of the party's active quests.
