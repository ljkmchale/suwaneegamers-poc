# Leanna

**Role:** Temple Attendant / Cleric  
**Location:** [[Everlight]] — Temple  
**Status:** Active  
**Affiliation:** Everlight temple

## Overview

A temple worker in [[Everlight]] who handled funeral arrangements for [[Tassarion Ranges]] after his death. She coordinated the preparations to return Tass's body to Mahruud.

## Known Interactions

- **Session 6.3–6.4:** Received [[Tassarion Ranges]]'s body and arranged its return to Mahruud.

## Notes

Mahruud is Tass's home; his body was to be sent back there for proper burial.
