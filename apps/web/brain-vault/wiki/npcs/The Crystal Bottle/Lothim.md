# Lothim

**Status:** Alive
**Reputation:** Friendly

## Description

Lothim is a silver dragon wyrmling encountered in a cave during [[TCB Session 02.1 - Silver Dragon|Session 2.1]]. The party traded him the Goblinbane Flameblade sword in exchange for 100 gold pieces, establishing a friendly relationship. Lothim is willing to vouch for the party with other dragons, making him a valuable connection in a world that includes both gold and green dragons.

The party can contact Lothim through [[Serrenia Netherfall]] at ALM ([[Arbescar Light and Magic]]). The party's [[ALM Crystal]] enables speed travel when coordinated through ALM.

## Role in Campaign

Lothim is a diplomatic resource — a friendly dragon who can open doors with other dragon factions. As the party encounters more draconic elements in their adventures, this relationship may prove increasingly useful.
