# Darveth

**Status:** Alive (Kravwynhold, likely heading to Tal'basar)
**Race:** Tiefling
**Role:** Blacksmith (Emberfang shop)

## Description

A tiefling blacksmith in Kravwynhold who operates **Emberfang**, producing higher quality weapons than Grisha. He makes Viktor's weapons. His shop burns hotter than Grisha's. He works under Harlowe's system but seems to want freedom — he told Tass that without Viktor, he could pursue magical crafting.

He was pleasant and professional in conversation with Tass. He knows of a smith on the other side of the mountains who might be able to repair the [[Sunblade Hilt]]. He refers to child laborers as "kids" rather than slaves or helpers — showing more humanity than others in the town.
