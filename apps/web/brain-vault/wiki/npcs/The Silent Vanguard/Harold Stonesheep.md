# Harold Stonesheep

## Summary

Harold Stonesheep (also called H'arald) is a thief and contact in Brinecross. He is the party's first field contact and carries the clue phrase that opens the Long Night investigation.

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Known Facts

- Based in Brinecross at the time of the zombie outbreak.
- Has an object of value and a location name to pass to the party; identified as the assignment target in Session 01.
- The party reaches him via a cliff cave near a crooked tree to a woodcutter shed.
- Found alive but sick during the Brinecross zombie outbreak.
- Carried a backpack with an ivory/statue object inside.
- Passed the party the key phrase: **The Long Night**.
- Supported by Brinecross contacts Bairwin and Jenkins (minor, not separately documented).

## Directly Documented Facts

- Silent Vanguard Session 01: Master Thorne sends the party to contact H'arald / Harold Stonesheep in Brinecross.
- Silent Vanguard Session 02: The party finds Harold alive but sick during the zombie outbreak.
- Silent Vanguard Session 02: Harold has/backpacks an ivory/statue object.
- Silent Vanguard Session 02: Harold gives or confirms the key phrase "The Long Night."
- Silent Vanguard Session 02: The party leaves Brinecross with Harold's status unresolved.

## Strong Inferences

- Harold is an intelligence carrier for the Silent Vanguard assignment.
- His thief/contact role suggests underworld access, but the scope of that access is not documented.
- His sickness may be tied to zombie fish exposure, but the exact cause is unconfirmed.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Harold survives.
- What the ivory/statue object does.
- Whether Harold knows the full Long Night / warehouse operation.

## Role

Field contact and intelligence carrier. His thief background suggests he moves in criminal or underground circles in Brinecross.

## Open Questions

- Is Harold still alive after the party escaped Brinecross?
- What was the ivory/statue object?
- How deeply is he tied to The Long Night or the warehouse operation?
