# Nora

## Summary

Nora is a Brinecross local who was infected and tied up during the zombie outbreak. The party helped her using herbs and vegetables.

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Known Facts

- Found tied up and infected during the Session 02 zombie outbreak.
- The party attempted to help her using the herbs/vegetables connected to Linda.
- Her survival status after the party's escape from Brinecross is not documented.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Silent Vanguard Session 02: Nora is found tied up and infected or possibly infected during the Brinecross outbreak.
- Session 02: The party attempts to help her using herbs/vegetables connected to Linda.
- Session 02: Her status after the party escapes is not recorded.

## Strong Inferences

- Her case may help distinguish infected from fully turned victims if revisited.
