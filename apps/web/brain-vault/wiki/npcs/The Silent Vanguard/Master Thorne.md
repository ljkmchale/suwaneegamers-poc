# Master Thorne

## Summary

Master Thorne (also called Thorin) is the patron and mission handler for the Silent Vanguard party. He operates out of Tratta and assigns covert jobs to the party.

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Known Facts

- Gathers the party in a plain canvas tent near the Lower Dock markets in Tratta.
- Provides cover identity tied to the Wavehammer, an old naval vessel under repair.
- Assigned the party's first mission: travel southwest to Brinecross to contact H'arald / Harold Stonesheep.
- Pays 50 gp per adventurer per job, plus operational funds (200 gp to pay Harold).
- Goes by both Thorne and Thorin across different source references; the variance has not been resolved.
- The party reports back to him after the Brinecross zombie outbreak and he receives their debrief.

## Directly Documented Facts

- Silent Vanguard Session 01: He gathers the party, gives the Brinecross assignment, and sets the Wavehammer as cover.
- Silent Vanguard Session 01: He gives operational money for Harold Stonesheep and defines pay per adventurer.
- Silent Vanguard Session 02: The party returns after Brinecross and reports back.

## Strong Inferences

- Thorne is a covert handler rather than a public patron; this is inferred from the cover identity, tent meeting, and mission framing.
- He may know more about The Long Night than he has told the party, but the notes do not prove that yet.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Thorne or Thorin is the correct canonical spelling.
- What organization he represents beyond the Silent Vanguard assignment structure.
- Whether he is trustworthy, compromised, or simply underinformed.

## Role

Patron and handler. Frames assignments, provides cover, and receives intelligence reports. His broader goals and allegiances are not yet documented.

## Open Questions

- What organization or faction does Thorne represent?
- Why does the source use both Thorne and Thorin?
- What is his interest in The Long Night, the warehouse lead, and the colosseum corruption?
