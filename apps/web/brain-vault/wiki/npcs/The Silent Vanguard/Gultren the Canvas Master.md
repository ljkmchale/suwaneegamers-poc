# Gultren the Canvas Master

## Summary

Gultren the Canvas Master is the named recipient of the secret warehouse letter recovered in Brinecross. He is connected to the trafficking and corruption lead pointing toward the south warehouse and the colosseum.

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Known Facts

- Letter recovered in Brinecross during Session 02 is addressed to him at the Sticky Pear Inn in Tratta.
- The letter references crates, chains, cages, fish, spice, people, guards, and a shipment in 20 nights.
- The party kept the letter rather than delivering it.
- His title suggests a canvas-trade or shipping cover.
- His actual role — receiver, fence, coordinator — is not yet confirmed.

## Directly Documented Facts

## Strong Inferences

- Gultren is connected to the south warehouse / trafficking thread.
- "Canvas Master" may be a trade cover, but that is not proven.
- Gultren may be a recipient, coordinator, or front-facing contact; the exact role is not documented.

## Unconfirmed Or Open

- Whether Gultren is at the Sticky Pear Inn.
- Whether he knows the letter was intercepted.
- Whether he is tied to the colosseum directly or through intermediaries.

## Role

Suspected trafficking coordinator or middleman tied to the south warehouse route and colosseum network.

## Open Questions

- What is his relationship to the Gilded Ledger or Salt and Cinder Press?
- Is he at the Sticky Pear Inn or has he moved?
