# Diverra Faith And Bloom Aeterna

## Summary

Diverra is the deity central to Aurelius's faith and the HoE campaign's religious backbone. The Bloom Aeterna is the original complete doctrinal text of the Diverra faith, recovered by Aurelius at the Lost Sanctuary.

## Campaign Scope

HoE. Do not merge with SoD or Silent Vanguard religious structures.

## The Faith

- Diverra is associated with sacred fires, prophecy, divine magic, and cycles of growth and renewal.
- The active Emberstran temple is led by Priestess Meladra, who represents the established (possibly incomplete) form of the faith.
- Hap received a divine vision that drew him and Aurelius together early in the campaign.

## Bloom Aeterna

- The complete original text of Diverra's teachings.
- Recovered by Aurelius at the Lost Sanctuary during Session 19.
- Contains the **Shattered Hearts** doctrine: a teaching about sacred hearts, their proper placement, and what happens when they are removed or corrupted.
- Aurelius withholds some of the Bloom Aeterna's contents from Priestess Meladra, either to protect her or because the revelations are too significant to share yet.
- The petrified rose petals Aurelius carries enable ritual return to the Lost Sanctuary.

## Shattered Hearts Doctrine

- Concerns hearts that have been shattered, removed, or defiled.
- Active connection to the HoE campaign: Hap's Phira thread, Lady Rook's Marrowstone, the Blackrend blight, and possibly the vault all touch on hearts, corruption, and sacred restoration.

## Lost Sanctuary And Bloomrest

- The Lost Sanctuary is the original Diverra sacred site, separate from Meladra's active temple.
- Bloomrest is tied to the Sanctuary and the Life Tree.
- Og was called there as E'erspan (a sacred guardian role).

## Relationship To Blackrend

- The Blackrend is a blight that corrupts sacred groves, Life Trees, and Diverra-linked locations.
- Clearing the Blackrend from Diverra's Grove in Session 06 was one of the early turning points.

## Open Questions

- Will Aurelius share the Bloom Aeterna with Meladra, and how will she react?
- What does the Shattered Hearts doctrine say to do about the defiled hearts in the HoE world?
- What is Dandelia of the Veil's role in the Og / E'erspan / Life Tree thread?
