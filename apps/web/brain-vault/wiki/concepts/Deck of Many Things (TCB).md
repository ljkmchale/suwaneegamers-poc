# Deck of Many Things (The Box)

A magical card box created by **Fizban / Tsuul'physs Bhan**. The party has been carrying it since early in the campaign. In session 5.4, Yvaine determined it "was made by Fizban" and is "at least a couple of hundred years old."

The box pulls people to it unpredictably (in 5.5 it yanked itself out of Sigil's backpack in Thal'astyr's meditation room).

## Cards Drawn

| Who | Card | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Tass | The Mist | Disappeared for multiple sessions; returned semi-transparent/ghost-like |
| Sigil | The Exercise Card | Dropped in the desert; had to run back to party |
| Sigil | The Mist (5.5) | Temporarily disappeared — allowed Tass to appear and speak |
| Ren | The Chains | Encased in chains; broke free with Strength |
| Ren | The Serpent's Bite | Poisoned for 1 hour (Scarlytt cast Lesser Restoration) |
| Ren | The Beast Howl | Giant rat appeared; Ren killed it |
| Narris | The Serpent's Bite | Con save passed; no effect |
| Narris | The Echo | Commanding presence; can cast Command (level 1) once in 24 hours |
| Scarlytt (sess. 5.5) | Unknown | In Thal'astyr; Sigil was currently inside the box |

## Known Properties of The Mist Card

When Tass disappeared into the Mist, the semi-transparent form he returned with in 5.5 could speak and perceive. He could draw the box magnetically, seemed to know things ("he will return when most needed"), and could send messages. However, he was still mortal — he died fully in session 6.1.
