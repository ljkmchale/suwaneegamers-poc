# Cult of the Immured Ritual Mechanics

## Summary

The Cult of the Immured is the SoD campaign's primary antagonist organization. Their rituals center on heart petrification, soul-tethering, fiendish servitude, and the eventual freeing of bound entities. Understanding their mechanics is essential for following the SoD threat threads.

## Campaign Scope

SoD. Do not merge with HoE heart/shattered-hearts theology.

## Core Mechanics

### Heart Petrification
- Victims' hearts are removed and petrified using **defiled black salt**.
- The black salt was delivered via Caldwynn shipping paperwork to the tannery in Adsuren, using Ganon as an intermediary.
- The Ganon letter confirms: the black salt is stored under Adsuren Center (A.C.) and is used to dry and petrify hearts.
- A petrified heart retains a soul tether and fiendish quality; Mollywop confirmed the black heart is supernatural.

### Familiars And Sentient Hearts
- The Ganon letter also orders familiars (imps) into town and demands a "sentient heart" — suggesting not all targets are regular humans.
- The imp killed Heckudor during the party's cover plan, consistent with familiars operating independently.

### The Immured
- The term "Immured" means something has been walled in or imprisoned.
- The ritual's goal is **freeing** a bound entity — Aeolenne was the entity freed in Session 08.
- Eyovar / Summoner Karranok was the ritual leader; he confessed that cultists came from Caldwynn, Gibuldon, Morrey, and elsewhere, indicating a wider network.

### Aeolenne's Release
- Aeolenne is a fiendish/succubus-like entity tied to Zephyra's heritage.
- She was freed from the Adsuren Center ritual chamber during Session 08 when the ritual circle was destroyed.
- She escaped south toward Caldwynn with an invisibility potion, suggesting an ally waiting there.

## Wider Cult Network

- Eyovar's confession names Caldwynn, Gibuldon, and Morrey as cult-origin cities.
- The Caldwynn barrel/spiral watermark thread suggests the network has shipping infrastructure.
- The hearts themselves (black heart, gertot head, Durgruck's removed heart) may represent separate victims or fuel sources for different rituals.

## Relationship To Zephyra

- Aeolenne presents herself as a mother figure to Zephyra, exploiting her fiendish heritage.
- Zephyra broke Aeolenne's possession control during Session 08 by recalling a childhood bully (Jimmy Meanpants), which snapped her out of the trance.
- Zephyra needs warding or protection against future Aeolenne-style mind control.
