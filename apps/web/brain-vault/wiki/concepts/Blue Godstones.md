# Blue Godstones

**Type:** Magical artifact / power source  
**Status:** In use — powering the portal in the [[Lost City (Athralis)]]

## Overview

Blue godstones are large magical crystals with divine energy. They appear as enormous blue stones and are used to power portals or divine structures. Their precise origin is unknown, but their energy is connected to celestial or divine forces.

## Known Instances

### The Lost City Portal

In the [[Lost City (Athralis)]], the party discovered the portal where [[Aurelion]] is chained. On either side of the portal stand **two huge blue godstones that are rotating and cracked**, providing energy to the cracked portal. Their degraded state reflects the portal's instability.

### ALM / Belzander Connection

[[Arbescar Light and Magic]] and [[Belzander Umbridge]] have shown interest in blue godstones, suggesting they have broader applications in magical infrastructure beyond the portal.

## Properties

- Provide sustained energy to magical structures (portals, gates).
- Appear to be of divine or celestial origin.
- Can crack and degrade over time, destabilizing the structures they power.
- Large in scale — described as "huge" in the Lost City portal chamber.
