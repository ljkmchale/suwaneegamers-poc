# Emberstran EGD And Civic Authority

## Summary

The Emberstran EGD (Emberstran Guard and Dominion, or similar) is the civic authority structure of Emberstran. The party operates within and around it using badges, scrolls, and faction relationships.

## Campaign Scope

HoE only.

## The EGD

- Led nominally by Lord Garrod Dusklight, who oversees Emberstran. Earlier source notes also spell this as Garris Dusklite.
- Represented in enforcement by Captain Vammad.
- The party received official Badges of Emberstran EGD through the Diverra temple in Session 21, blessed by Aurelius. This gives them legitimate civic standing in Emberstran.
- The Black Quill / Selvara Nendris previously gave Ainslie and Og an official EGD scroll through Etterset channels before the badges — suggesting the EGD's legitimacy can be manufactured or co-opted.

## Civic Factions And Their Relationships

| Faction | EGD Stance |
|---|---|
| Vintner's Guild / Lady Tarathial | Operates within civic structure; hired the party for legitimate record work. |
| Lady Rook Network | Courts civic legitimacy but works outside and against it; Ky'tha endorsed Rook at the Lord's Keep. |
| Cloak of Defiance | Subverts civic structure; uses assassins (Whisper), forged records, and political manipulation. |
| Etterset / Black Quill | Underground; supplies forged EGD identity documents. |
| Diverra Temple / Meladra | Religious authority adjacent to civic structure; the blessed badges tie faith to official standing. |

## Fake Inspector Elise / Whisper

- The Cloak used a fake "Inspector Elise" identity to manipulate the party in early sessions.
- This figure was later revealed to be Whisper, a Cloak assassin killed in Session 11.
- The fake inspector's neck mark (two horizontal lines with two vertical marks, like an upside-down T) was a partial identification clue.
- The Black Quill signature on the official EGD scroll creates ambiguity: does the party's legitimacy come from the Cloak, the Etterset, or genuinely the EGD?

## Igabell Orebane

- Dwarf magistrate in Emberstran's civic structure.
- Role and alignment with parties not yet fully documented.

## Open Questions

- Does Lord Garrod Dusklight know or approve of the party's EGD standing?
- Is the EGD itself corrupt, or is corruption coming entirely from external faction pressure?
