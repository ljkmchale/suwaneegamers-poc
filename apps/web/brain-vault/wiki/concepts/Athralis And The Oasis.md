# Athralis and the Oasis

*Sourced from "Whispers of the Sands" by T'suul'physs Bhan*

Athralis is an ancient city in the Blustery Waste and the current location of the party (as of Session 7.3). Its founding and its secret are recorded in the book "Whispers of the Sands," which the party obtained from the celestial figure T'suul'physs Bhan.

---

## Chapter 1: The Founding

Explorers crossing the Blustery Waste discovered an oasis unlike any they had seen before — iridescent waters in a canyon, impossibly alive in a dying desert. They took this as a divine sign and founded a city around it. The city, built from the canyon's sandstone, was named **Athralis**, a testament to their faith that the oasis had been guided into existence by divine will.

---

## Chapter 2: Flourishing and the Secret

Athralis grew into a great city: lush gardens, grand architecture, artisans, and scholars. It was a center of culture and learning. But beneath the prosperity, a secret festered. A hidden discovery was made in the oasis — an **ancient portal bridging the mortal realm and the domain of gods**. A select few kept this truth buried, understanding that its revelation would reshape everything. This discovery, they believed, heralded a new epoch.

---

## Present Day Significance

The ancient portal is now the centerpiece of the crisis in Session 7.3. [[Malik]] and the [[A'urea'Pura Cult]] have taken control of it. The angel [[Aurelion]] is chained within the portal, which is now powered by two cracked blue godstones and in an unstable state. The party is inside Athralis attempting to free Aurelion and stop Malik.

The city is described as the "Lost City" — six days' travel from Everlight according to the ancient druid encountered in Sessions 6.5 and 7. The party arrived on the 33rd of Hemist.

---

## Key Details

- **Location:** Blustery Waste, in a canyon
- **Founded by:** Explorers drawn to an iridescent oasis
- **Architecture:** Canyon sandstone
- **Secret:** An ancient portal between the mortal realm and the domain of gods
- **Current state:** Ruins; occupied by A'urea'Pura cultists and controlled by Malik
- **In-campaign name also used:** "The Lost City"
