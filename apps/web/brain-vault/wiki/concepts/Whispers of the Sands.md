# Whispers of the Sands

**Author:** [[Fizban]] (Tsuul'physs Bhan)  
**Type:** In-world written work  
**Subject:** The history and founding of [[Athralis And The Oasis|Athralis]]

## Overview

*Whispers of the Sands* is a book written by [[Fizban]] (true name: Tsuul'physs Bhan) that recounts the founding and rise of Athralis, the ancient city that now lies as the Lost City at the heart of the Blustery Waste. The party found the book in [[Fizban's Tower]], and [[Narris]] has been studying it.

## Summary of Contents

### Chapter 1 — The Finding

In the heart of the Blustery Waste, a band of explorers discovered a hidden oasis within a secretive canyon. The waters shimmered with an ethereal, iridescent glow. Taking it as a divine sign, they founded Athralis — a city built into the canyon walls from its own sandstone. The city's founding was an act of faith: they believed the oasis held "a whisper of fate."

### Chapter 2 — The Flourishing

Athralis grew into a magnificent city — lush gardens, grand architecture, artisans, scholars, and cultural exchange. It became a crossroads of knowledge and art. But beneath its prosperity lay a hidden secret: a portal in the heart of the oasis that connected the mortal realm to the divine domain of gods. Only a select few knew of it. The city's ambitions began to exceed the mortal sphere.

## Significance

- [[Fizban]] (Tsuul'physs Bhan) is one of the ancient figures connected to Athralis's history.
- The portal referenced in Chapter 2 is likely the same portal now sealed in the [[Lost City (Athralis)]] where [[Aurelion]] is chained.
- This book is the party's primary in-world source of Athralis's history.
- [[Narris]] is studying it for clues about the Blustery Wastes and the time distortions in [[Everlight]].
