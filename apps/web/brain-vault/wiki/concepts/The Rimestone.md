# The Rimestone

## Scope

Wyrm Bane campaign. World lore for Myrdae — the Yag'vur Sierra region.

## Summary

The Rimestone is a magical artifact originally created by dwarven mages to freeze an ancient undead aberration that was accidentally awakened beneath the Yag'vur Sierra. When the mages contained it, they broke the Rimestone into three pieces and scattered them through the mountains to prevent any single faction from using it as a weapon.

## Origin

Hundreds of years ago, the Yag'vur Sierra were rolling fields — not the mountains they are today. Both dwarves and giants built great cities there. The dwarves began using dark magic to dig deeper and deeper beneath the surface. They eventually stumbled upon a massive **undead corpse** — an aberration of unknown origin. Their dark magic awakened it. The awakened aberration began destroying dwarven civilization. Dwarven mages created the Rimestone to freeze it, then broke it apart and spread the pieces.

The Awakening (what Myrdae calls the event when the Old Gods were believed to have departed) further destabilized the region — pushing up the mountain ranges and displacing the dwarven kingdoms.

## The Coldbreath Threat

Master **Huldi**, leader of the Monks of Iohir (the Coldbreath cult), seeks to assemble all three Rimestone pieces. The cult believes this would freeze the world — ostensibly to re-seal the eldritch aberration, but at the cost of enormous death and destruction.

Harhet Steelfoot (Advent of Harmony, a monk faction) revealed that the original sealing required dwarven cooperation and did not require the Rimestone to be assembled. The Coldbreath faction's motives and exact plan remain partially unclear.

## The Eldritch Aberration

The entity sealed by the Rimestone is still present under the mountains. Its growing influence is causing:
- **Duergar nightmares** in Khagdaruhl.
- Albross's patron (The Holy Light) showed him a vision of the Dwarven King's throne wrapped in tendrils.
- Crop-freezing cold affects Rhody's farm and the Runegrip clan's fields — possibly a symptom of the aberration's re-awakening.
