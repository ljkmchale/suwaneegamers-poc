# Mirror Void

**Associated Characters:** [[Narris]], [[Sigil (TCB)]]  
**Connected to:** [[Morath]], [[Iuz'Obal]], "thread of shadow"  
**Source:** Fizban's vision; Session 6.5

## Overview

The "mirror void" is the name given to a dangerous planar space — a space between worlds — that [[Narris]] is destined to enter as part of his arc following the "thread of shadow." [[Fizban]] foresaw the danger: in a vision, he saw Narris dying, lost in the space between worlds, consumed by the mirror void.

Fizban responded to this vision by designating [[Sigil (TCB)]] as Narris's **anchor** — the person who must remain in the mortal world to keep Narris tethered and prevent him from being lost.

## Fizban's Vision

In Session 6.5, Fizban experienced a flash-vision:
> *He sees Narris dying, lost in space between worlds, consumed by the "mirror void." Another flash and he sees Narris… and Narris is holding a candle and Sigil is next to him. He hears: "You don't need the sword to begin… you are the scabbard for it whether it finds you or not."*

This vision established two things:
1. Narris will enter the mirror void — and will die there without his anchor.
2. Sigil is the scabbard — the anchor — whether or not the Sunblade Hilt (his mother's sword) has been found.

## The Anchor Relationship

- [[Sigil (TCB)]] is Narris's designated anchor for mirror void travel.
- [[Morath]], servant of [[Iuz'Obal]], has been guiding Narris toward the "thread of shadow" — the path that leads to the mirror void.
- Fizban also guides Narris through this arc, suggesting both light and shadow forces have an interest in Narris's planar journey.

## Open Questions

- What is the mirror void exactly — a demiplane, a shadow realm, the space between planes?
- What does Narris need to find or do within the mirror void?
- Is Iuz'Obal's interest in Sigil (via Morath's surveillance) connected to Sigil's role as Narris's anchor?
- What does "when the two are one, the veil will part" mean in context of Narris and Sigil?
