# Pantheon of Myrdae

## Scope

World lore — applies to all campaigns set in Myrdae.

## Summary

The gods of Myrdae are divided into two groups: the **New Order**, gods who rose to prominence after the Awakening when the eight greater deities were believed to have abandoned the world, and the **Old Gods** (the Assembly of Essence), who once oversaw prime domains and no longer answer prayers.

## Deity Entries

### Addan, the Eternal Guardian
God of order and protection. Safeguards the vulnerable and opposes harm, neglect, and chaos. Depicted as a towering figure in silvery plate armor with a tower shield and royal blue accents. **Rite:** The Silver Shield, first day of Briscarn (Paramor 9th) — martial tournaments, honoring town guards, silver lanterns lit at dusk. **Commandments:** Confront fear with courage; plan carefully; foster protection.

### Amriel, the Redeemer
God of redemption, resilience, and divine justice. Once a celestial angel who was deceived into betraying the goddess of magic Lyess; earned divinity through relentless penance against Di'Belgi's demonic forces in the Underdark. Depicted as a radiant figure in gleaming white mail with golden highlights, wielding a morning star. **Rite:** The Dawn of Redemption — performed monthly at sunrise, facing east, concluding with washing hands in blessed water. **Commandment:** Protect the innocent, confront darkness, guide the wayward back to the light.

*Myth note:* Amriel's betrayal involved the theft of the [[Crown of Lyess]], the scattering of its eight orbs, and the Blood Wars on Myrdae. His redemptive descent led to Runeweaving with Yibrix and the [[Affirmation of Strife]].

### Asmodeus, the Prince of Darkness
God of tyranny, indulgence, and fire. Infernal overlord of the Nine Hells. Depicted as a tall, handsome horned devil with crimson skin, piercing eyes, black hair, and a scepter tipped with a fiery ruby. **No universal rite.** **Commandments:** Obey without question; crush the weak; seek power relentlessly; punish defiance; indulge in desires.

### Brault, He Born Asunder
God of battle and might. Embodies raw power, unyielding force, and the will to dominate — including strength of will, not just physical might. Depicted as a battle-worn warrior with deep scars, unkempt hair, and a massive blood-stained weapon. **Rites:** Pre-battle Invocation of Might (fists to chest, chant); Post-battle Rite of Reckoning (breaking a fallen foe's weapon). **Commandment:** Victory belongs to the strong — seize it through strength, will, and relentless determination.

*Myth note:* The Layeth and Brault tale says Brault and Layeth were twins born to Tiash and Smott, separated by divine agreement and kept ignorant of their shared parentage.

### Celestine, the Night Whisperer
Goddess of dreams, stars, reflection, and prophecy. Does not speak in words but in echoes — music, wind, the tremor of conscience before dawn. Dreams can be mercy or revelation; she reveals, she does not judge. Depicted as a woman woven of pale light and darkness, hair like rivers of stars, constellation the Harp of the Silver Thread. **Tenets:** Seek the voice within; sleep is the loom on which the soul repairs itself; every dream holds truth.

### Cembus, the Seeker
God of exploration and travel. Encourages curiosity and journeying beyond familiar borders. Depicted as a tall, slender firbolg in neutral-colored attire and a brown hooded cloak, carrying a large satchel and the staff "Pathkeeper." **Rite:** No formal ceremony — a common morning prayer before journeys: *"Seeker of the Unknown, guide my steps on path untrod."* **Commandments:** Embrace curiosity; respect your surroundings.

### Coralei, the Tide Keeper
Goddess of the oceans and seas; represents the balance of life in vast waters and the serenity of watching the horizon. Depicted as a breathtaking sea-elf with light blue shimmering skin, aqua hair, and pearl-like eyes, wielding a glowing blue-steel trident. **Rite:** Tideform — once per season at a coastal location at sunset, with a communal bonfire, songs, and stories of the ocean. **Commandment:** Live with the strength, power, and beauty of the waters.

### Crael, the Soulbearer
God of fate and death. Guides souls to the afterlife; opposes manipulation of destiny for selfish gain. Depicted as an elderly man in a simple gray hooded robe, carrying a shepherd's staff, a raven often nearby. **Rite:** A solemn rite of passage into the afterlife, wrapping the body in a shroud with a symbolic personal item. **Commandments:** Embrace the path of fate; honor the cycle of life and death; seek balance in choices.

### Diverra, the Ardent One
Goddess of love, beauty, passion, and pleasure. Inspires followers to seek joy, embrace desire, and find beauty in all aspects of life. Depicted as a stunningly beautiful woman with flowing red hair, emerald eyes, and elegant crimson and gold robes with floral patterns. **Rite:** The Day of Radiant Hearts — celebrated in early Natali, exchanging tokens of affection, floating Radiance Candles on water. **Commandments:** Embrace love in all its forms; seek beauty in the world and share it. *Campaign note: the Diverra Faith and Bloom Aeterna are active in Emberstran — see [[Diverra Faith And Bloom Aeterna]].*

*Myth note:* The common ascension tale says Diverra was a beloved Bathaen sorceress who died confronting the demon Zarak'Nor at [[Ulgreer]], then ascended as the Ardent One.

### Eredra, the Spider Queen
Goddess of betrayal, deception, and cunning; matriarch goddess of the drow. Deception is an art form; followers seek to outmaneuver rivals and weave plots. Depicted as an alluring dark-skinned elf with silver hair and black robes of intricate silver web patterns — or as a massive drider when projecting dominance. **No standard rite** — many sacrificial rites exist, none universally observed. **Commandments:** Follow the spider queen without falter; wait for the right time; your intentions are yours alone.

### Fralee, the Sister of Solace
Goddess of purity and tranquility. Opposes imposed stress and tension. Depicted as a serene Eladrin elf with fuchsia-streaked lavender hair, pale blue eyes, soft white and light purple robes, and a gentle pink aura. **Rite:** The Day of Gentle Waters — gather near natural water, release floating lanterns carrying worries and burdens. **Commandments:** Embrace tranquility in all things; release the burdens that weigh upon your heart.

### Goldraen, the Evercast
God of craft and creation. Champions creativity, innovation, and artistry. Depicted as a stout, powerful dwarf with bronze braided hair and beard, dark green leathers with copper accents, a leather apron, and a large worn hammer. **Rite:** The First Forge — dawn of Evalee the 9th, lighting a communal forge, crafting items, blessing them, offering a portion back to the forge. **Commandments:** Create with purpose; honor the tools of creation; innovation over stagnation.

*Myth note:* Goldraen was the master smith chosen by Muerg to shape the first dwarves from living stone; the completion of that divine labor elevated him as the Evercast.

### Iuz'Obal, the Eyes Beyond
God of the ether, Shadowfell, and extraplanar travel. Sought by those pursuing wisdom and power from beyond Myrdae; oversees the vastness of space and distant realms. Depicted as a vast shadowy figure with countless glowing eyes, tentacles flowing from his head like mist, an amorphous ever-shifting form. **Rite:** The Ceremony of the Veiled Passage, on Nocturn — robes of black and silver, deep rhythmic chanting invoking truths beyond Myrdae. **Commandment:** Seek the shadows beyond the light, for true understanding lies in the realms unseen.

### Layeth, the Selfless One
Goddess of sacrifice and perseverance. Selflessness as a guiding principle for a compassionate world; every act of sacrifice contributes to a greater good. Depicted as a middle-aged woman in light blue with white accents, exuding empathy. **Rite:** A Moment of Selfless Offering at the end of every month at dusk — laying down a meaningful offering and speaking a vow of selflessness. **Commandments:** Give without hesitation; endure hardships with grace; honor the sacrifices of others with gratitude.

*Myth note:* Layeth's sacrifice domain is tied to Tiash's decision to separate her from Brault and hide the truth of their parentage.

### Muerg, the Earth Father
God of land and earth. Primal essence of the land — stability and resilience in peace, overwhelming force in conflict. Communicates through vibrations in the ground rather than spoken words. Depicted as a powerful hairless man with dark umber skin textured like weathered stone, eyes like glowing ore veins. **Rite:** The Rite of the Unyielding Stone — at dawn, kneeling at a rock formation, hands on earth, scattering soil at the close. **Commandments:** Show unwavering resolve; embrace necessary change; respect the land; act with the full force of the earth when the time comes. *Campaign note: Ky'tha's Dunduar vision involved a stone ring and her locket glowing near a Muerg-related mine shaft.*

*Myth note:* Muerg sensed the young world's unstable bones and gave Goldraen primordial earth essence to forge the first dwarves as anchors for Myrdae.

### Myrdris, the Grand Weaver
God of knowledge and magic. Embodies the pursuit of wisdom and the unraveling of secrets. Followers see themselves as custodians of both wisdom and magical responsibility. Depicted as a tall, slender man with flowing blonde hair and a lavender robe trimmed with shimmering silver. **Rite:** The Weaving of Threads — performed at each season's turn at twilight, weaving a tapestry together that represents the community's collective wisdom. **Commandment:** Seek knowledge relentlessly, for in understanding the world, we weave the fabric of our destinies.

### Natafae, the Mistress of Pain
Goddess of endurance, pain, and suffering. Pain is not to be feared but embraced as a transformative force; suffering is a necessary path to resilience and enlightenment. Some followers invoke pain upon others, believing they are offering guidance. Depicted as a tall, thin woman with long dark red hair streaked with black, black robes, pale skin, and a stoic expression. **Rite:** The Trial of Pain — during Sultra, fasting and ritual pain and suffering performed by trained clergy. **Commandments:** Embrace suffering as growth; find strength in struggle.

### Nigrum, the Netherlord
God of the undead. More than wickedness — the mysteries and complexities of defying mortality's end; the undead as a blend of life's remnants and death's truth. Depicted as a formidable warrior in blackened armor enveloped in dark mist, crowned helm with jagged spikes, wielding a great sword etched with glowing runes. **Rite:** The Eve of Nether Rising — at midnight between Evalee and Talil, gathering in crypts or ruins, cold blue or violet flames, meditating on the threshold between life and death. **Commandment:** Embrace the mysteries of life and death; seek knowledge beyond mortality's grasp.

### Ol'Farium, Crowned Glory
Dragon deity of good and honor. Champions fairness and valor tempered by ancient wisdom. The only platinum dragon in existence. Depicted as a towering platinum-scaled dragon with white eyes radiating wisdom, perched on a high peak watching the world. **Rite:** The Oath of the Platinum Sky — once per century at high noon on the longest day, all metallic dragons take to the skies simultaneously across the world. **Commandments:** Align to principles of good and honor (varies by follower).

### Osanna, the Smiling Duchess
Goddess of luck and fortune. Embodies hope, serendipity, and the gentle guidance of fate toward prosperity. Favored by sailors, traders, and those seeking a change in fortune. Depicted as a radiant woman with golden blonde hair, bright blue eyes, and flowing white and gold robes. **Rite:** The Festival of Fair Winds — annual coastal festival marking the start of the trading season; largest celebration in Oldport. **Commandments:** Share your fortune, for luck is best when passed on; embrace risk with a smile, for fortune favors the bold.

*Myth note:* Oldport tradition says Osanna once appeared there in mortal-seeming form, taught sailors to read the Tideless Span, and inspired the Festival of Fair Winds.

### Phoe, the Hooded Lady
Goddess of freedom, secrecy, and night. Darkness is not dishonor but privacy, defense, and hidden wisdom. Favored by spies, thieves, and those who prize anonymity. Depicted as a graceful woman in a deep violet hooded cloak, face concealed, sleek violet leather. **Rite:** The Unseen Service on Nocturn — followers gather in secret locations and undertake personal missions aligned with Phoe's commandments, never spoken of afterward. **Commandments:** Trust in the night; act in shadows; guard secrecy; let silence carry more weight than words.

### Sylunara, the Evening Radiance
Goddess of the moons and transformation. Guides the tides of time and the metamorphosis of all things under celestial light; constancy and change simultaneously. Revered by mystics, poets, and travelers. Depicted as a celestial figure in a flowing dark robe that transforms into the hues of Myrdae's moons — scarlet and light blue — with hair as ethereal starlight. **Rite:** The Moonlit Renewal — performed under the full moons or during a new moon. **Commandments:** Embrace transformation; illuminate the path for others.

### Tornia, the Deceiver
Goddess of chaos, trickery, and disruption. Sees rigid order as stifling; believes chaos is the essence of life, sparking change and creativity. Depicted as a strikingly beautiful woman with hair shifting between fiery orange and deep black, flowing in a storm-like tattered cloak, carrying a slender curved dagger. **Rite:** Twilight ceremony of masks and pranks — no formal name. **Commandments:** None universally observed.

### Tyvarion, the Master of Masks
God of art, music, poetry, comedy, song, eloquence, and inspiration. His divine spark ignites when followers create or perform. Depicted as a lean, agile figure in elegant shifting-colored clothing with wavy brown-sandy hair, holding a theatrical mask and a lyre or quill. **No dedicated rites** — celebrated through every performance given and witnessed. **Commandments:** Honor eloquence — speak with grace and intention; seek inspiration — let creativity flow as a gift to others. *Campaign note: Kenton (SoD) is a devotee of Tyvarion.*

### Urlich, the Regent of Ice
Goddess of cold, ice, and winter (Briscarn). Cold is purity — it strips away weakness and covers the world in serene stillness. Very few followers. Depicted as a striking ice-blue elven woman with frost-pale skin, snow-white hair, and frost-colored crystalline armor. **Rite:** The Frostfall Mass on the first day of Briscarn — general Myrdaens offer prayers for mercy from the cold; Urlich's true followers perform the private Chillfast Ritual, praying for a harsh, unrelenting winter. **Commandment:** Embrace the cold and wield its power, for in Briscarn's fury lies true strength.

### Utheri, the Truth Bringer
God of justice, moral order, and fair judgment. Deeply opposed to deception and manipulation of laws for personal gain. Depicted as a powerful chiseled figure with flowing gray hair, a full beard, golden plate mail, and radiant golden scales in hand. **Rite:** The Rite of Justice — held under high sun in town streets, participants share stories of justice and call out instances of unfairness. **Commandments:** Embrace truth in all matters; champion fairness and defend the oppressed.

### Villari, the Dawn Princess
Goddess of life and nature. Former dryad; deep connection to forests, wildlife, and ecosystems of Oberra. Opposed to any force that disrupts the natural cycle. Depicted as a tall ethereal dryad with skin glowing with dawn's radiance, leaf-like hair that shifts with the seasons, vine and flower patterns across her body. **Rites:** Spring's Awakening (Natali 1st — planting seeds, song and dance); Rite of the Blooming (full moon each spring month — floral offerings); Rite of the Dawn (summer solstice dawn — bonfires, pledges to protect nature). **Commandments:** Cherish life; honor the balance of life and death; revere the cycles of nature.

### Vo'egurn, the High Tempest
God of storms and destruction. Raw destructive power — his wreckage is not the cycle of life but a consequence; chaos to be harnessed. Depicted as a towering hairless goliath with grayish-blue skin and lightning-bolt markings that illuminate when he is angered. **Rite:** The Stormborn Sacrifice — venturing into an open area during a violent storm to offer tributes, shouting praises to Vo'egurn. **Commandments:** Strike down opposition with unrelenting force; embrace fury; stand unafraid in the face of destruction; challenge those who resist your will.

## The Old Gods — Assembly of Essence

The eight greater deities who once oversaw prime domains. After the Awakening, they were believed to have abandoned Myrdae and no longer answer prayers. They are referenced in lore but not actively worshipped.

| Name | Domain | Symbol |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Athuel | Knowledge | An all-seeing eye |
| Bellum | Life | A blossoming tree |
| Disgar | Death | A raven's skull |
| Lyess | Magic | A magic orb |
| Neera | Order | A winged hammer |
| Smott | Destruction | A monstrous talon |
| Tiash | Balance | Overlapping moons |
| Torec | Elements | The merging elements |

*Lore note: Amriel's origin involves stealing the crown of Lyess, the old goddess of magic, after being deceived by the demoness Di'Belgi.*
