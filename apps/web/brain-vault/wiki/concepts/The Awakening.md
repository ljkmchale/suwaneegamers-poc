# The Awakening

**Declared by:** [[Muerg]] (Old God)  
**Location:** [[Everlight]]  
**Session:** 6.3  
**Witnesses:** The party; townspeople of Everlight

## Overview

The Awakening is the name given to a cosmological shift declared by [[Muerg]], an Old God, during his appearance at [[Everlight]] in Session 6.3. Muerg and his daughter [[Ashe]] appeared in divine form before the townspeople and the party, performing a miraculous act — splitting a new mountain pass through the rock — and issuing a declaration about the changing of the world.

## Muerg's Declaration

Muerg's exact words:

> *"The old gods silent and spent have begun to retreat… a new order arises and I will not allow this world to fall into ruin without guidance."*
> *"Prepare yourselves… the changing of the world begins. Not in thunder, but in Awakening."*

## What Happened

- [[Ashe]] channeled divine flame into the main gates of Everlight, guided by Muerg, cleaving a new road through the mountainside and creating a new mountain pass in minutes.
- The townspeople watched in awe.
- Ashe turned to [[Yvaine]] and delivered [[Yvaine's +2 Studded Leather]] as a gift from [[Layeth the Selfless One]].
- Muerg and Ashe then ascended into the sky.

## Significance

- The 25-year time warp that had isolated [[Everlight]] was lifted around this event.
- Muerg declared Everlight under his protection as part of "this time."
- The declaration implies the Old Gods are weakening and a new divine order is rising.
- [[Yvaine]]'s warlock transformation into a Celestial warlock (choosing Layeth the Selfless One) is directly connected to this shift.
- [[Narris]] studied the Everlight time-distortion arch around the same period, connecting the Awakening to the broader cosmological mystery he is pursuing.

## Open Questions

- What caused the old gods to "retreat"? Is this a natural cycle or a specific event?
- What is the "new order" Muerg references?
- How does the Awakening relate to [[Iuz'Obal]], [[Morath]], and the "mirror void"?
- Is the A'urea'Pura Cult's goal related to the Awakening — are they trying to exploit the power shift?
