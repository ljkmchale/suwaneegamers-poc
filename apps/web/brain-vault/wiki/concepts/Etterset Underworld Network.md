# Etterset Underworld Network

## Summary

The Etterset (also called "Our Exquisites") is an underworld network operating in and around Emberstran. It is Ky'tha's primary external connection and provides intelligence, identity support, and political leverage against the Cloak of Defiance.

## Campaign Scope

HoE only.

## Structure

- A tiered internal rank system; members carry marked coins as identity proof.
- Rank-one entry coin: two curved daggers, given to the party through Emlee Tryndel in Session 03.
- Rank-two coin: a deeper internal marker. Ky'tha receives rank-two standing from Belavie Lightstrider in Session 12.
- The coins are "serious internal markers, not common street money."

## Key Figures

- **Tessa**: Ky'tha's first known Etterset contact, met at Charum's Oils. She explains the significance of the rank-one coin.
- **Belavie Lightstrider**: Grants Ky'tha rank-two standing. Relationship and role within the Etterset not fully documented.
- **Black Quill / Selvara Nendris**: Recruits Ainslie and Og through official EGD status and as an anti-Rook partner. Uses the Etterset as one possible backing structure. The connection between Black Quill and the core Etterset hierarchy is not fully documented.

## Relationship To The Party

- Ky'tha is the primary Etterset-linked party member.
- The Etterset appears positioned against Lady Rook and the Cloak of Defiance.
- The Black Quill gave Ainslie and Og an official EGD scroll through Etterset channels, legitimizing them within Emberstran's civic structure.

## Leverage And Risk

- The Etterset provides intelligence and political cover that the party cannot get through official channels.
- Rank-two standing gives Ky'tha deeper access but also deeper obligations.
- The relationship with the Black Quill introduces an anti-Rook stance that may conflict with Ky'tha's prior Rook endorsement.

## Open Questions

- What does the Etterset want long-term from the party?
- How does Belavie Lightstrider relate to Tessa and the broader hierarchy?
- Does the Etterset know about the vault or the Emberheart Tablet?
