# A'urea'Pura Cult

The A'urea'Pura Cult is the primary antagonistic organization in [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle]] campaign. The party has been tasked by ALM's Council of Six with stopping them.

---

## Overview

The A'urea'Pura Cult worships and serves the D'jinn King — the entity once imprisoned in the Crystal Bottle. The cult's goal is to harness the ancient portal in the Lost City ([[Athralis And The Oasis|Athralis]]) to connect the mortal realm to the domain of gods, granting their leadership heavenly power.

---

## The Crystal Bottle and the D'jinn

The campaign's name and inciting mystery center on a crystal bottle inscribed in Terran:

> *"Death to whomever frees him. Wishes to whomever puts him back."*

The bottle contained a D'jinn King. The party discovered it in Session 1.1 in an old mine. The D'jinn was subsequently freed (the exact circumstances are not fully detailed in notes), setting events into motion. The party's goal is to put the D'jinn back in the bottle — a dangerous task given the inscription's warning.

---

## Key Figures

### Malik
[[Malik]] is the cult's true leader — an air genasi and demonic entity who now inhabits the body of [[Kal'dar]]. Malik first appeared in Session 1.2, merging with Tassarion Ranges (Tass) and rendering him catatonic. He organized the cult's push toward Athralis and orchestrated Kal'dar's hollowing. As of Session 7.3, he has stepped out of the ancient portal in Athralis and claims to hold heavenly power.

### Kal'dar
[[Kal'dar]] was Fizban's former apprentice. He made a deal with the D'jinn for immortality, but the deal hollowed him out and made him a vessel for Malik. Killing Kal'dar is an active party quest.

### Erick the Alchemist
Erick ran the "Phantom Moon" — a cultist laboratory beneath his alchemist shop in Scarbrook. He was collecting profits tied to the cult's operations and had issued orders connected to the cult's enemies. He was stopped in Session 3.2.

### Gruk
Gruk is a half-orc cultist captain who led the team that captured Sigil (Session 7.2). He operates with blue-skinned humanoid allies.

---

## Cult Operations

- **The Oasis of the Night:** An early base of operations in a desert cave. The party fought cultists here in Sessions 2.2 and 2.3.
- **Phantom Moon (Scarbrook):** Erick's alchemist front — a cultist lab shut down by the party in Sessions 3.1–3.2.
- **Push toward Athralis:** The cult's main objective — reaching the ancient portal in the Lost City to harness divine power.

---

## The Portal in Athralis

The ancient portal bridging the mortal realm and the domain of gods was discovered by Athralis's founders but kept secret. The cult has now taken control of it. Two cracked blue godstones power the portal. The angel [[Aurelion]] is chained within it. Malik intends to use the portal to claim heavenly power.

---

## Party's Active Objectives Against the Cult

- Stop the A'urea'Pura Cult
- Stop cultists from reaching the celestial city (Ren carries the portal stone)
- Put the D'jinn back in the Crystal Bottle
- Free [[Aurelion]] (get key from Malik's neck)
- Kill [[Kal'dar]]
