## Campaign Status At A Glance

### Heroes of Emberstran (HoE) — 25 sessions
The party entered Moganna's vault beneath Mount Emberstran. Ainslie claimed the heart-shaped relic gem and was drained into exhaustion; Irragosa sent the party back to Emberstran's Temple of Diverra for recovery under divine light. The party also recovered a transmutation orb from the crashed flying ship. Lady Rook admitted she had the party followed, Arnol was ambushed while they were away, Phira is approaching from Ulgrey, and Tessa warns of Etterset misfortune in Ulgrey.

**Active fronts**: Soulroot and Starvein, Ainslie/heart gem recovery, Ahn'Tsuel's 50-year cycle, Jerik's threat, Lady Rook's surveillance/leverage, Dandelia of the Veil (Og's next step), Shattered Hearts (Aury's next step), Ulgrey/Phira/Etterset trouble, Zymve's drow-dragonrider ancestry.

### Souls of Destiny (SoD) — 8 sessions
The Cult of the Immured beneath Adsuren has been dismantled. Eyovar Dunajor (Summoner Karranok) is in custody. Aeolenne was freed and fled south toward Caldwynn. The party has departed Adsuren toward the mines (45th of Hemist). Zephyra is now known to Aeolenne.

**Active fronts**: Aeolenne in Caldwynn, mines investigation (Durgruck/Bristleforge), House Reyvennra response to Kenton's letter, Kenton's stolen tax pouch liability, Lila's crystal visions.

### The Silent Vanguard (TSV) — 3 sessions
The party is based in Tratta, operating under the Silent Vanguard via Master Thorne. They escaped a zombie outbreak in Brinecross with the key phrase "The Long Night" and a warehouse letter documenting a trafficking operation. Harold Stonesheep is left behind sick. Session 03 notes are not yet recorded.

**Active fronts**: The Long Night location, south warehouse 20-night shipment, Brinecross zombie outbreak, Harold's survival, Gultren the Canvas Master.

### Dungeons III - 10 sessions
The party began in Hesfal during Salvus, stopped the Boar's Best robbery, captured the K9 Crew, and delivered the prisoners to Scarwatch Hold. At the hold, the party named itself The kNight Watch, received Umlin's mapping job, learned the Radiant Eye cult had defaced the Sanctuary, and followed Isgo/Radiant Eye/Osmanae leads into the crags. Session 10 ends with two tan-robed figures attacking in the crags.

**Active fronts**: Radiant Eye cult, Isgo's abduction, Scorpion contact through the blue sending stone, Osmanae celestial instrument, Nova's Dunduar brooch, Umlin's mapping job.
