Campaign: [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]

## Background

Elf Ranger. Alteya joined the party in Session 5.6. Her husband and child had been missing for 25 years. [[Galtrelon - Personal History And Goals|Galtrelon]] gave her her husband's journal and badge upon joining the group. In Session 6.5, her family was freed from the frozen grove beneath [[Everlight]] through the unsealing ritual. She chose to stay in Everlight to reconnect with her family while the party departed for the [[Lost City (Athralis)|Lost City]] in Session 7.

## Key Moments

- Joined party in Session 5.6
- Received husband's journal and badge from Gal (sess. 6.3)
- Family freed from frozen grove in the Everlight cave unsealing ritual (sess. 6.5)
- Stayed in Everlight as party departed for the Lost City (sess. 7)

## Current Goals

1. Confirm full reunion with her husband and child
2. Support the party's mission against Kal'dar and the cult when able
