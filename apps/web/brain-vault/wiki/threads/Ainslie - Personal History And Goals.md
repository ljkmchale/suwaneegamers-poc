# Ainslie - Personal History And Goals

## Scope

HoE-only personal thread page for Ainslie, played by Sean Poole.

## Source Type

**Session-synthesized source.** Built from HoE entity notes, timeline, character beat matrix, and session summaries. No separate player-authored pre-campaign backstory doc has been found yet.

## Confirmed Personal Thread

- Ainslie is the HoE party's aberrant sorcerer and strongest arcane investigator.
- The Journal of Tsulanan causes pain, reorganizes its chapters, and increasingly points toward Ainslie's bloodline or inherited power.
- Irragosa recognizes Ainslie as tied to Tsulanan's line and asks Ky'tha to watch him.
- Ainslie absorbs/drains the Marrowstone's necrotic power; the exact long-term change is not yet known.
- Ainslie opens the Emberstran vault door when Aurelius cannot, making him central to the vault inheritance thread.

## Resolved And Progressed

**Resolved:**
- All four Emberheart Tablet pieces collected — Ainslie stole the fourth from the Eradic of Ghone. ✓
- The Journal of Tsulanan is in his possession and confirmed connected to his bloodline. ✓
- Vault door opened — he opened it when Aury could not, confirming the bloodline is the key. ✓

**Meaningful Progress:**
- Bloodline confirmed: Irragosa recognizes him as tied to Tsulanan's line, and the vault door responds to him.
- Accepted Black Quill's anti-Rook partnership — a commitment with unknown future cost.
- Marrowstone drained: he absorbed the necrotic energy, which "scrubbed something away." Changed in some way but the effect is not yet visible.

**Complications:**
- Absorbing the Marrowstone may have altered him in ways he cannot yet measure.
- The Black Quill partnership puts him in debt to the Cloak of Defiance.

## Goals And Personal Leads

- Understand his bloodline and what Tsulanan means for him.
- Learn what the Journal of Tsulanan is trying to reveal.
- Discover why Irragosa is interested in him.
- Understand what the Marrowstone did to him.
- Determine why he could open the vault and what responsibility comes with that.

## Open Gaps

- What is Ainslie's full family/bloodline history?
- What did the Marrowstone remove, change, or awaken?
- What is inside the vault, and why does Ainslie's bloodline matter there?
- Who or what is "Mogana," if anything beyond Ainslie's giant persona?
