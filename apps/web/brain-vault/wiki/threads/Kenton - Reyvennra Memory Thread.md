# Kenton - Reyvennra Memory Thread

## Scope

SoD-only thread. This page tracks Kenton's Gibuldon memory, the Reyvennra/Rodolf pursuit, the warehouse-fire accusation, and the related gaps. Do not merge this with Larry/Lawrence McHale's HoE or Silent Vanguard characters.

## Thread Status

**Active / unresolved.** Kenton sent a letter to House Reyvennra through Laudneyr in Session 06. No reply is documented through SoD Session 08.

## Directly Documented Sequence

### Before Adsuren

- Kenton traveled west through larger cities to sell fine clothes and silks.
- He partied with tradesmen in Gibuldon.
- He hitched a ride with Captain Amara Hilei.

### Gibuldon Memory

Kenton's memory returns during the Rodolf ambush in Session 05.

- He remembers being very drunk in a building, castle, or manor-like place in Gibuldon.
- He was partially undressed and believed he was at a party.
- A woman asked him to meet her in a room down the hall.
- The setting included a deep red rug, elegant hall, large window, candles, an armor stand, and a cracked door with light beyond it.
- He took a cape from the armor stand, threw up on the armor/cape, then became a little clearer headed.
- At the cracked door, he heard voices discussing:
  - a shipment of silks
  - a ring
  - a dwarf
  - strength to overthrow
  - becoming the rightful heir
  - needing someone to pass along silks
  - avoiding suspicion
- He heard three voices: one dwarf, one commanding, and one inquisitive/strategic.
- He saw a tan-skinned dwarf sitting at a table, hands bound to the table with shackles or rope, with cuts/blood on face and neck.
- When Kenton made noise at the door, someone reacted as if a guard should have been posted.
- Sir Rodolf Norran then grabbed Kenton and redirected him, saying Kenton had a woman waiting and handing him a bottle of nice wine.

### Session 05 Ambush

- A child later identified by context as Little Jimmy threw mucus-like tar/manure onto Kenton in Adsuren.
- Kenton chased the child and saw the child receive a small bag from a man.
- Sir Rodolf Norran appeared with hired men and recognized Kenton.
- Rodolf is described as a low-on-the-tree noble from Gibuldon whom Kenton had not seen in a long time.
- Rodolf accused Kenton of starting a fire and making away with silks or other goods.
- Rodolf stated the Reyvennra family had their hopes up too high and that he had more to get from Kenton.
- Rodolf paid men to break Kenton's legs.
- Kenton was badly hurt and went into death saves during the fight.
- Rodolf fled after the party arrived.
- A healing potion with the Reyvennra name/mark was left behind.

### Session 05-06 Party Interpretation

- Esylla recognizes the noble family Kenton describes and knows they lost a significant shipment in a terrible fire.
- Kenton says he did not start the warehouse fire and does not remember being at the warehouse.
- Kenton thinks he got the crate of silks from a cart, saying it fell off.
- Kenton has been living off the contents of the crate for harmons.
- Kenton does not remember who gave him the crate.
- Therric thinks the Gibuldon people may want Kenton alive, using the healing potion as evidence.
- Kenton suspects they may be after him because of what he saw/heard at the manor, not only because of the silks.

### Session 06 Letter

- Kenton writes a provocative letter to House Reyvennra.
- Therric encourages him to be kinder and seek information rather than only insult them.
- Laudneyr, an Adsuren Center assistant, agrees to deliver the letter to the Reyvennra house in Gibuldon.
- Laudneyr identifies Reyvennra as Vesci and says they are powerful.
- Esylla pays 3 gp to hasten the trip; Kenton pays 2 gp more.
- Laudneyr expects a stretch or two to return.
- No response is documented through Session 08.

## Strong Inferences

- Rodolf or someone connected to Reyvennra likely wants Kenton alive, because the attackers were paid to break his legs and a Reyvennra-marked healing potion was left after the fight.
- Kenton may be a witness, scapegoat, courier, accidental participant, or liability tied to the silks/fire plot.
- Rodolf's role is ambiguous: he may be acting for House Reyvennra, exploiting their concern, hiding his own agenda, or some mix of those.
- The dwarf, ring, silks, and overthrow language likely point to a noble succession or political conspiracy, but the target and actors are unknown.

## Not Yet Proven

- That House Reyvennra directly sent Rodolf.
- That Rodolf personally started the warehouse fire.
- That Kenton stole the silks knowingly.
- That the bound dwarf was tortured by House Reyvennra, Rodolf, or another named faction.
- That the Gibuldon thread is connected to the Cult of the Immured. Eyovar later says cult members came from Gibuldon, but no direct link to Reyvennra/Rodolf is documented.
- That Lila's "Kenton with a starry sword" vision refers to this exact thread; it remains unresolved.

## Known Gaps To Watch

- Who was the bound dwarf?
- What ring was discussed?
- Who was meant to be overthrown, and who claimed to be the rightful heir?
- Who arranged for someone to "pass along silks"?
- Who gave Kenton the crate or caused it to reach him?
- What exactly burned in the warehouse, and whose shipment was lost?
- What does Rodolf still need from Kenton?
- Will Laudneyr return safely, and what response will House Reyvennra send?
