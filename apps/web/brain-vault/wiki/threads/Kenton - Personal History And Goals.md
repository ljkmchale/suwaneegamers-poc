# Kenton - Personal History And Goals

## Scope

SoD-only character backstory page for Kenton Clawstar. This page records player-developed pre-campaign material from Lawrence/Larry McHale's Kenton document and separates it from events discovered during play.

## Source Type

**Player-authored pre-campaign character document.** These details are valid for Kenton's intended identity, motivations, and self-presentation unless later session notes contradict them. They should not be treated as independently confirmed in-world facts unless they also appear in session notes.

Source document: SoD - Notebook for Lawrence's Character

## Core Identity

- Name: Kenton Clawstar.
- Campaign: SoD.
- Player: Larry/Lawrence McHale.
- Public concept: high-end clothing designer, performer, market-stall seller, and flamboyant artiste.
- Visual presentation: tall, slender, carefully groomed, colorful, dramatic cape, rose accent, silks/sequins/fabrics.
- Voice/persona: bold, dramatic, intentionally fake Spanish-accented theatrical delivery.

## Pre-Campaign Backstory

- Kenton grew up in a bustling fashion district of a distant city.
- He was adored by nobles, courtiers, and scandal-seekers.
- He fled after too many romantic dramas, including an incident involving a lady, a masquerade ball, and a wardrobe malfunction.
- He came to the small village to find peace, or at least a better stage for himself.
- He set up a market stall selling high-end clothing and fabrics.
- He quickly judged the locals as having little taste and little money.
- Adventuring became a practical path to money, escape from obscurity, and stories strong enough to relaunch his career.

## Religion And Values

- Kenton follows Tyvarion, the Master of Masks.
- Tyvarion is important to Kenton as a patron of art, beauty, drama, poetry, music, performance, and self-expression.
- Kenton treats fashion and performance as devotional acts.
- He sees the world as a stage and himself as both performer and masterpiece.

## Guiding Principles

- Honor eloquence: language should be chosen and presented beautifully.
- Seek inspiration: color, laughter, heartbreak, music, and experience can all become art.
- Reject the mundane: Kenton frames ordinary life as something to escape through style, drama, and invention.

## Pre-Campaign Relationship Intent

These are player-developed starting impressions, not necessarily full session-confirmed relationships.

- Zephyra: Kenton calls her the "Crimson Goddess" when she gets upset and appreciates her keeping trouble away from his fabrics.
- Therric: Kenton believes Therric could become a star with proper costuming and polish.
- The sorcerer / Esylla: Kenton is both fascinated by and wary of her ability to damage his outfits; he sees her as colorful, expressive, and knowing more than she says.
- Escanor: Kenton wants Escanor to bless his tailoring scissors for precision and beauty.
- Local market crowd: Kenton is known, tolerated, and quietly admired by some younger locals.

## Motivations

- Boredom: Kenton wants something larger than ordinary village life.
- Underpayment: the local market cannot support his ambitions.
- Audience: he wants people worthy of his talent and drama.
- Possible debt: the source suggests he may owe money tied to expensive imported fabrics.
- Career restoration: he wants stories dramatic enough to return to high society.

## Goals

- Become the most legendary fashion designer in the realm.
- Escape the tiny village and re-enter high society.
- Fund and create his next great masterpiece.
- Design outfits inspired by each adventure.
- Keep capes, movement, and theatrical presentation central to his image.

## Resolved And Progressed

**Resolved:**
- Survived Sir Rodolf Norran's first ambush (Session 05). ✓
- Marketplace/cart disturbances survived without permanent civic fallout. ✓

**Meaningful Progress:**
- Reyvennra letter sent via Laudneyr toward Gibuldon — response pending.

## How This Connects To Session Events

- The pre-campaign focus on fine clothing, silks, fashion, debts, nobles, and scandal fits the later Gibuldon/Reyvennra memory thread.
- The session notes confirm Kenton travels to sell fine clothes and silks, runs a stall, cares deeply about clothing/laundry, performs with a fiddle, and uses flamboyant social presence.
- The backstory's noble/scandal/fashion-district origin may help explain why Kenton moves easily around noble drama, but the specific Gibuldon fire, bound dwarf, ring, and overthrow thread are session-discovered mysteries.
- The "possible debts from imported fabrics" detail is not yet tied to the Reyvennra silks in the notes. Treat that connection as possible but unproven.
