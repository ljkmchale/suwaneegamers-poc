# Escanor - Personal History And Goals

## Scope

SoD-only personal thread page for Escanor, played by Brian Winniford.

## Confirmed Personal Thread

- Escanor is a cleric-like spiritual authority tied closely to Nature's Oratory and Tanluar Rotvori.
- He repeatedly investigates evil, fiendish influence, undead activity, and corrupted ritual objects.
- He becomes the party's moral counterweight when theft, lies, and civic fallout threaten the mission.
- Escanor helps expose the cult's heart rituals and later turns evidence over to town/priestly authority.
