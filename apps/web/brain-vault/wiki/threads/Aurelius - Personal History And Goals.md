# Aurelius - Personal History And Goals

## Scope

HoE-only personal thread page for Aurelius Valeheart / Aury, played by Larry/Lawrence McHale.

## Source Type

**Session-synthesized source.** Built from HoE entity notes, timeline, character beat matrix, and session summaries. No separate player-authored pre-campaign backstory doc has been found yet.

## Confirmed Personal Thread

- Aurelius is an Aasimar cleric of Diverra and the party's primary divine authority.
- Hap's early Diverra vision ties Hap and Aury together before the party fully understands the Lost Sanctuary.
- In Session 12, Priestess Meladra tells Aury directly that *"his calling is now far beyond that of Diverra — he is bigger than that."* Aury rejects this, insisting he has been called by Diverra to protect the city. She says he is not lost but that Diverra is guiding him somewhere larger. This is the pivotal tension of his faith arc: is he outgrowing Diverra, or being called deeper into her?
- Aury finds the Lost Sanctuary of Diverra and receives Bloom Aeterna, the complete original teaching of Diverra.
- He learns about Shattered Hearts and the need to return them to sacred places.
- His bond with Priestess Meladra now carries two asymmetries: she told him he's bigger than the faith, and he now holds Bloom Aeterna — original doctrine she doesn't have. He is choosing how much to tell her.
