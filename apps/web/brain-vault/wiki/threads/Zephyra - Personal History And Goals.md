# Zephyra - Personal History And Goals

## Scope

SoD-only personal thread page for Zephyra, played by Jenny McHale.

## Source Type

**Session-synthesized source.** Built from SoD entity notes, timeline, character beat matrix, and session summaries. No separate player-authored pre-campaign backstory doc has been found yet.

## Confirmed Personal Thread

- Zephyra is a tiefling barbarian with a local Adsuren protector/watch-adjacent role.
- Captain Alinia and local guard structures know her.
- Aeolenne claims to be Zephyra's parent and possesses/manipulates her in Session 08.
- Zephyra breaks free by recalling Jimmy Meanpants, a strong personal memory.
- Mollywop says higher magic or costly protection may help prevent future possession/control.

## Resolved And Progressed

**Resolved:**
- Broke free of Aeolenne's possession by recalling Jimmy Meanpants — agency recovered. ✓
- Killed the imp that was operating in Adsuren during the cult arc. ✓
- Aeolenne expelled locally — Adsuren itself is no longer the immediate site of possession risk. ✓

**Meaningful Progress:**
- Fiendish heritage now known to the party and to Mollywop; no longer a hidden personal secret.
- Mollywop has indicated higher magic or costly protection could help prevent future possession.

**Complications:**
- Aeolenne fled to Caldwynn and is not contained — she will return.
- Whether Aeolenne is truly Zephyra's parent remains unconfirmed — Aeolenne's claims cannot be trusted.
- Protection from Mollywop carries unknown cost; Zephyra has not committed to pursuing it.

## Goals And Personal Leads

- Understand Aeolenne's claim and what Zephyra's fiendish heritage means.
- Gain protection against future possession or mind control.
- Keep Adsuren safe while Aeolenne remains loose.
- Balance rage/front-line violence with local authority expectations.
- Follow the Caldwynn/Aeolenne thread when the party can.

- What protection can Mollywop or another source provide?
