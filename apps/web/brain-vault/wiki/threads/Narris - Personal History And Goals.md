## Background

Bladesinger. Narris is guided by an advisor named Morith toward a mysterious "thread of shadow" that connects to planar mysteries. He has bonded with a familiar named Sable. Sigil is designated as his anchor if planar travel requires tethering. His skill set combines arcane swordsmanship with scholarly curiosity.

- Fought air genasi and hill giant; used Dust of Disappearance and Silence (sess. 4.3)
- Helped Ren disarm traps in the [[Haunted Mansion]] (sess. 5.1)
- Sent a Sending spell to his parents (sess. 5.1)
- Studied the [[Everlight]] arch, discovering time distortions (sess. 6.3)
- Led the Everlight cave exploration; uncovered a frozen ranger; fought howlers (sess. 6.4�6.5)

## Current Goals

1. Follow the "thread of shadow" with Morith; survive the "mirror void"
2. Study artifacts and texts (including a book recovered from Fizban's Tower)

4. Protect the party
