## Background

Elf Archdruid of the Howling Grotto. Galtrelon (Gal) joined the party in Session 6.3 as a replacement for the fallen Tassarion. He was sent by or connected to Lothim with a mandate to protect [[Everlight]]. His druidic expertise was critical in the Everlight cave expedition and grove unsealing.

## Key Moments

- Joined the party in [[Everlight]]; gave [[Alteya - Personal History And Goals|Alteya]] her husband's journal and badge (sess. 6.3)
- Fought an icy creature by wild-shaping into a snake (sess. 6.4)
- Identified druidic runes indicating the cave was a prison (sess. 6.4)
- Used Speak with Plants to negotiate passage through vine tunnels (sess. 6.5)
- Identified a preservation altar (sess. 6.5)
- Fought howlers and a mimic (sess. 6.5)
- Acquired the [[Staff of the Slumbering Grove]] (sess. 6.5)
- Led the grove unsealing ritual that freed trapped individuals including Alteya's family (sess. 6.5)

## Current Goals

3. Pursue and stop Kal'dar from raising an army
4. Aid Alteya's family until their reunion is confirmed complete
