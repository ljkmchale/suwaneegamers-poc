# Zymve - Personal History And Goals

## Scope

HoE-only personal thread page for Zymve, played by Emma Cooper.

## Source Type

**Session-synthesized source.** Built from HoE entity notes, timeline, character beat matrix, and session summaries. No separate player-authored pre-campaign backstory doc has been found yet.

## Confirmed Personal Thread

- Zymve first appears in play in HoE Session 15, Spitespirit Cache.
- She is a drow bard with a strong music/performance role and practical treasure-seeking streak.
- She is running from or marked by something in her drow/Underdark past.
- She admits a cult-past thread during the Emberstran return arc.
- Zymve uniquely hears music from the Journal of Tsulanan, linking her to Ainslie's book mystery.
- She kills Petal in Session 22, creating moral and spiritual fallout.

## Resolved And Progressed

**Resolved:**
- Joined the party fully as a member (Session 15+) — no longer a stranger or guide. ✓
- Experienced first daylight in Session 20 — a milestone moment for a drow on the surface. ✓

**Meaningful Progress:**
- Established telepathic bond with Makra — unique connection the rest of the party lacks.
- Journal of Tsulanan creates music for her; the connection to Ainslie's bloodline thread is live.
- At the Whisper Forge, contributed to crafting for the party; Lyra the gnome noted.

**Complications:**
- Killed Petal in Session 22 — Rook does not know; trust within party and with Ky'tha is strained.
- Reacts with fear or alarm to Szoradryn in Nunglthil; suggests a past entanglement not yet disclosed.
- Her cult-past admission raises unanswered questions that the party has not fully pressed.

## Goals And Personal Leads

- Understand her Underdark/cult past and whether it is still pursuing her.
- Learn why the Journal of Tsulanan creates music for her.
- Help the party while deciding how much of herself to reveal.
- Navigate daylight, trust, prejudice, and survival on the surface.
- Resolve the fallout of killing Petal.
