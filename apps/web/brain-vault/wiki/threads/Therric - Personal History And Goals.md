# Therric - Personal History And Goals

## Scope

SoD-only personal thread page for Therric, played by Chip Poole.

## Source Type

**Session-synthesized source.** Built from SoD entity notes, timeline, character beat matrix, and session summaries. No separate player-authored pre-campaign backstory doc has been found yet.

## Confirmed Personal Thread

- Therric is a half-orc ranger/monk with strong field instincts and physical discipline.
- He had a connection to a mystic named Areana/Ariana Duskwhisper, who has gone missing or out of contact.
- He experiences a hunting/clawed dream that begins a personal mystery.
- He is closest to Kenton, often rescuing, coaching, enabling, and worrying about him.
- Therric gains/keeps a +1 staff after the cult cellar and begins carving runes into it.

## Resolved And Progressed

**Resolved:**
- Rescued Kenton from Rodolf's first ambush — the moment that defined his protector role. ✓
- Durgruck undead resolved — his field instincts were central to the cemetery investigation. ✓
- Gained a +1 staff after the cult cellar fight; now actively carving runes into it. ✓

**Meaningful Progress:**
- Hunting/clawed dream noted and recurring — personal mystery is live.
- Staff is becoming something beyond a weapon; the rune path has started but not named.

**Complications:**
- Areana/Ariana Duskwhisper has gone missing or out of contact — no resolution and no leads yet.
- His protector dynamic with Kenton is straining: Kenton's theft liability creates situations where Therric has to enable or excuse someone he is closest to.

## Goals And Personal Leads

- Find or contact Areana/Ariana Duskwhisper.
- Understand the hunting/clawed dream.
- Keep Kenton alive without letting Kenton's choices destroy the party.
- Follow mine, swamp, and creature leads where his ranger instincts matter.
- Develop his staff/rune path after the cult cellar.

## Open Gaps

- Who exactly is Areana/Ariana Duskwhisper?
- What caused Therric's hunting dream?
- Is the dream linked to a beast, curse, patron, memory, or future threat?
- What will Therric's carved staff become?
- How far will he go to protect Kenton?
