## Confirmed Personal Thread

- Hap's family story is one of HoE's central personal arcs.
- Benoit, Hap's father, has conflicting death records; Jae falsified records under pressure.
- Phira is Hap's sister. She is alive and dangerous/protective, not simply dead as earlier records suggested. She is not Hap's mother.
- Hap receives family-linked items and clues: Phira's ring/apron, a "G" medallion, inherited land, and Shadowtrail.
- Lady Rook uses Hap's family, land, and Phira's husband as leverage.
