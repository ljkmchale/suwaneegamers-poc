# Silent Vanguard Relationship Map

## Party Trust And Friction

- [[Jett Blackwood]] is the physical anchor of the party: holds doors, inspects equipment, and acts under pressure.
- [[Axel Blackwood]] is connected to Jett by name; their exact relationship (brothers, family, cover identities) is not yet documented.
- [[Cletus]] and [[Lensworth]] are in the party but have not yet had beats extracted from source.
- Party cohesion appears functional — they debated fleeing versus saving people in Brinecross, suggesting some moral tension but no major split.

## Handler Relationship

- [[Master Thorne]] (also Thorin) is the party's patron, briefer, and receiver of field reports.
- The party trusts Thorne enough to accept assignments and report back honestly, but his full agenda is unknown.
- Thorne controls the cover story (Wavehammer) and the payment structure.

## Field Contacts

- [[Harold Stonesheep]]: trusted field contact, now sick and left behind in Brinecross; may become a liability or need rescuing.
- [[Gultren the Canvas Master]]: unknown; the party holds his letter and could approach him under cover or expose him.
- Linda and Nora: Brinecross locals left behind; moral thread if the party returns to resolve the outbreak.

## Adversarial Relationships

- The trafficking operation (south warehouse → colosseum) is the primary adversarial structure; no named enemy has been directly confronted yet.
- The [[Gilded Ledger]] and [[Salt And Cinder Press]] are corruption structures the party is working against.
- Whoever sent the warehouse letter is an unseen antagonist.

## Leverage Points

- The party holds the warehouse letter, which could be used as evidence or a cover approach.
- Harold's illness creates a rescue obligation the party may feel.
- The 20-night shipment window creates time pressure.
