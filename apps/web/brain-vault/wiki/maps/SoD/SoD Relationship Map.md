# SoD Relationship Map

## Party Trust And Friction

- [[Escanor]] trusts the Oratory, Zephyra, and lawful cleanup; he worries about Kenton's ethics but still supports the mission.
- [[Therric]] is closest to Kenton and Zephyra; he enables risky plans but acts fast to heal/rescue friends.
- [[Zephyra]] trusts Escanor, Therric, Mollywop for heritage questions, and the party, but Aeolenne can exploit her rage/identity.
- [[Kenton]] relies most on Therric and benefits from Esylla's social cleanup; Lila and Escanor challenge his theft/ethics.
- [[Esylla]] is close to Lila, linked to Mollywop, and useful for noble/shipping interpretation.
- [[Lila]] trusts Esylla and supports the party, but directly challenges Kenton's lies and theft.

## NPC Trust And Suspicion

- Mollywop is broadly useful and trusted, but may hide things.
- Captain Alinia is lawful authority but biased against Mollywop.
- Aeolenne is hostile/manipulative and focused on Zephyra.
- House Reyvennra/Rodolf are active threats to Kenton.
- MacCannain seems mostly stand-up but is linked to suspicious paperwork.

## Leverage Points

- Kenton's tax theft can be used against him or the party.
- Zephyra's heritage/mind-control vulnerability is exploitable by Aeolenne.
- Esylla's family/aunt/shipping background may unlock Caldwynn clues.
- Lila's crystal visions can validate or redirect investigations.
- Therric's dreams and Areana/Ariana lead may expose personal stakes.
