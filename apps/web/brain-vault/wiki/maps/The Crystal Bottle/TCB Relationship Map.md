# The Crystal Bottle — Relationship Map

Campaign: [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]

## Divine / Cosmic Tier

- **[[Muerg]]** (Old God) — declared Everlight under protection; announced the Awakening; companion to Ashe
- **[[Ashe]]** — channels divine flame; gifted Yvaine armor; calls Muerg "My lord"
- **T'suul (Tsuul'physs Bhan)** — celestial figure; confirmed Harlowe is in Bathaen Empire; source of the book *Whispers of the Sands*
- **[[Aurelion]]** — angel; currently chained by Malik in Lost City portal; told Yvaine to get key from Malik's neck
- **Layeth the Selfless One** — Yvaine's warlock patron (Celestial); also Og's deity in other campaigns
