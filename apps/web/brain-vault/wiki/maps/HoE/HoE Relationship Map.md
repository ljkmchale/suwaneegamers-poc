# HoE Relationship Map

## Party Trust And Friction

- Ainslie and Og become especially entangled in Sessions 09-13: cult contact, Eradic infiltration, and Black Quill's anti-Rook offer.
- Ainslie and Zymve are tied through the Journal of Tsulanan; trust is growing but tense.
- Ainslie and Og have a strong friendship/trust bond.
- Aury and Hap are linked by Diverra's vision and Lost Sanctuary validation.
- Aury and Ainslie/Og have a values split around dangerous faction play, Arnol, and the Cloak.
- Hap depends on Ky'tha for the Phira truth after Ky'tha confirms Phira is alive in Trailpoint.
- Ky'tha and Og have older friendship and field trust.
- Ky'tha and Zymve are trust-adjacent but tense after Petal's death and Zymve investigation.
- Aury serves as moral counterweight when Ainslie or the party handles dangerous power.

## External Bonds

- Lady Tarathial hires the party for old records and becomes the stabilizing Vintner's Guild option.
- Lady Rook courts the party through Hap's land, Ky'tha's trust, Og's thorn-leaf key, and the Marrowstone ritual.
- Lady Rook pressures Hap through land/Phira/Marrowstone and uses Petal.
- Imonorra is Rook's operational anchor — she hosts Rook's tower meetings, holds the Marrowstone rod, and teleported Ky'tha to Trailpoint on Rook's behalf. Never leaves her tower. Alignment unclear; a Cloak agent named her with contempt.
- Black Quill / Selvara Nendris recruits Ainslie and Og through official EGD status and anti-Rook partnership.
- Arnol admires Aury/Hap but is pulled into the Eradic of Ghone through Ainslie's risky "secret mission" handling.
- Tessa / Etterset is Ky'tha's underworld support against the Cloak.
- Belavie Lightstrider gives Ky'tha rank-two Etterset standing.
- Phira becomes Hap's living family anchor and is tied to Jerik's brother / Rook leverage.
- Zymve enters through Nunglthil as guide, artist, and Scalamank contact.
- Irragosa asks Ky'tha to watch Ainslie.
- Irragosa confirms she guarded Moganna's vault, recognizes Ainslie's intended role, sees Ky'tha as echoing Orah Nelcrath, and sees Zymve as echoing Vhurkir Inni.
- Meladra is Aury's temple authority; told Aury his calling is "bigger than Diverra" but may be surpassed by Aury's direct revelation via Bloom Aeterna.
- Dandelia is Og's Life Tree / E'erspan lead.

## Leverage Points

- Hap's family ring, Phira, inherited land.
- Hap's "G" medallion and Shadowtrail.
- Ainslie's bloodline and Journal of Tsulanan.
- Ainslie's heart gem, Soulroot, Starvein, and Ahn'Tsuel's 50-year cycle.

- Aury's Emberheart Tablet attunement and Diverra waypoint access.
- Ky'tha's contacts and Irragosa's request.
- Ky'tha's missing locket half, rank-two Etterset coin, Rook endorsement, and immovable rod.
- Og's Thornleaf black thorn.
- Og's Alferumn/Thornleaf bond and grave-digging remorse.
