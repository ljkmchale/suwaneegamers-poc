# Emberstran

## Scope

HoE-only location.

## Summary

Emberstran is a volcanic coastal city rebuilt after Mount Emberstran erupted. Its campaign-agnostic city canon is maintained at [[Emberstran]] in the world layer; this page tracks the Heroes of Emberstran party's experience, discoveries, factions, and consequences inside the city.

It is the central hub for HoE sessions 01-15 and the party's base of operations. The city is layered: the old town (Winbalt) was buried under lava in the northern highland areas; the new town flourishes along the sea. Wine is the city's primary trade. Civic power is contested between the Lord, the Cloak of Defiance, and Lady Rook's rising influence.

## Physical Layout

- **Northern section** — elevated; old lava-covered Winbalt; wealthy residents, vineyards, Vintner's Guild (~½ mile north of the gates through farmlands).
- **Southern/coastal section** — new town; flourishing port, docks, markets, inns. Busy and foggy at the waterfront.
- City has gates (southern gate, northern gate); guards recognize the party by their return in Session 20.
- Notable elevation: the old town was literally buried; land records from pre-eruption Winbalt may still exist underground.

## Civic Authority

- **Lord Garrod Dusklight (Lord Protector of Emberstran)** — came to power after floods rebuilt the town; appointed by the people; described as knowing "where the power is." Works within the constraints of the Cloak and Rook.
- **Captain Vammad** — long-tenured guard captain; oversees records and guard operations.
- **Captain Amilla** — female human guard captain; handles crime scenes.
- **Town Crier** — civic information channel; the Cloak reportedly feeds news to the Crier.
- **Council of Advisors** — Aury assessed it as 3 non-Cloak vs 2 Cloak-focused; the Lord leans toward the Cloak 2. Lady Rook was elected to civic leadership after Session 19.

## Named After

- Named for Mount Emberstran, the volcano to the north that both destroyed old Winbalt and made the region suitable for viticulture.

## Current Status (Post-Session 24)

- Lady Rook has been elected. Lady Tarathial remains at the Vintner's Mansion.
- Party returned in Session 20; leveled up; departed for the mountain in Session 22.
- The Etterset's base (Our Exquisites) is now the active underground network hub.
- Meladra is stable; Arnol is under watch; Ainslie's necrotic energy extraction is ongoing.
