# Lost Sanctuary And Bloomrest

## Scope

HoE-only location.

## Summary

The Lost Sanctuary of Diverra (also called Bloomrest) is the original sacred site of Diverra's faith, hidden underground and accessed through a gate in the Crimson Lake. It is a hidden grove of roses, wildflowers, and ancient trees on the surface side of the gate. The party reached it in Session 19, cleared a Blackrend blight attack, received Bloom Aeterna (Aury), and bonded the Life Tree (Og as E'erspan). They departed in Session 20 via a rose petal teleportation ritual back to Diverra's Grove.

## Accessing The Sanctuary

- Reached by descending through the underground harbor at Nunglthil, then navigating to the Crimson Lake — a massive underground lake with a dim, constant red glow on the surface.
- The gate is a stone circle/ring at the lake floor, metallic with gold threads. Aury identified it as the gate he had heard about.
- Party descended, gathered at the ring, and walked through — emerging in a surface clearing.

## Physical Description

- Surface location with natural sky; hidden grove with vines and wildflowers climbing over everything.
- Trees form a natural enclosure; roses everywhere; warm light.
- A building with open archways contains the altar — covered with roses; magical.
- Carving on the altar: hands offering a chalice of receiving (Ky'tha identified this).
- A massive Life Tree stands at the grove's edge — Og touched it and bonded.

## Key Events (Session 19)

- **Blight attack**: Dark shapes burst from the earth immediately on entry — tree-like creatures with black wispy energy; needle blights; vine-roots. Two dark druids (described as extraplanar beings) were present. Party fought and cleared them.
- **Aury's restoration**: During combat, Aury fell. Og carried him to the altar building. He opened his eyes, felt warmth, recognized this as the original Diverra sanctuary. Radiant light replaced his book's text with the words "Bloom Aeterna."
- **Og's E'erspan calling**: The thornleaf spoke directly to Og: "Answer its call." He touched the Life Tree and shared its pain — roots starved, poisoned. He became E'erspan, guardian of the Alferumn.
- **Zymve's music**: She heard whispers of a melody that no one else responded to; she was leaned against the archway.
- **Ainslie's journal**: The Journal of Tsulanan began rearranging chapters; words converging around "a catastrophe that came from hunger."

## After The Session (Session 20)

- The Life Tree had new life the next morning — the blight was driven back at this location.
- Aury performed the return ritual: reading from Bloom Aeterna, a petal floated in radiant light, and the party teleported to Diverra's Grove.

## The Chalice

- Ky'tha found a carving of hands offering a chalice of receiving on the altar.
- Aury interpreted this as a physical chalice he must find, to place a Shattered Heart in at this sanctuary.
- The sanctuary itself may serve as the proper place of worship for at least one Shattered Heart.

## Open Questions

- What does Og's E'erspan status require — is he bound to this location or to the Alferumn network broadly?
- Does this sanctuary have any connection to the vault at Mount Emberstran?
