# Docks And Anchor Point

## Scope

HoE-only location.

## Summary

The Emberstran docks are a busy, foggy port district along the city's coastal section. The party's first mission (Session 01) originated here: a vanished crew, otherworldly tadpoles, and a dry barrel that contained the first tablet clue. The docks remained a recurring access point and information source throughout early sessions.

## The Vanished Crew (Session 01)

- Quest posted by **Berg Stugmyer** (dockmaster): crew vanished, cargo untouched, no sign of struggle, ship moored. Party paid 60gp (25 extra available).
- Ship was inactive across the docks — Hap's owl Fleur spotted it first.
- In the hold: all crew were dead. Broken barrels had released "otherworldly" tadpole-like creatures that crawled across victims' faces. Ainslie identified them as extraplanar.
- First tablet clue: found at the docks when Aury and Bordi opened a dry barrel. A tablet fragment inside.

## Physical Description

- Busy, foggy coastal section.
- Two sides to the docks; the active commercial side and a quieter mooring area.
- Jae Vynaar's house was described as being "down at the docks" — older, thin, blotchy skin.
- **Darn** — dockhand and Ainslie's contact; fishes at the docks; relayed early ship information.

## Key Named Figures At The Docks

- **Berg Stugmyer** — dockmaster; contracted the vanished crew investigation.
- **Darn** — dockhand; Ainslie's casual contact; used as an information relay.
- **Devron** — dwarf owner of the Headless Hydra; also a dock-adjacent figure.
- **MacCannain** (SoD context note: in HoE, dock-related paperwork contacts are separate).

## Later Dock References

- In Session 04, a murder near the docks was investigated by Ainslie and Og (they found zombie with crossbow bolt at the scene).
- Jae Vynaar's house was at the docks — the party went there to find him.
- Lady Rook arrived in Emberstran originally through dock trading for "odd antiquities."

## Open Questions

- What were the otherworldly tadpoles, and where did they come from?
- Was the crew targeted because of the tablet cargo, or were the tadpoles the cargo?
- Is Jae Vynaar's dock-area house still relevant as a location?
