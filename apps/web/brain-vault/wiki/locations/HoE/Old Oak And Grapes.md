# Old Oak And Grapes

## Scope

HoE-only location.

## Summary

Old Oak & Grapes is an upscale pub in the hills above Emberstran. It was the murder site in Session 02 (Kelona's throat was slit there) and later the site of a Cloak of Defiance dinner-assassination attempt against Lady Rook. The party has returned here multiple times for information and meetings.

## Physical Description

- Located up in the hills; very nice, warm feel; booths; barkeep, two barmaids, a stage.
- Upscale establishment — the nicest residential/dining area of Emberstran outside the Crimson Swan.
- Described as having a warm, inviting atmosphere even when something violent is happening.

## Key Events

**Session 02 — Kelona's Murder**
- Og and Ainslie were seated in a booth when a woman's throat was slit. A performer stood and bowed as people clapped (thinking it was a performance). But there was real blood — she was genuinely murdered.
- The body was brought to the temple by the party. Aury confirmed poison was also involved.
- This murder opened the entire Vintner's Guild investigation thread.

**Session 08 — The Cloak Dinner Attempt**
- Lady Rook was apparently the intended target at a dinner here. The Cloak's agent "Whisper" poisoned a drink (per Tessa's account). Rook was the hit, not the party.
- an'Davo told Tessa the hit was the Cloak of Defiance's work; Whisper is described as a small female who disguises herself.

**Lady Rook's Dinner (later)**
- Rook hosted a party here at twilight; an "interesting group of like-minded individuals" was invited. Ky'tha and companions were invited but went to the dinner; a Cloak attempt was apparently made during it.

## Key Named Figures

- **Odith** — barkeep.
- Barmaids (unnamed).
- Kelona — victim of Session 02 murder.

## Sewer Connection

- The sewer network near the Old Oak & Grapes was explored by Ky'tha. She checked the dead end under/near it — saw only pipes; no sewer openings.
