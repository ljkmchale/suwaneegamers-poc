# Thallgrove And Bimblefol

## Scope

HoE-only location cluster.

## Summary

Thallgrove is a massive underground cave biome dominated by dangerous fungi. Very few can navigate it safely. The party hired a boat to take them there from Nunglthil. Bimblefol is the genling village inside Thallgrove, led by (or associated with) a genling named Makra who bonded with Zymve. The party passed through Thallgrove and Bimblefol on the route toward the Crimson Lake and the Lost Sanctuary.

## Thallgrove

- A cave mouth opening; rich soil smell, sweet bark; light visible inside.
- The primary danger: fungi that can cause hallucinations or worse. Nelka (Soothing Spores) warned that very few entities can navigate safely; a small creature type (possibly a gnome-type) is resistant to the fungi.
- The party took Tatti's filters (likely some protective item) and used Aniba's map for navigation.
- Session 17 ended as the party walked toward Makra's village.

## Bimblefol — The Genling Village

- Inside Thallgrove; the genling settlement.
- Genlings communicate via telepathy — small, wiry, unblinking eyes.
- **Makra** — the first genling the party met; unblinking eyes; used staff to hit specific fungi pieces and collect them (the fungi responded — said "ouch"). Bonded particularly with Zymve. Said "Bimblefol" while tilting head toward the village.
- Zymve communicated through Deep Speech and performance; the genlings responded strongly to her music.
- The genlings and Zymve's connection drew the group closer to the village than expected.

## Key Events (Sessions 17-18)

- Party navigated the fungi field; hallucination risk managed by protective filters.
- Ky'tha's Dunduar/Muerg/Cragfury vision occurred in this area — a sealed ring/flow vision that Ky'tha has not fully explained.
- Zymve's music resonated with the genlings and the environment in ways that surprised even her.
- The party hired a boat to reach the Crimson Lake route afterward.

## Route Significance

- Thallgrove lies between Nunglthil and the Crimson Lake (which contains the gate to the Lost Sanctuary).
- The passage through here is part of the underground path from civilized underdark (Nunglthil) to the original Diverra shrine.

## Open Questions

- Can the party return through Thallgrove the same way?
