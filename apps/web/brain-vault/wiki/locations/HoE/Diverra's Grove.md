# Diverra's Grove

## Scope

HoE-only location.

## Summary

Diverra's Grove is a surface sacred grove associated with the Diverra faith, located in a forested area near or outside Emberstran. It serves as a waypoint: the party first encountered it as a living faith site in Session 06, and returned here via teleportation from the Lost Sanctuary in Session 20 using a rose petal ritual. It is likely the surface counterpart to the Lost Sanctuary underground.

## What Is Known

- First appearance: Session 06. The party entered the grove as part of an early Diverra investigation.
- Described as a forested area with a clearing; consistent with a sacred/natural grove architecture.
- Connected via teleportation to the Lost Sanctuary (Bloomrest): Aury used a rose petal from Bloom Aeterna and read a ritual, and the party was transported from Bloomrest to this grove.

## The Return (Session 20)

- After waking at Bloomrest, the party opened an area in the Lost Sanctuary and performed the ritual.
- A petal floated, radiant light wrapped around the group, faded — they arrived at Diverra's Grove.
- From here they hitched a cart ride south to Emberstran with timber workers (Jorm, Veed, Sigur of the Emberstran Timber Company).

## Arnol Connection

- The grove is referenced in connection with Arnol's strange behavior; whether he came here or was observed near here is uncertain from the notes.

## Relationship To Other Diverra Sites

- **Lost Sanctuary (Bloomrest)**: the underground original site; connected via gate and rose-petal ritual.
- **Temple of Diverra, Emberstran**: Meladra's temple; the city-facing institutional presence of the faith.
- **Diverra's Faith doctrine**: full doctrine held in Bloom Aeterna; Aury used the grove as a waypoint back to that knowledge.

## Open Questions

- Can the grove be used as a permanent waypoint for the rose petal ritual — can the party return to Bloomrest from here?
- Does the grove have any inherent spiritual protections or properties?
- Is Arnol's strange behavior connected to this grove specifically?
