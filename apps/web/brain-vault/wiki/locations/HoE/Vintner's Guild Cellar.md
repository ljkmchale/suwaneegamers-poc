# Vintner's Guild Cellar

## Scope

HoE-only location.

## Summary

The Vintner's Guild cellar is a subterranean storage area beneath the Guildhouse, accessed via lean-to doors and stairs descending 10-15'. The party cleared it in Session 02-03, fighting a Grapevine Blight infestation that had destroyed most of the wine stock. Five barrels of the finest vintage (Tears of Baltwood) survived.

## Physical Description

- Lean-to doors with steps descending 10-15' into a well-constructed stone cellar.
- Old blood stains mixed with wine stains; no light; stale, thick, putrid air.
- The party found scores of dead bugs near the entrance hallway.
- Two door alcoves off a corridor — one open on the right (high quality wine, untouched), one closed and locked on the left.

## The Blight Combat

- A Grapevine Blight creature attacked from both sides of the room.
- Ainslie identified it as a Grapevine Blight type.
- Two animated barrels also rolled down the hall as threats.
- After clearing: five barrels of Tears of Baltwood remained undamaged — Emberstran's finest vintage.

## Aftermath

- Lady Tarathial was moved to tears when the party reported the cellar was secured.
- She offered the party 50gp; later offered a barrel of wine per harmon if they returned within a harmon with the land papers.
- The cellar was then locked up by Haggis.

## Significance

- The cellar clearance was one of the acts that made the party public figures ("Heroes of Emberstran") — the Town Crier spread the news.
- The blight in the cellar may or may not be connected to the broader Blackrend; it preceded the party's knowledge of the Blackrend by many sessions.

## Open Questions

- Was the Grapevine Blight natural, or planted to destroy evidence (the barrels may have held something other than wine)?
- Does the cellar connect to any underground passage system?
