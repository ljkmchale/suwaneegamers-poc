# Nunglthil

## Scope

HoE-only location.

## Summary

Nunglthil is an underground harbor settlement carved into cliff walls, accessible via Shenog's boat from the Rothenloch underground lake (reached through a Diverra waypoint). It is the party's underdark hub in Sessions 15-17, providing contacts, supplies, and information on the path to Thallgrove and the Lost Sanctuary. Key contacts here include Scalamank/Coppereyes (gem trader), Endavo (Ehkel an'Davo), Miss Placed Tomes bookshop, and Szoradryn.

## Access

- Reached via Rothenloch — an underground lake, likely connected through the Diverra waypoint passage. The party traveled by boat on the lake (about 1-2 hours to paddle with Shenog).
- On the lake path: sounds are muffled and distorted, lichen provides dim light, small ripples in the water below.
- Shenog the Fisher takes the party to the hamlet as guide.

## Physical Description

- Buildings carved into cliff walls.
- A large cave mouth opens from the middle of the cliff/town into an **underground harbor** — well lit by sconces.
- The lake Nunglthil sits on is huge — cannot see across.
- Gral'thune (dwarves and ulbin) walk around; strangers wearing earrings, bracelets, necklaces, charms on backpacks observed.

## Key Contacts

- **Shenog the Fisher** — guide; offered the group a way out in exchange for coin; paddled the party to the hamlet; shares smoked eel.
- **Scalamank (Coppereyes)** — gem trader; agreed to exchange the box of gems; holds a map of the underdark; told the party where to find the Sanctuary of Diverra direction (toward Thallgrove's eastern edge).
- **Ehkel an'Davo** — encountered here; familiar from Emberstran; reading a book called "Misplaced a Last Time"; gave the party local context about the underdark.
- **Szoradryn** — bookshop contact at Miss Placed Tomes; connected to Ainslie's Journal of Tsulanan thread.
- **Glayig** — guard at the iron portcullis leading to surface passages beyond Scalamank's cache route.
- **Nelka** — elderly deep gnome at Soothing Spores; expert on Thallgrove fungi.

## Intelligence Gathered

- The Cloak of Defiance's reach extends into the underdark (Shenog confirmed this).
- The Sanctuary of Diverra is near the eastern edge of Thallgrove (Scalamank's map confirmed approximate direction).
- Arcane waypoints scattered across Myrdae are very old and specific to their faith.
- Very few entities can navigate Thallgrove safely due to the fungi.
