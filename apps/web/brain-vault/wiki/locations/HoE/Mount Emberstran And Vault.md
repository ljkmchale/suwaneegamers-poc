# Mount Emberstran And Vault

## Scope

HoE-only location.

## Summary

Mount Emberstran is the volcano that destroyed old Winbalt and gives Emberstran its name. It is the destination of the HoE campaign's first major vault arc. The party fought through dire wolves, fire beetles, fire newts, and salamander-type threats in Sessions 23-24 to reach the vault door. Ainslie opened the door when Aury could not. In Session 25, the party enters Moganna's vault, meets Irragosa, and Ainslie claims the heart-shaped gem that is one of three relic pieces.

## The Approach (Sessions 22-24)

- The Baltwood trail leads toward the mountain; party followed Emberheart Tablet directions ("Where ember roots and thorns entwine, beneath the mount, the vault shall shine. In Baltwood's breath, you'll find the trail, my warmth, a heartbeat without fail.").
- **Petal** (Lady Rook's tracker) followed the party on this trail until Zymve killed her at a fork.
- **Irragosa** appeared to Ky'tha in humanoid form on the trail — asking about Ainslie, requesting Ky'tha watch him.
- A red-cloaked Cloak of Defiance rider intercepted the party on the trail, threatened that Jerik "now has your scent."

## Encounters On The Mountain (Sessions 23-24)

- Dire wolves and fire beetles on the approach.
- Fire newts: the party's most dangerous encounter. Og dies and is revived.
- Zymve contributed heavily to the fire newt combat and later worked at Sparkwhisper (a location near the vault).
- **Sparkwhisper** — a named site near the vault; details uncertain.

## The "T" Marking

- Ainslie found a "T" marking carved near the volcano, which he connected to Tsulanan — a dragon rider recorded in Irragosa's history. This connects the vault to Ainslie's bloodline (Ahn'Tsuel → Tsulanan → Irragosa).

## The Vault Door (Session 24 End)

- Aury attempted to open the vault door and could not.
- Ainslie then tried and the door opened.
- Ainslie's bloodline/inheritance is later supported by Irragosa's statements inside the vault.

## Inside The Vault (Session 25)

- A dwarven-built tunnel leads to two magical trial arches.
- The first arch is marked "flames of fervor" and induces fear until Ainslie calms the party's emotions.
- The second arch is marked "It is Within" and shows an Ainslie-like figure with a bronze lance; Ainslie's blood opens the barrier.
- The vault chamber is black obsidian with golden veins and a black metal dragon-talon pedestal.
- The pedestal holds a heart-shaped crystal; two sockets are empty.
- Irragosa identifies the vault as Moganna's and says she guarded this piece. The other pieces are Soulroot and Starvein.
- Ainslie touches/takes the heart gem and is exhausted by it. Irragosa says Diverra's light at the Emberstran temple is needed.

## Connections

- **Emberheart Tablet**: provided the directional poem to find the vault.
- **Irragosa**: a dragon from the dragonrider era; guarded Moganna's vault and confirms Ainslie's intended role.
- **Annagos**: Irragosa's sibling dragon (same hatch); relationship to the vault unknown.
- **Lady Rook**: wanted the vault's outcome; sent Petal and later admits she had the party followed because the tablet was tied to ancient bloodlines.
- **Ahn'Tsuel**: timeless aberrant being whose 50-year soul cycle may be breakable with the three relic pieces.

## Open Questions

- Where are Soulroot and Starvein?
- What exactly does the heart gem do by itself?
- What does it mean to combine the three pieces versus reopen the circle?
- Did Lady Rook know Ainslie would be the one to take the gem?
