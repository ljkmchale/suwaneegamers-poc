# Caldwynn

## Scope

SoD-only location page.

## Summary

Caldwynn is a major shipping hub 5 days south of Adsuren. It is where the black salt barrel was routed from (or through) before reaching Adsuren. Aeolenne fled south toward Caldwynn after the ritual chamber fight. The Cult of the Immured had members there, and Aeolenne has a named ally in Caldwynn. It is the most immediate "next threat" city in the SoD arc.

## Known Facts

- Shipping hub; Esylla's family is in shipping, giving her useful context.
- Black salt barrel paperwork was routed from Caldwynn to Adsuren, addressed to the Tanner, with an overpayment to avoid inspection.
- **Aeolenne fled south** toward Caldwynn after being freed. Mollywop confirmed this.
- **Aeolenne has an ally in Caldwynn** — confirmed from cult letters found during the investigation. Whether this ally is a surviving cult member, a noble house contact, or an independent entity is unknown.
- Some Cult of the Immured members came from Caldwynn, per Eyovar's confession.
- **Durgruck Bristleforge's younger sister** may have gone to Caldwynn — potentially a secondary follow-up thread.
- The spiral watermark on the Caldwynn shipping letter has possible noble house matches (Cunningter, Idsoul, Walgett) but has not been confirmed.

## Open Questions

- Who is Aeolenne's Caldwynn ally — a cultist, a noble, a fiend-contractor?

- Which noble house (if any) owns the spiral watermark?
