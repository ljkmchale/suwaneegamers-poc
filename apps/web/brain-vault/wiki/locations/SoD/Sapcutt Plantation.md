# Sapcutt Plantation

## Scope

SoD-only location page.

## Summary

Sapcutt Plantation is a former farming homestead in Hath'lao Swamp, known in Adsuren for the Sapcutt family's famous pies. Mollywop sent the party there after the family missed Market Day. The house had been fully overtaken by fungal corruption — the family was infected after Lady Sapcutt found and consumed a black fish from deep in the swamp and made a bargain with a plant intelligence. The party fought through three floors, rescued Lobella and Margaret, and escaped by boat.

## The House

- Strings of lights hanging from trees mark the plantation boundary.
- Sturdy thick dark wood front door. Multiple floors — ground floor, basement pits, upstairs rooms.
- The kitchen area was relatively less rotted; other areas heavily corrupted.

## Floor Breakdown

**Ground floor**: Dining room, kitchen. The Sapcutt family served the party gator stew laced with fungus (drugged). The floor opened beneath them and dropped everyone into basement pits.

**Basement pits**: Dark tunneled hallway; crates with skeletal limbs, torsos, body parts; rotted cloths, broken heirlooms. The fungal corruption had taken over the natural basement. Unnatural tendrils and grubs present throughout.

**Upper floor**: Metal doors; at least two bedroom areas. One room had a smaller female infected with a bulbous fungus on her chest (Esylla blasted it off). Master bedroom: Lady Sapcutt standing over another body on the bed — she was FUKWAD-infected and attacked. She is this campaign's FUKWAD (Fungipheliac Using Kinfolk With Abandoned Desires).

## Key Figures

- **Lady Sapcutt** — FUKWAD; fought the party in the master bedroom; killed or incapacitated.
- **Lobella** — rescued; revealed the black fish origin and Lady Sapcutt's bargain with a plant intelligence.
- **Margaret** — rescued; other surviving family member.
- Infected husband and two children were at the dining table when the party arrived.

## Items Recovered

- Red vials, chrysoprase gem, light blue fan, +1 short sword, Sapcutt pie recipe.

## Status (Post-Session 03)

- The house was left in unclear condition — the fungal infection was not fully eradicated, only the most dangerous occupants neutralized.
- Lobella and Margaret escaped with the party by boat to Adsuren.
- Whether the house remains dangerous (as a spreading fungal node) is unresolved.

## Open Questions
