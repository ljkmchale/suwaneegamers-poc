# Gibuldon

## Scope

SoD-only location page.

## Summary

Gibuldon is a large city where Kenton has a complicated past. He partied there, later remembered a manor/castle-like hallway scene involving silks, a ring, a bound/bloodied dwarf, and talk of overthrow, and is accused by Rodolf of starting a warehouse fire and making away with goods. House Reyvennra is a powerful Vesci-tier noble house connected to the thread. Some of the Cult of the Immured's members also came from Gibuldon, per Eyovar's confession, but no direct connection between the cult and the Reyvennra/Rodolf thread is documented.

## Known Facts

- Kenton traveled to Gibuldon to sell fine clothes and silks. He partied with tradesmen there; rode with Captain Amara Hilei.
- A warehouse fire occurred. Kenton says he didn't start it, doesn't remember being at the warehouse, and believes his crate of silks fell off a cart.
- He remembers a bound/bloodied dwarf, talk of a ring, "overthrow," "rightful heir," and passing silks along — but doesn't know who was going to overthrow whom.
- **House Reyvennra** (Vesci tier) is based here. Sir Rodolf Norran is connected to the house and tracked Kenton to Adsuren, but whether the house directly ordered him is unconfirmed.
- Kenton's letter was sent to Gibuldon via Laudneyr; estimated a stretch or two for a response.
- Per Eyovar's cult confession: some Cult of the Immured members came from Gibuldon.

## Relevance Going Forward
