# Adsuren

## Scope

SoD-only location page. For canonical world geography, see [[Adsuren]] in `wiki/world/locations/`.

## Summary

Adsuren is the party's home-base town and the setting for the entire SoD arc. It is a small town with a civic center, nature oratory, docks, and a Market Day that drives seasonal activity. Known for the Sapcutt family's pies. The entire first arc — poisoned well, black heart, grave theft, Cult of the Immured beneath civic authority, and Aeolenne's escape — plays out here.

## Civic Structure

- **Intendant** — Eyovar Dunajor; now removed and in custody. An assistant is acting Intendant.
- **Jerborin Omakian** — clerk/advisor at the Adsuren Center; snooty but cooperative; oversees the library.
- **Captain Alinia Sarralei** — guard captain; office in the Guard House (oldest building in town).
- **Tanluar Rotvori** — Nature's Oratory priest; half-elf, older.

## Market Day

- Market Day is a seasonal civic event. At least 12 guards on duty. Zephyra was hired as a Market Day guard.
- Creates elevated foot traffic and noise that also makes Mollywop's ward interpretation harder.

## Current State (Post-Session 08)

- Cult of the Immured exposed; Eyovar removed; assistant acting as Intendant.
- Tax theft rumor partially suppressed by the cult scandal but unresolved.
- Market Day still upcoming and significant.
- Party has departed toward the mines (45th of Hemist).
