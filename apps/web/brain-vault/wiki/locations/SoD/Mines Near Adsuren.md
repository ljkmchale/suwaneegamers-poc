# Mines Near Adsuren

## Scope

SoD-only location page.

## Summary

The mines near Adsuren are the party's next destination after Session 08 (departing the 45th of Hemist). They are connected to Durgruck Bristleforge's death, a "mine sickness," and a reported large bat-like creature that was supposedly found and killed. A reward exists for bringing in a large Bulas creature — possibly the same threat. The Tanner connection (bulas hides, White Horns leather) suggests the mine area and the swamp bulas territory overlap.

## Known Facts

- **Durgruck Bristleforge** — the dwarf whose body was found in the Adsuren graveyard, risen as undead. He had investigated trouble at the mines before dying of "mine sickness."
- **Large bat-like creature**: Reported as the cause of mine trouble; supposedly found and killed. Whether this story is complete or a cover is unknown.
- **Miners and guards** were tested afterward — suggesting the mine was re-entered post-incident.
- **Bulas**: A creature mentioned at the Tanner; described as large; a reward exists for bringing it down. Therric expressed interest in White Horns hide for leathers. The Bulas may be in the mines, the swamp, or the area between.
- Party departs toward the mines at 2 PM on the 45th of Hemist.

## Potential Connections

- Lila's night-forest roar/shadow vision may be connected to the mines or swamp area rather than the plantation.
- The mine sickness that killed Durgruck is unexplained — could be natural, magical, or cultist-adjacent.
- Durgruck rose as undead in the graveyard — suggesting magical or necromantic contamination at or near the mines.

## Open Questions

- What actually happened to Durgruck — natural death, mine sickness, or something worse?
- Is the big-bat story accurate, or was it a cover for a different creature?
- Is the Bulas in the mines, the swamp corridor, or both?
- Is Durgruck's younger sister (who went to Caldwynn) a follow-up thread?
- Are bulas connected to the fungal corruption or the cult's supply chain?
