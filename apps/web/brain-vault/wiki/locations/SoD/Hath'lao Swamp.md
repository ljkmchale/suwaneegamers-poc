# Hath'lao Swamp

## Scope

SoD-only location page.

## Summary

Hath'lao Swamp is the marshy area between Adsuren's river crossing and the Sapcutt Plantation. The party crossed it twice in Sessions 02-03. It is a multi-hour journey through mist, faint lights strung from trees, and unknown swamp creatures. Something large lives in or passes through the swamp — a roar and large silhouette were heard and seen after the party's escape from the plantation. Mollywop considered a dragon possible but uncertain.

## Physical Description

- Reached via ferry across the Adsuren river; dropped north of normal docks (swampy landing area).
- Travel time: several hours on foot to reach the Sapcutt Plantation.
- Mist throughout; strings of lights hanging from trees mark the plantation boundary.
- Small serpent-like or swamp creatures follow the party during transit.

## Key Events

- **Sapcutt Plantation visit**: The plantation sits at the far end of the swamp. Party reached it at near-sunset (Session 02) and escaped by boat (Session 03) toward Adsuren.
- **Black fish**: The Sapcutt family's fungal corruption began when they found a black fish from deep in the swamp. Lady Sapcutt made a "bargain with a plant intelligence" after eating it or being infected.
- **The Roar**: After escaping the plantation, the party heard a loud roar and saw/inferred a large silhouette in the swamp darkness. Mollywop said a dragon was possible but not confirmed.

## Fungal Corruption

- The fungal infection that took over the Sapcutt Plantation originated from a deep-swamp black fish.
- Lobella (rescued Sapcutt survivor) revealed that Lady Sapcutt found the black fish and that this was the origin.
- The connection between the swamp fungus and the Cult of the Immured's black salt / heart corruption is speculative but thematically noted.

## Open Questions

- What made the roar, and is it still in the swamp?
- Where exactly did the black fish come from — a deep lair, a source node, or something connected to the cult?
- Is the Bulas creature (referenced at the Tanner) in or near this swamp?
- Is the fungal corruption still active in the swamp, or contained to the plantation?
