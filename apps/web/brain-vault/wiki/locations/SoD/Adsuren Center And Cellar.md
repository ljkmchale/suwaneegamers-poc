# Adsuren Center And Cellar

## Scope

SoD-only location page. For world-canonical Adsuren Center description, see [[Adsuren]] in `wiki/world/locations/`.

## Summary

Adsuren Center is the civic building at the heart of Adsuren — where the Intendant (Eyovar Dunajor) operated, where records are kept, and where the party eventually uncovered the Cult of the Immured's full operation. The hidden cellar beneath it is a multi-room complex: storage, a secret storage room, a hallway to a bunkroom, disposal room, cultist prep room, and a final ritual chamber.

## Aboveground

- Entry: three individuals (clerk, two guards) when the party visited. Jerborin Omakian (clerk/advisor) manages the library.
- Library: noble-family books, land records, and ledgers. Kenton stole the noble-family book and tax pouches during an early visit.
- Intendant's office: Eyovar's working space; now empty/under acting management.
- Cellar hatch: behind the building, hidden behind overgrown bushes; large lock; discovered by Esylla.

## Cellar Layout (Top To Bottom)

**Cellar Storage Room (ground level)**
- Storage tools, bags of grain, emergency shelter supplies.
- Hidden floor line of dirt against one wall revealed by Escanor — lever behind a false wall.

**Secret Storage Room (15'x15')**
- Half filled with excavated dirt; shovels sticking in the dirt.
- One barrel (no lid) containing black powder — same black salt used in rituals; evidence of dried blood mixed in.
- A trapped chest containing **10 bags of powdered silver** (each worth 10 gp; 100 gp total) — Kenton set off the trap (poison dart) but pocketed all 10 bags.
- A second hidden door and lever (found by Lila); opens to a 30' hallway lit by a lantern.
- A dampening metal arch threshold — tested by Escanor dragging a body across it; purpose is uncertain but possibly masks magical signatures from Mollywop's ward.

**Hallway Junction**
- 30' hallway ends at a T-junction with three options: staircase down (right), door (left), door (straight ahead).

**Cultist Bunkroom (left door)**
- 6 bunkbeds; half occupied when the party arrived. Black clothing, sickles.
- Combat: cultists said "blood must be spilled for pleasure to be obtained" and attacked; one killed himself rather than surrender; one was captured, tried to kill himself.

**Disposal Room (adjacent)**
- Bloody room filled with dead animals and blood. Used for heart-carving practice. Animal sacrifices.

**Cultist Prep Room (straight/staircase)**
- Two stronger cultists setting up; used spiritual weapons; combat cleared them.
- A mimic chest in this room attacked Kenton (he was briefly downed); Therric found a **golden tiara with three blood orange gems** in the mimic ooze.
- Scribbled notes about "how to make a heart" — experiments and findings.
- Two scrolls: **Speak with Animals** and **Unseen Servant**.
- Tiara radiates evocation magic (Esylla's Detect Magic).

**Ritual Chamber (final)**
- Large octagonal room. Walls coated with dried red blood; lit by a dozen sconces.
- Circular pattern on the floor in dried blood with a raised element — ritual circle.
- Site of the final combat with Summoner Karranok (Eyovar).
- Aeolenne freed here when the ritual mechanism was destroyed. Mollywop arrived via Sending and marred/destroyed the ritual circle.

## Post-Session 08 State

- All cultists killed or captured. Eyovar captured and turned over to the Guard.
- Mollywop destroyed the ritual circle.
- Looted: 2 potions (Kenton took), Intendant's staff, tiara, scrolls.
- The cellar itself is now empty — but the passage, rooms, and hidden architecture remain intact.
