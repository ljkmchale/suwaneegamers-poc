# Jagged Waste Crags

## Scope

Dungeons III campaign location page for the rough crag region east/southeast of Hesfal and east of Scarwatch Hold.

## Summary

The Jagged Waste Crags are the rough rocky terrain where the K9 Crew hideout, Radiant Eye leads, Isgo trail, and possible Osmanae observatory all converge.

## Known Features

- Rugged trail beyond Sherdin's lean-to.
- Cave hideout used by the K9 Crew.
- Magic-amplifying cavern where communication magic carries strangely.
- Possible route toward the Underdark.
- Crag trail with fissures, rockslides, corpse, and xorn encounter.
- Abjuration remnants and arcane weave traces at a campsite shortcut.

## Current State

The party is in the crags at the end of Session 10. Meles saw two tan-robed figures with kahlbits; they attacked.
