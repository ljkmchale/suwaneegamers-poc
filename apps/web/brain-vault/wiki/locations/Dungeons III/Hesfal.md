# Hesfal

## Scope

Dungeons III campaign location page. Canonical world stub also exists at `wiki/world/locations/Hesfal.md` when populated.

## Summary

Hesfal is a hamlet in the Yearning Vale Territory and the opening hub of Dungeons III. The campaign begins here around Salvus, when heavy caravan traffic crowds the Covered Hooch and Boar's Best.

## Key Places

- **Covered Hooch** - large camping/yurt area led by Ravina Rool, with Frath and Mardy helping manage crowds.
- **Moonfern Sanctuary** - spa/sanctuary where Aeon stays; Ilyana and Alarine work there.
- **Hall of Many Hands** - multi-faith temple with graffiti to the gods; Aeon meets Kadella and Laestrik here.
- **Boar's Best Inn & Tavern** - tavern with stage and gaming area; site of the robbery/fight.
- **Town Ward** - jail/guard location; prisoner break happens here.
- **Artisan Exchange** - vendors/artists including Irick and Gimri.
- **Bru's Beastie Barn** - location tied to the murder weapon clue and horse rental.

## Campaign Events

- Party members first converge in Hesfal.
- Murder rumors circulate before Salvus.
- The Boar's Best robbery and Town Ward prisoner break expose the K9 Crew plot.
- The party receives its first public work from Mayor Windseek and pursues the bandits.
