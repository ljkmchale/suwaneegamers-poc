# Scarwatch Hold

## Scope

Dungeons III campaign location page.

## Summary

Scarwatch Hold is the fortified hold where the party delivers the K9 Crew prisoners and becomes recognized as [[The kNight Watch]]. Its campaign-agnostic location canon is maintained at [[Scarwatch Hold]] in the world layer; this page tracks Dungeons III events, discoveries, and open threads there.

It has an Outer Gate, Inner Gate, Palisades, Citadel, Sanctuary, Watchtower Inn, Hoot & Howl Tavern, stables, shops, bank, and a governing Warden.

## Key Figures

- Warden Naevaris Haliun.
- Nambra Maerth, drow advisor.
- Umlin Stonewatch, dwarven scribe.
- Nikalia, guard captain.
- Ireosh, stable boss and Sherdin's brother.
- Lyerith, stablehand.
- Warrall, current Sanctuary curate.
- Embryl Lonfren, Scarwatch House/library contact.

## Campaign Events

- Party delivers K9 Crew prisoners and Hesfal charges.
- Outer Gate mimic attacks after a wagon incident.
- K9 Crew judgment happens in the square.
- The party names itself The kNight Watch.
- Umlin hires the party to map the surrounding region.
- Sanctuary is defaced by the Radiant Eye Cult.
- Embryl sends the party after a possible Osmanae celestial instrument.

## Open Threads
