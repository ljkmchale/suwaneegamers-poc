# Blustery Waste

**Type:** Desert Region  
**Region:** Northern [[Arbescar]]  
**Status:** Active — party operating here

## Overview

The Blustery Waste is a vast, harsh desert in northern Arbescar. Howling winds and shifting sands define the landscape. It is the setting of the campaign's most significant ancient mysteries — the buried city of Athralis, the frozen ruins of [[Thal'astyr]], and the [[A'urea'Pura Cult]]'s excavation site.

## Key Locations Within

- **[[Lost City (Athralis)]]** — The ruined oasis city of Athralis; partially excavated by the cult; contains the portal where [[Aurelion]] is chained; current party location (Session 7+).
- **[[Thal'astyr]]** — Ancient underground ruins within the Waste; contained preserved treasures including the [[Sunblade Hilt]], [[Cloak of the Dawn]], [[Ancient Guards Gauntlet]], and the Staff (Sessions 5.3–5.5).
- **[[Everlight]]** — Mountain town at the edge of the Waste; experienced a 25-year time warp until [[Muerg]] declared the Awakening.
- **Cult Excavation Site** — The [[A'urea'Pura Cult]]'s active dig site; they hold the [[Portal Stone]] key (now carried by [[Ren (TCB)|Ren]]) to access the celestial plane.

## Hazards and Features

- Howling winds; shifting sand dunes; disorienting terrain.
- Ren received a vision here of her parents, who told her they did not abandon her.
- [[Narris]] studied the Everlight time-distortion arch here, connecting the Waste's history to the gods' involvement.

## Historical Significance

The Blustery Waste was once home to Athralis — a thriving oasis city founded on the belief that its waters held divine power. The city's fall and burial in the Waste is told in [[Whispers of the Sands]], [[Fizban]]'s in-world book about Athralis's history. The buried portal in the Lost City is a direct remnant of Athralis's divine connections.
