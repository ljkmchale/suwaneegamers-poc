# Bathaen Empire

**Type:** Empire / Political Entity  
**Status:** Active  
**Significance:** [[Harlowe]]'s current location; destination for Ren and Yvaine

## Overview

The Bathaen Empire is the region where [[Harlowe]] — the witch the party fought in [[Kravwynhold]] and [[Yvaine]]'s biological mother — fled after her defeat. [[Fizban]] (Tsuul'physs Bhan) confirmed her location there.

Both [[Ren (TCB)]] and [[Yvaine]] have active reasons to pursue Harlowe into the Bathaen Empire:
- Ren wants to confront Harlowe about her parents and avenge [[Tassarion Ranges]].
- Yvaine seeks answers about her mother, who she fought before knowing the truth.

## What Is Known

- Harlowe fled here after her defeat at Kravwynhold.
- Fizban confirmed her location to the party in Session 6.3.
- The Empire is also connected to the history of rune-tattooed children and Harlowe's operation.
