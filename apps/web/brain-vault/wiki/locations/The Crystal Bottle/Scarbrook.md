# Scarbrook

**Region:** Arbescar
**First visited:** Session 3.1

## Description

A town with an alchemist's shop (Erick's / Phantom Moon) that served as a front for [[A'urea'Pura Cult]] experimentation. [[Gwyneth]]'s forest is nearby. The party handed the shop over to [[Council of Six]] authorities after their investigation.

## Key Events

- **Session 3.1** — Recruited Yvaine (per Council request); investigated Erick's shop; snuck into cellar
- **Session 3.2** — Cleared cellar; handed shop to Council authorities
- **Session 4.1** — Investigated Erick's home; found safe; spoke with Gwyneth
