# Kravwynhold

**Region:** Arbescar
**First visited:** Session 6.1

## Description

A town where [[Harlowe]] the Witch was holding children captive. The party fought her here. [[Tassarion Ranges]] died saving [[Angela]] during the confrontation. The party fled via portal to [[Fizban's Tower]]. [[Grisha Thornhand]] (blacksmith, Ironclad Crucible) and [[Darveth]] (tiefling blacksmith, Emberfang) are notable townspeople. [[Viktor]] was Harlowe's enforcer in the town. [[Jaskin Varnn]] is another notable NPC encountered here. A magical shield around the town that fell in session 6.3.

## Key Events

- **Session 6.1** — Rescued children; Tass sacrificed himself saving Angela
- **Session 6.2** — Rallied townsfolk, final battle with Harlowe's minions, fled to Everlight via dragon
- **Session 6.3** — Magical shield around the town fell; townsfolk warned
