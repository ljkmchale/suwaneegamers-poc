# Everlight

**Region:** Northern Arbescar / mountain region
**First visited:** Session 5.5 (referenced); Session 5.6 (approached)

## Description

A snowy mountain town that was caught in a time warp. Conditions were improving from "Briscarn to Natali-like" as the party arrived. The time warp was removed in session 7 by [[Muerg]] and [[Ashe]]. Now connected to the rest of Arbescar by a new mountain pass Ashe carved. [[Fizban]] currently resides here.

Notable people in Everlight: [[Gretchen Lightfoot]] (innkeeper, Twinkling Fir inn), [[Toren Frostbrew]] (bartender), [[Leanna]] (temple priestess; received Tass's body), [[Erik the Ranger]] (warned the party about dangers in the caves). [[Angela]] (Ren's sister) is currently recovering here. [[Alteya]] stayed here with her family after the frozen grove was freed. [[Staff of the Slumbering Grove]] was acquired by [[Galtrelon]] in the cave expedition during Sessions 6.4�6.5.
