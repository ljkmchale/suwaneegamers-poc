# Tal'basar

**Region:** Arbescar
**First visited:** Session 1.2 (return)

## Description

A major city and home of [[Arbescar Light and Magic]] (ALM). Seat of the [[Council of Six]]. Increased security was noted in session 5.2 following [[Kal'dar]]'s confrontation. The party's [[ALM Crystal]] speed-travel is coordinated through ALM here.

## Key Events

- **Session 1.2** — Party returned after the temple incident
- **Session 5.2** — Investigated Carl's poster; learned Carl was sent home; Kal'dar confronted the town demanding surrender; party sided with the Council of Six
