# Mahruud

**Type:** Settlement / Region  
**Status:** Active  
**Significance:** [[Tassarion Ranges]]'s homeland; destination for his body

## Overview

Mahruud is the home of [[Tassarion Ranges]] and his family. After Tassarion died in [[Kravwynhold|Kravwynhold]] saving [[Angela]], the party arranged for his body to be returned to Mahruud so he could be given a proper burial among his people.

## Known Details

- Tassarion Ranges is from Mahruud.
- His parents live in Mahruud.
- [[Leanna]], the temple attendant in [[Everlight]], handled the funeral preparations and arranged for Tassarion's body to be sent there.
- [[Ren (TCB)]] committed to personally ensuring Tassarion's body reached his family in Mahruud.
