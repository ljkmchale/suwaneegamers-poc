# Haunted Mansion

**Region:** Arbescar (location per Cornelius's map)
**First visited:** Session 5.1

## Description

A ghost-guarded mansion claimed by the party as a hidden base after clearing it of ghosts and zombies. Contains loot chests with gold, bars, and magical stones. Now serves as the party's secret headquarters.

## Key Events

- **Session 5.1** — Fought ghost, cleared zombies and spirits, looted, claimed as party base; Ren received a vision to go to the Blustery Waste to save Angela
