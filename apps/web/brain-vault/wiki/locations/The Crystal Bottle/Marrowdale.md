# Marrowdale

**Type:** Town  
**Region:** [[Arbescar]]  
**Status:** Active (not revisited after Session 0)

## Overview

Marrowdale is the town where the campaign began. The party formed here at the local chapter of [[Arbescar Light and Magic]] during Session 0. It is the founding location of the party's L'idoneus Obscurado group.

## Session History

- **Session 0** — The party of five (Dalbyre Bramblesage, Opal Creekbed, Scarlytt Amyran, Sigil, Tassarion Ranges) formed here at [[Arbescar Light and Magic]]. They encountered L'idoneus Obscurado and began their adventure.
