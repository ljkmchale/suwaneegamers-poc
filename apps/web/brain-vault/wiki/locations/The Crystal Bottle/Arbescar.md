# Arbescar

**Type:** Region  
**Status:** Active  
**Campaign:** [[summaries/The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes|The Crystal Bottle - Campaign Player Notes]]

## Overview

Arbescar is the region in which most of The Crystal Bottle campaign takes place. It contains deserts, mountains, the Blustery Waste to the north, and multiple major settlements. [[Arbescar Light and Magic]] (ALM) is the primary magical institution of the region, headquartered in [[Tal'basar]].

## Key Settlements

- **Marrowdale** — Campaign starting location; site of ALM's founding chapter; where the party formed in Session 0.
- **[[Scarbrook]]** — Town where Erick the Alchemist operated; where Yvaine was recruited (Session 3.1).
- **[[Tal'basar]]** — Major city; home of ALM and the [[Council of Six]]; site of the Battle of Tal'basar (Session 5.2).
- **[[Kravwynhold]]** — Northern settlement; site of [[Harlowe]]'s witch operation; major battle in Session 6.2.
- **[[Everlight]]** — Mountain town; site of the Awakening; time-warped; [[Muerg]]'s declared protectorate.

## Key Geographic Features

- **[[Blustery Waste]]** — Major desert to the north; location of [[Thal'astyr]], the [[Lost City (Athralis)]], and the cult excavation site.
- **Eye of Arbescar** — Home of gold dragons [[C'mey]] and [[Wyndessa]]; location not precisely mapped.
- **[[Fizban's Tower]]** — Ancient tower in Longwood Forest; guarded by C'mey and Wyndessa.
- **[[Zeprock's Crown]]** — Small island off the southeastern coast; currently [[Kal'dar]]'s base.

## Regional Guardians

[[C'mey]] and [[Wyndessa]] are two gold dragons who serve as the guardians of Arbescar. They make their home in the Eye of Arbescar and patrol the region, engaging threats — including hostile dragons — that endanger the land. The party encountered them in Session 4.1 defeating the green dragon Elendore. They were reluctant to fight but bound to protect Arbescar.

## Key Organizations

- **[[Arbescar Light and Magic]]** — The region's primary magical institution; administers speed-travel crystals ([[ALM Crystal]]); connected to the [[Council of Six]].
- **[[Council of Six]]** — Governing council for the region; based in Tal'basar; party's primary institutional ally.
- **[[A'urea'Pura Cult]]** — Antagonist faction operating throughout Arbescar; responsible for the oasis attacks, cultist presence, and the attempted conquest of Tal'basar.
