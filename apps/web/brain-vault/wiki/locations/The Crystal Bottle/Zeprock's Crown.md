# Zeprock's Crown

**Type:** Island  
**Region:** Off the coast of Arbescar  
**Status:** Active — [[Kal'dar]] present  
**Significance:** [[Kal'dar]]'s base; seat of the unnamed D'jinn

## Overview

Zeprock's Crown is a small island off the southeastern coast of Arbescar. After his defeat at [[Kravwynhold]], [[Kal'dar]] fled here to answer to his master — the unnamed D'jinn who was freed from the [[The Crystal Bottle|Crystal Bottle]] when [[Malik]]'s skeleton released it.

## What Happened Here

- **Session 6.3:** After the Kravwynhold battle, Kal'dar's lieutenant revealed that Kal'dar had fled to Zeprock's Crown — a small island area off the coast — to answer for his failure. He was summoned back by the D'jinn (his master) after [[Malik]] (the air genasi vessel) was destroyed.

## Significance

Zeprock's Crown is where the campaign's primary remaining threat is consolidating. The unnamed D'jinn now rules from here, with Kal'dar as its agent. The party will likely need to reach this island to confront them.

## Key Connections

- **[[Kal'dar]]** — fled here after Kravwynhold; reporting to his master
- **[[The Crystal Bottle]]** — the D'jinn was freed from it; putting him back requires recovering the bottle, which is still in a cave linked to Kal'dar
- **[[Malik]]** — the air genasi who freed the D'jinn; now destroyed; the D'jinn has no vessel and acts as Kal'dar's direct master
