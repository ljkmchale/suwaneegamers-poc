# Thal'astyr

**Region:** Blustery Waste / near Everlight
**First visited:** Session 5.5

## Description

A ruined celestial structure with distinct rooms: mosaic/meditation room, courtyard, library, overgrown garden, living quarters, ritual chambers, and a Doorway. Connected to [[Everlight]]. Father Frostel was captured here. [[Alteya]] joined the party near here in Session 5.6 (Frostbite Mountain / ice castle).

## Notable Items Found Here

- **[[Ancient Guards Gauntlet]]** — recovered by [[Scarlytt Amyran|Scarlytt]] from guardian constructs (Sessions 5.3–5.5)
- **[[Sunblade Hilt]]** — found in the living quarters (Session 5.5); partially made of Dunduar ore
- **[[Cloak of the Dawn]]** — found in Thal'astyr

## Key Events

- **Session 5.5** — Explored all chambers; Sigil drew a card; discovered the Doorway; party proceeded to Frostbite Mountain
