# Brinecross

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Summary

Brinecross is a coastal hamlet southwest of Tratta, accessed covertly via a cliff cave near a crooked tree leading to a woodcutter shed. The party's first mission sent them there to contact Harold Stonesheep. They arrived during a zombie outbreak — general store fights, barricades, a dead dwarf rising in the stable — escaped on horses with the Long Night intelligence, and left several survivors behind. Harold ate the fish that started the outbreak and is now sick. The hamlet remains in active danger.

## Access Route

- From Tratta: cliff cave by a crooked tree → woodcutter shed → approach to town.
- Covert; designed to avoid being seen entering.

## Key Events (Session 02)

- **Zombie outbreak**: Already underway on arrival. General store fight; barricades.
- **Harold Stonesheep (H'arald)**: Found alive but sick in the stable. He ate the fish. Before collapsing, he passed the key phrase: **The Long Night**. Also had a backpack containing an ivory/statue object.
- **Dead dwarf in stable**: Rose as undead; the party killed it.
- **The fish connection**: The zombie contamination traces to fish. A "strange voice/water clue" was present. Herbs and vegetables from Linda may be relevant to understanding the cause.
- **Escape on horses**: Party left Brinecross on horseback.
- **Warehouse letter**: Found in Brinecross, addressed to Gultren the Canvas Master at the Sticky Pear Inn. Describes crates, chains, cages, fish, spice, people, guards, and a shipment in 20 nights. Reported to Thorin.

## Survivors Left Behind

- **Nora** — survivor; left behind.
- **Linda** — survivor; left behind; had herbs and vegetables that may explain the fish contamination.
- **Bairwin** — survivor; left behind.
- **Jenkins** — survivor; left behind.
- **Harold Stonesheep** — sick; left behind in stable. His condition is unknown.

## Open Questions

- What caused the zombie outbreak — fish contamination, curse, or a deliberate act tied to the trafficking operation?
- Did the survivors (Nora, Linda, Bairwin, Jenkins) escape or did the outbreak spread?
- Is the outbreak connected to the south warehouse and trafficking network?
- Is the fish and the Long Night warehouse letter's "fish" cargo the same supply chain?
- What is Harold's current condition?
