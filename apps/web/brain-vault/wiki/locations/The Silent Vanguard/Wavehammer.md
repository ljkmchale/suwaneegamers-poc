# Wavehammer

## Summary

The Wavehammer is an old naval vessel under repair docked in Tratta. It serves as the Silent Vanguard's cover identity and implied base of operations.

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Known Facts

- An old naval vessel; currently described as under repair.
- Master Thorne uses it as the party's cover story in Session 01.
- Provides legitimate-seeming reason for the party to be in the Tratta docks area.

## Open Questions

- Does the party actually use the ship for travel or only as a cover address?
- Who else knows the Wavehammer is a front?
