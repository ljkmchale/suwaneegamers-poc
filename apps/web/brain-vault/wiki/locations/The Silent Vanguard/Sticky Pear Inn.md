# Sticky Pear Inn

## Summary

The Sticky Pear Inn is a Tratta inn named in the warehouse letter as the delivery address for Gultren the Canvas Master.

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Known Facts

- Named as the address where the warehouse letter should be delivered to Gultren the Canvas Master.
- The party intercepted the letter in Brinecross and has not delivered it.
- May be a front, a meeting point, or simply a place where Gultren has a room.

## Open Questions

- Is Gultren currently at the Sticky Pear Inn waiting for the letter?
- Could the party use the letter as a way to approach Gultren under cover?
