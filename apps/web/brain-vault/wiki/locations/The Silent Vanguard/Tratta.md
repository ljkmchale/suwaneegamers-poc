# Tratta

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Summary

Tratta is a sandstone harbor city and the Silent Vanguard's base of operations. The party was briefed here in Session 01 and returned here after Brinecross to report to Thorin and level up. The city has an active information war between two broadsheets (Gilded Ledger vs Salt and Cinder Press) and is suspected to host or be adjacent to a colosseum trafficking operation.

## Physical Description

- Sandstone architecture; harbor city.
- Lower Dock markets — the party's first briefing location; **Master Thorne** met the party in a plain canvas tent near here.
- Docks area; multiple wharves.
- In-game date at Session 01: 18th of Ezale.

## Key Locations

| Location | Notes |
|---|---|
| Lower Dock markets | Master Thorne's briefing tent; Session 01 starting point |
| The Wavehammer | Old naval vessel under repair; party cover story and implied base |
| Sticky Pear Inn | Gultren the Canvas Master's address; warehouse letter destination |
| South warehouse (near old stone ramp) | Trafficking staging lead; 20-night shipment window |
| The Long Night | Suspected drug operation location; sign shows sleepy knight in armor, too big for the bed |
| Colosseum | Suspected trafficking endpoint; location within or near Tratta |

## Information Landscape

- **Gilded Ledger** — authority-friendly broadsheet; civic/power aligned.
- **Salt and Cinder Press** — raw anti-corruption broadsheet; opposition voice.
- Both named in Session 02 clues; they represent an active information war about whoever runs the trafficking and colosseum operations.

## The Silent Vanguard's Operations

- **Master Thorne** — handler; leads the organization in Tratta; briefed the party in the canvas tent.
- **Thorin** — reporting contact; the party briefed Thorin after returning from Brinecross.
- Party paid 50 gp each + 200 gp for Harold Stonesheep; leveled up after Session 02 report.

## Open Questions

- Where is the colosseum physically in Tratta — which district?
- Who knows the Wavehammer is a Silent Vanguard front?
- Is The Long Night an actual address in Tratta, or a code name?
- How does the Gilded Ledger's authority connection reach into civic government?
