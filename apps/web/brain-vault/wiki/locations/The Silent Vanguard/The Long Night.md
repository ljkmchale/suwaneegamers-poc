# The Long Night

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Summary

The Long Night is the key intelligence Harold Stonesheep passed to the party in Brinecross before collapsing. It refers to a suspected location in or near Tratta. The sign for the establishment shows a sleepy knight in armor, too big for the bed — suggesting it is a real physical location, likely an inn or tavern used as a cover for a drug operation or trafficking node.

## What Is Known

- Passed to the party by Harold Stonesheep in Session 02 as the intelligence he had been hired to hold.
- The sign shows a sleepy knight in armor, too big for the bed.
- The party's current interpretation: suspected drug operation location.
- The phrase "in 20 nights" in the warehouse letter may connect to The Long Night as the venue or operation name for the shipment's endpoint.
- Located in or near Tratta.

## Possible Roles

- An inn or tavern whose sign identifies it as a physical address.
- A front for drug distribution, launder money, or a human trafficking holding point.
- The endpoint or waystation for the south warehouse shipment.
- A meeting place used by figures in the trafficking/colosseum network.

## Open Questions

- Is The Long Night a place name, an operation name, or both?
- What drugs or contraband does it distribute?
- Who owns or operates it?
- Does the 20-night shipment window in the warehouse letter tie to The Long Night as the receiving venue?
- Has the party located it in Tratta yet?
