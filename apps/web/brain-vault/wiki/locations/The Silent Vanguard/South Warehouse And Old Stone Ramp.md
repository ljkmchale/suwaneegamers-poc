# South Warehouse And Old Stone Ramp

## Summary

The south warehouse near the old stone ramp is a major trafficking staging lead in Tratta. The warehouse letter recovered in Brinecross appears to document a shipment destined for this location.

## Campaign Scope

Silent Vanguard only.

## Known Facts

- Named in Session 02 as the destination for the Brinecross-intercepted warehouse letter.
- The letter references crates, chains, cages, fish, spice, people, guards, and a shipment in 20 nights.
- Assumed to be a staging area feeding into the colosseum network.
- Physical description: near an old stone ramp; likely a harbor-adjacent structure.
- Not yet visited by the party.

## Open Questions

- Who controls the warehouse?
- Is the 20-night shipment window still active or already passed?
- What exactly is being moved — people, animals, goods, or all three?
