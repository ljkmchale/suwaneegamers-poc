# A New Adventure Quick Reference

Campaign: A New Adventure

*A living quick-reference synthesized from the campaign's player notes, kept current with the latest session (37 - Halloween Treats, 29th of Paramor, 1246).*

## Current Status

The party ("The King's Outlanders") has just returned, bloodied and torn, from a bizarre Shadowfell/Feywild-tinged pocket realm accessed through a portal beneath The Bellows district of Basctdelm. They were led there by a possessed Relys (channeling an elder spirit of her destroyed home, Iparos), fought off a fiery "dark rider" on a flying horse and a monstrous cauldron-witch, and recovered several magic items (Terrifying Hood, Figure of Wonderous Power: Obsidian Steed, Jack-o-bolts, Witch's Sash, Cauldron Stir, Pet's Skull). The party is currently in Basctdelm, where they arrived via the Meridian faction to pursue multiple threads: Affy's search for her father (Oedabrin Thornage/Calden), Cerul's search for answers about his birthplace (Pekanon) and the mentor Sylvandor, and tracking the fate of the dangerous creature Vhaulmast, which has been rampaging through Halesworth before vanishing through a Shadowfell portal.

- **Pa Mottlebrook** – Halfling trader the party has repeatedly escorted; family now safe in Shademoor/Basctdelm-bound.

- **Nanise Rulendor** – Freed Ridley from House Rulendor service after exposing her cousin Urovil's infernal pact.

## Factions & Organizations

- **Rel'Udomina** – Secretive cult/criminal network ("Looms" led by Archons) that infiltrated O'naren, Qal'dynn, and the Abbey of Light; largely rooted out by the party.
- **Tegenwald Phet** – Magical order devoted to freedom, magic, and light; Cerul and Fungus have been invited to join.
- **The Meridian** – Faction based in Basctdelm (House of Myr) that Ridley now serves; helped escort the party.
- **The Keepers that Bind / Cult of Eternal Binding** – Worshippers of the demigod Malgri'n, operators of the Blackstone Crucible.
- **The Everdawn** – Druidic/ranger network Relys and Kaizo belong to.
- **House Rulendor** – Noble house of Caelora; Ridley's former employer, now severed ties after an infernal pact was exposed.
- **The Promissory** – Mysterious organization tied to Affy's father and Rueh'unar Heildove.
- **The Inherited** – Criminal gang ("take from the rich and keep it") operating in the Bathean Empire.

## Key Locations

- **Qal'dynn** – Elven capital city; site of the Sanctuary of the Three Elders spa (founded by Kaizo), Pale Lantern Inn, and the Academy.
- **O'naren** – Elven town where the party first rooted out Rel'Udomina influence.
- **Abbey of Light** – Monastic complex where the party freed the Abbey from Rel'Udomina control and discovered the Blackstone Crucible nearby.
- **The Blackstone Crucible** – Ancient black-stone temple/prison dedicated to Torec and the Cult of Eternal Binding; contains a portal (Anchoring Gate) linking to the Shadowfell and Varenwood.
- **Varenwood/Iparos** – Relys's destroyed gnome homeland; site of the failed Weaving Night ritual that opened the Shadowfell rift.
- **Caelora** – Oldest elven city; site of House Rulendor's fall and the party's harrowing trip to Stygia (Hell).
- **Adore** – Monastery of the Borealian Sway, recently destroyed by Rel'Udomina corruption; being rebuilt under Kaizo's new monk ally Taelis.
- **Basctdelm** – Current location; home of the Meridian, the Promissory, and reportedly Affy's father.
- **The Bellows** – A shadowy district beneath Basctdelm where a portal to a strange Feywild/Shadowfell hybrid realm was found.
