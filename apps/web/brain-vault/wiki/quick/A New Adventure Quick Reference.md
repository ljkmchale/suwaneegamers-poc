As of the 29th of Paramor, 1246, the party (now known as **The King's Outlanders**) has just arrived at a crossroads in Basctdelm (Greenscape/Central District), where a gabuk points out Affy to two threatening half-orcs — the session ended on this cliffhanger. The party recently tracked the monstrous **Vhaulmast** south from the ruined settlement of Bloodbeak, fought a Shadar-Kai strike team sent to bind it, and learned from spirits/divination that Vhaulmast will roam but is not an immediate threat to Qal'dynn. ## Factions & Organizations

- **Rel'Udomina** — Shadowy cult/thieves' network seeking to infiltrate and destabilize elven city-states (O'naren, Qal'dynn, the Abbey of Light); organized into "Looms" led by Archons; largely rooted out of Qal'dynn and the Abbey by the party.
- **The Tegenwald Phet** — Magical order championing freedom, magic, and light; Cerul and Fungus have been invited to join; Lorian is believed to be a member.
- **The Cloak of Defiance** — Thieves' guild/black market power in Myrdae, formed after overthrowing the Malfease before the Awakening.
- **The Everdawn** — A recruiting network of druids/nature protectors (Ferndale-based); Kaizo and Relys are members.
- **The Meridian** — Faction/guild with waypoint access across the Borealian Sway and into Basctdelm (House of Myr); Ridley carries their sigil; they investigate threats like Vhaulmast.
- **The Promissory** — Organization dealing in acquiring rare goods/artifacts; Affy's father has ties to it; Heildove also affiliated.
- **The Cult of Eternal Binding (Keepers that Bind)** — Ancient cult devoted to binding evil entities rather than destroying them; built the Blackstone Crucible; worship(ped) the old god Torec and serve Malgri'n.
- **House Rulendor** — Noble house of Caelora; secretly corrupted via a pact with the devil Ducrurith through Urovil Rulendor (now dead).
- **The Advents of Harmony** — Group connected to Affy's father, Oedabrin Thornage.

## Key Locations

- **Mossholde** — Hunting/trading outpost where the party first met.
- **The Broken Citadel** — Ruined structure with feywild portal, infernal summoning circle, and an ancient teleportation circle; birthplace of the Feywoven Glyphic cube.
- **O'naren** — Elven town; site of early Rel'Udomina investigations and the Silent Circle/Cackletongue Lair gnoll conflicts.
- **Qal'dynn** — Major elven city ruled by King Highvale; site of extensive Rel'Udomina purges; home to the Aetherion Academy and the Sanctuary of the Three Elders spa.
- **Abbey of Light** — Religious complex near the Cradlecrest Rise; home to Cerul's upbringing; site of Rel'Udomina infiltration and the Blackstone Crucible's cave entrance.
- **The Blackstone Crucible** — Ancient black-stone temple/dungeon dedicated to Torec, containing a portal to the Shadowfell and the Cult of Eternal Binding's prison chambers.
- **Varenwood / Iparos** — Relys's homeland; the gnome village of Iparos was destroyed when a corrupted ritual (the Weaving Night) opened a rift to the Shadowfell.
- **Caelora** — Oldest elven city in Myrdae; site of House Rulendor's corruption and the party's journey into Stygia (Hell) to rescue Urovil.
- **Stygia** — The frozen fifth layer of the Nine Hells, accessed via a cursed basin in House Rulendor's cellar; site of a major battle against devils.
- **Adore** — Monastery of the Borealian Sway, home to Kaizo's old order; recently destroyed/corrupted by Rel'Udomina influence and Heildove's estranged brother.
- **Southern Arch / Bloodbeak / Windbreak Camp** — Region devastated by the creature Vhaulmast on its rampage south.
- **Basctdelm** — Current location; home to the House of Myr (Meridian waypoint hub) and rumored location of Affy's father and Maester Sylvandor.
