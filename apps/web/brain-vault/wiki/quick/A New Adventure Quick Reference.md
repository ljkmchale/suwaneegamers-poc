## Current Status

The party (traveling as "The King's Outlanders," with Qal'dynn royal backing) is deep in Halesworth, tracking a monstrous entity called **Vhaulmast** that erupted from a Shadowfell rift and has been devastating the land — destroying Relys' home village of Iparos, Windbreak Camp, and the settlement of Bloodbeak. The party is pressing on, exhausted and under-rested, following Vhaulmast's trail south. ## Factions & Organizations

- **Rel'Udomina** — Secretive cult/thieves' network seeking to destabilize towns and leaders across Myrdae; organized into "Looms" led by Archons; heavily disrupted by the party in O'naren, Qal'dynn, and the Abbey of Light.
- **The Cloak of Defiance** — Thieves guild/black market power in Myrdae (O'naren region).
- **Tegenwald Phet** — Scholarly/magical order Cerul (and Fungus) have joined; focused on freedom, magic, and light.
- **The Meridian** — Investigative/enforcement organization Ridley has ties to; now assisting the party's travels.
- **The Promissory** — Organization that hires agents to acquire artifacts; employs Rueh'unar Heildove; tied to Affy's father.
- **House Rulendor** — Noble house of Caelora; formerly employed Ridley; exposed for infernal dealings.
- **The Everdawn** — Nature-protector group Relys has joined, recruited by Zaedra Windfury.
- **Cult of the Keepers That Bind (Blackstone Crucible)** — Ancient cultists who worship the demigod Malgri'n and bound Kael Vorduun; their Crucible connects to the Shadowfell and Varenwood.
- **Monks of Adore / Abbey of Light orders** — Religious/monastic institutions; Adore was recently destroyed by internal Rel'Udomina corruption and conflict.

## Key Locations

- **Qal'dynn** — Elven capital city; seat of King Highvale; home of the Sanctuary of the Three Elders spa (being built) and the Pale Lantern inn.
- **O'naren** — Trading town where the party first rooted out Rel'Udomina influence.
- **Abbey of Light** — Religious complex near Cradlecrest Rise; liberated from Rel'Udomina infiltration; has an arcane waypoint and ties to the stone giant clans and the Blackstone Crucible caves.
- **The Broken Citadel / Blackstone Crucible** — Ruins housing an ancient feywild portal, teleportation circle, and the Cult of Eternal Binding's temple; connects via portal to Varenwood and (formerly) the Shadowfell.
- **Varenwood / Iparos** — Relys' destroyed gnome homeland; site of the failed Weaving Night ritual that opened the Shadowfell rift.
- **Caelora** — Oldest elven city; site of House Rulendor's fall and the party's journey into Stygia (Hell).
- **Adore** — Monastery recently destroyed amid Rel'Udomina corruption and internal conflict.
- **Southern Arch / Bloodbeak / Windbreak Camp** — Region currently devastated by Vhaulmast; the party's current operating area.
- **Basctdelm** — Destination city; home to the Promissory and possibly Affy's father.
