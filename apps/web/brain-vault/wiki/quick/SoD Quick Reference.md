# SoD Quick Reference

Campaign: SoD

*This is a living quick-reference page, kept current with the campaign's player notes through Session 13.*

## Current Status (as of Session 13, 1st of Baegrum, 1246)
The party is inside the ruined **Scyldgar Keep** (north of Adsuren), having just defeated and killed **Ifryn Ciaradain** — Esylla's disgraced uncle and the assassin who mortally poisoned/wounded the archmage **Mollywop** during the Market Day finale in Adsuren. The party is deciding next steps: finish searching the Keep, travel to **Gibuldon** to confront the **Crimson Court** (who paid for the assassination) and the **Scyldgar** family, head south to **Caldwynn** (tied to Zephyra's fiendish mother and a lead on healing the dragon/Mollywop's poison), or investigate a necromancer's crypt in **Eustera Ridge**.

## Key NPCs
- **Mollywop the Magnificent** – Powerful archmage of Adsuren; stabbed and poisoned by an assassin at Market Day (Session 11); poison is nearly unhealable by magic; fate uncertain, being tended to by priests.
- **Ifryn Ciaradain** – Esylla's uncle, an assassin ("the Cockroach"/"the Disgraced") hired by the Crimson Court for 35,000gp to kill Mollywop; killed by the party at Scyldgar Keep (Session 12/13).
- **Aeolenne** – A succubus/fiend, Zephyra's true mother, freed from imprisonment beneath Adsuren Center when the Cult of the Immured was destroyed (Session 8); fled south toward Caldwynn.
- **Intendant Eyovar Dunajor** – Acting Intendant of Adsuren, secretly "Summoner Karranok," leader of the Cult of the Immured; surrendered and was taken into custody (Session 8).
- **Sir Rodolf Norran** – Minor noble from Gibuldon; ambushed Kenton over stolen silks and secrets Kenton overheard involving the Reyvennra house.
- **Captain Alinia Sarralei** – Head of the Adsuren guard; scarred, dismissive of Mollywop.
- **Rotvori (Nature's Oratory priest)** – Half-elf priest of Adsuren; ally to the party, aware of cult activity and graveyard disturbances.
- **Lix** – Mollywop's senior elven apprentice, named her successor.
- **Cabristrasz** – A young gold dragon corrupted by swamp fungus; slain by the party near a fisherman's hut (Session 10) after failed attempts to save it.
- **Lady Ellspeth** – Wealthy noblewoman encountered at Market Day; buys gems from Lila.
- **Peadar MacCaanain** – Adsuren dockmaster; helped trace a suspicious barrel shipment tied to the cult.
- **Almadia** – The town Tanner; her missing apprentice Ganon was a cultist (killed by the party).

## Factions & Organizations
- **Cult of the Immured** – Adsuren-based cult performing heart-sacrifice rituals to appease the fiend Aeolenne; dismantled Session 8.
- **The Crimson Court** – Mysterious group that paid Ifryn to assassinate Mollywop; identity/location unknown.
- **Red Jackals** – A gang from Tirncall; attacked Market Day and Mollywop's performance, led by the now-dead Brownwin Redmouth.
- **House Reyvennra (Vesci)** – Powerful noble house of Gibuldon; connected to Kenton's past and a warehouse fire/silk theft.
- **House Scyldgar** – Diminished noble family, former owners of Scyldgar Keep, tied to the Steoleahs of Glenleah.
- **Nature's Oratory** – Adsuren's temple, allied with the party.
- **Tegenwald Phet** – Group Mollywop once traveled/adventured with decades ago.

## Key Locations
- **Adsuren** – Home base village in Camburne Vec, Inverland; site of Market Day and the cult's downfall.
- **Mollywop's Tower** – Three-story misshapen tower near Adsuren.
- **Hath'lao Swamp / Sapcutt Plantation** – Fungal-corrupted swamp estate south across the river.
- **Scyldgar Keep** – Ruined keep north of Adsuren, current party location; site of undead guardians, gargoyles, a consecrated chapel, and Ifryn's death.
- **Gibuldon** – City with noble houses (Reyvennra); site of Kenton's past troubles.
- **Caldwynn** – City to the south; tied to Aeolenne's escape and rumors of a "dark presence" in a shrine to Amriel.
- **Eustera Ridge** – Site of the crypt of Jenku the Black, a legendary evil necromancer; source of the cursed sword Arcane Executioner.

## Recent Developments
- **Session 8:** Cult of the Immured exposed and destroyed beneath Adsuren Center; Intendant Eyovar Dunajor revealed as cult leader "Summoner Karranok" and arrested; the fiend Aeolenne was freed and fled south, briefly possessing/attempting to take Zephyra with her.

- **Session 12:** Party tracked Ifryn's blood trail to the ruined Scyldgar Keep, clearing undead skeletal guards and gargoyles; found a magic wand and a spectral warhorse.
- **Session 13:** Party cleared more of the Keep (chapel, crypts, a mummified guardian), recovered a Mace of Disruption, and confronted and killed Ifryn Ciaradain, learning he was hired by the Crimson Court for 35,000gp and that he retrieved the cursed sword from the crypt of the necromancer Jenku the Black in Eustera Ridge.
