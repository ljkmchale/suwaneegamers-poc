# SoD Quick Reference

Campaign: SoD

*This is a living quick-reference page, kept current with the latest campaign notes.*

## Current Status
It is the 6th of Baegrum, 1246. The party set out from Adsuren toward Caldwynn (via Tirncall) to pursue leads on Zephyra's fiendish mother, but a sudden storm forced them to shelter at a roadside inn, the **Everest Inn**. The inn has proven to be an extradimensional trap: doors lead to shifting hallways, banquet halls, and impossible spaces, and at least two other guests (a retired illusionist dragonborn, Aldric, and a tailor, Ciliren Maquin) have been stuck there for years, unaware of the passage of time. A young half-orc girl, Shogi, who draws recurring "doors," appears tied to the phenomenon. Esylla has cast Dispel Magic twice on the structure — the first attempt caused the building to flood, the second caused the entire inn to tilt and begin "falling" through open sky. The session ended here, mid-crisis.

## Key NPCs
- **Mollywop the Magnificent** — Powerful mage who protected Adsuren with wards; assassinated at Market Day (Session 11) by a hired killer; her body is being toured through the region by her old ally Bertholdt Yaseina.
- **Aeolenne** — Succubus/fiend revealed to be Zephyra's true mother; freed from imprisonment beneath Adsuren Center when the Cult of the Immured was destroyed; tried to take Zephyra with her and fled south (toward Caldwynn) after a battle.
- **Ifryn Ciaradain** — Esylla's disgraced uncle; the assassin hired to kill Mollywop for 35,000gp by "the Crimson Court"; tracked to Scyldgar Keep and killed by the party (Session 13); his body was later burned by Esylla's Aunt Ree.
- **Aunt Ree** — Esylla's aunt, a powerful spellcaster who can reach Esylla telepathically; quietly intervened to destroy Ifryn's remains and warned Therric to keep her visit secret.
- **Intendant Eyovar Dunajor** — Adsuren's former Intendant, secretly "Summoner Karranok," leader of the Cult of the Immured; confessed and surrendered to the party (Session 8); now in custody.
- **Alaric** — Mollywop's first apprentice, a necromancer of the Tegenwald Phet; took control of the slain gold dragon's skeleton and possesses knowledge of the necromancer Jenku's crypts; gave Therric a sending stone.
- **Lux (Lix Oriororos)** — Mollywop's elven apprentice, now overseeing her tower.
- **Captain Alinia Sarralei** — Head of the Adsuren Guard; resentful of Mollywop/magic users; short-staffed and underfunded.
- **Rotvori** — Priest of the Nature's Oratory in Adsuren.
- **Mayless Poysell** — A mysterious, ancient entity dwelling in Lila's crystal; claims to be a long-dead fortune teller; is guiding Lila toward Celemere, Midram Bay, and Daerswell.
- **Sir Rodolf Norran** — Minor Gibuldon noble who ambushed Kenton over stolen silks and ties to the Reyvennra house.
- **Shogi** — A young half-orc girl encountered at the Everest Inn who seems tied to its shifting magic.
- **Kane Elword, Aldric, Ciliren Maquin** — Other occupants/trapped guests of the Everest Inn.

## Factions & Organizations
- **Cult of the Immured** — Blood-ritual cult operating beneath Adsuren Center, led by the Intendant; dismantled by the party (Sessions 5–8).
- **The Crimson Court** — Shadowy group (believed based out of Gibuldon) that paid Ifryn to assassinate Mollywop; identity/motives still unknown.
- **Luminary Concord** — Mollywop's old adventuring company (Nashtil, Mollywop, Bertholdt Yaseina, Ariah).
- **Tegenwald Phet** — Order of magic users Mollywop was once affiliated with; Alaric is a ranking necromancer within it.
- **Reyvennra (Vesci) family** — Powerful noble house of Gibuldon tied to Kenton's past and the stolen silks.
- **Scyldgar/Steoleah family** — Former lords of the ruined keep found south of Adsuren; once linked to the Rayvenra/Glenleah bloodlines.
- **Red Jackals** — Gang from Tirncall whose leader, Brownwin Redmouth, attacked during the Market Day assassination attempt.

## Key Locations
- **Adsuren** — The party's home base in Camburne Vec, Inverland; site of most early adventures.
- **Nature's Oratory** — Adsuren's temple, home to priest Rotvori.
- **Mollywop's Tower (and new necromantic tower)** — Magical residence outside Adsuren, now jointly overseen by Lux and Alaric.
- **Hath'lao Swamp / Sapcutt Plantation** — Swamp territory where the fungal corruption and the "Sapcutt family" incident occurred; later the lair of a corrupted gold dragon.
- **Scyldgar Keep** — Ruined keep south of Adsuren tied to Therric's visions, Ifryn's hideout, and the Scyldgar/Steoleah family history.
- **Caldwynn** — River city to the south; destination tied to Aeolenne and Zephyra's heritage.
- **Gibuldon** — City tied to Kenton's past, the Reyvennra house, and possibly the Crimson Court.
- **Tirncall** — Troubled port town the party must pass; ships are reportedly being turned away.
- **Eustera Ridge** — Site believed to hold one of the necromancer Jenku's crypts.
- **Unstead** — Home of the scribe Kasi Um'cata, who studied Ifryn's antimagic condition.
- **The Everest Inn** — Current location; an extradimensional, shifting roadside inn trapping multiple guests.

## Active Quests & Objectives
- Escape the Everest Inn and resolve the mystery of its shifting rooms and trapped guests (Shogi, Aldric, Ciliren).
- Travel to Caldwynn to pursue Aeolenne and help Zephyra understand/control her fiendish heritage.
- Investigate the Crimson Court and discover who ordered Mollywop's assassination.
- Follow up with the Reyvennra family in Gibuldon regarding Kenton's past and the stolen silks.
- Investigate Jenku the Necromancer's crypts (locations provided by Alaric via sending stone).
- Investigate the scribe Kasi Um'cata in Unstead regarding Ifryn's antimagic nature.
- Fulfill Mayless Poysell's guidance for Lila — travel to Celemere, Midram Bay, and Daerswell to grow strong enough to "face the thing that put [Mayless] here."
