## Active Quests

- **Track Aeolenne** — She escaped Adsuren heading south with a Caldwynn ally. She is Zephyra's biological mother and the power behind the Cult of the Immured. Priority threat.
- **Zephyra's Mind-Control Vulnerability** — Aeolenne demonstrated she can dominate Zephyra. The party needs high-level/expensive protection. Mollywop said it is possible but costly.
- **The Mines / Bulas Lead** — A white-horned creature called a bulas and shadow creature sightings near the mines outside Adsuren. The party is en route.
- **Cult of the Immured Network** — Cultists came from Caldwynn, Gibuldon, Morrey, and other towns. The network extends beyond Adsuren.
- **Market Day** — Returning to Adsuren for the public Market Day event and its political fallout from the cult revelation.

## Setting

Adsuren, Inverland, Myrdae, year 1246. The city of Adsuren is in Camburne Vec. The party has operated in: the Market, Nature's Oratory (above and below), the Graying Grotto, Adsuren's civic center cellar, and Zephyra's house. The mines are their next destination.
