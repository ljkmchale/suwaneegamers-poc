# SoD Quick Reference

Campaign: SoD

*A living quick-reference synthesized from the campaign's player notes, kept current through the latest recorded session.*

## Current Status
The party (known publicly as the "Heroes of the Well," and later credited with defeating Mollywop's assassin) is departing Adsuren on the 4th of Baegrum, 1246, heading south via Tirncall toward Caldwynn to investigate leads on Zephyra's demonic mother, the Crimson Court, and the noble family behind Scyldgar Keep. Adsuren is in mourning and political turmoil following Mollywop's assassination during Market Day; the town is short on guards and funds, a necromancer (Alaric) has erected a new tower and controls a reanimated skeletal gold dragon to help protect the town, and Mollywop's body is being toured to her old adventuring companions before final rites. The cult (Cult of the Immured) has been destroyed, and the town Intendant (secretly "Summoner Karranok") has been arrested.

## Key NPCs
- **Mollywop the Magnificent** – Powerful archmage of Adsuren, former member of the Luminary Concord and the Tegenwald Phet; assassinated during Market Day (Session 11) by Ifryn Ciaradain; her body is being carried around Inverland by her old ally Bertholdt Yaseina.
- **Ifryn Ciaradain** – Esylla's uncle, an assassin ("the Cockroach") hired by the Crimson Court for 35,000gp to kill Mollywop; killed by the party at Scyldgar Keep (Session 12).
- **Aeolenne** – A succubus/fiend, Zephyra's biological mother, freed from imprisonment beneath Adsuren Center when the cult was destroyed; fled south with unknown intentions.
- **Eyovar Dunajor** – Former Intendant of Adsuren, secretly "Summoner Karranok," leader of the Cult of the Immured; arrested.
- **Alaric** – Necromancer, Mollywop's first apprentice and Magister of Necromancy in the Tegenwald Phet; erected a new tower near Adsuren and controls the reanimated corpse of the corrupted gold dragon; provided Therric a sending stone and leads on Jenku the Black's crypts.
- **Lix (Lux) Oriororos** – Half-elf apprentice of Mollywop, now overseeing her tower.
- **Captain Alinia Sarralei** – Head of the Adsuren town guard; understaffed and underfunded.
- **Rotvori** – Priest of the Nature's Oratory in Adsuren.
- **Aunt Ree** – Esylla's aunt, contacted via Mollywop's sending spell; arrived in Adsuren, provided gems/gold and info on noble houses; disposed of Ifryn's body.
- **Sir Rodolf Norran** – Minor noble from Gibuldon who ambushed Kenton over stolen silks and secrets Kenton overheard about an "overthrow" involving House Reyvennra.
- **Jackin & Margaret Sapcutt** – Halfling pie-making family in the swamp whose household was overrun by a sentient fungal parasite (FUKWAD); rescued by the party.
- **Naz'grim** – Half-orc armorsmith in Adsuren.

## Factions & Organizations
- **Cult of the Immured** – Secret cult in Adsuren performing heart-sacrifice rituals to the entity Aeolenne; dismantled by the party.
- **Crimson Court** – Shadowy organization (likely based in Gibuldon) that paid for Mollywop's assassination; not yet identified or located.
- **Luminary Concord** – Mollywop's former adventuring party (Nashtil - leader, Mollywop - abjurer, Bertholdt Yaseina - priest of Athuel, Ariah - scout).
- **Tegenwald Phet** – Powerful magical order Mollywop was once part of; Alaric is its Magister of Necromancy.
- **House Reyvennra** – Powerful noble house (Vesci) in Gibuldon connected to Kenton's past and the overheard conspiracy involving a bound dwarf and talk of "overthrow."
- **Scyldgar/Steoleah family** – Ruined noble bloodline once tied to House Rayvenra, former owners of Scyldgar Keep, descended from the Glenleah line.
- **Red Jackals** – Gang from Tirncall, led by Brownwin Redmouth, involved in the Market Day assault on Mollywop.

## Key Locations
- **Adsuren** – Main base of operations in Camburne Vec territory, Inverland.
- **Nature's Oratory** – Local temple in Adsuren; site of crypts and tunnels connected to the well incident.
- **Mollywop's Tower** – Original tower of Mollywop; now overseen by Lix, with Alaric's new necromantic tower built nearby.
- **Hath'lao Swamp / Sapcutt Plantation** – Site of the fungal corruption (FUKWAD) and origin of the tainted well water.
- **Scyldgar Keep** – Ruined keep north of Adsuren, ancestral home of the Scyldgar/Steoleah family; site of Ifryn's death and numerous supernatural encounters (ghosts, gargoyles, undead).
- **Caldwynn** – City south of Adsuren; destination for investigating Zephyra's mother and cult connections.
- **Gibuldon** – City tied to Kenton's past, House Reyvennra, and the Crimson Court.
- **Eustera Ridge** – Location of one of necromancer Jenku the Black's crypts, to be investigated.
- **Unstead** – Home of the scribe Kasi Um'cata, mentioned by Ifryn before his death.

## Active Quests & Objectives

- Travel to Caldwynn to investigate Zephyra's fiendish heritage and Aeolenne's whereabouts/allies.
- Investigate Jenku the Black's other necromancer crypts (via Alaric's information and sending stone).
