# D&D 2024 Spell Hierarchy Reference

Campaign: World

This reference explains the spell hierarchy symbols used by the Campaign Brain website. It is a rules-reference layer, not campaign lore.

## How To Read A Spell

In D&D 2024, each spell has a school of magic and may appear on one or more class spell lists. The school describes what kind of magic the spell is. The class list describes which spellcasting classes normally have access to it.

Example: **Wizard + Divination** means the spell appears on the Wizard spell list and belongs to the Divination school.

## Divination Prototype

The website prototype uses Divination as the first fully laid-out school.

### Divination Symbol

The eye rune represents Divination. It means magic that reveals information: omens, detection, hidden truths, remote viewing, understanding, prophecy, or supernatural perception.

### Divination Path

The Divination page is designed as the **Path of the Open Eye**. The path moves from small nudges of fate to direct communion, remote sight, perfect perception, and prophecy.

Read each tier left to right:

- **Level marker** — the spell level or cantrip tier.
- **Theme branch** — the spell's function, such as Detection, Omens, Mind, Remote Senses, or Fate / Prophecy.
- **Spell card** — the individual spell.
- **Theme rune** — a compact visual cue for the spell's role.

The long-term model is one scroll per school of magic. Each school should have its own crest image, school palette, thematic branches, and full spell-card hierarchy.
