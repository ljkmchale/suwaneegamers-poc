# HoE Quick Reference

Campaign: HoE

This page is a fast-lookup reference for the most commonly asked questions about the Heroes of Emberstran campaign. It is a living document — update after each session.

## Current Status

The party has returned to Emberstran after entering Moganna's vault beneath Mount Emberstran. Ainslie took the heart-shaped vault gem and is recovering under Diverra's light at the temple. In-game date: 44th of Talil, 1246.

## Active Quests

- **Soulroot and Starvein** — The vault heart gem is only one of three relic pieces. Irragosa names the other two as Soulroot and Starvein; their locations are unknown.
- **Ainslie and the Heart Gem** — Ainslie was meant to take the vault gem, but contact exhausted him and required recovery under Diverra's light.
- **The Vanished Crew** — Crew went missing at the Emberstran docks early in the campaign. Still unresolved.
- **Hap's Father's Murderers** — A personal thread for Hap. The identity and whereabouts of those responsible are unresolved.
- **Ulgrey Trouble** — Phira is approaching from Ulgrey, Arnol was ambushed, and Tessa says the Etterset has had misfortunes there.

## Open Plot Threads

- Where are Soulroot and Starvein?
- What is the heart gem's full power, cost, and relation to Ahn'Tsuel's 50-year cycle?
- The Blackrend — nature and threat level unknown.
- Diverra's Grove / Meladra's Grove — location and significance unresolved.
- Zombie ship connected to the vanished crew.
- Assassination at Old Oak & Grapes — perpetrator and motive.
- **Zymve's armor work for Og** — In Session 24, Zymve used the Whisper Forge at Sparkwhisper to enhance and resize scale mail Og looted from fire newt enemies. Whether the work is complete and what properties the armor has are unresolved.
- **Aury and Bloom Aeterna** — In Session 19, Aury received Bloom Aeterna — Diverra's complete original teachings — directly from Diverra's Lost Sanctuary. He has not told High Priestess Meladra. He senses that because he was chosen to receive it, she may not be worthy of it. The implications for his relationship with Meladra and his standing with Diverra are unresolved.
- **Flying ship orb** — Ky'tha recovered a transmutation orb with floating stone fragments from the crashed flying ship; Hap connects it to levitation/lodestone work by Iannan Rambibur of Tarnsport.

## Setting

Emberstran, Baltwood Territory, Myrdae, year 1246. The city of Emberstran sits at the base of Mount Emberstran. Key locations: the Dyed Cane, the docks, Headless Hydra tavern, Old Oak & Grapes, the Vintner's Guild and its cellar, and the temple district.
