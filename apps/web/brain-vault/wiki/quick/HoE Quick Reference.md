# HoE Quick Reference

Campaign: HoE

This is a living quick-reference synthesized from the HoE campaign player notes and kept current with the latest session.

## Current Status

As of the end of Session 37 (7th of Paramor, 1246), the party has left Basctdelm and is traveling the south road toward Padstow, having just arrived at a large building known as the Heavy Loaf. Hap has formally joined the Meridian (inheriting Meren Bahndoso's platemail) and is now seeking the lost **Iron Ledger** of the Iron Brotherhood, last known to be hidden somewhere in his hometown near Emberstran. Ky'tha has been recruited into the Cloak of Defiance at high rank, serving the Shadow Sovereign (Landra) as a double agent while still allied with the Etterset. Hap's sister Phira has settled safely in Basctdelm with a new partner, Vesper Sall of the Promissory. The party acquired six horses (one magical, able to transform into a figurine) via Cloak resources and is being trailed/watched by unknown parties. Og is bonded to the Ascension Tree (Alferumn) as its guardian ("E'erspan") and is seeking the druid Dandelia of the Veil for guidance. Ainslie carries the Emberheart (a piece of the Ahn'tsuel relic) and is searching for the other two pieces (Soulroot and Starvein) tied to dragon-rider lineages. The group is also pursuing a blight threat reported near Murfield/Shademoor and lingering business with Guigzien, a freed demigod of muck/swamp.

- **Lady Tarathial "Lady T" Rulendor** – Steward of the Vintner's Guild; retained her position after the Council vote.
- **Lady Bidva "Rook" Rook** – Tabaxi noble who sought (and won) control of the Vintner's Guild; secretly allied with multiple factions; holds leverage over the party via Phira's late husband's brother's family ties.
- **Imonorra (Immonara)** – Reclusive wizard, Magistrate of Emberstran, resides in the Tower; ally of Lady Rook.
- **Landra, the Shadow Sovereign** – New leader of the Cloak of Defiance in Emberstran/Basctdelm; recruited Ky'tha; seeks the Iron Ledger.
- **Phira Garemond** – Hap's long-lost sister; formerly of Trailpoint, now settled in Basctdelm with Vesper Sall.
- **Vesper Sall** – Tiefling member of the Promissory (artifact-recovery organization); Phira's new partner.
- **Irragosa the Griever** – Ancient bronze dragon/former dragon rider's mount; guardian of the Emberheart Vault; ally to the party.
- **Thaelex** – Powerful wizard of the Meridian, met in Ulgrey; gave Zymve a sending stone.
- **Meren Bahndoso** – High-ranking Meridian official in Basctdelm; knew Benoit Garemond; gifted Hap his armor.
- **Neris** – Former Iron Brotherhood member running the Iron Star tavern in Basctdelm; pointed Hap toward Myranda Fenberry.
- **Priestess Meladra** – High Priestess of the Temple of Diverra in Emberstran.
- **Arnol** – Former acolyte, briefly entangled with the Eradic of Ghone cult; sent to Stoneshore/Paendley for a fresh start.
- **Tessa / Belavie Lightstrider** – Etterset contacts in Emberstran; run "Our Exquisites."
- **Paeris** – Wizard operating the Tower (Gilded Vulture-linked) in Ulgrey; ambiguous ally.
- **Og's former captors** – The Gilded Vultures, led by blue dragonborn Vilmuss, still hunting Og and Ainslie.
- **Blackrend** – Corrupted undead druid, source of blight magic; defeated at the Lost Shrine to Guigzien (Session 33).
- **Benoit Garemond** – Hap's late father; former keeper of the Iron Brotherhood's Iron Ledger; his death remains tied to the Cloak and the Cetaen family debt.

## Factions & Organizations

- **Cloak of Defiance** – Criminal/thieves organization; now led by Landra (the Shadow Sovereign) after Jerik's death; Ky'tha is a ranked double agent.
- **Etterset** – Rival thieves'/information network; allied loosely with Ky'tha via Tessa and Belavie.
- **Vintner's Guild** – Emberstran's wine trade authority; power struggle between Lady T and Lady Rook (Rook prevailed).
- **The Meridian** – Order of knights/scholars tracking threats across Myrdae; Hap is now a member; maintains the House of Myr and Tower of Myr waypoints.
- **Advents of Harmony** – Scholarly/information network (Argent Halls) based out of the Oratory of Ulgrey; Ainslie was invited to join.
- **The Promissory** – Artifact-recovery organization; Vesper Sall's employer.
- **Gilded Vultures** – Mercenary group led by Vilmuss; hunting Og and Ainslie.
- **Eradic of Ghone** – Cult of Sijulous Ghone; largely destroyed by the party (Sessions 22-26).
- **Weftirin ("Murder" and "Skulk" groups)** – Traveling gypsy/hippie caravans encountered on the road; lost a member (Finn) to harpies.
- **Nunglthil enclaves** – Underdark trading hub factions: Kar'Solar, Grol/Thun, Selraeth, Durrak, Glimmerdale.

## Key Locations

- **Emberstran** – Coastal volcanic city; party's home base.
- **Mount Emberstran** – Volcano containing the Emberheart Vault, guarded by Irragosa.
- **Bloomrest (Lost Sanctuary of Diverra)** – Hidden original temple of Diverra, reached via undersea arcane waypoint; home to the Ascension Tree/Alferumn.
- **Diverra's Grove / Southern Baltwood** – Site of a cured blight and an arcane waypoint (Diverra followers only).
- **Nunglthil** – Underdark trading hamlet on the lake Rothenloch.
- **Thallgrove / Bimblefol** – Fungal Underdark region; home of the genling village Bimblefol.
- **Ulgrey** – Trade town; home of the Riverdog Inn, the Oratory, and Paeris's Tower.
- **Lost Shrine to Guigzien** – Swamp temple southwest of Ulgrey; site of the Blackrend/aboleth battle.
- **Basctdelm** – Major city; home to the House/Tower of Myr, the Bellows (Cloak territory), the Crimson Seat, and the Temple of Roses.
- **The Bellows** – Dangerous undercity district of Basctdelm controlled by the Cloak of Defiance.
- **Padstow / The Heavy Loaf** – Current location, en route to investigate the blight and Hap's hometown search.

- Find Hap's father's hidden **Iron Ledger** in his swamp hometown (consult elder Myranda Fenberry) to satisfy the Cloak's demand and protect Phira.
- Investigate and resolve the spreading **blight** near Murfield/Shademoor.
- Og must find **Dandelia of the Veil** (druid of Kahlbit's Veil) for guidance on his role as guardian of the Ascension Tree.

- Investigate the aboleth and the ritual that freed **Guigzien**, a demigod now loose in the world.
