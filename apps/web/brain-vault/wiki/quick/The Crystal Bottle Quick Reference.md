# The Crystal Bottle Quick Reference

Campaign: The Crystal Bottle

Fast-lookup reference for The Crystal Bottle campaign. Update after each session.

## Current Status

The party is in the [[Lost City (Athralis)]] (see [[Athralis And The Oasis]]) in the Blustery Waste confronting [[Malik]]. He has chained angel [[Aurelion]] inside a broken golden-white portal powered by two cracked blue godstones. [[Yvaine]] learned from Aurelion (in Celestial) that the key around Malik's neck will free him. In-game date: 33rd of Hemist, 1227 (2AM).

## Active Quests

- **Free [[Aurelion]]** — Get the key from [[Malik]]'s neck; unlock the angel to end the confrontation
- **Defeat Malik** — Djinn-hollowed entity seeking to ascend to the celestial plane
- **Kill [[Kal'dar]]** — [[Fizban]]'s former apprentice; fled to **Zeprock's Crown** (island off Arbescar coast); called there by the D'jinn to answer for his failure
- **Kill [[Harlowe]]** — Witch, Yvaine's mother; in the Bathaen Empire; T'suul confirmed location
- **Put the D'jinn back in the bottle** — [[The Crystal Bottle|Crystal Bottle]] inscribed: "Death to whomever frees him; wishes to whomever puts him back" — **bottle still unrecovered; in a cave linked to Kal'dar**
- **Stop the [[A'urea'Pura Cult]]** — Air genasi cult excavating the Blustery Waste; have portal stone key (Ren holds it)
- **Find Alteya's husband and child** — Missing 25 years; Gal gave Alt her husband's journal; partially resolved (freed from frozen grove in 6.5) — confirm full reunion
- **Retrieve Sigil's mother's sword** — In a cave in Nesbitt's Grove, east of Devil's Fork; tasked by Fizban
- **Return Tass's body to his family** — Family is in Mahruud
- **Identify Erick's two keys** — Taken from Erick's alchemist shop in [[Scarbrook]]; unknown locks
- **Find Sunblade smith** — [[Sunblade Hilt]] ([[Yvaine]]) is partially made of Dunduar ore; needs a skilled smith beyond the Blustery Waste mountains

## Key NPCs

- **[[Fizban]]** (Tsuul'physs Bhan) — Artificer; party patron and advisor; freed from Kal'dar's trap; Gwyneth's father; in [[Everlight]] (sess. 6.3)
- **[[Gwyneth]]** — Dryad of [[Scarbrook]]; **daughter of Fizban**; has lived hundreds of years ([[Fizban Tower Vault Items|Elixir of Timelessness]]); has a special upcoming role (secret with Scarlytt)
- **[[Lothim]]** — Silver dragon wyrmling; friendly; knows Wyndessa and C'mey
- **[[Malik]]** — Current main villain; air genasi possessed/hollowed by the D'jinn; now demonic; in the Lost City
- **[[Kal'dar]]** — Fizban's former apprentice; sought immortality; fled to **Zeprock's Crown** (island off Arbescar coast)
- **[[Harlowe]]** — Witch; Yvaine's mother; enemy; in Bathaen Empire
- **[[Aurelion]]** — Angel; chained in Lost City portal by Malik; waiting to be freed
- **[[Muerg]]** — Old God who appeared at [[Everlight]] declaring protection; announced the "Awakening"
- **Ashe** — Muerg's companion; gifted Yvaine +2 armor; channels divine flame
- **[[Angela]]** — Ren's **sister**; held unconscious 10 years by Harlowe; rescued session 6.1; now in Everlight recovering (PTSD)
- **[[Tassarion Ranges]]** (Tass) — Deceased elf cleric; died saving Angela; body to be returned to family in Mahruud
- **[[Morath]]** — "The shadow seer, weaver of secrets, servant of Iuz'Obal"; has watched Sigil since birth; message delivered to Narris by Fizban (session 6.3)
- **[[Grisha Thornhand]]** — Human blacksmith, [[Kravwynhold]]; mechanical gauntlet hand; declared new town leader after Viktor's death; heading to [[Tal'basar]]
- **[[Darveth]]** — Tiefling blacksmith (Emberfang, Kravwynhold); knows of a smith beyond the mountains who can repair the [[Sunblade Hilt]]
- **[[Serrenia Netherfall]]** — ALM Head Mistress; contact for Lothim
- **[[Belzander Umbridge]]** — ALM Head Master; wants blue crystals returned
