# Bloody Endeavor Quick Reference

Campaign: Bloody Endeavor

This page is a fast-lookup reference for the most commonly asked questions about the Bloody Endeavor campaign (party name: Wyrm Bane). It is a living document — update after each session.

## Current Status

The party is in **Khagdaruhl** (KAG-DA-RRULE), the dwarven mountain kingdom in the Yag'vur Sierra, for a private audience with **Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV**. The audience is ongoing at the end of Session 35. The Lord has heard: that Huldi of the Cold Breath stole the Runegrip clan's piece of the Rimestone; that an eldritch undead aberration lurks beneath the mountains; and that the party represents Magistrate Oddnel of Witguard. Dalasor Coast ambassadors are also in Khagdaruhl competing for dwarven allegiance. In-game date: 22nd of Paramor, 1246.

## Active Quests

- **Stop the Cold Breath** — Huldi and the Monks of Iohir are assembling the three pieces of the Rimestone to cause a new ice age. They have Piece 1 (stolen from Runegrip). The party must prevent them from getting Pieces 2 and 3.
- **Recruit the Dwarves for Rostiena** — Magistrate Oddnel of Witguard sent the party to bring the Runegrip clan and the kingdom of Khagdaruhl into alliance with the Rostiena Foothills against the Malithea Empire (Dalasor Coast). The audience with Thundermaul IV is the current step.
- **The Eldritch Threat** — An ancient undead aberration is sealed beneath the Yag'vur Sierra. It causes nightmares in the Duergar of Khagdaruhl and a vision showed tendrils binding the Dwarven King. The party must warn and potentially help address it.
- **Albross's Brother** — Breemos, Albross's brother, is at risk in Gevakaln from the Sable Shadows criminal organization.

## Open Plot Threads

- **The Rimestone** — Three pieces: Piece 1 with Huldi (stolen). Piece 2 location unknown (possibly in Khagdaruhl). Piece 3 location unknown. Huldi needs all three.
- **The Eldritch Aberration** — Sealed under the mountains, awakening. Affects dwarven minds through nightmares. Albross's patron showed tendrils binding Thundermaul IV.
- **The Rostiena / Dalasor War** — Malithea Empire (Dalasor Coast) is building armies on the Rostiena border. Witguard is the flashpoint.

- **Rhody's Spirit Mark (Sennogam)** — Rhody is marked by the spirit Sennogam after Session 30. Consequences unknown.

- **Sir Duvall** — Outstanding thread.

## The Rimestone

A three-piece dwarven artifact originally created to freeze and seal the undead eldritch aberration beneath the Yag'vur Sierra. The Cold Breath wants to reassemble it to cause a new ice age.

## Setting

Rostiena Foothills, Myrdae, year 1246. Key locations: Climbor (major city), Witguard (frontier town and quest base), Yag'vur Sierra mountains, Khagdaruhl (current location), Hol'gardur dwarven outpost, Fort Mornock, Madam Mabel's Orchard.
