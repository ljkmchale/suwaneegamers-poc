## Current Status
It is the 30th of Paramor, 1246. A botched Teleport spell cast by Ambassador Graythane in Khagdaruhl (Session 40) split the party: Lucerion, along with allies Armaros Eligora and Kotio Etric of the Sacred Crows, was sent to a hilltop overlooking Nurador amid orc encampments, while Caelion, Albross, Rhody, Pagern, and Mawl "Maw" Cargwyn were stranded on the prison island of Sciogost off the coast of Gevakaln. Lucerion's status and whereabouts near Nurador are currently unresolved in the notes.

## Key NPCs
- **Madam Mabel** – Rhody's elderly fey witch patron; cured Rhody's plague-curse in exchange for tasks; Rhody's soul remains bound to her even after death.
- **Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV** – Ruler of the dwarven kingdom Khagdaruhl; allied with the party, agreed to stay neutral in the Rostiena/Dalasor war, and gifted the reforged sword Kretopulus.
- **Ambassador Udohi Greythane** – Khagdaruhl ambassador who sponsors the party; his mis-cast Teleport spell split the group.
- **Huldi ("The Coldbreath")** – Leader of the Monks of Iohir/Cult of the Coldbreath, sought the three Rimestone shards to freeze the world; killed by the party in Session 38.
- **Admiral Xezev** – Eldritch aberration/servant of "Ziv'ik'nok," an ancient frozen horror beneath Khagdaruhl; fought and driven off/defeated in Sessions 39–40.
- **Saildreith ("Blair")** – Adult copper dragon rescued from an orc fort; now aiding Nurador against orc invaders at the party's request.
- **Armaros Eligora, Rina Sarthona, Hefeic Icehelm, Mhaenal "Maw" Cargwyn, Kotio Etric** – The Sacred Crows, a rival-turned-allied adventuring company also serving Witguard/Khagdaruhl.
- **Kibbit (Mr. Drimboort)** – Gabuk prisoner who helped the party escape Sciogost island.
- **Zeldrigos the Indomitable Serpent** – Gem-dragon controlling the waters around Sciogost prison island.
- **Esadi the Herrying** – A djinn posing as a sky goddess; controls the storms around Sciogost; made a worship pact with most of the party (Albross refused and was killed, then revived).
- **Lady Phéhldra Lightgarde** – Leader of Nurador (Lucerion's hometown), currently under orc attack.
- **Breemos** – Albross's brother, believed endangered in Gevakaln by the Sable Shadows.

## Factions & Organizations
- **Sable Shadows** – Criminal organization based in Gevakaln; threat to Albross's brother.
- **The Sacred Crows** – Allied adventuring company (Armaros, Rina, Hefeic, Maw, Kotio) working for Witguard/Khagdaruhl interests.
- **Monks of Iohir / Cult of the Coldbreath** – Sought the Rimestone shards to freeze the Yag'vur Sierra; leadership (Huldi) defeated.
- **Khagdaruhl (Dwarven Kingdom)** – Ruled by Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV; allied/neutral toward Witguard.
- **Witguard / Rostiena Foothills** – Territory at war with the Dalasor Coast; party has been acting as its ambassadors.
- **Dalasor Coast / Malithea Empire** – Rival territory to the Rostiena Foothills, seeking dwarven alliance.
- **Seventh Company of Paendley / Paragon Tear** – Order the party aided against a corrupting ritual (Tralar) south of Witguard.

- **Colbath's Keep** – Noble keep near Climbor, plagued by the Coldbreath cult.
