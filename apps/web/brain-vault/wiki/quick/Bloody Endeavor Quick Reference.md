# Bloody Endeavor Quick Reference

Campaign: Bloody Endeavor

*This is a living quick-reference page, kept current with the latest sessions of the Bloody Endeavor campaign notes.*

## Current Status

As of Session 42 ("I Believe I Can Fly," in-game 30th of Paramor, 1246), the party is split in two after a botched teleportation spell cast by Ambassador Greythane:

- **Group A** (Caelion, Albross, Rhody, Pagern, plus Sacred Crow Mhaenal "Maw" Cargwyn) landed on **Sciogost**, a prison island off the coast of Gevakaln, home to a powerful jacinth-gem dragon named **Zeldrigos** and a cloud-tower belonging to **Esadi the Herring**. They are currently trapped inside a pocket-plane of shifting paintings (treasure room, iron golem room, cloud room, and others) after investigating Esadi's tower, with Caelion separated from the rest of the group by the plane's shifting magic.
- **Group B** (Lucerion, Armaros Eligora, and Kotio Etric of the Sacred Crows) landed on a hill overlooking **Nurador** (Lucerion's home town), which is besieged by orc encampments.
- Ambassador Greythane's location after the teleport mishap is unknown.

Recent major arcs (Sessions 35–40) saw the party retrieve all pieces of the **Rimestone**, defeat **Huldi (the Cold Breath)**, seal an eldritch aberration (**Ziv'ik'nok**, guarded by its herald **Admiral Xezev**) beneath Khagdaruhl, and earn favor with **Lord Emperor Thundermaw VI**, including reforging Lucerion's ancestral blade **Kretopulus**. Rhody died and was revived (Revivify) during the fight beneath the throne room, but remains bound by his fey pact with **Madam Mabel**.

The party (as "Wyrm's Bane") called in a favor from **Saildreith the Copper Dragon** to defend Nurador and Witguard against invading orcs, and is working to formally warn allies of the growing war between the **Rostiena Foothills** and the **Dalasor Coast**.

## Key NPCs

- **Madam Mabel** – Rhody's elderly fey witch patron; cured Rhody's curse in exchange for tasks; holds power over his soul/afterlife.
- **Saildreith ("Blair")** – Adult copper dragon rescued from an orc fort; the "Shield of Witguard"; owed a favor via a magic horn, now aiding Nurador.
- **Huldi (the Cold Breath)** – Leader of the Monks of Iohir; sought all pieces of the Rimestone to freeze the world; slain in Session 38.
- **Admiral Xezev** – Astral Plane herald/servant of the eldritch entity Ziv'ik'nok; fought beneath Khagdaruhl.
- **Lord Emperor Thundermaw VI** – Ruler of the dwarven kingdom Khagdaruhl; allied with the party, gifted them the reforged sword Kretopulus and promised neutrality in the regional war.
- **Ambassador Udohi Greythane** – Khagdaruhl ambassador who sponsored the party; his failed Teleport scroll split the party in Session 40/41.
- **The Sacred Crows** – Rival/allied adventuring party (Armaros Eligora, Rina Sarthona, Hefeic Icehelm, Mhaenal "Maw" Cargwyn, Kotio Etric); fought Wyrm's Bane in the Ring of Respect, later allied.
- **Lady Phéhldra Lightgarde** – Leader of Nurador, Lucerion's home town, currently under orc attack.
- **Zeldrigos, the Indomitable Serpent** – A dragon made of jacinth gems who controls Sciogost prison island; holds Maw as collateral.
- **Esadi the Herring** – Mysterious figure with a cloud tower who has been causing shipwrecks; not yet contacted directly.
- **Mr. Drimboort ("Kibbit")** – A Gabuk prisoner escapee on Sciogost, currently traveling with Group A.
- **Lady Roysia Coarsepetal** – Young noblewoman from Climbor who guided the party into the Hole's catacombs.
- **Lilian** – Mysterious woman hunting the Cult of Verander/Coldbreath; has crossed paths with the party multiple times.

## Factions & Organizations

- **Wyrm's Bane** – The party's adopted group name.
- **The Sacred Crows** – Rival-turned-allied adventuring group working for the Magistrate of Witguard.
- **Monks of Iohir / The Cold Breath Cult** – Antagonist cult seeking to freeze the world using the Rimestone; leadership defeated at Iohir Monastery (Session 38), but the broader organization's remnants are unconfirmed as fully eliminated.
- **Sable Shadows** – Criminal organization based out of Gevakaln, connected to threats against Albross's brother Breemos.
- **Seventh Company of Paendley (Paragon Tear)** – Military/religious company the party aided near Witguard.
- **The Dalasor Coast / Malithea Empire** – Rival power to the Rostiena Foothills; sending ambassadors to court Khagdaruhl.
- **Khagdaruhl** – Dwarven kingdom (duergar, mountain dwarves, hill dwarves) that the party successfully courted as an ally, though it has pledged neutrality in the war.
- **Runegrip Clans** – Dwarven clan network at Fyrstholith (Hol'gardur, Hol'kulrum, Hol'durog), afflicted by the same crop plague as Rhody's home.

## Key Locations

- **Climbor** – City where the party spent early sessions; home of the Caifir family and "the Hole" catacombs.
- **Witguard** – Frontier town/fortress the party has repeatedly aided; borders the war between Rostiena Foothills and Dalasor Coast.
- **Yag'vur Sierra / Vanapur Mountains** – Mountain range containing the Runegrip dwarf clans, Iohir Monastery, and the dwarven ruin Fort Mornock.
- **Khagdaruhl** – Underground dwarven kingdom ruled by Lord Emperor Thundermaw VI; site of the Rimestone temple and the Ring of Respect.
- **Iohir Monastery** – Base of the Monks of Iohir/Cold Breath, where Huldi was defeated.
- **Nurador** – Lucerion's home town, currently besieged by orcs (Thunderspear clan and others).
- **Sciogost** – Prison island off Gevakaln where Group A is currently stranded; home to Zeldrigos and Esadi's cloud tower.
- **Gevakaln** – City tied to the Sable Shadows and Albross's kidnapped brother Breemos; the party's intended (missed) destination.
