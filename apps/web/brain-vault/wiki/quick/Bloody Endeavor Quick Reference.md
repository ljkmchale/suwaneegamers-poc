- **Esadi (the Herring)** – A Djinn posing as a goddess of the sky; the party now worships her publicly as "Esadi, Goddess of the West Wind" after a deal to leave Sciogost.
- **Zeldrigos the Indomitable Serpent** – A dragon made of Jacinth gems dwelling beneath Sciogost; controls who leaves the island.
- **Namys** – Elvish leader of the Cloak of Defiance in Gevakaln; broker for intel on the Sable Shadows and the contract to kill Count Teufel.
- **Count Tetravin Teufel** – Head of the Sable Shadows; owner of the Villa of Passion; current assassination target.
- **Adelaide Teufel** – The Count's daughter; member of Dissent Ablation, opposed to her father.
- **JB** – Leader of Dissent Ablation, a wealth-redistribution faction in Gevakaln's Underbelly.
- **Breemos** – Albross's brother; last contacted via Sending saying "I messed up," location unclear, believed held near/in the Villa of Passion.
- **Saildreith ("Blair")** – Adult copper dragon rescued from the orc fort near Witguard; aiding Nurador's defense per a deal.
- **Lord Emperor Thundermaul IV** – Ruler of the dwarven kingdom Khagdaruhl; allied Khagdaruhl's neutrality in the Rostiena/Dalasor war; gifted the party the reforged Kretopulus.

- **The Sacred Crows** – Rival/allied adventuring party: Armaros Eligora (wizard), Rina Sarthona (rogue), Hefeic Icehelm (cleric), Mhaenal "Maw" Cargwyn (ranger, now with Wyrm's Bane), Kotio Etric (barbarian), led loosely by Captain Hefeic's group under Ambassador Greythane's sponsorship.
- **Lady Phéhldra Lightgarde** – Leader of Nurador (Lucerion's hometown), currently besieged by orcs.
- **Huldi / "The Coldbreath"** – Leader of the Monks of Iohir (Cold Breath cult), slain by the party at Iohir Monastery (Session 38) after a prolonged fight over the Rimestone.

## Factions & Organizations

- **Wyrm's Bane / "Wyrmbane"** – The party's adopted name, also now serving as the name of their worship of Esadi.
- **The Sable Shadows** – Criminal organization based in Gevakaln; run the drug/poison trade (exploding-head poison) and a prisoner-fighting ring beneath the Villa of Passion; led by Count Tetravin Teufel.
- **The Cloak of Defiance** – A rival faction/vigilante group in Gevakaln controlling much of the city; employing the party to destroy Sable Shadows assets and assassinate Count Teufel.
- **Dissent Ablation** – A faction advocating wealth redistribution in Gevakaln's Underbelly; allied loosely with the party against the Sable Shadows.
- **The Cult of the Coldbreath / Monks of Iohir** – Sought the three Rimestone shards to freeze the world, inadvertently guarding against (and risking releasing) an ancient eldritch entity.
- **Khagdaruhl** – Dwarven kingdom (mountain dwarves, duergar, hill dwarves); has pledged neutrality in the Rostiena/Dalasor war.
- **The Rostiena Foothills vs. The Dalasor Coast (Malithea Empire)** – Ongoing regional war; Witguard controls the contested Thornfold Bastion.
- **The Sacred Crows** – Adventuring company sponsored by Ambassador Greythane, working parallel goals with the party.
- **Cult of Verander** – Cult beneath Climbor worshipping an elf named Verander (earlier arc, status unresolved).

## Key Locations

- **Gevakaln** – Crime-ridden city with tiered terraces (Brydove Heights, Mittler Ward, Fogwalk Underbelly); current main setting; home to the Sable Shadows and Villa of Passion.
- **Khagdaruhl** – Underground dwarven kingdom housing the Rimestone's resting place and the Great Forge.
- **Iohir Monastery** – Mountain monastery of the Coldbreath cult; site of Huldi's defeat.
- **Witguard** – Border town/fortress tied to the Rostiena-Dalasor conflict; the party's early patron (Magistrate Vilihame Oddnel) is based here.
- **Nurador** – Lucerion's hometown, currently besieged by orcs; Lucerion is there helping defend it.
- **Sciogost** – Prison island off Gevakaln, home to Zeldrigos the dragon and formerly Esadi's cloud tower.
- **Climbor** – City near the early campaign arc, home of the Caifir family and "the Hole."
- **Yag'vur Sierra / Fyrstholith (Yaggermon)** – Mountain range housing the Runegrip dwarf clans (Hol'gardur, Hol'kulrum, Hol'durog) and the Rimestone sites.
- **Madam Mabel's Orchard** – Feywild-linked location tied to Rhody's pact.
