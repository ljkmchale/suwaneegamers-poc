## Current Status

**In-game date:** 30th of Paramor, 1246 (Gevakaln group) / a few days earlier for the Nurador group — Session 44, "Count On Your Fingers."

The party is currently **split in two**:

- **Caelion, Albross, Rhody, and Pagern**, accompanied by new allies **Kibbit** (a Gabuk escapee) and **Mhaenal "Maw" Cargwyn** (of the Sacred Crows), are in the criminal-run city of **Gevakaln**. They are posing as worshippers of the goddess Esadi, searching for Albross's brother Breemos, who has gone missing/compromised among the **Sable Shadows**. They've allied with the vigilante **Cloak of Defiance** (contact: Namys) and agreed to destroy a Sable Shadows drug warehouse in exchange for information — Kibbit's initiation test into the Cloak is underway as the group prepares to breach the warehouse.
- **Lucerion**, separated by a botched teleport, landed near **Nurador** (his home town) alongside Sacred Crows members **Armaros Eligora** and **Kotio Etric**. Nurador is under orc siege; Lucerion has been helping organize its defense with leader **Lady Phéhldra Lightgarde**, and the copper dragon **Saildreith ("Blair")** has been summoned (via a one-time horn) to aid the town.

Recently resolved: the Monks of Iohir / Cold Breath threat was defeated, the Rimestone was restored to its pedestal beneath Khagdaruhl, and the party struck a bargain with the djinn Esadi (as "Wyrmbane," worshippers of Esadi) to escape the prison island of Sciogost off Gevakaln's coast.
