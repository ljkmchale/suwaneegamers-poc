## Current Status

As of Session 16 (22nd of Evalee, 1246), the party is deep inside **Khelar Watch Eastern Observation Point II**, an excavated ancient Osmanae ruin hidden within the **Jagged Waste Crags** east of Scarwatch Hold. Posing as fellow Radiant Eye cultists, the party infiltrated a small cult excavation team (Gault, Bolin, Garamond, and their leader Selris, a former Scarwatch Hold curate) who were preparing to open a massive sealed 20-foot door the next day. A telepathic tip-off led to a surprise fight; the party killed Garamond, Selris, Gault, Bolin, and several other cultists. The session ends as two more cultists — including an unnamed muscular woman — arrive at the gallery and discover the carnage. The party's characters are level 4 (3 sessions at this level). Isgo remains held by allied hobgoblins in a nearby cave pending the party's return.
