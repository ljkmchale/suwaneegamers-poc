# Dungeons III Quick Reference

Campaign: Dungeons III

This page is a fast-lookup reference for the most commonly asked questions about the Dungeons III campaign. It is a living document - update after each session.

## Current Status

The party began in Hesfal during Salvus, stopped the Boar's Best robbery, captured the K9 Crew, and delivered the prisoners to Scarwatch Hold. At the hold, the party named itself [[The kNight Watch]], received Umlin's mapping job, learned the Radiant Eye cult had defaced the Sanctuary, and followed Isgo / Radiant Eye / Osmanae leads into the crags. Session 10 ends with two tan-robed figures attacking in the crags.

## Active Quests

- **Bring in fugitives / bandits** - the party captured the K9 Crew and delivered them to Scarwatch Hold.
- **Find Isgo** - Isgo's missing-horse trail leads into the crags east of Scarwatch Hold.
- **Investigate the Radiant Eye cult** - the cult defaced the Scarwatch Hold Sanctuary and may have a crag outpost.
- **Map the area around Scarwatch Hold** - Umlin hired the party for mapping work.
- **Recover the Osmanae celestial instrument** - Embryl believes the party may find it at an ancient watchtower / observatory in the same region.

## Open Plot Threads

- Who is the Scorpion and what do they want from the party?

- Who or what healed Aeon's horrific back scar?

## Setting

Yearning Vale, Myrdae, year 1246. Key locations: [[Hesfal]], [[Scarwatch Hold]], the crags east of Scarwatch Hold, and the road toward Mahruud.
