# Dungeons III Quick Reference

Campaign: Dungeons III

*This is a living quick-reference page, kept current with the latest events recorded in the Dungeons III campaign player notes.*

## Current Status

It is the 22nd of Evalee, 1246. The party is deep inside the **Watch Eastern Observation Point II**, a ruined outpost of the ancient star-watching order the **Osmanae**, hidden in the **Jagged Waste Crags** east of Scarwatch Hold. In Session 16, the party infiltrated a Radiant Eye cult excavation crew posing as fellow cultists, then ambushed and killed the group's leader Selris, her lieutenant Garamond, and several other cultists (Gault, Bolin, and others). As the fight ended, two more cultists — including a previously-unseen muscular woman — arrived from a side corridor and discovered the carnage, reacting in shock. The session ended on this unresolved confrontation. The party has not yet opened the large, ornate 20-foot excavated door the cultists were preparing to breach ("the Observation door"), which is believed to guard the celestial instrument sought by Embryl.

## Factions & Organizations

- **The K9 Crew** – Mercenary bandit gang hired by "the Scorpion" to terrorize Hesfal during Salvus; leadership captured and sentenced at Scarwatch Hold.
- **The Radiant Eye** – A cult considered "an affront to all that's good"; defaced the Scarwatch Hold sanctuary and has been excavating the Osmanae ruins in the Jagged Waste Crags searching for relics/instruments. Largely wiped out at the observation point in Session 16, though at least two members remain unaccounted for.
- **The Osmanae** – Extinct ancient order of astronomers who built coordinated eastern/western observation posts to study the stars; central to the party's current dungeon delve.
- **Scarwatch Hold Guard / Realmwardens** – Local authority and paramilitary order; Dempsey aspires to join the Realmwardens.
- **Advents of Harmony / Argent Halls** – Collectors of history and lore in Scarwatch Hold; destination for recovered Osmanae artifacts.
