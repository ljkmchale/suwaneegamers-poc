## Overview

Dhá Chaomhnóir, commonly referred to as Dhá, is located on the southern coast of Inverland (Aelbon territory). Housing the largest of the Vaults in the Reserve, the city boasts the largest number of wealthy residents in all of Myrdae. With high-end trade and access to both money and a busy port, the city attracts tourists and merchants alike, but also attracts a criminal element. There is also a large working class, as one would expect with so many job opportunities.

Dhá is one of the busiest ports in Inverland, with an advantageous location of idyllic weather, calm water, and easy trade routes to mainland Myrdae. As such, the city is a hub for both imports and exports for Inverland.

As one of the richest cities in all of Myrdae, Dhá Chaomhnóir is known for its favorable climate, fair rules and regulations, and for having the largest Vault not only in Inverland but throughout Myrdae. Unlike other cities where the affluent live seasonally, the wealthy live here year-round. Dhá is known for its well-marked streets and neighborhoods, defined largely by class or purpose. The entire city is covered in the iconography of the Guardians — large twin statues in the Vault said to come to life to battle any threat to the city.

### Organizations

**Lionheart** — criminal syndicate (mob-like organization). Lion insignia on the heart. Appears as a group guarding Dhá. Operates throughout Dunmoore.

**Populi Verde** — environmental enthusiasts who worship Madre Verde (whom they mistakenly conflate with "The Mother"). Reviled by most towns who have heard of them. Led by Ainran, a powerful and charismatic leader who was the subject of a bounty from the Cur family. Ainran has not been seen since leaving for the most recent All Bright ceremony — the Greens insist he was taken by the Mother to become a god; outsiders assume he was killed for the bounty. The Great Houses keep them out of the city. After incursions into the city that were rebuffed by the Cur family, they have returned to patrolling roads and hassling merchants who don't show proper respect to Madre Verde. Were involved in the takedown of "The Woeborn" (cult of Suthrin). Were once "Wanted" in the city (wrongfully accused of much of the crime there).

## Government

### Family Ties

Dhá is controlled by a few powerful families in an uneasy truce, each controlling various aspects of the city.

**Cur Family** — the most powerful. Crest is a lion with a heart. Control many of the brothels and gambling houses in Dunmoore. Gold-laden wagons transport payments from businesses throughout Dunmoore to the Vaults. Leader is referred to as **Don Cur**.

**House of Krem** — was a powerful family. Head of house was a warlock of significant power who staffed the home with undead. Rumored to have attempted to usurp Cur power by raiding brothel payments along the road to Blackfield, and possibly to have robbed the Cur Vault (denied by both Vault Keepers and the Cur family). The Warlock Krem and his concubine were found murdered in their bed; the home was nearly burned to the ground. The Cur family led the cleanup and investigation. Heir: daughter Stacia (nee Lillitu); stepbrother Lionel also hoped to inherit. Stacia wed "Kivuss" (Lionel's brother) and they took the money and left Dhá. Lionel still uses the house, though it has fallen into disrepair.

## Notable Locations

### The Vaults (1)

Part of the Realm Depository & Exchange (RDE), a network of repositories acting as an exchange system for coins and legal tender across Myrdae. The Dhá location is the largest of the Vaults in Myrdae.

Pyramid-shaped building surrounded by 30' tall circular walls encompassing the center of the city. Three entrances, each manned by round-the-clock security. Grounds inside the outer wall are open and covered in gravel. Four smaller structures inside house associates, guards, and monks.

**Bottom Floor:** Circular chamber, 50' tall walls. Two large stone Guardian statues (actually stone golems that come to life when needed) seated on thrones in the center. 12 large wooden doors evenly spaced around the circle with elvish markings (numbered 1-12 like a clock). Arrangement by wealth: 12=wealthiest, 10-11=wealthy, 9=well-off merchants, 4-8=middle class, 1-3=poorest/unwashed. Additional guards at doors 9-12. Each hall is 300' with 5' niches every 10' for client use.

**Upper Chamber:** 30' stairs up to a small room with two 15'x15' outer rooms (for RDE transfer business) and one inner room housing a **teleportation circle** (with sigil) used for transferring goods between Vaults, and for elite travel upon request. The Lords of Magic was originally housed at the top of the Vault pyramid — this is why the teleportation circle exists here.

**Personnel:** All guards, associates, and Vault Keepers = male elves. Monks = hooded, various races/genders; speak only when spoken to, never reveal names; work 6-hour shifts. Associates = multilingual, excellent memory, gifted with math; work 6-hour shifts. Guards rotate shifts every 4 hours.

### Old Dicon House (2)

The old capital of Inverland. The Dicon moved the capital to Gibuldon roughly 100 years ago because the leaders didn't want money and political power in the same city. This property was left as public grounds — includes a library, an orangery, a reflecting pool, sports field, and the old manor house. The Lords of Magic was originally in the top of the Vault pyramid (hence the teleportation circle there).

Caretaker: **Tomberlin Dunmoore** (Dean Bozous Dunmoore's youngest brother), resident historian.

### Cur Manor (3)

Residence of the Cur family — the richest and most powerful family in Dhá. Crest is a lion with a heart. Don Cur is the family leader.

### House of Krem (4)

Former seat of the Krem noble family. Now in disrepair; Lionel (Warlock Krem's stepson) still uses it. The original Warlock Krem and his concubine were murdered in their bed; the house was nearly burned down. Cur family led the subsequent investigation.

### Lower Ward (5)

Poor community. Some establishments rent by the hour. City wardens don't police here, believing the community can police itself as long as activity stays contained.

### East Gate Village (6)

A growing village that has built up outside the city gates. Reasonable prices for general goods and services.

### Guardian Atrium (7)

A venue for performances and entertainment.

### Guardian Inn (8)

Recommended by the Great Houses to visiting tourists but privately shunned by locals — it has been the site of many crimes that the families conspire to keep quiet. Enjoys a better reputation than it deserves.

### Guardian Inn* — "the other Guardian Inn" (9)

### Bond Plaza (20)

High-end row of shops targeting the wealthy and elite. Fine linens, clothing, shoes, leather goods, accessories, home décor, and gift items.

### A Bud in Bloom (21)

Bathhouse and spa; also features male service staff for women. Tied to the Cur family for money laundering.

### House of Eight (22)

Traditional church where all major gods and several minor ones are worshiped. Grounds include burial sites and mausoleums for the wealthy.

### Temple of Light (23)

Open air temple where anyone is free to worship anything. Local gathering spot for events.

### Mother's Shrine (24)

Located in the hills outside the city gates. Open-air temple for Madre Verde. Unofficial home of the Populi Verde. A gathering spot for those who worship The Mother (locals are aware that Populi Verde confuses Madre Verde with The Mother).
