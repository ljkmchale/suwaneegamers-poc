01 – We Meet (11.19.25)
40th Hemist – Adsuren, Camburne Vec, Inverland
Market Day
Esylla has been traveling north … it is the middle of Sultra.  Staying at the Graying Grotto for a few stretches.  It is Market Day.
Kenton traveled west…via big cities…to sell his wares.  Partied with tradesmen in Gibuldon.  He hitched a ride with Captain Amara Hilei. Sells his fine clothes and silks. 
Therric made his way NW into the Celedon Forest and into Adsuran.  Made a connection with a mystic named Arena Duskwhisper? He has a routine going to the Market.  He now hasn’t seen the mystic in some time. 
Zephyra has been in Adsuren a few years.  Has a hut.  Helps with the village watch…but isn’t the best fit.  Has been hired on as a guard for the Market days each stretch.  Has connection to the village priest Tanluar Rotvori… and with Escanor.  She also knows Mollywop the sorcerer who comes around from time to time.
Escanor has been in Adsuren a little while, after traveling around Inverland.  He has taken up with the local priest.  Likes Market Day.
Market Day - twelve specialty stalls set up today, in addition to normal goods that are sold daily. Lots of traffic around today.  It is mid-morning. 
Therric walks by Kenton and reaches into his basket and tosses him a fig and they discuss Kenton’s sales today.  Therric is looking for his stall.  He sees that Perrin Patchwicket…claimed it.  He sees another spot next to Kenton where he can set up instead.  He tries to talk Perrin into taking the stall next to Kenton - and does.
We pause to describe each character…what we all see when we look at each other:
* Esylla -  5’9”, elf, auburn hair, pretty, “fancy” studded leather, 25 years old
* Kenton - Flamboyantly dressed, 5’11”, dark hair and mustache, 28 years old
* Therric - looks older than he is (mid-20s), dark and gray hair and beard, looks like he sleeps in the woods.
* Zephrya - 6’10” tiefling barbarian, dark brown long hair, lilac-hued skin, light green eyes, 25 years old
* Escanor - 6’3” human, chain mail, dark hair and big bushy mustache, mid-40s
Zephrya - observes a disturbance at a stall, heads over to intervene. The stallkeeper is holding up a teenager (Jodon) by the scruff of his neck…accusing him of consuming some of her product (which is not for eating/drinking).  They have a discussion - “how many times do I have to tell you to stay out of trouble?”  
Kid goes over to Escanor, per the suggestion of Zephyra.  He counsels him not to steal.  He knows that the kid arrived in town a couple of harmons ago - he has no parents.  He tells him to go the Graying Grotto…gives him 6cp.  Tells him who to talk to and what to ask for…but only if he asks if there are chores to do. Kid agrees.  
Sick Man & The Well
Esylla approaches Kenton and they talk about his wares - she is looking for something less colorful (“mundane”).  She passes on buying anything.  Goes over to talk to Escanor.  Tries to get Therric to come over with the last of his figs, but another patron comes over to complain about the water, buys a fig, then vomits.  
Zep walks over to see what’s going on.  Escanor and Therric check him out to see what is going on.  This patron is an older man, and he looks to be poisoned.  He claimed that he drank from the well.  As he keels over, Esylla “spares him from dying.”
Zep and Therric go to the Grotto to follow up with the kid.  Escanor goes over the well to check to see what’s wrong.  Has a strange smell.  He calls the herbalist over to check it out. She agrees it isn’t natural.  He sends for the priest.
Zep and Therric make it to the inn and talk to the proprietor. They ask to see the kid…and when he arrives, he is sweaty, but doesn’t seem as troubled as the man.  The youth had not had too much to drink.  Zep advises the innkeeper to tell others to avoid the well water.
The Nature's Oratory (NO) priest shows up (older, half elf) and he talks to Escanor about the tainted water. The man is in pain…they take him to the NO.  They also say they will alert the watch.  Priest tells those around that we need to check out the well to see what is going on.  Esylla asks if there is a way into the well, other than through this point.  There is not.
Priest says that he blesses the water each day.  Kenton overhears and volunteers to enter the well.  Discussion about this.  Kenton uses acrobatics to descend…along with his whip.  He makes it down about 30’ and it opens into a large cavern. He thinks this cavern is 50-60’ wide, but can’t estimate how deep it is (he guesses it is at least 30-50’ deep).  He sees two openings in the cavern wall (SE and SW).  SE has stream water pouring in; SW looks like a platform. He swings himself over to the wall on the SE side, standing now up to his boots in water.  He dips his finger in the stream water to taste and see if it tastes odd - it is odd.  He thinks that the stream is the problem. 
Discussion from below with those above about which directions the SE and SE tunnels face.  The SE stream might feed from the river…group agrees to try to find the source of the stream on land.  We ask Kenton to come back up…and he splashes into the water, but makes it up.  We decide to head to the river, looking for dead vegetation or anything amiss.  
Therric leads the group, looking for signs of something amiss.  He sits on the ground…looking at/tasting grass and dirt.  He thinks we need to explore and find a source of poison below ground.  So the group heads to the Nature’s Oratory to try to access the underground tunnel.
The Oratory
We make our way there and it is well maintained….lovely grounds.  We go inside and make our way into the building.  We notice the priest and acolytes taking care of the man who was poisoned.  We tell him why we are there and he steps away and comes back with a lantern that he hands to Kenton.  Takes us to a set of stairs and to a door that hasn’t been opened for a while.  It smells dank and musty. He leads us down the hall and see crypts, urns, other areas.  We come to a double door with a wooden plank.  He asks Zep and Therric to help with the wooden plank - and they get it open.  This leads to a narrow 10’ wide hallway that leads in the direction of the market.
The priest tells us that the original settler of the town wanted he and his to be buried in a crypt farther away from everyone else.  It has since been collapsed so “mind your way and please be sure to close up everything when you come back out.”
Oratory Crypt
Zep moves forward and starts to clear the rocks and it takes an hour to move the boulders.  Once cleared, there is a small hallway and there are double doors that are no longer hanging…there is a large room with double doors on the far side.   There are pillars in the room and a couple of wooden coffins. A couple are rotted with time and a couple have debris on them.  We see an opening - like a tunnel - that might lead to the cavern. Smell of death. 
Zep moves forward, followed by Escanor.  He sees a wispy figure over the coffin in a corner…and talks to it.  It appears to be a ghost in the corner over an open skeleton. Therric reads something in elvish over the door - “the founders of the town.”  Escanor pulls out a holy symbol and the ghost disappears, replaced by the skeletal hand that grasps his throat.
COMBAT – IT IS INITIATIVE
* Round One: Zep swings with an ax and misses; Esylla hexes a skeleton and tries to blast it and misses; Kenton misses his attack (after putting a mask); Therric disengages off the hand near him and hunter’s marks the hand.  Escanor attacks the hand on him and kills it, says rest in peace.  One skeleton crits on Zep, one attacks Kenton and misses; and the last hand attacks Escanor, but fumbles and he gets an opportunity attack and kills it, knocking it out of the air. 
* Round Two:  Zep rages and attacks the skeleton on her, and does a lot of damage.  Esylla tries to blast and misses again.  Therric moves his HM and misses. Kenton tries to attack and misses again. Escanor runs over the a skeleton and misses. The skeleton attacks Zep and hits.  The other skeleton attacks Kenton and hits, but he has him reroll it and he still hits. 
* Round Three:  Zep attacks the skeleton and kills it, shattering its bones against the wall.  Esylla blasts the last skeleton and does necrotic and force damage.  Kenton hits the skeleton and kills it, ripping its head off.
We think this was the founder’s family and that the head guy is entombed behind the double door.  Therric tries to make the bones tidy, as Escanor tries praying to his god to ask for help in blessing this area.  Therric heals Zep.
We hear a knock from the double doors leading to the crypt.  Therric opens the door and sees a small hallway that opens up into a larger room.  The source of the knock is not visible. Therric stealths into the room and sees a stone sarcophagus in the room.  He eases up to the side and listens in and doesn’t hear anything.  He sees a name, date, and other info carved into the stone in elvish. He tells us what he sees and we all come in.  Escanor tries to bless the room and the spirit, placing holy water on the sarcophagus.  The water beads up a little on top, but is mostly absorbed inside.  Esylla speaks elvish to the spirit and prays to her god. 
Escanor and Esylla leave the room, Zep tries to move the sarcophagus, fumbles, and shatters the lid.  The man inside is not skeletal, but is sunken and surrounded by coins and other items.  It sits up.  
COMBAT – IT IS INITIATIVE
* Round One:  The figure tries to bite Therric twice and misses twice. Kenton uses his whip to attack and hits. Therric moves the HM to the figure.  Esylla runs in and moves hex and hits it with a blast.  Escanor runs in and encourages everyone.  Zep tries to attack and misses.
* Round Two:  The figure attacks Zep and Therric and misses both. Kenton attacks and hits the creature and kills it, snapping its head off.
It is now revealed that Zep destroyed the lid.  We counsel her to take a wooden coffin lid and cover it and then cover it in stones.  We see Kenton try to palm coins and a crown…and tell him to put it all back - and he does.  Escanor apologizes to the spirit about Kenton.
We walk to the hallway and start to go down the hall.  We come to a large pool (this is familiar to Kenton) - this is the well from before. Zep hugs the ledge and walks over to where the stream is coming out; Therric behind her. He decides to stealth forward 10’ to check it out; Zep, Esylla, Escanor and Kenton following.
Therric comes to an area that opens up where several streams converge into the larger stream.  He follows the path and we all follow behind 30’.  Therric travels another 100’ and it comes to a rocky barrier where water is pouring underneath.  This is a natural formation. He thinks there has been a natural collapse from above here - the way is blocked by rubble.  We think this is debris we can move.  Therric starts to move some of the rocks and sees that it opens into a 15’x20’ tunnel where there are vines and roots in the area.  Growth seems to be amplified of the vines and roots.  He thinks he can clear the path.  The rest of the group catches up and sees him clearing the way.  The path is overgrown by roots from above…and we all see a room that is overgrown with a stream flowing down the middle of the room.  There is a small hole on the far end….and this is where the tainted water seems to be coming from. Therric takes his sword and reaches into the hole with it to see if there is anything there.  He sees something is blocking the hole.  He now tries to use his sword to “unclog” the hole.
A decayed gertot, a popular livestock of Inverland, head drops into the room, eating on a black, petrified human heart.  He pries the heart out of the gertot’s mouth.  A plume of smoke radiates out of the heart….and as Therric picks it up, a vine emerges.  
COMBAT – IT IS INITIATIVE 
* Round One: Esylla sees the vine emerge and become a figure.  She moves her hex and blasts it, doing almost full damage.  Therric prepares for battle.  Escanor attacks and misses.  Zep rages and moves forward and attacks with her ax, hitting it.  It seeps its hand into the floor and we all make strength saving throws.  The vines shoot up and restrain everyone except Escanor. Kenton tries to break free and can’t, but somehow puts on his mask.
* Round Two: Esylla blasts it and hits, but the damage isn’t as significant.  Therric tries to hit and misses.  Escanor attacks and misses.  Zep breaks free of the vines. It tries to wrap its vines around Therric’s head and does - almost absorbing his head completely.  Kenton can’t break free.
* Round Three:  Esylla tries to blast and misses.  Therric takes damage and tries to break free and can’t.  Escanor heals Therric. Zep swings at the plant and kills it, and all of the vines wither and die. 
We take the gertot head and heart and decide we need to see where it belongs and put it to rest. Escanor thinks this was placed in the stream - something nefarious.
We find 18gp in the stream and are able to get back to the surface and it is starting to get dark.
WE STOP HERE.  Plan to check in with the priest, discuss our plan about what to do with the heart and head. 
– The End of the Session – 


02 – Mollywop to Sapcutt (December 11, 2025)
40th of Hemist – Adsuren
Nature’s Oratory
We head back and go through the Oratory and we see a squat little person that wasn’t there.  Rotvori is there.  The little person’s name is Lila Tealeaf.  She is glamorous with jewels and crystals.  Introductions are met.
Rotvori asks us about the cause of the water taint.  Kenton tells them about what happened and exaggerates…but doesn't share all details.  Therric says he left out some details.  Esylla and Kenten smell a burned/sulphur smell - when Therric knocks on the heart.  They ask him to stop knocking it.  Therric asks Rotvori what would do this to a heart and cause this condition to happen. He casts a spell on the “rock” and senses evil on the heart.  Escanor wants the heart to be blessed.  Priest doesn’t want to do that.  We ask if anyone has been reported missing - alive or dead.  And ask about where there are Gertots….and are told they are native to the lands and live in the hills. Escanor wants to bless the heart himself - he prays to Amriel and casts a spell on it and it glows with golden light and then burns up in additional ash. 
We decide to visit Mollywop to see if they can determine more about the heart itself, but also decide to visit the graveyard. Esylla asks Zep about Mollywop - she says she lives in a tower on the edge of town.  Since it is late at night, we decide to rest and go in the morning.  We decide to go to the Greying Grotto for dinner (Esylla is treating).  Kenton wants to bathe.  All to eat and then rest.
Graying Grotto
As we get to the GG, the McLunge family (of 9) approaches us and thanks us for helping the town by offering us a free dinner and ½ off rates for the inn tonight.  Kenton offers them a silver for laundry (Skippy will do it).  Kenton follows him back and sees that he gets special treatment.  He offers to bring K his clothes when completed (he puts on a robe).  Kenton pays 1gp for his room; Esylla, Therric, and Lila pay 3sp.
We sit down to dinner.  We have a bottle of wine, food.  Kenton pulls out his fiddle and walks up to the stage (performance 14).  He earns 5cp.  Meal is decent.  Therric listens for rumors (he hears of rising tensions between the Camburne and Sutherford families - leading families of adjacent Vecs; he heard the Camburnes have amassed power of some sort and the Sutherfords don’t trust them - think the C’s are getting too powerful; he hears more badmouthing of Sutherfords…don’t think they are justified in being so distrustful). 
After dinner, Zep and Escanor visit the blacksmith.  They know the people who work there are “siblings” a dwarf and half orc.   Naz’grim (half orc) is the armorsmith.  He greets them and Zep inquires about scalemail. She tells him why she needs it - after an encounter today.  He finds something that might work and says he can put a rush order on it - it will be 50gp.  He tells his “sister” that he will close down the forge tonight…that she can go home to ma and pop (the Goldenmace family - dwarves).  He asks for partial payment now. 
Therric goes to the bartender and asks her about Ariana Duskwhisperer.  Everyone else goes to sleep - either at their homes, rooms, at the temple. Kenton’s clothes are delivered to him in his room. After he gets dressed, he heads back to the tavern to “pick up a local.” He gets shot down by the first group of women. But tries again later and succeeds…taking her up to his room. Therric observes all of this.  He asks for a carafe of wine and takes it over the group of women, apologizing for his friend’s actions and asking for feedback.  Women report that he was charming, but a little full of himself - he should be more subtle.  He asks for their names and introduces himself. 
BEDTIME and we LEVEL UP.
During the night, Therric has a nightmare.  He is on a field surrounded by flames. He feels like he is in hell (no clue of the plane). He hears screams in the distance in all directions. He then wakes up to a scream and someone about to grab him.  Upon waking, he inspects the heart, but it sees the same.
41st of Hemist – Adsuren
Therric comes to breakfast and sees Esylla.  He tells her that someone else needs to hold it (he is able to deceive her).  Escanor observes this - asks if she's okay taking the heart.  Zep enters (wearing new armor).  Lila enters.  All eat, except Kenton - who is eating in his room with his girl.  Eventually, he makes his way down.  Kenton says bye to “Heather” (real name is Rebecca).  We all eat and agree to leave to head to Mollywop’s tower.  As we head out, Therric pulls Escanor to the side to talk.
Therric shares with Escanor that he had bad dreams and that he thought he was in hell.  Therric shares that he thinks the heart could be from hell. Escanor will keep an eye on those who hold the heart. Thinks maybe Mollywop will be able to help.  Therric expresses that he is scared.  They discuss where or not Esylla is strong enough to hold the heart. 
As they walk, Esylla asks Lila about her crystals.  They have a discussion and she ends up giving Esylla a crystal on a leather cord that she can wear as a necklace. 
Mollywop’s Tower
After an hour’s walk, they come across a misshapen, 3-story tower. Flora and fauna surround the tower.  Oddly built, many fault…but there is a warm, welcoming presence. We see a large, stone (marble-like door) ahead (white), but the rest of the building is darker.  Flora/fauna is bright and colorful all around.  Tower is weathered, battlements are worn down.  Vines up there. We see a short man with light tan skin, bronze hair, watering the plants.  Daudery (halfling) is his name - he an apprentice.  He tells us that he will try to get her attention for us.  He walks up to the door and mutters an enchantment (password draconic - open sesame - noticed by Esylla).  Leads us to the first floor. 
We smell breakfast - it smells great.  Thunderfall (dwarf) cooked it (eggs and ham) - she is another apprentice).  Therric asks how tall Mollywop is (5’6”, says Zep).  He leads on, and casts an occasional spell here and there to open doors.  He leads us up stairs.   We enter a very dark room (oddly dark) and there seems to be a 6-8’ yellowish tree that is surrounded by a light blue arcane barrier.  Zep sees a lady in robes (hair over her eyes)....muttering a spell behind her.  Her hair is covering her face.  After a moment, she snaps her fingers and suddenly the room looks like a beautiful library and the lady before her is pretty and middle aged, with purple robes with gold trim.  Blue eyed - she no longer resembles the visage who was there before.  There are two others there - a dwarf and an elf. 
She greets us.  Mollywop the Magnificant…..lots of titles. Esylla asks her if she was aware of the problem with the water yesterday…as Kenton jumps in to talk about how we are here for something important.  Esylla pulls out the bag with the heart and uses a mage hand to lift it up.  She immediately talks of the Enchantment and Necromancy magic associated with it.  This is an object of torment.  We all notice the high ranking demon (the yellow tree) that she has under an arcane bubble.  Only Kenton knows that this is a demon from the Abyss.  This is “Zuz” - her nickname for the demon.  She says that she is trying to get his real name from him.  She says that she is teaching her students about the demon.  
We ask if she can help figure out where is came from.  Escanor adds that he wants to know if it is safe to handle.  She tells us that it is not safe to handle - it likely torments the one who carries it. We says that she would likely be able to get more information about the heart…if we leave it with her for a day or two.  Therric asks her why she is studying the demon.  And brings up the political tension in the town….could they be related?  She thinks likely not.  We ask if there is anything we could help with.  She asks if we saw the Sapcutt family at the market day yesterday.  In her scrying, she is worried about them.  Jackin and Margaret Sapcutt are known for their famous, sugary pies….they are made at the end of every harmon.  It is THE treat.  She didn’t see them last time, and is now curious where they might be - wanting to make sure they come next stretch since it is the last market day coming up.  We offer to check on them and their 3 children, Lebioun, Jotho, and Lobella.  And come back tomorrow.
More discussion about what she was teaching her students and why the demon is here.  Escanor asks if priests can tap into that type of ability.  She looks around and thinks that 66% of us could likely tap into that type of power - tapping into the “weave.”  She talks about distribution, expanding, augmenting, prolonging…different ways to enhance magic when you are in a group.
Escanor, Lila, and Esylla have heard tales of Mollywop and her traveling band from 30+ years ago.  We know that she’s with the Tegenwald Phet and associated with Dionara … helped bring the Phet into the light and helped their reputation improve in Myrdae.  Her actions still impact Inverland at large.  This tower was her first attempt at creating at tower; she has others in Inverland.  We agree to meet on the morning of the 43rd of Hemist (2 days from now).  She gives us a pouch and Kenton takes it (he thinks it has 30 odd coins inside).  As we are leaving, she and her crew cast “longstrider” on Lila.  Escanor passes close to the demon and speaks to it in common…and it replies in Abyssal.  He shows him his symbol of Amriel and it recoils.  As we leave, the darkness drops and they resume their studies.
The Docks
We depart and head back to the city…knowing we have to cross the river in town using the ferry system at the docks.  We recognize a man there - the dockmaster (MacCannain). We ask about the price for the ferry.  He says he recognizes us from the tales of yesterday.  Asks us our name - we don’t have one yet (though Therric suggest POTT).  He tells us that it takes a long time to cross the river.  There is a ship here and is available to take us across (it is a lumber ship) - it will leave where there are enough people to cross.  Escanor brings up that we are looking for the Sapcutts - and Mac admits that he hasn’t seen them for a while.
Esylla and Therric go over to see Farley McGuiness (a fisher) to see if he can take the group over on his ship.  Esylla pays him 1gp and 2sp. We go across and it takes 40 minutes with Zep and Escanor helping paddle.
The Swamps
We get to the other side…and he asks where we would want to be dropped off.  We reply that we are going to see the Sapcutts.  He drops off a little more northerly…in a swampy area (not on docks).  There is a misty swamp in front of us.  Therric leads us - we travel for a few hours in the warm, swampy marsh.   Therric feels like maybe he was off track a little at first, but recovers.  He notices some movement in the water - mosquitos, frogs, tadpoles.  Sees other movement in the water that seems to be following us. Escanor notices it as well. Therric notices that there is a swamp beast in the water and sees a “kerplunk” in the water. He sees three small serpent forms.  They have been following 1-2 minutes.  Harder to discern with the splash.   We pick up the pace and are able to put distance between us and them.  The occasional splash seems to help ward them off.
We travel another 2 hours, until the sun is starting to set.  When the light is just about to disappear, we see faint lights in the distance (a few hundred yards away).  As we get closer we notice they are strings of lights hanging from trees - that seem to be a border of a property. We come across a sizable ranch with a mansion-size house, large barn, another side building on the right.  There are large, well-grooming swamp trees that line the walk to the house.  We see swamp critters in the trees; Escanor knows they are a family of halflings, but we don’t see them.  We see dog prints on the ground…an occasional halfling/small footprint in the mud.  We see lights on in the house and a wraparound porch.  
The Sapcutts
We light lanterns as we approach the house and Zep says loudly - Sapcutts, are you here?  No one responds. We make our way to the front door (study thick dark wood). Zep knocks on the door and we hear movement.  Door opens and we see a 2-½’ tall lady open the door, ragged clothes and braided hair.  This is Margaret Sappcutt.  She offers for us to come in and join for dinner. We see a dimly lit hallway behind her and a set of double doors and an archway.  We don’t see anyone else.  Zep asks about her children and she says that two of them will be joining for dinner, while the other might be in her room.
She leads Lila to a dining room.  And the group follows.  We see 3 others at the table (her husband and two of her children).   They are having meat pie and gator stew. She ladles some stew into a few bowls for us.  Lila and Esylla sit down; the rest of the group sits, too.
The group starts to eat.  It is very hearty.  Tastes like chicken. Therric checks and thinks the stew has gator for sure, but also some other plant-like taste. There is a texture that is throwing him off.  Lila asks her how she makes the stew - gator, spices, and some fungus they recently found (that adds to the flavor profile).  Therric eats heartily; Lila and Esylla pick at their food; Escanor and Kenton slow rolls their food; Zep doesn’t eat at all. Character Creation
Here are some things that players should consider when making their characters:
1. vision – have a vision for your character to be renown throughout Myrdae when the campaign is over
2. cooperation – play a character that wants to work with other players in the party
don’t get hung up in “but that’s what my character would do” if it goes against the party
3. role – consider one of the roles below that you want your character to play;  take initiative during gameplay to fill that role
   * defender (tank) - quick to stand between the biggest monsters and the party members that die easily
   * face - someone that helps in npc interaction that relies on skills like deception, persuasion and intimidation
   * healer - able to help restore hit points and maybe assist with debuffs (curses, poisons, etc.)
   * scholar - can handle the knowledge skills, like arcana, nature and religion
   * scout - interesting in moving forward to check the path is clear (stealth, perception, investigation, survival)
   * support / utility - controls the battlefield, debuffs enemies and helps get through obstacles
think of your character as playing a primary role, but also having a secondary role if possible
   4. progression – when your character accomplishes something, think about what else would be good to work towards
make sure the dungeon master knows this and it goes with the flow of the campaign
