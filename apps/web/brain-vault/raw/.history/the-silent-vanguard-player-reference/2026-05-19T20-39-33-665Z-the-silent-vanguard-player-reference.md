# Sessions  

## 01 – We Meet (05.19.26) — 18th of Ezale – Tratta

Master Thorne gathered six adventurers in a plain canvas tent near the Lower Dock markets. Party receives cover story tied to the Wavehammer. First assignment: Brinecross, contact H'arald Stonesheep. Payment: 50 gp per adventurer, 200 gp to pay Harold. Entry route: cliff cave by crooked tree to woodcutter shed.

## 02 – The Town of Zombies (05.29.26) — 20th of Ezale – Brinecross

Zombie outbreak in Brinecross. General store fight, barricades. Debate about fleeing vs. saving people. Lenny (Lensworth) runs to stable, finds Harold alive but sick. Harold ate the fish. Harold passes The Long Night phrase. Dead dwarf rises in stable, killed. Party escapes on horses. Finds secret warehouse letter addressed to Gultren the Canvas Master at the Sticky Pear Inn. Returns to Tratta, reports zombie outbreak, levels up, briefs Thorin. The Gilded Ledger: authority-friendly broadsheet. Salt and Cinder Press: raw anti-corruption broadsheet. The Long Night: sign shows sleepy knight in armor, too big for the bed; suspected drug operation location. Warehouse letter describes crates, chains, cages, fish, spice, people, guards, shipment in 20 nights, south warehouse near old stone ramp.

Notable names from session: Ivy (character in session, identity unclear), Lenny/Lensworth, Cletus, Fossil (possible Chuck character), Tom heals Cletus.
