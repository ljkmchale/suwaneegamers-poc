Session Notes
01 – We Meet (05.19.26)
18th of Ezale – Tratta
Meeting Day
The missions and the drawings
Session Summary
The party’s first meeting took place in Tratta, a bustling sandstone harbor city watched over by a wizard’s tower and split between public spectacle, dockside crime, hidden tunnels, thieves, spies, and darker powers. Master Thorne gathered six adventurers in a plain canvas tent near the Lower Dock markets, using the noise and cookstalls as cover.
Thorne explained that this was more than an easy pickup job. Because of the object involved and the risk that others might be watching, he wanted all six working together. The party would be lodged aboard the Wavehammer, an old naval vessel nearby that was supposedly under repair. Their cover story was that no one should steal the refit goods, and the guildsmen working on the ship could not be trusted.
The first assignment was to travel about a day and a half southwest of Tratta to the coastal hamlet of Brinecross. There they were to meet a thief named H’arald Stonesheep, who had two things for Thorne: an object of value and the name of a location in the city.
Brinecross was described as small, rough, and unimportant, reached by marsh roads with good salt air and few travelers. The party was told to be subtle, avoid petty town guards, and use a small cave in the cliff near a crooked tree. That hidden route would bring them up inside a woodcutter shed, from which they should find H’arald.
H’arald Stonesheep was described as a lean, ordinary-looking human in worn studded leather, quick-handed and sharp-eyed. He was built for speed, stealth, survival, and slipping through crowds rather than fair fights.
The job paid 50 gp to each adventurer who went, and Thorne provided 200 gp to pay H’arald.
Key Details From The Briefing
- Patron/contact: Master Thorne.
- Base/cover: the Wavehammer, an old naval vessel under repair.
- First destination: Brinecross, a coastal hamlet southwest of Tratta.
- Contact: H’arald Stonesheep, a thief with an object and a city-location name.
- Entry route: cliff cave by a crooked tree, leading to a woodcutter shed.
- Instructions: avoid guards, stay subtle, work together, and draw as little attention as possible.
- Payment: 50 gp per participating adventurer, plus 200 gp to pay H’arald.






02 – The Town of Zombies (05.29.26)
20th of Ezale – Brinecross
Session Summary
The party survived the zombie outbreak in Brinecross, escaped the general store, found Harold at the stable, recovered the information and an ivory/statue clue, learned the phrase/location “The Long Night,” escaped town on horses, returned to Tratta, reported the zombie outbreak, returned a postal horse and mail, leveled up, and met with Thorin on the ship to discuss the clues. The next likely mission is scouting or disrupting a drug/slavery operation tied to **The Long Night**, the colosseum, and possibly old newspaper evidence.
The Zombies
Ivy listens to the head guy and decides to shove a carrot in his mouth while zombies are making noise at the door.
Lenny rifles through Cletus’s pack, finds a potion, and makes Cletus drink it, healing him.
The group searches a chest and finds:
- 1 alchemist’s fire
- 2 potions of healing
A tied-up zombie inside the building starts chewing through its rope. Ivy calls for rope and asks whether anyone would be upset if they kill it. The group notes the zombie has not killed anyone yet, but they would rather kill it than get bitten.
The party sees or hears lots of zombies outside the general store. They discuss several plans:
- Shoot zombies from the windows.
- Burn zombies outside.
- Lure zombies into a building and burn it.
- Use a fast character as bait.
- Climb onto a roof and lure zombies below.
- Burn the general store if needed.
They worry alchemist’s fire will not be enough for all the zombies, and they also worry about destroying the general store and supplies.
The first big section is combat inside and around the general store. Zombies are breaking free, pressing at doors, coming near windows, and attacking inside.
The party fights with spears, axes, fire attacks, and cleave/great-axe abilities. Several zombies are killed or knocked down. Cletus attacks from cover/from a backpack. Lenny climbs through a window while Jett with high Strength holds a door shut. Eventually the immediate zombies are defeated, and the party gets a short moment of calm.
The party barricades the door with a table. The shopkeeper gets angry because the party took or drank potions. The party argues they saved him; he argues they are stealing during a zombie attack.
The group discusses the priestess, who had run toward the church to ring a bell. Since no bell rang, some characters think she may already be dead. Others still consider checking on her.
The party confirms that zombies seem more attracted to sound than sight. Lanny tests this by making noise at a window, drawing a zombie, and the group kills it through the window.
The group debates going to the church, saving the priestess, finding Harold, or running for the stable. The strongest plan becomes sending the fast character, likely Lenny, toward the stable to find Harold.
Cletus  uses light/messaging during the run. Lenny sees zombies, avoids or disengages from them, gets attacked once, and keeps moving. The party watches from the general store while Lenny makes the dash.
The runner reaches the stable/barn and gets inside. A zombie is trying to push through a door near a dead dwarf. The party shuts and bars the door.
Inside the stable, they find:
- About six horses.
- A dead official-looking dwarf/body.
- A sick-looking man in the hay.
- Harold, who is alive but ill.
Harold has eaten the fish, which is bad news because the fish seems tied to the zombie sickness. He looks green/sick but says he has not been bitten. The party worries he may turn.
Harold wants the 200 gold and says the information is in his sack. The party searches the sack and finds supplies, rations, and an important object/clue.
They also find or recover a small statue/figurine, apparently ivory or carved. There is confusion about whether it was stolen from the dwarf/body.
Harold gives the key phrase/clue:
- **“The Long Night”**
They clarify that it means “night” as in darkness, not “knight.” Later they describe it as a sign with a sleepy knight in armor, too big for the bed. The party believes this is where the drugs are.
The dead dwarf rises as a zombie. The party fights it in the stable. It is hit with axe attacks and fire/force-type attacks. Eventually it drops face down again, basically back where it started.
After that, the party returns to dealing with Harold, who still looks sick.
**Harold, Fish, Herbs, And Cure**
The party realizes Harold may be infected because he ate the fish. They remember that another person was helped/cured with vegetables or herbs connected to magical water/spring.
There is discussion of Linda’s herbs, strange vegetables, magical water, and whether Harold can eat vegetables. Harold is reluctant and picky about food. Eventually the party gets herbs/vegetables from a sack on a horse, likely the mailman’s horse or Harold’s/Linda’s horse.
The plan is to leave Harold tied up until the herbs/remedy can help him, then leave town on horses.
**Escape From Brinecross**
The party decides not to stay and fight the whole zombie outbreak. They decide they did their job: get the information, find Harold, and survive.
They leave Brine/Brian Cross that night on horses, leaving the zombie plague behind. They acknowledge the town may still be in serious danger, but they intend to send help.
They also bring or recover:
- Harold’s backpack.
- The statue/figurine.
- The 200 gold, though they do not simply steal it.
- Horses.
- Mail from a postal horse.
**The Letter**
While traveling back, the party looks through the mail and finds one especially important letter. It is addressed to **Gultren, the Canvas Master**, to be held at the **Sticky Pear Inn**.
The letter is from someone who worked the lower warehouses. It reveals:
- Crates, chains, cages, fish, spice, and sometimes people move through the warehouses.
- People are brought in quiet, shackled, drugged, and marked for the arena.
- The writer knows routes, ships, and which guards drink or look away.
- A shipment is coming in **20 nights from now**.
- It will come after dark through the **south warehouse near the old stone ramp**.
- The writer asks for a signal: a scrap of red cloth tied to the third post by the loading yard.
- It is signed by “a man who’s hiding them.”
The party interprets this as evidence of people being trafficked into arena fights.
**Return To The City**
The party returns after about a day and a half of travel, gets a long rest, and reports the zombie outbreak to guards. The report is confused because nobody seems to know where Brine/Brian Cross is at first, but the guards eventually agree to make a report.
They also explain there are zombies/undead about a day and a half away, with survivors possibly still there.
**The Postal Horse And Mail**
The party has a postal horse, later called **Thunderhoof**, plus other horses they name. They are told to take the postal horse and mail to the municipal/mail building.
They return the postal horse and most of the mail. They keep the important letter. They try to get a reward for returning the horse, but only get about five gold rather than the hoped-for horse value.
There is discussion of horses, horse names, horseshoes, and whether the horseshoes may be special or magical. Jett with smithing knowledge/tools looks into the horseshoes and removes them for Cletus.
**Level Up**
The party receives enough XP to reach level 2. They take a long rest and recover hit points.
**The Newspaper / Gilded Ledger**
The party examines a ripped-out newspaper or broadsheet. It is recognized as **The Gilded Ledger**, one of two major broadsheets in Tratta.
The Gilded Ledger is described as:
- Official-sounding.
- Polished and calculated.
- Often taking the authorities’ side.
- Rarely lying outright, but choosing which truths to print.
The opposing paper is **Salt and Cinder Press**, described as:
- Raw, bold, dangerous.
- Focused on exposing corruption.
- Loved by common people.
- Hated by those in power.
- Interested in corruption tied to the colosseum, slavery, and black markets.
The article/paper is from about three years ago and seems connected to gladiators, the colosseum, Spiky the triceratops, and other famous arena figures.
**Meeting With Thorin**
Later, on the ship, a coach arrives and **Thorin** comes aboard. The party presents what they found:
- The ivory/statue object.
- The ripped newspaper/broadsheet.
- The letter.
- The phrase/location **The Long Night**.
- The information about drugs.
- The zombie outbreak.
They explain that **The Long Night** may be where the drugs are. Tron/Thorin knows about the drugs but not necessarily why they are being produced or how they connect to the other evidence.
He keeps or studies the paper and says the situation is concerning.
**The Long Night / Next Mission**
The party thinks **The Long Night** is tied to the drug operation. It may be a place with a sign showing a sleepy knight in armor.
The next likely move is to scout:
- **The Long Night**
- The colosseum
- The drug operation
- Possibly the south warehouse / old stone ramp connection
The party says they should disrupt the drug operation, but Tron/Thorin wants more information before final planning.
**Zombie Outbreak Explanation**
When asked about the zombies, the party explains that people heard a voice while fishing, fish jumped into nets, people ate the fish, and then turned into zombies. Another woman had abnormal vegetables/herbs from magical spring water that may cure or slow the infection.


The party left about 40 zombies behind in Brinecross, with some survivors possibly still there.
