Session 4.2 - Fizban’s Tower
(April 6th, 2024)
Morning - 23d of Hemist
Long rest in the forest.  Yvaine points out to all of us that she has healing potions on her belt - should anyone need.
We travel half a day and eventually come to a fork in the road.  At the fork is a rock with a floating, arcane “eye”.  Yvaine seems to think we are being watched (she used Razor to first examine it).  We consult our crude map and see we should head to the left.  As we go, there is a pink crystal a couple of feet tall - not sure of its purpose.  We continue and come to a waist-high wooden “light” post.  Dal checks to see if it emits light (with his cloak)- it does.  Sigil tries to pull the post out of the ground, but it is difficult.  We use digging and acid and spend half an hour messing with the post…and find it no longer works.  We continue down the path.


We walk another hour-ish and come up to a hill and see a floating blue crystal and a burned out tower - the tower is on an island in a lake.  The crystal is floating above the bridge and it looks just like the one at the citadel.  Razor is going to do a fly-by of the crystal (it is 60’ in the air; 3’ thick and tall).  We recall that the crystal powers the forcefield around the city; it enhances magic.  


Razor flies over the island and tower sees a huge telescope in the tower.  We see a boat docked on the base of the tower.  We advance to the bridge and stop at the edge.  We check it out and all seems ok.  Dal steps on the bridge and the crystal knocks us all prone.  But we are not injured.  Y tries to cast mage armor and it fails - she hears in her head (probably from her god Lyess) to be calm child, magic won’t work here (not those words, but that notion).


We cross the bridge and go to the island.  Sigil and Dal check out the boat; Ren checks out the water wheel (which is moving).  Y and Scar at base of stairs as Tass knocks on the door.  When that happens, the glass in the window above the door shatters and a creature (crystalline) appears behind Tass.  Initiative.


Ren goes first - cannot use Hunter’s Mark (no magic).  Shoots an arrow.  Being attacks Tass; then fires off some type of spell from his eyes.  Tass is blinded.  Tass goes inside.  Sigil dashes to the being.  Y tries to use patch and hears her goddess in her head again…no magic.  She kneels and clutches her belt and prepares a reaction in the event she is attacked (alchemist’s fire).  I try to talk to it - telling him that we mean he and the tower no harm and drop the names Gwyneth and Fizban and that we are there at G’s behest and I disengage and walk in.  Dal advances forward and takes dodge action (in case he is attacked).  Ren advances and uses disengage to go around him and goes in, but faces him once inside. Two more windows break and two more creatures appear outside.  Original one heals and attacks Sigil.  Tass advances to the staircase and starts to go up.  Scarlytt enters the fight - she and Sigil attack with swords.  Ren tries to shoot arrows.  Y appeals to Fizban out loud to stop his minions.  Tass continues to advance and search the tower.  Dal blinded; Scar and Sigil blinded again.  Tass advances up to the third floor. Everything is burned out.  Fight continues.  We discover that magic works in the tower.  We kill them and go inside.


Y casts detect magic and points it out to Scar…who shows Ren.  She then goes to investigate the rest of the tower.  Rest of the group investigates the study…Dal finds text and tries to comprehend languages.  Tass finds something in the desk and realizes it is a history of the blustery wastes.  Also find symbols that represent the gods.  Ren finds a hidden panel that uncovers a giant vault with elemental locks.  This vault is in the book that Dal finds…and realizes he needs to decipher the text to open the vault.  Y goes outside on the 5th floor…and 2 more glass golems break glass and try to come up to the roof to fight.  Y comes back inside.  She finds a chest that needs a key; a telescope that is magical (know known form of magic that she knows).  She comes down to tell us what she found.  Y looks at the lock and discerns that we could use elements to help unlock it.  We use water, torch, rocks, and blowing on the vault and hear a click….and the center lights up blue.  Keyhole illuminates.  After much discussion, Y casts the knock spell to open the lock.  The door begins to open and there is a large vault.  On the far wall, there is a trio mounted on the wall…with plaques.  A dagger, a bow/arrows, and a shield.  All magical.  There is a bottle and scroll as well…all magical.  There is a small book as well.    We find that Fizban created the potion of long life and administered it to Gwyneth years ago.  We think that Fiz is in the lost city described in the book.  Dal gets a dagger, Ren gets a bow, Y gets a bottle, Sigil gets an edible cookbook, Tass gets a scroll, Scar gets a shield.  


We all go up to the 5th floor.  Plan to open the chest (with key Tass found) and fight the glass golems.  We find a book from Fiz about all of the magic items he created that are across the lands.  We find the overalls mentioned in the book.  We know that Tass has a ring described in the book.  Tass puts on the overalls - he will no longer have the urge to use the restroom.  Also got 2 potions - red (not healing) and purple.  


We decide to go out on the roof.  Scar and Sigil lead the pack; everyone else stays inside.  We fight against 2 glass golems. We defeat them.  Tass goes to the telescope.  It is pointed to the NE.  As Tass looks through it - he can see an old ruined city, but he can see it clearly.  He sees a portal, an excavation site on the NW side of the city (w/tents).  He can see straight through all of the sand/wind that normally define the Blustery Wastes…the telescope is magical and has the ability to peer through to see clearly.  We mark the place where the telescope is facing.  Dal wants to move the telescope and move it to another location - he sees mountains and then ocean.  Telescope is moved with lots of dials.  Y investigates to see most worn spots on the scope.  Blustery Waste area is the most worn area.  We put the telescope in a false position before we leave it.  


We know that Kal’dar’s followers are excavating “a site of immense power” and are trying to raise an elemental army to take over the land.  We know that Kal’dar and the djinn are both tied to the Aurepura cult.  We see that the excavation site could be at the Blustery Waste….since we see evidence of that through the telescope.


We decide to take a long rest in the tower after gathering the cart and animals, and using identify to figure out what the potions we found are. Purple potion is a potion of mind reading.  


We plan to go to Tal’basar via horses/cart the next day taking the path we have not traveled through the forest (turning at the glyph).

Chip's Notes
Session Summary
Session 0 - Test (May 27, 2023)
In Marrowdale, the party (Dalbyre Bramblesage - Elf Bard, Opal Creekbed - Human Paladin, Scarlytt Amyran - Elf Paladin, Sigil - Half-Orc Fighter, Tassarion Ranges - Elf Cleric) forms at Arbescar Light and Magic (ALM). They encounter L’idoneus Obscurado and begin their adventure, though details are sparse.


Session 1.1 - The Old Mine (June 24, 2023)
Ren is missing, so the party searches for her. They fight wolf spiders, rescue Ren, and discover a trapped mine. They find a crystal bottle inscribed in Terran: "Death to whomever frees him. Wishes to whomever puts him back."


Session 1.2 - Possession & Temple (July 15, 2023)
Tassarion (Tass) becomes catatonic after merging with cult leader Malik from the A’urae’pura Cult. They learn Sillian killed Whisk, and all genasi are viewed negatively. Returning to Ren's rescue site, they find a temple, uncover Gwenyth and Fizban's relationship, and return to Tal’besar.


Session 2.1 - Silver Dragon (August 19, 2023)
Opal leaves the party. They explore another mine, spot dragons, find the "Goblinbane Flameblade" sword (which attracts goblins they kill), and encounter silver dragon Lothim in a cave. They trade the sword for 100gp.


Session 2.2 - The Oasis (September 23, 2023)
At the Oasis of the Night, they kill Talent for blocking a cave. His letter reveals profits for Erick the Alchemist in Scarbrook and orders to kill Whisk's murderer. They enter the cave and fight cultists but run out of time.


Session 2.3 - Deeper in the Oasis (November 3, 2023)
Continuing in the Oasis cave, they battle more cultists. Scarlytt dies but is revived via cult rituals. Tass and Ren confront and defeat the chef cultist.


Session 3.1 - The Alchemist (December 2, 2023)
In Scarbrook, they recruit Yvaine (female mage) per Council of Six request. They investigate suspicious Erick the Alchemist, who claims to turn lead to gold. Sigil gets a love potion. They sneak into his shop at night to access the cellars.


Session 3.2 - Alchemist’s Cellar (January 20, 2024)
Under Erick's shop, they investigate, kill enemies, and claim items before handing the shop over to authorities.


Session 4.1 - In Scarbrook (February 24, 2024)
On the 21st of Hemist, they explore Scarbrook: investigate Erick's keys and home (finding a safe), talk to Gwyneth (learning about Malik, Fizban, and cult splits), gather rumors at the inn, and travel toward Fizban’s Tower. They encounter green dragon wyrmlings, loot a caravan, witness gold dragons Wyndessa and C’mey defeat a larger green dragon (Elendore), converse with them, and camp.


Session 4.2 - Fizban’s Tower (April 6, 2024)
On the 23rd of Hemist, they reach Fizban’s Tower after navigating traps and fighting crystalline creatures. Magic fails outside but works inside. They explore, find a vault with magical items (dagger, bow, shield, etc.), learn Fizban created a potion of long life for Gwyneth, and view a ruined city via telescope. They rest and identify potions.


Session 4.3 - The Road Less Traveled (April 27, 2024)
Leaving the tower, they spot a giant and wyvern with air genasi holding a wanted poster for Tass. They battle, nearly losing Sigil and Yvaine, but prevail and loot. At a campsite, they confront Cornelius Woodrift (Sillian's father), agree to help with a ghost-guarded treasure for his aid, but later ambush and kill his group after freeing prisoners. They claim loot and level up.


Session 5.1 - Echoes of Ectoplasm (June 8, 2024)
Following Cornelius's map, they fight a ghost, explore a haunted mansion (killing zombies and spirits), loot chests (gold, bars, magical stones), and claim the mansion as a hidden base. Ren has a vision urging them to the Blustery Wastes to save Angela. They message Gwyneth for confirmation and prepare to travel.


Session 5.2 - Tal’basar and Beyond? (June 29, 2024)
Traveling to Tal’basar, they discuss Carl's poster. Guards note increased security. They investigate posters, learn Carl was sent home after arguing with Serina. Shopping and selling occur. Kal’dar confronts the town, demanding surrender; the party debates and sides with the Council of Six against him.
(Note: The document truncates here, but implies battles and events leading to later sessions, including cult disruptions and portal uses.)


Session 6.1 (Implied from context, date not specified)
The party deals with aftermath in Kravwynhold, rescues children from a witch (Harlowe), fights cultists, and Tass dies saving Angela. They escape via portal to Fizban’s Tower.


Session 6.2 (Date not specified)
In Kravwynhold, they rally townsfolk, confront the witch, battle her and minions. Tass sacrifices himself. They save children, flee to Everlite via dragon aid, and plan next moves.


Session 6.3 - Changes (April 26, 2025)
Emerging from the tower, the shield around Kravwynhold falls. They warn townsfolk, travel to Everlite (time-warped), reunite with locals, and meet Fizban. He reveals quests; Yvaine transforms into a warlock. Tass's funeral occurs.


Session 6.4 - A Cry from the North (May 25, 2025)
A ranger warns of a monster. They fight an icy creature, explore a cave prison, battle howlers and windwraiths, navigate traps and mists, and rest.


Session 6.5 - Sniff the Candle (June 22, 2025)
They navigate vine tunnels (losing memories), perform rituals, fight a mimic, loot chests, unseal a frozen grove, free trapped people (including Alt's family), and confront an ancient druid. They level up and identify items.

Whispers of the Sands
Book from Tsuul’physs Bhan
Chapter 1
In the heart of the Blustery Waste, a desolate expanse where sands danced to the whims of howling winds, destiny beckoned to a band of audacious explorers.  With determination etched into their brows, they embarked on a perilous journey that led them to an enclave concealed by towering dunes.  Nestled within this secretive canyon was an oasis, an unexpected haven where crystal-clear waters shimmered with an ethereal, iridescent glow.
It was as though the very land had gifted them a touch of the divine.  The explorers, their hearts ablaze with wonder, saw not just an oasis, but the promise of an extraordinary future.  United by their shared awe, they set the first stones of Athralis, a city that would rise from the embrace of the canyon’s walls like a phoenix reborn.  The walls, meticulously crafted from the sandstone gifted by the canyon, stood tall and defiant against the tumultuous elements, a testament to human resilience.
As the first rays of sunlight painted the dunes with shades of gold, Athralis began its journey.  The explorers’ dream had taken shape – a city founded not merely on practicality, but on the belief that the oasis held a touch of the divine, a whisper of fate that had guided them to this place.  With every stone laid, they etched their faith into the very foundations of the city, forging a bond between humanity and the arid realm that had bestowed this oasis upon them.
Thus, Athralis emerged, a sanctuary amidst the unforgiving waste, its walls a declaration of humanity’s unyielding spirit.  In the heart of Blustery Waste, where the desert’s challenges and secrets were woven into every grain of sand, the city stood as a living embodiment of determination, echoing the whispers of destiny that had led them to uncover the oasis and its mysteries.
Chapter 2
Amidst the arid expanse of the Blustery Waste, Athralis bloomed with a vitality that defied the desert’s harsh embrace.  The oasis, a shimmering oasis of life, became the very heart of the city’s prosperity.  Lush gardens, nurtured by the oasis’s life-giving waters, unfurled their vibrant petals alongside majestic architecture that reached for the heavens.  As the city’s walls held steadfast against the ceaseless winds, Athralis transformed into a resplendent jewel amidst the vastness of the desert.
Cultural symphonies played out in the city’s streets, as artisans brought forth intricate tapestries, gleaming jewelry, and masterful sculptures that seemed to breathe life into the stone.  Scholars, drawn by the city’s radiant aura, flocked to its libraries and halls of learning, eager to absorb the wisdom that had sprouted from the very sands beneath their feet.  Athralis became a crossroad of knowledge and art, where individuals from the farthest reaches of the wasteland converged to partake in the riches that only the oasis-born city could provide.
Yet, while the city thrived in the sunlight of its achievements, the shadows cast by its grandeur whispered of a hidden truth–a truth that only a select few dared to acknowledge.  In hushed tones and secretive gazes, the inhabitants of Athralis shared knowledge of a discovery that lay cloaked within the heart of the oasis, a discovery that held the potential to elevate the city’s prosperity to unparalleled heights.
An enigma concealed in the oasis’s depths–the notion sent ripples of intrigue through the city’s populace.  Whispers of an ancient portal, a portal that bridged the chasm between the moral realm and the ethereal domain of gods, danced through the air like grains of sand carried by the wind.  Those initiated into the secret began to tread carefully, as if the very air held the secrets of the universe, waiting to be unveiled.
In the shadows of Athralis’ grandeur, the stage was set for a new epoch–one that would blur the boundaries between human ambition and the mysterious forces that shaped their destinies.  As the city’s radiance shimmered across the desert sands, the secret within its heart beckoned, promising revelations that could alter the course of not on the Athralis, but the fabric of the Blustery Waste itself.
