## Citizenry

There are around 23,000 people living in Gevakaln, most of the population is within the city border, some are outside the walls in nearby farms. The island that the city watches over, **Sciogost**, hosts Rostiena's most dangerous prisoners — currently around 360 humanoids.

### Races

Gevakaln is a diverse city: Humans (25%), Half-orcs (25%), Elves (10%), Dwarves (20%), Weefolk (10%), Other (10%)

## Government

The ruler is **Alred Brydove** (Male High-elf Noble). He seldom leaves his keep and has his representatives take note of things happening in the city.

## Districts

The city is divided into three districts:

- **Fogwalk Underbelly** — the slums; high crime and equally high guard presence
- **Mittler Ward** — middle district
- **Brydove Heights** — where the rich live and prosper

Gevakaln presents a clean, tidy image to outsiders while its underbelly is deeply corrupt.

## Laws

Gevakaln follows standard Stoneshore Territory law — no additional city-specific rules. Publicly projects law and order; privately the corruption runs deep.
