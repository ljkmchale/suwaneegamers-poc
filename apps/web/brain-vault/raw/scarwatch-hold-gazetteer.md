Overview
Scarwatch Hold rises from the spine of the Jagged Waste Crags with pale stone walls and rising towers.  The architecture bears the marks of age and repair. Sections of the outer wall show darker stone where collapse and fire once bit deep during the Awakening, while newer masonry reinforces towers and parapets. Conical-roofed towers rise above the curtain wall, their narrow slits facing outward. Within the walls, the keep is functional rather than grand: drill yards worn smooth by boots, banners stiffened by constant wind, and lookout platforms where sentries linger longer than strictly necessary.
Historically, Scarwatch Hold has always been a border keep, never a seat of power. Its purpose is vigilance—monitoring movement through the crags, guarding the eastern approaches of the Yearning Vale, and serving as the last warning before danger spills westward. Though damaged during the Awakening, it was never abandoned.

Notable Locations
Bank
The Hold’s residents and visitors store their most valuable belongings and savings in the bank.  This bank is not part of the Realm Depository & Exchange System that exists in other locations in Myrdae.  The bank relies on the Hold’s soldiers for protection.  Guards rotate shifts here each harmon, but most don’t want this assignment because there isn’t much to do.


This one-story bank sports a teller’s window with a drop-down counter. A bespectacled gnomish woman in a silk tabard sits behind the glass. Outside, a bored guard stands watch.

Wearing glasses with her hair in an unkempt bun and clothing that is outdated, this middle-aged gnome is miserly with money but verbose with words.  She is good with coin, however, and is as wise as she is frugal.
	Bank Features
* Service Counter. Sturdy hinges connect a wooden drop-down counter to the bank’s window. At night, Thiddle folds up the counter and closes the window, then secures both with an iron grate.
* Vault. A locked, reinforced door connects the bank teller’s office to Thiddle’s private quarters and a vault. Inside the vault are several strongboxes containing the Hold’s deposited wealth.
Services Provided
* Take Out a Loan. A character can borrow up to 10 gp from the bank with an interest rate of 5 sp per day. There’s no minimum payment due, but if the loan is left unpaid for long, word of the character’s financial insolvency spreads throughout the Hold.
* Deposit and Withdraw Money. A character can deposit gold and other valuables at the bank. They can withdraw some or all the deposited savings during regular business hours at no cost.


Barn & Stables
The Barn & Stables shelter the Hold’s livestock. It also serves merchants and travelers who need a place to shelter their mounts and wagons while visiting the Hold. Animals housed here receive a box stall or pen, food, and grooming.  Animals are well taken care of in this clean, roomy location.

Citadel
The Warden governs the Hold from its Citadel, a stone stronghold solidly built to withstand assault. Multiple towers rise above the main building, which is 30 feet tall. The citadel shelters emergency resources, municipal chambers, and the Hold’s armed forces. Militaristic banners and upholstered furniture decorate its dignified interior.


Two round towers with pointed roofs flank this tiered stronghold. Archers march along its stone parapets, watching for signs of trouble. Two guards stand outside the citadel’s iron doors; they acknowledge passersby with stern nods.

	________________
Citadel Features
* Armory. Barding and lances for the Hold’s cavalry—thirty to forty troops strong—are stored here, though the equipment is off-limits to visitors.
* Barracks. Sparsely furnished quarters throughout the citadel can collectively accommodate up to thirty-six soldiers at once.  Because there are always guards on duty, the barracks are rarely full.
* Great Hall. A spacious hall in the Citadel hosts ceremonies, proclamations, and other receptions.  Three offices are adjacent to the Hall for the Warden and his two advisors.  The Warden’s features a small conference table with six chairs, as well as an ornate desk, littered with papers.  Umlin’s has a tidy desk and a neat collection of books and ledgers on various shelves with two wooden chairs for visitors.  Nambra’s has a few comfortable sitting chairs and art on display - with a private door to her storeroom where she keeps all things arcane.
* Private Chambers. Two lavishly decorated rooms are reserved for special guests of the Hold, while another belongs to the Warden.
* Towers. Two peaked towers rise from the citadel.  Nambra and Umlin each have a chamber in a Tower.  Nambra’s is decorated with carpets, tapestries, and other fineries; Umlin’s is more down to earth with a bed and many bookshelves.
* Undercroft. Beneath the citadel are cellars containing vast provision stores, a cistern, and a guarded dungeon with four stout cells for detainees and prisoners. 
Services Provided
* Defense. In times of crisis, the Warden can muster up to thirty-six trained soldiers from the Citadel to defend the Hold. The citadel also provides refuge during incursions.  
* Seat of Government. During the day, the Warden and his two assistants conduct business in the main building, where they hold an audience with citizens and visitors of the Hold.  When situations require a more public setting, the yard outside the Scarwatch House is used.  The Hold acts as a well-armed and judicial guardian for parts of the Yearning Vale, Gaelscape, and the Mahruud.


Gatehouse – Inner
This gatehouse houses on-duty soldiers and separates the civilian areas of the Hold from the Palisades and Citadel.


This gatehouse consists of a broad stone archway framed by a squat building constructed along the Hold’s inner wall. Battlements line its flat roof, atop which two guards stand watch.  Two guards are stationed at the base to question those who intend to enter.
Anyone who approaches the gates will be questioned about their business.  Guards will try to handle requests themselves, but will defer to the Captain of the Guard as necessary.

* Murder Holes. Guards atop the gatehouse can thrust spears down at invaders through holes in the ceiling of the archway.

* Guarding.  Guards from this Garrison are positioned in various roles around the Hold, including the Inner and Outer Gatehouses, the Bank, and on patrol. During the day, four soldiers are positioned at each gatehouse:  two on the roof and two on the ground.  At night, eight soldiers stand watch outside each gatehouse: six on the roof and another two on the ground.


Gatehouse – Outer
This gatehouse is the first stop for those seeking to enter the Scarwatch Hold. The guards here greet outsiders, watch for potential threats, and permit entrance to citizens and peaceful visitors.


Two stone towers flank an archway that leads into the Hold. A sturdy-looking gatehouse spans the archway and connects the towers. A wooden drawbridge allows passage over a steep ditch surrounding the Hold. The bridge is currently drawn shut.
Two guards stand before the raised bridge. As you approach, one of the guards shouts, “Hail! Do you bring peace or trouble?”
Any visitors must respond to the guards and say whether they mean well or ill. After confirmation, the guards call for the drawbridge to be lowered by others in the adjacent towers, then welcome visitors to the Hold.

	Gatehouse Features
* Drawbridge. The Hold’s first line of defense is a steep ditch, 10 feet wide and 10 feet deep, in front of the fortress. The only sure means to cross the dry moat is the drawbridge at this gatehouse.
* Gate. The stone arch behind the drawbridge contains an iron portcullis and large oak doors.
* Towers. Stairs inside each square, 30-foot-tall tower connect the ground floor to the roof. Arrow slits in the tower walls and roof battlements help archers defend the Hold.  The towers are staffed by guards who raise and lower the drawbridge.
Services Provided
* Lower the Drawbridge. The drawbridge usually remains raised night and day. To lower or raise the bridge, wheels in both towers must be turned to release or tighten the drawbridge’s chains. The portcullis operates in a similar manner.
* Orientation. The gate guards can describe and give directions to any location in the Hold. They can also tailor recommendations to a traveler’s needs.

Palisades
The sheltered palisades are where the Hold’s defenders live and practice combat drills. 
Targets, poles with rings, and practice dummies dot this very large courtyard, which also contains a large stable and storage facility, a greenhouse and garden area, and a seated viewing area that is used on occasion by the visiting public.  Cavalry soldiers train atop horseback while guards watch the area from the walls and towers that around it.

	Palisades Features
Performance/Jousting Field. This area serves as a training ground most days, but also houses tournaments that generally take place each harmon. 
Cavalry Stable. The palisades contain a stable with thirty horses. These war-trained horses serve as mounts for the Hold’s cavalry.
Greenhouse and Gardens. Crops and plants for the Citadel are grown and tended to in this area.
Guard Towers. Four towers rise above the courtyard, one in each corner. The smallest, to the southeast, holds up to eight guards. The southwest and northeast towers each hold one dozen guards, as well as food stores and other supplies. The Great Tower to the northwest is 60 feet tall.
Watches. Eight soldiers (Guard), two atop each tower, watch over the palisades at all hours.
