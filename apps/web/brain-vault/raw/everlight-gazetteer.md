## Overview

Welcome to Everlight, a hidden gem nestled in the heart of a snow-kissed valley surrounded by towering, frost-covered peaks. This charming village offers a warm haven for travelers and adventurers seeking respite from the harsh wilderness. At its heart stands the grand Twinkling Fir, a magnificent tree adorned with glowing lights and glittering ornaments that symbolize the unity and spirit of the town. Cozy wooden cottages line the streets, their snow-dusted rooftops framing a bustling central square alive with the scent of spiced cider and the sound of cheerful laughter.

The village is particularly magical during Winterfest Eve, when garlands of holly and festive decorations drape every building, and the community gathers to celebrate amid music and light. More than a peaceful retreat, Everlight serves as a gateway to legendary adventures, with paths leading to the perilous Frostbite Mountain, the desolate Blustery Waste, and the domain of Lithium, the Silver Dragon.

## History

Nestled within a secluded, snow-kissed valley, Everlight is a testament to the divine mercy of Urlich, the God of Winter, Frost and Ice. This village emerged from a cataclysm that reshaped the land 5,000 years ago — the fall of Sunlit Haven. As divine punishment for the hubris of Sunlit Haven's rulers, the surrounding lands became the Blustery Waste, a barren expanse of perpetual storm and sand. Yet, amidst the gods' wrath, the kind-hearted villagers who would become Everlight's founders were spared by Urlich. Shielding them within the inner mountains of the Waste, the god granted them two gifts: perpetual winter to insulate them from corruption, and a radiant artificial sun — the Everlight — which hangs eternally in their sky.

Over centuries, the villagers transformed their refuge into a thriving community. The Everlight itself became a symbol of hope, unity, and divine balance, infusing the village's culture with reverence for warmth amid cold and light amidst darkness.

Everlight's population reflects a mix of hardy and resourceful individuals adapted to perpetual winter and isolation. Demographics shaped by divine history and practical survival.

## Holidays and Traditions

### Winterfest

The Winterfest Celebration is an annual event honoring Urlich and the spirits of light and frost. During Winterfest, villagers light candles, leave offerings at the shrine, and share stories of hope and perseverance. Garlands of holly and festive decorations drape every building; the community gathers for music and light. The market is particularly vibrant at Winterfest Eve, drawing visitors for festive treats, enchanted winter gear, and decorative wares.

The **All Bright** ceremony (referenced in connection with the Populi Verde and Ainran) may be related.

### Town Meetings

Villagers gather in Town Hall once per month or during emergencies. Major decisions (expeditions to Frostbite Mountain, negotiating with Lithium) are brought for public input.

### Interaction with External Forces

1. **Frostbite's Influence** — The council debates how to handle the growing threat of Frostbite. Some advocate for alliances; others call for proactive defenses. Villagers fear Frostbite's power may one day overwhelm Everlight.
2. **Lithium the Silver Dragon** — Lithium controls the north route through the mountains. The council maintains a cautious but respectful relationship, offering tributes/gifts in exchange for safe passage and trade.

## Laws

Characters engaging in criminal activity are arrested by the city guard and charged per City Ordinances. Cases are brought before the Council for judgment.

## Economy and Trade

Everlight's economy is largely barter-based, with coins supplementing transactions. Adventurers often trade rare artifacts or services for supplies. The market is especially vibrant during Winterfest Eve.

**Specialized Craftsmanship:** Renowned for high-quality winter gear — frost-resistant cloaks, boots, and gloves.

**Local Resources:**
- **Snowberries (Frostberries)** — used to produce Snowberry Jam and Snowberry Wine; sweet yet refreshing.
- **Frostwood** — durable wood from frost-resistant trees near the village; used in construction and crafting.
- **Crystalline Veins** — crystals mined from nearby caves; used for magical enhancements or sold to mages and alchemists.

## Religion

### Urlich the Hearthkeeper

Villagers revere Urlich as "the Hearthkeeper" — a guardian of winter, frost, and ice whose symbol is a glowing flame or sun cradled by frosted branches, representing the balance between warmth and cold.

*(Note: The wider Myrdae canon portrays Urlich as "The Regent of Ice" — cold is purity, cold strips away weakness. Everlight's interpretation emphasizes Urlich's protective, warm aspect — the side that spared their founders from the Blustery Waste. These are compatible but distinct faces of the same deity.)*

**Beliefs:** The Hearthkeeper blesses those who foster community, offer warmth to the lost, and protect others from harm. Acts of kindness — sharing food, offering shelter — are sacred duties.

### The Balance of Elements

Beyond Urlich, Everlight acknowledges spirits of nature and the elements, especially fire and light. These spirits require respect and offerings to maintain balance. Angering them is believed to cause harsh winters or misfortune.

### Religious Practices

- **Daily Offerings:** Candles, food, or carved wooden tokens left at the Shrine or household hearths. Snowberry branches are a common offering.
- **Traveler's Blessing:** Adventurers visit the shrine for protection before heading into the wilds.
- **Hearth Ritual:** Each household maintains a small flame that is never allowed to go out during winter — a symbol of hope and endurance.

### Shrine of Everlight (5)

Small, tranquil temple near the south of Everlight. Smooth stone adorned with carved motifs of sun, hearths, and protective runes. Entrance flanked by evergreen wreaths and lanterns. Interior: scent of pine and beeswax; shimmer of magical light on walls; white-and-gold draped altar; offerings of food, candles, pine branches.

**Special features:**
- *Divine Glow:* Dim illumination in 30-foot radius; prevents frost from forming inside.
- *Urlich's Blessing:* Visitors who leave an offering at the altar gain advantage on saving throws against cold-based effects for the next 24 hours.
