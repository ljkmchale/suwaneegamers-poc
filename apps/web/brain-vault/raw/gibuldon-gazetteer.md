## Notable Locations

### Crown & Arms (Tavern / House of Ill Repute)

A large establishment with an open area for sitting/standing, a bar, a stage, and stairs leading to an upstairs balcony. Patrons fill the space — drinking, eating, and listening to the minstrel on stage. Food is notably good. Drinks are served with a half-salt rim. Some patrons use the upstairs rooms with companions — this is a house of ill repute as well as a tavern.

NPCs: Not yet named (owner, co-owner, and attendant roles exist — pending).

### Grand Tower

Home to the **Lords of Magic**. *(The Lords of Magic originally operated from the top of the Vault pyramid in Dhá Chaomhnóir before the capital was moved to Gibuldon ~100 years ago.)*

### MEAD Society Headquarters

The **MEAD Society** (Meet, Expose, Ascertain and Discover) carries out the work of the Dicon (Inverland's governing body). Formerly headquartered at "Ms. V's cottage." The caretakers are **Pallas** — goblins with green skin who drive carriages and tend the gardens.
