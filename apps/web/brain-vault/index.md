### World Locations

Canonical geography - what a place *is*, independent of campaign events.

- [[world/locations/Adsuren|Adsuren]] - village in Inverland on the Wehgyn River; Precura Fruit trade hub; 12 named locations, 18 NPCs; primary SoD setting.
- [[world/locations/Hesfal|Hesfal]] - hamlet in the Yearning Vale Territory; opening Dungeons III settlement.
- [[world/locations/Scarwatch Hold|Scarwatch Hold]] - repaired border keep on the Jagged Waste Crags; Yearning Vale warning post with gatehouses, citadel, palisades, bank, stables, and Dungeons III campaign links.
- [[world/locations/Tratta|Tratta]] - large Yearning Vale port city on the Broken Deep; key Silent Vanguard destination.
- [[Trailpoint]] - Orbansia crossroads town in Severed Bend.
- [[world/locations/Nunglthil|Nunglthil]] - neutral Underdark trading enclave on Rothenloch; five pact-bound enclaves, Pact Council, Favor, strict neutrality, layered cliffside layout, and lake trade.
- [[world/locations/Abbey of Light|Abbey of Light]] - multi-faith sanctuary/orphanage south of O'naren in the Borealian Sway, protected by Stone Giants.
- [[O'naren]] - elven Borealian Sway settlement west of Qal'dynn, centered on Driftglow Pond, Fralee worship, mining, craft, and Realmwarden protection.
- [[world/locations/Emberstran|Emberstran]] - Baltwood / Orbansia coastal city on the Glimmering Sea; wine, trade, guilds, temples, and elected civic rule.
- [[Ahndashere]] - Baltwood marsh-and-hill town where the Northern Stoneflow reaches the Glimmering Sea; stilted boardwalks, halfling hill burrows, elder-appointed rule, and old Torec shrine.
- [[Basctdelm]] - fortified Halesworth island capital on Lake Tribathe; Bathaen imperial seat ruled by Queen Breya Talward, with strict law, Watch patrols, Weave Wardens, and major archives.
- [[Dunduar]] - dwarven mining village and later mountain stronghold; source of Dunduar Ore.
- [[Oldport]] - Inverland port tied to Osanna and the Festival of Fair Winds.
- [[Bistron]] - village tied to the Laztyr and Suthrin tale.
- [[Ulgreer]] - village tied to Diverra's ascension myth.
