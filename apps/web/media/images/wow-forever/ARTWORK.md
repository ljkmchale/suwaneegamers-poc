## Visual rules for this page

- All new generated art must match Blizzard's official Forever key art: visibly painted brushwork, bold stylized Warcraft silhouettes, oversized timber and stone forms, lavender/blue atmospheric distance, and warm gold/peach light. Use the official hero as an actual image reference for new scenes. Avoid photorealistic or generic cinematic dark-fantasy rendering.
- Decorative icons must be generated painted inventory-style objects with substantial shapes and shaded materials. Do not substitute Lucide outlines, emoji, or flat vector symbols.
- Typography must follow the site's configurable `--font-heading` and `--font-body` tokens, with the same Cinzel/Lora fallbacks used in globals.css. Do not hard-code a separate display or body typeface.
- Character and faction details stay undecided until the group supplies them. These illustrations are atmospheric fan art, not documented game-night scenes.
