# Ladies Poker calendar artwork

`ladies-poker-v1.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool
on November 4, 2026, then resized to 1200px wide and encoded as WebP.
It depicts a fictional friendly poker gathering, with emerald felt, plum
clothing and warm amber light to fit the site's illustrated gaming artwork.
The people are illustrative and do not represent actual members.

Calendar title matching is owned by `apps/web/lib/calendarArtwork.ts`.
No scheduled content writer manages this mapping or image. Campaign headers
continue to use the existing campaign data and Drive synchronization.
