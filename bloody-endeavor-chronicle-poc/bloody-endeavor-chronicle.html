Up in Hol’durog

We have been searching Fyrstholith, the first peak of Yaggermon, that house the three Runegrip clans:

  • Hol’gardur - village at foot of mountain
  • Hol’kulrum - big city destroyed by giants
  • Hol'durog - another Runegrip community, highest of all

We have learned that the Runegrip clan dwarves are impacted by the same plague upon their crops that Rhody’s farm is impacted by. Our immediate goals are:

1. Cure Rhody – Get Rhody to the witch to deal with the infection he has and cure him before the 15th of Paramor!

2. Help Witgaurd – Get to Khagdaruhl (KAG-DA-RRULE) to get the dwarves on the side of the Rosteina Foothills

3. Finish the Cult & Avenge Bakah – Track down with the Cult of the Coldbreath (Monks of Iohir)

Back in Hol’gardur

1. a strange location in the mountains, and

2. another place in the mountains that we believe is likely the dwarven kingdom Khagdaruhl.

With the permission from Fomeck Runegrip (the ruler of Hol’gardur), we create a memorial cairn for Bakah.

It’s the next day, the 7th of Paramor

We travel for 4 days, heading to find Madam Mabel for a cure for Rhody. We see a gnome with wolves trying to take his scroll case. We help the gnome, learning him to be Pagern Stonebuckle, the wizard. Pagern agrees to adventure with us while he looks for gold to build a great wizard tower.

  • Sending sucks … we got the wrong bitch (dont give up on sending, just a shotty memory of the person)

We believe we arrive at the place we believe we are supposed to be, but it doesn’t seem right. We question if we’re lost. Turns out that Madam Mabel has an illusion spell set up for protection. Albross dispels magic long enough to see where we need to go. We travel through illusionary woods for some time. We come across a waterfall where someone is bathing. Madam Mabel is Rhody’s elderly witch lady. We begin to make a deal with her and she rips off the disease from Rhody, which we find we have to fight as a strange beast.

After some time, we defeat the creature and speak with Madam Mabel on what her ask is:

  • Get their bags that they will keep on them, unopened,
  • A piece of their body (hair, fingernail, etc), and

  • The party can have whatever else is there

She gives Rhody a monocle to see in the dark and hidden creatures. She steps up to a large tree that reveals a large portal in its side that we walk through …
