# Tratta

# **Overview**

Tratta stands as one of the great maritime cities of Myrdae, a vast port capital of the Yearning Vale and a central artery of trade along the Broken Deep. Purpose-built and carefully planned, the city is renowned as a commercial crossroads, where inland routes from the Vale converge with sea-lanes stretching toward the Bathaen Empire, the Mahruud, and beyond. Merchants, scholars, sailors, and diplomats all pass through Tratta, giving it a reputation as a city where coin, knowledge, and influence move with equal speed.

The city’s origins trace back to the **Anchored Spire**, an immense arcane and navigational structure raised in the Pre-Fracturing era. What began as a fortified waystation and harbor anchorage for early sea trade quickly drew permanent settlement. As ships increasingly relied on the Spire as a landmark and stabilizing point for navigation, a town known simply as *Trade Town* formed around it. Over generations, this settlement expanded outward into a walled city, formalizing its docks, archives, and civic institutions, eventually taking the name Tratta as it grew into the dominant port of the Yearning Vale.

Several landmarks define Tratta’s identity at a glance. Its **tiered harbor**—divided between lower working docks and elevated high docks—forms the city’s beating heart, always alive with cargo, shipwrights, and foreign sails. The **Anchored Spire** still rises above the skyline, both symbol and tool of Tratta’s mastery of the sea. Civic pride and spectacle are embodied in the **SeaRoute Colosseum**, where public games, ceremonies, and trials are held, while trade power is reinforced through institutions such as the **Trade Way Archives** and the heavily guarded **Reserve Vaults** that safeguard contracts, charters, and wealth.

## 

## Surrounded by towering sandstone walls, the city glows warmly under the sunlight, its architecture adorned with high arches and elegantly domed rooftops. The bustling port along the Broken Deep is alive with the sound of creaking ships, shouted orders, and the hum of trade, drawing merchants from across Myrdae and beyond. Tratta is a city of culture and spectacle, home to a grand arena where warriors and beasts clash to the roars of the crowd, a majestic theater where the finest performances captivate audiences, and an ancient mage tower that pierces the skyline, whispering of arcane secrets older than the city itself. The air is thick with a mix of salt and spices, echoing its position as a hub of maritime trade. Streets are vibrant and teeming with life, as merchants hawk their wares and travelers marvel at the intricate details etched into every wall and corner of this bustling coastal gem.

## **History**

In 807 PF (Pre-Fracturing), a large tower known as the Anchored Spire was completed, lending rise to a small surrounding village known only as Trade Town.  Becoming a hub of trade for the Mahruud, the small town grew quickly and over time became known as Tratta.  

In the earliest days, Tratta was primarily run by the **Runic Brotherhood**, a group of mages that leveraged the trade to its advantage for economic and informative gain.  In time, the Runic Brotherhood merged with the Tegenwald Phet, and the city grew to require governing beyond the interests of magic users.  Around 600 PF, Tratta became part of the Tor Alliance, gaining support from the Tor of the Mahruud and acquiring their own Mahlguard.

Following the Awakening, the Mahruud fractured into several independent territories. Tratta split from the old empire and became the capital of the newly formed Yearning Vale.

# **Citizenry**

Tratta is home to approximately 500,000 residents. Roughly two-thirds (about 333,000) live within the city walls, while the remainder dwell in the surrounding harbor towns, warehouse districts, and farming hamlets of the Yearning Vale that fall under Tratta’s direct control.

Humans form the clear majority, reflecting the city’s origins as a Mahruud trade port. Halflings, dwarves, gnomes, and elves are common, with smaller numbers of tieflings, dragonborn, and other races drawn by the constant flow of commerce.

Most citizens live a comfortable middle-class life tied directly to trade: dockworkers, sailors, warehousers, weavers, fishermen, artisans, merchants, and their families. The lower docks and warehouse district house the working poor and transient sailors, while the Upper City holds the homes and estates of wealthy guild masters and merchant families.

Tratta’s people are pragmatic and business-minded. They value contracts, fair dealing, and hard work above all. Locals are generally welcoming to outsiders who bring coin or goods, but they remain shrewd and quick to spot anyone who threatens the smooth flow of trade. Social status is measured more by wealth and guild standing than by bloodline, creating a bustling, competitive atmosphere where opportunity and rivalry walk hand in hand.

## **Holidays and Traditions**

Tratta observes two major annual holidays that reflect its history and its ties to the sea.

### **Vale Day**

The anniversary of Tratta’s formal separation from the fractured Mahruud comes in early Garnest and its recognition as capital of the Yearning Vale after the Awakening.

Guild masters and the Mahl lead a grand harbor procession of decorated ships flying the new Vale banners. At noon on the second day the Mahl reads the Proclamation of Independence from the balcony of Mahl Palace while merchants offer “sovereignty discounts” across both markets. The evening ends with fireworks launched over the Broken Deep.

### 

### **Brault’s Descent**

Held in the cold season, the ancient myth of Brault, He Born Asunder, being cast from the heavens and striking the sea to form the turbulent Broken Deep—honoring the god of battle and the raw power of the waters that both sustain and threaten Tratta.

Arena bouts and mock naval combats are held at the lower docks; participants and spectators offer broken weapons or shattered ship timbers to the Statue of Brault as offerings. The day closes with a silent vigil at the Broken Sea Pearl where priests of Coralei and Brault bless sailors for the coming year.

News and rumors travel fast around Tratta, thanks to the two notable and competing broadsheets.

  * Occasionally runs “exclusive” stories that undercut rivals just enough to discredit them

# **Government**

Tratta is ruled by the Mahl, a title originally granted by the Tor of the Mahruud and equivalent in status to a duke. After the Awakening fractured the Mahruud into independent territories, Tratta became the capital of the Yearning Vale, with its Mahl recognized as the sovereign ruler of the entire vale.

The current ruler is Mahl Garai Solsan, who governs from Mahl Palace in the Upper City. He is assisted by a council of guild leaders and trade magistrates who handle day-to-day administration of commerce, taxation, and civic affairs.

Law and order are maintained by the Mahlguard, a disciplined civic force that functions more as constabulary than standing army. The Mahlguard protects the docks, trade routes, city gates, and warehouses, with their primary loyalty to Tratta and the Yearning Vale—its people, its contracts, and its vital role as a trade hub of Myrdae.

### **Mahl Garai Solsan**

Mahl Garai Solsan has earned his way to leadership of Tratta by means of his infamous mercenary and trade practices.

## 

## The Mahl of Tratta’s unimposing stature is compensated for by his keen wit and demanding personality.  His skin is a sun-beaten brown with small points that reflect in the light as if dusted with glitter.  His thin build is marked with small patterns of scales that would appear to be tattoo markings if not for their raised texture.  He has short dark brown hair that naturally pushes away from his face, further accenting his piercing gaze with narrow eyes, a flatter than normal nose and slim mouth.

### **Mahlguard (Tratta Watch)**

Tratta’s guards are the Mahlguard, formerly the Torguard of the Mahruud. 

## 

## The presence of the Mahlguard is unmistakably intimidating as they patrol the docks, markets, and trade routes. Hulking figures in heavy plate armor and horned helms trail long blue plumes that snap in the sea wind. Each has survived the arenas as part of their brutal initiation; locals of the Yearning Vale know better than to cross one.

## Their shields bear a roaring leviathan maw in blue-green scales, jaws lined with hooked teeth and trailing stylized waves. The design honors Tratta’s seafaring roots while projecting raw, unrelenting force. Tunics display the crest of Tratta, completing the unmistakable uniform.

## **Laws**

Tratta maintains a pragmatic and commerce-focused system of laws designed to protect trade, contracts, and the efficient operation of the port city. The Mahlguard enforce all city ordinances with broad authority granted by the Mahl.

Individuals accused of crimes are detained by the Mahlguard and brought before a Trade Magistrate for a hearing. These magistrates, appointed by the Mahl, resolve most cases swiftly. Disputes involving large sums, influential merchants, or serious crimes are escalated to the Mahl’s Council for judgment.

The legal system is generally viewed as efficient and fair by the merchant class, though it heavily favors the protection of contracts and property. Common punishments include substantial fines, confiscation of goods or vessels, loss of trading rights, forced labor on the docks or in the warehouses, short-term imprisonment, or exile from the Yearning Vale.

# **Economy and Trade**

Tratta’s economy revolves around its role as the primary port and commercial crossroads of the Yearning Vale. Goods flow constantly through the tiered harbor: local catches of fish and shellfish from the Broken Deep, fresh produce and grains from the fertile vale farmlands, and woven textiles from local looms. These exports ship out to distant ports beyond.

Imports arrive daily—spices, rare woods, metals, luxury goods, and exotic wares—unloaded at the lower working docks, stored in the Lower Warehouse district, then moved to the bustling Lower Market or the more exclusive High Market for trade. The Promissory serves as the grand hall where major deals are struck and contracts sealed.

Trade is regulated and protected by the Tor’s councils of guild leaders and trade magistrates, with the Mahlguard enforcing order on the docks and routes. The Reserve Vaults safeguard wealth and charters, while the Trade Way Archives record agreements and navigational knowledge that keep commerce flowing smoothly.

This mercantile foundation supports much of the city’s 35,000 residents and reinforces Tratta’s reputation as an indispensable hub of coin and influence across Myrdae.

# **Religion**

The people of Tratta worship the full pantheon of Myrdae, with no single deity dominating daily life. Four sites serve as the main centers of faith, reflecting the town’s practical, seafaring, and scholarly character.

* **Coralei**, the Tide Keeper, receives the strongest local devotion. As goddess of seas and oceans, her blessings protect sailors, ensure safe voyages, and maintain the balance of tides that drive Tratta’s port economy. **The Broken Sea Pearl** serves as her primary temple.  High Priest Olgherrig Wahls leads services here, assisted by priests and acolytes.  
* **Myrdris**, the Grand Weaver is honored for knowledge and magic. The modest **Shrine of Knowledge** provides a quiet space for scholars, mages, and scribes to study, pray, and seek guidance on ethical use of the Weave.  
* A large **Temple of Tratta** stands as the central house of worship for all gods, used for community rites, festivals, and neutral ground when multiple faiths gather.  
* **Brault**, He Born Asunder—god of battle and might—earns respect from guards, mercenaries, and those who value raw strength. His **Statue of Brault** functions as an open-air shrine where warriors offer prayers before voyages or conflicts, often performing the Invocation of Might.

Worship across Tratta remains pragmatic rather than fervent, blending personal devotion at the specialized sites with occasional communal gatherings at the main temple.

# **Notable Locations**

## **Anchored Spire**

## The Anchored Spire is the oldest and tallest building (130 feet tall) in Tratta, which was a mainstay for the Veiled mages of the Tegenwald Phet.  The square two story building at the base of the building is surrounded by a ten-foot wall. The perimeter wall has its gates opened during the day but is secured at night.

## 

## Accented with auburn features, this relatively simple round tower reaches high above the rooftops of all other buildings in Tratta.  The tower rises off a two-story square building, slightly tapering as it rises over eighty feet before expanding into a larger two-story cylinder with a faded dark cone roof.

### **Sentient Stonelips**

The tower is a sentient being that is referred to most commonly as “Stonelips”. The name is in reference to large arched stone doors containing a mouth about 15 inches wide and set six feet off the ground are placed in the middle of each wall of the ground floor.  Each of the doors is topped with a keystone adorning the “Veil” symbol of the Tegenwald Phet. Stonelips can control one or all mouths at the same time, talking in a deep voice that speaks slowly.  The tower will not accommodate guests that are not invited unless that have been granted entry before and allowed to return.

Entering the tower is like stepping into pure darkness, impenetrable from light, blindsight or truesight.  Entrance immediately teleports the individual(s) to a common room with one door, couches, tables of food and drink, while the appropriate host or hostess arrives.

## **Travane College**

**Founded by:** *Arannis Travane (1154–1221)*  
 **Motto:** *“Chart the Mind, As We Chart the Sea.”*  
 **Commonly Called:** *The Travane* or *Travane College of the Vale*

Travane College stands as the intellectual heart of Tratta — a sprawling riverside campus of pale stone halls and copper-roofed towers that overlook the city’s harbor. Founded over a century ago by Arannis Travane, a half-elf historian and master navigator, the college began as a humble School of Navigation and Maritime Studies, offering structured instruction in mapmaking, astronomy, and tradecraft to sailors of the Yearning Vale. Over time, its reputation for precision, intellect, and moral integrity drew scholars from across Myrdae.

Now regarded as one of the **premier institutions of the New Era**, Travane College has expanded far beyond its nautical origins. Its curriculum embraces **natural philosophy, history, diplomacy, and magical cartography**, but its seal — a stylized sextant within a radiant compass rose — remains a proud reminder of its beginnings at sea.

## 

## \<description to read aloud to players\>

### **Founder – Arannis Travane**

Born to a merchant captain of the Bathaen coast and an Elle’berian scholar of Caelora, Arannis Travane embodied the meeting of exploration and intellect. As a young man, he served aboard countless trade ships that plied the coasts of Myrdae and the distant isles beyond the Brokenflow. By his middle years, he was both a trader of rare goods and a collector of knowledge, charting not just seas but philosophies.

After surviving a storm that wrecked half his fleet near the Tideless Span, Arannis retired to Tratta, where he invested his wealth into founding a college “to teach the art of never being lost.” His blend of navigation, history, and reason created a philosophy later dubbed **“Travane’s Compass”** — the belief that every soul carries an inner true north, found only through knowledge and reflection.

Arannis died peacefully in 1221, but the college honors him each year during the **Festival of Bearings**, when lanterns are floated on the river to symbolize all who seek direction in the world. His bronze statue, bearing a compass and scroll, stands before the Grand Hall of Bearings.

### **Reputation**

Travane College is renowned for producing cartographers, scholars, and diplomats of unmatched reputation. Admission is competitive, but merit outweighs lineage — a tradition Arannis himself insisted upon. Graduates of Travane are known for clear reasoning, disciplined curiosity, and a measured sense of ethics — qualities summed up in the college’s motto: *“Chart the Mind, As We Chart the Sea.”*

## **Tavern Games**

### **Bones**

(from *Descent into Avernus*)

* Each player requires several six-sided dice.   
* Each player puts the agreed ante in the pot.  
* Each player rolls three (3) dice.   
* Play then proceeds clockwise around the table, with the host of the game going last.  
* On their turn, a player can choose to “stand” or “roll.”   
  * Stand: If the player stands, the next player can take a turn.   
  * Roll:  A player who rolls takes an additional die and rolls it.   
  * Bust:  If the total of their dice exceeds 21, they “bust” and are out of the game. Otherwise they can keep rolling additional dice until they either stand or break.  
* After everyone has had a turn, the highest point total (excluding players who busted) wins the game and takes the pot.

### **Eye’s Out**

* Each player starts with four (4) six-sided dice.   
* Each player puts the agreed ante in the pot.  
* Each player rolls all the dice.   
* All ones (1) that are rolled are discarded.  
* Each player that wants to stay in must add another ante.  
* The last person with dice remaining wins the pot.

### **Three Pikes**

* Each player starts with five (5) six-sided dice.   
* Each player puts the agreed ante in the pot.  
* Each player rolls three (3) dice and keeps the rolls secret.   
* Play then proceeds clockwise around the table to bet, check or fold.  
  * Bet: The player raises the pot by the ante.   
  * Call:  The player must match the bets.  
  * Fold:  The player loses their ante and is out of the game.  
* All players still in the game roll an additional die, keeping the roll a secret.  
* Play then proceeds clockwise around the table to bet, check or fold.  
* All players still in the game roll their final die; again, keeping the roll secret.  
* Play then proceeds clockwise around the table to bet, check or fold.  
* Player with the best hand wins the pot determined by the following:  
  * Five of a kind  
  * Four of a kind  
  * Full house (three of one, two of another)  
  * Straight (five in a row)  
  * Three of a kind  
  * Two pair (two of one, two of another)  
  * One pair  
  * High number

### **Arm Wrestle**

* This is a Strength challenge.   
* You need three (3) steps to wrestle their arm to the table.  
* You make a contested strength check.  
  * Success: you move one (1) step closer to victory.  
  * Success (natural 20\):  you move two (2) steps closer to victory.  
  * Fail:  you lose one (1) step.

### **Dagger Juggling**

* This is a Dexterity challenge.   
* Bet is made on success, which can be done by number of rounds or by betting on each check.  
* Roll a DC check based on the number of daggers you are juggling:  
  * DC10: for 2 daggers  
  * DC15: for 4 daggers  
  * DC20: for 4 daggers  
* A failed throw caused 1d4 non-lethal damage.
