## About Alburest

Alburest perches on the eastern edge of Lochlorn, the vast lake born when the Awakening upheaved the land and swallowed a stretch of the old Deep Route. What was once a modest agricultural hub between Tratta and points west has become a city of weathered terraces and busy lakeside quays. On clear days, the faint drowned road can still be seen beneath the deep blue water. Life here moves to the rhythm of the lake—fishermen casting nets at dawn, boatwrights shaping hulls along the shore, and merchants filling the markets with goods from near and far. Alburest carries an air of contrasts: elegant manors of the well-to-do stand alongside the practical net houses and smokehouses that produce the region’s famed Lochlorn Smoked Eel, a delicacy shipped across the Yearning Vale and beyond. 

### **The People**

The diverse people of Alburest are industrious and resilient, their lives shaped by the lake that redefined their home.  Nobles and influential merchants preside from fine lakeside manors, directing much of the city’s direction and wealth. Craftsmen, traders, glassmakers, and boatwrights fill the workshops and bustling markets with skill and energy. This structure reflects Alburest’s transformation from inland grain hub to lakeside port with a shared adaptability.

### **Economy and Trade**

Alburest’s economy has fully embraced the lake that ended its old life as a grain stop. Fishermen harvest Lochlorn’s waters, boatwrights craft and repair the vessels essential for travel and transport, and glassmakers produce distinctive goods that travel well by water. The standout export is the Lochlorn Smoked Eel—prepared in the city’s smokehouses and regarded as a delicacy throughout the Yearning Vale and into Var’Shala. The quays and terraces host lively markets where goods flow in both directions, now centered on lake routes. A tight circle of powerful merchants and traders known as the Tide Ledger exerts significant control over this commerce, ensuring steady prosperity.

# Dungeon Master’s Reference

Alburest stands on the eastern edge of Lochlorn, a vast lake born of the Awakening’s upheaval that swallowed the western stretch of the Deep Route road. Once a modest agricultural hub between Tratta and Stouhg, the city now marks the end of the Deep Route, where trade wagons once bound for distant markets now find only water and horizon. From its weathered terraces and lakeside quays, Alburest gazes eastward across the deep blue expanse of Lochlorn, where the faint outline of the old road can still be seen beneath the surface on clear days.

The city’s people are industrious and divided by station—nobles presiding from lakeside manors, craftsmen and traders filling the bustling markets, and laborers working the quays and net houses that line the shore. The rise of Lochlorn transformed Alburest from a city of grain into a city of water: its famed Lochlorn Smoked Eel is now exported across the Yearning Vale and Orbansia, regarded as a delicacy of the region. Boatwrights, glassmakers, and fishermen thrive here, their livelihoods tied to the lake’s temperament as much as to trade.

Yet Alburest has not fully escaped the shadows of its past. Beneath the calm waters lie remnants of the old Deep Route and the drowned farmlands that once fed the city. Tales persist of structures still intact below the lake’s surface—ruins, roads, and even the tolling of a lost bell from a sunken chapel. Some whisper that the waters of Lochlorn hold memories of the Awakening itself, their shifting depths watched over by the city’s mercantile elite known as The Tide Ledger, who now control much of the lake’s commerce.

To travelers, Alburest is a city of contrasts: elegant and weary, bright with commerce yet heavy with mystery. It marks the end of the road and the beginning of the lake, where Myrdae’s old paths vanish beneath the reflection of a changed world.
