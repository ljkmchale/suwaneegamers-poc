## Overview

Oldport is a bustling yet weathered port town, where trade, superstition, and seafaring life dominate daily affairs. While the town thrives on commerce, it is also known for its brutal storms, treacherous currents, and lurking sea creatures, making it a place where fortune and disaster walk hand in hand. The town’s sailors, merchants, and dockhands are hardened folk, who respect the sea’s power and share legends of Osanna, the Smiling Duchess, a figure of luck and protection. The Festival of Fair Winds is a yearly event that draws crowds from across Myrdae, celebrating safe voyages and honoring the unpredictable waters. The town has a long history of shipwrecks and pirate activity, leading to a culture of both caution and reckless adventure. A DM should emphasize Oldport’s superstitions, rough tavern culture, and the ever-present tension between law-abiding traders and underhanded smugglers.

## 

## The first glimpse of Oldport is a windswept coastline, where the crash of relentless waves against weathered docks fills the air. Dozens of masts sway against the backdrop of a storm-battered but resilient town, nestled on a sloping rise along the shore. The buildings, made of aged stone and timber, are built close together, their rooftops slick from the frequent ocean mist. Large, looming lighthouse stands against the horizon, beacons guiding ships through the treacherous tides of the Tideless Span. The air is thick with the briny scent of the sea, mingled with the aroma of fish, tar, and ale drifting from the dockside taverns. Along the waterfront, sailors and traders haggle over cargo, while grizzled fishmongers call out the day’s catch. In the distance, an old, disused lighthouse stands like a solemn guardian, whispering of countless lost ships swallowed by the merciless sea.

# Religion

**Osanna**, **the Smiling Duchess**, draws the most attention of worshippers in Oldport.  There are two other gods that are worshiped frequently, **Goldrean** and **Fralee**.  There is a church to Goldrean that does have several smaller chambers that have shrines to other [gods of Myrdae](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PGWzoocfjPNQ69Q-JsVmNXCFo76a3Z_IkcBuBeDj4yQ/edit#heading=h.xprb82poeqvt). Near the center of the city is an old druidic circle that is used primarily for rituals to Fralee.

## Tavern Games

### **Bones**

(from *Descent into Avernus*)

* Each player requires several six-sided dice.   
* Each player puts the agreed ante in the pot.  
* Each player rolls three (3) dice.   
* Play then proceeds clockwise around the table, with the host of the game going last.  
* On their turn, a player can choose to “stand” or “roll.”   
  * Stand: If the player stands, the next player can take a turn.   
  * Roll:  A player who rolls takes an additional die and rolls it.   
  * Bust:  If the total of their dice exceeds 21, they “bust” and are out of the game. Otherwise they can keep rolling additional dice until they either stand or break.  
* After everyone has had a turn, the highest point total (excluding players who busted) wins the game and takes the pot.

### **Eye’s Out**

* Each player starts with four (4) six-sided dice.   
* Each player puts the agreed ante in the pot.  
* Each player rolls all the dice.   
* All ones (1) that are rolled are discarded.  
* Each player that wants to stay in must add another ante.  
* The last person with dice remaining wins the pot.

### **Three Pikes**

* Each player starts with five (5) six-sided dice.   
* Each player puts the agreed ante in the pot.  
* Each player rolls three (3) dice and keeps the rolls secret.   
* Play then proceeds clockwise around the table to bet, check or fold.  
  * Bet: The player raises the pot by the ante.   
  * Call:  The player must match the bets.  
  * Fold:  The player loses their ante and is out of the game.  
* All players still in the game roll an additional die, keeping the roll a secret.  
* Play then proceeds clockwise around the table to bet, check or fold.  
* All players still in the game roll their final die; again, keeping the roll secret.  
* Play then proceeds clockwise around the table to bet, check or fold.  
* Player with the best hand wins the pot determined by the following:  
  * Five of a kind  
  * Four of a kind  
  * Full house (three of one, two of another)  
  * Straight (five in a row)  
  * Three of a kind  
  * Two pair (two of one, two of another)  
  * One pair  
  * High number

### **Arm Wrestle**

* This is a Strength challenge.   
* You need three (3) steps to wrestle their arm to the table.  
* You make a contested strength check.  
  * Success: you move one (1) step closer to victory.  
  * Success (natural 20\):  you move two (2) steps closer to victory.  
  * Fail:  you lose one (1) step.

### **Dagger Juggling**

* This is a Dexterity challenge.   
* Bet is made on success, which can be done by number of rounds or by betting on each check.  
* Roll a DC check based on the number of daggers you are juggling:  
  * DC10: for 2 daggers  
  * DC15: for 4 daggers  
  * DC20: for 4 daggers  
* A failed throw caused 1d4 non-lethal damage.
