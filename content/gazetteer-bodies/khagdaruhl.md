## Lord Emperor’s Throne

This is the Lord Emperor's Throne Room for KhagDaruhl. Currently, it is the only place, beyond the specialized seat in the Ring of Respect, where anyone meets with Lord Emperor Thundermaul VI. In order to even be granted access to meeting him, one must go through the Ring of Respect, subsequent meetings require other trials though the Ring of Respect with varying levels of difficulty.

## Great Hall

A large great hall where different nobles can meet. This is the closest people may get to being near the Lord Emperor without a specialized meeting. There are nearly 100 pillars here, holding up the ceiling. Each has different decorative carvings depicting all sorts of dwarven lore, art, passions, wars, and anything and everything in between. However, a large portion of them are just dwarven runes to fill the space.

## Market

Less of an actual place, and more of a generalized trading-hub, the market sits in the “shopping” district of Khagdaruhl. This is where different locals, merchants, miners, and hunters all meet in different areas to sell goods, homemade items, or little trinkets. The market runs effectively daily at all hours of the day as different dwarfs are on a different clock given that they have no outside access to the sun. Sometimes, vendors have goods from outside the city, but those are usually bought or traded quickly as most dwarfs don't make it outside.

## Rusty Scales

Rusty Scales is the premiere leatherworking store for Khagdaruh located in the “shopping” district. While there is not normal access to hides of animals in the above ground world, Rusty Scales primarily focuses on procuring and selling hides and scales of beasts from mountain passes and the underdark. Due to the proximity near the main gate, many a guard come through here to get tuned up on their leather goods before they might go on an expedition out into the Yag’vur Sierra.

## Volcanic Gems

Volcanic Gems is a bit of a richer shop given its location in the “shopping” district of Khagdaruhl. The shop is noted for having the largest collection of gems in all of the Yag’vur Sierra, aside from the Lord Emperor of course. The artistry of the work is performed by one Tyrim Silverhand and his varying apprentices who come and go. Tyrim is a lesser noble who is more popular with the lower city dwarfs of Khagdaruhl as he takes time and offers to teach any dwarf who wants to apprentice under him. This usually does not come with any troubles, but he has been known to keep a few dwarfs as guards to watch.

## On the Mantle

On the Mantle, sometimes just referred to as “Mantles” is a general store of basic goods for citizens and, rarely, travelers in Khagdaruhl. Located in the “shopping” district, Mantles is one of the only stores that citizens can count on being open and selling the same specific goods without having to rely on the gamble that is the market. While it does not always have the most attention, this humble store is well received and made sure to be bought to keep open.

## Graffys Grub

Graffys Grub is a well-loved tavern located in the “shopping” district of Khagdaruhl. Known for allowing any dwarf in without a care in the world, no matter how smelly, drunk, or short they are, Graffys Grub is always a busy establishment. One can find almost any dwarf in the city in this place at a point in time as they never let the drinks run out. Graffys is run by the Cefdurt family, hilldwarfs who won the place over from the last owners, when they wish to pass it along, they will raffle it off to any dwarf.

## Main Gate

The gate of Khagdaruhl is the only entrance and primary exit for the mountain city. At all times, the gate is guarded by a dozen dwarfs, some who stay out of sight, as they fear any threats from the outside or any threats from the inside from leaving. It is usually overseen by Doldram Brawnbeard, a hill dwarf who also is the sole dwarf who decides when the gate opens or shuts, besides the higher ranking members of the Khagdaruhl hierarchy. The gate has a gatehouse, where administrative tasks, minor disputes, and expedition planning are handled.

## Roasts Head

Roasts Head is a high end tavern located in the “entertainment” district of Khagdaruhl. Similar to Graffys, Roasts Head is always busy, but that is mainly because of its prime location in the happiest district of the city. Name gets especially busy after a Ring fight, as people are high off of the energy of the fight. There is also a special depending on which champion wins. or if a champion loses… It's usually a good time regardless.  Roasts Head is run by the Strobrack family of dwarfs, and it is usually seen as the nigher of the two main taverns.
