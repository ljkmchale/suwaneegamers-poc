Mistforge

# Overview

On most days, Mistforge is covered in a light misty fog.  The buildings in Mistforge are built from logs, almost log cabin style, with clay and moss in the cracks to insulate them.  Most are all the same size, with a few that stick out.  The buildings are surrounded by a large wall of logs standing upright and with tops cut to sharp points.  Two guards are always on duty in the small village, operating out of Centerwatch (see \#4).

# Notable Locations

## Bearhall (communal hall)

Bearhall is a huge communal hall where everyone gathers, with a huge loft that has places for people to rest.  There is always a fire there, with the latest hunted meat, vegetables, and fruits available.  There is no cost to eat, but there is a small open chest by the door where people donate to go into the village funds.

## Fallaway (docks)

Small boats are usually available for rent to help with travel to and from Lasdale.  A boat can accompany 4 passengers.  To rent a boat to Lasdale, it costs 5 silver, plus 1 silver per rider.  Lanion works with the docks down the river to rent and return boats as needed.
