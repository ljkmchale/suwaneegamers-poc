# **Overview**

O’naren is a small elven settlement in the wooded foothills west of Qal’dynn, serving as both a waypoint for travelers and a place of quiet contemplation. Its finely carved stone buildings blend seamlessly with the gray-green forest, reflecting the elven reverence for nature. The town is centered around **Driftglow Pond**, a pristine circular body of water that catches the cascading falls from the hills. At its heart lies a small island, crowned by an ancient tree with striking pink foliage—believed to be a relic of the **Qal’daefar**, the original elves of Myrdae.

While primarily home to elves, O’naren welcomes a small number of non-elven residents who have earned the trust of the community. The town is known for its measured pace, a place where art, philosophy, and nature intertwine. O’naren is ruled by **Lord Itham Elle’neros**, a noble loyal to High King Artes Highvale of Qal’dynn. Under his governance, O’naren remains a peaceful settlement, valued for its skilled craftsmen, lorekeepers, and as a rest stop for those traveling the road between Qal’dynn and Raselle.

Although O’naren remains largely undisturbed, its deep-rooted traditions and elven heritage mean that outsiders must tread carefully, as respect and etiquette are highly valued. Those seeking wisdom, guidance, or merely a reprieve from the chaos of the world will find O’naren a place of quiet refuge—so long as they honor the elven way.

## **History**

O’naren was originally established as a small elven outpost to the west of Qal'dynn, designed as a defensive point to help protect the larger elven city from potential threats emerging from the western territories. The foothills surrounding the outpost proved rich in ore, and mining efforts soon began, providing valuable materials for weapons, tools, and trade. The region’s abundant wildlife also allowed the settlement to thrive, ensuring a steady supply of food. Over time, O’naren evolved from a simple outpost into a well-developed and respected elven town, known for its balance of natural harmony, resourcefulness, and strategic location. While no longer a military post, it remains an important settlement in the Borealian Sway, serving as both a waypoint for travelers and a vital provider of resources to its neighboring elven communities.

### **Style and Aesthetic**

The elves of O’naren wear elegant, flowing clothes made from fine, natural materials.  Elven men typically wear long, flowing tunics or robes, often belted at the waist with finely embroidered or beaded sashes. These robes may feature intricate patterns that reflect natural elements, like leaves or vines, woven into the fabric.  Female elves often wear long, flowing dresses or tunics that are richly adorned with delicate embroidery or stitching along the edges, often incorporating nature-themed motifs.

## **Holidays and Traditions**

### **The Verdant Oath (Celebration of Fralee)**

The Verdant Oath is an annual festival held in late spring to honor Fralee, the goddess of peace, harmony, and the land's vitality. On this day, the people of O’naren gather at Driftglow Pond, where offerings of flowers, woven garlands, and harvested herbs are placed at the base of the ancient tree on the island in the pond. A ceremonial oath is recited by the priest of Fralee, where the townsfolk reaffirm their commitment to protecting nature and living in balance with the world around them. The festival includes dances, music, and shared feasts, with a special emphasis on fruits and greens harvested from the land. In the evening, floating lanterns are set adrift upon Driftglow Pond, symbolizing gratitude for Fralee’s blessings and a wish for continued prosperity.

### **The Hunt of Renewal (O’naren’s Seasonal Festival)**

Held in early autumn, the Hunt of Renewal is a tradition unique to O’naren, celebrating the town’s deep connection to nature and the balance of life. It marks the changing of the seasons, recognizing both the bounty of the forest and the necessity of responsible stewardship. A ceremonial hunt is held in the surrounding foothills, where skilled hunters and foragers gather food for a grand communal feast. However, the hunt follows strict guidelines: only what is needed is taken, and offerings of thanks are left in the forest as a sign of respect to the land. The celebration concludes with an evening surrounding the Driftglow, where elders and hunters share stories of past hunts, survival, and the wisdom of the wild, reinforcing the town’s deep-rooted bond with the natural world.

## **Government**

O’naren is governed by Lord Itham Elle’neros, a respected and wise ruler who values harmony and balance in all aspects of life. He makes decisions with careful consideration, often seeking the counsel of his wife, Lady Naleana, and the town’s spiritual guide, Priest Mathaed Skymane, who serves as a devoted priest of Fralee. The town operates with a sense of communal well-being, where diplomacy and understanding guide leadership rather than strict enforcement of laws. The Realmwardens, under Captain Salaal Windseek, ensure order while respecting the tranquility of O’naren.

## **Economy and Trade**

O’naren’s economy is built on a foundation of resource gathering, craftsmanship, and trade, sustaining itself through a balance of mining, hunting, and artisanal goods. The foothills surrounding the town provide iron and rare minerals, which are refined into tools, weapons, and ornamental metalwork by local elven smiths. The dense forests yield hardwood, game, and natural dyes, supporting skilled bowyers, fletchers, and leather workers who craft goods both for local use and trade.

While largely self-sufficient, O’naren trades regularly with Qal’dynn, sending raw materials and finished products in exchange for luxury goods, fine textiles, and magical components. The town’s artisans are especially known for their intricately designed weaponry and armor, featuring nature-inspired engravings and elven craftsmanship. Seasonal herbs and alchemical ingredients from the surrounding wilderness are also gathered and sold to passing merchants, making O’naren a valuable stop for those in need of rare natural resources.

## **Religion**

O’naren’s spiritual heart is devoted to Fralee, the goddess of tranquility, renewal, and natural harmony. The town’s strong connection to nature and its history of peaceful prosperity align closely with Fralee’s teachings, making her worship an integral part of daily life. Many elves and other residents incorporate small prayers or rituals into their routines, whether offering thanks at dawn or meditating beneath the boughs of sacred trees. Seasonal ceremonies mark the shifting cycles of the land, reinforcing a communal reverence for balance and renewal.

The Temple of Tranquility serves as the town’s primary place of worship, where Priest Mathaed Skymane and his four acolytes guide the people in prayer, meditation, and ceremonies. While Fralee’s worship encourages personal reflection, it also fosters communal gatherings that bring the people of O’naren together. Visitors to the town are welcome to seek guidance or participate in the temple’s meditations, reinforcing O’naren’s reputation as a sanctuary of peace and wisdom.

# **Notable Locations**

## **Commons Area**

The Commons Area is a tranquil clearing on the first terrace in the southeastern part of O’naren, positioned on one side of the river. At its center stands a large stone gazebo, crafted with smooth, carved pillars and an open design that allows for a clear view of the cascading falls that feed the **Driftglow** pond. The area is used for various gatherings, from meditation and celebrations to public announcements and town discussions. The location provides a peaceful atmosphere for residents to connect, through contemplation, community events, or just to enjoy the beauty of O’naren.

## 

## As you step into the Commons Area, the sound of rushing water blends with the gentle breeze that rustles through the trees. A grand stone gazebo stands at the center of the clearing, its open pillars framing a wonderful view of the falls that tumble down into the Driftglow pond below. The space feels calm yet welcoming, where townspeople and visitors alike gather for quiet meditation, festive gatherings, or the occasional town speech. The air is fresh, carrying the scent of moss and water, making this a perfect place to pause and take in the beauty of O’naren.

## **Evonstride Stables**

The Evonstride Stables serve as the primary care facility for horses and mounts in O’naren. Attached to the Evonstride family home, the stables are well-maintained, with intricately carved wooden walls supported by sturdy stone columns. A fenced-in running area behind the structure allows for training and exercise, ensuring that the horses remain in excellent condition. The business is now run primarily by Maenari and Adryss Evonstride, as their father, Balalean, has mostly retired. Areysa and Erendaar, though still living in the home, have devoted themselves to their roles as acolytes of Fralee and do not participate in the stable’s operations. Maenari and Adryss are both skilled farriers, providing exceptional care, shoeing, and training services for visitors and locals alike. Despite the loss of their mother years ago, the family remains close-knit and deeply respected within the community.

## 

## Nestled against the gently sloping foothills of O’naren, the Evonstride Stables stand as a mix of stone and finely crafted woodwork. The barn is attached directly to the Evonstride home. A fenced running area extends behind the structure, where several horses graze or train under careful supervision. Inside the stables, rows of clean stalls house well-groomed mounts, while a small forge in the corner is used for crafting horseshoes and maintaining tack.

## **Hall of O’naren**

The Hall of O’naren serves as the center of governance for the town and the seat of Lord Itham Elle’neros. The structure consists of a large circular central chamber flanked by two rectangular wings. The main hall is a spacious, open forum where official town gatherings, judicial proceedings, and diplomatic discussions are held. The left wing houses meeting chambers where the lord and his advisors convene, while the right wing contains a dining hall used for hosting important visitors. Beneath the hall, a secured dungeon holds prisoners in a series of well-maintained cells, primarily used for those awaiting trial or exile. The daily affairs of the hall are managed by Attendant **Cadlas Saj’esuru**, a meticulous and authoritative figure who ensures the smooth operation of governance. While the hall is often a place of diplomacy and order, it is also where security matters are discussed with the Realmwardens, and where disputes among townsfolk are mediated.

## **Mines**

The mines of O’naren are located on the southwest side of town, where a cave entrance opens into the rocky foothills. The mines have long provided the town with high-quality stone and ore, contributing to the settlement’s economy and craftsmanship. The entrance is situated on the second terrace, accessible via a well-worn path leading up from the lower terraces. Outside the cave, a circular stone building serves as the director’s office, where mining operations are managed, records are kept, and workers check in at the start and end of their shifts. The mine itself extends deep into the foothills, with carved tunnels leading to various excavation sites.

## **Oracle**

The Oracle, Zanasee Tsal’daree, is known as the Weavereader, a magic user with deep ties in the Tegenwald Phet.  The Weavereader’s home is a circular two-story structure on the upper terrace of O’naren, located on the south side of town. The main floor of the sanctuary is spacious, arranged with various sitting areas that serve both for private counsel and small gatherings. Subtle arcane motifs are woven into the tapestries and carvings, a nod to her mystical insight. A large decorative rug at the center of the chamber conceals a secret entrance to a hidden chamber below, where she maintains an Arcane Waypoint—a fully functional teleportation circle known only to those she trusts. The upper floor serves as her private study and sleeping quarters, filled with ancient tomes, scrolls, and relics tied to her divinations.

The Weavereader is a known sage and seer, offering guidance to those who seek her wisdom. However, she is also an elusive figure, her knowledge often coming at a cost, requiring seekers to bring something of value—a story, an offering, or an experience—in return for her insights. Those familiar with arcane lore may recognize that her sanctuary’s construction and enchantments subtly reflect her association with the Phet, though she does not openly advertise this connection.

## **Ore Masters**

The Ore Masters is the collective name for three connected and meticulously kept buildings operated by the Tu'osaar siblings, whose lineage is renowned for producing some of the finest smiths and jewel-crafters in Myrdae. Situated just to the left of the mine entrance, these buildings house an armorsmith, a jeweler, and a weaponsmith, each managed by a Tu'osaar sibling. Despite the nature of their work, the interiors are immaculate.  Grand corridors link the three shops, showcasing some of their finest works on pedestals or within glass cases.

### **Armorsmith** 

The armorsmith’s building is closest to the mines and features a forge, anvil, and a well-stocked display of fine elven armor. Suonot specializes in elegantly crafted protective gear, ensuring each piece is not only functional but also artistically refined. His clientele includes local Realmwardens and visiting elven warriors, as well as those seeking custom orders tailored to elven battle techniques.

### **Jeweler**

The central building belongs to Easéthe, who specializes in gemstone cutting, engraving, and crafting fine jewelry. He is highly sought after by nobles and traders who pass through O’naren. His workspace is filled with delicate instruments, gemstone samples, and finely wrought metal settings.

### **Weaponsmith**

The last shop, furthest from the mines, is the weaponsmithing forge run by Siasel. The walls are lined with fine elven blades, spears, bows, and polearms, each balanced to perfection. She is widely respected for her knowledge of traditional elven weaponry.
