# **Overview**

Hesfal is a quaint hamlet located on the western edge of the Yearning Vale territory on the trade route called the Tor March.  With roughly 250 residents who call it home, it is a peaceful and prosperous colony full of artists and artisans, diverse residents of all ages, and a welcome stop for caravans and travelers.

## 

## A quaint stop on the Tor March, Hesfal is a  community which boasts a large market and craft grotto where artisans sell their wares and train others in their trade.  The area attracts artists and entertainers of all descriptions, as well as travelers from all over Myrdae who come for the rejuvenating spa and picturesque views. 

## **History**

Hesfal began as a single rest stop — a roadside forge and trading post for caravans crossing the Yearning Vale — its location between major cities a natural waypoint. Over time, inspired by the surroundings, artisans realized the value of settling here to sell wares directly to traders, birthing a permanent artisan’s hamlet.  Those who put down roots here—smiths, glassblowers, potters, and weavers—pledged to sell fair, share resources, and teach apprentices without fear of theft or guild reprisal. Their craftwork soon gained a reputation for quality and honesty, and Hesfal became a neutral haven for artisans escaping the restrictions of larger city guilds.  Over time, it has grown into a niche destination for those seeking solace from city-life, a place many will come to stay for a few days to a stretch or two. 

## **Citizenry**

While most of the locals are long term tenants, descendants from the original settlers, many artisans in their own right, the village has attracted a cult-like following of would-be artists as well, people looking to hone their crafts or groupies of those wishing to follow them.  All enjoy selling their goods or teaching their craft to passersby, including caravans, hunters, or other travelers along the Tor March.

All are welcome in this hamlet, as the arts seem to attract all kinds.  The area prides itself on peace and harmony, adopting the slogan:  “living, learning, and loving as nature intended.”

## **Holidays and Traditions**

No additional holidays are observed, outside of those typically found in Myrdae.  

## **Government**

The hamlet is run by a Council, consisting of five representatives, called Councilors, and an elected Mayor.  The group meets in the Town Ward, a small centrally located building, just off the Tor March.  The Ward houses a couple of rarely used cells where individuals are put when the order is disturbed, as well a large conference room with a group of doors that open to a large patio that can serve as an open-air auditorium when needed.  

The current Mayor is Estufus Windseek, and the five representatives (who are nominated and/or volunteer for 2 year terms) are:  Nova Hightrail, Ralyn Sunflare, Othen Stonemight, Ustrum Smithson, and Horvil Black.

### **Estufus (Stu) Windseek, Mayor**

## 

## An older elf with salt and pepper hair and a close-cropped beard of the same shade, Stu is a retired Realmwarden from O’naren.  Lean and still muscular, he moved from the Borealian Sway five years ago with his wife Olanda, an artist, to have a simple life.  He is a year into a four-year term, enjoying the simplicity of a peace-loving town and the diversity of a thriving artists’ hamlet, which keeps his wife happy. Their son, Salaal, is a captain in O’naren. 

### **Hesfal Reserves**

## 

## The town has a small group of volunteer militia, called the Hesfal Reserves who help keep the peace, and who, along with the Council, handle disagreements, squirmishes, and other disturbances.  Because of the proximity of the village to Scarwatch Hold, which houses a formal militia, serious problems \- which are rare \- are referred to the Hold.  Members of the Reserves are identified by a dark green sash they wear when needed or on duty.  When needed, they operate out of the Town Ward.

## **Artisan’s Exchange**

The hamlet is a thriving artists’ colony, where creators can hone their skills, train others, and sell their wares.  The **Artisan’s Exchange** is a huge, open air meadow with dozens of stalls that can be rented from the Council Ward for a stretch for 5sp (or 1sp/day for those passing through).  Prices for items are set by those who rent the stalls and the items vary from stretch to stretch or even day to day.

In addition to buying, selling, and trading, many artisans \- particularly those who live in the community \- also spend time training others in their craft.  In an area called **Hands Shape Stone**, located behind the Exchange, there are forges, furnaces, and kilns set up for glass blowing, pottery making, and simple smithing, as well as areas for painting and sketching.  Each maker, often a master in their trade, sets their own prices for training and materials.

There are no formal guilds in town, per the desires of the hamlet residents.  In fact, many have moved to Hesfel to escape the rules and formality associated with guilds and rules of larger towns and cities.

## **Boar’s Best (Inn & Tavern)**

Boar’s Best Inn and Tavern \- which is never closed and serves meals all day long \- is the heart of evening life in Hesfal—an establishment equally known for its food, performances, and a generous measure of mischief. Built into a sloping hillside, its street-level entrance descends to a lively cellar floor where the tavern proper comes alive each night. Round tables crowd the wooden hall, illuminated by lanterns hung from iron hooks and a broad chandelier made from a wagon wheel. The lower level houses the performance stage, bar, and game tables—where music, poetry, and the occasional magical performance fill the space, giving Boar’s Best its reputation as “what happens at the Boar, stays at the Boar.”

The main floor offers ten guest rooms of simple but comfortable design, while the top floor features four regional suites—each decorated after places the owner fancies: The Seaman’s cove (nautical theme), The Rongri’s Nest (jungle), The Goblin’s Den (cave-like), and The Sky’s Delight (modeled after Tudara \- the Azure moon). The inn is owned and operated by Gorin Redmane, a gregarious dwarf who prides himself on pairing fine food and good company. His wife, Yvenna, runs the kitchen and oversees performers with the discipline of a seasoned bard.

## **Bru’s Beastie Barn**

At the northern edge of Hesfal lies Bru's Beastie Barn, a whimsical and well-loved menagerie run by the eccentric but brilliant beast-master, Bru. This is no ordinary stable \- it's a home for creatures that range from the mundane to the magnificent.

In addition, Bru has a small workshop where he tinkers with inventions. One corner is dedicated to a farrier's forge where he crafts horseshoes and other metalwork. Another area is filled with bubbling concoctions and vials of glowing liquids, which he uses to create salves and potions for animals.

## 

## Just off the Tor March on the north side of the hamlet is the stables, a patchwork of recycled and scavenged materials, giving it a cozy, ramshackle charm. A crooked, cobbled path leads to the large, double-doors, which are carved with cheerful images of animals.

## **Covered Hooch**

The Covered Hooch is a rustic yurt campground on the west side of the Tor March in the southernmost section of the hamlet of Hesfal.  It offers travelers a comfortable and communal place to rest beneath the trees. The name comes from the variety of trees—willow, oak, and shadepine—that provide both natural cover and plentiful firewood. The campground is operated by Ravina Rool, a stout, middle-aged human woman known for her practicality and sharp wit. She manages the yurts, coordinates firewood gathering, and brews a signature herbal tea known locally as “Hooch Juice.” The Hooch also serves as an informal gathering place for hunters, trappers, and the occasional bard, where stories and local news are exchanged around large communal fires.

Ravina employs a few helpers from Hesfal who maintain the yurts and tend to guests, ensuring each has warmth, privacy, and a sense of belonging. The Hooch is also known as a neutral ground—local disputes are left outside the circle of trees, and travelers are expected to share the fire in peace.

## **Hall of Many Hands**

The only religious facility in Hesfal is an open air temple where all deities can be worshipped.  From the outside, the temple appears to be a majestic but weathered structure, its white marble and stone bearing the marks of time. Upon closer inspection, however, pilgrims will notice the walls are covered in a complex mosaic of artwork, a breathtaking riot of color and iconography, with each deity represented in their own unique style.

The graffiti is maintained not by priests or caretakers but by the faithful themselves. The temple's walls are a continuous, dynamic work of art, constantly being refreshed and added to.  A group of local artist-monks oversee the temple's artistic integrity. They don't remove older artwork but instead integrate new pieces, and occasionally re-patina or subtly alter older pieces to accommodate newer gods or recontextualize older ones.

## **Moonfern Sanctuary**

Moonfern Sanctuary is an upscale retreat nestled at the northeastern side of Hesfal, just south of the Sun Stork River, built around a cluster of natural hot springs and mineral-rich mud pools fed by underground vents. The Sanctuary’s name comes from the silver-veined ferns that grow in abundance near the springs—plants that shimmer faintly under moonlight and are believed to carry mild restorative properties.

The establishment serves all who desire luxury and renewal after long journeys along the Tor March, though for many travelers, this is a destination worth seeking. Operations are overseen by Alarine Joest, a refined wood elf healer who blends herbal alchemy with traditional bathing rituals. Her staff maintain immaculate grounds of carved stone basins, wooden walkways, and candle-lit bathing chambers. Patrons are encouraged to rest, meditate, and partake in therapeutic treatments prepared from local herbs and minerals drawn from the surrounding Vale.

### **Services Provided**

**Accommodations** \- on the main floor of this facility, there are five two-person suites available for 5 sp/night, minimum 2-nights reserved. Includes a basket of fruits and grains for breakfast. At any given time, at least 3 of the rooms are likely in use by the traveling elite.   Guests of the suites are given priority for scheduling spa services.

All spa services are a la carte.

**Massage (standard/stone)** \- accessed by stairs from the central greeting room, a rooftop grotto with various tables for massage are available by day and night.  A one hour massage is 2sp.

**Massage (mud)** \- if one would like to try a more healing-focused session, therapeutic mineral mud is used to help with recovery from fatigue or even mid poisons.  This one hour session is 4sp.

**Bath (clear springs)** \- on the private and secluded grounds, there are several natural clear springs available for baths.  A fresh towel is provided and guests may bathe with or without clothing.  A one hour bath is 2sp.

**Bath (mud)** \- following a narrow stone pathway down from the main area lies a large oval shaped mud pool that bubbles as faint smoke rises from the surface.  Two towels are provided to each guest who is encouraged to partake of this service with no clothing.  Guests are limited to 30 minute sessions (at 2sp), but may indulge up to 3 times per day.  A natural spring is adjacent to this area to rinse off guests before they head back upstairs.

**Lunar Bath Session** (clear or mud) – A private ritual offered only during the periods of Salvus \- when both moons are full (which happens 4 times a year); accompanied by harp music and incense, granting a faint boon to Wisdom saving throws until dawn.  Rates during this period are 5sp/bath.

**Whispered Silence Charm** – A service enchantment woven into the baths; conversations held within cannot be overheard beyond ten paces, ensuring discretion for merchants and nobles.  1sp/extra per patron.

**Moonfern Elixir** – A tonic brewed from silver-veined fern and springwater; restores vitality and calms nerves for 1 hour.  This is served in the rooftop grotto in a seating area opposite the massage area.  2sp/serving.

Midday and evening meals are usually taken at Boar’s by guests, but delivery services for such meals are available from the staff here for an additional fee of 2sp/meal.
