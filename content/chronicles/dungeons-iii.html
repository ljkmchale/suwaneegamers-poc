It’s Eye’on!

Hesfal: 9th of Evalee, 1246 (morning)

The campaign kicked off with the members of the party in Hesfal, a hamlet in the Yearning Vale Territory.

(ada, yada, yada … “fall”)

Intro for Player 1 - Æon

Æon awakens at the Moonfern Sanctuary and discusses activities of the day with Ilyana, the masseuse. As Æon mentions he is looking for something to do - and might be interested in speaking to others about their gods, Ilyana tells him about the temple in Hesfal named the “Hall of Many Hands”, which sounds promising to Æon. She also mentions that Salvus is tomorrow and there are many people expected to arrive in Hesfal today - so he will need to pay his tab. He goes to speak with Alarine (owner, Ilyana’s mother) and schedules a bath and massage and pays his bill for 2 more nights. (Double full moon! What does it mean?)

Intro for Players 2, 3 and 4

(the dorks are acquainted)

Draelith, Nixie, and Meles are acquainted at the Covered Hooch.

Draelith, 6’ and skinny human, with fair skin, blonde hair. He wears a muted blue pilgrim-like outfit, a steel headband and carries a short dark wood staff. (alambre? Ambre? Inordinately fond of olives. Like, seriously)

Nixie the svirneblin (gnome) is 3’10”, which is tall for a gnome, with big eyes. He wears leather, and has a staff. He is quiet, naive and awkward. Since arriving on the surface, he appears to be starstruck. (Reaching for the stars!)

Meles is a Wood Elf, 5’11’, thin, gaunt, jade brown eyes, coppering blonde ponytail. He wears dark leather armor (very nicely maintained armor), carries a short bow, scimitar with carefully placed leather ties to cover sound and reflection. He’s very relaxed. (Cute little ribbons everywhere)

Intro for Player 5 - Seraphine Veyne

Since her arrival 2 days ago, Seraphine (also referred to as Sera) has been walking around the Covered Hooch, a large camping area in Hesfal. She is tending to people who are burned or scratched. Seraphine is a 47 year old aasamir, that is 5’5”. She is sinewy, fit but not weird fit, with pale amber eyes and silver hair. Light dances on her skin, but you won’t see it since she keeps her covered. She is a healer that is very curious about injuries and wounds. She has a symbol of Natafae hanging from Surgical Wire. (Seraphine is a nosy female … 47 YO Ass)

In the Covered Hooch, Draelith shared a yurt with the following people:

  • Radorg
  • Bridoro

These two are firbolgs; brothers from Greenvale that are selling olives and olive oil, which Draelith ends up buying (oil has special properties that keep it from producing heat or smoke).

An individual named Larx from the flats area of Hesfal is roaming around camp, offering healing stuff. It ends up being moonfern weed, which has calming properties (increases vitality). Meles buys some (1 bag with 5 “servings” for 1sp) and starts smoking it.

A female half elf came in named Mimony. She heals for coin and says “Don’t believe the weed guy!” She is a traveling healer, having just served a couple of harmons at the Scarwatch Hold, but has been traveling for over 20 years and is ready to head home again. (My Money! from New Jersey)

Seraphine quizzes her about the cases she has handled over the years (gross stuff), such as headless half orcs, upset stomachs. Seraphine has seen it all and mentions seeing “Bodies falling from the sky!” They discuss the terror and destruction of the Awakening. Sera says she can heal, and that she has been changed. (WKRP in Myrdae)

Mimony says she’s heading to Basctdelm, She said she’s taking a short cut and mentions an Arcane Waypoint at the Anchored Spire in Tratta. She tells Sera to look her up if she’s ever in Basctdelm. (Wayfender the Tree!)

Æon heads to the Hall of Many Hands that morning, removing his spa toga and getting dressed. The Hall of Many Hands has graffiti to the gods. At the temple, he notices two people: a small female halfling, with chalk, sketching - female face, moon - and a guy meditating. Æon approaches the halfling and they strike up a conversation about the sketching. The halfling is Kadella Tenbrook from the Boar’s Best tavern and inn. They speak in depth about the sketch that represents the goddess Osanna, as Æon reveals he is looking for the right god to follow. Æon mentions that he followed Muerg, but that didn’t do it for him. Kadella sells Æon on Osanna (serendipity, needing to do good works and spread joy). (no crazy knives)

Æon approaches the second person, a young handsome potter in a long shirt and sandals. The guy is not so friendly, maybe because he was interrupted during meditation, but reveals himself to be someone named Laestrik. Laestrik is a follower of Goldraen. (Emo? Lazy? Not so friendly … in a Sundress)

Draelith, Meles and Nixie enter the Hall of Many hands and notice Æon (and overhear the conversations). We get a description of Æon. Æon is not pretty boy good looking, but scoundrel charming. He is a high elf, well, actually maybe half elf at best. He has dirty blonde hair, grey eyes, medium build, strong and is tough as nails. He is a sell-sword, ex-soldier with a broken moral compass that often goes awry. Never too far from a brothel or a chapel.

(“It’s pronounced Æon not Ion”)

Draelith speaks to the group about Celestine, explaining to Kadella why Celestine is important to him. After he draws a crude harp in chalk on the walls, he reveals that his parents thought she was his destined goddess because of a “harp-shaped” birthmark on his shoulder that he shows the group. This prompts Æon to show his horrific back scar (from being “cut in half” by an orc) and he talks about the “angel that healed him.” Kadella suggests maybe he should worship that angel (or her god/goddess), but he doesn’t know who she was. She makes another pitch for Osanna.

After some time, Æon goes back to Moonfern Sanctuary. There he tries his charm on Ilyana, trying with prayers to Osanna. The prayers go unanswered, so he sticks with the massage and dip in the steam pools (she accompanies him, but keeps it professional).

Meanwhile, at the Covered Hooch, a dwarf limps out of a Yurt, complaining about injuries … “Ma leg!”. Seraphine approaches to assist the dwarf, who reveals himself to be Wargus from the Scarwatch Hold (he is retiring because of his injuries). Seraphine attempts to help Wargus, and has some success with her healing hands. Wargus asks Seraphine to travel with him back to his home in Dunduar - says he can protect her since she is traveling alone. He warns her of a rumor that there was a murder of a young woman a few days ago. While Seraphine is declining his offer, a beautiful dark skin elven female approaches, and echoes the rumor. The elven female is named Saquel and adds that she heard it was a “Love connection gone sour”. A cute half-elf (named Gimdy) who has been relaxing, and catching rays in the Covered Hooch, claims “No, it was just straight up murder!” Gimdy adds that the guy was already caught and shouldn’t be an issue since he’s being held at the Town Ward. (Drama about the event! In the woods nearby? What!) Wargus tells Seraphine that if she ever goes there to look for Liquin in the Scarwatch Hold, he is a healer that may have a journal of his practice that she’d be interested in.

Draelith, Nixie and Meles went to the Artisan Exchange. Draelith found a vendor selling gems and trinkets; a gnome named Irick. Draelith explained that he’s looking for a “falling star” to affix to his headband. Irick calls over an artist to sketch out the headband (to scale). The artist is named Gimri, another gnome. Gimri became interested in Nixie and asked Nixie to describe the stars so that she could sketch and paint his words. As Nixie is telling his story, Draelith visualizes his words as stars floating above Nixie’s head using the Minor Illusion cantrip. Gimri sold Nixie the painting for 5 cp. Meles spends his time at the Exchange staring at a wooden wall.

After a bit, the three go back to the Hooch and reconnect with Sera. They tell her about their afternoon and Meles mentions that they met a guy with a nasty scar - since she seems to like that. They told her they invited him to the Hooch later. About that time, in strolls Æon and Sera is surprised, but calls him by name (she doesn’t forget patients apparently). Æon recognizes her and the two catch up quickly. Sera shows the group a sketch in her journal from 10 years before where she initially treated him and they compare the picture of the fresh wound with how it has healed now.

As they discuss plans for the night, they are interrupted by Frath - one of the attendants at the Hooch - yelling for people to make way. Ravina Rool (the Camp Leader) joins in and says this is the last big caravan of folks arriving for Salvus so everyone needs to claim their spots because it is about to get very crowded.
